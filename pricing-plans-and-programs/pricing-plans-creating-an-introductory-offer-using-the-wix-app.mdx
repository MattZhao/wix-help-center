---
title: "Pricing Plans: Creating an Introductory Offer Using the Wix App"
---

Let customers get to know your business before they commit to a full plan. You can offer:

**A free trial period: **Add a free trial period at the beginning of any recurring plans (e.g. 1 week free before you pay for the entire duration).  **A free plan: **Create a free plan that clients can purchase only once.

### To create an introductory offer:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Pricing Plans** under **Catalog**.  Tap **Create a Plan / Create New Plan**.  Create a plan with any of the available options.  Choose what you want to do:

### Create a recurring plan that starts with a free trial period

1. Scroll down and enable the **Free Trial** toggle.

[image: Screenshot showing how to add a free trial at the start of a recurring plan on the Wix Owner app.]
2. Enter the number of days the free trial lasts.
3. Complete the plan and tap **Save**.

### Create a free plan that clients can book only once

**Note: **Clients who try to purchase the free plan a second time receive a notice that the plan can only be purchased once.

1. Scroll down and enable the **Single Purchase Only** toggle.
2. Complete the plan and tap **Save**.
