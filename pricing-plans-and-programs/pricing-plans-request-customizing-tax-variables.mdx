---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Customizing Tax Variables"
---

Currently, you can [set one fixed tax rate](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-setting-up-tax-collection) for all of your plans. It is not possible to customize different tax variables (e.g. tax per state, country, or different tax groups).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
