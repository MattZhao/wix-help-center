---
title: "Wix Loyalty Program Request: Automatically Adjusting Points Balances When Refunds Are Issued"
---

Currently, it is not possible to have a customer's loyalty points balance automatically adjusted when they are issued a refund.

However, you can [manually set a customer's points balance](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-managing-your-customers-loyalty-points).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
