---
title: "Online Programs: Reordering Your Programs From the Wix App"
---

You can reorder your programs from the Wix App. Draw attention to specific programs by placing them first, or list them in the order they're happening. You can change the order again at any time.

### To reorder your programs from the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap the **Site & App **icon .  Tap **Customize** under Members App.   Tap **Online Programs** under **Edit Navigation Tabs**.  Tap **Edit Programs List**.

Tap **Customize Section**.  Under the **Sorting** section, select the **Select Manually** checkbox.  Tap **Open Display Settings**.  Tap and drag a program to reorder it in the list. The programs automatically rearrange themselves on the page.  Tap **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also reorder or hide your programs from the Editor. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-reordering-and-hiding-your-programs-from-the-program-list)
