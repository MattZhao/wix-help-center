---
title: "Online Programs: Importing a Step from an Existing Program"
---

Save time when adding content to a new program by importing steps from an existing program. This makes it easy for you to keep the settings of the imported steps and make edits to them as needed.

### To import a step:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant program you want to import steps to.  Scroll down to the **Content **/ **Schedule** section and click **+ Add**.  Click **Import Step**.  Click the **Select a program** drop-down and choose the program you want to import steps from.  Click the **Select a step** drop-down and choose the step you want to import.  Click **Add & Edit.

Tip: **You can also click **Create New** to add a new step from scratch.  (Optional) Edit the content of the imported step to suit your needs.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can import a [Quiz](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-and-managing-a-quiz) as a separate step, bringing in the entire quiz or survey. However, it is not possible to import specific quiz or survey questions.
