---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Creating Recurring Plans with Variable Pricing"
---

Currently, when you create a recurring plan, the plan holder is charged the same amount every payment cycle (e.g. $100 every month). It is not possible to create a plan where, for example, the first cycle costs more and then the price is reduced (e.g. $200 for the first month and then $100 every month).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
