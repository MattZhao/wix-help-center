---
title: "Online Programs: Removing a Program"
---

You can archive or delete online programs that you no longer need.

### To remove a program:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions **icon   next to the relevant program.
3. Choose what you want to do next:

- **Archive your program**: 

1. Click **Archive**.
2. Click **Archive** in the pop-up to confirm.
- **Delete your program**: 

1. Click **Delete**.
2. Click **Delete** in the pop-up to confirm.
[image: A screenshot of the Online Programs settings in the Wix site dashboard]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online** **Programs**.
4. Tap the relevant tab according to the status of your program: **Published**, **Drafts** or **Archived**.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.
6. Choose what you want to do next:

- **Archive:** Send your program to the archive. You can find it in the **Archived** tab.
- **Delete: **Delete your program and its information permanently.
[image: A screenshot of the Online Programs settings in the Wix app]

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want to show an archived program on your site again, duplicate the program and publish it as a new one.

> **Want to delete the Online Programs app from your site?**
>
> You can [delete Online programs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-deleting-the-online-program-app) from your site to remove the app altogether.
