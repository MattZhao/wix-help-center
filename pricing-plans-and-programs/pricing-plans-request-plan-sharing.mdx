---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Plan Sharing"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create pricing plans for groups of people to share and use. Pricing plans only apply to one site member at a time, and clients must log in and purchase their own plan for themselves.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
