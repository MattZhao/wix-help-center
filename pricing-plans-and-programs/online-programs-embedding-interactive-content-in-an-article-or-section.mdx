---
title: "Online Programs: Embedding Interactive Content in an Article or Section"
---

Add interactive elements to your program steps like videos, gifs, files, and images. This helps you improve participant understanding and engagement throughout your programs. You can also embed your offerings from Wix apps like Stores, Bookings, and Events. For example, create an online event for your participants and embed it in your step so people can sign up.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want to add a video to your article, consider adding a [video step](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-a-video-step-to-a-program) instead.

### To embed interactive content in an article or section:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant program.  Create a new step or edit an existing one:

- **Add a new step:** Click **+ Add** next to **Schedule** (for scheduled programs) or **Content** (for self-paced programs) and select **Article **or** Section**.
- **Edit an existing step:** Click **Edit** next to an **Article **or** Section**.
    Add or edit the basic info for the step or section.   Add or edit the content of the step or section.   (Optional) Add more interactive content from the icons at the bottom:

### Add interactive content

1. Select from the following icons at the bottom of the **Content** field:

- **Image** icon  to add an image.
- **Gallery** icon  to add an image gallery.
- **Divider** icon  to add a dividing line.
- **HTML code ** to add HTML code.
- **Video** icon  to add a video to a post. You can upload a video from your computer or paste a URL from YouTube, Vimeo, or Facebook.
- **GIF** icon  to add an animated GIF.
- **File** icon  to add a file from your desktop.
- **Table** icon  to add a table.
- **Audio file** icon  to add an audio file.
2. Click **Save** when you're done adding content.

### Add an event, service, or product

1. Select the relevant Wix app icon at the bottom of the **Content** field:

- **Events: **Click the **Wix Events **icon , select an event from your list, then click **Embed**.
- **Bookings: **Click the **Wix Bookings **icon , select a service from your list, then click **Embed**.
- **Stores:** Click the **Wix Stores **icon , select a product from your list, then click **Embed**.
2. Click **Save** when you're done adding content.

### Add a call-to-action button

1. Click the **Button** icon .
2. Click the newly added button in your post.
3. Click the **Settings** icon  .
4. Enter the text you want to display.
5. Paste the relevant link.
6. (Optional) Customize more of the button's settings and design.
7. Click **Save** when you're done adding content.
