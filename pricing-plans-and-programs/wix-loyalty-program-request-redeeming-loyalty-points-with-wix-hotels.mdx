title: "Wix Loyalty Program Request: Redeeming Loyalty Points with Wix Hotels"
---

Currently, it is not possible to redeem loyalty points with Wix Hotels. You can however, [offer coupons as rewards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-managing-your-loyalty-program#updating-your-rewards).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
