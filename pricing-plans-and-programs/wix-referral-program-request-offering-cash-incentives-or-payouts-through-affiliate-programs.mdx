---
title: "Wix Referral Program Request: Offering Cash Incentives or Payouts through Affiliate Programs"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set up an affiliate program offering cash incentives or payouts through a [Wix Referral Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-referral-program-adding-and-setting-up-your-referral-program).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
