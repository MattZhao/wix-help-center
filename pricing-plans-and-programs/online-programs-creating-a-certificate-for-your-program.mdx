---
title: "Online Programs: Creating a Certificate for Your Program"
---

Create a certificate of completion for participants who finish your program. You can design the certificate to match your look, and even add your own signatures and logo. This is a great way to motivate people and reward them with a certificate they can proud of.

---

### Step 1 | Create a certificate

To get started, choose the relevant program. From your program's settings page, you can create a certificate of completion for your participants.

#### To create a certificate:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.  Select **Edit Settings**.  Click **Rewards**.  Click **Create** **Certificate**.

---

### Step 2 | Design your certificate

Now it's time to design your certificate. Choose a template you like, then customize it to suit your program's needs. You can edit the text, select a background, add your own logo and signatures, and much more.

#### To design your certificate:

Click the tabs on the left to customize your certificate:

### Template

Select a design that suits your program.

1. Click **Templates**.
2. Select the desired template.

### Text

Select the text elements you want to edit or remove.

1. Click **Text**.
2. Hover over the relevant text and click **Customize**.
3. Choose what you want to customize:

- **Show / hide the text: **Click the **Content** tab and enable or disable the toggle.
- **Edit the text: **Click the **Content** tab and write new text using the available options.
- **Style the text:** Click the **Design** tab and format your text the way you want.

### Decorations

Add a badge, ribbon or stamp to your certificate.

1. Click **Decorations**.
2. Choose the desired decoration.

### Logo

Promote your brand by adding your own logo.

1. Click **Logo.**
2. Choose what to do next:

- **Replace the existing logo:** Hover over the logo and click **Replace**. You can choose a new file from the Media Manager.
- **Edit the existing logo:** Hover over the logo and click **Edit**. You can use the Wix Photo Studio to make adjustments.
- **Create a logo: **Use the [Wix Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-2200409) to create your own logo.

### Signatures

Upload one or two signatures to give your certificate a professional look.

1. Click **Signatures**.
2. Click the Add icon    below the relevant signature.
3. (In the Media Manager) Select or upload your signature.
4. Click **Add to Page**.

### Underlines

Choose the color and style for all the lines that appear on your certificate.

1. Click **Underlines**.
2. Choose how to customize the underlines:

- **Change the color: **Click the color box and select the color you want.
- **Select a different style:** Click any underline style to apply it.

### Frames

Wrap your certificate in a frame to give it clear structure and boundaries.

1. Click **Frames**.
2. Choose the desired frame for your certifictate.

### Background

Pick a color or upload an image for your certificate's background.

1. Click **Background**.
2. Choose how to customize the background:

- **Change the color: **Click the color box and select the color you want.
- **Opacity:** Drag the slider to change the transparency of the certificate's color.
- **Add an image: **Click the Add icon  below **Upload image**, then (In the Media Manager) select or upload your image.

2.  Click **Save**.

---

### Step 3 | Preview and save the certificate

Once you've designed the way your certificate looks, click **Save** at the top right. This connects the certificate to your program, so it's automatically emailed to participants when they complete the program.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about the available features for certificates.

### Can I resend a certificate to a participant?

Yes. You can resend a certificate to a participant.
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant program.
3. Click **Manage** next to **Participants**.
4. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant participant.
5. Click **Reissue certificate**.

[image: A screenshot of Reissuing a certificate to a participant.]

### How can I preview what a program's certificate looks like?

1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.
3. Select **Edit Settings**.
4. Click **Rewards**.
5. Click the **Manage** drop-down next to **Certificate.**
6. Select **View**.
7. (Optional) Click **Edit** to make any changes.
[image: A screenshot of the Manage drop-down to preview and edit your certificate.]

### Can I disconnect a certificate from a program?

Yes. You can disconnect a certificate from a program by clicking **Disconnect** next to the certificate, so it's no longer sent to your participants.

[image: A screenshot of Disconnecting a certificate from a program.]
