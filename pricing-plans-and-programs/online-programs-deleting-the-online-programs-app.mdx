---
title: "Online Programs: Deleting the Online Programs App"
---

If you decide to remove Online Programs from your site, you can delete the app. This will remove the app and all related pages from your site. Your data is saved, so you can re-add the app later if you change your mind.

DashboardWix EditorStudio EditorWix app
1. [Go to Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to **Wix Online Programs**.
3. Select **Delete**.

1. Click **Go to Editor**.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to **Wix Online Programs**.
3. Select **Delete**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix site app management screen]
1. Select your reason for deleting the app in the feedback form and tell us why (optional).
2. Click **Delete App**.
