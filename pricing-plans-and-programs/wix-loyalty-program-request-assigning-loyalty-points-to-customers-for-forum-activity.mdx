---
title: "Wix Loyalty Program Request: Assigning Loyalty Points to Customers for Forum Activity"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically give loyalty points to your members, customers, or visitors for interacting with your site's forum. You can however, [give loyalty points manually](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-managing-your-customers-loyalty-points).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
