### Find Event Data in Google Analytics

Sign in to your [Google Analytics](http://www.google.com/analytics/) account.  Click **Conversions** on the left side.  Click **Ecommerce**.  Select the report you want to view, or to see all events:

1. Click **Behavior**.
2. Click **Events**.

> **Note:**
>
> It can take a few days until the reports start receiving data.
