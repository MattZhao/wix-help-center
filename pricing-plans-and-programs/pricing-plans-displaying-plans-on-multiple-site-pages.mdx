---
title: "Pricing Plans: Displaying Plans on Multiple Site Pages"
---

After setting up your pricing plans, a site page is automatically added to show them to your clients. You can advertise your plans on other pages by showing a plan list or a specific one you want to highlight.

There are 2 panels in your Editor that let you add these elements to site pages - the Add panel, and the pricing plans' Settings panel.

**In this article, learn more about adding pricing plan elements:**

[From the Add panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-displaying-plans-on-multiple-site-pages#using-the-add-panel-to-display-pricing-plans)  [From the app's Settings panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-displaying-plans-on-multiple-site-pages#using-the-settings-panel-to-display-pricing-plans)

> **Notes:**
>
> - If you want to add the plans to a new page, first add a new page to your site:
>
> - [Show me how to add a new page with Wix Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-page-to-your-site).
> - [Show me how to add a new page with Editor X](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-managing-your-pages#adding-a-page).
> - If you [add a folder](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-and-managing-a-dropdown-submenu) to your site menu, you cannot display plans on pages that are in that folder.

### Using the Add panel to display pricing plans

The Add panel in your Editor includes elements that feature your plan list or a single plan. You can add these elements to promote your plans on any page of your site.

> **Note:**
>
> The steps below apply to the Wix Editor but not Editor X. Skip to the next section to learn how to [add Pricing Plans widgets using the Settings panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-displaying-plans-on-multiple-site-pages#using-the-settings-panel-to-display-pricing-plans).

#### To display pricing plans using the Add panel:

Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Payments**.   Choose whether to display a **Plan List** or a **Single Plan **and drag the element onto the page.  Select the plan(s) you want to display:

1. Click the element in the Editor.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Select the **Plans **tab.
4. Select the checkboxes next to the plans you want to display.
    [Customize the design and layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-design-of-your-pricing-plans-page#customizing-the-design-of-your-plans) of your new Pricing Plans widget.

### Using the Settings panel to display pricing plans

You can use the Pricing Plans settings panel in your Editor to show plans on more pages. From the Add-Ons tab, choose the additional pages where you want to promote a single plan or the full list.

#### To display plans using the Settings panel:

Go to the Pricing & Plans page in your Editor.   Click the Pricing Plans app on the page.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Add-Ons** tab.  Click the **Add to Page** drop-down next to **Plan List** or **Single Plan**.   Select the page where you want the widget to appear.

Select the plans you want to display on the new page:

1. Click the new Pricing Plans widget in your Editor.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Select the **Plans **tab.
4. Select the checkboxes next to the plans you want to display.
    [Customize the design and layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-design-of-your-pricing-plans-page#customizing-the-design-of-your-plans) of your new Pricing Plans widget.
