---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Allowing Clients to Choose Their Payment Frequency"
---

Currently, when creating a recurring pricing plan, you can choose how often the client pays: weekly, monthly, or yearly. It is not possible to create a single plan where clients can choose their payment frequency.

> **Tip:**
>
> As a workaround, it's possible to create similar plans with different payment frequencies.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
