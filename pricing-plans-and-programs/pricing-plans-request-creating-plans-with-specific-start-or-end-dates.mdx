---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Creating Plans with Specific Start or End Dates"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create pricing plans with specific start or end dates.

You can let your clients [choose a custom start date](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan#step-3-setting-the-price-and-duration-of-your-plan) for their subscriptions. You can also request they choose specific dates within your [plan policies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan#step-4-adjusting-your-plans-advanced-settings).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
