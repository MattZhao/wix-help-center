---
title: "Pricing Plans: Setting Up Payments"
---

To accept payments for pricing plans on your site you must select an online payment method. Wix offers a number of payment providers, which differ by country.

> **Important:**
>
> - The manual (offline) payment method is not available with Pricing Plans.
> - If you offer a pricing plan with [recurring payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments), [check which payment providers support recurring payments in your region](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments).
> - If you choose to switch payment providers, you must first cancel all active recurring plans, switch providers, and then ask your clients to repurchase the same plan.
> - To accept online payments, you need to [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-upgrading-your-site-to-sell-plans-online).

### To set up a payment method:

Go to [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/payments/) in your site's dashboard.   Choose what type of payments you want to accept:

### Offer clients plans with one-time payments (no recurring payments)

In the **Accept Payments **tab, you can choose any online payment methods available in your region. 
**Note: **You cannot use the manual method to accept payment for pricing plans.

### Offer clients plans with recurring payments

In order to accept recurring payments, you must [connect to one of these payment providers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments).
