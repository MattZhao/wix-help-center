---
title: "Online Programs Request: Customizing the Sender Details of Notifications Participants Receive"
---

Currently, it is not possible to customize the sender details for your Online Program notifications. The email notifications participants receive from your programs are always sent from **notifications@challenges.wix.com**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [customize the notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-sending-notifications-to-participants#customizing-email-notifications-for-participants), including their titles, messages, and buttons.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
