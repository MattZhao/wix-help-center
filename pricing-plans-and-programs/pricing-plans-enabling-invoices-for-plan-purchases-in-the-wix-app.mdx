---
title: "Pricing Plans: Enabling Invoices for Plan Purchases in the Wix App"
---

You can automatically create invoices for pricing plans. Once enabled, clients receive an invoice with each purchase. For recurring orders, an invoice is sent each payment cycle.

> **Important:**
>
> - Invoices are not sent when you [manually add a client to a plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-selling-plans-offline-using-the-wix-app).
> - Most customizations to your invoices [can only be made on desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-automatically-creating-invoices-for-purchased-plans).

### To enable sending invoices for plan purchases:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Pricing Plans** under **Catalog**.  Tap **Settings** at the top.  Enable the **Invoices** toggle to send invoices to clients who purchase plans.

> **To view invoices you've sent:**
>
> 1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
> 2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
> 3. Tap **Subscriptions **under** Sales.**
