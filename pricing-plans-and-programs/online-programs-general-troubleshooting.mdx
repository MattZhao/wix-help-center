### Troubleshooting live site issues with Online Programs

Click an issue below to find out how to resolve it.

### People can't see my program on the live site

Check your program's visibility. Secret programs are only visible to participants you invite. Public and private programs are visible to everyone.
**To change your program's visibility: **
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.
3. Click **Edit** **Settings**.
4. Click **Visibility and Pricing**.
5. Make sure the **Visibility** setting is **Public** or **Private**.

### The program isn't loading on my site

This is probably related to a local issue (e.g your device and/or internet connection) and can be resolved at home.
**To fix local issues:**
1. Disconnect and reconnect to your internet.
2. Clear your [cache](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-your-browsers-cache) and [cookies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-wix-cookies-from-your-browser).
3. Open your site in a different browser.

**Tip:** You can check if the issue you are encountering is cookie or cache related by opening your browser in [incognito / private mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/opening-your-browser-in-incognitoprivate-browsing-mode) and see if the problem persists.

### Members are unable to make a payment for a program

[Upgrade](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-your-site-to-premium-3066683) to a business and e-commerce Premium Plan to create paid programs and start accepting payments. Only Premium sites are eligible to accept payments.

### New participants are unable to join my program

Check your participant limit to make sure you did not exceed it.
**To check your group's participant limit:**
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant program.
3. Click **Edit** **Settings**.
4. Click **Visibility and Pricing**.
5. Scroll down to **Number of Participants**. If your program is set to **Limited**, edit the maximum number, or change the setting to **Unlimited**.

### I want to restore an archived program

Archived programs cannot be restored. However, you can duplicate the archived program and publish the new draft. 
**To duplicate an archived program:**
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **Archived** tab.
3. Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant program.
4. Click **Duplicate**.

**Tip:** The new duplicate program is in your **Drafts** tab.
5. Click the **Drafts** tab.
6. Click the More Actions icon  next to the duplicate program.
7. Choose to do one of the following:

1. **Publish:** Click **Publish** to set your program live.
2. **Edit Settings:** [Update or change your program settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs).

### Troubleshooting issues in the Wix app

If you are experiencing issues with Online Programs in the Wix app, try the following troubleshooting steps.

### Check that you're using a supported device

The Wix app is supported on:
- **iPhone:** iOS 11 and up
- **Android:** 6.0 and above

### Check that your app is up to date

Make sure you are using the most current version of the Wix app. 
**To check for an update:**
1. Go to the Apple App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
2. Search for 'Wix app'.
3. Click **Update** (if available).

**Note:** If no update is available, it means you already have the most up-to-date version.

### Log out of the app and log back in again

Try logging out of the Wix app before signing back in with your Wix credentials and restarting your device.
**To log out of the app and log back in:**
1. Open the Wix app.
2. Tap your profile icon at the top.
3. Tap **Log Out**.
4. Log back in using your Wix account credentials.
5. Restart your mobile device.
6. Reopen the Wix app.

#### Still need help?

If you still need assistance, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact) with the information below:

**For issues with Online Programs on your live site:**

The name or URL of your Wix site.  Include what happened and what you've already tried.  Prepare a [screenshot](https://support.wix.com/en/article/taking-a-screenshot-using-windows) or a video of the issue.  If you have an issue with the Wix app, provide the version number and the device you are using.

**For Online Programs in the Wix app:**

The version of the Wix app you're using. To find this:

1. Open the Wix app.
2. Tap your profile icon at the top.
3. Tap **About the App** under **Help & Feedback**.
4. Scroll down to the bottom of the screen and copy the number next to **Version**.
