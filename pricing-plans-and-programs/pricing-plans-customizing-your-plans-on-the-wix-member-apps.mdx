---
title: "Pricing Plans: Customizing Your Plans on the Wix Member Apps"
---

From the Wix app, you can customize how you display your pricing plans for your members on your Wix Member apps (Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix, Dine by Wix or Branded App by Wix).

Begin by adding widget that appears on the member apps' home pages. You can then include the pricing information and benefits of up to 5 of your pricing plans.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn [how to create pricing plans in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app).

### To customize how you display pricing plans on member apps:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Site & App**  at the bottom.  Tap **Customize**.  Tap **Edit Navigation Tabs**.   Tap **+ Add New Tab**, enter a title and tap **Save**.  Select **Pricing Plans** from the options at the top.  Select one of the templates:

- **Carousel:** Show 1 plan at a time. Your members need to swipe to see other plans. This option allows you to display more information about each plan.
- **List:** Show all plans in list format but display fewer benefits.
- **Member subscriptions:** Show members their different memberships and packages.
    (Optional) Enable the **Section Title** toggle to activate it and give the widget a name.  Tap **Edit** in Plan Display and choose up to 5 plans you want to display.   (Optional) Enable the **Display benefits list** toggle to show the plan's benefits.  Tap **Add** at the top.   Tap **Done.**
