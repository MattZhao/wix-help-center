---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Limiting Plan Cancellation within a Specific Timeframe"
---

Currently, you can [allow clients to cancel their own subscriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-allowing-clients-to-cancel-plans) anytime. However, it is not possible to limit clients to cancel their subscription within a set timeframe once they purchase it.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
