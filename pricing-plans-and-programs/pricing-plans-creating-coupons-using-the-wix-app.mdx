---
title: "Pricing Plans: Creating Coupons Using the Wix App"
---

Grow your list of recurring clients by creating discount coupons they can use on membership plans and packages. You can offer a fixed amount coupon ($20 off) or a percentage discount coupon (20% off).

> **Notes:**
>
> - You can also create coupons for [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-coupons-using-the-wix-owner-app), [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-coupons-using-the-wix-owner-app), and [Wix Events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-event-coupons-using-the-wix-owner-app).
> - Discount coupons for Pricing Plans cover the plans themselves, but not any setup fees you may have enabled.

### To create a Pricing Plans coupon:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Coupons **under** Marketing.**  Tap **Add a Coupon**.  Select an option:

- **Create a $ Discount coupon**:

1. Tap **$ Discount**.
2. Enter the amount that will be taken off the price.
3. Tap **Next**.
- **Create a % Discount coupon:**

1. Tap **% Discount**.
2. Enter the percentage of the discount you want to offer.
3. Tap **Next**.

Enter the **Coupon Code** your clients enter to get the discount.  Enter the coupon name for your own records.

**Note: **This name appears in your coupons list.   Tap **Next**.  Choose which plans the coupon applies to: 

- **All plans:** The coupon can apply to any available plans you offer.
- **Specific plan: **The coupon can apply to one specific plan you offer.

- Click the **Service **drop-down menu and select a service.

**Tip: **If your site has Wix Stores or Bookings, you see additional options.
    Choose the billing cycle that the discount applies to:

- **First billing cycle:** Choose this option to only apply a discount to the first billing cycle of a plan.
- **All billing cycles: **Choose this option to apply a discount to all future billing cycles of a plan until the coupon expires.
- **Custom: **Choose how many billing cycle you want the coupon to last for.

- Enter the number of first billing cycles the coupon applies to.
    Set the coupon's start and end date:

1. Tap the date under **Dates Valid**.
2. Select the start date from the calendar.
3. (Optional) Tap to select an end date.
4. Tap **Save**.
    (Optional) Enable the toggle under **Usage Limits **and enter the total number of times the coupon can be used.  Tap **Done**.  (Optional) Tap **Share** to share your new coupon.
