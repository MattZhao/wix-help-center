---
title: "Online Programs: Managing Your Programs"
---

Once you have [created your online program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-new-program), you can manage it from the **Online Programs **tab in your site's dashboard.

### To manage your online program:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions icon ﻿ next to the program you want to edit.  Select an option:

### Edit Settings

Edit program details like program name, description, price, groups and badges, and other settings. Learn more about [editing your program settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs).

### Publish

Publish your program if it is still a draft, then invite participants through an invitation link or an email campaign. Learn more about [publishing your online program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-new-program#step-5-publish-your-program-and-invite-participants).

### End

End an active program. (Participants are affected differently depending on the program schedule. Learn more about [editing program schedules](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#editing-scheduling).)  

**Note: **An ended program remains visible to only you and current participants. Only existing participants can complete remaining steps.

### Duplicate

Make a copy of your program. This duplicates your program in the **Draft** tab. This can be useful to keep the settings of one program to build out another.

### Archive

Send your program to the archive. You can find it in the **Archived** tab. Once a program is archived, you cannot unarchive it and participants will no longer be able to view the program. Learn more about [archiving a program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-removing-a-program).  

**Note:** When you archive, current participants can still see the program, but it will not be visible on your live site. To show an archived program on your site, you must duplicate the program and publish it as a new program.

### Delete

Delete a program that you no longer need. The program and its information will be permanently deleted. Learn more about [deleting a program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-removing-a-program).
