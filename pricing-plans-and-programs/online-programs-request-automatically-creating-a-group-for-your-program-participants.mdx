---
title: "Online Programs Request: Automatically Creating a Group for Your Program Participants"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer a feature that would automatically create a [group](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-about-groups) with your online program's participants. If needed, you can always [create your group manually](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-creating-a-new-group) and invite the relevant participants.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
