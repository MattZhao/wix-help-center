---
title: "Pricing Plans: Connecting a Plan to Content and Services"
---

Monetize the services and content you create on Wix by including them as benefits in your pricing plans. This can help you to create a community around your business and lets members get to know each other better.

For example, clients who buy a monthly membership plan would be able to book your Zumba class (created in Wix Bookings) using their membership.

### Show me how to connect Wix services and content to my plans

1. [Go to Pricing Plans](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Pricing%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/membership/) in your dashboard.
2. Click **Edit** to update an existing plan or click **+ Create Plan** [to make a new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan).
3. Enter or update the name, tagline and benefits as required.
4. Select which content and services you want to include in the plan under **Connect & manage benefits**.
5. Select the checkbox beside the services or content types to connect them to your plan.
6. Click **Save**.

You can create new content or services and add them to your pricing plans whenever you like.

---

### Connecting your services

You can connect existing Wix Bookings services while creating a new pricing plan. You can also add new services when you create plans later.

When you connect a pricing plan with Wix Bookings, you can offer your clients:

**Memberships: **Select** "Unlimited" **to [provide unlimited access to selected services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings) for a time frame you choose, (e.g. monthly membership gives you unlimited access to exercise classes at your gym).   **Packages: **Select** "Limited" **to give clients the option of [buying a set number of services at a discounted rate](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-packages), (e.g. Buy a package of 10 manicures at your nail salon).

---

### Connecting your events

Offer clients [discounted access to your events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-a-membership-plan) when they buy a membership plan.

You control what percentage discount you offer, which events are included, and how long the membership lasts. In addition, choose whether to offer just one membership plan or more.

---

### Connecting your online programs

Create programs with steps along the way that participants need to perform, like a 30-day gratitude building challenge. You can then [offer these programs as part of a pricing plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-an-overview).

If you already created programs with the Online Programs app, you can connect them while creating a pricing plan.

---

### Connecting site pages

Offer your clients access to exclusive site pages by [connecting your plans to pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-making-site-pages-members-only).

Clients who buy a membership can log into your site and view your exclusive content.

---

### Connecting your blog

You can limit access so that only plan holders have access to selected blog posts. When site visitors click to view a post, they are prompted to subscribe.

[Learn how to set up subscriptions to blog posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-creating-blog-post-subscriptions).

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also limit access to your entire blog by [making the blog feed a paying members-only page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-making-site-pages-members-only).

---

### Connecting your forum

You can [create forum categories](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-limiting-specific-categories-to-paying-subscribers) that only paying members can access.

If you already created categories in Wix Forum, you can connect them while creating a pricing plan. Any categories you connect here to pricing plans automatically becomes private.

---

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also limit access to your entire forum by [making the forum page a paying members-only page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-making-site-pages-members-only).

---

### Connecting a video channel

If you already created video channels with Wix Video, you can connect them while creating a pricing plan.

Alternatively, when setting up a new video channel, you can [make it exclusive to paying members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-connecting-a-pricing-plan-to-a-video-channel) by selecting plans you already created.

---

### Connecting a file share library

With the [File Share app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-about-the-file-share-app), you can create a library where your community can securely upload, download, and share documents in organized folders. You can limit access to selected pricing plan holders.

You can also connect existing folders while creating a pricing plan.

The folders you connect automatically become private and are accessible only to plan holders.

> **Additional options:**
>
> - Limit library access: Follow these [instructions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-making-site-pages-members-only) to limit access to the entire library page.
> - Manage permissions within the File Share app:
>
> - [Limit who can upload files](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-managing-library-permissions-in-the-file-share-app).
> - [Limit who can manage the library](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-managing-library-permissions-in-the-file-share-app).
> - [Limit access to a folder](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-managing-permissions-per-folder-in-the-wix-file-share-app).

---

### Connecting a group

Use Wix Groups to help build a community around your business. Encourage your members to learn and share tips and advice together. You can limit access to selected pricing plan holders.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can connect existing groups to your pricing plans or [create a new](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-creating-a-new-group#step-1-choose-a-type-of-group), exclusive group.
