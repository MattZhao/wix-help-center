---
title: "Wix Referral Program Request: Restricting Customers from Creating a Referral Link"
---

Currently, it is not possible to restrict specific customers from creating a referral link (e.g. customers who have never made a purchase).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
