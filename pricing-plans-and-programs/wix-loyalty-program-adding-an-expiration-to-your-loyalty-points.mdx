With Wix's Loyalty Program, you can add an expiration date to your loyalty points, helping to encourage repeat visits and purchases from your customers. This can be great for your business, as it motivates your customers to use their points before they expire, driving more sales and enhancing customer engagement.

Add expiration dates to the points in your own loyalty program to boost your business in a similar way.

### Adding and setting up points expiration

Add points expiration to your Loyalty Program from your site's dashboard.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To launch a Loyalty Program, you first need to [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan).

#### To add a points expiration date to your Loyalty Program:

[Go to Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Program**.  Click the **Points and rewards** tab.  Click the toggle next to **Points expiration** to set it as active.  Select the terms of your loyalty points expiration:

- **Expiration period:** Choose how long customers have to redeem points before they expire.
- **Share of points to expire: **Choose the percentage of points that will expire after the expiration period.
- **Send an email reminder: **Automatically send an email notification 14 days before your customers' points expire.
    Click **Save**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about loyalty points expiration.

### What happens if I make changes to point expiration rules, such as changing the percentage of points to expire?

If you edit your points expiration rules, the updated program starts from that date for all customers, and their inactivity period will be calculated from that date onwards.
For example, if your customers' expiration date was 1st January 2024 with an inactivity window of 6 months, and you changed the window to 3 months on the 31st December 2023, the new point expiration date for customers would be the 31st March 2024.

### Why do the last activity date and point expiration date in my dashboard not match?

Earning and redeeming points are the only customer actions that count for point expiration. Other actions such as program signups are included under a customer's last activity. These dates do not impact each other, and may appear differently in your dashboard.

### Does issuing refunds affect points expiration?

Refunding points to a customer via your dashboard will not affect their points' expiration date. Only customer actions affect the expiration date.

### Do expired points affect the Tier program?

Points expiration does not affect the [Tiers program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-adding-tiers-to-your-loyalty-program).
