> **Important:**
>
> The article below discusses the Wix subscriptions app Pricing Plans. If you're looking for information about upgrading your site with Wix Premium plans, please check [this article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan).

The Contact List tab in your site's dashboard is where you can view payment information and subscription and payment history for each client.

### To view a client's subscription and payment history:

Go to the [Contact List](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Contact%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/contacts/) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the relevant client and click **View**.  Click the **Subscriptions **tab.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Learn how to [manually mark a subscription as paid](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-viewing-and-managing-plans-that-clients-purchased) (for example, when your client pays you in person).
> - Hover over any subscription and click **View** to see more details.
