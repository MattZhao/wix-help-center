---
title: "Pricing Plans: Making Site Pages Members-Only"
---

Want to offer your subscribers access to exclusive content? Just connect your pricing plans to your members-only pages to set up a paywall.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> 1. [Add the Pricing Plans app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-adding-the-pricing-plans-app).
> 2. [Add a Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-add-and-set-up-a-members-area).
> 3. [Create the plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan) you want to offer on your site.
> Learn more about [creating](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-member-roles) and [managing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-your-member-roles) your site member roles.

### To make a site page members-only:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Hover over the relevant page and click the **More Actions** icon .

**Tip: **Make sure you don't restrict the main (original) Pricing Plans page. You can, however, restrict other pages where you display plans. You can also hide the main page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click **Permissions**.
6. Click **Site members**.
7. Select **Specific members**.
8. Click the **Members with plan subscriptions** toggle to enable it.
9. Click **Manage Plans**.
10. Select the checkbox next to the relevant pricing plans.
11. Click **Apply**.
