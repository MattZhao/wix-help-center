---
title: "Pricing Plans: Limiting Your Blog to Members Who Purchased a Pricing Plan"
---

With the Pricing Plans app, you can create and sell plans that limit your blog to plan holders.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> This article shows you how to limit your blog so that only people who purchased a pricing plan can access the feed and all of your posts. First, make sure to:
> 1. [Add Wix blog to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-setting-up-your-blog).
> 2. [Add the Pricing Plans app to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-adding-the-pricing-plans-app).
> 3. [Create the plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan) you want to offer on your site.

### To limit access to your blog to visitors who purchased a pricing plan:
