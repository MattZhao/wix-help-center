---
title: "Pricing Plans: Adding Pricing Plans in the Wix App"
---

With the Pricing Plans app, you can offer plans for recurring or one-time payments, as well as free plans. This is great for selling memberships, subscriptions or packages (e.g. 5 classes for $100). You can choose the duration and pricing of the plan, as well as the available payment methods.

> **Note:**
>
> After adding the Pricing Plans in the Wix app, it appears as a 'pending app' in your site's dashboard. You can then [choose if you want to add it to your desktop site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-pending-app-in-your-editor) as well.

### To add the Pricing Plans app in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Scroll down and tap **Manage Apps**.  Tap **Add** next to **Pricing Plans**.

> **What's next?**
>
> Now you're ready to [create your first pricing plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app).
