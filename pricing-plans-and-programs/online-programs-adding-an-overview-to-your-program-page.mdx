---
title: "Online Programs: Adding an Overview to Your Program Page"
---

Display an overview of the program steps on the main Program Page. An overview allows your participants to see the details of each step in one place - such as the name of the step and the suggested date for the participant to complete it.

### To add an overview to your Program Page:

Click the **Pages ** on the left side of your Editor.  Click **Program Pages**.  Click the Program Page element on your site's page.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Display** on the **Visitor** tab.  Enable the **Content Overview** toggle to display your program's overview details.
