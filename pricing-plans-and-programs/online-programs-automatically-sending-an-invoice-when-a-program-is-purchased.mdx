### Automatically send an invoice when a program is purchased

When automatically sending an invoice, a detailed invoice is tailored to the purchased program and is sent to the relevant customer. You can enable this feature for both new and existing programs.

#### To automatically send an invoice:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Choose an option:

- **If this is a new program: **Click** Create New **to [create a new program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-new-program#step-2-enter-your-program-details). Once you choose a template, click **Edit **to enter your program details.
- **Existing Programs:** Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the program you want to edit.

- Select **Edit Settings**.
    Click **Visibility and Pricing**.  Select the **Automatically send invoices **checkbox.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Customize Invoice **to [tailor the details](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-customizing-your-invoices) of your invoices. This includes the invoice header, transaction details and incorporating company details from your [Business Info](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-sites-business-information).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### How do participants receive an invoice after they purchase a program?

Participants receive an email with a link to download the invoice.

### Can I enable automatic invoicing using the Wix app?

No. This feature is currently not available on Wix app. However, you can enable it directly from your site's dashboard.
