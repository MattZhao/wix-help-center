---
title: "Online Programs Request: Allowing Participants to View Each Other's Progress"
---

Currently in [Online Programs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-an-overview), only the owner can view the list of program participants as well as view their progress. The participants are unable to see or monitor each other's progress.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
