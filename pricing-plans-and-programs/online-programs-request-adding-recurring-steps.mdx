---
title: "Online Programs Request: Adding Recurring Steps"
---

Currently it is not possible in [Online Programs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-an-overview) to select a step and make it repeat daily, weekly, or periodically throughout the program.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can [manually duplicate a step](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-sections-and-steps#managing-your-online-program-steps) to make it repeat as often as you'd like.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
