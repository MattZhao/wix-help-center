---
title: "Online Programs: Managing Your Notification Settings in the Wix App"
---

Never miss an update from your online programs. The Wix app sends push notifications to your phone with important highlights such as new participants, requests to join the program, steps that were missed and more. You can customize your notification settings at any time.

### To manage your program's notification settings:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap your** **Profile icon at the top left .  Tap **Notification Settings**.  Select the site you want to update notification settings for.  Scroll down and tap **Online** **Programs**.  Enable or disable the toggles for the following notifications: 

- **New Participants:** Receive notifications for new participants joining or requesting to join your program (if it's private).
- **Participant Progress: **Receive notifications every time your participants complete or miss steps.

> **Tip:**
>
> Your participants can manage the following notifications for online programs they are participants of:
> - Program updates: Every time you add a new step to your program.
> - Reminders: Every time your participant misses a step.
