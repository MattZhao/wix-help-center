title: "Online Programs: Managing a Quiz"
---

Use multiple-choice, open-ended, and other question types to test what participants learned. Or, use the quiz as a survey to collect participants' feedback. A quiz can become a survey if you don't set a correct response, and the questions become the answers to choose from. A quiz can be optional if you don't require a passing grade. You can manage your quiz in a variety of ways, such as editing or removing a question and answer. You can also view your participants' progress by monitoring their responses to each question.

>
> Make sure you [added a quiz to your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-and-managing-a-quiz).

### Manage your quiz questions

After you create your quiz or survey, you can go back at any time and make changes to the questions and answers. You can also delete questions and answers that you no longer need.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant program.
3. Scroll down to the **Content **/ **Schedule** section.
4. Choose what you want to do next:
Change the quiz's basic info
1. Hover over the relevant quiz and click **Edit**.
2. Choose what you want to do next:

- Edit the name of your quiz.
- Select a different section or date for your quiz.
3. Click **Save**.

Change the quiz's passing grade
1. Hover over the relevant quiz and click **Edit**.
2. Choose what you want to do next:

- Select or deselect the checkbox next to **Require a passing grade**.
- Edit the required percentage for passing the quiz.
3. Click **Save**.

Manage your quiz questions
1. Hover over the relevant quiz and click **Edit**.
2. Click **Edit** next to the **Edit questions **section.
3. Choose what you want to do next:

- **Edit an existing question:** Click the relevant question, then click **Settings **and make changes to the question.
- **Edit a layout element:** Click the relevant layout element, then click **Edit text **and make changes to the text.
- **Remove a question or layout element:** Click the relevant question or layout element, then click the **Delete** icon  to delete the question.
- **Duplicate a question: **Click the relevant question, then click the **Duplicate** icon  to replicate the question.
- **Add a new question or layout element:** Click the **Add** icon  in the sidebar and select the relevant question or element to add to your quiz/survey.
- **Reorder questions or layout element within the quiz: **Click the relevant question or layout element, then click and hold the **Drag **icon   to reorder it in the quiz.
4. Click **Save**.

Duplicate the quiz
1. Hover over the relevant quiz and click the **More Actions** icon .
2. Select **Duplicate**.
3. (Optional) Edit your quiz.

Delete the quiz
1. Hover over the relevant quiz and click the **More Actions** icon .
2. Select **Delete**.
3. Click **Delete** again to confirm.

### Viewing participants' quiz answers

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant program.
3. Scroll down to the **Participants** section.
4. Click **Manage** under the **Participants** section.
5. Hover over the relevant participant click **View Steps**.
6. Click the **All steps** drop-down and select the questions you want to see: 

- **Steps with questionnaire and/or quiz:** View all steps that have a questionnaire or quiz.
- Click the drop-down next to the relevant step to view participant answers.
7. (Optional) Click **Message** in the pop-up window to write to your participants directly. You can send them a motivational note for answering a question correctly or encourage them to complete the program.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix site dashboard open on Wix Online Programs Quiz management]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the relevant program.
5. Tap **See All** next to **Participants**.
6. Tap the relevant participant.
7. Tap the **View Results** next to the relevant quiz.
8. (Optional) Click **Manage Participant** to write to your participant directly. You can send them a motivational note for answering a question correctly or encourage them to complete the program.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I receive an automated notification when a participant completes a quiz?

Yes. You can receive an automated notification when a participant completes a quiz using [Wix Automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-about-automations-for-your-program). Choose the trigger: **Participant Completes a Step** and select the relevant program and quiz. Then select the action: **Get an Email**. This will send you an email notifying you that a participant has completed the quiz.

### Can I export quiz results for all participants?

No. You cannot export a CSV file containing all your participants' quiz results. If this is a feature that you would like to see in the future, vote for it here: [Wix Online Programs Request: Exporting Quiz Results for All Participants](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-online-programs-request-exporting-quiz-results-for-all-participants)

### Can I add images to a quiz?

No. You cannot add images to quiz questions. If this is a feature that you would like to see in the future, vote for it here: [Wix Online Programs Request: Adding Images to a Quiz](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-online-programs-request-adding-images-to-a-quiz)
