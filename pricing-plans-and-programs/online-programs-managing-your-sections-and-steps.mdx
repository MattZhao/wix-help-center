### Managing your program sections

Once you've created your sections, you can duplicate them, reorder them, and edit the titles and descriptions to make sure everything is clear.

> **Note:**
>
> Sections are only available for self-paced programs.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant program.
3. Scroll down to the **Content** section.
4. Manage your sections using the following options:

- **Edit a section**:

1. Hover over the section and click **Edit**.
2. Edit the relevant details and/or cover image.
3. Click **Save**.
- **Duplicate a section**: 

1. Hover over the section name and click the **More Actions **icon .
2. Click **Duplicate**. The duplicate section appears beneath the original section.
- **Delete a section**:

1. Hover over the section name and click the** More Actions **icon .
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Click **Delete** to confirm.
- **Reorder a section**: Click and drag a section to move it up or down on the page.
- **Add a section**: Click **+ Add section** at the bottom of your program content.
Tip:Click the drop-down arrow to the right of a section to expand or collapse the steps within that section.

[image: A screenshot of the drop-down arrow to expand or collapse steps within a section of an Online Program.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage ** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the relevant program.
5. Tap the** Content** tab.
6. Reorder or add sections: 

- **Reorder a section**: Hold down the **Drag** icon  to reorder your sections.
- **Add a section**: Tap **+Add Section** at the bottom.
7. Edit sections: 

1. Tap the relevant section.
2. Tap the **More Actions **icon  at the top right and select an option:

- **Edit: **Edit the section name, description, and cover image and tap **Save**.
- **Duplicate: **Create a copy of the section.
- **Delete: **Permanently remove the section. Tap **Delete** to confirm.

### Managing your program steps

While program sections help keep you and your participants organized, steps are where you provide the crucial information about how to reach the program goal. You can edit, rearrange, or delete steps as needed.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant program.
3. Scroll down to the **Content **/** Schedule** section.
4. Click the drop-down arrow next to the section that contains the steps you want to manage.
5. Manage your steps using the following options:

- **Edit a step**: 

1. Hover over the step and click **Edit**.
2. Edit the step content.
3. (Optional) Click the **Section** drop-down and select the relevant section to move the step to a different section.
4. Click **Save**.
- **Duplicate a step**:

1. Hover over the step and click the **More Actions **icon .
2. Click **Duplicate**.
3. Update the relevant information.
4. Click **Save**.
- **Delete a step**:

1. Hover over the step and click the **More Actions **icon .
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Click **Delete** to confirm.
- **Reorder a step**: Click and drag a step to move it up or down within the section.

**Note:** Steps can be arranged in any order
- **Add a step**: Click **+ Add Step** at the bottom of the relevant section.
[image: A screenshot of the available options to manage a. step in an Online Program.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage ** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the relevant program.
5. Tap the** Content** tab.
6. Tap the relevant section.
7. Reorder or add steps:

- **Reorder a step**: Hold down the **Drag** icon  to reorder your sections.
- **Add a step**: Tap **+Add Step** at the bottom and select the type of step you want to add. Create the step and tap **Save**.
8. Edit steps:

1. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant step and select an option:

- **Edit: **Update the step details and tap **Save**.

**Tip: **Tap **Section** to move the step to a different section.
- **Duplicate: **Create a copy of the step.
- **Delete: **Permanently remove the step. Tap** Delete** to confirm.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about managing your program's sections and steps.

### Can I add steps to published programs with participants?

Yes. You can add steps at any time to a program that is live and has participants.

### What happens when I add steps for past or future days in a program?

Creating steps for future days adds them to the program curriculum. However, if you add steps for days that have passed, they will be marked as missed (in red) for participants.

### What happens to newly added steps for participants who have completed a program?

Newly added steps won't be visible for those participants who have already finished the program.
