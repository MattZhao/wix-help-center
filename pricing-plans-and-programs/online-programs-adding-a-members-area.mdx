---
title: "Online Programs: Adding a Members Area"
---

To access program content, participants must be site members. To enable sign-ups, add a Members Area to your site. Once logged in, members can view their private program information, including active and past programs, program duration, and pricing.

Wix EditorStudio EditorDashboard
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Community**.
4. Click **Members**.
5. Click **Add to Site**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix editor displaying the Add panel, with the Wix Members Area app highlighted.]
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Community**.
4. Click **Members Area**.
5. Click **Add to Site**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Studio editor displaying the Add panel, with the Wix Members Area app highlighted.]
1. [Go to the Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market) in your site's dashboard.
2. Search for the **Wix Members Area** app in the search bar.
3. Click **Add to Site**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix editor dashboard displaying the Wix App Market, with the Wix Members Area app highlighted.]
1. Select the site in which you want to add a Members Area.
