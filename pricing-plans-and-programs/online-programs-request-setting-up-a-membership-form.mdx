---
title: "Online Programs Request: Setting Up a Membership Form"
---

Currently, it is not possible to use a membership form to gather information about participants before they register for your program.

However, you can collect information from site visitors by [adding a membership form to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-form-on-your-site). This form does not connect directly to your Online Program registration.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
