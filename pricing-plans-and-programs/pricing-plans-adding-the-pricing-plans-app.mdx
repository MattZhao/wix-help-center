title: "Pricing Plans: Adding the Pricing Plans App"
---

With Pricing Plans, you can offer clients plans with recurring or one-time payments. This is great for selling memberships or subscriptions as well as one-time packages. You choose the duration and pricing of the plan, as well as the available payment methods.

### To add the Pricing Plans app:

Wix EditorStudio Editor

1. Click **Add Apps **  on the left side of the editor.
2. Search for the Pricing Plans app in the search bar.
3. Click **Add to Site**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to add the Pricing Plans app to your site.]Tip:The Pricing Plans app creates a new page on your site called **Plans & Pricing**.
