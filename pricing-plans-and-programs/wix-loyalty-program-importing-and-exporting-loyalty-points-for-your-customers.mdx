### Importing loyalty points for customers

The import tool allows you to import points for existing customers from any 3rd party loyalty provider. You can export your customers' points into a CSV file, then import them into your Wix Loyalty Program.

#### To import loyalty points:

[Go to Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  at the top.  Click **Import**.  Prepare your CSV file for import:

- Include a header row in your file.
- Make sure each row in your file contains an email address and customer points balance.
    Click **Next**.  Click **Upload file**.  Select the file containing the loyalty points you're importing and click **Open**.   Click **Next**.   Check that the data is matched correctly:

1. Ensure the **Email** and **Points balance **columns are correct.
2. (If needed) Click **Match Data** and use the drop-down menus to correct the column headers.
    Click **Save and Import**.  Click **View Customers**.

### Exporting loyalty points for customers

The export tool allows you to download your customers' loyalty points data into a CSV file.

#### To export loyalty points:

[Go to Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the checkbox next to the customers whose points you want to export. 

**Tip:** Click the checkbox at the top left of the list to select all. You can also use the search bar to find specific customers  Click **Export**.

### FAQs

Here are some common questions about importing and exporting loyalty points for your customers:

### Can I import loyalty points for new customers?

Yes, you can import new contacts to your Loyalty Program by importing them to [Wix Contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-importing-your-contacts-into-your-contact-list) first. Once you've added new customers to Wix Contacts, you can import their points in the Loyalty Program to update their balance.

### Are there any technical requirements for the CSV import file?

- The import CSV file is limited at 25,000 data rows, +1 for the header.
- The import CSV file is limited to 10 MB in size.
- The import CSV file is limited to 200 columns.
- The import CSV file must be in [UTF-8 encoding](https://www.fileformat.info/info/unicode/utf8.htm).
