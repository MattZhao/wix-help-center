---
title: "Wix Online Programs: Translating Your Online Programs"
---

Expand your [Online Programs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-an-overview) to a wider audience using Wix Multilingual. You can streamline the translation process for your programs through the Translation Manager in your site's dashboard.

Whether your programs are scheduled or self-paced, you can manage translations for each program type.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> First, you need to [add and set up Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-adding-and-setting-up-wix-multilingual). You can add Wix Multilingual from your editor, your site's dashboard, or from the Wix App Market.

### To manage translations in your site's dashboard:

[Go to Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/multilingual?&title=Translation%20Manager&buttonText=Go+To+Translation+Manager) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over **Online Programs** on the left and click on the relevant program type: **Scheduled programs **or** Self-paced programs 

Note:** It is only possible to translate published and active programs.  Choose the relevant option:

- **If you auto-translated your site:** Review the automated translations and edit if necessary.
- **If you didn't use auto-translation:** Translate the custom fields and policies manually.
    Click **Save and Publish**.

> **Note:**
>
> Currently, it is not possible to override the Online Programs widget texts in secondary languages in your editor.
