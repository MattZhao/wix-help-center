---
title: "Wix Online Programs Request: Allowing Customers to Pay in Installments"
---

Currently, it is not possible to let customers pay for online programs in installments. When customers buy an online program, they can pay the full price upfront or sign up for a pricing plan.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
