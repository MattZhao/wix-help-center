---
title: "Online Programs: Managing Your Program in the Wix App"
---

You can easily access your program on the go using the Wix app. Manage your programs at any time by publishing, editing or duplicating them. If you have a program that is no longer relevant, you can also archive or delete them.

### To access your programs in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Online** **Programs**.   Tap the relevant tab according to the status of your program: **Published**, **Drafts** or **Archived**.  Tap the **Show More** icon  next to the program you want to manage and do one of the following:

- **Edit:** Edit your program's details.
- **Duplicate:** Make a copy of your program. This creates a copy of your program in the **Draft** tab.
- **End: **Discontinue your program at any time.
- **Publish:** If your program is still a draft, you can publish it from here.
- **Archive:** Send your program to the archive. You can find it in the **Archived** tab.
- **Delete: **Delete your program and its information permanently.
