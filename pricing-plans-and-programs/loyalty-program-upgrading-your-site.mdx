Upgrade your site to launch your loyalty program. While you can set up your program for free, you need to upgrade for customers to start using your loyalty program. Customers who join your program earn loyalty points and get rewards (e.g. coupons) by taking various actions on your site.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Upgrading your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/loyalty-program-upgrading-your-site#upgrading-your-site)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/loyalty-program-upgrading-your-site#faqs)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - If you have not already done so, add [Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/loyalty-accounts/) to your site for free.
> - Wix Loyalty Program is currently available for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, Wix Restaurants, Wix Pricing Plans and Wix Events.

You can conveniently upgrade your site either from your site's dashboard or directly from the Wix app (on Android).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about upgrading your site.

### What are the benefits of upgrading?

Upgrading your site allows you the option to launch the Wix Loyalty Program and offer a way to reward customer loyalty. Customers can redeem loyalty points directly from check-out (for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants (New)). 
In addition to improving customer retention, you can customize your points and rewards system to tailor the program to align with your business goals.

### What happens when my premium plan expires?

Once your premium plan expires, your loyalty program will no longer be available for your site customers. All automated emails that you had enabled for the loyalty program will no longer be sent to your site customers. 
If you're showing loyalty programs on your site, customers would see the message "Loyalty Program is not available".

If your plan also included the Loyalty tiers and point expiration features, they will be disabled when the plan expires. If you re-activate a premium plan, you will need to set up those features again.

### Do Editor X premium plans include the Loyalty Program?

Yes, Editor X premium plans include the Loyalty Program for the duration of your subscription. 
**Note: **As all Editor X sites will soon be updated to Wix Studio, it is no longer possible to upgrade to the Editor X plans.

### Do the Business VIP and Business Unlimited plans include the Loyalty program?

Yes, these plans include the Loyalty Program.
