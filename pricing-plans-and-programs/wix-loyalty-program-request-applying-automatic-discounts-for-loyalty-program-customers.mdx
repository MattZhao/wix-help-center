---
title: "Wix Loyalty Program Request: Applying Automatic Discounts for Loyalty Program Customers"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically apply discounts for customers who are a part of your Loyalty Program. You can, however, [automatically give loyalty points to customers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-automatically-giving-loyalty-points-or-responding-when-customers-have-enough-points-for-rewards).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
