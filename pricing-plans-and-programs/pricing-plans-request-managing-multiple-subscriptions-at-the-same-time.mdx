---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Managing Multiple Subscriptions at the Same Time"
---

Currently, it is not possible for you to update more than one pricing plan subscription at the same time. You must update and manage each one individually.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
