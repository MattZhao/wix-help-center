---
title: "Pricing Plans: Editing the Text in Your Pricing Plans Page"
---

You can edit the default header, button and ribbon text in the Pricing Plans app to suit your business and brand.

> **Note:**
>
> To edit plan names, descriptions, or benefits, go to the [Pricing Plans](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/membership/) tab and select the plan you want to edit.

### To edit the pricing plan text:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Plans & Pricing page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Plans & Pricing page.
3. Click the Plans & Pricing element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Text **tab on the left.
6. Edit the following text:

- **Page title:** The title that appears above the plans.
- **Page subtitle:** The subtitle that appears above the plans. Here you can add more details or highlight newer plans, for example.
- **Button: **The button clients click to select a plan.
- **Button (with free trial): **The button clients click to select a recurring membership plan that has a free trial.
- **Ribbon:** The "Best Value" ribbon on the highlighted plan (if a plan is highlighted).
[image: A screenshot showing how to customize the text on the pricing plans element in the editor.]

- **Button: **The button clients click to select a plan.
- **Button (with Free Trial): **The button clients click to select a recurring membership plan that has a free trial.
- **Ribbon:** The "Best Value" ribbon on the highlighted plan (if a plan is highlighted).

> **Notes:**
>
> It is not possible to edit button text separately for different plans.
