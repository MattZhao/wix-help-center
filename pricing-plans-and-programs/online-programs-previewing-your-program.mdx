---
title: "Online Programs: Previewing Your Program"
---

Preview how your program looks on your live site to see how it appears to both current participants and site visitors who haven't joined yet. This provides insight into the information visible before joining and offers a glance at the details that your participants see.

> **Before previewing your site:**
>
> - If you have not already done so, [add Online Programs to your site](https://www.wix.com/app-market/challenges) for free. Some Wix templates already include Online Programs, making it easier for you to get started.
> - Make sure you publish your site.

### To preview your program:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant program.  Click **Preview**.  Choose how you want to view of your program:

- **Visitor View: **People who have not joined your program can see program details such as program name, program duration, number of steps and price (if any).
- **Participant View:** Participants can click the steps to expand and view the details. They can also view the discussion tab in your group to see what everyone is talking about.
