---
title: "Online Programs Request: Adding Participants Back to a Specific Step in the Program"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a participant back into a specific step of your program, if they were removed from the program for any reason. They will need to start the program from the beginning after you re-add them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
