---
title: "Wix Online Programs Request: Sorting Programs in the Dashboard"
---

Currently, it's not possible to index online programs and change their order in the site's dashboard. Programs are sorted by publication date, with the newest programs appearing first.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
