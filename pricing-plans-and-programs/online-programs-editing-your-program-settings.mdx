After [creating an online program,](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-new-program) you can update or change the settings from the Online Programs** **tab in your site's dashboard. Change your program's name, describe what your program is about, set your prices and schedules, or decide whether to assign groups and badges to your participants.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Edit your basic program info](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#editing-your-programs-basic-info)  [Update your program visibility and pricing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#updating-your-programs-visibility-and-pricing)  [Manage your program schedule](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#managing-your-programs-schedule)  [Customize your program's content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#customizing-your-programs-content)  [Connect a group to your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#connecting-a-group-to-your-program)  [Add rewards to your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#adding-rewards)  [Set the notifications your participants receive](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#setting-notifications-for-your-program)  [Edit your SEO settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#editing-your-programs-seo-settings)

### Editing your program's basic info

Give your program a name and an image that tells your participants what they can expect. Create a description that encourages participants to join and add categories to allow participants to filter programs by category.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the program you want to edit and click **Edit Settings**.
[image: A screenshot of the edit settings button in Wix Online Programs on the site dashboard]
1. Click **Basic Info**.
2. Customize the settings based on the following options:

- **Program name:** Enter a new name or edit the existing one.
- **Category:** Add category labels to appear in your program list to help organize your list to make it easier to find.
- **Cover Image:** Customize your cover image:

- **Add a new image: **Click** **the **Add Image** icon  to add a new cover image.
- **Replace the image**: Hover over it and click the **Update** icon .
- **Delete the image: **Hover over the image and click the **Delete** icon .
- **Description: **Update your program description or enter a new one.
- **Instructors:** [Add instructors and assign them to your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-program-contributors).
3. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Basic Info panel for Online Programs in a site's dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Basic Info**.
7. Edit the following program info:

- **Cover Image:** Tap the **Edit** icon  to add or change your program's cover image.
- **Program Name:** Enter a new name or edit the existing one.
- **Description: **Change the description of what your program is about. 

1. Tap **Edit Description**.
2. Enter what your program is about.
3. Tap **Done**.
- **Categories: **Allow members to discover your programs by category.

1. Tap **Add Categories**.
2. Enter a category name.
3. Tap **Done**.
8. Tap **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the  Wix Online Programs Program Info panel.]

### Updating your program's visibility and pricing

Customize the audience, participant capacity, and payment options for your program. Then, automatically generate invoices upon purchase.

> **Important:**
>
> You must [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-upgrading-your-site) so people can start joining your program.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the program you want to edit and click **Edit Settings**.
[image: A screenshot of the edit settings button in Wix Online Programs on the site dashboard]
1. Click** Visibility & Pricing**.
2. Customize the settings based on the following options:

- Decide who can view and join the program under **Visibility**: 

- **Public:** Anyone can view and join the program.
- **Private:** Anyone can view the program, but you need to approve any new participants.
- **Secret: **Only participants that you invite can view the program.
- Decide whether people pay to join a program under **Pricing**:

- **Free: **There is no charge to join your program.
- **Pay to join:** Participants pay a set price to join the program. 

- **One-time payment:**  Set a price for participants to pay and join your program. Then, [automatically send an invoice](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-automatically-sending-an-invoice-when-a-program-is-purchased) when someone purchases your program.
- **Include in a Pricing Plan: **Include the program in a subscription or pricing plan. Then, turn on automatic invoices for pricing plans in Pricing Plan Settings.
- Set how many people can join the program under **Number of Participants**:

- **Unlimited: **There is no limit on how many participants can join.
- **Limited: **Set the maximum number of participants who can join.
3. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Program Settings panel for Pricing and Visibility in a site's dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon   next to the relevant program.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Pricing & Visibility**.
7. Customize the settings based on the following options:
VisibilityDecide who can see and join your program. Select the relevant checkbox under the **Visibility** section:
- **Public:** Anyone can view and join your program.
- **Private:** Anyone can view and requires approval to join.
- **Secret: **Only participants that you invite can view.
Single PaymentCharge a one-time payment to join your program.

1. Enable the **Single Payment** toggle under the **Single Payment **section.
2. Enter the price.
Pricing PlansInclude your program in a subscription. Make sure you [add pricing plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-adding-pricing-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app) if you don't have it added.

1. Tap **Add Pricing Plans **under the** Pricing Plans **section.
2. Choose the relevant option:

- **If you don't have a pricing plan set-up:** Tap **Get Started** to [create a pricing plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app).
- **If you have a pricing plan set-up:** Tap **Connect** under the relevant pricing plan.
Tap **Done**.Number of ParticipantsSet how many people can join a program. Select the relevant checkbox under the **Number of Participants** section:
- **Unlimited: **There is no limit on how many participants can join.
- **Limited: **Set the maximum number of participants who can join. Then, enter the **Maximum** number of participants.
8.  Tap **Save**.[image: A screenshot of the Wix Online Programs Pricing & Visibility settings panel.]

### Managing your program's schedule

Decide if your program is self-paced or set a schedule and choose its duration.

> **Important:**
>
> - Ongoing programs are no longer available. If you already have an ongoing program set up, you cannot duplicate it.
> - Pacing options cannot change once participants have joined the program.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the program you want to edit and click **Edit Settings**.
[image: A screenshot of the edit settings button in Wix Online Programs on the site dashboard]
1. Click **Schedule**.
2. Set your program to be self-paced or to run according to your schedule:
Self-PacedParticipants can complete steps whenever they like.
Choose the **Timing** for how long participants have to complete the program:
- **No Time Limit: **Participants can complete the program at their own pace.
- **Time Limit: **Participants have to complete the program within a certain timeframe.
ScheduledParticipants are expected to complete specific steps on the specific days you define.
Choose the **Program Duration** for when the program starts and ends:
- **Fixed Duration: **Set the exact amount of time your program should run. In this case, every new participant starts their program from day one, regardless of the time they join. Set the amount in either days or weeks under **Duration**.
- **Specific Dates: **The program starts and ends on the dates you select. Select a start date and an end date for your program.
6.  Click **Save**. [image: A screenshot of the Schedule panel of an online program.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon   next to the relevant program.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Scheduling**.
7. Set the **Pacing** of your program:
Self-Paced
1. Tap **Self-Paced** under the **Pacing** section.
2. Select the relevant checkbox under the **Timing** section to set how long participants have to complete the program:

- **Unlimited: **Participants can complete the program at their own pace.
- **Time Limit: **Give participants a set number of time to complete the program.

1. Select the duration from the **Time Limit** drop-down.
2. Tap **Done**.
Scheduled
1. Tap **Scheduled** under the **Pacing** section.
2. Select the relevant checkbox under the **Timing** section to set how long participants have to complete the program:

- **Flexible Start Date: **Lasts a set duration, participants can start anytime.

1. Select the duration from the **Flexible Start Date** drop-down.
2. Tap **Done**.
- **Specific Dates: **The program starts and ends on the dates you select. Select a start and end date for your program.
8.  Tap **Save**.[image: A screenshot of the Wix Online Programs Scheduling settings panel in the Wix Owner app.]

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [add steps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-sections-and-steps) to a published program that is live and has participants.

### Customizing your program's content

Customize how participants engage with and progress through the program. For example, you can set the sequence of steps your participants should follow.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the program you want to edit and click **Edit Settings**.
[image: A screenshot of the edit settings button in Wix Online Programs on the site dashboard]
1. Click **Content**.
2. Choose the relevant options based on your program type:

- **For a scheduled program: **Click the **Hide future steps** toggle to hide the content of future steps until a current step is completed.
- **For a self-paced program:** Set the sequence of steps your participants should follow:

- **Any Order: **Participants can complete steps in the order they want.
- **Fixed Order: **Participants can complete steps in the order you set for them. Enable the **Hide future steps** toggle to hide the content of future steps until current steps are completed.
3. Enable the relevant toggles to customize your video step experience:

- **Require to watch videos: **Set a required percentage participants must watch to complete the step.
- **Autoplay next video step: **Automatically play the next video after the current one ends. **Note:** These settings apply to all video steps in your program.
4. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Content panel of an online program.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon   next to the relevant program.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Content**.
7. Choose in what order participants view and complete steps:

- **For a scheduled program: **Enable the **Hide future steps** toggle to hide the content of future steps until a current step is completed.
- **For a self-paced program:** Set the sequence of steps your participants should follow:

- **Any Order: **Participants can complete steps in the order they want.
- **Fixed Order: **Participants can complete steps in the order you set for them. Enable the **Hide future steps** toggle to hide the content of future steps until current steps are completed.
8. Enable the relevant toggles to customize your video step experience:

- **Require to watch videos: **Set a required percentage participants must watch to complete the step.
- **Autoplay next video step: **Automatically play the next video after the current one ends. **Note:** These settings apply to all video steps in your program.
9. Tap **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Owner app Online Programs app Content settings panel.]

### Connecting a group to your program

Connect a group to your program to encourage ongoing discussions and progress sharing among participants. This dedicated group enables engagement through polls, posts, comments, and updates.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the program you want to edit and click **Edit Settings**.
[image: A screenshot of the edit settings button in Wix Online Programs on the site dashboard]
1. Click **Group**.
2. Click **Connect Group**.
3. Select the checkbox next to the relevant group.
4. Click **Done**.
5. (Optional) Enable the **Suggest posting to group** toggle to show participants a suggestion to share their progress on the step they completed.
6. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Group panel for Online Programs in a site's dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Group**.
7. Decide what you want to do: 

- Tap **Connect Group **and select the relevant existing group or [create a new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-groups-in-the-wix-owner-app) and tap **Done**.
- Tap **Disconnect** to remove a group. Then tap **Yes, Disconnect** to confirm.
- Enable the **Share Progress **toggle to allow program participants to share their progress to the group on mobile when they complete steps.
8. Tap **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Online Programs Group settings screen in the Wix owner app.]

> **Tip:**
>
> Once you've created a group for your program, you can [manage the group's settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-group-for-your-program) such as member list, and content in each tab. Additionally, you can disconnect or connect a different group as needed.

### Adding Rewards

Acknowledge participants' achievements with customized badges reflecting their accomplishments in the program. Additionally, boost motivation by designing and sharing certificates upon program completion, encouraging participants in their journey.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the program you want to edit and click **Edit Settings**.
[image: A screenshot of the edit settings button in Wix Online Programs on the site dashboard]
1. Click **Rewards**.
2. Click **Add Reward**.
3. Choose what reward you want to add to your program: 

- **Certificate:** 

1. Click **Create** **Certificate**.
2. [Start creating your certificate](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-certificate-for-your-program).
- **Badges: **

1. Click **+Add badge**.
2. Select the checkbox(s) next the relevant badge or click **+New Badge **to create a new one.
3. Click** Save**.
4. Select the relevant checkbox to decide when participants earn a badge:

- Complete all steps.
- Complete at least one step.
- Everyone gets a badge.
4. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Rewards panel with a certificate and badges connected to a program.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Rewards**.
7. Decide what you want to do: 

- Tap **Disconnect** to remove a certificate from your program.
- Tap [**Create a new badge**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-and-managing-member-badges)** **to connect a badge to your program.
- Tap **Manage Badges** to add or remove badges from your program.
- Tap **How do they earn it **to select when to award participants with a badge.
8. Tap **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Online Programs Rewards settings screen in the Wix owner app.]

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [creating and setting up a member badge](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-and-setting-up-a-member-badge).

### Setting notifications for your program

Set which notifications your participants receive about their program progress. Choose from email, mobile, or web notifications.

#### To set notifications for your program:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the program you want to edit and click **Edit Settings**.

Click **Notifications**.   Enable the toggle to notify your participants: 

- **Invitation: **This is always enabled to notify site members when they’ve been invited to join this program.
- **Steps For Today:** Notify participants on days they have steps to complete.
- **Program Has Ended:** Let participants know that the program has ended.
- **All Steps Complete:** Congratulate participants when they complete all steps in the program.
    (Optional) Click **Go To Automations** under **Want to explore more notifications? **to [create custom automated emails for your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-about-automations-for-your-program).    Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Customize Email **next to a notification to tailor the content of the email your participants receive.
