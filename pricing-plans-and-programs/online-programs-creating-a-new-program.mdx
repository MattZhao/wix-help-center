---
title: "Online Programs: Creating a New Program"
---

Create an online program to inspire, teach, and engage your audience with your business. Use our templates for courses, fitness challenges, nutrition plans, or personal coaching, or build your own program from scratch. Once your program is set up, fill it with content and start inviting people to join.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - [Add Online Programs to your site](https://www.wix.com/app-market/challenges). Some Wix templates already include Online Programs, making it easier for you to get started.
> - [Upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-upgrading-your-site) to allow people to join your program.

---

### Step 1 | Choose a program template

After [adding Online Programs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-and-setting-up-online-programs) to your site, you can now create a program. Choose an existing template to customize or start fresh and build your own program.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click** + Create New**.
3. Click the type of program you want to create:

- **Template: **Choose a program template from the list (e.g. Online Course).

1. Click **Next**.
2. Select one of the templates and click **Finish**.
- **Start Fresh:** If none of the templates suit your needs, you can create a program from scratch.

1. Click **Next**.
2. Enter a name and click **Next**.
3. Set the pace and click **Next**.
4. Set the duration and click **Let's Go**.
[image: A screenshot of Creating a New Program in the Wix site dashboard]What's next?A specific page for the new program is created in the **Draft** tab of the Online Programs section of your dashboard. From there, you can edit its content and settings.
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Online Programs.**
4. Tap **+** **Create a Program**.
5. Tap the type of program you want to create:

- **Start from scratch:** Create a program from a blank canvas.

1. Enter a name for your program and tap **Next**.
2. Choose the pace (**Self-Paced** or **Scheduled**) and tap **Next**.
3. Set the duration (**Unlimited** or **Time Limit**) and tap **Let's go**.
- **Edit a premade template: **Choose a program template that fits your needs.

1. Tap the relevant program topic (e.g. Fitness Challenge).
2. Scroll through the available templates and tap the desired one.
3. Tap **Edit This Template**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Owner app, Online Programs: Create a new program]

### Step 2 | Enter your program details

Your new program is saved as a draft and ready for you to customize. Introduce the program to your members, set the pricing, and choose between a self-paced structure or a scheduled one. You can also connect groups and assign badges or certificates to participants.

DashboardWix app
1. Click **Edit** next to **Program settings**.
2. Edit any of these details:
Basic Info
1. Click **Basic Info**.
2. Add the following program info:

- **Program Name: **Enter a new name or edit the existing one.
- **Category: **Add** **category labels to appear in your program list.
- **Cover image: **Customize your cover image.
- **Description: **Write what your program is all about.
- **Instructors: **[Add instructors and assign them to your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-program-contributors).
3. Click** Save**.
Visibility and Pricing
1. Click **Visibility and Pricing**.
2. Customize the following program info:

- **Visibility: **Decide who can view and join the program.
- **Pricing:** Decide whether people pay to join a program.

**Tip:** [Automatically send an invoice](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-automatically-sending-an-invoice-when-a-program-is-purchased) when someone purchases your program.
- **Number of Participants:** Set how many people can join a program.
3. Click** Save**.
Schedule
1. Click **Schedule**.
2. Set your program to be self-paced or have a predefined schedule: 

- **Self-Paced: **Participants can complete steps whenever they like. Set the **Timing**.
- **Scheduled: **Participants are expected to complete specific steps on specific days. Choose the **Program Duration** for when the program starts and ends.
3. Click **Save**.

**Tip: **[Create an automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-about-automations-for-your-program) to control participant access once the program is done. Content
1. Click **Content**.
2. Choose the order in which participants view and complete the steps: 

- **For a scheduled program: **Enable the **Hide future steps** toggle to hide the content of future steps until a current step is completed.
- **For a self-paced program: **Choose the step order for your participants to follow:

- **Any Order: **Participants can complete steps in the order they want.
- **Fixed Order: **Participants can complete steps in the order you set for them.
- Enable the **Hide future steps** toggle to hide the content of future steps until a current step is completed.
3. Enable the relevant toggles to customize your video step experience:

- **Require to watch videos: **Set a required percentage participants must watch to complete the step.
- **Autoplay next video step: **Automatically play the next video after the current one ends.

**Note:** These settings apply to all video steps in your program.
4. Click **Save**.
Group
1. Click **Group**.
2. Click **Connect Group**.
3. Select the checkbox next to the relevant group.
4. Click **Done**.
5. (Optional) Enable the **Suggest posting to group** toggle to show participants a suggestion to share their progress on the step they completed. This lets them post in the program's group using the relevant [members app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps).
6. Click **Save**.

[image: A screenshot of a participant's post in a group using the members' app]RewardsReward your participants to encourage and motivate them. You can create a certificate for completing your program and assign badges after completing certain steps.  
1. Click** Rewards**.
2. Choose what reward you want to add to your program: 

- **Certificate:** 

1. Click **Create** **Certificate**.
2. [Create your certificate](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-certificate-for-your-program).
- **Badges: **

1. Click **+Add badge**.
2. Select the checkbox(s) next the relevant badge or click **+New Badge **to create a new one.
3. Click** Save.  **
4. Select the relevant checkbox to decide when participants earn a badge:

- Complete all steps.
- Complete at least one step.
- Everyone gets a badge.
3. Click **Save**.
Notifications
1. Click **Notifications**.
2. Enable the relevant toggles to choose how and when to notify your participants: 

- **Invitation: **This is always enabled to notify site members when they’ve been invited to join this program.
- **Steps For Today:** Notify participants on days they have steps to complete.
- **Program Has Ended**: Let participants know that the program has ended.
- **All Steps Complete:** Congratulate participants when they complete all steps in the program.
3. (Optional) Click **Go To Automations** under **Want to explore more notifications?** to [create custom automated emails for your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-about-automations-for-your-program).
4. Click **Save**.
SEO
1. Click **SEO**.
2. Click the relevant tab to customize the SEO settings: 

- **Basics: **View how your program shows up on search engines, update title tags, meta descriptions, or your URL.
- **Advanced: **Mark up elements on your program so that search engines can understand the data, index the content for bots to interpret, and help search engines get important info about your program.
- **Social share:** Customize how search engines & social networks show the preview of your program.
3. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Program Settings to enter the program details.]
1. (In your new program draft) Tap the **Info** tab.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Owner app Online Programs app settings]
1. Choose which settings to edit in your program:
Basic Info
1. Tap the **Edit** icon  next to **Basic Info**.
2. Add the following program info:

- **Cover Image or Video: **Add a new cover image / video or replace the existing one.
- **Program Name: **Enter a new name or edit the existing one.
- **Description: **Write what your program is all about.
- **Category: **Add** **category labels to appear in your program list.
3. Tap **Save**.
Pricing & Visibility
1. Tap the **Edit** icon  next to **Pricing & Visibility**.
2. Customize the following program info:

- **Visibility: **Decide who can view and join the program.
- **Single Payment: **Enable the toggle to charge a flat fee for joining your program.
- [**Pricing Plans**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-an-overview)**:** Decide whether or not to include this program in a Pricing Plan.
- **Number of Participants:** Set how many people can join a program.
3. Tap **Save**.
Scheduling
1. Tap the **Edit** icon  next to **Scheduling**.
2. Set your program to be self-paced or have a predefined schedule: 

- **Self-Paced: **Participants can complete steps whenever they like.

1. Set the **Timing **(**Unlimited **or **Time Limit**).
2. Choose the **Step Order **(**Any Order **or **Fixed Order**).
- **Scheduled: **Participants are expected to complete specific steps on specific days. Choose the **Timing** for when the program starts and ends (**Flexible Start Date** or **Specific Dates**).
3. Tap **Save**.
Group
1. Tap the **Edit** icon [image: undefined] next to **Group**.
2. Tap **Connect a Group**.
3. Select the checkbox next to the relevant group or tap [**Create a New Group**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-groups-in-the-wix-owner-app#creating-a-group).
4. Tap **Save**.
Rewards
1. Tap the **Edit** icon  next to **Rewards**.
2. Tap **+** **Choose a Badge**.
3. Select a badge or tap [**Create New Badge**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-and-managing-member-badges#creating-a-badge).** **
4. Tap **Save**.
Tip:[Add and assign instructors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-program-contributors) to your program from your site's dashboard.

### Step 3 | Add sections and steps to your program

Build your program content by adding steps that guide participants toward their goals. Include multimedia elements and interactive activities to keep participants engaged, and group steps into sections for better organization.

> **Important:**
>
> Sections are only available for self-paced programs.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant program.
3. Click **+** **Add **next to **Content **or** Schedule**.
4. Select the relevant content type:

- [Add section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-sections-and-steps-to-your-program): Add a new section to your program.
- [Article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-embedding-interactive-content-in-an-article-or-section): Add an article to a section, include images, videos, buttons, files, and tables. If you're using Wix apps like Stores, Bookings, or Events, you can embed products, services, and events.
- [Video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-a-video-step-to-a-program): Add a video-based step to a section.
- [Quiz](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-and-managing-a-quiz#adding-a-quiz-after-a-completed-step): Add a quiz to keep participants engaged while completing this step.
- [Import Step](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-importing-a-step-from-an-existing-program): Add a step from one of your existing programs for quick editing.
5. [Customize](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-sections-and-steps-to-your-program) the step you created.
6. Click **Save**.
7. Repeat steps 3-6 to add more steps to your program.
[image: A screenshot of the Content panel of a Wix Online Program]
1. (In your new program draft) Tap the **Content** tab.
[image: A screenshot form the Wix Owner app open on Online Programs edit page]
1. Add new content to your program or edit existing content if you're using a template.
Add a new step
1. Tap **Show Steps **in the relevant section, then tap **+ Add Step**.
2. Select the step type (e.g. Article, Video, Quiz by AI).
3. Enter a name for your step.
4. Enter a description of your step explaining how to complete it.
5. Customize any remaining details.
6. Tap **Save **at the top right.
7. Repeat these steps to add more steps to your program.
Edit an existing stepIf you're using a template, you may want to change the content of a specific step. 

1. Tap the relevant step to edit it.

**Note: **If your program has sections, tap **Show Steps** first.
2. Update the step details, add a quiz or feedback form, or move the step to a different section (if relevant).
3. Tap **Save**.
Add a new section
1. Tap **Add Section**.
2. Customize any of the following section details:

- **Cover Image/Video:** Add a cover photo/video for the section.
- **Section Name:** Name your section.
- **Available:** Tap the **Available** drop-down to select when people can join. Tap **Done**.
- **Description: **Add a short description of the section.
3. Tap **Save**.
Edit an existing sectionIf you're using a template, you may want to change section names, their cover image, description or availability. 

1. Tap the relevant section.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
3. Tap **Edit**.
4. Edit any of the section details.
5. Tap **Save**.
Tip:Reorder your steps and sections using drag and drop.

### Step 4 | Publish your program and invite participants

After setting up your program, you’re ready to make it go live. Publish it and invite participants to join.

> **Note:**
>
> When you invite someone to join your program they are not automatically added. Participants cannot preview the steps and content of your program before they join.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant program.
3. Click **Publish** at the top right.
4. Click **Publish** to confirm.
5. (Optional) Invite people to join your program:

1. Click **Invite Participants**.
2. Choose how you want to invite people to join:

- **Invite site members: **Click** Invite to Program** and select the checkbox next to the relevant person. Then, click** Invite to Program**.
- **Share invite link to the program:** Copy the link and share it with the people you want to invite.
- **Send an email campaign:** Click **Create Email Campaign** to design an appealing email campaign that invites people to join your program.
[image: A screenshot of a published program to invite participants to.]
1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
2. Tap** Publish**.
3. Tap **Publish** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot form the Wix Owner app open on Online Programs]
1. (Optional) Invite people to join your program:

1. Tap **Invite Participants**.
2. Choose how you want to invite people to join:

- **Invite members: **Type a member's name in the text box or select the checkbox next to the relevant member(s), then tap **Done**.
- **Invite Another Way:** Invite people to join your program by sending them an SMS, a link to join your program or through social media.
