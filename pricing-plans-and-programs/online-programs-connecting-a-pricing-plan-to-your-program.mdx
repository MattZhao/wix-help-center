---
title: "Online Programs: Connecting a Pricing Plan to Your Program"
---

You can monetize your program by linking it to a pricing plan. Offer different membership packages to encourage subscriptions. You can even enroll people when they buy the right plan. Already have existing plans, for apps like blogs, events, or groups? You can connect the same plans to your programs too. Finally, if a plan isn't relevant anymore, disconnect it.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - [Connect a payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments) to get paid for your subscription plans.
> - [Upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-upgrading-your-site) so people can start joining your program.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Pricing Plans](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Pricing%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/membership/) in your site's dashboard.

**Tip:** You can also [link a pricing plan to your program using the Online Programs app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#updating-your-programs-visibility-and-pricing).
2. Choose what you want to do next:
Connect a new pricing plan
1. Click **+ Create Plan**.
2. Create the relevant plan: 

- [**Free plans**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-free-plan)**: **Offer your clients a free membership plan.
- [**One-time payment**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan#step-3-set-the-price-and-duration-of-your-plan)**: **Create a one-time plan that lasts for the time period you choose.
- [**Recurring payment**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-recurring-payment-plan#step-3-set-the-price-and-duration-of-your-plan)**: **Create a subscription plan with recurring payments and select how often the plan and payment reoccurs: weekly, monthly, or yearly.
3. Connect and manage benefits for this plan:

1. Select the checkbox next to **Online Programs**.
2. Click **+ Add Program** under **Programs Included** and select the checkbox next to the program(s) you want to include in the pricing plan.
3. (Optional) Select the **Automatically add subscribers to these programs when they purchase this plan **checkbox. This means people who purchase this pricing plan are automatically added to the program(s) you selected.
4. (Optional) Review the **Plan preview** to see how your plan will appear online.
5. Click **Save**.

**Note: **You can have up to 75 active pricing plans at the same time.Connect an existing pricing plan
1. Hover on the relevant plan and click **Edit**.
2. Scroll down to **Benefits**.** **
3. Click **+ Add Program** next to **Online Programs**.
4. Select the program(s) you want to include in the pricing plan.
5. (Optional) Select the **Automatically add subscribers to these programs when they purchase this plan **checkbox. This means people who purchase this pricing plan are automatically added to the program(s) you selected.
6. Click **Save**.
Disconnect a pricing plan from a program
1. Click **Edit **next to the relevant plan under **Active plans.**
2. Scroll down to **Benefits** and click **X** next to the relevant program.
3. Click **Save**.

**Note: **
- [Manually added members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-program-participants) have access to your program regardless of pricing plans.
- If someone joins a program by buying a plan, they only have access as long as that plan is active. If you remove the plan, they lose access. They can get it back by renewing their plan.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Pricing Plans** under **Catalog**. 

**Tip: **You can also [link a pricing plan to your program using the Online Programs app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-your-programs#updating-your-programs-visibility-and-pricing).
4. Connect a new pricing plan:

1. Tap **+ Create New Plan**.
2. Create the relevant plan: 

- [**Free plans**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-free-plan)**: **Offer your clients a free membership plan.
- [**One-time payment**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan#step-3-set-the-price-and-duration-of-your-plan)**: **Create a one-time plan that lasts for the time period you choose.
- [**Recurring payment**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-recurring-payment-plan#step-3-set-the-price-and-duration-of-your-plan)**: **Create a subscription plan with recurring payments and select how often the plan and payment reoccurs: weekly, monthly, or yearly.
3. Connect benefits for this plan:

1. Tap **+ Connect Programs** under **Online Programs**.
2. Select the checkbox next to the program(s) you want to include in the pricing plan.
3. Tap **Done**.
4. Tap **Save**.

**Note: **You can have up to 75 active pricing plans at the same time.

> **Tip:**
>
> Once you create a pricing plan, it is not currently possible to delete it. You can [vote for this feature here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-request-permanently-deleting-plans). You can, however, [hide or archive your plans as necessary](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-removing-a-plan-from-your-site) to create new plans.
