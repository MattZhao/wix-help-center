---
title: "Online Programs: Approving Requests to Join Your Program in the Wix App"
---

When you create a private program, members of your site can see its details but must submit a request to join and access any content.

> **Important:**
>
> - You must [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-upgrading-your-site) so people can start joining your program.
> - If you've set a price for your private program, new participants are automatically approved once they make the payment.

### To approve requests to join your program:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Online Programs**.  Tap the program you want to manage.   Tap **Request to Join** at the top.  Tap **Approve** next to the member you want to approve.   When ready, tap **Done**.
