---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Ability to Switch Plans"
---

Currently, it is not possible to switch a client from one plan to another one.

> **Workaround:**
>
> However, you can do one of the following:
> - [Cancel the existing plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-canceling-client-plans) and [manually add your client to a new plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-selling-plans-offline).
> - [Have your customer cancel their plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-allowing-clients-to-cancel-plans) and then have them purchase a new plan online.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
