---
title: "Wix Online Programs Request: Offering Free Sections or Steps in Paid Programs"
---

Currently it's not possible to add free steps or sections within a paid program, or the opposite; paid sections or steps in free programs.

> **Workaround:**
>
> Create a single-use [free promotional plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-free-plan) for potential participants.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
