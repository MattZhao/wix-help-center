---
title: "Pricing Plans: Reordering How You Display Your Plans"
---

Change the order of your pricing plans on your site to highlight specific options or create a more logical flow. For example, prioritize the most popular plans, or those with exclusive offers to capture visitors' attention.

You can easily reorder your plans while editing the Plans & Pricing page in your editor, or on-the-go from the Wix app and Studio app.

Wix EditorEditor XStudio EditorMobile Apps
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Plans & Pricing page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Plans & Pricing page.
3. Click the Plans & Pricing element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click **Plans**.
6. Click **Reorder Plans**.
7. Drag and drop the plans into your preferred order.
[image: A screenshot showing how to reorder your pricing plans in the Wix Editor.]
1. Click **Publish** to make the change live on your site.
2. Click **Done**.

You can reorder your pricing plans from both the Wix app or Studio app. Click the relevant app below to reorder your plans:Wix app
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Site & App.**
4. Tap **Customize** under **Mobile Apps**.
5. Tap the **Pricing Plans** element.
6. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of the element.

[image: A screenshot of the Pricing Plans element in the Wix Owner app.]
7. Tap **Customize element**.
8. Decide how you want to organize your Pricing Plans under **Sorting**:

- **Oldest to newest**.
- **Newest to oldest**.
- **Custom: **Reorder your plans the way you want to showcase them on your site.

1. Tap** Set Custom Order**.
2. Tap and drag the plans into your preferred order.
9. Tap **Save**.
10. Tap **Save** to confirm.
Studio app
1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant site.
3. Tap **Manage site**.

[image: A screenshot of the available options when you tap Manage site in the Studio app.]
4. Tap **Site & App.**
5. Tap **Customize** under **Mobile Apps**.
6. Tap the **Pricing Plans** element.
7. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of the element.
8. Tap **Customize element**.
9. Decide how you want to organize your Pricing Plans under **Sorting**:

- **Oldest to newest**.
- **Newest to oldest**.
- **Custom: **Reorder your plans the way you want to showcase them on your site.

1. Tap** Set Custom Order**.
2. Tap and drag the plans into your preferred order.
10. Tap **Save**.
11. Tap **Save** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot of the Set Custom Order panel in the Wix Mobile Apps.]
