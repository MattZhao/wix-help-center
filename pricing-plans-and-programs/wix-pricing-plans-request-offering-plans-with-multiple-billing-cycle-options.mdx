---
title: "Wix Pricing Plans Request: Offering Plans With Multiple Billing Cycle Options"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create pricing plans with multiple different billing cycles. For example, one plan could have a monthly price and also a yearly price.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
