---
title: "Online Programs: Customizing Your Program List"
---

The Program List displays a preview of all your programs. Your site visitors can click to view the program's full details. You can adjust displayed details and personalize layout, text and design.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Program Pages**.
4. Click your Program List page.
5. Click the Program List** **element on your page.
6. Click **Settings**.
7. Customize the Program List** **element using the available tabs:
Categories
- Select the categories you want to display on your program list.
- Drag and drop category tabs in the order you want them to appear.

**Note: **Can't see the Categories tab? Go to your site's dashboard to [create categories](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-and-managing-online-program-categories) and assign them to your programs.
Programs
- Select the box next to a program to display it on the list.
- Drag and drop programs in the order you want them to appear.
- Click **Add a Program** to create a new program in your site's dashboard.

DisplayChoose which details you want to display:
- **List Details: **Enable the** **toggle to show the title on the Program List. 

**Note: **You can edit the default "Programs" title from the **Text** tab.
- **Program Details: **Click the checkbox next to each detail you want to show on the list:

- **Image: **Show the program cover image.
- **Duration: **Show the duration of your program (e.g. 18 days).
- **Participants: **Show the number of participants in the program.
- **Price:** Show the price of each program.

Layout
- Select between a Grid or Side by side alignment: 

- **Grid: **Displays your programs in rows and columns.
- **Side by side: **Displays your programs in a vertical stack.
- Customize the layout according to your preferences.

Design
- Select elements to design: 

- **Background & dividers: **Design the app background and the card background.
- **Text Style: **Style text elements like program name, participants, duration, and price.
- **Buttons & badges:** 

- **Buttons: **Adjust the **Default **and **Hover **states of your buttons.
- **Badges: **Display the label text you can customize in the **Text **tab.
- **Categories:** Customize the **Default **and **Selected** states for your categories.

**Note:** Click **Reset to Original Design** at the bottom of the panel to undo the changes. 
Text
- Click the relevant fields to update the text:

- **List title: **Edit the title that displays at the top of the list.
- **Buttons: **Edit the text that displays on buttons.
- **Labels: **Edit the text that displays on labels.
