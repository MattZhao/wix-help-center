### Upgrading your site

When you upgrade your site, you can invite participants to your programs. Additionally, people can join your program and become participants, connecting with others who share similar goals.

> **Important:**
>
> Make sure to choose a plan that accepts payments.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about upgrading your site.

### What are the benefits to upgrade?

Upgrading your site allows you to invite as many participants as you like to your program, which can include features like quizzes to enhance participant engagement. You can also send out certificates and create a discussion group to keep them motivated throughout their journey.

### I already have a plan, but I am unable to invite participants. Why?

You might have purchased a plan that does not include accepting payments, which is necessary to invite unlimited participants to your program. You need to upgrade to any plan that accepts payments.
