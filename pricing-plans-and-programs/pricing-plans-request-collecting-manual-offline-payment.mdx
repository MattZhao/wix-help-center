title: "Pricing Plans Request: Collecting Manual (Offline) Payment"
---

Currently, it is not possible to allow clients to pay for pricing plans with an offline method, such as paying in cash or by check.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
