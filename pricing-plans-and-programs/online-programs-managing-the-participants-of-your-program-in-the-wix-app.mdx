title: "Online Programs: Managing the Participants of Your Program in the Wix App"
---

Keep up-to-date with your online program participant's progress in the Wix app. From the app, you can track participant activity, start chats and update participant program status, request payment, approve join requests, and invite more people to join your program while you're on the move.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Make sure to [add Online Programs to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-adding-and-setting-up-online-programs) so you can access it in the Wix app.
> - You must [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-upgrading-your-site) so people can start joining your program.

**In this article, learn more about: **

[Inviting participants](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-the-participants-of-your-program-in-the-wix-owner-app#inviting-participants)  [Approving join requests](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-the-participants-of-your-program-in-the-wix-owner-app#approving-join-requests)  [Viewing participant profiles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-the-participants-of-your-program-in-the-wix-owner-app#viewing-the-profiles-of-your-participants)  [Tracking participant progress](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-the-participants-of-your-program-in-the-wix-owner-app#tracking-participant-progress)  [Chatting with participants](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-managing-the-participants-of-your-program-in-the-wix-owner-app#chatting-with-participants)

### Inviting participants

When you send an invitation from the Wix app, it takes participants to your site in the relevant [member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps) and, from there, they can complete steps on the go. Managing your invitees is simple and, if a participant is no longer active, you can remove them from the program too.

> **Important:**
>
> When you create a private program, members of your site can view information about the program, but need to submit a request to join and access the content. Learn more about [approving requests to join your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-approving-requests-to-join-your-program-in-the-wix-owner-app).

#### To add participants:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Online Programs**.  Tap the relevant program.  Tap **Invite Participants**.  Choose what you want to do: 

- **Invite Participants: **

1. Tap the box next to the member(s) you want to invite to join your program.
2. Tap **Done**.
- **Invite another way: **

1. Tap **Invite Another Way**.
2. Choose from the following options:

- **Email Wix Contacts:** Send an email to contacts saved in your Wix account (site members, customers, contacts etc.).
- **Send an SMS:** Send the link and invitation code to people by SMS (text message).
- **Copy Link: **Copy the link and paste it into a new message in any application.
- **Invite via Social & More:** Share the link and invitation code to Gmail or any social network connected to your phone.

> **To remove participants from your program:**
>
> 1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
> 2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
> 3. Tap **Online Programs**.
> 4. Tap the relevant program.
> 5. Tap the participant you want to remove.
> 6. Tap the **More Actions** icon ﻿.
> 7. Tap **Remove Participant**.
> 8. Tap **Remove** to confirm.

### Approving join requests

When you create a private program, members of your site can see its details but must submit a request to join and access the content. You can approve or decline these requests in the Wix app. If your program is public (not private), you don't need to approve join requests.

> **Important:**
>
> If you've set a price for your private program, new participants are automatically approved once they make the payment.

#### To approve requests:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Online Programs**.  Tap the relevant program.  Tap **View** next to the pending request.   Tap **Approve** next to the member you want to approve. 

**Tip:** You can approve multiple requests by tapping **Approve All** at the bottom.

### Viewing the profiles of your participants

Each participant has their own profile that includes their contact information, join date, active subscriptions, and any activity since the program started. From participant profiles, you can monitor program tasks, track progress, view payment requests, assign collaborators, add notes or reminders for yourself, assign badges, and more.

#### To view profiles:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Online Programs**.  Tap the relevant program.   Tap **See All **next to **Participants**.   Tap the relevant participant. 

**Note: **If you have no participants yet, tap **Invite Participants **to invite them.   Tap **View Profile**.   Choose what you want to do:

### Send a message

1. Tap **Message**.
2. Use the message composer at the bottom to create and send your message.

Learn more about [sending messages through the Wix App](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-accessing-and-managing-messages-from-your-inbox).

### Invite them to join the members app

1. Tap **Invite**.

When you [invite people to join your members app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps), they receive a link to download the relevant members app. After they download the app, your members can engage with your business and online community from their mobile device.

### Book an appointment

1. Tap **Book** under **Manage**.
2. Select the relevant booking information and tap **Save**.

Learn more about [managing bookings from the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-bookings-in-the-wix-owner-app).

### Sell a Pricing Plan

1. Tap **Sell **under **Manage**.
2. Choose the plan you want to sell and tap **Next**.
3. Tap **Collect Payment**.
4. Tap the relevant option: **Mark as paid **or **Mark as unpaid**.
5. Tap** Next**.

Learn more about [managing purchased plans in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-managing-purchased-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app).

### Add a task

1. Tap **More**.
2. Tap **Add a task**.
3. Enter a description for the task.
4. Tap **Assign to **and choose who you want to assign the task to.
5. Tap **Add **next to **Set due date **and select the date in which the task is due.
6. Tap **Add**.

Learn more about [managing tasks in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-tasks-reminders-from-the-wix-mobile-app-9029833).

### Add a note

1. Tap **More**.
2. Tap **Add note**.
3. Enter a note for the participants profile.
4. (Optional) Tap **Select Type (optional) **and choose a note type.
5. Tap **Save**.

### Call the participant

1. Tap **More**.
2. Tap **Call**.

To call your participant, they must have a phone number in their profile. Learn more about [making outbound calls from the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-making-outgoing-calls-from-the-wix-owner-app).

### Assign / manage badges

1. Tap **More**.
2. Tap **Assign badges **or tap **Manage badges**.
3. (If this is your 1st badge) Tap **Create Badge** and [create a member badge](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-and-managing-member-badges).
4. Select the badge you want to assign and tap **Save**.

### Assign contact to a collaborator

1. Tap **More**.
2. Tap **Assign contact**.
3. Tap the relevant collaborator and tap **Save**.

Learn more about [assigning contacts to collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-assigning-a-contact-to-a-collaborator).

### Block member

1. Tap **More**.
2. Tap **Block member**.
3. Tap **Block **to confirm.

To unblock a member: 
1. Tap **More**.
2. Tap **Unblock member**.
3. Tap **Unblock **to confirm.

### Tracking participant progress

Check how your participants are doing with their program. Track their progress, see how many steps they've completed on time, find out how steps many were missed, and see anything they've uploaded.

Next to each participant's name are labels that can be used to monitor their status. Gray labels with a tick represent the steps that your participant has completed on time, and red labels with exclamation marks represent any steps that they've missed or are yet to take.

#### To track progress:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Online Programs**.  Tap the relevant program.  Tap **See All** next to **Participants**.   Tap any participant's name to see the participant's activity once the program has started. From there, you can view: 

- If they have started your program.
- The date they joined your program.
- When they were last active.
- A calendar that tracks their scheduled steps, completed steps, and/or missed steps.
- (If available) Feedback they've left for each step.

### Chatting with participants

Communicate with your program participants on their preferred channel directly from the Wix app. Select which channel to communicate through, and share information or media. Chatting with your participants can boost morale and enables you to give guidance and useful tips.

#### To chat with participants:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage** at the bottom.  Tap **Online Programs**.  Tap the relevant program.   Tap **See All **next to **Participants**.   Tap the relevant participant.   Tap **Message**.   Choose the relevant communication channel from the dropdown and create your message.

1. If you would like to share media in the chat, choose from the following options: 

1. **Saved replies:** Tap the** Saved replies** icon  and choose from a pre-saved reply (e.g about shipping info, return policy etc.).

**Tip:** Tap **New Saved Reply** to create a new reply.
2. **Upload media: **Tap the** Upload media** icon  to: 

1. **Take a photo: **Tap **Take a photo** to add media directly from your device's camera.
2. **Choose from device: **Tap **Choose from device** to select a media file to add from your device.
3. **Select from Media Manager:** Tap **Select from Media Manager** to choose from uploaded media.
3. **Send an item:** Tap the **Send an item** icon  to: 

1. Send photos: Tap the **Photos** icon  to share a photo in the chat.
2. Send saved replies: Tap the **Saved** **replies **icon  to share a saved or new reply in the chat.
3. Send products: Tap **Products **icon  to share an in stock product in the chat.
4. Send coupons: Tap **Coupons **icon  to share discounts and offers in the chat.
5. Send services: Tap **Services icon ** to share a service (online program) in the chat.
6. Get Payment:** **Tap **Get Payment** icon  to send a payment request in the chat.

**Note:** You need a payment method connected to request payment through chat. Learn how to connect a [payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview) to your site.
    Tap **Send**.
