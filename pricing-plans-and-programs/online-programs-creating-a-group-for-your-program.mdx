[Create a group for your program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-group-for-your-program#creating-a-group-for-your-program)  [Manage your group settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-creating-a-group-for-your-program#managing-your-group-settings)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> If you haven't added the [Wix Groups app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-and-setting-up-wix-groups) yet, it will automatically install when you create a group for a program.

### Creating a group for your program

Create a group for participants to make it easy for them to meet and share information. This encourages participants to come together in a group where they can create polls, post, comment and share with each other.

> **Notes:**
>
> - If you set up the group after people have joined your program, existing participants need to be added manually or join on their own.
> - When new people join your site, you can set up an automation to enable them to [join your group automatically](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-creating-automations-for-your-group).

#### To create a group:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.  Select **Edit Settings **from the drop-down.  Click **Group**.  Click** Connect Group**.    Choose what you want to do:

- **Connect an existing group: **Select the relevant group.
- **Create a new group:** 

1. Click **Create Group **at the bottom.
2. Enter the desired group name.
3. Choose who can see and join this group under **Privacy**:

**Tip:** Existing site members can join any group, regardless of whether they have a public or private profile. 

- **Public: **Anyone can view this group.
- **Private: **Only members can view the group's content. Click the **Visibility** drop-down to decide if the group appears on your site.
- **Only paying members:** Only members who purchase the relevant [Pricing Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-connecting-a-pricing-plan-to-a-group) can view this group.
4. Click** Create.  **
    Click** Done**.  Click **Save **at the top right.

### Managing your group settings

Once you've created a group for your program you can manage the settings. Start by customizing the details of your group, the member list and the information in each tab like group discussions and media.

You can also disconnect the group if you no longer need it or if you want to connect a different one to your program.

#### To manage your group settings:

[Go to Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant program.  Select **Edit Settings **from the drop-down.  Click **Group**.  Click the** Group Settings **drop-down and choose what you want to do:

- **Customize Group: **Manage the settings of your group such as its info, posts and a variety of admin tools.
- **Disconnect: **Remove the group from your program.

1. Click** Disconnect**.
2. Click **Disconnect** to confirm.

**Note: **When you disconnect a group, the group is not deleted and no members are removed. However, new program participants are no longer automatically added to the group.
    Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Enable the **Suggest posting to group** toggle to show participants a suggestion to share their progress on the step they completed. This lets them post in the program's group using the relevant [members app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps).
