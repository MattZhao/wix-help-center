---
title: "Wix Loyalty Program: Adding Tiers to Your Loyalty Program"
---

Add tiers to your loyalty program to make it a levels-based program that assigns members to a particular tier (e.g. "Basic", "Silver", "Gold") depending on the points they have earned.

### Read an example of how one business uses tiers

Jane owns a homeware business. Using Wix Stores, she sells furniture and accessories. She'd like to reward her customers for their loyalty and encourage them to keep coming back to her brand.

Jane has added tiers to her loyalty program to reward and incentivize customers:
1. **Basic:** All loyalty program members will automatically be added to this tier. They'll enjoy basic benefits such as 5 points for every $1 they spend, and earn basic rewards such as 5% off when they spend $50.
2. **Silver: **When a member has earned 100 points, they'll enter this tier. They'll enjoy silver benefits such as 10 points for every $1 they spend, and earn silver rewards such as 10% off when they spend $50.
3. **Gold:** When a member has earned 200 points, they'll enter this tier. They'll enjoy gold benefits such as 15 points for every $1 they spend, and earn gold rewards such as 15% off when they spend $50.

> **To add tiers, you must have:**
>
> - A [Business or Business Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) (for Wix Editor users) or a [Plus plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) (for Studio users).
> - At least one of these apps: Wix Stores, Wix Events, Wix Bookings, Wix Pricing Plans.
>
> **Note:** Currently, tiers cannot be added to Wix Restaurants loyalty programs. Vote for the ability to add tiers to Wix Restaurants loyalty programs in [this feature request](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-request-adding-tiers-to-your-wix-restaurants-loyalty-program).

### Step 1 | Add and customize tiers

Choose how many tiers you'd like to use, and customize tier names, icons and descriptions. Define how many points are needed to enter each tier.

> **Note:**
>
> If you haven't set up your loyalty program yet, [follow these steps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-adding-and-setting-up-your-loyalty-program). You'll be able to add tiers during setup or after launching your program.
> If you add tiers before launching your loyalty program, 3 predefined tiers will appear: "Basic", "Silver" and "Gold". If you add tiers after launching your loyalty program, your existing "ways to earn" and rewards from your previous non-tiered program will be assigned to a "Basic" tier.

#### To add and customize tiers:

Go to [Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Program** at the top right.  Click the **Points and rewards** tab.  Click **Set Up Tiers**.  (If you've already launched your program) Click **Continue to Set Up** in the pop-up that opens.

Your program now features tiers. Add new tiers or edit existing tiers:

1. Click **+ Add New Tier** or hover over an existing tier and click **Edit**.
2. Enter the tier details:

1. **Tier name**: Enter a name for your tier.
2. (Optional) **Description:** Enter a description of the benefits of this tier.
3. **Minimum points to enter tier:** Enter the points a member must earn to enter this tier.
4. (Optional) **Icon:** Click the **+** and select or upload an icon to represent this tier.
5. Click **Save**.

Repeat step 6a above for every other tier you'd like to add.

**Note:** Click the More Actions icon  next to a tier and click **Delete** to delete a tier.

> **The "Basic" tier:**
>
> The "Basic" tier cannot be deleted and the points needed to enter the tier can’t be edited - this will be always be set at 0 points. This means customers who collect loyalty points will enter this tier automatically.

---

### Step 2 | Assign ways to earn and rewards to each tier

Set the ways you'd like members to be able to earn and redeem points. You can make these different for each tier.

#### To assign ways to earn and rewards:

Assign ["ways to earn"](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-adding-and-setting-up-your-loyalty-program#step-2-choose-how-members-earn-points) to your tiers:

1. Scroll down to the **Earn points** section.
2. Hover over one of your **Ways to earn** and click **Edit**.
3. Select the checkbox next to each tier you want to be available for this point-earning method.
4. Enter the total points members earn for each tier and click **Save**.

Repeat step 1 above for each of your point-earning methods.  Assign [rewards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-adding-and-setting-up-your-loyalty-program#step-3-set-up-loyalty-rewards) to your tiers:

1. Scroll down to the **Rewards** section.
2. Hover over a **Reward type** and click **Edit**.
3. Select the checkbox next to each tier you want to be available for this reward.
4. Enter the total points members can redeem to get this reward and click **Save**.
    Repeat step 3 above for each reward type.

> **Note:**
>
> Each time you create a new "way to earn" or reward, you'll be able to edit points earned or redeemed for each tier.

---

### Step 3 | Setting your tier eligibility period

Tiers have an eligibility period. If joining a new tier requires 200 points and the tier eligibility period is 1 year, a customer will need to earn another 200 points each year to ensure they remain in that tier.

> **Note:**
>
> The tier eligibility period you select will apply retroactively. If you already have customers with loyalty points, they will automatically join the tiers that align with the points they've earned over the previous 1, 2 or 3 years (depending on the eligibility period you've selected).

#### To set your tier eligibility period:

Click **Edit** next to **Tier eligibility period** at the top of the **Points and rewards** tab.  Click the **Duration** drop-down to select the period of time customers have to use their tier benefits before these reset.  Click **Save**.

---

### Step 4 | (Optional) Customize the tiers information on your site pages

When you [launch a Wix Loyalty Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-adding-and-setting-up-your-loyalty-program), 2 new pages are automatically generated to be added to your site: a Loyalty Program page and a My Rewards page. When you add tiers to your Loyalty Program, these pages will automatically update to reflect this new tier structure. Page content and layout will be updated so visitors and members can view the details of your tiered program.

> **Notes:**
>
> - If your program is already live when you add tiers, the layouts of your Loyalty Program page and My Rewards page will be updated automatically without you publishing your site in your Editor.
> - If a tier exists with no "ways to earn" or rewards assigned to it, it won’t be visible on your site pages. If a member gains the number of points required to enter this tier, they will enter it and will not benefit from any "ways to earn" or rewards specific to this tier until these have been added.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about adding tiers to your Loyalty Program.

### Why do I see an "Upgrade" button next to "Tiers" even though I already have an upgraded plan?

You may not have the type of upgraded plan required. To add tiers to your Loyalty Program, you must have a [Business or Business Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) (for Wix Editor users) or a [Plus plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) (for Wix Studio users).

### Will I be able to return to my non-tiered program if I add tiers and then decide not to use them?

You can disable tiers at any time by clicking the Tiers toggle on the **Points and rewards** tab.[image: A screenshot of the Tiers toggle.]If you enable and then later disable tiers, all of the values attached to "ways to earn" and rewards will relate to what you had assigned to the "Basic" tier. If you enable tiers, then edit parts of the "Basic" tier, and then disable tiers, your edits will remain.
Enabling or disabling tiers will have no effect on your members' points balances (because your loyalty program will remain active throughout).

### What happens to my program members and their points if I change my upgraded plan?

If you cancel your upgraded plan or change it to a plan other than [Business or Business Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) (for Wix Editor users) or a [Plus plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) (for Wix Studio users), this will have no effect on your members' points balances (as long as your loyalty program remains).

### Can I change my tier eligibility period?

You can change your tier eligibility period at any time. If you change your tier eligibility period after your program has been launched, the new period will apply to all customers (both new and existing).

### Can I manage my program's tiers in the Wix app?

Yes, you can manage your tiers in the Wix app. Any changes you make to "ways to earn" and rewards will reflect in the relevant tiers. You can also edit tier details and add new tiers on the go.

> **Keep track of the tiers your members belong to:**
>
> Go to [Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard to view a list of your program members and the tiers they belong to. You can also see an overview of your program and tiers by clicking **Manage Program** and selecting the **Program info** tab.
