---
title: "Pricing Plans Request: Adding Optional Extra Benefits to a Plan"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add optional extra benefits to a Pricing Plan (either free or paid) and allow customers to choose if they want to include them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
