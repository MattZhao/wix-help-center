---
title: "Online Programs: Approving Requests to Join Your Program"
---

When you create a private program, members of your site must submit a request to join and access the content.

> **Important:**
>
> - You must [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-upgrading-your-site) so people can start joining your program.
> - If you've set a price for your program, new participants are automatically approved once they make the payment.

### To approve requests to join your program:

Go to [Online Programs](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Online%20Programs&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/challenges/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant program.  Click **Manage** next to **Participants**.  Click the **Pending **tab.  Click **Approve** next to the member(s) you want to approve.
