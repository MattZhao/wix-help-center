---
title: "CMS Request: Displaying Multiple Items per Slide in a Slideshow Repeater"
---

Currently, in the Studio Editor, it is not possible to show more than one item in each slide of a [slideshow repeater](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-slideshow-repeater). This feature would allow you to show multiple items, side by side, in each of the slideshow repeater's slides.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can, however, display multiple CMS (Content Management System) items at the same time in a [repeater](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-dynamic-content-in-a-repeater), [gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-collection-content-in-a-pro-gallery), or [table](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-displaying-collection-content-in-a-table).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
