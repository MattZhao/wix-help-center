---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Adding a Pin to Screen Option for Your Widget"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add Pin to Screen as an installation setting for a widget. This feature would allow your app users to pin your widget, so it's always visible, even when scrolling down the page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
