---
title: "CMS Request: Linking Directly to Site Pages in the Rich Content Editor"
---

Currently, when adding links in the [Rich Content Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-rich-content-and-adding-it-to-a-dynamic-page), it is not possible to select your site pages as link destinations. This includes selecting dynamic pages, blog posts, and other pages as links.

> **Workaround:**
>
> However, you can paste the URL of any web address, such as your live site pages' URLs, when linking in the Rich Content Editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
