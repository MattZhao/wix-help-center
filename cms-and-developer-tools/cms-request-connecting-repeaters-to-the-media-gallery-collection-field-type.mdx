---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Repeaters to the Media Gallery Collection Field Type"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect repeaters to the media gallery field type in your CMS collection. You cannot connect the media gallery field to repeater backgrounds or image elements that are attached to the repeater.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
