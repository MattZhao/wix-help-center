---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Deleting a Blocks App"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks it is not possible to delete a Blocks app from your workspace. You can, however, [archive an app you no longer need](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-archiving-your-app).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
