---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Designing Apps Locally Before Submitting Changes"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, it is not possible to design your apps in an isolated environment that only you can see.

This feature would allow you to work on designs locally and submit changes for others to see once you are happy with them.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
