---
title: "CMS Request: Increasing the Total Field Storage Limit for an Item in a Collection"
---

Currently, there is a limit to how much data you can store in a single item's combined collection fields. You can store up to 500KB of data in an item's total collection fields.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
