---
title: "CMS Request: Data Binding Wix Video with a Collection"
---

Currently, you can link elements such as repeaters, images, buttons and text to content in a collection by connecting (data binding) the element to a dataset. One of the elements you can't data bind to a dataset is Wix Video.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
