---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Creating Dashboard Modals"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, you have the ability to [build dashboard pages for an app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-creating-and-managing-blocks-dashboard-pages) to provide site creators with a back office to manage the app's data and settings. However, it is not possible to build modals for these pages (e.g. confirmation, warning) that pop up and provide information.

This feature would allow you to show site creators vital info that appears on top of the page they're viewing as they navigate around the app's dashboard.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
