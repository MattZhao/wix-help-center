---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Outer-Widget Action Button to Open Inner-Widget Panel"
---

Currently in Blocks, when you have a [widget-in-widget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-creating-and-managing-widgets-within-widgets), you cannot configure an action button from the outer widget to open the panels of the inner one.
