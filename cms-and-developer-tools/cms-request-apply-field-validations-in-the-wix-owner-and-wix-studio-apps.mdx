---
title: "CMS Request: Apply Field Validations in the Wix Owner and Wix Studio Apps"
---

Currently, if you [set validations for a CMS collection field](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-setting-field-validations-in-your-collections), the validations do not apply when you add content to the field from the Wix Owner or Wix Studio app. From these apps, you can add content that ignores field validations you set including; **Make this a required field**, **Limit character count**, and **Accept specific values only**. When you view the collection from a desktop computer, you will see a Warning icon  letting you know to fix any values that do not match the validations you set.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
