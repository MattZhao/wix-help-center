---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Designing Widgets According to Breakpoints"
---

Currently, in [Wix Blocks](https://support.wix.com/en/article/blocks-unboxed-a-quick-tour), you can build responsive layouts and define which layout is shown on mobile or desktop. However, it is not possible to design widgets that adjust according to specific custom breakpoints.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
