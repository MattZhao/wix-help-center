---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Adding Help Articles to Your App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add your own help articles to your Blocks app. These would open when a user clicks the ? icon in the action bar, offering your users a place to access support and further information.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
