---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Customizing Actions for Apps in the \"My Business\" Tab"
---

Currently, when you publish your Blocks app in App Market, users who install the app get a default "My Business" tab in their Wix Editor or Studio Editor. This tab includes several fixed actions that users can do, such as configure the app in their editor, add or remove some of the app's widgets and upgrade the app.

This feature would allow developers to customize these actions based on the specific needs of their app. It would also improve the app installation experience, allowing users to configure and prioritize actions that are most critical during initial setup and ongoing usage, resulting in a smoother onboarding process and better long-term engagement.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
