---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Importing & Exporting Widgets from One App to Another"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks it is not possible to import or export widgets from one custom app to another. This feature would allow you to build a widget in the Blocks editor and then add the widget to another Wix Blocks app you're building.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
