title: "CMS: Choosing a Format for Displaying Date and Time Collection Fields"
---

When you connect elements to date and time collection fields in the [CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview), you can control how the dates and times are formatted on the live site.

### To choose a format for displaying date and time fields:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Connect an element, such as text, to a [dataset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-elements-to-a-collection-using-a-dataset) that connects to your collection.   Click the relevant field (e.g. **Text connects to**) in the dataset connections and select the date, or time field you want to display. If needed, you can select **Add new field **to create and connect a [new collection field](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-a-field-to-a-collection).

Click the **Choose a format **drop-down and select the format you want to display your dates or times in. View the format options in the table below:

5.  Click **Publish** when you are ready to make the changes go live.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The date and times displayed on your live site adjust according to the visitor's local time zone.
> - Your site's [regional settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-sites-regional-settings) determine date and time language, time zones and other regional formatting options.
> - When you [export a collection to a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-exporting-content-from-your-collection), the time fields in the file are formatted as UTC+0. They are not formatted to local time.
