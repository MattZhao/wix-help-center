title: "CMS: Restoring Collections with Backups"
---

Safeguard your collections effortlessly with CMS (Content Management System) backups. Automatic backups store weekly archived copies of all your collections, while manual backups allow you to instantly save your collections. You can restore backups to undo collection changes made after the backup date.

**In this article, learn about:**

---

### How backups work

Whenever you edit a collection, an automatic backup stores an archived copy of all your collections on a weekly basis. You can also create manual backups whenever you want to instantly save a copy of all your collections. Each backup includes all your collections' content, structure fields, settings, and named views.

---

### Manually backing up your collections

Create manual backups when you want to instantly create archived copies of all your collections. You can create up to 3 manual backups. If you already have 3 manual backups, you can still create another one that will override your oldest manual backup.

#### To create a manual backup:

Go to [CMS](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Content%20Manager&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/database/) in your site's dashboard.  Click** More Actions **at the top.   Click **Backups**.  Click **+ Add Backup**.   (If necessary) Click **Add Backup **again if you already have 3 manual backups. This replaces your oldest manual backup.

---

### Restoring a Backup

You can restore both automatic and manual backups of all your collections. When restoring a backup, you can choose to restore all your collections saved in the backup or only specific collections. All the fields, content, structure, and settings of the selected collections are restored.

#### To restore a backup:

Go to [CMS](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Content%20Manager&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/database/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **More Actions **at the top.   Click **Backups**.  Click **Restore **next to the backup you want to restore.   Click the checkbox next to each collection you want to restore.   Click **Restore [#] Collections**.   Click **Yes, I am sure** to confirm you want to permanently override changes made after the backup creation date.   Click **Restore**.

### FAQs

Click below for answers to our most frequently asked questions about backups.

### How many backups can I save?

You can save up to 3 manual backups at a time. When you have 3 manual backups saved, and you save another, a message lets you know that you are saving over your oldest manual backup.
Your collections get automatic backups saved every week unless you haven't made any changes to your collections. After 3 automatic backups are created, the next one saves over the oldest automatic backup. 

You can also delete both manual and automatic backups if you don't need them.

### What happens when I import a CSV file to my collection?

When you [import a CSV file into a collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-importing-content-into-a-collection), a manual backup is created to capture the state of your collections prior to the import. This allows you to restore the collection to the way it was before the import.

### What if I create a collection, then restore a backup created before the collection?

When restoring a backup, any collections you created after the backup date are saved. This means you do not lose any collections you created after the backup you are restoring.

### How do GDPR and other laws affect my data?

Restoring a backup does not affect the [Personally Identifiable Information (PII)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-personally-identifiable-information-pii) status of a field. If necessary, you can change PII manually in your collections.
Wix deletes and restores collections in compliance with [GDPR regulations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/preparing-your-wix-site-for-the-gdpr#about-data-storage-and-data-transfers282).

### What happens if I restore a saved version from my site history?

[Restoring your site from site history](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-and-managing-your-site-history) reverts its pages and their designs to a previous version. This applies to your [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages) and their layouts. 
However, the content and data structure (schema) in your CMS collections are not reverted when restoring your site. To restore previous versions of your collections, use CMS backups.
