### Accessing and syncing sandbox and live collections

Go to the relevant collection in your editor to access and sync your collections. You can sync items from your sandbox to your live collection or vice versa. Sync all the items at once or select only specific items you want to sync.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **CMS**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Your Collections**.
4. Click the relevant collection. You should see **Sandbox Collection **at the top to indicate you're in the sandbox testing collection.
[image: Screenshot of a sandbox collection.]
1. Choose what you want to do:
Sync your sandbox collection items to your live collection
1. Select the items you want to sync to your live site. If you want to sync all items, skip this step.
2. Click **Sync content **at the top right.
3. Select the relevant option and click **Sync**: 

- **Sync all items from Sandbox to Live: **Sync all the items from your sandbox to your live collection.
- **Sync \{#\} selected items from Sandbox to Live: **Sync only the selected items from your sandbox to your live collection.
4. Click **Sync **to confirm.
Access your live collection and sync it to your sandbox collection
1. Click **Live Collection **at the top of the collection to access your live collection.
2. Select the items you want to sync to your sandbox collection. If you want to sync all items, skip this step.
3. Click **Sync content **at the top right.
4. Select the relevant option and click **Sync**:

- **Sync all items from Live to Sandbox: **Sync all the items from your live collection to your sandbox collection.
- **Sync \{#\} selected items from Live to Sandbox: **Sync only the selected items from your live collection to your sandbox collection.
- **Erase items in Sandbox and replace with Live items: **Replace the items in your sandbox collection with the live collection items.
5. Click **Sync **to confirm.
