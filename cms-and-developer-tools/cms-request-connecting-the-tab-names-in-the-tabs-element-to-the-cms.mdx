---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting the Tab Names in the Tabs Element to the CMS"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect the names of the tabs in the Tabs element to CMS collections. This would allow you to replace the static navigational text at the top of the Tabs element with text from a collection field.

> You can, however, [connect elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-elements-to-a-collection-using-a-dataset) that are attached to each tab, such as text, images, and buttons, to the CMS.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
