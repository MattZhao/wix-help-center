---
title: "CMS Request: Adding a Thumbnail or Cover Image to Videos Connected to a Collection"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a custom thumbnail or cover image to a [video connected to a collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-displaying-collection-content-in-the-single-video-player). This applies to videos connected to gallery or video fields of a collection.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
