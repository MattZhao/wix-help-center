---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Multiple Addresses from One Collection Item to the Google Maps Element"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect more than one address for the same collection item to the Google Maps element. This feature would allow you to bind multiple address fields from your collection to pins in a map on your site. You can connect only one address field to your map to display one location per collection item.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
