title: "CMS: Displaying Blog Posts using Dynamic Pages"

Unlock new blogging possibilities with the [CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview) and [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Make sure your site has the [Wix Blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-setting-up-your-blog) and you've published posts.
> - [Add the CMS to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-getting-started), if you haven't already.
> - Make sure to [enable Wix App Collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-wix-app-collections#viewing-wix-app-collections-in-your-cms) in your CMS.

### Step 1 | Create dynamic pages for your blog posts

Create a dynamic item page that will be the structure for all your individual blog posts. Then create a dynamic list page that serves as an index of your posts. Later you can customize these pages and link between them.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **CMS**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Wix App Collections**.

**Note: **If you don't see **Wix App Collections**, make sure you've [enabled them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-wix-app-collections#viewing-wix-app-collections-in-your-cms).
4. Hover over **Posts **under **Blog **and click the **More Actions **icon .
5. Select **Add dynamic page**.
[image: Screenshot of adding a dynamic page for a Posts Wix app collection.]
1. Select **Item page **and click **Add to Site**.
2. Click **+ Add dynamic pages**.
3. Click the **Choose a collection **drop-down and select your **Posts** Wix app collection.
4. Select **List page **and click **Add to Site**.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **CMS**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Wix App Collections**.

**Note: **If you don't see **Wix App Collections**, make sure you've [enabled them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-wix-app-collections#viewing-wix-app-collections-in-your-cms).
4. Hover over **Posts **under **Blog **and click the **More Actions **icon .
5. Select **Add dynamic page**.
[image: Screenshot of adding a dynamic page for a Posts Wix app collection.]
1. Select **Item page **and click **Add to Site**.
2. Click **+ Add New Page** at the bottom of the **Site Pages **panel.
3. Select **Add **under **Dynamic Page**.
4. Click the **Choose a collection **drop-down and select your **Posts** Wix app collection.
5. Select **List page **and click **Add to Site**.

### Step 2 | Customize and connect your dynamic pages

Most of the page elements on your dynamic pages automatically connect to your blog. You can add elements to these pages and [connect them to your blog posts through the page dataset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-elements-to-a-collection-using-a-dataset).
