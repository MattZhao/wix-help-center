---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Copying & Pasting Panels Across Widgets"
---

Currently, it is not possible to copy and paste a panel across Wix Blocks widgets. This would allow you to copy user interface elements and panel code, allowing for quicker customization of your other widgets.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
