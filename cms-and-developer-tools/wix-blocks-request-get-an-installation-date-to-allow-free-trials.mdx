---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Get an Installation Date to Allow Free Trials"
---

Currently, when you [set up your Blocks app's pricing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-setting-up-and-managing-your-apps-pricing), there is no built-in way to provide users with a free trial. You must rely on your own external database to save the date when a user first installed your app. Your records should also take into consideration that users can install, delete and install again.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
