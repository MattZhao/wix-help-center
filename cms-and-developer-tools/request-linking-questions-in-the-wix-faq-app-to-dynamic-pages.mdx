---
title: "Request: Linking Questions in the Wix FAQ App to Dynamic Pages"
---

The Wix FAQ app lets you add FAQs to your site's page to give your visitors quick answers to common questions. Currently, it is not possible to link [questions ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-questions-in-the-wix-faq-app)in the Wix FAQ app to [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
