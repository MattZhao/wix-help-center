---
title: "CMS Request: Using an Email Field Type in Collections"
---

Currently, CMS (Content Management System) collections do not have a [field type](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-about-your-collection-fields#field-type) used specifically for email addresses.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - **To create dynamic links that open the visitor's mail client: **[Create a URL field type](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-a-field-to-a-collection) in your collection for storing the email addresses. Then, add the email addresses to the field with **mailto: **at the beginning of each address (e.g. **mailto:support@email.com**). Lastly, [link a button or image to a dataset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-a-button-to-a-dataset) that connects to this URL field. When visitors click the link, their email client opens with the relevant address in the 'To:' field.
> - **To store email addresses submitted through custom CMS forms: **You can use the [Text Input element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-adding-and-setting-up-a-text-input) set to collect emails. Then, connect it to a Text or URL field type in your collection where the emails will be stored.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
