---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Specific Roles and Permissions"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, as part of teamwork support, you can [share your app with other collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-managing-collaborators-in-blocks). However, you can only assign them the role of **Co-admin**, which provides them with full permissions to access and edit the app.
