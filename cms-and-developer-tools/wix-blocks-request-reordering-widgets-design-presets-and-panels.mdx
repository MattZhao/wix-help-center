---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Reordering widgets, design presets, and panels"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, it is not possible to reorder widgets, design presets and panels. This applies to both building the app in the Blocks editor as well once the app has been installed on a site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
