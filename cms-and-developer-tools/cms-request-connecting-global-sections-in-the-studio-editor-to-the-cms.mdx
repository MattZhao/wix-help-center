---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Global Sections in the Studio Editor to the CMS"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect global sections, such as the header or footer, in the Studio Editor to the CMS (Content Management System). Page elements that are attached to global sections also cannot be connected to the CMS.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
