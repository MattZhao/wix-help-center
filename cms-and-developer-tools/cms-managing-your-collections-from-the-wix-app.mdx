---
title: "CMS: Managing Your Collections from the Wix App"
---

Access and manage your CMS (Content Management System) collection content on the go with the Wix app.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Install the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app) on your iPhone or Android device and make sure you have the latest version.
> - Make sure to [add the CMS to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-getting-started) before accessing it in the Wix app.

**In this article, learn how to:**

---

### Managing items

You can add new items, as well as edit, duplicate, and delete existing items.

> **Important:**
>
> [Default field values](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-adding-default-values-to-fields-in-your-collections) are not applied to items added via [input elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-user-input-elements) or the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-managing-your-collections-from-the-wix-owner-app). The default fields only apply to items that you or site collaborators add within your CMS collections from desktop computers.

#### To manage items:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Scroll down and tap **Settings**.  Tap **CMS**.  Tap the relevant collection.  Choose what you want to do:

### Add new items

1. Tap **+ Add New Item** at the bottom.
2. Enter the details of the new item and tap **Save**.

### Edit items

1. Tap the relevant item.
2. Edit the relevant fields.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Duplicate items

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant item.
2. Tap **Duplicate**.

### Delete items

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant item.
2. Tap **Delete**.
3. Tap **Delete** again.

> **Note:**
>
> Currently it is not possible to update the schema (layout and structure) of a CMS collection in the Wix app.

---

### Selecting different views

You can see custom views you've created for your collections in the Wix app. The views must be created using the desktop version of the CMS. Learn more about [creating custom views](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-creating-custom-collection-views) in the CMS.

#### To select a view:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Scroll down and tap **Settings**.  Tap **CMS**.  Tap the relevant collection.  Tap the **View **drop-down at the top and select the relevant view.

---

### Searching within collections

Easily find what you're looking for by searching within CMS collections in the Wix app.

#### To search within a collection:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Scroll down and tap **Settings**.  Tap **CMS**.  Tap the relevant collection.  Tap the search bar at the top.  Enter keywords you want to search for.  Tap **Return**.

---

### Viewing filters and sorts in your collection view

View active filters and sorts you've added to your collection view. You must first add the sorts and filters from the desktop version of CMS. Learn more about [filtering and sorting content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-hiding-sorting-and-filtering-fields-in-your-collection-view) in your collection views.

> **Note:**
>
> These filters and sorts affect how you and site collaborators view your collection but do not affect your live site content. Learn how to [add filters and sorts to datasets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-filters-and-sorts-to-datasets-to-control-live-site-content) that control your live site content.

#### To view filters and sorts:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Scroll down and tap **Settings**.  Tap **CMS**.  Tap the relevant collection.  Tap the **Sort & filter items** icon  at the top.  View your active sorts and filters.
