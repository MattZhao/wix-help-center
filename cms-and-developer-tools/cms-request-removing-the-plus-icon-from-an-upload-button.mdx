---
title: "CMS Request: Removing the \"Plus\" Icon From an Upload Button"
---

Currently, there is no way to make an upload button completely transparent. A transparent upload button would give users a way to turn elements, such as images, into clickable items for uploading files. The option to remove the "plus" icon from the existing upload button would make this possible.

As a workaround, you can customize your upload button so that its color matches your page's background.

#### To customize your upload button's color:

Click the upload button in your site's Editor.  Click the Design icon .  Click **Customize Design**.  Click the Fill Color & Opacity icon  and drag **Background** slider to 0%.  Click the Shadow icon  and disable the shadow toggle.  Click the Text icon  and set the color to match the element underneath.  Click **Settings **and select the **Show custom text** button.  Type a space in the **Custom** text field.  Publish your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
