---
title: "CMS Request: Attaching a Repeater onto Another Repeater"
---

Currently, it is not possible to attach a repeater onto another repeater. This feature would allow you to group items within a repeater based on a reference field. For example, you would be able to group dishes within the main repeater by meal (e.g. breakfast, lunch, or dinner).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
