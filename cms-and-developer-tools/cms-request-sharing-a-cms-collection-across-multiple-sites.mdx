---
title: "CMS Request: Sharing a CMS Collection Across Multiple Sites"
---

Currently, it is not possible to share a collection (database) from the CMS (Content Management System) between one site and another.

An alternative is to [copy and paste content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-copying-and-pasting-content) between different collections.

> **Enterprise solution:**
>
> It is possible to share collections across sites with the enterprise solution. [Contact our Enterprise team](https://www.wix.com/enterprise) to learn more.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
