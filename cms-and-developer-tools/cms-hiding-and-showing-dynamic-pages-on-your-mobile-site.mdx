Choose to show or hide your dynamic pages on the menu of your mobile site. ﻿﻿﻿If your site pages include elements that link to your dynamic pages, you can also hide these from your mobile site to prevent visitors navigating to dynamic pages.

> **Important:**
>
> Changes made to the mobile version of your site are not reflected on your desktop site.

### Showing and hiding dynamic pages on your mobile menu

You may want to hide pages on the mobile version of your site, as they contain content and elements relevant to your desktop site only.

As your site develops, you can add dynamic pages to the mobile menu in order to show them.

#### To hide dynamic pages from your mobile site menu:

Click the **Switch to Mobile** icon on the top bar of the Editor. 

  Click the mobile menu** **icon  at the top of your page.  Click **Edit Menu**.  Click **Manage Menu**.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the dynamic page you want to hide.  Click **Hide on Mobile**.

#### To show dynamic pages in your mobile site menu:

Click the **Switch to Desktop** icon  on the top bar of the Editor.   Click the menu** **at the top of your page.  Click **Manage Menu**.  Click **Site Menu**.  Click the **Add a menu item** icon .  Under the Which page? drop-down, select the dynamic page you want to add.  (Optional) If you select a dynamic page with an extra value in Step 6, select an option from the Which item? drop-down.  (Optional) Click the New Window button if you want the page to open in a new window.  Click **Done**.

---

### Hiding elements that lead to dynamic pages

To ensure the dynamic pages you are hiding are not accessible, it's also important to hide any elements that direct visitors to those pages.

#### To hide elements that direct visitors to a dynamic page:

Click the **Switch to Mobile** icon on the top bar of the Editor.   Click the element that redirects visitors to a dynamic page.   Click the **Hide Element **icon .
