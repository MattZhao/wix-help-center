---
title: "CMS Request: Importing Content from a Collection into a Wix Stores Collection"
---

Currently, it is not possible to import content from your collections into a Wix Stores collection. For example, you cannot use pictures that site visitors upload to a collection as product images in a Wix Stores collection.

Learn more about [managing your collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-managing-your-collections) and working with [Wix App collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-wix-app-collections).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
