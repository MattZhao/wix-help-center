---
title: "CMS Request: Supporting Non-Latin Characters in CSV File Imports to Collections"
---

Currently, non-Latin languages are not supported when importing a CSV file into a collection in the CMS (Content Management System). Any non-Latin characters are imported as 'unknown' type.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
