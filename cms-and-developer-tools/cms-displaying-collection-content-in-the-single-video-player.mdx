---
title: "CMS: Displaying Collection Content in the Single Video Player"
---

Display video content from your CMS (Content Management System) collections in the Single Video Player. You can upload the videos directly to a Video field in your collection, or use a URL field type to embed videos hosted on platforms like YouTube, Vimeo, or Facebook.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Learn about the [size limitations for videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes) uploaded to your site.
> - If the field you connect to the video source is blank, the dafault video uploaded to the player plays instead. You can set up [default values](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-adding-default-values-to-fields-in-your-collections) for the field to specify a default video to play instead.

### To connect the Single Video Player to the CMS:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Add a Single Video Player:

1. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Video & Music**.
3. Click **Single Video Players **and drag the **Video Upload **player onto your page.
[image: Screenshot of adding the Single Video Player in the Wix Editor.]
1. Select the Single Video Player you just added and click the **Connect to CMS** icon  .
2. Click the **Choose a dataset** drop-down and select an existing dataset that connects to your collection. Alternatively, click** Add a Dataset**, then choose the collection you want to connect, give the dataset a name and click **Create**.
3. Under **Connections**, click the drop-downs to connect the video player to the relevant collection fields:

- **Video title connects to: **Select the Text, Address, or Tags field you want to use as the video title. Select **Add new field** if you want to add and connect a new collection field for your video title.
- **Video source connects to:** Select the Video or URL field you want to use as the source of the video. Select **Add new field** if you want to add and connect a new collection field for your video source.
Note: If you choose a URL field type as the video source, the actual title of the embedded video may override the field you chose for your video title.[image: A screenshot of the CMS connection options available for the player.]
1. (If needed) Adjust the [dataset settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-and-setting-up-a-dataset#step-3-edit-the-dataset-settings): 

1. Click **Dataset Settings** in the **Connect Video Player **panel on the right.
2. Click the permission type next to **Permissions **to edit your collection's [permissions & privacy settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-changing-your-collection-permissions).
3. Click the **Dataset mode** drop-down and select the relevant option: 

- **Read:** Elements that are connected to the dataset can read and display content from your collection. The elements cannot modify the collection content.
- **Read & Write:** Elements that are connected to the dataset can display content from your collection. Input elements can add or edit content in your collection.
[image: Screenshot of the dataset settings.]
1. Customize your video player's settings, design, and layout. Learn more about [the settings here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-and-setting-up-the-single-video-player) and the [design and layout options here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-customizing-the-design-of-your-single-video-player).

**Tip: **If the video source is a Video field type, you can enable the video title from the layout settings. Click the video player and select the **Layout** icon . Then enable the **Show video title **toggle.
2. Click **Preview** at the top right to test your connections.
3. Click **Publish** if you are ready to make the changes go live.
