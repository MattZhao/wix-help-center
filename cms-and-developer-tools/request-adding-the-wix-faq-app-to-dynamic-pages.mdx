---
title: "Request: Adding the Wix FAQ App to Dynamic Pages"
---

The Wix FAQ app lets you add FAQs to your site's page to give your visitors quick answers to common questions. Currently, it is not possible to add the Wix FAQ app to [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
