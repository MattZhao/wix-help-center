---
title: "CMS Request: Using the Collection Search to Find the Total Matching Values and Navigate to Them"
---

Currently, when entering keywords in the **Search **field of a [CMS collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-managing-your-collections), it is not possible to see how many matching values exist, or to navigate to them in your collection. When you enter keywords in the **Search** field, the matching values get highlighted in orange. However, you need to scroll through your collection to find the matching values, if they exist.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
