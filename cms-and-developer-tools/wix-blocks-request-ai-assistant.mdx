---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: AI Assistant"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, there is no AI Assistant to help with the app building process.

This feature would introduce an AI Assistant within Wix Blocks, providing you with personalized, real-time assistance throughout the app building journey. Guidance on design elements, tips on optimization and answers to questions relating to app development could be provided within the low-code drag-and-drop editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
