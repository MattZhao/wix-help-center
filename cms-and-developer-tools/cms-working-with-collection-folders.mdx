---
title: "CMS: Working with Collection Folders"
---

Use collection folders in the CMS to keep collections in their own groups. This helps to keep your collections organized, especially when working with content across many collections.

For partners working with clients, using collection folders improves the handover process. You have a tool to organize collections in a way that makes it easy for clients to find the required collection. This way, they don't have to navigate a long list of collections.

**In this article, learn about:**

[Creating a collection folder](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-collection-folders#creating-a-collection-folder)  [Managing your collection folders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-collection-folders#managing-your-collection-folder)

---

### Creating a collection folder

When creating a collection folder, you can also select a color for it, and add a description.

#### To create a collection folder:

Go to CMS in your Editor:

- **Wix Editor:** Click **CMS**  on the left side of the Editor.
- **Editor X:** Click **CMS**  at the top of Editor X.
    Click **Collections**.  Click the Settings icon .  Click **View all Collections**.  Click **More Actions**.  Click **Create folder**.  Add a name for your folder in the **Folder Name** field.  Select a color for your folder.  (Optional) Add a description for your folder.  Click **Create**.

---

### Managing your collection folder

You can manage your collection folders from the CMS home screen. When you create a folder, and move a collection into it, your collection no longer appears on the CMS home screen.

In the same way, when you delete a collection folder, all the collections stored in it appear on the CMS home screen.

> **Note:**
>
> All types of collections can be moved into folders, including [Wix app collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-wix-app-collections), and [external collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-integrating-external-databases-with-your-wix-site).

#### To manage your collection folder:

Go to CMS in your Editor:

- **Wix Editor:** Click **CMS**  on the left side of the Editor.
- **Editor X:** Click **CMS**  at the top of Editor X.
    Click **Collections**.  Click the Settings icon .  Click **View all Collections**.  Click the More Actions icon  on the folder you want to manage and choose an option:** Open folder**, **Edit folder**, or **Delete folder**.
