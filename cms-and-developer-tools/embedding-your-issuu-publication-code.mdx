### Embedding the code

If you have an account with Issuu Publications and used the old app, you can still add your content from Issuu to your Wix site. You just need to copy a piece of code and paste it using the Embed Code feature in your Wix Editor.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Log in to Issuu Publications.
2. Click **Publications **in the side panel.
3. Select the publication you want to embed and click **Embed on website**.
4. Customize the relevant settings.
5. Click **Get embed code**.
6. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
7. Click **Add Elements **** **on the left side of the editor.
8. Click **Embed Code**.
9. Click **Embed HTML**.
[image: A screenshot of the the Add Elements panel showing the option to Embed HTML.]
1. Copy and paste the code from Issuu Publication into the **Add your code here (HTTPS only)** field.
2. Click **Update**.
3. (Optional) Enter alt text that describes what you're embedding in the **What's in the embed? Tell Google **field.
4. When you are ready, publish your site.

1. Log in to Issuu Publications.
2. Click **Publications **in the side panel.
3. Select the publication you want to embed and click **Embed on website**.
4. Customize the relevant settings.
5. Click **Get embed code**.
6. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
7. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
8. Click **Embed & Social**.
9. Click **Embed Code**.
10. Click the Add icon   next to **Embed Code**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to embed code in Wix Studio Editor.]
1. Click **Enter Code.**
2. Copy and paste the code from Issuu Publication into the **Add your code here (HTTPS only)** field.
3. Click **Apply**.
4. (Optional) Enter alt text that describes what you're embedding in the **What's in the embed? Tell Google **field.
5. When you are ready, publish your site.

### Adding the PDF Viewer app

You can use the PDF Viewer app to upload and display your digital publications.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Apps**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Search for the PDF Viewer app in the search bar.
4. Click **Add to Site**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to add the PDF viewer app to your site.]
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **App Market **** **on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **App Market**.
4. Search for the PDF Viewer app in the search bar.
5. Click **Add to Site**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to add the PDF viewer app to your site.]
