---
title: "CMS: Changing the Maximum Items Displayed in Your Dataset Settings"
---

Control how many items can load in your dataset's connected elements by adjusting the **Maximum items displayed **field. By default, the 'Maximum items displayed' is set to 12, meaning up to 12 items can load in the dataset's connected elements. You can set this field to show up to 1,000 items at a time.

> **Optimize your page loading time:**
>
> Use a lower number in the 'Maximum items displayed' field to help your pages load faster with fewer items on the initial load.

---

### FAQs

Click below for answers to the most common questions about dataset settings.

### How do I create a 'Load More' button?

'Load More' buttons allow visitors to click to load more items in repeaters or galleries connected to datasets. The total maximum items that can load at a time is determined by the **Maximum items displayed** field in the dataset settings. You can set this field to show up to 1,000 items at a time. 
To create a 'Load More' button, add a button or image, connect it to the dataset and set the element's click action to **Load more**. 
Learn how to add a 'Load More' button to a: 
- [Repeater](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-dynamic-content-in-a-repeater#step-5-optional-create-a-load-more-button) on a dynamic list page.
- [Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-collection-content-in-a-pro-gallery#step-4-optional-create-a-load-more-button) on a dynamic list page.

**Note: **If you [connect a Pro Gallery to your collection's Media Gallery field type](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-the-media-gallery-collection-field-type-to-a-pro-gallery), you can use the gallery's built-in 'Load More' button to restrict the number of images or videos displayed on the page.

### Can I add a navigation bar to repeaters and galleries?

Yes. You can add pagination bars that allow visitors to switch between pages of items in repeaters and galleries. The maximum number of items in each pagination page is determined by the **Maximum items displayed **field in the dataset settings. 

Learn how to [add and connect pagination bars](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-adding-and-setting-up-a-pagination-bar) in your Editor. 
**Note: **This feature is not yet available in Editor X or the Studio Editor.

### Can I add 'Next' and 'Previous' buttons to cycle through items?

Yes, you can create ['Next' and 'Previous' buttons or images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dataset-click-actions-for-buttons-and-images#next-page-previous-page) visitors can click to see other collection items. 
**To create 'Next' and 'Previous' buttons:**
- **On static pages:** Set the dataset's **Click action connects to** field to **Next item** or **Previous item**. When visitors click the buttons, the dataset's connected elements change to reflect the next or previous item retrieved by the dataset.
- **On dynamic list pages: **Set the **Click action connects to **field to **Next page** or **Previous page**. When visitors click the buttons, the next or previous 'page' of items loads in the connected repeater or gallery. The **Maximum items displayed** field determines the maximum items per 'page'.
- **On dynamic item pages: **Set the **Click action connects to **field to **Next dynamic page **or **Previous dynamic page**. When visitors click the buttons, they navigate to the next or previous item's version of the page at the item's dedicated dynamic URL.

Learn more about [the various click actions you can set for buttons and images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dataset-click-actions-for-buttons-and-images).

### What is the dataset mode, and how does it affect visitor interactions?

The [dataset mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-working-with-the-dataset-mode-and-collection-permissions) determines what actions can be done between the connected elements and your collection. There are 3 dataset modes: 
- **Read:** Elements on your page can read and display content from your collection. Site visitors cannot modify the content in your collection.
- **Write:** Elements on your page cannot display content from the collection. Visitors can interact with [user input elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-user-input-elements) and a [submit button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dataset-click-actions-for-buttons-and-images#submit) to add content to your collection. However, visitors cannot edit the content in your collection.
- **Read & Write:** Elements on your page can read and display content from your collection. Visitors can interact with user input elements and a submit button to add or edit content in your collection.

### What do filters and sorts do in the dataset settings?

You can add filters and sort conditions to datasets to only show specific items and control the order in which they appear in connected elements. Learn more about [adding filters and sorts to datasets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-filters-and-sorts-to-datasets-to-control-live-site-content).

### What is a dataset?

Datasets are the vital link between the elements on your page and the content in your CMS collection. You can use datasets to create a two-way connection, allowing you to display collection content, capture user inputs, or both. 
Datasets allow you to control which parts of your page elements connect to which fields in your collection. Each dataset connects to a collection and each element connected to the dataset connects to a field in the collection. This unlocks a new way of managing which content appears and where.
Learn more about [adding and setting up a dataset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-and-setting-up-a-dataset).
