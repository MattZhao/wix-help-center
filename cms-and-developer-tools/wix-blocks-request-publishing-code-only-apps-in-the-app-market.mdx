---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Publishing Code-Only Apps in the App Market"
---

Currently in the App Market, you cannot publish apps that only contain [code files](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-adding-code-files-and-folders-to-your-app) (previously known as Velo Packages).

This feature would allow you to monetize code-only apps, by publishing them in the App Market. Site builders would then be able to use your public functions and custom API's from these apps on their sites.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
