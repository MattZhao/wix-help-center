---
title: "CMS Request: Adding a Drag-and-Drop Upload Feature"
---

Currently, it is not possible for your site visitors to drag and drop files onto your site to upload them to a CMS (Content Management System) collection. However, you can allow site visitors to upload files to a collection by [setting up a clickable upload button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-uploading-multiple-documents-with-the-upload-button).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
