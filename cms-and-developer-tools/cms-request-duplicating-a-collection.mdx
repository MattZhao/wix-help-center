---
title: "CMS Request: Duplicating a Collection"
---

Currently, in the CMS (Content Management System) it is not possible to duplicate a collection to create a copy. This feature would allow you to easily duplicate entire collections, including their fields, items, values, and settings.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can [export a collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-exporting-content-from-your-collection) as a CSV file, then [import the file to a new collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-importing-content-into-a-collection) to create copy of the original collection.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
