---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Allowing One-Time Payment for Apps on Installation"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, it is not possible to allow site creators to make a one-time payment for an app on installing it.

[Learn more about available pricing options for your app in Wix Blocks](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-setting-up-and-managing-your-apps-pricing) (e.g. free app installation, Premium paid recurring, dynamic pricing).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
