---
title: "CMS: New Datasets Experience in the Wix Editor"
---

We've made [datasets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-datasets) easier to locate and use by moving them to a panel on the right of your editor. Check out the improvements we've made below:

---

### View and manage all your page's datasets in one place

You can now easily access and control all your page's datasets from a single centralized panel located on the right side of the Wix Editor. The panel opens automatically whenever you manage an element's connections or add new ones.

---

### Easily connect elements to datasets in the panel

Now, when you select an element and click the **Connect to CMS **icon [image: undefined], a panel opens on the right of your editor. In the panel, you can connect the element to a dataset, manage the existing connections, change the dataset settings, or add a new dataset.

> Learn more about [connecting page elements to datasets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-elements-to-a-collection-using-a-dataset) that bind to your CMS collection content.
