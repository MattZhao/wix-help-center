---
title: "Request: Embedding Velo by Wix into IMVU"
---

Currently, Wix does not have a feature which would embed code into
 [IMVU](http://www.imvu.com/) directly.

> **Tip:**
>
> We do have many users who have managed to do this by taking the general code and customizing it in the IMVU editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
