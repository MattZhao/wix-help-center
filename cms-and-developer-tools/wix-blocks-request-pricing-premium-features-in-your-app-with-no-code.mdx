---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Pricing Premium Features in Your App With No-Code"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, it is not possible to [implement pricing plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-setting-up-and-managing-your-apps-pricing) without code. This means that you must use code to make your entire app, or some of its features, require premium pricing.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
