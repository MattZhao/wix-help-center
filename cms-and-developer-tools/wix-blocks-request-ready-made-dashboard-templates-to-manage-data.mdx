---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Ready Made Dashboard Templates to Manage Data"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, while you can build dashboard pages to manage your app's data and collections and have the freedom to design these pages, there is a lack of ready-made templates specifically tailored for displaying lists of data items and managing individual data items efficiently.

This feature would introduce ready-made dashboard templates to manage app data. These templates will include fully functional elements to display lists of data items with sorting and filtering and a form to manage a single data item.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
