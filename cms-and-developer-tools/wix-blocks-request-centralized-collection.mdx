---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Centralized Collection"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, centralized collections are not supported. This means that when a Blocks app is installed on several sites, each site receives its own instance of the collection and manages its data individually (learn more about [collections in Blocks](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/frameworks/wix-blocks/cms-collections/about-cms-collections-in-blocks)).

This feature would introduce centralized collections in Wix Blocks, enabling app builders to access a unified collection of data across all sites where their app is installed. By utilizing centralized collections, site builders can maintain consistency and efficiency in managing app data, ensuring that updates and changes reflect universally across all instances of the app.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
