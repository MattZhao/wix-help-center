---
title: "CMS Request: Making a Pay Button Dynamic"
---

Currently, there is one option for [adding a pay button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-a-pay-button) to your site to accept payments for an item.

But, there is no option for a dynamic pay button. A dynamic pay button would add the payment value from a field in a collection.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
