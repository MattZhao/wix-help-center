---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Online Programs to the CMS"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect Wix Online Programs to the CMS (Content Management System). This feature would allow you to connect content from the Online Programs app to page elements on your site such as text, images, repeaters, and more.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
