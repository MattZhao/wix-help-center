Streamline your workflow when [managing your CMS collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-managing-your-collections) with keyboard shortcuts.

### Keyboard shortcuts in your collection's table layout:

ActionDescription**Ctrl **+ **A** **⌘ **+** A**Select all cells**Ctrl **+ **C**⌘ + **C**Copy one or more cells**Ctrl **+ **X**⌘ + **X**Cut one or more cells
**Ctrl **+ **V**⌘ + **V** Paste cut/copied content**Shift **+ **Enter**Insert an empty row below**Tab**Go to the next cell**Shift **+ **Tab**Go to the previous cell**Shift **+ **Click **Select a range of cells between two clicks**Right Click ****Ctrl **+ **Click

> **Tip:**
>
