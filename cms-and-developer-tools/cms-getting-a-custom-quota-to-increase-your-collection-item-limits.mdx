---
title: "CMS: Getting a Custom Quota to Increase Your Collection Item Limits"
---

You can get a custom CMS (Content Management System) quota if you need more collection items than your Business Elite or Elite Premium plan allows. This way, you can continue to grow your largest projects, even if you're not quite ready for the [enterprise solution](https://www.wix.com/enterprise).

### What are the collection item limitations with the Business Elite and Elite Premium plans?

Currently, the Business Elite and Elite Premium plans allow your site to have up to 500,000 collection items. If your site already has one of these plans and you need more items, [contact our sales team](https://www.wix.com/enterprise/form?referrer=cms-upgrade-en) for a custom quota. If your site has a plan that allows less CMS items, [upgrade to a higher Premium or Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-existing-premium-plan) to increase your CMS quota. 
Alternatively, you can [upgrade to an Enterprise plan](https://www.wix.com/enterprise) to gain access to larger scale solutions for your business.

### Who is eligible for receiving custom CMS quotas?

If you have a Business Elite or Elite Premium plan, you are eligible to get a custom CMS quota. [Contact our sales team](https://www.wix.com/enterprise/form?referrer=cms-upgrade-en) to increase the total CMS items your site can have. 
If your site has a lower subscription plan and you need a higher CMS quota than the plan allows, [upgrade to a higher subscription plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-existing-premium-plan) to increase your CMS quota.

### What happens to my current quota when I upgrade to a custom quota?

When you get a custom CMS quota, you increase the total number of collection items your site can have. The new quota replaces your current quota rather than adding to it.

### What if I need more CMS collection items and I do not have a Business Elite or Elite Premium plan?

You can upgrade to a plan that includes higher CMS limits if your site is approaching its CMS limits. Learn more about [Wix's available subscription plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan).

### Do free sites have CMS limitations if I'm a Wix Partner?

Yes, free Wix Partner sites now have higher CMS limits. We've increased CMS quotas on free sites to 10,000 collection items. Learn more about [CMS limits on free Wix Partner sites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-cms-limits-on-free-sites).

### Does the 10,000 collection item limit include Wix app collections?

The 10,000 collection item limit specifically applies to collections you create. This means it does not extend to collections created by Wix apps. This limit offers plenty of room to develop your site, without impacting the available space for your apps, products, and CRM contacts and members. If you need to expand your collections further beyond this limit, consider upgrading to a Premium or Studio plan that accommodates your needs.

### What are the CMS quotas for free sites?

Your free site CMS quota is based on whether or not your Wix account is in the Wix Studio platform. 
If your account is in the Wix Studio platform, your free sites have the following CMS quota: 
- 10,000 collection items

If you have **not** joined the Wix Studio platform, or choose to leave Wix Studio, your free sites have the following CMS quota: 
- 1,000 collection items

Learn more about [switching to the Wix Studio platform](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-switching-to-wix-studio).

### What happens to my CMS quota if I leave Wix Studio?

If you choose to leave Wix Studio, your free site CMS quota reverts to 1,000 collection items. This is also the free site CMS quota if you never joined Wix Studio. 
**Keep in mind:** If you are a Wix Partner, and you choose to leave Wix Studio, you also opt-out of the Partner Program entirely. This means you'll lose access to exclusive benefits, features and progress within the program. Learn more about [choosing to leave Wix Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-reverting-after-switching).
