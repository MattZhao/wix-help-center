---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Wix Music to a Dataset"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect Wix Music albums or playlists to a dataset in the CMS. It is only possible to [connect the audio player element to data](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-the-wix-audio-player-to-a-collection).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
