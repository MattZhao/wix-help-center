---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Page Backgrounds in the Studio Editor to the CMS"
---

Currently, in the Studio Editor, it is not possible to connect page backgrounds to the CMS (Content Management System). This feature would allow you to manage the content that appears in your site's page backgrounds in CMS collections.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
