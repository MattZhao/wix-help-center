---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Document Buttons to Files in Collections"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect [Document buttons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-document-button) to content in the CMS (Content Management System). This feature would allow visitors to click Document buttons to download files you store in CMS collections.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can set regular buttons and images to download documents from your collections. Connect the button or image to the CMS, then choose the [Download Document](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dataset-click-actions-for-buttons-and-images#download-actions) dataset click action. When visitors click the button or image, the relevant document is downloaded to their computer.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
