---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Installing an App to the Header or Footer of a Site"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, it is not possible in the app's Installation Settings to choose to install it directly to the header or footer of a site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
