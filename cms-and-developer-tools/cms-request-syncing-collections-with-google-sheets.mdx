---
title: "CMS Request: Syncing Collections with Google Sheets"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically sync CMS collection changes to Google Sheets. This feature would allow you to make changes to your CMS collection and have them automatically apply to a synced Google Sheets spreadsheet. You would also be able to sync in the opposite direction, making changes in a Google Sheets spreadsheet to have them apply in your CMS collection. You could then use formulas and logic in Google Sheets to make calculations based on your CMS data.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - You can [export your CMS collection data as a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-exporting-content-from-your-collection), then import the CSV file to Google Sheets. This allows you to work with your collection content in a Google Sheets spreadsheet.
> - You can export your Google Sheets spreadsheet as a CSV file, then [import the CSV file to your CMS collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-importing-content-into-a-collection). This allows you to replace your CMS collection content with data imported from Google Sheets.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
