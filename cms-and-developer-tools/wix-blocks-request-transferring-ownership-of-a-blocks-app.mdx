---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Transferring Ownership of a Blocks App"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, it is not possible to transfer the ownership of a custom app to another user and/or account. This feature would be helpful for those who want to build apps for clients, etc. in Wix Blocks, and then transfer app ownership over to the client afterwards.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
