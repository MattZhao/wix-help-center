---
title: "CMS: Adding a Preset Collection"
---

A CMS (Content Management System) preset acts as a template for creating dynamic pages that connect to CMS collections.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **CMS**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Add a Preset**.
[image: Screenshot of adding a preset in the Wix Editor.]
1. Hover over the preset that you want to add and click **Add to Site**.

**Tip: **The **Portfolio **preset has a dynamic list page with a [Pro Gallery that connects to the collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-collection-content-in-a-pro-gallery). The dynamic list page with all the other presets includes a [repeater with attached elements that connect to the collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-dynamic-content-in-a-repeater).
[image: Screenshot of selecting a preset in the Wix Editor.]
