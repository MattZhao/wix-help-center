---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting the CMS to Slideshow Repeater Backgrounds in the Studio Editor"
---

Currently, in the Studio Editor, it is not possible to connect the backgrounds of the [slideshow repeater element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-slideshow-repeater) to the CMS (Content Management System). This feature would allow you to use images or videos from your CMS collection as dynamic backgrounds for each slide (item).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
