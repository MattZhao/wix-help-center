---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Adding Wix Chat as an App Dependency"
---

Currently, it's not possible to add Wix Chat as an [installation dependency](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-setting-wix-app-dependencies) on Wix Blocks. This would allow you to integrate direct communication lines between you and your site visitors within your app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
