---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Connecting Elements to Custom Panels Without Code"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, there is no way to connect your app elements and widgets to [custom panel elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-velo-api-reference-for-panel-elements) (e.g. toggles, dividers, buttons) without the use of code.

This feature would allow you to connect your app widgets and elements to custom panels, the same way you have it with default panels. This means that, for example, moving a toggle in the panel will immediately change something in your app.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
