---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Dynamic Page Backgrounds to Collections"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect the page background of [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages) to the CMS (Content Management System). This feature would allow you to manage the content that appears in your dynamic page backgrounds in CMS collections.

> **Workaround for the Wix Editor only:**
>
> You can, however, connect regular static page backgrounds to the CMS:
> 1. Go to the relevant page in the Wix Editor.
> 2. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of your editor.
> 3. Click the More Actions icon  next to the page you are on.
> 4. Click **Connect to CMS**.
> 5. Click the **Choose a dataset** drop-down and select an existing dataset that connects to your collection. Alternatively, click** Add a Dataset**, then choose the collection you want to connect, give the dataset a name and click **Create**.
> 6. Click the **Page background connects to **drop-down and choose the Image or Video file type you want to connect.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
