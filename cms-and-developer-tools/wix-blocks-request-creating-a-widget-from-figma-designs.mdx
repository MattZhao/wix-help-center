---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Creating a Widget from Figma Designs"
---

Currently, it is not possible to export your Figma designs to Wix Blocks to create app widgets. This feature would allow you to seamlessly transfer your design work from Figma into Wix, enabling a more efficient workflow.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
