Restore deleted collections from your [CMS (Content Management System) backups](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-backing-up-your-collections). Each backup is an archived copy of all your collections. In each backup, you can restore all or only certain collections to override changes made after the backup date. In the collections you restore, all the fields, content, structure, and settings get reverted to the saved version in the backup.

### Restoring collections from backups

Restore a backup to choose which collections you want to revert to their previous versions.

#### To restore deleted collections:

Go to [CMS](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Content%20Manager&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/database/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **More Actions **at the top.   Click **Backups**.  Click **Restore **next to the backup you want to restore.   Click the checkbox next to each collection you want to restore.   Click **Restore [#] Collections**.

Click **Yes, I am sure** to confirm you want to permanently override changes made after the backup creation date.   Click **Restore**.

### FAQs

Click a question below for answers to the most frequent questions about restoring collections.

### How do backups work?

Whenever you edit a collection, an automatic backup stores an archived copy of all your collections on a weekly basis. You can also create manual backups whenever you want to instantly save a copy of all your collections. Each backup includes all your collections' content, structure fields, settings, and named views. 
When you restore a backup, you choose whether to restore all your collections or specific ones. Restoring a collection permanently deletes any changes made to the collection after the backup was created. This includes site visitor submissions saved after the backup date.

### What if I create a collection, then restore a backup created before the collection?

When restoring a backup, any collections you created after the backup date are saved. This means you do not lose any collections you created after the backup you are restoring.

### How do GDPR and other laws affect my data?

Restoring a backup does not affect the [Personally Identifiable Information (PII)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-personally-identifiable-information-pii) status of a field. If necessary, you can change PII manually in your collections.
Wix deletes and restores collections in compliance with [GDPR regulations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/preparing-your-wix-site-for-the-gdpr#about-data-storage-and-data-transfers282).
