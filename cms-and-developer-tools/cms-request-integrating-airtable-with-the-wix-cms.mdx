---
title: "CMS Request: Integrating Airtable with the Wix CMS"
---

Currently, it is not possible to integrate [Airtable](https://www.airtable.com/) with the Wix CMS (Content Management System). This feature would allow you to use content from your Airtable account while working on your Wix site.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - With Wix Automations, you can [use Zapier to automate connections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-connecting-your-automations-with-a-third-party-app-using-zapier) with Airtable and other third-party apps. [View Airtable workflow templates in Zapier](https://zapier.com/apps/wix/integrations/airtable)
> - You can use the [Sync: Connect to data sources app](https://www.wix.com/app-market/sync-connect-to-data-sources) to connect site elements with data from Airtable.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
