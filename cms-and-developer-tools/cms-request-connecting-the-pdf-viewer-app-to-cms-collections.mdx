---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting the PDF Viewer App to CMS Collections"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect the [PDF Viewer app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-pdf-viewer-app) to CMS (Content Management System) [collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-creating-a-collection). This feature would allow you to dynamically display PDF files that are uploaded to CMS collections in the PDF Viewer app. You would also be able to create [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages) from PDFs uploaded to the PDF Viewer app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
