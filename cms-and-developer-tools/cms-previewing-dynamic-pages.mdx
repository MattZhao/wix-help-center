---
title: "CMS: Previewing Dynamic Pages"
---

Check each version of your dynamic item page to make sure the items and elements all look and function as they should. This is a great way to ensure all the dynamic elements on your dynamic item page look great on each version. You can use the Preview toolbar to switch between versions while you edit, or go to Preview mode to test how each version works.

### To preview your dynamic item page's unique versions:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu**on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Dynamic Pages**.
4. Click the relevant dynamic page.
[image: A screenshot of selecting dynamic pages in the Wix Editor.]
1. Click the drop-down in the **Preview **toolbar and select the version of the dynamic page you want to preview. 

**Tip: **You can click and drag the **Move** icon  on the toolbar to move it in your editor.
[image: Screenshot of selecting another version of the dynamic item page from the Preview toolbar.]
1. Click **Preview** at the top right of your editor to test the page's functionality.

**Tip: **In Preview mode, you can also select other versions of the dynamic page from the **Preview **toolbar.
2. Click **Back to Editor** to return to editing your site or click **Publish **if you are ready to make your changes go live.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Dynamic Pages**.
4. Click the relevant dynamic page under **[Collection_Name] Pages (Dynamic)**.

1. Click the drop-down in the **Preview **toolbar and select the version of the dynamic page you want to preview. 

**Tip: **You can click and drag the **Move** icon  on the toolbar to move it in your editor.

1. Click the **Preview** icon  at the top right of your editor to test the page's functionality.

**Tip: **In Preview mode, you can also select other versions of the dynamic page from the **Preview **toolbar.
2. Click **Edit Site** to return to editing or click **Publish **if you are ready to make your changes go live.
