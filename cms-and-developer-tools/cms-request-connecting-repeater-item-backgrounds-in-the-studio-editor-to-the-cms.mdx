---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting Repeater Item Backgrounds in the Studio Editor to the CMS"
---

Currently, in the Studio Editor, it is not possible to click a repeater item and then connect the background to the CMS (Content Management System).

### To connect a repeater item's background to the CMS:

[Go to the Studio Editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).   Select a repeater item in the relevant repeater.  Click **Add Media**.

Select an image or click **+ Upload Media** to upload a new image to your media manager. Then click **Add to Page**.  Click the **Connect to CMS **icon .  Click the **Choose a dataset** drop-down and select a dataset that connects to the collection with the images you want to use as backgrounds.   Click the **Image source connects to **drop-down and select the image field you want to connect.

(Optional) Click the remaining drop-down menus under **Connections **to connect the image's alt text, tooltip, and link.

> Learn more about [connecting repeater items to your CMS collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-dynamic-content-in-a-repeater).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
