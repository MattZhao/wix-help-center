---
title: "CMS Request: Pinning Collections in the Editor or Dashboard"
---

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can work in your site's editor in one browser tab, while keeping the collection you're working on in another tab. This allows you to easily switch between tabs to work in your editor and collection at the same time.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
