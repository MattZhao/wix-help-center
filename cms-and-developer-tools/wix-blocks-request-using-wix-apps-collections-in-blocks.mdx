---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Using Wix Apps' Collections in Blocks"
---

Currently, when building Blocks apps that integrate with [Wix apps](https://support.wix.com/en/business-solutions-apps) such as [Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-wix-stores) or Bookings, you can only use their APIs. However, you cannot access their database collections directly, cannot [use datasets to bind your widget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-about-collections#connecting-elements-to-collection-fields-data-binding) to these collections, and cannot test your widgets in Blocks to see how they appear with sample data for the Wix apps.
