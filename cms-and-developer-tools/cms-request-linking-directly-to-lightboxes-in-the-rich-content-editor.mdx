---
title: "CMS Request: Linking Directly to Lightboxes in the Rich Content Editor"
---

Currently, in the [Rich Content Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-rich-content-and-adding-it-to-a-dynamic-page), it is not possible to add links to your site's [lightboxes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-lightbox). This would allow you to create links in Rich Content fields used in blog posts and the CMS (Content Management System) that, when clicked, open lightboxes on your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
