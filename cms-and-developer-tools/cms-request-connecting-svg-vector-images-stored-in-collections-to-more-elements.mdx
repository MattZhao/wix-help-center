---
title: "CMS Request: Connecting SVG Vector Images Stored in Collections to More Elements"
---

Currently, you can connect [SVG vector images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-vector-art) stored in collections to Basic Shape and Vector Art elements in your editor. However, you cannot connect these SVG vector images to regular image elements, backgrounds, icon buttons, and other elements that can bind to image fields.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
