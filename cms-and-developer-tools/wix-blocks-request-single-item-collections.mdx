---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Single Item Collections"
---

Currently in Blocks, it is not possible to add a [single item collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-differences-between-single-item-and-multiple-item-collections) to your app. Single Item collections are used when there is information that you want to pass together with your app, but it does not have a repeatable nature.
