---
title: "CMS Request: Make Custom Private Fields that are Added to the Members Area Visible in the App Collection"
---

Currently, when you add a custom private field to the Members Area, it is not possible to see it in your regular collections.

The custom field gets stored in a Wix App collection under** Members **called **PrivateMembersData**. Learn how to [show or hide Wix App Collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-wix-app-collections#how-to-show-or-hide-wix-app-collections).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
