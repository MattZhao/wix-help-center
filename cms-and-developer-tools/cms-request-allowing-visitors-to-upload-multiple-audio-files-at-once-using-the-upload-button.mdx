---
title: "CMS Request: Allowing Visitors to Upload Multiple Audio Files at Once Using the Upload Button"
---

Currently, the upload button only allows people to upload one audio file at a time. As an alternative, you can add multiple upload buttons to your site and connect them to the relevant fields in your collection.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
