title: "Wix Blocks Request: Organize Apps into Folders in Your Wix Studio Workspace"

Currently, it is not possible to organize apps into folders for easy management within the **Custom Apps** section in the [Wix Studio workspace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-a-tour-of-the-workspace), as it is possible for sites.
