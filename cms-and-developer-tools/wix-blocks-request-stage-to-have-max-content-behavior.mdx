---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Stage to Have Max-Content Behavior"
---

Currently in Wix Blocks, the canvas does not have a responsive max-content behavior. This means that the canvas size does not automatically adjust to fit the content within it, potentially leading to unnecessary spacing or improper alignment.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
