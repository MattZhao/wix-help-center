---
title: "Wix Blocks Request: Changing Design Presets through Code"
---

Currently in [Wix Blocks](https://support.wix.com/en/article/blocks-unboxed-a-quick-tour), it is not possible to dynamically change [design presets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blocks-about-design-presets) through code.

This feature would allow you to change the design presets based on specific conditions you specify in your code.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
