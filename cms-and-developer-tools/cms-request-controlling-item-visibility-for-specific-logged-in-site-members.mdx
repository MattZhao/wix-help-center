---
title: "CMS Request: Controlling Item Visibility for Specific Logged-in Site Members"
---

Currently, it is not possible to control which collection items are visible to specific logged-in site members without the use of [member roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-member-roles).

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - You can [limit access to dynamic CMS content that appears on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-restricting-access-to-dynamic-content-by-member-roles-or-by-paying-subscribers) to members that have specific member roles or are paying subscribers.
> - You can [limit access to specific pages on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-limiting-pages-on-your-site-to-specific-member-roles) to members that have specific member roles.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
