---
title: "Can't See Premium Services Purchased Using SEPA in Your Wix Account"
---

> From April, 2024, Wix no longer accepts SEPA as a payment method for first-time purchases.

When purchasing premium services using SEPA, Wix needs to receive confirmation of the payment before the service becomes active.
