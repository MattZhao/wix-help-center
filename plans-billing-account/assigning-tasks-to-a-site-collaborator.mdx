---
title: "Assigning Tasks to a Site Collaborator"
---

After [inviting site collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site), you can create and assign tasks to them. They will get reminders to manage their tasks.

### To assign a task to a collaborator:

[Go to Tasks & Reminders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Your%20Tasks&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Ftasks/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.  Click** + New Task** at the top right.  Create your task:

1. Write your task in the text box.
2. (Optional) Click **Due Date** to select a due date from the calendar.
3. (Optional) Click **Link to Contact **to link the task to a contact.
    Click **Assign to Your Team**.  Select a collaborator from the drop-down list.
