title: "Privacy: Retrieving Your Wix Account Data"
---

Here at Wix, we respect your privacy and are fully committed to the protection of your personal information. We collect data from our customers so that we can create the best and safest products. You can read more about this in our [Privacy Policy](https://www.wix.com/about/privacy).

#### We collect the following types of data:

---

### Retrieving data from your Wix account

Most of [your data is accessible directly from your Wix account.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-and-reviewing-data-from-your-wix-account-and-sites) This includes information about sites in your account, premium services, and personal information, such as your address and billing information.

---

### Retrieving data that isn't accessible via your account

Alternatively, you can choose to download an archive of your data. This archive includes your information that you're unable to find in your Wix account, including technical data and personal information that is collected by Wix.

Click to [request an archive of your data](https://www.wix.com/account/support/get-archive).
