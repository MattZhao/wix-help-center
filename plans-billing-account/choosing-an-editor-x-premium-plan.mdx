---
title: "Choosing an Editor X Premium Plan"
---

Premium Plans offer many benefits - removing Editor X branding, connecting a personal domain to your site (e.g. www.mystunningwebsite.com), Ad vouchers, premium support, additional storage, video streaming, accepting payments, and much more.

The Editor X Premium Plans are divided into Website Plans and Business & eCommerce Plans. Website Plans have all the features you need to get your Editor X site up and running. Business and eCommerce Plans have additional features which allow you to accept payments through your site, view detailed Analytics, gain Ad vouchers and other advanced Editor X Business applications.

> **Note:**
>
> The available Premium Plans may differ depending on your geographic location. You can find detailed information about which plans are available to you on the [Upgrade page](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=View%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fpremium.wix.com%2Fwix%2Fapi%2FpremiumStart%3FsiteGuid%3D%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D).

For a site that’s better for you and your site visitors, check out our Website Premium Plans. If you run a business with an online presence, check out our Business and eCommerce Plans.
