---
title: "Assigning an Editor X Premium Plan to a Wix Site"
---

If you purchased a Premium Plan for your Editor X site, you can assign that plan to a Wix site at any time.

### To assign your Editor X Premium Plan to a Wix site:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the Show More icon  next to the plan you want to reassign.
