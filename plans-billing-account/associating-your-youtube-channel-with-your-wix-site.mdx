Associating your YouTube channel with your Wix site lets you link to your site directly from the video using cards and annotations.

### To associate your YouTube channel with your site:

[Verify your site (domains) with Google Search Console](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-google-search-console-5033765).Add a card or annotation to your video which links to your site (step 2 in [this article](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2887282?hl=en)).
