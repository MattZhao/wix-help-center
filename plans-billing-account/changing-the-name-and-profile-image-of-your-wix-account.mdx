---
title: "Changing the Name and Profile Image of your Wix Account"
---

You can update your username and profile image in your Account Settings.

Click to change your account name and profile image

        [
                Update My Profile
      ](https://manage.wix.com/account/account-settings)

#### To change your username and image:

[Go to Account Settings](https://manage.wix.com/account/account-settings) in your Wix account.  Update your first name and last name under **Basic Info**.  Click the the Camera icon  to add or change your profile image.
