---
title: "Adding or Updating an SRV Record in Your Wix Account"
---

A Service (SRV) DNS record provides information about where a service in your system is located. You can add or update SRV records from the Domains page in your Wix account.

> **Important:**
>
> If your domain is connected to Wix via pointing, you must add or update SRV records with your domain host (not Wix).

### To add or update an SRV record:

Go to the [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) page.Click the **Show More **icon  next to the relevant domain and select **Manage DNS Records**.

Add or update your domain's SRV record:

### Show me how to add an SRV record

1. Click **+ Add Record **in the **SRV** section.
2. Click the **Service** drop-down and select the service type.
3. Click the **Protocol** drop-down and select a protocol type.
4. Enter the **Weight**, **Port**, **Target**, and **Priority** in the relevant fields.
5. Click the **TTL** drop-down and select a TTL value.
6. Click **Save**.

### Show me how to update an SRV record

1. Hover over the SRV record you'd like to update and click **Edit**.
2. Edit the SRV record.
3. Click **Save**.
