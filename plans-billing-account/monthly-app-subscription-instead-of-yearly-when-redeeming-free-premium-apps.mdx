---
title: "Monthly App Subscription Instead of Yearly When Redeeming Free Premium Apps"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This promotion is not yet available to all users.

The [Free Premium Apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/redeeming-your-free-premium-apps) that come with certain Premium Plans give you a full year free, regardless of what you see on the purchase page.

The Visitor Analytics, 123 Form Builder and Events Calendar apps are renewed on a monthly or yearly basis, depending on which subscription you choose (in both cases the total cost is $0.00 for the year).

The Site Booster app is renewed on a monthly basis, which is why you may see the monthly amount rather than the yearly.
