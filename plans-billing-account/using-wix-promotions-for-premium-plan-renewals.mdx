---
title: "Using Wix Promotions for Premium Plan Renewals"
---

Promotions offered by Wix (e.g. the [Wix promo code](https://www.wix.com/promo-code/coupons)) are valid for the **first** month/year of service (depending on the plan purchased). Promotions cannot be used to renew an existing Premium plan.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is possible to [upgrade an existing Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-existing-premium-plan) using a promotional coupon.

In addition, the discounted price is valid for the first year/month **ONLY**. All subsequent renewals are charged at the original price of the plan (without the discount).
