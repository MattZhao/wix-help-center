---
title: "Changing the Subscription Period of Your Plan"
---

Change the subscription period of your [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) as you need to. You can choose to move to a shorter term contract, or alternatively receive the cost benefits of a longer term plan.

> **Important**
>
> - If you switch to a shorter plan cycle, your billing cycle will switch over on the date when your current cycle ends.
> - If you switch to a longer plan cycle, the upgrade takes effect immediately. We will credit you for any remaining time on your current plan. Learn more about [how credit is calculated when upgrading your plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-existing-premium-plan).

### To change your subscription period:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant plan.   Click **Change Billing Cycle**.

Select your new billing cycle.  Click **Continue**.  Review your billing information and click **Confirm**.
