---
title: "Request: Reassigning Premium Apps to Another Wix Site"
---

Currently, reassigning Premium apps from one Wix site to another within the same Wix account is not supported.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is possible to transfer Premium apps, along with your site, when [transferring the site to another Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-premium-site-to-another-wix-account).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
