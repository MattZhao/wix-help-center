---
title: "Editor X Site Upgraded to a Non-Editor X Plan"
---

During the initial offering of Editor X, Wix allowed Editor X sites to be upgraded to a [Wix Premium Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan). This was an introductory offer. Editor X sites must now be upgraded to Editor X Premium Plans.

For users who purchased a plan during the initial offer, here are some points to be aware of:

This introductory offer will last as long as you have your site on this plan. Any future renewals will continue under the same Wix Premium Plan you have chosen.  If you reassign your Wix Premium Plan to another Editor X site, or would like to upgrade to a another plan, you will need to upgrade that site to an [Editor X Premium Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-an-editor-x-premium-plan).
