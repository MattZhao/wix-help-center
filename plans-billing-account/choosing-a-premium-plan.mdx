### Types of plans

We have different types of plans to suit your needs.

For a site that’s focused on establishing an online presence, our **Light** plan allows you to create a great looking and easy-to-use site, free from Wix branding and with a custom domain.

### FAQs

Click on a question below to learn more about Premium plans.

### Why am I asked to upgrade if I already purchased a plan?

Since different plans offer different features, it's possible that you're trying to use a feature that's not included in your plan. For example, if you purchased the Light plan, you won't be able to accept payments through your site. Go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) to check which plan you purchased.
Another possibility is that you upgraded the wrong site. Site plans are valid for a single site only. However, if you upgraded the wrong site, you can easily [reassign the plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-premium-plan-to-a-different-site-in-your-wix-account) to the correct one.

### Can I access all Wix products without purchasing a plan?

Free Wix sites offer access to most, but not all, Wix products. Some products, such as Velo by Wix, provide a limited degree of access. Learn more about [using Velo with a free Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-velo-with-a-free-wix-site).

### How do I get my free domain?

Selected yearly or multi-year plans come with a [1 Year Free Domain voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-free-domain-voucher). You can choose your own domain name with suffixes such as .com, .net, and .org, among others. You can find your available vouchers [in your Premium Vouchers page](https://manage.wix.com/account/vouchers?referralAdditionalInfo=%7Btheir). 
**Notes:**
- The domain voucher is only included with the first time purchase of a yearly upgrade plan and cannot be used for renewals or upgrades.
- The voucher is redeemable within 60 days of your purchase, and can only be used to purchase a new domain from Wix.

### How can I get my own personalized email address?

Once you have your own domain, you can [purchase a Google Workspace plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-purchasing-a-personalized-business-email) to get a custom email address, such as info@mystunningwebsite.com. The Google Workspace plans include access to Google Calendar, Docs, Sheets and Slides.

### Where can I find my billing information?

To view your billing information and history, sign into your Wix account and go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings).

### What payment methods are accepted for plan subscriptions?

Wix accepts a wide range of payment methods, including major cards such as VISA, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, JCB and Diners. Other payment methods may also be available depending on where you are located.

### How do I know if an enterprise solution is right for my business?

The Wix Studio enterprise solution provides large-scale businesses with the agility they need. The enterprise solution offers powerful and customized solutions for leading businesses. With an exclusive multi-site dashboard, businesses can access account-level tools that boost efficiency with access to centralized assets management, account data, marketing tools, and more. Learn more about [our enterprise solution](https://www.wix.com/enterprise).
