---
title: "Test Sites: Creating a Test Site"
---

Publish a version of your site (a test site) to a percentage of your site visitors. This is a great way to test new features on your site or get feedback on changes you have made before publishing to 100% of visitors.

---

### Step 1 | Create your test site

Go to your site's editor to create a test site based on your most recent edits.

> **Note:**
>
> You can only create a test site after your site has been published at least once.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Site** on the top bar.
3. Click **Deploy Test Site**.

**Note:** Your test site opens in a new window.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the **Wix Studio** icon at the top left of the editor.
3. Hover over **Site**.
4. Click **Create Test Site**.

**Note:** Your test site opens in a new window.

---

### Step 2 | Set the exposure for your test site

Choose what percentage of your site's audience should see the test site.

#### To set the exposure:

Go to the [Release Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/release-manager-client?&title=Your%20Social%20Content&buttonText=Open+Release+Manager) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Show More** icon  next to your test site.  Click **Edit exposure**.  Enter the exposure percentage as a value between 0-100.  Click **Save**.
