---
title: "Site Management Request: Restore Automations When Restoring Deleted Site"
---

Currently, when you restore a previously deleted site, your automations are not automatically restored with it. You will need to [create your automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation) again.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
