---
title: "Viewing Your Plans and Other Subscriptions"
---

Your Premium plans, Studio plans, or other purchases made through Wix, can be viewed in the Premium Subscriptions page of your Wix account.

Click to view your premium subscriptions

        [
        View Subscriptions
      ](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings)

> **Note:**
>
> If you are unable to find the plan you're looking for, you might be looking in the wrong account. Our [account email locator](https://users.wix.com/account-recovery/forgot-email) can help you find your account.
