---
title: "Pausing or Delaying Activation of Premium or Studio Plans"
---

Your [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) site plan (or upgrade) begins the moment that you purchase it. It is not possible to delay the activation of your site plan, or pause the service.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you are not ready for your site to be made public, you can add a [Coming Soon (landing page)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-landing-page-eg-coming-soon-page) page and hide the rest of your site.
