---
title: "Claiming and Applying Vouchers After Upgrading Your Site"
---

As a promotional gift, you may receive complimentary vouchers with the initial purchase of any yearly or multi-year site plan. For example, select plans include a 1 year free domain voucher.

---

### Claiming a voucher

To use a voucher, you'll need to first claim it within 2 months of purchasing your plan. You can then apply it to your site.

#### To claim a voucher:

[Go to Premium Vouchers](http://www.wix.com/wix/api/voucherConsole?referralAdditionalInfo=%7Btheir) in your Wix account.  Click **Claim Voucher** or **Get Your Domain **next to the voucher you want to claim.

---

### Applying a voucher

Once you've claimed your voucher, follow the on-screen instructions to apply it. Make sure to apply your voucher by the **Valid until** date listed on the voucher.

#### To apply a voucher:

[Go to Premium Vouchers](http://www.wix.com/wix/api/voucherConsole?referralAdditionalInfo=%7Btheir) in your Wix account.  Click **Go to (Company) **under **Claimed** next to the voucher you want to apply.  Follow the on-screen instructions to redeem it.

---

### Checking the expiration date of a voucher

You can view the expiration dates of your vouchers to the right of each voucher listed. The **Valid until** date is the date by which you must claim your voucher before it expires. If you have already claimed your voucher but haven't applied it yet, this is the date by which you must apply your voucher before it expires.

#### To check the expiration date of a voucher:

[Go to Premium Vouchers](http://www.wix.com/wix/api/voucherConsole?referralAdditionalInfo=%7Btheir) in your Wix account.  View the expiration date to the right of the relevant voucher.

---

### FAQs

Here are some common questions about claiming and applying vouchers.

### When do I receive vouchers?

You receive vouchers after purchasing a new yearly or multi-year Premium plan or Studio plan. Vouchers are limited to the initial purchase of a plan.

### Can I get vouchers if I am on a monthly billing cycle for my Premium plan?

No. Vouchers are only offered for yearly and multi-year plans, and are not included with plans on a monthly billing cycle. Learn more about [Wix billing cycles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-billing-for-premium-plans) for site plans.

### Why is the expiration date different before and after I click 'claim'?

Before you claim the voucher, the expiration date you see is the 2-month claim window. After you claim the voucher, the expiration date changes. It now shows you how long you have to apply the voucher.

### I got different vouchers from what I was expecting. Why is this?

Voucher availability may depend on your location. Vouchers may not be offered in all countries.

### How long before the domain voucher expires?

You have two months from the date you purchased your plan to claim the free domain voucher. You can check the expiration date of the domain voucher on the [Premium Vouchers](http://www.wix.com/wix/api/voucherConsole?referralAdditionalInfo=%7Btheir) page in your Wix account. Learn more about the [Wix free domain voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-free-domain-voucher).

### I'm a collaborator on someone else's site and I need to purchase a domain. Can I apply a voucher?

Yes. Billing Managers can apply a domain voucher on behalf of the site owner. Learn more about [domain voucher roles and permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-voucher-roles-permissions).
