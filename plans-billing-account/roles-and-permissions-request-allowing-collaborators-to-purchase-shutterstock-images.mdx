---
title: "Roles & Permissions Request: Allowing Collaborators to Purchase Shutterstock Images"
---

Currently, only site owners can purchase Shutterstock images. It is not possible for collaborators to purchase these images.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
