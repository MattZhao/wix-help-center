---
title: "SSO: Adding Team Members to Your Account and Assigning a Role"
---

Single Sign-On (SSO) is an authentication method that lets users in an organization sign in to their Wix account using their organizational profile login details.

When members of your organization use their profile credentials to sign in to Wix using SSO, they're automatically added to your account as a team member. You can choose to either to give them an assigned role during the sign-up process or assign them a role after they've signed up. Roles let you control the level of access that they have on your account.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adding team members by a team invite](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sso-adding-team-members-to-your-account-and-assigning-a-role#adding-team-members-by-a-team-invite)  [Adding team members by allowing them to join by themselves](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sso-adding-team-members-to-your-account-and-assigning-a-role#adding-team-members-by-allowing-them-to-join-by-themselves)

### Adding team members by a team invite

You can create invitations to send to your team members to that they can join your account. Add the email address of the team members that you want to invite, then select a role to assign to them and their level of access to your account. Their role and access level are automatically assigned to them when they sign in. [Learn more about roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/default-team-management-roles-permissions)

You can send invites to up to 50 email addresses at a time. After you send the invite, your team members receive an email with an invite link to your account's SSO login page.

#### To add a team member:

Go to your Wix account.  Click **Partner Dashboard **at the top.  Click **Settings**.  Select **Team Management**.   Enter the email addresses of the team members you want to invite.

Select a role for your team member under **Roles & Site Access**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also click **Create New Role** if you need to create [a custom role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-partners-creating-custom-roles-for-your-team-management-account).

Select the level of access that the team members have on your account:

- **All sites and folders: **They can access every site and folder on your account.
- **Specific sites or folders: **You can choose which sites and folders they have access to.
    (Optional) Click **Add another role on different sites** if you want to give them a different role and level of access on another one of your sites.   Click **Send Invite**.

### Adding team members by allowing them to join by themselves

You can let your team members join your account by themselves without assigning them a role. The team member can sign in using either the Wix log in page or your SSO Login Page URL. After they sign in, they are automatically added to your account.

You can then assign them a role in the Team Management section of your Partner Dashboard.

> **Note:**
>
> It's not currently possible to set a default role for all new team members who join by themselves. [Vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sso-feature-request-setting-a-default-role-for-new-team-members)

#### To add a team member:

Ask your organization member to go to the SSO sign-in page using one of these methods:

- Ask them go to [the Wix log in page](https://users.wix.com/signin) and click **Continue with SSO**.
- Send them your organization's [SSO Login Page URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sso-setting-up-single-sign-on-sso-login-for-your-organization#step-3-note-the-sso-login-page-info) from your SSO Settings.
    Tell them to follow the steps to sign in.

After they have signed in, go to your Wix account.  Click **Partner Dashboard **at the top.  Click **Settings**.  Select **Team Management**.   Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant teammate.  Select **Change Role**.

Select the role(s) that you'd like to assign to your teammate.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Click **View Role Permissions **next to any role to view all of its permissions.
> - Click **Create New Role **to [create a role for your teammate(s) with custom permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-partners-creating-custom-roles-for-your-team-management-account).

Click **Save**.

> **Want to remove a teammate?**
>
> Click the** **More Actions icon  next to the relevant teammate and select **Remove** **from Team**.
