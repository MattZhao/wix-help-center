---
title: "Roles & Permissions Request: Allowing Collaborators to Purchase Shipping Labels"
---

Currently, only site owners can purchase shipping labels. It is not possible for collaborators to purchase shipping labels.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
