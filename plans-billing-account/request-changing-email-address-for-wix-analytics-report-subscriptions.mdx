---
title: "Request: Changing Email Address for Wix Analytics Report Subscriptions"
---

Currently when you subscribe to a Wix Analytics report, it can only be sent to the email address you use to sign in to your Wix account. It is not possible to change the email address you want this report to be sent to.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
