Use Boleto to pay for Wix services in Brazil. Boleto transactions can be made without the need for an up-front credit card payment.

> **Important:**
>
> This payment method is currently only available for Premium plans and Studio plans with yearly and multi-year subscriptions.

### Paying for Wix services using Boleto

You can choose Boleto as a payment method on the checkout screen when completing your purchase.

#### To pay using Boleto:

[Go to the Upgrade page](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).

**Tip: **If you have more than one site, click **Upgrade Site** next to the relevant website.  Click **Select **under the plan you wish to purchase.  Select a billing cycle and click **Continue to Checkout**.  Select **Boleto Bancário** as your payment method.

Enter your details.  Click on **Generate Boleto**.  Choose a payment option: 

- Pay online (recommended): Select your bank from the drop-down and click** Log in to Bank**.
- Pay offline: Click **Print** to pay your Boleto offline.

> **Tip:**
>
> The Boleto is issued in the name of DLocal Brasil Ltd or Mercado Pago at the service of DL7505830. DLocal is the payment processor for Wix in Brazil.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about paying for upgrades with Boleto.

### Can I use Boleto to purchase a domain?

Yes, you can use Boleto to purchase domains with the following domain extensions: .com, .com.br, .net, .org, .info, .coach, .biz, .fit, .fitness, .live, .rocks, .pictures, .club, .space, .shop, .store, .studio, .training, .online, .co.uk, .xyz, .yoga, .art

### How long do I have to pay the Boleto?

The Boleto must be paid within 5 days of being issued. After this period, a new Boleto is needed.

### How long before my plan becomes active after paying the Boleto?

Wix services which are purchased with Boleto will become active within 1 business day of paying a Boleto.

### What if my domain becomes unavailable after paying?

If your domain name is no longer available after payment confirmation, you will be fully refunded.

### Can I delete my site while my payment is pending?

No, you cannot delete a site if you have a pending Boleto payment.
