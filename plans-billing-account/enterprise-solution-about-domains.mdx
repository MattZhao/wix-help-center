From the enterprise dashboard, you can manage all the domains for your sites. Transfer or connect an existing domain or, if you need a new domain URL, you can get one straight from your dashboard.

> Learn more about [Wix Studio for enterprise](https://www.wix.com/enterprise) or [contact our sales team](https://www.wix.com/enterprise/form?referrer=kb-domains-en).

---

### Transfer a domain

Transfer your domain so that Wix becomes your domain host. Once the transfer is complete, your domain's contact information, DNS settings, and registration payments are all managed from within your Wix Studio for enterprise account.

Domain transfers can take up to 7 days to complete. If you want your domain to link to your site in a shorter timeframe, you can connect your domain to Wix first.

---

### Connect a domain

Connect a domain you already own to Wix by changing its name servers. With this connection method, Wix hosts your DNS, while your domain remains registered with its current host.

To connect your domain, you need to update the DNS records in your domain host account (usually the company you bought your domain from). Wix Studio for enterprise provides a step-by-step outline to help you.

Once the domain has been connected, it can take up to 48 hours for your domain's new DNS records to propagate and your site to appear worldwide.

---

### Get a new domain

Get a domain from Wix to give your site a personalized, unique web address. This makes it easier for visitors to find your site by providing a distinct online space for your company and brand.

The Wix Studio enterprise solution provides a complimentary domain voucher every time you upgrade a site. Use this voucher to get your desired domain.

---

### Assign a domain

Once you've transferred, connected or purchased a new domain, you can assign it to any site in your Wix Studio or enterprise account. You can assign a domain to a site that has no assigned domains. Alternatively, you can reassign a domain as the new primary domain for a given site.
