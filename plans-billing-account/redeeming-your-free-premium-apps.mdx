[Learn More](https://www.wix.com/app-market/visitor-analytics/overview)

### Promotion Terms and Conditions

This promotion is not available to all users.  You must redeem this promotion within **60** **days** from the purchase date of the Premium Plan.   This promotion lets you upgrade the participating apps for free for a period of 1 year. The app purchase page displays may display the monthly pricing, but you will receive a full year free.  The promotion is valid for the above mentioned apps only.  The promotion is provided per account. You may use it on any site in your account (it does not have to be the site you just upgraded).

### Adding and Upgrading Your Premium Apps

You will receive an email containing the promotion details in the email address associated with your Wix account. Once your receive this email, follow the instructions below to add and upgrade the apps of your choice.

> **Notes:**
>
> - This email is in addition to your upgrade confirmation email and is sent approximately an hour after you upgrade to an eligible Premium Plan.
> - Alternatively, you can go directly to the **Manage Apps** tab in your dashboard and claim the app there. Follow the instructions below to claim the app of your choice.

> **Already added the apps to your site?**
>
> No problem! You can still use this promotion to upgrade the apps, even if you're already using them (for free) on your site. Simply select the relevant app in your Editor or Manage Apps in your dashboard and follow the steps to upgrade it. You'll see the discount on the purchase page.

#### Adding and Upgrading the Site Booster App

Go to the [Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) tab in your site's dashboard.   Click the **Claim App** button next to the Site Booster app.   Click **Claim App**.   Click **Allow and Add**.   Click **Upgrade** this app in the top right.  Click **Select**.   Review the details and click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Note:**
>
> The value of the up purchase should be 0.

#### Adding and Upgrading the Visitor Analytics App

Go to the [Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) tab in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Claim App** button next to the Visitor Analytics app.  Click **Claim App**.  Click **Allow and Add**.   Choose to do one of the following:

- **Go to the Editor**: Finish the setup and view the app in your Editor
- **Start the Setup**: Start the setup directly from your dashboard.
    Go back to the [Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) tab in your site's dashboard.   Click the **Claim Upgrade** button next to the Visitor Analytics app.   Choose your plan and click **Select**.   Review the details and click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Note:**
>
> The value of the purchase should be 0.
