Different roles give collaborators access to different parts of your site and dashboard. You can update a collaborator's role as their responsibilities change over time.

### Checking a collaborator's role

If you're not sure what role your collaborator has, or what they can access, you can check it on the Roles & Permissions page.

#### To check a collaborator's role:

Go to [Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.  Browse or search for the relevant collaborator.  Check the role in the **Role** column and hover over it to learn more.

---

### Changing a collaborator's role

You have full control over your collaborators' roles. You can change them at any time according to your needs or add an additional role.

#### To change a collaborator's role:

Go to [Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions ** icon next to the relevant collaborator.  Click **Change Role**.

Select or deselect the relevant roles.  Click **Save**.

---

### Removing a collaborator from your site

Remove a collaborator's access, so they can no longer log in or make any changes to your site and dashboard.

#### To remove a collaborator's access:

Go to [Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions**  icon next to the relevant collaborator.  Click **Remove**.

Click **Remove** to confirm.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about changing collaborator roles.

### Can collaborators remove other collaborators?

Collaborators with management-level roles can remove other collaborators, but only if they have the same permissions as the role they are removing.

### How do I remove myself as a collaborator on a site?

If you no longer wish to contribute to a site, you can [remove yourself as a collaborator](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-information-for-site-collaborators#removing-yourself-as-a-collaborator).

### How can I learn more about the different collaborator roles?

Learn more about the [predefined collaborator roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview) or how to [create your own custom role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators).
