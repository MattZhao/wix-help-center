---
title: "Managing Your Wix Mailing List Subscription Settings"
---

From time to time, Wix sends out newsletter emails with new exciting features and tips for existing features. You can choose whether you want to receive these emails or not.

### To manage your Wix mailing list subscriptions:

[Go to Account Settings](https://manage.wix.com/account/account-settings) in your Wix account.  Scroll down to **Email Preferences**.  Click **Manage Preferences**.  Click the toggle next to each email type to choose those you want to receive.
