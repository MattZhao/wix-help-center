---
title: "Site Management Request: Restore Contacts When Restoring Deleted Site"
---

Currently, when you restore a previously deleted site, your contacts are not automatically restored with it. If you have a CSV file with your contacts, [you can import](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-importing-your-contacts-into-your-contact-list) it again.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
