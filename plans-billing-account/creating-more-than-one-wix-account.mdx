title: "Creating More than One Wix Account"
---

You are welcome to create more than one account with Wix, it's completely free. Each account must have a different email address and username.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Creating another Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-more-than-one-wix-account#creating-another-wix-account)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-more-than-one-wix-account#faqs)

---

### Creating another Wix account

Before you begin, make sure you are [signed out](https://support.wix.com/en/article/signing-out-of-your-wix-account) of your first Wix account.

#### To create another Wix account:

Click to go to the [Wix.com sign up page](https://users.wix.com/signin?overrideLocale=en&loginDialogContext=signup).  Enter an email address for the new account.

**Note:** It must be different to the email of your first account.  Enter the email address again.  Enter a password for the new account.  Enter the password again.  Click **Sign Up**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Alternatively, click the **Facebook** or **Google +** button to sign up using one of these platforms.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about Wix accounts.

### How do I switch between multiple Wix accounts?

To switch between accounts, sign out of your current account, then sign in with the email and password of the other account.

### Can I build multiple websites under one Wix account?

Yes, you can create multiple websites under a single Wix account. Each website can be managed separately within the same account. Learn more about [managing multiple sites in one account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-multiple-sites-under-one-account).

### Can I transfer a website from one Wix account to another?

Yes, you can transfer a website from one Wix account to another. Learn more about [transferring sites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-free-site-to-another-wix-account).

### Can I change the email address associated with my Wix account?

Yes, you can change the email address associated with your Wix account. Go to your account settings and update your email address. Learn more about [changing your account email address](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-email-address-of-your-wix-account).
