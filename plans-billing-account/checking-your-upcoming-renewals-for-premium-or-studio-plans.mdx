---
title: "Checking Your Upcoming Renewals for Premium or Studio Plans"
---

To make sure your site remains live at all times, [Premium plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) and [Studio plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) renew automatically at the end of your subscription period. You can check the date and amount of your upcoming renewals at any time.

### To check your upcoming renewals:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  View renewal information in the **Next payment due** column.  Click **View payment details** to see the specifics of your next payment, including the amount due.

> **Renewal date vs. expiration date:**
>
> The **Next payment due** column shows the renewal date. This date may differ slightly from the expiration date. Hover over the **Info** icon  to learn more. If the auto renew has been turned off, the expiration date of your plan will display.
