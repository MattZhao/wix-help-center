### Click to view step-by-step instructions

**To change your password:**
1. [Go to Account Settings](https://manage.wix.com/account/account-settings) in your Wix account.
2. Under **Edit Password:**

1. Enter your current password.
2. Enter your new password.
3. Enter your new password again.
3. Click **Save**.

> **Notes:**
>
> When [creating a secure password](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-secure-password-and-keeping-your-wix-account-secure) for your Wix account:
> - Your password must be at least 6 characters (recommended at least 12 characters) and may contain:
>
> - English upper and/or lower case characters
> - Numbers
> - Any of the following special characters:**  _ - ! @ # $ % ^ & ***
> - Your password cannot contain a period (**.**).
> - We recommend you add a [Recovery Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/updating-the-mobile-phone-number-in-your-wix-account) to help you recover your account in the event you have forgotten, or lost access to, your email.
