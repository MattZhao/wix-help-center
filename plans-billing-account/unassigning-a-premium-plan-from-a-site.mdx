---
title: "Unassigning a Premium Plan from a Site"
---

You can unassign a Premium plan from your site at any time. Unassigned plans can be [assigned to another site in your account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-premium-plan-to-a-different-site-in-your-wix-account) at any time.

You can view your plan in the **Unassigned Subscriptions** section of your Wix account for easy access after you unassign it.

> If you reassign a Premium plan, the premium features (e.g. connecting a domain, removal of Wix ads) are removed from the original site.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Unassigning a Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unassigning-a-premium-plan-from-a-site#unassigning-a-premium-plan)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unassigning-a-premium-plan-from-a-site#faqs)

---

### Unassigning a Premium plan

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant plan.   Click **Unassign Plan**.

Click **Unassign Plan **in the pop-up to confirm.

> **Already unassigned your Premium plan?**
>
> Learn how to [assign your plan to another site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-an-unassigned-premium-plan-to-your-site).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about unassigning a plan from your site.

### What happens to my domain after I unassign a plan?

When you unassign a plan from your site, your primary domain and any redirecting domains will be disconnected. Learn how to [assign your domain to a site in your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-domain-to-a-site-in-your-wix-account).

### What happens to my site data if I unasign a plan?

Statistics and data related to [email campaigns](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-using-wix-email-marketing-for-free) do not transfer with the email campaigns themselves. In addition, data related to site-specific features (e.g. apps, SEO settings and tracking tools, analytics) does not transfer.

### Is unassigning a plan the same as canceling it?

No, unassigning a plan does not cancel it. Your subscription will remain active until you cancel the plan. Learn more about [canceling a site plan.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-a-wix-premium-plan).

### Why can't I unassign my Business Premium plan?

Some older plans cannot be unassigned (e.g. legacy Business Premium plans). If you have Business Premium plan, you can [assign it to another site in your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-premium-plan-to-a-different-site-in-your-wix-account) instead.

### Can I assign a Premium plan to a Wix Studio site?

Assigning a Premium plan to a site created with Wix Studio requires upgrading to a [Wix Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites). Learn more about [assigning a Premium plan to a Studio site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-assigning-a-wix-premium-plan-to-a-wix-studio-site).
