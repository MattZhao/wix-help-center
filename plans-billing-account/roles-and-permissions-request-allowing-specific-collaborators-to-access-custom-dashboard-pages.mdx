---
title: "Roles & Permissions Request: Allowing Specific Collaborators to Access Custom Dashboard Pages"
---

With Velo, you can [create custom dashboard pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-working-with-dashboard-pages) for you and your collaborators. Currently, it is not possible to choose which collaborators can and can't access custom dashboard pages.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
