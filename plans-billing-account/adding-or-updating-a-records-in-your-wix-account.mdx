---
title: "Adding or Updating A Records in Your Wix Account"
---

An A (Host) DNS record is used to point your domain name and host names to a static IP address. You can add or update your A records from the Domains page of your Wix account.

> **Important:**
>
> If your domain is connected to Wix via pointing, you must add or update A records with your domain host (not Wix).

### To add or update an A record:

Go to the [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) page.Click the **Show More **icon  next to the relevant domain and select **Manage DNS Records**.

Add or update your domain's A record:

### Show me how to add an A record

1. Click **+ Add Record **in the **A (Host) **section.
2. Click **Got it** in the pop-up message.
3. Click the **Host Name** field and enter the host name.
**Note:** Leave this field blank if instructed to add an **@** sign.
4. Click the **Value** field and enter an IP address the record points to.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Save Changes** in the pop-up message.

### Show me how to update an A record

1. Hover over the A record and click **Edit**.
2. Click **Got it** in the pop-up message.
3. Click the **Host Name** field and enter a host name.

**Note:** Leave this field blank if instructed to add an **@** sign.
4. Click the **Value** field and enter an IP address the record points to.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Save Changes** in the pop-up message.
