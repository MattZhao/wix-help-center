### Uploading your media files

Upload images and videos directly to your Wix Studio for enterprise account to use across all of your sites. Choose a category for your media files, or add a new one, to keep your media assets organized and labeled. You can then filter your media assets by the categories you've created.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [supported file types and sizes for videos and images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes).

#### To upload your media:

[Go to Media](https://manage.wix.com/enterprise-dashboard/media) in your enterprise dashboard.  Click **+ Upload Files** at the top right.  Click **Upload Files** to select the files you want to upload.  (Optional) Choose a category for your files, or click **+ Add New Category** to add a new one.  Click **Upload**.

### Managing categories for media files

Effortlessly manage your media file categories to maintain a well-organized digital library. By assigning files to specific categories or creating new ones, you can ensure easy navigation and quick access to your media assets. Enjoy full control by editing, deleting, or rearranging categories with easy hover and drag-and-drop actions. You can always add or remove categories to your media files.

#### To manage categories:

[Go to Media](https://manage.wix.com/enterprise-dashboard/media) in your enterprise dashboard.  Click **Manage Categories**.  Click **+ Add New Category** to add a new category for media files.  Enter a category name and click the checkmark icon to save it.

> **Tip:**
>
> Hover over a category to edit or delete it. You can also drag and drop categories to rearrange their order.

### Archiving media files

When you're no longer using specific media files, but you still want to retain them for future use, archiving them is a great way to keep your library organized and reduce clutter.

> **Note:**
>
> You cannot delete media from the enterprise Media Manager, you can only delete media at a site level. Learn more about [deleting media files from your media storage](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-deleting-media-files-from-your-media-manager).

#### To archive files:

[Go to Media](https://manage.wix.com/enterprise-dashboard/media) in your enterprise dashboard.  Select the files you want to archive.  Click **Archive**.

> **Tip:**
>
> To view your archived files, click the **Archived** tab. To unarchive files and have them available for use again, select the archived files and click **Unarchive**.
