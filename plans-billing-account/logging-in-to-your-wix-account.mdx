### Logging in to your Wix account

To log in to your Wix account, you'll need the email address and password you used to create the account (or your current email address and password if you updated them). Alternatively, you can log in using SSO or your Facebook, Google, or Apple account.

You need to use the same login method that you used when you initially signed up.

#### To log in to your Wix account:

Go to [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/).  Click **Log In** at the top right.  Choose your login method:

### Email and password

1. Enter the email address associated with your account.
2. Click **Continue with Email**.
3. Enter your password in the **Password** field.
4. Click **Log In**.

**Tip: **Forgot which email address you used? Use our [Find your login email tool](https://users.wix.com/account-recovery/enter-website) to locate your account.

### Social login (Google/Facebook/Apple)

1. Click the relevant social account.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions.

**Tip: **You can choose which social accounts you want to log in with from your Account Settings. [Learn how to manage social logins](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-social-logins)

### Single Sign-On (SSO)

Single Sign-On (SSO) is an authentication method that lets users in an organization log in to different websites using the same set of login details.
Logging in with SSO is only available to certain users. If you think that you should be able to sign in using SSO, contact your organization's IT administrator.

### Troubleshooting login issues

Click the issue you're experiencing below to learn how to resolve it.

### I forgot my password

If you can't log in to your Wix account because you've forgotten your password, you can get a reset link sent to your email address. From there, you can create a new password to sign in to your Wix account.

1. Enter your email address and click **Continue with Email**.

**Note:** If you're not sure which email address you used, try our [email locator](https://users.wix.com/account-recovery/enter-website).
2. Click **Forgot Password?**.

[Learn more about getting a password reset link](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unable-to-sign-in-to-your-wix-account-forgot-password)

### I forgot my email address

If you can't remember the email address that you used to create your Wix account, you can recover your account in 2 ways:
- **Use our email locator tool: **If you're not sure of your Wix account's email address, you can use [our email locator tool](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unable-to-sign-in-to-your-wix-account-forgot-email#recovering-your-login-email-using-the-wix-email-locator) to find the correct login email.
- **Use your recovery phone number: **If you have a recovery phone number added, you can [recover your account using your phone](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unable-to-sign-in-to-your-wix-account-forgot-email#recovering-your-account-using-your-recovery-phone-number).

### I lost access to my email address

If you've lost access to the email address that you used to create your Wix account, you can recover your account in 2 ways:
- **Get an SMS code: **If you added a recovery phone number in your Wix account, you can [get a one-time SMS code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/recovering-your-wix-account#recovering-your-account-using-your-recovery-phone-number) sent to your mobile phone to access your account.
- **Submit an ownership verification form:** If you haven't added a recovery phone number, you can [fill out our ownership verification form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/recovering-your-wix-account#recovering-your-account-without-a-recovery-phone-number) to confirm that you are the genuine account owner.

### I can't log in with my Google account

The Google sign in option opens a new window where you can sign in to your Google account. Make sure that your browser isn't blocking cookies or popups which may prevent this window from opening (for example, if you're using an adblocker or popup blocker). 
If you're already signed into your Google account, make sure it's the same account you used to sign up to Wix. You can sign out of your Google account at [google.com](https://www.google.com/) before signing in to your Wix account again. 
If you have third-party cookies disabled in your browser, you may have difficulties trying to log in with your Google account. Follow the steps below for your browser to enable third-party cookies.
- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/61416?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- [Safari for Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/view-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-a7d95376-f2cd-8e4a-25dc-1de753474879)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/websites-say-cookies-are-blocked-unblock-them)

**Note: **If you don't want to enable third-party cookies, you can log in with your email address and password instead.

### I can't find my site after I log in

If you can't find the site you're looking for on the Sites page, check that you are [logged into the correct account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooter-unable-to-find-a-site-in-your-account). Use the Account Email Locator to check which email address that you used when creating your site.

1. Go to the [Account Email Locator](https://users.wix.com/account-recovery/enter-website).
2. Enter your site's URL.
3. Click **Next**.

You'll find the email address associated with your account under **Your Wix account email is**. If you know your password, click **Log In**, if not, click **Reset Password**.
[Learn more about logging in to the correct account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooter-unable-to-find-a-site-in-your-account)

### I can't access my phone (2-step verification)

If you have enabled 2-step verification by email, you can click **Send code by email** to receive a code to your email address instead. Learn more about [verifying your account by email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-2-step-verification-for-your-wix-account#signing-in-to-your-wix-account-with-2-step-verification). 
If you haven't enabled 2-step verification by email, please fill out the [ownership verification form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unable-to-sign-into-your-wix-account-cant-access-phone) and our team will be able to help.

### I didn't receive an SMS code

If you don't receive an SMS, you can click **Call me **to get a phone call from Wix and listen to the verification code instead.

### I'm asked to enter a number from a picture

Wix uses [Google's reCAPTCHA tool](http://www.google.com/recaptcha/intro/index.html) to help protect against potential spam and other types of automated abuse.
reCAPTCHA asks users to enter numbers or words seen in distorted images. reCAPTCHA is simply a test to tell humans and robots apart.
You must type exactly what you see in the reCAPTCHA image, whether it's a number, a word, or several words. When you are done, press the enter key on your keyboard. 

**Tip: **You can click the audio button to listen to an audio reCAPTCHA option. The set of numbers is read out and you must enter the numbers you hear in the provided space. When you are done, press the enter key on your keyboard. 
[Learn more about reCAPTCHA and the Wix login process](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-recaptcha-in-the-wix-sign-in-process)

### The login page isn't loading

Try opening [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/) in an incognito window or private browsing mode. Learn how to use  [private browsing mode for your web browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/opening-your-browser-in-incognitoprivate-browsing-mode). 
You can also try [clearing your browser's cache](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-your-browsers-cache) if you're experiencing issues with the login page.

### My site is managed by someone else and I can't log in

If you don't have access to your login email, or you need help recovering access to your account, we recommend contacting the site owner. They'll be able to send you a new invite to access the site.

### My bookmark link to the login page doesn't work

If a bookmark link to the Wix login page that you previously used no longer works, the link may have expired. Follow the steps below to fix this issue.

1. Delete the bookmark that's causing the issue.
2. Go to [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/).
3. Click **Log In** at the top right.
4. Log in to your account.
5. Create a new bookmark.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I change the email address associated with my Wix account?

Yes, you can [change the email address](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-email-address-of-your-wix-account) associated with your Wix account from your Account Settings. Make sure to use the new email address when logging in.

### How do I enable or disable two-step verification?

You can enable or disable [two-step verification](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-2-step-verification-for-your-wix-account) from your Account Settings. This adds an extra layer of security to your account.

### How do I log out of my Wix account?

To log out, click on your profile picture at the top right corner of your site's dashboard and select "Log Out."

### What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access to my account?

If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately and enable two-step verification. Contact Wix Customer Care for further assistance.
