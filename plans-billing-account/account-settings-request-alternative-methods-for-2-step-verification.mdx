---
title: "Account Settings Request: Alternative Methods for 2-Step Verification"
---

You can [verify your account using 2-step verification](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-2-step-verification-for-your-wix-account) with the Wix app, by SMS or phone call, an authenticator app, or by email. However, it is not currently possible to use 2-step verification via backup codes.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
