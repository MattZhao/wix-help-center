---
title: "Changing a Premium App Subscription"
---

If you have a premium app subscription, you can choose to either upgrade or cancel your plan. You cannot downgrade to a lower-tier plan.

If you choose to cancel, your premium app subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle, and auto-renewal will be turned off.

### Changing an app subscription

Choose the right subscription for your business needs for each app installed on your site.

#### To change an app subscription:

[Go to Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant app.   Click **Change pricing plan**.

Select the plan you want to change to.  Click **Change Plan**.

### How does Wix calculate the extension of my subscription?

To determine how many days to add to your new subscription, the following calculation is automatically performed: Extension = (Balance / New Daily Plan Price) - Days Left
**What do these terms mean?**
- **Balance: **Days Left x Old Daily Plan Price.
- **New Daily Plan Price: **The price of the new plan divided by the total number of days (i.e. 365).
- **Days Left: **The number of days left of your original plan.
- **Old Daily Plan Price: **The price of the original plan divided by the total number of days (i.e. 365).

### Is the change in price immediate or do I have to wait until my subscription renews?

Yes, the change in the price is immediate.
