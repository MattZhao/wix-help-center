The Wix Studio enterprise solution is designed specifically for large businesses and organizations that need advanced features and support to build and maintain their websites.

> Learn more about [Wix Studio for enterprise](https://www.wix.com/enterprise) or [contact our sales team](https://www.wix.com/enterprise/form?referrer=kb-enterprise-overview-en).

---

### Manage all of your sites from one dashboard

Stay in control and keep track of all the sites managed by your enterprise. You can filter your site list to see a list of specific sites, and save filtered views that you want to access regularly.

Click on a site in your site list to see detailed information regarding the site's plan and status, site address and domain info, template used and more. You can also access the site's dashboard or perform site-level actions like editing, renaming, previewing and duplicating. Learn more about [managing all of your sites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-enterprise-managing-all-of-your-sites).

> **Filter sites & save your view:**
>
> Click **Filter** or use the advanced search to look at specific sites. Click **Save View **to add a quick shortcut and get back to the filtered view of the list at any time.

### Create custom templates

From Custom Templates, you can create templates with your preferred design, layout and custom apps installed. This means that you don't have to start from scratch when creating a new site for your organization or clients, improving efficiency and strengthening of your brand. Learn more about [creating custom templates for your enterprise](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-enterprise-creating-custom-templates).

> **Note:**
>
> You can create a custom template, either for your organization or for your clients. One template can't be used for both.

---

### Build custom apps for your sites

Build custom apps for your enterprise and use them on any of your sites. From the Custom Apps tab, click **+ Create Custom App** to get started with a new app, or hover over one of your custom apps and click **Manage App** to access your custom app in Wix Developers. Learn more about [building custom apps for your enterprise](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps).

### View and manage all of your media assets

Upload images and videos directly to your Wix Studio for enterprise account to use across all of your sites. Once you've uploaded a batch and want to share the news, click **Notify Clients** to notify your clients that you've uploaded new media that they can use in their sites. Learn more about [managing your media assets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-enterprise-managing-all-of-your-media-assets).

> **Note:**
>
> Find out more about [supported file types and sizes for videos and images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes).

Use enhanced authentication with Single Sign-On (SSO) and let employees and/or clients log in with their Wix credentials using the Open ID Connect protocol. This kind of login can be used by employees or clients to access several related, yet independent, online platforms.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the Wix Studio enterprise solution.

### How do I add teammates?

Add people to your Wix Studio for enterprise account and assign them different roles, permissions and access levels.
1. Click the **Team** tab on the left.
2. Click **+ Invite People **at the top right.
3. Enter the email address(es) of your teammate(s).

**Note:** To add multiple invitees, enter each email separated by a comma (**,**).
4. Select the role(s) you'd like to assign to your teammate(s).
5. Choose which sites they can access under **Give Site Access**:

- **All sites and folders:** All sites and folders under your Wix Studio for enterprise account.
- **Specific sites or folders: **Specific sites and/or folders that you select.
6. Click **Send Invite**.

### If I update an existing template, will it affect all of the sites using that template?

No. When you create a new site from an existing template, it creates a duplicate of the original template for the new site.

### If I delete a template that's used on multiple sites, will those sites be deleted?

No. When you delete a template, it won't affect existing websites using that template.

### What happens when I update a custom app?

When you update a custom app, those changes take effect on all of the sites that are using that app.

### If my clients create a website themselves, can I still manage it from my dashboard?

Yes. You can manage all the sites under your Wix Studio for enterprise account.
