---
title: "Roles & Permissions Request: Viewing Your Collaborators' Edit History in the Dashboard"
---

Currently, it is not possible to view the dashboard edit history of your collaborators in the same way that you can [view the your site's edit history](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-and-managing-your-site-history#accessing-your-site-history).

This would let you see the changes that your collaborators make in your site's dashboard such as [updating your site's SEO settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929) or [updating your domain's DNS records](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-dns-records-in-your-wix-account).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
