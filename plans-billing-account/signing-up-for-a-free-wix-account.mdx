---

One of the great things about Wix is that it's 100% free to sign up. You can create a fully functioning website without needing to pay a thing.

After you've [created your website](https://www.wix.com), you can decide if you want to upgrade to make use of Wix's premium features, such as connecting a domain, removing Wix ads and more. Learn more about [upgrading your site](https://support.wix.com/en/upgrading-to-a-premium-plan-1283774).

---

### Signing up for a Wix account

Sign up to Wix with your email address, Facebook account or Google credentials.

#### To sign up for a Wix account:

Go to the [Wix.com sign up page](https://users.wix.com/signin?overrideLocale=en&loginDialogContext=signup).  Choose how you want to register and enter your details.  Click **Sign Up**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about signing up to Wix.

### What do I do once I have signed up?

Once you're signed up, you can start creating! Start from a site template or use our AI tools to create a site for you.
Follow our [Getting Started guide](https://support.wix.com/en/article/getting-started-with-wix) to begin your Wix journey and check out our [11-step guide to building a website from scratch](https://www.wix.com/blog/how-to-build-website-from-scratch-guide).

### What type of website can I build on Wix?

With Wix, you have the freedom to create a website that perfectly fits your needs. You can build [business websites](https://www.wix.com/business/website) for any industry with a whole host of different features. 
You can sell products with an [online store](https://www.wix.com/ecommerce/online-store), sell services and [take bookings](https://www.wix.com/scheduling-software), share your thoughts on a [blog](https://www.wix.com/start/blog), showcase your work with a beautiful [portfolio](https://www.wix.com/portfolio-website), and much more. Our solutions are built not only for a variety of industries but also for businesses of all sizes, from a personal brand to an [enterprise corporation](https://www.wix.com/enterprise).

### What's the difference between Wix and Wix Studio?

Wix uses the Wix Editor which is the platform you use to build and edit your website. It is packed full of features to help you create a unique, sophisticated site. It is intuitive and easy to use with simple drag and drop. You don't need any coding or prior design knowledge to build the site you envisioned.
If you are an agency or enterprise, we recommend that you use Wix Studio - our flexible, end-to-end platform for those who offer bespoke and scalable web creation services. The advanced responsive editor has game-changing design tools like responsive AI, open platform capabilities, and one workspace to manage your sites, assets and team. It allows you to efficiently deliver exceptional websites that exceed your clients' vision. [Learn more about Wix Studio](https://www.wix.com/studio)

### Can I build a website without knowing how to code?

Absolutely. Wix is for everyone and makes it possible to create a high-quality website without knowing how to code. In the Wix Editor, you can drag and drop any feature you want and customize it to match the look and feel of your site. Of course, if you do know how to code, you can add advanced functionality to your site with Velo.

### Can I hire someone to build a site for me?

Sure, if you need some extra help, you can hire a Wix Partner. Discover 100s of talented designers and explore their portfolios at a price that fits your budget. [Go to the Wix Marketplace](https://www.wix.com/arena/web-designers/)

### What are the premium features that come with upgrading?

You can use Wix completely for free. However, you might want to upgrade your site with a plan to access premium features including:
- Connecting your own domain
- Removing Wix ads
- Accepting payments
- Additional storage and bandwidth
- Google Analytics
- Premium support
[Compare our plans and their features](https://www.wix.com/premium-purchase-plan/dynamo)

### If I decide to upgrade with a plan, can I cancel it?

Don't worry about committing to a plan. You can change plans or cancel a plan at any time to finish at the end of the billing cycle (monthly or yearly). You can also assign a plan to another site in your Wix account. Learn more about [upgrading your site](https://support.wix.com/en/upgrading-to-a-premium-plan-1283774).
