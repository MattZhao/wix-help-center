---
title: "Request: Moving Contacts from One Site to Another in Your Wix Account"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically move contacts from one Wix site to another within your account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated. In the meantime, you can use the following workaround.

### To move your contacts:

### Step 1: Export your contacts from the first site.

1. Click [here](http://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/my-account/sites/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/contacts/) and click **Manage Contacts** next to the first site.
2. (Optional) To choose individual contacts to export, select the checkbox beside those contacts.
3. Click **More Actions** and select **Export Contacts**.
4. Select the contacts you want to export: 
- **Selected contacts: **Selects the contacts chosen in step 2.
- **By group: **Click the drop-down menu to select a contact group, or select** All Contacts**.
5. Select a file format: 
- Google CSV file
- Outlook CSV file
6. Click **Export**
7. Save the file to your computer.

### Step 2: Import your contacts to the second site.

1. Click [here](http://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/my-account/sites/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/contacts/) and click **Manage Contacts** next to the second site.
2. Click **More Actions** and select **Import Contacts**.
3. Click **Connect **under **CSV File**.
4. Click **Upload**.
5. Select the file from your computer.
6. Click **Import**.

> **Note:**
>
> If your contacts are organized in groups and you want to maintain this structure, each group should be exported and imported separately.
