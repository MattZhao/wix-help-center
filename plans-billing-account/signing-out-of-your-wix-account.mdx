---
title: "Signing Out of Your Wix Account"
---

You can sign out of your Wix account from your site's dashboard or your Sites page.

### To log out from your Wix account:

Go to [your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/home/) or go to [Sites](https://manage.wix.com/account/sites) in your Wix account.   Click your profile at the top right.  Click **Log Out**.

> **Important:**
>
> Signing out on one device does not sign you out on any other devices that you previously signed in on. It's not possible to sign out from all of your devices at the same time. [Vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/account-settings-request-automatically-logging-out-of-your-wix-account-on-all-your-devices)
