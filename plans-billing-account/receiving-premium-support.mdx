title: "Receiving Premium Support"
---

Purchasing a Premium plan entitles you to receive premium support. When you [contact us](http://www.wix.com/contact) as a premium user, we handle your request ahead of free user requests.

In addition, we encourage you to explore our Help Center which is filled with useful help articles and detailed walk-throughs.
