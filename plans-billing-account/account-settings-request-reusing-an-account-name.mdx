---
title: "Account Settings Request: Reusing an Account Name"
---

Currently, it is not possible to reuse an account name that you or someone else previously used. This includes closed accounts and accounts that no longer use the account name.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
