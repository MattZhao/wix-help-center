---
title: "Test Sites: Viewing Your Test Site URL"
---

Access your test site URL and share it with your employees, friends or anyone else you want to test the version on.

### To access your test site URL:

Go to the [Release Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/release-manager-client?&title=Your%20Social%20Content&buttonText=Open+Release+Manager) in your site's dashboard or [editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/test-sites-accessing-the-release-manager).  Hover over the test site.  Click the **Open** icon.  Copy the URL.
