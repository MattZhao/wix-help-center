Wix.com opposes any unauthorized infringement of copyrights.

It is Wix.com's policy to respond to clear notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the requirements of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (the "DMCA"). When adding content to your site, it is important to make sure that you have the rights to use that content.

We have set out some guidelines for you to help keep your site and content safe from copyright complaints.
