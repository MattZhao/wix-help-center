---
title: "Account Settings Request: Automatically Logging Out of Your Wix Account on All Your Devices"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically log out of your Wix account on all of your devices at the same time. You need to manually log out of each device that you have signed in to.

> **Workaround:**
>
> When you change your Wix account password, Wix automatically ends any active sessions associated with your previous password within 15 minutes. Try changing your password if you want to log out of your account on all of your devices.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
