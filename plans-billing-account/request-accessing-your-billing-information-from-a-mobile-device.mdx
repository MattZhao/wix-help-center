---
title: "Request: Accessing Your Billing Information From a Mobile Device"
---

Currently, it is not possible to access the billing information for Wix services from a mobile device.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [access your Billing History](https://manage.wix.com/account/billing-history) at any time while using a desktop device.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
