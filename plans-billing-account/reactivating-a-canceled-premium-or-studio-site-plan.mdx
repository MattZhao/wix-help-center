---
title: "Reactivating a Canceled Premium or Studio Site Plan"
---

You can reactivate a canceled site plan **before** the subscription ends by turning the auto renew back on. If the subscription has already ended, you can simply purchase a new plan.

### To reactivate your Premium or Studio plan:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click **Turn Auto Renew On** under the relevant subscription.

> **Note:**
>
> Site plans canceled during the [14-day money back period](https://support.wix.com/en/article/requesting-a-refund-for-a-premium-plan) are canceled immediately. This means that they cannot be reactivated by turning the auto renew back on. You can go ahead and upgrade the site again to purchase a new plan.
