The promotion includes a 70% discounted rate for a 2-year Wix Premium plan to create and host one website. The subscription also includes a free, 1-year [domain registration voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-free-domain-voucher).

### About the Wix/TechSoup offer

The 70% discounted rate applies to 2-year Premium plan subscriptions. Organizations can request 1 coupon code every 2 years. If you would like to extend the discount, please visit [TechSoup.org](https://www.techsoup.org/wix).

If you do not [cancel your subscription](https://support.wix.com/en/article/turning-off-the-auto-renewal-for-a-wix-premium-plan) or request a new offer before the end of the second year, you will be automatically billed by Wix for an additional two years of service at the [standard rate](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website).

The promotion also includes a free 1-year [domain registration](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domains) voucher. If you do not cancel the domain before its renewal date, you will be automatically billed by Wix for an additional subscription period (usually a year).

### FAQs

Click on a question below to learn more about this discount and TechSoup.

### What is TechSoup?

[TechSoup Global](https://www.techsoup.org) is a global non-profit organization that supports charities, non-profits and libraries by providing them with access to discounts and donations of software, hardware, and technical services, as well as technical support.

### What happens after I apply for this discount?

If your request is approved by TechSoup, you will receive an email with instructions for receiving your Wix subscription.

### Is there a limit on how many discounts an organization can receive?

This discount applies per organization. The 70% discounted rate is applicable to 2-year Premium plan subscriptions. Organizations can request 1 coupon code every 2 years. If you would like to extend the discount, please visit [TechSoup.org](https://www.techsoup.org/wix).

### Is this discount offer available to organizations that have already upgraded to a Wix Premium plan?

No, this offer is not available to organizations that have already upgraded their site with a Premium plan.

### Is this offer available to organizations that have a free website with Wix?

Yes, organizations that have a free website with Wix are eligible.

### Where can I learn more about the terms and conditions?

For more information about the terms and conditions of this discount, visit [TechSoup.org](https://www.techsoup.org/products/wix-unlimited-premium-plan--2-year-subscription-%E2%80%93-access-to-discounted-rates--G-51063--).
