### What are Core Web Vitals?

Core Web Vitals are the industry standard for measuring site speed, ranked based on your actual visitors' experiences, and the color-coded system follows Google's benchmarks. 
The Core Web Vitals displayed in the account level monitoring dashboard include:
- **Largest Contentful Paint (LCP):** An indication of a site's loading time - more specifically, how long it takes for the largest content element in the site's viewport to load.
- **Interaction to Next Paint (INP): **An assessment of your site's responsiveness to all click, tap, and keyboard interactions that occur throughout each visit on every page. The final INP value is the longest interaction observed.
- **Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS):** A measurement of unexpected movements of elements that change the layout of the page.

### Who has access to the account level monitoring dashboard?

Account admins, co-admins and those with [custom roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview#create-custom-roles) with the relevant permissions can view the monitoring dashboard.

### Does my PageSpeed score affect my SEO ranking?

Not exactly. Even though site speed is one of the many factors affecting your SEO ranking, only data collected from real user sessions counts. The PageSpeed score you see here is based on a lab test and doesn’t affect [your site’s SEO](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-about-core-web-vitals#core-web-vitals-and-seo) directly.

### Why are my scores different for desktop and mobile?

[PageSpeed scores for mobile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-understanding-pagespeed-insights#mobile-and-desktop-variations) are often lower than for desktop. That’s because mobile devices have slower processors and take more time to resize texts and images. Lab settings are another reason: Google’s mobile test is on a simulated 3G connection, which is different from the 4G and Wifi connections most visitors use to access your site.
