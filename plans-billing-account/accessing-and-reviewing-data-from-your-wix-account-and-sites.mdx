---
title: "Accessing and Reviewing Data From Your Wix Account and Sites"
---

Most of your data is accessible directly from your Wix account. This includes information about sites in your account, premium services, and personal information, such as your address and billing information.

---

### Account information

Click to view instructions on how to access your account information.

### Account settings

To access your account settings:
1. [Sign in to your Wix account](http://www.wix.com/).
2. Hover over the user panel and click **Account Settings**.

### All sites

This section includes all the current sites in your Wix account. [Sign in to your Wix account](http://www.wix.com/) to review your sites.

**Tip**: For Wix Studio users, switch between [workspaces](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-a-tour-of-the-workspace) to locate the relevant site.

### Chats, messages and other activity from your site visitors

In your Wix account you can review chats, form submissions, bookings, and all other activity from your site visitors. 
**To review your visitor activity:**
1. [Sign in to your Wix account](http://www.wix.com/).
2. Click **Select Site** on the site you wish to review.
3. Click the Inbox icon  at the top right.

### Domains

In your Wix account you can review and manage all your domains that are managed through Wix. [Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) to review your domains.

### Business email

In your Wix account you can review and manage all your personalized business email accounts. [Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) to see your business email accounts.

### Premium and Studio site plans

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://manage.wix.com/account/subscriptions) view your Wix site plans.

### Invoices

You can review and download all of your Wix invoices directly from your Wix account.

**To review your invoices for Wix services:**
1. [Go to Billing History](https://www.wix.com/account/billing-history?referralAdditionalInfo=account-manager) in your Wix account.
2. Click the filters or use the search field to search for an invoice.
3. Click the** More actions **icon  next to the relevant service.
4. Click **View Invoice**.

### Premium Subscriptions

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://manage.wix.com/account/subscriptions) to view all premium subscriptions purchased through Wix, including premium apps, logos, and more.

### Media

You can review and manage all of the media that you've uploaded to your account. Select an option below to view your media:

**Images:**
- [Image files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=picture): Images that you uploaded to your site.
- [Secure images files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=secure_picture): Images that you uploaded for apps using secure servers (e.g. Wix Art Store, Wix Photo Albums)
- [Wix Video cover images](https://vod-meta.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=picture): Images that you used as covers in Wix Video.

**Music:**
- [Music files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=music): Music files that you uploaded to your site (e.g. MP3 files).
- [Wix Music tracks](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=secure_music): Music files that you uploaded to Wix Music.

**Video:**
- [Background videos](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=video): Videos that you uploaded for your site background.
- [Wix Video files](https://vod-meta.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=secure_video): Videos that you uploaded in Wix Video.
- [Wix Video cover videos](https://vod-meta.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=video): Videos that you used as covers in Wix Video.

**Other:**
- [Flash files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=swf): Files that you uploaded in SWF format.
- [Shape files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=shape): Shapes that you uploaded to your site.
- [Document files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=document): Documents that you uploaded to your site (e.g. PDF or DOC files).
- [Secure document files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=secure_document): Documents that you uploaded in apps using secure servers (e.g. Wix Art Store).
- [Font files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=ufont): Fonts that you uploaded to your site.
- [Archive files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=archive): Files that you archived.
- [Secure archive files](https://files.wix.com/users/user-data/view?mediaType=secure_archive): Files that you archived in an app using a secure server (e.g. Wix Art Store).

### Third party apps

Each app is unique and can be set up differently. Your information might be located in a different location, depending on the app. For example, some apps have a dashboard with your information, while other apps have a Settings panel in your editor.

> **Note:**
>
> To [retrieve data from third party apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/retrieving-data-from-third-party-apps-9235769), contact the app owner directly.

Click to view instructions on accessing your apps:

### Dashboard apps

1. [Sign in to your Wix account](http://www.wix.com/).
2. Go the [dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard) of the relevant site.
3. Select the app you wish to view.

### Editor apps

1. Go to your site [editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-editor).
2. Navigate to the page with the relevant app.
3. Select the app in your editor and then click **Settings**.
