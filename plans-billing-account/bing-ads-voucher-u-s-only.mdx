### Accessing your voucher

Access your voucher easily through your Wix account and start advertising today. The voucher is available 48 hours after purchase of one of the Premium plans listed above, and valid for 2 months from the date of purchase.

#### To access your voucher:

[Go to Vouchers](http://www.wix.com/wix/api/voucherConsole?referralAdditionalInfo=%7Btheir) in your Wix account.  Scroll down to find your voucher under Bing Ads.  Click** Claim Voucher**.

**Note:** This button now reads 'Go to Bing' and the word 'claimed' appears above it.  Click **Go to Bing** and follow instructions there.

This promotional code must be entered before its expiration date. You can find this date in the Offer Details section of the Bing Ads Terms and Conditions.** **
