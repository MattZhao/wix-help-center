---
title: "Understanding Wix Analytics Subscription Reports"
---

With subscription reports you can explore sales, status of pricing plans, product subscriptions, and recurring invoices. You can find out which of your subscriptions generate the most revenue, expected revenue from in the next six months, which subscriptions are due to expire, and more.

#### In this article, learn more about your subscription reports:

[Subscriptions Sold over Time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-analytics-subscription-reports#subscriptions-sold-over-time)  [Subscription Revenue over Time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-analytics-subscription-reports#subscription-revenue-over-time)  [Revenue by Subscription](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-analytics-subscription-reports#revenue-by-subscription)  [Expected Revenue from Recurring Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-analytics-subscription-reports#expected-revenue-from-recurring-payments)  [Active Subscriptions over Time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-analytics-subscription-reports#active-subscriptions-over-time)  [Expiring Subscriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-analytics-subscription-reports#expiring-subscriptions)

### Show me how to access subscription reports

1. Go to [Analytics & Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Analytics%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll to the **Subscription Reports** section.
3. Select the relevant report.

### Show me how to switch views within a report

Click the relevant tab at the top of the report to access different chart types. Depending on the report, you may see different views.

### Subscriptions Sold over Time

Your Subscriptions Sold over Time report shows how many subscriptions have been purchased during different time periods.

### Measures available in your Subscriptions Sold over Time report

In Line Chart View:
- Subscriptions
- Purchase Date

### Parameters you can use to split your data in Subscriptions Sold over Time report

In Bar Chart Split view, click **Split by **to choose another metric. Available metrics to split your report include:
- Plan Name
- Payment Method

### Columns you can add to Subscriptions Sold over Time table report

In Table view, click **Select Columns. **Data you can add as columns include:
- Date
- Plan Name
- Start Date
- End Date
- Payment Method
- Subscription Type
- Current Status
- Customer Name
- Customer Email
- Customer Phone
- Subscriptions

### Filters you can select in all report views

In Line Chart, Bar Chart Split, and Table view, click **Select a Filter** and choose to filter your report by:
- Show all
- Exclude non-converted trials
- Exclude free plans
- Exclude free plans and trials

### More available filters

Click **More** to choose from the following filters: 
- Plan Name
- Start Date
- End Date
- Payment Method
- Subscription Type
- Current Status
- Customer Name
- Customer Email
- Customer Phone

### Expected Revenue from Recurring Payments

Use your Expected Revenue from Recurring Payments report to see how much revenue you're expected to receive from active subscriptions over the next six months.

> **Note:**
>
> This report shows expected revenue from recurring subscriptions that are currently active. New subscriptions and subscriptions that might be cancelled before their end date are not taken into account in this report.

### Measures available in your Expected Revenue from Recurring Payments report

### Active Subscriptions over Time

Use your Active Subscriptions over Time report to see how many active subscriptions you have by comparison to a previous period, or compared to last year.

> **Note:**
>
> When data is grouped by week or month, the report counts every plan that was active during this time period, even if it expired later in the week or month.

### Parameters you can use to split the data in your Active Subscriptions over Time report
