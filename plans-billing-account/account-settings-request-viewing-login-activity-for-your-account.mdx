---
title: "Account Settings Request: Viewing Login Activity for Your Account"
---

Currently, it is not possible to view information about your account login activity. This would let you review login attempts to your account, the location of the attempt, the device used, and the date and time of the attempt.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
