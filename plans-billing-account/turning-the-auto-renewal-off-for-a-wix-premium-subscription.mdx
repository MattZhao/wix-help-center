---
title: "Turning the Auto Renewal Off for a Wix Premium Subscription"
---

To prevent any disruption of service to your site, subscriptions for Wix services are set to renew automatically at the end of the subscription period. To prevent a subscription from renewing, you can turn the auto-renewal function off.

### Select a service below to learn more:

### Premium or Studio site plan

1. [Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the relevant plan.
3. Click** Cancel Plan**.
4. Click **Cancel Now **in the pop-up to confirm.

**Note:** This does not cancel your plan immediately. Instead your plan does not renew at the end of the subscription period.

### Domain

1. [Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the** **More Actions** **icon next to the relevant domain.
3. Click **Cancel domain**.
4. Select **Cancel auto renew **and click **Submit**.

### Business email

1. [Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the** **More Actions** **icon  next to the relevant business email.
3. Click **Cancel subscription**.
4. Select the **I still want to cancel** checkbox and click **Cancel mailbox**.

### Premium App

1. [Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the** **More Actions** **icon  next to the relevant app.
3. Click **Cancel App**.
4. Select **Cancel auto renew** and click **Cancel now**.

> **Changed your mind?**
>
> You can simply turn auto-renew back on again before the end of the subscription period.
