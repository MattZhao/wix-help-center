---
title: "Roles & Permissions Request: Using Permissions to Limit Access to Custom Dashboard Pages"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set role permissions to limit access to certain [custom dashboard pages you create](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-working-with-dashboard-pages). Collaborators with roles that have access to your site's custom dashboard pages can access all of your custom dashboard pages.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
