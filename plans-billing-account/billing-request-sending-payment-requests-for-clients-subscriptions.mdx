---
title: "Billing Request: Sending Payment Requests for Clients' Subscriptions"
---

We are happy to announce that you can now [let clients pay for Wix Premium plans using a payment request](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-letting-clients-pay-for-premium-plans-via-a-payment-request) in Wix Studio. Once you send a request, your client can review the plan and upgrade the site themselves.
