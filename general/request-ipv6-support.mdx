---
title: "Request: IPv6 Support"
---

Wix currently supports Internet Protocol Version 4 (IPv4) as this is the standard protocol. We do not support IPv6 at this time.

> **Note**
>
> If you have AAAA records (as opposed to A records) in your DNS, your domain is the unsupported IPv6 type.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
