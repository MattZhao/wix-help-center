---
title: "Reporting Abusive Content"
---

Reporting abusive content helps Wix to maintain the high ethical standards practiced within our community of users.

Millions of sites are built on Wix every month and we do everything in our power to prevent abuse.

If you encounter any Wix sites that you believe to be abusive (other than Electronic Spamming), you can [file a complaint](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=lKLN9S6dps8s0ldU4ssh&referral=abuseMainPage).
