title: "Creating Titles With the Wix Title Generator"
---

Quickly generate engaging titles and headlines to use in your blog posts, email campaigns, social media and more. Get ahead of your content creation game using the AI-powered Title Generator.

A compelling headline or title can improve click-through rate, broaden your audience and increase your potential to monetize your content.

### To generate a title:

Go to the [Title Generator](https://www.wix.com/tools/title-generator).   Enter your topic or keywords into the generator.   Click **Get Your Title**.   Click the copy icon  next to a title to copy it to your clipboard. You can then paste it to your blog, articles, emails etc.   (Optional) Select a subject from the drop-down to refine your search results.

> **Tip:**
>
> Check out the [Blog Title Generator](https://www.wix.com/tools/title-generator/category/blog) and the [YouTube Title Generator](https://www.wix.com/tools/title-generator/category/youtube) to get started creating your blog and YouTube titles.
