Create a stunning portfolio website that shows people who you are and what you do best. The great thing about creating a portfolio website is that it gives you that professional and creative space to be seen and acknowledged — a space that's truly dedicated to showcasing your work in a way that only you can.

> **Getting Started:**
>
> - Check out our essential guide to [creating an online portfolio](https://www.wix.com/blog/creative/2018/04/how-to-make-online-design-portfolio-guide) and get started with your very own [portfolio website](https://www.wix.com/portfolio-website) today.
> - Alternatively, try out the new [Wix Portfolio app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-portfolio-an-overview) and create projects and collections with as many photos and videos as you like.

### Choose a Portfolio Template

We have tons of [portfolio templates](https://www.wix.com/website/templates/html/portfolio-cv/portfolios) for you to choose from. Pick the one that speaks to you the most and then start customizing your way.

> **Stuck for inspiration?**
>
> Discover some of the [best portfolio websites](https://www.wix.com/blog/creative/2020/03/best-portfolio-websites) to help you choose the template that's right for you.

### Personalize Your Site

Add your own special touch to the site, so that your site visitors instantly get a sense of who you are and what you're all about. For example, if you're a fashion designer that likes clothes that make a statement, you want to get that across in your site too. Sleek, bold, confident — find what speaks to you and your brand.

> **Ready to get started?**
>
> Start from scratch or choose a template. Then drag and drop elements into place in the Wix Editor. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-wix-editor)

---

### Upload Your Media

Adding media is an essential step in your portfolio journey, after all, what's a portfolio without any images? Using the Wix Media Manager, you can effortlessly upload your work to your site. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-about-the-media-manager)

---

### Add a Page About You

It's super important to add a page that tells your site visitors about you, your brand and everything your portfolio stands for. You can add an about page by clicking the **Add** icon in your Editor.

> Learn how to create pages in the [Wix Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-page-to-your-site).

---

### Add a Contact Form

Give your site visitors a way to get in touch with you directly from your site. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-form-on-your-site)

Once you've got the essential parts of your portfolio website complete, you can then start thinking about promoting your site across social media. [Wix Video Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-about-wix-video-maker) is perfect for this.
