---
title: "Enabling Yandex Metrica Session Replay"
---

With Yandex Metrica Session Replay, you can replay users visits (mouse clicks, scrolls, keystrokes, and movements) to get insights on how to improve their overall experience.

> **Note:**
>
> To enable Session Replay, you must first connect Yandex Metrica to your site. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-your-yandex-metrica-code-to-your-wix-site).

### To enable Session Replay in Yandex Metrica:

Sign in to your [Yandex Metrica](https://metrica.yandex.com/list) account.Click **Settings** from the left side menu. Click the **Tag **tab.Click the **Session Replay, scroll map, form analysis** toggle to enable the option. Click **Save**.
