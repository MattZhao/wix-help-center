---

A paystub is a document given to an employee that breaks down the details of their salary.

---

### To generate and send a paystub:

To generate a paystub, fill in the required fields such as your business details, employee details and salary information. Generate the pay stub and preview it before sending it to your employee.

#### To generate and send a paystub:

Go to the [Wix Paystub Generator](https://www.wix.com/tools/pay-stub-generator).   Click **Create a Pay Stub**.   Fill in the fields under the **Your Business Info** section.   Fill in the fields under the **Employee Info** section.   Fill in the fields under the **Salary Details** section.   (Optional) Preview the paystub under the **Pay Stub Preview** section.   Choose how you wish to receive your paystub: 

- **Email Pay Stub: **Send the paystub directly to your employee.
- **Download Pay Stub: **Download a copy of the paystub to your device.
