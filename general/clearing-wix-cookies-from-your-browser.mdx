---
title: "Clearing Wix Cookies from Your Browser"
---

When you visit a website, it is common for a file known as a cookie to be created and stored in your web browser. Cookies store user-specific information such as authentication data and help you stay logged in to your favorite websites. 

We recommend clearing your browser's cookies periodically, especially if you're noticing problems when browsing websites.

ChromeFirefoxSafariEdge
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Click the More** **iconat the top right.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Privacy and security** on the left.
5. Click **Cookies and other site data**.

1. Scroll down and click **See all cookies and site data**.
2. Type **wix** in the search field at the top right. 

**Note: **Make sure to type "**wix**" with lowercase letters.
3. Click **Clear displayed data**.
4. Click **Clear**.

1. Open Firefox.
2. Click the Open Menu** **icon  in the upper-right corner.
3. Select **Settings**.
4. Select the **Privacy & Security** tab.
5. Click **Manage Data **in the **Cookies and Site Data **section.
6. Type **wix **in the Search** **field.

**Note: **Make sure to type "**wix**" with lowercase letters.
7. Click **Remove All Shown**.
8. Click **Save Changes**.

1. Open Safari.
2. Click **Safari**.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Privacy**.
5. Click **Manage Website Data**.

1. Type **wix **in the **Search **field.
Note:Make sure to type "**wix**" with lowercase letters. 
1. Click **Remove All**.
2. Click **Done**.

1. Open Microsoft Edge.
2. Click the Menu icon   at the top right.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click the **Cookies and site permissions **tab.
5. Click **Manage and delete cookies and site data**.
6. Click **See all cookies and site data**.
7. Type **wix **in the Search field. **Note: **Make sure to type "**wix**" with lowercase letters.
8. Click **Remove all shown**.
9. Click **Clear**.

> **Note:**
>
> Wix no longer supports the editing or viewing sites on IE11. Make sure to use a [supported browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers) to view or edit your site.
