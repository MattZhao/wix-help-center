title: "Request: Repurchasing the Same Business Phone Number"
---

Currently it is not possible to repurchase the same [business phone number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-started-9797633) after it has already been purchased once.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
