---
title: "Request: Exposing a Test Site to Specific Geographical Locations"
---

Currently, the percentage exposure refers to the percentage of your site visitors that see the test site when they access your live site in** all geographical locations**. You cannot select specific geographical locations to be exposed to a test site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
