---
title: "Reporting Trademark Infringement"
---

Wix.com takes trademark violation very seriously and opposes any unauthorized infringement of trademark rights.

If you encounter any Wix sites that you believe to be infringing your trademark rights, you can file a complaint by filling out [this form](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=05e54612-fae1-4366-946a-ca187280b695&referral=abuseMainPageTrademark).
