---
title: "Changing the Title Displayed in the Browser Tab"
---

By default, your page's [title tag](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-pages-seo-settings-in-the-seo-panel#optimizing-your-pages-title-tags-meta-descriptions-and-url-slugs) is displayed as the title in your browser tab.

If you want to change the browser tab title, you can customize your page's title tag in the [SEO Panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-pages-seo-settings-in-the-seo-panel) of your page.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Change the browser tab title for your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-title-in-the-browser-tab#changing-the-browser-tab-title-for-your-live-site)  [Change the Editor browser tab title](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-title-in-the-browser-tab#changing-the-browser-tab-title-for-your-editor)

---

### Changing the browser tab title for your live site

You can edit your browser tab title of your live site at any time in your site's Editor.

> Search engines will update your page's title tag the next time that they crawl your site. If you want to try to speed up the process, you can [submit your page's URL to search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/submitting-your-sitemap-and-urls-directly-to-search-engines#submitting-individual-urls-to-search-engines).

#### To change the browser tab title for your live site:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant page.
4. Click **SEO basics**.
5. Enter the page title under **Title tag**.
6. Publish your site.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant page.
4. Click **SEO Basics**.
5. Enter the page title under **Title tag**.
6. Publish your site.

> **Important:**
>
> Adding [custom code to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-tracking-tools) may affect your browser tab title. Try removing any custom code you added to your site if you see an incorrect browser tab title.

### Changing the browser tab title for your editor

You can also change the browser title of the editor version of your site. This helps you quickly identify the relevant site in your browser bar, especially if you are working on multiple Wix sites.

> These steps are only possible if you haven't connected a domain to your site. If you have a domain, you must unassign the domain first and then reassign the domain when finished.

#### To change the browser tab title of your site editor:

(Optional) [Unassign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unassigning-a-domain-from-your-site) your domain.  [Go to site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).  Click **Site Actions**.  Click **Rename Site**.  Under **Site Address (URL)**, enter the new browser title.  (Optional) [Reassign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-domain-to-a-site-in-your-wix-account) your domain.
