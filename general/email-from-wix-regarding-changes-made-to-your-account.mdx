---
title: "Email from Wix Regarding Changes Made to Your Account"
---

The security of our users' accounts is of utmost importance to us here at Wix and we are dedicated to online safety.

If you logged in from a social login, such as Google or Facebook, or if you updated your email address or password, you will receive an email saying "Your account settings have been updated". If you didn't authorize these changes, [change your Wix account password](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-wix-password) immediately and then contact [Wix Support](http://www.wix.com/contact/en).

Click [here](https://users.wix.com/account-recovery/reset-account-information) if you are experiencing issues accessing your account, or if someone took over your account.
