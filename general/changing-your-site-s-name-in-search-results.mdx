---
title: "Changing Your Site's Name in Search Results"
---

The name that appears for your site in search results is the site name tag of your homepage. If you have not entered a site name tag, your title tag is automatically displayed.

> **Important:**
>
> Google uses a number of sources to [automatically choose the name](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names) that displays in search results for your site. While your homepage's title tag can influence what Google chooses, other factors can influence it such as the content of your site, [your site's heading tags](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-pages-heading-tags), and the [structured data markup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-structured-data-markup-to-your-sites-pages-2546962) on your site.

### To change your site name tag:

Wix EditorEditor XStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the homepage.
4. Click **SEO Basics**.
5. Click **Advanced SEO**.
6. Click **Additional tags**.
7. Click **og:site_name** and edit the **Tag value**, then click **Apply**.
8. Click **Publish **at the top right of the editor.

> **Important:**
>
> Search engines will update your site name the next time that they crawl your site. If you want to try to speed up the process, you can [submit your page's URL to search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/submitting-your-sitemap-and-urls-directly-to-search-engines#submitting-individual-urls-to-search-engines).
