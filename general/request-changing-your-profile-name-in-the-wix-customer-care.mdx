---
title: "Request: Changing Your Profile Name in the Wix Customer Care"
---

Your profile name in Wix Customer Care is the Wix account name you used when you submitted your first support request. You can easily [change your account name](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-site-url-prefix), however this does not change your support profile name.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
