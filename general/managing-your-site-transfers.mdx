After you transfer a site to another Wix account, you can check the status of the transfer, or resend the invite under Roles & Permissions on your site's dashboard.

### Checking the status of a transfer

Site recipients have 3 days to accept the transfer invitation. You can check the status of the transfer to see if it's been accepted yet.

#### To check the status of a transfer:

[Go to Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.  Search for the recipient and check the status next to their name.

---

### Resending a site transfer

If the recipient did not accept the transfer in time, you can to resend the invitation via email through Wix or to retrieve a link to the invitation and send it directly to the recipient.

#### To resend a site transfer:

[Go to Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the new site owner, and click the More Actions icon .  Choose how you want to resend the invitation:

- Send it through Wix: Click **Resend Transfer Ownership**. Wix will resend the invitation to the email address that you entered.

**Tip: **Ask the recipient to check their spam folder if they don't see it.
- Send it myself: Click **Get Transfer Link**. The link is automatically copied to your clipboard. Paste it in an email or chat message to your collaborator.

### Canceling a site transfer

A site transfer displays a 'PENDING OWNERSHIP' label until the new owner accepts the transfer. You can cancel a site transfer invite at any time until it is accepted by the new owner.

#### To cancel a site transfer:

[Go to Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the new site owner, and click the More Actions icon .  Click **Cancel transfer**.

### Troubleshooting site transfer issues

If you're having trouble transferring a site to another Wix account, check the possible issues and solutions below:

### The transfer invite has expired

The transfer invite is valid for 3 days only. If the recipient does not accept the transfer within 3 days, the invite will expire. You can resend the invite as described above.

### The assigned Premium plan was canceled

After initiating the transfer of a site with an assigned Premium plan, you cannot cancel the Premium plan. This refers to plans both within the initial 14 day trial period and after.

### The connected domain was disconnected or canceled

After initiating the transfer of a site with a connected domain, you cannot disconnect or cancel the domain.

### The primary domain was replaced

After initiating the transfer of a site with a connected domain, you cannot change the site's primary domain.

### The connected business email was canceled

If the site you are transferring has a connected domain that is associated with a business email, you cannot cancel the business email after initiating the transfer of the site.

### A third-party app was added or canceled

After initiating the transfer of a site with a third-party app, you cannot cancel the third-party app subscription. In addition, you cannot add a new third-party app subscription after you initiate the transfer.
