---
title: "Wix and Terrorist Content Online"
---

The [EU Terrorist Content Online (TCO)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/784) regulation is a European Union regulation aiming to address the dissemination of terrorist content online.

Pursuant to articles 15 and 17 of the TCO regulation, Wix appoints Wix Online Platforms Limited as a legal representative for inquiries from member state authorities, the EU Commission, European Board for Digital Services, and recipients of the service.

Additionally, Wix appoints [legal@wix.com](mailto:legal@wix.com) as a point of contact for inquiries concerning TCO regulation. Please submit inquiries in English.
