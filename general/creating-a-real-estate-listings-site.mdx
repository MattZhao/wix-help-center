---
title: "Creating a Real Estate Listings Site"
---

With Wix you can create a real estate listing site that allows you to showcase properties effectively and attract potential buyers or renters. By using a real estate template from Wix, you get pre-built pages and elements specifically designed for property listings, saving you time and effort. You can also use the [CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview) to easily manage all your listings.

---

### Using a real estate template

Choose a real estate template from our ready-made site templates. These templates are designed specifically for real estate listings and come with pre-built pages and elements tailored to showcase properties effectively.

> **Check out the templates:**
>
> View [templates for the Wix Editor](https://www.wix.com/website/templates), or [templates for Wix Studio Editor.](https://www.wix.com/studio/templates)

---

### Using the CMS

If you already have a site, [add the Property Listings CMS preset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-collection-presets), then replace the demo properties with your own. This preset includes a database structure for properties, making it easy to add, edit, and organize your listings.
