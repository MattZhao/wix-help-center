With a monthly [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites), you don't have to commit to a long-term subscription, and your plan automatically renews each month. By default, you are charged every month for your selected plan on the plan's expiration date.

### Upgrading with a monthly plan

You can upgrade your site at any time from your Wix account to enjoy all the benefits and features of a monthly plan.

> **Important information regarding site upgrades:**
>
> - Site upgrades are only valid for one site. To upgrade multiple sites in your Wix account, you can purchase multiple plans.
> - Plans are set to renew automatically so that your site never experiences downtime. You can [turn off the auto-renewal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/turning-off-the-auto-renewal-for-a-wix-premium-plan) if you don't want your plan to renew. Make sure to do this after the first 14-day money back period, otherwise your plan is cancelled immediately.

#### To upgrade with a monthly plan:

[Go to the Upgrade page](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).  Click **Select** under the plan you wish to purchase.  Select **Monthly** and click **Continue to Checkout**.

Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Note:**
>
> After submitting your purchase, [your bank may require you to verify your identity](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds). If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.

### Do monthly plans include Wix vouchers or premium apps?

Monthly plans do not** **include Wix vouchers or premium apps.

### Can I buy a Wix Logo plan that includes a monthly site plan?

Currently, it is not possible to buy Wix Logo plans that include a monthly website subscription. It is only possible to purchase these subscriptions with a yearly site plan. 
You can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-request-wix-logo-monthly-plans) and we'll make sure to keep you updated. As an alternative option, you can purchase a monthly plan for your website and purchase Wix Logo separately.
