---
title: "Request: Disabling Fuzzy Search in Wix Site Search"
---

Currently, when you perform a search using Wix Site Search, you get some results that are not really relevant to your search. This is because Site Search performs a search to match similar words to what you've searched. For example, if you searched for the word "Cat", you are matched with words that contain the letters "Cat".

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
