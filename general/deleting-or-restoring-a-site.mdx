---
title: "Deleting or Restoring a Site"
---

You can delete sites that you're not using directly from the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview) and the new [Wix Studio mobile app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-app-an-overview). When you delete a site, it becomes unpublished and you can no longer edit it.

If you decide you need it again, you can restore it within 90 days. After 90 days, your site is permanently deleted.

---

### Deleting a site

When removing a site, you have a couple of options to choose from. You can move it to the trash folder or permanently delete it.  All sites are easily located from your site list, which vary depending the app you're using.

Click the relevant app below to locate the site you want to delete.

Wix appStudio app
1. Go to the Wix app on your mobile device.
2. Tap the **Site List** drop-down at the top.
3. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant site.
4. Tap **Move to Trash**, then tap **Move to Trash** to confirm.
5. (Optional) Tap the **Trash** folder to permanently remove a site.

1. Tap the site you want to delete.
2. Tap **Remove Site**, then tap **Remove** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot after tapping the More Action icon next to a site in the Wix Owner app.]Notes:
- Only site owners can delete a site.
- When you permanently delete a site, all site and member data will be erased and you cannot restore it.

1. Go to the relevant workspace in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Sites**  at the bottom.
3. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant site.
4. Tap **Move to Trash**, then **Move to Trash** to confirm.
5. (Optional) Tap the **Trash** folder to permanently remove a site.

1. Tap the site you want to delete.
2. Tap **Remove Site**, then tap **Remove** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot after tapping the More Action icon next to a site in the Studio app.]Notes:
- Only site owners in your workspace can delete a site.
- When you permanently delete a site, all site and member data will be erased and you cannot restore it.

---

### Restoring a site from the trash

If you change your mind and want to restore a deleted site, you can do so from the site list. The site list is easily accessible from your site list in the app you're using.

Click the relevant app below to locate the site you want to restore.

Wix appStudio app
1. Go to the Wix app on your mobile device.
2. Tap the **Site List** drop-down at the top.
3. Tap the **Trash** folder.
4. Tap the relevant site.
5. Tap **Restore site**, then tap **Restore** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot of restoring a site from the Trash folder in the Wix Owner app.]
1. Go to the relevant workspace in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap Sites  at the bottom.
3. Tap the **Trash** folder.
4. Tap the relevant site.
5. Tap **Restore site**, then tap **Restore** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot of restoring a site from the Trash folder in the Studio app.]

> **Note:**
>
> Once you restore a site, it is moved back to your site list and remains unpublished until you publish it again.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about deleting or restoring your site on the Wix app and the new Wix Studio mobile app.

### Can site visitors still view my site if I move it to the trash folder?

Moving a site to the trash folder means your site will no longer be available, whether it's from desktop or mobile.

### Will my site always be available to restore from the trash folder?

Once you move a site to the trash folder, you can restore it within 90 days. If you do not restore your site within 90 days, it will be permanently deleted.

### Why can't I move my Premium site to trash?

It's not possible to move a Premium site to the trash folder. If you wish to do so, first [cancel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-a-wix-premium-plan) or [reassign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-premium-plan-to-a-different-site-in-your-wix-account) the Premium plan to another site in your account.

### How can I permanently delete my information from the Wix databases?

You can [send a request to delete your account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-deleting-your-account) from the relevant app if you want your account information permanently deleted from the Wix databases.
