---
title: "Reporting a Privacy Issue"
---

Wix takes privacy issues very seriously and is committed to protecting our users' data and private information.

To help make your site privacy compliant and to discover how Wix processes the data of both you and your site visitors, our [Help Center](https://support.wix.com/en/privacy-gdpr) can assist you with privacy issues including:

If you believe you've found a privacy issue, or have a query regarding our privacy practices, please [contact our team](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=kKKuIuXsiPa1kiePMmcA&type=privacyShieldPolicy&referral=privacyPolicy).

> **For other issues related to Wix policy:**
>
> - If you wish to report specific content such as phishing, spam, copyright infringement, trademark infringement, or otherwise abusive content created on a Wix site, please [visit our report abuse page](https://www.wix.com/abuse).
> - If you are experiencing issues accessing your account, or if you believe someone has taken over your account, complete our [account recovery form](https://users.wix.com/account-recovery/reset-account-information).
> - If you have a security issue, please [contact our Security team](https://www.wix.com/contact?&nodeId=0b6e14e6-6841-459c-8ad3-8d35736f2dc8&referral=securitykb).
