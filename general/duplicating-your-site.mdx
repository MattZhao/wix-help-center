Learn how to duplicate your site from your editor or dashboard.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### What content is not copied to my duplicate site?

- Store orders and invoices
- Contacts and members
- Email campaigns and triggered emails
- Settings for third-party apps
- Google Operations logs
- Velo by Wix: 

- Secrets stored in your [Secrets Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-working-with-the-secrets-manager).
- Any elements that were hidden require [enabling Velo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/enabling-velo) in your new site.

### Can I duplicate my site if it was built by a Wix Professional or agency?

If a Wix Professional or agency built your site with the Studio Editor, you cannot duplicate it from your account.
