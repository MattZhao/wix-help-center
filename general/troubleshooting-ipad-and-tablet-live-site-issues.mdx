---
title: "Troubleshooting iPad and Tablet Live Site Issues"
---

Wix sites are compatible for viewing on iPads and tablets. All tablet devices, regardless of operating system, display the desktop version of your site. 

Keep in mind that iPads and tablets do not have the same capabilities as desktop computers, and larger sites may take longer to load. Additionally, some features may not function the same on iPads and tablets (e.g. visitors can tap their screens but cannot hover over elements on your site).

---

### Resolving general issues

To resolve general iPad or tablet live site issues:

### Supported browsers and operating systems for iPads and tablets

Wix supports viewing live sites from iPads and tablets. However, the Wix Editor is not supported on iPads or tablets.

**Supported browsers on iPads and tablets**:
- Google Chrome
- Safari

**Supported operating systems on iPads and tablets**:
- iPads: iOS 14 and above
- Android: 9.0 and above

---

### Resolving specific issues

Click below to learn how to resolve specific iPad or tablet live site issues:

### Content is cut off on the sides of your screen

On iPads and tablets Wix sites are cropped within the [vertical gridlines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-working-with-the-editor-gridlines) of the Editor.

**To resolve this issue**:
Make sure to place your content within the vertical gridlines in your Editor. You can also [use columns](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-columns-on-your-strip) which are responsive to your screen's width to fit your content within most screens.

### Text appears as two lines

Fonts render differently on different devices and browsers. This can cause a single line of text to expand and appear over two lines on the live site.

**To resolve this issue**:
1. Remove formatting from the text:

1. Copy and paste the text into Notepad (TextEdit on Mac).
2. Paste the text into a [new text box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text) by pressing **Ctrl** + **Shift** + **V** (or **Cmd** + **Shift** + **V** on Mac) on your keyboard.
3. (If necessary) [Format your text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-formatting-your-text) using the options in the **Text Settings** panel.
2. In your Editor, expand the width of your text box slightly so that there is room for your text to widen without it appearing on the next line.
3. [Publish your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site).
4. [Clear your browser's cache](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-clearing-your-browsers-cache-on-a-mobile-device).
5. View your live site to make sure the text appears correctly.

### Text and images are misaligned

Wix doesn’t support [text wrapping](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-request-adding-images-inside-text-boxes-text-wrapping). If you've placed an image on top of an empty area in a text box, they will not align the same across all browsers and devices which use different rendering engines.

**To resolve this issue**:
Split up your text into multiple text boxes and make sure images are not placed on top.

### Other alignment issues

If text is misaligned with other elements on your live site it may be related to [fonts rendering differently](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-font-looks-different-on-different-browsers) on different browsers and devices.

**To resolve this issue**:
- Use columns
- Use the Repeaters element
- Use container boxes

Learn more about [aligning your site to display correctly on different browsers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-aligning-your-site-to-display-correctly-on-different-browsers).
