---
title: "Request: Supporting CAA Records"
---

Currently, CAA records are not supported by Wix.

If you have connected via pointing method, please make sure to remove the existing CAA record at your domain host's side.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
