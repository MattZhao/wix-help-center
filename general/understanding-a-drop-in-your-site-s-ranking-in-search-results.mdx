However, it doesn’t automatically guarantee that the ranking of your site will always go up.

### Factors that affect your site's ranking

Search engines use many factors to decide which sites to show on results pages. Some factors that can affect your site's ranking are:

The [keywords and search phrases](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-keywords-on-your-sites-pages) you use in your content  The number of [websites that link to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/gaining-backlinks-for-your-site)  The [age of the content ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/optimizing-your-sites-content-for-seo)on your site  Technical issues like broken internal links  The location of the searcher  The ranking of [your SEO competitors](https://www.wix.com/seo/learn/resource/competitive-analysis-for-seo)

### Search engine algorithm changes

If you notice that your site's ranking is going down instead of up, it could be due to a change in the way the search engine chooses results. Sometimes search engines modify their algorithms to trial new ways to decide rankings.

---

### Recovering your ranking after a drop

If you see a definite drop and want to recover your site's ranking, here are a few things you can try to get it going in the right direction again.

#### Check your recent changes

If you recently made significant changes to your site design or content, your ranking might be affected. This is often not something to worry about unless your ranking doesn't recover.

If you notice an unexpected drop after making a change, try the following:

Use [Google Search Console's URL Inspection Tool](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-search-console-using-the-url-inspection-tool) to check your pages for warnings or errors  Check for changes to the [canonical tags of your pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-canonical-tags-for-your-sites-pages)  Check for [edits to your site's robots.txt file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editing-your-sites-robotstxt-file) that stop search engines from showing your pages in results  Check for any changes to [title tags and meta descriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-seo-title-tags-and-meta-descriptions-to-your-pages), [additional meta tags](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-additional-meta-tags-to-your-sites-pages), [heading tags](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-pages-heading-tags), or page content  Identify any [duplicate content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/guidelines/duplicate-content?hl=en&visit_id=637535692468933099-4145384571&rd=1) on your site  Make sure that the desktop and mobile versions of your site [contain the same content](https://developers.google.com/search/mobile-sites/mobile-first-indexing#content-is-same)  Check for changes to the internal link structure of your site or menus, especially on your homepage  If you recently moved your site to Wix, check that you [created new pages with URLs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-page-url) that match the page URLs from your old site. If it's not possible to match them, you can [set up 301 redirects from your old URLs to your new ones](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-a-301-redirect-for-a-page-on-your-site)

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want to change the URL of an existing page and keep the previous ranking for it, you can [set up 301 redirects for the old URL of the page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-a-301-redirect-for-a-page-on-your-site).

#### Identify search engine actions

Although it's uncommon, some changes to your site may trigger a ranking penalty from search engines. This happens when your content [does not comply with the search engine's quality guidelines](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/guidelines/webmaster-guidelines?visit_id=637535627837191519-2435368477&rd=1#quality_guidelines), or the search engine thinks that you're trying to manipulate your ranking.

If you think your site may have been penalized, check that all of [your site's pages are still indexed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/checking-if-your-site-is-indexed-by-search-engines). You can also check in Google Search Console for [penalties that you need to address](https://search.google.com/search-console/manual-actions) in the **Security & Manual Actions** section.

#### Give your content a refresh

Search engines give higher ranking to sites with high-quality content that is regularly updated. If you haven't updated your content in a while, it may be time to give it a refresh.

Some ways that you can keep your site up to date with regular new content are [creating a blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-about-the-wix-blog), [making an FAQs page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-faq-app), and [adding a testimonials page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-customer-testimonials-to-your-site). [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/optimizing-your-sites-content-for-seo)

#### Optimize your keywords

If your ranking has dropped after making changes to your site, make sure you haven't accidentally removed important [keywords or search phrases](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-keywords-on-your-sites-pages) from your site's content.

#### Check your backlinks

Backlinks (also known as incoming links, inbound links, or inward links) are links that direct users to your site from other relevant sites. Sites that have [backlinks from reputable sites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/gaining-backlinks-for-your-site) rank higher in search results.

If your site has low-quality backlinks or loses some of its backlinks, it can have a negative effect on your site ranking.

We recommend using a backlink checking tool to analyze the quality of your backlinks and whether any of your pages have recently lost backlinks.
