### User-friendly and intuitive editors

Creating your dream website has never been simpler thanks to the Wix editors. With an intuitive drag-and-drop interface, building and customizing your site is a breeze, and the best part is, you don't need to write a single line of code!

We have 2 intuitive editors for you to choose from, so whether you're a beginner or an experienced user, we got you covered. The **Wix Editor **offers a user-friendly experience for those who prefer simplicity, while the **Wix Studio Editor **is better suited to website designers and other professionals who want to implement intricate design elements.

Whichever editor you choose, you can create your site just the way you want. Both editors provide a variety of pre-designed templates and elements, making them accessible for beginners while still offering flexibility for more advanced users.
