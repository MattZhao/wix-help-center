---
title: "Unable to View Your Live Site"
---

If you are unable to view your live site, make sure that you are entering your domain name or site URL correctly in your browser. Review the questions below to determine the cause of the issue.

### Click below to solve your live site viewing issue:

### Do you receive an error message on your site?

Below are some of the common error messages you might see when there is a problem loading your site. Select the error message you're receiving for more information:

- [Error 401 (unauthorized)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/live-site-error-error-401-unauthorized)
- [Error 403 (forbidden)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/live-site-error-error-403-forbidden)
- [Error 404 (not found)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/live-site-error-error-404-not-found)
- [Error 500 (internal server error)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/live-site-error-error-500-internal-server-error)
- [Error 502 (bad gateway)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/live-site-error-error-502-bad-gateway)

### Is your site published?

Make sure that the site your domain is connected to is indeed published. You can check this on your [site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fhome%2F). If your site is not published, open your editor and click **Publish**.

### Has your domain expired?

Wix domains are set to renew automatically, however, if you've canceled your domain's renewal or if there were payment problems your domain may have expired.

You can check the status of your domain on your [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains?referralAdditionalInfo=account&alwaysfs) page. If your domain has expired, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/retrieving-an-expired-domain-during-the-redemption-period) to learn how to repurchase.

**Note**: If you purchased your domain outside of Wix, contact your domain host to make sure your domain hasn't expired.

### Is your plan active?

In order for your site to remain connected to your personal domain, it needs to have a suitable plan assigned to it. Premium and Studio Plans are set to renew automatically, however, if you canceled your plan's renewal or there were payment problems it may have expired.

You can check the status of your plan under [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/plans). If your plan has expired, simply [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Upgrade%20Site&title=Select+a+Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/premiumStart?siteGuid=\{\{metaSiteId\}\}) again.

### Is your domain assigned to a site?

You may have accidentally disconnected your domain from your site. You can check to see if your domain is connected to your site on the [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains?referralAdditionalInfo=account&alwaysfs) page. If the domain is not connected, select the domain and click **Assign** next to **Assigned to site**.

### Are your DNS settings correct?

When you connect your domain it can take **up to 48 hours** for your domain to propagate and your site to appear live. If after 48 hours you're still unable to view your live site on your domain, use the [Wix Domain Assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-domain-assistant-3777632) to check your domain connection.

### Is your browser supported and up to date?

Make sure that you're using the latest version of a [supported browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers). You can check which browser version you're using [here](http://www.whatismybrowser.com).

### Are you using a public computer?

Some public institutions such as schools, universities, public libraries or government facilities, restrict access to certain URLs, including the Wix servers.

If you are unable to access your site from one of these institutes, we recommend that you contact the relevant IT department at your location (if possible) to see if they can whitelist Wix. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unable-to-access-your-wix-site-or-editor-from-a-public-computer)
