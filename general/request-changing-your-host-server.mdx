---
title: "Request: Changing Your Host Server"
---

Wix has host servers in multiple locations around the world. Currently, it is not possible to select or change which host server your data is stored on.

> Select service custom hosting solutions may be available with [Velo by Wix](https://www.wix.com/velo). Velo solutions are included with the [enterprise solution](https://www.wix.com/enterprise/trust/privacy).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
