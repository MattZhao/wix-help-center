---
title: "Marking a Task as Complete"
---

Mark your task as complete to remove it from your task list.

### To mark a task as complete:

Go to your [site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fhome%2F).Click **Customer Management**.Click **Tasks & Reminders**.Check the circle to mark your task as complete. (Optional) Check off the circle again to mark the task as incomplete.

> **Note:**
>
> Once you mark a task as complete, it disappears from your view and appears under **Completed Tasks **with a strikethrough.
