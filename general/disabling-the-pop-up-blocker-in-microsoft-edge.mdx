---
title: "Disabling the Pop-Up Blocker in Microsoft Edge"
---

To enjoy all of the Wix features, you need to allow Wix.com pop-ups.

### To disable the pop-up blocker in Microsoft Edge:

Open Microsoft Edge.   Click the **Settings and more **icon  at the top right.  Select **Settings**.  Click **Cookies and site permissions** on the left.   Scroll down and click **Pop-ups and redirects **under **All permissions**.   Choose what you want to do:

### Allow all pop-ups

Disable the **Block (recommended) **toggle.

### Allow pop-ups only from specific sites

1. Click **Add **in the **Allow **section.
2. Type **[*.]wix.com** in the **Site **field and click **Add**.
