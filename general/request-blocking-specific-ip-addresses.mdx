---
title: "Request: Blocking Specific IP Addresses"
---

Currently, it is not possible to block specific IP addresses or visitors from specific locations from accessing your site.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can use the [Blocky IP & Country Blocker app by Effective Apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/third-party-app-blocky-ip-country-blocker-by-effective-apps) from the Wix App Market to block IP addresses and countries.

> **Note:**
>
> Wix prevents registration and use of services [from certain countries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-account-country-limitations).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
