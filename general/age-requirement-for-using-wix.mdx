---
title: "Age Requirement for Using Wix"
---

Wix requires its users to be over the age of thirteen (13) years, or sixteen (16) years if you are an individual within the European Union (EU), or of the legal age of majority in your jurisdiction.

> Learn more about our [Terms of Use](http://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).
