### Access multiple workspaces

Wix Studio has a brand new workspace with everything you need to create and manage hundreds of sites, different teams, multiple premium subscriptions, and more.

You can belong to multiple workspaces using the same account email address, and switch between them easily. For example, if you have your own business but are also working for another agency, you can have one workspace where you manage your business sites, and also belong to the agency's workspace.

You can access the sites in each workspace from the Sites page. Find a specific site using advanced filters such as Editor type, Premium plans and publish status. You can also view and manage all of your workspace subscriptions in one place. Any subscriptions that need your urgent attention will be highlighted at the top for your convenience.

Your workspace is also filled with useful information to help you grow your business. From the [Discover page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-about-the-discover-page) you can stay ahead of the curve with access to new releases, product updates, exciting product betas, and an insightful roadmap for all upcoming Wix features.

### Manage your team in one place

Wix Studio offers a collaborative environment designed to support your entire workflow. Invite your teammates to join your workspace, enabling seamless teamwork on your projects.

As a team owner, you have the flexibility to customize roles and permissions for each teammate within the workspace and for specific sites. This personalized approach ensures that every teammate gets a tailored experience according to their expertise, and can focus on their specific tasks.

For example, you might want to give a website designer in your team permission to access the Studio Editor of every site in the workspace so that they can create and update them, and give a marketing expert permission to access only the dashboard of certain sites that you are promoting for clients.

### Collaborate with others

When working on sites, you can work with your teammates, or collaborate with external people such as freelancers and clients. Choose the role and permissions for each collaborator, so that they can only perform actions according to their expertise.

If there's several people working on a site in the Studio Editor at the same time, they can create together on the canvas in real-time with [concurrent editing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-collaborating-on-a-site). For example, a designer, writer and developer can all be working on a site together and see each other’s changes instantly.

Anyone working on a site can [leave comments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-collaborating-on-a-site#use-comments-to-stay-in-sync) on the canvas, tag others, and attach comments to specific elements for context.** **This means teams don’t need to leave the canvas to share feedback or ideas and the comments are in context, making communication more efficient. It's also a great way for your clients to give you feedback on their site so you can track and resolve any issues they raise.

> **Working with clients?**
>
> Set specific permissions for your clients to define what they can and can't do on their site. If you want, you can prevent your clients from touching the design of their site at all, or give them access only to the [Content Mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-understanding-how-clients-edit-their-site).

### Get creative with the Studio Editor

Unleash your creativity with our [new Wix Studio Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-a-guided-tour), designed to let you build captivating sites intuitively. Our new creation flows revolutionize the way you create sites, making the process easier and more efficient than ever before.

Enjoy the familiarity of building on a visual canvas with smooth drag and drop, and let the out-of-the-box tools deal with more complex design logic. As with any new platform, there will be a short learning curve, so we recommend that you check out [Wix Studio Academy](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy) to get started, but we'll provide you with everything you need to onboard and become an expert.

The Studio Editor is a reliable and stable tool, ensuring seamless live site performance that allows you to create beautiful websites with confidence. Below are some of the new features:

### AI features

Our Studio Editor uses responsive AI, patented technology that adds responsiveness to any page or section in the click of a button. The [AI tool](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-ai-to-make-sections-responsive) analyzes and places a responsive structure on the design, allowing faster and more efficient site creation.

Use the [AI Text Creator](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-the-ai-text-creator), a customized version of the GPT model by OpenAI, to generate text for your site when you need to work fast or require creative inspiration. You can also turn your text prompts into an image in seconds with our AI image generator.

Add more functionality to the sites you build with the help of the [Wix AI Assistant](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites/articles/workspace-tools/developer-tools/wix-ai-assistant/working-with-the-wix-ai-assistant-in-the-wix-ide). It can generate code, give instructions on how to use the code correctly, and help with resolving errors.

Discover even more AI tools to auto-enhance images, remove image backgrounds, auto-generate trailers and recommend products. We have many more in the works - keep an eye out for what's coming your way.

### Interactions & effects

Take complete control over element movements, allowing you to bring your site to life with [smooth transitions and captivating animations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-about-animations). Stand out with [custom cursors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-a-custom-cursor), choosing from a range of styles or uploading your own, to create a unique user experience. Make a statement with [text masking](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-text-masks), shaping your text into visually striking forms.

### Layout tools

Create intricate layouts more intuitively with our responsive layout tools. [Stack](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-about-stack) elements together to keep them in a vertical or horizontal order, and split sections into [grid cells](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-about-cells-and-grids) to lock your layout into place on all screens and devices.

### CSS control

Add custom CSS code to create unique styles. Adding your own code also gives you more control over animations and responsive behaviors, so they behave according to your exact specifications.

### Seamless scaling and pixel editing

The Studio Editor prioritizes [proportional scaling](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-size-of-your-elements#choosing-an-elements-responsive-behavior) by default. No matter how you resize or adjust an element, it maintains its incredible appearance.
In addition to proportional scaling, there are other responsive behaviors to choose from – all of which take care of the way elements are automatically resized on different screens.  
With pixel editing, the resizing experience is easy and precise, ensuring control over every element. Design in pixels that represent the size on the current canvas ([px*](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-size-of-your-elements#setting-the-size-in-px)), and the chosen responsive behavior does the rest.

### Open platform capabilities

When you code with Wix Studio, you are working in an open development platform that accelerates the way you build web applications. You can use [APIs](https://www.wix.com/velo/reference/spis/getting-started) to add custom functionality and interactions.
Code directly in Wix Studio's built-in [Code panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-working-with-the-code-panel), in a [VS Code-based IDE](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-about-the-wix-ide), or with your favorite IDE and [integrating with GitHub](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-about-git-integration-wix-cli).
Regardless of how you choose to code, you can enjoy serverless coding in both the frontend and backend, all in an open, extendable platform. 
To extend your site’s functionality and take full advantage of our open platform, you can:

- Style using [CSS](https://www.wix.com/velo/reference/$w/styling-elements-with-css) to customize the appearance of your elements' colors, fonts, sizes and positions.
- Install [NPM packages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-working-with-npm-packages), [packages built by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-working-with-velo-packages-built-by-wix), or create your own apps using [Wix Blocks](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-working-with-your-velo-packages).
- Design an independent site or app that takes advantage of Wix Studio’s powerful infrastructure, tools, and capabilities in a [headless](https://dev.wix.com/docs/go-headless/getting-started/about-headless/about-wix-headless) environment.
- Create built-in database collections in the [Wix CMS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview) or connect your site to [external database collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-integrating-external-databases-with-your-wix-site).
- Integrate with service providers to further expand your site’s functionality using SPIs and [custom extensions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-custom-app-extensions-using-spis).
- Use the Wix AI Code Assistant to capitalize on artificial intelligence for getting your code up and running quickly and efficiently. The assistant can help write, and even fix, your code.
- A set of [developer tools](https://support.wix.com/en/developer-tools-2438098) is available to help you monitor, test, and debug the code on your site.
- Explore these APIs for additional open-platform functionality:

- Use our [fetch API](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-accessing-3rd-party-services-with-the-fetch-api) to integrate 3rd-party services.
- Expose your site’s functionality as an API with our [http functions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-exposing-a-site-api-with-http-functions).
- Use [routers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-about-routers) for handling incoming requests to your site’s pages/sitemap and customizing the responses.

[Learn more about the open platform capabilities](https://www.wix.com/studio/development)

### Create reusable assets

Wix Studio's reusable assets let you create, reuse, and share sections, custom templates, and widgets across your projects, making your workflow more efficient and versatile.

**Design libraries: **Create your own custom assets, such as designed sections, typography themes and color palettes to make your design process more efficient. You can store them in a design library and reuse whenever needed, whether it's on another page, or on a different Studio Editor site in your workspace. Your team can also access your design libraries too! This keeps your team's projects consistent and saves your teammates valuable time.

>
> [Using design assets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-saving-and-reusing-design-assets)

**Custom templates: **Once you create a site, you can easily transform it into a reusable template. This means that everyone in your agency can leverage the template across all the sites they are building, saving time and ensuring consistency in design and functionality.
