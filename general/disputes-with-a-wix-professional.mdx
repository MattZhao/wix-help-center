title: "Disputes with a Wix Professional"
---

You may encounter issues while working with a Wix Professional. It is important that you and your Professional both set guidelines and expectations for the job being done and fully communicate with one another.

> **Important:**
>
> Wix provides the web platform for the Wix Marketplace and the list of Wix Professionals. This means it's up to you and the Wix Professional you hire to decide the costs and terms of your agreement. The Wix Marketplace is not liable for any transaction or communication between you and the Wix Professional you're working with. View the full [Wix Marketplace Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/marketplace/terms-of-use).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### What can I do if my Wix Professional doesn't respond to me?

While all disputes must be resolved directly between you and the Professional, we’ll do everything we can to help. 
If a Wix Professional isn't responding to you, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact) with the information below:
- Professional's full name
- Professional's email address
- Any more details you’d like us to know about your experience with this Professional

We will further investigate this Professional’s behavior in the Wix Marketplace.

### I'm having issues with my Wix Professional. What should I do?

Reach out to your Wix Professional and try to figure things out together. ﻿﻿﻿﻿Try to see if there was a miscommunication and explain how the work completed didn’t meet your expectations.
View all [project requests you've submitted](https://manage.wix.com/account/hire-a-partner) to the Wix Marketplace to locate and contact your Wix Professional. Learn more about [managing submitted project requests](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-project-requests-sent-to-wix-partners).

### Can I cancel a request to hire a Wix Professional?

Yes. You can cancel a request you've submitted to hire a Wix Professional and we'll notify your matches that this project is no longer available.
**To cancel a request:**
1. Go to [your projects page in the Wix Marketplace](https://www.wix.com/marketplace/my-projects).
2. Hover over the relevant project and click the** More Actions** icon  on the right. Then choose **Cancel Request**.
3. Select a reason for canceling the request.
4. Click **Cancel Project** to confirm.

### How can I get a refund from a Wix Professional?

If you'd like a refund for any payments you've made to a Wix Professional, you need to discuss it with them. When you pay a Wix Professional, the payment is deposited into their bank account. The Wix Marketplace is not liable for any transaction between you and the Wix Professional you're working with.
