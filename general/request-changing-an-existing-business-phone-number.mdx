---
title: "Request: Changing an Existing Business Phone Number"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change your [business phone number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-started-9797633) to a different one once you've purchased it. Additionally, you cannot update your billing cycle (e.g. from monthly to yearly payments).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
