---
title: "Checking Your Browser Version"
---

To find out what browser version you are using, visit [www.whatismybrowser.com](http://www.whatismybrowser.com). If your browser is not up to date, we recommend updating it to the latest version.

> **Is your web browser supported?**
>
> View the [list of browsers supported by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers).

Additionally, within most browsers you can find the version number in the **Help** or **About **section. For example, in Google Chrome, you can click the **More** icon  at the top right, then hover over **Help **and click **About Google Chrome**.
