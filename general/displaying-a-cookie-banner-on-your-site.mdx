title: "Displaying a Cookie Banner on Your Site"
---

A cookie consent banner is a notification that informs your visitors on how their cookies are being used on your site.

When a site visitor first enters your site, only essential cookies and scripts are loaded. In order to use other types of cookies and scripts (e.g., Functional, Advertising, Analytics), you must obtain consent from your visitor. Visitors can either approve all cookie types by simply clicking **Accept**, or only allow specific types by clicking the **Cookie Settings** button.

### Important information about Marketing & Analytics integrations

Once you add the cookie banner to your site, it automatically disables the usage of non-essential cookies and scripts until your visitor provides consent. 
As a result, certain Marketing and Analytics integrations will only work after a visitor approves using the relevant cookies. Below are some examples of the apps that will not work before obtaining visitor consent:
- **Functional cookies: **Hubspot and apps created by POWr.
- **Marketing / Advertising cookies: **Marketing integrations such as [Facebook Ads](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-understanding-your-store-campaign-data#how-cookie-banners-affect-your-campaign-data), Google Ads and Facebook Pixel.
- **Analytics cookies: **Analytical tools like Google Analytics, Hotjar and Wix Analytics.

**What isn't covered by the Cookie Banner?**There are [elements and apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/important-information-about-editor-elements-third-party-apps-custom-code-and-the-cookie-banner) that the cookie banner cannot control. In other words, Wix cannot guarantee that the cookie banner is able to block potential non-essential cookies from these parties:
- Third-party apps from the app market
- Custom code can be categorized so browsers can recognize their purpose. Learn about [categorizing custom code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/categorizing-your-custom-code).

### Step 1 | Enable the cookie banner

To make the cookie banner appear on your site, all you have to do is enable the **Display Cookie Banner on Site** toggle and save your changes. Once you save and publish, a banner instantly appears on your site with our default settings and design which you can customize at any time.

#### To enable the cookie banner:

[Go to Privacy & Cookies](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Privacy%20Center&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/cookie-consent-settings-app?referralInfo=article_displaying-a-cookie-banner) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Get Started **next to Display a cookie consent banner.  Enable the **Display Cookie Banner on Site** toggle.  (Optional) Click **Save & Publish **to display the default banner on your site.

### Step 3 | Link the banner to your privacy policy

Link the banner to a privacy policy so visitors are aware of all of the ways your site collects, uses, discloses, and manages their data. This helps in protecting their privacy if there is information they don't want to share with your site. You can either link to a page on your site or to an external web address providing the info.

#### To link the banner to your privacy policy:

Select the checkbox next to the relevant option under **Privacy Policy**:

- **Page on My Site: **Select a page from the drop-down menu.
- **External Web Address (URL):** Paste the link in the text box.

> Learn more about [creating a privacy policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-privacy-policy) for your site.

---

### Step 4 | Review cookie policy requirements

Before you can complete the process and add the cookie banner to your site, you must review the requirements and select the checkbox next to **I've read and understood the above**.

While the standard cookie banner prevents certain types of cookies being placed, [some elements are outside of its control](https://support.wix.com/en/article/important-information-about-editor-elements-third-party-apps-custom-code-and-the-cookie-banner). For those elements, you need to collect consent separately or remove them from your site to stay compliant.

#### About element placeholders

Some elements, such as Google Maps and Facebook Likes will not display on your site until your visitor has accepted the relevant cookies. On first view, the element will remain obscured and only a placeholder will display.

> The placeholder feature is not yet available to all users.

### Step 5 | Customize your banner's advanced settings

Access your banner's advanced settings to customize the texts explaining about the cookies your site uses. The banner includes default texts about Essential, Marketing, Functional and Analytics cookies which you can customize to your needs.

If your visitor wishes to view the component, they are invited to revisit their cookie preferences and enable the relevant cookies.

#### To customize your advanced settings:

Click **Advanced Options**.  Click **Customize Cookie Categories Text**.  Edit the following text:

- **Save Button Text: **The button your visitors click to log their consent.
- **Cookie Categories Text Content:** Edit the explanation about the types of cookies you collect. **Notes:** 

- You can also change the headline text for all cookie types, except for Essential.
- You can always click **Restore Default Text **to go back to the banner's default texts.

---

### Step 6 | Save and publish

After enabling the banner and linking it to your privacy policy, you're ready to click **Save & Publish** at the top right. Once you do, the banner appears on your site and starts collecting consent from your visitors.

> **Tip:**
>
> Access your Consent Log to view and manage consent collected through your cookie banner. If needed, you can [export the log and search for specific site members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-sites-consent-log).

### My cookie banner does not display for visitors. Why is this happening?

There are 2 possible reasons for your cookie banner not displaying once it's been enabled:
- Once a site visitor accepts or declines cookies, the banner remembers that preference, and no longer appears for that visitor. By [adding a Cookie Settings Widget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/allowing-visitors-to-change-their-settings-on-your-cookie-banner), your visitors can re-access your cookie banner to view or change their cookie preferences.
- If your domain is not properly connected, the cookie banner won't appear. You can troubleshoot domain connection issues using the [Wix Domain Assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-domain-assistant-3777632).

### Can I view my cookie banner while editing my site?

No. Your banner can't be viewed in the Editor or **Preview** mode. To see your changes, save and go to your live site.

### How do I create a privacy policy for my site?

Wix provides a guide to [creating a privacy policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-privacy-policy) for your site.

### Can I customize the text that shows on the component placeholder?

No. Currently the component placeholder text is not customizable.

### Can I disable the component placeholder?

The component placeholder is enabled along with the cookie banner. The only way to disable the component placeholder is by disabling the cookie banner.
