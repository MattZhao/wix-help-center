title: "Request: Blocking Spam Calls"
---

Currently, it is not possible in [business phone number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-started-9797633) to block incoming calls.

However, you can choose to block phone numbers and and unwanted messages from your conversation list in your Inbox. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-blocking-a-phone-number)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
