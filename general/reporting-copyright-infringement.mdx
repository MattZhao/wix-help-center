---
title: "Reporting Copyright Infringement"
---

Wix.com opposes any unauthorized infringement of copyrights. It is Wix.com's policy to respond to clear notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the requirements of the [Digital Millennium Copyright Act](https://www.copyright.gov/legislation/dmca.pdf) (the "DMCA").

To report a copyright infringement by a Wix user, please fill out our [automated DMCA Copyright Complaint Form](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=om98g6kPvnc8SE5FUBqC&referral=termsOfUse). This form is the fastest way to report a case of copyright infringement.
