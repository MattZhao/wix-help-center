Get solutions faster by sending us a screenshot of the problem you're experiencing. Make sure to take a screenshot of your entire browser window so we can understand and solve the issue as soon as possible.

>
> If you are taking a screenshot of a billing page or bank statement, make sure you **conceal your full credit card number and CVV number**. Leave only the last 4 digits of the credit card visible.

### Sending a screenshot to Wix Customer Care

While interacting with our Customer Care chatbot, you can attach screenshots to accompany your description of your issue.

#### To attach a screenshot:

Click the **Attachment **icon .

**Note: **If **Send **does not display, click **Edit my description** then click the **Attachment** icon .

Select a file from your computer and click **Open**.   Enter a description of the issue you're experiencing and click **Send**.

> **Supported file types:**
>
> You can upload image files in these formats: JPEG, JPG, PNG, GIF, ICO, BMP, and TIFF.
