---
title: "Moving a Site to Trash"
---

You can delete unused sites from your [Sites](https://manage.wix.com/account/sites) page by moving them to the Trash folder. Deleting a site unpublishes it, making it inaccessible to visitors online. However, if you change your mind, you have 90 days to restore the site from trash before it is permanently deleted.

### Moving a site to trash (deleting a site)

You can move a site to trash directly from your site's dashboard.

> **Need to move a premium site to trash?**
>
> It's not possible to move a premium site to the trash folder. If you wish to do so, first [cancel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-a-wix-premium-plan) or [reassign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-premium-plan-to-a-different-site-in-your-wix-account) the plan to another site in your account.

#### To move a site to trash:

[Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/home/).  Click the More Actions icon .   Click **Move to trash**.

Click **Move to Trash **to confirm.

### Moving multiple sites to trash

You can clean up your Sites page by moving multiple sites to the Trash folder at once.

#### To move multiple sites to trash:

[Go to Sites](https://manage.wix.com/account/sites) in your Wix account.  Click the **List **icon  at the top right.

Select the checkboxes next to the sites you want to move to trash.  Click **Move to Trash**.

---

### Restoring a site from trash

You can restore a site from the trash folder if you deleted it accidentally, or if you decide you want to use it again. You have 90 days to restore a site from trash before it is permanently deleted.

> **Note:**
>
> Restoring a site from trash does not automatically publish it. Make sure to open your site's editor and publish it to make it live again.

Select the view you're using on your Sites page to learn how to access the trash folder and restore a site.

#### To restore a site from trash:

Grid ViewList ViewIn the Grid view, the Trash folder appears in the Folders section at the top of the page.
1. [Go to Sites](https://manage.wix.com/account/sites) in your Wix account.
2. Click the **Trash** folder.

1. Hover over the relevant site and click **Restore Site**.
2. Click **Restore** to confirm.
In the List view, the trash folders appears in the site list.
1. [Go to Sites](https://manage.wix.com/account/sites) in your Wix account.
2. Click the **Trash** folder.

1. Hover over the relevant site and click the **More Actions** icon  .
2. Click **Restore site**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### What do I do with my domain after I move my site to trash?

In order to move a site to trash, you need to unpublish it first. This means that the domain remains unassigned in your Wix account until you decide what to do with it. You can [reassign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-domain-to-a-site-in-your-wix-account) it to another site or [cancel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-a-wix-domain) it.

### Can I delete a site with an active Third-Party app subscription?

No, it is not possible to move a site with an active Third-Party app subscription to trash. You must first [cancel the subscription](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-an-app-subscription) and wait until it expires.

### Will my site always be available to restore from trash?

Sites that have been in trash for more than 90 days will be permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

### Why is the option to move a site to trash not available to me?

If the option to move a site to trash is grayed out, it's possible that you're a collaborator on the site and do not have permissions to delete it.
Check with the site owner to see if they've given you the relevant permissions.
