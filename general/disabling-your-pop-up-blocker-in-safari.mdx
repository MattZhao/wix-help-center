---
title: "Disabling Your Pop-Up Blocker in Safari"
---

Pop-up blockers are great for preventing unwanted ads, but may lead you to miss out on important notifications and features. If a feature you're trying to use is not working, try allowing Wix.com pop-ups in your Safari browser.

> **Important:**
>
> Make sure you are using a [supported Safari version](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers#supported-browsers-for-desktop-devices). If you have an outdated version, update it to the latest version. You can go to [www.whatismybrowser.com](https://www.whatismybrowser.com) to see if you're using the latest version.

### To allow pop-up windows on Wix.com in Safari:

In your Safari browser, go to **www.wix.com**.  Click **Safari **from the top menu and select **Settings**.  Click the **Websites** tab.  Select **Pop-up Windows** under **General** on the left.  Click the **When visiting other websites** drop-down at the bottom right.  Select **Allow**.
