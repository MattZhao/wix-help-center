title: "Protecting Your Site's Content"
---

Content protection is important, especially when it comes to intellectual property on your site.

Add a watermark while editing images in the [Photo Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools), or enable it in your [Photo Albums](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-creating-a-photo-album) and the [Art Store](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-art-store-about-the-wix-art-store).

You can also add a [Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-and-setting-up-your-gallery) that includes a built-in right-click protect feature for your photos and videos. Alternatively, to protect your entire site, you can add the [Right Click Protect app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-adding-and-setting-up-the-right-click-protect-app) to prevent visitors from downloading your site content.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adding a watermark with Photo Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/protecting-your-sites-content#adding-a-watermark-with-photo-studio)  [Enabling watermarks in Photo Album and Art Store](https://support.wix.com/en/article/protecting-your-sites-content#enabling-watermarks-in-photo-album-and-art-store)  [Right click protect in Pro Galleries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/protecting-your-sites-content#right-click-protect-in-pro-galleries)  [Using the Right Click Protect app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/protecting-your-sites-content#using-the-right-click-protect-app)

### Adding a watermark with Photo Studio

Wix Photo Studio is a versatile tool that allows you to edit, adjust, and customize your images. Use the text tool to manually add a watermark to your photos. You can even adjust the font, size, format, and opacity.

4. Click **Text** on the left.
5. Select a text style and click to add it to your image.

- **Alignment:** Choose your text alignment: **Left**, **Center**, **Right** or **Justify **and adjust your line spacing.

- **Rotate and flip:** Change your text orientation: **Rotate left **, **Rotate right** , **Flip horizontally**  or **Flip vertically **.

### Enabling watermarks in Photo Album and Art Store

You can enable and display a built in watermark with Photo Albums and Art Store. You can edit the text and symbol, and even upload your own file to use as a watermark.

Click an option below to see how to add a watermark from each app.

### Photo Albums

1. [Go to Photo Albums](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Photo%20Albums&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fphotography-albums/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Manage Album **next to the relevant album.
3. Click the drop-down in the **Watermark** section to expand it.
4. Enable the **Add watermark** toggle.
5. Select the **Images**, **Gallery thumbnails** or **Cover** checkboxes to choose where your watermark is displayed.
6. (Optional) Click **Customize watermark** and choose how you'd like your watermark to look:

- Click **Upload Watermark** to upload an image file from your computer.
- Drag the **Opacity** slider to change the transparency of the watermark.
- Drag the **Size** slider to change the watermark's size.
- Use the **Position** grid to set where on an image your watermark appears.
7. Click **Save**.

### Art Store

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the Wix Art Store app.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Settings** from the menu on the left.
5. Enable the **Show watermark** toggle.
6. (Optional) Click **Customize Watermark** to adjust the watermark's opacity, size, position, or upload your own.

> **Note:**
>
> It is not possible to add watermarks to product images in Wix Stores, although you can manually add a watermark to the image before uploading it.

### Right Click Protect in Pro Galleries

Keep your images safe by displaying them in a Wix Pro Gallery. Pro Gallery has built-in protection from right-clicking on desktop, so visitors can't save your images with a right click.

Pro Galleries have many different layout options available, and you can easily [add them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-and-setting-up-your-gallery) from your editor.

> **Note:**
>
> If you decide later that you do want to allow site visitors to download your images, you can enable this option in the [Pro Gallery settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-gallery-settings).

> **Tip:**
>
> Want to add a watermark to your pro gallery images? [Vote for this option](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-adding-a-watermark-to-your-images)

### Using the Right Click Protect app

The Right Click Protect is an app available from the Wix App Market. When a visitor right clicks your content, they'll see a message informing them that the content is copyrighted.

You can [add it to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-adding-and-setting-up-the-right-click-protect-app) through the App Market, and edit the message that is displayed through the settings.

> **Important:**
>
> The Right Click Protect app does not prevent visitors using Ctrl+C to copy your content. [Vote for this option](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-request-right-click-protect-app-supporting-ctrlc-copying)

#### To add the Right Click Protect app:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Apps**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Search for the Right Click Protect app in the search bar.
4. Click **Add to Site**.
5. (Optional) Click **Settings** to edit the message shown.
