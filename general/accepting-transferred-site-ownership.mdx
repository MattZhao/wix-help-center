---
title: "Accepting Transferred Site Ownership"
---

When someone transfers a site to you, you'll receive an email with a link to accept the transfer. If the transferred site has Premium services (e.g. a Premium plan, domain etc.), you'll be asked to enter your billing information for future renewals of those services.

**In this article:**

[Accepting site ownership](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-transferred-site-ownership#accepting-site-ownership)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-transferred-site-ownership#faqs)

---

### Accepting site ownership

Site ownership must be accepted in the Wix account associated with the email address that the site was transferred to. If you do not have a Wix account with this email address, you can create one.

#### To accept site ownership:

Check your email account for an email with the subject line **Wix Website is Being Transferred to You**.Click **Accept Transfer** in the email and sign in to your Wix account (if you are not already signed in).
**Note:** If you don't have an account, click **Sign Up** to create one.Review the transfer details and click **Next**.Enter your billing information and click **Next**.Enter the domain contact information and click **Complete Transfer**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you receive a site without any Premium services, simply click **Accept Transfer** and sign in to your account (or click **Sign Up** to create one). You do not need to enter any billing or domain transfer information.

### FAQs

### How do I get my site transferred to me?

Site transfers must be initiated from the site owner's account. Once they initiate the transfer, you'll receive an email and you can follow the steps above. If someone created your site for you in another account, contact them and ask them to follow the relevant steps below to transfer the site to you:
- [Transferring a free Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-free-site-to-another-wix-account)
- [Transferring a Premium Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-premium-site-to-another-wix-account)

If the site owner is no longer available, or you wish to verify ownership, you can fill out the [Wix Account Recovery](https://users.wix.com/account-recovery/reset-account-information) form. Make sure to fill out the entire form (even if you're not completely sure of the answers), and add a detailed explanation of your case, so we can properly verify your ownership. Don't forget to provide us with a valid contact email so we can respond to you.After you submit the form, our team will analyze and review your request.

### Why do I have to enter my billing information when accepting a site transfer?

The billing information that you add during the transfer process is used to renew your paid services at the **end** of the subscription period. You are **not** charged while accepting the transfer. These services are set to renew automatically. If you do not want them to, you can [switch the auto renewal option off](https://support.wix.com/en/article/turning-the-auto-renewal-off-for-a-wix-premium-subscription).

### Can I transfer a Wix Payments account to another Wix account?

No, currently it is not possible to transfer a Wix Payments account.

### How long is the transfer invite valid?

The transfer invite is valid for 3 days only. If you don't accept the transfer within 3 days, the invite expires. Once it expires you can ask the original owner to send the invite again either [via email or direct link](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-site-transfers).

### Will I have access to the transferred site's media files?

Yes, site media is associated with a specific Wix site. If you receive a transferred site, you'll have access to all the media files that were uploaded to it. You can find these files in the transferred site's Media Manager. Learn how to [access the Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager).
