Currently, Wix Services are not compliant with the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-services-and-hipaa).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
