---
title: "Accessing Hopp.bio in Indonesia"
---

We are aware that users in Indonesia may experience issues accessing Hopp pages with "hopp.bio" or, in some cases, "hopp.to" URLs due to governmental restrictions.

To resolve this issue, you can try the following solutions:

**Connect a Custom Domain**: [Upgrade to a Hopp Pro plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-upgrading-to-a-pro-plan) to connect a custom domain to your Hopp Link in Bio page.  **Try using "hopp.to" instead of “hopp.bio”**: Change "hopp.bio/example" to "hopp.to/example" whenever you share the link or in your browser.

> **Please note**
>
> Custom domains can only be connected to Link in Bio pages. For landing pages and short links created with Hopp, please use the "hopp.to" URL instead of "hopp.bio".
