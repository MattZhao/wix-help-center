---
title: "Submitting a Copyright Infringement Counter-Notification"
---

If content from your Wix site was removed, or access to your Wix account was disabled, due to a copyright infringement claim, you may send a counter-notification.

You can find additional information, and submit a counter-notification on our [DMCA counter-notification contact page](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=19240239-47f0-41f8-b806-08bf6b64868b).
