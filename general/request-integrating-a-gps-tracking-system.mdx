---
title: "Request: Integrating a GPS Tracking System"
---

Currently, Wix does not have a GPS tracking feature or app.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you have an external GPS tracking system, you can try embedding it using a HTML iFrame element. Learn how to embed HTML in the [Wix Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget) and [Studio Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-an-html-iframe-element).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
