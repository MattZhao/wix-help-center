### Accessing your site's dashboard when signing in

If you only have one site in your Wix account, when you sign in, you are taken directly to your site's dashboard.

### Switching to another site dashboard in your account

You may have multiple sites in your Wix account. You can easily switch from one site's dashboard to another.

#### To switch to another site's dashboard:

Click the name of the current site you're working on at the top left of the dashboard.   Click the site you want to access.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Why can't I find my site in my account?

If you have a large number of sites in your account, you can use the search bar on the [Sites](https://manage.wix.com/account/sites) page to search for a specific site.
If you're still unable to find your site, it may be that you used a different email address when first signing up, or that you have more than 1 account. Using our [Account Email Locator](https://users.wix.com/account-recovery/enter-website), you can check which email address you used when creating your site.
Read more about [finding your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooter-unable-to-find-a-site-in-your-account).

### Why can't I see some sections in the site dashboard?

This is likely due to your role and permissions as a site collaborator. Contact the site owner to make changes to your permissions.

### Why can't I log into my account?

If you are having trouble logging in to your account, view [Logging into Your Wix Account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/logging-in-to-your-wix-account).

### Why can't I find what I'm looking for in the site dashboard?

In 2024, we made some changes to the dashboard menu to make it cleaner and more focused. We've also grouped similar items by action and intent rather than by business app. [Learn more about these changes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-new-dashboard-menu).
If you are still looking for something, you can use the search bar at the top of the dashboard to find what you need.

### Can I restore my site if I accidentally deleted it?

Yes, deleted sites are stored in the Trash folder on the [Sites](https://manage.wix.com/account/sites) page. Go to the Trash folder and find the site you want to restore. Learn more about [restoring sites from Trash](https://support.wix.com/en/article/moving-a-site-to-trash#restoring-a-site-from-trash).
