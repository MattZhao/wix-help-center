---
title: "Allowing Visitors to Change their Settings on Your Cookie Banner"
---

Allow your visitors to update their cookie preferences at any time by adding a Cookie Settings Widget to all the pages on your site. Once clicked, the widget expands to display the [cookie banner](https://support.wix.com/en/article/displaying-a-cookie-banner-on-your-site) settings. This allows visitors to view or change their cookie preferences whenever they wish to.

### To add a Cookie Settings Widget to your site:

Go to [Privacy & Cookies](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Privacy%20Center&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/cookie-consent-settings-app?referralInfo=article_displaying-a-cookie-banner) in your site's settings.  Click **Get Started** or, if you already have a cookie banner enabled, **Edit Cookie Banner.**  Enable the **Display Cookie Banner on Site **toggle.  Select the **Add cookie settings widget to all pages **checkbox**.**  (Optional) Customize your Settings Widget text.  (Optional) Customize your Settings Widget position.
