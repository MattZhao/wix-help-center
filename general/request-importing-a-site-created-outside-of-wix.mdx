---
title: "Request: Importing a Site Created Outside of Wix"
---

Currently, importing a site created outside of Wix is not supported.

> **Moving from WordPress to Wix?**
>
> You can import posts from your WordPress blog to Wix. Whether your posts are on a WordPress.com or a WordPress.org site, you can transfer them in just a few clicks. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-importing-blog-posts-from-wordpress-to-the-wix-blog)

> Learn more about [Wix vs Wordpress](https://www.wix.com/wix-vs-wordpress).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
