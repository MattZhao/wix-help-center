---
title: "Purchasing a 2 or 3 Year Plan"
---

Our multi-year [Premium plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) and [Studio plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) offer great savings over the yearly or monthly subscriptions.

If you purchase a multi-year subscription, you are charged for the entire subscription period. To prevent service disruption, your plan will renew automatically and you will be charged at the end of your initial subscription period. For example, if you purchase a 2 year subscription, you will be charged every 2 years to cover the entire 2 year period.

> **Important:**
>
> 2 year and 3 year plan subscriptions are not available in all regions.

---

### Purchasing a 2 or 3 year plan

[Go to the Upgrade page](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).  Click **Select** under the plan you wish to purchase.  Select **2 Years **or** 3 Years **and click **Continue to Checkout**.

Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Note:**
>
> After submitting your purchase, your bank may require you to [verify your identity](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds). If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about 2 or 3 year plans.

### How do I renew my 2 year or 3 year plan?

Upgrade plans are set to renew automatically so that your site never experiences downtime. You don't need to do anything to renew your plan, but you can [turn off the auto-renewal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/turning-off-the-auto-renewal-for-a-wix-premium-plan) if you don't want your plan to automatically renew.

**Note:** If you're turning auto-renew off, make sure to do this after the first 14-day money back period. Otherwise your plan is cancelled immediately.

### Do multi-year plans come with a domain and business email?

No, upgrade plans do not include domains or business emails. These can be purchased separately. However, some of our longer-term subscriptions do [include a voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-free-domain-voucher) for a 1 year free domain subscription.

### How do I upgrade multiple sites in my account?

Site upgrades are only valid for one site. To upgrade multiple sites in your Wix account, you can purchase multiple plans.
