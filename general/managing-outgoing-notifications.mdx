The Outgoing Notifications page helps you keep track of all the ways you send notifications to your customers. Turn notifications on or off, edit your automated notifications, and view stats to assess the effectiveness of your communications.

### Viewing your notifications

View all your active and inactive notifications, across all apps, on the Outgoing Notifications page. The notifications are divided by the Wix apps you have installed, for more precise control.

In addition, you can see which notifications you've created (using an automation), and those that were installed for you.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also see a summary of every notification, including the total times triggered and the last trigger date. This can help you check if the automation is working and how effective it is. For example, if the automation was triggered 0 times, it might not be working correctly.

#### To view your outgoing notifications:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Outgoing notifications**.

### Managing outgoing notifications

Manage and edit notifications sent from your site to your customers (e.g. emails and mobile push notifications). You can activate or deactivate a notification, view its activity summary, and access the [automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-managing-your-automations) to make any necessary changes.

> **Note:**
>
> Outgoing notifications are managed through [Wix Automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-getting-started). You can view and [manage all your automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-managing-your-automations) from the Automations page in your site's dashboard.

#### To manage outgoing notifications:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Outgoing notifications**.  Hover over the relevant notification and click **View Summary**.  Choose what to do next:

> To return to the Outgoing Notifications page at any point, click the **Back** arrow.

### Edit a notification

Make changes to the notification's flow to ensure the [automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation) works for your purposes.
1. Click **More Actions**.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Edit the automation as necessary.
4. When you're finished editing, click **Save**.

**Note:** Editing the timing of an automation [can affect "pending" actions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-troubleshooting-wix-automations#automations-not-functioning-as-expected). "Pending" actions are ones that have been triggered, but haven't yet taken place (the timing has been set for them to occur after a certain period of time).

### Duplicate a notification

Save time by duplicating an existing notification and make any edits needed. For example, you can use the same email campaign design to respond to different triggers.
1. Click **More Actions**.
2. Click **Duplicate**.
3. Edit the relevant details within your duplicated automation.

**Tip:** Don't forget to update your duplicated notification's name, or it will remain 'Copy of...'.
4. When ready, click **Activate**.

### Rename a notification

For automations you've created, rename the notification so you and your site collaborators can easily identify it.
1. Click **More Actions**.
2. Click **Rename**.
3. Edit the notification's name.
4. Click **Save**.

### View email automation stats

See how your email notifications are doing with [statistics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-your-email-marketing-statistics) like delivery rate and the number of clicks. These stats are available for any automation you've created, or pre-installed ones that you modified.
1. Click **More Actions**.
2. Click **View email stats**.
3. (In the pop-up) Scroll down to see the statistics.
**Note:** Your email statistics are made up of unique recipients. If an email is triggered for the same contact more than once, it counts as only one email.

### Deactivate a notification

Disable the **Active** toggle to deactivate a notification. This will stop the notification from getting sent without deleting it. In addition, trigger data will no longer be collected. **Tip: **You can reactivate the notification at any time by clicking the toggle again.
[image: A screenshot of a notification's settings in the outgoing notifications page.]

### Delete a notification

Deleting a notification will remove it from the page and you won't be able to restore it.
1. Click **More Actions**.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Click **Delete** in the pop-up to confirm.

**Notes:**
- If you wish to use this notification in the future, consider deactivating it rather than deleting it.
- Notifications marked as "Installed for you" can be deactivated but not deleted.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about notifications.

### Why aren't my notifications getting sent?

There are several reasons why your notifications may not get sent. It could be an issue with the way the automation is set up or a local issue such as with your internet connection. [Follow this troubleshooting guide to resolve the issue](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-troubleshooting-wix-automations).

### Where can I manage the notifications I receive?

You can manage the notifications you receive on your desktop (web browser), dashboard, and email under **Notification Preferences **in your site's dashboard.
You can do the same for notifications on your mobile device through the Wix app or the Wix Studio app. [Learn more about managing your site's notification preferences](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-sites-notification-settings).

### Do "Installed for you" automations count towards my monthly email limit?

No, "Installed for you" automations are not deducted from your monthly email balance.
You can monitor your quotas on your [Automations](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Automations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/triggers/) page to keep track of usage and know when it's time to upgrade. Certain automation actions, like "Send an email," count toward your site's [email marketing plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-understanding-your-monthly-balance) quotas.
