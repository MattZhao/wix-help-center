### FAQs

As you're embarking on your journey with Wix, you probably have a few questions. Check out these FAQs to get started:

### How many free websites can I create on Wix?

You can create as many free websites as you want on Wix. There is no obligation to upgrade unless you want to make use of our premium features. Learn more about [upgrading and what plans we offer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan).

### Can I use AI to build a website on Wix?

Yes, you can use AI to build your site on Wix. With Wix's AI tools, you can create a personalized website by answering a few questions about your preferences and needs. The AI will generate a custom design that you can further customize using the Wix Editor. Wix has a number of additional AI tools available. Learn more about [Wix's AI tools](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wixs-ai-tools).

### Can I change the template for my site after it’s created?

No, you can't change the template once the site is created, but you can continue to edit it to make any adjustments that you need. You can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-request-switching-an-existing-sites-template-with-content). 
You're always welcome to start over with a new template. If you upgraded your site, you can easily move your domain and plan over to your new site. Learn more about [getting a new template for your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-a-new-template-for-your-site).

### Can I connect a domain to a free Wix site?

No, connecting a custom domain requires [upgrading to a plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan). However, after you publish your free site, it will be available online with the Wix free URL in the format: siteprefix.wixsite.com/siteaddress

### I am a designer, can I use Wix Studio?

If you are a designer, we recommend that you use [Wix Studio](https://www.wix.com/studio) which offers a seamless solution to elevate your projects. Wix Studio is perfect for anyone who manages multiple sites or works with various teams and agencies.
As with Wix, Wix Studio is also free, you only need to pay to upgrade the sites you want to be premium.
