---
title: "Security of Wix Sign Up Services"
---

The security of our users' accounts is of extreme importance to us here at Wix and we are 100% committed to online safety.

Wix's sign up and login services are completed through a secure server. In addition, Wix does not keep users' passwords in its database, but rather encrypts them in a one-way encryption method. This means that, even in the event of a database breach, the passwords cannot be restored by any means.

For advice on securing your account please [see our password recommendations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-secure-password-and-keeping-your-wix-account-secure).
