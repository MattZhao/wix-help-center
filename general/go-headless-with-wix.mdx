Wix Headless enables you to take advantage of Wix's business management platform from any app or site you create, on any platform.

Build with any tech stack across multiple frontends. Leverage the proven power of Wix's business solutions to build what you want, the way you want to build it.

> Learn more about [using Wix Headless](https://dev.wix.com/docs/go-headless).

---

### About headless

Wix's business platform provides advanced functionality for managing and operating a business. The business management platform offers solutions for managing [bookings](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/api-reference/bookings/introduction), [events](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/api-reference/events/introduction), [eCommerce](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/api-reference/ecom/setup), [loyalty programs]((https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/api-reference/loyalty/setup)), [CMS](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/api-reference/data/introduction), and [more](https://dev.wix.com/docs/go-headless/getting-started/about-headless/wix-headless-business-solutions). Wix Headless makes this platform available independently and flexibly, with or without a Wix site.

With Wix Headless, you can use APIs to access and manage business data in Wix's platform, giving you the flexibility to implement sites or applications on the platforms of your choice.

---

### Key benefits

Here are some of the key benefits of using Wix Headless:

**One-stop shop**: All the services you need in one place.
**Build your way**: Use your preferred tech stack to build your frontend and backend. Use Wix's business solutions to take care of the business logic. Wix's services are available as [REST APIs](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest) and using our [JavaScript SDK](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk).
**Flexible**: Maximize the ways customers can interact with your business, by connecting multiple clients to a single Wix project. For example, multiple websites, a mobile app, and a smartwatch app can all share the same business data.
**Secure**: Use industry-standard OAuth 2.0 for authorizing API calls. Enjoy Wix's enterprise-grade security for peace of mind.
**Admin API**: Utilize account and site-level APIs to build dashboards and other admin features.
