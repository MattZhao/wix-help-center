### Accessing the Avalara dashboard for the first time

To log in for the first time after you [started calculating tax rates with Avalara](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-automatic-tax-calculation), you must set up a password according to the instructions below.

#### To log in for the first time:

Click [this link](https://identity.avalara.com/forgotpassword) to set your Avalara password.  Enter the email you used to sign up with Avalara.  Click **Request reset**.  Click the link in the email you receive.   Create a new password.  Click **Save**. ** **

---

### Accessing the Avalara dashboard subsequent times

If you've already reset your Avalara password and logged in once, you can click [here](https://identity.avalara.com/account/login) and log in using your username (usually your email address) and your password.
