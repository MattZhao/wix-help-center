---
title: "Enabling Cookies in Your Browser"
---

If your browser is set to block third-party cookies, you may have trouble logging in to your Wix account. Enable cookies to make sure you can log in successfully.

> **Supported Browsers:**
>
> We recommend using the most current version of a [browser supported by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers). Go to [www.whatismybrowser.com](http://www.whatismybrowser.com) to make sure your browser is up to date.

### Select your browser below to learn how to enable cookies:

### Google Chrome

1. Click the **More** icon  at the top right of the browser.
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Click **Privacy and security**.
4. Click **Cookies and other site data**.
5. Select **Allow all cookies**.
**Note:** 
- Make sure **Block third-party cookies** is disabled if you're using [Database Collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-creating-a-collection).
- You can learn more in [Google's article about managing cookies in Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647).

### Safari

1. Click** Safari **in the menu bar and select **Preferences**.
2. Click **Privacy**.
3. Uncheck the box next to **Block all cookies**.

**Note:** Make sure the version of Safari you're using is supported by Wix. [View Supported Browsers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers)

### Mozilla Firefox

1. Click the **Menu** icon  at the top right of your browser.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Select the **Privacy & Security** panel on the left.
4. Select **Custom** under **Enhanced Tracking Protection **and make sure **Cookies** is unchecked.

### Microsoft Edge

1. Click the **More** icon  at the top right and select **Settings**.
2. Scroll down and select **View advanced settings**.
3. Click the drop-down under **Cookies **and select **Don't block cookies**.

**Note:** Make sure the version of Edge you're using is supported by Wix. [View Supported Browsers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers)

### Internet Explorer 11

1. Click the **Settings** icon at the top right and select **Internet options**.
2. Click the **Privacy** tab.
3. Click the **Advanced** button.
4. Click **Accept** under **First-party cookies**.
5. Click **Accept** under **Third-party cookies**.
6. Click **OK**.

**Note:** Wix no longer supports editing or viewing live sites on IE11. We recommend using an updated browser such as Chrome to view and edit your site. Learn more about [Wix's compatibility with Internet Explorer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-compatibility-with-internet-explorer).
