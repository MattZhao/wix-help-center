---
title: "Request: Creating Multiple Test Sites"
---

Currently, you can only have one test site in your Release Manager. If you want to create another test site, you must first [discard the previous one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/discarding-a-test-site).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
