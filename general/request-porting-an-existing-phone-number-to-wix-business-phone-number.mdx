---
title: "Request: Porting an Existing Phone Number to Wix Business Phone Number"
---

Currently, it is not possible to port an existing phone number to your [Business Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-started-9797633). This means you must purchase a local / toll-free number from Wix in order to use the Business Phone Number tools.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
