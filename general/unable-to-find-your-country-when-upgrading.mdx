---
title: "Unable to Find Your Country When Upgrading"
---

If you are unable to find your country in the drop-down menu when upgrading your site, it is possible that your country is listed as a region of another country.

**For example:** If you are trying to select Curaçao when upgrading your site, you must first select the Netherlands as the country, and then select Curaçao as the state.

If you are still unable to find your country, please [contact us](http://www.wix.com/contact) and we will be happy to assist you.
