---
title: "Confirming Your Email With Wix"
---

Keep your contact details up to date and confirm your email address. This helps us ensure that we're sending updates and notifications to the right person. As a new user, you receive a confirmation email request automatically.

### To confirm your email:

[Go to Account Settings](https://manage.wix.com/account/account-settings) in your Wix account.  Click** Confirm Your Email **under **Login Info**.

  Go to your email inbox and open the confirmation email. This email is sent from: **wix-team@notifications.wix.com**.   Click **Confirm Email**. Make sure you confirm your email within 24 hours after receiving the email verification email, or you will receive a Wix account notification.

> **Note:**
>
> If you don't see the email in your inbox check your junk or spam.

> **Tip:**
>
> Want to change your account email address? [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-email-address-of-your-wix-account)
