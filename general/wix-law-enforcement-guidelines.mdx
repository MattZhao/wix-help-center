---
title: "Wix Law Enforcement Guidelines"
---

Wix.com is committed to fostering a safe online environment while facilitating lawful access for law enforcement agencies.

Authorities seeking information or cooperation with Wix are required to follow our [Law Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.wix.com/about/law-enforcement-requ). These guidelines outline the procedures for authorities to contact us and how we can respond to such requests.

We ask that all court orders or subpoenas be sent to [subpoenas@wix.com](mailto:subpoenas@wix.com).

For any additional questions regarding law enforcement, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact/en) and we'll be happy to assist.
