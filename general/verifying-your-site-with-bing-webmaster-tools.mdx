---
title: "Verifying Your Site with Bing Webmaster Tools"
---

Verifying ownership of your domain on search engines helps to improve your presence in search results. You can verify your site with Bing Webmaster Tools by adding a meta tag to your Wix site.

---

### Step 1 | Get your meta tag from Bing Webmaster Tools

First, you need to get a meta tag for your site from your Bing Webmaster Tools account.

#### To get your meta tag:

Sign in to [your Bing Webmaster Tools account](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/about).   Enter your site's URL under **Add your site manually**.

Click **Add**.   Copy the meta tag under **HTML Meta Tag.**

**Important: **The code in the image below is a sample code. To get your code, you must sign in to your own Bing account.

Leave Bing Webmaster Tools open.

---

### Step 2 | Add your meta tag to your Wix site

Next, you need to add the meta tag from Bing Webmaster Tools to your Wix site using the Site Verification tool.

#### To add your meta tag:

In a new tab, [go to your SEO Dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-f3388913-140f-47a9-b2ba-4bf86b60e04e&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO).  Select **Go to Site Verification **under **Tools and settings**.  Click **Bing Webmaster Tools**.  Enter the meta tag under **Site verification tag**.  Click **Save**.

---

### Step 3 | Complete the site verification on Bing Webmaster Tools

The final step is to use Bing Webmaster Tools to verify the meta tag that you've added to your site.

#### To verify your site:

Go back to Bing Webmaster Tools.  Click **Verify**.  Click **Done**.
