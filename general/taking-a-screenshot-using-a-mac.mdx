---
title: "Taking a Screenshot Using a Mac"
---

Communicate what you're seeing on your screen better by taking a screenshot.

### To take a screenshot using a Mac:

Press **Shift **+** Command **+ **4 **on your keyboard. Click and drag your cursor to select the area of your screen you wish to capture.Release your mouse or trackpad button.

> **Sending your screenshot to Wix Customer Care?**
>
> Learn how to [add a screenshot to a ticket](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-screenshot-to-a-ticket).
