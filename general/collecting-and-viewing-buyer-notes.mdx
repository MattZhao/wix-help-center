---
title: "Collecting and Viewing Buyer Notes"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New) orders.

Customers sometimes need to communicate with you about a purchase. They can easily leave notes when checking out by clicking **Add a note **in the Cart Page.

Buyer notes appear in the email notification you receive when a purchase is made. In addition, in the **Orders** tab in your dashboard, any order with a note has a note icon.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Viewing buyer notes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/collecting-and-viewing-buyer-notes#viewing-buyer-notes)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/collecting-and-viewing-buyer-notes#faqs)

---

### Viewing buyer notes

[Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant order.  Read the note under the customer's name on the right.

> **Tip:**
>
> Wix Stores merchants can also [add a message box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-allowing-customers-to-add-a-message-when-purchasing-a-product) to a specific product, for example, to ask customers to enter the text they want engraved.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about buyer notes.

### Can I change the 'Add a note' link text?

Yes, you can. Learn how to [change the "Add a note" link text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-links-on-your-cart-page#customizing-the-promo-code-and-add-a-note-links).

### Can I remove the 'Add a note' option?

You can remove the **Add a note** option completely by selecting the Cart Page in your editor, clicking **Settings**, and then selecting the **Settings **tab. There, you can deselect the **Add a note** checkbox.
