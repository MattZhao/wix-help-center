---
title: "Request: Multiple Business Phone Numbers Per Site"
---

Currently, you can have one [business phone number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-a-phone-number) per site. It is not possible to have multiple business phone numbers associated with one site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
