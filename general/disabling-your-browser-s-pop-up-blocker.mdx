---
title: "Disabling Your Browser's Pop-Up Blocker"
---

Pop-up windows are small browser windows that open on top of the main window, often used to display additional information or interactive content. They can also be used by websites to display advertisements, so most current browser versions have built-in pop-up blockers.

> **Learn how to disable pop-up blockers in the following browsers:**
>
> - [Google Chrome](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-the-google-chrome-pop-up-blocker)
> - [Firefox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-the-mozilla-firefox-pop-up-blocker-option)
> - [Safari](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker-in-safari)
> - [Microsoft Edge](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-the-pop-up-blocker-in-microsoft-edge)
