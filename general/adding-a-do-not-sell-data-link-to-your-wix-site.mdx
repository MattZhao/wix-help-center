---
title: "Adding a \"Do Not Sell Data\" Link to Your Wix Site"
---

According to privacy regulations like the [CCPA](https://support.wix.com/en/article/preparing-your-wix-site-for-ccpa), your visitors may have control over the sale of their personal information. They should have the option to refuse the "sale" (as defined in the applicable regulation) and transfer of their data to any third party.

Adding a **Do Not Sell My Personal Information** link, that visitors can click to disable data transfers, can help you comply with the CCPA regulation.

### To add a Do Not Sell Data link:

Go to [Privacy & Cookies](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Privacy%20Center&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/cookie-consent-settings-app?referralInfo=article_adding-a-do-not-sell-data-link-to-your-wix-site) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Add a 'Do Not Sell Data' Link **and click **Add Link in Editor**.  Click **Add to Site**.  Drag the link to the relevant position in your site.

**Tip: **We recommend keeping the link in the footer.  (Optional) Click **Settings **to style the link.  Publish your site.

> **Important:**
>
> We recommend adding your Do Not Sell Data link to your site's footer. By adding an element to your site's footer, it will appear on all your site pages. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-placing-elements-in-your-header-footer-and-page#moving-elements-to-the-footer).
