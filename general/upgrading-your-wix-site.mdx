### Upgrading your site

Upgrade your site at any time to access premium features and benefits.

#### To upgrade your site:

[Go to the Upgrade page](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).  Click **Select** under the plan you wish to purchase.  Select a billing cycle and click **Continue to Checkout**.

Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Note:**
>
> After submitting your purchase, your bank may require you to [verify your identity.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds) If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.

### FAQs

Click below to learn more about upgrading your Wix site.

### Will my domain become a custom domain if I upgrade?

Upgrading your site does not automatically change your domain to a custom domain.  Instead, you can either connect a domain you already own or purchase a new domain from Wix. Learn more about [connecting a domain to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-your-site-195136).

### Do I receive a business email if I upgrade with a plan?

Upgrade plans do not include a business email. You can purchase a professional email address as long as you have a domain in your Wix account. Learn more about [setting up a business email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-purchasing-a-personalized-business-email).

### If I purchase a plan, does it upgrade all of the sites in my Wix account?

No. Site upgrades are only valid for one site, so each site requires its own plan. You can upgrade multiple sites in your Wix account by purchasing multiple plans.
