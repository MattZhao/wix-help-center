title: "Managing Your Tasks & Reminders"
---

After creating a task you can easily edit its details, mark it as done or delete it from the list

### To manage your tasks & reminders:

Go to [Tasks & Reminders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Your%20Tasks&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Ftasks/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard. Hover over the relevant task and manage it by doing one of the following:

### Edit a task

You can update any detail necessary. Rephrase the task, assign it to someone else, link it to a different contact or change the due date. 

1. Click **Edit**.
2. Edit the following details:
- Assignee: 
1. Click the navigation arrow next to **Assigned to**.
2. Select a new assignee from the drop-down menu.
- Due Date:
1. Click the due date.
**Note:** Click **Due Date** if there's no existing due date.
2. Use the calendar to select a due date.
- Linked Contact:
1. Click the contact's name.
**Note:** Click **Link to Contact** if there's no existing linked contact.
2. Select a contact from the list.
3. Click **Save **at the bottom right corner of the task.

### Mark a task complete

Select the checkbox next to a task to check it off the list.

### Delete a task

Click the **Delete** icon ﻿﻿ to remove the task completely. This action cannot be undone.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can access your completed tasks at any time. Click **To-Do** at the top left and select **Completed** from the drop-down menu.
