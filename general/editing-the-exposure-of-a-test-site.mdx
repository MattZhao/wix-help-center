---
title: "Editing the Exposure of a Test Site"
---

Once you have created your test site, you can increase or decrease the exposure. 

The percentage exposure refers to the percentage of your site visitors that will see the test site when they access your live site. It includes desktop, mobile devices and all browsers over all geographical locations.

You can increase the exposure which means that more of your site visitors will see the test site, or decrease it, so that fewer site visitors will see the test site version.

### To edit the exposure of a test site:

Go to the [Release Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-the-release-manager).  Click the **Show More** iconon the test site.  Click **Edit exposure**.  Enter the exposure percentage as a value between 0-100.  Click **Save**.

> **Important:**
>
> Some features on your site may not be compatible with test sites, such as anything that contains data.
