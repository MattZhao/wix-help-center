---
title: "Available Languages in the Wix Help Center"
---

In the Wix Help Center, you can find hundreds of articles and detailed tutorials in the following languages: English, Dutch, French, German, Hebrew, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish and Ukrainian.

### To view the Wix Help Center in another language:

Go to the [Wix Help Center](https://support.wix.com/en/).   Click your profile at the top right and select the **Languages** icon  .

**Note:** If you're signed in to Wix, click your user profile at the top right, then click  **English**.

Select the relevant language.

> **Note:**
>
> Wix offers phone support in several languages. Learn more about [contacting Wix Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support).
