### What are hemp & hemp-derived products?

As a U.S. merchant, you must comply with federal and state law, as well as FDA and other applicable regulations, defining what hemp-derived CBD products you can legally sell.

> **Important:**
>
> The sale of hemp and hemp-derived CBD products is subject to a complex set of rules (on federal, state and local levels). You must make sure that the products you sell and the manner in which they are described, marketed and shipped is legal. We highly recommend consulting with an attorney before starting to sell these products.

#### Federal law

The U.S. federal law* defines Hemp as the following:

The Cannabis Sativa L. plant with no more than 0.3% THC concentration on a dry weight basis. This includes any part of that plant, including the seeds thereof and all derivatives, extracts, cannabinoids, isomers, acids, salts, and salts of isomers, whether growing or not.

*Agriculture Improvement Act of 2018 ("Farm Bill")

#### State law

In addition to the federal law, states in the U.S. have issued their own regulations or declarations regarding the sale, purchase, possession and consumption of hemp and CBD products. Certain states have no explicit law that either allows or prohibits the retail sales of such products.

These laws, declarations and policy statements are separate and unrelated to any laws governing medical use of cannabis and other THC-containing products. As a Wix user, it is your responsibility to be familiar with the regulations relevant to your business.

Wix **does not** support the sale or purchase of hemp and hemp-derived CBD products in the following U.S. states:

HawaiiIdahoIowaMississippiNew HampshireSouth Dakota

This means that your business cannot be located in the above states, nor can you ship hemp or hemp-derived CBD products to customers residing there.

#### FDA (Food and Drug Administration) regulations

In addition to state and federal law, the FDA also governs the sale of hemp and hemp-derived products. We recommend checking [the FDA's website](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/public-health-focus/fda-regulation-cannabis-and-cannabis-derived-products-including-cannabidiol-cbd) on a regular basis for more instructions on selling these products, as well as cosmetics and supplements.

Every Wix site owner interested in selling hemp and hemp-derived products in the U.S. must comply with the following requirements:

Your business must be managed in the U.S., from a [state where Wix supports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/selling-cbd-on-your-wix-site#state-law) the sale of these products. ### FAQs

Take a look below to find the answers to our most frequently asked questions on selling hemp and hemp-derived products.

---

**What does the term "Hemp" mean?**

Under federal law, constituents derived from hemp means the plant *Cannabis sativa L.* and any part of that plant, including the seeds thereof and all derivatives, extracts, cannabinoids, isomers, acids, salts, and salts of isomers, whether growing or not, with a delta-9 tetrahydrocannabinol concentration of not more than 0.3 percent on a dry weight basis.

---

**Can I sell CBD products derived from marijuana?**

No. As stated in our Declaration for Selling Hemp and CBD Products, you are only allowed to sell **hemp**-derived CBD products. These products cannot contain more than 0.3 percent of delta-9 tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) concentration (on a dry weight basis). Additionally, they must not contain any Controlled Substances as defined in Schedule IV of Title 32 United States Code Controlled Substances Act.

---

**Can I sell hemp and/or hemp-derived CBD products in my store while also selling other kinds of products?**

Yes. You can use the same Wix site to sell hemp and/or hemp-derived CBD products alongside other products of any kind, as long as your chosen payment provider allows it in their terms and conditions. **My business is located outside of the United States. Can I sell hemp and/or hemp-derived CBD products using Wix?**

Wix's solution is designated for users selling hemp and hemp-derived products in the [U.S. states that we support](https://support.wix.com/en/article/selling-cbd-on-your-wix-site#state-law).

As with any other product you want to sell on Wix, you should check local regulations before actually adding them to your site. If you're located outside of the U.S. and would like to start selling hemp and hemp-derived products, we highly recommend consulting with an attorney to find out what regulations apply to you.

Additionally, you must verify with your [payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview) that they're able to process payments for hemp and hemp-derived products.

---

**Are Wix site owners responsible for knowing the regulatory aspects of selling hemp and hemp-derived CBD products? If the law changes, will Wix notify its users? **

Like any other Wix user, you are responsible for your site's content and the products you sell. When using Wix to sell hemp and/or hemp-derived CBD products, you are responsible for understanding the federal, state and local regulations that apply to them.

As U.S. laws regarding hemp and CBD products continue to develop, we recommend reviewing them on a regular basis, as well as the FDA's regulation of Cannabis and Cannabis-derived products ([Learn More](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/public-health-focus/fda-regulation-cannabis-and-cannabis-derived-products-questions-and-answers)).

Wix will not notify users of changes made to these laws. We highly recommend consulting with an attorney before selling these products on your site.

You can use any carrier that can legally deliver help and/or hemp-derived CBD products. We recommend contacting the relevant shipping service to check this in advance.

**I'm currently using Wix Payments to process my payments. What happens if I decide to start selling CBD using Wix?**

Wix Payments cannot process any transactions related to hemp and/or hemp-derived CBD products. For this reason, we'll disconnect and close your Wix Payments account once you [submit the declaration form](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=&buttonText=Select%20Site&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2Fcbd-attestation-form%2F%3FreferralInfo%3Dcbd-kb-article).

Any credit remaining in your Wix Payments account will be paid out according to your current payout cycle, as long as our compliance team can confirm that these funds aren't associated to the sale of [restricted or prohibited products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-prohibited-products-and-services).

If your Wix payments account was closed due to the sale of [restricted or prohibited goods / services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-prohibited-products-and-services), any credit remaining in your account has been or may be refunded to the credit card owner (your customer).
