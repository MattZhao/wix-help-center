---
title: "Request: Automatic Privacy Policy Generator"
---

Currently, Wix does not have an automatic privacy policy generator.

Take a look at our recommendations on [creating a privacy policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-privacy-policy). Keep mind that Wix cannot provide you with legal advice on how to write a privacy policy. We recommend that you seek legal advice to assist you.

> **Tip:**
>
> Users in Germany can enable a 3rd party app, [Legal Texts by Trusted Shops](https://www.wix.com/app-market/legal-texts-by-trusted-shops), to help them generate a privacy policy. This app is available from the [Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market/?referral=menu_navigation).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
