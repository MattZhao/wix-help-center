---

Organize and prioritize your work. Create tasks, set due dates and [receive notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-sites-notification-settings) when tasks are due. You can assign tasks to yourself or anyone on your team, and link them to your contacts. When you're done, mark the task as complete.

### How do I access Tasks & Reminders?

1. Go to your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Customer Management**.
3. Click **Tasks & Reminders**.

#### Learn how to:

[Create a task](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-tasks-reminders#create-a-task-1)[Link a task to your contact](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-tasks-reminders#link-tasks-to-your-contacts)[View and manage your tasks from your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-tasks-reminders#view-manage-your-tasks)[Get notified when tasks are due](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-tasks-reminders#get-notified-when-a-task-is-due)

### Create a Task

Add new tasks to your board in a few clicks. Simply click **New Task** and describe what needs to be done - any other detail is optional. Make sure your task gets done by setting a due date and assigning it to yourself or the relevant person on your team.

> **Create tasks automatically:**
>
> Set up automations that create tasks for you in response to a trigger you choose. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-tasks-instantly-with-wix-automations)

### Show me how

1. Go to [Tasks & Reminders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Your%20Tasks&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Ftasks/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click** + New Task** at the top right.
3. Write your task in the text box.
4. (Optional) Add the following details:
- **Due Date: **Click **Due Date **and use the calendar to select the date.
- **Contact: **Click **Link to Contact** and select the relevant contact from the drop-down menu.
- **Assignee: **Click **Assign to Contributor **to assign the task to yourself or a teammate.
5. Click **Done **to save your task.

### Link Tasks to Your Contacts

Is the task at hand related to a specific contact? While creating the task, link the relevant contact so that it appears in their Contact Card. From here, you can edit the task at any time or mark it complete when you're done.

### View & Manage Your Tasks

Go to [Tasks & Reminders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Your%20Tasks&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Ftasks/?referralInfo=KB_Article) to see a list of all your pending tasks, assigned to you or anyone on your team. From here you can edit your tasks, delete those you don't need and check completed tasks off the list.

### Show me how

1. Go to [Tasks & Reminders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Your%20Tasks&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Ftasks/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the relevant task and do one of the following:
- Select the checkbox next to the task to mark it complete.
- Click **Edit** to update the task's details.
- Click the **Delete** icon  to remove the task.

[Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-tasks-reminders)

### Get Notified When a Task is Due

Enable notifications for overdue tasks to stay on top of your work at all times. From your site's Settings, set a notification to appear on your dashboard whenever a task is due. You can also get the notification on your web browser, even when you're not on Wix.com.

> **Note:**
>
> Changes made to notification settings only apply to the site you're working on, and don't affect other site contributors.

### Show me how

1. Go to [Notification Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Notifications&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/notifications) in your site's dashboard.
2. Check that the **Enable Notifications for this Site** toggle is enabled .
3. Scroll down to** Tasks & Reminders**.
4. Click the toggle next to **Task is due** to enable the notification.
5. (Optional) Click **Turn On **next to **Enable Desktop Notifications **to get notified even when you're not on Wix.com.
