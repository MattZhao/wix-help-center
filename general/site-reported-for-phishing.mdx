Web Forgery, also known as phishing, is a form of identity theft that occurs when a malicious website impersonates a legitimate one in order to trick visitors into giving up sensitive information such as passwords, account details, or credit card numbers.

If you feel that your site was reported in error, you can submit a request to be removed from the list of phishing sites by clicking the link below.
