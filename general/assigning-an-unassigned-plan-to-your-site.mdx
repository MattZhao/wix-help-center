---
title: "Assigning an Unassigned Plan to Your Site"
---

When you upgrade a site, the plan you purchase is assigned to that specific site. However, it is possible to [unassign your plan from your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unassigning-a-premium-plan-from-a-site). You can reassign the plan to a different site in your Wix account at any time.

> **Working with Wix Studio?**
>
> You can transfer a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) to a site created with Wix Studio by upgrading to a [Wix Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites). Learn more about [assigning a Premium plan to a Studio site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-assigning-a-wix-premium-plan-to-a-wix-studio-site).

### To assign an unassigned plan:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://manage.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the unassigned plan.   Click **Assign to a Site**.

Select the site you want to assign the plan to and click **Next**.  Click **Assign.**
