---
title: "Request: Creating a Rental Site with Wix"
---

Currently, Wix does not have a designated solution for rental services. If you offer house rentals, you can manage your reservations using [Wix Hotels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-wix-hotels).

Alternatively, use [Velo by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-velo-by-wix) to add any custom features your rental service requires, or check out [Twice Rentals](https://www.wix.com/app-market/twice-rentals?searchLocation=home) a third-party solution designed to add rentable products to your website in a few clicks.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
