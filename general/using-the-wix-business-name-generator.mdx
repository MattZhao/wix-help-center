### Generating a business name

Enter some keywords and the business industry to get ideas for your business' name

#### To generate a business name:

Go to the [Wix Business Name Generator](https://www.wix.com/tools/business-name-generator).   Enter a word or two that you want to include in the name.  Click **Generate Names**.  Browse the list of generated names for ones that suit your business.   (Optional) Refine the suggestions:

1. Add / edit the **Keywords** and select an **Industry** at the top.
2. Click **Generate New Names**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn what to do next.

### How do I get a site branded with my new business name?

Once you have your business name, click **Get Free Site** to start working on a Wix site already branded with your new name.

### How can I get a logo for my new business name?

After you have chosen your business name, click **See More Logos** to quickly design a logo for your business.

### Can I get a domain to match my new business name?

Certainly! Once you have your business name, click any domain suggested at the top to check its availability, and you can then register the domain with Wix.Learn more about [purchasing a domain from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977).
