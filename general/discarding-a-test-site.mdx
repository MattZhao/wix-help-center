---
title: "Discarding a Test Site"
---

You can discard a current test site if you don't need it anymore. Once discarded, you can no longer publish that version, and you will need to create a new one.

### To discard a test site:

Go to the [Release Manager.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-the-release-manager)  Click the **Show More** iconon the relevant test site.  Click **Discard**.  Click **Discard** in the pop-up window.
