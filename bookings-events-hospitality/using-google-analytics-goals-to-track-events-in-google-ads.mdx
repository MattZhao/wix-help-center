With Google Ads conversion tracking, you can easily measure the effectiveness of your ad campaigns. Google Analytics will tell you which of your ad clicks lead to purchases, downloads, newsletter sign-ups, or any other goals you set.

> **Note:**
>
> When setting up goals it's important to use the same Google login for the Analytics and Ads accounts.

Wix recommends importing your Google Analytics goals to your Google Ads account. [Learn More](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1034306?hl=en)

---

### Create Goals in Your Google Analytics Account

Create goals to set what you want to track. You can create up to 20 goals in one view.

Make sure to select “Event” type. The goals are based on tracking events that Wix reports to Google Analytics and other tracking tools. [Learn More](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1032415)

### Show me how

1. Sign in to [Google Analytics](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/).
2. Click **Admin**.
3. Under the **View** column click **Goals**.
4. Click **New Goal**.
5. Select **Custom**.
6. Click **Continue**.
7. Enter a **Goal Description** based on the appropriate category below, e.g. Enhanced Ecommerce - Bookings.
8. Select a goal type to be **Event**.
9. Click **Continue**. 

**Note:** You need to define at least one of the conditions but we recommend setting at least two conditions in case you use more than one Wix application. 

- You can create a Goal for each Event Action from the table under Reported Events - Ticketing in this article. You can use "Purchase", "Product Click", "View Content" and so on. [Learn More](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1032415)
10. Make sure the **Use the Event value as the Goal Value for the conversion** is set to **Yes**.
11. Click **Save**.
