---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Adding Tips or Extra Fees When Collecting Payment from Clients"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [add tips when collecting payment from your clients](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-new-wix-tips-app). Collect tips from any bookings dashboard where you can collect payment, including the booking list, booking calendar and the Orders dashboard.

You can also [add extra fees directly from your Orders dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editing-orders-8769551). For example, add an extra processing fee for online payments or a last-minute booking fee for appointments scheduled on short notice.
