---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Displaying Your Health Waiver in the Member Apps"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display your [health waiver](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-setting-up-a-waiver) on the Wix mobile apps (Spaces, Fit by Wix, Branded app and Dine).

Instead, you can [add a checkbox field to the Booking Form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-setting-up-your-booking-forms), make it mandatory and assign a hyperlink to it, that redirects to your health waiver.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
