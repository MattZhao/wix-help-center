---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Viewing Weekly Calendar on the Wix App"
---

Currently, you can [view upcoming sessions by schedule, day, or a 3-day view](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-viewing-your-upcoming-sessions-in-the-wix-owner-app) in the Booking Calendar of the Wix app. However, it is not possible to view the schedule on a weekly basis.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
