---
title: "Wix Events Request: Importing Event Details from Previous Events"
---

Currently, you can [duplicate](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-your-events-in-wix-events#viewing-event-drafts) an entire event to copy its details to new events. You can also copy and paste specific details such as [policies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-adding-a-policy-checkbox) and [registration messages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-customizing-registration-settings-messages#editing-the-thank-you-registration-closed-messages).

However, it is not possible to import all the details from previous events in one action.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
