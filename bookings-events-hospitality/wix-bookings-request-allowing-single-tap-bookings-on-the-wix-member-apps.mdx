---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Allowing Single Tap Bookings on the Wix Member Apps"
---

We are excited to announce that it is now possible for clients to book a class session in a single tap using their Wix Member apps (Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix, Branded App by Wix).

To enable single tap bookings, select the **Skip this form when clients book on a Wix mobile members app** in your Booking preferences. Learn more about [customizing your class with Booking preferences](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-availability-for-classes-in-wix-bookings#step-6-customize-your-class-with-booking-preferences).
