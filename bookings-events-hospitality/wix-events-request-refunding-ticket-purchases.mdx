---
title: "Wix Events Request: Refunding Ticket Purchases"
---

Currently, it is not possible to refund ticket purchases for your guests in Wix Events. Instead, the refund must be processed directly through the payment gateway used for the purchase (e.g. PayPal, Stripe) and [the guest must be manually removed from the guest list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-managing-your-guest-list).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
