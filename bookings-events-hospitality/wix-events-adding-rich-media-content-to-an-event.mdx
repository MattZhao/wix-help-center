title: "Wix Events: Adding Rich Media Content to an Event"
---

Make your Event Details Page stand out with a variety of rich media options—what you include is up to you. Start with an AI-refined event description for clarity, then boost your page further by adding galleries, videos, and files to capture attention.

Additionally, connect your social media accounts, like Instagram and TikTok, to introduce interactive content that keeps guests engaged.

---

### Adding rich media to your event details

When creating or editing an event, scroll down to the 'About the event (optional)' field to add rich content.

#### To add rich media:

[Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/events/) in your site's dashboard.  Access the relevant event:

- **New event:** Create a new event.

1. Click **+ Add Event**.
2. Select a type of event (this can't be changed after you click **Create Draft**):

- [**Ticketed Event**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-a-single-event): Sell tickets for your event. This can include both paid and free tickets.
- [**RSVP Event**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-customizing-the-registration-form-for-rsvp-events): Guests to register for the event without purchasing tickets.
- **Existing events:** Edit an existing event.

1. Click** Manage **next to the relevant existing event.
2. Click **Edit Details **under the **Event details** section.
    Scroll down to **About the event (optional) **and use the available options:

### Create a description with AI

Use the AI tool If you're undecided how to describe an event. A "Content AI" prompt will appear where you can describe the text you want or tweak what you've already written.
1. Click **Create with AI**.
2. (In the pop-up) Type what you want AI to create or refine and press **Enter** / **Return** on your keyboard.
3. Choose the relevant option:

- **Insert:** Adds text to the 'About the event (optional)' section.
- **Rephrase:** Improves the text for clarity or style.
- Click the **More Actions** icon  for additional options (e.g. copying the text, adjusting the tone to professional or casual.).

### Add an image

Upload images that visually represent your event.
1. Click **Add Image**.
2. (In the Media Manager) Select an image and click **Add to Page**.
3. Customize your image using a variety of options:

- **Size **** / Alignment ****:** Adjust the size and positioning of the image.
- **Link to a web address or section ****: **Make the image clickable and direct users to a specific webpage or a part of your post.
- **Settings ****:** Add a caption for context, provide alt text for accessibility, enable or disable image downloads, and mark as a spoiler to blur content until clicked.

[image: A screenshot of the available image settings in the About the event (optional) section of an event.]

### Add a gallery

Adding a gallery lets you showcase a collection of photos, enriching your event with an appealing display.
1. Click **Add Gallery**.
2. (In the Media Manager) Select the images for your gallery and click **Add to Page**.

**Tip:** You can hold the **Ctrl** / **Command** key on your keyboard to select multiple images.
3. Manage your media with a range of options:

- **Add media ****:** Click **Manage Media** to add more images.
- **Edit an image:** Click **Manage Media **and select the relevant image. Then click** Edit **and adjust what you want (e.g. add a caption for context and provide alt text for accessibility).
- **Customize the layout:** 

1. Click **Manage Media**.
2. Click the **Layout** tab.
3. Choose a gallery layout, set image orientation, and adjust the spacing between images.
- **Settings:** 

1. Click **Manage Media**.
2. Click the **Settings** tab.
3. Click the toggle(s) to enable or disable image downloads and mark images as spoilers.
- **Size **** / Alignment ****:** Adjust the size and positioning of the gallery.

[image: A screenshot of the available gallery settings in the About the event (optional) section of an event.]

### Add a video

Adding a video offers an engaging way to share event details and provide a dynamic preview of what guests can expect.
1. Click **Add Video**.
2. Choose where to add the video from:

- **Embed:** Paste a URL from YouTube, Vimeo or Facebook.
- **Upload:** Upload a video from your computer or device.
3. (Optional) Adjust the video settings: 

- **Size **** / Alignment ****:** Adjust the size and positioning of the image.
- **Settings ****: **Click the toggle(s) to enable or disable video download and mark video as spoilers. Then click **Save**.

[image: A screenshot of the available video settings in the About the event (optional) section of an event.]

### Add a file

Click** Add File** to attach files that guests might find useful, such as event schedules, brochures, or additional information.

### Link to a post / video on social media

Add links to Instagram posts, TikTok videos, or X (Twitter) posts to boost your event page with compelling social media content.
1. Click the relevant social media icon at the bottom (e.g. the **TikTok**  icon).
2. Enter the link to the relevant post or video.
3. Click **Embed**.

### FAQs

Click below for answers to common questions about adding rich media content to events.

### Can I use dynamic pages to create my own custom Events page?

Yes, with the [Wix CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview) you can use dynamic pages to create your own custom Events page. Dynamic pages allow you to design a unique layout for your event details, which automatically updates based on the data from your CMS collections. This allows you to create a unique and consistent look across all your event pages. 
Learn more about:
- [Using a dynamic item page as your Event Details & Registration page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-creating-a-custom-event-details-page)
- [Displaying all your events in one dynamic list page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-your-events-in-a-dynamic-list-page)

### What should I do if I previously connected an element to the 'About (Rich Text)' collection field in the CMS?

If you previously connected an element to the '**About (Rich Text)**' Events collection field, remove this connection as it is being deprecated. Wix Events has released a new '**About the event (optional)**' field where you can add rich media to showcase details about your events. This new field appears as the '**Long description (Rich Content)**' collection field when you connect a rich content element to it.

Learn how to [connect the rich content element to the new 'Long description (Rich Content)' collection field](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-creating-a-custom-event-details-page#step-3-customize-your-new-dynamic-item-page).

### Can I add multiple images at once to my Event Details page?

Yes, you can add a gallery to showcase multiple images. Click **Add Gallery**, select the images you want to include, and manage them using the Media Manager. You can customize the layout and settings for the gallery as well.
