---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Ability to Edit Menus and Dishes in Bulk"
---

Currently, it's not possible to edit your menus or dishes in bulk. For example, if you want to delete a bunch of dishes you'll have to delete them one at a time.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
