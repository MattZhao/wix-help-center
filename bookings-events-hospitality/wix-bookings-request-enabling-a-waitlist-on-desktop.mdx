---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Enabling a Waitlist on Desktop"
---

Currently, when a class is full, your site visitors can only join a waitlist using the Wix mobile apps and not on desktop. It is not possible to automatically add site visitors to a waitlist on desktop.

If your visitors try to join a full class via desktop, you (the site owner) must manage this manually by keeping your own waitlist.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
