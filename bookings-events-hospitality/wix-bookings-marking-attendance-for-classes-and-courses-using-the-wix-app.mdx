### Marking class attendance in the Wix app

Mark attendance at your classes using the Wix app and see which of your classes are most in-demand. You can also make decisions about moving class times or days using attendance data when needed.

> **No-show fees:**
>
> No-show fees do not apply to group bookings. Learn more about [no-show and cancellation fees](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee).

#### To mark class attendance using the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.  Tap the relevant class in the Booking Calendar.  Tap **Check-In**.  Do one of the following:

- **Check in all participants at once: **Tap** Check-In All **at the bottom.
- **Check in one participant: **Tap **Check In **next to the relevant participant.
- **Mark as no-show: **Tap the **Checkmark **and then tap **Mark as no-show**. If relevant, you can click **Apply a no-show fee **to charge the participant for missing the session.

**Note:** This option is only available for classes that include [cancellation and no-show fees in their booking policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee).

### Marking course attendance in the Wix app

Check your clients in when they arrive for your course sessions using the Wix app. This is useful for marking attendance at specific sessions of the course, not the entire course itself. You can mark clients as checked-in or as "no show" as required.

> **Note:**
>
> No-show fees do not apply to courses.

#### To mark course attendance in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage** at the bottom.  Tap **Bookings**.  Tap the relevant course in the Booking Calendar.  Tap **Check-In**.   Tap the relevant course session.

Do one of the following:

- **Check in all participants at once: **Tap** Check In All **at the bottom.
- **Check in one participant: **Tap **Check In **next to the relevant participant.
- **Mark as no-show: **Tap the **Checkmark **and then tap **Mark as no-show**.

> **Note:**
>
> Learn more about [how to mark attendance on desktop here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-marking-attendance-for-classes).

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I charge a no-show fee for courses?

If you mark a client as no-show, it will not trigger a no-show fee. You are able to remove the client from the entire course and charge a cancellation fee.

### How do I update a participant's payment status manually?

If a participant pays in person for the session, you can update their payment status manually. Tap the relevant sessions, then tap the relevant participant and tap **Paid in Person**.

### How does marking attendance work for group bookings?

When your client makes a group booking for a session, you mark attendance for the client but not their guests. For your site analytics, when you mark a no-show on a group booking, it counts all participants in the group.

### Can clients pay for group bookings using a package?

Clients can pay for group bookings using their purchased packages. Each participant in a group booking uses up 1 session in the package.

For example, if a client buys a 10-session package and makes a group booking with 2 friends using their package, it counts as 3 sessions used. They then have 7 sessions remaining.

### Can clients pay for group bookings using a membership?

Clients cannot use memberships to pay for group bookings, they can only pay for themselves using their membership. The rest of the participants in a group booking pay for their sessions in person.
