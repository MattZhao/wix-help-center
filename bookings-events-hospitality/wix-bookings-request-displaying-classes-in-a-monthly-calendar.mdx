---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Displaying Classes in a Monthly Calendar"
---

Currently, when a customer signs up for Classes, they see the available times in a weekly calendar. It is not possible to display the available times in a monthly calendar.

> **Tip:**
>
> Your clients can change the week by changing the date range or using the back and forward arrows.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
