---
title: "Wix Events Request: Adding a Subscription Field When Guests Checkout"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a subscription opt-in field to a registration form during guest checkout. This would label guests as 'Subscribed,' converting them into subscribers with one click.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
