---
title: "Wix Events: Rescheduling Events"
---

Reschedule your event when unexpected circumstances occur. You have the flexibility to select a new date and time. Alternatively, update your guests that the event date is pending until you have more information to share. This ensures transparency as you work to finalize the details.

You can do this easily using your site's dashboard, the Wix app, or Wix Studio app.

> **Important:**
>
> - Learn how to [refund guests for their tickets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-canceling-and-refunding-orders-for-events-with-tickets#step-3-refund-guests) when necessary.
> - It is not possible to make changes to the date or time of an event which has already started.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/events/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Manage** next to the relevant event.
3. Click **Edit Details**.
[image: A screenshot of clicking to edit an event's details in a site's dashboard..]
1. Select an option:

- **Change to a specific date or time:**

1. Click the **Start** **date / End date **field and select the relevant dates from the calendar.
2. Click the **Start** **time / End time** drop-down and select the relevant times.
3. (Optional) Click **Add End Time** and select the end time.
- **Set event date as to be determined:**

1. Select the **To Be Decided (TBD) **checkbox.
2. (Optional) Edit the default note in the **Display a message **field.
2. Click **Save**.

**Note:** After saving, a pop-up appears at the bottom of the **Overview** tab, confirming your changes and prompting you to notify guests.
3. (Recommended) Notify your guests about the change: 

1. Click **Notify Guests** in the pop-up.
2. Click **Continue** to [customize a template](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-customizing-your-email-campaign) with the relevant information to send to your guests.
[image: A screenshot of the Email your guests about recent changes pop-up after you click Notify in a site's dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Events**.
4. Tap the relevant event status: **Published Events** or **Draft Events**.
5. Tap the event you want to manage.
6. Tap the **Manage **tab.
7. Tap **Event details **and update the relevant info:

- **Change to a specific date or time: **Tap the detail you want to change (e.g. Start date, End time).

**Tip: **You can also **Remove / Add End Date and Time**.
- **Set event date as to be determined: **

1. Disable the **Date & Time** toggle.
2. (Optional) Edit the default note in the **TBD Message **field.
8. Tap **Save**.
9. (Recommended) Tap **Continue** in the pop-up that appears to notify guests of the changes. This lets you customize a template with the relevant information.
[image: A screenshot of taping to edit an event's details in the Wix Owner app.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Manage**.
3. Tap **Events**.
4. Tap the relevant event status: **Published Events** or **Draft Events**.
5. Tap the event you want to manage.
6. Tap **Event details **and update the relevant info:

- **Change to a specific date or time: **Tap the detail you want to change (e.g. Start date, End time). **Tip: **You can also **Remove / Add End Date and Time**.
- **Set event date as to be determined: **

1. Disable the **Date & Time** toggle.
2. (Optional) Edit the default note in the **TBD Message **field.
7. Tap **Save**.
8. (Recommended) Tap **Continue** in the pop-up that appears to notify guests of the changes. This lets you customize a template with the relevant information.
[image: A screenshot of taping to select the relevant event status in the Studio app.]
