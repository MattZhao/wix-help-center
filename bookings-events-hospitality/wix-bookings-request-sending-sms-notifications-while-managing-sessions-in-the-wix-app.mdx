---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Sending SMS Notifications While Managing Sessions in the Wix App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to notify clients via SMS when scheduling, editing, or canceling a session from the Wix app. However, you can [automatically send SMS reminders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sending-sms-reminders-to-clients-in-wix-bookings) to members that they receive 24 hours before their booked session begins.

> **Workaround:**
>
> - You can [send SMS notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sending-sms-reminders-to-clients-in-wix-bookings) when scheduling, editing, or canceling a session from your desktop.
> - You can [use Omnisend to automatically send SMS messages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-sending-custom-sms-messages-with-omnisend) for booking confirmations, reminders, cancellations and rescheduling.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
