### Set up your business for success

Allow clients to book services anytime, anywhere with customizable, responsive booking pages. Boost sales by enabling multi-service appointments, and by offering dynamic pricing. Simplify staff management with features like customizable working hours, calendar syncing, and staff permissions.

[Online Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings) - Let your clients book appointments at any time and from anywhere using your customized booking pages.  [Multi-service](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-and-managing-multi-service-appointments) - Grow sales by allowing clients to book multiservice appointments.   [Booking policies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-setting-up-your-booking-policies) - Set a booking and cancellation policy for each service separately. This allows you to keep your most in-demand services as fully booked as possible. You can also set your appointment bookings as "[request to book](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-requiring-approval-for-appointment-bookings)".  [Resources](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-your-resources) - Create resources like rooms, stations, or chairs and assign them to services that require them so they also get booked when clients make their appointments.  [Multi-location](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-services-at-multiple-locations) - Add and manage all of your business locations.  [Dynamic pricing per staff](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-understanding-price-options) - Set different pricing for each staff member.  [Conferencing link ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-advanced-video-conferencing-for-online-services)- Let every staff member connect their own video conferencing accounts.

### Optimize your online and in-person payments with Wix Bookings

Simplify your payment processes with Wix Bookings. Offer flexible options like Afterpay and streamline in-person transactions with our POS solutions. Simplify invoicing, request payments with pay links, offer convenient methods like Credit Card on file, and more.

#### Get paid online

[Online payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-getting-paid-online) - Let your clients book and pay in advance on your website with [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments).   Wix also supports a [variety of payment providers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country#all-countries), based on your preferences and location.   [Afterpay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-afterpay-as-a-payment-provider-3515230) - Allow customers to pay in installments with Afterpay.

#### Get paid in person, or after the service

[Retail POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-wix-pos-for-brick-and-mortar-stores) - Make your phone a point of sale, so clients can accept payment for services that have already been booked.   [Mobile POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-booking-services-and-accepting-payments-with-the-wix-owner-app) - Use your phone as a point-of-sale (POS).  [CC on file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-saving-and-charging-clients-credit-cards) - Save clients' credit card details and charge for future bookings in a more convenient way.  [Charge manually with cc details](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-by-manual-card-entry) - Accept payments by typing in the customers' credit card details. This works great for accepting bookings over the phone.  [Pay links](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pay-links-creating-and-managing-pay-links) - Instantly request payments from contacts by sharing unique pay link URLs that allow them to make secure online payments. Share pay links via email, social media, SMS, and other channels. You can also create and send QR codes as pay links.  [Invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-sending-an-invoice) - Create and send professional invoices in seconds. Simply email or text your invoice so your clients can make payment.

#### Limit no shows by charging deposits

[Charge deposits](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-selecting-how-you-charge-for-bookings-services#charging-a-deposit-before-booking) - If you prefer not to charge your clients the full service fee upfront, you can accept an initial deposit fee at the time of booking, and receive the rest in person or with an [automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-sending-payment-requests-to-clients-using-automations) to complete the payment after the service is provided.

### Streamline your day-to-day business management

Manage staff, schedules, appointments, and more from anywhere. Stay organized by blocking holiday hours, tracking attendance, and managing meeting notes.

#### Manage your staff members

[Set up your staff members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-staff-members-in-wix-bookings-7354860) - Add your staff to your site, and [give them different roles and permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-staff-permissions-in-wix-bookings) so they can handle their clients and bookings.  [Working hours](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-editing-your-staff-members-working-hours) - Set the working hours of each employee and create an easy-to-manage work schedule.  [Sync staff members' personal calendars](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-syncing-staff-members-personal-calendars-with-the-bookings-calendar) - Allow staff members to sync their personal calendars with your site's Booking Calendar to keep your availability as up-to-date as possible.  [Holiday mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-blocking-off-time-for-multiple-staff-members) - You can block off hours for yourself or for staff members on your Wix Bookings calendar. During those blocked time slots, customers will not be able to book appointments.  [Payroll management](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-the-payroll-calculator) - Set pay rates for your staff and calculate gross pay for payroll. Choose from a flat rate per service, per participant, or a percentage rate based on total session price.

#### Manage your bookings

[Calendar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-the-wix-booking-calendar) - With one click, easily view all your service details, and quickly review each employee's upcoming schedule to check their availability. Choose the view that is most convenient for you by using the various filters in the Booking Calendar.  [Attendance management](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-participants-for-appointments-in-wix-bookings) - Using the Bookings calendar, you can keep track of who pays and who attends services at your business. You can also cancel sessions and issue refunds if needed.   [Meeting notes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-a-note-to-a-specific-booking) - Whether you want to jot down information about an upcoming booking or keep track of what happened in a service you already provided, you can easily add a note to your appointments and classes. This is great for creating rapport with regular clients.  [Booking list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-bookings-with-the-booking-list) - Use the Booking List in your site's dashboard to track and review all booking activity on the site. You can also add different filters and search parameters to review past, present and future bookings.  [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/using-the-wix-owner-app-to-manage-your-bookings) - Run your business from your mobile device while you're on-the-go. The Wix app allows you to keep track of your business from anywhere, including: scheduling services, processing payments, marketing, form management & analytics.
