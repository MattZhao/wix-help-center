---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Customizing the Dish Title Design in Mobile View"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to edit the way the name of restaurant dishes display on mobile devices. This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Menus (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new).

> **Important for sites that offer online ordering:**
>
> - The new menu app works with the new online ordering app [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).
> - The new online ordering app already offers many advantages over the old app, however, a number of features available in the old app are still missing. Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
