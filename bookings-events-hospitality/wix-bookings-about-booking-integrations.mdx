---
title: "Wix Bookings: About Booking Integrations"
---

Manage your bookings, engage with clients and grow your business by combining different integrations with Wix Bookings.

Add a 'Book Now' button to your [Facebook](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-a-book-button-to-facebook) and [Instagram](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-a-book-button-to-instagram) business pages to boost bookings on your Wix site. Allow potential new clients to book your services directly from Google, using a [Google 'book' button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-a-google-book-button). You can also allow clients to book services through [ClassPass](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-letting-clients-book-your-services-with-classpass) and [Hopp - Link in Bio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-adding-setting-up-your-link-in-bio-page).

---

### Allow clients to book directly from your social media pages

When you promote your services on social media, adding a 'Book' button helps clients to book faster. This is useful for launching new services or when you run a marketing campaign.

Choose which social platform you want to connect from the options below:

> **Important:**
>
> - If you experience any technical issues, contact Facebook for support. ﻿You can also [visit the Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help) for more information.
> - You can add a 'Book' button to either Facebook or Instagram, but not both.

---

### Make it easier for clients to find you on Google

[A Google 'book' button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-a-google-book-button) is a great way to help new clients find your business while they search for services you offer in your area. Once you've set up a Google 'book' button, new clients can book directly from their browser.

---

### Increase bookings with ClassPass and Hopp - Link in Bio

Make it easier for potential new clients to find and book your services using the ClassPass and Hopp - Link in Bio third-party integrations.

Learn more about [letting your clients book your services using ClassPass](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-letting-clients-book-your-services-with-classpass).  Learn more about [adding and setting up your link in bio page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-adding-setting-up-your-link-in-bio-page) using Hopp.
