---

Manage your Dayful service business on the go using the Wix mobile app. Book clients in for sessions, share links to your services, and update your business and service info, all from your mobile device.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> You can download the Wix app to your phone using these links: 
> - [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wix-owner-websites-apps/id1545924344)
> - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wix.admin&hl=en&gl=US)
> Learn more about [downloading the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app).

---

### Offer great customer service

From the Wix app, you can [book clients in for your services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-manually-adding-bookings-using-the-wix-owner-app) rather than asking them to do it themselves. This is a great way to lock in bookings at the end of an introductory meeting.

---

### Make it easy for clients to book services

Make it easy for clients to book your services by sharing a direct link to your Booking page. This is a great way to win more clients and increase your revenue.

---

### Add and update your services any time, any place

Using Dayful on the Wix app gives you the freedom to add new services or edit existing ones directly from your mobile device. You can also add new staff members to services as your business grows.

This is useful when demand for your service grows, for example after a successful marketing campaign or conference.

**Learn more about:**

[Creating appointments using the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-managing-appointments-using-the-wix-owner-app).   [Creating classes using the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-classes-using-the-wix-owner-app).

> **Important:**
>
> To create more than one service or add more than one staff member, [upgrade your Dayful plan](https://manage.wix.com/premium-purchase-plan/dynamo?siteGuid=bbf72f63-0d36-414f-af68-cf0dc62b11e2&referralAdditionalInfo=bizMgrHeader).

### Boost your bookings by offering clients more way to pay

[Upgrade your Dayful](https://manage.wix.com/premium-purchase-plan/dynamo?siteGuid=bbf72f63-0d36-414f-af68-cf0dc62b11e2&referralAdditionalInfo=bizMgrHeader) plan to accept payment for your services online.
