Dayful by Wix is an all-in-one business management and scheduling platform. This allows you to run your service business, get booked and get paid, wherever your online presence is.

### Benefits of Dayful by Wix

Dayful by Wix provides freedom and flexibility to service business owners like you. Get booked and get paid no matter the size or scale of your online presence.

**Don't need a website?** Dayful creates a landing page for your clients to book your services. You can then share links to your services in emails, on social media and through other digital marketing channels.

**Already have a website?** Embed your Dayful by Wix widgets on your existing site and manage your service business from the Dayful dashboard.

### FAQs

Here are some frequently asked questions about Dayful by Wix:

### How do I get started with Dayful by Wix?

First, sign up at [Dayful.com](https://www.dayful.com/home) to set up your account. Answer a few questions about your business so we can provide the best solution for you. You can then access your Dayful by Wix dashboard and begin adding your staff and services.

### How do I add my business details?

The [Business Info](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Info&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/business-info/) section of your dashboard is where you can add or update important details about your business. These include: 

- Your business' name, category, logo and description.
- Your default location.
- Your primary contact info.

### How do I create a service?

The [Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings?referralInfo=sidebar) tab is where you create or update your services. These include: 
- 1-on-1 service:

- **Appointments: **Services which clients can book during any available time slot during your business hours. Clients can only book when the relevant staff member is available to host the service.
- Multiple participants services:

- **Classes:** A regular service you run on a recurring basis. Clients can book each session separately.
- **Courses:** A one-time service which takes place over days or weeks. Clients book all sessions together.

### How do I add staff members?

The [Staff tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Staff&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/staff?referralInfo=sidebar) is where you add and update your staff member profiles. Learn more about [adding staff members to your Dayful account.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6WAj45XgWio&ab_channel=Dayful/utm=support=dayful_yt_add_staff_member) You can also:
- Set your default staff hours.
- Customize individual staff hours in their profiles.
- Invite staff members to sync their personal calendars with their Dayful by Wix calendars.
- Set up your video conferencing account and provider to offer online services.

### How do I get paid when clients book my services?

The [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/payments/) section of the Settings tab is where you connect your payment providers. Learn more about [setting up payments on Dayful](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JAC81KN46rQ&ab_channel=Dayful/utm=support=dayful_yt_set_up_payments).

**Note: **Upgrade to a premium Dayful by Wix plan to accept online payments. 
Dayful by Wix currently supports the following payment providers, depending on your location:
- Stripe
- Square
- Paypal
- Manual payments

### How do I share my services online?

If you don't have a site yet, you can share links to your services in emails or on social media from your dashboard.
[Learn more about sharing your Dayful by Wix services online](https://support.wix.com/en/article/dayful-by-wix-sharing-your-services-online).

### How do I embed my services on my existing website?

If you already have your own site, you can generate code to embed your service widgets on your existing web pages.
[Learn more about embedding your service widgets on your website](https://support.wix.com/en/article/dayful-by-wix-embedding-your-widgets-online).
