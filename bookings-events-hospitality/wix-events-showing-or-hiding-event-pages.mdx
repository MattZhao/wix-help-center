---
title: "Wix Events: Showing or Hiding Event Pages"
---

Wix Events has 2 dynamic pages where the content changes depending on which event is selected; the Event Details page and the Registration Form page.

The Registration Form page is required, but the Event Details page can be hidden if your site has only RSVP events.

> **Important:**
>
> The Event Details page cannot be hidden on sites that have any ticketed events, including  canceled ticketed events.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to the Event Details & Registration page: 

1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Event Pages**.
3. Click **Event Details & Registration**.
3. Click the Event Details** **element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click **Pages**.
6. Enable the **Show this page when visitors register** toggle to show or hide this page.
[image: A screenshot of enabling the Show this page when visitors register toggle in the Wix Editor.]
