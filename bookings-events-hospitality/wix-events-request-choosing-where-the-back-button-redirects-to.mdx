---
title: "Wix Events Request: Choosing Where the \"Back\" Button Redirects to"
---

Currently, a number of buttons in Wix Events direct guests back to your home page. It is not possible to change where the back button links to. These buttons are:

The "Back to site" button that guests see after they register for an event  The "See other events" button that appears when registration is closed

**Tip: **The text on this button can be edited in the Registration tab in your site's dashboard.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
