---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Changing the Delivery Options"
---

We're excited to announce that it is now possible to [enable or disable the option for delivery in online orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-online-ordering-for-your-restaurant). This feature is also available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

> **Important:**
>
> The new online ordering app already offers many advantages over the old app, however, a number of features available in the old app are still missing. Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
