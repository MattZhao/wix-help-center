### Checking your upcoming sessions in the Booking Calendar

Access your Booking Calendar from the Wix app, to view and organize your sessions on the go. There are different views to choose from, depending on your personal preference. Tap on any booking to see the details, including the participant list, time and date.

#### To check your upcoming sessions in the Booking Calendar:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.

Choose from the following calendar views, depending on your needs:

### Schedule view

This is a convenient way to book clients in for their next session. All upcoming sessions appear one after the other, regardless of the time between them (e.g. if your business closes on weekends, sessions on Fridays and Mondays will appear next to each other).

1. Tap the **Change View **icon  at the top right.
2. Tap **Schedule** to see a list of upcoming sessions.
3. (Optional) Select the relevant location(s) under **View by Location **at the bottom.

**Note:** This option only appears if you offer services in multiple locations.

[image: A screenshot showing how to select the Schedule view for your Bookings calendar on the wix owner app.]

### Day view

Use this view to see all upcoming sessions for a given day. In this view, all time slots appear, including empty ones. This is useful when booking clients in for appointments or when creating new services.

1. Tap the **Change View** icon  at the top right.
2. Tap **Day view** to see a list of upcoming sessions.
3. (Optional) Select the relevant location(s) under **View by Location **at the bottom.

**Note:** This option only appears if you offer services in multiple locations.

[image: A screenshot showing how to select the Day view for your Bookings calendar on the Wix Owner app.]

### 3 day view

Use this view to see all upcoming sessions over a three-day period. This is great for scheduling new bookings, especially during busy periods.

1. Tap the **Change View **icon  at the top right.
2. Tap **3 day view**.
3. (Optional) Select the relevant location(s) under **View by Location **at the bottom.

**Note:** This option only appears if you offer services in multiple locations.

[image: A screenshot showing how to select the 3 day view for your Booking Calendar on the Wix owner app.]

> **Tip:**
>
> The color coding you apply to sessions in your Booking Calendar on desktop also appears in the Wix app.

### Scheduling new sessions in the Booking Calendar

Use the Booking Calendar on the app to schedule new sessions and add more people to existing bookings.

#### To schedule sessions in the Booking Calendar:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.  Choose what you want to do next:

### Add participants to an existing session

1. Tap the relevant session in your calendar.
2. Tap **Manage Participants**.
3. Tap the **Booked** tab.
4. Add more participants to the session:﻿ 

1. Tap **Add Participant**.
2. Choose a participant from your contact list, or tap **+ Add New Contact** to fill out a new client's details.
3. Tap **Add **to confirm.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Tip:** Tap the **Waitlist** tab to view those specific participants (make sure to [enable waitlists](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-waitlists) in your booking policy to access this tab).

### Add a new appointment / class to the calendar

1. Tap the **Add **icon .
2. Select **Appointment** / **Class**, depending on what you need to add.

**Note: **You can add courses only on desktop.

[image: A screenshot showing how to add an appointment or a class session in the Booking calendar on the Wix owner app.]
3. Select the relevant **Service** from the drop-down.
4. Select the **Staff Member** from the drop-down.
5. Set the **Service Location**.
6. Set the **Date & Time** of the service.
7. (Optional, for classes only) Set your class to repeat:  

1. Choose how often the class repeats from the drop-down (e.g. weekly, every 2 weeks)
2. Tap the days of the week this class takes place in.
3. (Optional) Tap the checkbox to **Set an End Date** for the class.
8. Tap **Save**.

### Updating and rescheduling sessions

In the Wix app, you can update appointments, single sessions or all future sessions. You can also change staff members, locations or add any notes for yourself.

#### To update or reschedule sessions:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar.**  Tap to select the relevant date.   Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant session.  Depending on the booking type, follow the steps below:

### Appointments

1. Tap **Edit**.
2. Make the relevant changes, including:

- **Date: **Select a new date for the appointment.
- **Location**: Choose a new appointment location.
- **Session starts & Duration:** Edit when the session takes place and how long it lasts.
- **Staff:** Assign a different staff member.

**Note:** If a staff member is not available or there is a conflict in scheduling, you'll see a notification
- **Show free time slots:** View other available time slots of the staff member and select a new time.
- **Add note to self:** Add a personal note about the appointment.
- **Notify client with an email: **Check the box to update clients about any changes.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Classes

1. Tap **Edit session**.
2. Make the necessary changes, including:

- **Staff:** Assign a different staff member.
- **Max. Number of Participants:** Edit how many people can join the session.
- **Location: **Change where the session takes place.
- **Time & Duration:** Edit when the session takes place and how long it lasts.
- **Additional Information: **Add a note to yourself about the session.
3. Tap **Save**.
4. (Optional) Tap **Yes **to send your client an email with the updated details.

### Course

1. Tap **Edit session**.
2. Make the necessary changes, including:

- **Staff:** Assign a different staff member.
- **Time & Duration:** Edit when the session takes place and how long it lasts.
- **Additional Information: **Tap to leave yourself a note about the session.
3. Tap **Save**.
4. (Optional) Tap **Yes **to send your client an email with the updated details.

### Blocking off time using the Wix App

If you or a staff member are going to be unavailable, block the time off your calendar to prevent clients from booking your services.

#### To block off time using the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.  Tap the **Add **icon .  Tap **Blocked time**.  Tap to select the relevant staff member from the drop-down.  Tap to enter a **Title **for the blocked time.   Select a start date and time.  Select an end date and time.  (Optional) Tap **Notes** to add more information about why the staff member's time is blocked (this note is not visible to clients).  Tap **Save **to save your changes.
