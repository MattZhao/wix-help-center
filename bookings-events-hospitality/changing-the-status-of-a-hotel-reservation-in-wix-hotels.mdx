Reservations can have four possible statuses:

ReservedChecked inChecked outCancelled

### To update the status of a reservation:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Reservations **tab.Click the relevant reservation.Select a status from the **Reservation Status** drop-down list.Click **Save**.
