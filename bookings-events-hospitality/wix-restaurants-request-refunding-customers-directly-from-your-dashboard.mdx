title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Refunding Customers Directly from your Dashboard"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to [refund customers directly from your site dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refunding-customers). This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).
