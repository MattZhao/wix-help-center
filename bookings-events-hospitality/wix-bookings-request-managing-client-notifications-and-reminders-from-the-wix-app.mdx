---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Managing Client Notifications and Reminders from the Wix App"
---

Currently, you can choose which notifications to send your clients from [Emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-your-outbound-client-emails) & [SMS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sending-sms-reminders-to-clients-in-wix-bookings) Reminders in your site's dashboard. You can also turn the notifications on and off from here. It is not possible to manage these notifications from the Wix app on your mobile device.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
