---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Printing Your Daily Agenda or Weekly Schedule"
---

Currently, it is not possible to print out your schedule of upcoming sessions from the Booking Calendar to display in your premises.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is possible to export reports on past bookings. If your schedule remains the same each week, you can export and print a .CSV file to display a physical copy of your weekly schedule. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/exporting-reports-on-bookings-and-staff-in-wix-bookings)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
