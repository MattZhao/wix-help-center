---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Ability to Order from Over-the-Counter Restaurants"
---

Currently, customers cannot place orders to be collected at over-the-counter restaurants (such as pizzerias) and then eaten on the premises (without being packed for pickup).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
