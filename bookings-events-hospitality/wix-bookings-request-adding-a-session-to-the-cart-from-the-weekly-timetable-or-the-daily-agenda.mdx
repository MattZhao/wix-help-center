title: "Wix Bookings Request: Adding a Session to the Cart From the Weekly Timetable or the Daily Agenda"

Currently, it is not possible to display the 'Add to Cart' button on the [Weekly Timetable](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-a-weekly-timetable) or [Daily Agenda](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-a-daily-agenda-on-your-site) widgets.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
