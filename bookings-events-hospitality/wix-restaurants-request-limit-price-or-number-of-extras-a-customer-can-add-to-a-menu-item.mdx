---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Limit Price or Number of Extras a Customer Can Add to a Menu Item"
---

Some restaurant offer their customers optional extras to restaurant items, such as optional pizza toppings.

Currently, it is not possible to limit the number of extras a customer can add. Additionally, it is not possible to put a cap on the price so that, for instance, extras after the third one are free.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
