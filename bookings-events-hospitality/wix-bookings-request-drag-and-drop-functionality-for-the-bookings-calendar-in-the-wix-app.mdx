title: "Wix Bookings Request: Drag and Drop Functionality for the Bookings Calendar in the Wix App"

Currently, you can [reschedule or update appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-managing-appointments-using-the-wix-owner-app#updating-an-upcoming-appointment-in-the-wix-owner-app) from the Booking Calendar tab in the Wix app, however you cannot drag and drop sessions to change the schedule.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
