---
title: "Wix Events Request: Ability to Display Events in Guests' Time Zones"
---

Currently, event times are displayed in your time zone, based on your [Language & Regional settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-editing-the-date-and-time-format). It's not possible to display event times in your guests' local time zones.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
