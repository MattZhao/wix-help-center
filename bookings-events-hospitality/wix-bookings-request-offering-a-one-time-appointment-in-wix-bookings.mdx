---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering a One-Time Appointment in Wix Bookings"
---

Currently, it is not possible to offer a one-time Appointment set on a specific date.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can create a class or course and set the maximum number of participants to 1. Then create a one-time session by adding the [class](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-your-classes) or [course](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-your-courses) through the [Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is appreciated.
