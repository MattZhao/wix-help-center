---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Charging for Multiple Booking Services from Different Orders at the Same Time"
---

Currently, it's not possible to charge for multiple booking services from different orders at the same time. On both desktop and on the Wix app, you need to charge each order separately.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
