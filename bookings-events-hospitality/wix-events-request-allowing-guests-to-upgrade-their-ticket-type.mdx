---
title: "Wix Events Request: Allowing Guests to Upgrade Their Ticket Type"
---

Currently, it is not possible for guests to upgrade tickets after purchase. However, they can contact you [for a refund](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-canceling-and-refunding-orders-for-events-with-tickets#step-3-refund-guests), then they can buy a new ticket.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
