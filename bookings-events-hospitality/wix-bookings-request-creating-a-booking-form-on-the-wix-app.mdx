---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating a Booking Form on the Wix App"
---

Currently, on desktop, you can [create a Booking Form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-setting-up-your-booking-forms) for a service that allows clients to fill out the relevant details when signing up.

However, it is not possible to create a Booking Form from the Wix app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
