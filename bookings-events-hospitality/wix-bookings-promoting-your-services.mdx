### Sending an email campaign about a service

Create a tailored email campaign to engage with your clients and promote your service. You can create a customized email that perfectly matches your brand - select the colors, choose the text and media etc. You can then choose who to send the email to from your contacts.

#### To send an email campaign:

[Go to Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the relevant service and click the **More Actions** icon .  Click **Promote** .

Click **Create email campaign**.  Customize your email using the following options:

- [Add elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-elements-to-your-email-campaign) like text, images, videos and links.
- [Choose a theme](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-theme-of-your-email-campaign).
- [Choose a background](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-background-of-your-email-campaign).
    Click **Next**.  Add participants to the email campaign by:

- Typing contact names in the **To:** field.
- Selecting a group of contacts by their label.
- Selecting the checkbox next to the specific contacts.
    Click **Next**.  Click **Publish**.  (Optional) Click **Get Link** or any of the social media icons to share your campaign on different platforms.   Click **Done**.

### Sharing a post about your service on social media

Drive traffic and business from social media back to your site using a designer-made graphic featuring your service information to share on Facebook or Instagram.

#### To share a post about your services on social media:

Go to [Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the relevant service and click the More Actions icon .  Click **Promote** .

Click **Create Social Post**.  [Customize your social post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-posts-creating-and-customizing-a-social-post) using the following options:

- Choose a design template.
- Choose a background.
- Add text.
- Add graphic elements.
    Click **Next**.  [Connect to your social media accounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sharing-your-social-post-on-facebook):

- **Facebook:**

1. Add a caption.
2. Add hashtags
3. Click **Connect** and follow the steps to connect to Facebook.
- **Instagram:**

1. Select your country from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter your mobile number.
3. Click **Send**.
4. Check your mobile phone for your Instagram post.
    Choose what you want to do:

- **Publish now:** Publish the social post on your Facebook instantly. 

- Click** Publish** at the bottom right.
- **Schedule for later: **Publish the social post at a different date and time:

1. Select a date and time.
2. Click **Schedule **at the bottom right.

### Sharing a link to a service

Share a link that brings visitors directly to a specific service page. This drives more traffic to your service to generate more bookings. Share this link via text, email, or directly through popular social media channels.

#### To share a link to a service:

Go to [Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the relevant service and click the More Actions icon .  Click **Promote** .

Choose how you want to share a link to your service:

- **Copy link to your clipboard:** Click **Copy Link **** **and paste it into another app, for example your email or messenger app.
- **Share the link on social media: **Click one of the social media icons to share the link directly from that app or site.

### Creating a promo video for a service

Create a customized video for sharing on popular social media channels including Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. Sharing promotional video content is a powerful way to drive more traffic to your site and promote a specific service.

#### To create a promo video for your services:

Go to [Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the relevant service and click the More Actions icon .  Click **Promote** .

Click the Next icon  on the right to view more options.   Click **Create Video**.  [Add video content and text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-a-promotional-video-for-your-site), then click **Next**.  Choose the video style, layout, colors and font.  Click **Next**.  Choose if you want to add music to this video: 

- **Video with music: **

1. Enable the **Music On** toggle.
2. Choose a music track for your video from the options available.
- **Video without music: **Disable the **Music On** toggle.
    Click **Next**.  Decide if you want to include your business information in the video: 

- **Video with business info: **

1. Enable the **Include info at the end of this video** toggle.
2. Fill in the information to appear on the video.
- **Video without business info: **Disable the **Include info at the end of this video** toggle.
    Click **Apply & Preview**.  (Optional) Click **Back to Editing** to make changes.  Click **Save & Share**.  Choose the relevant social media platform where you want to share your video.  (Optional) Click **Download File** to save the video to your desktop.

> **Note:**
>
> Your video will be saved under the **My Videos** tab in the [Video Maker](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Video%20Maker&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvideo-maker-home/my_videos), and under **Site Files** in your [Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-about-the-media-manager).

> **Tips:**
>
> - You can [customize your services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-social-share-settings-for-your-sites-pages) to set the title, description, and image that appears when you share your service on social networks.
> - You can also [customize your Twitter Settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-twitter-settings-for-your-sites-pages) to display a different text and image for services that you share on Twitter.
