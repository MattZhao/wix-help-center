---
title: "Wix Events: Viewing and Managing Your Event Tickets"
---

Manage your event tickets directly from your site's dashboard. You can see how many tickets you've already sold, edit the ticket details, hide tickets that are sold out, and more.

### To view and manage your tickets:

[Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/events/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant event.  Click the **Tickets** tab at the top.  Click the** More Actions** icon  next to the relevant ticket.  Select how you want to manage your tickets:

- **Hide: **Click **Hide **to temporarily remove a ticket from your live site.
- **Duplicate: **Save time when creating new tickets by duplicating existing ones.

**Note: **It is not possible to use a duplicated ticket for a different event.
- **Preview: **Click to view a ticket as your guest sees it (your guests receive tickets as PDF files).
- **Edit Design:** [Customize ticket PDF](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-designing-a-ticket-pdf-for-your-event) to match your event theme.
- **Delete: **Permanently delete a ticket if you no longer need it.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Edit** next to the relevant ticket type to update details like the description, pricing, or the number of tickets you want to make available to guests.
