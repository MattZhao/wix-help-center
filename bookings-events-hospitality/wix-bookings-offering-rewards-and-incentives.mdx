---
title: "Wix Bookings: Offering Rewards and Incentives"
---

Attracting new clients is important for your business to grow. But it's also important to reward your existing clients to ensure repeat business. Offering rewards and incentives is a great way to both win new clients, and keep existing clients coming back again and again.

---

### Create a loyalty program

Create a loyalty program to boost bookings, increase client retention, and reward existing clients. Choose a program name, a name for your points, and an icon that represents your brand.

Clients get points every time they perform set actions, such as making a certain number of bookings. They can then redeem their points using coupons. You decide which actions clients take to earn points, how many points they get per action, and what the rewards are.

> **Get started:**
>
> Learn how to [create your own loyalty program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-a-loyalty-program-for-your-business).

---

### Offer gift cards

Attract new clients and keep existing clients coming back by offering digital gift cards.

Customers purchase cards on the Gift Card page of your site. You can let customers purchase gift cards for themselves or for others. You can choose the gift card amounts or let customers decide on their own. Later, customers can redeem the cards when making a booking online.

> **Get started:**
>
> Learn how to [create gift cards for your booking business](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-wix-gift-cards-9451439).

---

### Launch a referral program

With the Wix Referral Program, your site members can refer friends by sharing referral URLs. You have the option to reward both the existing client sharing the referral, and their friend being referred.

> **Get started:**
>
> Learn how to [launch a referral program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-referral-program-adding-and-setting-up-your-referral-program).
