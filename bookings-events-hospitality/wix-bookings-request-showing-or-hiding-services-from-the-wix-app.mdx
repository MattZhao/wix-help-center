---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Showing or Hiding Services from the Wix App"
---

Currently, from your site's dashboard, you can [choose which services to show or hide from the Services List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-making-services-unbookable#hiding-a-service-from-your-site). However, it is not possible to show or hide services from the Wix app on your mobile device.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
