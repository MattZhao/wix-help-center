---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Integrating Wix Table Reservations with Wix Members Area"
---

Currently, Wix Table Reservations is not integrated with [Wix Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area).

Using the Members Area, guests would be able to log in to your site and access their personal member pages to get information on previous and current reservations.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
