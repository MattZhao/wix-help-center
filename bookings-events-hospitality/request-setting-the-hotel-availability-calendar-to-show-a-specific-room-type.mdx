---
title: "Request: Setting the Hotel Availability Calendar to Show a Specific Room Type"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set your [hotel availability calendar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-hotel-availability-calendar-to-your-site) to show a specific room type only. Guests can select the room type themselves on the calendar.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
