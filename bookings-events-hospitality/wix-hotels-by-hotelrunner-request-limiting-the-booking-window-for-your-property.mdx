---
title: "Wix Hotels by HotelRunner Request: Limiting the Booking Window for Your Property"
---

Currently, it is not possible to limit or customize the booking window for your property in Wix Hotels by HotelRunner. Guests can book up to 2 years in advance of their stay, and it is not possible to shorten this booking window.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
