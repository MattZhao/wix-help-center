title: "Understanding Wix Restaurants Analytics Reports"
---

With Wix Analytics you can access reports for your Wix Restaurants site. Find out about your most popular dishes, your busiest times and days of the week, how many site visits turn into orders, and more.

#### Learn more about your Wix Restaurants reports:

[Restaurant Conversion Funnel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-restaurants-analytics-reports#restaurant-conversion-funnel)  [Restaurant Conversion over Time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-restaurants-analytics-reports#restaurant-conversion-over-time)  [Popular Dishes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-restaurants-analytics-reports#popular-dishes)  [Orders over Time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-restaurants-analytics-reports#orders-over-time)  [Popular Order Times](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-restaurants-analytics-reports#popular-order-times)

### Accessing your Wix Restaurant reports

1. Go to [Analytics & Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Analytics%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to the Restaurants section.
3. Select the relevant report.

### Switching views within a report

Click the relevant tab at the top of the report to access different chart types. Depending on the report you may see different viewing options.
