---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Selecting the Same Modifier More than Once"
---

Currently, it's not possible for customers to select the same modifier more than once for an item. For example, when choosing flavors for a box of cookies, they cannot pick the same flavor multiple times.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
