---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Removing the Login Bar from the Booking Form"
---

Currently, it is not possible to remove the login bar from the Booking form.

> **Note:**
>
> If you don't have a [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-my-bookings-page) on your site, the login bar doesn't appear on the booking form.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
