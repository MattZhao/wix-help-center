---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Adding a Cutlery Option to Online Orders"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically add a cutlery option to all online orders so that customers can indicate that they do not need you to include cutlery with their order.

As a workaround, you can add cutlery as a [deselection option ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-managing-dish-options)to each dish. Customers can check the cutlery option to indicate that you do not need to send it with your order.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
