---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Vacation Mode"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set vacation mode in Wix Bookings.

> **Workaround:**
>
> - If you [sync your Google calendar with Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-syncing-bookings-and-google-calendars), simply add the vacation days in your Google calendar. **Note: **Wix Bookings does not block off days added to your Google calendar as All Day events. When adding your vacation days in Google calendar, make sure to add the times as well (e.g., 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM). Learn more about [syncing your personal calendars with Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-syncing-personal-calendars-with-wix-bookings).
> - Manually add the vacation days to your Wix Bookings calendar as [blocked time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-blocking-off-time-in-your-calendar).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
