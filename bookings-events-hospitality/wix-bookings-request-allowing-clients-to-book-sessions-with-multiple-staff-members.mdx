---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Allowing Clients to Book Sessions with Multiple Staff Members"
---

Currently, there is no option for your clients to book sessions with multiple staff members (e.g. a couple's massage) in Wix Bookings. You can, however, manually block off the time slot in the additional staff member's calendar to make sure they are available to provide the service.
