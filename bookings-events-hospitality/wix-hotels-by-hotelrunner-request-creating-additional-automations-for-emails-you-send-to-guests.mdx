---
title: "Wix Hotels by HotelRunner Request: Creating Additional Automations for Emails You Send to Guests"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create additional automations for emails you send to your guests. For example, you cannot create an automation to send emails a specific number of days before your guests' check-in or after their stay.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
