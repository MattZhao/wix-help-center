Receive restaurant orders from customers directly on your site and manage them from your site's dashboard.

### To add the Wix Restaurants Orders app:

Go to **Add Apps** in your Editor:

- **Wix Editor:** Click **Add Apps**on the left side of the Editor.
- **Editor X:** Click the **App Market** icon  on the top of Editor X.
    Search for the "Wix Restaurant Orders" app in the search bar and select it from the list.  Click **Add to Site**.
