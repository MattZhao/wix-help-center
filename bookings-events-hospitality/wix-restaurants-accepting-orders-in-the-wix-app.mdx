> **Important:**

> - This article is about the **original Restaurants Orders app. **
> - We've released a new online ordering app. Learn how to [add and set up the new Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

View incoming orders in real-time directly from the Wix app and accept them with just a swipe to the right. You'll get a notification as soon as a customer places an order on your restaurant site. You can tap an order to see its details and a price breakdown, and print or email the order. In cases where you're unable to fulfill an order, you can also reject it directly from the app.

### To accept incoming restaurant orders in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage** at the bottom right.  Tap **Restaurant** **Orders**. The New tab displays the new orders waiting to be accepted or rejected.  Tap on the order you want to accept.  Review the order and then tap **Accept Order** at the bottom to accept it.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can swipe an order to the right to quickly accept it.
