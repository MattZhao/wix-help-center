### Accepting payment for booked sessions at a POS

From the Wix app, select the session your client wants to pay for, then accept payment using your mobile device.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure you [set up your mobile device as a point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale).

#### To accept payment for booked services at a mobile POS:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Bookings Calendar**.  Select a service from the calendar:

- **Appointment: **

1. Tap to select the relevant date.
2. Tap to select the relevant appointment.
3. Tap **Collect Payment**.
- **Class or Course: **

1. Tap to select the relevant class or course.
2. Tap **Manage Participants**.
3. Tap to select the relevant participant.
    Select an option:

- **Go to checkout:** Select this option to collect cash, card, tap-to-pay, or gift card payment. You'll be able to add discounts, tips, coupons, or a custom charge to the sale. If you sell Wix Stores products, you can add products to the sale, as well.
- **Charge with invoice: **Issue an invoice requesting payment for the session.
- **Mark as paid:** Accept payment offline and mark the session as paid.
    If you selected **Go to checkout:**

1. (Optional) Tap **Add More Items** to add additional products or services to the order.
2. (Optional) Tap **Add Custom Charge** to add a custom amount to the order.
3. (Optional) Tap **Add a Discount **to add a price reduction.
4. Tap **Charge**.
5. Choose how you want to accept payment:

### Cash

1. Tap **Cash**.
2. Select an option:

- Enter the amount you received and tap **Accept Cash**.
- Select a preset cash amount.
3. Give your customer the change.

**Tip:** The amount appears on the screen.

### Card

1. Tap **Card**.
2. If prompted, select **Bluetooth card reader**.
3. Let the customer swipe, insert, or tap their credit card.
4. Do one of the following:

- Tap **Send Email **to send your customer a receipt via email.
- Tap **No Thanks **if your customer doesn't want one.

### Manual card entry

The option of manually entering card details is not yet open to all eligible merchants. Manual card payment is only available for merchants using a [payment provider that allows manual card entry payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-by-manual-card-entry). **Important: **Entering credit card details manually is the least secure way of accepting card payments. We recommend accepting manual payment only from buyers you trust. Note that in case of fraud, it is the merchant who is liable. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/best-practices-for-accepting-card-payments#handling-offline-card-payments) 

1. Tap **Manual card entry**.
2. Tap **Go to Checkout**.
3. Enter all the credit card details and tap **Next**.
4. Enter the customer's zip code / postal code.
5. Tap **Confirm & Pay**.
6. Do one of the following:

- Tap **Send Email **to send your customer a receipt via email.
- Tap **No Thanks **if your customer doesn't want one.

**Note: **[Wix Payments processing fees](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-service-fees) for manual card entry are the same as for other card payments.

### (US only) Tap to Pay

After [setting up Tap to Pay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-tap-to-pay), you can use your phone as a card reader.

**To accept payment using tap to pay:**
1. Select **Card**.
2. If prompted, tap to select the relevant **Tip** amount, or tap **No Tip**. 

**Note: **Learn how to [enabled tipping](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-booking-services-and-accepting-payments-with-the-wix-owner-app#enabling-tips-at-checkout).
3. If prompted, select **Tap to Pay**.
4. Let the customer tap their credit card on your mobile device.
5. Choose what you want to do:

- Tap **Send Receipt:**

1. **Email receipt: **Confirm the email and name and then tap **Send Receipt**.
2. **Text receipt:** Confirm the phone number is correct and then tap **Text Receipt. **
3. **Print receipt: **If you [set up a printer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-printing-receipts), tap to print a receipt.
- Tap **No Receipt**.

### Gift card

If you sell [virtual Gift Cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-gift-cards) on your site or [physical gift cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-selling-physical-gift-cards-at-a-mobile-pos) at your POS, you can allow customers to redeem the cards when making purchases. **Tip: **If the price of the sale is worth more than the value of the card, customers can pay for the remaining cost using any other payment method.
**To accept gift card payment:**
1. Tap **Gift card**.
2. Enter your customer's gift card code or scan their physical gift card and tap **Apply**.

**Tip:** Your customer's remaining balance appears at the top.
3. Tap **Complete Sale**.
4. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- **Send a receipt:**

1. Tap **Send Receipt.**
2. Select how you send the receipt:

- Tap **Print Receipt: **This option is available if you connected a printer.
- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Text Receipt**, enter the phone number, including country code, and tap **Text Receipt**. (US & Canada only)
- **Do not send a receipt: **Tap **No Receipt**.

### Split payment

Using split payments, customers can pay using more than one payment method (e.g. they can pay using 2 credit cards or a combination of cash and credit card).
**To process split payments:** 
1. Tap **Split Payment**.
2. Enter the first payment amount.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. Select a payment method (e.g. cash) and complete the first part of the payment.
5. Tap **Continue**.

**Tip: **You can also tap the **More Actions **icon  and then **Mark as Partially Paid** to collect the 2nd payment later.
6. (Optional) Edit the second payment amount.

**Tip: **You can further split the balance owed.
7. Click **Continue**.
8. Select a payment method (e.g. card) and complete the payment.
9. Tap **Continue**.
10. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- **Send a receipt:**

1. Tap **Send Receipt.**
2. Select how you send the receipt:

- Tap **Print Receipt: **This option is available if you connected a printer.
- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Text Receipt**, enter the phone number, including country code, and tap **Text Receipt**. (US & Canada only)
- **Do not send a receipt: **Tap **No Receipt**.

### Save order and charge later

With this option, you can create an unpaid order which is added to the Orders tab. When you're ready, you can accept payment on mobile, from the Wix dashboard, or at a Retail point of sale.
To save your order, simply tap **Save order and charge later**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about selling services at a mobile POS.

### What payment method do I need to connect?

To process credit or debit card payments at a point of sale on your phone, you need to create a Wix Payments account and [get it verified](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-verification-process).
You do not need to connect any specific payment provider to accept cash payments.

### Are there limitations to what I can do on the mobile POS?

Currently, it is not possible to do the following:
- Sell recurring membership plans or packages
- Redeem membership plans or packages
- Offer refunds
- Collect tax
- Edit the payment total

### Can I collect tips from clients?

Yes. After you enable tipping, a screen where clients can optionally leave a tip appears during checkout.**Note: **It's not possible to collect tips for cash payment or when your processing split payments.
**To enable tipping:**
1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Services**.
4. Select the **Settings** tab.
5. Tap **Collect Tips** toggle to enable it.
6. (Optional) Change the preset tip percentages:

1. Tap the tip option you want to update.
2. Enter a new percentage.
7. (Optional) Tap the **Allow a custom tip amount** toggle to enable it.
8. Tap **Save**.

Learn more about [tipping in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-collecting-tips).

### How can I send (or resend) my client a receipt at a later date?

You can send (or resend) a receipt later from the Orders tab.
1. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
2. Select **Orders**.
3. Select the relevant order.
4. Tap the More Actions icon  at the top right.
5. Selecting **Email receipt**.

### Can I customize receipts?

Yes, you can add your company logo, display your return policy, and more. Learn how to [customize Mobile POS receipts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-customizing-receipts).

### Are there other Mobile POS options available for Wix Bookings?

You can also charge clients for services using a point of sale in the following ways:
- [Square point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale-with-square)
- [SumUp point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale-with-sumup)
