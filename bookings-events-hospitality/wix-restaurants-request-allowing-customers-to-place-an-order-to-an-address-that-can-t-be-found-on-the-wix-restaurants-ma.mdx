---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Allowing Customers to Place an Order to an Address that Can't Be Found on the Wix Restaurants Map"
---

Currently, when a customer places an order in your online ordering system, they are required to enter their address.

Wix Restaurants then checks that the address exists and is located in one of your delivery areas. Therefore customers cannot enter an address which cannot be found on the Wix Restaurants map.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
