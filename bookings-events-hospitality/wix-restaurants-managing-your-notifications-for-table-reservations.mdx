---
title: "Wix Restaurants: Managing Your Notifications for Table Reservations"
---

There are 3 types of notifications available with the Wix Table Reservations app:

When a reservation is created   When a reservation is requested  When a reservation is canceled

Choose to receive these notifications via your site's dashboard and/or email. By default, these notifications are enabled, but you can turn them on or off at any time.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Table Reservations](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/restaurants/table-reservations?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Table+Reservations) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Settings** at the top right.
3. Click **Notifications**.
4. Click **Manage Notifications** under **Notifications preferences**.
5. Click the **Dashboard** drop-down and enable the notifications you want to receive in your Wix dashboard.
6. Click the **Email** drop-down and enable the notifications you want to receive to your email.
[image: A screenshot of setting your notifications for table reservations.]Make sure to allow notifications from the Wix app in the settings of your mobile device. 
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap the **Notifications** icon  at the top.
3. Tap the **More Actions** icon .
4. Tap **Manage notifications**.
5. Tap the **Allow Push Notifications** toggle to enable or disable the relevant notifications:

- **Enabled:** You receive push notifications from the notification types of your choice. Tap the toggle next to the relevant notification type to enable or disable it. After disabling a notification, choose how long you want to mute notifications for.

- **Note:** To enable notifications for specific locations, tap one of the Wix Table Reservations notifications and select which locations you want to receive notifications from.
- **Disabled:** All notifications are disabled. Select the time frame you want to disable all notifications for.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Table Reservations notifications in the Wix Owner app..]

> **Note:**
>
> Email notifications for table reservations are sent to your Wix account email address and not the business address set in your business info. If you'd like others to also receive these notifications, you can [invite them to manage your restaurant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-inviting-people-to-manage-your-restaurant). You can also [set up an automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation) to select additional notifications for table reservations and select the email addresses that should receive them.
