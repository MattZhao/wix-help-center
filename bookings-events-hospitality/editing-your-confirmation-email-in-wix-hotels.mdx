> This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

You can edit the default text in the confirmation email sent to guests when they book.

### To edit the text in the confirmation email:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab. Click the **Settings **tab.Click **Emails**.Edit the confirmation email details: 
1. Enter the email title.
2. Enter the introduction text.
3. Enter the closing text.
Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> It is not possible to edit the design, spacing, or color of the confirmation email.
