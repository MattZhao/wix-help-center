---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Connecting a Service to a Pricing Plan While Creating It on the Wix App"
---

While you can [connect a service to a pricing plan from the service form on desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-selecting-how-you-charge-for-bookings-services#offering-a-service-as-part-of-a-membership-plan-or-package), it is not possible to do so on the service form on the Wix mobile app. You can, however, [connect a service](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-membership-plans-and-packages-using-the-wix-mobile-app) to a pricing plan **after** creating it. This can be done from the service list.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
