---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Integrating Square POS with DoorDash Drive"
---

Currently, Square POS doesn't integrate with [DoorDash Drive deliveries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-delivering-orders-with-doordash-drive). If you [connect Square POS to your Wix Restaurant site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-connecting-your-wix-restaurant-to-square-pos-point-of-sale), you'll manage your orders directly from Square POS.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
