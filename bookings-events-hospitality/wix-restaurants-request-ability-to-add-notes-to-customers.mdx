---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Ability to Add Notes to Customers"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to add notes or instructions to your restaurant's customers. This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

>
> Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
