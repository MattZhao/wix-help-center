---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating a Multi-Day Service"
---

Currently, Wix Bookings does not offer a multi-day service type.

> **Workaround:**
>
> Create services and set a custom duration to cover multiple days (up to 99 hours):
> - For [appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-an-appointment): Set the duration in the service details.
> - For [classes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-availability-for-classes-in-wix-bookings) and [courses](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-a-course): Set the duration when scheduling the class and course times.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
