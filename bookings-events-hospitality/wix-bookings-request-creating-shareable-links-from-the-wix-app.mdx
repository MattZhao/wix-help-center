---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating Shareable Links from the Wix App"
---

Currently, you can [create shareable links](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-managing-shareable-links) (e.g. to bookings pages, memberships, and packages) from the Sharable Links tab in your site's dashboard. It is not possible to create shareable links from the Wix app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
