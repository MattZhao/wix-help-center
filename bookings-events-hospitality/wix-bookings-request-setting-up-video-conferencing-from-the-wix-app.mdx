---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Setting Up Video Conferencing from the Wix App"
---

Currently, you or your staff members can [set up video conferencing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-advanced-video-conferencing-for-online-services) from the Booking Services or Staff tab in the dashboard. It is not possible to set up video conferencing from the Wix app on a mobile device.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
