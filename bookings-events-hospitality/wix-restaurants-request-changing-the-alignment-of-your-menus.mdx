---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Changing the Alignment of Your Menus"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the alignment of your restaurant menu (e.g., right-to-left), for either the Menus app or the Orders app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
