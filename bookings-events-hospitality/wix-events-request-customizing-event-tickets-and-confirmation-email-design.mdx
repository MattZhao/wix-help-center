title: "Wix Events Request: Customizing Event Tickets and Confirmation Email Design"
---

While you can [edit the text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-managing-emails-you-send-and-receive) on the event tickets and confirmation emails, it is not possible to customize them with design features such as logos, images, or videos.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Customize the ticket PDF](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-designing-a-ticket-pdf-for-your-event) and include it in the email to excite guests for the upcoming event.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
