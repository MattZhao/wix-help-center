---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Displaying Tax Details for Your Services"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display whether tax is included or excluded from the price of your services on your site. This includes on your service list, service page and at checkout.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
