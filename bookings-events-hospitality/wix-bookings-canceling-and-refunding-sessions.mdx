### FAQs

Click below to learn more about canceling a session.

### Can I refund a class or course?

For classes and courses, refunds are not issued automatically. You can refund classes and courses through the Orders tab, Participant List or the payment provider.
Learn more [about refunding classes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refunding-clients-in-wix-bookings).

### Can I customize the text of a cancelation email?

Yes, you can [customize the text of the cancelation email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-your-outbound-client-emails) in the [Booking Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/settings) tab in your site's dashboard. You can also add a personalized message to the email.

### How do I refund clients who book with a plan?

Clients who booked with a plan will receive an automatic session credit when you cancel.

### Can I refund sessions from my Orders page?

Yes, you can refund booking sessions from your Orders page. 
**From desktop:**
1. [Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant order.
3. Click **Actions** in the **Payment info **section.
4. Click **Refund**.
5. Click to select the checkbox(es) next to the relevant service(s) you want to refund.
6. Select a reason from the **Reason for refund** drop-down.

**Note: **This is for internal use only and customers won't see this.
7. (Optional) Check the **Send a confirmation email to the customer** checkbox and add a note to the cancelation email.
8. (Optional) Edit the refund amount in the **Summary** section to offer a partial refund.
9. Click** Refund**.

**From the Wix app:**
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Sales** and select **Orders**.
4. Tap the relevant order.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
6. Tap **Issue Refund**.
7. Tap to select the checkbox(es) next to the relevant service(s) you want to refund.
8. (Optional) Tap **Edit** **Amount** to offer partial refunds:

1. Enter the relevant amount you want to refund.
2. Tap **Apply**.
9. Tap **Next**.
10. (Optional) Select the reason you provided a refund from the **Refund Reason** drop-down.
11. (Optional) Notify your customer:

1. Enable the **Send a confirmation email**.
2. Enter a note to your customer. The note appears in the confirmation email.
12. Tap **Next**.
13. Tap **Issue** **Refund** to confirm.

**Note: **You cannot undo this action.
