The [Check-in by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/check-in-by-wix-an-overview) app makes managing event attendees easy. You can add new guests, update their status to "Going" or "Arrived", or place them on your waitlist.

### Adding new guests

You can add new guests to both ticketed and non-ticketed events. This can be done on the day of your event to accommodate last-minute attendees, fill any remaining seats and increase your potential revenue.

> **Adding guests before the big day?**
>
> You can [customize each Ticket PDF](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-designing-a-ticket-pdf-for-your-event) for easy event organization based on seating, entrance location, or ticket type (e.g. child and adult).

Check-in appDashboard**Note:** Only event owners can add guests through the app.
1. Open the Check-in by Wix app.
2. Tap the relevant event.
3. Tap **Guest List** under **Event management**.
4. Tap **Add New Guest**.
Ticketed events
1. Enter the **Guest Details **(e.g. name and email).
2. Select the status from the **Guest Status **drop-down (Going or Arrived).
3. Choose the ticket type and status under **Ticket Details**: 

- Select a **Ticket Type** from the drop-down (if you created more than one type of ticket).
- Select the relevant** Ticket Status** (e.g. Not Paid or Paid).
- Decide to send your guest a **Ticket PDF**.
Non-ticketed events (RSVP)
1. Enter the **Guest Details **(e.g. name and email).
2. Select the relevant status from the **Guest Status **drop-down (Arrived, Going, Waitlist).
3. Tap **Save**.

1. [Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant published event.
3. Click the **Guests** tab.
4. Click **Add New Guest**.
5. Enter the relevant guest details.
6. Select the relevant **Ticket details**:
Event without tickets
1. Select an **RSVP** status: Going, Not going or Waitlist.
2. Click **Save **at the top right.
Events with tickets
1. Select a ticket from the **Ticket Type **drop-down.
2. Select a **Ticket Status**: Not Paid, Paid, or Free.
3. Select or deselect the **Send guest ticket via email **checkmark.
4. Click **Save **at the top right.
Ticketed event with a seating map
1. Click **Go to Seating Map**.
2. Select a seat in the seating map.
3. Click **Select Ticket**.
4. Select a **Ticket Status**: Not Paid, Paid, or Free.
5. Select or deselect the **Send guest ticket via email **checkmark.
6. Click **Save **at the top right.

### How can I change the 'Check-In' status of my guest from the app?

1. Open the Check-in by Wix app.
2. Tap the relevant event.
3. Tap **Guest List** under **Event management**.
4. Tap the relevant guest.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
6. Choose the relevant status.

[image: A screenshot of changing the status of a guest in the Check-in by Wix app.]

### Can I change the status of my guest from my site's dashboard?

Yes. You can modify a guest's status at any time. For instance, if a guest is on the waiting list and a spot becomes available (e.g. due to a cancellation), you can remove them from the waitlist.
1. [Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant published event.
3. Click the **Guests** tab.
4. Click the **Status** drop-down next to the relevant guest to change the status.
