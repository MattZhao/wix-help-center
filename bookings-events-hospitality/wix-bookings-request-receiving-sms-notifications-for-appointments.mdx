---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Receiving SMS Notifications for Appointments"
---

Currently, Wix Bookings does not send SMS notifications when an Appointment is booked. Notifications are only sent by email.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
