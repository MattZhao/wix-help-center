---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Accepting the Calendar Sync Request from the Wix App"
---

Currently, when you invite staff members to sync their Bookings and personal calendars, your staff members can only [accept the invitation on desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-syncing-staff-members-personal-calendars-with-the-bookings-calendar#accepting-your-invite-to-sync-a-calendar). It is not possible to accept the invitation from the Wix app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
