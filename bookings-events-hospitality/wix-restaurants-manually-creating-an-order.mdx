title: "Wix Restaurants: Manually Creating an Order"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article is about integrating the **new **[Wix Restaurants Orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new) app.

Add orders made in person or over the phone to your orders list, so it always stays up to date. You can add existing menu items, or custom items that are specific to the order.

---

### Step 1 | Add items to the order

You can add any of your existing menu items to a manual order. In addition, if you're creating a custom item for your customer, you can add it and set its price.

#### To add existing items to an order:

[Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Click **+ Add New Order**.  Choose what you want to add:

### Add an existing menu item

1. Click **+** **Add Item **next to **Items**.
2. Select **Menu item**.
3. Select the item you want to add and click **Continue**.
4. (If the item has [modifiers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-understanding-modifiers)) Choose the relevant modifiers for the item.
5. (Optional) Enter any **Special Requests** your customer asked for.
6. Click **Add to Order**.
7. (Optional) Repeat steps 1-6 to add more items.

### Add a custom item

1. Click **Add Custom Item **next to **Items**.
2. Enter an item title.
3. Enter the price.
4. (Optional) Enter a description of the item under **Item info**.

**Note: **This description is visible to customers.
5. Click **Add**.
6. (Optional) Repeat steps 1-5 to add more custom items.

### Step 2 | Add a customer to the order

Now you can add your customer's details to the order. If this is a return customer, you can simply select them from your customer list. Otherwise, you can add them as a new customer.

#### To add a customer to the order:

Click the **Add a customer to the order **drop-down.   Select an option:

- **Existing customer:**

1. Select a customer.
2. (Optional) Click the **Edit customer** iconto view or edit the customer's details.
- **New customer:**

1. Click **+ Add new customer**.
2. Enter the customer's details.
3. (Optional) Click **Add New Field** to add more client info and fill out the new field.

**Note:** If delivery is your [fulfillment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new#step-3-choose-pickup-delivery-preferences), make sure to add a delivery address.
4. Click **Save**.

---

### Step 3 | (Optional) Add a discount

Now you can optionally provide your customer with a discount. Note that it is not possible for customers to redeem a coupon on a manual sale, but you can replace this with a discount of the same value.

#### To add a discount:

Click **Add Discount **under **Discounts and delivery method**.  Enter the discount amount.  (Optional) Enter a note about the discount (not visible to customers).  Click **Apply**.

---

### Step 4 | Select a delivery method

Before you create the order, you need to set how that order is going to get to your customer. Choose pickup methods available in your restaurant or create a custom delivery rate.

#### To select a delivery method:

Click **Add Delivery Method **under **Discounts and delivery method**.  Select an option: **Pickup** or **Delivery**.  Add the date and time.   (For deliveries) Add a fee, if applicable.   Click **Apply**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also take phone orders and [assign DoorDash Drive for delivery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-manually-creating-an-order#faqs) after creating the manual order and collecting payment.

---

### Step 5 | Create the order

Once you've double-checked the details, it's time to click **Create Order**. The new order will instantly appear in the Orders tab in your site's dashboard and in the **Accepted** column, featuring a distinctive "Manual Order" badge for easy identification.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Orders that only contain custom items won't appear in the Status view.
> - While orders with no delivery time appear in the Orders view, they are not available from the Status and Calendar views.

---

### Step 6 | Charge the customer

Finalizing payment arrangements is crucial, especially since creating a manual order doesn't automatically generate a payment request. Instead, you can choose from the following options:

[Accept payment by manually entering credit card details](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payment-by-manually-entering-credit-card-details)  [Request payment by sending an invoice](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-invoices-for-store-orders#requesting-payment-via-invoice-for-an-unpaid-order)  [Set up your phone as a point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-pos-selling-products-at-a-mobile-point-of-sale)  [Set up a mobile point of sale Wix card reader](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale)  [Set up a dashboard card reader](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-dashboard-card-reader-setting-up-the-dashboard-card-reader)

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I assign DoorDash Drive for delivery of an order placed over the phone?

Yes. Once you’ve entered the details for a manual order (e.g. over the phone), you can assign DoorDash Drive to handle the delivery.

**Note:** DoorDash Drive is available only for prepaid orders.
**To update the delivery method to DoorDash Drive:**
1. [Go to Online Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Incoming%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants-orders-prep-board) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon .
3. Select **Delivery with DoorDash Drive**.
4. Click **Yes, Continue** to update the delivery method to DoorDash Drive.
5. Enter the time for **How soon should a driver pick up the order?**.
6. Click **Submit**.

### How can I send an order confirmation email to the customer?

[Set an automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-getting-started) to send the customer a pre-installed email once you create a manual order.

### Can I send a manual order to my thermal printer?

Yes. Manual orders automatically [print on a thermal printer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-auto-printing-online-orders-from-the-wix-owner-app) when connected to your mobile device using the Wix app.
