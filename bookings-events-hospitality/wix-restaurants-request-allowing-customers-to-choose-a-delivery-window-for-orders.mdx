---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Allowing Customers to Choose a Delivery Window for Orders"
---

We're excited to announce that customers can now [choose a delivery window](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-order-scheduling-in-wix-restaurant-orders-new) when placing an order. This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

> **Important:**
>
> Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
