---
title: "Wix Bookings: Displaying a Featured Service on Your Site"
---

Promote a single service anywhere on your site using the Featured Service element.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also [display multiple services on any page of your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-displaying-selected-services-on-different-pages).

### To add and set up the Featured Service element:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Bookings**.
4. Drag the **Featured Service **element onto the page.
5. Click the **Featured Service** element on the page.
6. Click **Settings**.
7. Click the **Services **tab.
8. Select the checkmark next to the service you want to display. ** 

Tip: **Click **Add a New Service** to add a new appointment, class or course.
9. Click the relevant tabs to customize the layout, display, text and design of the Featured Service element.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The Featured Service Element is not a standalone element. It works together with Wix Bookings.
> - Make sure to delete the element if the service displayed is no longer available.
