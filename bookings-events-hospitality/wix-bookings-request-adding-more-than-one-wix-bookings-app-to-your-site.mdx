---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Adding More than One Wix Bookings App to Your Site"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add more than one Wix Bookings app to your site.

> **Note:**
>
> Therefore, it is currently not possible to create a multilingual booking site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
