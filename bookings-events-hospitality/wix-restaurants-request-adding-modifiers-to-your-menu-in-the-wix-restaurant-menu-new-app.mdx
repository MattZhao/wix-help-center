---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Adding Modifiers to Your Menu in the Wix Restaurant Menu (New) App"
---

Currently, you can [add modifiers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-understanding-modifiers) to your menu using the [Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new). It is not possible to do so when only using the Wix Restaurants Menus (New) app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
