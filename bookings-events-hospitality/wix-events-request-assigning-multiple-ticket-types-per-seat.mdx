---
title: "Wix Events Request: Assigning Multiple Ticket Types Per Seat"
---

Currently, you can only [assign one ticket type](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-a-seating-map#step-5-assign-tickets-to-seats) per seat. It is not possible to offer multiple ticket types (e.g. Early Bird and VIP) for the same seat.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can[ assign different prices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-tickets-for-your-event#step-2-create-one-or-more-ticket-types) to various seats or sections on a seating map using Price Options.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
