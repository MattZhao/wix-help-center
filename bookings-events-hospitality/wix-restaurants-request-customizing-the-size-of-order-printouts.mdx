---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Customizing the Size of Order Printouts"
---

Currently, orders printed from your online ordering system are printed in A4 size by default and cannot be customized.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can print receipt-sized orders with the Wix app using specific Star Micronics and Epson Bluetooth printer models. You can also choose your size preferences in the printer dialog box when printing a specific order. Learn more about [printing an order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-printing-an-order).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
