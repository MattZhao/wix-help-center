title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Automatically Notifying Customers of Rejected Orders"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to automatically notify customers when you reject their order. This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).
