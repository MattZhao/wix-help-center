---
title: "Setting Up Tax in Wix Hotels"
---

Add a tax rate to the cost of your bookings from the payments area of your Hotel Dashboard. The desired tax is added to the price of a reservation once customers have made a selection.

> **Important:**
>
> - This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).
> - Wix hotels displays prices without the tax added until a customer selects a room and dates of stay, then the tax is included in the final price breakdown of the reservation.
> - If you want tax included in the initial room prices, build the tax in when you set up your rates instead of using the tax field in Price Settings.

### To add tax to your prices:

Go to your [Hotels Dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-wix-hotels-dashboard).  Click the **Payments** tab.  Scroll down to **Price Settings**.  Enter the tax rate you want to add to your prices in the **Tax** field.   Click **Save** at the bottom.
