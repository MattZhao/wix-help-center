---
title: "Wix Bookings: Creating View-Only Services"
---

View-only services let you offer appointments, classes or courses without requiring users to book in advance. This is useful when you have a large venue and don't need to know who is attending your service ahead of time (e.g. free yoga session in the park).

DashboardWix appStudio app
1. [Go to Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **+ Add a New Service**.
3. Scroll to the type of service you want create: **Appointment**, **Class** or **Course**
4. Choose what you want to do:

- Click **Edit** below the relevant service template.
- Click **+ Start from Scratch** to use a blank template.
5. Complete the form to create your service.
6. Scroll down and click the **Booking preferences **section.
7. Disable the **Online bookings **toggle** **to create a view-only service.
[image: A screenshot showing where to disable the Online bookings toggle to make view-only service.]
1. Click **Save**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Services**.
4. Tap **+ Create New Service** at the bottom.
5. Choose the relevant service type: **Appointment** or **Class** 

**Note:** To create a course, go to your site's dashboard from a desktop computer.
6. Complete the form to create your service.
7. Scroll down to the **Advanced Settings **section.
8. Disable the **Accept online booking **toggle** **to create a view-only service.
[image: A screenshot showing where to disable the Online bookings toggle to make view-only service on the Owner app.]
1. Tap **Save **at the top.

1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Manage**.
3. Tap **Booking Services**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Booking Services**.
4. Tap **+ Create New Service** at the bottom.
5. Choose the relevant service type: **Appointment** or **Class** 

**Note:** To create a course, go to your site's dashboard from a desktop computer.
6. Complete the form to create your service.
7. Scroll down to the **Advanced Settings **section.
8. Disable the **Accept online booking **toggle** **to create a view-only service.
[image: A screenshot showing where to disable the Online bookings toggle to make view-only service in the Wix Studio app.]
1. Tap **Save **at the top.
