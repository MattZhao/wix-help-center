We're excited to announce that it's now possible to [add tax to a dish](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tax-collection-an-overview). This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).
