Upgrade your site to sell tickets for your events. This also removes the Wix branding from event emails sent to guests, adding a more personal touch (excluding the "Connect Domain" plan).

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Upgrading your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-upgrading-your-site#upgrading-your-site)  [Frequently asked questions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-upgrading-your-site#faqs)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure you [add Wix Events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-wix-events-to-your-site) to your site. Some Wix templates already include Wix Events, making it easier for you to get started.

---

### Upgrading your site

When you upgrade your site, you can sell tickets for events and create different pricing tiers for each one.

> **Important:**
>
> Make sure to choose a plan that accepts payments (e.g. Core, Business).

#### To upgrade your site:

[Go to the Upgrade page](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).  Click **Select** under the plan you want to purchase.  Select a billing cycle for your plan and click **Continue to Checkout**.  Enter your payment details.  Review your total and click **Submit Purchase**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about upgrading your site.

### Where can I find details about each available plan for upgrading my site?

View our newest [plan offers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) and see a wide range of benefits for your site. Among them is a free one-year [calendar integration](https://support.wix.com/en/article/third-party-app-events-calendar-by-inlight-labs) to showcase all your upcoming events. This integration also includes the option to connect external calendars to automatically display them in one place.

### What should I do if I have trouble with identity verification after a purchase?

After submitting your purchase, your bank may require you to [verify your identity](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds). If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.

### Do I need to upgrade my site if I already purchased a plan?

Since different plans offer different features, it's possible that you're trying to use a feature that's not included in your plan. For example, if you purchased the Light plan, you won't be able to accept payments through your site. Go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) to check which plan you purchased.
Another possibility is that you upgraded the wrong site. Site plans are valid for a single site only. However, if you upgraded the wrong site, you can easily [reassign the plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-premium-plan-to-a-different-site-in-your-wix-account) to the correct one.
