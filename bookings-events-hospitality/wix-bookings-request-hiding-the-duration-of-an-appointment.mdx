---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Hiding the Duration of an Appointment"
---

Currently, it is not possible to hide the duration of an Appointment on the Calendar page. However, you can [hide the duration](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-customizing-your-service-pages#choosing-the-information-you-display) on the Service Page and the Book Online page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
