### Adding the Wix Restaurants Menus (New) app

Add the Menus app to your site from the App Market either in your editor, or from your site's dashboard. You can also add it directly from your mobile device using the Wix app.

Wix EditorStudio EditorWix appDashboard
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Apps**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Use the search bar to find **Restaurant Menu (New)**.
4. Click **Add to Site**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the new Wix Restaurants Menus app.

### I already have the existing Wix Restaurants Menus app. What happens if I download the new Wix Restaurants Menus app?

Want to advance to the new Menus app but your menu is set up on the old app? Now you can import most of the information from your current menu to the new app. 
[Learn](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-importing-your-menus-to-the-new-wix-menus-app) what requires your attention in the process, and what has changed between the apps.

### What does the set-up process include?

[Create the menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-wix-restaurants-menus-new) (or menus) you need for your business. Start by giving your menu a name and description (e.g. lunch menu) and then add all the menu sections you need (e.g. sandwiches, salads, beverages). Once that's done, you can add individual menu items and [customize](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-customizing-the-look-of-wix-restaurants-menus-new) them to suit your needs.

### How do I set up online ordering?

To set up online ordering using the new Menus app, you'll need the [new online orders app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

### How can I translate my menus?

You can use the [Wix Multilingual app to translate your menus](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-restaurant-menu) into different languages.
