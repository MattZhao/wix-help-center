---
title: "Removing Imported Reservations from Your Wix Hotels Reservation Calendar"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

If you [imported reservations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/importing-reservations-from-an-external-calendar-ical-to-wix-hotels) from an external calendar (using iCal), you can remove the reservations by deleting the relevant iCal URL.

### To remove imported reservations:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Reservations** tab.Click the **iCal** tab. Click the **Delete** icon  next to the iCal URL you want to delete.

> **Note:**
>
> If you added only one iCal URL, you do not see the **Delete** icon. Instead, you can manually remove the URL from the iCal URL field and click **Save**.
