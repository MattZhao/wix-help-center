> **Important:**

> - We've released a new online ordering app. Learn how to [add and set up the new Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

Add a Members Area to your restaurant website so guests and loyal customers can log in and easily access information about upcoming reservations and order details.

### Wix Editor | Show me how

1. Click **Add**  on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click the **Community** tab.
3. Click **Members**.
4. Click **Add to Site**.

### Editor X | Show me how

1. Click **Add Elements**   at the top bar of Editor X.
2. Click the **Members **tab.
3. Click **Add to Site**.

> **Note:**
>
> Depending on the template you select, you may already have a Members Area on your site. You can check this by [viewing the apps installed on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-apps) from your site's dashboard or the Editor.

Once a guest is logged in as a member, they can also view their private information, including:

My Address  My Account  My Wallet
