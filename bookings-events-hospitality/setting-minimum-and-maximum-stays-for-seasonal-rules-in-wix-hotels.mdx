You can easily set the minimum and maximum number of nights visitors can stay during periods when seasonal rates are in effect.

### To set minimum and maximum nights visitors can book during a season:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Price Options** tab.Click **Seasonal Rules**.Click  to edit an existing rule or click **Create New**.Enter the minimum number of nights in the **Min Nights** field.Enter the maximum number of nights in the **Max Nights** field.Click **Save**.
