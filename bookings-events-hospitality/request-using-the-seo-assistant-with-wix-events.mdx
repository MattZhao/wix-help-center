---
title: "Request: Using the SEO Assistant with Wix Events"
---

Currently, the SEO Assistant is not available to use with Wix Events.

> **What can I currently use the SEO Assistant with?**
>
> The Wix SEO Assistant is only available for [your site pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/optimizing-your-site-with-the-wix-seo-assistant) and [Wix Blog post pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/improving-your-pages-seo-with-the-wix-seo-assistant).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
