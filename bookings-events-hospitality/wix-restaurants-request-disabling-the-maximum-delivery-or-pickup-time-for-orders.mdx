---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Disabling the Maximum Delivery or Pickup Time for Orders"
---

Currently, you are required to enter the maximum delivery or pickup time when setting up your delivery areas or the pickup option. It is not possible to leave these fields blank and manually inform your customers of the relevant times.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
