---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Creating a Holiday Menu"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create a holiday menu for your restaurant in Wix Restaurants. A holiday menu is designed for a specific holiday with items that you offer only for the holiday season.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> You have the flexibility to create a new menu, save it as a draft, and publish it when the holiday season arrives. You can also manually unpublish it and save it as a draft until the next holiday.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
