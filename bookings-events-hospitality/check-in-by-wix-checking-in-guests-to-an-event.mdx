### Checking in guests to an event

Easily manage guest check-ins for your event by customizing your preferences. You can filter attendees based on ticket type and status, or sort by arrival status for efficient coordination.

Check-in appDashboard
1. Open the Check-in by Wix app.
2. Tap the relevant event.
3. Choose what you want to do:
Manually check in a guest
1. Tap **Guest List** under **Event management**.
2. (Optional) Filter your guest list:

1. Tap the **Filter**  icon.
2. Choose what you want to sort by: status, ticket type or alphabetically.
3. Tap **Save.**
3. Tap the **Checkmark **icon  next to the relevant guest.
Scan a guest's ticketIf your event offers various ticket types (e.g. VIP, Early Bird, Standard), you can streamline guest check-ins by scanning their ticket when they arrive.
1. Tap **Scan tickets **under **Event management**.** **
2. Scan the QR code found on event tickets.

**Tip:** You can choose to scan a specific ticket type by tapping **Change** next to **Scan by ticket type**. If you scan the wrong ticket, an error message will pop up, asking if you want to continue scanning that ticket type.

1. [Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant ticketed event.
3. Click the **Guests **tab.
4. Select an option:

- **Check in an individual guest: **Select the guest in the **Check-in **column.

### I accidentally checked someone in to an event. Can I change their status?

Yes. You can tap next to the relevant guest to update the correct status. For example, if a guest hasn't paid, you can update their status until they until they pay for their ticket.
**From the Check-in by Wix app:**Tap **Arrived** to change the guest's arrival status. 

**From your site's dashboard:**Deselect the guest's checkbox under **Check-in**.

### What happens if I try to check in someone who hasn't paid?

If you set up [manual payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments) as a payment option, guests can purchase tickets and pay you offline. 
If they go to check in and still haven't paid, you'll receive a pop-up notification that the guest hasn't paid. You can then decide whether to go ahead and check them in, or not.

### What happens if I try to check in someone who I manually removed from the guest list?

If you scan the QR code of someone you removed from the guest list, you'll get a notification that the guest was removed. You then have the choice whether to let them check in or not.
