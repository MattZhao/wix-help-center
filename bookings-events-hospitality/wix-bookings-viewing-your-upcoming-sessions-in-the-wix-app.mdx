### Viewing the booking calendar in the Wix app

Access your booking calendar in the Wix app to view and manage your sessions. Choose from different views based on your preference, and filter them to quickly find and manage sessions. You can tap any booking to see details like the participant list, time, and date.

#### To view the booking calendar in the Wix app

Wix appWix Studio app
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Calendar**.
4. Tap **Calendar**.
5. Tap the **Change View** icon  at the top right.
6. Choose your **Calendar Settings**:
Calendar viewTap to select your preferred view:
- **Schedule:** See all upcoming sessions in order, regardless of time in between them (e.g. sessions on Friday and Monday will appear next to each other if your business is closed on weekends). This is a convenient way to book clients in for their next session
- **Day ****view****: **See all upcoming sessions for a specific day, including empty time slots. This is useful for scheduling clients or creating new services.
- **3 day: **View sessions over a 3-day period. This is great for scheduling new bookings, especially during busy periods.
Advanced filtersTap the filter(s) you want to apply to your calendar:

**Note:** If you leave the Bookings area, your calendar filters won't be saved.
- **Staff:** Tap the checkbox next to the relevant staff member(s) you want to display, then tap **Select**.
- **Location: **Tap a location: **All locations**, **Business locations**, **Custom location**, or **Client's place**, then tap **Select**.

**Note: **If you have multiple **Business locations**, tap the checkbox next to the relevant location(s).
- **Services: **If relevant, tap the category and then tap the services you want to filter. Tap **Select**.

**Tip: **Tap the **All** checkbox next to a category to add an entire category.
- **Other events:** Tap the checkbox next to the event you want to display: **From synced calendar** or **Blocked time**.
7. Tap **A****pply**/**Clear Filters**.

### Viewing and managing pending appointments in your Wix app

For appointments where you [enable manual approval](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-requiring-approval-for-appointment-bookings#enabling-approval-for-appointment-bookings), you can manage booking requests directly in the Wix app. A notification appears at the bottom of your booking calendar when a new request comes in. You can tap this notification to approve, decline, or reschedule the requests.

Wix appWix Studio app
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Calendar**.
4. Tap **Calendar**.
5. Tap **View** on the appointment request notification at the bottom of your screen.
6. Tap the relevant appointment request.
7. Choose what you want to do:

- **Approve the appointment: **Tap **Approve** to add the appointment to your calendar.
- **Decline the appointment: **Tap **Decline**. If you want to send your client an email, tap the **Notify client with an email **checkbox to select it. Then tap **Decline **to confirm.
- **Reschedule the appointment: **Tap **Reschedule **to [move the appointment to a different time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-rescheduling-a-session). Edit the session details and then tap **Save**.

**Tip: **Make sure to let your clients know of any schedule changes.
