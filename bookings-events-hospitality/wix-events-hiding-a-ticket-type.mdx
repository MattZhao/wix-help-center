---
title: "Wix Events: Hiding a Ticket Type"
---

You can hide any ticket types (e.g. VIP ticket, early bird ticket) from your site if the ticket is no longer or not yet available.

### To hide a ticket type:

[Go to the Events tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Edit** on the relevant event.  Click the **Tickets** tab.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant ticket.  Select **Hide**.

> **Tips:**
>
> - To display a hidden ticket, click the More Actions icon  and select **Show**.
> - You can also [preselect dates when ticket purchases for an event opens and closes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-setting-registration-opening-closing-times-in-advance) (e.g. registration opens a month before the event and closes a day before the event).
