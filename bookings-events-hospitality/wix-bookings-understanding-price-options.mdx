---
title: "Wix Bookings: Understanding Price Options"
---

Price options allow you to charge for your services based on different factors. You can create custom price options for each service to suit your business, your staff, and your clients.

### Show me Price option examples:

Below are some examples of how you can use price options to offer more flexible pricing for your clients. Offer different price options based on:

- **The type of participant booking the session: **Offer different price options for adults, children, students and senior citizens.
- **The type of a particular service the client wants to book: **Offer different different prices for different types or durations of massage, for example.
- **The staff member providing the service:** Allow clients to book services based on the staff member and set different prices for each staff member, if required.
- **The location of the service:** Offer discounts to encourage clients to book at your new location, for example.
- **The time or date of the service:** Give clients discounted rates during off-peak hours, for example.
- **The number of participants: **Allow clients to make group bookings at different rates, with price breaks for larger groups.

> **Important:**
>
> Please note that your clients won't be able to reschedule sessions paid with price options.

---

### Offer different prices based on staff members' expertise

You can create pricing options for appointments based on the staff member in charge of the service. This is a great way to offer clients more flexible pricing options to suit their needs. It also allows your more experienced staff to focus on high value clients.

---

### Create custom pricing options

You can also offer different custom pricing options depending on your needs. For example, you can charge different rates for ski lessons during the off season and during the high season. Or you can offer discounted rates to students and senior citizens.
