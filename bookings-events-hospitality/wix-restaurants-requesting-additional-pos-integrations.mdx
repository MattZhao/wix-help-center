---
title: "Wix Restaurants: Requesting Additional POS Integrations"
---

Currently, integrating Wix Restaurants with a POS (Point of Sale) system is partially supported. You can [connect your Wix Restaurant site to Square POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-connecting-your-wix-restaurant-to-square-pos-point-of-sale) in the US, or [use a webhook](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-using-a-webhook-to-integrate-wix-restaurants-with-third-party-services) to integrate Wix Restaurants with a POS.

We are constantly working to add additional Point of Sale systems and we'd like to hear from you. Click on a POS listed below to vote for it:

[Clover POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-request-integrating-wix-restaurants-with-clover-pos-point-of-sale-system)  [Toast POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-request-integrating-wix-restaurants-with-toast-pos-point-of-sale-system)  [Revel POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-request-integrating-wix-restaurants-with-revel-pos-point-of-sale-system)

> **Can't find the POS system you want on the list?**
>
> If you want to request another POS system, please [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact/en) to let us know.
