---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Limiting the Number of Dishes Customers Can Order"
---

Currently, you can't limit the number of dishes a customer can order from a specific menu section (e.g. only 2 appetizers per order).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
