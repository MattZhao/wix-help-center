> **Important:**
>
> This article is about the [New Wix Restaurants Orders app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

Add a service fee to online orders in your Wix Restaurant site. Cover operational costs and state regulation fees.

### To add a service fee:

[Go to Service Fees](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-restaurants-orders-new/settings/service-fee?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Service+Fees) in your site's dashboard.  Click **+ New Service Fee**.  Add your service fee details and set the conditions: 

1. **Service name: **Add a name for your service fee.
2. **Amount: **Enter your service fee. It can be a fixed amount ($) or a percentage (%).

**Note:** Customers will see the service fee name and amount during checkout.
3. **(Optional) Add sales tax: **Click the checkbox to add sales tax to this service fee and select a [tax group](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-tax-groups-using-manual-tax-calculation).
4. **Apply based on fulfillment types: **Choose to apply on pickup orders, delivery orders, or both.
5. **Apply based on subtotal: **Choose what orders to apply the service fee to:

- **Set minimum****: **Orders placed above the amount you set will be charged this service fee.
- **Set range: **Orders between a minimum and maximum amount will be charged this service fee.
6. Click **Save**.
