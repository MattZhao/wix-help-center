---
title: "Wix Events Request: Adding an Attachment to the Confirmation Email"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add an attachment to the confirmation email sent to guests.

> **Workaround:**
>
> It is possible to create an automated email that is sent whenever an action happens (e.g. whenever a visitor RSVPs for an event). [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-about-automated-emails-for-events)While editing your automation, you can add a button and link it to a document.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
