Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

Add a calendar to any page of your site to show your hotel availability. Customers can click the **Show Availability For** drop-down menu to select which room they want to view.

### To add a calendar:

Click Wix Hotels app inside the Editor.Click **Settings**.Click the **Add-Ons** tab icon.

Click **Add to Site** drop-down menu next to the **Calendar**.Select the page you want to place the calendar on. Drag the calendar to where you want it to appear on the page.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-hotel-availability-calendar) to learn how to change the design of the calendar.
