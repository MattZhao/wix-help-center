---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Using Employee Meal Cards to Order"
---

Currently, it is not possible to have your customers place orders on your Wix Restaurant site using employee meal cards. Employers issue these prepaid cards or vouchers to facilitate convenient payment that covers the cost of their employees' meals at affiliated restaurants and cafes.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
