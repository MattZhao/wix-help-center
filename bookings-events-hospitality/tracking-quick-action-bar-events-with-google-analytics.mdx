Using Google Analytics, you can track any visitor's interaction with the Quick Action bar on your mobile site. Get notified for every time a user engages or tries to contact you through the Quick Action bar. To get started, [connect Google Analytics to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tracking-events-on-your-wix-site-with-a-google-analytics-property).

### Which events can I track?

By default, Wix sends information to Google Analytics about the following Quick Action bar events:

**Leads: **Whenever a visitor on your mobile site tries to contact you using the Quick Action bar, by clicking the phone, email, WhatsApp or LINE buttons. **Engagement: **When a visitor clicks any other item on your mobile site's Quick Action bar.

#### To view the events in your Google Analytics account:

Sign in to your [Google Analytics](http://www.google.com/analytics/) account.   Click **Reports **in the menu on the left.  Select **Engagements**.  Select **Events**.

> **Important:**
>
> It can take up to 24 hours for your events to appear in Google Analytics.

> **Tip:**
>
> Use the Google Analytics Real-Time report to test your events and make sure they're logged. [Learn More](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1638635?hl=en)
