You can set up a stunning Wix Hotels site for free, but [upgrading your site](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) gives you access to additional features to offer your guests even better service.

**Wix Hotels upgraded features:**

**Allow guests to make reservations on your site:** Let your guests book directly from your website for a seamless experience.  **Accept secure online payments: **Ensure your guests' payments are safe and secure.  **Remove Wix branding: **Customize your site by removing Wix branding for a more professional look.  **Advanced pricing options (seasonal rates, extra charges): **Optimize your revenue with flexible pricing strategies.  **Free trial for the Wix Hotels Channel Manager (by HotelRunner): **Test out the Channel Manager with no initial cost.

### To upgrade Wix Hotels from the Editor:

If you have not already done so, [add Wix Hotels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-wix-hotels) to your site.  Go to the Hotel page in the Editor.  Click the Wix Hotels app inside the Editor.  Click the **Upgrade** icon .  Click **Select **under the relevant you want to purchase.  Select a subscription period and click **Continue to Checkout**.  Enter your payment details and follow the on-screen instructions to complete your purchase.

> **Important:**
>
> After submitting your purchase, your bank may [require you to verify your identity](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds). If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.
