---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Filtering Booking Service Lists on the Wix Member Apps"
---

Currently, your clients can filter the service list on your site by various parameters like staff member, location, service, or category.

However, it is not possible for clients to filter this list on the relevant member apps.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
