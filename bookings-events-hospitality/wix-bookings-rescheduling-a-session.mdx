---
title: "Wix Bookings: Rescheduling a Session"
---

Plans change sometimes, but with Wix Bookings, you're covered. Reschedule client sessions directly from your Booking calendar. If a service cannot take place at the scheduled date or time, reschedule it from your Booking calendar. You can also update any other session details as needed and choose whether to send an email to participants about the change.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Rescheduling appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-rescheduling-a-session#rescheduling-an-appointment)  [Rescheduling class or course sessions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-rescheduling-a-session#rescheduling-class-or-course-sessions)

---

### Rescheduling an appointment

Reschedule appointments directly from your Booking calendar. Change the date, time, and location to keep your schedule organized. You can also edit additional appointment details and notify clients about the changes.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the appointment you want to reschedule.
3. Click the **Edit** icon at the top of the session.
4. Edit the appointment date and/or time and location.
5. (Optional) Click the **Edit** iconnext to the appointment name to edit additional session details. Then click **Update**.
6. (Optional) Select the **Notify client via email** checkbox and add a message to update your customer.
7. Click **Save & Collect Payment** or **Save**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Calendar**.
4. Tap **Calendar**.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon next to the relevant session.
6. Tap **Edit**.
7. Edit the date, location and session start time.
8. (Optional) Edit additional session details, such as duration, staff member and relevant resources.
9. (Optional) Tap **Show free time slots **to view other available time slots of the staff member and select a new time.
10. (Optional) Tap **Add note to self **to add a personal note about the appointment.
11. (Optional) Tap the **Notify client with an email **checkbox and add a message to update your customer.
12. Tap **Save**.

### Rescheduling class or course sessions

Reschedule a class or course session directly from your Booking calendar. Adjust the timing or details of any upcoming sessions.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant session to reschedule.
3. Click **Edit.**
4. (If necessary) Choose which sessions you want to edit:** This session**, **All future sessions**, or **Full schedule**. 

**Note: **For** All future sessions**,** **sessions that take place on other days and times have to be edited separately.
[image: A screenshot showing where you can click to reschedule your session on your desktop.]
1. Choose what you want to do:

- Edit start date and/or time of the session.
- (Optional) Edit any additional details of the session.
- Optional) Click **+ Add a note to yourself **to add a personal note about the session.
- (Optional) Click the **Update participants about changes with an email **checkbox and add a message to update your clients.
2. Click **Save**.
Tip:You can completely reschedule the times, dates, locations and staff members for a class by clicking **Edit** and then clicking** Full schedule**. [image: A screenshot of where to click Full schedule to edit your whole schedule.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Calendar**.
4. Tap **Calendar**.
5. Tap the relevant session that you want to reschedule.
6. Tap the **More Actions** icon at the top of your screen.
7. Tap what you want to edit: **Edit this session **or **Edit all sessions** (for the specific day and time).
[image: A screenshot of how to edit and reschedule your sessions on the Wix app.]

2. Tap **Save**/**Add Sessions**.
3. (Optional) Tap **Yes** to send booked participants an email about any changes.
