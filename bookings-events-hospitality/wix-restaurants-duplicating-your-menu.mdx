Duplicate your menu to create another draft of it. This option is perfect for experimenting with new ideas or creating tailored versions of your menu for different occasions.

All menu sections and items are duplicated, and if you're using Wix Restaurant Orders (New), modifiers, variants, tax, stock and settings are duplicated as well.

### Duplicating your menu

[Go to Menus](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/restaurants/menus-management?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Menus+(new)) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the title of your menu.  Select **Duplicate menu**.  Click **Duplicate**.  Review, make changes or publish your duplicated menu.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about duplicating your menu:

### Which elements are copied over when I duplicate my menus?

The elements that get duplicated are the menu, its sections and items (including names, descriptions, prices and images). You can make any edits you need on the duplicated version without affecting the original menu. 
You'll also see the variants, labels and modifiers you've set up in the original menu. These elements are not copied but reused, which means updating them on the duplicated version also affects the original menu.

### I'm also using the Online Orders app, is anything copied over from there?

If you're using the Wix Online Orders (New) app, your stock, tax settings and modifier groups get duplicated as well.
