title: "Wix Events POS Request: Assigning a Seat at a Ticket Sale"
---

Currently, it's not possible to assign a specific seat in your [seating map](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-a-seating-map) while selling events tickets at a mobile POS.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
