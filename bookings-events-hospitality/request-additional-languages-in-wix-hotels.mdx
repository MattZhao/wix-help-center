> **Note:**
>
> Currently, Wix Hotels supports the following languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Korean, Japanese, Dutch, Danish, Norwegian, Polish, Russian, Swedish and Turkish. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-languages-in-wix-hotels) to learn how to add languages in Wix Hotels.

What languages would you like to add to Wix Hotels? Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/contact?skipTroubleshooters=true) to submit a ticket with your feedback and suggestions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
