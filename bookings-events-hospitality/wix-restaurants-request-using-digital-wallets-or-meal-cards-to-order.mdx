---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Using Digital Wallets or Meal Cards to Order"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to [accept payments with digital wallets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview). This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

> **Note:**
>
> Currently, Wix Restaurants does not support online ordering using employee meal cards. [Vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-request-using-employee-meal-cards-to-order),.

> **Important:**
>
> Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
