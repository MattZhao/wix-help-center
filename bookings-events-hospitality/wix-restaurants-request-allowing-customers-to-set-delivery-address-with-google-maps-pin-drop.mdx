---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Allowing Customers to Set Delivery Address with Google Maps Pin Drop"
---

Some people do not have a street address and can only indicate their location in Google Maps by dropping a pin or inputting their latitude and longitude. Currently, Wix Restaurants does not support this feature.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
