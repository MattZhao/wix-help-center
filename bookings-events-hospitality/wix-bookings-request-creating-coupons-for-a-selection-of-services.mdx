---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating Coupons for a Selection of Services"
---

Currently, it's not possible to create coupons that are valid for a selection of your services.

However, you can create coupons that are valid for individual services, or coupons that are valid for all your services. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-discount-coupons)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
