> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about the **original Restaurants Orders app. **
> - We've released a new online ordering app. Learn how to [add and set up the new Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

Easily reject a restaurant order from the Wix app. Once you reject an order it is also removed from the **New Orders** and **In Progress** tabs.

### To reject an order in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Orders** under **Business**.  Tap on the order you want to reject in the **New** tab. You can also do this for an order you've already accepted.  Tap the Show More  icon at the top right.  Tap **Reject Order**.

  Enter a reason for why you are rejecting the order.

**Note:** The reason for the rejection is shown on the customer's order page.  Tap **Reject** at the top right.  [Contact](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-contacting-restaurant-customers-from-the-wix-owner-app) the customer to let them know that their order has been rejected.

> **Important:**
>
> - After you reject an order you must contact the customer to let them know that their order has been unsuccessful. To learn how to contact a customer from the Wix app, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-contacting-restaurant-customers-from-the-wix-owner-app).
> - Rejecting an order does not automatically void or refund the transaction. You must void the transaction in the dashboard of your chosen payment gateway. To locate the transaction ID, tap the order in the **Orders** tab. The ID is under the payment information.
