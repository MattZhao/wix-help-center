---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Separating Online Ordering Menus into Pages"
---

Currently, it is not possible to break long online ordering menus up into numbered pages.

As a workaround solution, you can create separate [menu sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-displaying-multiple-restaurant-menus-on-your-site#displaying-different-restaurant-menus-on-different-pages) for each "page" and name them *Dinner Menu - Page 1, Dinner Menu - Page 2, *for example.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
