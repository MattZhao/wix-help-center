> **Important:**

> - You must [connect a domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-you-own-to-your-wix-site) to your site to add a 'Book' button to your Instagram page.

Add a Book Now button to your Instagram business page to make it easier for clients to book your services while browsing social media. This is a great way to boost bookings for your new services during promotional campaigns on social media.

### Adding a Service List 'Book' button to your Instagram business page

Add a single service calendar 'Book' button to your Instagram business page to direct clients to the calendar for a specific service. They can then choose the session date and time that suits them.

#### To add a Service list 'Book' button to Instagram:

[Go to Booking Integrations](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fbookings%2Fintegrations%3F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Booking+Integrations) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Connect** under Instagram.  Click the drop-down and click **Service List**.  Click **Continue**.  Review the link settings for the Facebook Business Manager, Facebook Page and Meta Pixel. **Note:** [Visit the Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help) for more information.  (Optional) Enable the **Automatic advanced matching** toggle to increase the visibility of your business to users on Instagram.  Click **Continue**.  Click **Next**.  Click **Done**.

### Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

Here are some frequently asked questions about adding a 'Book' button to Instagram:

### Can I update my 'Book' button links?

You can disconnect or change the 'Book' button link at any time by clicking **Manage** under Instagram in Booking Channels in your site dashboard.
**To update your 'Book' button link:**
1. Clicking **Manage** under Instagram in Booking Channels in your site dashboard.
2. Enter the updated link in the **Where are you linking to?** field.
3. Click **Save**.

**To disconnect the 'Book' button:**
1. Clicking **Manage** under Instagram in Booking Channels in your site dashboard.
2. Click **Disconnect**.
3. Click **Save**.

### What happens if I delete a service linked to my 'Book' button on Instagram?

When you delete the linked service, the 'Book' button no longer leads your clients anywhere. You see an error message in Bookings channel in your site dashboard, but you can link the 'Book' button to another service or link.

### Can I link a course to my single service calendar 'Book'?

Single service calendar is not available for courses.

### Troubleshooting adding a 'book' button to Instagram

Click to choose from the list of common errors below, where it is not possible to add a 'book' button to Instagram.

### Your request is pending review.

Your request is pending review. Assigning new Facebook Page admins can take up to 7 days to be approved. [Learn more about the approval process](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/183277585892925?id=420299598837059&helpref=search&sr=3&query=business%20manager%20admin)

### Contact the admin of "Business Manager Name" for permission to connect this page.

Contact the administrator of the Facebook page and request admin permission to connect this page to Wix Bookings. [Learn more about admin permissions](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/183277585892925?id=420299598837059&helpref=search&sr=3&query=business%20manager%20admin)

### You need permission from the admin of "Ad account name" to connect this pixel. Contact the account admin for access.

You need to request admin permissions from the Business Manager account to connect a pixel. Learn more about [removing or changing permissions on Facebook ad accounts](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/186007118118684?id=829106167281625).

### "Page Name" is not connected to "Commerce Account Name". Please check your Commerce Manager settings

1. Select the page associated with the "chosen" CMS.
2. Go back and change the asset associated with that different CMS.
3. Delete the CMS associated with the previous assets.

Learn more about [changing your Commerce manager settings](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1084884704856011?id%3D533228987210412&sa=D&source=docs&ust=1690040756612229&usg=AOvVaw03RLyvBOj1qKFqR_rK0WPe).

### This is the primary page of "Business Name". Choose another page or review your Business Manager settings.

You can resolve this error in one of two ways:
- Go back and select a different business.
- Go to Business Manager and remove the page as the primary page and then restart the flow from the beginning.
Learn more about [changing your primary page in the Business manager](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1809771339071540?id=180505742745347&helpref=search&sr=12&query=business%20manager%20primary%20page).

### Request permission from the admin of "Business Manager Name" to continue.

You need to request admin permissions in Business Manager.
Learn more about [adding people to a business account and assigning a business asset](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2169003770027706?id=2190812977867143).

### Contact the admin of "Business manager name" for permission to connect this page.

You need to request permission from owner of the Business Manager account.
Learn more about [requesting access to a page in the Meta Business Manager](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/183277585892925?id=420299598837059&helpref=search&sr=3&query=business%20manager%20admin).
