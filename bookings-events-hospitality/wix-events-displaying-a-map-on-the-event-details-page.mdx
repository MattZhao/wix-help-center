---
title: "Wix Events: Displaying a Map on the Event Details Page"
---

With Wix Events, you can display a map on your Event Details page. This handy tool not only makes your event easy to locate for your attendees but also adds a professional touch to your site.

By adding a map to your Event Details page, you're simplifying the journey for your guests, ensuring they find the venue without any hassle.

### To display a map on the Event Details page:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to the Event Details & Registration page: 

1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Event Pages**.
3. Click **Event Details & Registration**.
3. Click the Event Details** **element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Display** tab.
6. Enable the **Map** toggle to display a map on your Event Details page.
[image: A screenshot of enabling the Map toggle in the Wix Editor.]
