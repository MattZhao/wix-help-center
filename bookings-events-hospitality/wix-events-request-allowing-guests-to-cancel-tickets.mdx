---
title: "Wix Events Request: Allowing Guests to Cancel Tickets"
---

Currently, guests cannot cancel their participation in a ticketed event themselves. They need to contact you so you can [remove them from the guest list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-managing-ticket-orders). If your guest bought an event ticket, you'll need to [provide a refund](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-canceling-and-refunding-orders-for-events-with-tickets#step-3-refund-guests).

> **Tip:**
>
> Guest can, however, cancel their participation in RSVP events. They can do so through their Members Area.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
