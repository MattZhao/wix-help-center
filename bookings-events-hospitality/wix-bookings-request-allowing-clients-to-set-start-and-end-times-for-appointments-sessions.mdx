---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Allowing Clients to Set Start and End Times for Appointments Sessions"
---

Currently, customers cannot choose the start and end times of their Appointments in Wix Bookings. The time slots are determined when setting up the service.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
