---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Limiting Multi-Service Appointments to a Single Staff Member"
---

Currently, it is not possible to limit [multi-service appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-and-managing-multi-service-appointments) to a single staff member. For example, at present, when a client books a manicure and pedicure, they have the option to book a separate staff member for each service.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
