---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Adding Multiple Pickup Locations"
---

Currently, you can [set up one pickup location](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-pickup-and-delivery-in-wix-restaurant-orders-new#setting-up-pickup-and-delivery) for online orders on your live site. However, it is not possible to add more than one pickup location.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also vote to [manage services across multiple locations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-request-managing-restaurant-services-across-multiple-locations) with the Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
