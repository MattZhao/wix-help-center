Wix Bookings is an easy-to-use scheduling system that lets your members book and [pay for](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-getting-paid-online) services online, so you can focus on managing your business.

Whether you offer [services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-the-right-booking-service-for-your-business) like workshops, courses, or private sessions, online or in-person, Wix Bookings has you covered.

> **Important:**
>
> You can add Wix Bookings to your site for free, but you must [upgrade your site](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) to accept bookings. Some Wix templates come with Wix Bookings included.

### Adding Wix Bookings to your site

Add Wix Bookings to your site from the Wix App Market. Then complete the setup in your site's editor.

#### To add Wix Bookings to your site:

Go to the [Wix Bookings app in the App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market/wix-bookings).  Click **Add to Site**.  Go to your editor.   Complete the setup by clicking **Add to Site** or** Add App**.

### Accepting online and offline payments

With Wix Bookings, you can offer your clients flexibility by accepting both online and offline payments options. Choose from credit card, Paypal or in-person payments that best suit your business and your clients.

> **Important:**
>
> You must [upgrade your site](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) in order to accept online payments.

### Show me how

1. Go to [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2F) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the payment methods you want to use, or click** See More Payment Options**.
3. Click **Complete Setup** / **Connect** for each payment method required and follow the steps provided.

Learn more about [accepting](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-getting-paid-offline) payments on Wix Bookings.

### Selling membership plans and packages

Offering membership plans and packages is a great way to keep your clients coming back for more, filling up your sessions and offering the best value for money.

> **Important:**
>
> Membership plans and packages are currently not available for courses.

### Show me how

1. Go to [Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings) in your site's dashboard.
2. Create a new appointment or class, or edit an existing one.
3. Scroll down to the **Price and Payment **section.
4. Choose the relevant price option in the **Price type** drop-down:

- **Fixed Price:** Set a standard price for the service.
- **Free:** Choose this option for free service.
- **Varied prices:** Create different price options depending on either the client type or the staff member.

**Note: **This feature is not available for everyone yet.
- **Custom Price:** Describe how you charge for the service, for example, "Price negotiable".
5. Create the pricing plan and connect the booking service to it:

1. Click **Create New Plan **next to **Memberships & Packages**.
2. Click **Membership**.
3. Click **Next**.
4. Add all the relevant details about the membership including the name, description, benefits, price, duration, and more. **

**Learn more about [creating membership plans and packages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings).

### Offering services in multiple locations

As your business grows, add additional locations where you offer your services while keep the same ease of use for your clients through Wix Bookings.

### Show me how

1. [Go to Business Info](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/business-info?referralInfo=settings-lobby?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Business+Info) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to the **Location & Contact Info** section.
3. If you have not already done so, enter your first location:

1. Enter your business address.
2. (Optional) Enter a description (e.g. Enter from the back).
3. Enter contact info relevant for this location.
4. Click **+ Add New Location **and repeat the steps above.
5. Click **Save**.

Learn more about [offering services at multiple locations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-services-at-multiple-locations).

### Managing your calendars

The Wix Bookings calendar is where you manage your appointments, classes and courses and make quick scheduling changes when needed.

> **Tip:**
>
> Avoid scheduling conflicts between your business and personal life by [syncing your personal calendar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-syncing-personal-calendars-with-wix-bookings) with your Wix Bookings Calendar.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [Bookings Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Choose how to [filter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-filtering-your-bookings-calendar) the calendar view between daily or weekly.
3. Next choose whether to: 

1. [Manually add an appointment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-manually-scheduling-an-appointment).
2. Schedule when a [class](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-your-classes) or [course](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-your-courses) takes place.
3. [Cancel an existing session](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-canceling-a-session).

Learn more about [managing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-the-wix-booking-calendar) your calendars.

### Customizing your booking form

The booking form is a great way to learn more about your clients and their needs before they attend one of your sessions.

Whether you require a heads up about allergies, specific health details such as injuries or recent surgeries, or any other need-to-know information in order to provide the best service possible, make sure to add it to your site's booking form.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings) tab in your site's dashboard.
2. Click More Actions   beside the relevant service.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Scroll down to Additional Settings and click **Show More**.
5. Select **Edit Default Booking Form**.

Learn more about ways to [customize](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-setting-up-your-booking-forms) your booking form.

### Sending reminder emails or SMS messages to clients

Make sure your clients remember to show up for their upcoming services by sending them SMS (text message) reminders or emails 24 hours before a session starts.

### Show me how to enable reminder emails

1. Go to [Booking Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/settings) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Emails & SMS Reminders**.
3. Enable the toggles next to the relevant emails you want to send to clients automatically.

Learn more about [setting up reminder emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-your-outbound-client-emails#customizing-your-outbound-client-emails).

### Show me how to enable SMS reminders

1. Go to [Booking Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/settings) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Emails & SMS Reminders**.
3. Click the **Session Reminders **toggle to enable it.

Learn more about [activating](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sending-sms-reminders-to-clients-in-wix-bookings) SMS reminders.

### Viewing clients' past bookings

View and update clients' past bookings, including payment and attendance information, using the contacts tab in your site dashboard.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [Contacts tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Contact%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/contacts/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the relevant client and click **View**.
3. Click the **Bookings **tab.

Learn more about [reviewing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-viewing-a-clients-booking-and-payment-history) clients' past bookings.
