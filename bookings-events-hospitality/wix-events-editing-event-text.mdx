---
title: "Wix Events: Editing Event Text"
---

You can edit the text that appears in the Wix Events app so that it matches your site's style. To edit the name of an event, head over to the [Events tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Editing options in the **Text** tab vary depending on the selections you made in the **Layout** and **Display **tabs.
> - Text options vary depending on selections you make in the **My Events**, **Layout **and **Display **tabs.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to the Events page: 

1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Event Pages**.
3. Click your Events page.
[image: A screenshot of selecting the Events page in the Wix Editor.]
1. Click the Event List element on your page.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Text **tab.
4. Edit the available text options.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to the Events page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Events page.
3. Click the Event List element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
[image: A screenshot of the Event List element in the Studio Editor.]
1. Click the **Text** tab.
2. Edit the available text options.
