---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Duplicating Dish Extras"
---

Currently, you [can add extras to dishes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-managing-dish-options) in your online ordering menu, but it is not possible to duplicate these extras and add them to multiple dishes.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
