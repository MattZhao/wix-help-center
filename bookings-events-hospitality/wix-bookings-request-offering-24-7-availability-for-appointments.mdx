---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering 24/7 Availability for Appointments"
---

Wix Bookings currently does not support setting appointments with true 24/7 availability. This feature would allow businesses to offer appointments at any time of day, every day of the week, without any gaps in availability.

> **Workaround:**
>
> To offer 24/7 availability in Wix Bookings, you can set each day from 00:00 to 00:00 (+1 day) both as [default hours](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-setting-default-work-hours) and for [staff hours](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-editing-your-staff-members-working-hours). 
>
> **Note:** Setting hours from 00:00 to 23:59 leaves one minute unaccounted for and can cause issues with availability.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
