---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Ability to Pre-Order Meals for In-House Dining"
---

Some restaurants allow customers to place orders before arriving at a full service, sit-down restaurant, for example, to help beat the lunchtime rush.

Currently, pre-ordering meals in this way is not supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
