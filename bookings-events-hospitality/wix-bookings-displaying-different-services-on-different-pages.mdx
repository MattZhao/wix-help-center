### Adding and setting up an additional service list

Categorize different services you offer by creating another service list page. This makes it easier for your clients to find and book the service they need.

#### To add another service list:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Bookings**.
4. Click **Service List **then select a Service List template.
[image: A screenshot showing how to add a Service List to a page in the Editor.]
1. Click the Service List element on your page.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Services **tab.
4. Under **How do you want to choose which services to display?**, select the checkbox next to the relevant option: **Choose by service category**, **Choose by service location**,** **or **Choose services directly**.
5. Click to select the checkbox next to the relevant categories, locations, or services you want to display. The options you see depend on your choice in step 9 above.
[image: Screenshot showing how to choose how you display services on your service list.]
1. Click **Save**.
2. Click **Publish** when you're ready to make your changes go live.

### Setting up a page with services for site members only

Offer certain services for site members only. This is a great way to reward your regular clients.

#### To set up a page with services for site members only:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Hide the Book Online page from your site's menu: 

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Bookings Pages**.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the Book Online page and select **Settings**.
4. Enable the **Hide from menu** toggle.
[image: Screenshot of hiding a page in the Wix Editor.]
1. Click **Site Menu **in the **Site Pages and Menu** panel.
2. Click **+ Add Menu Item** at the bottom of the **Site Pages and Menu **panel and select **New Page**. Then select a preset page or a blank page for displaying your members only services.
3. Add a Service List (see [adding and setting up an additional service list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-displaying-selected-services-on-different-pages#adding-and-setting-up-an-additional-service-list)).
4. Click the Service List widget on your page.
5. Click **Settings**.
6. Click the **Services **tab.
7. Select only the services that you are reserving for site members.
8. Set the page permissions to **Members Only**: 

1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the new page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click the **Permissions** tab.
5. Select **Members Only**.
6. Select **Only selected members or paying customers**.
7. Select which **Member Roles** and **Pricing Plans** gain access to the new page.
[image: A screenshot showing how to make a page for members-only in the Editor.]
1. Click **Save**.
2. Click **Publish** when you're ready to make your changes go live.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. [Hide the Book Online page from your site's menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-managing-pages#hiding-a-page).
3. Add a new page for your members-only page: 

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **+** **Add New Page** at the bottom.
3. Click **Add **under **Page**.
4. Enter a name for your new page and press **Enter **on your keyboard.
4. Set the new page permissions: 

1. Hover over your new page in the **Site Pages** panel and click the **More Actions** icon  .
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Click the **Permissions **tab at the top.
4. Select **Members only**.
5. Select **Only selected members or paying customers**.
6. Select which **Member Roles** and **Pricing Plans** gain access to the new page.
[image: Screenshot of setting page permissions to Members only.]
1. Add a Service List (see [adding and setting up an additional service list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-displaying-selected-services-on-different-pages#adding-and-setting-up-an-additional-service-list)).
2. Click the Service List widget on your page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click the **Services **tab.
5. Select only the services that you are reserving for site members.
6. Click **Save**.
7. Click **Publish** when you're ready to make your changes go live.
