---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Adding an Allergen Info Section to Menu Items"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a separate allergen info section to each menu item. You can, however, [add labels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-wix-restaurants-menus-new) such as 'Vegan' or 'Gluten-Free' to menu items to guide customers with allergies.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
