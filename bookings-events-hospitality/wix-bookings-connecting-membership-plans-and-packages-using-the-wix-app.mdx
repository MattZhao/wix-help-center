### Connecting pricing plans to booking services

Connect your pricing plans to your booking services while you're on the go, using the Wix app. You can connect specific plans to specific services as required. This is a great way to encourage more of your members to sign up for your pricing plans.

#### To connect plans to booking services:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap the **Manage ** icon.  Tap **Bookings** **Services**.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant service.

Tap **Manage plans**.  Tap **Connect** under the relevant package or plan or tap [+ Create new plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app).   Tap** Done** at the top right.

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### What do clients see when they use their plan while booking a service on the Wix Member apps?

When your clients select the session they want to book, they see the payment option to redeem from their package or membership plan. They also see how many sessions they have remaining as part of their package. 
[image: Screenshot showing how clients can redeem sessions from their pricing plan.]
If they have not purchased a plan yet, they see the option to buy a plan before continuing with booking the session. 
[image: Screenshot showing how clients can buy a plan before completing their booking.]

### Can clients pay for group bookings using a package?

Clients can pay for group bookings using their purchased packages on both desktop and in their member apps. Each participant in a group booking uses up 1 session in the package.

For example, if a client buys a 10-session package and makes a group booking with 2 friends using their package, it counts as 3 sessions used. They then have 7 sessions remaining.

### Can clients pay for group bookings using a membership?

Clients cannot use their memberships to pay for group bookings.
