Introducing Check-in by Wix - the ultimate mobile solution for effortless event door management.

As an event organizer, you can assign staff members to manage the guest list and scan tickets, ensuring a smooth check-in process.

Moreover, you can gain valuable insights into event stats to ensure a well-organized event. This includes guest order details such as ticket purchases, payment status and sales trends for your future planning needs.

### To download the Check-in by Wix app:

Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).  Type 'Check-in by Wix' in the search field.  Click **Get** or **Install** to download the app.  Log in with your Wix credentials.

> **You can also download the Check-in app right to your mobile device using these links:**
>
> - [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/6467876058)
> - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wix.events)
