> **Important:**
>
> This article is about the original Wix Restaurants Menus App. Try out our new menu app. Learn how to [add and set up Wix Restaurants Menus (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new).

If your restaurant is located in the US or Canada, you can add tax for each individual dish on your menu. The tax is added to all orders placed through the online ordering system.

### To add tax to a dish:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)  Click the** Menus** tab.  Hover over the relevant dish and click the **Edit** icon  or click **New Dish** to add a new one.  Enter the tax percentage in the **Tax **field.  Click **Save Changes **for an existing dish or** Add Dish **for a new dish.

> **Important:**
>
> Currently, it is only possible to add tax if your restaurant is located in the US (including Puerto Rico, Guam, Western Samoa, Virgin Islands, Northern Mariana Islands), or Canada. If your country or region has similar tax rules to the US and Canada and requires tax to be listed separately, please [click here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-request-adding-tax-to-a-dish-outside-of-the-us-and-canada) to vote for this feature.
