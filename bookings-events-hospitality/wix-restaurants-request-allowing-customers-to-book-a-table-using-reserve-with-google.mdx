---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Allowing Customers to Book a Table Using 'Reserve with Google'"
---

Currently, customers can't book a table at your restaurant using '[Reserve with Google](https://www.google.com/maps/reserve/)'. This would allow your customers to reserve a table directly via Google Search, Google Maps and Google Assistant.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
