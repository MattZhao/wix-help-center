---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Setting Different Payment Methods for Fulfillment Types"
---

Currently, it's not possible to set up different payment methods per fulfillment type. For example, accepting credit card or cash for pickup orders while only credit cards for deliveries.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
