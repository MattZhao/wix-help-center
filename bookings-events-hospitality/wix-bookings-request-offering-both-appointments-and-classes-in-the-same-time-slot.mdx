---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering Both Appointments and Classes in the Same Time Slot"
---

Currently, clients cannot book an Appointment with you if you have a Class offered in the same time slot.

> **Tip:**
>
> As a workaround, it is possible to offer two Class services at the same time. While creating one of them, limit the number of participant to one.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
