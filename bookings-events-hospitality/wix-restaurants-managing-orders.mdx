### Accepting incoming orders

Accept incoming orders from customers and easily keep track of all your restaurant orders.

#### To accept an order:

Go to the [Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/orders) tab in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant order.  Click **Accept** at the top right.

After you accept an order, it moves to the **In Progress** tab. From here, you can mark the order as delivered when you are ready.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can set additional notifications methods (email, text, fax etc.), and backup notifications that are triggered after a set time that an order has not been handled. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-notifications-for-online-orders)

---

### Marking an order as delivered

After you process an order, you can mark the order as delivered. Delivered orders move from the **In Progress** tab to the **All Orders** tab.

#### To mark an order as delivered:

Go to the [Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/orders) tab of your site's dashboard.  Click the **In Progress** tab at the top.  Click the relevant order.  Click **Mark Delivered** at the top right.

---

### Rejecting an order

If you are unable to fulfill an order, or if a guest calls to cancel, you can reject the order no matter what status it's in.

> **To avoid chargebacks and complaints:**
>
> - You have to contact the customer after you reject an order to let them know that their order has been unsuccessful.
> - You must void the transaction in the dashboard of your chosen payment gateway. Marking an order as rejected does not automatically void or refund the transaction. To locate the transaction ID, go to the [**Payments**](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments-dashboard%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Payments) tab in your dashboard. The transaction ID appears under the payment method.

#### To reject an order that has not yet been accepted:

Go to the [Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/orders) tab of your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant order.  Click the **Show More **icon  at the top right and select **Reject**.  Enter the reason for rejection.  Click **Reject **to confirm.

#### To reject an order that has already been accepted:

Go to the [Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/orders) tab of your site's dashboard.  Click the **In Progress** tab.  Click the relevant order.  Click the **Show More **icon  at the top right and select **Reject**.  Enter the reason for rejection.  Click **Reject **to confirm.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also reject an order once it has been marked as delivered. Simply click the **All Orders **tab to find the relevant order.
