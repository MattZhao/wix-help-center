---
title: "Adding a Deposit Fee to Reservations in Wix Hotels"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

If you do not wish to charge your guests the full reservation fee upfront, you can set up Wix Hotels to accept a deposit fee.

### The deposit fee can be the:

Total Charge1 night25% of the cost of the reservation 50% of the cost of the reservation

The remainder of the fee is paid in person, when your guests arrive at your hotel, not through the Wix Hotels system.

> **Important:**
>
> - This option is only available for online payment options ( PayPal, Stripe).
> - When the 1 night, 25%, or 50% options are selected, taxes as well as any extra charges (e.g. room service) need to be collected in person, outside of Wix Hotels.

### To add a deposit fee:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Payments** tab.Scroll down to the **Price Settings** section.Click the **Deposit** drop-down menu and select a deposit option.Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Once your guest has paid the remainder of the fee, you can manually [mark the reservation as Paid](https://support.wix.com/en/article/marking-a-reservation-as-paid-in-wix-hotels) from your hotel reservation calendar.
