---
title: "Wix Bookings POS Request: Assigning a Cashier Role for Mobile POS for Wix Bookings"
---

Currently, you can allow someone else to accept payment at a mobile POS by assigning them roles such as Admin or Back Office Manager + Payments Admin. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-letting-staff-members-accept-payments)

The Cashier role, which gives permission to accept mobile POS payment only, is not currently available for Wix Bookings.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
