---
title: "Request: Listing Dorm Rooms or Shared Accommodation in Wix Hotels"
---

Currently, listing dorm rooms or shared accommodation
  is not supported as it is not possible to list individual beds.

As a workaround solution, you can do the following:

[Add](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-rooms-to-your-hotel-1157755) a room.  Enter the number of beds in **Units**.  Enter 1 guest under **Max Guests**.  Enter 1 bed.  Write in the room description that it is a dorm room.  Enter the rest of the room details.  Click **Save**.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
