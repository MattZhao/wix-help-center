---
title: "Wix Bookings: Sending a Broadcast Message in the Wix App"
---

Send a single chat message, email or SMS to everyone booked for your service at once. This is called a "broadcast message", and when you broadcast to your clients, they receive each message individually and not as a group message.

> **Note:**
>
> Sending marketing texts is regulated worldwide and you maybe be subject to [3rd-party regulations](https://www.twilio.com/legal/messaging-policy). Your SMS messages could be filtered if they are in violation of our terms of use. Learn more about Wix's [messaging terms of use](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-about-wixs-messaging-terms-of-use).

> **Important:**
>
> You must have a [Business Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-a-phone-number) to send an SMS message.

### To send a broadcast message in the Wix app:

Open the Wix app.   Tap the **Manage **icon .  Tap **Bookings**.  Tap the relevant booking.  Tap **Broadcast**.  Select the checkboxes next to the contacts you want to send an SMS broadcast to.  Tap **Next**.  Tap the channel drop-down and select how you want to send your broadcast:

- **Chat: **Send a live chat broadcast to clients.** **They receive this message in their inbox in the member app.
- **Email: **Send a broadcast email to the clients whose email addresses are saved in your contact list.
- **SMS: **Broadcast via SMS to clients if they have provided their contact numbers.

**Note: **Make sure you've [purchased a business phone number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-a-phone-number#step-1-purchase-a-business-phone-number).
    Compose the message that will be sent to your selected contacts.** **You can [attach elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-sending-messages-and-attaching-elements) to your message.  Tap **Send**.
