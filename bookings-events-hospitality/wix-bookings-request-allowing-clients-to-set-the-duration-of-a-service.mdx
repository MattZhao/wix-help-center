---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Allowing Clients to Set the Duration of a Service"
---

Currently, clients can only book services for pre-determined time slots (for example, one-hour treatments offered throughout the day). It is not possible for clients to select the start time and end time of a service.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
