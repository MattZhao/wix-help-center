---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Customizing Different Services' Settings Separately"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [customize the settings of individual services in Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-setting-up-your-booking-policies). This includes adding options for early or late bookings, and cancelling or rescheduling individual services independent of one another.
