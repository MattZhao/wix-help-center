---
title: "Wix Bookings: Hiding the Number of Session Participants in the Wix Member Apps"
---

You can choose to hide the number of registered participants for a class or service in the Wix member apps. When hidden, members only see if the class or service is full or available to be booked.

> **Tip:**
>
> You (the site owner) can still see the number of participants registered for a session.

### To hide the number of participants registered for a session:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Scroll down to Settings and tap **Bookings Settings**.  Tap **Customize Display**.  Tap to disable the **Show Occupancy **toggle.  Tap **Save**.
