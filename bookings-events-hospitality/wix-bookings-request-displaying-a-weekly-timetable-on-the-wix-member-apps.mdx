---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Displaying a Weekly Timetable on the Wix Member Apps"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now display a weekly timetable in the Wix Member apps (e.g. the Spaces App) by [adding an Agenda widget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-adding-bookings-widgets-on-the-wix-member-apps). Members can see a list of your services and view them divided up day by day.
