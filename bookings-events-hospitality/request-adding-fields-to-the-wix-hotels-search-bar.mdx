---
title: "Request: Adding Fields to the Wix Hotels Search Bar"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add additional fields (such as "location") to the Wix Hotels search bar. You can, however, add or remove the "kids" field.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
