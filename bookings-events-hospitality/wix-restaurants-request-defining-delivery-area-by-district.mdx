---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Defining Delivery Area by District"
---

Currently, it is not possible to select a geographical area (e.g. a city, district or state) when setting up a delivery area for online restaurant orders.

You can, however, manually define a delivery area using the **Draw your own **function. To learn more, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-delivery-areas-for-orders).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
