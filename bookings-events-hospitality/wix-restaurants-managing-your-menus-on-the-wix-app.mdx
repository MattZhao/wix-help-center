From [the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview) you can create and manage your Wix Restaurants menus directly from your mobile device. You can add menu sections and menu items, customize labels for menu items, and more.

> **Important:**
>
> Currently, the new Wix Restaurants Menus app doesn't support multi-location, online ordering, or [POS integration](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-connecting-your-wix-restaurant-to-square-pos-point-of-sale). Learn more about the [differences between the old and new Online Ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).

### Building your menu

> **Before you begin:**
>
> [Download the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app) to your mobile device.

Each menu is made up of sections and menu items. Before building your menu, it's a good idea to decide which sections you need, for example, its common for restaurants have an Appetizers, Main Course, Desserts and a Drinks section.

#### To build your menu:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Catalog**.  Tap **Restaurant** **Menus (New)**.  Tap the **+ Add Menu **button.  **Menu name:** Enter a name for the menu, for example: Specials.

1. **Menu description:** Enter a description for the menu. For example: "Dive into our specials menu, where our chef's creativity shines! Discover delightful seasonal treats and delicious innovations that'll make your taste buds dance with joy."

**Note: **You can add line breaks to your descriptions.
2. **Mark as a draft:** Select the checkbox to mark the menu as a draft while you make changes. When a menu is marked as a draft, it won't show in the Editor or your live site.
    Tap **Save**.  Tap the menu you just created.  Tap the **Add Section **button: 

1. **Section name:** Enter a name for the menu section. For example: Vegan.
2. **Description:** Enter a brief description of the menu section for customers to see on your live site. For example: "Indulge in our plant-based wonders, where every bite of our vegan dishes embodies the perfect balance of deliciousness and healthy nourishment."
    Tap the section you just created.  Tap **Save**.  Repeat steps 9-10 for all of the menu sections. Tap **+ Add Section** to add another section to this menu.  Tap **Add Item**:

1. **Item name:** Enter the name of the menu item.
2. **Item description:** Describe the menu item so that customers know what they're ordering.
3. **Item labels:** Add labels to help customers identify key information about the dish (Vegan, Gluten Free, Extra Hot, etc.). Tap the checkbox near the label to select it.
4. **Price: **Enter either a single price or a varying price to your menu item. The currency is set based on your [settings in business info](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-your-restaurant-address-and-phone-number).
5. **Image: **Select or upload an image to your item. 

**Note:** You have to enter a name and price for your menu item. You can choose to hide prices in the Editor.
    Tap **Save**.  Repeat steps for all of your menu items.

### Editing your menu

In the Wix app, you have the tools to efficiently edit your menu. Follow these step-by-step instructions to customize sections, add menu items, and maintain a well-organized menu for your restaurant. Keep your menu up-to-date and tailored to your customers' preferences.

#### To make changes to your menu:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Catalog**.  Tap **Restaurant** **Menus (New)**.  Select the menu you want to edit.  Choose what you want to do:

### Edit your menu

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  on the top right.
2. Tap **Edit menu details**.
3. Edit your menu.
4. Tap **Save**.

### Edit section details

1. Select the section you want to edit.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  on the top right.
3. Tap **Edit section details**.
4. Edit your section name and description.
5. Tap **Save**.

### Edit items

1. Select the item's section.
2. Select the item you want to edit.
3. Edit the item:

- **Item name:** Enter the name of the menu item.
- **Item description:** Describe the menu item so that customers know what they're ordering.
- **Item labels:** Add labels to help customers identify key information about the dish (Vegan, Gluten Free, Extra Hot, etc.). Tap the checkbox near the label to select it and tap **Save**.
- **Price: **Enter either a single price or a varying price to your menu item. The currency is set based on your [settings in business info](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-your-restaurant-address-and-phone-number).
- **Image: **Select or upload an image to your item.
4. Tap **Save**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about editing your menu.

### Can I hide a menu?

Yes. Hide a menu when necessary by setting it as a draft. This feature allows you to temporarily remove a menu from your live site, providing flexibility in managing the visibility of your restaurant's offerings as needed.

1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Catalog**.
4. Tap **Restaurant** **Menus (New)**.
5. Select the menu you want to edit.
6. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
7. Select **Edit Menu Details**.
8. Check the **Mark as a draft** checkbox.
9. Tap **Save**.

### Can I reorder sections in my menu?

Yes. In the Wix app, you have the flexibility to rearrange sections within your menu. This functionality allows you to customize the flow and organization of your menu items to best suit your restaurant's needs, ensuring a well-structured and user-friendly menu for your patrons.

1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Catalog**.
4. Tap **Restaurant** **Menus (New)**.
5. Select the menu you want to edit.
6. Tap the More Actions icon  on the top right.
7. Select **Reorder Section**.
8. Hold the **Reorder** icon  and drag a section to reorder it in the list.
9. Tap **Save**.

### How to add a section to my menu after I have created it?

1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Catalog**.
4. Tap **Restaurant** **Menus (New)**.
5. Select the menu you want to edit.
6. Tap the **Add Section **button and add the **Section name **and **Description**.

### How to add items to my menu after I have created it?

1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Catalog**.
4. Tap **Restaurant** **Menus (New)**.
5. Select the menu you want to edit.
6. Select the Section where you want to add the item.
7. Tap **Add Item and** add the** Item name**,** description**, **labels**,** price **and **image**.

### Can I delete a menu or parts of a menu?

Yes. You can delete a menu, section or item that you no longer wish to use. Once deleted, it cannot be restored.

1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Catalog**.
4. Tap **Restaurant** **Menus (New)**.
5. Select the menu you want to edit.
6. Choose what you want to do:

- **D****elete yo****ur menu: **Tap the More Actions icon  at the top right and select **Delete menu**.
- **Delete a section: **Tap the More Actions icon   by the relevant section and select **Delete section**.
- **Delete a menu item: **Tap the More Actions icon   by the relevant item and select **Remove from section**.
