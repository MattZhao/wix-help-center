---
title: "Wix Events: Removing Wix.com from Your Notification Email"
---

When using Wix Events on a free Wix site, all event notifications sent to your guests have the following line at the end: **This event was created using Wix.com. Try it out.**

To remove this line, upgrade to any Premium Plan that includes removing Wix ads from your site (any plan other than the Connect Domain Premium Plan).

Click to upgrade your site.
