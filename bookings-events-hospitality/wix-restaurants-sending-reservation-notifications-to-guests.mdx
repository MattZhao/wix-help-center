### Turning email and SMS notifications on and off

With the Wix Table Reservations app, you can manage the automated notifications sent to your guests. You can turn off both email and SMS notifications or select specific channels for each notification, giving you control over the information your guests receive about their table reservations.

#### To turn notifications on or off:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Outgoing notifications**.  Hover over a **Wix Table Reservations** notification and click **View Summary**.  Choose what you want to do:

- **Turn both Email and SMS notifications on/off: **Click the toggle at the top to turn both email and SMS notifications on or off for this notification.
- **Choose the channel for notification: **Hover over the channel you want to disable and click **Edit Action**. Click **Delete Action** to remove it, then click **Delete** again to confirm. Click **Save** to apply your changes.

> **Note:**
>
> Your monthly SMS message limit is determined by your SMS quota, which varies based on your site's plan. To view your quota, go to [SMS Settings](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-sms?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+SMS+Settings) in your site's dashboard.

### Customizing email notifications sent to guests

Customize the email notifications that are automatically sent to your guests about their table reservations. You can also preview your email notifications and send a test to an email of your choice.

#### To customize the email notifications you send:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Outgoing notifications**.  Hover over the **Wix Table Reservations** email notification you want to customize and click **View Summary**.  Click **More Actions** and select **Edit**.  Click **Edit** under **Create an email** to customize your automated email.

**Tip:** Click **Preview & Test** to preview and send a test email. Click **Back to Editing** to continue editing the email.  Click **Save &** **Continue** at the top right.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [customizing your automated emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-customizing-automated-emails).

### Can I edit the sender details for email notifications?

Yes, you can edit the name and reply-to email address in notifications sent to your guests.**To update your sender details:**
1. [Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Outgoing notifications**.
3. Hover over any **Wix Table Reservations** email notification and click **View Summary**.
4. Click **More Actions** and select **Edit**.
5. Click **Edit **under** Sender details**.
6. Click the **More Actions** iconto update your sender details:

- **Sender's name:** Edit the name that will appear in the recipient's inbox.
- **Reply-to email:** Edit the email address people reach when they reply.
7. Click **Save**.

**Note:** Before saving the updated sender details, you must confirm access to this account. A confirmation code will be sent to the new reply-to email address. Enter the code and click **Confirm**.
8. Click **Save **to apply your changes.

### How do I manage the notifications I receive for table reservations?

You can choose to receive notifications via your site's dashboard and/or email. By default, these notifications are enabled, but you can turn them on or off at any time. Learn more about [managing your notifications for table reservations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-managing-your-notifications-for-table-reservations).

### Is there a quota for SMS notifications?

Yes. The number of SMS messages you can send each month is determined by your SMS quota, which varies based on your site's plan. To view your quota, go to [SMS Settings](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-sms?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+SMS+Settings) in your site's dashboard. Learn more about [Wix SMS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-sms).
