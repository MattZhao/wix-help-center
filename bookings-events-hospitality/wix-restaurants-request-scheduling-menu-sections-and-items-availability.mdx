---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Scheduling Menu Sections and Items Availability"
---

Currently, it is not possible to schedule the availability of specific menu sections or individual menu items on certain days or at specific times using the [Wix Restaurant Orders app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new). All menu sections and items appear by default in your online ordering menu.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - [Set items as "out of stock"](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-preparing-your-menus-for-online-ordering#setting-items-as-in-stock-or-out-of-stock) so they are not available for online ordering.
> - [Limit a menu's availability](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-preparing-your-menus-for-online-ordering#limiting-menu-availability) to display it for online ordering at specific timeframes.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
