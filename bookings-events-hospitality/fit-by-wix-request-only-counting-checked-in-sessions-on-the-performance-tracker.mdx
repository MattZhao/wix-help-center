---
title: "Fit by Wix Request: Only Counting Checked-in Sessions on the Performance Tracker"
---

Currently, it is not possible to track only checked-in sessions on the performance tracker on the Fit by Wix app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
