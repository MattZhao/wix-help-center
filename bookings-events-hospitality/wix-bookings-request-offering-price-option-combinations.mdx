---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering Price Option Combinations"
---

Currently, it is not possible to offer combinations of [price options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-understanding-price-options) for a service. This would allow you to combine different price options (e.g. location, duration, etc.) to determine the cost of your service.

> **Workaround:**
>
> Create your desired combinations using custom price options. For example, create price options for each service duration, at each location, with the relevant prices. 
> You then need to manually assign the relevant staff member, at the relevant location, to the service.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
