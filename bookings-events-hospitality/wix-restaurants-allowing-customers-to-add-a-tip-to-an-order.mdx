---

> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about the **original Restaurants Orders app. **
> - We've released a new online ordering app. Learn how to [add and set up the new Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

Make payments easier for customers with a good checkout experience by allowing them to add a tip to their credit card when paying. You can customize the default tip options and allow customers to leave a tip in their requested amount.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Verify that you have set up your payment methods in your [payment settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/payments). Tips don't work with manual/offline payments.

### To allow customers to add a tip:

Go to [Order Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Order%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/ordersSettings) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Advanced** tab at the bottom.  Enable the **Tips **toggle to allow customers to leave a tip when placing an order.

(Optional) Customize the preset tip options:

1. Click to select a default unit for the tip (**Percentage** or **Currency**).
2. Enter 3 different values for the preset tip in the text fields.

**Note:** You can click **Set as default** by one of the preset amounts to select it as the default for tips.
    Enable the **Allow customers to leave a custom tip** toggle to allow customers to leave a tip in their desired amount.  Click **Save**.
