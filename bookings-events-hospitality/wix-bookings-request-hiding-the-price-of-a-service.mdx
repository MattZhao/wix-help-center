---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Hiding the Price of a Service"
---

Currently you can offer services through Wix Bookings, but you cannot hide the price of the service.

> **Update:**
>
> For Appointments, you can now select a "No fee" pricing option. No price will be displayed on your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated
