---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Customizing the 'Special Requests' Option"
---

Currently in Wix Restaurants, it is not possible to customize the 'Special Requests' option for online orders and give customers different guidelines for how they can make requests.

You can, however, remove the option to make special requests from a specific dish. By hiding the special requests option, customers cannot ask for changes to the dish(es) you've specified. Learn how to hide special requests in Step 7 of [this article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-choosing-which-menus-sections-and-dishes-to-display-for-online-ordering).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
