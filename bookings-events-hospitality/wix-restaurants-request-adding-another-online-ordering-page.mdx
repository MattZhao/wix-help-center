title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Adding Another Online Ordering Page"
---

We're excited to announce that you can now [create another online ordering page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-creating-another-online-ordering-page) with different settings. For example, you can have one page for your take-away orders and another for your catering orders.
