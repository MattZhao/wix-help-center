---
title: "Request: Brute Force Attack Prevention on Checkout Page"
---

We are pleased to announce that Wix has added an extra security feature to prevent bot attacks on your checkout page.

This feature works by automatically detecting potential attackers in your checkout. Once identified, they are required to face a challenge to prove they're legitimate human site visitors.
