---
title: "Wix Restaurants: About Printing Online Orders (New)"
---

The new Online Orders app enables you to print upcoming, current, and past orders. You can print orders manually from both your dashboard and the Wix app using an A4 printer. Additionally, with the Wix app, you use auto-print with supported thermal printers.

> **Important:**
>
> This article is about the **new Restaurants Orders app. **

---

### Printing orders using a thermal printer from the Wix app

[Print incoming orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-auto-printing-online-orders-from-the-wix-owner-app) using a thermal printer right from your device with the Wix app. Connect a printer to the Wix app and print restaurant orders from wherever you are. Once you've connected a printer, orders will be automatically printed when you have the app open.

> **Tip**
>
> See the list of [supported thermal printers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-compatible-thermal-printers-for-online-orders-new) by Star and Epson.

---

### Printing orders from your dashboard

Print your restaurant's orders right [from your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-printing-online-orders-from-your-dashboard). You can print current orders from the Online Orders tab, or any past order from the Orders tab. You also have the option to bulk print from the Orders tab for added convenience.

---

### Printing orders manually using an A4 printer from the Wix app

Print your orders using an [A4 printer connected to your mobile device](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-manually-printing-online-orders-from-the-wix-owner-app). Once your printer is connected, you can manually print new and past orders.
