---
title: "Wix Events Request: Customizing the Thank You Message When Guests Reply to an Invitation"
---

When guests reply to an event invitation, they are automatically directed to a thank you message on your site. Currently, it is not possible to disable the Thank You message or customize the fonts and colors.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can however, edit the text of the thank you message for RSVP events. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-customizing-the-registration-form-for-rsvp-events)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
