---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Filtering by Services on Your Wix App Calendar"
---

Currently, it is not possible to filter your calendar by services in the Wix app. However, you can [filter by services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-filtering-your-bookings-calendar#filtering-your-calendar-by-services) on your desktop calendar.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
