---
title: "Wix Events: Editing the Date and Time Format"
---

Dates and times are displayed differently in different regions around the world. Your site's display is based on the regional settings you enter for your site.

### To edit your regional settings:

[Go to the Settings tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Edit%20Site%20Info&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Language & Region**.  Select your region from the **Regional settings** drop-down.  (Recommended) Complete the other Language & Region settings.

You can preview how dates, numbers, currency and more display on your site.
