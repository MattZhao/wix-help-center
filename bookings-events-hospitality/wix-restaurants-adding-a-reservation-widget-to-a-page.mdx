---
title: "Wix Restaurants: Adding a Reservation Widget to a Page"
---

Make it easy for your customers to reserve a table by adding a reservation form or button to any page on your site. Choose from different widget styles to fit your design and boost bookings.

### To add the reservations widget:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Reservations page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Add-Ons**.
5. Click **Add to Page **next to your preferred widget style: **Stacked**, **Strip**, or **Button**.
6. Select the page in which you want your selected widget to appear.
7. (Optional) Reposition the widget on the page and design it to your liking.
