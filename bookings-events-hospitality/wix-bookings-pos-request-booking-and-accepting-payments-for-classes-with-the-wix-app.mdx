---
title: "Wix Bookings POS Request: Booking and Accepting Payments for Classes with the Wix App"
---

Currently, it's not possible book and accept payment for new class sessions at a mobile POS.

> **Workaround:**
>
> It is, however, possible to 
> - [Accept payment at a mobile POS for sessions that have already been booked](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-selling-services-at-a-mobile-point-of-sale).
> - [Book and accept payment for appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-booking-services-and-accepting-payments-with-the-wix-owner-app).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
