---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Filtering the Calendar by Staff Member in the Wix App"
---

Currently, you can [view and filter upcoming sessions by staff member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-filtering-your-bookings-calendar) in the Booking Calendar on desktop (e.g. you can organize sessions by the assigned staff member and their session availability).

However, it is not possible to view and filter the Booking Calendar by staff member in the Wix app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
