title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating and Managing Multi-Service Appointments for Your Clients"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [create, manage, reschedule or cancel multi-service appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-and-managing-multi-service-appointments).

You can now enable your clients to book multiple services in a single appointment. This way, you can optimize your schedule and upsell additional services—while offering a more convenient customer experience.

Once a customer selects all the services they want and sets their preferences, the calendar automatically calculates availability and offers relevant time slots for the entire appointment.
