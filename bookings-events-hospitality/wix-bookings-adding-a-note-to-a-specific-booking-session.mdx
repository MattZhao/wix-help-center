---
title: "Wix Bookings: Adding a Note to a Specific Booking Session"
---

Whether you want to jot down information about an upcoming booking or keep track of what happened in a service you already provided, you can add a note to an appointment, class or course.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also [add notes about specific clients](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-viewing-and-editing-client-info-using-contact-cards) to give your staff more info about them.

### To add a note to a booking:

[Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Depending on the session type, follow the steps below:

### For appointments

1. Click the relevant appointment.
2. Click the **Add note to self **icon .
3. Enter your note.
4. Click the **Checkmark** icon  to save.
**Note:** This note is not visible for clients. You can view it by clicking the appointment and hovering over the **Note** icon .

### For classes and courses

1. Click the relevant class/course.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Click **+ Add a note to yourself**.
4. Enter your note.
5. Click **Save**.

**Note:** This note is not visible for clients. You can view it by clicking the session on your booking calendar.
