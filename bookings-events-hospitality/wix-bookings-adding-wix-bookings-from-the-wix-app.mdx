---
title: "Wix Bookings: Adding Wix Bookings from the Wix App"
---

Wix Bookings lets customers book your services through their member app (Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix) or on your desktop site.

> **Note:**
>
> After you add the app from the Wix app, it appears as a pending app in your Editor. You can then [choose if you want to add it to your desktop site as well](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-pending-app-in-your-editor).

### To add Wix Bookings from the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Manage Apps**.  Tap **Add** next to **Bookings** under Business.

(Optional) Add the app to your desktop site:

1. Go to your site editor on desktop.
2. Click the **Add Apps** icon.
3. Click the **Manage Apps **tab.
4. Click **Add to Site** next to **Wix Bookings.**
