---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Managing Restaurant Services Across Multiple Locations"
---

Currently, it's not possible to manage restaurant services across multiple locations using a single Wix Restaurants site with the Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app. This includes separate menus for each branch, or customizing online ordering options for each location.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
