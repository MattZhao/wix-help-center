---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Hide the Agenda Filters on the Member Apps"
---

Currently, on the Wix Member apps, it's not possible to hide filters on the Agenda element. However, in your editor, [you can select which filters you want members to see](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-customizing-your-booking-calendar-page#choosing-which-elements-to-display-on-the-booking-calendar-page).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
