---
title: "Wix Events Request: Creating Separate Events for Different Languages"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create separate events for different languages on your site. However, you can [translate a variety of elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-translating-your-event) on your event pages.

> While you can’t currently translate the event schedule, you can [vote to request](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-request-translating-your-events-schedule-in-wix-events) this feature.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
