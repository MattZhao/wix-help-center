---
title: "Wix Bookings: Upgrading Your Site"
---

Allow clients to start booking your services by upgrading your site. Alternatively, you can display your services (without allowing bookings) on a free Wix site.

Wix offers a range of site plans especially designed for businesses. To start getting booked and accepting payments, [visit our Upgrade page](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) to find a plan that best suits your business needs.

### To choose a plan:

If you have not already done so, [add Wix Bookings to your site](https://www.wix.com/app-market/wix-bookings).  [Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).  Click [**Upgrade**](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).

   Click **Select **under the plan that suits your needs.  Click to select the relevant subscription period and click **Continue to Checkout**.  Enter your payment details.  Click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Important:**
>
> - After submitting your purchase, your bank may require you to [verify your identity](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds). If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.
> - If you've previously purchased a site plan that doesn't support Wix Bookings, you can [upgrade to a plan that does](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter). When switching plans, if the new plan costs more than the original plan you purchased, you pay only the difference in price.
