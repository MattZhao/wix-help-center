---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering Appointments With Any Available Staff Member"
---

Currently, it is not possible to schedule appointments without assigning a specific staff member in charge of the session. This is useful for clients who don't have a preference which staff member takes care of their session. For example, a busy salon could allow clients to book a haircut with any available stylist, ensuring faster service and reducing the chance of time slot conflicts.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can [create a generic staff profile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-staff-members) to represent unstaffed sessions. This allows you to offer appointment services without needing to assign them to individual staff members at the time of booking.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
