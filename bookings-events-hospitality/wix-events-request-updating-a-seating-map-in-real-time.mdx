---
title: "Wix Events Request: Updating a Seating Map in Real Time"
---

Currently, seats only show as 'unavailable' after a guest completes the checkout process. It is not possible to update the [seating map](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-a-seating-map) in real time to reflect seats that are in a guest's cart, before they have checked out.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
