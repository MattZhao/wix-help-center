---
title: "Wix Events: Collecting Guest Info for Individual Event Tickets"
---

Guests are required to fill out a registration form when purchasing tickets to your event. By default, the buyer fills out one form per order, no matter how many tickets they buy. You can require buyers to fill out a form for each ticket that they purchase so that you can collect information about each guest.

> **Tip:**
>
> This is great for conferences, sporting events, summer camps, or any other event where one person may be buying tickets on behalf of multiple guests.

### To require a registration form for each ticket:

[Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/events/) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant ticketed event.  Click the **Settings **tab.   Click **Edit** next to **Registration form**.  Select **Fill out a registration form for each ticket in an order**.  Click **Save** at the top right.

You can view the purchase history and buyer information on the **Orders **tab, but the information about the individual guests is saved on the **Guests **tab.
