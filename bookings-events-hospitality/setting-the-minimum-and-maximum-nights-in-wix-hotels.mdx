title: "Setting the Minimum and Maximum Nights in Wix Hotels"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

Make sure you get the bookings you want by setting the minimum and maximum number of nights guests can stay at your property.

For example, if you're renting an apartment, you might not want someone to book for a single night. Or you might not want the same person to stay for more than a month.

### To set the maximum and minimum number of nights:

Go to your [site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).Click the **Hotels** tab on the left.Click the **Settings**.Scroll down to the **Length of Stay **section.Enter the minimum number of nights your guests can stay under **Min Nights.**Enter the maximum number of nights your guests can stay (up to 365 nights) under **Max Nights.**Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also set the minimum and maximum nights for periods when seasonal rates apply. To learn more, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-minimum-and-maximum-stays-for-seasonal-rules-in-wix-hotels).

> **For Channel Manager users:**
>
> Minimum stay rules are synced with the [Channel Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-hotels-channel-manager-2980855) while maximum stay rules are not.
