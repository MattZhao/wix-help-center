In Wix Bookings, you can add multiple staff members and assign different services to each one. Setting a staff member's hours allows your clients to clearly see when they can book appointments on a given day. You can also give your staff members [permission to manage their own sessions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-staff-permissions-in-wix-bookings) on your site.

### Adding a staff member

Add new staff members and customize their profiles with information such as a profile photo, contact details, and working hours. This helps provide visitors with more information about your staff on your site.

#### To add a staff member:

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Staff](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Staff&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/staff?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **+Add Staff**.
3. Enter the staff member's basic details including their full name, a description of their role, their email address, phone number and a profile picture.

**Note: **Make sure you have permission to use staff members’ personal details and image.
4. Add the staff member's **Staff settings**:

- **Booking permissions: **Click **Give** **Permissions** to allow the staff member to view their personal booking calendar, and to manage their own clients and sessions. [Learn more about giving staff permissions in Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-staff-permissions-in-wix-bookings).
- **Personal calendar syncing (requires booking permissions): **Click **Invite to Sync Calendar** to allow the staff member to sync their personal and work calendars. The staff member must have the relevant permissions (see **Booking permissions** above).
- **Video conferencing account: **Click **Add Individual Account** to override the default video conferencing account for your site and add the staff member's personal account. Choose between adding Zoom or a custom video conferencing account. [Learn more about managing multiple video conferencing accounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-advanced-video-conferencing-for-online-services).
5. (Optional)** **Click **Add Working Hours** to [customize the staff member's working hours](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-editing-your-staff-members-working-hours).
6. Click **Save**.
Note: If you added the [Staff Widget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-customizing-a-staff-widget-on-your-site) to your site, visitors will see your staff members' working hours in the widget.
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Booking Settings**.
5. Tap the **Staff **tab.
6. Tap **+ Add New Staff Member**.
7. Enter the staff member's details, including their profile picture, full name, email and phone number.
8. (Optional)** **Scroll down under **Working Hours** to [customize the staff member's working hours](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-editing-your-staff-members-working-hours?tabs=Wix-app).
9. Tap **Save** at the top.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about adding staff members.

### How many staff members can I add to my site?

The number of staff you can [give permission](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-staff-permissions-in-wix-bookings) to collaborate on your site depends on your [Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-site-collaborator-seat-limit) or [Wix Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-collaborator-seat-limit) plan.

### How can I edit a staff member's details on the site?

**To edit staff member's details:**
1. [Go to Staff](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Staff&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/staff?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the relevant staff member and click the **More Actions **icon** **** **.
3. Click **Edit info **and edit the details.
4. Click **Save**.

### How can I edit a staff member's details on the app?

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Booking Settings**.
5. Tap the **Staff **tab.
6. Tap the relevant staff member.
7. Edit the details and tap **Save**.

### Where can I find more details on setting policies and team roles for my site?

You can find the full course in Wix Learn about [setting policies and team roles](https://www.wix.com/learn/online-course/fitness-management/build-your-fitness-business-online#set-policies-and-team-roles) for your site.
