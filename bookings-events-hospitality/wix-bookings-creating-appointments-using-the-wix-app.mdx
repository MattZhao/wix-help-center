title: "Wix Bookings: Creating Appointments Using the Wix App"
---

The Wix app enables you to create new appointment services from your mobile device, while you're on the go.

Appointments are services that clients can book whenever a time slot is available. For example, a hairdresser may be available from 9 AM until 7 PM and clients can book any time that isn't already taken.

> **Notes:**
>
> - You can also [create Classes using the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-classes-using-the-wix-owner-app).
> - Go to your desktop if you need to [create Courses in Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-a-course).

### Step 2 | Fill out your service details

Add information about what clients can expect from the appointment. Include a detailed description to give clients a clearer idea of what you offer. You can also set the duration of your appointment and add any buffer time you may need before the next appointment.

#### To fill out your service details:

Add your service details:

- **Name: **Enter a name for the appointment.
- **Category: **Select a category from the drop-down menu (if you created categories).
- **Tagline (optional): **Enter a tagline that will appear below the service name on your app.
- **Description (optional): **Enter a description that will appear on the service page of your app.
- **Duration: **Select how long your session lasts from the drop-down menu.
- **Buffer time:** Select the amount of time you want between sessions to create a short break before the next one begins.

**Note:** Duration and buffer time affect your appointment availability. [Learn more about what effects appointment availability](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-troubleshooting-availability-for-appointments).
    Tap **Continue**.

---

### Step 3 | Set your price and payment details

Make your pricing clear and easy to understand. Choose how clients can pay for appointment, per session, with a plan, or have both options. You can choose to set a fixed price, offer a customized price, or provide the service for free. You can also set varied prices from your desktop.

#### To set your price and payment details per session:

Choose how you want your clients to pay for the service:

### Per session

Let your clients pay for their appointment based on the price you set.
1. Tap **Per session**.
2. Tap the **Price type **drop-down and select how you want to charge your client per session:

- **Fixed price: **You charge a set price for the appointment:

1. Tap **Amount** and enter the price.
2. Choose how clients pay from the** Payment preferences **drop-down:

- **Entire amount online:** Only accept online payments for the appointment.
- **Entire amount in person: **Only accept in-person payments (before or after the appointment).
- **Entire amount either online or in person: **Allow clients to choose whether they pay online or in person.
- **Online deposit and the rest in person: **Accept an online deposit for the appointment and have clients pay the rest in person. Add the deposit amount that clients need to pay online.
- **Free: **Offer the service free of charge. Great for introductory appointments with clients.
- **Varied Prices:** Go to your Wix account on desktop to [add varied prices to your appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-an-appointment#step-3-set-your-pricing-payment-details). You can charge different rates for appointments depending on the staff member, client type, service type and more.
- **Custom price: **Enter a short description of your pricing to display on your site. Clients aren't charged online when booking custom-priced services. Learn more about[ getting paid offline or in person](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-getting-paid-offline).
2. Tap **Create service**.

### With a plan

Clients can buy a membership or package and use it to book a session. 
1. Tap** With a plan**.

1. Tap **Connect Pricing Plans** to connect to an existing plan.

**Note:** If you do not have any pricing plans connected, tap [**+ Create new plan**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app)** **to connect a** Package **or** Membership**.
2. Enable the toggle next to the relevant plans or tap **+ Create new plan**.
3. Tap **Done**.
2. Tap **Create service**.
**Tip: **If you're having trouble connecting booking services with plans, try doing it on your desktop. Learn more about [membership plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings) and [packages ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-packages)in Wix Bookings.

### Per session or with a plan

Let** **your clients choose how to pay for the appointment. They can pay per session or with a pricing plan they've purchased.
1. Tap **Per session or with a plan**.
2. Follow the steps from the **Per session** and **With a plan** section above.
3. Tap **Create Service.**

### Step 4 | Edit or add additional service details

Finalize your service details by adding pricing plans, selecting your service location, and assigning staff members. Decide whether to show the appointment on your service list or keep it hidden until you're ready to take bookings. Make your service visible right after creating it to maximize exposure and attract clients quickly.

#### To edit or add additional service details:

Choose what you want to edit or add:

### Add Image

Tap **Add Image **and select an image that clients see when booking appointments.

### Service Info

Edit any of your existing appointment details, like its name, category or duration.

### Price & Payments

Edit how you want clients to be able to pay for the session and edit any payment details.

### Locations

Display where you offer the appointment, for example, at your business location(s) or the client’s place. You can also add the option to meet online with a video link. 
1. Tap **Locations**.
2. Choose where your clients receive this service: 

- **Business location:** Tap the **Choose locations** drop-down and select the checkboxes next to the location(s) you provide this service.

**Note:** You can [add a new business location](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-services-at-multiple-locations) on your desktop.
- **Custom location: **Enter a description of the location in the field (e.g. online meeting).
- **Client's place: **The client will add their location once they book their appointment.
- **Video conferencing:** Enable the toggle to offer this service online.

**Note: **[Make changes or set up video conferencing on your desktop.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-advanced-video-conferencing-for-online-services).
3. Tap **Save**.

### Resources

Make sure your staff and clients have the resources they need for the best experience.
1. Tap **Resources**.
2. Tap the checkboxes next to the relevant resources. 

**Note: **You can create [new resources](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-your-resources) on your desktop.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Staff members

Choose which staff members are available for this appointment. You can [add more staff memebers from your desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-staff-members).
1. Tap **Staff Members**.
2. Tap the checkboxes to select the staff members who are available for this service.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Images

Add a cover image that clients see at the top of your service page. You can also add a gallery to display your service anywhere on your service page.
1. Tap **Images**.
2. Choose what you want to edit:

- **Cover image:** Tap **Add Image** and select an image for the background of your service's header
- **Gallery:** Tap the **Add **icon and select the photos you want to add to a gallery for your service page. Then, tap **Next**.
- Tap **Save**.

### Booking preferences

Add a booking policy or make the appointment view-only if you want clients to contact you for a consultation before booking.
1. Tap **Booking Preferences**.
2. Choose your what you want to update:

- **Booking policy:** Tap the drop-down and select a policy for your appointment. Learn more about [creating new policies from your Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-your-booking-policies-from-the-wix-owner-app).
- **Online booking: **Tap the **Accept online bookings** toggle:

- **Enabled: **Clients can book this service online. Under **Booking requests**,** **choose whether bookings are accepted automatically or require your approval.

**Note:** This is only available if the appointment is not connected to a membership plan or package.
- **Disabled:** Disable this toggle to [make the appointment view-only](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-view-only-services?tabs=Owner-app).
3. Tap **Save**.

2. Tap the **Visibility** icon at the top when you're ready to show the appointment on your site.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about appointments on the Wix app and member apps.

### How can I share my service?

You can share your service right after creating it. Click the **More Actions** icon   on the top right of the 'Create service' page. Then tap **Share service**. Learn more about [promoting your service on the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-promoting-your-services-from-the-wix-owner-app).

### How can I delete a service I no longer offer?

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Services**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Booking Services**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant service.
5. Tap **Delete service**.
6. Tap the **I understand that deleted items can't be recovered **checkbox.
7. Tap **Yes, Delete**.

### How can I duplicate a service?

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Services**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Booking Services**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant service.
5. Tap **Duplicate**.
**Note: **The duplicated service will be called "Copy of \{service name\}". You can tap the service to make any edits.

### How do I quickly find a service on my Services screen?

Use the search bar at the top of the screen to quickly find the service you are looking for.

### Can I create a multi-day appointment on the Wix app?

Yes, to create a multi-day appointment:
1. Follow the steps in the section above to create an appointment.
2. In Step 2, tap the **Duration** drop-down and select the session duration to cover multiple days (up to 99 hours).

**Tips: **
- Make sure to [edit the hours for the staff](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-editing-your-staff-members-working-hours) providing this service so they are available 24 hours a day on the relevant days.
- To run a similar service in future that starts on another date, simply duplicate the event you created and set a new start date.

### Can I create classes and courses through the Wix app?

Currently, you can only create appointments and [classes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-classes-using-the-wix-owner-app) on the Wix app. Courses can only be created on a desktop. If this is a feature you want to see in the future, you can [vote for it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-request-creating-courses-using-the-wix-owner-app).
