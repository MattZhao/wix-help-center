---
title: "Removing the 'Kids' Option in Wix Hotels"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

You can disable the option for guests to add kids to a reservation in Wix Hotels. If disabled, the Kids field does not appear in the Select Dates tool.

### To remove the Kids option:

Click the Wix Hotels app in the Editor.
 Click **Settings**.
 Click the **Rooms list** tab.
 Deselect the **Kids** checkbox next to **Fields**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you've added the Wix Hotels Search Bar to your site, you need to update its settings separately to remove the Kids option. To do so, click the Search Bar in the Editor click **Settings, **and then deselect the checkbox next to **Kids**.
