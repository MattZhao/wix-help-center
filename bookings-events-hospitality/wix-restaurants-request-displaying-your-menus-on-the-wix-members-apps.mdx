title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Displaying Your Menus on the Wix Members Apps"
---

Currently, your customers cannot view your restaurant's menus from the [new Wix Restaurants Menus app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new) on the Wix member apps (Spaces or your own branded app).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
