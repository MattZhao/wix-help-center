---
title: "Wix Bookings: Changing Your Currency"
---

The currency that you select in the General section of your dashboard settings is used for Wix Bookings and any other paid transactions connected to your site.

### ​To change your currency:

Go to the [**Language & Region**](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Language/Region&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/language-and-region/) section of your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Site Currency**.  Select a currency from the **Currency** drop-down.

> **Tip:**
>
> If your currency is missing from the list, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-request-additional-currencies) to vote for additional currencies.
