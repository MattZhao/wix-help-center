---
title: "Wix Events Request: Editing all Occurrences of a Recurring Event"
---

Currently, you can edit each occurrence of a recurring event separately. It is not possible to make a change that affects all occurrences at once.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
