---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Setting Up Contactless Dine-in"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set up contactless dine-in with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new). This feature would allow customers to view your menu, place orders, pay online, and have food brought right to their table.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
