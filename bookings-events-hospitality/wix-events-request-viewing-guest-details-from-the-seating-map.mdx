---
title: "Wix Events Request: Viewing Guest Details from the Seating Map"
---

Currently, while you can see sold-out seats on your [seating map](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-a-seating-map), there is no way to view specific guest details for each ticket purchased directly from the map. This feature would allow you to easily access guest information from the seating map itself.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
