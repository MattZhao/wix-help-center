---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Creating Different Pickup & Delivery Rules for Different Menus"
---

Currently it is not possible to create different pickup and delivery rules for different menus at your restaurant (i.e. Lunch vs. Dinner menu). When you set your pickup and delivery options, those settings apply to all menus.

You can set different delivery charges and minimum order amounts for different [delivery areas](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-delivery-areas-for-orders). You can also set a minimum order amount for [pickup orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-offering-pickup-for-orders).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
