---
title: "Wix Bookings POS Request: Adding a Discount or Custom Charge to an Order"
---

We're thrilled to announce that it's now possible to add a discount to a POS Bookings order or charge your client a custom amount.

You can add a discount or custom charge when you [accept payment for services that were already booked](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-selling-services-at-a-mobile-point-of-sale) or when you [book services yourself and accept payment for them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-selling-services-at-a-mobile-point-of-sale).
