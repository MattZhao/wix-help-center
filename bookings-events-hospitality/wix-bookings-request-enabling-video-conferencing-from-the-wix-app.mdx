---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Enabling Video Conferencing from the Wix App"
---

Currently, from your desktop computer, you can [enable video conferencing to offer your service online](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-advanced-video-conferencing-for-online-services). However, you cannot enable video conferencing from the Wix app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
