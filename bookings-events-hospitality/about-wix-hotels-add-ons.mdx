Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

Wix Hotels comes with two free Add-Ons. Add these features to enhance your customer's experience.

**Search Bar: **Add a search bar to any page on your site. To learn how to add and set up a search bar, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-hotel-search-bar-to-your-site).

**Calendar: **Add a calendar to any page of your site to show your hotel availability. To learn how to add and set up a calendar, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-hotel-availability-calendar-to-your-site).
