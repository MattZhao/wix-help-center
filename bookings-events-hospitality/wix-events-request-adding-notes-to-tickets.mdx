---
title: "Wix Events Request: Adding Notes to Tickets"
---

Currently, you cannot add custom notes to tickets that staff can view when scanning with the Check-in app or Wix app. This would be useful for including details or instructions about a guest, such as special requests.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
