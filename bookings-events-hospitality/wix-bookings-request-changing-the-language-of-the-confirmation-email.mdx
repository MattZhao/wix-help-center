---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Changing the Language of the Confirmation Email"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [change the language of all the text in the confirmation email sent to customers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-translating-automated-emails-with-wix-multilingual) after they book a service on Wix Bookings.

> **Note:**
>
> You need to [add Wix Multilingual to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-adding-and-setting-up-wix-multilingual) to change the language of the confirmation email.
