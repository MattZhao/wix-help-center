---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Creating Conditional Sales for Restaurant Orders"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to create conditional sales for restaurant orders by [creating coupons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-creating-a-coupon) and [automatic discounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-creating-automatic-discounts). This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

> Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
