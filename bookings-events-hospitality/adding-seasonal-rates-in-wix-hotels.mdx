Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

Set seasonal rates to charge different rates for your rooms according to season.

> **Important:**
>
> Creating a Seasonal Rule overrides the length of stay as well as regular unit rates.

### To add seasonal rules to your hotel:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Price Options** tab.Click **Seasonal Rules**.Click **Create New**.Set up your seasonal rate: 
1. Enter the name of the season.
2. Select the start date of the season under **Season starts**.
3. Select the end date of the season under **Season ends**.
4. (Optional) Enter the minimum number of nights.
5. (Optional) Enter the maximum number of nights.
6. Enter the relevant prices for each hotel room: 
- **Weekday:** The price per night during the week.
- **Weekend: **The price per night during the weekend.
- **Weekly: **The price per week.
- **Monthly: **The price per month.
- **Extra Adult: **The price per night for an extra adult.
Click **Save**.
