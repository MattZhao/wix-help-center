title: "Wix Bookings: Creating a Class"
---

Classes are recurring services that clients can book without committing to attend every session. The service can be a weekly Zumba class, for example, or a daily beginner yoga class.

Start by creating the class and then schedule it as often as you like. You decide when and how often you offer a class, and how long the class lasts.

> **Tip:**
>
> Wix Bookings offers three different types of services - classes, courses and appointments. Make sure you [choose the right type of service](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-the-right-booking-service-for-your-business) for your business.
> Check out the full course in Wix Learn about [adding classes and courses](https://www.wix.com/learn/courses/business-solutions/build-your-fitness-business-online#add-classes-and-courses) to your site to build your business.

### Step 1 | Create a class

Start by creating the class and adding the necessary information. Make sure to include specifics about what clients can expect from the class. For example, images showcasing the class experience.

#### To create a class:

[Go to Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **+ Add a New Service **in the top right.  Under Class, choose what you want to do:  

- Click **Edit** below the relevant class template.
- Click **+ Start from Scratch** to use a blank template.
    Fill out the **Service details**:

- **Name: **Enter a name for the class.
- **Tagline (optional): **Enter a tagline that will appear below the class name on your site.
- **Description (optional): **Enter a description that will appear on the service page of your site. **Tip: **Click **Generate AI Description** to let AI (Artificial Intelligence) help you create a description.
- **Image (optional): **Add an image for clients to see when they look through your list of classes.
- **Max participants:** Enter the maximum participants for each class.

### Step 2 | Set the price and payment options for your class

Once you add the basic information about the class, you can then choose from several pricing and payment options. Offering more options can help boost your sales.

#### To set the price and payment options for your class:

Scroll down to the **Price & Payment **section.   Select the relevant option under **Choose how clients can pay for this service:**

- **Per session:** Clients pay for sessions based on the price you set.
- **With a plan:** Clients pay using one of your memberships or packages.
- **Per session or with a plan: **Clients pay either per session or with a membership or package.
    Depending on the payment method you chose, follow the steps to set it up:

### Per session

Select the relevant pricing option from the **Price type **drop-down:
- **Fixed price:** A set amount you choose:

- Enter the price under **Amount**.
- Choose how clients pay you under **Payment preferences** (e.g. entire amount online, online deposit, etc.)** **
- **Free: **Offer this class for free.
- **Varied prices: **Create different price options depending on the client type.

1. Enter the **Default price **for this class.
2. Click **+ Add Price Options** or **Manage Price Options** to edit your existing price options.
3. Set the pricing:

1. In the **Price options label **field, enter the text that clients will see above the price options.
2. Enter the option name and set the price next to it. Repeat this step for each different price option you want to create.

**Tip: **Click **+ Add New Price Option **to add another option.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Choose how clients pay under **Payment preferences** (e.g. entire amount online, online deposit, etc.)** **
- **Custom price:** Enter a description of how you charge for classes. For example, "Starting from $25."

### With a plan

Click an option to connect this class to a membership plan or package:
- **Connect an Existing Plan: **Enable the toggle(s) next to existing plans you want to connect. Then click **Done**.
- **Create New Plan: **Create a new [package](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-packages) or [membership plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings).

### Per session or with a plan

This option allows your clients to choose how to pay for the class. They can use a pricing plan they've purchased, or pay the price you set per session. 
1. Set the **Price per session**.
2. Connect the relevant pricing plan(s) under **Memberships & Packages**. 

**Tip:** See **Per session** or** With a plan** above for detailed steps.

### Step 3 | Setting your class location

Display where you offer the class, for example, at your business location(s) or choose a custom location somewhere else. You can also add the option to meet online with a video link.

#### To set your class location:

Scroll down to the **Location** section.  Click **Edit** to choose where your service is provided:

- **Business location:** Click the checkboxes next to the business location(s) that will have this service available.

**Tip**: Learn more about adding [additional locations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-services-at-multiple-locations).
- **Custom location: **Choose a custom location (e.g. online meeting).
    Click **Save**.   (Optional) Enable the **Video Conferencing** toggle to [offer the class online](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-an-online-service-via-zoom).

**Note:** If this is your first online service, click **Add**. Then, select Zoom or a custom link and click **Continue**.

---

### Step 4 | Set your class times

Now you can set the class times and add specific details for different days of the week. Add the instructor, individual class durations depending on the day of the week, and the start and end dates for the class term.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can create one-time services that start on a specific day and run for several days in a row. This is perfect for multi-day seminars, workshops, guided tours, and more.

#### To set your class times:

Click **Add Sessions** under **Schedule**.   Edit the relevant details:

- **Location: **Select where the class takes place from the drop-down.

**Note: **This is useful if you run the class in different places during the week.
- **Staff: **Select the relevant staff member from the** **drop-down.
- **Start Time: **Choose the date and time that the first session begins.
- **Duration: **Select the class session length from the **Duration** drop-down.

**Tip: **To create a multi-day service, select **Custom** from the drop-down and set the session duration to cover multiple days (up to 99 hours).
- **Repeat: **Select how frequently you offer the class from the **Repeat** drop-down.

**Tip: **To create a session that doesn't repeat, [schedule a class through your Booking Calendar.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-your-classes)
- (Optional) **Set an end date: **Choose a date when these sessions end.
    Click and choose a saving option:

- **Save & Add New: **Save and schedule the same class at a different time, day, location or with a different staff member.
- **Save: **Save the schedule and return to editing the class.

---

### Step 5 | Add images to your class

Add a cover image and an image gallery to show clients what your class is all about. The cover image appears at the top of the service page, and the gallery appears next to the information about your class.

#### To add images to your class:

Scroll down and click **Images**.  Add images to your class by doing the following:

- **Add Cover image:** Select an image for the background of your service's header.
- **Add Service gallery:** Add more high quality images to a gallery for your service page. You can customize the gallery's layout and design in your editor.
    (Optional) Click **+ Add alt text** to add alt text to your images that describes them for search engines and screen readers.

---

### Step 6 | Customize your class with Booking preferences

Further customize your new class in the Booking preferences section. Add a customized booking policy for the class or make the class view-only to generate interest in upcoming services.

> **Note:**
>
> Clients cannot skip the booking form if the class service has different price options, allows multiple participants in a single booking (group bookings), or if their required personal details, such as name and number, are missing.

#### To customize your class with Booking preferences:

Scroll down and click **Booking preferences**.  Select a Booking policy from the drop-down or choose what you want to do:

- **Add new policy: **Click** + Add New Policy **to [create a new booking policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-setting-up-your-booking-policies#adding-new-policies).
- **Edit policy: **Click **Edit policy** to make changes to an existing booking policy.
    Enable the **Online bookings **toggle to allow clients to book classes online:

**Note: **Disable the **Online bookings **toggle to [make the class view-only](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-view-only-services).

1. Choose which **Booking form** you want to use from the drop-down:

- **Custom form:** Click** Custom booking form **to [create a new booking form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-setting-up-your-booking-forms#customizing-the-booking-form) for this service.
- **Edit form: **Click **Edit Form** to make any changes to your booking form.
2. (Optional) Click the **Skip this form when clients book on a Wix mobile members app** checkbox to give your clients a faster booking experience on the app.
    Click **Save**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about creating and managing classes.

### How can I edit an existing class's details?

You can edit or [cancel a class](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-canceling-a-session) at any time in the [Booking calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar). You can edit the details of a single session or all future sessions of the class.

### How can clients book a class with a single tap on the member apps?

To enable single tap bookings on the member apps, select the **Skip this form when clients book on a Wix mobile members app** checkbox in your Booking preferences.**Note:** Single-tap booking is only available after the client's first booking.
Single tap booking will not be available and clients will need to complete the booking form if:
- The class has different price options.
- Group bookings (multiple participants in a single booking) are allowed.
- Required personal details, like name and number, are missing.

### What do my clients see when a class is fully booked?

When clients attempt to book a class that is full, they see two different buttons depending on your settings. If you [enable waitlists for classes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-waitlists#enabling-waitlists-for-classes), clients can click **Join Waitlist**.

**Note:** Clients can join the waitlist only through a Wix members app. When they try booking from your website, they'll be prompted to download the app.

If you don't enable waitlists for your classes, clients can click **Check Next Availability** to book into the next upcoming class session.
You can [edit the text which appears on your booking calendar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-customizing-your-booking-calendar-page), including on the next available button, in your site editor.

### Which factors affect the way available classes appear in my Booking Calendar?

The times you choose when creating a class and [your booking window](https://support.wix.com/en/article/limiting-the-booking-window-in-wix-bookings) affect the way available classes appear in your Booking Calendar.

### Do other services scheduled at the same time as my class affect the available classes in my Booking Calendar?

No, other services scheduled at the same time as a class do not affect the availability of classes on your Booking Calendar. 
The available classes are also not affected by:
- Time blocked manually in your Bookings Calendar.
- Events synced from you or your staff members' Google calendars.

### How can I create a multi-day class?

To make multi-day class:
1. Follow the steps in the section to create a class.
2. Click **Add Sessions** under the **Schedule** section.
3. Click the **Duration** drop-down and select **Custom**.
4. Set the session duration to cover multiple days (up to 99 hours).
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Save **to save the class.

**Tips: **
- Make sure to [edit the hours for the staff](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-editing-your-staff-members-working-hours) providing this service so they are available 24 hours a day on the relevant days.
- To run a similar service in future that starts on another date, simply duplicate the event you created and set a new start date.
