### Enabling SMS reminders

You enable reminders from the business settings section of your site's dashboard. This is a great way to provide better customer service and make sure clients never miss a session. The SMS reminders are automatically sent 24 hours before a session takes place.

> **Important:**
>
> - You need to [upgrade to a Business Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-upgrading-wix-bookings-to-a-business-premium-plan) in order to send SMS reminders.
> - SMS Reminders must comply with the [Wix SMS terms of use](https://www.wix.com/about/virtual-number-terms).

#### To enable SMS reminders:

[Go to Booking Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Emails & SMS Reminders**.  Enable the **Session Reminders** toggle under **Notifications clients get**.  (Optional) Click **View **to preview the message your members receive.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about SMS reminders.

### How do my clients ask for SMS reminders?

Your clients can opt-in to receive SMS reminders when filling out your booking form.

### Can I set the timing for my SMS reminders?

You can only send SMS Reminders 24 hours before a session starts. There is currently no option to customize the timing.

### What number do I send my SMS reminders from?

The SMS reminders arrive from the following number: +16476938840. Clients receive the SMS reminder at the phone number they enter in your Booking Form.

### Can I customize my SMS reminders?

You cannot customize the SMS text, timing, and "sent from" number.

You can, however, [create more advanced SMS reminders using the Omnisend app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-sending-custom-sms-messages-with-omnisend).
