### Customizing your menu page

Customize your menu to reflect your unique restaurant style. Adjust headings, text, labels, images, width, and prices to your preference. Experiment with different layouts to choose which elements you want to highlight, providing you the flexibility to design your menu according to your vision.

#### To customize your menu page:

Go to the **Menus (New)** page in your editor.  Click the **Menus (New)** app on the page.  Choose how you want to customize your menu:

### Display specific menus

Click **Choose Menus** to select the menus you want to display and reorder them.
**Tip:** Learn how to [show menus on multiple pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-separating-a-menu-on-the-wix-menus-app-new) and not just on the menu page.

### Select which elements you want to display on your menu

Click the **Elements** icon  to show or hide elements from your menu such as the section names, item descriptions, prices, currencies and labels.

### Select different menu settings

Click the **Settings** icon  to:
- Choose how to show the price for free items marked 0.00. You can set this rule to all sections and menus or select specific sections.
- Choose a default image to display on items that don't have an image. You can set this rule to all sections and menus or select specific sections.

**Note: **This is only relevant if you have images for your items.

### Change your preset

Click the **Change ****Preset** icon  and select your preferred design. Some presets show a single column and others multiple columns, some display images and others are text-only. 
**Note: **If you wish to display images on your menu, make sure to select a layout that includes images, like Side by side, Grid, and Strip.

### Change the layout of your menu

Click the **Layout** icon  and control the spacing between menu elements. 
**Note:** The available settings depend on the look you've chosen in the **Change Presets** panel.

### Design each part of your menu

Customize the design of your menu, including the menu background, name, section descriptions, item details, labels, prices and more. Choose from a variety of colors, fonts, and other design options to create a menu that fits your business.

**In the Wix Editor: **
1. Click the **Design **icon .
2. Select the element you want to design.
3. Design the background, text, colors, style and more.

**In the Studio Editor:**
1. Click the **Open Inspector** icon  at the top right of the editor.
2. Click the **Design** tab.
3. Select the element you want to design.
4. Design the background, text, colors, style and more.

### Stretch the menu to full-width

Click the **Stretch** icon  to stretch the menu across the full width of the page.

### Manage your menus

Click **Manage Menus** to enter your site's dashboard where you can edit your menus, publish or unpublish them, and create new ones.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### How will my menu look on mobile?

The Menus app automatically creates a mobile-friendly version of your menus, so it's already designed to look ideal on every screen size. You can adjust the layout and design and choose what to show customers to make your menus stand out on mobile. The design changes you make on mobile don't affect your desktop version.

### How do I preview my menu in different layout options?

Just click the different options in the **Change Presets** panel to see how your menu looks. You can also click the **Preview** button at the top of the Editor to see how your menu will look on your site before you go live.

### Can I showcase different menus on separate pages?

Yes. By default, all your published menus are displayed on the same page, separated by tabs for navigation. However, you can create multiple menus and display them on separate menu pages. For example, you may want to highlight today's specials on the homepage or highlight a new menu on a dedicated page. Learn more about [displaying your menus on multiple pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-separating-a-menu-on-the-wix-menus-app-new).
