title: "Wix Restaurants: Deleting the Menus by Wix Restaurants App"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article is about the original Wix Restaurants Menus App. Try out our new menu app. Learn how to [add and set up Wix Restaurants Menus (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new).

Menus by Wix Restaurants is a full-page app, therefore in order to delete the app, you must delete the page itself from your site's Editor.

> **Important:**
>
> When you delete your Menus app, any menus and orders associated with it will be permanently deleted.

Wix EditorEditor X
1. Click the **Pages **iconon the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Main Pages**.
3. Hover over the Menus page and click the More Actions icon .
4. Click **Delete**.
5. Click **Delete** to confirm.
