---
title: "Fit by Wix: Inviting Clients to Your Site"
---

Grow your fitness business and community by inviting your clients and contacts to become members of your site on the Fit by Wix app. When you send an invite to people, they receive a link to download the Fit by Wix app. From there, your members can engage with your fitness business and online community from their mobile device.

### To invite new members to your site on the Fit by Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage ** at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.  Tap the **Share** icon.  Choose one of the following:

### Email Wix Contacts

Email the contacts saved in your Wix account.**Note:** To use this feature, you need to [upgrade your site with a plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).

1. Tap the available email provider (e.g. Gmail).
2. Customize your email:

- **To:** Choose who should receive your email.
- **Title: **Enter the title that appears at the top of your email.
- **Message: **Write a short message telling your contacts why they should join your site on the Fit by Wix app.
3. Click **Send**.

### Send an SMS

Send the link and invitation code to people by SMS (text message).
1. Tap **Send an SMS**.
2. Choose if you want to allow the Wix app to access your contacts.
3. Select the contacts you want to invite.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Tap **Send** in the message field on your phone.

[image: A screenshot showing an example of an invite SMS you send to clients to join the Fit by Wix app.]

### Share via Social & More

Share the link and invitation code to any social network connected to your phone.  
1. Tap **More**.
2. Choose how you want to send your invite to. For example, Facebook, WhatsApp, Gmail and more.
3. Tap **Send**.

### Copy Link

Copy the link and paste it into a new message in any application. 
1. Tap **Copy link**.
2. Paste the link into a new message using the application of your choice.
