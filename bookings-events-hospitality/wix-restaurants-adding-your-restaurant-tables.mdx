title: "Wix Restaurants: Adding Your Restaurant Tables"
---

The Wix Table Reservations app allows guests to book tables online based on your restaurant’s availability, helping you manage seating capacity. You can add, remove, or update tables and the number of seats they hold.

To accommodate larger parties during busy times, combine tables as needed, and increase your reservation potential. You can also mark specific tables as unavailable, giving you more control over which tables are bookable online.

### Adding your tables

Add your restaurant tables with the number of seats they can hold. You can also select whether or not each table is available for guests to book online.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want to create a visual floor plan, we recommend creating tables in the floor plan itself, rather than in **Table management** settings. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-creating-a-restaurant-floor-plan)

#### To add your tables:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Table Reservations](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/restaurants/table-reservations?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Table+Reservations) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Settings** at the top right.
3. Click **Table management** to add the tables you have in your restaurant.
4. Click** + Add Table **and add your tables:

- **Table name:** Enter a name for this table. For example: small table, big table, bar seats.
- **Number of seats (min-max):** Set the minimum and maximum number of seats at this table.
- **Available online:** By default, your tables are available for guests to book online. Deselect the checkbox if you don't want a table to be available for guests to book online.
5. Repeat step 4 to add all of your restaurant's tables.
6. Click **Save**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.

8. Tap **Save**.

### Adding table combinations

The Wix Table Reservations app lets you combine multiple tables to accommodate larger parties and optimize seating capacity. You can also decide whether each table combination is available for guests to book online.

#### To add table combinations:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Table Reservations](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/restaurants/table-reservations?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Table+Reservations) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Settings** at the top right.
3. Click **Table management** to add the tables you have in your restaurant.
4. Click the **Table Combinations** tab.
5. Click **Add Table Combination **and combine your tables:

- **Table combination:** Select the tables you want to combine.
- **Number of seats (min-max):** Adjust the minimum and maximum number of seats available at this table.
- **Available online:** By default, your tables are available for guests to book online. Deselect the checkbox if you don't want a table combination to be available for guests to book online.
6. Repeat step 5 to combine more of your restaurant's tables and click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of creating table combinations in Wix Table Reservations app.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Table Reservations **and select **Table Reservations**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon .
5. Tap **Settings**.
6. Tap **Manage tables**.
7. Tap the **Table combinations** tab, then tap **+ Add Table Combination**.
8. Tap the **Select tables **drop-down and select the checkbox next to the tables you want to combine. Then tap **Select**.
9. **Number of seats (min-max):** Adjust the minimum and maximum number of seats available at this table.
10. **Make available online:** By default, your tables are available for guests to book online. Tap the toggle to disable it if you don't want a table combination to be available for guests to book online.
11. Tap **Save**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about your restaurant tables.

### What is the preset guest capacity for online reservations?

Online reservations are set to accommodate parties of 1 to 6 guests.

### Can I change the maximum party size for online reservations?

Yes. You can change the maximum party size. Click **Online Reservations** at the top and update the maximum allowed party size.

### How can I duplicate a table in the Wix Table Reservations app?

Save time by duplicating existing tables and then editing their details. To duplicate a table, hover over the relevant table and click the **Duplicate** icon.
