---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Customizing the Mobile Layout"
---

Clients can view Wix Bookings on both the desktop and mobile versions of your site.

Any changes in the desktop version of your Wix Bookings app appear in the mobile version as well. It is currently not possible to make separate changes to all elements of Wix Bookings on mobile.

> **Update:**
>
> It is now possible to [customize the layout of your service list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-setting-up-your-service-list#step-2-customize-the-layout-of-your-service-list) and the [calendar layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-customizing-your-booking-calendar-page#choosing-the-layout-for-the-calendar-page) for mobile.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
