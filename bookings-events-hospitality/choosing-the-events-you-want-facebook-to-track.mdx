title: "Choosing the Events You Want Facebook to Track"
---

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Your domain must be verified on Facebook before you can choose which 8 events you want Facebook to track. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-domain-on-facebook)

Due to the release of iOS14 in February 2021, tracking [events](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/964258670337005?id=1205376682832142) in Facebook is now limited to 8 conversion events. Facebook initially pre-assigns these 8 events based on data from your recent campaign spend, but you can edit the event configurations from the Events Manager. To edit the events [follow these instructions from Facebook.](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/422408905612648)

### Events in Wix that are trackable by Facebook

The following table shows all the possible events in Wix that are trackable by Facebook. When you [verify your domain on Facebook](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-domain-on-facebook), you will be asked to select the 8 events you want to track. Use this table as a reference. The events you can track are marked 'x'.

> **Important:**
>
> If you do not select 8 tracking events, Facebook will automatically choose them for you.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The "WixOrderPlaced" event always fires when a customer places an order.
> - The "Purchase" event fires when a payment processor reports on a successful transaction. There may occasionally be a delay with a processor’s response. In these cases, the "Purchase" event will not fire.
