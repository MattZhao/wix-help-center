You can edit the translated text, including your 'room' titles, on your Wix Hotels by HotelRunner site to improve the visitor experience and increase bookings from different markets. This includes translations for your 'Book a room' page and each room type's name and amenities.

For example, you may not offer 'rooms' but 'villas' or 'cottages'. You can change these terms across all languages available on your site.

### Changing the 'Room' text in Room Types

Ensure guests know what room type they are booking by updating your 'room' text across all languages you support on your site. You can do this from the Room Types section of the Languages tab under Property Setup in the dashboard. This provides a better experience for both your guests and your staff.

> **Note:**
>
> Updating the 'Room' text in room type changes the appearance for you and your staff in the backend of your site. You should also update the 'Room' text in the Booking Engine so visitors and guests see what the staff sees (see section below).

#### To change the 'room' text on your site:

Go to [Languages](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp%2F826670ba-3a6b-4157-ac72-7c4fca9ce220%2F1c5b7dec-159e-48cf-b618-6d77d7a17f8c%7C10%3F&title=Select+a+site&primaryButtonText=Open+Languages) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to the **Translate your content** section.   Select the language in which you want to edit your site text from the drop-down on the right.   Click **Translate** next to **Room Types**.   Hover over the relevant room type and click **Translate**.   Select the relevant language from the **Translate into** drop-down at the top right.  Click to edit any of the translated text.   Click **Save**.   Repeat steps 6-8 for all relevant languages.

### Changing the 'Room' text in the Booking Engine

The Booking Engine allows visitors to book rooms directly from your website. It displays available rooms, prices, and amenities, and enables guests to make reservations. Make sure you update your 'room' text in the Booking Engine so it matches the text you chose in the Room Types section under Languages.

#### To change the 'Room' text in the Booking Engine:

Go to [Languages](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp%2F826670ba-3a6b-4157-ac72-7c4fca9ce220%2F1c5b7dec-159e-48cf-b618-6d77d7a17f8c%7C10%3F&title=Select+a+site&primaryButtonText=Open+Languages) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down and click to expand **Property** **Settings**.   Hover over **Booking Engine** and click **Translate**.   Select the relevant language from the **Translate into** drop-down at the top right.  Enter "Room" in the search bar in the top right.   Enter the relevant text for each relevant reference to "room" on the booking engine.   Click **Save**.   Repeat steps 4-7 above for all relevant languages.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more:

### Can I edit the translations for each room type I offer?

Yes, you can edit the translations for each room type, including the room name and amenities.

### How do I select the language I want to translate into?

After clicking "Translate" for the room type, select the desired language from the "Translate into" drop-down menu at the top right.

### Do I need to repeat the translation process for each room type?

Yes, repeat the steps for each room type to ensure all translations are accurate and complete.
