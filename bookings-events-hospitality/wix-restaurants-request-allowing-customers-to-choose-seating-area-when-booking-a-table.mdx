---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Allowing Customers to Choose Seating Area When Booking a Table"
---

Currently, customers can't choose a specific area of the restaurant when booking a table. For example, some guests might prefer a quieter, more intimate setting, while others might prefer sitting at the bar or in a busier part of the restaurant.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
