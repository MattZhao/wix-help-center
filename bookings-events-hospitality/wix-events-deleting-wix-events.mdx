If you no longer wish to use Wix Events, you can delete the app from your site. When you delete Wix Events, all the event elements (Event Details & Registration and Schedule Page) are removed.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Deleting Wix Events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/deleting-wix-events#deleting-wix-events)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/deleting-wix-events#faqs)

### Deleting Wix Events

Deleting Wix Events from your site might be necessary if you no longer plan to host events. If this is the case, removing Wix Events allows you to declutter your site and focus on other priorities.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **Actions **iconnext to **Event Pages**.
4. Select **Delete Events**.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Actions icon on the Events Pages to delete the element.]
1. Click **Delete** to confirm.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon   next to **Event Pages**.
4. Select **Delete**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about deleting Wix Events.

### If I re-add Wix Events later, will all my events be restored?

Yes. If you decide to add Wix Events again at a later date, your events are restored. However, the customizations you set in the editor revert back to the default settings.

### How can I display my events again after I delete them?

If you want to display your events again, [reverting to a previously saved version](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-and-managing-your-site-history) with the events will restore it.

### Can I temporarily hide the Wix Events app?

Yes. If you temporarily don't want to display your events list, [you can hide the page it's displayed on](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu#hiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu-1).
