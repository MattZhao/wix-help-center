---
title: "Wix Hotels by HotelRunner Request: Renting Out Individual Rooms at a Villa"
---

Currently, it is not possible to allow booking individual rooms at a villa-type property. For example, if the whole villa is booked, the individual rooms can't be rented, and if any of the rooms are booked, the entire villa can't be rented.

> **Note:**
>
> Make sure to [change the 'room' text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-changing-the-room-text-on-your-site) to 'villa' to clarify to clients what type of property is available for booking.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
