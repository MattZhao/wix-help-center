---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Making Health Waivers Mandatory Before Clients Can Book"
---

Currently, it is not possible to make it mandatory for clients to fill out a health waiver before they can book your services.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
