---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Charging Guests a Late Cancellation or No-Show Fee"
---

Currently, it is not possible to charge guests a fee for last-minute cancellations or no-shows with Wix Table Reservations.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can set up prepayment rules to reduce last-minute cancellations and no-shows by collecting payment upfront. Learn more about [setting up prepayment for table reservations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-prepayment-for-table-reservations).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
