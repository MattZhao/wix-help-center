---
title: "Wix Bookings: Changing the Number of Sessions in a Package"
---

You may want to reward or compensate a client by adding extra sessions to their [package](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-packages). Alternatively, you may also want to deduct a session if your client paid for it separately before buying a package.

> **Notes:**
>
> - When you change the number of sessions, you do not charge your client an additional amount.
> - You cannot edit an expired plan. Once a subscription expires, the number of remaining sessions resets to 0.
> - You cannot edit a recurring plan.

### To change the number of sessions in a package:

Go to [Subscriptions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Subscriptions&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/subscriptions) tab in your site's dashboard.   Select the relevant package you want to edit.  Click the **Edit **icon  under **Sessions Remaining**.

Click the up or down arrows to add or remove sessions.   Click **Save**.
