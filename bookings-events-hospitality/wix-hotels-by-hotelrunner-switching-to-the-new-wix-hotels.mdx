---
title: "Wix Hotels by HotelRunner: Switching to the New Wix Hotels"
---

You can add the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner from the [Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market). This makes it easier than ever to manage your properties, reservations, staff and guests from your site's dashboard.

To switch from the old Wix Hotels to the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, first add the Wix Hotels by HotelRunner app to your site and set it up.

Once you've set up the new app, disable the old Wix Hotels app to stop accepting reservations on your site. Then add your existing reservations to Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, before deleting the old Wix Hotels app.

---

### Step 1 | Add and set up the Wix Hotels by HotelRunner app

Start by [adding and setting up the Wix Hotels by HotelRunner app](https://wix.to/ZB4ZcR9). You can then customize your "Book a room" page, create your rooms, add your business information and set up your payment methods. Once you add and set up the app, you can manage your reservations, staff and guests from your site's dashboard.

#### To add the new Wix Hotels:

[Log in to your site using this link.](https://wix.to/ZB4ZcR9)  Once you're logged in using the link above, [go to the Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market).  Search for 'Wix Hotels'.   Click **Add to Site** below Wix Hotels by HotelRunner.  Choose the relevant site if you have more than one.  Click **Agree & Add**.   (In your Editor) Click **Add to Site **next to Wix Hotels.   Click **Save** at the top right.   You can then continue [setting up your new Wix Hotels pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-adding-and-setting-up-your-hotel#step-2-set-up-the-book-a-room-page-in-your-editor).

### Step 2 | Hide the old Wix Hotels app on your site

Now that you've added and set up the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner app, hide the old app to stop accepting reservations with it on your site.

#### To hide the old Wix Hotels app:

Go to the Book a Room page in your [Editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages and Menu**  on the left side of the Editor.  Hover over **Book a Room**.  Click the More Actions icon .  Click **Hide**.

---

### Step 3 (Optional) | Contact HotelRunner to switch to the new channel manager

If you used the channel manager in the old Wix Hotels app, contact HotelRunner at wix@hotelrunner.com. Tell them you're switching to the new app, and they will help transfer your existing channel manager account to the new app.

---

### Step 4 | Add your existing reservations to Wix Hotels by HotelRunner

Next, add all your reservations from the old Wix Hotels app to the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner app. Review all of your reservations in the old app, and then manually add them to the new app.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Make sure you [add all the relevant room types](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-adding-and-setting-up-your-hotel#step-3-create-your-rooms) in the Wix Hotels by HotelRunner app before copying your existing reservations from the old Wix Hotels.
> - Make sure you [export your existing reservations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/exporting-your-reservations-from-wix-hotels) from the old Wix Hotels.
>
> **Note: **You can only export reservations that were made through your Wix site or added manually by you or your staff. You cannot export reservations from external sources.

#### To add your existing reservations to the Wix Hotels by HotelRunner:

[Go to Hotels](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp/135aad86-9125-6074-7346-29dc6a3c9bcf%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Hotels) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Reservations **tab.  Hover over a reservation to see more details.  In a separate tab in your browser, open [Reservations](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp%2F826670ba-3a6b-4157-ac72-7c4fca9ce220%2F1a6e0874-2039-49cf-bef3-9138bb5c3b16%7C0%3F&title=Select+a+site&primaryButtonText=Open+Reservations) in the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner.  Click **+ Add Reservation** at the top.   Fill out the relevant for the first reservation you are copying over, including the reservation, contact and extra details.  Click **Add Reservation**.   Repeat steps 3 - 7 for each of your existing reservations.

---

### Step 5 | Delete the old Wix Hotels app from your site

Once you've added all of your existing reservations, you can delete the old Wix Hotels app from your site.

#### To delete the old Wix Hotels app:

[Go to Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions icon  next to Wix Hotels.   Click **Delete**.

**Note: **The word "pending" appears next to the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner app, this lets you know which is the new app and which is the old one you want to delete.

> **What's next?**
>
> With the old Wix Hotels removed from your site, you can now:
> - [Create your rate plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-creating-and-managing-your-rate-plans).
> - [Set your general, search and guest details rules](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-managing-your-reservation-settings) for your site.
> - [Set your opening seasons, check-in/out times, currency conversion rules and your child pricing policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-setting-your-open-seasons-pricing-and-general-info).
> - [Set your cancellation and deposit policies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-creating-and-managing-your-cancellation-and-deposit-policies).
