---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering Extras or Add-ons to Services for Clients"
---

Currently, it is not possible to offer extras or add-ons for booked services. This might include a post-training massage or additional service, such as a consultation. Add-ons can affect the price or duration of the original service that the client booked.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
