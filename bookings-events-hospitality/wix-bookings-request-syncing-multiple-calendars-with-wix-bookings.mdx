---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Syncing Multiple Calendars with Wix Bookings"
---

Currently, you and your staff members can each sync to one external calendar (Google, iCalendar, Outlook) at a time. It is currently not possible to sync with more than one external calendar per staff member.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is appreciated.
