title: "Wix Restaurants: Upgrading Your Restaurant Site"
---

With Wix Restaurants, you can showcase your restaurant [menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new) on your site, add [online orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new), use [table reservations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-table-reservations), and [automatically thermal-print](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-printing-an-order-in-the-new-restaurants-orders-app-overview) your orders, among other things.

By upgrading your restaurant site, you can accept payments and connect your own domain, ensuring a personalized online presence. You'll also gain access to extra bandwidth and storage, allowing you to accommodate growing needs. If you need help, our premium support services are available to provide you with dedicated assistance throughout your experience.

### To upgrade your site:

[Go to the Upgrade page](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) to compare plans.

**Tip:** To access advanced features available for Wix Restaurants, upgrade your site to a plan that supports payments.  Click **Select **under the site plan you wish to purchase.  Select a subscription period and click **Continue to Checkout**.  Enter your payment details.  Click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Note:**
>
> After submitting your purchase, your bank may require you to [verify your identity](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds). If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.
