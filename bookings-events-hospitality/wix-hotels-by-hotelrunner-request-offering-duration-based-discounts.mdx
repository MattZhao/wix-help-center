---
title: "Wix Hotels by HotelRunner Request: Offering Duration-based Discounts"
---

Currently, it is not possible to offer duration-based discounts using Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, for example, offering a discounted rate for 3 days, 1 week, 1 month, etc.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
