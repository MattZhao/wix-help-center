The Hotels tab in your site's dashboard is the main center of operation for your hotel. From here you can edit your property settings and manage your reservations. You can reach the Hotels tab from:

[Inside the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-wix-hotels-dashboard#accessing-the-hotels-tab-from-the-editor-1)[Your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-wix-hotels-dashboard#accessing-the-hotels-tab-from-your-sites-dashboard)

---

### Accessing the Hotels Tab from the Editor

Click the **Switch Page **drop-down on the top bar of the Editor and click to open the Hotel page.

Click the Wix Hotels app.Click **Manage**.

---

### Accessing the Hotels Tab from Your Site's Dashboard

Go to your [site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).Click the **Hotels** tab on the left.
