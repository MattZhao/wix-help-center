---
title: "Wix Hotels by HotelRunner Request: Supporting Right to Left Text"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display text that reads from right to left in Wix Hotels by HotelRunner. This means that, while languages such as Hebrew or Arabic are supported, they cannot be displayed from right to left.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
