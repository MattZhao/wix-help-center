---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Syncing Your Bookings Calendar with Additional Google Calendars in the Same Google Account"
---

Currently, when you [sync your Google Calendar with Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-syncing-bookings-and-google-calendars), you can only sync with your primary Google calendar (the first calendar created when you opened your Google account). It is not possible to sync with additional calendars in the same Google account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
