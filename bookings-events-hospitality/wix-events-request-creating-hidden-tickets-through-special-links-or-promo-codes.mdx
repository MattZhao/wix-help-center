---
title: "Wix Events Request: Creating Hidden Tickets through Special Links or Promo Codes"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create hidden tickets that are only available through special links or promotional codes. This would allow you to create VIP or discounted tickets hidden from the general public.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
