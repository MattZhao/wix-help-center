---
title: "Wix Bookings: About the \"My Bookings\" Page"
---

Wix Bookings integrates with the Members Area to provide a personalized dashboard, called "My Bookings," which allows your members to view, manage, and reschedule their bookings.

---

### How clients log in to the "My Bookings" Page

Clients can sign up as members and then log in to your site from the **Login Bar**.

---

### How clients use the "My Bookings" page

Once logged in, they can access their personal "My Bookings" page. Each client only sees their own details.

In the **My Bookings** page, clients can:

Quickly re-book a new session without re-entering their details.  Cancel an upcoming booking.  Reschedule an upcoming booking (if the service is not part of a [membership plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings)).  Check the time, location, and payment details of a past or upcoming booking. If the session was booked with a plan, members can see the plan details and remaining sessions

---

### Adding a Members Area to your site

If you don't already have a Members Area, you can add it from the Add Panel:

### Show me how

1. Go to the Add panel:

- **Wix Editor:** Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the Editor.
- **Editor X: **Click **Add Elements ** at the top of Editor X.
2. Click the **Members **tab.
3. Click **Add to Site**.
