---
title: "Wix Restaurants: Translating Your Restaurant Reservations"
---

Enhance your restaurant reservation experience by translating your Wix Multilingual reservation form and confirmation page. This allows your customers to choose their preferred language from your site's languages, making it easier for them to make reservations in a language they understand.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> First you need to [add and set up Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-adding-and-setting-up-wix-multilingual). You can add Wix Multilingual from your editor, your site's dashboard, or from the Wix App Market.

---

### Step 1 | Manage the translations in your site's dashboard

Your restaurant reservation form and confirmation page both include default fields which translate automatically.

#### To manage your translations:

[Go to Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/multilingual?&title=Translation%20Manager&buttonText=Go+To+Translation+Manager) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Table Reservations** on the left.  Choose the relevant option:

- **If you auto-translated your site:** Review the automated translations and edit if necessary.
- **If you didn't use auto-translation:** Translate the custom fields and policies manually.
    Click **Save and Publish**.

---

### Step 2 | Manage the translations in your editor

Next, head over to your editor and translate the [Reservation widget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-table-reservations-adding-a-reservation-widget-to-a-page) that appears on your site.

Wix EditorStudio Editor

2. Click the **Switch languages **drop-down menu at the top of the editor.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Switch Languages drop-down at the top of the Wix Editor.]

2. Go to the page with your Table Reservations widget: 

1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Select the relevant page.
3. Click the Table Reservations widget on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Text** tab.
6. Update the relevant text as needed.
7. Click **Save **or **Publish**.

> **What's next?**
>
> Continue translating the [other pages in your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-text-buttons-and-links). We also recommend [translating your automated emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-translating-automated-emails-with-wix-multilingual) into your site's languages. This allows you to speak to your audience in their language, offering a more local experience.
