---
title: "Wix Hotels by HotelRunner Request: Creating a Maximum Stay Rule"
---

Currently, it is not possible to control the length of a guest's reservation by creating a maximum stay rule for your Wix Hotel. All reservations, whether made online or manually, have a maximum duration of 90 days by default, and this limit cannot be changed.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
