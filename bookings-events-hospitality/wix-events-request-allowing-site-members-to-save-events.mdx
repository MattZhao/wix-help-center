---
title: "Wix Events Request: Allowing Site Members to Save Events"
---

Currently, it is not possible for site members to save events. This would allow potential guests to add events to their calendars, receive updates, and decide on participation later without needing to RSVP.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
