---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Receiving Notifications About Rejected Orders"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to get notified about rejected orders your customers placed on your site (e.g. declined by the payment gateway, address is out of delivery range, etc.). This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

> **Important:**
>
> Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
