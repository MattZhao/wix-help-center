---
title: "Wix Events Request: Adding a Shopping Cart"
---

Currently, Wix Events does not have a shopping cart. Clients who want to buy tickets for multiple events need to purchase tickets separately for each event.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
