title: "Wix Events: Adding Wix Events to Your Site"
---

When you add [Wix Events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-events) to a site, a dedicated "Events" page is created. This page serves as a central hub where visitors can find information about upcoming events, RSVP, and purchase tickets.

Utilize Wix Events to send customized tickets, manage ticket sales, organize guest lists, and check in attendees upon arrival. You can add Wix Events from the editor or the Apps tab in the site's dashboard.

#### To add Wix events:

Wix EditorEditor XStudio EditorDashboard
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Apps**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Search for the **Wix Events & Tickets** app in the search bar.
4. Click **Add to Site**.
