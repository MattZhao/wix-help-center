---
title: "Wix Bookings POS Request: Accepting Payment for Custom Pricing and Varied Pricing"
---

Currently, when pricing for a service is set using the Custom Pricing or Varied Pricing options, it's not possible to process payment for that service at a mobile point of sale.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
