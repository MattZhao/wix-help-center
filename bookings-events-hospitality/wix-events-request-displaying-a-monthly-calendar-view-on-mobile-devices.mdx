---
title: "Wix Events Request: Displaying a Monthly Calendar View on Mobile Devices"
---

Currently, mobile devices display the calendar view as a list. It is not possible to change it to a monthly calendar view.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
