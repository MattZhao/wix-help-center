---
title: "Dayful by Wix: Switching to Wix Bookings"
---

Dayful is no longer available as a standalone solution. Instead, it is now part of Wix Bookings, as a feature called [shareable links](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-managing-shareable-links). Wix Bookings provides more capabilities in addition to customizable booking links, such as [social media integrations and additional booking channels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-booking-integrations), [marketing tools](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-your-outbound-client-emails), [payment options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-getting-paid-online), and more.

You can still enjoy the freedom and flexibility to run your service business, get booked, and get paid, regardless of your online presence. However, when you need to scale your business with a full website, Wix Bookings has you covered.

Learn more about [adding and setting up Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings).
