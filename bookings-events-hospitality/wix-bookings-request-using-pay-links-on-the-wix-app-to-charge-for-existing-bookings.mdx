---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Using Pay Links on the Wix App to Charge for Existing Bookings"
---

Currently, it is not possible to use pay links to charge for existing bookings on the Wix app, it is only possible on desktop.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
