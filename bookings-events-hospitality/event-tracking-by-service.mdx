---
title: "Event Tracking by Service"
---

Some marketing integrations come with a list of pre-defined events that are available 'out of the box' once installed. Click an integration below to learn more about the pre-defined events for that service. Once you connect an integration, the events automatically start reporting.

> **Note:**
>
> Google Merchant Centre (GMC) and Facebook Catalog integrations do not track events. These integrations help you manage your product feed.

**In this article learn more about:**

[Google Analytics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/event-tracking-by-service#google-analytics)  [Google Tag Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/event-tracking-by-service#google-tag-manager)  [Facebook](https://support.wix.com/en/article/event-tracking-by-service#facebook)  [TikTok Pixel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/event-tracking-by-service#tiktok-pixel)
