---
title: "Wix Bookings: Hiding Images from the Service List"
---

If you add an image to a service you created, it is displayed on your Service List page.

You can choose to hide the service images from your service list if you'd like.

### To hide images from the service list:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the Service List element on your page.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Display** tab.  Deselect the **Image** checkbox under **Service Details**.
