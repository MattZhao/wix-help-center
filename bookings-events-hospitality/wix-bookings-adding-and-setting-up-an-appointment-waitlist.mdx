The appointment waitlist helps increase your earnings by filling schedule gaps caused by last-minute appointment cancellations or reschedules. It also gives you the flexibility to take more appointments on a certain day, even if there are no cancellations. It is also a great way to collect leads.

For example, if your client sees no relevant slots are available for a haircut on their preferred day, or if they can’t find a relevant slot, they can join the waiting list. As soon as a spot becomes available, you can contact them and book them in.

### Adding the appointment waitlist in the dashboard

You add the appointment waitlist from the Booking Calendar in the dashboard. This allows you to stay as booked as possible throughout each day, as cancellations or reschedules occur.

#### To add the appointment waitlist in the dashboard

[Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Waitlist** icon  in the top right.   Click **Get it now**.

### Adding clients to your appointment waitlist

You can add clients to the waitlist for your appointments at any time, using the Booking Calendar in your site's dashboard.

#### To add a client to your appointment waitlist:

[Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Waitlist** icon  in the top right.   Click **+ Add New**.   Enter the **Client Name**.   Enter the appointment details, including: the **Date**, **Start** **Time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)**, **Location**.  Select the relevant appointment from the **Service** drop-down.   Select the relevant staff member from the **Staff** drop-down.   (Optional) Enter a **Note to self** with any additional instructions, for example: "Client requested a phone call if no slots become available so they can schedule an appointment next week."  (Optional) Click **+ Add Another Service** if the client wants to be added to the waitlist for another service, and repeat steps 4-8 above.

### Managing clients on the appointment waitlist

You can sort and filter your appointment waitlist to make it easier to manage. You can also share the service calendar for the requested service to arrange alternative dates and times with the client.

#### To manage clients on the appointment waitlist:

[Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Waitlist** icon  in the top right.   Choose what you want to do:

### Filter and sort the appointment waitlist

1. Click **Filter** at the top.
2. Select the relevant filters from the drop-downs, including: **Date**, **Staff**, **Service**, and **Location**.
3. Click **Apply**.
4. Click the **Sort** icon .
5. Select the relevant option: **By session date** or **By request date**.

### Schedule an appointment from a waitlist application

1. Click **Schedule** next to the relevant entry on the appointment waitlist.
2. Select the relevant client.
3. Click **Continue**.
4. Check and confirm the details are correct.
5. (Optional) Select the **Notify client with an email** checkbox.
6. Choose the relevant option: 

- Click **Book** to accept payment later.
- Click **Book & Collect Payment** to create an order and charge the client for the service using the available options.

### FAQs

Click on a question below to learn more.

### Can clients change their time slot preferences on the waitlist?

No, clients cannot change their time slot preferences online once they have joined the waitlist. They can choose as many time slots as they wish on the waitlist, though these waitlist slots do not appear in the My Bookings section of the Members Area.

### Are clients notified immediately when an appointment slot becomes available?

No, currently you must manage the waitlist manually. This is to prevent automated bookings that no longer suit the client.

### Is it possible to prevent clients from adding themselves to the waitlist?

Yes, you can choose whether to publish the appointment waitlist on your live site, or only allow you and your [site Admins (Co-Owners)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview) to manage the appointment waitlist from the dashboard. You can remove the appointment waitlist plugin from your live site at any time.

### Can I allow my staff members to manage the waitlist?

Currently, it is only possible for you, the site owner, and [site Admins (Co-Owners)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview) to manage the appointment waitlist. Staff members without the relevant permissions do not have access to view or manage the appointment waitlist.
