---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Allowing Your Customers to Order on the Wix Member Apps"
---

Currently, if you're using the [new Wix Restaurant Orders app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new), your customers cannot place orders on the Wix member apps (Spaces or your own branded app).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
