Wix automatically sends events to Google Analytics about your [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings) services on your site. Receive detailed data related to your services, such as customer behavior and popular booking times, to analyze key metrics and identify trends.

### What other events can I see on Google Analytics?

The events you see in your Google Analytics account are:
- **Product Impression**: When a visitor views a service you offer.
- **Product Click**: When a visitor clicks on a button or link that redirects to a service page.
- **View Content: **When a visitor views a service page.
- **Initiate Checkout**: When a visitor clicks on the "Book Now" button.
- **Start Payment**: When the visitor is asked to pay (but before he adds his payment information).
- **Add Payment Info**: After the visitor added his payment information.
- **Purchase: **When a visitor books a service and is taken to the "Thank-you" page.

### Viewing event data in Google Analytics

Track and monitor event data directly in Google Analytics. Choose to view a specific report or see all events to help you gain valuable insights into your site performance.

#### To view event data in Google Analytics:

Sign in to your Google Analytics.   Click **Reports** on the left.  Click **Monetization**.  Select the report you want to view.

> **Notes:**
>
> - You can see all of the events by clicking **Behavior **and then **Events**.
> - It can take a few days until the reports start receiving data.
