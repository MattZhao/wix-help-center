title: "Wix Restaurants: Printing Online Orders from the Wix App"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about the **new** Restaurants Orders app.
> - Before you can start printing orders, make sure you have a [compatible printer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-compatible-thermal-printers-for-online-orders-new).

Automatically print incoming orders right from your device using the Wix app. Once you've connected a thermal printer, orders will be printed automatically whenever you have the app open. You also have the option to manually print your orders directly from the app.

---

### Step 1 | Pair your printer with your device

Set up your thermal printer to automatically print orders placed by customers on your site. First, decide how you want to pair the printer with your device (Bluetooth, WiFi or Ethernet). Then, click a connection type below to see the steps.

### Bluetooth

Ensure your printer's Bluetooth is turned on. In some printers, you need to turn on pairing mode. Then, open the Bluetooth settings in your mobile device / tablet and pair it with your printer.
**Note: **Allow location and nearby devices discovery permissions on your mobile device.

### WiFi and Ethernet

Follow your thermal printer manufacturer instructions to connect it to the same network as your mobile device / tablet.

> **Important:**
>
> If you have any issues with pairing, contact the printer manufacturer's support for assistance. If you're having trouble connecting a printer, [follow these troubleshooting steps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-troubleshooting-thermal-printing-of-online-orders-new-from-the-wix-owner-app).

---

### Step 2 | Connect the printer to the Wix app

Once your printer and device are paired, open the Wix app to connect the printer to Wix Online Orders.

#### To connect the printer to the Wix app:

Access your online ordering settings in the Wix app: 

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Restaurants**.
4. Tap **Online Orders (New)**.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
6. Select **Manage settings**.
    Connect your printer to the app:

1. Tap **Thermal printing** under **Printing**.
2. Tap **Get Started**.
3. Tap **Next**,** **then tap **Next** again.
4. Verify your printer is the one selected from the list and tap **Next**.

**Tip: **You can tap another printer on the list to select it instead.
5. Tap **Done**.

**Tip:** Verify the printer is connected by tapping **Print a test order**.

> **What's next?**
>
> Now that the printer is connected, it will automatically print incoming orders. Auto-printing requires the app to be constantly open – for your convenience, your Online Orders page always stays on (even if you're inactive for a while).

### How can I turn off auto-printing?

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Restaurants**.
4. Tap **Online Orders (New)**.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
6. Select **Manage settings**.
7. Tap **Thermal printer**.
8. Tap to disable the **Auto-print new orders** toggle.

### How can I adjust the number of copies for printing an order?

You can set the auto-print to make between 1 to 10 copies of every order.
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Restaurants**.
4. Tap **Online Orders (New)**.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
6. Select **Manage settings**.
7. Connect your printer to the app:

1. Tap **Thermal printing** under **Printing**.
2. Tap **Thermal printer**.
3. Tap the **+** or **-** icons next to **Number of copies**.

**Note:** This option is only available for auto-printing.

### How can I manually thermal-print an order from the status view?

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Restaurants**.
4. Tap **Online Orders (New)**.
5. Tap **More Actions**** **icon  on the relevant order.
6. Tap **Print order**.
