title: "Wix Bookings Request: Booking Sessions for Clients on the Wix App Without Entering Their Email"
---

Currently, it is not possible for you to book sessions for clients on the Wix app without entering their email address.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
