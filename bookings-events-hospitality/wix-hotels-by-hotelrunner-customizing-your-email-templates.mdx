### Using dynamic values in your email templates

Dynamic values are variables that you can insert into your email templates and replace with personalized information when you send emails to an individual recipient. You can edit the text of the subject line and email body and then add dynamic values to offer a better experience to your guests.

#### To use variables in your email templates:

Go to [Email Templates](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp%2F826670ba-3a6b-4157-ac72-7c4fca9ce220%2F1a6e0874-2039-49cf-bef3-9138bb5c3b16%7C7%3F&title=Select+a+site&primaryButtonText=Open+Email+Templates) in your site's dashboard.   Click **Edit** next to the email template you want to customize with dynamic values.   Click the **Variables** drop-down next to the **Subject** or **Body** content sections.

Choose the relevant variable from the different variable types: **Property**, **Customer**, or **Reservation**.

**Note: **You can also add different **Content block** variables to the body of the email. These include: Contact info, Policy tables, Payment content blocks and even a Reservation details button.   Copy and paste the variable to the correct location in the email **Subject line** or **Body content**.   When you are happy with the changes made to your email template, click **Update**.

### Customizing your automated emails

You can customize your automated emails with text and with variables. This allows you to maintain consistency with your guests across all of your communications, while adding a personalized touch to welcome your guests.

With Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, you can customize the following email templates:

**Information email content: **The first email guests receive after they make their initial booking.  **Modification email content: **The email guests receive after they make any changes to their booking.   **Cancelation email content:** The email guests receive after they cancel their booking.  **Confirmation notification email content: **The email guests receive after they confirm their reservation. This email can also be sent manually by your staff members if guests require additional confirmation or did not receive their initial confirmation email for any reason.

#### To customize your automated emails:

Go to [Email Templates](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp%2F826670ba-3a6b-4157-ac72-7c4fca9ce220%2F1a6e0874-2039-49cf-bef3-9138bb5c3b16%7C7%3F&title=Select+a+site&primaryButtonText=Open+Email+Templates) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Edit** next to the email template you want to customize.  Click to edit the text in both the subject line and the body of the email.   (Optional) Add any variables you require in the **Subject** or **Body content** of the email, following the steps in the section above.   Click **Update** at the top.   (Optional) Click the More Actions icon  at the top, and then click **Send test email** to preview the changes you made.

**Note: **You can revert to the original template text by selecting **Apply default template**.

### FAQs

Here are some common questions about customizing automated emails:

### What are dynamic values?

Also known as variables, dynamic values are pieces of code that display different information to each user based on their data. Dynamic values can show your contacts personalized text (e.g. their name) or information related to your business relationship (e.g. room number, reservation details).

### Can I create more automated email templates in Wix Hotels by HotelRunner?

Currently, it is not possible to create additional email templates in Wix Hotels by HotelRunner. However, you can add [Wix Automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-getting-started) to your site to create additional automated emails.
