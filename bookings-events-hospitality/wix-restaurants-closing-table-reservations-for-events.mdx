---
title: "Wix Restaurants: Closing Table Reservations for Events"
---

Got a special event coming up? Make sure your guests know when your restaurant isn't available by adjusting your table reservations accordingly.

### To add events to table reservations:

[Go to Table Reservations](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/restaurants/table-reservations?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Table+Reservations) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Settings** at the top right.  Click **Online reservations**.  Click **Edit** under **Online availability **to set the days and times that table reservations are unavailable at your restaurant.  Click **+ Add Special Day**.  Enter a name for the event.  Click the **Availability **drop-down and choose **Unavailable**.  Set the event's **Start Time **and **End Time **from the relevant fields.   Click **Apply**.  Click **Save**.
