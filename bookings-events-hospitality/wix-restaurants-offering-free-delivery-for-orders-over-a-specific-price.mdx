> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about the **original Restaurants Orders app**.
> - We've released a new online ordering app. Learn how to [add and set up the new Wix Restaurants Orders (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).

To allow free delivery over a specific minimum price you'll need to create 2 separate delivery areas for the same location, 1 with the regular delivery price for that area, and another with a minimum order fee and free delivery.

### To set up free delivery for orders above a minimum price:

Set up your first delivery area following the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-delivery-areas-for-orders). You may have already done this, if so, skip to the next step.  Add another delivery area with a minimum order price and 0 delivery charge:

### Show me how

1. Click **+ New Area** at the top right of the **Delivery** tab.
2. Enter a name for this area so you'll know what it's for (e.g., Delivery - Free).
3. Enter the minimum order amount that you require for free delivery.
4. Make sure the delivery charge is set to 0.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also use this method to set up different delivery charges based on the price of your customers' orders. For example:
> - For orders over $30 - Free delivery
> - For orders between $15 and $30 - $3 delivery charge
> - For orders under $15 - $5 delivery charge
> To create different delivery charges based on price, simply create delivery areas for the same geographical area - one for each type of delivery charge.
