title: "Wix Restaurants: Translating Your Restaurant Menu"
---

> This article is about [the new Wix Restaurants Menus app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new).

Translate your restaurant menu using Wix Multilingual to target your diverse customer base. You can translate your restaurant menus into a variety of languages so that your site visitors can choose to read your menus in a language that they understand.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> First you need to [add and set up Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-adding-and-setting-up-wix-multilingual). You can add Wix Multilingual from your editor, your site's dashboard, or from the Wix App Market.

---

### To manage your menu translations:

[Go to Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/multilingual?&title=Translation%20Manager&buttonText=Go+To+Translation+Manager) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Restaurant Menus** on the left.  Select what you want to translate first: Items, Item labels, Menu names, Sections, Modifier group names, Variants & modifiers.  Choose the relevant option:

- **If you auto-translated your site:** Review the automated translations and edit if necessary.
- **If you didn't use auto-translation:** Translate the custom fields and policies manually.

> **What's next?**
>
> Continue translating the [other pages on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-text-buttons-and-links). We also recommend [translating your automated emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-translating-automated-emails-with-wix-multilingual) into your site's languages. This allows you to speak to your audience in their language, offering a more local experience.
