---
title: "Wix Events Request: Setting a Ticket Limit per Event"
---

Currently, you can limit the number of guests that can purchase a particular type of ticket (i.e. you can limit sales of VIP tickets to 20). You cannot set a general ticket limit of how many tickets you can sell per event.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
