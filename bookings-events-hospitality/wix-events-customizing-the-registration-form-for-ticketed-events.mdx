### Customizing the registration form of a ticketed event

Update the fields in the registration form to get the details you need from your guests (e.g. phone numbers).

> **Note:**
>
> First name, last name, and email are required fields on the registration form. It is not possible to remove them.

#### To customize the registration form:

[Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant event.  Click the **Settings **tab.  Click **Edit** next to **Registration Form**.  Choose whether to collect info per order or per ticket:

- **Fill out 1 registration form per order: **Guest fills out the registration form once, even if buying multiple tickets.
- **Fill out a registration form for each ticket in an order:** Guest fills out a separate registration form for each ticket. 

**Tip:** Learn more about [collecting guest info for individual tickets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-collecting-guest-info-for-individual-event-tickets).
    Edit the form text:

1. Hover over any the text fields and click the **Edit** icon .
2. Edit the text and click the checkmark to save.
    Add additional preset fields to the form:

1. Click** **+ **Add More Options**.
2. Select the field you want to add: **Phone number**, **Address, Date**, **Comments** or **Custom Question**.
3. Edit the option using the available customizations.

**Note:** If you've added a custom question and selected **Dropdown Menu**, **Multiple Choice**, or **Checkboxes **as the response type, you can enter all the options your guests can choose from (e.g. Yes, No) by pressing **Enter** on your keyboard after every choice.
4. (Optional) Select the **Make this info required** checkbox.

**Note: **When selected, guests cannot RSVP without entering the requested information.
5. Click the checkmark to save.
    (Optional) Edit the 'Continue' button text: 

1. Hover over **Continue **and click the** Edit** icon .
2. Edit the button text and click the **checkmark** to save.
    Click **Save **at the top right.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about customizing the registration form.

### How can I read responses to registration form questions?

The **Guests **tab is the place to view responses that individuals guests entered in the registration form. To view a table with all your guests responses, you can export the guest list.
Learn more about [viewing guest responses](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-managing-your-guest-list#viewing-responses-to-registration-form-questions) and [exporting your guest list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-exporting-your-guest-list-or-order-list).

### How can I customize the "Tickets Unavailable" and "Registration Closed" messages?

1. [Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant event.
3. Click the **Settings **tab.
4. Click **Edit** next to the message you want to customize.

Learn more about [editing the "Thank You" and "Registration Closed" messages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-customizing-registration-settings-messages#editing-the-thank-you-registration-closed-messages).

### How can I customize the design of the registration form?

You can update the colors, font, and more to get a look that suits your site. Learn how to [customize the registration form design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-customizing-your-registration-form-page).
