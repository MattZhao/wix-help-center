title: "Request: Customer Care Ticket History"
---

Currently, the Wix Help Center does not offer a Ticket History section where you can look up all the interaction you've had with Wix Customer Care in the past.

> If you are using Wix Studio, you can [view your team's ticket log](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-viewing-your-customer-care-ticket-log) from your workspace.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
