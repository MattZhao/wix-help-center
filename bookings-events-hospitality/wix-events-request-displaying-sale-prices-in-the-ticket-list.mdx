---
title: "Wix Events Request: Displaying Sale Prices in the Ticket List"
---

Currently, you can reduce ticket prices by [creating coupons or automatic discounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-event-coupons). However, it is not possible to show these discounted prices directly in the ticket list.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
