---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Alert Icon for New Bookings on the Wix App Calendar"
---

You can [view your daily schedule](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-viewing-your-upcoming-sessions-in-the-wix-owner-app) by looking at the Agenda view on the calendar in the Wix app. Whenever a new booking is made, it's automatically added to your schedule. Currently, there is no alert icon or function to automatically navigate you to the new booking.

We are always working to update and improve our products,  and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
