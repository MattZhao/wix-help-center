title: "Wix Bookings: Deleting a Service"
---

Stay organized by deleting services that you no longer want to offer.

> **Important:**
>
> - Deleting a class service removes all its future sessions from your Bookings calendar and site. To avoid losing upcoming bookings, you can [make it unbookable instead](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-making-services-unbookable).
> - **Disconnecting Wix Bookings from Google Calendar, or deleting your site, does not remove the services from Google Calendar. **To remove a service from Google Calendar, you must delete the service, as described below.

### Deleting a service

Delete a service on your dashboard or through your Wix mobile app. You can also decide if you want to email participants that the service is cancelled.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Booking Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click** **the** More Actions** icon  on the relevant service.
3. Click **Delete**.
4. Click **Yes, Delete **to confirm.
[image: A screenshot showing how to delete a service on your dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Services**.
4. Tap** **on the service you want to delete:
For appointments
1. Tap **Delete service**.
2. Tap **Delete** to confirm.
For classes
1. Tap **Delete service**.
2. Tap **Delete** to confirm.
3. (Optional) Tap **Yes** to send a notification email to your booked participants.
For courses
1. Tap the **More Actions icon ** at the top right of the screen.
2. Tap **Cancel course.**
3. Tap **Cancel** to confirm.
4. (Optional) Tap **Yes** to send a notification email to your participants.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about deleting a service.

### What happens to clients who used a plan to book a service that I delete?

Clients who booked with a plan will receive automatic session credit if you delete the service.

### Can I delete one session without deleting the entire service?

You can cancel and remove sessions from your calendar as needed. [Read more about it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-canceling-a-session).

### How do I edit a notification email when a service is cancelled?

You can personalize your cancellation email through your Booking Settings. [Read more about it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-canceling-a-session#faqs).
