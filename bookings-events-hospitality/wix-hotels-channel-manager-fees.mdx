> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

The Wix Hotels Channel Manager lets you sync your hotel room availability and rates with over 150 hotel services, including Booking.com, Expedia, Orbitz and more.

Wix works with HotelRunner to provide this integration. The service comes with a 14 day free trial. After the trial period, the service costs $15 a month.

> **Note:**
>
> This is an optional service and is not included in your Premium Plan.

For information on how to set up the Wix Hotels Channel Manager, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-your-hotel-to-booking-channels-using-the-channel-manager).
