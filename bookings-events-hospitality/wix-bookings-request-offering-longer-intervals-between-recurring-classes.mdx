---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering Longer Intervals Between Recurring Classes"
---

Currently, you can offer a maximum interval of 4 weeks in between recurring classes. Therefore, it is not possible to schedule recurring class sessions months, quarters, or even years apart.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
