---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering Appointment Waitlists"
---

Currently, it is not possible to offer a waitlist for your appointment slots.

By offering an appointment waitlist, clients could join when their desired spot is unavailable, and you can contact them directly once a spot opens up or offer them an alternative appointment slot.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
