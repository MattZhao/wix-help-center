---
title: "Wix Events: Inviting Guests to Join an Event from the Wix App"
---

After you create an event, let people know about it by sending an invitation right from your mobile device.

### To invite guests to join an event:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Events**.  Select **Published Events**.  Tap the **More Actions **** **icon next to a specific event.

  Tap **Invite Guests**.  Select an option:

- **Email Wix Contacts:** Send an email directly to the contacts saved in your Wix account (site members, customers, contacts).
- **SMS: **Send an SMS** **(text message) to the phone contacts you select.
- **Share via Social & More:** Send the link and invitation code to people through the share links available on your phone.
- **Copy Link: **Copy the link and paste it into a new message in any application.

> **Guests inviting guests:**
>
> Members can invite friends and family to join an event by clicking the **Events** tab in the [Spaces by Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview) and then **Invite **under the relevant event.
