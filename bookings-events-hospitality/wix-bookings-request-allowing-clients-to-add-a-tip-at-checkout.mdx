---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Allowing Clients to Add a Tip at Checkout"
---

We are excited to announce that [clients can now add tips at checkout on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-new-wix-tips-app). When they book a session, the tip will automatically be distributed to the staff member(s).

**Note:** You can also assign tips to specific staff members when collecting payments from clients.
