---
title: "Wix Restaurants: Manually Printing Online Orders from the Wix App"
---

Print your orders using an A4 printer connected to your mobile device. Connect your printer to your mobile device to print orders. Once your printer is connected, you can manually print new orders.

> **Important:**
>
> This article is about the **new** Restaurants Orders app.

### To manually print an order in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Restaurants**.  Tap **Online Orders (New)**.  Tap the Manage Order icon  on the relevant order.  Tap **Print order**.

> **Tip**
>
> You can also print from the orders tab by selecting an order and tapping **Print Order**.
