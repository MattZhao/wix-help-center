title: "Fit by Wix: Selling Your Fitness Merchandise"
---

Adding an online store to your Fit by Wix app can boost your income while providing your members with the gear they need to train.

---

### Step 1 | Add Wix Stores

If you haven't already added Wix Stores to your site, you can add it now.

#### To add Wix Stores:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Scroll down and tap **Manage Apps**.  Tap **Add** next to **Store**.  Go to your editor and [add the pending app from the App Market](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-pending-app-in-your-editor).

---

### Step 2 | Add products

Start building your online store by creating products. For products that have different sizes, colors, or styles, you can add the options you need.

> **Tip:**
>
> Selling branded merchandise with your logo helps promote your business while giving members a sense of belonging to your fitness community.

#### To add products:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Store** **Products**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Store** **Products**.  Tap **Add a Product** to [create your first product](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-product-in-the-wix-owner-app).

**Tip: **Alternatively, you can edit one of the sample products.

---

### Step 3 | Connect a payment method

To accept payment from customers, you need to set up at least one payment method. These can include a payment provider for credit / debit cards, PayPal, or the manual (offline) method.

> **Important:**
>
> Some payment providers can only be connected through desktop. To manage, change or see a wider list of available providers, such as [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments), go to the [Accept Payments tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) on your site's dashboard.

#### To connect a payment method:

Go to your site in the Wix app.    Tap the **Manage **icon .  Tap **Settings **and select **Accept Payments.**  [Connect one or more payment methods](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-connecting-a-payment-method).

---

### Step 4 | Set up shipping

There are a variety of ways to [get products to your customers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-a-guide-to-setting-up-shipping-delivery-and-pickup). Choose from:

Shipping options: Free shipping, flat rate shipping, shipping by weight, etc.  Local delivery: Offer in-person delivery in your region  Store pickup: Let customers pick up their order from your business location

#### To set up shipping:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.   Tap **Orders**.  Tap the **Settings **** **icon.  Select **Set shipping**.  Tap **+ New Region**.

1. Enter a region name.
2. Tap **+ Add Destination**.
3. Tap the destination(s) you want to add to this shipping rule.
4. Tap **Save**.
    Choose what you want to do:

### Set up free shipping

Free shipping is the default shipping rule. Just tap **Save** to save this rule.

### Set up flat rate shipping

To simplify shipping, you can simply set one shipping rate for all your orders (e.g. shipping for all orders is $8).
**To set up flat rate shipping:**
1. Tap **Edit **next to **Free Shipping**.
2. Tap **Change **next to **Free Shipping**.
3. Select **Flat Rate**.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Create a delivery option (e.g. Standard Shipping, Express Shipping):

1. Enter a title in the **Delivery Option Name **field.

**Note: **This is the name customers select (e.g. Standard Shipping) if you offer more than one delivery option.
2. (Optional) Enter the estimated delivery time for this option in the **Est. Delivery Time** field.
3. Enter the shipping fee in the **Rate** field.
4. (Optional) To offer free shipping on orders above a specific price:

1. Tap to enable the** Offer free Shipping **toggle.
2. Enter the price above which shipping is free and tap **Done**.

**Note: **If you create additional shipping services (e.g. priority shipping), this checkbox must be selected and the amount specified to apply to each service.
3. Tap **Save**.
6. (Optional) Create an additional delivery option (e.g. Express Shipping) by tapping **+Add Delivery Option** and following the instructions above.
7. (Optional) Add a handling fee:

1. Tap **Add** next to **Handling Fee**.
2. Enter the fee amount.
3. Tap **Save**.
8. Tap **Save**.
9. Tap **Save **again.

### Set up shipping rates by weight

Setting your shipping rate by weight is ideal for stores that sell items that cost very different rates to ship because of their weight (e.g. a store that sells jewelry and winter coats). 
To set it up, create 2 or more weight categories and set a shipping price for each. For example, shipping can cost $5 for orders under 1 pound, $10 shipping for orders between 1 - 3 pounds, and $15 shipping for orders that weigh over 3 pounds.
**To set up shipping rates by weight. **
1. Tap **Edit **next to **Free Shipping**.
2. Tap **Change **next to **Free Shipping**.
3. Select **Rate by Weight**.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Create a delivery option (e.g. Standard Shipping, Express Shipping)

1. Enter a title in the **Delivery Option Name **field.

**Note: **This is the name customers select (e.g. Standard Shipping) if you offer more than one delivery option.
2. (Optional) Enter the estimated delivery time for this option in the **Est. Delivery Time** field.
3. Enter the top limit of the first weight range (e.g. enter 2 lb. to create a rule for purchases from 0 - 2 lb).

**Note: **The top limit of the first weight range is automatically set as the bottom limit of the second weight range.
4. Enter the rate (shipping price) for the first weight range (e.g.$4).
5. Create the second weight range: (e.g. 2 lb- 4 lb) and add the shipping rate (e.g.$8):

- If this is your last weight range (e.g. 2 lb and up), just enter the shipping rate.
- If you plan to add more weight ranges, tap **+ Add Range** to enter the new range and shipping rate.
6. (Optional) Tap **Add Range** to add another weight range and rate.
7. (Optional) To offer free shipping on orders above a specific price:

1. Tap to enable the** Offer free Shipping **toggle.
2. Enter the price above which shipping is free and tap **Done**.

**Note: **If you create additional shipping services (e.g. priority shipping), this checkbox must be selected and the amount specified to apply to each service.
8. (Optional) Repeat step 5 to create additional shipping rule(s).
9. Tap **Save**.
6. (Optional) Create an additional delivery option (e.g. Express Shipping) by tapping **+ Add Delivery Option** and following the instructions in step 5 above.
7. (Optional) Add a handling fee:

1. Tap **Add** next to **Handling Fee**.
2. Enter the fee amount.
3. Tap **Save**.
8. Tap **Save**.
9. Tap **Save **again.

### Set up shipping rates by price

You can set your shipping fees so that the price of the order determines how much customers pay for shipping. For example, you can charge $10 for shipping for orders between $0 - $100. Then, you can charge less or more for shipping, when the orders is for $100 and over.
**To set up shipping rates by price:**
1. Tap **Edit **next to **Free Shipping**.
2. Tap **Change **next to **Free Shipping**.
3. Select **Rate by Order Price**.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Create a delivery option (e.g. Standard Shipping, Express Shipping)

1. Enter a title in the **Delivery Option Name **field.

**Note: **This is the name customers select (e.g. Standard Shipping) if you offer more than one delivery option.
2. (Optional) Enter the estimated delivery time for this option in the **Est. Delivery Time** field.
3. Create the first price range: (e.g. $0 - $15) and add the shipping rate (e.g.$4).
4. Create the second price range: (e.g. $15 - $30) and add the shipping rate (e.g.$8):

- If this is your last price range (e.g. $15 and up), just enter the shipping rate.
- If you plan to add more price ranges, tap **+ Add Range** to enter the new range and shipping rate.
5. (Optional) To offer free shipping on orders above a specific price:

1. Tap to enable the** Offer free Shipping **toggle.
2. Enter the price above which shipping is free and tap **Done**.

**Note: **If you create additional shipping services (e.g. priority shipping), this checkbox must be selected and the amount specified to apply to each service.
3. Tap **Save**.
6. (Optional) Create an additional delivery option (e.g. Express Shipping) by tapping **+ Add Delivery Option** and following the instructions in step 5 above.
7. (Optional) Add a handling fee:

1. Tap **Add** next to **Handling Fee**.
2. Enter the fee amount.
3. Tap **Save**.
8. Tap **Save**.
9. Tap **Save** again.

### Set up store pickup

You can set up store pickup (curbside pickup) as the only delivery option, or in addition to another shipping option, such as free shipping. 
To set store pickup as an additional option, enable both toggles.To set store pickup as the only option, disable the shipping toggle.
**To set up Store Pickup:**
1. Scroll down and tap the **Store Pickup** toggle to enable it.
2. Enter a Pickup title.
3. Do one of the following: 

- Select the **Use my business address **toggle.** **
- Enter the address where customers can pick up products.
4. (Optional) Enter when the product will be ready for pickup.
5. Enter pickup instructions for your customer.
6. Tap **Save**.
7. Tap **Save **again.

### Step 5 | Setting up tax

Make sure you comply with local tax laws by setting up the correct tax rules for your store. Most merchants are required to collect taxes only from their own country, or only from their local state or province. There are, however, exceptions to this guideline. Learn more about [tax collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tax-collection-an-overview).

> **Important:**
>
> Different tax rules apply in different regions. Consult with an accountant or with your local tax authority for specific information relevant for your store.

#### To set up tax:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.   Tap **Orders**.  Tap the **Settings** icon** **.  Tap **Set tax**.  Select a country or region and [set up tax collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tax-collection-an-overview#step-2-select-manual-or-automatic-tax).

### Step 6 | Setting up a point of sale

By making your phone a point of sale (POS), you can sell and collect cash or credit card payments right on your mobile device - all without requiring your members to access your site or download Fit by Wix.

Once you have the Wix app, you can start accepting cash payments with no further setup. To accept credit / debit card payments you need to create a Wix Payments account and order a card reader.

#### To set up a point of sale:

[Create a Wix Payments account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-wix-payments-account).   [Order a card reader](https://www.wix.com/pos/hardware/product-page/card-reader).   Connect the card reader:

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Getting Paid** and select **Point of Sale**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon    at the top right.
5. Select **Connect Card Reader**.
6. Press the power button to turn on the card reader.
7. Tap **Connect** in the app, next to the reader.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [setting up a mobile point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale).

### Step 7 | Handling store orders

After members start making purchases, it's time to handle the incoming store orders. This can include adding a tracking number, marking the order as paid and fulfilled, and shipping the order.

#### To handle store orders:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Sales**.   Tap **Orders**.  Tap the relevant order.  [Handle the order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-your-orders-from-the-wix-owner-app).
