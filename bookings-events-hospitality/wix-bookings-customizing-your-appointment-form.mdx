The Appointment Form is made up of sections and sub-sections, each of which you can customize to suit your needs. Change the elements that appear in this form and design every part to match your style.

### To customize your Appointment Form:

Go to the page with the Appointment Form in your Editor.   Click the Appointment Form widget.  Choose what you want to customize:

### Widget elements

Choose which details you want to display in the Appointment Form, including the duration, price and location. The more details you display, the easier it is for clients to get booking. 

1. Click the Elements icon .
2. Select the checkbox next to each of the elements you want to display on the form: **

**[image: A screenshot showing the service details you can choose to display.]**

Note: **The elements vary depending on the settings you choose when creating the appointment.

### Design

Customize the elements inside the Appointment Form widget to suit your brand and highlight certain details of your service. 

1. Click the Design icon .
2. Adjust the design of the text, background color, borders, and corners using the available formatting options. You can customize the following widget elements:

- **Input fields**
- **Book Button**
- **Background**
