---
title: "Wix Events Request: Integration with Wix Restaurants"
---

Currently, Wix Events is not integrated with [Wix Table Reservations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-table-reservations). This would let restaurant customers see events and buy tickets when they book a table.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
