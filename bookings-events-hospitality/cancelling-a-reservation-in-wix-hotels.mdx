> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

At any time you can cancel a reservation from your hotel calendar.

### To cancel a reservation:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Reservations **tab.Click the relevant reservation on the **Reservation Calendar**.Click the **Reservation Status** drop-down menu.Click **Cancelled**.Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> Cancelling the reservation on your calendar will not inform the guest. Make sure to contact your booking and confirm the cancelled reservation.
