---
title: "Wix Bookings: Extending the Duration of a Membership Plan or Package"
---

Want to give your client extra time to take advantage of a [membership plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings) or a [package](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings)? You can extend the period of time a valid plan is good for. This can come in handy if you temporarily weren't able to provide services or your client was sick and wasn't able to make use of the plan.

> **Notes:**
>
> - When you extend a plan, no additional money is collected.
> - You cannot edit an expired plan.
> - You cannot edit a recurring plan.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Subscriptions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Subscriptions&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/subscriptions) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant subscription.
3. Click **Manage Subscription**.
4. Select** Extend subscription**.
5. Click the date and select a new end date for the plan.
6. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Dashboard showing where to confirm extended subscription date.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Sales** and select **Subscriptions**.
4. Tap the relevant subscription.
5. Tap the **More Actions **icon  at the top right.
6. Tap **Extend subscription**.
7. Tap the **End date **drop-down.
8. Select a new end date for the plan.
9. Tap the **Checkmark** icon .
10. Tap **Save**
[image: A screenshot of the Owner app showing where to add and save an extended subscription date.]
1. Tap **Yes, Extend** to confirm.

1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Manage**.
3. Tap **Sales** and select **Subscriptions**.
4. Tap the relevant subscription.
5. Tap the **More Actions **icon  at the top right.
6. Tap **Extend subscription**.
7. Tap the **End date **drop-down.
8. Select a new end date for the plan.
9. Tap the **Checkmark** icon .
10. Tap **Save**.
11. Tap **Yes, Extend** to confirm.
