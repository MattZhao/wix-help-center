Keep track of potential leads coming from Wix Chat. Use Google Analytics to get information about every interaction with your chat window and reach out to those who left their contact details. To get started, [connect Google Analytics to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tracking-events-on-your-wix-site-with-a-google-analytics-property).

### Which chat events can I track?

By default, Wix sends information to Google Analytics about the following chat events:

**Leads:** Whenever a visitor submits the contact form in the chat. **Engagement: **Whenever a visitor opens or closes the chat window.

#### To view the events in your Google Analytics account:

Sign in to your [Google Analytics](http://www.google.com/analytics/) account. Go to the relevant View, located in the top left corner.
**Note:** If you only have 1 view in your Google Analytics account the View will appear automatically on your home page. Click **Behavior** in the menu on the left.Click **Events**.Click **Overview**.

> **Important:**
>
> It can take up to 24 hours for your events to appear in Google Analytics.

> **Tip:**
>
> Use the Google Analytics Real-Time report to test your events and make sure they're logged. [Learn More](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1638635?hl=en)
