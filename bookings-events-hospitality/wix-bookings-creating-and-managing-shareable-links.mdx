Create shareable links to your bookings pages, memberships, and packages, to make it easy for clients to book your services. You can customize the links to show different services or even showcase the services specific staff members offer.

Shareable links are a great way to get bookings, even if you haven't finished creating your website. We'll make a functional mini-site for you so that you don't miss any bookings while you're adding the finishing touches to your site. Getting the word out about your services is a crucial part of growing your business.

### Sharing a link to your service list

Share a link with your clients that directs them to a list of services from a single staff member or at a specific location.

#### To share a link to your service list:

[Go to Shareable Links](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/dashboard?referralInfo=sidebar?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Shareable+Links) in the dashboard.   Click **Create Link** under **Service list**.   Select the relevant person from the **Staff member **drop-down or select **All staff members**.   Click **Continue**.   Choose what you want to do:

- **Copy link:** Choose this option to copy the complete link. This is a great option for hyperlinked text in promotional emails.
- **Share via:** Choose the relevant platform to share the service link directly: **Send via WhatsApp** , **Send with Email **, **Generate a QR Code **.
- **Publish to social: **Follow the steps below depending on your account:

1. (If you haven't yet) [Connect the social accounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-media-marketing-connect-to-your-social-media-accounts) you want to publish to.
2. Customize how your social post will appear on each social channel. The options vary depending on which social accounts you connect: 

- **Publish as:** If you're posting to Facebook and/or Instagram, choose whether you want to publish your image as a Post, Reel or Story. Stories allow you to publish up to 5 images. You can't enter a caption for this type of post. 

**Note: **Publishing to Stories is not yet available in all Wix accounts.
- **Caption:** Create a caption to add to your social post which will display to your audience.  

**Tip:** Click **Create with AI** to open the AI text creator menu. Enter a brief description of the content, select a tone of voice and click **Create Text** to generate a caption for your post.
- **Add Tracking Link: **Include a link to your site so that when people click it, you can view the analytics of who came from your social post.
- **Media:** Hover over the relevant media item and click the **Replace media** icon  to swap out your image or video for another.
- **When do you want to publish?: **Select **Publish now** or [**Schedule for later**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-posts-scheduling-a-social-post) to choose when you want to publish.
    Click **Publish** at the bottom right.

**Note:** The number next to **Publish** indicates how many posts you are about to publish. For example, if you have created both a Facebook and Instagram post, the number will show (2). You can also see the platform names next to **Publish**.

### Redirecting existing links to your published site pages

You can share links to your services even before you've finished your site. Once you're ready, publish your site and go back to the Shareable Links section in your dashboard. You'll see the option to redirect your current links at the top of the page. Use this option to update your existing links, so they redirect your clients to your full website.

> **Important:**
>
> These steps apply only if your site is unpublished. If your site is already published, [you can unpublish it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-unpublishing-your-site), share your links, then republish your site. Once you publish your site, you'll see the option to redirect the link(s) to your published site pages.

#### To redirect your existing links to your published site pages:

[Go to Shareable Links](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/dashboard?referralInfo=sidebar?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Shareable+Links) in the dashboard.   Click **Redirect Links **at the top.  Click **Redirect Links** to confirm.

### I haven't finished my website yet, can I still share links to my services?

When you use shareable links with a new Wix site, Wix creates a "mini site" in the background for your existing services, so your clients can still book sessions with you. 
Once you publish your finished site, you can then create new shareable links to direct your users to the relevant page on your completed website. 

**Notes:** 
- Make sure to [upgrade your site to one of our Premium or Studio plans](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fstore%2Fplans%3FsiteGuid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%26referralAdditionalInfo%3DHelpCenter&title=Select+a+Site+to+Upgrade&primaryButtonText=Upgrade+Site&referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) to accept online payments for your services.
- If you already completed and published your site, Wix doesn't create a mini site for you.

### If I don't have a Premium plan yet, can I still share links to my services?

Yes, you can still share links to your services without a Premium plan, but you won't be able to accept online payments.
