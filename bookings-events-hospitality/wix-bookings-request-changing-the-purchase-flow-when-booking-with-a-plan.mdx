---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Changing the Purchase Flow When Booking with a Plan"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the purchase flow when your clients purchase a pricing plan in order to book a plan-only service. At present, they:

Select a plan.  Go to the **Checkout Page**.  Sign up or log in to your site.  Complete payment.  Go to the **Thank You Page**.  Select the date/time of the session they want to book, and proceed with the booking.

For example, it is not possible to change the order of these actions so that clients sign up after completing payment, or book the service and buy the plan in the same transaction.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.

> **Note:**
>
> If you want to request a change to the purchase flow of the Pricing Plans app itself (and not with Bookings), please vote here: [Pricing Plans Request: Changing the Purchase Flow](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-request-changing-the-purchase-flow)
