title: "Wix Bookings Request: Choosing the Start Day of the Bookings Calendar"
---

Currently, you cannot choose the day of the week the Wix Bookings calendar starts on.

> **Update:**
>
> The day which is used as the first calendar day is now determined by the Regional format in your Language & Region settings. To learn how to change your regional format settings, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-sites-regional-settings).
