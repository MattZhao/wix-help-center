---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating Multiple Schedules for a Course"
---

Currently, when creating a course with sessions that run on more than one day of the week (or with different staff members, durations, etc.) you cannot modify each set of sessions individually.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
