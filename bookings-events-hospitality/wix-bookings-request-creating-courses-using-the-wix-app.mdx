---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating Courses Using the Wix App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create new courses using the Wix app on your mobile device. You can only [create and schedule courses on desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-a-course).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
