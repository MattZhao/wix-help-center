---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Allowing Non-Member Clients to Cancel Bookings"
---

Currently, it is not possible for clients who have not registered as site members to cancel their bookings on your site themselves.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
