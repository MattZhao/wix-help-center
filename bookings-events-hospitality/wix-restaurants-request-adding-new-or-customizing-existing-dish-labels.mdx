---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Adding New or Customizing Existing Dish Labels"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to create new labels with your own icons. This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Menus (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new).

> **Important for sites that offer online ordering:**
>
> - The new menu app works with Wix's new online ordering app [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).
> - Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
