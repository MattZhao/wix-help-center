---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating Waitlists for Courses"
---

Currently, it is not possible to allow clients to join a waitlist for a course that is fully booked. However, clients can join a waitlist for a class that is not part of a course. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-waitlists#enabling-waitlists-for-classes)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
