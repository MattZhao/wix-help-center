### To charge per person:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Rooms **tab.Click the **Edit** icon  for an existing room or click **Create New** to create a new one.Scroll down to the **Price** section.Click the **Rate type** drop-down menu and select **Charge per person**.Add or edit the weekday and weekend price.Click **Save**.
