title: "Wix Events POS Request: Printing Receipts at a Mobile POS"
---

Currently, it's not possible for Wix Events users to connect an external printer to a mobile point of sale and use it to print receipts for ticket sales.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
