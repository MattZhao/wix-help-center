### Viewing guest stats

You can view guest stats from the Contact panel or their reservation history in your contact list.

#### To view a guest's stats:

[Go to Table Reservations](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/restaurants/table-reservations?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Table+Reservations) in your site's dashboard.  Click the contact's name on the relevant reservation.   Scroll down and click the **Table Reservations** section to view the guest's stats.

## FAQs

### How can I update a guest's contact details?

1. Click the **Edit info** icon  in the **Contact** panel.

[image: A screenshot of clicking the edit info icon in the contacts panel to update contact info.]
2. Do one of the following: 

- **Edit details: **Edit the relevant details in the pop-up.
- **Add new details:** Click **Add New Field** at the bottom. You can select one of the fields on the list or create a custom field.
3. Click **Save**.

### How can I view a guest's full reservation history?

You can view a guest’s full reservation history by accessing their contact card.**To view your guest's reservation history:**
1. [Go to Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the relevant contact and click **View**.
3. Click the **Table Reservations** tab.
