---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Setting Different Price Rates Based on Service Location"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set different prices based on the location of a given service.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - You can create multiple versions of the same service with different durations and the relevant price increases.
> - You can also set custom price options for your service to offer it in varying prices.
>
> **Note: **When using this workaround, you need to manually block off the relevant amount of time in the staff member's calendar.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
