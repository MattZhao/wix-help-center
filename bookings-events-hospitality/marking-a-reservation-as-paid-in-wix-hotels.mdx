Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).

You can manually mark a reservation as Paid once your guest has paid in full. This is useful if you accept payments offline or if you only require a deposit fee (e.g. 25%) when the booking is made.

### To mark a reservation as paid:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Reservations** tab.Click the reservation on the calendar.Scroll down to the **Payment Details** section.Click **Mark as paid**.Click **Yes, I'm Sure**.
