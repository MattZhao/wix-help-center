From the Fit by Wix app, you can interact with your favorite fitness sites and businesses on the go. Become a member of a site's fitness community, book sessions and classes, and manage your own fitness journey, all directly from your mobile device. [Learn more about the Fit by Wix app.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/fit-by-wix-app-an-overview)

Your members can download and use Fit by Wix for free by searching for the app in the relevant app store or by using the direct download links.

### To download the Fit by Wix app:

Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).  Type 'Fit by Wix' in the Search field.  Click **Get **or **Install** to download the app.

> **You can also download the Fit by Wix app right to your phone using these links:**
>
> - [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fit-by-wix/id1552621043)
> - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wix.fitness&hl=en&gl=US)
