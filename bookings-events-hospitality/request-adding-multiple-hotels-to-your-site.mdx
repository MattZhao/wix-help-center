---
title: "Request: Adding Multiple Hotels to Your Site"
---

Currently, it is only possible to add one Wix Hotels app per site
 . If you manage more than one hotel, we recommend that you create a separate site for each one.

> **Notes:**
>
> - If you have multiple rental properties (e.g., apartments) in different locations, you can [add them as rooms with different addresses](https://support.wix.com/en/article/renting-multiple-properties-in-different-locations).
> - A Premium Plan is for a single site only. If you want to upgrade a second site, you must purchase a second Premium Plan.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
