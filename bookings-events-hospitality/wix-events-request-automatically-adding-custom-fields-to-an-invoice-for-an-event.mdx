---
title: "Wix Events Request: Automatically Adding Custom Fields to an Invoice for an Event"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically add custom fields (e.g. VAT or guest billing address) when creating an invoice for an event.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
