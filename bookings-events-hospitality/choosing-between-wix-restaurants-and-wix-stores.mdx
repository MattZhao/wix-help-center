---

Wix offers 2 main solutions that sometimes may overlap: Restaurants and Stores. While some businesses are straightforward restaurants or stores, others can decide what solution is better for them according to their needs.

For example, flower shops don't sell food, but, much like a restaurant, they do sell perishable items that you want to get the same day. So if you own a flower shop you may choose to use Wix Restaurants and not Wix Stores.

### Can I combine Wix Restaurants and Stores?

In some cases you may choose to combine several apps on your site.
For example, you own a hotel that has a spa and restaurant.
- You sell digital vouchers to the hotel spa.
- Your restaurant also sells fresh pastries online.
- You sell exclusive hotel merchandise like your signature soap set travel kit.

For this case, you will use Wix Hotels, Bookings, Restaurants, Stores, and possibly Events and Blogs.

### Can I use a mobile app with both Wix Restaurants and Stores?

Yes. The Wix app on your mobile device is available for both options, as well as the Wix Branded app, and Wix Spaces app. Notice, Wix Dine+ is only available for restaurants.
