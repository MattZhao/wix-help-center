It can be hard to remember the rates you set for different rooms on different days, especially when setting weekend and/or seasonal rates. With the Wix Hotels Calendar, you can easily get an overview of all of your rooms individual rates.

### To show the room rates:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Reservations** tab.Select the checkbox next to **Show Rates**.
