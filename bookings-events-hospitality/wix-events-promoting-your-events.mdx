---
title: "Wix Events: Promoting Your Events"
---

Wix offers several easy ways to promote your next event. You can create a social media post, send an email campaign, run ads on Facebook and Instagram, or make promotional videos. You can even promote your event from your phone using the Wix app.

To help get people to attend, you can create coupons, send emails, or post on social media. All the event details are included when you start your promotion.

### To create a promotion for your event:

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the ** More Actions** icon  next to the relevant event.
3. Click **Promote events** on the relevant event.
4. Choose a template to promote your event:  

- **Email campaign:** Capture your subscribers' attention with [visually appealing emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-getting-started).
- **Social post: **Show your online presence by [sharing updates on social media](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-media-marketing-an-overview).
- **Facebook & Instagram ads: **[Target the right audience](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-an-overview) using ads on Facebook and Instagram.
- **Promotional video:** [Create professional-looking videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-a-promotional-video-for-your-site) by adding photos and choosing your favorite design style and music for promotions on YouTube and social media.
- **Share a link to your event: **Click **Copy Link** at the bottom and send it to potential guests.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Events**.
4. Tap **Published Events **or **Draft Events**.

**Tip: **Some promotion options are only available for published events or for ticketed events.
5. Tap the event you want to manage.
6. Tap the **Promote **tab.
7. Choose how you want to promote your event: 

- **Email Marketing:** Create an email campaign and send it to your subscribers. [Get started now](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-and-sending-a-campaign-from-start-to-finish).
- **Social Posts:** Create posts and drive traffic from social media back to your site. [Explore tips](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-posts-creating-and-customizing-a-social-post).
- **Discussion:** Post event updates for your guests.
- **Coupons:** Boost ticket sales by offering discounts. [Find out how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-event-coupons-using-the-wix-owner-app).
- **Memberships:** Create discounts for members. [Discover the benefits](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-membership-plans-using-the-wix-owner-app).
- **Invite Guests:** Tap **Invite Guests** at the bottom to send invites via email, social media, or more (RSVP events only).
