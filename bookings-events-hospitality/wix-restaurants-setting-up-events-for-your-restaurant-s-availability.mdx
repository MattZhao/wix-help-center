You may have events coming up that affect your restaurant's availability to serve customers. You can add these events to your business hours, menu items, online orders (delivery and pickup), and discounts. For example, you can temporarily close your restaurant for a private event or add a holiday schedule for your online orders.

**In this article, learn more about:**

---

### Adding events to your business hours

If you have a private event at your restaurant which affects your normal business hours, you can edit your schedule to reflect your unavailability during this event. If a customer tries to order from your restaurant, they'll be notified that you're currently closed.

#### To add an event that occurs during business hours:

Go to [Business Info](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Business%20Info&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/business-info) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to the **Business Hours **section and click **Edit** next to the hours.   Click **+ New Event**.  Click the **Event Name **field and enter the name of the event.   Click the **Availability **drop-down and choose an option: 

- **Available: **Your restaurant is available during the time you set for the event.
- **Unavailable: **Your restaurant is not available during the time you set for the event.
    Set the event's **Start Time **and **End Time **from the relevant fields.   Click **Apply**.

Click **Save**.

### Adding events to your menu items

Add events to specific dishes, sections, or menus if they will be unavailable for online ordering.

#### To add an event to your menu items:

Go to [Menus](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Menu&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/Menus) in your site's dashboard.  Navigate to the relevant menu, section, or dish.  Click **Edit** next to **Online Ordering**.  Click **Edit** under **Availability**.  Click **+ New Event**.  Click the **Event Name **field and enter the name of the event.  Click the **Availability **drop-down and choose an option: 

- **Available: **Your restaurant is available during the time you set for the event.
- **Unavailable: **Your restaurant is not available during the time you set for the event.
    Set the event's **Start Time **and **End Time **from the relevant fields.   Click **Apply**.

Click **Save**.

### Adding events to your online orders (delivery and pickup)

Certain events can affect your restaurant's availability to fulfill online orders. Add an event to your online orders to notify customers when you're unavailable to fulfill delivery or pickup orders.

#### To add an event to your online orders:

Go to [Order Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Order%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/ordersSettings) in your site's dashboard.  Choose what you want to do:

### Add an event to your pickup hours

1. Click the **Pickup** tab.
2. Click **Edit** under **Availability**.
3. Click **+ New Event**.
4. Click the **Event Name **field and enter the name of the event.
5. Click the **Availability **drop-down and choose an option: 

- **Available: **Your restaurant is available during the time you set for the event.
- **Unavailable: **Your restaurant is not available during the time you set for the event.
6. Set the event's **Start Time **and **End Time **from the relevant fields.
7. Click **Apply**.[image: screenshot of adding an event and setting your availability for pickup hours]
8. Click **Save**.

### Add an event to your delivery hours

1. Click the **Delivery & Delivery Partners **tab.
2. Hover over the delivery area you want to update and click **Edit**.
3. Click the Edit  icon under **Availability**.
4. Click **+ New Event**.
5. Click the **Event Name **field and enter the name of the event.
6. Click the **Availability **drop-down and choose an option: 

- **Available: **Your restaurant is available during the time you set for the event.
- **Unavailable: **Your restaurant is not available during the time you set for the event.
7. Set the event's **Start Time **and **End Time **from the relevant fields.
8. Click **Apply**.[image: screenshot of adding an event and setting your availability for delivery]
9. Click **Save**.

### Adding events to your discounts

Add events that affect your discounts. For example, your restaurant has an ongoing seasonal discount but you don't want it to apply during Black Friday.

#### To add an event to discounts:

Go to [Restaurant Discounts](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Frestaurants/discounts%3F&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+Discounts) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the discount you want to edit and click **Edit**.  Click **Edit** under **Available**.  Click the **Event Name **field and enter the name of the event.  Click the **Availability **drop-down and choose an option: 

- **Available: **Your restaurant is available during the time you set for the event.
- **Unavailable: **Your restaurant is not available during the time you set for the event.
    Set the event's **Start Time **and **End Time **from the relevant fields.   Click **Apply**.

Click **Save**.
