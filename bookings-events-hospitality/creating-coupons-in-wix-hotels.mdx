Treat your guests by offering discount coupons for your hotel.

### To create a coupon:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Price Options** tab.Click **Coupons**.Click **Create New**.Enter a name for your coupon in the **Name your coupon** field.Click the **Set coupon type** drop-down menu and select a type:
- **Payment Discount:** Amount based discount (e.g. $10)
- **% Discount:** Percentage based discount (e.g. 10%)
Enter the discount amount or percentage.Click the **Valid from** field and select the start date of the coupon.Click the **Valid to** field and select the end date of the coupon.(Optional) Enter the coupon limit in the **Limit number of coupons** to field.Enter the code that you want your customers to use in the **Coupon Code** field.Click **Save**.
