---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Requiring Advanced Ordering for Certain Menu Items"
---

Currently, it is not possible to require advanced ordering for certain menu items. For example, if you need two days to create a fruit platter, customers must order the fruit platter at least two days in advance.

It is possible for a customer to [place an order for a future date](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-faq-can-customers-place-orders-for-a-future-date), however it is not possible to make this mandatory.

> **Tip:**
>
> In the meantime, we recommend placing notice on your site stating that certain items must be ordered in advance.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
