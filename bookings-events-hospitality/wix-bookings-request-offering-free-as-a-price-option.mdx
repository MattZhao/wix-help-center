---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering 'Free' as a Price Option"
---

Currently, it is not possible to offer a '0' amount or 'free' option as part of your price options for a service. For example, you cannot create a "kids under 5 get free access" option while charging adults and teenagers.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
