---

If you need to cancel a published event, you can do so in a few clicks. Once you cancel an event, all registered guests automatically receive a cancellation email.

> **Before you cancel:**
>
> - Check to see that cancellation emails are enabled and decide if you want to customize the message inside the email. Learn how to [enable and edit cancellation emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-managing-emails-you-send-and-receive).
> - You cannot restore an event once you cancel it. However, you can [duplicate the canceled event](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-canceling-an-event#faqs) and publish it again.

### Canceling an event

You can cancel a published event from your site's dashboard or the Wix mobile apps. Circumstances for canceling an event are entirely up to you.

> **Important:**
>
> For ticketed events with ticket sales, you need to [cancel the event and refund orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-canceling-and-refunding-orders-for-events-with-tickets).

### How can I duplicate an event that I canceled?

1. [Go to Events](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fevents%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Manage+Events) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the relevant event and click the **Duplicate as Draft **icon .
3. Click **Save**,** **then click **Publish **when you're ready.
[image: A screenshot of hovering over the relevant event and clicking Duplicate as Draft icon.]

### How can I access the guest list of a canceled event?

1. [Go to Events](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fevents%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Manage+Events) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Filter** and select **Canceled**.
3. Select the relevant event and view the guests in the **Guests** tab.

### Are guests automatically added back to a duplicated event?

No. However, [you can manually add guests](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-manually-adding-guests-to-an-event) to your event after you duplicate and publish it.
