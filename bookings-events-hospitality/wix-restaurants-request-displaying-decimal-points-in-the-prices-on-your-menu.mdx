---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Displaying Decimal Points in the Prices on Your Menu"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now choose how to display prices for your menu items: [with or without decimal points](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-formatting-your-menus-new-prices). This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Menus (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new).

> **Important**
>
> The new menu app works with Wix's new online ordering app. Learn about [the differences between the old and new online ordering apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-about-the-differences-between-the-old-and-new-online-ordering-apps).
