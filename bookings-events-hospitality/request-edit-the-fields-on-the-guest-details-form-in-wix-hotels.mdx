---
title: "Request: Edit the Fields on the Guest Details form in Wix Hotels"
---

Currently, it is not possible to edit, add or remove fields on the Guest Details form which guests are asked to fill in when booking a room in your hotel.

The form currently includes the following fields:

First NameLast NameEmail AddressPhone NumberCountrySpecial Requests

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
