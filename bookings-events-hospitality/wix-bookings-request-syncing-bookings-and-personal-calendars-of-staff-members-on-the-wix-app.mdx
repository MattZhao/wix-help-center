---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Syncing Bookings and Personal Calendars of Staff Members on the Wix App"
---

Currently, on desktop, you can [sync the Bookings calendar and personal calendars of staff members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-syncing-staff-members-personal-calendars-with-the-bookings-calendar)  (e.g. Google, iCal, Outlook). However, it is not possible to do this from the Wix app on a mobile device.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
