title: "Wix Bookings Request: Offering More Services on Reserve With Google"
---

We are excited to announce the new [Reserve With Google](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-up-a-google-book-button) integration that leads visitors from Google to your services on your site. With the new integration, you can offer more services that are eligible for Google than before, including: services with online payment, zoom-based services, and multi-location services.
