---
title: "Wix Bookings: Uninstalling Zoom"
---

As your needs change, you may wish to switch your video conferencing (VC) service provider from Zoom to another app such as Google Meet or Skype.

> **Important:**
>
> If you disconnect your main VC account, VC links won't appear for any sessions, even if your staff have their own individual VC accounts.

### To uninstall the Zoom app:

[Go to Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon   next to the **Zoom** app.  Click **Delete**.   Select your reason for deleting the app from the dialogue box.  Click **Delete App**.
