---
title: "Request: Refunding Reservations in Wix Hotels"
---

Currently, it is not possible to refund payments made for reservations through Wix Hotels. Instead, you must process the refund directly through the payment gateway used to make payment (i.e., PayPal, Stripe etc.).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
