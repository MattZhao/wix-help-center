title: "Wix Restaurants: Adding and Setting Up DoorDash Drive"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - Delivering with DoorDash Drive is currently available only in the US and Canada.
> - This article is about integrating the **original Restaurants Orders app** with DoorDash Drive. We've released a [new restaurants orders app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new), which is also [integrated with DoorDash Drive](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-doordash-drive-for-your-restaurant) in the United States.

Save yourself the trouble of hiring and managing your own delivery drivers by letting DoorDash Drive deliver for you.

Orders will still be coming in through your restaurant site. This means you don't lose traffic and can still manage all your orders in one place. If you want, you can handle some deliveries yourself. Best of all - you're only charged a flat rate fee.

---

### Before you begin

To set up DoorDash Drive as a delivery partner, make sure to:

[Set up an online payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-payment-methods-for-orders) so customers can pay online for orders (e.g. credit card, PayPal). DoorDash only accepts online payment options and doesn't accept cash.  [Enable tips for online orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-allowing-customers-to-add-a-tip-to-an-order). You can customize the default tip settings for orders you fulfill but not for DoorDash Drive deliveries. When deliveries are fulfilled with DoorDash Drive, the tips customers leave at checkout are paid to DoorDash.

---

### Step 1 | Add DoorDash Drive & create an account

To add DoorDash Drive as a delivery partner, add the DoorDash app to your site and sign up for a DoorDash Drive account.

DoorDash Drive allows customers to order from your site (not from the DoorDash Marketplace) and get delivery from a DoorDash delivery person.

The app is free to install. After, you pay a flat-rate fee per order.

> **Note:**
>
> If you have an existing account with DoorDash Drive, you still have to sign up for a new account to add them as a delivery partner.

#### To add DoorDash Drive:

Go to the [Order Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Order%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/ordersSettings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Delivery & Delivery Partners**.  Click **+ Delivery Partner**.  Select **DoorDash Drive for Restaurants**.  Click **Add to Site**.** **Click **Add to Site** again to confirm.  Click **Register**.  Click **Copy **to copy your Unique Business Identifier number, and then click **Continue**.

---

### Step 2 | Sign the DoorDash agreement

Before you can begin to deliver orders, DoorDash Drive requires you to fill out the agreement you accessed in Step 1 and sign it.** This agreement is between you and DoorDash Drive.**

It typically takes a few minutes for DoorDash Drive to approve your account, but it can sometimes take up to a few hours. When they approve, DoorDash Drive will send you an email and your status changes to Active.

---

### Step 3 | Create a delivery area

You can create a DoorDash Drive delivery area for every location you have. DoorDash Drive does not define a specific area within which they deliver. The size of the delivery area is dynamic and depends on the amount of traffic at any particular time.

> **Combining self-delivery with DoorDash Drive delivery:**
>
> Incoming orders are assigned to you first when the delivery address is within your area. If outside your area and within the DoorDash Drive delivery area, the order is assigned to DoorDash.

#### To set up a DoorDash Drive delivery area:

Go to [Order Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Order%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/ordersSettings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Delivery & Delivery Partners**.  Click **+** **New Area**.  (Optional) Enter a minimum order amount for this area.  Click the **Delivery method** drop-down and select **DoorDash Drive for Restaurants**.  (Optional) Click the **Cover part of the delivery fee** checkbox to pay part of the delivery fee that DoorDash charges your customers and enter the amount you want to cover.  Under **Pickup instructions**, enter instructions for the DoorDash courier.   Under **Maximum Delivery Time**, enter an estimate of how long it should take from the time you receive an order until your customers receive it (i.e. prep time + delivery time).  (Optional) Click the Edit icon   under **Availability** to edit your delivery hours. Then click** Apply**.**

Note: **You can also temporarily make this delivery unavailable by disabling the **Availability** toggle.  Click **Save**.   If you have more than one location, you can repeat the steps above to set up additional delivery areas.   (Recommended) Place a test order with delivery DoorDash to make sure delivery works smoothly.

> **Next steps:**
>
> Now that you're all set up, you can start [delivering orders through DoorDash Drive](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-delivering-orders-with-doordash-drive).

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### How is DoorDash Drive different from DoorDash Marketplace?

[DoorDash Drive](https://help.doordash.com/dashers/s/article/What-is-DoorDash-Drive?language=en_US) allows customers to place orders on your site and get delivery via a DoorDash delivery driver. For DoorDash Drive you pay a flat-rate fee.  
With [DoorDash Marketplace](https://help.doordash.com/merchants/s/article/How-does-DoorDash-Marketplace-work?language=en_US), customers place orders on [doordash.com](https://www.doordash.com/) or through the DoorDash app.

### Can I take phone orders with DoorDash Drive?

Yes. You can [take phone orders and have DoorDash fulfill the delivery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-manually-creating-an-order).

### What fees are involved?

The DoorDash Drive app is free to install. When DoorDash starts delivering, you pay DoorDash a flat-rate fee per order. 
Note that the DoorDash Drive fees are considerably less than for DoorDash Marketplace.

### Can customers pay cash for DoorDash Drive deliveries?

No. DoorDash only accepts online payment options, and not manual payments. Customers cannot pay for an order with cash.

### Can I create more than one DoorDash Drive delivery area?

If you have one restaurant location, you can create one DoorDash Drive delivery area. If you have multiple locations, you can create a DoorDash Drive delivery area for each of them.

### Can I set up DoorDash Drive alongside my own delivery?

Yes. You can choose to deliver orders yourself in a certain radius or area. When a customer submits an order, if you cover that area for delivery, it will be assigned for you to deliver. If it's not covered by you, the order gets sent to DoorDash to deliver.

### How do I know where DoorDash Drive delivers to from my restaurant's location?

DoorDash's delivery area is dynamic. For example, when traffic is low, they may deliver to a larger area than when demand is high.

### Can I use the pinpointing addresses feature with DoorDash Drive orders?

When you deliver orders yourself, you have the option of enabling the [pinpointing addresses features](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-using-pinpointing-for-customer-addresses-in-online-ordering) to help make sure that addresses can be found on a map. Currently, DoorDash Drive doesn't support this feature.

### Can I customize the DoorDash Drive tip settings?

Tips with DoorDash Drive are displayed in percentages with the 20% option always preselected. These settings cannot be edited or turned off, as the tip is paid to DoorDash in these cases.If you offer your own delivery in addition to DoorDash Drive, you can customize the tip options for orders that you deliver.

### Can I charge the customers more than DoorDash charges for deliveries?

No. You can't charge the customers more than what DoorDash currently charges for delivery fees.
