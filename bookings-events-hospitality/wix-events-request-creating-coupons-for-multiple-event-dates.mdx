---
title: "Wix Events Request: Creating Coupons for Multiple Event Dates"
---

Currently, you can [create a coupon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-event-coupons) for all events or one specific event. However, it's not possible to use the same coupon for multiple event dates. This includes various dates of a recurring event, or different single-date events.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
