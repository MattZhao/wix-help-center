---
title: "Wix Events Request: Waiving the Ticket Service Fee for Non-profits"
---

Currently, there is no option to waive the [ticket service fee](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-about-wix-ticket-service-fees) for events held by non-profit organizations.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
