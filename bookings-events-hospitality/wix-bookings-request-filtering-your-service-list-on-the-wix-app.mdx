---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Filtering Your Service List on the Wix App"
---

Currently, you can [filter your Services List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-service-categories-in-wix-bookings) from your site's dashboard by various parameters such as staff member, location, service, or category.

However it is not possible to filter this list on the Wix app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
