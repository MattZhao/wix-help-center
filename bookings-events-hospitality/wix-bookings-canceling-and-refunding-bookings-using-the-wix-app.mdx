---
title: "Wix Bookings: Canceling and Refunding Bookings Using the Wix App"
---

The Wix app allows you to manage cancelations and refunds while you're on the go. After you cancel and/or refund an order, the order status updates to reflect the change. You can also cancel and refund individual sessions from the Booking Calendar.

> **Important:**
>
> When you cancel and refund an order or a session, you cannot undo it.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Canceling and refunding an order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-canceling-and-refunding-bookings-using-the-wix-owner-app#canceling-and-refunding-an-order)  [Canceling and refunding sessions in the Booking Calendar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-canceling-and-refunding-bookings-using-the-wix-owner-app#canceling-and-refunding-sessions-in-the-booking-calendar)

### Canceling and refunding an order

You can process PayPal or Stripe refunds right from the Wix app. Orders made using different payment providers can only be processed through those providers, outside of Wix.

#### To cancel and refund an order:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales** and select **Orders**.   Tap the relevant order.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.  Tap **Issue Refund**.  Tap to select the checkbox(es) next to the relevant service(s) you want to refund and cancel.  (Optional) Tap **Edit** **Amount** to offer partial refunds.

1. Enter the relevant amount you want to refund.
2. Tap **Apply**.
    Tap **Next**.

(Optional) Select the reason you provided a refund from the **Refund Reason** drop-down.  (Optional) Notify your customer:

1. Enable the **Send a confirmation email**.
2. Enter a note to your customer. The note appears in the confirmation email.
    Tap **Next**.  Tap **Issue** **Refund** to confirm.

**Note: **You cannot undo this action.

### Canceling and refunding sessions in the Booking Calendar

You can cancel a session and offer a full refund when clients are unable to attend, and give you enough notice according to your [Booking Policies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-your-booking-policies-from-the-wix-owner-app).

#### To cancel and refund a booking:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.   Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant session.  Tap **Cancel**.  Follow the steps below, depending on the session type:  (Optional) Tap **Apply cancellation fee **to charge a [cancellation and no-show fee](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee).  (Optional) Tap **Notify client with an email **and add a message for your customer.  Choose the relevant to option to refund your client, depending on how they paid for the session:

- Tap to select **Issue** **refund** checkbox.
- Tap to select the **Credit** **session** **back** **to** **client's** **plan** checkbox.
    Tap **Cancel Session** to confirm.

### Appointment without cancellation fees

1. (Optional) Tap **Mark as refunded **to issue a refund.

**Note: **Go to your orders tab to issue a partial refund.
2. (Optional) Tap **Notify client with an email **and add a message for your customer.
3. Choose the relevant to option to refund your client, depending on how they paid for the session:

- Tap to select **Issue** **refund** checkbox.
- Tap to select the **Credit** **session** **back** **to** **client's** **plan** checkbox.
4. Tap **Cancel Session** to confirm.
5. Choose what you want to do: **Mark as Refunded** or **Continue without Refund**.
