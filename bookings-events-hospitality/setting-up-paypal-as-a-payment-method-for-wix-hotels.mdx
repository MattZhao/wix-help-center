---
title: "Setting Up PayPal as a Payment Method for Wix Hotels"
---

You can set up PayPal as a payment method in Wix Hotels and use any of the currencies that PayPal supports. Click [here](https://www.paypal.com/cgi-bin/webscr?cmd=p/sell/mc/mc_receive-outside) to learn more.

> **Important:**
>
> - This article refers to the original Wix Hotels. If you are using the new Wix Hotels by HotelRunner, which is not yet available to all users, please [read the information here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview). Learn how to [check which version of Wix Hotels you are using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-wix-hotels).
> - If you use a currency that is not supported by PayPal, guests will be unable to proceed to PayPal to make the payment for their hotel stay. If PayPal does not support the currency you want to use, we recommend using a [different payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-your-payment-method-in-wix-hotels).
> - You must have a [PayPal Business account](https://www.paypal.com/webapps/mpp/merchant) to accept payment in Wix Hotels.

### To set up PayPal as a payment method:

[Go to your dashboard.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard)Click the **Hotels **tab.Click the **Payments** tab.Click **Connect** next to** PayPal**.Sign in to your PayPal account.Click **Grant Permission**.Click **Save** at the top right of the dashboard.
