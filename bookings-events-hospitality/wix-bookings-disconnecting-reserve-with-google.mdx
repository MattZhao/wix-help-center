---
title: "Wix Bookings: Disconnecting Reserve With Google"
---

You can opt out of Reserve with Google at any time. There are two ways of opting out of the service, depending on when you first registered your services with Google.

When you opt out of Reserve with Google, your clients will no longer be able to book your services directly from Google Search and Google Maps.

### How do I know which method to use when opting out?

If you opted in to the Reserve with Google service before December 2023, you have the Reserve with Google tab under Marketing & SEO in your site's dashboard.        [image: A screenshot showing the reserve with google tab under catalog tab in the booking channels section in your site's dashboard.]

If you opted in after December 2023, you need to manage the Google integration from Booking Channels in your site's dashboard.

[image: A screenshot showing the latest version of reserve with Google in the Booking Channels tab in your site's dashboard.]

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Opting out of Reserve with Google from the Reserve with Google tab](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-disconnecting-reserve-with-google#opting-out-of-reserve-with-google-from-the-reserve-with-google-tab)  [Opting out of Reserve with Google from the Booking Channels tab](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-disconnecting-reserve-with-google#opting-out-of-reserve-with-google-from-the-booking-channels-tab)

### Opting out of Reserve with Google from the Reserve with Google tab

If you opted in to Reserve with Google before December 2023, you can opt out using the Reserve with Google tab in your site's dashboard.

#### To opt out from the Reserve with Google tab:

[Go to Reserve with Google](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Reserve%20with%20Google&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/reserve-with-google/main-page?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions **icon  at the top right.  Select **Disconnect**.  Click **Disconnect **to confirm.

---

### Opting out of Reserve with Google from the Booking Channels tab

If you opted in to Reserve with Google after December 2023, you need to opt out using the Booking Channels tab in your site's dashboard.

#### To opt out from the Booking Channels tab:

[Go to Booking Integrations](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fbookings%2Fintegrations%3F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Booking+Integrations) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage** under Reserve with Google.  Click the **More Actions **icon  at the top right.  Select **Disconnect**.  Click **Disconnect **to confirm.
