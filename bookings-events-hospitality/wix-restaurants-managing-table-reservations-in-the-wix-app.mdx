---
title: "Wix Restaurants: Managing Table Reservations in the Wix App"
---

Manage your restaurant's table reservations on the go with the Wix app. See all of your upcoming reservations, add new reservations, and update or cancel existing ones.

---

### Adding a table reservations in the app

Add new table reservations for your customers right from the Wix app.

#### To add a new reservation:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Table Reservations **and select **Table Reservations**.  Tap **+ Add New **at the bottom.  Choose the type of reservation you'd like to add:

- **Reservation: **Enter the reservation details, assign a table, enter the guest's details or add a guest from your contacts, and add notes for your team about this reservation.
- **Walk-in: **Enter the reservation details and assign a table. You can add the guests' details.

**Note:** You can also save them as contacts, add existing contacts, and add notes for your team about this reservation.
    Tap **Save**.

---

### Managing your reservations in the app

View and manage your upcoming table reservations from the Wix app. You can access more details about the reservation, edit or cancel the reservation in case of need, and call the reservation party.

#### To manage your reservations:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Table Reservations **and select **Table Reservations**.   Tap on a reservation and choose what you want to do:

- **View Reservation: **View and edit the reservation details. You can also Tap the **Phone** icon  to call the reservation party.
- **Edit Reservation: **Edit the reservation details, and then tap **Save**.
- **Cancel Reservation: **Cancel the reservation.
