---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Selling Services Without Creating a Booking Right Away"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sell services on Wix Bookings without scheduling a session.

For example, an interior designer might want to showcase their services online and then direct visitors to request a quote, or to buy a consultation package online. Scheduling an appointment would then become relevant at a later date, based on their renovation timelines and availability.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
