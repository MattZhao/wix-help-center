---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Accessing Cart on the Member Apps"
---

Currently, clients can access [the cart](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-a-cart-to-your-site) directly from your site on desktop or the mobile web version of your site, enabling them to add multiple services before checking out.

However, it is not possible for clients to access the cart through the relevant member apps.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
