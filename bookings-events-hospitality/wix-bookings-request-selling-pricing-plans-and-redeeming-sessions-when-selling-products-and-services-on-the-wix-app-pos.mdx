---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Selling Pricing Plans and Redeeming Sessions When Selling Products and Services on the Wix app POS"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sell pricing plans, or redeem sessions from an existing pricing plan, when selling products and services [using the Wix app as a point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-booking-services-and-accepting-payments-with-the-wix-owner-app). For example, if one of the sessions in the cart is plan-only, you can't redeem the session or sell a plan at checkout.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
