---
title: "Wix Events: Setting Up a Payment Method"
---

Before you can start selling event tickets, you need to set up at least one payment method. Choose from various online payment options available in your region, or set up manual payments to collect offline payments (e.g. in person, through another app, by mail).

> **Important:**
>
> You must upgrade your site to a [Premium or Studio plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website) that supports payments in order to accept payments.

### To set up payments:

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.

**Tip:** Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down to change it.  Click **Connect **next to the payment provider you want to use, or click **See More Payment Options **at the bottom to view more options.  Choose the relevant payment method(s):

### Wix Payments

Connect to [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments), offering a secure system to manage orders, payments, payouts, refunds, and chargebacks from one dashboard. 
Wix Payments accepts all major credit cards and popular payment methods like Apple Pay, Google Pay, depending on your location.[image: A screenshot of Wix Payments set up in a site's dashboard.]

### Credit Card

Accept [credit card payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-by-credit-card) from the most suitable provider available in your region, based on your preferences and location (e.g. Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express). [image: A screenshot of connecting credit/debit cards set up in a site's dashboard.]

### PayPal

With PayPal, you can [accept PayPal payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider), credit cards, and debit cards. Guests are directed to PayPal for payment, and they can use a credit card even without having a PayPal account.

### Apple Pay

Let guests on iOS devices [checkout using Apple Pay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-apple-pay-as-a-payment-method). The Apple Pay button is displayed only to people viewing your site with a Safari browser on IOS or macOS devices. 

[image: A screenshot of Apple Pay Express Checkout.]

**Tip: **You can add Apple Pay to your site even if you are not an Apple user.** **

### Manual

Accept cash, payment though apps, checks, bank transfers, or any other form of payment that is not tracked online. When [selecting this method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments), guests see instructions at checkout explaining how to complete payment.

> **Authorize payments and capture funds later:**
>
> When a customer orders, funds are held temporarily. You can [capture or void](https://support.wix.com/en/article/authorizing-payments-at-checkout-and-capturing-funds-later) the payment later, making it easy to manage ticket purchases.
> 1. [Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.
> 2. Click **Checkout** under **Business Solutions**.
> 3. Click **Authorize payments and capture funds later **toggle to enable it.
> 4. (Optional) Select or deselect the **Automatically capture payments before the authorization period ends **checkbox.
