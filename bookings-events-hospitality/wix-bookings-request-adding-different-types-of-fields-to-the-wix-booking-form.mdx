---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Adding Different Types of Fields to the Wix Booking Form"
---

Currently, you can add the following types of custom fields to your booking form: short text fields (up to 40 characters), paragraph fields (up to 255 characters), or checkbox fields.

It is not currently possible to add other types of fields, such as drop-downs or multiple choice options.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
