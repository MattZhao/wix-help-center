---
title: "Request: Using Google Ads with Wix Hotels by HotelRunner"
---

Currently, it's not possible to use Google Ads in your site's dashboard to create campaigns with the [Wix Hotels by HotelRunner app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-hotels-by-hotelrunner-an-overview).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
