---
title: "Wix Events: Changing the Email Address for RSVP Notifications"
---

When a guest RSVPs or buys a ticket to an event, you get an email at the address associated with your Wix account. If you'd prefer to receive emails at a different address, you can change it in the site settings.

### To change your business email address:

[Go to Business Info](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/business-info?referralInfo=settings-lobby?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Business+Info) in your site's dashboard.  Enter a new email address in the **Email** field under **Contact info**.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can use Wix Automations to [send additional notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-about-automated-emails-for-events) to you or other team members every time a guest RSVPs or buys a ticket.
