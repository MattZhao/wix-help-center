---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Creating Service Bundles"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create service bundles, or a pre-defined set of services with shared durations, price and staff members your clients can book together.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can [create a new appointment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-managing-appointments-using-the-wix-owner-app) service which combines the services, duration and staff of several different services.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
