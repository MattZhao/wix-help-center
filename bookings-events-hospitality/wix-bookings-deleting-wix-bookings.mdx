---
title: "Wix Bookings: Deleting Wix Bookings"
---

You can remove Wix Bookings from your site by deleting the Bookings Pages in your editor. Once you delete them, all elements connected to Wix Bookings disappear from your site and dashboard.

Wix EditorEditor XStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Hover over **Bookings Pages **and click the **Actions **icon .
4. Click **Delete Bookings**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to delete Wix Bookings in the Wix Editor.]
1. Click **Delete **to confirm.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages**  at the top of the editor.
3. Hover over **Bookings Pages **and click the **More Actions** icon .
4. Click **Delete Bookings**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to delete Wix Bookings in Editor X.]
1. Click **Delete** to confirm.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Hover over **Bookings Pages **and click the **More Actions** icon .
4. Click **Delete Bookings**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to delete Wix Bookings in the Studio Editor.]
1. Click **Delete** to confirm.

> **Important:**
>
> Deleting Wix Bookings does not cancel your site upgrade. Learn how to [cancel a Premium or Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-a-wix-premium-plan).
