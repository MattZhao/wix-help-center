---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Setting Availability Per Service"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set separate availability settings for individual appointment services. For example, if a staff member works five days a week, all their associated appointments are available to book for those five days.

Creating different availability settings for different appointment types would offer more flexibility to your overall business operations. For example, you could offer one appointment type only on weekends, and another type only in the afternoons on weekdays.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [what factors affect the availability of your appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-troubleshooting-availability-for-appointments).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
