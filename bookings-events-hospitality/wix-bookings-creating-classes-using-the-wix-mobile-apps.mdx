Create and schedule new class services on the go using the Wix app. Classes are recurring services with sessions that clients can book, like a weekly yoga class. You have the flexibility to offer the same class with different instructors or duration, and make classes part of a pricing plan as well.

---

Create a new service and choose 'Class' to get started.

3. Tap **Booking Services**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Booking Services**.

5. Tap **Class**.

### Step 2 | Fill out your service details

Add information about what clients can expect from the class. Include a detailed description to give clients a clearer idea of what you have to offer. You can also set the maximum number of participants that can join the class.

#### To fill out your service details:

Add your service details:

- **Name: **Enter a name for the class.
- **Category: **Select a category from the drop-down menu (if you created categories).
- **Tagline (optional): **Enter a tagline that will appear below the service name on your app.
- **Description (optional): **Enter a description that will appear on the service page of your app.
- **Max participants: **Set the maximum number of participants that can book your service.
    Tap **Continue**.

---

### Step 3 | Set your price and payment details

Make your pricing clear and easy to understand. Choose how clients can pay for classes, per session, with a plan, or have both options. You can choose to set a fixed price, offer a customized price, or provide the service for free. You can also set varied prices from your desktop.

> **Note:**
>
> You need to create the service before you can connect a pricing plan.** **

#### To set your price and payment details per session:

Choose how you want your clients to pay for the service:

### Per session

Let your clients pay for their appointment based on the price you set.
1. Tap **Per session**.
2. Tap the **Price type **drop-down and select how you want to charge your client per session:

- **Fixed price: **You charge a set price for the appointment:

1. Tap **Amount** and enter the price.
2. Choose how clients pay from the** Payment preferences **drop-down:

- **Entire amount online:** Only accept online payments for the appointment.
- **Entire amount in person: **Only accept in-person payments (before or after the appointment).
- **Entire amount either online or in person: **Allow clients to choose whether they pay online or in person.
- **Online deposit and the rest in person: **Accept an online deposit for the appointment and have clients pay the rest in person. Add the deposit amount that clients need to pay online.
- **Free: **Offer the service free of charge. Great for introductory appointments with clients.
- **Varied Prices:** Go to your Wix account on desktop to [add varied prices to your appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-an-appointment#step-3-set-your-pricing-payment-details). You can charge different rates for appointments depending on the staff member, client type, service type and more.
- **Custom price: **Enter a short description of your pricing to display on your site. Clients aren't charged online when booking custom-priced services. Learn more about[ getting paid offline or in person](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-getting-paid-offline).
2. Tap **Create service**.

### With a plan

Clients can buy a membership or package and use it to book a session. 
1. Tap** With a plan**.

1. Tap **Connect Pricing Plans** to connect to an existing plan.

**Note:** If you do not have any pricing plans connected, tap [**+ Create new plan**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app)** **to connect a** Package **or** Membership**.
2. Enable the toggle next to the relevant plans or tap + Create new plan.
3. Tap **Done**.
2. Tap **Create service**.
**Tip: **If you're having trouble connecting booking services with plans, try doing it on your desktop. Learn more about [membership plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings) and [packages ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-packages)in Wix Bookings.

### Per session or with a plan

Let** **your clients choose how to pay for the class. They can pay per session or with a pricing plan they've purchased.
1. Tap **Per session or with a plan**.
2. Follow the steps from the **Per session** and **With a plan** section above.
3. Tap **Create Service.**

Tap the **Price type **drop-down and select how you want to charge your client per session:

- **Fixed price: **You charge a set price for the class.

1. Tap **Amount** and enter the price.
2. Choose how clients pay from the** Payment preferences **drop-down:

- **Entire amount online: **Only accept online payments for the class.
- **Entire amount in person: **Only accept in-person payments (before or after the class).
- **Entire amount either online or in person: **Allow clients to choose whether they pay online or in person.
- **Online deposit and the rest in person: **Accept an online deposit for the class and have clients pay the rest in person. Add the deposit amount that clients need to pay online.
- **Free: **Offer the service free of charge. No price will be displayed for this service.
- **Varied Prices:** Go to your Wix account on desktop to [add varied prices to your class](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-an-appointment#step-3-set-your-pricing-payment-details). You can charge different rates for classes depending on the staff member, client type, service type and more.
- **Custom price: **Enter a short description of your pricing (up to 20 characters). The description appears on your site.

**Note:** This means clients can't pay you online.
    Tap **Create Service**.

### Step 4 | Edit or add additional service details

Finalize your service details by adding pricing plans, selecting your service location, and assigning resources. Decide whether to show the class on your service list or keep it hidden until you're ready to take bookings. To maximize exposure and attract clients quickly, make your service visible right after creating it.

#### To edit or add additional service details:

Choose what you want to edit or add:

### Add Image

Add an image for clients to see when they look through your list of services.
1. Tap **Add Photo**.
2. Select an image for clients to see when they look through your list of services.
3. (Optional) Tap **Edit** to make any changes. Then tap **Save**.

**Note:** You can't edit photos added from **Free by Wix**.
4. Tap **Next**.

### Service Info

Edit any of your existing class details, like its name, category or max participants.

### Price & payments

Edit any of your existing appointment details, like its name, category or duration.

### Locations

Display where you offer the class, for example, at your business location(s) or you only offer the class online. You can add the option to meet online with a video link. 
1. Tap **Locations.**
2. Choose where your clients receive this service: 

- **Business location:** Tap the **Choose locations** drop-down and select the checkboxes next to the location(s) you provide this service.

**Note:** You can [add a new business location](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-offering-services-at-multiple-locations) on your desktop.
- **Custom location: **Enter a description of the location in the field (e.g. online meeting).
- Enable the Video conferencing toggle to offer this service online.

**Note: **Make any changes or set up and add video conferencing from your desktop.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Resources

Click the checkboxes to select the available resources for this service. You can create [new resources](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-your-resources) on your desktop.
1. Tap **Resources**.
2. Tap the checkboxes next to any of the relevant resources.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Images

Add a cover image that clients see at the top of your service page. You can also add a gallery to showcase your service anywhere on your service page.
1. Tap **Images**.
2. Choose what you want to edit:

- **Cover image:** Tap **Add Image** and select an image for the background of your service's header
- **Gallery:** Tap the **Add **icon and select the photos you want to add to a gallery for your service page. Then, tap **Next**.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Booking preferences

Add a booking policy or make the class view-only if you want clients to contact you for a consultation before booking.
1. Tap **Booking preferences**.
2. Choose your what you want to update:

- **Booking Policy:** Tap the drop-down and select a policy for your class. [Learn more about creating new policies from your Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-adding-and-setting-your-booking-policies-from-the-wix-owner-app).
- **Online Booking: **Toggle **Accept online booking** to enable or disable online bookings.
- **Let clients skip the booking form when booking on a Wix Members app:** Tap the checkbox to allow clients to [skip the booking form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/skip-booking-form-on-wix-member-apps) and book a class with one tap.
3. Tap **Save**.

2. Tap the **Visibility** icon at the top when you're ready to show the class on your site.

### Step 4 | Schedule when the class is offered

Now you can schedule when the class is offered, how often it repeats (e.g. every 2 weeks), who leads the class, and more.

#### To schedule class sessions:

Tap **+ Add Sessions**.  Tap the **Staff Member **drop-down and select the staff member leading the class.  Set the **Time & Duration**: 

- Tap the **Start Date** drop-down to select the first date the class is available, then tap the **Checkmark** icon .
- Tap the **Start Time** drop-down to select the session start time, then tap **Done**.
- Tap the **Duration** drop-down to select how long the class lasts, then tap **Done**.
- Tap the **Repeat** drop-down to select how often you want to offer this class session: 

1. Select the relevant option from the drop-down:** Weekly**, **Every 2**,** 3 **or** 4 weeks**.

**Note:** Tap **Never** if the course doesn't repeat.
2. Select the day(s) of the week the class takes place.
3. (Optional) Tap the **Set an end date** checkbox and choose the date of the last class.
    Tap **Add Sessions**.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Click a question below to learn more about classes on the Wix app and member apps.

### Can my members make group bookings for classes?

Yes. Through their Wix member apps, your members can add multiple participants to their class sessions. When they go to book a class session, they can tap the **Number of participants** drop-down and select their group size. Members then proceed to checkout and select the relevant payment option.
[image: Screenshot showing how members can make group bookings on their member apps.]

### How can I share my service?

You can share your service right after creating it. Click the **More Actions** icon   at the top right of the 'Create service' page. Then tap **Share service**. Learn more about [promoting your service on the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-promoting-your-services-from-the-wix-owner-app).

### How can I delete a service I no longer offer?

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Services**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Booking Services**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant service.
5. Tap **Delete service**.
6. Tap the **I understand that deleted items can't be recovered **checkbox.
7. Tap **Yes, Delete**.

### How can I duplicate a service?

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Services**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Booking Services**.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant service.
5. Tap **Duplicate**.
**Note: **The duplicated service will be called "Copy of (service name)". You can tap the service to make any edits.

### Can members make group bookings with pricing plans? What about classes with different price options?

Yes, your members can make group bookings and pay with their pricing plans. 
[image: Screenshot showing how members can make group bookings using pricing plans on the wix owner app.]
Members can also make group bookings with different price options (e.g. 2 adults at $40 each and 2 children at $30 each). They then proceed to checkout and pay using their pricing plans or online payment method.

**Note:** The number of available slots remaining in the class session appears at the top.

[image: Screenshot showing how to add extra participants to a class.]

### How can I quickly find a service on my Services screen?

Use the search bar at the top of the screen to quickly find the service you are looking for.

### Can I create multi-day classes on the Wix app?

Yes, to create multi-day classes:
1. Follow the steps in the section above to create a class and add sessions.
2. In the **Time & Duration** section, tap the **Duration** drop-down and select the session duration to cover multiple days (up to 99 hours).

**Tips: **
- Make sure to [edit the hours for the staff](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-editing-your-staff-members-working-hours) providing this service so they are available 24 hours a day on the relevant days.
- To run a similar service in future that starts on another date, simply duplicate the event you created and set a new start date.

### Can I create courses and appointments through the Wix app?

Currently, you can only create classes and [appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-creating-and-managing-appointments-using-the-wix-owner-app) on the Wix app. Courses can only be created on a desktop. If this is a feature you want to see in the future, you can [vote for it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-request-creating-courses-using-the-wix-owner-app).
