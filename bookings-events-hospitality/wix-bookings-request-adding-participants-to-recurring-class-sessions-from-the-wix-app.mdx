---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Adding Participants to Recurring Class Sessions from the Wix App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add participants to future sessions of an existing recurring class in the Booking Calendar on the Wix app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
