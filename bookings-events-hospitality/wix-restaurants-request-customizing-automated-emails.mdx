title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Customizing Automated Emails"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to [customize the confirmation emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-customizing-automated-emails) sent to customers about their orders. This feature is available with the [Wix Restaurants Orders (NEW) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).
