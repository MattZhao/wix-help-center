---
title: "Wix Bookings: Creating Coupons Using the Wix App"
---

Boost your sales and keep clients happy by creating discount coupons they can use when booking services. You can offer a fixed amount coupon ($20 off) or a percentage discount coupon (20% off).

> **Tip:**
>
> If your site has Wix Stores and Wix Bookings, you can create coupons that can be used on products or services or both. [Learn more about the 5 types of coupons available for Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-coupons-using-the-wix-owner-app).

### To create a bookings coupon:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).﻿  Tap the **Manage **icon .  Tap **Coupons **under** Marketing.**  Tap **Add a Coupon**.  Select the discount type for your coupon:

- **$ Discount: **Enter the amount that will be taken off the price.
- **% Discount: **Enter the percentage of the discount you want to offer.
    Tap **Next**.  Enter the coupon code your clients will enter to get the discount.  Enter the coupon name.

**Note: **This name appears in the coupons list in your site's dashboard.  Tap **Next**.  Choose which services the coupon applies to:

**Tip: **If your site also has a store, you see additional options.

- **All services:** Applies to any available booking service you offer.
- **Specific service: **Applies to one specific booking service. Tap to select the relevant service.
    Set the coupon's start and end date:

1. Tap the date under **Valid from**.
2. Select a start date from the calendar.
3. (Optional) Select an end date from the calendar.
4. Tap **Save**.
    (Optional) Apply limits to the number of times the coupon can be used:

- **Limit number of total uses:**

1. Tap the **Limit number of total uses** toggle to enable it.
2. Enter the number of times the coupon can be used.
- **One use per customer:** Tap the toggle to enable it.
    Tap **Done**.  (Optional) Tap **Share** to share your new coupon.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Clients cannot apply coupons to [membership plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-membership-plan-in-wix-bookings), plans that have no fee, or to services that are set to Custom Price (Free/Prices Vary).
> - Clients can apply coupons to group bookings. Both the monetary and the percentage discount apply to the total value of the booking.
