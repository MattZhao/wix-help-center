title: "Wix Events: Adding a Members Area"
---

Add a Members Area to your site so guests who log in can easily access information about upcoming and past events.

Members can:

View complete events details  Download tickets (for ticketed events)  Save their details so they can quickly sign up for future events  See who else is going to an event ([if enabled](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-setting-up-your-event-details-page))  Share events on social media (if enabled)  and more

> **Tip:**
>
> You can limit registration for an event to site members only.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Add the Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-adding-a-members-area#adding-a-members-area)  [Add Wix Event to the Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-adding-a-members-area#adding-wix-events-to-your-members-area)

---

### Adding a Members Area:

If your site doesn't have a Members Area, you can add one in just a few clicks.

### Wix Editor | Show me how

1. Click **Add** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click the **Community** tab.
3. Click **Members**.
4. Click **Add to Site**.

### Editor X | Show me how

1. Click **Add Elements**   at the top bar of Editor X.
2. Click the **Members **tab.
3. Click **Add to Site**.

---

### Adding Wix Events to Your Members Area

If your site has an older version of the Members Area, it may not include a Wix Events section.

You can update the Members Area to add Wix Events.

#### To update the Members Area to include Wix Events:

Click the Wix Events app in the Editor.  Click the Settings icon .   Click the **Members** tab.  Click **Add Now**.
