---
title: "Wix Bookings: Exporting Reports on Bookings and Staff"
---

Get the reports you need to manage your business efficiently. You can export your bookings data in the .CSV file format. This allows you to open these reports using software such as Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

> **Tip:**
>
> To contact a group of clients (e.g. all the clients who attended on a specific day), you can export a report. The report gives you a list of names and contact details for a specific time period, all in one place.

---

### To export a report:

[Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions **icon  at the top of the page.   Select **Export booking data**.  Click the drop-down and choose the type of report you want to export:

### Booking List

View data about the bookings you've received within a set date range, including:
- Session Start Time
- Date of Booking
- Client Name
- Client Email
- Client Phone
- Client Address
- Group Size
- Service Name
- Service Type
- Staff Member
- Booking Status
- Attendance Status
- Payment Status
- Payment Method
- Payment Details
- Amount Paid
- Amount Due
- Coupon Name
- Replies to custom Booking Form fields

### Staff Hours

View data on staff members and the sessions they are running within a selected time period, including:
- Service Name
- Service Type
- Session Start Time
- Duration (minutes)
- Number of Booked Participants
- Number of Attendees

5. Select the date range of the data you want to export.
