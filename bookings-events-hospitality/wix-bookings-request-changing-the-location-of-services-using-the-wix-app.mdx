---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Changing the Location of Services Using the Wix App"
---

Currently, you can offer your services at multiple locations with Wix Bookings. It is not possible to change the location of services using the Wix app on a mobile device. You are directed from the app to your Wix account on desktop to do this.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
