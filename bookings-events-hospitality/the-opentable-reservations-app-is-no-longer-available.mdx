---
title: "The OpenTable Reservations App is No Longer Available"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This app has been retired from the Wix App Market and is no longer supported. As an alternative, you can add [Wix Table Reservations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-table-reservations) to your site.
> - If you would like to remain with OpenTable, you can [create a widget in your OpenTable account](https://support.opentable.com/s/article/How-do-I-install-the-reservation-widget-to-take-reservations-on-my-website-and-Facebook?language=en_US#HowtoinstallawidgetonaWixwebsite) and [embed the code in your Wix Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/embed-html). This allows you to continue having the option to reserve seats on your site.
