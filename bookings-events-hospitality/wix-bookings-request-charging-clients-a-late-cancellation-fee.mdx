---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Charging Clients a Late Cancellation Fee"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [charge clients for cancellations and no-shows](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee). This feature helps reduce late cancellations and missed appointments. Clients can either cancel and be charged automatically, or you can handle it manually.
