---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Allowing Customers to Edit Their Orders Before the Delivery Deadline"
---

Currently, the only way for your customers to make changes to their orders in Wix Restaurants is to contact you (the Wix restaurant owner) and ask you to make the relevant changes. They are unable to edit their order themselves.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
