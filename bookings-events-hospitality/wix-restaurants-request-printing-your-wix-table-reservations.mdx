---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Printing your Wix Table Reservations"
---

Currently, it is not possible to print your table reservations list.

> **Workaround:**
>
> Export and download the reservations to print them out. [Go to Reservations](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/restaurants/table-reservations?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Table+Reservations+(New)) in your site's dashboard and click the export icon  . Afterward, you can save the file as .pdf and print it as needed.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
