### Deleting an event

Whether you're deleting canceled events or unused drafts, clearing out unnecessary items helps you stay focused on active and upcoming events.

> **Important:**
>
> Deleted events cannot be restored. If you only want to remove it from your live site, you can [go to your editor to hide the event](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-choosing-which-events-to-display-on-a-site-page).

#### To delete an event draft:

[Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/events/) in your site's dashboard.  Choose what you want to delete:

### An event draft

Deleting an event draft helps declutter your dashboard and removes any unfinished or unnecessary events, allowing you to focus on finalized plans and active tasks.
1. Click the **Drafts** tab.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant event.
3. Click **Delete Draft**.
4. Click **Delete Draft** to confirm.

[image: A screenshot of deleting an event draft.]

### A canceled event

Deleting canceled events from your dashboard helps keep your event list organized and ensures you're only managing active events.
1. (Optional) Filter your events to access canceled events or search for the relevant event using the search bar.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant event.
3. Click **Delete Event**.
4. Click **Delete Event** to confirm.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the options for removing an event.

### Can I delete a past event that has already taken place?

No. It's not possible to delete past events.

### Can I delete a published event that hasn't occurred yet?

No. Currently, it is not possible to delete an event that is published and hasn't taken place yet. Instead, [you can cancel it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-canceling-an-event).  Be sure to notify guests and issue refunds if needed.

### What should I do once I cancel an event?

Be sure to [notify guests and issue refunds](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-canceling-and-refunding-orders-for-events-with-tickets) if needed.
