---
title: "Wix Bookings: Managing Bookings Clients in the Wix App as a Staff Member"
---

Help your staff members manage their bookings and clients more efficiently by inviting them to download the Wix app. This way they can make updates on the go, directly from their mobile device.

> **Before staff can start managing bookings:**
>
> - [Assign staff members the role of Bookings Staff Member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-staff-permissions-in-wix-bookings). Adding staff members to your business doesn't automatically make them Bookings Staff Members.
> - Once that is done, [send the Bookings Staff Member an invite](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-app-inviting-and-managing-collaborators), so they can join your site on the app.

**In this article, learn how Bookings Staff Members can:**

[Add appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-bookings-in-the-wix-owner-app-as-a-staff-member#adding-appointments)  [Mark attendance for appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-bookings-in-the-wix-owner-app-as-a-staff-member#marking-attendance-for-appointments)  [Block off time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-bookings-in-the-wix-owner-app-as-a-staff-member#blocking-off-time)  [Manage class participants](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-managing-bookings-in-the-wix-owner-app-as-a-staff-member#managing-class-participants)

### Adding appointments

Staff members can book a session on behalf of a client for a service they provide. This is helpful when the client makes a booking with the staff member over the phone or at the end of a session. Share the instructions below with your staff so they can manage their own time.

#### To add an appointment:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.  Tap the **Add** icon   Tap **Appointment**.  Select the relevant existing contact or tap **Add new contact**.  Select the relevant service from the list.  Add the booking details.   Tap **Book**.

---

### Marking attendance for appointments

Allow staff members to manage attendance for their appointments. Your staff members can view the list of booked clients and mark them as 'checked-in' or 'no-show'.

#### To mark attendance for appointments:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.  Tap the relevant appointment.  Tap the **Booked** drop-down.  Select an option:

- **Checked-In: **Your client attended the appointment.
- **No-Show:** The client booked the appointment but didn't show up. If you want to [charge a no-show fee](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee), tap **Apply Fee**.

**Note: **Staff members can apply a no-show fee but do not have permission to [edit no-show and cancellation fees](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee).

---

### Blocking off time

Allow staff members to block off time for themselves in the calendar so that time is unavailable for booking.

#### To block off time:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.  Tap the **Add** icon .  Select **Blocked time**.  (Optional) Add a title to your blocked time.  Select a start date and time.  Select an end date and time.  (Optional) Add a note.  Tap **Save**.

### Managing class participants

Enable your staff members to manage the participant list for their class. They can add new participants if someone who wasn't on the list shows up, view more info about a participant, or check people in so you can keep track of attendance.

#### To manage class participants:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Booking Calendar**.  Choose what you want to do:

### Add a participant to a class session:

1. Select the relevant class.
2. Tap **Manage Participants**.
3. Tap the **Add** icon  at the top right of the screen.
4. Choose what you want to do: 

- **Add an existing member: **

1. Enter the name of the relevant client or select them from the list.
2. Fill out the client details for the class:

- **Group Of: **How many participants are in the booking
- (**Optional) Notify your client with an email: **Send the client the booking details.
- **(Optional) Mark as checked in: ** Mark the client as checked in for the session.
3. Tap **Add Participant**.
- **Add a new contact:**

1. ** **Tap **+ Add New Contact **and enter the client's details.
2. Tap **Save**.
3. Fill out the client details for the class:

- **Group Of: **How many participants are in the booking
- **(Optional) Notify your client with an email: **Send the client the booking details.
- **(Optional) Mark as checked in: **Mark the client as checked in for the session.
4. Tap **Add Participant.**

### Mark attendance:

1. Select the relevant class.
2. Tap **Check In**.
3. Select an option:

- **Check-In All: **Change the status of all participants to "Checked-In".

**Note:** You can still change the status of selected participants to "No-Show".
- **Check In: **Your client attended the appointment.
- **Mark as no-show:** The client booked the appointment but didn't show up. If you want to [charge a no-show fee](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee), tap **Apply Fee**.

**Note: **Staff members can apply a no-show fee but do not have permission to [edit no-show and cancellation fees](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee).
