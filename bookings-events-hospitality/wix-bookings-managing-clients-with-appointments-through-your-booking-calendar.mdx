In your booking calendar, you can keep track of who pays and who attends an appointment at your business. You can also cancel sessions and issue refunds when necessary.

### Marking attendance

Mark attendance and no-shows at your appointments within the booking calendar. Keeping track of attendance can help you make decisions about how you offer appointments.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant appointment in your calendar.
3. Click the **Booked** drop-down and select the relevant option:

- **Booked: **The client who booked the appointment hasn't arrived yet.
- **Checked-In:** The client has arrived at the appointment and checked in.
- **No-Show:**  The client didn't arrive for their appointment.  If relevant, you can apply a no-show fee** **to charge for the missed session.

**Note:** This option is only available for classes that include [cancellation and no-show fees in their booking policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-charging-a-cancellation-or-no-show-fee).

### Updating payment status

When you receive payment from clients, you can change their payment status. If the client pays a deposit online and the rest in person, you can follow the same steps to mark the session as paid.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant appointment in your calendar.
3. Click the **Collect Payment** drop-down.
4. Choose what you want to do:

- **Charge with pay link: **Create a pay link and send it to your client.
- **Charge card manually: **Charge a client's credit card manually.

**Note:** Make sure you [connect a payment provider that supports credit card payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-by-manual-card-entry).
- **Charge with invoice:** Charge a client through [an invoice](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-sending-an-invoice#managing-booking-payments).
- **Mark as paid:** Select this option if the client pays you in person.

**Note: **This action cannot be undone.

2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Booking Calendar**.
4. Tap the relevant appointment in your calendar.
5. Tap **Collect Payment**.

- **Go to checkout: **Go to your [checkout page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-selling-services-at-a-mobile-point-of-sale) to charge your client.
- **Charge with invoice: **Choose this option to [send your client an invoice for their appointment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-sending-payment-requests-using-the-wix-owner-app#sending-an-invoice-for-an-existing-booking).
- **Mark as paid: **Register an offline payment for the session. This can include cash payments or payments by check or cash app.

**Note: **You cannot undo this action.

### Issuing refunds for appointments

Depending on your payment provider, you can issue full refunds while canceling an appointment in the bookings calendar.

> **Charging a cancellation fee?**
>
> Charge the cancellation fee first. Then, go to the Order page to process the refund for the paid session.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Booking%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the appointment you want to cancel.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon   at the bottom of the pop-up and select **Cancel appointment**.
4. Follow the steps below depending on the type of session you are cancelling:
Individually paid sessions (without cancellation fees)
1. (Optional) Select the **Send cancelation email** checkbox and add a message for your customer.
2. (Optional) Select the **Mark as refunded **checkbox to refund your customer.
3. Click **Cancel Session**.
4. Click **Mark as Refunded **to confirm the refund.
Individually paid sessions (with cancellation fees)
1. (Optional) Select the **Apply cancellation fee** checkbox to charge a cancellation fee.
2. (Optional) Select the **Send cancelation email** checkbox and add a message for your customer.
3. Click **Cancel Ses****sion**.
4. (Optional) Edit the cancellation fee.  

**Note: **You cannot charge more than the fee stated in the policy
5. Click **Confirm Fee & Continue**.
6. Click **View Order**.
7. Click** Issue refund**.
8. Enter the refund details:

- **Refund amount:** Enter the amount to refund your client.
- **Reason for Refund (optional): **Select a reason for this refund.

**Note: **Your answer appears only in the order's activity section.
- **Send a confirmation email to customer: **Send your customer an email confirming the refund and amount.
- **Add a personal note in the email (optional) : **Add a personal note to your customer
9. Click **Continue**.
10. Click** Mark as Refunded**.

**Note: **This cannot be undone.
Sessions paid using a plan
1. (Optional)Select the **Apply cancellation fee **checkbox to charge a cancellation fee.
2. (Optional) Select the **Send cancelation email** checkbox and add a message for your client.
3. (Optional) Select the **Credit session back to client's plan **checkbox to credit back the session.
4. Click **Cancel Session**.
5. (Optional) Edit the cancellation fee.

**Note: **You cannot charge more than the fee stated in the policy.
6. Click **Confirm Fee & Continue**.
7. Click **Collect Payment **and choose how you want to charge your client: [**Charge with pay link**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pay-links-creating-and-managing-pay-links)**, **[**Charge card manually**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-getting-paid-offline#making-credit-card-payments-on-behalf-of-clients)**, **[**Charge with invoice**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-sending-an-invoice), **Mark as paid**.
8. Click **Issue Session Credit **to confirm.

### Managing clients' details

Click on any appointment in the Booking Calendar and select the client to view their contact card. Here, you can see their previous bookings, store important documents they shared and save notes about your client. You can also chat to your clients and send them service links or coupons.

2. Click the relevant appointment in your calendar.
3. Click the name of your client to open their contact card.
4. Choose what you want to do:

**Note: **You might see different options depending on the Wix features you use.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about managing appointment clients.

### Can I offer a partial refund for an appointment?

Yes, you can [issue partial refunds for appointments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/providing-a-partial-refund-in-wix-bookings) by going to the relevant appointment in your Orders page.

### Can I see a client's full booking history on my Wix app?

Yes. Click on the relevant client's name and tap **See All** under **Agenda** to see the client's complete booking history with you.

### Can I add notes or extra information to a client's profile?

Yes, you can. To add a note for a client, click [**Add tas**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-task-from-the-contact-card)[**k**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-task-from-the-contact-card)** **so you can follow up with this client. You can also add a note with extra information about the client.

### What can I do if I want to charge the cancellation fee but also refund the order?

In this case, you need to first charge the fees and the order, and then [issue a refund for the order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-canceling-a-session).
