---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Allowing Clients to Schedule Multi-Service Appointments from the Member Apps"
---

Currently, clients can [schedule appointments with multiple services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-scheduling-and-managing-multi-service-appointments) (e.g. a manicure and pedicure) when booking directly through your Wix site. It is not possible for clients to schedule multi-service appointments when booking through any of the Wix member apps (i.e. Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix, or your own branded app).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
