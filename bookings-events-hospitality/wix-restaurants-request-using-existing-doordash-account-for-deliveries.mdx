---
title: "Wix Restaurants Request: Using Existing DoorDash Account for Deliveries"
---

Currently, you can't connect an existing account when adding DoorDash as a delivery partner for your Wix site. You have to sign up for a new account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
