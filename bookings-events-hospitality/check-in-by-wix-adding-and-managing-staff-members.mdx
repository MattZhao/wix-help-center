### Adding staff members

Add staff members to an event so they can check in guests, scan their tickets and help manage the guest list.

Check-in appDashboard
1. Log into the Check-in by Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage Staff **at the bottom.
3. Tap **Add Staff**.
4. Enter the person's name.
5. Select the **Event access **you want your staff member to have:

- **All events:** All existing and new events you create.
- **Specific events:** You decide which events this staff member can access.

1. Tap the **Select Events** drop-down and choose the relevant event.
2. Tap **Done**.
6. Tap **Save **at the top right.

### Managing staff members

Once you've added staff members and invited them to the app, you can manage their details and event access as needed.

Check-in appDashboard
1. Log into the Check-in by Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage Staff**.
3. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant staff member.
4. Choose what you want to do:
View infoTap **View info **to see your staff member's info, as well as the Login link/QR code they should use in order to access the app.

Edit accessChange the events your staff member has access to (e.g. add new or remove expired events).
1. Tap **Edit staff info**.
2. Change the relevant event access.
3. Tap **Save**.
Pause accessYou can temporary stop access for a staff member (e.g. if they are on vacation or on leave).
1. Tap **Pause access**.
2. Tap **Pause Access** to confirm.

You can resume access when the time is right. The staff member will need to log in to the app again with a new login link or QR code.
1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant staff member.
2. Tap **Give access.**
3. Tap **Resume** to confirm.
Delete staff memberRemove a staff member who no longer works for you.
1. Tap **Delete staff member**.
2. Tap **Delete** to confirm.

1. [Go to Events](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/events/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Staff **on the left.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon** ** next to the relevant staff member.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about managing your staff members.

### What can my staff do in the Check-in by Wix app?

Staff members can scan tickets and check-in guests at events. They also have access to event details and guest information.

### Is there a limit to how many staff members can be added to an event?

Yes. You can add up to 100 staff members to each event.

### Can I give a username and password to staff members?

No. Staff members do not have their own username and password.

### How can my staff members log into the Check-in app?

Staff members can access the app using event specific invite link or QR code that you share with them.
