---
title: "Wix Bookings Request: Changing Price Options for a Booked Service on a Clients' Behalf"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the price option for a client after they have already booked a service.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can cancel and refund the original booking and then manually create a new booking with the correct price option.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
