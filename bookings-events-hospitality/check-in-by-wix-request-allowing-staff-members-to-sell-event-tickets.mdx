---
title: "Check-in by Wix Request: Allowing Staff Members to Sell Event Tickets"
---

Currently, you can [add staff members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/check-in-by-wix-adding-and-managing-staff-members) to an event so they can check in guests, scan tickets and manage the guest list.

However, it is not possible for staff members to sell tickets, which would also allow you to track the number of tickets each staff member sells.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
