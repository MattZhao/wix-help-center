---
title: "Wix Events: Deleting an Event Ticket"
---

You can delete any ticket type if you no longer wish to sell it.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Deleting a ticket does not affect tickets you've already sold.
> - Instead of deleting it, you can also [hide a ticket type](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-hiding-a-ticket-type).

### To delete a ticket:

[Go to the Events tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Events&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/events/) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant event.  Click the **Tickets** tab.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant ticket.

Click** Delete**.  Click **Yes, Delete** to confirm.
