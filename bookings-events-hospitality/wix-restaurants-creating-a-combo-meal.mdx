> **Important:**
>
> This article is about the original Wix Restaurants Menus App. Try out our new menu app. Learn how to [add and set up Wix Restaurants Menus (New) app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new).

You can offer a combo meal on your menu when you want to sell two or more dishes for a special price. For example, you may offer a special family dinner combo with multiple dishes, or a lunch special that includes a starter, main course and a beverage.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure that the dishes you want to add to the combo meal are available on your menu. Learn how to [add dishes to your menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-managing-dish-options).

### To create a combo meal:

[Go to Menus](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Restaurant&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/restaurants/menus) in your site's dashboard.  Navigate to the relevant menu section.  Click **Add Dish**.  Enter the combo meal details:

1. **Name:** Enter a name for the combo meal.
2. **Price: **Enter a price for the combo meal.
3. **Tax: **Enter taxes for the combo meal, if applicable.
4. **Description: **Enter a description for the combo meal.
5. **Dish Labels: **Add relevant labels to your combo meal (Special, Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten Free, Organic and Spicy levels).
6. **Visibility:** Select to show this combo meal to customers in the menu.
7. **Image: **Click to add an image to the combo meal.

Click **Save**.  Click + **New Option** and then **New Option**.  Click **New Choice** at the top right and then **Select Existing**.  Hover over and select the existing dishes you'd like to add to the combo meal.  Click **Add**.

**Note:** The dishes are added with their dish options. To remove the dish options, click **Use without**.  Click **Add**.
