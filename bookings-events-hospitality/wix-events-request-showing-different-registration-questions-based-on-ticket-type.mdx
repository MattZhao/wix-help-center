---
title: "Wix Events Request: Showing Different Registration Questions Based on Ticket Type"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display different questions on a registration form based on the ticket type. However, you can mark a question as 'optional' for both [RSVP](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-customizing-the-registration-form-for-rsvp-events) and [ticketed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-customizing-the-registration-form-for-ticketed-events) events.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
