title: "Google Ads with Wix: Creating a Campaign"
---

Create a Google Ads with Wix campaign by following a step-by-step process on your site's dashboard. Throughout the process, you'll find tips on creating the best converting ad content.

After you launch your ad campaign, all the data is automatically synced to [Google Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Google%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/google-ads/eligibility) in your Wix site's dashboard. It also syncs with Wix Analytics Marketing reports and Marketing Overview so you can see all your paid and organic marketing efforts in one place.

---

### Step 1 | Get your business ready to advertise

Before you create a Google Ads campaign with Wix, make sure you've completed the following setup steps in order to make your site eligible.

Publish your site.   Purchase a Premium plan.   Connect a custom domain.

To check if your business is ready to advertise, [go to Google Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Google%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/google-ads/eligibility) in your site's dashboard and complete the checklist. Then click **Create Ad Campaign.**

---

### Step 2 | Customize your ad settings and keywords

After you've completed the setup steps, choose your ad settings including where you want to advertise, as well as what keywords to target.

#### To customize your ad settings and keywords:

[Go to Google Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Google%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/google-ads/eligibility) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Create Ad Campaign** once your business is ready to advertise.

**Note: **If you do not see this option, make sure you've completed the checklist in Step 1.  Click the **Site page your ad lead to **drop-down and choose the page you want visitors to navigate to after clicking your ad (e.g. your Wix Stores product page).  Click the **Language **drop-down to choose the main language for the site page that appears in your ad. You can only pick one language  Under **Targeted locations** choose where you want to advertise:

- **Specific cities, zip codes or regions: **Enter a location in the text field and choose an option from the drop-down. You can add up to ten areas.
- **Near a street address: **Enter a street address in the text field and select a radius from the drop-down.

Under **When your ad will appear** choose if your ad should be shown all the time or at specific times.

- **Anytime** **(recommended):** Your ad appears at all times on Google search results. This is recommended as the more your ad is shown, the more views it will get.
- **At specific times:** Only show your ad during certain hours. This is recommended if you are expecting calls and want to be available during business hours.

1. Click** Edit ad schedule**.
2. Select the checkboxes next to the days you want to show your ad and pick specific hours for each day.
    Under **Keyword themes **enter up to 10 keyword themes to help people find your ad in searches. Pick keywords that match your service, product or offering. You can also use Google Ads suggested keywords. Learn more about [choosing keyword themes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-keyword-themes).

Click **Next** to create your ad content.

---

### Step 3 | Create your ad content

Create engaging ad content that encourages visitors to click and visit your site. Choose three headlines and two descriptions. As you create your ad content you can see a live preview at the right of how the ad appears in Google search results.

> **Important:**
>
> Make sure you write headlines and descriptions that can go together in any order. Google Ads rearranges and tests your content to best optimize your ad.

---

### Step 4 | Set your ad budget and launch

Next you need to choose your campaign budget. Your budget is applied to your total ad credits each month that will be spent on clicks. The higher your budget, the more your ads are shown on Google.

> **Need help setting your budget?**
>
> Read some [frequently asked questions about choosing a budget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/faqs-about-google-ads-with-wix-campaign-budget) for your Google Ads with Wix campaign.

Once you have set your budget, checkout and launch your campaign.

#### To set your ad budget and launch:

Drag the slider under **Set your campaign budget** to select a daily spend. Below the daily spend is the amount you pay monthly for your Google Ads subscription. You can also see the estimated clicks your ad might receive per day with this budget.

Click **Save & Checkout **once you've set your budget.  Enter your payment details.  Click** Submit Purchase**.

### FAQs

Here are some frequently asked questions about creating a Google Ads campaign with Wix.

### What setup is required before I launch a campaign?

Before you can run a Google Ads campaign with Wix, make sure you've completed the following prerequisites:
- Publish your site
- Upgrade to a Premium plan
- Connect a custom domain

### Does Wix charge a fee for running and optimizing my campaign?

Wix gets 15% of your payment for running and optimizing your campaign, the remaining 85% goes towards your ad budget which is spent on ad clicks.

### How do I know what budget to set for my business?

We recommend a monthly budget that is about 5% of your monthly business revenue. However, you can start as low as $150 USD a month.

### What does my ad credit include?

Your ad credit may include your monthly subscription, any rollover ad credit from previous months, as well as any qualifying Google Ads credit applied to your account. If you pay in a supported currency (e.g. EUR, GBP, JPY, USD) the ad credits are in the same currency. For all other currencies, ad credits in your dashboard show as USD.
It is possible to continue running an ad with existing ad credit, even if your Google Ads with Wix subscription has expired.

### What do I include in my ad headlines and descriptions?

Learn more about [writing effective headlines and descriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/writing-headlines-and-descriptions).

### Can I connect an existing Google Ads account to my Google Ads with Wix campaign?

Currently, Google Ads with Wix does not offer the option to connect an existing Google Ads account with your campaign. Any campaigns and campaign data launched through Google Ads with Wix can only be managed through the Wix dashboard. 
All data from your personal Google Ads account, in the Google platform, or the Google Ads interface can only be managed through Google.
