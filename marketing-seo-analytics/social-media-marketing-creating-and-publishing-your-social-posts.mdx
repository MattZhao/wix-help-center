### Creating an image to share on social media

Create custom images for your social media posts using Wix. Ensure your visuals are unique and aligned with your brand identity. There are helpful templates or you can start from scratch, adding text, symbols, media and more.

#### To create an image with Wix:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Social Media Marketing](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fsocial-marketing-web%2F%3F&title=Social+Media+Marketing&primaryButtonText=Social+Media+Marketing) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Quick Actions **on the top right.
3. Click **Create image with Wix**.
4. Select an option from the settings on the left to begin customizing your post:

- **Template:** Select a template to customize.
- **Palette: **Select a pre-made palette of colors that are designed to look well together.

**Tip:** The colors can be changed and adjusted if you need specific colors.
- **Text: **Select from the suggested fonts or add your own.
- **Symbols: **Add your business' logo to make your social post look like your own. Or choose from a selection of pre-made icons and symbols to add to your post.
- **Shapes: **Add shapes to your post. You can even build them on top of each other to make a truly unique design.
- **Images: **Upload your own image or choose from the library of pre-existing ones.** **
- **Background:** Select a background color for your post.
5. Choose what you want to do:

- **Back to Social Media Marketing: **Save the image to the **Your Social Posts **tab in your Social Media Marketing dashboard.
- **Publish: **Share your image on social media.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing **and select **Social Posts**.** **
4. Tap any template to get started.
5. Customize your post using the available options:

- **Overlay:** Tap the **Overlay** icon  to pick a different overlay with preset text and stickers.
- **Text:** Add your custom text to the post:

1. Tap the **Text** icon  from the bottom menu.
2. Tap **Add Text**.

6. When you're done, share your post by tapping **Share** at the top right. Select where you want to share:

- **Facebook: **Share the post to your Facebook business page. Follow the on-screen instructions to connect to the relevant page.
- **Instagram: **Share the post to your Instagram feed or story.
- **Save Image: **Save the post as an image in your mobile device.
- **More: **Share your post to any of the available apps in the menu.

### Creating a video to share on social media

Create high-quality, unique videos for your social media. Tailor your content to fit your brand identity by customizing text, style, colors, music, and more. Choose from a variety of templates or start from scratch, giving you full creative control to craft videos that stand out and engage your audience..

#### To create a video:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Social Media Marketing](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fsocial-marketing-web%2F%3F&title=Social+Media+Marketing&primaryButtonText=Social+Media+Marketing) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Quick Actions **at the top right.
3. Choose **Create video with Vimeo**.
4. Choose how to create your video.
Wix Video Maker
1. Add the video content and text:

1. Click the **Add **icon** ** under **Add Photos and Videos**.
2. Select the photos or videos you want to add and click **Add to Video**.
3. Enter the text for your video:

- **Opening Text: **Use it to introduce the concept of the video.
- **Second Text:** Use it to explain more about the concept.
- **Third Text:** We recommend making this a call-to-action to explain what you would like the viewers to do, such as "Visit Our Store".
- **Closing Text:** We recommend entering your URL or contact details so your viewers know how to find you.
2. Click **Next**.
3. Customize the design of your video:

- **Choose a Video Style: **Select the style of your video. Hover over the styles to preview them.
- **Choose a Layout:** Choose the video layout: **Horizontal**, **Vertical**, or **Square**
- **Choose Colors and Font:** Click the color boxes to set the colors and use the drop-down to choose the text font.
4. Click **Next**.
5. Select the music track for your video. Click the tracks to listen to a preview. **Tip:** Alternatively, disable the **Music On **toggle if you do not want to play a track.
6. Click **Next**.
7. Click the **Include Business Info **toggle to show or hide your business info. **Note:** Logos must be 48x48 pixels in order to be rendered properly.
8. Click **Apply & Preview**.

### Publishing your social posts

Publish your social posts to get them seen by your audience. You can edit previously saved designs before sharing, and customize each post's caption accordingly.

> **Important:**
>
> You can publish up to 10 social posts a month or you can [upgrade to publish more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-media-marketing-upgrading-your-social-media-marketing-plan).

#### To publish a post:

[Go to Social Media Marketing](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fsocial-marketing-web%2F%3F&title=Social+Media+Marketing&primaryButtonText=Social+Media+Marketing) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Your Social Posts **at the top.  Hover over the post you want to publish and click the **More Actions** icon .  Click **Preview & Publish**.

**Tip: **Click **Download **to save your post to your computer.  (If you haven't yet) [Connect the social accounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-media-marketing-connect-to-your-social-media-accounts) you want to publish to.  Customize how your social post will appear on each social channel:

**Note: **The options vary depending on which social accounts you connect. 

- **Publish as:** If you're posting to Facebook and/or Instagram, choose whether you want to publish your image as a Post, Reel or Story.
- **Caption:** Create a caption to add to your social post which will display to your audience.  **Tip:** Click **Create AI Text** to open the AI text creator menu. Enter a brief description of the content, select a tone of voice and click **Create Text** to generate a caption for your post.
- **Add Media: **Add multiple media files to your post.
- **Add Tracking Link: **Include a link to your site so that when people click it, you can view the analytics of who came from your social post.
- **Hashtags: **Add any hashtags you think are relevant to your post.
- **Media:** Hover over the relevant media item and click the **Replace media** icon  to swap out your image or video for another.
- **When do you want to publish?**:** **Select **Publish now** or [**Schedule for later**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-posts-scheduling-a-social-post) to choose when you want to publish.
    Click **Publish** at the bottom right.

**Note: **The number next to **Publish** indicates how many posts you are about to publish. For example, if you have created both a Facebook and Instagram post, the number will show (2).

> **Posting as a story?**
>
> - This kind of post includes up to 5 images.
> - This option is not yet available to all Wix accounts.

### FAQs

Click to learn more.

### Can I add a specific caption for each platform that I publish to?

Yes, you can add a caption specific for each platform.
For example, if you've connected both your Instagram and Facebook accounts you will see two areas to add post content. You can write different captions or add different hashtags for each platform.

### Do I need a Vimeo account to create videos with Vimeo on Wix?

Yes, you will need to [sign up for a Vimeo account](https://vimeo.com/join) in order to create videos using Vimeo in Wix.

### Can I publish to multiple social media platforms at the same time?

Yes, make sure you have signed into all of your social accounts. You can then toggle on and off where you want the post to publish.
**Note:** YouTube will automatically toggle off if you are posting an image post.

### Can I edit my images and videos after saving them?

You can edit your videos from **Your Social Posts** in the dashboard. Hover over the relevant video and click **Edit**.

### Can I edit my post after publishing it?

If you need to edit your post's content after publishing you must do so from your social platforms.

### Can I add an image or video that I created for a social post in my Wix site?

Yes, everything you create is saved to your [site files in your editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-viewing-your-site-files-in-your-media-manager) you can add it to your page as you would add any other images or videos.
