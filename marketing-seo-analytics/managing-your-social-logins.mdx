---
title: "Managing Your Social Logins"
---

Quickly log in to your Wix account using the identity provided from your Facebook, Apple or Google accounts.

Click to enable or disable login to Wix using a Facebook, Apple or Google account.

        [
            Manage Social Login
        ](https://manage.wix.com/account/account-settings)

                Takes 1 minute

### Show me step-by-step instructions

**To manage your social logins:**
1. Click [here](http://www.wix.com/) to sign in to your Wix account.
2. Hover over the user panel and click **Account Settings**.
3. Click **Enable **or** Disable **next to the relevant social account.

- **Enabled: **Sign in to the social account and allow access.
- **Disabled: **The social account is disconnected.

> **Notes:**
>
> - If you disable social logins, you'll still be able to log in using your Wix email and password.
> - Apple gives you the option to create an Apple generated email address. If using this option, be sure to remember the correct Apple email for use in your Wix account. Learn how to [manage your Apple ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT210426).
> - You can always update your email address in [Account Settings](https://manage.wix.com/account/account-settings).
