### Eligibility for Google Ads credit

In order to be eligible for the ad credit offer:

You must be new to Google advertising and have not previously run a Google Ads campaign with your site.  You must spend $500 USD (or your currency equivalent ) on ad clicks within the first 60 days of your campaign.

If you are eligible for this ad credit, Google applies it directly to your available ad credits and you will receive a notification via email as well as in your site's dashboard.

This ad credit is not refundable and you must spend it within 60 days of receiving it in your account. [Read more about Google's terms and conditions](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/1396257).

> **Tip:**
>
> Since your ad budget is spent on clicks, you have a better chance of being eligible if you set your monthly subscription to at least $300 USD (around $10 USD per day).

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about how the Google Ads credit offer works.

### How long do I have to spend the ad credit after receiving it?

You need to spend the ad credit within 60 days of receiving it in your account. After 60 days, the ad credit expires.

### How do I know if I am eligible to receive this credit?

You are eligible for the ad credit as long as you meet the following conditions: 
1. You are new to Google advertising.
2. You have not run a Google Ads campaign previously with your site.
3. You have spent $500 USD (or your currency equivalent ) on ad clicks in the first 60 days after launching your campaign.

**Note:** If you have run a campaign in the past with Google Ads, you will not be eligible, even if this is your first campaign with Google Ads with Wix.

### What if I am running a campaign in a currency other than USD?

If you are running your campaign in a supported currency other than USD (GBP, EUR, JPY) the ad credit breakdown is as follows:
- **GBP:** Spend 400 GBP within the first 60 days to receive 400 GBP ad credit.
- **EUR:** Spend 400 EUR within the first 60 days to receive 400 EUR ad credit.
- **JPY: **Spend 60,000 JPY within the first 60 days to receive 60,000 JPY ad credit.

All other currencies which are converted to USD need to spend $500 USD to receive the $500 USD ad credit.

### How is the ad credit applied to my account?

After you have spent $500 USD within the first 60 days, Google automatically applies the amount to your available ad credits. You will receive an email about the new credit, as well as a dashboard notification.

### Does turning off auto-renewal of my subscription affect the credit?

No, if you turn off auto-renewal your Google Ads credit is not affected. For example, if you purchase a $1K subscription and then turn off auto-renewal, you can still receive the credit as long as you spend $500 within the first 60 days.

### Why am I not seeing the credit immediately?

Purchasing a Google Ads with Wix subscription does not apply the credit immediately. The promotional credit is applied to your account after you spend $500 USD on ad clicks. Then it is applied to your account.
**Note:** Since the credit comes directly from Google, it may take a few days to see the credit applied to your account after you have fulfilled the requirements.
