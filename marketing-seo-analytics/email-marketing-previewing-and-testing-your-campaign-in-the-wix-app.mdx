---
title: "Email Marketing: Previewing and Testing Your Campaign in the Wix App"
---

Once you've finished customizing your campaign in the Wix app, preview it and send a test email to an address of your choice. See how the campaign looks in an inbox and view the design and text elements from a mobile device.

> **Note:**
>
> Sending test emails does not count towards your monthly email marketing campaign allowance.

### To preview your campaign and send a test email:

Go to the [email marketing dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-accessing-the-email-marketing-dashboard-in-the-wix-owner-app) in the Wix app.  Tap the **Drafts** tab.  Tap **Edit**.

**Note:** If you don't see this option it means your campaign was created on a desktop. You can still preview the email by tapping **Preview** but you can only send a test email from a desktop.  The customization menu opens at the bottom. Tap **Preview & Test**.

  Preview your email. When you're ready tap **Send Test **at the top right.  Enter the email address that the test campaign should be sent to.  Tap **Send **at the top right.  Check the inbox of the recipient address to see your test email campaign.

#### If you do not receive your test email:

> **Don't see the test email?**
>
> Check your spam folder or try sending the test to another email address.
