---
title: "Disabling the Google Chrome Pop-Up Blocker"
---

In order to enjoy all of the Wix features, you need to allow Wix.com pop-ups in your browser. You can either allow all sites to send pop-ups and use redirects or allow only specific sites.

### To disable the Google Chrome pop-up blocker:

Click the **More** icon  at the top right of your browser.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Privacy and security** on the left.   Click **Site settings **in the **Privacy and security **section.   Scroll down and select **Pop-ups and redirects**.   Choose what you want to do:

### Allow all pop-ups

Select **Sites can send pop-ups and use redirects**.

### Allow pop-ups only from specific sites

1. Click **Add **next to **Allowed to send pop-ups and use redirects**.
2. Type **https://[*.]wix.com **and click **Add**.
