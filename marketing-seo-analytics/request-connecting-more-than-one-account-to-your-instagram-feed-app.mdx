---
title: "Request: Connecting More than One Account to Your Instagram Feed App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect more than one account to the Instagram Feed app.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can add several widgets and connect each of them to the same/different Instagram accounts.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
