### Viewing your keywords in the SEO Setup Checklist

The keywords in your SEO Checklist are the one that you entered when you initially set up your checklist. You can view your keywords at the top of [your SEO checklist](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-tools/seo-setup/dashboard?referralInfo=KB-9952e53a-03b8-4b04-92b7-e34e771d40be&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO+Checklist).

### Updating your keywords in the SEO Setup Checklist

If you decide you want to try different keywords, you can edit them in your SEO checklist in your site's dashboard.

#### To edit your keywords:

Go to [your SEO checklist](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-tools/seo-setup/dashboard?referralInfo=KB-9952e53a-03b8-4b04-92b7-e34e771d40be&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO+Checklist) in your site's dashboard.  Click the Edit details** **icon  beside **Keywords**.

Add new keywords or delete existing keywords under **Keywords**.  Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> You can add up to five keywords to your checklist.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about keywords in the SEO Setup Checklist.

### Can I add more than 3 keywords to the SEO Setup Checklist?

We allow adding up to 3 keywords to the SEO Setup Checklist when you [build your checklist](https://support.wix.com/en/article/improving-your-online-presence-with-the-wix-seo-setup-checklist) as it's easier to focus on a small number of keywords when optimizing your site for the first time. 
After you've built your checklist, you can add up to 5 keywords in [your checklist settings](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-tools/seo-setup/settings?referralInfo=KB-a54f322d-a66e-4e68-8b34-112b388d854c&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+Checklist+Settings).
However, the Wix SEO Setup Checklist only uses these keywords to create a plan for your site. This means that you can apply the same recommendations to any other keyword you need to incorporate in your content. 
For example, you can add as many keywords as needed to your home page's text as long as it's an organic part of the content.

### Can I add keywords in more languages?

Yes. You can follow the same plan suggested by the SEO Setup Checklist with keywords in any other language. 
[Learn how to optimize your SEO settings for secondary languages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-optimizing-seo-settings-for-multilingual-sites)

### Why aren't my keywords shown in the site's source code?

The SEO Setup Checklist doesn't automatically add any keyword to your site's source code as it's generally not a good practice. 
When reviewing your site, Google and other search engines don't search for a list of keywords in your site's source code to understand what it's about. Rather, they look for keywords that are organically implemented in your content.

### How do I choose good keywords?

Keyword research is the process of finding the best keywords for your website, so your site will rank higher in search engine results pages. Check out [this video about choosing the right keywords](https://www.wix.com/seo/learn/resource/what-are-keywords-and-how-to-choose-the-right-ones?utm_source=kb) on the SEO Learning Hub.

### Why can't I find my site in search engines when using my keywords?

It takes time for search engines to crawl, review and rank your site. Generally, the more competitive your keywords are, the more time and effort it will take to boost your site's ranking in search results. 
Check out [this article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/improving-your-ranking-on-google-after-completing-seo-setup-checklist) to learn more about ranking high on Google after completing the SEO Setup Checklist.
