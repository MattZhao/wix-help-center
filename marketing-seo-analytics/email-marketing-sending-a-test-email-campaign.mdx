### Sending a test email campaign

Send a test email campaign from a new or existing campaign. It is best to send a test email to your own email address so you can check it from your own inbox.

You can also choose to edit the Subject, From name and Reply-to fields in your test email.

#### To send a test campaign:

Open a [new campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-a-new-campaign) or edit an [existing campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editing-and-reusing-existing-email-campaigns).  Click **Preview & Test** at the top right.  Click **Send Test Email** at the top right.  Enter the email address that the test email should be sent to (your own).  (Optional) Edit the **Subject**, **From name** and **Reply-to email **fields.

Click **Send**.  Click **Done**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about sending test email campaigns.

### Can I send a test email to multiple contacts?

No, a test email can only be sent to one contact.

### What is the Reply-to-Email?

The **Reply-to email **is the email address your subscribers reach when clicking **Reply**.

### What email address will my test emails be sent from?

When sending a test email, the email is sent from ﻿no-reply@usrmailtest.com

### What can I do if I am not receiving my test emails?

If you do not receive your test emails, try the following:
- Check your spam folder.
- Send your test to a different email address.
- Learn how to [troubleshoot common issues](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-undelivered-test-email-campaign).
- If you need further assistance, [contact us for help](http://www.wix.com/contact/en).

### Why can't I see personalized content (dynamic values) in my test emails?

[Personalized content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-personalizing-your-campaigns-with-dynamic-values) doesn't appear in test email campaigns. Your recipients should still see them when you send the campaign.

### What happens if I click the 'unsubscribe' link in a test email?

The **Unsubscribe** link in test emails will lead to the Wix help center article, [About the Unsubscribe Link in Your Email Campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-unsubscribe-link-in-your-email-campaign-2071623).
