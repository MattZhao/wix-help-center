If you are trying to share your site on Facebook but you are receiving an error message like the ones below, it is because your site has been blocked by Facebook.

### Information about blocked sites on Facebook:

Your site being blocked is Facebook's decision. Wix has no control over the sites that Facebook blocks. We advise you to ensure that your site adheres to [Facebook's community standards](https://www.facebook.com/communitystandards).To resolve the issue, you should contact Facebook and submit a request for them to unblock your site. It may take some time for them to process the request as they review each site individually.
