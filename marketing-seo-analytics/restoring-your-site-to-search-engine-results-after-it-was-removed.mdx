---
title: "Restoring Your Site to Search Engine Results After it was Removed"
---

If your site no longer appears in search results, it's possible that the search engine has deliberately removed your site. There are many possible reasons that a search engine might do this.

For example, black hat SEO is a type of search engine optimization that uses unethical techniques to manipulate search ranking results for the site. These techniques don't comply with [search engine guidelines for quality](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/guidelines/webmaster-guidelines). If you use these techniques, intentionally or unintentionally, your site might be removed from search results. [Learn more about black hat SEO.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-black-hat-seo-techniques)

---

### Step 1 | Resolve any issues with your site

Usually, search engines will let you know what issues exist on your site. For example, Google gives you with a [Manual Actions report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9044175?hl=en) in Google Search Console that details the issues with your site.

Make sure to follow any advice from search engines to make sure your site is compliant with [their quality guidelines](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/guidelines/webmaster-guidelines) before you request a review.

---

### Step 2 | Request a review of your site

After you've removed any black hat SEO optimization and resolved any other issues, you can [request a reconsideration review](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9044175).

These reviews can take several days or weeks to complete, and the search engine will notify you when it's done.

---

### Step 3 | Add original content to help improve your site's ranking

One of the best ways to [rank higher in search engine results](https://support.wix.com/en/article/getting-your-site-found-on-google-seo-best-practices) is to regularly add original content to your site. The rank of your page depends on the quality of your content and [how relevant it is to certain keywords](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-keywords-on-your-sites-pages). You should also try to use your keywords and search phrases organically throughout your content.

Here as some ways that you can create high-quality content that organically uses your keywords:

Describe the main purpose of your site in different ways with a variety of keywords  Go into as much detail as you can about what you do, what services or products you offer, and what your users can get from your site  Write content that answers popular questions related to your keywords  [Create a blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-about-the-wix-blog) for news updates and new trends  [Make an FAQs page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-faq-app) to help customers with common questions  [Add a testimonials page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-customer-testimonials-to-your-site) for customer reviews
