---
title: "Google Search Console: \"Page Resources Couldn't Be Loaded\" Error"
---

Sometimes Google finds pages that it cannot load fully. When this happens, you may see a "Page resources couldn't be loaded" error in Google Search Console when you click **View Tested Page **and open the **More Info **tab.

If you get this message for URLs beginning with "frog.wix.com",** **please note that these URLs are part of the structure of your Wix site. They don't affect Google's ability to crawl your site's content or impact your site's SEO.
