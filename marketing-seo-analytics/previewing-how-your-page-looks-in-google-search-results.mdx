---
title: "Previewing How Your Page Looks in Google Search Results"
---

When Google displays one of your site's pages in a search result, it includes the page's title tag, the page's URL, and a snippet from the page's meta description.

You can preview the information that will appear in search results from your page's SEO settings.

---

### Previewing your page in Google Search Results

Use the **SEO Basics **tab of your page's SEO panel to preview how it will look in Google search results.

#### To preview your page:

Wix EditorEditor XStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant page.
4. Click **SEO basics**.
5. Check your page preview under **Preview on Google**.

### Previewing your Wix Stores product page in Google search results

You can also see how individual product pages will look in Google search results in your site's dashboard.

#### To preview your product page:

Go to the [Products page](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Store%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.   Click the product you want to view.   Click **Edit SEO Settings** in the **Marketing and SEO **section on the right.   Click **SEO Basics**.  View your product's preview under **Preview on Google**.

> **Important:**
>
> It takes time for changes you make to your SEO settings to appear on Google. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/time-it-takes-for-site-changes-to-appear-in-search-engine-results)

> **Tip:**
>
> You can customize your page's SEO settings like meta tags, social share settings, and structured data markup in the page's SEO panel. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-pages-seo-settings-in-the-seo-panel)
