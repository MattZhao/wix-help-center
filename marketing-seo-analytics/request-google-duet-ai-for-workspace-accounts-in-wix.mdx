title: "Request: Google Duet AI for Workspace Accounts in Wix"
---

Currently, Duet AI is unavailable for Google Workspace accounts purchased through Wix.

Google Duet AI offers a range of features designed to enhance collaboration and streamline workflows. This can be applied to document creation, data analysis, presentations, email management, and more.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
