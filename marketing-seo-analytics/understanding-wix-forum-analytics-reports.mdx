---
title: "Understanding Wix Forum Analytics Reports"
---

Gain insights about your forum members' engagement, top forum posts by traffic, most active collaborators, and more. With Wix Forum analytics reports, you can see how your traffic changes over time so you can make data-driven decisions to boost activity.

**In this article, learn more about the following reports:**

### How to access your Forum Reports

1. Go to [Analytics & Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Analytics%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll to the Forum Reports section.
3. Select the report you'd like to view.

### How to switch chart types within a report

Click the relevant tab at the top of the report to access different chart types: **Bar Chart**, **Table**, or** Line Chart**.

---

### Top Forum Posts

The Top Forum Post report shows which of your posts receive the most views.

### Measures available in your Top Forum Posts report

Click the **Select a measure** drop-down to choose the metric you want to view:
- **Views:** The number of views your forum has.
- **Unique visitors: **The number of different people who visited your forum.
- **Comments:** The amount of comments made by visitors on your forum
- **Likes: **The amount of 'likes' made by visitors on your forum.

Learn more about [the measure definitions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-reports-glossary#blog-report-measures).

### More available filters

Click **More** at the top of the report to access the following filters:
- Post title
- Publish date
- Category name
- Date created
- Header title
- Member name
- Sign-up date
- Last login
- Member role
