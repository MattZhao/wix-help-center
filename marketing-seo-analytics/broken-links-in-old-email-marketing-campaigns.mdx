---
title: "Broken Links in Old Email Marketing Campaigns"
---

Links added to an email campaign are only available for a limited time (one year). If your campaign was created over a year ago, the links will no longer work.

You can reuse an old email campaign, but make sure to check each of the links inside to make sure they're still available.
