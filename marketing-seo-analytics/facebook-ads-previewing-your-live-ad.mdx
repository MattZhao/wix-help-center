title: "Facebook Ads: Previewing Your Live Ad"
---

See an example of how your live ad looks on Facebook from the Facebook Ads dashboard. If you are running a [dynamic ad](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-picking-an-ad-format-for-your-campaign#dynamic-ads) campaign, you will see an example of what an ad may look like to a subset of your audience.

> **Note:**
>
> It can take up to 72 hours for Facebook to generate the live preview link if you are creating or editing a campaign.

### To preview your live ad:

Go to [Facebook Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Facebook%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/paid-ads/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Campaign Actions **drop-down at the right.  Click** Preview ad**.  Click **Preview on Facebook **to view one of the ad variations on Facebook.

> **Notes:**
>
> - When previewing your ad, you are only seen an example of what a variation of your ad may look like. With a dynamic campaign, there are several versions of your ad running at the same time updated according to the audience and their interests.
> - The number of likes, views and comments on an ad may differ from the total stats of the campaign, as it is only one of the variations running simultaneously.

### Troubleshooting previewing your live ad

If you are having trouble previewing your ad on Facebook, try the following troubleshooting steps:

### Step 1 | Disable your ad blocker

Follow the instructions to disable ad blocking on Wix if you're using AdBlock or Ad-Blocker. Otherwise, follow your ad blocker's instructions to turn it off or pause it temporarily. 
**I'm using AdBlock**
1. Click the AdBlock button in your browser.
2. Click **Don't run on pages on this site** (Chrome) or **Don't run on pages on this domain **(other browsers).
3. Click **Exclude**.
4. Refresh the page to see your ad preview.

**I'm using Ad-Blocker**
1. Click the Ad-Blocker button in your browser.
2. Click **Enabled on this site** to disable the option.
3. Refresh the page to see your ad preview.

### Step 2 | Check in incognito mode

The problem may be local for your browser or device. To test if this is the issue:

1. Open an [incognito browser window](https://support.wix.com/en/article/opening-your-browser-in-incognitoprivate-browsing-mode) on your desktop.
2. Log in to your Wix site.
3. Go to your Facebook Ads dashboard.
4. Check the ad preview step again.

### If you see the preview link but can't select it:

If you can see the preview link but not select it, it means Facebook hasn't provided the new link yet. It can take up to 72 hours for a link to go live when creating or editing a campaign.
