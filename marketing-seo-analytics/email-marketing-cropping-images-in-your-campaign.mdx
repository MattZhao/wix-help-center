---
title: "Email Marketing: Cropping Images in Your Campaign"
---

You can crop any image in your email campaign to show only part of the image. When you crop an image, you are taken to the [Photo Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools). There you can choose from a variety of editing tools to make any other adjustments necessary.

### To crop an image in your email campaign:

[Go to Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Edit** next to an existing campaign or [create a new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-a-new-campaign).  Select the relevant element in your email campaign.   Click the **Crop** icon  from the toolbar on the right.

Choose how to crop your image:

- **Crop Ratio: **Crop your image to a predefined size and ratio (e.g. 1:1, 9:16, 3:4).

**Tip: **Select the **portrait**  or **landscape**  orientation to change the ratio options.
- **Image size (px):** Set the size your image will be cropped to, using the **Width **and **Height **fields.

**Note:** The maximum image size is 3000px x 3000px.
- **Free hand:** Manually drag the corners of the image with your mouse.
    (Optional)** **Change your image orientation: **Rotate left **, **Rotate right** , **Flip horizontally**  or **Flip vertically **.

**Tip:** Rotate your images at custom angles using the degree rotation tool on the right of the Photo Studio.  Click **Save**.
