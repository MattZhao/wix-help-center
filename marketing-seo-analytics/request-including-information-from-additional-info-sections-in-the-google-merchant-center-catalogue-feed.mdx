---
title: "Request: Including Information from Additional Info Sections in the Google Merchant Center Catalogue Feed"
---

Currently, it is not possible to include information from any [Additional Info Sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-info-sections-to-products) of your Wix Stores products in the Google Merchant Center catalogue feed. Only your product's basic info appears in Google Merchant Center.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
