title: "Reporting Email Marketing Spam"
---

Reporting spam content helps Wix to maintain the high ethical standards practiced within our community of users.

Millions of sites are built on Wix every month and we do everything in our power to prevent any type of abuse.

If you encounter any Wix sites that you believe are sending electronic spam, you can [file a complaint](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=aQY9eGiQNAZIhtaPT4oN&referral=abuseMainPage).
