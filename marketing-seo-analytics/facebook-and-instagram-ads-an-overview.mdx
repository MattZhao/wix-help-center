The first step before you can create ad content and upload your media is to make sure your site is set up for advertising. Once you've completed the setup checklist, connect your Meta assets (Facebook page, etc.) and then start building out your campaign content.

### FAQs

Below are some frequently asked questions about creating a Facebook & Instagram Ads campaign with Wix.

### What setup is required before I launch a campaign?

Before you can run a Facebook & Instagram Ads campaign with Wix make sure you've completed the following steps:
- Publish your site
- Upgrade your site with a plan
- Connect a custom domain

### What steps are involved in creating a Facebook & Instagram Ads campaign?

You create your campaign from your Wix dashboard. The dashboard walks you through step-by-step instructions for each element of the campaign, including:
- Picking locations you want to advertise (cities, countries, etc.)
- Creating your ad content and uploading ad creative (image, video)
- Deciding on an ad budget that works for your business
- Launching your campaign

### Do I have to connect my existing Meta Ads account?

No. When you create Facebook & Instagram Ads with Wix campaign and purchase a subscription, Wix creates a new Meta Ads account for you. If you already have an existing Meta Ads account, that account is separate from this one.

### How much does it cost to run a campaign?

Facebook & Instagram Ads with Wix is paid through a monthly subscription. When you set your campaign budget (daily spend on ads) you can see how much the monthly subscription amounts to. [Learn more about your monthly subscription and budget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-instagram-ads-faqs-about-the-campaign-budget-and-subscription).

### Can I connect an existing Meta Ads account to my Facebook & Instagram Ads with Wix campaign?

Currently, Facebook & Instagram Ads with Wix does not offer the option to connect an existing Meta Ads account with your campaign. Any campaigns and campaign data launched with Wix can only be managed through the Wix dashboard. 
All data from your personal Meta Ads account, in the Meta platform, or the Meta Ads interface can only be managed through Meta.
