---
title: "Google Search Console: Missing Field \"Description\" Error in Videos Tab"
---

Google Search Console is a handy tool for discovering errors on your website.

To review the status of the videos on your site, go to the Google Search Console Videos Tab.

You may see the following error - ** Missing field "description"**.  

To improve your site's Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Google requires you to add a description so that the Search Engine knows what the video is about. Learn how to [add information to your videos here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-information-to-your-videos).

Following these steps will help your site's ranking on Google search results.

**Example: **

The Videos report shows a Search Console report for a Videos>Missing field 'description' error. If error message is red, this issue requires attention.
