title: "Social Networks and SEO"
---

Never underestimate the power of Social Networks. Not only is social networking a great way to boost your site's visibility, but linking back to your site from social networks is a fantastic way to boost your page ranking.

However, as with the content you add to your site, make sure your posts are relevant and hold value. If they aren't, this can in fact set you back in terms of SEO.

**Wix offers several quick and easy ways to help boost your site on social networks. **Add various [social tools](https://support.wix.com/en/the-wix-editor/social) to your site allowing visitors to interact with their respective social networks. For example, you can allow a visitor to 'like' your site, thereby posting your site on their Facebook page.
