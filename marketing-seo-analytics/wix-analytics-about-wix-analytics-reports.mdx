### Viewing your reports

Wix Analytics reports include a broad range of editable pre-built reports to display your data.

To view your data in a specific format, click an icon displayed next to a report:

Table  displays your data in rows and columns.  Bar Chart  displays and compares your data.  Line Chart  shows how your data has changed over time.  Map  displays geographical data.  Pie Chart  displays your data in sections.  Heat Map  displays your data using intensity of color in grid format.  Funnel  displays your data in steps.    Combo Chart and Bar Chart Split  display multiple data series in one report.

### Understanding partial reports

Wix provides you with tools to build [GDPR compliant websites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/preparing-your-wix-site-for-the-gdpr). If your sites have Wix's cookie banner enabled, Analytics does not report visitor data before visitors actively consent to analytics cookies to be placed on their device. In such cases, Traffic and Behavior reports will be partial.

### Customizing your reports

You can view your reports in different ways and customize them to present the data according to your preferences. [Learn more about customizing your reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-wix-analytics-reports).

> **Important:**
>
> To create a new report or duplicate and edit a report, you must upgrade your Wix site to a [Business Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) (for Wix Editor users) or an [Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) (for Studio users).
