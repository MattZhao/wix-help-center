---
title: "Google Search Console: 403 Error"
---

If you have password protected a page on your site, or set a page as member-only, Google will get a 403 response (forbidden) and log it under Crawl Errors.

If you receive a 403 error message for a URL you should review the list of your site pages to check that no pages were restricted by mistake with passwords or with member-only restrictions.
