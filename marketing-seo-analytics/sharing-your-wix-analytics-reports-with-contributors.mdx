---
title: "Sharing Your Wix Analytics Reports with Contributors"
---

Sharing your Wix Analytics reports is a great way to keep your team informed about your site's performance, tailored to the metrics that matter most to you.

By default, pre-built Analytics reports are visible to all [site collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview) with permission to view Analytics data. [Newly created or duplicated reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-new-wix-analytics-report) are only visible to the creator and site owner, unless they are shared with others.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Sharing reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sharing-your-wix-analytics-reports-with-contributors#sharing-reports)  [Making reports private](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sharing-your-wix-analytics-reports-with-contributors#making-reports-private)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sharing-your-wix-analytics-reports-with-contributors#faqs)

> The reports you created, as well as those you duplicated, appear under **Custom Reports **in the [All Reports](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fanalytics%2Freports%2F%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=All+Reports) page in your site's dashboard.

---

### Sharing reports

Share reports you have created with the relevant stakeholders. This will allow them to access important data and help you make informed decisions.

#### To share reports:

[Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Analytics%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the** ****More Actions** icon  next to the relevant report.  Select **Add Contributors**.  Click **Add Contributors** in the pop-up.

**Tip: **This will instantly make the report available to all collaborators with permission to view Analytics data.

---

### Making reports private

If it's not necessary for other people to view your reports, you can make them private. This means they will only be visible to you and site co-owners.

#### To make a report private:

[Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Analytics%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the** ****More Actions** icon  next to the relevant report.   Select **Make Private**.

### How do I know which roles can view site analytics?

You can check which permissions each role has in your site dashboard.
1. [Go to Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click** Manage Roles**.
3. Hover over the role you want to check and click **View **/ **Edit**.
4. Scroll down and click **Analytics**. If **View Site Analytics **is checked, that means they have permission.

[Learn more abo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview)[ut roles and permissions on your site.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview)

### Are customized reports available in my Premium / Studio plan?

Customized reports are available in the [Business Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) (for Wix Editor users) or the [Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) (for Studio users).

### Can I share a report view that I created?

No, report views are only visible to yourself. [Learn more about customizing report views](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-wix-analytics-reports).
