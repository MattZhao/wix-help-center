---
title: "Facebook Ads Request: Hiding Your Product or Service Prices in Your Ad"
---

Currently in Facebook Ads, if you're using [dynamic ads](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-picking-an-ad-format-for-your-campaign#dynamic-ads) for your campaign, the prices of your products or services are displayed on the ad. It is not possible to hide the prices.

If you want to take full control over your ad's creative including, its image and messaging, create a [classic ad](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-picking-an-ad-format-for-your-campaign#classic-ads).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
