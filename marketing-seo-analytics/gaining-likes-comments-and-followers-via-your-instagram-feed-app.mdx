---
title: "Gaining Likes, Comments and Followers via Your Instagram Feed App"
---

Your site visitors can give your images likes and comments or become followers.

After clicking an image, a pop-up with the image appears. Visitors can click the pop-up image or username to go to Instagram. There, they can like or comment on the image or follow you.
