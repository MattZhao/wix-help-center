---
title: "Request: Using AI for SEO Settings"
---

We are excited to announce that can now use AI Text Creator to write your pages' title tags and meta descriptions. You can either choose a focus keyword or get AI text suggestions without adding a specific keyword.

The **Create AI text** option is available in both the SEO panels of your installed Wix apps and the SEO Settings "Edit by Page" tabs.

[Learn how to use AI Text Creator for your SEO settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-seo-tags-using-ai).
