---
title: "Google Ads with Wix Request: Running Image Campaigns"
---

Currently in Google Ads with Wix you cannot create and run image campaigns for your ads. You can only create [text ads](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-ads-with-wix-creating-a-campaign) that include a headline and description.

If you have Wix Stores you can also create a [Google Ads Performance Max Shopping campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-ads-with-wix-about-google-ads-performance-max-shopping-campaigns) that includes product images.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
