---
title: "Request: Changing the \"Priority\" and \"Frequency\" in a Sitemap"
---

Currently it is not possible to change the **"Priority"** and **"Frequency" **info submitted with a sitemap.

Learn more about [your site's sitemap file as well as the types of sitemaps that Wix supports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-your-sites-sitemap-file).

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
