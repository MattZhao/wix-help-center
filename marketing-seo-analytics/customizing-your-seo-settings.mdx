title: "Customizing Your SEO Settings"
---

Your SEO settings are your go-to place to save time by applying the same SEO logic to many pages at the same time. Instead of editing each page's individual settings, you can customize the settings that apply to entire sections of your site. This way, you can create a systematic SEO strategy much more quickly.

Wix automatically creates default SEO settings for all the pages of your site according to their type (e.g. Products, Main Pages, etc). These settings are based on common SEO best practices and include basic meta tags and other advanced settings that you can customize to your needs.

### Accessing your SEO settings

You can customize your SEO settings by accessing them in your site's SEO Dashboard.

#### To access your settings:

[Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Tools and settings**.  Click **Go to SEO Settings** under **SEO Settings**.  Select the page type that you want to edit. 

**Note:** You will only see options for the pages that are part of your site. For example, if you [added Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) to your site, you'll see an option to customize settings for **Products**.

Click **Edit **next to the relevant SEO settings to view or edit them.

### Customizing your SEO Basics settings

You can customize the title tag and meta description of your pages and let search engines index them in the SEO Basics section. Use static text to apply the same title and description to every page, or [use variables](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-variables-in-seo-settings) to create rules with different values for each section or page of your site.

#### To edit your SEO basics settings:

[Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Tools and settings**.  Click **Go to SEO Settings** under **SEO Settings**.  Select the page type that you want to edit. 

**Note:** You will only see options for the pages that are part of your site. For example, if you [added Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) to your site, you'll see an option to customize settings for **Products**.  Click **Edit **next to **Basics & social share**.  Customize the SEO Basics settings:

### Title tag

This is the title of your page(s) in search results. By default, Wix adds variables to this field to automatically create a title tag for each page. For example, with your Main Pages, Wix adds the variables "Page Name" and "Site Name" for the title tags. 
Click the **Title tag** field to edit existing variables, add new ones, or enter static text.

### Meta description

This is the description of the page(s) that appears in search results below the URL and title. By default, Wix does not add any variables to the meta description field. This is because search engines like Google often use a page's content to create a meta description that relates to a user's specific search query.
You can [add variables to this field](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-variables-in-seo-settings) to create a rule that applies to all of your pages. For example, for Wix Stores product pages, you could add the "Product Description" variable.
Click the **Meta description **field to enter static text. You can also click **+ Add Variable **to use  dynamic values in your meta description. 
**Note:** Some SEO audit tools may detect an empty field for the meta description. This is not an issue. Search engines like Google will create a meta description for your page.

### Let search engines index your page(s)

Enable the toggle to allow search engines to [crawl and index](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/beginner/how-search-works#crawling) the page(s) so they can appear in search results. This applies to all pages within the same page type (e.g. all Products pages or all Booking Services pages). 
If you disable the toggle, search engines will not be able to crawl and index the pages. This means people will not be able to find the pages in search engines like Google.

7. Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> Many page types show a **Page URL **field to show the URL slug structure of the pages. Hover over the **Info **icon  to learn more about the specific page type's URL.

### Customizing your social share settings

Customize the way your pages appear on social networks like Facebook, Instagram, and X (Twitter). You can edit the [Social share settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-social-share-settings-for-your-sites-pages) to use the same rules for the title, description, and image that appear when someone shares your page. You can also use separate [X (Twitter) settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-twitter-settings-for-your-sites-pages) to display a different title, description, and image for pages that you share on X (Twitter).

#### To customize your social share settings:

[Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Tools and settings**.  Click **Go to SEO Settings** under **SEO Settings**.  Select the page type that you want to edit. 

**Note:** You will only see options for the pages that are part of your site. For example, if you [added Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) to your site, you'll see an option to customize settings for **Products**.  Click **Edit **next to **Basics & social share**.  Scroll down to **Social share** and customize the relevant settings:

### Social share

You can use static text to apply the same title and description to every page, or use [variables](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-variables-in-seo-settings) to create rules to use different values for each section or page of your site.
By default, the title tag and meta description from your **SEO Basics** section are used in this section as variables: 
- **og:title:** The **Title tag** from the **SEO Basics** section.
- **og:description:** The **Meta description** from the **SEO Basics** section.

1. Customize the following settings:

- **og:title:** The title of the post when users share your page on social media. It can be the same as your page title tag.
- **og:description: **The description (caption) of the post that displays under the **og:title** when you share on social networks. Use variables to show specific information about each page.
- **og:image: **The image that displays next to the title and description when shared on social networks. Use variables to show a different **og:image** on each page. If you prefer one image for all your pages, add an image URL.

**Tip: **Click **Get an Image URL from Site Files **to copy the URL of one of your site's media files. Then, hover over an image, click the **More Actions** icon  and select **Copy URL**.
2. Click **Save**.

### X (Twitter)

Use static text to apply the same title and description to every page, or use [variables](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-variables-in-seo-settings) to create rules to use different values for each section or page of your site.
By default, the title tag and meta description from your **Social share** section are used in this section as variables: 
- **x:title:** The **og:title** from the **Social share** section.
- **x:description: **The **og:description** from the **Social share** section.

1. Customize the following settings:

- **Select card size:** Choose the card size displayed when sharing on X: **Large** or **Small**.
- **x:title:** The title displayed when shared on X. It can be the same a your title for other social networks.
- **x:description: **The description displays below the **x:title** on X. It tells people more about your site. Use variables to show specific info about each page.
- **x:image:** The image that shows on X. Use an image variable if you want each page to have a different image. If you prefer one image for all your pages, add an image URL.

**Tip: **Click **Get an Image URL from Site Files **to copy the URL of one of your site's media files. Then, hover over an image, click the **More Actions** icon  and select **Copy URL**.
2. Click **Save**.

### Customizing URL structures for Products and Blog Posts pages

If you have Wix Stores or Wix Blog on your site, you can customize the URL structures of your Products and Posts pages. This helps search engines better understand the content. Learn more about [customizing your page's URL structure](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-pages-url-structure-in-seo-settings).

> **Tip:**
>
> Search engines recommend using a simple structure that is easily understandable to your site visitors. Learn more about [URL slug structure best practices from Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/url-structure).

#### To customize URL structures for a Products or Blog Posts page:

[Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard. Click **Edit **next to **Page URL**.  Click the **URL structure **field and edit the URL. Click **+ Add Variable** to add a dynamic value to the URL structure.

**Note: **Your URL structure must include at least one variable.

Choose if you want Wix to create automatic redirects for your pages.

- Leave the **Automatically redirect all traffic **checkbox selected to automatically redirect people and search engines to the new URL if they visit the old URL.
- Deselect the **Automatically redirect all traffic **checkbox if you want to manually [add a group redirect in the URL Redirect Manger](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-group-redirect-301) after saving.
    Click **Save**.  (Optional) Click **View all in CSV format** to download a file with all your URL redirects.   Click **Confirm Changes**.

### Customizing your structured data markup

Structured data markup gives search engines detailed information about your page. Adding structured data markup to your site's page can help improve your website's search results and ranking. Search engines like Google can also use markup to [create rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/search-gallery) like article previews, product previews, and FAQs for your page's search results. Learn more about [adding structured data markup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-structured-data-markup-to-your-sites-pages-2546962) to your site's pages.

> **Tip:**
>
> When you add a business name and location to your site, Wix automatically creates a [Local Business markup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-local-business-markup-for-your-sites-homepage) for your homepage to improve your search ranking.

> **Important:**
>
> - You can have up to 5 markups per SEO Setting (including the preset markup).
> - Structured data markup is an advanced topic. Wix Customer Care cannot help with any changes to your site's structured data. If you have problems with structured data for your site, check out our [structured data troubleshooting help article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-your-structured-data-markup).
> - Need help from an SEO expert? You can [hire an SEO professional](https://www.wix.com/marketplace/hire/marketing-promoting/seo-services) from the Wix Marketplace.

#### To edit your structured data markup:

[Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Tools and settings**.  Click **Go to SEO Settings** under **SEO Settings**.  Select the relevant page type under **Edit by page type**.  Click **Edit **next to **Structured data markup**.

**Note: **If you selected **Main Pages **in step 4 above, click **Edit by Page **at the top and click the relevant page. Then click the **Advanced **tab in the panel on the right and click **Structured data markup**.  Choose what you want to do:

### Preview, edit or switch a preset markup

Some of your site's pages already come with [preset markup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-preset-markups-for-your-sites-pages) when you create them. These are Wix Stores product pages, Wix Blog posts, Wix Events pages, and dynamic item pages. 
To preview or switch a preset markup: 
1. Hover over the relevant prest markup and click the relevant option:

- **Preview Preset: **Preview the dynamic code used in the markup.
- **Switch Preset: **Click the **Switch markup type** drop-down and select another preset to change the preset used for your page.
2. (Optional) Click **Convert to custom markup** to duplicate the markup and make changes to the copy. This automatically excludes the preset markup. Click the new markup to edit it.
3. Click **Done **or **Apply**.

### Include or exclude a preset markup

Hover over the relevant preset markup and click the **Exclude/Include** icon  to exclude or include the markup.

### Create a new custom markup

1. Click **+ Add New Markup**.
2. Enter a name for the markup.
3. Write your markup in JSON-LD format in the provided field. Click **+ Add Variable **to add dynamic values.

**Tip: **If you need help writing your custom markup, we recommend generating a markup with [this 3rd party tool](https://technicalseo.com/tools/schema-markup-generator/).
4. Click **Apply**.

### Edit or remove a custom markup

Hover over the relevant custom markup and choose an option:
- **Edit: **Modify the markup name and code then click **Apply**.
- **Remove: **Click the **Remove **icon  to delete the custom markup.

### Customizing your additional meta tags

Meta tags are snippets of code that describe your page to search engines and social networks. They are not visible on your site but they do exist in the page code. By default, Wix adds [meta tags](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-your-sites-default-seo-settings-6447365) to your pages based on common SEO best practices.

#### To customize additional meta tags:

[Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Tools and settings**.  Click **Go to SEO Settings** under **SEO Settings**.  Select the relevant page type under **Edit by page type**.  Click **Additional meta tags**.  Edit or add a meta tag:

- **Edit: **Click the relevant meta tag field to edit the tag. Click **+ Add Variable **to add dynamic values to your meta tags. A preview of displays below the field to show how it appears in your site's code.
- **Add: **Click **+ Add New Tag **at the bottom and write a new tag in HTML format. Click **+ Add Variable **to add dynamic values to your meta tags. When finished, click the **Apply **icon .

> **When adding new tags:**
>
> - You can add up to 10 tags, but can't add a meta tag that [already exists](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-your-pages-default-seo-settings).
> - You can only use the following tag types: \<meta> and \<link>
> - If you want to add a tag that is not supported you can use the [Custom Code section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-custom-code-on-your-site).
> - The new tags are visible in your editor's [SEO page panels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-pages-seo-settings-in-the-seo-panel).

Click **Save**.

#### Click below to learn more about:

### Default meta tags for all pages

Wix automatically adds the following tags when you create a page:
- **canonical** [The primary URL for the page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-canonical-tags-for-your-sites-pages) when there are multiple versions.
- **og:type **The type of object (e.g. website, product, video file).
- **og:url **Your page's web address.
- **og:site_name **The name of your site.

**Note: **Social networks like Facebook read your site's og tags to get information to display about your site.

### Additional default meta tags for Wix Blog and Wix Forum posts

In addition to the default tags that are added to all pages, blog and forum posts also have these default tags:
- **article:author **The author of the post.
- **article:modified_time **The last time that the post was edited.
- **article:published_time **The time that the post was first published.

### Additional default meta tags for Wix Stores product pages

In addition to the default tags that are added to all pages, product pages also have these default tags:
- **Product:availability** Indicates if the product is currently in stock.
- **Product:price:amount **The product's price (e.g. 40)
- **Product:price:currency** The currency of your product price (e.g. USD)

### Resetting your default SEO settings

Your default SEO settings are based on SEO best practices. You can restore the default settings if you made changes and want to revert back.

#### To restore your default SEO settings:

[Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Tools and settings**.  Click **Go to SEO Settings** under **SEO Settings**.  Select the relevant page type under **Edit by page type**.  Click the setting that includes the default(s) you want to restore.   Choose what you want to do:

### Reset a single field to its default

1. Click the relevant field.
2. Delete all the content in the field.
3. Click **Save**.

### Reset the entire section to its default

1. Click **Edit** beside the section you want to reset.
2. Click the **Reset to Wix Default **icon  at the top of the section.
3. Click **Save**.

### Bulk editing the SEO settings of individual pages

You can change the indexable statuses and restore [default SEO settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-your-sites-default-seo-settings-6447365) for several main pages or blog posts at once.

> **Important:**
>
> - Bulk editing is currently available for your site's main pages and blog posts only. We're working on adding more page types soon.
> - Several factors affect how long it takes search engines to recrawl your pages and get the updated SEO details. After updating your SEO, you can try and speed up the recrawling process by manually [submitting the page URLs to search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/submitting-your-sitemap-and-urls-directly-to-search-engines#submitting-individual-urls-to-search-engines).

#### To bulk edit SEO settings for your pages:

[Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Tools and settings**.  Click **Go to SEO Settings** under **SEO Settings**.  Select the relevant page type under **Edit by page type**.  Select the **Edit by Page **tab at the top.

**Note: **The tab name depends on the type of page (e.g. **Edit by Product**, **Edit by Post**).  Click the checkboxes next to the relevant pages.

**Tip: **Click the checkbox next to **Page name** at the top of the list to select all the pages.

Choose what you want to do:

### Set indexable status

Pages must be indexable for search engines to crawl and index them for displaying in search results. Change the indexable status for all the selected pages at once. 
Click the **Set indexable status **drop-down at the top of the list and choose an option:
- **Allow indexing: **Let search engines crawl your pages to display them in search results.
- **Block indexing: **Add a **noindex** tag to all selected pages, which means they will not show up in search results. Click **Block Indexing **to confirm.
[image: A screenshot of setting the index status of a group of pages]
**Note: **If you don't see the option to change the indexable status of your pages, make sure that [search engines can index your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/enabling-search-engines-like-google-to-index-your-site).

### Reset to default

Restore multiple pages back to their default SEO settings. This publishes your updated SEO fields on all the selected pages and cannot be undone. 

1. Click the **Reset to default** drop-down at the top of the list.
2. Select the SEO setting(s) you want to restore to their default settings.
3. Click **Reset**.
4. Click **Reset & Publish **to confirm.
