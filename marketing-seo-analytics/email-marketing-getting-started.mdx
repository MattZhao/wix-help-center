title: "Email Marketing: Getting Started"
---

Use Wix Email Marketing to create, send and manage professional email marketing campaigns. Start by picking a stunning template, then add your content, contacts and send.

All of your emails are fully customizable and exist as standalone landing pages. Plus, Wix Email Marketing makes it easy to [keep track of your statistics and performance](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-your-email-marketing-statistics).

> **Ready to give it a go?**
>
> - View a [step-by-step tutorial for creating and sending campaigns](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-and-sending-a-campaign-from-start-to-finish).
> - [Start your first email campaign](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/shoutout/).
> - Grow your contact list with a [subscribe form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-subscribe-form).
