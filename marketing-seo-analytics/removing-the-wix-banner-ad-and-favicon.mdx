---
title: "Removing the Wix Banner Ad and Favicon"
---

Wix gives you the tools you need to create a stunning website for free. Your free site includes a Wix banner ad at the top and has a Wix favicon in the browser tab.

To remove the Wix banner ad from your site and use your own favicon, you need to [upgrade to a Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) that includes these features.

Click to upgrade your site now.

        [
        Upgrade Now
      ](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter)

> **Tip:**
>
> The **Proudly Created With Wix** text, which appears in the footer inside your Editor, can be edited at any time. You do not need to purchase a Premium plan to edit it. Simply double click the text box to change what it says.
