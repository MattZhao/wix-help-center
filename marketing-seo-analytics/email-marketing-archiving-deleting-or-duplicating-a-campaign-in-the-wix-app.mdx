---
title: "Email Marketing: Archiving, Deleting or Duplicating a Campaign in the Wix App"
---

If you no longer need an email campaign you can delete it or archive it from the Wix app. If you want to edit a campaign you already sent, you can duplicate it and create a new campaign using the same layout.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Archive a campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-archiving-deleting-or-duplicating-a-campaign-in-the-wix-owner-app#archiving-an-email-campaign-in-the-wix-app)  [Delete an archived campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-archiving-deleting-or-duplicating-a-campaign-in-the-wix-owner-app#deleting-an-archived-campaign-in-the-wix-app)  [Duplicate a campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-archiving-deleting-or-duplicating-a-campaign-in-the-wix-owner-app#duplicating-a-campaign-in-the-wix-app)

---

### Archiving an email campaign in the Wix App

Archive old email campaigns that you don't want to see in your dashboard but aren't ready to delete. When a campaign is archived, it can be brought out of archive again if you want to duplicate it or resend it.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [email marketing dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-accessing-the-email-marketing-dashboard-in-the-wix-owner-app) in the Wix app.
2. Tap the email campaign you want to archive (in the Published or Drafts tabs).
3. Tap **Archive**.

---

### Deleting an archived campaign in the Wix App

Delete an email campaign if you no longer need it. You can only delete a campaign after you've archived it. Once you delete the campaign, you will not be able to view it again.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [email marketing dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-accessing-the-email-marketing-dashboard-in-the-wix-owner-app) in the Wix app.
2. Tap the **Archived** tab.
3. Tap the email campaign you want to delete.
4. Tap **Delete Permanently**.

> **Note:**
>
> Archiving an email campaign does not delete it. You can still access it through the **Archived** tab in your Email Marketing dashboard.

---

### Duplicating a campaign in the Wix App

Duplicate a published email campaign or one of your drafts if you want to create a similar campaign. You can tweak it as needed or send the duplicate campaign as is.

> **Note:**
>
> You can only duplicate email campaigns in the Wix app that were originally created on mobile.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [email marketing dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-accessing-the-email-marketing-dashboard-in-the-wix-owner-app) in the Wix app.
2. Tap the email campaign you want to duplicate (in the Published or Drafts tabs).
3. Tap **Duplicate**.
4. Edit your email campaign using the available options. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-editing-campaign-elements-in-the-wix-owner-app)
