---
title: "Email Marketing: Archiving Campaigns"
---

You can archive your old email campaigns if you no longer need them, and bring them out of the archive if you ever need them in the future.

### To archive an email campaign:

Go to [Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage **next to **Most recent email campaigns**.

Hover over the relevant campaign and click the More Actions icon  on the right.   Click **Archive**.

> **Need to bring a campaign back from archive?**
>
> 1. Click the **Archived **tab at the top.
> 2. Hover over the relevant campaign and click the More Actions icon  on the right.
> 3. Click **Move to active**.
