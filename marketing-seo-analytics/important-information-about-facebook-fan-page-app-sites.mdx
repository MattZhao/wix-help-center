---
title: "Important Information About Facebook Fan Page App Sites"
---

At the beginning of 2021, Adobe [ended support for Flash technology](https://www.adobe.com/il_en/products/flashplayer/end-of-life.html). This affects all websites built with Flash, including any Facebook Fan Page app sites that you created on Wix. Any sites built using the Facebook Fan Pages app have been deleted, and visitors aren't able to access them anymore.

**Click an option below to learn more about this change and what it means for your Facebook Fan Page site. **

### Can I bring my Facebook Fan Page site back online?

The sites have been removed from Wix. This means that they have been completely deleted, and cannot be restored.
You can build a new site with the Wix Editor with better performance for your visitors and plenty of design features, layout options and business apps.
Depending on your needs, there are several options to choose from:
- [Create a new site using a pre-designed template](http://www.wix.com/website/templates)
- [Create a new site from scratch](http://www.wix.com/website/templates/html/blank/1)

### Can I use the Facebook Fan Page Editor?

No. Since the beginning of 2021,** **you are no longer able to access the Facebook Fan Page Editor at all, including the dashboard of Facebook Fan Page sites.

### Do visitors still have access to my Facebook Fan Page site?

Facebook Fan Page sites were disabled on all browsers. This means that visitors cannot view the Facebook Fan Page site that you created with Wix.

### Can I still see files from my Facebook Fan Page site in the Media Manager?

No. At the beginning of 2021, your site's files were removed from Wix. 
**Tip:** After creating a new Wix site, you can easily upload all of your files in one go. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-uploading-media-to-the-media-manager)
