---
title: "Facebook Ads Request: Using Product Categories"
---

While [Facebook Ads](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-an-overview) are dynamic and can use a different product from your categories each time your ad is displayed, you cannot currently promote an entire product categories in a single Facebook ad. You can also choose specific products and take control of the ad's creative (caption, headline, etc.) with [classic ads](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-picking-an-ad-format-for-your-campaign#classic-ads).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
