Earn $500 USD (or the currency equivalent) in ad credit directly from Google to help run your [Google Ads with Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-ads-with-wix-an-overview) campaign. With Google Ads, you can create simple, effective ads and display them to people who are searching online for information related to your business.

> This Google Ads offer replaces the Google Ad voucher that used to be available with new Premium plans.

### Eligibility for Google Ads credit

If you are eligible for this ad credit, Google applies it directly to your available ad credits and you will receive a notification via email as well as in your site's dashboard. [Read more about Google's terms and conditions](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/1396257).

#### In order to be eligible for the ad credit offer:

You must be new to Google advertising, with no previous Google Ads campaigns related to your site.  You must spend $500 USD (or your currency equivalent, see below) on ad clicks within the first 60 days of your campaign:

- **USD:** Spend 500 USD within the first 60 days to receive 500 USD ad credit.
- **GBP:** Spend 400 GBP within the first 60 days to receive 400 GBP ad credit.
- **EUR:** Spend 400 EUR within the first 60 days to receive 400 EUR ad credit.
- **JPY: **Spend 60,000 JPY within the first 60 days to receive 60,000 JPY ad credit.

> **Note:**
>
> All other currencies which are converted to USD need to spend $500 USD to receive the $500  ad credit.

> **Tip:**
>
> Since your ad budget is spent on clicks, you have a better chance of being eligible if you set your monthly subscription to at least $300 (around 10 USD per day).

You can track your eligibility from your Google Ads dashboard in **Ad credit promo**.

### FAQs

Click on a question below to learn more about the Google Ads credit offer for your Wix site.

### How is the ad credit applied to my account?

After you have spent $500 USD within the first 60 days, Google automatically applies the amount to your available ad credits. You will receive an email about the new credit, as well as a dashboard notification.

### Does turning off auto-renewal of my subscription affect the credit?

No, if you turn off auto-renewal your Google Ads credit is not affected. For example, if you purchase a $1K subscription and then turn off auto-renewal, you can still receive the credit as long as you spend $500 within the first 60 days.

### Why am I not seeing the credit immediately?

Purchasing a Google Ads with Wix subscription does not apply the credit immediately. The promotional credit is applied to your account after you spend $500 USD on ad clicks. Then it is applied to your account.
**Note:** Since the credit comes directly from Google, it may take a few days to see the credit applied to your account after you have fulfilled the requirements.

### Does the ad credit expire?

Yes. You will lose any remaining ad credit in your account if you don't use it within 60 days.
