---

Make changes to a [scheduled email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign#scheduling-an-email-marketing-campaign) campaign at any time. Pause it from being delivered or reschedule it to be sent to your recipients at a different time. If you no longer wish to send your email, you can delete it too.

**In this article, learn more about: **

[Managing your scheduled email campaigns](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-scheduled-email-marketing-campaign#managing-your-scheduled-email-campaign)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-scheduled-email-marketing-campaign#faqs)

> **Important:**
>
> Creating a scheduled campaign uses one of your campaigns from your monthly quota.

### Managing your scheduled email campaign

Manage your scheduled email campaigns from the Email Marketing tab in your site's dashboard.

#### To manage your scheduled email campaign:

[Go to Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage**.  Click the **Scheduled** tab.   Find the scheduled email campaign and manage it by doing one of the following:

### Pause your scheduled campaign

1. Click the **More Actions **icon** ** next to the relevant email campaign.
2. Click **Pause **.
3. Click **Pause** in the popup to confirm.

**Note:** Pausing a scheduled email campaign prevents it from being sent until you choose to [send](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign) or [schedule it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign#scheduling-an-email-marketing-campaign) again.

### Edit your scheduled campaign

1. Click **Edit** next to the relevant scheduled email campaign.
2. [Edit your email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-customizing-your-email-campaign) using the available options.
3. When ready, click **Next** to [send your email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign) or [schedule it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign#scheduling-an-email-marketing-campaign) again.

### Reschedule your campaign

1. Click the** Reschedule **icon  next to the relevant scheduled email campaign.
2. Select the date you want using the calendar.
3. Set the time in the drop-down.
4. Click **Reschedule**.

### Delete your campaign

1. Click the **More Actions** icon** ** next to the relevant email campaign.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Click **Delete**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about managing your email marketing campaigns.

### If I add new contacts after scheduling a campaign, will they receive it as well?

If you add new contacts (members, subscribers, etc.) to your contact list after you've already scheduled a campaign, those new contacts are not automatically added to the campaign. You need to edit the campaign to add new recipients and then reschedule.

### If I delete or pause an email campaign, will that increase my available quota?

If you delete or pause scheduled email campaign, they will not be returned to your email quota.

### If I change my default sender details, will it affect scheduled campaigns?

Yes. If changes were made to your default sender details before a scheduled campaign is sent, the scheduled campaign will inherit those updated details. It will not keep the sender details set before the change.
