Easily move from another email marketing platform to Wix Email Marketing and get to know all the great features it has to offer.

**In this article, learn more about:**

### Importing your mailing list

The first step is to import your mailing list to your Wix account. To do so you should export your mailing list in a CSV format from the platform you're using. You can find instructions for some common platforms below:

[Constant Contact](https://knowledgebase.constantcontact.com/articles/KnowledgeBase/33114-Export-Contacts-Out-of-Constant-Contact-and-into-a-Spreadsheet)  [Aweber](https://help.aweber.com/hc/en-us/articles/204028816-How-Do-I-Export-My-Subscribers-)  [Campaign Monitor](http://help.campaignmonitor.com/topic.aspx?t=146)  [Get Response](https://www.getresponse.com/help/how-can-i-export-contacts.html)

Next, [import your mailing list to your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-importing-your-contacts-into-your-contact-list) so that you can send email campaigns to your subscribers. Make sure to select the **Mark as subscribers** checkbox when importing.

> **Important:**
>
> Only set contacts as Subscribers if they've given you permission to do so. Learn more about [Wix Email Marketing best practices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-email-marketing-best-practices).

---

### Growing your mailing list

Grow your mailing list and gain new subscribers by [adding a Get Subscribers form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-subscribe-form) to your site. You can [customize the design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-designing-your-site-forms) of the form to match your brand and style.

Anyone who signs up using the form is automatically added as a Subscriber. To access your list of Subscribers, you can [filter your contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Contacts%20&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/contacts).

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [changing the subscription status of your contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-subscription-status-of-a-contact).

---

### Setting up automatic responses with Wix Automations

If you set up automatic responses with your previous platform - you can do the same using Wix Automations.

For example, you can [set up an automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation) which automatically sends visitors a Thank You email after they submit a contact form, subscribe to your newsletter, or purchase from your store.

> **Note:**
>
> Emails sent using Wix Automations use Wix Email Marketing but do not count towards your monthly campaign quota.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [Wix Email Marketing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-getting-started).
