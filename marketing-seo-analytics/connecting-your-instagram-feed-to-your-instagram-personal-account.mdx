### Connecting your Instagram personal account

Add the Instagram Feed app to your site from the Wix App Market and connect it to your personal account.

> **Have more than one Instagram account?**
>
> Make sure you log into the account you want to display on your site before connecting the account.

### Connecting to a specific feed

If you have multiple feeds on your Instagram account, you need to choose a feed to display on your site. You cannot display multiple feeds using this version of the app. However, you can display multiple feeds using the [new Instagram Feed app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-instagram-feed-app).

> **Note:**
>
> This will only work if you've previously connected your Instagram to your Wix site.

#### To connect a specific feed:

Click the Instagram app in your Wix editor.  Click** Settings**.  Click** Connect Personal Account**.  Click** Allow** on the popup.  Publish your site.  Go to your Instagram account.  Log out of Instagram to refresh the connection.  Log back into your Instagram account.  Go back to your Wix editor.  Click the Instagram Feed app.  Click** Settings**.  Click the** Select feed **drop-down and select the feed you want to display.**

Note:** This option is only available if you have multiple Instagram feeds linked to your account.  Click** Continue **on the popup.  Publish your site.

### Disconnecting your Instagram personal account

Disconnect your Instagram personal account to connect a different account.

#### To disconnect your account:

Select the Instagram Feed app in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Disconnect **at the bottom of the panel.

### Troubleshooting Instagram personal account issues

If you are experiencing issues connecting the Instagram Feed app for your Instagram personal account, try the steps below:

### Step 1 | Make sure that you're signed in to the correct Instagram account

If you're not seeing the correct content in your Instagram Feed, go to [your Instagram account ](https://www.instagram.com/)and make sure that you're signed into the correct account. 
If you're not signed in to the correct account, follow the steps below. 
1. Sign out of the incorrect account.
2. Sign in to the correct account.
3. Go back to your Editor.
4. Select the Instagram Feed app.
5. Click **Settings**.
6. Click **Disconnect **at the bottom of the panel.
7. Click **Connect your personal account**.
8. Click **Allow**.

### Step 2 | Reconnect your Instagram account if it was disconnected

Your account may get disconnected if there is a connection issue with Instagram, or if you recently changed your Instagram password. Follow the steps below to reconnect your account.
1. Select the Instagram Feed app in your editor.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Connect Instagram**.

### Step 3 | Make sure that off-Facebook activity is enabled in your Facebook account

Follow the steps below if you get an error message that says "Your Facebook account's off-Facebook activity is switched off". 
1. Go to your Facebook account.
2. Click your profile account icon at the top right.
3. Select **Settings & privacy**.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click **Your Facebook information **on the left.
6. Click **View **next to **Off-Facebook activity**.
7. Click **Disconnect future activity. 

**[image: A screenshot showing the Disconnect future activity setting in Facebook.]
8. Enable the **Future off-Facebook activity** toggle if it isn't enabled to allow Facebook to get information from Instagram.
