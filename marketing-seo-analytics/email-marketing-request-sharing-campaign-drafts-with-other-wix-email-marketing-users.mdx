---
title: "Email Marketing Request: Sharing Campaign Drafts with Other Wix Email Marketing Users"
---

Currently, it is not possible to share email campaign drafts with other Wix Email Marketing users, or transfer draft newsletters to other Wix accounts.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
