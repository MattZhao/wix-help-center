---
title: "Discrepancy in Wix Email Marketing Statistics"
---

It may take some time for recipients to read your email campaign, and for the information in your Wix Email Marketing Dashboard and statistics to update accordingly.

If you [view your statistics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-your-email-campaign-statistics) more than 24 hours after sending an email campaign and your statistics don't seem accurate, please submit a support ticket and include the following information:

The URL of the email campaignThe date you sent the newsletterThe number of contacts you sent toThe names of contact groups you sent toA few examples of email addresses you've sent toDescribe what is inaccurate about your statistics
