### Understanding the review process

After you create an ad campaign, the status label at the top changes to 'In Review'. Campaigns must be approved by Facebook before they can go live. In the review process Facebook checks the campaign's content and audience and makes sure everything complies with their [advertising policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/ads/).

Facebook typically reviews ads within 72 hours, but in some cases it may take longer. When your campaign is approved, the status at the top of your dashboard changes to 'Live'.

> **Important:**
>
> If you decide to edit your campaign at any point after it goes live, it is automatically submitted to Facebook for another review.

---

### Resubmitting a campaign if it's rejected

If your campaign is rejected by Facebook, it is most likely because it didn't comply with their advertising policies. Common reasons campaigns get rejected include:

Adult content  Alcoholic beverages  Dating services  Election or political content

To learn more about Facebook Ads violations that result in campaign rejection click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-campaigns-rejected-by-facebook).

If your campaign was rejected, an error message in the Facebook Ads dashboard will tell you to check specific issues that may have caused the rejection. Once you've fixed the issue, resubmit your campaign for approval.

### Show me how to resubmit my campaign

1. Go to [Facebook Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Facebook%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpaid-ads%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Edit & Resubmit** in the error message.
3. Make the necessary changes to your ad content as explained in the notification (remove certain products, change the text, etc.).
4. Click **Submit Changes** at the top right.
5. Click **Submit Changes** in the pop-up to confirm.
