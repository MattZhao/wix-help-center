---
title: "Facebook Ads Request: Switching the Facebook Page Connected to Your Site"
---

Currently in Facebook Ads, you cannot change the Facebook Business page you connected to the Facebook Ads dashboard.

However, it is possible for our team to switch the page for you. Please [contact us](https://www.wix.com/support/conversations/category/contact) and provide the following information:

Your Wix site URL  The URL of the new Facebook Business page that you want to connect

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
