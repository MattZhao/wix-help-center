### Benefits of creating a Google Ads with Wix campaign

When you create a Google Ads campaign with Wix, you get the opportunity to advertise on Google's search engine in addition to Wix's 24/7 customer support. You also get access to:

A guided, step-by-step setup process from your site's dashboard  Wix's integrated paid advertising solution  Content and keyword theme suggestions from Google Ads to help in ad creation  Seamless integration between Google Ads and your Wix site for the most accurate conversion tracking from ads to website.  If you've never advertised on Google Ads before, you'll be eligible for up to $500 USD in Google Ads credit.*

**Note:** Campaigns that spend $500 USD in the first 60 days are automatically eligible for this Google Ads offer.

*[Terms and conditions](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/1396257) apply

### How do I get started?

[Go to Google Ads](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{metaSiteId\}/google-ads?referralInfo=KB_Google_ads_overview) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Start Advertising**.   Complete the **Get ready to advertise on Google** checklist which includes:

- Publishing your site
- Purchasing a Premium plan that fits your business
- Connecting a custom domain
    Build your Google Ads campaign including customizing your ad settings, creating your ad content and setting a campaign budget.   Purchase a Google Ads with Wix subscription and launch your campaign.

> **Note:**
>
> A new Google Ads account is created for you when you launch a Google Ads campaign with Wix. Any existing Google Ads accounts you might have are separate from this account.

### FAQs

Below are some frequently asked questions about creating a Google Ads campaign with Wix.

### What setup is required before I launch a campaign?

Before you can run a Google Ads campaign with Wix make sure you've completed the following steps:
- Publish your site
- Upgrade to a Premium plan
- Connect a custom domain

### What are the steps involved in creating a Google Ads campaign?

You create your campaign from your Wix dashboard. The dashboard walks you through step-by-step instructions for each element of the campaign, including:
- Picking locations you want to advertise (cities, countries, etc.)
- Picking keyword themes for your ad targets
- Creating your ad content
- Deciding on an ad budget that works for your business
- Launching your campaign

### How much does it cost to run a Google Ads campaign with Wix?

Google Ads by Wix is paid through a monthly subscription or purchasing credits with a one time payment. When you set your campaign budget (daily spend on ads) you can see how much the monthly subscription amounts to.

### What is the difference between my monthly subscription and a one-time payment?

Your monthly subscription is the amount that is billed monthly for all the campaigns you're running with Google Ads with Wix. The monthly subscription amount is applied directly to your total ad credit each month (ad credit available across all your campaigns).
However, you might want to purchase additional ad credit for one or more campaigns running in your account. You can pay for these additional ad credits with a one-time payment. This doesn't affect your overall monthly subscription.
[Learn more about setting your Google Ads with Wix budget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/faqs-about-google-ads-with-wix-campaign-budget).

### Can I connect an existing Google Ads account to Google Ads with Wix?

Currently, Google Ads with Wix does not offer the option to connect an existing Google Ads account. Any campaigns and campaign data launched through Google Ads with Wix can only be managed through the Wix dashboard. 
If you already have an existing Google Ads account, that account is separate and all data can only be managed through Google. You cannot access any Google Ads with Wix data from Google's interface.
