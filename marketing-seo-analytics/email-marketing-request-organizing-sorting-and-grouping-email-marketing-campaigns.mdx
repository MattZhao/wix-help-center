title: "Email Marketing Request: Organizing, Sorting and Grouping Email Marketing Campaigns"
---

In the [Email Marketing dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-about-your-email-marketing-dashboard), your email campaigns are shown in chronological order. Currently, it is not possible to organize, sort or group your email campaigns in different ways.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
