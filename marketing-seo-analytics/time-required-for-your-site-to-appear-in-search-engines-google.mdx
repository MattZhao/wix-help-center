---
title: "Time Required For Your Site to Appear in Search Engines (Google)"
---

There is no guaranteed time frame in which your site will be indexed and appear in Google or other search engines.

Wix guarantees that all of the text and meta tags on your site are scanned by search engines, however, it is the quality of your website that determines if and how you will rank. Read [best practices for getting your site found on Google](https://support.wix.com/en/article/getting-your-site-found-on-google-seo-best-practices) for more tips.

Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/checking-if-your-site-is-indexed-by-search-engines) to check if your site has been indexed.

You can speed up the time that it takes for your site to appear in search engine results by verifying your site:

[Verify your site ownership with Google Search Console](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-your-site-to-google-in-the-wix-seo-setup-checklist)[Verify your site ownership with Bing Webmaster Tools](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-bing-webmaster-tools)

Once you have verified your site, you can submit your sitemap directly to Google via Google Search Console to make it easier for Google to crawl and categorize your site. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/submitting-your-sitemap-and-urls-directly-to-search-engines) to learn more.

> **Note:**
>
> The waiting period for your site to be indexed is determined by the search engines themselves and is the same for all websites (not just Wix sites).
