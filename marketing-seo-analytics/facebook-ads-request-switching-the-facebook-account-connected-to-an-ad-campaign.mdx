---
title: "Facebook Ads Request: Switching the Facebook Account Connected to an Ad Campaign"
---

Currently in Facebook Ads you cannot change the Facebook account you connected initially when first setting up a campaign.

In order to change the account you need to [contact Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact). Our team will review the issue and provide you with available options. It is possible for us to remove your connected account altogether, but this is not recommended.

> **Important:**
>
> - If we remove your connected account you will lose all data from any previous campaigns that launched with that account.
> - Once a Facebook account is removed from our system, we no longer have access to any information about the previous connection.

If you still want to remove your account and go through the setup process again, provide the following information when you contact Customer Care.

Your Wix site URL  Written approval that you understand the implications of us removing your connected Facebook account

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
