title: "Low Engagement Rate in Wix Email Marketing"
---

Email engagement refers to your recipients' behavior. Specifically, it can refer to the number of recipients who **open** an email campaign, or the number of recipients who **click on links** in your email campaigns.

### The importance of engagement rates

- A low engagement rate may mean that people are not reading your email campaigns.
- Low engagement rates can cause Internet Service Providers (ISPs) to restrict or even ban your email campaigns.
- Low engagement can be as harmful as being marked as Spam or having a high bounce rate.

### Possible reasons for low engagement rates

In many cases, low engagement results from one or more of the following: 
- Outdated contact list.
- Ineffective subject line or content.
- Poor timing.

### Tips for improving your engagement rates

- Send your email campaigns only to your [active contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-about-active-and-inactive-contacts) to increase your customers engagement and avoid compliance restrictions.
- Clean your contact list periodically. Delete any subscribers who have not opened or clicked on an email from you in the last six months.
- For best results, send your emails between 8am to 2pm on Mon, Tues, Wed or Thurs.
- When people sign up to your email list, let them know how often they can expect an email from you.
- Write interesting and inviting subject lines. Avoid using phrases such as "100% guaranteed", adding symbols or writing in capital letters.
- Try to segment your contact list to different groups of interest, and tailor your content to each group.
