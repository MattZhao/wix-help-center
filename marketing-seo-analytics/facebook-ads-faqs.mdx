### What is the full step-by-step process to get my campaign live?

Getting your campaign from set up to live is quick and easy, because the Wix AI does most of the work. Here’s how it works:

- **STEP 1: Getting Started**

Once you’re ready to try it out, click “Get Started” in the Welcome Screen. You can also schedule a call with a Wix Expert anytime to get help or sales advice.
- **STEP 2: Choosing a campaign goal**
- You have three options of campaign goal: 'more store orders' for campaigns run with Wix Stores, 'more leads or bookings,' or 'promote a page.' Pick the goal that's right for you. **Note: **Once you pick a goal, you cannot change it.
- **Step 3: Connecting to Facebook

**In the connect page, choose to connect to your Facebook business page. Once you’ve done it, an ad preview will be created. This is based on a number of things like the campaign goal you chose, what products you advertise if you have Wix Stores, Meta pixel, Business Manager and more. Keep in mind that you must be the admin of the Facebook Business page in order to connect it. Follow the prompts that come up.
- **STEP 4: Your Ad Content**

Now you’re ready to set up your ad. In the ad content section, you’ll see an ad already set up for you based on information pulled from your site. The smart algorithm does a full analysis of your website and creates the ad. You can change your ad caption and image at any time, but it’s better to leave them as is, so that Wix’s algorithm can run tests to check how effective these are.
- **STEP 5: Target Audience**

Next on the list is finding the right audience. The Wix algorithm will take care of this based on its analysis of what’s best for your business. Feel free to make changes to the gender, age range, interests and geographic location but keep in mind that it’s best to stick to the recommended parameters. That way, Wix AI can fully test out what’s best for your ad and how to automatically redefine your audience.
- **STEP 6: Budget**

Set the budget that best suits your business. The more you spend, the better your chances of reaching more people as your ad will be shown more frequently. The result? More site visits and more purchases of products or services. In this step, take note that your campaign is set to run automatically, renewing your subscription on a monthly basis until you cancel. It’s best to let your campaign run for a couple of months so that the smart algorithm has time to get your valuable data and statistics. You can also change your subscription while a campaign is running. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-changing-your-budget-during-a-campaign)
- **STEP 7: Reviewing Your Ad**

Finally, review your ad and make sure it’s ready to launch. Check the budget, audience and creative - you can edit them anytime - and when you’re satisfied, go ahead and launch your campaign.
- **STEP 8: Your Ad Will Go Live Soon**

Once you’ve completed the checkout process, Wix will submit your ad to Facebook for review. This is a relatively quick process and most ads go live within 72 hours. When your ad is live, you’ll be notified immediately.

And that’s it. In a few seamless steps, all of which take less than 10 minutes to complete, your campaign can be up and running.

### What are Dynamic Ads?

**Note:** Dynamic ads are only available for campaigns run with Wix Stores.
Dynamic ads at first glance, look similar to other ads you may have already seen on Facebook and Instagram. The difference is that rather than displaying just one or some of your online store products at a time, they showcase multiple products within the same template. 
Using a sophisticated algorithm, these ads automatically understand who to showcase your products to - potential customers who are most likely to buy your products - and which of your items to feature. The algorithm learns from the way your target audience behaves online and then shows them the most relevant products for them. In this way, they’re really effective at driving more traffic to your online store and boosting your sales.

#### Understanding Your Campaign Dashboard

### What is ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)?

The best way to explain ROAS or return on ad spend is with an example:

- If you spent $100 on FB ads and sold $300 worth of products, your ROAS is 300%.
- If you spent $100 on FB ads and sold $50 worth of products, your ROAS is 50%.

ROAS is not your profit (ROI). This is calculated differently. Using the first example, here’s how to calculate your ROI: 
If you spent $100 on FB ads and sold $300 worth of products, but made only a $50 profit on each product, your ROI would be calculated as follows:

- 3 products sold = $150
- Your ROAS = 300%
- Your profit (ROI) = 50%

When you use Facebook Ads with Wix, your return on ad spend is automatically calculated for you. Check your campaign dashboard to see how well your campaign is doing, and remember, the Wix AI algorithm is always changing and improving your campaign. This means the results you see show what’s happening in your campaign right now, not what could happen in future.

### What's the difference between ad clicks and site visits?

Ad clicks - as the name implies - is the total number of clicks on your ad. This number includes the same person clicking on your ad multiple times. For example, if Jane sees your ad 3 times and clicks on it 3 times, you have a total of 3 clicks. 
Site views are counted based on the site visitor's unique digital footprint. They're counted just once per person. So, following from the example above, if Jane visits your site 3 times, it's counted as 1 site visit.

#### In-depth Look at the Wix AI Algorithm

### Are all products or services sold on FB & Instagram counted as sales by the Wix AI algorithm?

No, the algorithm only calculates sales based on products sold by your Facebook Ads with Wix campaign. So if you sold 10 products on Facebook but only 4 were the result of clicks on your Facebook Ads with Wix campaign, only those 4 will be counted. We only count the direct results of FB ads algorithm, nothing else.

As mentioned, campaign success and return on ad spend can take time. We recommend giving the Wix AI at least 2 months to gather data, optimize, test and refine your ad.

- Changing the products displayed in your ad

- Making adjustments to your store or bookings service
- Redefining your target audience

At any time, you can also schedule a call with a Wix Expert who can guide you in improving your campaign, and give you advice on how to get more sales.

### What’s the difference between Facebook Ads and a Facebook Shop?

The major difference between Facebook ads and shopping on Facebook is the place you complete the purchase. In the simplest terms, a shop on Facebook is part of the Business Page where shoppers can buy products directly. They can browse a product catalog, see items they want to buy and make payments without leaving the page. 
Facebook ads work by promoting the products or services you sell through social media, and getting people to your site where they can browse your catalog, add items to their cart and make payment. 
Right now, Facebook Ads with Wix is the best way to sell your products, because it’s integrated with your site, and all of Wix’s products are available to you, including Email Marketing, abandoned cart automations, secure payment systems and more.
