You don't need to connect your site in your SEO Setup Checklist. If the step is marked as not completed in your checklist, you can ignore it.

The Wix SEO Setup Checklist is a step-by-step checklist designed to help you get found online. The SEO Setup Checklist gives you advice about the information your site needs to connect and [verify your site with Google](https://www.wix.com/seo/learn/resource/verifying-your-site-with-google).

**In this article, learn more about:**

---

### Connecting your site to Google

You can connect your site to Google in **Marketing & SEO **in your site's dashboard.

> **Important:**
>
> To connect to Google Search Console, you must have a [Premium plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website) and a domain connected to your site. This can be either a domain [purchased from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-from-wix) or a domain purchased [from another domain host](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

#### To connect your site:

[Go to the SEO Setup Checklist](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp%2F1480c568-5cbd-9392-5604-1148f5faffa0%3FreferralInfo%3Dseo_wiz_launch_lp&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=SEO%20Setup%20Checklist) in your site's SEO dashboard.   Complete the checklist in Step 1 of your SEO Checklist.   Click **Connect to Google**.

---

### Important information about verifying with SEO Setup Checklist

Only site owners and admin collaborators can access SEO Setup Checklist. It is not available to back-office managers. [Learn more about site collaborator roles and permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview).  Verifying your site with Google Search Console with Wix SEO Setup Checklist is only available for [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977) sites with a connected domain.   If your site is missing any information necessary to submit your site to Google, it will be listed in the pop-up. Follow the instructions in the popup to learn how to add your missing information.   It's possible to skip a step and complete the steps after it. However, any step you skip will show as incomplete until you finish it. You also won't be able to complete your SEO Checklist until you finish all of the steps.

It is not possible to verify your site using SEO Setup Checklist more than once. If you need to verify your site again, you must do it manually. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-google-search-console-5033765)
