### Facebook & Instagram Ads with Wix refund policy

Ad credits added to Facebook & Instagram Ads with Wix are paid with a monthly subscription. These payments are non-refundable.

#### Additional information:

Wix gets 15% of your payment for running and optimizing your campaign, the remaining 85% goes towards your ad budget which is spent on ad impressions.  Prices do not include VAT, which is determined based on your billing country.  To prevent your campaign being disapproved by Meta, make sure the campaign complies with [Meta's Advertising Policies](https://transparency.fb.com/policies/ad-standards).

### How do I view my Facebook & Instagram Ads with Wix invoices?

All invoices for Wix services can be viewed in the [Billing History](https://www.wix.com/account/billing-history?referralAdditionalInfo=account-manager) page of your account. Invoices may include VAT, which is determined based on your billing country.

### What happens to my budget if I pause a campaign?

You can pause a campaign at any time. As soon as you pause, the following changes happen:
- Your ad is no longer shown on Meta platforms.
- Any remaining ad credits remain in your account to be spent on another active campaign or a new campaign.
- If the paused campaign was your last active campaign on the account, auto renew for the subscription will be turned off automatically.
