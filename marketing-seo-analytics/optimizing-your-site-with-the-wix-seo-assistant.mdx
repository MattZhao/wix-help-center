---
title: "Optimizing Your Site with the Wix SEO Assistant"
---

The Wix SEO Assistant helps you optimize your site for search engines. It identifies potential issues with your site and gives you recommendations that you can follow to improve your page's content, structure, and SEO attributes. For example, it will remind you to perform a site inspection if it’s been a while, or write a blog post every 30 days.

The SEO Assistant is available in the SEO section of the Marketing & SEO tab in your dashboard.

> **Note:**
>
> To view tasks in the Wix SEO Assistant, you must have a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan).

---

### Step 1 | (Optional) Complete your SEO Setup Checklist

We recommend first completing your Wix SEO Setup Checklist. This step-by-step checklist helps you improve your site's SEO, based on your business's information and your [keywords](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-keywords-on-your-sites-pages). It also lets you connect and verify your site with Google so that it appears in search engine results.

> **Important:**
>
> To connect to Google Search Console, you must have a [Premium plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website) and a domain connected to your site. This can be either a domain [purchased from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-from-wix) or a domain purchased [from another domain host](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

#### To complete your setup:

[Go to SEO](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Continue Setup** /** Start Setup **/** Go to Setup **below **SEO Checklist Setup **and [complete your checklist](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-creating-your-seo-checklist).

---

### Step 2 | Fix current issues

Next, work to complete any tasks that the assistant has identified as an issue. These issues are organized by priority, ranging from critical to low.

It's important to complete any tasks labelled as critical. A "Critical" label means, for example, that search engines may not be able to index your site, and it may not appear in search results. [Learn more about indexing issues](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-or-pages-not-appearing-in-search-engine-results)

#### To fix issues:

[Go to SEO](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Click **View tasks** below **Issues.**  Click a task to expand it and learn more about the issue.  Follow the instructions to resolve the task.

---

### Step 3 | Review recommendations

After you've resolved issues with your site, you can review your recommendation tasks. These tasks contain suggestions on how to improve your site, and instructions on how to do them.

#### To review recommendations:

[Go to SEO](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Click **View tasks** below **Recommendations.**  Click a task to expand it and see a recommendation.  Follow the instructions to resolve the task.

---

### Step 4 | Keep track of your completed tasks

You can keep track of any tasks you've completed and learn more about how they'll benefit your site's SEO in the Wix SEO Assistant.

#### To view your completed tasks:

[Go to SEO](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard.  Click **View tasks** below **Completed tasks**.  Click a task to expand it and view details about the task.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about using the Wix SEO Assistant.

### Do I have to use the SEO Assistant?

You don't have to use the SEO Assistant for your site to rank well in search results. Using the SEO Assistant can help improve your site's SEO, but it doesn't guarantee that your site will rank higher. Your site's ranking also depends on other factors, such as the quality of the content across your site, and each search engine's individual algorithms.

### Can I use the Wix SEO Assistant with Velo?

No. Currently, the Wix SEO Assistant is not available to use with Velo.
