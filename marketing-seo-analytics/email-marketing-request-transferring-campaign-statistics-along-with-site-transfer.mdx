---
title: "Email Marketing Request: Transferring Campaign Statistics Along with Site Transfer"
---

Currently in Wix Email Marketing, if you transfer your site to a new owner, your past email marketing campaign statistics will not transfer accurately (you may see incomplete data or no data at all). Even if you transfer the site back to the original owner, the statistics may not display properly.

Having the ability to transfer campaign statistics along with a site transfer ensures all campaign data is accurate and up-to-date for the new site owner.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
