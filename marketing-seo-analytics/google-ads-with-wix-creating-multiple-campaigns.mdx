### The campaigns page

When you go to Google Ads in your site's dashboard, the first element you'll see is the main campaigns page. Your campaigns page gives you a quick breakdown of each campaign you've created. Every campaign has its own card with key performance indicators including impressions, total clicks, ad spend used, and daily spend.

> **Tip:**
>
> Hover over a campaign and click **Go to Dashboard **to see all the data for that campaign.

Labels next to each campaign title show you the status of that campaign: **Live**, **Paused**, **In Review**, or **Disapproved**.** **Any live campaigns appear at the top of the list in chronological order.

From the campaigns page you can also manage your Google Ads with Wix subscription and budget as well as create a new campaign.

### Creating multiple Google Ads with Wix campaigns

You can create as many campaigns as you want from Google Ads in your site's dashboard. Keep track of your spend for each campaign as well as the campaign status. For every new campaign, choose a daily budget that is allocated from your total ad credits.

#### To create multiple campaigns:

[Go to Google Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Google%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/google-ads/eligibility) in your site's dashboard.  The campaigns page shows all your current campaign(s) in a list. Click **Create New Campaign** at the top right to make another Google Ads with Wix campaign.  (For Wix Stores only): Choose an advertising goal for your new campaign: **Drive site traffic** or **Boost product purchases**.  Customize the settings and ad content of your new ad campaign. [Learn more about creating campaign content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-ads-with-wix-creating-a-campaign#step-2-customize-your-ad-settings).  Set your new campaign's daily budget. The daily budget is taken from your total monthly subscription and applied as ad credits. Drag the slider to see an estimate of ad clicks per day with your chosen budget.

> **How your budget works with multiple campaigns:**
>
> You pay for one Google Ads with Wix subscription regardless of how many campaigns you run. You can also always buy extra ad credits with a one-time payment that are added on top of your subscription. Your ad credits are used to run your ads.
> When you choose a daily budget for a new campaign, it's taken from the total 'bucket' of ad credits available for all campaigns in your account. To get more ad credits, Click **Manage ad credits & budget** in the campaigns page and increase your subscription.

Click **Launch** to launch your new campaign.

Your new campaign appears in the campaigns page. The status label is **In Review** until the campaign content is approved by Google.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about creating multiple campaigns with Google Ads with Wix.

### Is there a limit on the number of campaigns I can create?

No. You can create as many Google Ads with Wix campaigns as you need to, but there is a minimum daily spend for each campaign. You can also only run one [Google Performance Max campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-ads-with-wix-about-google-ads-performance-max-shopping-campaigns) per site.
**Note:** Google Ads restricts its users to having a maximum of 5 live [Smart campaigns](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7652860?hl=en) at any given time to ensure focus, budget allocation, and campaign management efficiency.

### How do I change the daily spend of my campaign?

1. From the main campaigns page, click **Manage Spend & Credits** at the right.
2. View the ad credits remaining in the current billing cycle. Click **Adjust Daily Spend** to change your daily spend.
3. Enter a new daily spend for the campaign(s) you want to change. If you are running more than one campaign, you can see the total daily spend across all your campaigns.

**Note:** If you don't have enough for the new daily amount, you can increase your monthly subscription.
4. Click **Save** to update your daily spend. If you have also increased your subscription, you will go through the checkout process at this step.

### What happens when I pause a campaign?

When you pause a campaign, the campaign stops running and does not spend ad credits. The campaign status also changes to 'Paused' on the Campaigns page.

### How long should I keep multiple campaigns running if I am comparing them for performance?

You should give campaigns at least two weeks in order to see any potential patterns and identify what is working and where you can adjust. Start by checking in after seven days to see which campaign is driving more traffic. After that, if one is vastly underperforming, you can reassess.
