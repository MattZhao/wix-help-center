title: "Email Marketing Request: Editing the Text Appearing at the Top and Bottom of Your Email Campaign"
---

Currently, it is not possible to edit the text that appears at the top or bottom of the email campaign.

> **Note:**
>
> The language of this text changes based on the [language of your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-language-of-your-account-dashboard-and-editor).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
