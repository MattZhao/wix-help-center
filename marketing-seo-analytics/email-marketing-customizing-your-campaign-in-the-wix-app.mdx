---
title: "Email Marketing: Customizing Your Campaign in the Wix App"
---

Once you've [created an email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-a-campaign-in-the-wix-owner-app) from the Wix app by choosing a template or starting from scratch, you can customize it to match your brand. Change the background color of the email and add elements like images, text and custom buttons to encourage recipients to read.

### To customize your campaign in the Wix app:

Go to the [email marketing dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-accessing-the-email-marketing-dashboard-in-the-wix-owner-app) in the Wix app.  Tap the **Drafts** tab.  Tap the campaign you want to customize.  Tap **Edit**.

**Note:** If you don't see the option to edit it means your campaign was created on a desktop and you must customize it from there.  The customization menu opens at the bottom of the screen. Tap **Background** to update the campaign's background color.

### Text

1. Tap **Text**.
2. Tap the text box to edit the text.
3. (Optional) Customize your text using the available options:

- **Format ****: **Swipe to view the different options and tap one to choose it.
- **Font ****: **Swipe to view the different options and tap one to choose it.
- **Size ****:** Drag the slider to increase or decrease the text's size.
- **Color ****: **Choose a text color.
- **Alignment ****: **Choose how your text should be aligned: left, center or right.

### Image

1. Tap **Image**.
2. Choose if you want to upload an image from your device, the Media Manager, or by taking a photo.

### Button

1. Tap **Button**.
2. Tap the new button to edit the button text.
3. (Optional) Customize your button using the available options:

- Tap **Style**  to select a different style for your button.
- Tap **Color**  to change your button color. Choose from a preset color or tap the **Add **icon   on the left to pick a custom color.

### Divider

1. Tap **Divider**.
2. Tap **Format ** to select a style for your divider.
3. Tap **Color ** to change the divider's color. Choose from a preset color or tap the **Add** icon  on the left to pick a custom color.

### Logo

1. Tap **Logo**.
2. Choose if you want to upload your logo from your device, the Media Manager, or by taking a photo.

7.  (Optional) When you're done customizing tap **Preview & Test** to send a test email.
