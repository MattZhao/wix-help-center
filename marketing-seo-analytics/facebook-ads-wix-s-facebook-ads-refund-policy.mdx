---
title: "Facebook Ads: Wix's Facebook Ads Refund Policy"
---

Facebook Ads campaigns with Wix are paid with a monthly subscription and payments are non-refundable. If you [end your campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-ending-a-campaign) before the month is up, your campaign ends instantly, and auto-renew is turned off.

You can restart an ended campaign at any time before your subscription expires by clicking **Edit & Restart** from your Facebook Ads dashboard.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Resuming a campaign also turns auto-renew back on.
> - Any unused ad spend will remain in your account until you resume the campaign.
> - If the [subscription expires](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-pausing-and-resuming-your-ad-campaign-9219531#resuming-after-your-subscription-has-expired), you need to purchase a new subscription in order to activate the campaign and use the remaining ad spend.

If you don't want your campaign to renew after the current subscription cycle ends you can [turn off auto-renewal of your subscription](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-turning-off-auto-renewal-for-your-facebook-ads-subscription).

### Additional Information

What's Wix's cut of my purchased premium plan? 

- Wix gets 15% of your payment, the remaining 85% goes to Facebook.
- Prices do not include VAT, which is determined based on your billing country.
    To prevent rejections from Facebook, make sure your campaign complies with [Facebook's Advertising Policies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-campaigns-rejected-by-facebook).   If you feel you deserve a refund, [contact us and tell us why](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) and we will review your request.
