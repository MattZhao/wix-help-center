---
title: "Email Marketing: Campaign Email Declined from Certain Sender Addresses"
---

Wix does not allow sending email marketing campaigns from certain email domains. We automatically decline any campaign sent from these addresses.

If your email address is associated to one of the below domains, [update your sender email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-changing-the-sender-details-of-your-campaigns) in order to send future campaigns.

### Most common declined email addresses:

@mail.com (including all domains used by mail.com services)  @email.com  @store.com  @contact.com  @shop.com

> **Note:**
>
> The above addresses are only the most common examples of declined email addresses. To avoid these errors in the future, do not apply a mailbox you don't own in the sender address.
