---
title: "Email Marketing: Confirming Your Email Address"
---

When sending an email campaign, you are required to confirm your email address to ensure that your newsletter reaches its destination.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you sign up with an [email address purchased with your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-purchasing-a-personalized-business-email), the email address is automatically verified.

### To confirm your email address:

[Create and edit](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-a-new-campaign) your email campaign.  On the final step, when you click **Send Now**, you will be asked to confirm your email address.  Check your email account for the code. If you don't receive it, check your Spam/Junk folder. The email titled "Your Email Confirmation Code" is sent from **no-reply@verification.ascendbywix.com**.

**Note: **You can confirm your email without a code if you verify the email by signing in via a Google or Facebook account.  Enter the code in the pop-up window.  Click **Confirm**.

**Note:** If you click **Not Now**, you still need to confirm your email address prior to sending your email campaign.
