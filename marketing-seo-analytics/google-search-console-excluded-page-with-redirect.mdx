---
title: "Google Search Console: Excluded (Page With Redirect)"
---

If you see a "Page with redirect" message in your Coverage report, it means that Google found a page with a redirected URL on your site and did not index it. Google excludes pages like this from search results to avoid duplicate results or because it has detected errors.

A "Page with redirect" error could be caused by these situations:

You [upgraded your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-your-site-to-premium-3066683). The redirect is from [your free Wix URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-free-wix-url-8591528) to your own custom domain URL.  You set up [301 redirects](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-a-301-redirect-for-a-page-on-your-site) for these pages in the URL Redirect Manager.  The redirect is from the HTTP version of your site's URL to the HTTPS version.  The redirect is from the **https://**mydomain.com version of your site's URL to the **https://www**.mydomain.com version.

In these cases, this behavior is normal and won't affect your site's ranking.
