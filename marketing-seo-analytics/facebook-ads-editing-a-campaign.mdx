---
title: "Facebook Ads: Editing a Campaign"
---

Make changes to your campaign from the **Campaign Actions** drop-down. Edit your ad content, change your target audience and update your monthly subscription.

> **Important:**
>
> - If you make any changes to your campaign, it must go back through the Facebook review process. While your campaign is in review, your ads are not live on Facebook. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-about-the-campaign-review-process-with-facebook)
> - If you want to change your campaign budget, you will pay the new amount immediately, regardless of where you are in your subscription. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-changing-your-budget-during-a-campaign)

### To edit your campaign:

Go to [Facebook Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Facebook%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpaid-ads%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Campaign Actions** drop-down.  Click **Edit campaign.**

You return to **Review & Launch **in campaign setup to make edits. Click **Edit ** next to the section where you want to make updates.

When you've finished making edits, click **Submit Changes.**  Click **Submit Changes** in the pop-up to confirm. Your ads will not be live on Facebook as the changes first go to Facebook for review.
