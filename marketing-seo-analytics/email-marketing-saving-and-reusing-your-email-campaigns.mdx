### Saving and reusing email designs

Save your email campaign designs as a template to use again and again. Once you create your email, you can save its design as a template.

#### To save an email design:

Go to [Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant email.   Select **Save as template**.

Enter a name for your template.  Click **Save Template**.

> **What's next?**
>
> Your template is available to use. Learn more about [building your email campaigns using a template](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-and-sending-a-campaign-from-start-to-finish#step-1-choose-a-template-or-start-from-scratch).

### Saving a custom color

Save up to 6 colors to reuse them throughout all your site's email campaigns on text, buttons, dividers, and more. If you want to remove a saved color, save more saved colors until the one you don't want is replaced by a new saved color.

#### To save a color in your campaign:

Go to [Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Edit** next to an existing campaign or [create a new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-a-new-campaign).  Click any text element in the template.   Click the Color icon  in the panel on the left.

Select a custom color or enter a HEX value in the text box.   Click the Add Color icon  next to the HEX number.

Click the checkmark to close the color panel.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about saving and reusing your email campaigns.

### Are the templates I save available to use in other sites in my account?

No, the templates you create are only available to use for the site that you create them in.

### Are my custom colors available in the editor too, or just in my email campaigns?

No, the custom colors you create are available to use across your email campaigns only.
