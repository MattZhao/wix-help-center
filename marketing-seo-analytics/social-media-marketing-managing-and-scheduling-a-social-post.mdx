title: "Social Media Marketing: Managing and Scheduling a Social Post"
---

Schedule posts ahead of time for holidays or important events so you don't have to worry about being near your computer. Create your content in advance and schedule Facebook and Instagram posts to be published at a later date.

You can see a list of all your scheduled posts from the Social Media Marketing dashboard where you can also edit the timing.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Schedule a new social post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-posts-scheduling-a-social-post#scheduling-a-new-social-post)  [Schedule a draft post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-posts-scheduling-a-social-post#scheduling-a-draft-post)  [Manage your scheduled posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-posts-scheduling-a-social-post#managing-your-scheduled-posts)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Scheduling a social post is available if you have [upgraded your social media plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-media-marketing-upgrading-your-social-media-marketing-plan).

### Scheduling a new social post

After you connect your social accounts and create your social post content, you can choose to schedule the post for a specific date and time.

#### To schedule a social post:

[Go to Social Media Marketing](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fsocial-marketing-web%2F%3F&title=Social+Media+Marketing&primaryButtonText=Social+Media+Marketing) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Quick Actions** and select an option from the drop-down:

### Select media to publish

1. Select a file or click** + Upload Media**.
2. Click **Select media** at the bottom right.** **
3. Click **Schedule for later** under **When do you want to publish?**
4. Use the date picker to choose the day you want the post to be published.
5. Choose a time of day the post should be published.

**Note:** Make sure to check the time zone. If it is incorrect, change it in your browser's settings.
6. Click **Apply**.
7. Click **Schedule **on the bottom right to schedule the post.

### Create image with Wix

1. Choose a template and customize it.
2. Click **Publish** at the top right.
3. Click **Schedule for later** under **When do you want to publish?**
4. Use the date picker to choose the day you want the post to be published.
5. Choose a time of day the post should be published.

**Note:** Make sure to check the time zone. If it is incorrect, change it in your browser's settings.
6. Click **Apply**.
7. Click **Schedule **on the bottom right to schedule the post.

### Create video with Vimeo

1. (If necessary) Choose between the Wix Video Maker or Vimeo.
2. Customize your video. When you're done, click **Save & Share**.
3. Click **Share**.
4. Click **Schedule for later** under **When do you want to publish?**
5. Use the date picker to choose the day you want the post to be published.
6. Choose a time of day the post should be published.

**Note:** Make sure to check the time zone. If it is incorrect, change it in your browser's settings.
7. Click **Apply**.
8. Click **Schedule **at the bottom right to schedule the post.

Learn more about creating video social posts using [Wix Video Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-a-promotional-video-for-your-site) or [Vimeo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-integrating-with-vimeo).

> **Note:**
>
> You will see 'Scheduled' instead of 'Published' in the final publishing status.

### Scheduling a draft post

Schedule your draft posts to go out at a time that best suits you and your audience.

#### To schedule a draft post:

[Go to Social Media Marketing](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fsocial-marketing-web%2F%3F&title=Social+Media+Marketing&primaryButtonText=Social+Media+Marketing) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Your Social Posts**.   Hover over the relevant draft post and click the **More Actions **icon .   Click **Preview & Publish**.

Click **Schedule for later** under **When do you want to publish?**  Use the date picker to choose the day you want the post to be published.  Choose a time of day the post should be published.

**Note:** Make sure to check the time zone. If it is incorrect, change it in your browser's settings.  Click **Apply**.  Click **Schedule **on the bottom right to schedule the post.

### Managing your scheduled posts

View all your scheduled posts from the Social Media Marketing dashboard. You can edit an existing post, reschedule it for another day, or delete the post.

#### To manage your scheduled posts:

[Go to Social Media Marketing](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fsocial-marketing-web%2F%3F&title=Social+Media+Marketing&primaryButtonText=Social+Media+Marketing) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Your Social Posts**.  Select **Scheduled** from the** All statuses** drop-down.

Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the post you want to manage.  Manage your post using the following options:

### Reschedule

1. Click **Reschedule**.
2. Choose a new date from the date picker when the post should be published.
3. Choose a time of day the post should be published.

**Note:** Make sure to check the time zone. If it is incorrect, change it in your browser's settings.
4. Click **Reschedule**.

### Reuse Design

Create a new social post quickly by reusing the existing design from the post you selected.
1. Click **Reuse Design**.
2. Choose whether you want to keep the design the same, or customize it to make changes.
3. Click **Publish **at the top right.

### Preview & Publish

Publish your scheduled post right away. 
1. Click **Preview & Publish**.** **
2. Click** Publish now **under** When do you want to publish?**.
3. Click **Publish** at the bottom right.

### Cancel Scheduling

1. Click **Cancel Scheduling**.
2. Click **Cancel Scheduling** in the popup to confirm.

**Note:** Once you cancel, you can still view the post in both the 'Scheduled' and 'All' tabs.

### Download

Click **Download** to save a copy of your post to your computer.

### Delete

1. Click **Delete**.
2. Click **Delete Content** to confirm you want the post deleted from your Social Posts dashboard.

**Note:** This does not delete the post from your social media platforms if you have previously published them.
