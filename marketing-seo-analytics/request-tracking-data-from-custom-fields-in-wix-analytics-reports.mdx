---
title: "Request: Tracking Data from Custom Fields in Wix Analytics Reports"
---

Currently it is not possible track or gather data from custom fields in Wix Analytics reports. Examples of custom fields include a custom sign up form or a checkout page in Wix Stores.

Having the ability to track this data would allow you to create custom reports based on fields you created (e.g. creating a report based on information from a sign up form).

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
