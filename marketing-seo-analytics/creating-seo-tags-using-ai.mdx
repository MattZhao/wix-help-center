### Creating SEO tags using AI

You can alter title tags and meta descriptions in both [the SEO panels of your installed Wix apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-pages-seo-settings-in-the-seo-panel) and [the SEO Settings "Edit by Page" tabs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929#editing-the-seo-settings-of-individual-pages). You'll find the AI Text Creator in both of these places.

#### To create SEO tags using AI:

Access your page title tag and meta description settings in one of two places:

- [The SEO panels of your installed Wix apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-pages-seo-settings-in-the-seo-panel).
- [The SEO Settings "Edit by Page" tabs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929#editing-the-seo-settings-of-individual-pages).
    Click **Create AI text** next to the **Title tag** or **Meta description** field.  The **AI Text Creator** window will open, presenting 3 suggestions. Choose what you want to do:

- **Use Text:** Click **Use Text** next to the suggestion you want to use. You can then edit this text.
- **Refine suggestions:**

1. Provide key details and select a tone of voice to generate 3 refined suggestions.
2. Click **Create**.
3. Click **Use Text** next to the suggestion you want to use.

(Optional) Edit the text that now appears in the **Title tag** or **Meta description** field.  Click **Save**.

### FAQs

### What kind of information is used to generate these tags?

The AI Text Creator uses your business name, business category, page title, page description and location to generate these tags. It also uses your keywords if you're using it for a Wix Blogs page.

### Where can I use the AI Text Creator?

You'll find the AI Text Creator in:
- [The SEO panels of your installed Wix apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-pages-seo-settings-in-the-seo-panel).
- [The SEO Settings "Edit by Page" tabs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929#editing-the-seo-settings-of-individual-pages).

### What should I do if I'm told the creator was unable to create suggestions for me?

Review your page content to make sure all names and description make sense. If you've used [keywords](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-keywords-on-your-sites-pages) across your site, ensure they make sense and don't include special characters or commas.
