### What is IndexNow?

The IndexNow protocol makes the process of search engines crawling your site and indexing them more efficient. [IndexNow explains its functionality](https://www.indexnow.org/index) as "a simple ping so that search engines know that a URL and its content has been added, updated, or deleted." This allows search engines to quickly identify that a page was changed and reflect the change in search results. Without IndexNow, it can take anywhere from hours to months for changes to be discovered by search engines.

### Benefits of using IndexNow

Normally it can take time for search engines to crawl and index your site. This depends on your site update rates, size and other factors. Therefore any changes you've made, as well as any improvements to your site's SEO, may not come into effect until the search engines have found and indexed that page.

With a Premium site, Wix automatically makes IndexNow aware of any site changes without you having to keep on top of it. For example, if you add a new blog post to your site, Wix notifies IndexNow and IndexNow then pings relevant search engines about the changes. This allows the search engines to crawl and index the page faster (depending on their indexing factors).

### Do I have to set up IndexNow?

No. The IndexNow benefits and features are automatically included as part of your Premium plan.

### Do I submit my site content changes?

No. As part of your Premium plan, Wix checks when there are content changes to your site pages. We then submit the changes for you to the IndexNow protocol.

### What search engines support IndexNow?

IndexNow is supported by Bing, Yandex, Naver, and more, and the list is always updating. [Learn more about IndexNow and supported search engines](https://www.indexnow.org/index).

### I've made content changes to my site, why don't I see the updates?

IndexNow makes sure that search engines are aware of your site changes. However, it does not guarantee that the page will be crawled or indexed. Search engines can choose not to crawl and index URLs if they do not meet their selection criterion. It can also take time to see the changes reflected.
