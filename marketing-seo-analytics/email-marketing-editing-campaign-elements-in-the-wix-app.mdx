---
title: "Email Marketing: Editing Campaign Elements in the Wix App"
---

Your email campaign is divided up into sections based on the elements you've added (buttons, text, images). You can update the design and format of each element from the Wix app by tapping the element. The design options change based on the section you're working on.

> **Important:**
>
> You can only edit email campaigns in the Wix app if the campaign was originally created from mobile. If a campaign was created or edited from a desktop, you must edit it from there.

### To edit the elements of your campaign in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Marketing **and select **Email Marketing**.  Tap the **Drafts** tab.

**Note:** You can only edit draft campaigns. If you want to edit a campaign that was already sent, duplicate the campaign first.  Tap the **More Actions** icon ﻿ next to the campaign you want to edit.  Tap **Edit**.

**Note: **If you don't see this option, it means your campaign was created from desktop.

  Tap the element in the campaign that you want to edit.

  The relevant menu opens at the bottom. Choose what you want to edit:

### Change the design of an element

Customize the element using [the available design options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-customizing-your-campaign-in-the-wix-owner-app-2833777).

### Add a link to an element

Link an element to your site, your member app, or any custom URL:
1. Tap the **Link ** icon at the top of the customization menu.
2. Tap the **Include Link** toggle to enable it.
3. Choose where to link the element:

- **My Wix Site:** The element links to your Wix site.
- **My Spaces App:** The element links to your [member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps).
- **Custom Link: **Enter a custom URL where the element should link to.
4. Tap the place you want to link to.
5. Tap **Done**.

**Note:** You cannot add a link to a divider.

### Duplicate an element

Tap the **Copy ** icon to duplicate the element. The new element will appear directly beneath the one you copied.

### Reorder an element

Tap the navigation arrows   to move your element up or down the page.
