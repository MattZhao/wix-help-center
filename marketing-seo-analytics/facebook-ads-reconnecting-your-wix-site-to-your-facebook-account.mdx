### To reconnect your site to your Facebook account:

[Go to Facebook Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Facebook%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpaid-ads%3FreferralInfo%3Dkb-article) in your site's dashboard.    Click **Reconnect Now** in the banner at the top. 

  Follow the onscreen prompts to sign back in to your Facebook account.

**Note: **You must reconnect the same Facebook account that you used to set up your campaign. It is not possible to switch accounts associated with a campaign, but [you can vote for that feature here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-request-switching-the-facebook-account-connected-to-an-ad-campaign).

> **Note:**
>
> You cannot create a new campaign until your Facebook account is reconnected. If you already have an existing campaign it continues to run, but you must reconnect before you can edit any part of the campaign.
