title: "Improving Conversion Tracking with Google Analytics"
---

Improve your conversion tracking and remarketing capabilities by passing additional information like purchase value, currency, and transaction IDs through Google Analytics. You can use a Universal Analytics property or Google Analytics 4 property to import events from Google Analytics. [Learn more about the differences between them](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9964640?hl=en)

### Step 1 | Link your Google Analytics and Google Ads accounts

First, you need to link your Google Analytics account and Google Ads account. Follow the relevant steps below depending on whether you want to use Google Analytics 4 or Universal Analytics:

### Using Google Analytics 4

1. Sign in to both your [Google Analytics account](https://analytics.google.com/analytics/web/) and [Google Ads account](https://ads.google.com/aw/overview) using the same Google login ID.
2. In your Google Analytics account, click **Admin** in the bottom left of the page.
3. Click **Google Ads Linking **under **Property**.
4. Click **Link**.
5. Select **Choose Google Ads accounts** in the **Link setup** pop-up.
6. Check the box beside the Google Ads account you want to use.
7. Click **Confirm**.
8. Click **Next**.
9. Choose if you want to [enable auto-tagging](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1733663) or to [leave your auto-tagging settings as they are](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1033981) under **Enable Auto-Tagging**.
10. Click **Next**.
11. Click** Submit**

### Using Universal Analytics

1. Sign in to both your [Google Analytics account](https://analytics.google.com/analytics/web/) and [Google Ads account](https://ads.google.com/aw/overview) using the same Google login ID.
2. In your Google Analytics account, click **Admin **in the bottom left of the page.
3. Click **Google Ads Linking **under **Property**.
4. Click **+ New Link Group**.
5. Select the Google Ads account you want to use.
6. Click **Continue**.
7. Add a name for the link group.
8. Enable linking for each view in the property you want to use.
9. Choose if you want to [enable auto-tagging](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1733663) or to [leave your auto-tagging settings as they are](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/1033981) under **Advanced settings**.
10. Click **Link accounts**.

---

### Step 2 | Create a conversion in Google Ads

Next, you need to create a conversion in Google Ads and import the events you want to track from Google Analytics.

#### To create a conversion:

Go to [your Google Ads account](https://ads.google.com/aw/overview),   Click **Tools & Settings** in the top right corner.  Select **Conversions **under **Measurement **in the drop-down menu.  Click **Add conversion **.   Click **Import**.

Select **Google Analytics (UA)** or **Google Analytics 4 properties **(**App **or **Web**) depending on the linking method you used in Step 1 above.

Click **Continue**.  Select the analytics events that you want to track.   Click **Import and continue**.   Click **Done**.
