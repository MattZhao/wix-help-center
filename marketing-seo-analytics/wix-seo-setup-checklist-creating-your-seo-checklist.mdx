---
title: "Wix SEO Setup Checklist: Creating Your SEO Checklist"
---

The Wix SEO Setup Checklist is a step-by-step checklist designed to help you improve your site's search engine optimization (SEO) based on your business's information and your [keywords](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-keywords-on-your-sites-pages). It also lets you connect and verify your site with Google so that your site appears in search engine results.

You can create your SEO Checklist in your site's SEO dashboard. Enter the name of your business, select whether you serve your customers from a physical address or online, and add three keywords that describe your business and what you offer.

> **Important:**
>
> To connect to Google Search Console, you must have a [Premium plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website) and a domain connected to your site. This can be either a domain [purchased from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-from-wix) or a domain purchased [from another domain host](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere). Learn more [about the SEO Setup Checklist requirements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/improving-your-online-presence-with-the-wix-seo-setup-checklist#important-information-about-the-seo-wiz).

### To create your SEO Checklist:

[Go to your SEO dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-f3388913-140f-47a9-b2ba-4bf86b60e04e&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO).  Scroll down to **Tools and settings**.  Click **Get Started **under **SEO Setup Checklist**.  Click **Get Started**.  Enter a name for your site under **What's Your Business or Site Name?**.

**Tip: **You should make sure to keep the name consistent with the name that's written on your site.   Click **Next**.  Select whether you serve your customers from a physical location or online.

> **Tips:**
>
> - If you have a physical location, you can add up to five addresses.
> - You can [edit the addresses later](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-adding-multiple-locations) if you need to change them.

Click **Next**.  Add [up to 3 keyword phrases](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-viewing-and-updating-your-keywords) that describe your business and what it offers.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Your keywords should be 2-4 words long.
> - Press **Enter** to separate the phrases.
> - You can change these keywords later if you want to try different ones.
> - Click **See examples **to see some good examples and bad examples of keywords.

Click **Find high performing keywords **if you want suggestions for strong keywords for your site. [Learn more about keyword research tools](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-keyword-research-tools-to-optimize-your-sites-seo)  Click **Next**.

Your personalized SEO Checklist will be ready in seconds. Follow the steps of your checklist to connect your site to Google.

> **Important:**
>
> It can take some time for Google to index your site's pages and display them in search results.
