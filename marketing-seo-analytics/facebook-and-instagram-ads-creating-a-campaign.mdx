title: "Facebook & Instagram Ads: Creating a Campaign"
---

Create a campaign with Facebook & Instagram Ads with Wix and get your business in front of thousands of people. The entire campaign setup, creation, and launch process takes place in your Wix site dashboard. Choose your ad targeting audience using Wix's AI powered targeting tool, create your content, and pick a daily budget for your campaign.

> **Before you create a campaign:**
>
> Make sure you've completed the following setup steps:
> 1. Publish your site.
> 2. Upgrade your site by purchasing a plan.
> 3. Connect a custom domain.
> To complete the setup checklist, [Go to Facebook & Instagram Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Facebook%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpaid-ads%3FreferralInfo%3Dkb-article) in your site's dashboard and select a campaign goal to get started.

---

### Step 1 | Choose a campaign goal

The first step in setting up a campaign is choosing a campaign goal. Depending on the Wix apps you have installed and if you have a payment method connected, you can select a **Sales** or **Leads** campaign.

> **Note:**
>
> To create a Sales campaign, you must connect a payment method.

#### To choose a campaign goal:

[Go to Facebook & Instagram Ads](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Facebook%20Ads&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpaid-ads%3FreferralInfo%3Dkb-article) in your site's dashboard.  Select a goal:

- **Boost sales:** Create a Sales campaign to promote your business to your audience likely to purchase your product and/or service.
- **Get sales (For Wix Stores): **Create a Sales campaign with a dynamic image carousel or single image/video to advertise your products.
- **Generate leads: **Create a Leads campaign to promote your business to your audience likely to share contact info or submit a form.
    Click **Get Started**.  Sign in with your Facebook account.

---

### Step 2 | Connect your Facebook assets

The next step before is to connect your Facebook assets to Wix. These include:

If you have previously connected a Facebook Pixel in Marketing Integrations, your assets should already be connected to Wix.

> **If you want to advertise on Instagram:**
>
> Make sure your Instagram Business account is connected to the same Facebook page you're using for your Facebook assets. You can do this from Facebook. [Learn more about connecting your Instagram](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/345675946300334?id=419087378825961).

#### To connect your Facebook assets:

Click **Add Facebook Assets**.  Go through the steps to add your assets.  Next click **Connect Assets to Wix **and go through the steps to connect your accounts.   Click **Continue to Create Campaign**.

---

### Step 3 | Choose your ad settings and targeting

Now that the initial requirements are completed and you've connected Meta assets, you are ready to start building your campaign content. First you need to decide who you want your ad to target as well as the site page your ad will lead to.

> **Note:**
>
> At the top of this section you can see the status of your Meta platforms. If your accounts are linked correctly the status is 'Linked.' If an account isn't linked you may have to go back and edit your Meta assets. [Learn more in the FAQ section of this article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-instagram-ads-creating-a-campaign#faqs).

#### To choose your ad settings and targeting:

Click the **Site page that your ad will lead to** drop-down to select the page visitors navigate to after clicking your ad (e.g. your Wix Stores product page).  Next select an option for campaign targeting. Targeting defines how you want to reach your audience.

- **AI targeting:** Allow Meta's AI find the optimal audience for your campaign. We recommend this option as the AI continuously monitors campaign performance and adapts to the data.

1. Enter the countries you want to target under** Targeted countries**.
2. Click **Continue**.
- **Custom targeting:** Define your own audience using specific demographics of people.

1. Enter the places (cities, countries, zip codes) you want to target under **Targeted countries**.
2. Continue refining your audience by selecting a gender, age range, and audience interests.
3. Click **Continue**.

---

### Step 4 | Create your ad content

Next create content for your ad that engages your audience and attracts attention. Your ad content consists of four main parts: primary text, media, ad headline, and a call to action button. The content is the same across both Facebook and Instagram ads.

> **Note:**
>
> Once you save a campaign, you can't update its ad format.

#### To create your ad content:

Choose an ad format and follow the instructions to create your content. You will only see the dynamic carousel option if you are running a campaign with Wix Stores.

### Dynamic carousel (Wix Stores only)

A dynamic carousel personalizes your product catalog for the ad viewer. For example, if someone searches for 'Navy backpacks' online, a dynamic ad showcases your products that match the search term.

1. Enter your ad content. The limit is 500 characters. Focus on the value and benefits of your products and speak directly to your audience.
2. (Optional) View a preview of what a dynamic carousel might look like and switch between platforms and ad types.
3. Click **Continue**.

[image: An example of a dynamic carousel Facebook ad.]

### Single image or video

1. Enter your primary ad text. The limit is 500 characters. Focus on the value and benefits of what you're offering and speak directly to your audience.
2. Upload media for your ad. Once you've added a media file, you can preview how it will appear in the live ad. Make sure your image or video meets [Meta's media requirements](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update?content_id=6v7AXR08Qqq4Kiu&refsem_smb&utm_termdsa-1598818772528&gclidCj0KCQjwwYSwBhDcARIsAOyL0fj3kK0Nt4_cRA_fe4b5GCIg56r-RtpgS9i0Lds9ZjfrTwb45esNezcaAiPQEALw_wcB&gad_source=1&gclid=Cj0KCQjwwYSwBhDcARIsAOyL0fj3kK0Nt4_cRA_fe4b5GCIg56r-RtpgS9i0Lds9ZjfrTwb45esNezcaAiPQEALw_wcB).
3. Enter an ad headline to attract attention and inspire urgency in your audience. The limit is 40 characters.
4. Click the **Call to action button** drop-down to choose an option that inspires your audience to click your ad (e.g. Learn more, Buy now, Subscribe).
5. Click **Continue**.

See how the ad appears online under **Ad preview**. Click between Facebook and Instagram at the top to see the ad on different platforms. You can also view how the ad appears in different formats (feed, story, reel).

### Step 5 | Set your ad budget and launch

The last step before launching a campaign is to choose a campaign budget and monthly ad credits subscription. Your ad credits are applied to your total ad credits each month to be spent on campaign impressions. The higher your budget, the more your ads are shown on Meta platforms.

Once you've set a budget and purchased your ad credits, you can launch your campaign.

> **What's the difference between my monthly ad credits and campaign budget?**
>
> Think of your ad credits as going into one big bucket. When you set a daily budget for your campaign, you're deciding how many ad credits the campaign should take from the bucket every day. Each month when you pay your subscription, you 'top up' your bucket of ad credits. You can also make a one-time payment to get more ad credits if you want to top up the bucket before your next subscription cycle.

#### To set your ad budget and launch:

Choose an option under** Set campaign budget**:

- **Select preset budget:** Choose a preset daily budget based on our recommendations that shows the average daily impressions you will receive as well as your total monthly subscription with this budget.
- **Enter custom budget:** Enter an estimated daily spend for your campaign. When you enter a daily spend, the monthly ad credit subscription changes by default.
    Click **Save & Checkout **to finish creating your campaign and submit your purchase.

**Note: **If this isn't your first campaign, you won't see the checkout step. Instead, click **Save & Launch **to launch the newest campaign.

Your campaign now goes into review for Wix and Meta approval. This process can take up to 72 hours. Go to Facebook & Instagram Ads in your site's dashboard to check on the approval progress.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about Facebook & Instagram Ads by Wix.

### How does Meta's AI targeting work?

Meta's AI finds the right audience for you by:
- **Defining your target audience:** The AI shows ads to people with relevant interests and demographics.
- **Adapting to your ad content: **After analyzing your ad content, the AI will modify your target audience to get the best results.
- **Learning from your campaign performance:** The AI continuously monitors clicks, conversions and ad engagement and keeps refining your audience.

### What are Meta's media requirements for ads?

Make sure you choose media for your ad that meets Meta's requirements. 
- **Image resolution:** Minimum 500x500 pixels
- **Image file size: **Maximum 30MB
- **Video resolution:** Minimum 120x120 pixels
- **Video file size: **Maximum 1GB
- **Video duration:** Between 1 second and 20 minutes

[Learn more about Meta's media requirements for image and video ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update).

### Why is my Facebook account linked but my Instagram account shows 'not linked?'

If the Instagram status shows 'not linked' sign into the relevant page on Facebook and connect the relevant Instagram account. Once it's connected, refresh the campaign set up in your site's dashboard. [Learn how to connect to your Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/345675946300334?id=419087378825961).
You can also go back to **Edit Meta assets** and change the Facebook page to a new one that has a connected Instagram account.
**Note: **Make sure the connected Instagram account is an Instagram Business account.

### What is included in my monthly ad credits subscription?

Each month you'll pay the subscription amount (including Wix's 15% fee) and it will be applied to your total ad credits.

### Why is my daily spend estimated?

Your estimated daily spend refers to the number of ad credits your campaign uses for impressions each day. It is estimated as the spend appears in your local currency and will get converted into USD to pay Meta to show your ads.

### What are impressions?

An impression is counted each time an ad appears on the screen for the first time. For example, if an ad is on screen and someone scrolls down, then scrolls back up to see the same ad again, it still only counts as one impression. The actual total of impressions may be higher or lower depending on the daily spend, ad content, and campaign optimization.
[Learn more about impressions](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/675615482516035).
