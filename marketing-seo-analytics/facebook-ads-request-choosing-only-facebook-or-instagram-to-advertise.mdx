---
title: "Facebook Ads Request: Choosing Only Facebook or Instagram to Advertise"
---

Currently in Facebook Ads, it is not possible to choose whether your campaign runs only on Facebook or only on Instagram. When you create a campaign it advertises on both platforms simultaneously.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
