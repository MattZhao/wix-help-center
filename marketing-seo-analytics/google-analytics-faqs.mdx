Using Google Analytics can help you gain valuable insights into your site performance. Click a question below to learn more.

### How long does it take until I start to see statistics in Google Analytics?

Typically it takes 24-48 hours before you can view your stats after setting up your tracking code. If you can't see the statistics, it's a good idea to make sure that your [domain is connected to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-domain-assistant-3777632). Other factors such as high traffic can also affect processing time.
If you are still having problems viewing your statistics, try signing out of your Google Analytics account and then signing back in.

### I have embedded or redirected my site. Why is Google Analytics not working?

Wix supports embedding sites only to social networks (Facebook, for example). We host all of your Wix sites on our servers. The advantages of using Wix as your host includes improvements to your site's loading time, search engine optimization, using Google Analytics and more.

### Can I use Google Analytics if I use the free Wix site version?

It is not possible to use Google Analytics with a free Wix site. This is because Google Analytics requires that you have your own URL (e.g. www.mystunningwebsite.com). This service is therefore only available when you [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) and connect a domain to it.

### Can I use Google Analytics on my mobile-friendly site?

Yes. Using Google Analytics for your mobile-friendly site works the same as it does for your desktop site.
The desktop and mobile versions of your site represent different viewing modes of the same site, which share the same domain.
