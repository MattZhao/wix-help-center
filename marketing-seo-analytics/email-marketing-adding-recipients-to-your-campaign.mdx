### Adding recipients to your email campaign

Select target groups or include all of your contacts to send your campaign to. When selecting target groups, you can add segments, labels, or individual contacts to send to groups of contacts.

#### To add recipients as contacts:

[Create an email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-and-sending-a-campaign-from-start-to-finish) or [edit an existing one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-managing-your-campaign).  [Customize the elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-elements-to-your-email-campaign) in your campaign to your liking.

**Tip: **[Preview and test your email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign#sending-a-test-email-before-sending-your-campaign) to make sure it is perfect before you add recipients and send.  Click **Next**.  (Optional) [Import contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-importing-contacts-from-a-gmail-account) from a CSV file or your Gmail account:

1. Click **Import Contacts **at the bottom right.
2. Click **Import Contacts** in the popup to confirm.
3. Choose between **CSV file** or **Gmail account** and follow the steps on-screen.
4. When ready, click **Back to email marketing** at the top.
    Select who you want to send this email to:

- **Target group: **Select specific groups of contacts to receive your campaign. You can include all subscribers, specific [segments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-campaign-to-a-segment) or [labels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-contact-labels), or select individual contacts.
- **All Contacts: **All contacts on your list will receive your campaign.
    Click **Next **at the top right.  [Send your email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign).

> **Note:**
>
> You can enable the **Filter out inactive contacts** toggle to remove [inactive contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-about-active-and-inactive-contacts) from your list. **We do not recommend sending campaigns to inactive contacts** as this can increase your bounce rate, cause emails to be marked as spam, and decrease your [email deliverability](https://support.wix.com/en/article/increasing-your-mail-deliverability).

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### Why can't I add some contacts as recipients?

You may not be able to add specific contacts if:
- They have unsubscribed.
- They do not have an email address listed in your [Contacts](http://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/my-account/sites/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/contacts).
- You have the same email address for two different contacts.
- Previously sent emails to your contact have bounced.
- There are typos in the email address.

Additionally, if your recipient is an inactive contact, they will not appear unless you turn off the "Filter out inactive contacts" toggle. **We do not recommend sending campaigns to inactive contacts** as this can increase your bounce rate, cause emails to be marked as spam, and decrease your deliverability.
Learn more about [active and inactive contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-about-active-and-inactive-contacts).

### Can I change a subscribers subscription status?

Yes. [Go to Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard to edit subscription statuses. Learn more [about changing the subscription status of your contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-subscription-status-of-a-contact).
