---
title: "Email Marketing Request: Recalling or Unpublishing a Newsletter"
---

Currently, it is not possible to recall or unpublish a newsletter. We recommend sending a [test newsletter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sending-a-test-email-campaign) before sending it to your subscribers.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
