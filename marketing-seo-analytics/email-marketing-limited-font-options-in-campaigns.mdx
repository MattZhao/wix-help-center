---
title: "Email Marketing: Limited Font Options in Campaigns"
---

Currently, there are a limited number of fonts available when adding or editing text in your email campaigns. Wix Email Marketing provides fewer font options in order to align with email marketing best practices. This decision is also due to the fact that larger mail services, such as Gmail and Yahoo, automatically change all fonts to their preferred default fonts.
