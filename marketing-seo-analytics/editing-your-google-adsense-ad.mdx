---
title: "Editing Your Google Adsense Ad"
---

Want to change the way an ad looks on your site? No problem! You can make changes to such things as the size, color, or whether the ad displays text, an image, or both.

> **Important:**
>
> Every time you make a change to an ad, a new ad code is generated. This code needs to be pasted and activated in the Wix Monetize with AdSense app.

### To edit an existing Google AdSense ad:

In your editor, click the Wix Monetize with AdSense app.   Right-click and click **Copy**.  Navigate to where you want to place the ad.   Right-click and click **Paste**.  Go to the AdSense dashboard and create a new ad.

**Important:** Remember to select Display Ads as the ad type, and not to select "Responsive" as the ad size.  Save the ad and copy the ad code.  Go back to your site and select the Wix Monetize with AdSense app in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Paste the code and click **Activate**.  **Delete the old AdSense App in the editor.**

**Note:** It may take up to 48 hours for the ad to be displayed on your site.
