---
title: "Request: Removing Test Data in Wix Analytics Reports"
---

Currently, it's not possible to remove data from Wix Analytics. However, if you can cancel test orders created in your Store Orders tab or in your Bookings calendar, it will be removed from your Sales reports. Please allow one hour for your reports to update.
