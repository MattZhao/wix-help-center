---
title: "Adding Instagram Videos to Your Instagram Feed"
---

The Instagram Feed app by Wix is a great way to connect your site to your Instagram account and display your posts in customizable gallery on your site.

[Instagram Video](https://business.instagram.com/blog/introducing-instagram-video-combining-igtv-and-instagram-feed) is a new format that combines IGTV videos and feed videos. Instagram Videos that you add in your Instagram app will appear in your Instagram Feed gallery.

[Learn how to add videos on Instagram](https://help.instagram.com/456185931138729)

> **Note:**
>
> - If can take some time for your video to appear in your Instagram Feed gallery after you post it.
> - It is not currently possibly to display Reels in the Instagram Feed app. [Vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-displaying-your-reels-on-your-instagram-feed-app)
