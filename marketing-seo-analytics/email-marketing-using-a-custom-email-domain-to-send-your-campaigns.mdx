A custom email is a branded domain name that can be used for your marketing campaign. Custom email domains reinforce your brand with customers and make it easier for them to find your business later.

Front and center in your campaign, they contain the name of a network or the computer hosting your email address.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you already own a domain, you can add a [business email address](https://www.wix.com/business/email-address) and send your email campaigns from a custom email domain.

### Benefiting from your custom email domain

**Strengthen your brand:** Choosing a custom domain for can help you build brand awareness.   **Build trust:** Emails sent from a custom domain are less likely to be marked as Spam.   **Discoverability:** Search engines look at the root domain, so the more campaigns you run using the same domain, the better your chances are of appearing as a top search.

> **Note:**
>
> Some mail services may reject [email marketing campaigns sent from free public domains](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-campaigns-from-free-public-domains) such as Gmail, Yahoo, and AOL.
