---
title: "Duplicating an Email Marketing Campaign"
---

Easily duplicate an email marketing campaign so you can use it again and again.

### To duplicate an email marketing campaign:

[Go to My Email Campaigns](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Email%20Marketing&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/shoutout/).Click **Manage** next to **Most Recent Email Campaigns**.Hover over the relevant campaign and click the **Show More** icon .Click **Duplicate**.

> **Note:**
>
> If one of your campaigns was declined, you can duplicate it and send again.
