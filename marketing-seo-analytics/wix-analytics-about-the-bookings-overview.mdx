Analyze your bookings performance and gain insights to optimize your business in one place with Wix Analytics’ Bookings Overview. Track the performance of your classes or appointments, see which services are most popular, and monitor staff and client stats.

Whether you run a gym offering classes or a hair salon with individual appointments, see the stats that matter to you and adjust your schedule or class offerings accordingly. This data helps you make informed decisions to better meet client demand and improve your business operations.

You can find the **Bookings Overview** page in the **Overviews **section under **Analytics** in your site's dashboard.

### Choosing your view

To get started, choose a view at the top right according to your [service type](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-the-right-booking-service-for-your-business): **Classes** or **Appointments**. Each view changes the metrics accordingly. For example, the Classes view shows your top classes while the Appointments view shows your most popular times and services.

Once you've chosen your view, display the data range you need:

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **See full report** in a section to view an [in-depth report](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-bookings-analytics-reports) about that Bookings activity.

### Spots filled / Appointments

What you see in this section depends on your chosen view. The Classes view shows you the number of spots that were booked in sessions that occurred in this time period. The Appointments view shows  the number of appointments booked to occur in this time period.

Use the **All services** and **All staff **drop-downs to select specific services and staff members. This data can help you evaluate services and staff performance, and make data-driven decisions.

The progress bar shows you the distribution of spots by attendance status:

Below the progress bar you can see the percentage of cancelled bookings. This is the number of cancelled spots out of the total bookings for sessions that took place in the specified time period.

> **Note:**
>
> For clients to be marked as checked in or a no-show, you must update the [attendance status](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-marking-attendance-for-classes) after each session. This will give you the most accurate attendance breakdown.

### Predicted occupancy / Predicted appointments

Understand and anticipate client demand by checking the upcoming week's predicted occupancy or appointments, so you can be best prepared for what's to come.

What you see in this section depends on your chosen view. The Classes view shows you the predicted occupancy while the Appointments view shows you the predicted appointments.

Click the drop-down to show the predicted occupancy or predicted appointments for the **next week **(7 days starting next Sunday) or the **next 7 days **(7 days starting tomorrow).

The progress bar shows you the following details:

> **Note:**
>
> Predicted occupancy or appointments is the sum of confirmed spots that you already have for the next week/next 7 days and the predicted spots. The predicted numbers rely on the number of confirmed bookings in the past 3-4 weeks.

### Popular times & Popular services

This section shows you what your popular services and times are. This can help you determine if to increase staff or add more services, based on performance. Switch between the **Popular times **and **Popular services **tabs to see different data.

> **Note:**
>
> This section is only available in the Appointments view.

#### Popular times

The heatmap allows you to easily identify the peak times of your business by the number of clients present during each hour. The darker the time slot, the more appointments booked for that time. Hover over a time slot to see the exact number of appointments held at that time.

> **Note:**
>
> The heatmap considers both the start times of sessions and their duration. For example, if a client starts an appointment at 12 pm and it lasts for 2 hours, they are counted as present for the 12 pm and 1pm timeslots.

#### Popular services

See your most popular services and the number of appointments booked per service type. The percentages next to the appointments indicate an increase (green) or decrease (red) in the number of appointments booked, compared to the previous time period.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the Bookings Overview.

### How do I know which view is for me?

Choose your view [according to your business model](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-the-right-booking-service-for-your-business). If you have set times and days that your service takes place, choose the Classes view. If clients can book your services at any time during your working hours, then choose the Appointments view. 
If you use both, such as a yoga studio that provides yoga classes and health treatments, then you can switch between the views.

### Is there a view available for Courses?

Currently, there is not a Courses view available in the Bookings Overview. You can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-request-adding-a-courses-view-to-the-bookings-overview) and use [Bookings analytics reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-bookings-analytics-reports) in the meantime to find the data you need on your courses.

### What's the difference between bookings and spots filled?

**Bookings** include all the bookings that were made in the selected time period, for both completed and upcoming sessions. 
**Spots filled **refers only to the number of booked spots in sessions that have already happened.

### How are at-risk clients determined?

Clients are marked as at-risk when their booking frequency has reduced. For example, a client who used to visit every week and has not visited for three weeks will be marked as at-risk. 
Clients who are considered for this category must have booked more than 4 times in the year and attended at least one session in the last 3 months. This filters out clients who haven't been active for a longer period of time.
