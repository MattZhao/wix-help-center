---
title: "Publishing a Google Sheets Spreadsheet from the Table Master App"
---

The Table Master app makes it possible to upload your Google Sheets spreadsheet and display the data on your site. Before you can upload your spreadsheet, you need to publish it.

### To publish your spreadsheet:

Open your Google Sheets spreadsheet.  Click **File** at the top left.  Click **Share**.  Select **Publish to the web **from the drop-down menu.  Click **Publish**.

**Note:** If your spreadsheet has more than one tab, you need to select the tab you want to use in the Table Master app from the first drop-down menu.

Click **OK **in the pop-up window.  Copy the URL displayed on the **Link** tab and paste it in the **Spreadsheet URL** field in the app settings.
