---
title: "Wix Analytics Request: Adding a Courses View to the Bookings Overview"
---

Currently, it is not possible to choose a [Courses](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-the-right-booking-service-for-your-business#courses) view in the [Bookings Overview](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-about-the-bookings-overview) on your site dashboard. This would allow you to see data about your courses and track their performance.

As a workaround, you can use [Wix Bookings Analytics Reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-bookings-analytics-reports) to see data about your courses.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
