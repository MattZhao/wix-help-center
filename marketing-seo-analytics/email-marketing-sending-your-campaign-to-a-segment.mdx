---
title: "Email Marketing: Sending Your Campaign to a Segment"
---

Send an email campaign to a segment or group of contacts. This allows you to address specific groups of people based on their attributes and activity (e.g. their location, the last time they opened your emails, etc). This targeted approach can help keeping your recipients engaged.

For example, if you create a segment of all contacts who have clicked a link in an email within the last 7 days, you can send only that group a targeted campaign with a special offer, coupon or other incentive.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> [Create your segments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-creating-a-segment) and set filters for these segments by clicking the Segments tab in [Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard.

### To send an email campaign to a segment:

Go to [Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  [Create an email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-and-sending-a-campaign-from-start-to-finish) or [edit an existing one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-managing-your-campaign).  [Customize the elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-elements-to-your-email-campaign) in your campaign to your liking.

**Tip: **[Preview and test your email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign#sending-a-test-email-before-sending-your-campaign) to make sure it is perfect before you add recipients and send.  Click **Next** at the top right.  Select **Target group** under **Who are you sending this email to**.  Click **+ Add Segment** under **Segments**.   Select the checkbox next to the relevant segments to receive your campaign.   Click **Choose Segments**.

**Tip:** If you want to create a new segment with filters for this email campaign, go to the Segments tab in [Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts).  (Optional) Add individual contacts to your recipient list who aren't in the segment.  Click **Next** at the top right to publish your email campaign.  Edit the sender details of your campaign including the subject line, 'From name' and reply-to email.   Click **Send now** to send your campaign to the segment.

**Tip: **You can also send a test email to see how the campaign appears, or click **Schedule** to schedule your campaign.

> **Note:**
>
> Sending an email campaign to a segment counts towards your monthly email marketing balance (both campaign and email limits). You can view all stats in [My Email Campaigns](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20My%20Campaigns&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/shoutout/contacts?referralInfo=sidebar#/my-shoutouts).
