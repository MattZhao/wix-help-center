title: "Google AdSense Request Rejected by Google"
---

Before you can start displaying Google AdSense ads, Google reviews your site. Websites can be rejected for different reasons.

To learn more about what is required to pass the review, refer to this [Google help page](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182?hl=en) and read the information below. After completing the necessary changes, you can resubmit your site.

### Why is my site rejected by Google?

The reason your site has been rejected, is due to insufficient content. Not having enough content on your site will cause Google AdSense to reject your application due to "insufficient content".

Wix sites are built to ensure that all site content is visible and easily extracted by search engines. The structure of Wix sites does not prevent a Google AdSense application from being approved.

We suggest adding more content to your site and resubmitting your site to Google AdSense.

### When to contact Wix:

We recommend adding more content to your site, to see if this solves the issue.
