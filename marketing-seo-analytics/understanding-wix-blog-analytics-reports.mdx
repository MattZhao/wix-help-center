---
title: "Understanding Wix Blog Analytics Reports"
---

Explore trends and gain insights in visitor behaviour on your blog. Wix Analytics reports provide data to answer questions such as "Which blog post has received the most reactions?" and "What day of the week does my blog receive the most views?".

> **Note:**
>
> When a site owner logs in to their Wix account, any visits using the same device & browser to their own sites won't be counted in Wix Analytics reports.

#### Learn more about your blog reports:

[Blog Activity over Time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-blog-analytics-reports#blog-activity-over-time-report)  [Top Blog Posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-blog-analytics-reports#top-blog-posts-report)  [Blog Activity by Time of Day](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-blog-analytics-reports#blog-activity-by-time-of-day)

### Accessing your Blog Reports

1. Go to the [Analytics & Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Analytics%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) page in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to the Blog Reports section.
3. Select the relevant report.

### Switching views within a report

Click the relevant tab at the top of the report to access different chart types. Depending on the report you may see different viewing options.

### Blog Activity over Time Report

The Blog Activity over Time report shows which days, weeks, or months your blog has received the most post views, visitors, reactions, and more.

### Measures available in your Blog Activity over Time Line Chart report

In Line Chart View, click **Select a Measure** to choose another available metric. Measures for this report include:
- Post Views
- Unique Visitors
- Reactions
- Site Sessions
- Comments
- Likes
- Shares

### Columns you can add to your Blog Activity over Time Table report

Click **Select Columns** to add and remove columns of data, including:
- Post Image
- Post Title
- Last Edit Date
- Publish Date
- Featured
- Pinned
- Action Date
- Post Views
- Site Sessions
- Unique Visitors
- Comments
- Likes
- Shares
- Facebook shares
- Linkedin shares
- Link shares
- Reactions

### More available filters

Click **More** to choose from the following filters: 
- Post Title
- Post URL
- Last Edit Date
- Publish Date
- Featured
- Pinned

### Blog Activity by Time of Day
