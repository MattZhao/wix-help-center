A sitemap is an XML file that contains a list of the URLs of the pages on your site. It also contains useful information such as the relationships between pages and when each page was last updated. Every Wix site has a sitemap that updates automatically with your site’s most current information. [Learn more about your site's sitemap](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-your-sites-sitemap-file)

Search engines such as Google use sitemaps to help them crawl and index websites. Wix automatically updates your sitemap when you make changes to your site.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can view your sitemap index by adding *sitemap.xml* to the end of your domain name in the address bar. For example: https://mystunningwebsite.com/**sitemap.xml**.

### Your sitemap and the Wix SEO Setup Checklist

When you connect your site to Google Search Console using the Wix [SEO Setup Checklist](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home/seo-setup?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=SEO+Setup+Checklist), Wix automatically submits your sitemap to Google.

When you make changes to your site, your sitemap will be automatically updated with the changes. There's no need to submit your sitemap to Google again.

> **Important:**
>
> To connect to Google Search Console, you must have a [Premium plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website) and a domain connected to your site. This can be either a domain [purchased from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-from-wix) or a domain purchased [from another domain host](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

> **Tip:**
>
> It can take some time for changes to your site's pages to appear in Google search results. If you want your changes to appear quicker, you can try to [request indexing for the page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/crawling/ask-google-to-recrawl).

### Manually submitting your sitemap to search engines

Each search engine has their own webmaster tools and their own method for submitting sitemaps. Check the steps below for the search engine that you want to submit your sitemap to.

> **Important:**
>
> Before you can submit your sitemap to search engines, you need to verify your ownership of your site's domain with them. [Learn how to verify your domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-the-wix-site-verification-tool)

#### Learn how to submit a sitemap to:

### Google

1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Select your site from the drop-down.
3. Select **Sitemaps**.
4. Enter the URL slug of the sitemap you want to submit under **Add a new sitemap**.

For example, to submit the sitemap for your entire site, enter *sitemap.xml*. 

[image: Submitting a sitemap in Google Search Console]
5. Click **Submit**.

### Bing

1. Go to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters).
2. Select your site from the drop-down.
3. Click **Sitemaps**.
4. Click **Submit sitemap**.
5. Enter the URL of the sitemap you want to submit under **Submit sitemap**. For example, to submit the sitemap for your entire site, enter *https://mystunningwebsite.com/sitemap.xml*. 

[image: Submitting a site map in Bing Webmaster Tools]
6. Click **Submit**.

Tip:You can also resubmit existing sitemaps by selecting the sitemap from the list and clicking **Re-submit**.

### Yandex

1. Go to [Yandex Webmaster Tools](https://webmaster.yandex.com/site/).
2. Select your site from the drop-down.
3. Click **Indexing**.
4. Select **Sitemap tools**.
5. Enter the URL of the sitemap you want to submit under **Add sitemap file**. For example, to submit the sitemap for your entire site, enter https://mystunningwebsite.com/sitemap.xml. 

[image: Submitting a sitemap in Yandex Webmaster Tools]
6. Click **Add**.

Tip:You can also resubmit existing sitemaps clicking the Resubmit icon  beside the sitemap that you want to resubmit.

### Naver

1. Go to [Naver Webmaster Tools](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/console/board#).
2. Select the site that you want to submit the sitemap for.
3. Click **요약** (Request).
4. Select** 사이트맵 제출** (Submit your sitemap).
5. Enter the URL slug of the sitemap you want to submit under **사이트맵 URL 입력** (Enter the sitemap URL).

For example, to submit the sitemap for your entire site, enter sitemap.xml.
6. Click **확인** (Confirm).

### Submitting individual URLs to search engines

You can also submit individual page URLs directly to search engines. For example, if you make changes to one of your blog posts, you should only re-submit that page's URL as it will be more accurate.

Each search engine has their own webmaster tools and their own method for submitting URLs. Check the steps below for the search engine that you want to submit your sitemap to.

> **Important:**
>
> Before you can submit URLs to search engines, you need to verify your ownership of your site. [Learn how to verify your site with search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-the-wix-site-verification-tool)

#### Learn how to submit URLs to:

### Google

1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Select your site from the drop-down.
3. Enter the URL that you want to index in the search bar at the top and click the Enter key.

**Note:** Make sure you enter the full URL, including **"https://www"** in the beginning.
4. Click **Request Indexing**.

### Bing

1. Go to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters).
2. Select your site from the drop-down.
3. Click **URL submission**.
4. Click **Submit URLs**.
5. Enter the URL that you want to index under **Submit URLs**.

**Note:** Make sure you enter the full URL, including **"https://www"** in the beginning.
6. Click **Submit**.

### Yandex

1. Go to [Yandex Webmaster Tools](https://webmaster.yandex.com/site/).
2. Select your site from the drop-down.
3. Click **Indexing**.
4. Select **Reindex pages**.
5. Enter the URL that you want to index under **Reindex pages**.

**Note:** Make sure you enter the full URL, including **"https://www"** in the beginning.
6. Click **Send**.

### Naver

1. Go to [Naver Webmaster Tools](https://searchadvisor.naver.com/console/board#).
2. Select the site that you want to submit the sitemap for.
3. Click **요약** (Request).
4. Select** 웹 페이지 최적화** (Web page collection).
5. Enter the URL slug of the page that you want to index under **수집 요청 URL 입력** (Enter the collection request URL).
6. Click **확인** (Confirm).
