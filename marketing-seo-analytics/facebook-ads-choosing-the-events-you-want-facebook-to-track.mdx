Due to the release of iOS14, Facebook created a new way of managing data called [Facebook Aggregated Event Measurement](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/721422165168355). Tracking on Facebook is now limited to 8 conversion events. Facebook pre-assigns these 8 events based on data from your recent campaign spend, but you can edit the event configurations from the Events Manager.

### Choosing and Configuring Your Events

From your Facebook Events Manager, rank the top 8 events that Facebook should track. Ranking events helps Facebook prioritize what data to collect on your site once someone interacts with your ad. We recommend choosing events in the following order:

Purchase  Initiate Checkout  Add to Cart  View Content

### Full guide to configuring your events

1. Log in to Facebook's [Events Manager](https://www.facebook.com/events_manager).
2. Select the pixel you want to use. If you setup Pixel through Facebook Ads by Wix and have not changed the name, the name begins with '**Wix Pixel**'.
3. Click the **Aggregated Event Measurement **tab under the **Overview** graph.
4. Click **Configure Web Events**.
5. Read through the introductory screens and click **Next.**
6. Select the **Wix domain** you want to configure events for.
7. Click **Manage Events**.
8. Click **Edit.

**
9. In the top right corner, click **Add Event.**
10. Under** Pixel/Custom Conversion**, select the Wix pixel used for Facebook Ads.
11. Select the 4 events mentioned above, plus any additional events you would like.
12. Click **Submit.

**
13. Select the checkbox and click **Apply.
