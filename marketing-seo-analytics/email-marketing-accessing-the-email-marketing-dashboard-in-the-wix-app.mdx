---
title: "Email Marketing: Accessing the Email Marketing Dashboard in the Wix App"
---

Use the email marketing dashboard in the Wix app to access and manage your email campaigns on the go. Create new campaigns, manage your existing ones and look at the most recent statistics and campaign opens all from a mobile device.

> **Note:**
>
> You can only access the dashboard once you've created a campaign.

### To access the email marketing dashboard in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).﻿  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Marketing **and select **Email Marketing**.  Tap the relevant tab:

- **Published:** See all the email campaigns you've sent from mobile and desktop and view the campaign status. You can also [duplicate, archive or send the email to more recipients](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-archiving-deleting-or-duplicating-a-campaign-in-the-wix-owner-app).
- **Drafts: **Access and edit email campaigns you've started working on but haven't sent.
- **Archived: **View all the email campaigns you moved to the archive (either drafts or published campaigns). You can unarchive any campaign or delete it permanently if you no longer need it.
