Wix Analytics lets you download and subscribe to reports for easy access to your site's performance. Download reports to review your data offline and easily share it with others. Subscribe to reports to automatically receive report data without manually checking your dashboard.

For example, if you run an online store, you can download sales reports to analyze your monthly performance and share them with your team. Subscribing to weekly traffic reports can help you stay on top of visitor trends and make timely decisions.

You can find the **All Reports** page under **Analytics** in your site's dashboard.

### Downloading a report

Download your Wix Analytics reports to your computer for offline review or sharing. You can choose from various file formats like Excel, CSV, or Image (PNG).

#### To download a report:

[Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=All%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Select a report.  Click the **Download** drop-down at the top right.  Select a file format (**Excel**, **CSV**, or **Image**).

### Subscribing to a report

Set up automatic email delivery for your reports to stay updated without having to manually check your dashboard. Customize your subscription settings, including the report format, frequency, and delivery time.

#### To subscribe to a report:

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=All%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select a report.
3. Click **Subscribe**.
4. Customize the look and timing of the automated emails:

1. Choose how to view your schedule (e.g. line chart, table).
2. Choose the report time period (e.g. last 7 days, last 30 days).
3. Select the attachment type (CSV, Excel, none).
4. Choose the frequency, day of the week, and hour for your report to be emailed.
5. Add an email subject line.
6. Add a description of the report.
5. Click **Subscribe**.

### Editing a report subscription

Edit your existing report subscriptions to better match your needs. You can adjust the frequency, format, and other settings to ensure you receive the information you need.

#### To edit your subscription:

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=All%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select a report.
3. Click **Edit Subscription **at the top right.
4. Make the relevant changes to the subscription:

1. Choose the report time period (e.g. last 7 days, last 30 days).
2. Select the attachment type (CSV, Excel, none).
3. Choose the frequency, day of the week, and hour for your report to be emailed.
4. Add an email subject line.
5. Add a description of the report.
5. Click **Save**.

### Unsubscribing from a report

You can unsubscribe from a report if you no longer wish to receive the report by email. You can always go back and re-subscribe to the report.

#### To unsubscribe from a report:

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=All%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select a report.
3. Click **Edit Subscription **at the top right.
4. Click **Unsubscribe **at the bottom left.
5. Click **Unsubscribe** again to confirm.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about downloading and subscribing to reports.

### What is the maximum number of rows when exporting a report?

The maximum number of rows you can export in a table report is 5,000 rows.

### Can I subscribe to a custom report?

Currently, it's not possible to subscribe to [custom reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-new-wix-analytics-report). It is only possible to download them.
