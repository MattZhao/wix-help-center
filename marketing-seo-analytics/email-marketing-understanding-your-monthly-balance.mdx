Your email marketing balance shows the number of emails you can send to recipients each month. You can send an unlimited amount of email marketing campaigns as long as you remain within your email limit. You can send 200 emails a month for free, or upgrade your plan to send more emails.

### Actions that impact your monthly balance

Your balance is impacted every time you send or schedule an email, including automated emails.

#### Actions that impact your monthly balance:

**Email marketing campaigns** (including sent, scheduled or resent)  **Email sent using **[**Wix Automations**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-getting-started#reach-out-with-automated-emails-and-track-their-success)  **Scheduling an email campaign:** If you schedule a campaign to be sent to 50 contacts in one week, those 50 emails are automatically deducted from your quota.

#### Actions that do not impact your monthly balance:

**Sending a test email: **You can send yourself a test email or [campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sending-a-test-email-campaign) and see how it looks to subscribers.  **Transactional emails: **Order confirmation, shipping confirmation, password reset, and other transactional emails do not impact your monthly balance.  **Sharing a campaign that has already been published **

### Checking your monthly balance

You can check how many emails you've sent in the current month from your email marketing dashboard. You can also see the date your balance resets. If you've used up your limit for the month you can either wait until the balance resets or upgrade your plan.

#### To check your monthly balance:

Go to [Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Email%20Marketing&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Under **Monthly balance **you can view your current balance as well as a progress bar that shows how close you are to the limit.

### When does my balance reset?

The date your balance and email quota resets is determined by your plan.
- **Free email marketing plans:** The quota resets on the first day of each month at 00:01 UTC for all time zones.
- **Premium email marketing plans:** The quota resets at the same date and time you purchased the plan. For example, if you upgraded your plan on January 5th at 8:15 UTC, your balance renews on February 5th at 8:15 UTC.

### What happens if I try to send an email when my quota is reached?

If you try to send an email once your quota is reached, no new email marketing campaigns will be sent. However, automations will continue to be sent and are not stopped.
