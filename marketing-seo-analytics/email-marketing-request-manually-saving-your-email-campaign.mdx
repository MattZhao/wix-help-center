---
title: "Email Marketing Request: Manually Saving Your Email Campaign"
---

Currently, it's not possible to manually save your email campaigns.

The email campaign composer has an autosave feature to prevent you from losing valuable information if you forget to save or your internet disconnects. As you work in the email campaign composer, you'll see **Saving… **and** Saved** at the top left corner.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
