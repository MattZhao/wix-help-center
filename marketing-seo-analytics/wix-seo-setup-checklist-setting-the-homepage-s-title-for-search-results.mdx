After you update your homepage's [title tag](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-seo-title-tags-and-meta-descriptions-to-your-pages), you may see an error message in your [Wix SEO Setup Checklist](https://support.wix.com/en/article/improving-your-online-presence-with-the-wix-seo-setup-checklist). This can happen if you add a title tag that does not include your keywords or business name. Follow the steps below to resolve the issue.

---

### Updating your homepage's title tag

Update your homepage's title tag from your SEO Setup Checklist.

Wix EditorStudio EditorDashboard
1. [Go to the SEO Setup Checklist](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp%2F1480c568-5cbd-9392-5604-1148f5faffa0%3FreferralInfo%3Dseo_wiz_launch_lp&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=SEO%20Setup%20Checklist) in your site's SEO dashboard.
2. Hover over the error message and click **Go for it**.
3. Select a suggested title tag from the list, or select **Other **to enter your own title tag.

**Tips:**

- Use at least one of your keywords and your business name.
- Enter between 55-70 characters.
- If you create your own title tag, we recommend checking our tips for writing your own title tag.

3. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
4. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to your homepage.
5. Click **SEO basics**.
6. Paste the page title under **Title tag (title in search results)**.
7. Click **Publish**.
8. Go back to your SEO Setup Checklist.
9. Click **Refresh**.

### Tips for writing title tags

We recommend following the tips below if you decide to create your own title tags for your pages.

Each page on your site should have its own unique title tag.  The title tag should provide concise information on what the page is about.   You should include one of your keywords and your business or site name.   It's also a good idea to include the location of your business to your homepage title tag. This will help Google know to display your site to searchers in your area.  The length of your title tag should be 55-70 characters. Use the pipe sign ‘|’ to distinguish different parts of your title tag.
