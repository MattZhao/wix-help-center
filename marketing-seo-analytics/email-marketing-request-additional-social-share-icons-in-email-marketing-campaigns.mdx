---
title: "Email Marketing Request: Additional Social Share Icons in Email Marketing Campaigns"
---

We are excited to announce that you can [add social share icons to the footer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-customizing-the-footer-of-your-campaign?tabs=Design#customizing-the-footer-of-your-campaign) of your email campaigns. This allows your subscribers to share it to their Facebook, Twitter and Pinterest accounts and now other social media networks like LinkedIn and Instagram.
