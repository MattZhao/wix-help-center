---
title: "Email Marketing Request: Dark Mode for Email Campaigns"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create a "Dark Mode" version of your email marketing campaign that appears when a subscriber's device has Dark Mode enabled. Wix does not have the ability to control how different viewers see your email campaign as it depends on the subscribers' settings.

We are always working to improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
