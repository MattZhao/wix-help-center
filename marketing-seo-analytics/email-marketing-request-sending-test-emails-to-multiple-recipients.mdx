---
title: "Email Marketing Request: Sending Test Emails to Multiple Recipients"
---

Currently, when sending a test email campaign, it is only possible to send it to one email address at a time. If you want to send it to more recipients, you need to send your test email to each email address individually.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
