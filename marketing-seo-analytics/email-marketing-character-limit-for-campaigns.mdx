---
title: "Email Marketing: Character Limit for Campaigns"
---

Your email campaign does not have a character limit, however short and sweet is usually the way to go. Email campaigns with extensive amounts of text are more likely to be marked as spam by your subscribers.

> **Tip:**
>
> Unless you are sending your subscribers a blog post or a message that relies on text, it is preferable to stick to 1 to 3 paragraphs.
