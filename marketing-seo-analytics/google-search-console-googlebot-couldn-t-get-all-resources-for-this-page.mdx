---
title: "Google Search Console: \"Googlebot couldn't get all resources for this page\""
---

Sometimes Google encounters URLs that it cannot crawl. When this happens it may show an error in Google Search Console saying "Googlebot couldn't get all resources for this page".

If you received this message for URLs beginning with **frog.wix.com**, please note that these URLs are part of the structure of your Wix site. They do not affect Google's ability to crawl your site's content, and do not impact on your site's SEO.

If you encounter a blocked resource that you believe should not be blocked, contact us with a [screenshot](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-screenshot-to-a-ticket) of the full screen along with the domain name (e.g., subdomain.wix.com).
