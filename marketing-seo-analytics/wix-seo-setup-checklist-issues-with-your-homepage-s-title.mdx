title: "Wix SEO Setup Checklist: Issues with Your Homepage's Title"
---

The business name or site name in your SEO Checklist is determined by the information you entered when you initially set up the SEO Setup Checklist.

In order to resolve this issue, make sure that the business and site name in the SEO Setup Checklist matches your page's [title tag and meta description](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-seo-title-tags-and-meta-descriptions-to-your-pages), and the content of your site.

You can check your Business or Site Name by clicking the Edit details icon** ** next to your keywords. Here, you can also edit your location to match the location written on your site.
