---

Send visitors who click on your Google Ads campaign to a specific landing page in order to drive conversions and attract potential customers. By creating a well-designed landing page with a tailored offer, you can showcase an individual product or service, or persuade your audience to take a specific action.

> **Not sure what to include in your landing page?**
>
> We've crafted some suggested AI prompts to help get you writing. Once you create a landing page, add these prompts [into the AI text generator](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-ai-generated-text).

### How do I access the AI text generator in the editor?

1. [Add a text element or edit an existing text element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text).
2. Click **Create AI text**.

---

### Create a captivating headline

The headline is the first thing your audience sees, so it should be attention-grabbing and compelling. Choose language that directly addresses your target audience's needs or pain points.

### View AI prompt

Craft a captivating headline that highlights the transformative power of **\{your business\}** and creates a sense of excitement and urgency.

### View an example

"Unlock Your Inner Zen: Yoga for the Stressed Professional"

---

### Choose an engaging description

Expand on the headline by providing more details about what your business offers. Highlight the unique benefits and features that set your business apart from competitors.

### View AI prompt

Write an engaging description that showcases the key benefits of **\{your business\}** and encourages potential customers to take the next step.

### View an example

Discover true balance and tranquility through our tailored yoga programs. Increase flexibility, and promote overall well-being--all in a serene, judgment-free environment.

---

### List the main business benefits

Clearly list out the main 3-5 benefits your business provides. Use concise, impactful phrases that resonate with your target audience and convey key value propositions.

### View AI prompt

List the main benefits of **\{your business\}** in a concise and compelling way using bullet points.

### View an example

Breathe Yoga
- Personalized yoga sessions
- Stress relief and relaxation
- Increased flexibility
- Holistic mind-body balance

---

### Include a compelling form

Include a form on the landing page to capture leads. Add a motivating title to encourage form submissions. Also make sure you provide a clear description of what to expect after someone submits the form.

### View AI prompt

Craft a motivating form title, a clear description, and a creative CTA button that encourages potential customers to submit the form for **\{your business\}**.

### View an example

- **Form title:** Find your inner peace
- **Description:** Take the first step toward a more balanced, centered life. Complete the form to claim your free introductory session.
- **CTA: **Book my free session

---

### Build credibility with social proof

Build trust in your business by showcasing customer testimonials and success stories. Select a short, compelling quote that demonstrates the positive impact your business has had.

### View an example

"This yoga studio has been a sanctuary for me. After only a few sessions, I was able to calm my restless mind and rediscover my inner peace."

---

### Highlight additional benefits

Beyond the core benefits, highlight any other unique advantages, special features, or value-adds your business provides.

### View AI prompt

List any additional unique benefits or features of **\{your business\}**, along with attention-grabbing headlines and concise descriptions.

### View examples

- **Headline: **World-class instructors
- **Description:** Our renowned yoga instructors bring decades of experience, rigorously trained to guide you toward optimal well-being.

- **Headline: **Serene studio environment
- **Description: **Practice yoga in our tranquil, distraction-free studio designed to calm your mind and rejuvenate your spirit.

---

### Explain why they should choose you

Dedicate a section to explaining why potential customers should choose your business over competitors. Highlight your unique business offerings and success stories.

### View AI prompt

Write a compelling section that explains why potential customers should choose **\{your business\}**, highlighting your proven track record, satisfied customers, and commitment to their success.

### View an example

For over 15 years, our yoga studio has been a sanctuary for busy professionals seeking a rest from life's stresses. Hundreds of clients have experienced the transformative power of our holistic yoga programs tailored to their unique needs. Our entire team is devoted to your journey toward a more balanced, centered life.

---

### Have strong calls-to-action

Throughout the landing page, integrate clear calls-to-action that motivate visitors to take the desired next step, such as making a purchase or scheduling a consultation.

### View AI prompt

Craft a short motivating call-to-action that encourages potential customers to take the next step with **\{your business\}**, using persuasive language and creating a sense of urgency or scarcity.

### View an example

Find your inner calm today. Our limited-time new client offer provides 25% off your first month unlock this exclusive deal now before it's gone!

---

### End with an engaging 'thank you' page

After someone takes action, send them to a thank you page that reinforces the value of their decision. Make sure to also clarify what the next steps will be.

### View AI prompt

Write an engaging thank you page headline and description that reinforces the value of taking action with **\{your business\}** and sets expectations for the next steps.

### View an example

- **Headline:** Congratulations! You're one step closer to achieving balance
- **Description:** We're delighted to welcome you to our yoga community. One of our compassionate instructors will reach out soon to discuss your needs and craft your personalized journey to a more centered life.
