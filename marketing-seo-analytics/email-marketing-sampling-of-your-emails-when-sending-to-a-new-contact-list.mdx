---
title: "Email Marketing: Sampling of Your Emails When Sending to a New Contact List"
---

When you send an email campaign to a new list of contacts, Wix may first send a sample mailing. This means that a portion of your recipients receive your content before the email is distributed to all of the contacts on that list.

### Why is a sample mailing sent at all?

This is done for your protection. If a list contains too many wrong addresses, or too many emails are bounced/rejected, your sending reputation can be damaged. A low sending reputation impacts the deliverability of your future mailings.

### How many of my contacts will receive the sample?

Exactly how many samples are sent is calculated on a case-by-case basis.

### How can a sample mailing fail?

If a certain percentage of your emails are bounced or rejected, your email won't be sent to the rest of your contacts yet.

### What happens if my sample fails?

In the event that your sample fails, two things occur:
1. This mailing is listed as "Not Sent to all" under My Email Campaigns.
2. The contacts that did not receive your email will be listed in the [Statistics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-your-email-marketing-statistics)  of that specific campaign, with the "Not Sent" label.

### So what should I do?

1. Make sure that your list contains only valid addresses. Once the emails are validated, make sure you're only adding valid contacts before you send out a new campaign. Any invalid contacts must be removed from your [Contact list](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Contact%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/contacts/).

**Tip:** Additionally make sure you only send your emails to contacts who have given you their consent, using a [subscribe form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-subscribe-form).
2. When your list is ready, try sending your email campaign again.
