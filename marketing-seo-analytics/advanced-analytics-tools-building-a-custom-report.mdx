title: "Advanced Analytics Tools: Building a Custom Report"
---

Create a new Wix Analytics report to answer specific questions that weren’t addressed in existing pre-built reports. Learn how to create a new report, and how to use tools such as report tiles, analytics tables, Measures and Dimensions.

> **Important:**
>
> - Before creating a new report, check if a [pre-built report](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-analytics-reports#about-the-report-categories) matches your needs. You can also [customize the report and save a new view](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-wix-analytics-reports) to focus on the data that interests you most.
> - To create a new report or [duplicate and edit a report](https://support.wix.com/en/article/duplicating-and-editing-a-wix-analytics-report), you must upgrade your Wix site to the [Business Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) (for Wix Editor users) or an [Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) (for Studio users).

---

### Step 1 | Create a new report

Get started by giving your new custom report a name and a description.

#### To create a new report:

[Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=All%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  at the top right and select **Create a custom report**.  Click **Get Started**.  Add your report name and description.  Click **Create Your Report**

---

### Step 2 | Add a tile to your report

A tile is a visualization of your data. To create a tile you’ll need to choose a topic (Explore), the data you are interested in (Measures and Dimensions), and how you want to display your data (Visualization).

#### To add a tile to your report:

Click **Edit Dashboard.**  Click **Add** and select **Visualization**.

---

### Step 3 | Choose an Explore

Choose an Explore from the list on the left. Each Explore focuses on different elements of your data. The Explore is considered the topic of your tile.

### How to choose an Explore

Each Explore provides you with the ability to add content to your report based on the data it contains.
**There are ****various Explores**** you can choose from:**
- Use the **Sales** Explore if you want your tile to answer questions about your sales. For example:

- How many orders have used a coupon?
- How many orders got a refund?
- Use **Item Sales** Explore if you want your tile to answer a question about items you sell. For example:

- What are my best seller products?
- How many items are being ordered on average per order?
- Use **Traffic** Explore if you want your tile to answer questions about your site traffic or about your site visitors. For example:

- What are your most viewed pages?
- What are your most popular traffic sources (e.g. facebook, google, instagram).
- What device your visitors are using?
- How many visitors visited your site in the past month?
- From where are they coming from?
- Use **People** Explore if you want your tile to answer questions about your site visitors: For example:

- How many times has a person ordered something?

### Step 4 | Choose Measures and Dimensions

Measures and Dimensions include all of the metrics you can include in your reports.

You can choose:

Once you've chosen your Measures and Dimensions, name your Tile in the **Edit Tile** window.

### How to choose Measures and Dimensions

The first thing you need to do is decide what you want to find out. Then, you’ll need to break it down and think about the data you want to display in terms of Measures and Dimensions.
Understanding the difference between Measures and Dimensions will help you to choose the right content for your tile. For example, data related to “Customers” can be both dimensions, and measures. To demonstrate this we‘ve used the Sales Explore, and the data fields we can choose from are related to sales.
[image: dimensions and measures]
**Customer name is a Dimension**
- It’s qualitative.
- You can’t operate math functions within it.
More Dimensions examples include: Order date, product name, Customer address, Phone number, etc.**Customers as a Measure**
- Customer as a measure refers to “Customers count”, the amount of customers that did something.
- It’s quantitative - you can **count** how many customers bought something.
- You can operate math functions within it, for example “ sum of how many customers bought something yesterday”

More Measures examples: Site visits count, total, money spent, number of orders, etc.
**Let's practice:**When you know what data to add to your report, you’ll need to find the relevant data fields in the data fields menu.
For example: Let’s find out **how many customers** used **coupon codes**. To find out, we chose “coupon name” as a Dimension, and “Customers count” as a Measure.

### Step 5 | Run your report

To run your report, click **Run **at the top right. Your results will load in the data pane.

---

### Step 6 | Choose how to view your report

You can choose to see your data in a table, bar chart, columns, scatterplot, line, area, pie chart, map, or single value.

Click your preferred visualization icon, and your data will be displayed accordingly.

---

### Step 7 | Save your changes

Click **Save**, and the tile will be added to your report.

  (Optional) To resize a tile after saving, click the lower right corner of the tile and drag to adjust.

  Click **Save **to create your report.

**Tip:** Once saved, your report will be available in your **Custom reports** list.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about custom reports.

### Can I subscribe to a custom report?

Currently, it's not possible to subscribe to custom reports. It is only possible to download them.

### Can I combine different metrics are data models in the Custom report?

It is not possible to create a custom report combining different data models (and combining metrics from different models). However, custom dashboards can have multiple reports (or tiles) built over different models.

### After upgrading my site, how long does it take for custom reports to be available?

Custom reports are available in your account as soon as you purchase a [Business Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) (for Wix Editor) or an [Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) (for Studio), and assign it to your site.
