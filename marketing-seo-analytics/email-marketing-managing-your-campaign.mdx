title: "Email Marketing: Managing Your Campaign"
---

View and manage your email campaigns from the Email Marketing dashboard. You can continue editing an email you started working on, reuse a campaign you already sent, reschedule a campaign and much more.

The dashboard is divided into 4 tabs, based on the campaign status:

**Published: **Campaigns that have been sent.  **Scheduled: **Campaigns that have been set to be sent at a later date.  **Draft:** Campaigns that haven't been sent or scheduled yet.   **Archive:** Campaigns that are no longer in use.

### Managing your email campaigns

You can manage your campaigns at any point from your site's dashboard. The actions available vary based on the email campaign status. For example, you can edit drafts and scheduled emails, reuse a published campaign, etc.

#### To manage an email campaign:

[Go to Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **View all Campaigns**.  Choose the type of campaign you want to manage:

### Published

1. Click the **Published** tab.
2. Choose what to do next:

- **Reuse this campaign:** Click **Reuse** to use the published campaign again. Edit as needed and send or schedule as a new campaign.
- **View campaign statistics:** Click the **View statistics **icon  to see stats on your email campaigns, recipients, or social media.
- **Send this campaign again: **Resend an email to recipients who haven't opened it after 24 hours.
- **Other management options:** Click the **More Actions** icon  to view the campaign, share to social media, duplicate, rename, save as a template, archive or delete.

### Scheduled

1. Click the **Scheduled** tab.
2. Choose what to do next:

- **Edit a campaign: **Click **Edit** to edit your campaign before it sends.
- **Reschedule a campaign: **

1. Click the** Reschedule **icon  next to the relevant scheduled email campaign.
2. Select the date you want using the calendar.
3. Set the time in the drop-down.
4. Click **Reschedule**.
- **Other management options: **Click the **More Actions** icon  to view the campaign, share to social media, duplicate, rename, save as a template, archive or delete.

**Notes:** 
- Pausing a scheduled email campaign prevents it from being sent until you choose to send or schedule it again.
- Creating a scheduled campaign requires an email marketing plan

### Drafts

1. Click the **Drafts** tab.
2. Choose what to do next:

- **Edit a campaign: **Click **Edit** to continue editing your draft.
- **Other management options: **Click **More Actions** icon  to view the campaign, share to social media, duplicate, rename, save as a template, archive or delete.

### Archive

1. Click the **Archive** tab.
2. Choose what to do next:

- **Edit a campaign: **Click **Edit** to continue editing an email you've previously worked on.
- **Other management options: **Click **More Actions** icon  to view the campaign, share to social media, duplicate, rename, save as a template, archive or delete.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about managing your email marketing campaigns.

### Can I restore a deleted campaign?

No, deleting a campaign permanently removes it. When you delete a campaign, it is no longer viewable at its previous URL or in your Email Marketing dashboard. 
The email will still appear in the inbox of your recipients, however, it will no longer be possible to view the campaign, or any of its links in a** browser**. 
**Tip:** If you think you might need the campaign sometime in the future, you can archive it instead.

### Can I share a campaign to social media?

Yes, share a campaign to social media right after sending it. Click **Share on Social **and then select the relevant social media platforms where you want to share the campaign.

### If I add new contacts after scheduling a campaign, will they receive it as well?

If you add new contacts (members, subscribers, etc.) to your contact list after you've already scheduled a campaign, those new contacts are **not** automatically added to the campaign. You need to edit the campaign to add new recipients and then reschedule.

### If I delete or pause an email campaign, will that increase my available quota?

If you delete or pause scheduled email campaign, they will not be returned to your email quota.

### If I change my default sender details, will it affect scheduled campaigns?

Yes. If changes were made to your default sender details before a scheduled campaign is sent, the scheduled campaign will inherit those updated details. It will not keep the sender details set before the change.
