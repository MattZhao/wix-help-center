### Upgrading your social media marketing plan

When you start creating a new post, you can click the option to upgrade your social media marketing plan.

#### To upgrade your social media marketing plan:

[Go to Social Media Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Social%20Media%20Marketing&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/social-marketing-web) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Create New Post**.   Click **Upgrade to Publish More**.  Choose the plan and checkout.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the social media marketing plan.

### Why should I maintain a social media presence for my business?

Publishing to social media is essential for your business’s marketing strategy - to get noticed, connect with customers, and build your brand. Regularly posting engaging, relevant content is a great way to keep your audience engaged, drive site traffic and sales, and ultimately grow your business.

### What are the benefits of upgrading your social media marketing plan?

Upgrading your plan will provide valuable tools to enhance your social media presence and support a comprehensive strategy. With a plan, you can publish unlimited posts, essential for maintaining regular and consistent activity. Connecting multiple accounts allows you to manage and view all your business's posts in one place. Also, accessing Wix Analytics data provides insights into your content’s performance across platforms. Scheduling posts not only saves time but also helps you plan ahead effectively.

### Why should I publish to social media through Wix?

Publishing to social media through Wix allows you to manage your entire online presence (site and social media) in one place. Also, you’ll get holistic data tracking that seamlessly links your site and social media performance, offering insights unavailable on standalone social platforms.

### Can site collaborators also publish to social media using these tools?

Yes. Site collaborators can also use social media marketing tools, if they are a site owner and/or have Marketing Manager [permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview#roles-for-integrated-wix-apps).

### How does the social media marketing plan differ from other plans at Wix?

Wix offers several plans, which are all different subscription services. A Premium plan enhances your site's capabilities, while a social media marketing plan allows you to connect social accounts and manage your social media strategy, all from your site's dashboard. Other plans include the [Logo Maker plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans#logo-and-online-presence-plans) and [Hopp by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-an-overview).

### What payment methods are accepted for plan subscriptions?

Wix accepts a wide range of payment methods, including major cards such as VISA, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, JCB and Diners. Other payment methods may also be available depending on where you are located.
