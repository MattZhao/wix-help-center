Duplicate and edit an existing Wix Analytics report to create tailored reports for your business needs.

> **Important:**
>
> - Before duplicating and editing a report, check if a [pre-built report](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-analytics-reports#about-the-report-categories) matches your needs. You can also [customize the report and save a new view](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-wix-analytics-reports) to focus on the data that interests you.
> - To create a new report or [duplicate and edit a report](https://support.wix.com/en/article/duplicating-and-editing-a-wix-analytics-report), you must upgrade your Wix site to the [Business Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) (for Wix Editor users) or an [Elite plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) (for Studio users).

### Step 1 | Duplicate a report

When a report is duplicated, only the current view (table, chart, map) is copied over into the new version. The duplicate report will not include other views. You can customize the duplicate report through the report builder interface.

#### To duplicate a report:

[Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=All%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Select the report you want to duplicate.  Click the **More Actions** icon  at the top right and select **Duplicate and edit report**.

Click **Start Now** on the pop up window.  Add your report title, select the display type (e.g. line chart, table), and a description which any collaborator you’re working with may find useful.  Click** Duplicate**.

> **Tip:**
>
> After duplicating and saving a report, you'll be able to access it in your **Custom Reports** list on the **All Reports **page.

### Step 3 | Edit an existing tile

A tile is a visualization of your data which answers a specific question - For example, “Where are my customers located?”.

To edit an existing tile you’ll need to edit the data it holds (Measures and Dimensions). You can also edit the way your data is displayed by changing the visualization type.

> **Important:**
>
> When you edit an existing tile you should take into account that you cannot change the tile [Explore](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-new-wix-analytics-report#step-4-choose-an-explore). This means that your tile topic cannot be changed, and can only contain data of the same topic (Sales, Item Sales, Traffic, People, etc).

#### To edit an existing tile:

Click the **More Actions** icon .  Click **Edit**.

Change the Tile data by selecting and deselecting Dimensions and Measures.

### How to choose Dimensions and Measures

Dimensions and Measures include all of the metrics you can include in your reports. You can choose:
- Single measures. This is ideal to present a single value chart, such as total value, number of customers, or number of orders.
- Measure + Dimension. This is useful when you want to add a line chart with an x and y axis, bar chart, or pie chart.
- Multiple dimensions + multiple measures. This is useful when you want to add a table tile.

The first thing you need to do is decide what you want to find out. Then, you’ll need to break it down and think about the data you want to display in terms of Dimensions and Measures.Understanding the difference between Dimensions and Measures will help you to choose the right content for your tile. 
For example, data related to “Customers” can be both dimensions, and measures. To demonstrate this we‘ve used the Sales Explore, and the data fields we can choose from are related to sales.

**Customer name is a Dimension**
- It’s qualitative.
- You can’t operate math functions within it.
More Dimensions examples include: Order date, product name, Customer address, Phone number, etc.**Customers as a Measure**
- Customer as a measure refers to “Customers count”, the amount of customers that did something.
- It’s quantitative - you can **count** how many customers bought something.
- You can operate math functions within it, for example “sum of how many customers bought something yesterday”
More Measures examples: Site visits count, total, money spent, number of orders, etc.
**Let's practice:**
When you know what data to add to your report, you’ll need to find the relevant data fields in the data fields menu.
For example: Let’s find out **how many customers** used **coupon codes**. To find out, we chose “coupon name” as a Dimension, and “Customers count” as a Measure.

### Step 4 | Run your report

To run your report, click **Run **at the top right. Your results will load in the data pane.

---

### Step 5 | Choose how to view your report

You can choose to see your data in a table, bar chart, columns, scatterplot, line, area, pie chart, map, or single value.

Click your preferred visualization icon, and your data will be displayed accordingly.

---

### Step 6 | Save your updated tile

Click **Save**, and the tile will be updated.

---

### Step 7 | (Optional) Add additional tiles

You can [add additional tiles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-new-wix-analytics-report#step-2-add-a-tile-to-your-report) to enrich your reports. Your new tiles can have different subjects such as sales, items sales, traffic or people.

---

### Step 8 | Save your updated report

Once you're done editing your report, click **Save **at the top right.
