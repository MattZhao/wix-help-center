---
title: "Request: Moving Marketing Integrations to Another Wix Site"
---

Currently, you cannot transfer any tracking tools or analytics that you've set up in Marketing Integrations from one site to another site in your Wix account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
