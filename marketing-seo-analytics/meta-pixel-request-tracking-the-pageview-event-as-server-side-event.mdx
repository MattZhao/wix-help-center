---
title: "Meta Pixel Request: Tracking the PageView Event as Server-side event"
---

Currently, it is only possible to track a PageView event for [Meta Pixel conversion tracking](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/meta-pixel/implementation/conversion-tracking/) as a browser-side event. This means that when a visitor's opens your page on your site, their browser loads your Meta Pixel and sends a PageView event. It is not possible to track a page visit as a server-side event sent from Wix's servers.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
