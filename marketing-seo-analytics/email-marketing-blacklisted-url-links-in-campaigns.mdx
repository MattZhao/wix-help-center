If you receive an error when trying to add a URL to your campaign, it's likely the URL is blacklisted. URLs are blacklisted by major ISPs like Google, YAHOO!, MSN, and anti-spam organizations like Spamhaus, Barracuda, and Spamcop, among others.

> **Important:**
>
> Wix does not send campaigns containing blacklisted URLs as they can harm recipients' accounts or devices, Wix's reputation, or even lead to ISPs blocking all emails from your email or IP addresses.
