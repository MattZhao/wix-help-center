---
title: "SEO Settings for Multilingual Sites"
---

Wix Multilingual lets you maintain a site in multiple languages to drive traffic from international visitors. You can use SEO Settings to apply changes to all languages automatically.

Instead of editing each page's individual settings, you can customize the settings that apply to entire sections of your site. This way, you can create a systematic SEO strategy much more quickly.

Any changes you make to your SEO Settings affect the related pages regardless of their language (e.g. Product pages, Site pages, etc). Once you save your edits, no action is required to ensure the pattern supports other languages.

[Learn more about SEO Settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929)

> **Tip:**
>
> - You can also [customize the SEO Basics settings of individual pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-optimizing-seo-settings-for-multilingual-sites) for every version of your site.
> - By default, Wix adds hreflang tags to your pages so search engines can identify their language and show the correct version to your visitors.
