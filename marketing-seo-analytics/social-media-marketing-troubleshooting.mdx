### Troubleshooting local issues

Often issues arise locally, which means they can be resolved at home. Fixing local issues helps your computer's performance. We recommend trying the following:

Disconnecting and reconnecting to your internet. Clearing your [cache](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-your-browsers-cache) and [cookies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-wix-cookies-from-your-browser).Opening your site in a different browser.

### Troubleshooting publishing your post

If you receive an error message or the account you're looking for isn't appearing at the connection stage, try the troubleshooting steps below for the relevant social network.

#### Troubleshooting publishing to Facebook

### Step 1 | Make sure you have a Facebook Business page

The option to share to Facebook is only available for Facebook business pages and not personal profile pages. If you don't have a Facebook Business page yet, learn how to create one [here](https://www.facebook.com/pages/creation/).

### Step 2 | Check you have given Wix the right Facebook sharing permissions

1. Click **Sharing Options** in the error message.
2. Make sure you've selected all the checkboxes including **All Pages** at the top.
3. Click **Next**.
4. Click **Confirm**.

#### Troubleshooting publishing to Instagram

### Step 1 | Make sure you've switched your Instagram account to a Instagram Business account

1. Open the Instagram app.
2. Go to your profile.

**Note**: If you see **View professional dashboard** at the top, you already have a business account. If you don't, continue with the steps below.
3. Tap the Menu icon .
4. Tap **Settings**.
5. Tap **Account**.
6. Tap **Switch account type**.
7. Tap **Switch to business account**.
8. Tap **Switch**.

### Step 2 | Check that your Facebook page is connected to your Instagram Business account

Follow the steps below to check that your Facebook page is connected to your Instagram Business account, or connect a page if you haven't already. 

1. Open the Instagram app.
2. Go to your profile.
3. Tap **Edit Profile**.
4. Tap **Page **under **Public business information**.
5. Check that you have a Facebook page connected. If you don't, tap **Connect an Existing Page.**
6. Choose the Facebook Business page you want to connect.
7. Tap **Done**.

### Step 3 | Make sure you're signed in to the correct Facebook account

Check in [Facebook Business Manager](https://business.facebook.com/settings/pages) that the Facebook account you're using contains the Facebook page linked to your Instagram Business account. 
If you previously connected an Instagram personal account, you may need to [clear Wix cookies from your browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-wix-cookies-from-your-browser) before connecting.

### Still need help?

If, after following these steps, you are still having issues, please [contact us](http://www.wix.com/contact) and provide these details:

### Information to add to your support ticket

- The name or URL of your Wix site.
- The name and version of the web browser you're using.
- The name and version of your computer's operating system.
- A description of the steps you've taken so that we can recreate the issue on our end
- A screenshot of the issue
