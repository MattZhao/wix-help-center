---
title: "Uploading a Google Sheets Spreadsheet to the Table Master App"
---

You can upload a Google Spreadsheet to the Table Master app to display the contents directly on your site.

> **Important:**
>
> Some organizations do not allow spreadsheets to be fully public, which means that you won't be able to share them on the app.

#### Learn how to:

[Publish your Google Sheets spreadsheet](https://support.wix.com/en/article/uploading-a-google-sheets-spreadsheet-to-the-table-master-app#step-1-publish-your-google-sheets-spreadsheet)  [Add your spreadsheet URL to Table Master](https://support.wix.com/en/article/uploading-a-google-sheets-spreadsheet-to-the-table-master-app#step-2-add-the-url-to-your-table-master-app)

---

### Step 1 | Publish Your Google Sheets Spreadsheet

Before you can upload your Google Sheets spreadsheet, you need to publish it and get the public URL for it.

#### To publish your spreadsheet:

Open your Google Sheets spreadsheet.  Click **File** at the top left.  Click **Share**.  Select **Publish to the web **from the drop-down menu.  Click **Publish**.

Click **OK **in the pop-up window.  Copy the URL displayed on the **Link** tab.

---

### Step 2 | Add Your Spreadsheet URL to Table Master

After you publish your Google Sheets spreadsheet, you can add it to your Table Master app in your site's Editor.

#### To add the URL:

Click the Table Master app in your Editor.  Click **Settings**.  Select **Data**.  Select **Google Spreadsheet URL**.  Paste your Google Sheets spreadsheet URL under **Spreadsheet URL**.  Click **Submit**.

> **Note:**
>
> It may take some time for updates to be visible on your live site.
