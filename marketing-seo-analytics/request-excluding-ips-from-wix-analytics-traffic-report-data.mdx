---
title: "Request: Excluding IPs from Wix Analytics Traffic Report Data"
---

Currently, it's not possible to exclude a specific IP address from [Wix Analytics Traffic Reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-about-your-traffic-reports).
