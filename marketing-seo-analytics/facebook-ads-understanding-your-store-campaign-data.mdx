> **Important:**

> This article is only for campaigns who have selected the 'get more store orders' goal.

Your Facebook Ads dashboard provides you with extensive campaign data imported directly from Facebook. For campaigns run with Wix Stores, you will see what purchases and revenue has been attributed to your campaign.

### How Facebook Decides Which Purchases Come From a Campaign

Facebook uses an **attribution model** to determine which customer actions (such as 'add to cart' and purchases) were as a result of your campaign. An [attribution model](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/370704083280490?id=399393560487908) is a rule, or set of rules, that determine how to assign credit for sales and conversions based on online touchpoints.

### Circumstances In Which Campaign and Store Order Data Might Differ

When viewing your campaign from your store orders, you may see a discrepancy between Facebook's data and your purchases list. This gap could be happening due to the following reasons:

### Facebook counts the total transactions that come from your campaign, including those made in other places on your site

If a customer visits your site after clicking an ad and makes a payment, it counts as campaign data even if the transaction came from somewhere other than your store. For example, if you own a fitness studio and a customer buys a store product but then also purchases a fitness class on your Bookings page, both transactions show as Facebook Ads dashboard data even though you only sold one store product.

### Facebook counts purchases based on a specific site 'event'

Facebook tracks purchases based on customers completing a certain 'event' or action on your site such as making a purchase and seeing a 'thank you page.' However, if there's an issue after this event, such as a declined payment, the purchase won't appear in your store orders list even though it still counts as campaign data.

### Facebook provides campaign revenue data in $USD before converting

Facebook reports revenue data from your campaign in US dollars. This data is then converted to your store's local currency. Between conversion and exchange rates, you may see a slight gap between your dashboard data and store orders list.

---

### How Cookie Banners Affect Your Campaign Data

If you've activated the [cookie consent banner](https://support.wix.com/en/article/displaying-a-cookie-banner-on-your-site) it could affect your campaign data. Facebook Ads with Wix uses a Facebook Pixel placed on your site to track visitors and optimize ads. However, if a customer does not consent to the cookie banner (thus not consenting to the Facebook Pixel tracking) your Facebook Ads campaign won't be able to see or collect any data from them.
