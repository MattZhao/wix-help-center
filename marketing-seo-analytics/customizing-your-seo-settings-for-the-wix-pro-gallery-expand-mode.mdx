Your Wx Pro Gallery can display images, videos, and text in multiple layouts. You can set it to open [Expand Mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-an-overview#let-visitors-take-a-closer-look-at-images) when an item is clicked. Each of these Expand Mode pages aren't regular pages on your site, but they do have their own URLs and search engine information.

The Wix Pro Gallery has a default SEO Settings setup to provide search engines good information about your gallery's items like its title tags and meta descriptions. You can customize these settings to edit what's indexed by Google.

### Editing your SEO Settings for the Wix Pro Gallery

You can edit the default SEO setting of the Wix Pro Gallery by [adding or removing variables](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-variables-in-seo-settings). A variable is placeholder text whose value is unique for each item. For example, using an Item Description variable adds each item's unique description to its search results.

> **Important:**
>
> The URL of a gallery item in an Expand Mode cannot be changed.

#### To edit your SEO Settings for the Wix Pro Gallery:

[Go to your SEO Dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-f3388913-140f-47a9-b2ba-4bf86b60e04e&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO).  Select **Go to SEO Settings **under **Tools and settings**.  Click **Pro Galleries**.   [Customize your SEO Settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929).

> **Note:**
>
> The gallery's SEO settings apply to all Expand Mode pages. It isn't possible to set different settings for different Expand Mode pages.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about customizing your SEO Settings for the Wix Pro Gallery Expand Mode.

### How do I prevent my Expand Mode pages from appearing in search results?

To prevent your Expand Mode pages from appearing in search results, go to **Basics & social share **in your SEO Settings, and click the **Let search engines index your gallery items** toggle to disable it.

### Can I share the Expand Mode URL on social media?

Yes, the Expand Mode URL can be shared on social media. When the URL is shared, the Expand Mode page displays along with some SEO information like its title tag and meta description.

### Can I have different SEO settings for different Expand Mode pages?

No, the gallery's SEO Settings apply to all Expand Mode pages. It isn't possible to set different settings for different Expand Mode pages.

### Can I change the URL of a gallery item in Expand Mode?

No, the URL of a gallery item in an Expand Mode cannot be changed.

### Can my Expand Mode pages be indexed as separate pages from the gallery page?

Yes, you can change the canonical variable to **Expanded Item URL** under** Additional meta tags **to have your Expand Mode pages indexed as separate pages from the gallery page.
