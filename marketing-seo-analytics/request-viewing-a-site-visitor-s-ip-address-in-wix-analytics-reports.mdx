---
title: "Request: Viewing a Site Visitor's IP Address in Wix Analytics Reports"
---

We are pleased to announce that you can now view by IP address in Wix Analytics traffic reports. Access this metric from Table view in any traffic report. [Learn more about traffic reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-about-your-traffic-reports).

### To view a report by IP address:

[Go to Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Analytics%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant traffic report.  In **Table** view, click **Select columns**.  Scroll down and select the **IP address **checkbox.
