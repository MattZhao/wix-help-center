title: "Troubleshooting Errors When Connecting Your Site to Google"
---

You may sometimes run into technical issues when submitting your request to connect to Google. The steps in this article help you resolve the most common connection issues.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Check that your email address is the correct one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#check-that-your-email-address-is-the-correct-one)  [Make sure that you connected your domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#make-sure-that-you-connected-your-domain)  [Make sure your homepage is not password protected](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#make-sure-your-homepage-is-not-password-protected)  [Check your Google Search Console account properties](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#check-your-google-search-console-account-properties)  [Add an exception to your browser's adblock extensions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#add-an-exception-to-your-browsers-adblock-extensions)  [Check your cookie settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#check-your-cookie-settings)  [Click "Allow" in the Connect to Google permissions pop-up](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#click-allow-in-the-connect-to-google-permissions-pop-up)  [Clear your browser's cache and cookies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#clear-your-browsers-cache-and-cookies)  [Try a different browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google#try-a-different-browser)

### Check that your email address is the correct one

If you have more than one Google account, make sure that the correct email is listed when you sign in. If it isn't, log out of all your Google accounts and then sign in with the correct Google account.

If you use a university or educational email address which includes ".edu", your university may block access to certain services. You can [change the email address of your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-email-address-of-your-wix-account) and then connect to Google.

---

### Make sure that you connected your domain

To connect to Google, you need to have a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-premium-plan-pricing) and a connected domain.

---

### Make sure your homepage is not password protected

If your site's homepage access is for members only or password protected, search engine bots won't be able to [crawl and index](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/beginner/how-search-works#crawling) it.

---

### Check your Google Search Console account properties

If you have more than 1000 properties in your Google Search Console account, you may have issues connecting your site to Google.

---

### Add an exception to your browser's adblock extensions

If you use adblock extensions in your browser, you may not be able to open the **Connect to Google** pop-up to complete your connection to Google.

---

### Check your cookie settings

If you have blocked third-party cookies and site data in your browser, you may not be able to connect to Google.

Try [enabling cookies](https://support.wix.com/en/article/enabling-cookies-in-your-browser) in your browser's settings or preferences. If you're still having issues, you can also try [clearing Wix cookies from your browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-wix-cookies-from-your-browser).

---

### Click "Allow" in the Connect to Google permissions pop-up

Your site needs certain permissions in order to connect to Google.

Make sure to click **Allow **on the **Connect to Google** pop-up which appears when you submit your request.

---

### Clear your browser's cache and cookies

Websites often create files known as cookies when you visit them. Cookies contain user-specific information, such as authentication data, and are stored by your web browser.

To speed up loading times, your browser also stores (or "caches") data about each website you visit. Over time, your browser's cached data can differ from the actual live webpages, potentially causing issues.

If you're having issues connecting your site to Google, you can try [clearing Wix cookies from your browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-wix-cookies-from-your-browser) and [clearing your browser cache](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-your-browsers-cache).

---

### Try a different browser

Sometimes an issue connecting your site to Google can be related to your browser's settings. We recommend that you use the latest version of a [supported browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers) to ensure optimal performance and security.

To test if your browser is the issue, you can try [using an incognito window](https://support.wix.com/en/article/opening-your-browser-in-incognitoprivate-browsing-mode) or try using a different browser.

> **Note:**
>
> If using an incognito window or a different browser resolves the issue, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact) for help troubleshooting the problematic browser. Make sure to send us the name and version of the supported browser you're having trouble with.

---

#### Still not working?

If the problem persists, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/support/conversations/category/contact) and provide the information below.

### Info to send to Customer Care

- The name or URL of your Wix site.
- The name and version of the web browser you're using.
- The name and version of your computer's operating system.
- A description of the steps you've taken so that we can recreate the issue on our end.
- A [screenshot](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-screenshot-to-a-ticket) of the issue.
