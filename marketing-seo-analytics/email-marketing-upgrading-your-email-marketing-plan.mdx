---
title: "Email Marketing: Upgrading Your Email Marketing Plan"
---

To get access to more email marketing features upgrade your email marketing plan and choose the package that works for your business.

### To upgrade your email marketing plan:

[Go to Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Under **Monthly Balance** click **Upgrade**.  Choose a plan that works best for you and checkout.

> **Want to compare plans before upgrading?**
>
> [Compare the different email marketing plans](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Select%20Site&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fwix-emails-plans%3FmetaSiteId%3D%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%26origin%3Dkb) to see which is right for your business. Choose your plan by the number of emails you’re planning to send a month and any additional features you are interested in.
