This site holds the 6,221 help-center articles of the [WixQA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Wix/WixQA) knowledge-base snapshot (revision `d662dc42479c14e202eccd832f8c4b66a035c4cc`, July 2025).
Wix released the snapshot under the MIT license.
This is not an official Wix site, and the content can be out of date.
Each page links to its original article at the end.

We use this site to benchmark documentation assistants.
