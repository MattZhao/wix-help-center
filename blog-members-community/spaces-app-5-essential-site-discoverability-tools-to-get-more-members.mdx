---
title: "Spaces App: 5 Essential Site Discoverability Tools to Get More Members"
---

Get more people to join you on your Spaces app by choosing from a variety of discoverability tools. Add an invite banner or a 'Download My App' app to your site to increase your site's exposure. You can also make it easy for people to discover your site using the site search feature or invite people directly.

> **Tip:**
>
> After you [choose your preferred](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app)[ member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app), make sure you [customize your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-site-on-the-member-apps) on the relevant Wix member apps.

---

### Help people discover your site

Help people discover your site and join as members on the Spaces App. By [enabling the site search](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-allowing-visitors-to-search-for-your-site) feature, visitors can search for your site by name or by URL. Use this site discoverability tool to boost the chances of people joining your site.

---

### Turn site visitors into members

Turn mobile site visitors into members of your site by [adding an invite banner on your mobile site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-managing-the-invite-banner-of-the-mobile-web-version-of-your-site). The banner appears at the top of your mobile site, inviting new people to download the Spaces app and join your site.

> **Important:**
>
> If you have a [Premium plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website), you can show or hide the invite banner from your site's dashboard. If you don't have a Premium plan, the banner is always displayed.

---

### Invite people to join your site

[Invite people to become members of your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps). You can choose how to invite people to join your site. For example, turn contacts into members with an email campaign or send people a direct link to download your members app. You can also save time by setting up an invite automation every time someone visits your site.

> **Important:**
>
> To send out an invite automation, make sure you install the [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-add-and-set-up-a-members-area) on your site.

---

### Add a “Download My App” app to your site

[Add a “Download My App” app to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/download-the-mobile-app-adding-and-setting-up) and let site visitors know why they should join you on the members app. You can place the app on any page on your site to increase the visibility and encourage people to connect with you on the go.

You can also add additional apps from your editor, your site's dashboard, or the Wix app. Learn how to [add apps to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-an-app-to-your-site).

---

### Share your content on the "Discover" tab

[Share your posts in the "Discover" tab](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-submitting-your-post-to-the-discover-tab-in-spaces-by-wix) on the Spaces app so more readers can enjoy them. Posts are a great way to connect with people and make it easy for them to interact with your content and follow their favorite topics. They can comment or like your post and decide to join your site and become a member.
