### Editing your group details

From the Wix app, you can edit your group's name, description, and cover image. Set the group's privacy to choose whether anyone can view the group or only specific member types.

#### To edit your group details:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Groups**.  Tap the relevant group.  Tap **Edit** next to the name of your group.  Edit the following details:

- Group name.
- Choose who can view this group's content under **Privacy**:

- **Public: **Anyone can view the group's content and join it too.
- **Private: **Only members can view the group's content. Tap the **Visibility **drop-down and select an option: 

- **Visible: **Anyone can find this group on the Groups page of your live site. Choose who can join this group:

- **Only members approved by admin**.
- **Only pricing plan holders: **Tap **Select Pricing Plans **to connect an existing plan or [create a new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan).
- **Only event guests: **Tap **Select Events **to set up this option, then tap **Done**.

**Tip:** If you don't have any events yet, you can [create an event from the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-creating-an-rsvp-event-using-the-wix-owner-app).
- **Hidden: **Only group members can see the group's content.
    Tap **Save**.

### Managing your group members

Manage member permissions and decide who can post, invite or add people to the group. You can also make a member an admin to help manage your group.

> **Note:**
>
> When you remove a group member, they are still members of your site.

#### To manage your group members:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Groups**.  Tap the relevant group.  Choose from the following options:

### Requests to join

Select which member requests to approve to join your group.
1. Tap **Requests to join **under **Members**.
2. Choose who you want to approve:

- Tap **Approve** or **Decline** under the relevant person.
- Tap **Decline All** or **Approve All**. 

[image: A screenshot of the Member Requests panel in the Wix Owner app.]

### Manage member permissions

Choose who can post, invite or add members to this group.
1. Tap **Permissions** under **Members**.
2. (Optional) Tap the **Member List** toggle to show or hide the member list to all group members.
3. Select the relevant option for each type of member permission:

- **Invite:** Decide who can send out invitations to join this group.
- **Add members:** Decide who can add members of your site to this group, without an invite.
- **Post:** Decide who can post and publish in this group.

[image: A screenshot of the Member Permissions panel in the Wix Owner app.]

### Invite members

Suggest this group to new members with a shareable link, or add members from your contact list to your group.
1. Tap **+ Invite Members** under **Members**.
2. Choose how you want to invite people:

- **Send a direct link:** Tap **Create** next to** Invite link**. This copies the link to your clipboard, so you can paste it anywhere you want.
- **Add your site's contacts:** Tap **Add Members **at the top right. Then, tap **Add** next to the contacts you want to invite. 

**Tip:** Click **Add All** to simply add all of your site's contacts to the group. Public members are automatically added to your group. For [members with private profiles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-member-privacy-settings-for-groups), you'll need to send them invitations.

**Tip: **Members can see a list of all available groups in their [Members app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app). Existing site members can join any group regardless of whether they have a public or private profile.

### Make a member an admin

When you assign an admin, they can create topics, pin important posts, approve new member requests, and moderate group discussions. 
1. Tap **(#) Members** under **Members**.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon ﻿  next to the relevant member.
3. Tap **Make admin **to give them admin permissions.

### Membership questions

Create membership questions, edit existing ones and choose which questions are mandatory for your members to answer before joining your group. 
1. Tap **Membership questions** under **Members**.
2. Choose from following options:

- **Add a question: **

1. Tap **+ Add a Question**.
2. Enter your question.
3. (Optional) Enable the **Make this question required** toggle to make this question mandatory.
4. Tap **Create**.
- **Edit a question: **

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon ﻿  next to a question.
2. Tap **Edit.**
3. Edit your question in the text field.
4. Tap **Save**.
- **Delete a question**:

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon ﻿  next to a question.
2. Tap **Delet****e**, then tap** Delete **again** **to confirm.

### Managing group content

Managing your group involves empowering its content to stand out and align with your brand identity. You can customize access levels for members, fostering seamless interaction and engagement in discussions, media sharing, and updates.

Additionally, establishing group guidelines, arranging tabs to showcase new content, and organizing discussions by topic enhance organization and community vibrancy.

#### To manage your group content:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Groups**.  Tap the relevant group.  Choose what you want to manage under **Content**:

### Description

1. Tap **Description**.
2. Edit what your group is all about.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Rules

Set group rules to ensure members post responsibly and respectfully. 
1. Tap **Rules**.
2. Tap **Add Group Rules**.
3. Select a rule template or create one from scratch.
4. Tap **Create**.

### Tabs

You can change the order of the tabs that appear on your group page, rename them and create custom ones with your own content.
1. Tap **Tabs**.
2. Choose what you want to do:

- **Update content tabs: **Hide, rename, reorder or create custom tabs. 

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon ﻿ next to the relevant tab.
2. Tap the relevant option:

- **Show/Hide Tab: **Tap the relevant option.
- **Rename: **Edit the tab name on your group page.
- **Settings: **Update the relevant tab settings (e.g. member permissions, group description).
- **Reorder content tabs: **Change the order of your groups' tabs to your liking.

1. Tap **Edit**.
2. Tap and drag a tab to reorder it in the list.
3. Tap **Save**.

**Tip: **Tap** + Add Custom Tab **to** **add a tab with your own content.
[image: A screenshot of the Group Tabs panel in the Wix Owner app.]

### Auto posts

Set posts to show automatically in the group in specific situations. For example, when new members join or when you change the group description.
1. Tap **Auto posts**.
2. Choose whether to automatically post or not in the group discussion when:

- New members join.
- The cover image or description is changed.
- A new event was connected.

### Topics

Add a new topic to organize posts in the group feed.
1. Tap **Topics**.
2. Choose what you want to do:

- **Add a topic:** 

1. Tap **+ New Topic**.
2. Enter the topic name and tap **Create**.
- **Edit an existing topic:** Tap the **More Actions** icon ﻿ next to the relevant topic and choose what to do next:

- Tap **Edit** to make the desired changes, then tap **Save**.
- Tap **Delete **to remove a topic, then tap **Delete** to confirm.

**Note:** Removing a topic will also remove it from any assigned posts.

**Tip:** You can also [assign topics to posts from your desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-topics).

### Can I give members permission to create a group?

Yes, you can allow members to create a group. Once you choose the members, they can create a group from the relevant member app.
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Groups**.
4. Tap **Group preferences**.
5. Select the relevant option under **Group Creation**:

- **All members: **Any member of your site can create a group with other members.
- **Members with approval: **Any member of your site can create a group, but it requires your approval.
- **Only admins: **Members aren't allowed to create groups. ** **

### Who can remove a group?

Only site owners and group admins can delete a group.**Important: **Deleting a group erases all of its content, which cannot be restored. 

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Groups**.
4. Tap the relevant group.
5. Tap** Delete Group **under** Notifications**.
6. Tap **Delete **to confirm.
