---
title: "Branded App Request: Adding Dynamic Fields"
---

Currently, the Branded App does not support adding dynamic fields to personalize your visitors' app experience (e.g. adding a personalized welcome message with your visitor's name).

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
