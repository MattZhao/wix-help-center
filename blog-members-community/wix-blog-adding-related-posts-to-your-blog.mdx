---

Your Blog Post page automatically displays a Related Posts element at the bottom of every post to allow your readers to discover similar content to what they are currently reading. You can select up to 3 related posts for each blog post.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adding related posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-related-posts-to-your-blog#adding-related-posts)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-related-posts-to-your-blog#faqs)

### Adding related posts

Start by adding related posts to individual blog posts. Go to the desired post and select up to 3 related posts to accompany it.

#### To add related posts:

[Go to Blog in your site's dashboard.](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog)  Click **Create New Post** or **Edit** to edit an existing post.  Create or edit the post to include the relevant information.  Click the **Settings** tab on the left side panel.  Click the **Advanced** tab.   Click **Choose Posts** to choose additional posts.

Select the relevant posts.

Click **Done**.

---

### FAQs

### How do I remove a related post?

You can remove a related post that you've selected by going back in to **Choose Posts** and deselecting the relevant checkbox. Click **Done** to save your changes.

### Can I change the settings of the Related Posts section?

Related Posts do not have their own dedicated settings. The settings that appear here are pulled from the Post Page settings.

### Can I change the location of the Related Posts section?

No, related posts always appear below the blog post. It is not possible to choose where related posts appear on a post page.
