---
title: "Spaces App: Allowing Visitors to Search for Your Site"
---

Enable the site search feature in the [Spaces app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-app-how-members-use-the-app) to boost your site's visibility and help people find and join it easily. Visitors can search for your site by name or URL on the invite code or home screens, adding it to their list.

You can manage this feature from your dashboard or the Wix app.

### To allow visitors to search for your site:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to **Growth and marketing tools**.
3. Click **Enable Search** under **Site search**.
4. Enable the **Allow site search **toggle to let people find and join your site on the Spaces app.
[image: A screenshot of the Allow site search toggle in a site's dashboard.]Before you begin, make sure you've [downloaded the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app) to your mobile device.
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App **under** Manage**.
4. Tap the **More actions** icon  at the top.
5. Tap **Manage app settings**.
6. Enable the **Allow site search **toggle to make your site searchable on the Spaces app.
[image: A screenshot of the Spaces search visibility toggle in the Wix app.]

> **Tip:**
>
> Encourage your members to download the Spaces App by [enabling the mobile web banner](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-managing-the-invite-banner-of-the-mobile-web-version-of-your-site).
