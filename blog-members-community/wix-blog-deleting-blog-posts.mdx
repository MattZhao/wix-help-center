---
title: "Wix Blog: Deleting Blog Posts"
---

Remove unwanted posts from the Publish or Drafts** **tabs by moving them to Trash. If you change your mind, you can always restore them later.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Delete a published post or a draft](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-deleting-blog-posts#delete-a-published-post-or-draft)[Restore a deleted post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-deleting-blog-posts#restore-a-deleted-post-or-draft)[Permanently delete a post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-deleting-blog-posts#permanently-delete-a-post)

---

### Deleting a published post or draft

Send a post or draft to the trash. When you send a post or draft to the trash, it is stored in your trash until you choose to permanently delete it, or restore it to your blog feed.

#### To delete a published post or draft:

[Go to Posts](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/posts?&title=Go%20to%20Posts%20&buttonText=Manage+Posts) in your site's dashboard.    Click the More actions** **icon  next to the post you want to delete.  Click **Move to Trash**.  Click **Move to Trash **to verify.

> **Note:**
>
> Once a post is moved to trash, it's views, likes, and comments are deleted permanently. Even if you restore the post, views, likes, and comments are not restored.

---

### Restoring a deleted post or draft

Any posts you restore from the Trash tab are moved to the Drafts tab.

#### To restore a post:

[Go to Posts](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/posts?&title=Go%20to%20Posts%20&buttonText=Manage+Posts) in your site's dashboard.    Click **Trash**.  Hover over the post and click **Restore**.

**Note: **The post is moved to the **Drafts **tab.

---

### Deleting a post or draft permanently

Want to get rid of a post for good? Delete it from the Trash tab.

#### To permanently delete a post or draft:

[Go to Posts](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/posts?&title=Go%20to%20Posts%20&buttonText=Manage+Posts) in your site's dashboard.    Click **Trash**.  Click the Delete** **icon  next to the post you want to permanently delete.  Click **Delete** to confirm.

> **Note:**
>
> You can delete posts from your live site (while logged in as admin) or while in preview mode. Just click the More actions icon next to a post. Posts deleted this way will be permanently deleted.
