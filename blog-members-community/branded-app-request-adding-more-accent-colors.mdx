---
title: "Branded App Request: Adding More Accent Colors"
---

Currently, you can [select various base colors for your branded app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content), including primary, text and icon, and background colors. However, it is not possible to add accent colors. This feature would allow you to apply different colors throughout your app to better align your color scheme with your brand identity.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
