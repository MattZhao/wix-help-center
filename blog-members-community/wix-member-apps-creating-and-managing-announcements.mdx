title: "Wix Member Apps: Creating and Managing Announcements"
---

Announcements are a great way to interact with your members and let them know about important updates. Create new announcements to deliver big news like product launches or upcoming events.

Duplicate existing announcements on the fly and make necessary edits to save time. You can also check your statistics to see how people are responding to your announcements.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Creating an announcement](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-announcements#creating-an-announcement)  [Duplicating and editing an announcement](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-announcements#duplicating-and-editing-an-announcement)  [Viewing stats on an announcement](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-announcements#viewing-announcement-statistics)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-announcements#faqs)

> **Note:**
>
> Your site members see your announcements when they access your site using the [Member App](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps) you chose.

### Creating an announcement

Create announcements from your site's dashboard, the Wix app, or Wix Studio app. Share upcoming events, enticing sales, or extend a warm welcome to engage your community.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to display the announcement on.
5. Click **Elements **on the left.
6. Click **Communication channels**.
7. Click the **Announcements** element to add it.
8. Customize your announcement under **Edit Announcements** on the right:

1. (Optional) Enable the **Show title** toggle to show a title above the announcement. Then, enter your title under **Element title**.
2. Select the relevant option under **Element visibility **(**Android**,** iOS **or** All**).

**Tip:** Learn more about [showing or hiding elements on different operating systems](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-showing-or-hiding-elements-on-different-mobile-operating-systems).
3. Click on **Create new announcement** to start writing your announcement.
4. Enter your announcement.

**Tip:** You can add many different elements to your announcement. Upload your own media,  embed videos from social media or show one of your events, products or services.
5. (Optional) Click the checkbox to **Also send this announcement as a push notification**.
6. Click **Post**.
9. 3. Tap **Mobile Announcements **under the** Marketing**.

5. Write your announcement title and body text.

**Tip: **Tap the **Add** icon  at the bottom left to include media in your announcement (image, video, GIF or a divider).
6. Tap **Next**.

1. (Optional) Enable the **Send announcement as a push notification **toggle to send notifications from your site to your members' mobile devices.

### Duplicating and editing an announcement

Save time creating new announcements by duplicating existing ones. Make any necessary changes to them such as the title or content as needed to tailor messages to your audience.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and select the screen that shows your announcement.
5. Select the announcement to edit it.
6. Choose what you want to do:

- **Duplicate:** Click the **Duplicate** icon   to create a copy of your announcement.
- **Edit: **Make the changes that suit your content best:

- **Show title: **Enable this toggle to display a title above your announcement.
- **Element title: **Edit your announcement's title.
- **Element visibility:**  Choose whether to display the element on Android devices, iOS devices, or both. Learn more about [showing or hiding elements on different operating systems](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-showing-or-hiding-elements-on-different-mobile-operating-systems).
- **Edit announcement:** Click **Create new announcement** and make the desired changes.

**Tip: **Scroll down to **Recent announcements** to reuse a past announcement. Then, hover over the relevant announcement and click **Reuse text**.
7. Click **Post**.
8. Go back to [Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard and click **Save & Publish**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile Announcements **under** Marketing**.
4. Tap the relevant announcement.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon .
6. Tap **Duplicate and edit**.
[image: A screenshot of the Duplicate and edit option on the Wix Owner app.]
1. Make your desired changes.
2. Tap **Next**.
3. (Optional) Enable the **Send announcement as a push notification **toggle to send notifications from your site to your members' mobile devices.

**Note: **Disabling the toggle creates the announcement on your site only and members don't receive any notifications.
4. Tap **Publish**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Manage**.

### Viewing announcement statistics

Keep track of your announcement statistics, like the number of views and the specific members who have seen them. This can help you determine the visibility and impact of your announcements.

> **Important:**
>
> This information is only visible to you and cannot be seen by site members.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and select the screen that shows your announcement.
5. Select the relevant announcement on the screen.
6. Click **Create New Announcement**.
7. Scroll down to **Recent Announcements** to check the number of members the announcement was sent to and how many have viewed it.
[image: A screenshot of the stats from a site's dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile Announcements **under** Marketing**.
4. Tap the relevant announcement to view the members who have seen your announcement.
[image: A screenshot of the available stats of an announcement in the Wix Owner app.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Manage**.
3. Tap **Mobile Announcements **under** Marketing**.
4. Tap the relevant announcement to view the members who have seen your announcement.

> **Note:**
>
> If you duplicate an announcement, the members statistics are reset for the new announcement and start from scratch.

### Can I update the name and profile image that appear next to my announcements?

Yes. You can edit the name and profile image displayed next to the announcements you create. 
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap your profile icon at the top.
3. Tap **Manage Account Settings**.
4. Tap** Basic info**.
5. Make the relevant changes then tap **Save**.

### What is the difference between announcements and push notifications?

Announcements are displayed within the app and directly engage members in conversations with your business. These are great to deliver product launches or upcoming events.
[Push notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-sending-customized-push-notifications) are the notifications your members get on their mobile devices, if they enabled notifications for your member app. These notifications are completely customizable.
You can have the announcement sent as a push notification to your member's device to ensure they never miss important updates.
