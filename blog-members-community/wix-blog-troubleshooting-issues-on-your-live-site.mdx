The issues you are experiencing may be a local issue caused by your browser's cache or cookies. We recommend trying the following:

### Step 1 | Try to open your blog page in an Incognito window

We recommend trying to open your blog page in an Incognito window. Select the browser you are using from the list below.

> **Cache and Cookies**
>
> If this solves the issue, make sure you complete step 2 and 3 to clear your browser. This should allow your browser to open as normal.

### Chrome

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Press **Ctrl** + **Shift** + **N **on your keyboard (**Cmd **+ **Shift** + **N **on Mac).

### Firefox

1. Open Firefox.
2. Press** Ctrl** + **Shift** + **P **on your keyboard (**Cmd **+ **Shift** + **P **on Mac).

**Note:** Alternatively, can click the Menu icon  at the top right and select **New Private Window**.

### Safari (Mac)

1. Open Safari.
2. Press** Cmd** + **Shift** + **N **on your keyboard.

### Microsoft Edge

1. Open Microsoft Edge.
2. Press** Ctrl** + **Shift** + **N **on your keyboard.

### Internet Explorer 11

1. Open Internet Explorer.
2. Press **Ctrl **+ **Shift** + **P **on your keyboard.

**Note:** As well as the Editor and ADI Builder, Wix no longer supports live sites on IE11. We recommend using one of our supported browsers to view and edit your site. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers)

---

### Step 2 | Clear your cache

To clear your cache, follow the steps 1-3 and then select the relevant browser from the list below to view the remaining steps.

Close all windows and tabs of your browser.   Open a new tab on your browser and make sure Wix.com is not open on any tabs or windows.   Press **Ctrl** + **Shift** + **Del **(press **Cmd **+** Shift **+** Delete** using a Mac) then click below for browser-specific instructions.

**Note:** This keyboard shortcut is relevant for all supported browsers except Safari (see below).

### Chrome

1. Click the drop-down next to **Time range** and select **All time**.
2. Make sure the box is checked next to **Cached images and files** then click **Clear data**.
3. Close and reopen Chrome.

### Firefox

1. Click the drop-down next to **Time range to clear:** and select **Everything**.
2. Make sure the box is checked next to **Cache** and click **Clear now**.
3. Close and reopen Firefox.

### Safari

1. Press **Cmd** + **,** to open Preferences.
2. Click the Advanced tab.
3. Check the box next to **Show Develop menu in menu bar** (at the bottom of the window).
4. Click **Develop** in Safari's menu bar and select **Empty Caches**.
5. Close and reopen Safari.

### Microsoft Edge

1. Make sure the box is checked next to **Cached data and files** and click **Clear**.
2. Close and reopen Microsoft Edge.

**Note**: Wix supports live sites on Microsoft Edge, however, we do not support the ADI builder on Microsoft Edge. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-request-using-adi-in-microsoft-edge) to vote for this feature.

### Internet Explorer 11

1. Make sure the box next to **Preserve Favorites website data** is unchecked and the box next to **Temporary Internet Files** is checked.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Close and reopen Internet Explorer 11.

**Note**: As well as the Editor and ADI Builder, Wix no longer supports live sites on IE11. We recommend using one of our supported browsers to view and edit your site. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers)

---

### Step 3 | Clear your cookies

Next, clear your cookies. This can be done every once in a while to help maintain optimum browser performance. Select the browser you are using from the list below.

### Chrome

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Click the Show More**  **iconin the upper-right corner.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Under **Privacy**, click **Cookies and other site data.**
5. Type **Wix **in the Search field at the top right.
6. Click **Remove all shown**.
7. Click **Done**.

### Firefox

1. Open Firefox.
2. Click the **Open Menu **icon  in the upper-right corner.
3. Select **Options**.
4. Select the **Privacy** tab.
5. Under **History**, click **Remove individual cookies**.
6. Type **Wix** in the **Search** field.
7. Click **Remove Selected**.
8. Click **Close**.

### Safari (Mac)

1. Open Safari.
2. Click **Safari**.
3. Click **Preferences**.
4. Click **Privacy**.
5. Click **Manage Website Data**.
6. Type **Wix **in the **Search **field.
7. Click **Remove All**.
8. Click **Done**.

### Microsoft Edge

1. Open Microsoft Edge.
2. Press **Ctrl** + **Shift** + **Del **on your keyboard.
3. Select the checkbox next to **Cookies and saved website data**.
4. Click **Clear**.

**Note**: Wix supports live sites on Microsoft Edge, however, we do not support the ADI builder on Microsoft Edge. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-request-using-adi-in-microsoft-edge) to vote for this feature.

### Internet Explorer 11

1. Open Internet Explorer.
2. Click the **Settings **icon  in the top right.
3. Select **Internet Options**.
4. In the **General **tab under **Browsing History**, select **Settings**.
5. Click **View Files**.
6. Type **Wix **in the Search Temporary Internet Files field.
7. Click on a cookie then select all cookies using **Ctrl **+ **A**.
8. Press **Delete **on your keyboard.

**Note:** As well as the Editor and ADI Builder, Wix no longer supports live sites on IE11. We recommend using one of our supported browsers to view and edit your site. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers)
