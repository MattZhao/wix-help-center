Whether you run an online store, restaurant, or a booking-based service, you can add business elements to your app. For example, if you run a booking-based business, add an element that lists all of your services. ### Elements for your online store

Add and customize store elements to create a smooth, on-the-go shopping experience for your customers. Add a Product gallery or carousel to showcase your products exactly the way you want to. You can also let customers add filters to make it easier to browse and buy.

---

### Elements for your restaurant

Add the table reservations element so visitors can book tables directly through your app. You can add this element on your desktop or phone, and edit it anytime using the Wix app or Wix Studio app.

---

### Elements for your booking-based business

Boost your booking-based business by adding elements that give your customers quick access to their upcoming bookings and full booking history. They can schedule or reschedule sessions directly from your app, view a weekly agenda, explore your full list of services, and a preview upcoming sessions. This keeps your clients engaged, making it a great way to keep everyone on track and organized.

---

### Elements for your events

Whether you're planning a party, hosting a convention, or selling tickets to a show, Wix Events helps you promote your event and manage your guest list. Use the Events carousel or Events list to showcase upcoming events and customize the details displayed. Allow quick ticket purchases directly through your app, and keep guests informed by adding an Event schedule element with upcoming events. Encourage engagement through live discussions and track participation with a list of attendeees.

### FAQs

Click below to learn more about business elements for your app.

### Can I customize my Product gallery element for my branded app?

Yes, you can customize your product gallery to match your brand and business goals. Your branded app offers even more customization options.
Tap the **Edit** icon next to your Product gallery element to choose a category you want to display in your gallery, adjust the image layout, and enable filters for your clients. Under **Display Settings **you can enable toggles for more features. For example, enable the **Wishlist** toggle to add a heart icon, so customers can save their favorite items directly to their wishlist. You can also let customers add items to their cart and choose quantities directly from the product images.

### Can I customize the button for an empty wishlist?

**Note: **The** **Wishlist element is only available on the branded app.

Yes, you can customize how the images appear on your Wishlist element and edit the text for the button that shows when the wishlist is empty. To edit the button, tap the **Edit** icon  next to your Wishlist element and tap **Customize element**. Enable the **Show button** toggle and enter your button text. Then tap **Save**.

### How do I let customers add items to a wishlist?

To let customers add items to a wishlist, enable the **Wishlist** toggle in your product gallery settings. Customers can add products to their wishlist directly from your gallery and then access or remove their saved items in the Wishlist element.

### Which Bookings elements can I add to my app?

Add Bookings elements to make it easy for members to schedule and manage their appointments, including:
- **Upcoming bookings: **Remind clients of their upcoming bookings, allow them to easily book a new appointment and see their booking history.
- **Agenda:** Add a weekly timetable and make it easier for your clients to find and book the right service.
- **Services:** A list of all your services where your clients can book services straight from the app. Clients can also filter services by location.
- **Upcoming sessions: **A preview of your [upcoming sessions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-viewing-your-upcoming-sessions-in-the-wix-owner-app).

### Can I edit my Table reservation element on my desktop?

No, currently you can only edit the Table reservation element through the Wix app and Wix Studio app.
