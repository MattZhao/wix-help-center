---
title: "Site Members: Using Permissions to Limit Collaborator Access to Member Pages"
---

Currently, it is not possible to limit your collaborator's access to member pages on your site according to their role. Collaborators can access any member's page on your site regardless of their role.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
