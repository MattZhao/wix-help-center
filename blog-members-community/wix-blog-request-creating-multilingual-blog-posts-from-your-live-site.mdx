---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Creating Multilingual Blog Posts from Your Live Site"
---

Currently, it's not possible to create multilingual blog posts directly from your live site. Any posts that you [create on your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-writing-posts-in-wix-blog#writing-posts-from-your-live-site) are automatically assigned to your site's primary language.

> **Tip:**
>
> After you've created your post, you can use Wix Multilingual to [translate it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-blog) and create unique content for each language on your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
