---
title: "Wix Blog: Adding a Cover Image to a Blog Post"
---

Adding a striking cover image to your blog post is a simple way to captivate your readers' attention. The cover image is displayed on each post in your blog feed, your [Post List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-displaying-different-blog-posts-on-different-site-pages), and when you [share a post on social media](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-sharing-a-blog-post-on-social-media).

You can add a cover image to your post in your post's settings in the post composer.

### To add a cover image to a post:

Go to [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) in your dashboard.  Select the post that you want to add a cover image to.  Click** Settings**.  Enable the **Display Cover Image **toggle.

Click the image box to add a new cover image.  Click **+Upload Media **to add an image from your computer, or select an image from your site files or one of the stock image options such as **Media from Wix**.   Click **Add to Page.**  Click** Save **or** Publish **to apply the changes.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also add a cover image by adding an image in the content of your post.
