title: "Site Members: Members Reporting Other Members"
---

To keep your Members Area a safe space for everyone, site members can report other members for reasons like spam, hate speech and harassment. You can then choose what to do with the report - dismiss it, or block the relevant member from your Members Area.

**In this article, learn how to review reports:**

[From your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-members-reporting-other-members#reviewing-reports-from-your-sites-dashboard)  [From the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-members-reporting-other-members#reviewing-reports-from-the-wix-app)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-members-reporting-other-members#faqs)

---

### Reviewing reports from your site's dashboard

Once a member submits a report about someone, you get notified about it via email and your site's dashboard. Head over to the member list to review the report details and decide how to proceed with that member.

#### To review reports from the dashboard:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Reported **tab.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant member.  Choose what to do with this member:

- **Review Reports: **See the report details, including the member who submitted the report, the date and the reason, if the member specified it.

- According to how you want to handle this report, click **Block Member** or **Remove Reports**.
- **Remove Reports: **Delete and dismiss the report that was submitted about this member.
- **View member: **Before making your decision, open the reported member's Contact Card to see more details about them.
- **Block member: **Block this member from your Members Area, so they can't access it nor sign up again using the same email address.

> **Note:**
>
> When removing a report, it gets automatically deleted with no option to see it again in the future.

### Reviewing reports from the Wix app

You can use the Wix app to review reports about your members, directly from your mobile device.

#### To review reports from the Wix app:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap** Members** under** Customers & Leads**.  Click **Reported **at the top.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant member.  Choose what to do with this member:

- **Dismiss Reports: **Delete the report that was submitted about this member.
- **Review Reports: **See the report details, including the member who submitted the report, the date and the reason, if the member specified it.

- According to how you want to handle this report, click **Block Member** or **Dismiss Reports**.
- **Block member: **Block this member from your Members Area, so they can't access it nor sign up again using the same email address.

> **Note:**
>
> When dismissing a report, it gets automatically deleted with no option to see it again in the future.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### How do members report each other?

Your members can report each other from two places: 
- The person's [profile page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-profile-page).
- [Your members page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-members-page-that-displays-all-members), if you added it to your Members Area.

When submitting a report, members need to choose a reason (e.g. spam, harassment, impersonation, etc.) They can also leave a note to share more details about what happened.

### Do members know if someone reports them?

No. Reported members don't get notified if someone reports them on your site.

### Can members report a person more than once?

Yes. Members can report the same person as many times as they need to.

### Can members report me or my site's Admins?

Members can only report other members. This means they can't report you (the site owner) or anyone with the Admin role on your team.
