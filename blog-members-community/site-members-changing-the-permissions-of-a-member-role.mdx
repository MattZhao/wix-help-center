---
title: "Site Members: Changing the Permissions of a Member Role"
---

When [creating a member role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-member-roles), you give that role permission to access specific pages on your site. If you add new pages, or the role changes, you can come back and change the permissions at any time.

### To change the permissions of a member role:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.  Click **More Actions** at the top right.  Click** Set Permissions**.  Select the checkbox next to the pages each role should access.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can set these permissions for member badges and pricing plans as well.
