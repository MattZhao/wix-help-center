---
title: "Wix Blog: Editing a Blog Post in the Wix App"
---

Need to make a change to a blog post while you're on the go? Got a few minutes to finish that draft? The Wix app on your mobile device lets you edit blog drafts and published posts directly from your phone.

### To edit a blog post:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage** at the bottom right.  Tap **Blog**.   Tap **Posts**.  Tap the post you want to edit.

**Note: **If the post has not yet been published, tap **Drafts** at the top and then tap the post.  Tap **More Actions **.  Tap **Edit Post**.  Make your changes and then tap **Next**.  Tap **Update / Save & Publish**.
