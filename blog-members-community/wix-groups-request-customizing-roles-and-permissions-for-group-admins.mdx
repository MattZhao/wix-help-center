---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Customizing Roles and Permissions for Group Admins"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [customize the permissions for group admins](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-your-group#editing-your-groups-admin-tools) (see the "Member permissions" drop-down). You can decide if group admins can post content, invite or add people to a group.

When you want to [make a member a group admin](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-your-group#managing-your-groups-members), you can do so from the **Members **tab.

> **Did you know?**
>
> Group admins can also: 
> - Moderate group discussions.
> - Pin important posts.
> - Assign topics to posts.
> - Approve new member requests.
> - Remove members who should no longer be part of the group.
