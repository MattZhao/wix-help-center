### Types of Members App reports

Understanding the different types of Members App reports helps you gain insights into your app's performance and user engagement. These reports provide valuable data on app traffic, member activity over time, and trends in new app member sign-ups.

Click a report type below to learn more about it:

### App Traffic by Time of Day

The App Traffic by Time of Day report helps you to identify trends in your app activity by hour and day of the week.
**Use this report to:**
- See when your app is most active during different hours of the day.
- Discover trends in user activity on specific days of the week.

### App Traffic over Time

The App Traffic over Time report helps you to identify trends in your app members activity over different time periods. 
**Use this report to:**
- See your total amount of active members over time.
- Discover trends for new and returning members.
- Find out how much time your app members spend engaging with your app.

### New App Visitors over Time

The New App Visitors over Time report helps you to identify trends in your new app members activity over different time periods.
**Use this report to:**
- Find out how many new app members have signed up during different time periods.
- Discover where new app members are referred from.
- Find out how much time your new app members spend engaging with your app.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about your Wix Analytics Members App reports.

### How do I save my favorite reports so that I can access them quickly?

On the [All Reports](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fanalytics%2Freports%2F%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=View+All+Reports) page, click the **Add to Saved reports** icon  next to a specific report to add it to your saved reports. Your saved reports appear at the top of the page.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Add to Saved reports icon.]

### How do I change the type of graph in the report?

On the [All Reports](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fanalytics%2Freports%2F%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=View+All+Reports) page, hover over a report to see the different views available. The offered graphs vary according to the type of report (e.g. tables, line charts, maps).[image: Screenshot of Members app reports hovering over a type of report to see possible graphs]
You could also switch between report views within a report by clicking the options at the top.
