---
title: "Wix Blog: Filtering Blog Posts"
---

If you're looking for a specific post in a hurry, you can filter through your blog posts using the filter drop-down in your dashboard. Here you can filter by writer, if you have multiple writers and also filter by category.

### To filter blog posts:

Go to the [Blog tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Blog%20Posts&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Filter** drop-down  at the top right.  Click the relevant checkboxes.   Click **Apply** to apply the filter to your search.
