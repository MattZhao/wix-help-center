---
title: "Tagging Community Members in the Shared Gallery App"
---

Bring your community together by tagging photos. Anyone with permission to upload photos also has permission to tag community members.

### To tag a photo:

On your live site, click a photo to go to the expand mode.   Click **Tag Members**.  Start typing the name of the community member you want to tag and select it from the list.  Click **Done**.
