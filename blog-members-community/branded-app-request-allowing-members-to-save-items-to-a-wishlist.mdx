---
title: "Branded App Request: Allowing Members to Save Items to a Wishlist"
---

Currently in the Branded App, your app members cannot add individual products or services to a personalized wishlist.

Having the ability to create a wishlist on your app helps your logged in members keep track of their favorite products and/or services by adding them to a personal list. They can refer back to their wishlist items at any time to decide what to purchase. [Learn more about how Wix Stores uses the wishlist feature on desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-a-wishlist).

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
