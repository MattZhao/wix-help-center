---
title: "Wix Blog: Filtering Blog Posts in the Post Manager"
---

Does your blog have more than one writer? You can easily filter posts in the Post Manager to view just your own post or those by other writers.

### To filter posts:

Go to your [Blog posts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Blog%20Posts&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/) in your dashboard.  Click the **Filter by** drop-down.  Select a filter option.  Click Apply.
