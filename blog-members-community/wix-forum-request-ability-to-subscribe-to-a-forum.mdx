---
title: "Wix Forum Request: Ability to Subscribe to a Forum"
---

Currently, it is not possible for forum members to subscribe to receive notifications whenever there is a new post or to receive periodical updates (e.g. a monthly update).

It is, however, possible to [follow individual forum categories](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-following-and-unfollowing-categories) and to receive updates when someone posts to them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
