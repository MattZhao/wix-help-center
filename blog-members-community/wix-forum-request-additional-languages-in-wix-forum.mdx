Currently, Wix Forum supports [all of the languages supported by your Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/languages-available-in-wix). What additional languages would you like to use in Wix Forum? Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/contact?skipTroubleshooters=true) to submit a ticket with your feedback and suggestions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
