---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Importing Blog Posts from Tumblr"
---

Currently, you can import blog posts from [WordPress](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-importing-blog-posts-from-wordpress-to-the-wix-blog) and from [another Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-importing-blog-posts-from-another-wix-site). It is not possible to import blog posts from Tumblr.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
