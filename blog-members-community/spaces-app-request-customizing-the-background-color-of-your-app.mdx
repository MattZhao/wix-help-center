---
title: "Spaces App Request: Customizing the Background Color of Your App"
---

Currently, it is only possible to [customize the background color of your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-color-theme) when using the Spaces App. It is not possible to customize the background color of your Spaces app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
