---
title: "Wix Blog: Enabling a Printer-Friendly Version of Your Posts"
---

Add a "Print Post" icon to create a printer-friendly version of your blog posts for your site visitors to print out. For example, if you have a cookery blog, you can add a Print Post icon to your recipe posts so your visitors can print them out to try for themselves.

### To enable a printer-friendly version of your posts:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Post page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click **Post**.
3. Click the Post Page element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Display** tab.
6. Click **Share & Print**.
7. Enable the **Enable share & print icons **toggle.
8. Select the **Print Post **checkbox.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to enable a print post icon.]
1. Publish your site.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Post page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Post** under **Blog Pages**.
3. Click the Blog Post element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Display** tab.
6. Click **Share & Print**.
7. Enable the **Enable share & print icons **toggle.
8. Select the **Print Post **checkbox.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to enable a print post icon.]
1. Publish your site.
