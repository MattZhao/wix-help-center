Spaces by Wix is an app for your members to engage with your business and online community.

**In this article, learn how your members can:**

### Joining a site

There are 2 ways for people to become [members of a site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/how-members-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps):

[Join with an invite code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-app-how-members-use-the-app#join-with-an-invite-code)  [Invite people to join](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-app-how-members-use-the-app#invite-others-to-join)

#### Join with an invite code

Visitors can join your site through the Spaces app using a [unique invite code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/how-members-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps#how-members-use-your-invite-code) which you, the site owner, provides them with.

If they don't have an invite code, app members can tap **Search sites** at the bottom to search for your site.

#### Invite others to join

Existing site members can share the experience by [inviting others to become part of your community](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-inviting-new-members-to-a-site).

> **Note:**
>
> If invitees don't have the Spaces app, they'll be directed to download the app from the invite link. From there, they can join and be taken directly to your site.

### Managing their sites on the app

When members open the Spaces app, they see their [site list (sites they are members of)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-managing-your-sites). They can prioritize their favorites by pinning them to the top and hide less frequented sites. They can also adjust notification preferences and opt out of sites they wish to leave

#### Organize their site list

Members can[ organize their home screen](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-managing-your-sites#organizing-your-site-list) by pinning favorite sites to the top, hiding infrequently visited ones, and leaving sites they no longer wish to be part of.

#### Search for sites

Members can use the search bar at the top of the Spaces app to [find a site they're a member of or interested in](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-managing-your-sites#searching-for-a-site). They can search by name, URL, sites they've interacted with, or been invited to in the past.

> **Tip:**
>
> As a site owner, you can help people discover your site by [enabling the site search feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-allowing-visitors-to-search-for-your-site?tabs=Owner-app) from your dashboard or the Wix app.

#### Manage their notification settings

Members can [personalize their notification preferences](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-managing-your-sites) by selecting between push notifications or email. They can also specify the types of notifications they wish to receive, such as general announcements, updates, or reminders.

### Browsing your site

Your members can browse your site using the navigation tabs. They can view everything on your home page, shop for your products, view and join events, groups or programs, read your blog, and more.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can restrict some navigation tabs so that only members with specific badges and pricing plans can access them. Learn more about [setting tab permissions for members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-setting-viewing-permissions-for-your-members).

---

### Chatting with you and other site members

Your members can [chat with you and other site members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-creating-and-managing-a-group-chat) directly from their mobile devices. They can also initiate group chats, control participation, and leave chats at your their discretion.

> **Important:**
>
> - To chat with your site visitors, you must have the [Wix Chat app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-an-overview) installed on your site.
> - Group chat is only available for your members if you've added a [Blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-about-the-wix-blog) or a [Forum](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-about-wix-forum) to your site, and have [Members Chat](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-allowing-members-to-chat-with-each-other-using-members-chat) enabled.

### Viewing their activity

Once a member books a service, RSVPs to an event, or purchases a paid plan, their activity is logged under **My Profile **  in the Spaces app.

Members can then access their upcoming classes, appointments, events, orders, and subscriptions by tapping an option under **My Activity** to manage their engagements.

---

### Managing credit card information

Your members can securely store their credit card details to simplify the payment process for bookings, event registrations, and subscription plans, eliminating the need to repeatedly input information.

Additionally, members have the flexibility to manage multiple payment methods with the ability to add, update, or remove credit card information at any time.

#### To manage their credit card info:

Open the Spaces by Wix app.  Tap **My Profile**  at the bottom.  Scroll down to **Settings** and tap **Credit Cards**.   Choose one of the following:

- **Add a card:** Members can add a new card for payment.

1. Tap **Add **at the top right.
2. Enter the credit card information.
3. Tap **Done**, then tap **Save**.
- **Make primary card (if they have** **multiple cards saved): **Members can select their preferred card as primary from their saved options.

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant card.
2. Tap **Make primary card**.
- **Delete card: **Members can remove a card they no longer use. 

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant card.
2. Tap **Delete** card.
3. Tap **Yes** to confirm.

> **Tip:**
>
> Your members may request that you permanently delete their personal information from your site. [Learn more about managing deletion requests](https://support.wix.com/en/article/gdpr-about-your-customers-data#permanently-deleting-your-customers-data).
