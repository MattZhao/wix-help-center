---
title: "Branded App: Adding a WebView to Your App"
---

Embed web content in your branded app with the WebView element to enable seamless viewing without the need for an external browser. You can add content from your own site or an external source, making the content feel like an integral part of the app. By adding the WebView element, users don't need to open an external browser to view the content.

> **Note:**
>
> This element is only available for the Branded app, make sure to purchase a [Branded App plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-purchasing-a-branded-app-plan).

### To add a WebView element to your branded app:

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to the Branded Mobile App tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Your%20Branded%20App%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=branded) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
3. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
4. Click **Elements **on the left.
5. Click **Embed** and select the **WebView** element.
6. Customize the following in the panel on the right: 

- **Content:** Enter the page URL of the site you want to embed.
- **Layout: **Choose a height for your WebView:

- **Dynamic**: The height of the WebView auto-adjusts with the screen.
- **Fixed:** Set the height of the WebView in pixels.
- **Display: **Enable the toggles to show the website header and footer. [image: A screenshot showing how to customize your webview element on your desktop.]
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding around the WebView.

**Note: **Click the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins.
- **General Settings: **Click **General Settings **and choose which device to show this element on: **All**, **Android,** or **iOS**.

**Note: **You can show or hide elements of your site depending on what operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. Your elements are visible on all operating systems by default, but you can decide what to show on iOS, Android, or both.
7. Click **Save**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing a different mobile app:

1. Tap the mobile app name at the top.
2. Tap **Branded Mobile App** or tap the relevant mobile app (e.g. Spaces by Wix app).

**Tip: **If you want to edit a different mobile app (e.g. Fit by Wix, Dine by Wix), choose the app as [your preferred member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app).
5. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
6. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
7. Tap **+ Add Element** in the relevant location.
8. Tap **Embed **at the top, then tap **WebView**.
9. Customize your element using the options below:

- **Content:** Enter the page URL of the site you want to embed.
- **Layout: **Choose a height for your WebView:

- **Dynamic**: The height of the WebView auto-adjusts with the screen.
- **Fixed:** Set the height of the WebView in pixels.
- **Display: **Enable the toggles to show the website header and footer. [image: A screenshot showing how to customize your webview element on your owner app.]
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding around the WebView.

**Note: **Click the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins.
- **General Settings**: Choose which screen and device you want this element to appear on.

- **Visibility**: Choose which device to show this element on: **All**, **Android** or **iOS**.

**Note: **[You can show or hide elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-showing-or-hiding-elements-on-different-mobile-operating-systems) of your site depending on what operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. Your elements are visible on all operating systems by default, but you can decide what to show on iOS, Android, or both.
- **Location: **Select which screen you want the button displayed on.
9.  Tap **Add**.
