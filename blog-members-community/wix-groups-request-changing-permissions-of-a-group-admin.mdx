---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Changing Permissions of a Group Admin"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [change the permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-your-group#editing-your-groups-admin-tools) of what a group admin can do.

> **What permissions do group admins have?**
>
> - Invite and add members to a group.
> - Moderate group discussions.
> - Pin important posts.
> - Approve new member requests.
> - Remove members who should no longer be part of the group.
