Access and manage your sites on the 'My Sites' screen in the Spaces app. From there, organize your site list according to your preferences and customize notifications to get the latest updates.

### Organizing your site list

Organize your home screen by pinning your favorite sites to the top, hiding sites that you don't visit very often, and leaving sites that you no longer want to be a part of.

#### To organize your site list:

Open the Spaces by Wix app.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right of your site list.  Tap** Manage My Sites**.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant site.  Select what you want to do from the available options:

- **Pin to top: **Stick your favorite site to the top of the home screen in the app.
- **Hide from My Sites: **Remove a site from your home screen while still being its admin/member.
- **Add to device home screen: **Create a shortcut from the home screen of your mobile device.
- **Manage notifications:** Choose the updates you want to receive and how you want to receive them (e.g. Push notifications or Email).
- **Invite members: **Invite people to [join your favorite site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-inviting-new-members-to-a-site) on the app.
- **Leave:** Leave a site you are a member of or no longer want to follow.

Use the search bar at the top to find a site you're a member of or interested in. Search by name, URL, sites you've interacted with or been invited to in the past.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about being accessing your site as an admin in the Spaces app.

### Can I manage the sites I'm an admin of using the Spaces app?

No, you can't manage sites you are an admin of in the Spaces app, but you can view them. Tap **Admin** at the top of the 'My Sites' screen to filter the relevant sites.[image: A screenshot of tapping on the Admin button in the Spaces by Wix app.]If you want to manage your sites in your Wix account, do it from the Wix app.

### What options are available through the Admin Actions?

Tapping each Admin Action, directs you to the Wix app, where you'll have [full control over your sites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-managing-your-app-from-the-wix-owner-app). 
The Dashboard gives you an overview of how your site is doing. You can see summaries of important metrics such as site traffic, sales, and customer behavior. 
You can also [get your site discovered](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-allowing-visitors-to-search-for-your-site?tabs=Owner-app) by enabling the site search feature from the Wix app.

### Can I experience my site as a member?

Yes. Tap on any site to see how it appears to your members.
