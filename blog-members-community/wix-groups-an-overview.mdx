---
title: "Wix Groups: An Overview"
---

Wix Groups is a great platform to build a community for your members with shared interests. Get members talking about your brand, and to each other, using your group's discussion board. As a group owner, you can post announcements and promote exclusive events for members to join.

> **Ready to get started?**
>
> [Add Wix Groups to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-and-setting-up) and set up your group.

---

### Create unique groups for your members

Create unique groups for members who share similar interests. Groups make it easy for you to give your members a place to get the conversation going and encourage them to engage with your business / brand.

Choose from [pre-existing templates or create one from scratch](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-creating-a-new-group) to create your first group.

---

### Build relationships with potential group members

[Create membership questions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-new-members-to-your-group) for people to answer when they request to join your group. This gives you an opportunity to get to know your members before they join.

> **What's next?**
>
> When new members join, you can post on your Groups Feed to welcome them and introduce them to the other members.

---

### Create a community with your Groups Feed

Your [Groups Feed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-about-the-groups-feed) lets members easily discover new group content in real-time. Members can also interact with each other and join new groups based on their interests.

---

### Promote engagement in your group

Upload a variety of content to generate buzz around a topic by [creating and managing posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-creating-and-managing-your-groups-posts). For example, you can post business updates and announcements you want members to know about.

> **Organize posts in your group:**
>
> [Assign feed topics to posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-topics) in your group to help your members find what they are looking for.

---

### Bring group members together with exclusive events

[Create exclusive events for your group members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-displaying-events-in-your-group) to get them excited and bring them together. Showcasing events in your group makes it easy for members to sign up and RSVP.
