---
title: "Wix Forum: Adding Hashtags to Forum Posts"
---

Hashtags help readers find posts related to the topics they're interested in. When readers click a hashtag, they get a list of forum posts tagged with the same hashtag.

You can add a hashtag anywhere in your post. Just write the hashtag symbol (#) plus text, with no spaces between them, and then publish your post.
