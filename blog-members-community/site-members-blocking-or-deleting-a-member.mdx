---
title: "Site Members: Blocking or Deleting a Member"
---

You can remove a member from your Member's Area in two ways: either deleting or blocking the member entirely. Blocking someone prevents them from signing up again (using the same email address), while deleting only removes them from the current member list.

**In this article, learn more about blocking or deleting members:**

[From the Member List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-blocking-or-deleting-a-member#blocking-or-deleting-a-site-member-from-the-member-list)  [From the member's Contact Card](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-blocking-or-deleting-a-member#blocking-or-deleting-a-member-from-their-contact-card)  [Unblocking a member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-blocking-or-deleting-a-member#unblocking-site-members)

> **Note:**
>
> The change may take a number of hours to take effect.

---

### Blocking or deleting a site member from the Member List

While browsing your Member List, you can block or delete a member to remove them from your Member's Area.

#### To block or delete a member from the Member List:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Site%20Members&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.   Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the relevant member.  Click one of the following options:

- **Block member: **This blocks a member, so they're removed from your Member's Area and cannot register again using the same email address.
- **Delete member: **This removes a member from your Member's Area. However, their email address isn't blocked so they can always sign up again. 

**Tip**: You can click multiple checkboxes to delete multiple site members simultaneously.
    Click **Block **or** Delete **in the pop-up to confirm.

---

### Blocking or deleting a member from their Contact Card

While viewing a member's [Contact Card](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-viewing-your-members#viewing-a-members-contact-card), you can quickly delete or block them from the **Member Info** section.

#### To block or delete a member from their Contact Card:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Site%20Members&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.   Click the relevant member to open their contact card.  Click **Manage Member** next to **Member Info**.  Click one of the following options:

- **Block member: **This blocks a member, so they're removed from your Member's Area and cannot register again using the same email address.
- **Delete member: **This removes a member from your Member's Area. However, their email address isn't blocked so they can always sign up again.
    Click **Block **or** Delete **in the pop-up to confirm.

---

### Unblocking site members

Changed your mind about a blocked member? You can always restore their access to your Member's Area.

#### To unblock a site member:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Site%20Members&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.   Click **Blocked** next to **Site Members**.  Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the relevant member.  Click **Unblock member**.   Click **Unblock** in the pop-up to confirm.
