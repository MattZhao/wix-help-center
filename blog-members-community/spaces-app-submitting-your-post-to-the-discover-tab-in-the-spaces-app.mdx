### Submitting a post from the Wix app

Create a new post or use an existing one to share on the "Discover" tab of the Spaces app. This makes it easy for people to find and read more of your content. From there, readers can decide to join your site and become a member.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Make sure your site in the app is not [requiring approval of new members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/approving-a-site-member). In addition, set [your profile to public](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-editing-your-profile#editing-your-profile-privacy-settings).
> - Make sure you [add the Blog app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-adding-apps-to-your-site) to your site.

#### To submit a post from the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Blog**.  Choose what kind of post you want to submit:

- **Create a new post:** Tap **Create New Post** to [create a new blog post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-creating-a-blog-post-in-the-wix-owner-app).
- **Choose an existing post:** Tap **Posts** to choose an existing post you want to share. Tap the **More Actions** icon   next to the relevant post.
    Tap **Submit to Discover**.   Tap **Submit**.

> **Important:**
>
> Posts and the order in which they appear in the "Discover" tab are at Wix’s full discretion.

### Guidelines for submitting posts

Click below for recommendations to increase the chances of your post being featured in the "Discover" tab.

> **Tip:**
>
> Currently, you can only submit posts in English.

### Cover image

We only accept posts that [include images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-uploading-media-to-the-media-manager). Having a cover image helps improve engagement.
We recommend avoiding low quality images, unrelated to the text, selfie or logo.

### Title and text formatting

- Make concise and attractive titles.
- Avoid capital letters and emojis.
- Legible font size.
- Keep text alignment left-to-right.
- Avoid writing in all **bold** and *italic*.
- We recommend using pictures, videos, references and hyperlinks that relate to content you are writing about.

### Topics to avoid

- Specific niche topics.
- Controversial topics.
- Harmful, unstudied science or spam.
- Hate speech.
- Plagiarism.
- Any type of drugs, homemade recipes for alternative medicine or promoting unhealthy habits.

### Legal recommendations

- We recommend citing credible sources when discussing scientific or legal issues.
- If you offer or promote a particular product on your site, make sure to place the relevant affiliate links.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about sharing your posts to the "Discover" tab in the Spaces app.

### Is my post immediately added to the "Discover" tab in the Spaces app?

No. Our team will review your post first and approve it based on the quality and content that members of the Spaces by Wix app are looking to read.

### How will I know if my post gets approved?

You receive a push notification to the Wix app when your post is published and in which category.

### How will I know if members are interacting with my post?

You are notified when members comment on your post.
