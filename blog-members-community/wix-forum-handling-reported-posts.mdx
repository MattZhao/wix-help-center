---
title: "Wix Forum: Handling Reported Posts"
---

Forum readers have the option of reporting content they find offensive or that they think is spam. When they do, you receive a notification of this report.

You can review the report and decide if you want to take action.

> **Important:**
>
> Members can only report other members' posts, comments, and replies. Posts, comments, and replies created by Admins and Moderators cannot be reported.

To learn how to delete a post, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-deleting-posts).To learn how to block or delete a site member, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-blocking-or-deleting-site-members).
