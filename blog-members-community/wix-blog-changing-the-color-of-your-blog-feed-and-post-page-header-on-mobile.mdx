---
title: "Wix Blog: Changing the Color of Your Blog Feed and Post Page Header on Mobile"
---

When viewing your blog feed or post page from a mobile device, you'll see a header at the top of the screen. You can change the color of this header from the Settings panel of your blog page or post page in your desktop Editor (not the Wix app on your mobile device).

From there, you can switch over to the mobile view to see how it looks.

### To change the background color of a header:

Go to the page that you want to edit in your Editor.

### Wix Editor | Show me how

1. Click **Pages**on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Choose to customize your main blog feed or the post page: 

- Main Blog Feed: Click **Blog**.
- Post Page: Click **Post**.

### Editor X | Show me how

1. Click **Pages**  at the top of Editor X.
2. Scroll down to **Blog Pages**.
3. Choose to customize your main blog feed or the post page: 

- Main Blog Feed: Click **Blog**.
- Post Page: Click **Post**.

2.  Click the Blog or the Post element on your page.
