The Wix member apps are free tools for your members to connect with your business. From your site’s dashboard or the Wix mobile app, choose which member app suits your needs best: [Spaces by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview) or [Fit by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/fit-by-wix-app-an-overview).

For example, salon owners might choose the Spaces app to enhance client experience. Learn more about the [Wix member apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps).

### Choosing your preferred member app

Choose your preferred member app from your site's dashboard or the Wix app.

> **Note:**
>
> If you have a branded app live in the app store, it automatically becomes your preferred member app.

#### To choose your preferred member app:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions** drop-down at the top and select **Mobile App Settings**.
3. Click the relevant mobile app tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Your own mobile app**).
4. Click **Choose App** next to **Mobile app preference**.
5. Select the mobile app that you would prefer your members to use.
6. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot showing where to choose your preferred mobile app.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch from editing your own mobile app to editing the relevant member app (e.g. Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix):

1. Tap the name of your own mobile app at the top.
2. Tap the relevant member app (e.g. Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix).
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top.
6. Tap **Manage app settings**.
7. Tap **App preference **and select the mobile app that you want your members to use.
8. Tap **Save**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about choosing your preferred app.

### Who can set the preferred member app?

Only site owners can choose the preferred app, [collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-app-inviting-and-managing-collaborators) cannot.

### What happens if I change my preferred member app?

Your members have the option to use the new app or continue using the previous app. Your updates continue to be sent to whichever app your members use.

### Why don't I see my branded app as an option when choosing my preferred member app?

You can only set the Branded app by Wix as your preferred member app once it is live in app stores. Your members can use another member app until your branded app is available.

### What happens to my member app once my branded app is live in app stores?

Once you switch apps, your members are encouraged to download your branded app for the best visitor experience. However, anyone who continues to use another member app (e.g. the Spaces app) will still see content updates.
