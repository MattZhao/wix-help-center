---
title: "Wix Groups: Customizing Your Group Page from the Wix App"
---

Customize the look of your group pages in the [member apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps) using the Wix app. To match the theme of your group, you can edit the names of each tab that appears in your group such as the Discussion Board, Media and the About tab. You can also get creative and create a custom tab for your members to meet the needs of your group.

### To customize your Group Page from the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Groups**.  Tap on the group you want to customize.  Tap **Edit** next to your group name.

Choose what you want to customize:

### Group cover photo

1. Tap the** Camera **icon** **.
2. Choose one of the following:

- Tap **Take Photo**.

1. Tap **Use Photo**.
- Tap **Choose from Device**.
3. Do one of the following:

- Tap **Upload**.
- Tap **Edit **to adjust the way your photo looks.

1. Tap **Save**.
2. Tap **Upload**.

### Group name

Enter the name of your group in the **Group Name** field. **Tip:** You can edit this at any time.

### Group type

Tap the **Group Type **drop-down to select the privacy level of your group: 
- **Public:** Anyone (including anonymous visitors) can see the group's posts and media. They still need to sign up in order to post.
- **Private:** The group appears on the group list, but members must request to join it.
- **Secret:** The group is only visible to members you invite.

7.  Tap **Save**.

### Discussion

1. Tap **Group Tabs** under **Content**.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to **Discussion**.
3. To change the name of your **Discussion** tab:

1. Tap **Rename**.
2. Enter a new name in the **Tab Name** field.
4. Tap **Done**.

### Media

1. Tap **Group Tabs** under **Content**.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to **Media**.
3. To change the name of your **Media** tab:

1. Tap **Rename**.
2. Enter a new name in the **Tab Name **field.
3. Tap **Done**.
4. (Optional) Tap **Hide Tab** to no longer display this to your group members.
5. (Optional) Tap** Show Tab** to display this to your group members.

### About

1. Tap **Group Tabs** under **Content**.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to **About**.
3. Tap **Settings** to edit your group description: 

1. Enter a new group description.
2. Tap the **Gallery** icon  to add multiple images group description:

- Tap **Take Photo **to take a photo on the spot.
- Tap **Choose from Device **to select an existing photo from your phone.
- Tap **Select from Media Manager** to choose a photo from the media manager.** **
3. Tap the **Video** icon    to add a video to your group description:

- Tap **Take a Video** to record a video on the spot.
- Tap **Choose from Device **to choose an existing video from your phone.
- Tap **Select from Media Manager **to choose a video from the media manager.
- Tap **Create with Video Maker **to** **[**create your own video using Wix Media**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-a-promotional-video-in-the-wix-owner-app).
4. Tap the** GIF **icon  to add a GIF to your group description.
4. Tap **Save**.
5. To change the name of your **About** tab:

1. Tap **Rename**.
2. Enter a new name in the **Tab Name** field.
3. Tap **Done**.

### Events

1. Tap **Group Tabs** under **Content**.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to **Events**.
3. Tap **Settings** to select which events to display to group members: 

1. Select the** **checkbox next to each event you want to display.
2. Tap **Save**.
4. To change the name of your **Events** tab:

1. Tap **Rename**.
2. Enter a new name in the **Tab Name **field.
3. Tap **Done**.
5. (Optional) Tap **Hide Tab** to no longer display this tab on your Group Page.
6. (Optional) Tap **Show Tab** to display this tab on your Group Page.

### Custom Tab

Create a custom tab for your members.

1. Tap **Group Tabs** under **Content**.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to **New Tab.**
3. (Optional) Tap **Hide Tab** to no longer display this to your group members.
4. (Optional) Tap **Show Tab** to display this to your group members.
5. Tap **Manage **to customize your new tab:

1. Enter a name for your new tab.
2. Write a description for your custom tab.
3. Tap the **Gallery** icon  to add multiple images group description:

- Tap **Take Photo **to take a photo on the spot.
- Tap **Choose from Device **to select an existing photo from your phone.
- Tap **Select from Media Manager** to choose a photo from the media manager.** **
4. Tap the **Video** icon    to add a video to your group description:

- Tap **Take a Video** to record a video on the spot.
- Tap **Choose from Device **to choose an existing video from your phone.
- Tap **Select from Media Manager **to choose a video form the media manager.
- Tap **Create with Video Maker to **[**create your own video using Wix Media**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-a-promotional-video-in-the-wix-owner-app).
5. Tap the** GIF **icon  to add a GIF to your group description.
6. Tap **Save**.
7. To change the name of your **Events** tab:

1. Tap **Rename**.
2. Enter a new name in the **Tab Name **field.
3. Tap **Done**.
8. Tap **Delete Tab** to remove the tab completely.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also [customize your Group Page from your desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-customizing-your-group-page).
