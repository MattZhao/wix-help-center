---
title: "Wix Blog: Unpublishing a Blog Post"
---

You can unpublish a post that you want to remove from your blog by reverting it to "Draft" status. This way, the content is still available for you to refer to, or republish in the future.

### To unpublish a post:

Go to your [site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).  Click the **Blog **tab.  Click **Edit** on the post you want to unpublish.  Click the **Expand ** icon in the top right.  Click **Revert to Draft**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also unpublish a post from the Wix mobile app by clicking the **Show More **icon  beside a post and selecting **Revert to Draft**.
