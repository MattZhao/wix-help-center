---
title: "Wix Member Apps Request: Allowing Only Specific Collaborators to Post Announcements"
---

Currently, any of your site collaborators can post announcements from the Wix mobile app to your members. It is not possible to give only specific collaborators permission to post announcements.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
