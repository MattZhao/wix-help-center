---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Ability to Share Blog Posts Automatically on Social Platforms"
---

Currently, it is not possible to share automatically to your social platforms when you publish a post. However, it is possible to [manually share your blog or to share individual posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-sharing-a-blog-post-on-social-media).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
