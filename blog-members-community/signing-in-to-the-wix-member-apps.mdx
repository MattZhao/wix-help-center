---
title: "Signing in to the Wix Member Apps"
---

The Wix member apps, including Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix, Dine by Wix, and the branded app, allow your site members to sign in and engage with your content and community on the go. Members can sign in using their email address or third-party social logins (except in the [branded app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-request-adding-more-login-options)). You can learn more about social login privacy with [Google](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/10130420#siwg&zippy=), [Facebook](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/facebook-login/overview/), and [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/sign-in-with-apple/).

### To sign in to the Wix member apps:

Open the Wix member app you want to sign in to.  Tap **Log in**.   Choose a method for signing in:

### Google

1. Tap **Continue** **with Google**.
2. Select your Google account and follow the sign in instructions.

### Apple

1. Tap **Continue** **with Apple**.
2. Tap **Continue** under **Sign in with Apple**.
3. Choose whether you want to show or hide your email address:

- **Show your email:** Your real email is visible in the app and you will be able to sign in with it from a desktop.
- **Hide your email:** Apple creates a dummy email address with an automatic password to hide your personal information.

### Facebook

1. Tap **Continue** **with Facebook**.
2. Tap **Continue** to allow Wix to use facebook.com to sign in.
3. Follow the sign in instructions on Facebook to sign in to your account. When you're signed in tap **Continue**.

### Email

1. Tap **Log in with Email**.
2. Enter your email address and tap **Continue**.
3. Enter your password and tap **Log In**.

> **The Wix member apps are supported on the following devices:**
>
> - **iPhone:** iOS 14 and above (iPhone 6 and above)
> - **Android: **7.0 and above
