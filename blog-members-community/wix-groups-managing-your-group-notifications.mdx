### Managing your group notifications

Manage notifications from your live site or the Wix app to stay updated.

Live siteWix appYou can share these steps with members who want to manage their own notifications from the live site.
1. Go to your live site and log in.
2. Go to the relevant group on your live site.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  at the top right of the group.
4. Click **Notification Settings**.
5. Choose where you want to get notifications from:
Website notificationsClick the **Web****site notifications** toggle to manage site notifications (in the bell icon) when there are group updates:
- **Enabled:** You receive site notifications when there are group updates. Select the checkboxes next to the updates you want to know about:

- **Posts:** When someone posts in the group.
- **Comments: **When someone comments in the group.
- **Reactions: **When someone reacts to your post (e.g. with an emoji).
- **Member Requests: **Get notified about new member requests.
- **Group Updates:** When an admin makes changes to the group.
- **New Members:** When new members join the group.
- **Disabled: **You are not notified about group updates.
EmailClick the **Email** toggle to get notified by email when there are group updates:
- **Enabled:** You receive email notifications about the group updates you choose. Select the checkboxes next to the updates you want to know about:

- **Posts:** When someone posts in the group.
- **Comments: **When someone comments on your post.
- **Member Requests:** Get notified about new member requests.
- **Group Updates: **When an admin makes changes to the group.
- **New Members: **When new members join the group.
- **Disabled: **You do not receive email notifications about group updates.

**Note:** Notifications are sent to the email address you enter in the [business info](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-sites-business-information) of your site's dashboard.Mobile appThe Mobile App settings control the updates you receive on the Wix app and the members app when relevant.
Click **Mobile App **to get notifications on the Wix mobile app for the following group updates:
- **Enabled:** You receive notifications on the app about the group updates you choose. Select the checkboxes next to the updates you want to know about:

- **Posts:** When someone posts in the group.
- **Comments: **When someone comments in the group.

- **Member Requests: **Get notified about new member requests.
- **Group Updates:** When an admin makes changes to the group.
- **New Members:** When new members join the group.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about group notifications.

### How can I notify my members of with the latest group updates?

You can use the [Groups Weekly Digest](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-deactivating-the-weekly-digest-email) to email a weekly roundup of top posts, stories, and reactions, giving members a quick snapshot of what's happening directly to their inbox.

### Can group members manage the notifications they receive?

Yes. Group members can control their notification preferences through their [member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps). Share the steps below with your group members:

1. Go to the relevant site on the members app.
2. Tap the **Groups** tab.
3. Tap the relevant group.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon   next to the group name.
5. Choose how you want to receive notifications: 

- **Push Notifications:** Tap the **Group Notifications **toggle to get notified about updates from the group on your mobile device.

- **Enabled:** All notifications for the group are enabled. Tap the toggles next to the updates you want to turn off:

- **Posts: **When someone posts in the group.
- **Comments: **See when someone comments on your posts.
- **Reactions: **Get notified when someone reacts to your posts.
- **New Members: **Find out when someone new wants to join your group.
- **Members Joined: **When new members join the group.
- **Disabled:** No notifications are sent for this group.
- **Email notifications**: Tap the **Group Notifications **toggle to get notified about updates from the group to your email:

- **Enabled:** You receive email notifications about the group updates you choose. Tap the toggles next to the updates you want to turn off:

- **Posts:** When someone posts in the group.
- **Comments: **When someone comments on your post.
- **Members Joined: **When new members join the group.
- **Disabled: **You do not receive email notifications about group updates.
