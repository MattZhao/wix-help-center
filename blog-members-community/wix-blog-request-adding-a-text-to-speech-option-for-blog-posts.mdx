---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Adding a Text-to-Speech Option for Blog Posts"
---

Currently, there is no option to convert the text of your blog posts into audio (also known as text-to-speech).

As a workaround, you can record yourself reading the blog post and [add the audio file to the post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-uploading-files-in-wix-blog).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
