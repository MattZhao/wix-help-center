title: "Wix Forum Request: Allowing Guests to Comment Anonymously in Wix Forum"
---

Currently, it is not possible to disable the login requirement so that forum readers can comment on posts without logging in to your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
