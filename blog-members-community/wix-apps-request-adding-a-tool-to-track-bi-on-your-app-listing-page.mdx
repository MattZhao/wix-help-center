title: "Wix Apps Request: Adding a Tool to Track BI on Your App Listing Page"
---

Currently, it is not possible to track business information statistics such as: impressions, keyword search (utm tracking), app rankings, and page views on the app listing page in the Wix App Market.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
