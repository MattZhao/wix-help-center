Stay connected with your members by sending them personalized push notifications. For example, remind members about items in their cart, or pending bookings that may expire.

You can also resend messages to specific members and see how many notifications were opened and delivered for better communication.

### Creating and sending push notifications

Create and send push notifications conveniently from your site's dashboard, the Wix app, or the Wix Studio app. You can direct your members to a specific item (e.g. product or service), a particular app tab, or a custom screen.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Create Notification** under **Push notifications**.
[image: A screenshot of the Create Notification call to action.]
1. Click **+ Create Push Notification.**
2. Enter the message you want to show in the notification.
3. Choose what opens when members tap the notification under **Notification action**: 

- **An Item:** Send members to view a specific product or service. Choose the relevant product / service from the **Select item** drop-down.
- **A Screen: **Direct members to a specific screen on your site, like 'home' or 'shop'. Choose the relevant screen from the **Select a screen **drop-down.
- **Custom Screen:**  Create your own screen with unlimited text, links and media.

1. Click **+ Create a Custom Screen**.
2. Add custom text,  add URLs, images and more.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Choose who should receive this notification under **Select recipients**:

- **Contacts:** Select the checkboxes next to the members you want to receive the notification.
- **Labels:** A category that you've applied to contacts, or one that was created for you (e.g. Contacted Me, Customers).
- **Segments: **A segment you've defined for a specific group of contacts.
5. Click **Send**.

1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Push notifications **under** Marketing**.
4. Tap **Send Push Notification**.
5. Tap **Start Creating**.
6. Enter the message you want to appear in the notification.
7. Choose what opens when members tap the notification:

- **An Item:** Send members to view a specific product or service. Choose the relevant product / service from the **Select an item** drop-down.
- **A Screen: **Direct members to a specific screen on your site, like 'home' or 'shop'. Choose the relevant screen from the **Select a screen **drop-down.
- **A Custom Screen:** Create your own screen with unlimited text, links and media.

1. Tap **+ Create a Custom Screen**.
2. Add your content.
3. Tap **Save**.
8. (Optional) Tap** Send a test notification to my phone **to see how members will see your notification.
9. Tap **Next**.
10. Select the members you want to receive the notification and tap **Next**.
11. Review the notification details. When ready, tap **Send Push Notification**.

### Resending push notifications

Track the opening rate of your push notifications to view which members have interacted with your notification and those that have not, and decide whether to resend the notification.

Sometimes, members may not have seen or opened a notification. In these cases, resending the notification can encourage engagement.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Create Notification** under **Push notifications**.
3. Click **Edit and Resend **next to the relevant push notification.
[image: The Edit and Resend button next to a push notification in the dashboard]
1. (Optional) Make the desired changes to the notification.
2. Scroll down to **Select Recipients** and** **select the checkboxes next to the members you want to re-send the push notification to.
3. Click **Send**.

1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Push Notifications **under** Marketing. **
4. Tap on the relevant push notification.
5. View the **Member Stats** of the notification:

- **Opened:** Members who opened your push notification.
- **No Interaction Yet: **Members who read your notification on their home screen, haven't seen it yet, or have their notifications turned off.
6. Tap the **More Actions **icon  at the top right.
7. Tap **Resend this push notification** to send the notification again.
8. (Optional) Make the desired changes to the notification.
9. Select the checkboxes next to the members you want to re-send the push notification to.
10. Tap **Next**.
11. Tap **Send Push Notification**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about push notifications.

### Where do my members view their push notifications?

Your members will receive the push notification on their mobile device if they have enabled notifications for your app.

### Why can't my members see my push notifications?

If your members aren't receiving push notifications they should check their device's settings. They must enable push notifications for the members app in the general notification settings of their mobile device.

### A test notification was sent to my phone, so why can't I see it?

If you can't see the test notification you just sent, ensure that:
- The relevant [member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps) is installed on your phone and you're logged in with the same Wix account.
- Check your phone settings – the app notifications should be switched on.

### Is it possible to delete a push notification after I've sent it?

You cannot undo sending the push notification once it has been sent.
