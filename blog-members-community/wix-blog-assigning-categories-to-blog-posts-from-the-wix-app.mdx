---
title: "Wix Blog: Assigning Categories to Blog Posts from the Wix App"
---

Blog categories help readers find what interests them in your blog. When you create a blog post, you can assign up to 10 categories to your post. From the Wix app, you can assign categories to a new blog post or edit the categories of an existing post.

> **Note:**
>
> For additional category management, go to your blog's [Categories tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/categories) on desktop, where you can edit, delete or reorder categories.

### To assign categories to a new blog post:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage** at the bottom right.  Tap **Blog**.   Tap **Posts.**  Tap the relevant post.  Tap the **Show More **icon  next to the relevant post.  Select **Post Settings**.  Tap the **Categories** tab.  Select up to 10 categories or tap **Create New Category** to create a new one.  Tap **Next**.  Select an option:

- **Save Changes: **Save your changes in draft form.
- **Publish Post: **Publish your post with the updated categories.
