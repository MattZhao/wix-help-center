---
title: "Creating a Group Redirect (301)"
---

When moving your site to Wix, it's important to set up 301 redirects since the URL structure from your old site host, might look slightly different to how it looks at Wix. You can redirect from [one URL to another](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-a-301-redirect-for-a-page-on-your-site) or from [one domain to another](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-a-301-redirect-from-one-domain-to-another).

You can also create a group redirect which redirects related URLs that share the same path.

### To set up a group redirect:

[Go to your SEO Dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-f3388913-140f-47a9-b2ba-4bf86b60e04e&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO).  Select **Go to URL Redirect Manager **under **Tools and settings**.  Click + **Create** **New Redirect**.  Select **Group redirect**.  Enter the original path for the group of pages under **Old URL Path**.

**Note:** For the example mentioned above, we would enter "products".  Enter the new path for the group of pages under **New URL Path**.

**Note:** For the example mentioned above, we would enter "product-page".  Click **Save & Close**.

> **Limitations of the Group Redirect Tool:**
>
> It is not possible to:
> - Add spaces in either the old or new path.
> - Use a path which has already been used in another redirect (single or group).
> - Redirect from your site's home page.
> - Add group redirects for external domains.
> - Redirect a group of pages to a single page (e.g. mystunningwebsite.com/**products/product-name **to mystunningwebsite.com/**product-gallery**).
