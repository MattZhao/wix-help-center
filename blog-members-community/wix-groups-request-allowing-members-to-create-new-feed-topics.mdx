---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Allowing Members to Create New Feed Topics"
---

Currently, members can [assign existing feed topics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-assigning-topics-to-your-posts) to their posts in Wix Groups, but they cannot create new feed topics.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
