### Inviting people to collaborate on a site

Invite people to collaborate on a site and choose a specific role depending on their expertise.

> **Note:**
>
> Only site owners and co-owners can invite people to collaborate on a site.

#### To invite collaborators:

Go to the relevant workspace in the Wix Studio app.  Tap **Sites**  at the bottom.  Tap the More Actions icon next to the relevant site.  Tap **Invite Collaborators**.   Tap** Invite Others**.   Enter the email address of the person you're inviting.  Select the checkbox next to the relevant role under **Choose Roles**.

**Tip:** [Create a custom role on your desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators) for your collaborators.   Tap **Send**.

### Managing collaborators on a site

Manage a collaborators' role and permissions based on site and project needs. You can modify roles, remove access, or resend invitations as necessary.

#### To manage collaborators on a site:

Go to the relevant workspace in the Wix Studio app.  Tap **Sites**  at the bottom.  Tap the More Actions icon  next to the relevant site.  Tap **Invite Collaborators**.  Tap the More Actions icon  next to the relevant person.  Choose how you want to manage them:

### Change role

1. Tap **Change Role**.
2. Select the checkbox next to the new role(s).
3. Tap **Save**.

[image: A screenshot of changing a role for a collaborator in the Wix Studio app.]

### Resend invite

1. Tap **Resend Invite**.
2. Tap **Resend** to confirm.

[image: A screenshot of Resending an invite to collaborate on a site in the Wix Studio app.]

### Remove collaborator

When needed, you can remove a collaborator from a site. Collaborators do not receive a notification when they are removed, but they won't be able to access your site anymore.
1. Tap **Remove**.
2. Tap** Remove **to confirm.
