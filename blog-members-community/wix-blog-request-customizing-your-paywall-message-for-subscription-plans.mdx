Currently, it is not possible to customize the message that unsubscribed readers see when they view a post that is [connected to a subscription plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-creating-blog-post-subscriptions).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
