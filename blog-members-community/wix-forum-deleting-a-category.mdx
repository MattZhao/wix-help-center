---
title: "Wix Forum: Deleting a Category"
---

When you delete a category, all the posts and comments in that category are also deleted. If you would like to keep certain posts from the category, you can move them to a different category before you delete the original category. [Learn how to move posts in your forum.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-performing-admin-functions-on-posts)

You can delete a category directly from your editor, your site's dashboard, or on-the-go using the Wix app on your mobile device.

### To delete a category:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Forum](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Forum&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/forum/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Categories** on the left.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant category.
4. Click **Delete Category**.
5. Click **Delete** again to confirm.
[image: A screenshot showing how to delete a category from your site's dashboard in Wix Forum.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Forum**.
4. Tap **Categories**.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant category.
6. Tap **Delete**.
7. Tap **Delete** again to confirm.
