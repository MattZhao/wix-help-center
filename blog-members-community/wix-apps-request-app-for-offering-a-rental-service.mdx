---
title: "Wix Apps Request: App for Offering a Rental Service"
---

Currently, Wix does not have a dedicated app for renting cars, sports gear and equipment, inflatables, or second-hand high fashion.

However, you can add the third-party app [Twice Rentals](https://www.wix.com/app-market/twice-rentals) to your site directly from the Wix App Market.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
