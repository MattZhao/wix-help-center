Assigning blog roles to specific people is a great way to share some of your blog's responsibilities and tasks.

For example, if you have a blog that requires staff writers or editors, you can give them certain roles and permissions as part of managing your busy blog. You can also [create custom roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators) for any type of role to match your staffing needs.

Add writers and editors from Roles and Permissions in your site's Settings or from your Writers list.

### Blog roles and their associated permissions

You can choose from three different roles to assign when you invite people to collaborate on your blog: Blog Editor, Blog Writer, and Guest Writer.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also add Managed Writers to your blog without giving them any permissions. Adding a writer this way means that you can attribute a specific post to them on a one-time basis. [Learn more about adding Managed Writers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-managed-writers-to-your-blog)

Each blog role has different permissions associated with it. For example, Blog Editors can write and manage their own posts and other writers' posts, whereas Blog Writers can only write and manage their own posts.

Blog Editors, Blog Writers, and Guest Writers are automatically added to [your site's Member's Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) as members. Managed Writers are not added as members.

### Adding a writer or editor from Roles and Permissions

You can add new writers or editors to your blog from Roles and Permissions in the Settings section of your site's dashboard. New writers appear in your drop-down list of authors only after they access the Blog Manager.

People that you add receive an invitation to the email address that you enter when you invite them. However, they can accept with any email address by signing in or creating a new Wix account. The invite is valid for 30 days. If your collaborator doesn't accept in this time, you can [resend it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site).

> **Note:**
>
> - If you feel that the email address that accepted an invitation is suspicious, you can [remove it from your collaborator list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-removing-a-collaborator-from-your-site).
> - New writers appear in your drop-down list of authors only after they access the Blog Manager.

> **Important:**
>
> By sending an invite to collaborate, you agree to the [Wix Terms of Use](http://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).

#### To add from roles and permissions:

Go to the [Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/manage-website/authorization) tab in your site's dashboard.  Click **Invite People**.  Enter the person's email address under **Email(s)**.

**Note: **You can add multiple email address by separating each address with a comma.   Select the required role under **Blog Roles**.

Click **Send** **Invite**.

### Add a writer or editor from your Writers list

The Writers list is the place where you manage all your current writers and invite new ones to your blog. You can add new writers or editors from the Writers List in the Blog section your dashboard.

People that you add receive an invitation to the email address that you enter when you invite them. However, they can accept with any email address by signing in or creating a new Wix account. The invite is valid for 30 days. If your collaborator doesn't accept in this time, you can [resend it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site).

> **Note:**
>
> - If you feel that the email address that accepted an invitation is suspicious, you can [remove it from your collaborator list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-removing-a-collaborator-from-your-site).
> - New writers appear in your drop-down list of authors only after they access the Blog Manager.

> **Important:**
>
> By sending an invite to collaborate, you agree to the [Wix Terms of Use](http://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).

#### To add a blog writer or blog editor from the Writers list:

Go to your [blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) in your dashboard.  Click the **Writers** tab in the panel on the left.  Click **Add Writer** at the top right.   Select the type of writer that you want to add and click **Continue**.   Choose what you want to do:

### Invite a new writer

When you choose to add a new writer, you're giving that person additional permissions and responsibilities on your blog. You can choose to make them a Blog Editor or a Blog Writer. 

1. Enter the person's email address.
2. Select what type of role you want to give:

- **Blog Editor:** They can write posts and manage your blog. They cannot manage other areas of your site.
- **Blog Writer:** They can write and publish posts, but cannot manage your blog.
- **Guest Writer:** They can write posts, but cannot publish them.
3. Click **Send Invite**.

### Create a managed writer

You can manually add managed writers to your blog without giving them any permissions to your site. Managed writers have no permissions on your site and cannot post themselves or customize their profile. Adding a writer this way means that you can attribute them to a specific post on a one-time basis.

1. Enter a name for the managed writer under **Name**.
2. (Optional) Add a profile picture and any other additional information for the writer.
3. Click **Save**.

### Changing a writer's role

After you've added a writer to your blog, you can change their role in the Roles and Permissions section of your site's dashboard.

#### To change a writer's role:

Go to [Roles & Permissions in your dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Froles-and-permissions%2F%3F&title=Select+a+site&primaryButtonText=Edit+Roles+).  Click the More Actions icon  next to the writer that you want to edit.  Select **Change Role**.  Scroll down to the **Blog Roles **section.  Select the role you want to use.

Click **Save**.
