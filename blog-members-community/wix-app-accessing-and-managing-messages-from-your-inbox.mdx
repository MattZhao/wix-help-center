### Accessing your inbox

When using the Wix app, you can access your inbox to see who's on your site in real-time, start a conversation with them on the go, manage conversations and more.

> **Tip:**
>
> To chat with your site visitors, you must have the [Wix Chat app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-an-overview) installed on your site.

#### To access your inbox:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Inbox ** at the bottom.  Once you access your inbox, you can perform the following actions:

- **Chat with site visitors: **Chat with site visitors in real-time to let them know you're available. 

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
2. Enable the **Website chat status **toggle to show people you are available to chat in real-time.
- **Continue conversations with visitors: **Keep ongoing chats active to maintain conversation flow with visitors.

1. Tap the relevant conversation.
2. Tap the channel drop-down to** **[choose how you want to respond](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-accessing-and-managing-messages-from-your-inbox#sending-messages-from-your-inbox).

### Sending messages from your inbox

Choose your preferred communication channel to instantly reply to messages and share updates with your contacts, new site visitors, and customers. Using the inbox, you can continue conversations where you left off, ensuring a smooth communication experience.

#### To send a message:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Inbox ** at the bottom.  Choose what you want to do:

### Reply to a message

1. Tap on the relevant contact you want to reply.
2. Tap the channel drop-down and select how you want to send your message:

- **Chat: **Send a chat message to members.** **To use this option, you must have the [Wix Chat app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-an-overview) on your site.
- **Email:** Send an email to members whose email addresses are saved in your contact list.
- **SMS: **The SMS channel is available when you purchase a [Business Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-a-phone-number) and the member's number is saved in your contact list.

### Send a new message

1. Tap the **Compose** icon  at the top right.
2. Choose the relevant option:

- **Select an existing contact:** Choose a contact from the available list.
- **Add a new contact:** Manually add someone new to your contact list.

1. Tap **Add contact** icon   and enter the details.
2. Tap **Save**.
3. Tap the channel drop-down and select how you want to send your message:

- **Chat: **Send a chat message to members.** **To use this option, you must have the [Wix Chat app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-an-overview) on your site.
- **Email:** Send an email to members whose email addresses are saved in your contact list.
- **SMS: **The SMS channel is available when you purchase a [Business Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-a-phone-number) and the member's number is saved in your contact list.

4. (Optional) Tap the **Send an item** icon  and choose what you want to send:

### Setting up saved replies in your inbox and chat

Save time by setting up reply messages to send to visitors through your inbox or chat. With saved replies, you can use a pre-existing reply messages, edit them, or create a customized reply message.

#### To set up a saved reply in your inbox:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Inbox ** at the bottom.  Tap the **More Actions** icon .  Tap** Settings**.  Tap **Saved replies **under** General Settings**.  Choose the type of reply you want to work on:

- **Change an existing saved reply: **Tap the **Edit** icon  next to the relevant reply.
- **Create a new saved reply:** Tap **New Saved Reply** to create a custom message for your visitors.** **
    Tap **Save**.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about managing your inbox from the Wix app.

### Can I request payment through chat without a connected payment method?

No.** **You need a payment method connected to request payment through chat. Learn how to connect a [payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview) to your site.

### Can I keep attached items when switching channels (e.g email to chat) without needing to upload them again?

If you have attached items to your message and decide to move channels (e.g. from email to chat), you will not lose the attached items and will not have to upload them again.

### Can I message multiple people at once?

Yes. You can save time by [sending a single message to multiple recipients simultaneously](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-sending-a-broadcast-message-to-your-contacts-in-the-wix-owner-app).
