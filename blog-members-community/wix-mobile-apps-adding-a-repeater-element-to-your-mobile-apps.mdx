title: "Wix Mobile Apps: Adding a Repeater Element to Your Mobile Apps"
---

Add a repeater element to your Wix mobile apps (Fit by Wix, Spaces by Wix, or your own native mobile app) to create a more organized and user-friendly experience.

For example, if you run a fitness app, you can use a repeater to show a list of available classes and trainers, so members can see all their options in one place.

---

### Adding a repeater element to your mobile apps

You can add or remove items on your repeater element and customize each item individually. Choose between a grid or vertical list layout, adjust image sizes and text, add a repeater title, and more.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Your own mobile app**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
5. Click **Elements **on the left.
6. Click **List** and select a **Repeater** element.
[image: A screenshot how to get to repeater on your desktop.]
1. Choose what you want to edit in the panel on the right:
ContentAdd new items or edit existing items on your repeater.
1. Click the **Content** tab and choose what you want to do:

- **Add a new item: **Click **+ Add New Item** and then add a photo, text and behavior to your item. For example, have the item open to a new screen or send an email. Then click **Add**.
- **Edit an existing item: **Click the **More Actions** icon [image: undefined] next to the relevant item, then choose **Edit**, **Duplicate**, **Hide**, or** Delete**.
- **Reorder: **Click and drag an item to a new position on the list.
2. Click **Save & Publish.**
SettingsSet the style for all your items. Adjust the layout, spacing, and other design elements.
1. Click the **Settings **tab and choose what you want to customize: 

- **Layout:** Select a repeater layout type, set the spacing between items, choose a crop ratio, and enable the text toggle if you want to add text on the items.
- **Design: **Select the text font, image style, and corner radius, and choose whether to show an overlay.
- **Show title: **Enable the toggle to add a title to your repeater.
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding around the repeater.

**Note: **Click the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins.
- **General Settings: **Click **General Settings **and choose which device to show this element on: **All**, **Android,** or **iOS**.

**Note: **You can show or hide elements of your site depending on what operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. Your elements are visible on all operating systems by default, but you can decide what to show on iOS, Android, or both.
2. Click **Save & Publish.**

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about adding a repeater element to your mobile apps.

### Can I customize the design of each item in a repeater?

Yes, you can. Tap the **More Actions** icon [image: undefined] next to the relevant item, then tap **Edit**. You can change the picture, title, subtitle, and behavior of each repeater item.

### What types of content can I add to a repeater item?

You can add photos, text, and set behaviors for each repeater item. Click the **More Actions** icon [image: undefined] next to the relevant item, then click **Edit **to add content.

### Can I add links or actions to items in a repeater?

Yes, you can add different actions when a user interacts with a repeater item. Click the **More Actions** icon [image: undefined] next to the item and then click **Edit**. From the **Select action** drop-down, choose a behavior from one of the many options such as **Open URL** or **Send us a message**.

### Can I use multiple repeaters on the same page?

Yes, you can add multiple repeaters to the same page to organize different sets of repeating content. Follow the steps in the section above for each repeater.

### Can I add different types of content in one repeater?

Yes, you can display various types of content in a single repeater by customizing each item. This flexibility allows you to mix and match different types of information in one cohesive design. Click the **More Actions** icon [image: undefined] next to an item, then click **Edit **to adjust the content individually.
