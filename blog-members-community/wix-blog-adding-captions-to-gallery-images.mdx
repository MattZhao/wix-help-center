---
title: "Wix Blog: Adding Captions to Gallery Images"
---

Adding a gallery to a blog post is a great way to group multiple images together. The images in the gallery can be captioned individually to help explain more about each particular image.

> **Note:**
>
> If you haven't already added a gallery, click here to learn [how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-a-gallery-to-a-blog-post).

### To add captions to gallery images:

Go to the [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) tab in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant blog post.  Click the gallery in the post.  Click the **Organize Media** icon  on the blog toolbar.  Click on the image you'd like to caption.  Click **Edit**.  Click on the text box under **Title** to add a caption to your image.  Click **Done**.
