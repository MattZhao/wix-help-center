Categories are a great way to sort your blog into different topics and give your readers a general idea what your blog posts are about. Once you've set up your categories, you'll be able to display them on your blog pages as a category menu. You can also set post feeds to display specific categories.

### Creating blog categories

Create categories for each of the topics your blog covers. Your can assign posts to more than one category (up to 10 per post) so you have the freedom to divide your posts however you want. Give your categories easily understandable titles and descriptions so readers know what to expect.

#### To create a category:

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/categories) in your site's dashboard.  Click **New Category**.

**Note:** Your blog comes with a few default categories, click **Edit** to customize one.  Enter the category details:

- **Category Label: **The label appears on your blog menu and at the bottom of blog posts in this category. Make sure to keep it short and informative.
- **Category URL Slug: **This is the URL of the blog category which readers can see in the browser bar (and use to access the category directly). By default, it takes the category label as the slug (end of the URL), but you can edit it if you need to.

> **Note:**
>
> The URL slug must be at least two characters long and can't have more than one slash.  For example, you can change the slug to **recipes/vegetables**, but you cannot change it to **recipes/vegetables/tomatoes

Enter the SEO and social sharing settings of the category.  This info appears in search engines and when you share the category on social media.

- **Category title:** Enter a title that describes your category. It does not have to be the same as the category label.
- **Category description:** Enter a description for the category so readers know what to expect when they find your category on Google or see it shared on social media.

### Assigning posts to categories

You can assign posts to categories while you're writing the post, or you can come back to the Post Composer later to add it to categories.

#### To assign posts to categories:

[Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog/overview) in your site's dashboard.  Select the post that you want to edit, or click **Create New Post** to add a new one.  Select the categories you want to assign the post to. (Up to 10 categories).

**Tip:** Click **Create Category** to create a new category.   Click **Publish**.

### Reordering your categories

Your categories appear in the category menu and feed in the order that you create them. You can change this order by dragging them up and down the list on the Categories tab.

#### To reorder your categories:

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/categories) in your site's dashboard.  Click and drag the handle to move the categories up or down.

### Deleting a category

You can delete categories if you no longer need them. Deleting a category does **not** delete the posts inside it. The posts still appear under **All Posts **and any other categories they were assigned to.

#### To delete a category:

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog/categories) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over a category and click the More Actions** **icon.   Click **Delete**.  Click **Delete** to confirm.

### FAQs

### How do I rename the "All Posts" category?

The "All Posts" category holds all of your blog posts, regardless of which categories they were assigned to. You can rename this category to change it on the blog menu.

To change the title of the "All Posts" category:
1. [Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog/categories) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Rename** next to **All Posts**.
3. Enter a new title and click **Save**.

### How do I change my "main" category?

Although a post can belong to more than one category, it only appears with a single category label on your blog feed. This label is the "main" category.
To change the "main" category:
1. [Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) in your site's dashboard.
2. Do one of the following:

- Click **Create new post**.
- Click **Edit** to edit an existing post.
3. Click the **Categories** tab in the panel on the left.
4. Click the checkbox next to the relevant category.
5. Click **Set as main**.

### Some of my categories are missing, why can't I see them?

You can create as many categories as you want, however in some places, there is a limit to how many you can see:
- **Dashboard: **You can see (and create) up to 100 categories from the Categories tab in your site's dashboard.
- **Category label drop-down: **You can see up to 30 categories on the category label drop-down (where you select a category for a blog post)
- **Category menu: **You can display up to 50 categories on the blog menu.

### Can I create subcategories?

Currently, it is not possible to create subcategories in Wix Blog. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-request-creating-subcategories-in-wix-blog) to vote for this feature.
