---
title: "Branded App: Setting Up Apple In-App Purchase"
---

Sell your digital services, products and more directly from your branded app with [Apple's In-App purchase](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase). Some examples of what you can sell include bookings services, digital downloads, event tickets, online programs and videos.

If you are selling physical goods from your app, you do not need to set up in-app purchase.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Right now, in-app purchase supports single payments only. Recurring payments (e.g. subscriptions) are not currently supported.
> - Pricing plans are not supported, both single payment plans and recurring payment plans. This includes any apps integrated with pricing plans (e.g. online programs sold via pricing plan). 
>
> **Note: **Single payment plans are paying for a service upfront, without having to worry about a recurring monthly charge.

---

### Step 1 | Set up Apple's in-app purchase

You complete most of the in-app purchase setup steps from your Apple Developer account. To make the process easier, we've added detailed instructions inside your Branded app dashboard to guide you through the process.

#### To set up Apple's in-app purchase:

Go to your [Branded app dashboard](https://www.wix.com/branded-apps?path=welcome&referralInfo=HelpCenter) in your Wix account.  Click **Apple-In-App Purchase** on the left.  Click **Get Started**.  Click the drop-down next to **Step 1: Sign Apple's Paid Applications Agreement **and follow the instructions provided.   When you're finished select the checkbox at the bottom and click **Save & Continue**.  Click the drop-down next to **Step 2: Set up your banking and tax information** and follow the instructions provided.  When you're finished select the checkbox at the bottom and click **Save & Continue**.

---

### Step 2 | Choose which digital goods and/or services to submit to Apple

Once you've agreed to Apple's terms, any digital good or service you provide that's available to sell with in-app purchase appears on the submission page. You can choose what to include or exclude from your submission. Some examples of what you can sell include:

> **Important:**
>
> - Currently in-app purchase supports single payments only. Any recurring payments (e.g. invoices or subscriptions) are not currently supported.
> - Pricing plans are not supported, both single payment plans and recurring payment plans. This includes any apps integrated with pricing plans (e.g. online programs sold via pricing plan).

#### To choose which digital goods and/or services to submit:

Go to your [Branded App dashboard](https://www.wix.com/branded-apps?path=welcome&referralInfo=HelpCenter) in your Wix account.  Click **Apple-In-App Purchase** under **Manage** on the left.  Click the drop-down next to **Step 3: Submit your services to Apple**.  Every available digital good and/or service is included in a list in the **Awaiting submission** tab. Review the list to make sure the information is accurate. **Included** or **Excluded** labels appear next to each listing to show you whether it's included in the submission or not.

(Optional) If you don't want to include one of the listed goods or services in your submission:

1. Click the More Actions icon next to a good or service.
2. Click** Exclude from app submission**.
3. Click **Exclude** to confirm.

> **Note:**
>
> Some services such as online programs require you to delete or archive the program first before you can exclude it from submission.

---

### Step 3 | Resubmit your branded app for approval

Once you have approved each good or service, resubmit your branded app for approval.

#### To resubmit your branded app for approval:

Go to your [Branded App dashboard](https://www.wix.com/branded-apps?path=welcome&referralInfo=HelpCenter) in your Wix account.  Click **Apple-In-App Purchase** under **Manage** on the left.  Click the drop-down next to **Step 3: Submit your services to Apple**.   In the **Awaiting Submission** tab, click **Continue to App Submission**.  Click **Go to Branded App Overview** to resubmit your app so Apple can review it with the new services you added.  From the **Branded App Overview** tab, click the **Review** drop-down to confirm the digital services you're submitting to Apple for approval.

Click **Resubmit**.  Click **Submit** to confirm.

---

### Step 4 | Wait for Apple to approve your services

Your application is now in review with Apple. The label in the** Branded App Overview **tab says **In Review**.

You can track what services are currently in review and approved from the** Apple In-App Purchase** tab. Click the tabs at the top to see which services are **Awaiting submission**, **In Review by Apple**, or **Approved for sale**.

### FAQs

Below are some frequently asked questions about setting up Apple's in-app purchase for your branded app.

### What types of bookings services do I need to set up in-app purchase for?

You only need to set up Apple in-app purchase for certain types of bookings services. Locate your type of service below to see if in-app purchase set up is required or not.
**Required:** You must set up in-app purchase to accept payment**Not required:** Payment possible via all payment methods

- **Booking in-person appointments: **Not required
- **Booking online 1:1 appointments:** Not required
- **Booking an online group service: **Required
- **Booking in-person group service: **Not required
- **Selling physical items via Wix Stores:** Not required
- **Selling online/digital items via Wix Stores (e.g. a logo):** Required

### What if I have a pricing plan connected to a service, program, or course?

Pricing plans are not currently supported with in-app purchase. This includes pricing plans connected to other Wix apps like Online Programs, Wix Events, or subscriptions. 
To make sure your app is approved for in-app purchase, make sure you remove any pricing plan element within your app, or [change the section visibility to Android only](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-showing-or-hiding-elements-on-different-mobile-operating-systems).

### What percentage does Apple take if I sell my digital services in-app?

Apple takes up to 30% of a developer's total in-app purchase revenue in the first year of a subscription. (Digital goods and services only, sales of physical products are exempt). After 1 year, the subscription commission falls to 15%.

### Can I edit my services before I add them to my submission?

You can edit your service at any time from [Bookings Services](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Booking%20Services&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/bookings/scheduler/owner/offerings?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard. The changes automatically update when you go back to the Branded App dashboard.
