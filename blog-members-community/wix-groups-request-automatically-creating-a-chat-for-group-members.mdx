---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Automatically Creating a Chat for Group Members"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically create a group chat for all members who join a group.

> **Workaround:**
>
> However, with a [members area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-add-and-set-up-a-members-area) and [members chat](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-allowing-members-to-chat-with-each-other-using-members-chat), any group member can manually create a group chat. In the chat, only members who join the group would be able to chat with each other.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
