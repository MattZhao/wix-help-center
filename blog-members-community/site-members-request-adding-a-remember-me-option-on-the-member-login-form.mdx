---
title: "Site Members Request: Adding a \"Remember Me\" Option on the Member Login Form"
---

Currently, it is not possible to let your members save their login details on the member login form so that they can automatically sign in to their account in the future (i.e. a "Remember Me" option). Your members need to enter their login details every time they access your site.

However, your members can enable password saving in their browser's password settings to remember their credentials as a workaround.
