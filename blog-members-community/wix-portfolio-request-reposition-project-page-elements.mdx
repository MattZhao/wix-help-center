---
title: "Wix Portfolio Request: Reposition Project Page Elements"
---

Currently, project pages are available in a range of preset designs. The individual elements within those designs are locked into position and cannot be freely repositioned.

If you want to change the position of the elements in your project page, you can choose a different preset design. Learn how to [edit the project page design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-portfolio-customizing-your-wix-portfolio#customizing-the-project-pages).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
