---
title: "Wix Apps Request: Making Changes to a Published App"
---

Currently, when you make small changes to a published app in the Wix App Market, it needs to go through the approval process again. It is not possible to bypass the approval process.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
