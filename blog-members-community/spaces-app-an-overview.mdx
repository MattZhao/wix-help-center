---
title: "Spaces App: An Overview"
---

The Spaces by Wix App is a dedicated app for your members to easily engage and stay updated with your site on the go.

Your members can connect with your online store, business, website or community. With the Spaces App, members can view all their upcoming events, browse and shop online, read, share, and interact with blog posts or book services on the go.

> **Note:**
>
> The Wix Member Apps are free to use and download from the relevant app stores. You do not need a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-your-site-to-premium-3066683) to access them.

Members can also invite people to join a site they are part of to encourage an online community with shared interests.

> **Members can download the Spaces App right to their phone using these links:**
>
> - [iPhone](https://itunes.apple.com/app/wix/id1099748482)
> - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wix.android)
