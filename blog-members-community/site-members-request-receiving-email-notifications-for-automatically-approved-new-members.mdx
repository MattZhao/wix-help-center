---
title: "Site Members Request: Receiving Email Notifications for Automatically Approved New Members"
---

Currently, when a site visitor signs up for a members-only page that does not require manual approval, you receive a notification in your site manager . You do not, however, receive an email notification.

Note that if you [require manual approval for site members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-editing-the-signup-settings-for-the-default-and-custom-forms), then you do get an email when a visitors ask to join.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
