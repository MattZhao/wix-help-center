---
title: "Branded App Request: Additional Design Options for Buttons in the App"
---

Currently, you can choose between 3 design options for buttons that you add to your app: **Primary**, **Secondary** or **Link**. At this time, it is not possible to design your buttons further.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
