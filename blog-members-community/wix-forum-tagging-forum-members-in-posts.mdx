---
title: "Wix Forum: Tagging Forum Members in Posts"
---

Do you want to tag a forum member in a post?

All you need to do is start typing"@" plus the member's name, with no spaces (e.g. @johnsmith), and select it from the list.

After the post is published, other readers who click on the tagged member are directed to their profile.

The tagged member receives a notification that they've been tagged.
