---
title: "Wix Blog: Managing Blog Categories from the Wix App"
---

With the Wix app, you can manage your blog categories on the go, making it easy to keep your blog structured and up-to-date. The app gives you full control from the convenience of your mobile device, helping you enhance your blog’s organization and user experience effortlessly.

### To manage your blog categories:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Blog**.   Tap **Categories**.   Choose what you want to do:

### Rename the All Posts category

By default, every post appears in the "All Posts" category. You can rename this category at any time.
1. Tap **Edit **next to **All Posts**.
2. Enter a name for this category.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Create a new blog category

1. Tap **Create Category**.
2. Enter the **Category name**.

**Note: **This appears in the navigation bar.
3. Enter the **Category description**.
4. Add an image:

1. Tap **Add Category Image**.
2. Select **All media**.
3. Select an image to upload.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Tap **Save**.

### Edit a blog category

1. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the category you want to edit.
2. Tap **Edit Category**.
3. Edit the category.
4. Tap **Save**.

### Delete a blog category

1. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the category you want to delete.
2. Tap **Delete Category**.
3. Tap **Delete **to confirm.

### Assign categories to a new blog post

1. Tap **Manage** at the bottom right.
2. Tap **Blog**.
3. Tap **Posts.**
4. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant post.
5. Tap** Post Settings**.
6. Tap the **Categories** tab.
7. Select up to 10 categories, or tap **Create New Category** to create a new one.
8. Tap **Save**.
9. Tap **Save **and tap an option:

- **Save changes: **Save your changes in draft form.
- **Save and publish****: **Publish your post with the updated categories.
