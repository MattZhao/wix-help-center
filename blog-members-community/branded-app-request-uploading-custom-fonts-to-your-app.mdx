---
title: "Branded App Request: Uploading Custom Fonts to Your App"
---

Currently, you can [select various fonts for your branded app's heading and body](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content). However, it is not possible to upload your own fonts. This feature helps keep your brand consistent by allowing you to use fonts that match your brand identity.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
