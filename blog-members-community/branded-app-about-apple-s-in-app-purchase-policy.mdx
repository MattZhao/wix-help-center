Apple's in-app purchase policy affects the way you sell online goods and services to customers using iOS devices. Read more below to see if you are affected by this [policy update](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#in-app-purchase).

---

### About Apple's in-app purchase policy

> **Important:**
>
> This policy only affects the sale of online goods and services, not physical products.

You are affected by Apple's in-app purchase policy if you want to sell the following on your app:

Pricing plans (both single payment plans and recurring plans).  Products integrated with pricing plans (e.g. online programs or events sold via pricing plan).  Recurring payments (e.g. subscriptions).

To sell most other online goods and services via single payment, you can [set up in-app purchase](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-setting-up-apple-in-app-purchase) from your Branded app dashboard.

### FAQs

### What if I currently have pricing plans on my app?

Apple's in-app purchase policy affects any pricing plans that include online classes or services. Examples include online group workout classes or any online programs added as part of a pricing plan.
You can either remove all elements associated with pricing plans from your app, or you also have the option of hiding the element from iOS devices before submitting your app for approval. [Learn how to hide elements of your app for different mobile operating systems](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-showing-or-hiding-elements-on-different-mobile-operating-systems).

### Does Apple have a price limitation for products sold with in-app purchase?

Yes. Apple has a price limitation of $1000 USD for products sold as IAP products. This is Apple's policy. **Note: **This number changes depending on the currency.

### Does this policy affect members with Android devices?

This policy only applies to members attempting to purchase online content with an iOS device. It does not apply to anyone using an Android device.

### What if I have online bookings services?

If you offer online bookings services through [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings), you can [set up in-app purchase](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-setting-up-apple-in-app-purchase). You only need to set up in-app purchase for certain types of bookings services. Locate your type of service below to see if in-app purchase set up is required or not.
**Required:** You must set up in-app purchase to accept payment**Not required:** Payment possible via all payment methods

- **Booking in-person appointments: **Not required
- **Booking online 1:1 appointments:** Not required
- **Booking an online group service: **Required
- **Booking in-person group service: **Not required
- **Selling physical items via Wix Stores:** Not required
- **Selling online/digital items via Wix Stores (e.g. a logo):** Required*

*Not currently supported
