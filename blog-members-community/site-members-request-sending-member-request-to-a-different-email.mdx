---
title: "Site Members Request: Sending Member Request to a Different Email"
---

> **Note:**
>
> This is in relation to notifications that are sent for new pending site members when manual approval is enabled.

Currently, all new member requests are sent to the email address in the [business information](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-sites-business-information) of the site. If you have manual approval enabled, then it is not possible to set a different email address specifically for approving site members.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
