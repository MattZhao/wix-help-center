When you add Groups to your site, a [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) is automatically added too. Group members then get their own account, where they manage personal information and connect with others through their profile.

### Does every member need to have a public profile?

There are two types of profiles:
- **Public profiles:** Members' profiles are visible to other site members. They can follow and be followed, post, leave comments, and react to posts and activities.
- **Private profile:** Only members' names and profile pictures are visible. They can follow or be followed, comment, post, and react to site content.

You can customize the profile privacy settings in the Member Sign Up Form for new site members. Members can adjust this setting according to their preference after joining.

### Changing the privacy of a member's profile

Members can set their profile as public or private, deciding what to share with others. They can switch between these settings on their Profile page in their member account.

You can send the instructions below to your members to help them switch their profile privacy.

#### To change the privacy of a members' profile:

(When signed in) Click the login bar to open the drop-down menu.   Click **Profile**.  Click **Edit Profile**.  Click **Profile Visibility** under **Account Settings **and select the privacy of your profile (e.g. Make Profile Private or Make Profile Public).  Click **Confirm**.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about member privacy settings.

### How is being a site member different from being part of a group?

- **Becoming a member:** Site membership grants your visitors access to members-only pages, such as Wix Stores order history and Wix Bookings appointments. You can use the Members Area with [these apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area#using-a-members-area-with-wix-apps).
- **Joining a group:** Existing site members can join any group regardless of whether they have a public or private profile.

### What do visitors see when the signup form is set to "Footnote only"?

When new members sign up, they see the footnote informing them they are signing up with a public profile. It also lets them know they can change this in their profile settings after joining.
[image: A screenshot of the Footnote Sign Up without the option to choose privacy setting.]

### What do new members see when the signup form is set to "Checkbox for members to choose profile privacy on signup"?

When new members sign up, they can select the checkbox if they want to join with a public profile. Otherwise, their profile remains private.
