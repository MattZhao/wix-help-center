---
title: "Wix Forum: Unblocking Site Members"
---

You can unblock site members you [blocked](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-blocking-or-deleting-site-members), so that they can once again participate in the forum.

### To unblock a site member:

Go to your **Members** page in your live site.  Click the contact you want to unblock  Click the **Show more** icon .  Click **Unblock Member**.
