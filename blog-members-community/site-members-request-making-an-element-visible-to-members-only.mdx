---
title: "Site Members Request: Making an Element Visible to Members Only"
---

While you can [restrict entire pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-applying-page-permissions) so only members are allowed to access them, it's not possible to do the same with individual elements. For example, you cannot change the viewing permissions of an element so it doesn't appear for visitors who aren't members.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
