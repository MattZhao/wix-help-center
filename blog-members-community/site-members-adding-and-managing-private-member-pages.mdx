### Adding a private member page

Add as many private pages as you need to your Members Area.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Pages and Menu**  on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Members Area**.
3. Click **+ Add Private Member Page** at the bottom.
4. Click **Add Private Member Page** in the pop-up to confirm.
5. Give the new page a name:

1. Hover over the new page and click the More Actions icon .
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Enter the new name under **What's this page's name (on your menu)?**.
[image: Naming the new private member page in the Site Pages and Menu panel in Wix Editor]
1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the Studio Editor.
2. Click **+ Add New Page **at the bottom.
3. Click **Add** under **Private Member Page**.
4. Double-click the new page to give it a new name.
[image: Naming a new private member page in the Pages panel in the Studio Editor]

### Hiding a member page from the Login Bar Menu

Still working on the new page? You can hide it from the Login Bar Menu at any point.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click the Login Bar element.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Hover over the relevant item and click the More Actions icon .
4. Click **Hide from menu**.
[image: Hiding a private member page from the login bar menu in Wix Editor]
1. Click the Login Bar element.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Hover over the relevant item and click the More Actions icon .
4. Click **Remove from menu**.
[image: Hiding a private member page from the login bar menu in the Studio Editor]
