---
title: "Branded App Request: Adding More Login Options"
---

Currently, in the Branded App you cannot login in using third party accounts such as Facebook or Google. You can only log in with an email address.

> **Note:**
>
> If your members have previously used a third party log in for another Wix member app (e.g. Spaces by Wix) they need to create a new account via email for your branded app.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
