---
title: "Wix Forum: Creating a Category"
---

Create categories in Wix Forum to organize discussions and keep your community engaged. This is useful when you have a lot of different topics in your forum.

---

### Step 1 | Create a category in your forum

Structure your forum by adding some categories from your site's dashboard.

#### To create a category in your forum:

[Go to Forum](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Forum&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/forum/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Categories** on the left.  Click **Add Category **at the top right.  Enter a name in the **Category Name** field (appears in the navigation menu).  Customize the **New Post** button text.  Enter a name in the **Category Header Title **field (appears in the header).  Enter a short description of what your category is about in the **Category Header Subtitle **field (maximum 65 characters).  Choose a background for your category header:

- **Color:**

- Click the **Background** **color** field to choose a color for your header background.
- Click the **Opacity** field to increase or decrease the level of opacity of your header.
- Click the **Text color** field to choose the color of your header text.
- **Image:**

- Click the **Add** icon  to add an image to your header.
- Click the **Overlay color** field to choose the color for your image overlay.
- Click the **Opacity** field to increase or decrease the level of opacity on your image.

Click **Edit** next to SEO to customize your category SEO.   Choose who has access to the category and its content:

- **Everyone:** Any member can access this category.
- **Logged in members:** Only members that are logged in can access this category
- **Selected members:** Only specific members, with certain roles, badges, or subscriptions can access this category.
    Click the **Category guidelines** toggle to enable or disable it:

- **Enabled:** A set of guidelines created by you are added to the category. These can include asking all members to stay within the topic of the category, etc.
- **Disabled:** Guidelines do not appear on your category.

Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Wix Forum comes with a sample category. Don't forget to either rename and customize the sample category, or delete it altogether.

---

### Step 2 | Set up your category posts

Once you've created your category, you can then move on to setting up your category posts. Choose if you want post reactions and decide who has permission to create posts in this category.

#### To set up your category posts:

Click the **Posts** tab at the top.  Select what type of reactions you would like under posts.  Select one main **Like** reaction.  Enable the toggle to add 5 more reactions.  Choose who can post in this category:

- **Everyone with access:** All members that have access to the category can write posts in this category.
- **Admins and moderators only:** Only admins and moderators can write posts in this category.

Click **Save**.

---

### Step 3 | Set up your category comments

Next, set up your category comments. You can customize the comment section labels and decide if you want post reactions under each comment.

#### To set up your category comments:

Click the **Comments** tab at the top.  Enter the text for your comments header in the** Header label text **field.  Enter the button text in the **Publish button text** field.  Select what type of reactions you would like under posts.  Select one main **Like** reaction.  Enable the toggle to add 5 more reactions.

Click the **Allow comments** toggle to enable or disable comments.  Click the **Allow comment reactions** toggle to enable or disable comment reactions.   Select how your comments are sorted, **Newest**, **Oldest**, or **Most reactions**.   Click the **Label text** drop-down and select from the available options or add custom text.   Select a label icon for your marked comments.  Click the **Enable comment replies **toggle to enable or disable it.

Click **Save**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I create subcategories?

Yes. Once you create at least 1 category, you can [create subcategories](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-a-subcategory).

### How can I edit my forum name and description?

You can [customize your forum name and description](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-adding-and-setting-up-your-forum#step-2-create-a-category) and more directly from your dashboard.

### Can I earn money from my forum?

You can generate income by [limiting certain categories to subscribers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-limiting-specific-categories-to-paying-subscribers) only.

### Can anyone create a category in my forum?

No, only admins have the ability to create categories.
