---
title: "Inviting People to Join Your Site on the Wix Member Apps"
---

Grow your business and expand your community by inviting people to become members of your site on the [Wix member apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps) (e.g. Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix and Dine by Wix). You can choose to invite people using a variety of ways in the Wix app or from your site's dashboard.

**In this article, learn how to invite people to join your site:**

[From the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps#inviting-people-from-the-wix-app)  [With automated email invites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps#setting-up-automated-email-invites-from-the-wix-app)  [From your dashboard via email campaigns](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps#inviting-members-via-email-campaigns)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> You can only invite members once you [choose your preferred members app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app).

---

### Inviting people from the Wix app

Invite people to your site from the Wix app on your mobile device.

#### To invite people:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage** at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.   Tap the** Invite new members** tab.  Choose to invite people using the following methods:

### Email Wix Contacts

Send an email to the contacts saved in your Wix account.
1. Tap the available email provider (e.g. Gmail).
2. Customize your email:

- **To:** Choose who should receive your email; all your contacts, or manually select contacts.
- **Title: **Enter the title that appears at the top of your email.
- **Message: **Write a short message telling your contacts why they should join you on the mobile app.
3. Tap **Send**. 

[image: A screenshot of the email template before you email your wix contacts from Wix Owner app.]

### Send an SMS

Send the link and invite code to people by SMS (text message).
1. Tap **Send an SMS**.
2. Select the contacts you want to invite.
3. Tap Send in the message field on your phone.

[image: A screenshot of the SMS before you send to your desired contacts.]

### Share via social networks

Share the link and invitation code to any social network connected to your phone.  
1. Choose how you want to send your invite to. For example, Facebook, WhatsApp, or Gmail.
2. Tap **Send**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Turn your visitors into engaged members by [adding an invite banner](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-managing-the-invite-banner-of-the-mobile-web-version-of-your-site) to the mobile version of your site.

### Setting up automated email invites from the Wix app

Save time by setting up automated email invites. By enabling this feature, each new member who joins your [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) receives an automated email that invites them to join your site on the app.

> **Important:**
>
> To send out an automated email invite automation, make sure you install the [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-add-and-set-up-a-members-area) on your site.

#### To set-up automated email invites:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Site Discoverability **under **Marketing**.   Tap **Automated Email Invites**.  Tap the **Enable Invite Email Automation** toggle to activate the automated email invites.   (Optional) Preview and customize the details of the automated email:

1. Tap **View & Edit Email Details **and choose what to do next:

- Edit the general details (e.g. Sender Name and Reply to Email).

**Note:** Some details (e.g. Subject Line) can only be edited on desktop, from the Automations tab in your site's dashboard.
- Tap the **Timing** drop-down to choose how soon to send the email after members join your site.
- Tap **View Email **to see a preview of your automated invite looks like to new members on the app.
- Tap **Send test to my email** to see what your members receive.
2. When ready, tap **Save Details**.

### Inviting members via email campaigns

Invite people to join your site on the mobile apps by sending an email campaign from your site's dashboard on desktop.

#### To invite people via email campaigns:

Go to [Mobile App Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/new-mobile-tab/lobby-page?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App+Manager) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Turn contacts into members with an email campaign**.   Create your [email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-and-sending-a-campaign-from-start-to-finish).   Click **Next**.  Choose who to send the email campaign to.

**Tip: **Learn more about [sending an email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign). For example, adding recipients, subject line and newsletter details.  Click **Next**.   When ready, click **Publish**.
