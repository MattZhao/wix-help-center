### Managing members

In the members tab you can add members to your group, view a member's profile, review answers to membership questions, set group members as admins, and remove members.

DashboardWix appLive site
1. [Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant group.
3. Click the **Members** tab.
4. Choose what you want to do next:
Add members to your groupYou can add members to your group in different ways. Choose the one that's relevant for you. 

1. Click **+ Add Members** on the right.
2. Choose how you want to add members to your group: Send email invites, add existing site members, or copy a sharable link to send.

**Tip: **Add your group's invite link to an [email marketing campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-creating-and-sending-a-campaign-from-start-to-finish) and send it to people you'd like to join your group.
Review answers to membership questionsIf your group is set to private, when potential members want to access it, they need to request to join. You can set up membership questions to examine if you wish to approve the pending requests or not. You can also review the answers even after you approved the request.
**To review membership questions for pending members: **
1. Click **View All **next to **Member Requests**.
2. Click **Review Answers **next to the relevant pending member.
3. Review the answers then choose an option: **Approve** or **Decline**.

**To review answers by existing members: **
1. Click the **More Actions **icon   next to the relevant member and select **View answers**.
2. Review the answers and click **Close** when you're done.
View a member's profileYou can edit a contact's information at any time to update their details or add details or custom fields that are relevant to them.

1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant member.
2. Select **View contact**.
3. [View and manage](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-managing-your-contact-list) your contact.

They can make changes only through the live site. If you want someone to in make changes in your group via your site's dashboard, create a [create a custom role for a group collaborator](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-inviting-people-to-manage-your-group). 
Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant member and select **Make group admin**. 
Remove a memberWhen you remove a group member, they are still members of your site. However, blocking a member means they can't access your site at all. 
Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant member and select **Remove member**.

### Customize members settings

You can customize your members' permissions and privacy settings. Create and manage membership questions, choose who can create groups on your site, and limit access to certain members.

DashboardWix appLive site
1. [Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Choose what you want to do next:
Customize your group's members' permissionsYou can choose what people can do in your group by setting member permissions. Decide which group members can post in your group, who can send invitations to join, and who can add site members manually. You can also choose what to call "members" of your group (e.g. members, students, coworkers).  

1. Click the relevant group.
2. Click the **Settings** tab.
3. Click **Edit Settings** next to **Members**.
4. Select the relevant options for each setting: 

- Who can post in the group?
- Who can invite members to join?
- Who can add site members?
- What are group members called?
5. Click **Save**.

Create and manage membership questions Create questions for people to answer when requesting to join your group. Ask visitors how they found out about the group, why they're interested in joining, or collect some basic details. Potential members can answer these questions from a desktop or [Spaces by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview) or [Fit by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/fit-by-wix-app-an-overview). 
1. Click the relevant group.
2. Click the **Settings** tab.
3. Click **Manage Questions **in the **Membership questions **section.

**Tip: **Click **+ Add Question** if this is the first question you're adding.
4. Choose what you want to do next: 

- Click **+ New Question**.
- Decide if the question is required or optional.
- Edit your questions.
- Delete your questions.

Choose who can create groups on your site
1. Click the **More Actions** icon  at the top right and select **General settings**.
2. Select an option under **Who can create groups?**: 

- **All site members: **All members can create a new group without needing an admin's approval.
- **Members with approval: **All members can request to create a new group, but you (or another admin) need to approve the request.
- **Only admins: **Only you and other admins may create new groups.
3. Click **Save**.
