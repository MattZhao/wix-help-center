---
title: "Wix Blog: Changing the Order of Blog Posts in the Wix App"
---

Your blog posts generally appear in the order they were posted. You can, however, change the order by updating the creation dates.

### To change the date of a post:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage** at the bottom right.  Tap **Blog**.   Tap **Posts**.  Tap the post you want to edit.  Tap the **Show More **icon** **.  Tap **Post Settings**.  Tap **Edit** next to the publish date.  Select a new publish date.  Tap **Update**.
