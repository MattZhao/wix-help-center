---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Selecting Which Categories Are Displayed in the Blog Menu"
---

Currently, it is not possible to exclude categories from the blog menu. All the categories you create are displayed.

> **Tip:**
>
> [You can choose to show or hide your blog menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-showing-or-hiding-the-blog-menu). Hiding your blog menu means all of your categories in your blog menu are hidden from view.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
