---
title: "Site Members: Assigning a Badge to a Member"
---

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To use member badges, you must install a [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) on your site.

Assign badges to specific site members so that they stand out from all the other members. For example, you can create a badge such as "Star Blogger" for members who contribute the most to your blog, or a badge for members who are experts in a subject, for example "Yoga Expert".

**You can assign a badge to a member in 2 ways:**

[From Site Members in the dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-badge-to-a-member#to-apply-a-badge-to-a-member-from-the-dashboard-1)[From your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-badge-to-a-member#to-apply-a-badge-to-a-member-from-your-live-site)

---

### Assigning a Badge to a Member from the Dashboard

Go to [Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members). Click **More Actions** at the top right and click **Manage Badges**.Click the **Show More **icon on the relevant badge.Click **Edit**.Click the **Members** tab at the top.Click **Assign Members** .Select the members you want to assign the badge to, then click **Add**.

---

### Assigning a Badge to a Member from Your Live Site

Go to your Members page on your published site.Click the **Show More **icon  on the member you want to assign the badge to.Click **Assign Badges**.

Select the badge.Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> You must be the site owner to assign badges from the live site.

> **Tip:**
>
> Assign badges to your members automatically when they perform a certain action on your site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-assigning-badges-automatically-to-site-members)
