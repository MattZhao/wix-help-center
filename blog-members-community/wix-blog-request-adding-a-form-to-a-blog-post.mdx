---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Adding a Form to a Blog Post"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a Form to a blog post, to help you generate new subscriber information without your site visitors having to leave your blog.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can add a [hidden page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu) to your site, add a form to the page using [Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-an-overview), then [link to the page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-editing-the-text-formatting-of-your-blog-posts) in a blog post.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
