---
title: "Wix Blog: Adding Managed Writers to Your Blog"
---

You can add managed writers to your blog to attribute them to a specific post on a one time basis. Managed writers have no permissions on your site and cannot post themselves or customize their profile.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also add permanent blog collaborators to your blog if you need to. [Learn more about adding writers and editors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog)

#### In this article, learn more about:

[Adding a managed writer from the post composer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-managed-writers-to-your-blog#adding-a-managed-writer-from-the-post-composer)  [Adding a managed writer from the Writers page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-managed-writers-to-your-blog#adding-a-managed-writer-from-the-writers-page)

### Adding a managed writer from the post composer

You can add a managed writer to an individual post directly from the post composer in your site's dashboard.

#### To add a managed writer:

[Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) in your site's dashboard.  Select the post that you want to add a managed writer to.   Click **Settings** on the left side of the post composer.  Scroll down and click the **Author **drop-down menu.  Click **+New Author**.

Enter a name for the managed writer under **Author Name**.  (Optional) Add a profile picture and title for the managed writer.  Click **Add**.

Click **Publish**.

> **Deleting writers**
>
> You can delete a writer at anytime. Go to the Writers page in your dashboard, click the **Show more** icon  next to the writer's name you want to delete, and click **Delete**. [Learn more about managing writers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog)

### Adding a managed writer from the Writers page

You can add a managed writer to your blog in your site's dashboard. After you've added them, you can add them to specific posts.

#### To add a managed writer:

Go to [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Writers**.  Click **+Add Writer**.

Click **Create a managed writer**.  Click **Continue**.  Add profile information like a name, profile picture, and any other additional information for the writer.  Click **Save**.  Select the post that you want to add the new managed writer to.   Click **Settings** on the left side of the post composer.  Scroll down and click the **Author **drop-down menu.  Select the new managed writer.  Click **Publish**.

> **Deleting writers**
>
> You can delete an writer at anytime. Click the **Show more** icon  next to the writer's name you want to delete and click **Delete**. [Learn more about managing writers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog)
