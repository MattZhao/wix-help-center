---
title: "Wix Member Apps Request: Displaying a Banner in the App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a banner (e.g. advertising a sale or making an announcement) in your Member Apps.

> **Tip:**
>
> As a workaround, you can add an image to your app and link it to the relevant screen or URL.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
