Monetize your group by creating exclusive content for members with active pricing plans. For example, offer a series of post-based tutorials visible only to paying members. When people see one of these exclusive posts, they'll see an "Exclusive Post" label at the top. People who are members and have that specific pricing plan will be able to read it. If they're not members, they'll see a "Subscribe Now" button.

>

> - [Connect a payment method ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments)to get paid for your subscription plans.

### Creating subscription based posts

Want to create exclusive content that's only available to paying members? Give extra value to paying group members by composing subscription-based posts. Comprise posts that have a high-quality, unique content which your audience finds beneficial. For example, tutorials, exclusive videos, downloadable resources, and more.

### Converting an existing post to a subscription-based post

If you already have a post that you'd like to make exclusive, you can turn it into a subscription post. Non-subscribers can see the pricing plan cost to access the exclusive post. They can also view the content type (e.g., images, videos, files), reactions, and comments.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant group.
3. Click **Posts**.
4. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant post.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Groups site dashboard, open on Posts]
1. Click **Connect Pricing Plan**.
2. Choose the relevant option:

- **Create a new pricing plan:** Click **+Create New Plan** and set up your new plan.
- **Connect an existing pricing plan: **Enable the toggle next to the relevant plan.
3. Click **Done**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap** Groups.**
4. Tap **Manage Posts**.
5. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant post.
6. Tap **Edit**.
7. Tap the **Monetize** icon .
8. Tap **Connect **next to the relevant Pricing Plan.
9. Tap **Done**.
10. Tap **Update**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about subscription-based posts.

### Can I connect multiple pricing plans to one subscription post?

Yes. You can connect as many pricing plans as you want to a post.

### Can members create subscription-based posts?

No. Only group admins can create subscription-based posts.

### How can I manage my pricing plans?

You can [manage your pricing plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-editing-an-existing-plan) from your site's dashboard. Go to the Pricing Plans section to create, edit, or delete plans as needed. Ensure your plans are clearly described so members know what they are subscribing to.

### Can I track which members have subscribed to my exclusive content?

Yes, you can view all your [group members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-new-members-to-your-group) in your site's dashboard. You can also [track your subscribers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-viewing-a-clients-subscription-and-payment-history) to see who has subscribed to which plans.

### Can I see analytics for my exclusive posts?

Yes, you can view [group analytics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-about-analytics-reports) to see how your exclusive posts are performing. You can also check your [Pricing Plans analytics reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-viewing-and-downloading-analytics-reports). Use these insights to improve your content strategy.
