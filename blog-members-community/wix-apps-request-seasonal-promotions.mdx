---
title: "Wix Apps Request: Seasonal Promotions"
---

Currently, it's not possible to create seasonal promotions using Wix Promos for certain events such as Black Friday.

As a workaround, you can send a [promotional email marketing campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-sending-invitations-or-promotions-using-email-marketing) to your customers to let them know about your upcoming promo.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
