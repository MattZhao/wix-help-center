---
title: "Wix Groups: Adding the Groups List Add-On"
---

The Groups List Add-On allows you to add a list of your groups to any page of your site. This makes it easy for visitors to view and join your groups.

You can add your groups list to your home page, for example, so it's the first thing people see when they visit your site. You can also show your active groups as cards or in a feed for everyone to see.

### To add the Groups List Add-On:

Go to the Group List page in your Editor:

- **Wix Editor:**

1. Click **Pages and Menu ** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Groups pages**.
3. Click **Groups List**.
- **Editor X:**

1. Click **Pages**  at the top of Editor X.
2. Click **Groups** under **Groups pages**.
    Click the Group List element in your Editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Add-Ons**.  Click the** Add to Page **drop-down next to the relevant add-on:

- **Group list widget:** Let visitors see a list of all of your groups.
- **Side by Side:** Show your groups as cards to site visitors.
- **Feed Widget:** Show your public groups in a feed to site visitors.
    Select which page should show the add-on.

> **What's next?**
>
> The Groups List Add-On is automatically placed on the page you selected.
