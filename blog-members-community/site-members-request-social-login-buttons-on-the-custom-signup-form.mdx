---
title: "Site Members Request: Social Login Buttons on the Custom Signup Form"
---

Currently, you cannot add social login buttons to the custom signup form in the Member's Area. They are only displayed on the default signup form.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
