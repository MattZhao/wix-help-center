---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Sharing Posts Directly From Your Site to Instagram"
---

You can share your blog posts directly to Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn, or choose to add a link to the post in an email. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-sharing-a-blog-post-on-social-media).

Currently, it is not possible to share blog posts directly from your site to Instagram.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
