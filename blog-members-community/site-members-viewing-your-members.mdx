title: "Site Members: Viewing Your Members"
---

View and manage your site members directly from the dashboard. The members in your member list include anyone who joined your site's Members Area, and includes the details they entered upon signup.

When viewing your member list, you can make changes to your members' information, assign [roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-member-roles) and give [badges](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-member-badges).

**In this article, learn how to:**

[View a list of your members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-viewing-your-members#viewing-your-member-list)  [Customize the columns in the member list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-viewing-your-members#customizing-the-columns-in-the-member-list)  [View a member's Contact Panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-viewing-your-members#viewing-a-members-contact-panel)

### Viewing your member list

You can access a list of all of your members from your site's dashboard. The list is divided into current members, members who are waiting to be approved, as well as members you've reported or blocked.

#### To view your member list:

Go to your [site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).  Click **Contacts** on the left.  Click the **More Actions** icon at the top right.  Select** Site members**.  Click a tab at the top to view members and their details:

- **Site Members: **All current members of your Members Area.
- **Pending: **View any member requests if you have chosen to require approval for potential members.
- **Reported: **View any members who have been reported by other members.
- **Blocked:** View [any members you've blocked](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-blocking-or-deleting-a-member) from your Members Area.

> **Note:**
>
> The member list in your site's dashboard shows the total number of members on your site. This may differ from what you see in the Wix app and member apps, where only members that joined your site on the member app are shown.

### Customizing the columns in the member list

Your member list appears in a table with columns of information on each member. Choose which columns to show and their order in the table, based on the information that's most relevant to you.

#### To customize the columns in your member list:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.  Click the Customize columns icon .  Choose what you want to customize:

- **Choose which columns to display:** Select the checkboxes next to the columns you want in your table.
- **Reorder the columns: **Hold the Reorder icon** ** next to a column and drag it to the desired location.

> **Note:**
>
> It is not possible to hide the Avatar and Login email columns.

### Viewing a member's Contact Panel

Just like in your Contact List, you can select a member on the Member List to see more details. The Contact Panel that opens on the right includes information about them (e.g. contact info, member status, etc.) as well as any communication done through [Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-an-overview).

#### To view a member's Contact Panel:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.   Click the relevant member to open up their Contact Panel.  (In the Contact Panel on the right) View your member's information:

- **Overview:** This tab shows your member's contact info, their member status and role, any labels or tasks assigned to them and more.
- **Inbox:** This is where you can see any previous communication you've had with this member. You can also send them messages from here.
