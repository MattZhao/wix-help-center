---
title: "Site Members: Adding and Customizing the Members List Element"
---

Add the Members List element to showcase your site members. You can add this element to any site page, making it your own by adjusting the layout, refining the design, and personalizing member badges.

You decide if this list is visible to all site visitors or just your logged-in members.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Add the Members List element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-members-page-that-displays-all-members#adding-the-members-list-element)  [Adjust the layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-members-page-that-displays-all-members#adjusting-the-layout-of-the-members-list-element)  [Design the Members List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-members-page-that-displays-all-members#designing-the-members-list-element)  [Making the Members List public](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-members-page-that-displays-all-members#making-the-members-list-public)  [Personalizing how badges look on the list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-members-page-that-displays-all-members#personalizing-how-badges-look-on-the-members-list)

> **Can't click on members in your live site?**
>
> Clicking a member on the Members List leads to their profile – unless that page / tab doesn't exist on your site. You can quickly [add it from the Pages panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-adding-a-profile-page-to-your-members-area#adding-the-profile-page-to-the-members-area).

### Adding the Members List element

Head over to the Add Elements panel to add the Members List element. You can show the element anywhere on your site, whether it's a page in your Members Area or a site page that is visible to all visitors.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Community**.
3. Drag the Members List element onto your page.
[image: The Members List element in the Add Elements panel in Wix Editor]
1. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Members**.
3. Drag the Members List element onto the page.
[image: The Members List element in the Add Elements panel in the Studio Editor]

### Adjusting the layout of the Members List element

Choose from a Cards or List layout for the Members List. Then, customize the chosen layout to your needs, deciding how member profile pictures should look.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Select the Members List element.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Layout**.
4. Select the layout you like best; **Cards** or **List**.
5. Choose a shape for your members' profile pictures; **Circle** or **Square**.
[image: The Layout tab in the Settings panel of the Members List element, in Wix Editor]
1. Select the Members List element.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Layout**.
4. Select the layout you like best; **Cards** or **List**.
5. Choose a shape for your members' profile pictures; **Circle** or **Square**.

### Designing the Members List element

Make your Members List complement your site's design by customizing the fonts, buttons, colors, background, borders, and more.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Select the Members List element.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Design**.
4. Customize the design of this element to make it your own. Click below to learn more about the areas you can design:
Text
- **Member name: **Choose a font, font size, and color for your members' names.
- **Following / Followers counters: **Choose a font, font size, and color for the counters.
Button
- **Text: **Choose a font and font size for your buttons.
- **Opacity and color: **Click the color box to select a new color for your buttons. Drag the slider to adjust the color's transparency.
- **Corner radius: **Drag the slider to make the buttons more round.
- **Border color and opacity: **Choose a new color for the button borders and drag the slider to adjust the opacity.
- **Border width: **Drag the slider to make the border thinner or thicker.
Background & Borders
- **Show cover photo:** Enable the toggle to show each member's cover photo on their card. 

- **Choose default color: **Choose a color to appear when a member does not have a cover photo on their profile.
- **Default cover photo opacity: **Drag the slider to change the transparency of the cover photo / default color.
- **Set default cover photo:** Choose a cover photo to appear for all members unless they customize it from their own account.
- **Page background opacity & color: **Click the color box to select a new background color for the list. Drag the slider to adjust the color's transparency.
- **Profile background opacity & color: **Click the color box to select a new background color for the member cards on the list. Drag the slider to adjust the color's transparency.
- **Border / divider width: **Drag the slider to change the width of borders and dividers on this list.
- **Border / divider opacity and color: **Click the color box to select a new color for the borders and dividers. Drag the slider to adjust the color's transparency.
- **Border / divider corner radius: **Drag the slider to make the corners of the member cards more round.

### Making the Members List public

You can choose whether to display your Members List to the public or keep it private, available to your signed in members only. If the list is set to private, visitors will see an option to log in (hiding the actual element).

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Select the Members List element.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Enable the toggle to allow all site visitors to see this list.
[image: The toggle that controls whether the Members List is public or private, in Wix Editor]
1. Select the Members List element.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Enable the toggle to allow all site visitors to see this list.

### Personalizing how badges look on the Members List

Inject some extra personality into your Members List by customizing the [badges](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-member-badges) you assign to your members. Choose a badge layout (e.g. Name & Icon, Icon Only) that meets your needs, and change its size to match your style.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Select the Members List element.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Badges**.
4. Customize the appearance of member badges using the following options:

- **Layout: **Choose a badge layout and your preferred badge size.
- **Style: **Drag the slider to adjust the roundness of the badges.
- **Text:** Choose a font for the badges' names.
[image: The Badges tab in the Settings panel of the Members List element, in Wix Editor]
1. Select the Members List element.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Badges**.
4. Customize the appearance of member badges using the following options:

- **Layout: **Choose a badge layout and your preferred badge size.
- **Style: **Drag the slider to adjust the roundness of the badges.
- **Text:** Choose a font for the badges' names.
[image: The Badges tab in the Settings panel of the Members List element, in the Studio Editor]
