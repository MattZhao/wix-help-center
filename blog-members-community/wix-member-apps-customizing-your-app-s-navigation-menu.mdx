---
title: "Wix Member Apps: Customizing Your App's Navigation Menu"
---

Your Wix member apps, like Spaces app, Fit app, or Branded app, are made up of screens. Each screen, such as the Home screen or the Services screen, acts as a separate "page" in your app. Your app's navigation menu enables your members to navigate between these screens seamlessly.

You can customize the navigation menu by choosing which screens appear in it and in what order, and whether it appears at the top or bottom of the screen (Branded app only).

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Reorder the screens in your navigation menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-apps-navigation-menu#reordering-the-screens-in-your-navigation-menu)  [Hide a screen from your navigation menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-apps-navigation-menu#hiding-a-screen-from-your-navigation-menu)  [Change the location of your navigation menu in your branded app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-apps-navigation-menu#changing-the-location-of-your-navigation-menu-in-your-branded-app)

### Reordering the screens in your navigation menu

Rearrange your app's navigation menu to prioritize the most important screens for your members.

DashboardWix App
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Your own mobile app**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click **Screens** on the left.
5. Click and drag the screens up or down the list.
[image: An example showing how to reorder screens by dragging and dropping.]Tip:To add new screens to your app and navigation menu, click **+ Add** under **Manage Screens**.[image: An example showing how to add new screens to your navigation menu]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing a different mobile app:

1. Tap the mobile app name at the top.
2. Tap **Branded Mobile App** or tap the relevant mobile app (e.g. Spaces by Wix app). 

**Tip: **Go to your site's dashboard on desktop to choose your preferred member app.
5. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
6. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the bottom.
7. Tap **Reorder**.
8. Hold and drag the screens up or down the list.
[image: An example showing where the Reorder button is.]Tip:To add new screens to your app and navigation menu, click **+ Add** **New Screen **at the bottom of the **Manage Screens **page. [image: An example showing how to add new screens to your navigation menu]

### Hiding a screen from your navigation menu

By default, every screen that you add to your app appears in your navigation menu. You can hide a screen from the navigation menu to streamline your member's experience by decluttering irrelevant or seldom-used screens.

DashboardWix App
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Your own mobile app**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click **Screens** on the left.
5. Hover over the screen you want to hide and click the **More Actions** icon .
6. Click **Remove from navigation**.
[image: An example showing how to access the option to remove a screen from navigation]Tip:After removing the screen from your navigation menu, it appears under the **Screens not in navigation** section. You can add it back at any time.

[image: An example showing how to add a screen back to the navigation menu]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing a different mobile app:

1. Tap the mobile app name at the top.
2. Tap **Branded Mobile App** or tap the relevant mobile app (e.g. Spaces by Wix app). 

**Tip: **Go to your site's dashboard on desktop to choose your preferred member app.
5. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
6. Tap **Screens** from the bottom menu.
7. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the screen you want to hide.
8. Tap **Remove from navigation**.
[image: An example showing to find the Remove from navigation button]Tip:After removing the screen from your navigation menu, it appears under the **Screens not in navigation** section. You can add it back at any time. Tap the** More Actions** icon  next to the relevant screen and then tap** Add to navigation**.

### Changing the location of your navigation menu in your branded app

When using your own branded app, you can change the location of the navigation menu from the top bar to the bottom bar. When you place your menu on the bottom bar, it changes from a text menu to an icon menu (with or without text).

DashboardWix App
1. [Go to the Branded Mobile App tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Your%20Branded%20App%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=branded) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
3. Click **Screens** on the left.
4. Click **Edit Navigation** at the bottom left.
[image: An example showing where to find the Edit Navigation button]
1. Select an option under **Edit Navigation** on the right:

- **Top tab bar: **Your navigation menu remains in the top bar of the screen.
- **Bottom tab bar: **Your navigation menu moves to the bottom bar of the screen, and changes to an icon menu instead of a text menu. You can customize the menu as follows:

- Enable or disable the **Show screen names under the icons** toggle to show or hide the screen name text.
- Hover over an icon and click **Change icon** to change to a different icon.
[image: An example of where users can switch the location of the navigation menu]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing your branded app:

1. Tap the mobile app name at the top.
2. Tap **Branded Mobile App**. 

**Tip: **Go to your site's dashboard on desktop to choose your preferred member app.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Design** from the bottom menu.
7. Tap **Navigation**.
8. Select an option under **Type**:

- **Top tab bar: **Your navigation menu remains in the top bar of the screen.
- **Bottom tab bar: **Your navigation menu moves to the bottom bar of the screen, and changes to an icon menu instead of a text menu. You can customize the menu as follows:

- Enable or disable the **Show screen names under the icons** toggle to show or hide the screen name text.
- Tap one of the icons to change to a different icon.
