---
title: "Wix Apps: Focus Timer"
---

The Wix Focus Timer is an add-on that helps you with time management.

> The Wix Focus Timer add-on is only available in the Wix Studio Editor.

### To add the Wix Focus Timer to your site:

Click the Wix Studio icon  at the top left.  Click **Tools**.   Click **Editor Add-ons**.   Click **Get Add-ons**.   Click **Focus Timer**.
