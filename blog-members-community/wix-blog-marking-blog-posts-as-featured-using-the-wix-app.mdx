---
title: "Wix Blog: Marking Blog Posts as \"Featured\" Using the Wix App"
---

You can mark posts as "featured posts" to display them on any page of your site in a custom feed.

> **Tip:**
>
> To learn how to add a custom feed and use it to display featured posts, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-featuring-posts-in-wix-blog).

### To mark a post as "featured":

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage** at the bottom right.  Tap **Blog**.   Tap **Posts**.  Tap **More Actions**  next to the relevant post.  Tap **Post Settings**.  Enable the **Feature Post **toggle.  Tap **Next**.  Click **Save**.   Select an option:

- **Save Changes: **Save your changes in draft form.
- **Publish Post: **Publish your updated post.
