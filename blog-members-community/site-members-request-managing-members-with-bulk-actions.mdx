---
title: "Site Members Request: Managing Members with Bulk Actions"
---

Currently, you can [block](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-blocking-or-deleting-a-member) and [approve](https://support.wix.com/en/article/approving-a-site-member) your site members individually. It is not possible to block or approve multiple members in bulk.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you need to [delete multiple site members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-blocking-or-deleting-a-member#blocking-or-deleting-a-site-member-from-the-member-list), you can perform this as a bulk action.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
