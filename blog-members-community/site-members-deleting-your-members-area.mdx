---
title: "Site Members: Deleting Your Members Area"
---

Want to remove the Members Area from your site? You can do it in a few quick steps directly from your editor. Deleting this area removes all member pages, including the default ones that were added automatically (e.g. My Account, My Orders) and any pages you've created on your own.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Deleting your Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-deleting-your-members-area#site-members-deleting-your-members-area)  [FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-deleting-your-members-area#faqs)

> **Before you delete:**
>
> Deleting our Members Area may affect other installed applications.

---

### Deleting your Members Area

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Hover over **Members Area** and click the **Actions** icon .
3. Click **Delete Member's Area**.
4. Click **Delete** to confirm.

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Hover over **Members Area** and click the **More Actions** icon .
3. Click **Delete Member's Area**.
4. Click **Delete** to confirm.

### FAQs

Take a look at the most frequently asked questions about deleting the Member's Area, or specific elements related to the app.

Click a question below to see the answer:

### What happens if I only delete the existing member pages?

This doesn't delete the application itself. Meaning, the Member's Area app stays installed on your site.

### What happens if I delete the login bar element?

This doesn't delete the application itself. Meaning, the Member's Area app stays installed on your site.

### What happens to my members after deleting Member's Area?

You can still view, add and manage your members in your site dashboard, from the [Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmember-permissions%2Fmembers) tab.
