---
title: "Wix Blog: Previewing Drafts in Wix Blog"
---

Before publishing your blog post, see what it looks like on your live site using preview mode.

> **Important:**
>
> Make sure to [publish your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site) in order to preview blog post drafts.

### To preview blog posts:

Go to the [Blog tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fblog) in your dashboard.Click **Drafts **on the left.Click the **Show More**icon next to the draft you want to preview.Click **Preview Draft**.
