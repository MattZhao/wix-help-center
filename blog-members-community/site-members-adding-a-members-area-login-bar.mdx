---
title: "Site Members: Adding a Members Area Login Bar"
---

Once you add a Members Area to your site, a login bar is automatically added to the header section. This ensures the login bar appears on all pages so members get quick and easy access to their account.

If, for any reason, you need to re-add your log in bar, or want to add more log in bars, you can do so easily.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Community**.
3. Click **Login Bar**.
4. Click or drag and drop the login bar to add it to your page.
[image: The Login Bar in the Add Elements panel in Wix Editor]
