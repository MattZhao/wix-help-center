---
title: "Site Members Request: Placing the Login and Signup Forms on Pages"
---

Currently, the [login](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-member-login-form) and [signup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-member-signup-form) forms are displayed using [lightboxes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-lightbox). These pop up on the screen rather than appearing on the page directly. It is not possible to place these forms directly on a site page, like you can with a contact form.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
