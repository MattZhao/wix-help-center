---
title: "Branded App: Previewing Your App on a Mobile Device"
---

Once you've customized your branded app from the Branded App dashboard, you can view a preview from the Wix app. Seeing your app's experience from a mobile device allows you to make sure you're completely satisfied with the design and branding before you submit for review.

### To preview your app on your mobile device:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.   Tap **Preview** under your site name or the name of your branded app.

> **What's next?**
>
> A preview of your Branded App's home screen opens in the Wix mobile app. Tap each screen to see how app navigation works, and view your app's sections and header design. You will not be able to take actions from the app preview such as fill out a form or make a purchase.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also click **Preview** under the app preview on your [Branded App dashboard](https://www.wix.com/branded-apps?path=welcome&referralInfo=HelpCenter) on desktop to scan the QR code to download or open the Wix app.
