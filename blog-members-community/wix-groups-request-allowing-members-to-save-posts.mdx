---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Allowing Members to Save Posts"
---

Currently, it is not possible for group members to save group posts to a 'favorites' list. This feature would allow them easy access to posts later on.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
