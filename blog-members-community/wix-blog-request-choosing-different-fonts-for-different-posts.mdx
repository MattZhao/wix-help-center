---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Choosing Different Fonts for Different Posts"
---

Currently, when you select a font style (e.g. Times New Roman) for body text, the font you choose appears in the body text for all blog posts. It is not possible to choose different fonts for different posts.

However, you can still do a lot with your fonts. [You can customize your fonts by size, color and font style](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-customizing-your-blog-post-page).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
