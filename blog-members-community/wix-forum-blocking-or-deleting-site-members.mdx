---
title: "Wix Forum: Blocking or Deleting Site Members"
---

Take control of your forum by blocking or deleting any members who do not follow the forum rules.

**Blocked members: **Cannot like or comment on a post or follow a writer. Blocked members do, however, appear on the members page. **Deleted members: **Cannot like or comment on a post or follow a writer. All the site member's posts, comments, and likes are removed from your forum. The member no longer appears on the member's page.

> **Tip:**
>
> Any site admin can block or delete members.

### To block or delete a site member:

Go to the forum on your live site.  Go to a post or comment written by the member you want to block.   Click the member's name.** **  Click the More Actions icon   on the relevant site member.  Select an option:

- Block a member:

1. Click **Block Member**.
2. Click **Block **to confirm.
- Delete a member:

1. Click **Delete Member**.
2. Click **Delete **to confirm.

> **Note:**
>
> To learn how to unblock a member, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-unblocking-site-members).
