---
title: "Wix Blog: Pinning Posts to the Top of the Feed"
---

Select which posts you want to appear at the top of your blog feed by pinning these posts. All other posts appear beneath the pinned posts, in the order they were published.

> **Note:**
>
> You can only pin posts on your main blog feed. It is not possible to pin posts on a Post List. However, you can use a Post List to display featured posts on other pages of your site. Learn more about [the differences between featured posts and pinned posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-featuring-posts-in-wix-blog).

### To pin a post to the top of your blog feed:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Pages and Menu**on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click **Blog **or click the name of your Blog page if you renamed it.
4. Click the Blog Post element in your editor.
5. Click **Settings**.
6. Click **Display**.
7. Select the checkbox next to **More actions menu**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to add the 'More actions menu' on your blog post element.]
1. Publish your site.
2. Go to your live site and log in with the email address associated with your Wix account.
3. Go to the blog on your live site.
4. Click the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant blog post.
5. Click **Pin to Feed**.

> **Unpinning posts:**
>
> You can unpin a post by following the steps above and selecting **Unpin Post**.
