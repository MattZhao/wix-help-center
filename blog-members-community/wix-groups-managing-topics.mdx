### Renaming and deleting topics

You can edit and delete topics as needed. Deleting a topic removes it from any post that it is assigned to, but the post remains live on your group's feed.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant group.
3. Click the **Topics **tab.
4. Choose what to do next:

- **Rename a topic:**

1. Hover over the topic you would like to rename and click **Edit**.
2. Enter a new name for your topic.
3. Click **Save**.
- **Delete a topic: **

1. Click the **More Actions** icon ﻿ next to the relevant topic.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Click **Confirm**.
[image: A screenshot of the Wix Groups app on a Wix site dashboard]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Groups**.
4. Tap the relevant group.
5. Tap **Topics**.
6. Click the **More Actions** icon ﻿ next to the relevant topic.
7. Choose what to do next:

- Tap **Edit** to change the topic name. Enter a new name for your topic and tap **Save**.
- Tap **Delete **to remove a topic and tap **Delete** to confirm.

**Note: **Removing a topic will unassign it from posts but won't delete the posts.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about group topics.

### Can members suggest new topics?

Members cannot create new topics directly, but they can suggest new topics to you. Consider creating a suggestion post or form where members can submit their ideas for new topics.

### How can I ensure my members use topics effectively?

Educate your members about the importance of using topics. You can create a pinned post explaining how to use topics for better navigation and encourage them to categorize their posts appropriately.

### Are members notified when a topic is edited or removed?

No, members are not notified when you edit or remove topics.

### Can I customize the appearance of topics?

Yes. The topic label design is based on your overall group page design. To change the color of the topic label text, modify the **Member name** text in your editor. In your group's page, Click **Settings**, select the **Design** tab and click **Member name** to change the text color.

### What are some best practices for naming topics?

Keep topic names clear and concise. Use descriptive names that accurately represent the content. Avoid using overly generic names to ensure each topic serves a specific purpose.

### Can I restore a deleted topic?

No. Once a topic is deleted, it cannot be restored. However, you can create a new topic with the same name and reassign posts to it.

### Can I assign multiple topics to a single post?

Yes, you can assign multiple topics to a single post. This allows the post to be categorized under different relevant topics, making it easier for members to find.

### How can I use topics to improve member engagement?

Use topics to create focused discussions and events. For example, you can have topics for different challenges, events, or themes. This helps members find content that interests them and fosters engagement.

### Is there a limit to the number of topics I can create?

No. There are no strict limits on the number of topics you can create. However, keep the number manageable to avoid overwhelming your members. Too many topics can make navigation difficult.
