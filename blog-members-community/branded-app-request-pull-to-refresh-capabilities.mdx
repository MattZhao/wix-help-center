---
title: "Branded App Request: \"Pull to Refresh\" Capabilities"
---

Currently, Branded Apps do not have "pull to refresh" capabilities, which would allow visitors to refresh the content by dragging their finger down the screen.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
