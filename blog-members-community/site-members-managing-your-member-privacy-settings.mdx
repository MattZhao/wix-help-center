Manage the privacy of [your members' profiles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-profile-page), based on how much you want members to interact with each other. Decide whether or not members can make their profile public, and whether it's visible to all visitors or logged-in members only.

Public profiles help to foster a sense of community, especially (but not only) when using social apps like Wix Blog, Wix Forum and Wix Groups.

### Managing the member privacy settings

Decide if your members should have the option to [make their profile public](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-making-a-member-profile-public), and who can see their profile. When a profile is public, the member's name and image appear next to their activities (e.g. any comments, likes or [files](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-file-share-app) they post). These details are clickable, leading to the member's profile.

### Where can members set their profile to public?

Site members can always [change their profile's privacy level](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-making-a-member-profile-public) from their My Account page. 
They'll find this setting under **Profile Visibility**.[image: A screenshot of clicking Make Profile Public in your Members Area account settings]

### Do these settings also apply to Wix apps?

Yes. Member privacy settings sync between your site and Wix member apps (e.g. Spaces app, Branded app, or Fit by Wix). 
Any changes you make via your site dashboard or the Wix mobile app will apply to both your site and the app. 
**Note:** If you created your Wix account before April 8th, 2024, member privacy settings only apply to your site; they do not apply to the apps.
