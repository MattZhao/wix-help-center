### Creating a branded app for an online store

** Add a tab for each product category:** If you have multiple categories of products, add a navigation tab at the top of your app for each one so it's easier for your customers to browse.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap **Screens** at the bottom.
6. Tap **Add New Screen**.
7. Add a title for your first product category under **Screen Name **and tap **Save**.
8. Choose an element to add to this new tab. Recommended elements for stores include:

- **Images:** Tap **Basic Items** and **Image**.
- **Product carousel:** Tap **Store** and **Product Carousel**.
- **Content**: Tap** Basic Items** and **Content Box**.
9. Customize the content of the tab. When you're done tap **Add** to add it to your navigation bar.
10. Tap **Screens **again to add a second tab for your second category.
11. Repeat steps 5-8 until you've created as many tabs as you need. 

**Tip:** When you're finished adding the tabs, tap **Screens** and **Reorder** to change the order of tabs in the menu.

** Include a product video:** Add a video element to your app and upload a video that showcases your products in a whole new way.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
6. Tap **Add Element **in the relevant location.
7. Tap **Basic Items**.
8. Tap **Video**.
9. Tap the **Add** icon  to upload a video from the Media Manager, Video Maker, YouTube or your personal device.
10. Tap **Add** at the top right.

** Add a product gallery: **Showcase your store products from the branded app and let members add items to their cart and purchase straight from the app.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.

** Add a 'My Cart' icon to the header: **Add a shopping cart icon to your app's header so members can easily navigate to their cart from anywhere on the app.

> The option to add a cart icon is only available for the **Minimal** header design.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap **Change Header Design** at the top.
6. Click **Customize**.
7. Scroll down and click the **Show Shopping Cart Icon** toggle to enable it.

- **Enabled:** Your branded app header includes a shortcut so members can navigate to their shopping cart.
- **Disabled**: There is no shopping cart icon included in your branded app header.
8. Tap **Save**.

### Creating a branded app for a restaurant

** Add online ordering and reservations sections: **Add an ordering tab so customers can order meals and a reservation section so people can easily book tables from the app.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
6. Tap **+ Add Element **in the relevant location.
7. Scroll through the element menu and tap **Restaurants**.
8. Choose the element you want to include:

- **Online Orders**: Customers can view your menu and place orders from your branded app.
- **Reservations**: Customers can book a table from your branded app.
9. Enter a title for the new element under **Element Title**.
10. Tap **Add**.

** Showcase your dishes in a gallery: **Add a gallery to your branded app of all your dishes to encourage customers to order food.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
6. Tap **+ Add Element **in the relevant location.
7. Tap the **Basic Items** tab.
8. Tap **Content Box**.
9. Edit the element title for your gallery e.g. 'Our Menu'.
10. Tap the **Add** icon  at the bottom left.
11. Tap **Gallery**.
12. Upload media from your device or the Media Manager.
13. When you're done tap **Publish** at the top right.
14. Tap **Add**.

** Create a custom 'Order Now' button **Add an 'Order Now' button to your branded app's header to grab customer attention and make it easy for them to order a meal.

> The custom button option is only available for the** Detailed** header design.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap **Change Header Design **at the top.
6. Click **Customize**.
7. Scroll down and enable the **Show Custom Button** toggle.
8. Tap the **Select Action** drop-down and select **Order from restaurant**.
9. Edit the text that appears on the header button under **Button text**.
10. Tap **Save** at the top right.

**Tip: **You can also add custom order buttons to a specific navigation tab.

### Creating a branded app for events

** Create a group for your event**:** **Create an event-specific group for people to join and discuss the event and share media after the event is over. Then add a 'group feed' section to your branded app to encourage people to chat.

### Show me how

1. [Create a new group](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-groups-in-the-wix-owner-app) for your event. Add the event name, description and attendees.
2. Go to your site in the Wix app.
3. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
4. Tap **Mobile App**.
5. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
6. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
7. Tap **Add Element **in the relevant location.
8. Swipe through the elements menu and tap **Groups**.
9. Choose a groups element to add to your branded app:

- **Group Feed:** Give members a quick overview of the main activities in your different groups.
- **Group List:** Show all your groups in a list and let members join or create their own.
- **Group Carousel:** Display your groups in a carousel and let members discover them one by one.
10. Update the element settings and tap **Add**.

### Creating a branded app for communities

** Include announcements:** Keep members updated with an announcements section and share any updates to the community.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
6. Tap **Add Element **in the relevant location.
7. Swipe through the elements menu and tap **Communication channels**.
8. Tap** Announcements**.
9. Tap **Edit Mobile Announcements **to add a new announcement and post it to your members.
Learn more about [sending an announcement as a push notification](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-sending-an-announcement-as-a-push-notification) in your branded app.

** Highlight your community with a members section:** Encourage member interaction by adding a members list or carousel.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
6. Tap **Add Element **in the relevant location.
7. Scroll through the elements menu and tap **Members**.
8. Choose a members element:

- **Member Badge List: **Highlight any badges you've created for your community as well as which members have received them.
- **Member Profile Carousel: **Displays the same info as a members list but in a carousel format that members can swipe through.
- **Members Strip:** Shows your member count and encourages members to invite friends.
- **Members Profile List: **Your members are displayed in a list that includes their profile photo and a follow button.
9. Customize the new element then tap **Add**.
