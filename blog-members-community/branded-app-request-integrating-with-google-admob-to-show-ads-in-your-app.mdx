---
title: "Branded App Request: Integrating with Google AdMob to Show Ads in Your App"
---

Currently there is no option to display ads in your branded app to earn revenue using Google Admob. [Google Admob](https://developers.google.com/admob) is a way to monetize mobile apps by making ad space available on your app for advertisers to promote their products. The ads are created and paid for by advertisers and then displayed in your branded app.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
