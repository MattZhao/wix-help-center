---
title: "The Blogger Feed App is No Longer Available"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This app has been retired from the Wix App Market and is no longer supported. As an alternative, you can [explore Wix Blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-setting-up-your-blog) and see what it has to offer, or choose to [embed a non-Wix blog on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget) using the embed option in the Wix Editor.
