---
title: "Site Members: About the Login, Signup and Password Windows"
---

There are 3 different login windows that you may have on your site. Two of them are related to the Members Area, and one is related to password protected pages.

**If you have a Members Area on your site, you will have a:**

### Member Signup Form

This form is displayed when a site visitor wants to sign up to become a member of your site. 

There are 3 types of member signup forms:
- Default
- Custom
- Velo Form

Learn More about [member signup forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-member-signup-form).

### Member Login Window

This form is displayed when an existing member wants to log in to your site.

You can customize the login window by changing your font themes and site colors. Learn more about [member login forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-member-login-form).

**If you have password protected a page on your site, you will have a:**

### Password Window

This window is displayed when a visitor accesses a page that you have protected with a password. Only those visitors who know the password can view the page.

While you can't customize the password window, you can change your site's theme to change how the password window looks. Learn more about [password protecting a page on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site).
If you would like to be able to customize this window, you can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-request-customizing-the-password-window-of-a-password-protected-page).
