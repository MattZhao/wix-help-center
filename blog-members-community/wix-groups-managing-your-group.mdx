---
title: "Wix Groups: Managing Your Group"
---

Ready to take your Wix Group to the next level? You can manage and customize every aspect of your group, from its basic information and privacy settings to its posts, members, tabs, and topics. Learn how to build a thriving online community that reflects your vision and goals.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant group.
3. Select **Manage**.
4. Select the relevant tabs to manage your group:
Info
- Add or change the group's name and description.
- Add or change your group's cover image.
- Choose who can view and access your group.

- **Public: **Anyone can view this group’s content and join.
- **Private: **Only group members can view this group’s content.
- **Only paying members: **Members [pay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-an-overview) to view this group’s content.

PostsLearn more about [managing your posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-creating-and-managing-your-groups-posts). 
MembersLearn more about [managing your group's members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-new-members-to-your-group).
TabsLearn more about [managing your tabs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-designing-your-group?tabs=Dashboard#customizing-your-group-tabs).
**Using Wix Events?** You can also edit the **Events** tab to choose which events are connected to the group. 
SettingsLearn more about [customizing your settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-customizing-your-settings).
TopicsLearn more about [managing your group's topics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-topics). 
What's next? [Design your group](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-designing-your-group) to reflect your content and site style.
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Groups**.
4. Tap the relevant group.
5. Choose what you want to manage:

- Add or change the group's name.

- **Public: **Anyone can view this group’s content and join.
- **Private: **Only group members can view this group’s content.
MembersLearn more about [managing your group's members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-new-members-to-your-group).Content
- Update your group description.
- Learn more about customizing your:

- [**Rules**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-your-group-rules)
- [**Tabs**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-designing-your-group?tabs=Wix-app#customizing-your-group-tabs)

EventsLearn more about [connecting events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-displaying-events-in-your-group). NotificationSelect the App and Email notifications you want to get from your group. [image: A screenshot of the Wix app open on Group settings]

> **Do you want to delete your group?**
>
> You can delete your group from the same location in your site's dashboard. 
>
> 1. [Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
> 2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant group.
> 3. Select **Delete**.
> 4. Click **Delete** in the pop-up to confirm.
