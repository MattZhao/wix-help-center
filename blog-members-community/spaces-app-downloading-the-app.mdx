Spaces by Wix is an app designed for your new and existing site members. People can book your services, RSVP to upcoming events and buy your products. They can also join discussions or read your blog posts on the go. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview)

Your members can download and use the Spaces App for free by searching for the app in the relevant app store** **or by using the direct download links.

### To download the Spaces App:

Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).  Type 'Spaces by Wix' in the **Search** field.  Click **Get** or **Install** to download the app.

> **You can also download the Spaces App right to your phone using these links:**
>
> - [iPhone](https://itunes.apple.com/app/wix/id1099748482)
> - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wix.android)
