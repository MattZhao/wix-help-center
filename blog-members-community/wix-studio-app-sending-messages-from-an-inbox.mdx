### Sending messages from your business site's inbox

Access your business site's inbox from the Home screen of your workspace so you never miss a message. You can choose to respond to your customers' needs using your preferred channel.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure you [set your business site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-app-setting-and-managing-your-business-site) to the relevant workspace.** **

#### To send a message from your business site's inbox:

Go to your relevant workspace in the Studio app.  Tap **Home**  at the bottom.  Tap ﻿**Inbox ﻿﻿** at the top.  Tap on the relevant contact you want to message.  Tap the channel drop-down and select how you want to send your message:

- **Chat: **Send a chat message to customers.** **To use this option, you must have the [Wix Chat app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-an-overview) on your site.
- **Email:** Send an email to customers whose email addresses are saved in your contact list.
- **SMS: **The SMS channel is available when you purchase a [Business Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-a-phone-number) and the member's number is saved in your contact list.

**Note: **You can't send SMS without saving a contact.

### Sending messages from a site's inbox

You can access a site's inbox if you're the site owner or have the relevant [role and permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview). For example, as 'Customer Relations Manager' for a site in an agency's workspace, you can use the inbox to communicate with their clients.

#### To send messages from a site's inbox:

Go to your relevant workspace in the Studio app.  Tap **Sites**  at the bottom.  Tap the relevant site.  Tap ﻿**Inbox ﻿﻿**.  Tap on the relevant contact you want to message.  Tap the channel drop-down and select how you want to send your message:

- **Chat: **Send a chat message to customers.** **To use this option, you must have the [Wix Chat app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-an-overview) on your site.
- **Email:** Send an email to customers whose email addresses are saved in your contact list.
- **SMS: **The SMS channel is available when you purchase a [Business Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-a-phone-number) and the member's number is saved in your contact list.

**Note: **You can't send SMS without saving a contact.

### What other features can I use with the inbox?

Once you select how you want to send your message (e.g. chat or email), you can choose what you want to include from the following options:
- **Saved replies:** Send saved replies to respond faster by saving the messages you send most often.
- **Share products and coupons: **Increase sales by sending the right offers & items directly to your visitors.
- **Share files and images:** Keep your customers and contacts up to date with invoices, designs or other documentation.
- **Request a payment: **Send a payment request with a convenient payment link. Your contact can use their credit card or PayPal account to make this payment.

### Can I give someone access to communicate using the inbox of a site?

Yes. For example, you can [invite](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-app-inviting-and-managing-clients-as-collaborators-on-their-sites) and assign a client the relevant role to manage their inbox while you work on their site. You can also [create a custom role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators) with this permission for your own business site so people can help manage the communication with your customers.
