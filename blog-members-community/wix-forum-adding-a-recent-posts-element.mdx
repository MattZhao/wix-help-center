---
title: "Wix Forum: Adding a Recent Posts Element"
---

Add a recent posts element to your site to let your readers know what you've been posting!

### To add and set up a recent posts element:

Click the Wix Forum app in your editor.  Click **Settings**.   Click the **Add-Ons **tab.  Click **Add to Page**.  From the drop-down list, select the page where you want the feed to appear.  Drag the recent posts element to position it on the page.  Click the recent posts element.  Click **Settings**.  Customize the settings:

1. Click the **Settings **tab.
2. Choose which category to display from the drop-down list.
3. Select how the posts are ordered.
    Customize the Layouts:

1. Click the **Layout **tab.
2. Select a layout.
3. Click the **Show social stats** toggle to show or hide the views, likes, and comments counters.
4. Drag the slider to select the number of posts you want to display.
    Customize the design:

1. Click the **Design** tab.
2. Customize any of the colors, fonts, borders or backgrounds.

> **Tips:**
>
> - You can drag the stretch handles to resize the app.
> - You can display up to 30 posts in the Recent Posts element.
