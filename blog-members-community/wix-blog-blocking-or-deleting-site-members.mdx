---
title: "Wix Blog: Blocking or Deleting Site Members"
---

Site admins can either block site members or delete them completely from the Wix Blog.

**What happens when a site member is blocked?**

The member cannot:

The member still appears on the members page. You can unblock a member at anytime.

**What happens when a site member is deleted?**

The member cannot:

All the site member's posts, comments, and likes are removed from your blog. The member no longer appears on the member's page.

---

### To block a site member:

Go to the Wix Blog on your live site.Go to the Members page.Click the relevant site member.Click the **Show More** icon  .Click **Block Member**. Click **Block **to confirm.

### To unblock a site member:

Go to the Wix Blog on your live site.Go to the Members page.Click the relevant site member.Click the **Show More** icon  .Click **Unblock Member**. Click **Unblock **to confirm.

### To delete a site member:

Go to the Wix Blog on your live site.Go to the Members page.Click the relevant site member.Click the **Show More** icon  .Click **Delete Member**. Click **Delete **to confirm.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also remove a writer if you have [given a member writing privileges](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog).
