---
title: "Request: Embedding Media from Your File Share App in Blog and Forum Posts"
---

Currently, it is not possible to embed files from your File Share app in Wix Blog posts and Wix Forum posts.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
