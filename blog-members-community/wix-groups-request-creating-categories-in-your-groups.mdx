---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Creating Categories in Your Groups"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create categories within your groups. For example, you may want to split a group for a language course into different categories; beginner, intermediate and advanced, so that your group participants can access the information relevant to them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
