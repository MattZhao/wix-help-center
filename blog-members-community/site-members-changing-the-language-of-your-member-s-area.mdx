You can change the language of your Member's Area to make it feel more localized and personal. This requires changing the language of your site from the dashboard, in your Language & Region settings.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Changing your site's language](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-language-of-elements-inside-the-members-area#changing-your-sites-language)  [FAQ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-language-of-elements-inside-the-members-area#faq)

### Changing your site's language

[Go to Language & Region](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Regional%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsettings%2Flanguage-and-region%2F) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Site Language** drop-down and select the desired language

Click** Save**.

---

### FAQs

Below are some of the most frequently asked questions about changing your site's language and how it affects the Member's Area. Click a question to learn more:

### What gets translated once I change my site's language?

Changing the language of your site automatically translates all Member's Area related widgets into the desired language (e.g. My Account, Profile, Events, My Orders, etc.)
This **doesn't change** the member pages names, also appearing on your [Member Menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-customizing-your-members-area#choosing-the-pages-in-your-members-area). To localize the pages' names, you can either:
- Rename each page individually, from the Page Settings panel.
- Change your Wix account's language.

**Note:** The "My Wallet" widget, where members can see their saved payment methods, is also controlled by your account's language.

### Does my site's language have to be the same as my account's language?

Your site's language does not need to be the same as your Wix account's language. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-language-of-your-account-dashboard-and-editor) to learn how to change the language of your account, dashboard and Editor.

### Can I use Wix Multilingual to localize my Member's Area?

While components with system text are automatically localized to your secondary language, Member's Area is currently not integrated with Wix Multilingual. If this is something you would like to see in the future, please [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-request-translating-the-members-area-in-multilingual-sites).
