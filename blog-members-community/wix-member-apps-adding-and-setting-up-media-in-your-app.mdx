title: "Wix Member Apps: Adding and Setting Up Media in Your App"
---

You can add media to your app to make it more enjoyable and exciting for your users. Videos and images provide visual engagement in a way that textual content can't always do. For example, a guitar school can use videos to show how to play certain musical notes.

It can also improve your app's visibility in search results and attract more users. Additionally, they make apps more user-friendly and appealing, which can also boost retention.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Adding and setting up an image in your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-adding-and-setting-up-media-in-your-app#adding-and-setting-up-an-image-on-your-app)  [Adding a video to your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-adding-and-setting-up-media-in-your-app#adding-a-video-to-your-app)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-adding-and-setting-up-media-in-your-app#faqs)

### Adding and setting up an image on your app

Use images to elevate your brand on your app and showcase exactly what your visitors can expect. You can use your own images or select from various photos from places like Wix.

#### To add an image:

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
5. Click **Elements **on the left.
6. Click **Image** under **Basic Items.**
7. Click **Change **on the panel on the right to add an image. Then click **+** **Upload Media** to upload an image from your computer or from your **Media Manager**.
8. Click **Add to Page**.
[image: A screenshot of where to change the stock photo and add a photo element to your app.]
1. Choose what you want to do:

- **Adjust: **Use the [Photo Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools) to edit your image.
- **Design: **Select a **Crop ratio **and **Corner radius**.
- **Behavior:** Choose what happens when tapping the image:

- **Nothing: **Nothing happens when the image is clicked.
- **It opens in a pop-up: **The image opens in a pop-up window.
- **It triggers an action: **The image acts as a button and can be linked to another screen, an external URL, a form, a phone number, or an email address.
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding for the button(s).

**Note: **Click the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins.
- **General Settings: **Click **General Settings **and under **Element visibility**, choose which devices to show this button on: **All**, **Android,** or **iOS**.

**Note: **You can show or hide elements of your site depending on what operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. Your elements are visible on all operating systems by default, but you can decide what to show on iOS, Android, or both.
2. Click **Save &** **Publish**.

2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.

4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.

6. Tap **+ Add Element** in the relevant location.
7. Tap **Image** under **Basic Items.**
8. Tap **Change** to add a photo.
9. (Optional) Tap **Edit** to [adjust your photo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-uploading-and-managing-your-media#editing-your-images).
10. Tap **Next**.

### Adding a video to your app

You can add videos to your app from your Media Manager or add a link from YouTube or Vimeo. Videos help users understand your product or feature and keep them engaged.

#### To add a video:

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g., **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
5. Click **Elements **on the left.
6. Click **Video** under **Basic Items**.
7. Click **Change Video** in the panel on the right and add a video from one of the sources below:
[image: Screenshot of adding a video element to your app.]Media Manager
1. Click **Media Manager** to upload a video from your computer and then click **Add to Page**.

**Tip:** You can click the **Media from Wix** tab to choose from thousands of professional videos and illustrations, free of charge.
2. Choose what you want to edit:

- **Design: **Select a **Crop ratio **and add a **Thumbnail** to your video.
- **Display: **Toggle **Hide video controls** to customize controls

**Note:** This disables custom Behavior options.
- **Behavior: **Customize the playback options:

- **Autoplay (on mute):** Toggle to enable or disable autoplay on mute. Visitors can turn on the sound manually.
- **Play in loop:** Toggle to enable or disable video looping.
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding for the button(s).

**Note: **Click the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about adding images and videos.

### How can I add other media besides images and videos?

You can add more advanced media directly from the **Add Elements** menu.

### Can I edit my uploaded YouTube videos in Wix?

Videos from YouTube cannot be edited through Wix, they must be edited in the platform where the video was originally edited and then re-uploaded to Wix.

### What types of videos and images can I add? How large can the files be?

You can upload various file types and sizes. [Learn more about supported media on Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes?tabs=Videos).
