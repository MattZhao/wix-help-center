title: "Branded App: Customizing Your App's Features and Content"
---

Once you've [designed your branded app's theme](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-designing-your-app), customize the content and features inside your app. Your branded app is made up of screens, similar to site pages. Choose how many screens you need as well as what each one displays.

Add elements to your screens based on your business such as a Wix Stores product page, an events list, or a Wix Bookings class schedule. Then customize the screen further with buttons, images, videos and more. You can also further customize the design of your app by choosing different colors and fonts.

Choose whether to customize your app's content from your site's dashboard or by downloading the Wix app and customizing from mobile.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Customizing the header](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#customizing-the-header)  [Customizing the navigation menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#customizing-the-navigation-menu)  [Adding elements to your screens](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#adding-elements-to-your-screens)  [Managing your screens](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#managing-your-screens)  [Customizing your app design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#customizing-your-app-design)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#faqs)  [Video tutorial](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#video-tutorial)

### Customizing the navigation menu

Your branded app is made up of screens to help your members navigate between different areas of your app. You can choose whether the navigation menu appears at the top of bottom of your app. Then customize the design by adding custom menu icons.

DashboardWix app Wix Studio app

2. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
3. Click the navigation bar of your app. It appears either under your app's header or at the bottom of the app.
[image: A screenshot of the bottom bar navigation panel in the web editor.]
1. Choose a navigation layout:

- **Top Tab Bar:** Members navigate between screens at the top of your app directly under the header.
- **Bottom Tab Bar: **Members navigate between screens at the bottom of your app. You can add custom icons for each screen if you select this option.

- **Labels: **Click the toggle to show or hide the name of the screen in the navigation menu. The name appears under the screen's icon. It is recommended to include the screen name for accessibility reasons.
- **Icons: **Customize the icons in your navigation menu. Each icon corresponds to a screen. Hover over an icon and click **Change icon** to replace it.
2. Click **Save** at the top right.

### Adding elements to your screens

Elements make up the content of your app. Add elements to showcase who you are, what you do, and what you offer app members. For example, if you own an event planning business, add an Events element so people can RSVP straight from the app.

Once you've added elements to your app, move them up or down the same screen or switch them to a different screen entirely. You can also duplicate elements, remove unwanted ones, and edit the content within each element.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [adding elements for specific businesses](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-tips-for-customizing-your-app-for-your-business) such as online stores, restaurants and bookings.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about customizing your branded app's features.

### How do I change my app language?

You can choose the default language your visitors see when they download your app. To change your app language:
1. Go to **App Settings** in your branded app dashboard.
2. Click the **Default language** drop-down and select a language.

**Note:** Choosing an app language updates all Wix apps content (Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, Wix Blog etc.). Any content you've created on your own, such as adding a new text section, appears in the language you selected. The Branded App does not currently support [multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-an-overview).

### Why do I need to download the Wix app?

Not all customization options are available in the web editor in your site's dashboard. The Wix app allows you to build out your app's content and see how it appears.

### If I make changes to the features and content of my app, do I have to resubmit it for review?

You must resubmit your app for review if you make changes to the app's branding or design. You do not need to resubmit your app if you are making content changes. Learn more [about what updates need another review](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-submitting-app-changes-to-apple-and-google-8954339).
