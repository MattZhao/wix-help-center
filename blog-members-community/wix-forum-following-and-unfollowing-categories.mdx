---
title: "Wix Forum: Following and Unfollowing Categories"
---

As the admin of your site, you automatically follow all categories you create. This means you receive notifications about each new post. You can choose to unfollow any of these categories at anytime.  Members can also choose to follow and unfollow categories as they want. This is a great way to ensure they don't miss anything of interest in the forum.

Learn how to:

[Follow a category](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-following-and-unfollowing-categories#follow-a-category-1)[Unfollow a category](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-following-and-unfollowing-categories#unfollow-a-category-1)

> **Tip:**
>
> You can control exactly which notifications you receive. To learn how to customize your notification settings, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-adding-and-setting-up-forum-notifications).

### Following a Category

Go to your forum on your live site. Click **Follow** next to the relevant category.

---

### Unfollowing a Category

Go to your forum on your live site. Click **Following** next to the relevant category.Click **Unfollow**.
