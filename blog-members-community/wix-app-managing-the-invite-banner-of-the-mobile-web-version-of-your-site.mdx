title: "Wix App: Managing the Invite Banner of the Mobile Web Version of Your Site"
---

Display a banner on your mobile site inviting visitors to become site members and download [the member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps). The app gives your members a better mobile experience as they can interact with your site on-the-go, purchase products, or book services, and chat with you directly.

When needed, you can remove the invite banner to help simplify site upkeep, allowing for concentrated content modifications and adjustments free from interruptions.

> **Important:**
>
> - You can only add the mobile invite banner if you have [upgraded your site and connected a domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connect-domain-premium-plan-overview).
> - If you haven't upgraded your site, a free banner without the name of your site is always displayed. You need to [upgrade](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) to be able to hide the banner or to display the name of your site.

### Adding an invite banner

You can add the invite banner from your site's dashboard, the Wix app, or the Studio app.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to **Growth and marketing tools**.
3. Click **Add Banner** under **Mobile invite banner**.
4. Click **Add Banner**.
[image: A screenshot of the Mobile Invite Banner in a site's dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing **and select **Site Discoverability**.
4. Tap** Mobile invite banner**.
5. Enable the **Add Banner** toggle to show the banner.
[image: A screenshot of the Mobile Invite Banner panel in the Wix Owner app.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Manage**.
3. Tap **Mobile Marketing Tools** under **Marketing**.
4. Tap **Mobile invite banner**.
5. Enable the **Add Banner** toggle to show the banner.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you purchase a selected yearly [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) site plan, you are eligible to receive a [voucher for a free domain for one year](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-free-domain-voucher).

### Removing an invite banner

You can remove the invite banner from your dashboard or mobile app.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to **Growth and marketing tools**.
3. Click **Remove Banner** under **Mobile invite banner**.
4. Click **Remove** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot of clicking to remove the mobile invite banner in a site's dashboard.]Mobile web visitors won’t be directed to join your site on the app. You can always add the banner back another time.
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing **and select **Site Discoverability**.
4. Tap** Mobile invite banner**.
5. Disable the **Add Banner** toggle to remove the banner.
[image: A screenshot of tapping on Mobile invite banner under Site Discoverability in the Wix Owner app.]Mobile web visitors won’t be directed to join your site on the app. You can always add the banner back another time.
1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Manage**.
3. Tap **Mobile Marketing Tools** under **Marketing**.
4. Tap **Mobile invite banner**.
5. Disable the **Add Banner** toggle to remove the banner.
[image: A screenshot of the Mobile Invite Banner panel to disable it in the Wix Studio app.]Mobile web visitors won’t be directed to join your site on the app. You can always add the banner back another time.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the invite banner.

### Can I change the design of my invite banner?

Currently it is not possible to customize the design of your invite banner.

### What does the banner look like if I haven't upgraded my site?

If you haven't upgraded your site, a different banner appears. The text on the banner invites visitors to join your site on your [preferred member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app), but it does not display the name of your site. 
[image: A screenshot of the invite banner for non Premium plan users.]
**Tip:** [Upgrade your plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan#premium-plan-features) at anytime and increase the chances of visitors becoming members.

### Visitors are joining my site through the invite banner but it's not ready yet. What can I do?

You can change the permissions so that members can only join your site after you approve them. 
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Settings **and select **Roles & Permissions**.
4. Enable the** Join Approval **toggle to allow members to join your members app only once you approve them.

[image: A screenshot of the Roles & Permissions screen in the Wix Owner app.]
