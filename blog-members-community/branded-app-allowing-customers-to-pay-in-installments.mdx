---
title: "Branded App: Allowing Customers to Pay in Installments"
---

Currently in [Branded App](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview), when your app users purchase a product or service, they need to pay the entire amount upfront. It is not possible to allow customers to pay in installments.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
