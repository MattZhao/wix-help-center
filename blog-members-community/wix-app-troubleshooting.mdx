### General issues in the Wix app

Below are some common notification issues that have been reported when using the Wix app. Click on the issue you're having to learn how to resolve it.

### I'm not seeing push notifications

If you can't see push notifications on your device's notification screen try the following steps:

1. Make sure you have enabled push notifications for the Wix app in the general notification settings of your phone. This differs from one device to another.
2.  Check your site notification settings in the Wix app:
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap your profile icon at the top.
3. Tap **Notification Settings **under** App Settings**.
4. Select your site from the list (if you have more than one).
5. Enable the **Allow Push Notifications **toggle to allow site notifications to your mobile device.
6. (Optional): Manage the notification settings for the apps on your site by enabling or disabling the toggle next to the app's name.*

*
**Note:** If you had both Spaces by Wix and the Wix app downloaded and stopped receiving push notifications after uninstalling the Wix app, re-download the Wix app and try again.

### I'm not getting notified about live site visitors

If you aren't getting notified about site visitors, first make sure you've added [Wix Chat](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-adding-wix-chat-to-your-site) to your site. You won't be able to see live visitors without the Wix Chat app.
Learn more about [resolving live site visitor issues in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors#troubleshooting-live-site-visitor-issues).

### I'm not able to find my site

Every site you create can be found in the Wix app. If you have multiple sites, you can switch between them using the site drop-down at the top.

1. Open the Wix app.
2. Tap the site drop-down at the top.

[image: Screenshot of where to select your site in the Wix Owner app.]
3. Tap the site you want to manage from your site list.

### Local issues in the Wix app

If you are experiencing local issues including performance and loading issues, make sure you are using a device that supports the app and that you're running the most updated version.

### Check that your app is up to date

1. Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).
2. Search for 'Wix app.'
3. Click **Update** (if available).

**Note:** If there's no option to update it means you're running the most up-to-date version of the app.

### Check that you're using a supported device

The Wix app is supported on:
- **iPhone: **iOS 11 and above
- **Android:** 6.0 and above

### Log out of the Wix app and log back in

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap your profile icon at the top.
3. Tap **Log Out **at the bottom.
4. Log back in using your Wix account credentials.
5. (If the issue isn't resolved) Restart your mobile device.

### Clear cache on your device

**For Android devices:**
1. Long press the app icon on your device's home screen.
2. Tap **App Info**.
3. Tap **Storage and cache**.
4. Tap **Clear cache**.
5. Log back in using your Wix account credentials.

**For iOS devices:**
1. Go to **Settings**
2. Tap **Safari**
3. Tap **Clear History and Website Data**.

### Still having trouble?

If the problem persists, please [contact us](http://www.wix.com/contact) and have the following information ready:

A description of the steps you've taken so that we can recreate the issue on our end  A [screenshot](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-screenshot-to-a-ticket?tabs=Mobile-device) of the issue  (For notification issues): Specify which notifications you're not receiving  The manufacturer, model and operating system of your mobile device (e.g. Apple iPhone 6s, Samsung Galaxy S4)  The type of connection you're using (e.g., 3G, WiFi) and the name of your mobile network (if you are using a 3G or 4G connection)  Your location  The version of the Wix app you're using. To find this:

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap your profile icon at the top.
3. Tap **About the App** under** Help & Feedback**.
4. Scroll down to the bottom of the screen and copy the number next to **Version**.
