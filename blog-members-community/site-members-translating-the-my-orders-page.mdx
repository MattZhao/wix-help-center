---
title: "Site Members: Translating the \"My Orders\" Page"
---

Sites with Wix Stores have a [My Orders page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-your-customers-member-pages) where customers can track orders. When using Wix Multilingual, the text in your Members Area is automatically translated. However, you can translate [custom profile fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-customizing-your-member-profile-fields) in the translation manager.

Wix EditorStudio EditorDashboard
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the **Switch languages **drop-down menu at the top of the editor.
3. Select the language you want to edit.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Switch Languages drop-down at the top of the Wix Editor.]
1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click the **Member Page**.
3. Select the **My Orders** tab from the floating tab selector on the canvas.
[image: Selecting the My Orders tab from the floating bar to translate it in Wix Editor]
1. (In the My Orders tab) Select the **My Orders** element.
2. Click **Edit translation**.
3. (In the [translation manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-using-the-translation-manager)) Click **Site Members** on the left.
4. Translate the custom fields.
[image: Translating custom member profile fields in the Translation Manager]
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the **Switch languages **drop-down menu at the top of the editor.
3. Select the language you want to edit.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Switch Languages drop-down menu at the top of the Studio Editor.]
1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click the **Member Page**.
3. Select the **My Orders** tab from the floating tab selector on the canvas.
[image: Selecting the My Orders tab from the floating bar to translate it in Studio Editor]
1. (In the My Orders tab) Select the **My Orders** element.
2. Click **Edit translation**.
3. (In the [translation manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-using-the-translation-manager)) Click **Site Members** on the left.
4. Translate the custom fields.
[image: a screenshot of the translation manager]
1. [Go to the Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmultilingual%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Multilingual) in your site's dashboard.
2. Choose the relevant language at the top left.
3. Click **Site Members** on the left.
4. Translate the custom fields.
5. Click **Save**.
[image: a screenshot of the translation manager]
