---
title: "Wix Blog: Using the Wix Blog on a Site with Members-Only Pages"
---

Members-only pages are pages that visitors need to sign up for in order to get access.

Site membership, blog membership, and forum membership (if you have a forum) is the same. In other words, once visitors sign up to your site, they can:

Comment on blog postsAccess members-only pagesContribute to a forum

Access to member-only pages, as well as your blog or forum, is through the Login Bar. By default, it appears next to your site menu.

You can limit access to your blog and Post Page to password holders (or only to the Post Page). To do so, go to the **Permissions** tab for those pages.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also limit access to your blog to [paying members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-limiting-your-blog-to-paying-members).
