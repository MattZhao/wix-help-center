---
title: "Wix Apps Request: Subscription Promotions"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create subscription promotions using Wix Promos.

As a workaround, you can create [product subscriptions using Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-selling-product-subscriptions).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
