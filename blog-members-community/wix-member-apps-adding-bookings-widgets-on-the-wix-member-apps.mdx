### Displaying your Bookings widgets

Select the Booking widgets you want to include on your member apps and customize them to your liking. Rename widgets, select which screens in your member apps to display them on, choose which devices are able to see these elements, and more.

#### To display your Bookings widgets:

Go to your site in the [Wix App](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Site & App**.  Tap **Customize** under **Mobile Apps**.

Tap the **Screen **drop-down at the top and choose the screen in which you want to place the widget.   Tap **+ Add Element**.   Tap **Bookings **at the top.   Tap the element you want to add:

### Upcoming bookings

Remind clients of their upcoming bookings, allow them to easily book a new appointment and see their booking history.

1. Edit the following details:

- **Element title: **Enable the **Element title**** **toggle to show a title at the top of the element. Then enter the text you want to use.
- **Show avatar preview: **Enable the **Show**** avatar preview** toggle to show avatars of participants who have booked the sessions already.
- **Empty state text: **Add a message for clients who have no upcoming sessions booked.
- **Online sessions: **Choose a layout for virtual sessions: **With image **or **Text only**.
- **Booking history:** Enable the **Booking history** toggle to show previously booked classes and appointments so members can quickly book again.
- **Visibility:** Choose which devices you want to show these elements: **All**, **Android**,** **or ** iOS**.
2. Tap **Add** at the top right.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I move widgets from one screen to another?

Yes. Once you add a widget, you can move it to another screen. Here's how:
1. Tap the **Screen **drop-down at the top and select the relevant screen.
2. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of the element.
3. Tap **Customize element**.
4. Tap the **Choose screen** drop-down under **Location** and select which screen you want the element to appear on.
5. Tap **Save** at the top right.
[image: A screenshot showing where to change element screen.]

### How do I customize an existing widget?

To customize an existing widget:
1. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom of your screen.
2. Tap **Site & App**.
3. Tap **Customize** under **Mobile Apps**.
4. Tap the **Screen **drop-down at the top and select the relevant screen.
5. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of an existing element.
6. Tap **Customize element**.
7. Edit the element and tap **Save** at the top right.

### Can I delete a widget?

Yes. Once you delete a widget, you are still able to add the same element again, but any customizations made will need to be reapplied.

1. Go to your site in the Wix App.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Site & App**.
4. Tap **Customize** under **Mobile Apps**.
5. Tap the **Screen **drop-down at the top and select the relevant screen.
6. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of the element.
7. Tap **Delete element**.
8. Tap **Delete element** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot showing where to confirm and delete an element.]

### How do I add the same widget to two different screens?

You can duplicate a widget, then move the duplicate widget to another screen. 
To add the same widget to two screens:
1. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
2. Tap **Site & App**.
3. Tap **Customize** under **Mobile Apps**.
4. Tap the **Screen **drop-down at the top and select the relevant screen.
5. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of the element.
6. Tap **Duplicate**.
7. Tap **Edit** icon   at the top right of the duplicated element.
8. Tap **Customize element**.
9. Tap the **Choose screen** drop-down under **Location **and select which screen you want the element to appear on.
10. Tap **Save** at the top right.

### How can I change the order of my widgets?

You can move your widgets up or down in the screen to organize them.
To reorder your widgets:
1. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
2. Tap **Site & App**.
3. Tap **Customize** under **Mobile Apps**.
4. Tap the **Screen **drop-down at the top and select the relevant screen.
5. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of the element.
6. Tap the relevant option: **Move up** or **Move down**.

### Can clients filter what they see on widgets?

Yes. In their Member App (Fit by Wix or the Spaces App), on the Agenda page or the Weekly Timetable, clients can click **Filters** to view sessions by location, service or staff member.
