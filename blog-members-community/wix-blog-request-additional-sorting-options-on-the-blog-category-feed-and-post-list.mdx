---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Additional Sorting Options on the Blog/Category Feed and Post List"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add additional ways to sort your blog posts. Your [Post List and Blog Feed displays posts in chronological order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-changing-the-order-of-blog-posts) (from newest to oldest) only.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
