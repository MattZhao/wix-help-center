Using the Get Image Colors add-on, you can make your own unique color palette from the images on your site.

> The Get Image Colors add-on is only available in the Wix Studio Editor.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adding the Get Image Colors add-on](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-get-image-colors)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-get-image-colors#faqs)

---

### Adding the Get Image Colors add-on

Add the Get Image Colors add-on from the tools in your Studio Editor.

#### To add the Get image colors add-on:

Click the Wix Studio icon  at the top left.  Click **Tools**.   Click **Editor Add-ons**.   Click **Get Add-ons**.   Click **Get Image Colors**.

---

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more:

### Can I create palettes from any element on my site?

You can only create palettes from images on your site.

### Can I generate different palettes from the same image?

Yes, you can generate a different palette from the same image by clicking **Generate New Palette**.

### Can I download my color palette?

Yes, just click **Download palette** to download the palette.
