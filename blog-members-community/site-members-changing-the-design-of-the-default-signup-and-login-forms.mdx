title: "Site Members: Changing the Design of the Default Signup and Login Forms"
---

Design the window that visitors see when [signing up](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-member-signup-form) or [logging in](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-member-login-form) to your Members Area. This also applies to any password-protected page on your site.

The default form is already designed for you. It matches your site colors and fonts so your design stays consistent. While you can't customize the design of the default form from scratch, you can still control some design elements by customizing your site styles.

**In this article, learn how to change the:**

[Page title font](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-changing-the-design-of-the-password-window#changing-the-font-of-the-pages-title)  [Text elements font](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-changing-the-design-of-the-password-window#changing-the-font-of-other-text-elements-on-the-page)  [Text color](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-changing-the-design-of-the-password-window#changing-the-text-color)  [Button and link color](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-changing-the-design-of-the-password-window#changing-the-button-and-link-color)  [Page background color](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-changing-the-design-of-the-password-window#changing-the-background-color)

### Changing the font of the page's title

The font for the title text on the password window is taken from the **Heading 2** text theme. If you want to change the font, you can customize the text theme for your site.

> **Important:**
>
> This also changes any other text with the **Heading 2** theme on your site.

#### To change the Heading 2 font:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Site Design ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Text theme**.
3. Click **Customize heading styles**.
4. Hover over **Heading 2** and click the **Edit** icon .
5. Choose a new font.
6. Click **Apply **to save your changes.
[image: A screenshot of the Heading 2 font in the Wix Editor and the corresponding title text on the members area sign up form.]
1. Click **Site Styles**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Typography**.
3. Click the **Edit typography** icon  next to **Heading 2**.
4. Choose a new font.
5. Click **Apply**.
[image: Choosing a new font for the Heading 2  text theme in the Studio Editor]

### Changing the text color

Pick a different color for the title and text of the password window by customizing the darkest shade of your **Base** / **Background & Text** colors in your site's palette.

> **Important:**
>
> This also changes the color of any other elements on your site where this shade is applied.

#### To change the color for text elements:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Site Design ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Color theme**.
3. Click the darkest color under **Base colors**.
4. Use the color picker in the **Custom Color **box to change the color.
5. (Optional) Use the sliders to change the **Saturation** and **Brightness**.
6. Click **Apply**.
[image: A screenshot of the Base colors in the Wix Editor and the corresponding text colors on the members area sign up form.]
1. Click **Site Styles**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Colors**.
3. Under **Background & Text**, click the rightmost shade.

**Tip: **This shade is also usually the darkest, but it depends on your specific design.
4. Click the color box next to **HEX **to choose a new color.
5. (Optional) Use the sliders to change the **Saturation** and **Brightness**.
6. Click **Apply**.
[image: Changing the rightmost shade of the Background & Text color in the Studio Editor, to affect the text color]

### Changing the button and link color

Choose a different color for links and buttons by customizing a specific shade in your site's **Accent **/ **Action** colors.** **This shade is used across your site for many actionable elements like buttons, links and menu items.

> **Important:**
>
> This also changes the color of any other elements on your site where this shade is applied.

#### To change the color for buttons and links:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Site Design ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Color theme**.
3. Click the first color in the **Accent colors** section.
4. Use the color picker in the **Custom Color **box to change your accent color.
5. (Optional) Use the sliders to change the **Saturation** and **Brightness**.
6. Click **Apply**.
[image: A screenshot of the Accent colors in the Editor and the corresponding button colors on the members area sign up form.]
1. Click **Site Styles**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Colors**.
3. Under **Action Colors**, click the middle shade.
4. Click the color box next to **HEX **to choose a new color.
5. (Optional) Use the sliders to change the **Saturation** and **Brightness**.
6. Click **Apply**.
[image: Changing the middle shade of the Action color in the Studio Editor, to affect buttons and links]

### Changing the background color

To change the background color of the password window, adjust the lightest shade of your **Base **colors** **/ **Background & Text **colors in your site's color theme. This shade is automatically used across your site, mostly for page and element backgrounds.

> **Important:**
>
> This also changes the color of any other elements on your site where this shade is applied.

#### To change the color for page backgrounds:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Site Design ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Color theme**.
3. Click the lightest color under **Base colors**.
4. Use the color picker in the **Custom Color **box to change the background color.
5. (Optional) Use the sliders to change the **Saturation** and **Brightness** values.
6. Click **Apply**.
[image: A screenshot of the Base colors in the Wix Editor and the corresponding text colors on the members area sign up form.]
1. Click **Site Styles**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Colors**.
3. Under **Background & Text**, click the leftmost shade.

**Tip: **This shade is also usually the lightest, but it depends on your specific design.
4. Click the color box next to **HEX **to choose a new color.
5. (Optional) Use the sliders to change the **Saturation** and **Brightness**.
6. Click **Apply**.
[image: Changing the leftmost shade of the Background & Text color in the Studio Editor, to affect the background]

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Publish **to view the changes on your live site. You won't be able to see the password window by previewing your site.
