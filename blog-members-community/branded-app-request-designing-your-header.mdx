---
title: "Branded App Request: Designing Your Header"
---

Currently, when designing your Branded App, you can choose from 2 preset header designs; Detailed and Minimal. While it's possible [choose what info appears in the header](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-header), it is not yet possible to design the header further.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
