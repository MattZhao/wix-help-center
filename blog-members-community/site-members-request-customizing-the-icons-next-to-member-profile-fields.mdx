---
title: "Site Members Request: Customizing the Icons Next to Member Profile Fields"
---

While you can [customize the fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-customizing-your-member-profile-fields) that appear in your members' profile page, it is not possible to change the icons next to the fields.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
