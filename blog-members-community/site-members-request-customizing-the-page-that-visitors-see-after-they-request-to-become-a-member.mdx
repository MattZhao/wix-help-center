---
title: "Site Members Request: Customizing the Page that Visitors See After They Request to Become a Member"
---

Currently, it is not possible customize the page that your visitors see after they request to become a member of your site. Customizing this page would allow you to add your own message to explain that their membership request is pending your approval.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
