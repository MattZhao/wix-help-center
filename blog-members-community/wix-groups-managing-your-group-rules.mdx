---
title: "Wix Groups: Managing Your Group Rules"
---

Create a set of guidelines for your group members' communication with group rules. These rules help set a positive tone for discussions and avoid conflicts. You can create your own rules or customize templates. Members can access these rules from your group's "About" tab.

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant group.
3. Click the **Settings** tab.
4. Choose what you want to do next:

- **Create a new rule:**

1. Click** Add Rule** in the **Group rules **section and choose what you want to do:

- Click** + Start from scratch** and enter the rule title and description.
- Click **Use Template **next to the relevant rule and customize the rule's title and description.
2. Click **Save**.
- **Edit a rule:** 

1. Click the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant rule and select **Edit**.
2. Edit the rule's title or description.
3. Click **Save**.
- **Delete a rule:**

1. Click the** More Actions** icon  next to the relevant rule and select **Delete**.
2. Click **Delete** to confirm.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Groups**.
4. Tap the relevant group.
5. Tap **Rules**.
6. Choose what you want to do next:

- **Create a new rule:**

1. Tap** + Create New Rule **and choose what you want to do:

- Tap** + Create New Rule** and enter the rule title and description.
- Tap a rule template and customize the rule's title and description.
2. Tap **Create**.
- **Edit a rule:** 

1. Tap the relevant rule.
2. Tap **Edit** to change the rule's title or description.
3. Tap **Save**.
- **Delete a rule:**

1. Tap the relevant rule.
2. Tap **Delete**.
