title: "Site Members: Creating and Setting Up a Member Badge"
---

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To use member badges, you must install a [Member's Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) on your site.

Assign badges to specific site members so that they stand out from all the other members. For example, you can create a badge such as "Star Blogger" for members who contribute the most to your blog, or a badge for members who are experts in a subject, for example "Yoga Expert".

### To create and set up a member badge:

Go to your [member list](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members). Click **More Actions** at the top right and click **Manage Badges**.

Click** New Badge**.Enter the badge details:
- **Badge Name and Description:** Enter a unique name and description to let everyone know what the badge is about.
- **Background Color: **Click the field and select a color for the badge background.
- **Text Color: **Click the field and select a color for the badge text.
- Click **Add Image** under **Icon** and select an icon for your badge from the free vector art in the Media Manager, or upload your own .svg file.
Click **Save**.Click **Assign Members** and select the members you want to assign the badge to, then click **Add**.Click the **Permissions** tab and enable the toggle if you want to add permissions to badge holders.

> **Tip:**
>
> Assign badges to your members automatically when they perform a certain action on your site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-assigning-badges-automatically-to-site-members)
