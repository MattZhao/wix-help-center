---
title: "Wix App Request: Managing Your Domain from the App"
---

When a Wix site is published, it receives a "free domain" or URL with the name Wix in it, that can be managed from your dashboard. Currently, it is not possible to update your domain information, switch your primary domain, or purchase a new one, from the Wix app.

For example, if you want to add a domain name that is currently designated to another site, or use a domain to get a Business Email, you have to do this from the [domains page on your dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-domains-page).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
