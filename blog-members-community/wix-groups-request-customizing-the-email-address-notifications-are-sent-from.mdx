---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Customizing the Email Address Notifications Are Sent From"
---

Currently, Wix Groups email notifications (e.g. group updates such as new posts) are sent from notifications@groups-wix.com. It is not possible to change this email address.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
