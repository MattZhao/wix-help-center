---
title: "Branded App Request: Uploading Your Own Screenshots to Store Listings"
---

Currently, in the Branded app, it is not possible to upload your own screenshots to Store Listings when preparing to submit your app to the App Store and Google Play.

As a workaround you can [upload your own screenshots to the Wix Chatbot](https://www.wix.com/contact?nodeId=101c61ab-8645-4ef5-81df-a9f337378bb2&referral=brandedAppByWixKb). Once you submit them, the Branded App by Wix submissions team will get notified and upload the screenshots to Store Listings on your behalf.

> **Make sure your screenshots meet the Apple and Google requirements:**
>
> - **Apple:** Requires 3 images for the 3 different device types (sizes 6.5, 5.5, and 12.9).
> - **Google: **Requires specifications for an app icon, feature graphic, and phone screenshots.
>
> [Learn more about Apple and Google requirements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/requirements-for-uploading-your-own-screenshots-to-store-listings-via-the-wix-chatbot).

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
