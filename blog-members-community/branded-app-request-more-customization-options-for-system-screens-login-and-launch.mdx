---
title: "Branded App Request: More Customization Options for System Screens (Login and Launch)"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add more customization to your app's login and launch screens, such as different animations, extra fields, and additional elements. However, you can [customize your login and launch screens by choosing the colors, text, and image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-system-screens-launch-and-login) for your brand.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
