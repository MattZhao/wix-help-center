---
title: "Wix App Request: Managing Your Premium Plans and Subscriptions from the Wix App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to manage your plans and subscriptions from the Wix app. This includes assigning a plan to a different site, canceling a plan, viewing your vouchers and upgrading a plan.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can manage your plans and subscriptions through the [desktop](https://manage.wix.com/account/subscriptions?originWidgetName=billings&referralAdditionalInfo=my-sites#/billings).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
