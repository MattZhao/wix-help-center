---
title: "Wix Blog: Adding Divider Lines to Blog Posts"
---

You can add divider lines to blog posts to separate your post into sections. Choose the line style that best suits your blog.

### To add a divider to a blog post:

Start writing a post from your live site or from the Post Manager.Click the **Divider** icon  at the bottom of the page.(Optional) Customize the divider line:
1. Click the **Divider Style** drop-down menu and select a style.
2. Select a line size: Small, medium, or large.
3. Select a justification setting: Left, right, or center.
