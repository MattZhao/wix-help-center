---
title: "Site Members Request: Moving Members from One Site to Another in Your Wix Account"
---

Currently, is it not possible to move site members from one Wix site to another within your account (or to another account). Although it is possible to export your contact list and import it to another site, due to privacy concerns, it is not possible to export and import your member list.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
