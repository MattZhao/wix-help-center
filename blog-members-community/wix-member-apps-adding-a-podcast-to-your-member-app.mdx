---
title: "Wix Member Apps: Adding a Podcast to Your Member App"
---

Embed a podcast for your members to listen to on the go, directly from their [member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps). You can add a podcast player to an existing screen or create a dedicated screen for it.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Go to the settings section of your podcast hosting platform (for example, [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasters.apple.com/support/837-change-the-rss-feed-url), [Google Podcasts](https://support.google.com/podcast-publishers/answer/9889544?hl=en), [Spotify](https://support.spotifyforpodcasters.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043988572-Updating-an-RSS-feed-link-or-hosting-provider), [SoundCloud](https://help.soundcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003453807-Getting-started-with-podcasting) etc.) and copy the RSS link.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
5. Click **Elements **on the left.
6. Scroll through the top menu and click **Podcasts**.
7. Click the podcast element.
8. Choose what you want to do:

- Paste your RSS link in the **Link to podcast **field.
- Enable the **Show title** toggle to display and edit your podcast name.
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding for the button(s).

**Note: **Click the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins.
- **General Settings: **Click **General Settings **and under **Element visibility**, choose which devices to show this button on: **All**, **Android,** or **iOS**.

**Note: **You can show or hide elements of your site depending on what operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. Your elements are visible on all operating systems by default, but you can decide what to show on iOS, Android, or both.
9. Click **Save &** **Publish**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing a different mobile app:

1. Tap the mobile app name at the top.
2. Tap **Branded Mobile App** or tap the relevant mobile app (e.g. Spaces by Wix app).

**Tip: **Go to your site's dashboard on desktop to choose your preferred member app.

1. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
2. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
3. Tap **+ Add Element** in the relevant location.
4. Scroll through the top menu and tap **Podcasts**.
5. Tap the podcast element.
6. Choose what you want to do:

- Paste your RSS link in the **Link to podcast **field.
- Tap the **Element Title** toggle to show or hide the name of your podcast:

- **Enabled: **The title of your podcast appears on your site. Enter the name you want to display.
- **Disabled: **The title of your podcast is not displayed.
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding for the button(s).

**Note: **Tap the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins.
- **General Settings**: Choose which screen and device you want this button to appear on:

- **Visibility**: Choose which devices to show this button on: **All**, **Android** or **iOS**.

**Note: **[You can show or hide elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-showing-or-hiding-elements-on-different-mobile-operating-systems) of your site depending on what operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. Your elements are visible on all operating systems by default, but you can decide what to show on iOS, Android, or both.
- **Location: **Select which screen you want the button displayed on.
7. Tap **Add**.
