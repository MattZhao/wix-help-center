---
title: "Wix Forum: Featuring Selected Forum Posts"
---

Create "sticky posts" which appear at the top of a forum category. All other posts appear beneath the pinned posts, in the order they were published.

### To pin / unpin a post to the top of a forum category:

Do one of the following:

- **For published sites: **

1. Log in to your live site using the email address associated with your account.
2. Navigate to the forum page.
- **For unpublished sites: **Go to your Wix Forum in [preview mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site#previewing-your-site).
    Click the **Show More** icon on a post.  Do one of the following:

- Click **Pin post**.
- Click **Unpin post**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Pinned posts appear in the order they were pinned.
