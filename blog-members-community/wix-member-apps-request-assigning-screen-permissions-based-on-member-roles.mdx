---
title: "Wix Member Apps Request: Assigning Screen Permissions Based on Member Roles"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set screen permissions in the Wix member apps based on a [role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview) that a member has on your site (e.g. site owner, admin, billing manager, domain manager etc).

However, you can set [screen permissions for pricing plans and badges](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-setting-viewing-permissions-for-your-members).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
