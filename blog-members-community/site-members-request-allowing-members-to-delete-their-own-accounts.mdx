---
title: "Site Members Request: Allowing Members to Delete Their Own Accounts"
---

Currently, it is not possible for members to delete their own accounts (including profile pages). Members can do this by contacting the site owner.

As a workaround, your site members can use the Spaces by Wix app to leave your site. This removes them as members of your site and deletes their profile page.

### To leave a site:

Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).  Type "Spaces by Wix" in the search field.  Tap **Get** or **Install** to download the app.  Open the app.  Tap **I already have a Wix Account**.  Sign in to your account.   Tap the More Actions icon .  Tap** Manage my sites**.  Tap the More Actions icon  next to the relevant site.  Tap **Leave**.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
