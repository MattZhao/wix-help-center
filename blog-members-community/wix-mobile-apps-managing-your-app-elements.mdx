title: "Wix Mobile Apps: Managing Your App Elements"
---

Each screen of your mobile app (e.g. Spaces by Wix, your own native app) contains different elements, similar to the elements on your site pages. You can add the elements that are most relevant to your business. For example, you can add a service element to show a list of all your services and let your app users book them.

---

### Adding an element to your mobile apps

Add an element that suits your business and customize it according to your needs. Elements are organized by category so you can quickly find the one you want.

For example, if you have a store and want to add a gallery of all your products, you can add the product gallery element. Select the element and then directly place it onto the screen where you want it displayed.

**Note:** Not all elements can be customized from the web editor. You can update those elements from the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app).

### Reordering elements on your mobile apps

Set up your screen by putting the most important elements at the top of the screen. You can adjust the order by moving them up or down on the screen as needed.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app

2. Click the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Your own mobile app**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the relevant screen.
5. Click on the element you want to reorder.
6. Choose what you want to do:

- Click the **Move** **up** icon to move the element up.
- Click the **Move down** icon move the element down.

### FAQs

Click below to learn more about managing your app elements.

### Can I add the same element to two different screens?

You can duplicate an element and then add the duplicated element to another screen through your Wix app.

**To add the same element to two screens from your Wix app:**
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing a different mobile app:

1. Tap the mobile app name at the top.
2. Tap the relevant mobile app.

**Tip: **If you want to edit a different mobile app (e.g., Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix), choose the app as [your preferred mobile app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-app-for-your-members).
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
7. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of the element.
8. Tap **Duplicate**.
9. Tap **Edit** icon   at the top right of the duplicated element.
10. Tap **Customize element**.
11. Tap the **Choose screen** drop-down under **Location **and select which screen you want the element to appear on.
12. Tap **Save** at the top right.

### I added an element to the wrong screen, how can I move it to another screen?

You can move an element through the Wix app.
**To move an element on your Wix app:**
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing a different mobile app:

1. Tap the mobile app name at the top.
2. Tap the relevant mobile app.

**Tip: **If you want to edit a different mobile app (e.g., Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix), choose the app as [your preferred mobile app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-app-for-your-members).
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
7. Tap the **Edit** icon   at the top right of the element you want to move.
8. Tap **Customize element**.
9. Tap the **Choose screen** drop-down under **Location **and select which screen you want the element to appear on.
10. Tap **Save** at the top right.

### How many elements can I add to my app?

There is no limit on the number of elements you can add, but keep in mind that too many elements can make your app cluttered and harder to navigate.

### Can I customize the look of each element?

Yes, each element has its own settings that you can customize to match your app's design and functionality requirements.

### Can I restore an element once I delete it?

No. Once an element has been deleted, you cannot get it back.
