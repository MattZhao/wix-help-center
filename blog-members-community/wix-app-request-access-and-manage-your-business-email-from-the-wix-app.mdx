---
title: "Wix App Request: Access and Manage Your Business Email from the Wix App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to manage your business email on the Wix app. You can [access your business email in your account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-accessing-your-business-email-through-your-wix-account) on desktop.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
