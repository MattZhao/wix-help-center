---
title: "Wix Blog: Searching for a Specific Blog Post"
---

Looking for a specific blog post? You can quickly find the posts you want using the search bar in your blog manager. This is a great way to save you time scrolling through other posts and categories to find the one you need.

### To search for a specific blog post:

Go to the [Blog tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Blog%20Posts&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog/) in your site's dashboard. Click the search bar at the top right. Enter a keyword.Click the post you want to view.
