You can customize and share a specific blog category on your social media. For example, if you have a Lifestyle blog, you can share your "Health and Fitness" category on your social media, so others can discover your blog and visit your site.

### Customizing your category information

Think about the text that you want to display when you share. This can help let people know what the category is about and help your site's SEO.

#### To customize your category:

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog/categories) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Edit** next to the relevant category.  Enter a category title in the** Title** field.   Add a description of your category in the **Description** field.

Click the **Category Image** box and upload an image.  Click **Edit** next to **SEO settings**.   Customize the URL slug, title tag, and meta description.

Close the SEO settings.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you use a [Post List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-displaying-different-blog-posts-on-different-site-pages) to display different categories on different pages, you can follow [these instructions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-ogimage) to upload an image for each page.

### Sharing your blog category on social media

Once you've customized the category image and display text, you're ready to share the category on your social accounts.

#### To share a blog category:

Go to your live site.  Log in using your Wix credentials, in the member login bar.  Go to the category you want to share.  Copy the category URL (e.g. https://www.sunyogablog.com/blog/categories/news).  Paste the link where you want to share it.

### Troubleshooting outdated social share information

Click an issue below to find out how to resolve it:

### In Facebook

Note that even after making updates, Facebook sometimes continues to display old information it previously saved. You can fix this by updating Facebook.
1. Go to the [Facebook Crawler tool](https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/).
2. Enter your site's web address (URL) in the **Input URL **field.
3. Click **Debug**.
4. Click **Fetch new information **or **Scrape again**.
Facebook should now display the most up-to-date information when your site is posted or shared.

### In LinkedIn

Note that even after making updates, LinkedIn sometimes continues to display old information it previously saved. You can fix this by updating LinkedIn.
1. Go to the [LinkedIn Post Inspector tool](https://www.linkedin.com/post-inspector/inspect/*).
2. Enter your site's web address (URL) in the **URL **field.
3. Click **Inspect**.
**Tip: **If you've followed the steps and the outdated info is still displayed, click the **Inspect **button a few more times.
