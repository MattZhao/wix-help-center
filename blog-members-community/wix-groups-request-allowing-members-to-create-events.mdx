---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Allowing Members to Create Events"
---

Currently, it is not possible for your site members to create events within in a group.

However, you can [allow members to create their own group](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-new-members-to-your-group) based on shared interests to form an online community.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
