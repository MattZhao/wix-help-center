title: "Wix App: An Overview"
---

The Wix app makes it simple to manage your website from your mobile device. Whether you own a gym, freelance, or run a small business, the app lets you update your site, handle customer inquiries, and manage your online store.

Encourage your members to download a [member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps) to access your site and stay connected. From there, they can book appointments, buy products, check announcements, and more, based on your business services.

---

### Customize your site on the go

With the Wix app, you can customize your site directly from your mobile device to keep it up-to-date. Add your brand colors, logo, and media to keep your content fresh and appealing.

You can also use your site's dashboard or the Wix app to refine [how your site appears on the member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-site-on-the-member-apps), ensuring a seamless experience for your members.

---

### Invite people to a seamless mobile app experience

[Invite your site members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps) to download the member app and stay connected to your business on the go. With the app, they can book appointments, shop, check announcements, and more.

As they join the app, your members can enjoy an interactive experience and build a stronger connection with you and others in your community.

---

### Optimize your business operations

Add [available apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-app-adding-apps-to-your-site) to streamline your business and stay connected with your members. Share blog posts, sell products online, create member groups for discussions, and host events with ease.

By [setting up notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-managing-inbox-notifications), you’ll always be in the loop—whether it’s a customer making a purchase, paying an invoice, or RSVPing for your event. It’s an easy way to keep your business running smoothly and your audience engaged

---

### Chat live with visitors

Engage visitors right from the start by setting up automatic greetings in your live chat. Responding instantly to site visitors' messages allows you to address their questions and needs in real time.

[With live chat](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors), send coupons to encourage repeat purchases, increase loyalty, and engage customers with your brand.

---

### Track analytics in real-time

Stay informed with real-time analytics to improve your business strategies. Keep an eye on live site stats, track your top campaigns, and review your store’s performance over time.

This data helps you make informed decisions and optimize your business for success.
