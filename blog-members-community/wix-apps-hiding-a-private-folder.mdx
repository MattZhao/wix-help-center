---
title: "Wix Apps: Hiding a Private Folder"
---

With the Wix File Share app, you can hide certain [folders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-creating-folders-in-the-file-share-app) from view so that only members with the required permissions can view them. You can always unhide the folder later if you need to.

> **Note:**
>
> Hiding a folder from view requires changing the permissions so that it's no longer a public folder, visible to all your members.

### To hide a private folder:

Go to [File Share](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp%2F1537b24e-29d1-6d8f-b8e1-d6860f2f70b9%3F&primaryButtonText=Manage+Files) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon** ** next to the relevant folder.   Click **Permissions**.   Click the drop-down next to **All members** and select **Remove**.  Select the checkbox at the bottom of the pop-up to hide the folder.  Set up the new permissions for this folder:

1. Use the search bar to find a specific member, role or pricing plan.
2. Choose their permission level from the drop-down: **Can view**, **Can upload** or **Can manage**.
3. Select or deselect the checkbox next to **Notify members**.
4. Click **Add** / **Add & Notify**.
    Click **Save**.
