### Uploading images to the 360 Images app

Upload and enable 360 images to auto rotate on your site. Add directional controls to assist site visitors when they interact with the image.

#### To upload images to the app:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26) and click the app.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Images** tab.   Do one of the following:

- Upload a 360° image.
- Select one of the app's images.
    Click the **Settings** tab.  Choose a rotation starting point (from 0 to 360 degrees).  Click the **Auto rotate image** toggle:

- **Enabled: **Image rotates automatically. Site visitors can click to pause the rotation.
- **Disabled: **Image can be rotated manually by site visitors.
    Click the **Show navigation controls** toggle:

- **Enabled: **Directional controls and zoom controls appear on the image.
- **Disabled: **No controls appear on the image.

### What file type should I use for my VR view images?

VR view images can be stored as .png, .jpeg, or .gif files. We recommend using .jpeg since it allows for better compression.

### What are the recommended image dimensions?

For optimal compatibility and performance, image dimensions should be in powers of two (e.g., 2048 or 4096).The aspect ratio for mono images (also known as 'standard') should be 2:1 (e.g. 4096 x 2048).The aspect ratio for stereo images (also known as 'Stereoscopic' i) should be 1:1 (e.g. 4096 x 4096).

### How can I improve the quality of my image displayed on Google Chrome?

Update to the latest version of Chrome for the best viewing experience.
