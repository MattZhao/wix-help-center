title: "Spaces App Request: Adding an Audio Player Element"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add audio content to the Spaces app from your Mobile App Editor, the Wix app, or the Wix Studio app.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
