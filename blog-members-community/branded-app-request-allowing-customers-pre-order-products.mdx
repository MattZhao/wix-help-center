---
title: "Branded App Request: Allowing Customers Pre-Order Products"
---

Currently in the [Branded App](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview), your app members cannot pre-order a product or service. They can only pay for a product once it is available for purchase in your online store.

Giving your app users the ability to pre-order products would allow you to generate excitement about an upcoming launch, gauge the amount of interest your community has in a product, and better understand inventory needs.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
