---
title: "Wix Portfolio: Managing Your Integrations"
---

After [connecting your portfolio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-portfolio-connecting-integrations) to Instagram, Google Photos, DeviantArt or Dribbble, you can add and manage projects from these apps.

Sync media from these apps to your portfolio, update project details and remove those you no longer want to showcase.

### To manage your portfolio integrations:

[Go to Portfolio](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/portfolio?&title=Manage%20Portfolio&buttonText=Manage+your+portfolio) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Integrations** tab on the left.  Click **Manage **next to the relevant integration.   Choose what you want to do:

### Sync projects

1. Click **Manage **next to the relevant integration.
2. Click **Sync** next to the relevant project. Your project's status appears as **Synced** once it's updated.

### Manage projects

1. Click **Manage Project** next to the project you wish to manage.

**Note:** You must sync the project to your portfolio before you can manage it.
2. Update your project details.
3. Click **Save & Close** on the top right.

[Learn more about managing your projects](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-portfolio-managing-your-wix-portfolio#managing-your-projects).

### Remove a project from your portfolio

1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant project.

**Note:** You must sync the project to your portfolio before you can remove it.
2. Click **Remove from portfolio**.
