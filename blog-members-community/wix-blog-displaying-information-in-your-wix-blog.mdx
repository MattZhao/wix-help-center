You can choose exactly what information and sharing options (date, author name, Facebook, etc.) you want to display in the different views and blog elements of your blog. For example, you may want to display fewer details in mobile view to give readers a cleaner reading experience.

**In this article, learn more about showing or hiding information:**

[In the blog or category feed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-displaying-information-in-your-wix-blog#showing-or-hiding-information-in-a-blog-or-category-feed)  [On the Post page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-displaying-information-in-your-wix-blog#showing-or-hiding-information-on-a-post-page)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-displaying-information-in-your-wix-blog#faqs)

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click your Blog page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click the **Display **tab.
5. Select or deselect the relevant checkboxes.

6. Click the Enable social sharing toggle to enable or disable it:

- **Enabled:** Social share icons for Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Shareable link icons appear on your blog.

### Showing or hiding information on a Post page

Your Post page is the dedicated blog page which opens when your reader selects a specific post. You can choose what information you want to display on this page. Any settings you apply here apply to every post in your blog.

Wix EditorWix Studio
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Post page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click **Post**.
3. Click the Post page element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Display** tab.
6. Click the checkboxes next to the information you want to display.
[image: A screenshot showing the display options available for the post page.t]
1. Click **Blog Menu**.
2. Enable / disable the **Show blog menu** toggle to show or hide the blog menu.
3. (If enabled): Select what shows on your blog menu.
4. Click **Back**.
5. Scroll down and click **Share and print**.
6. Customize your share and print options by enabling/disabling the toggles:

- **Enable share and print icons:  **Adds social share and print icons to the post, e.g. Facebook, LinkedIn and Print post. Select the checkbox next to the icon(s) you want to show on posts.
- ** Add "Save" Button to images: **A Pinterest Save button is added to your image to allow your blog readers to share the image on their Pinterest boards.
- **Share text on X/Twitter:  **A share text on X/Twitter button is added to your post to allow your blog readers to share the text on X/Twitter.
7. Click **Back**.
8. Scroll down and click **Related Posts**.
9. Click the **Show related posts** toggle to show or hide related posts.

- **Enabled: **A related posts element displays at the bottom of your post. This shows snippets of similar posts from your blog.
- **Disabled: **A related posts element does not display at the bottom of your post.
10. (Optional): Customize the related post settings.
Note:Save and [publish](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site) your site to ensure your changes appear on the live site.

### What is the difference between my blog feed and my blog Post page?

Your blog feed is your main blog page. This is where snippets of all your blog posts are displayed. Your site visitors can scroll through your collection of posts and select the ones they want to read. 
Your Blog Post page shows one single blog post. It is the page that is shown when a visitor clicks on a blog post from your blog feed. The post opens in a new page (your Post page) where they can read the full post. 
Read our article [Understanding Your Blog Pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-understanding-your-blog-pages) to find out more.

### Can I customize what is displayed on a mobile view?

The information in this article is for desktop views. If you want to customize what information is displayed to your visitors using a mobile device, read our article [Customizing Your Blog's Mobile View](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-customizing-your-blogs-mobile-view).

### Can I hide the view counters and likes on my posts?

Yes. You can choose whether you want to display or hide the counters, which indicate how many comments and likes each post has. 

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Blog page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click your Blog page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click the **Display **tab.
5. Deselect the comments counter.
6. Deselect the likes counter.

**Tip:** If you decide you want to display them again, you can enable them at any time.
