---
title: "Wix App: Showing or Hiding Elements on Different Mobile Operating Systems"
---

Show or hide elements of your site depending on what operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. Your elements are visible on all operating systems by default, but you can decide what to show on iOS, Android, or both.

> **Note:**
>
> Sections are referred to as 'Elements' in the Wix app. For example, Add Element, Customize Element etc.

### To show or hide an element:

Go to your site in the Wix app.   Tap **Manage** at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.  Tap **Edit**.    Tap the navigation tab that includes the element you want to manage.  Tap **Edit** icon next to the element.  Tap **Customize Element**.   Tap the** Visibility **drop-down under **General Settings **and select the device want to show the element on.  Tap **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you hide an element, you can show it again by tapping **Show on Android** or **Show on iOS** next to the relevant element.
