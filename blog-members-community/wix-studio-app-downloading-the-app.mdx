---

Stay in control while on the move with our brand new Wix Studio mobile app.

### To download the Studio app:

Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).  Type 'Wix Studio' in the search field.  Click **Get** or **Install** next to **Wix Studio** to download the app.  Log in with your Wix credentials.

> **You can also download the Studio app right to your mobile device using these links:**
>
> - [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/wix-studio/id6450957812)
> - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wix.studio)
