---
title: "Wix App Request: Performing Site Actions"
---

Currently, you can create, rename, edit and delete your site right from the Wix app on your mobile device. However, it is not possible to perform additional site actions such as duplicating and transferring your site from the Wix app.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can perform all [site actions from your dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/managing-your-site/site-management).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
