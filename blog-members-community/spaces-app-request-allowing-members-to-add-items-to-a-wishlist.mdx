---
title: "Spaces App Request: Allowing Members to Add Items to a Wishlist"
---

Currently, members of your site cannot add or save individual products or services to a personalized wishlist on the Spaces App.

> **Workaround:**
>
> With Wix Stores, you can [add a wishlist on your desktop site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-a-wishlist).  This lets your members keep track of their favorite things by adding them to a personal wishlist. From there, they can refer back to them at anytime to decide what they want to purchase.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
