---
title: "Wix Forum: Using Your Forum on a Mobile Device"
---

Forum management can be done on the go! Members can write and edit as normal, using their mobile phones!

To provide forum readers with an easy-to-read experience, the forum has one optimal layout for mobile.

> **Wix Owner App:**
>
> Did you know that Wix has an app? Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app) to learn how to download the Wix Owner App.

> **Tip:**
>
> - We'd recommend that you do not place a forum on your homepage.
> - Having a site with multiple pages is better than having everything on one page.

> **Note:**
>
> The category header image does not appear on mobile devices.
