---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Changing the Position of Elements on Your Blog Post Page"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the position of elements that appear on your blog post page in your Editor. For example, it is not possible to move the related posts element to the left/right of the post, or place it above the text.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
