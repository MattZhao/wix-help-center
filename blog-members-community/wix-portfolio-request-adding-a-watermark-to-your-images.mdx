---
title: "Wix Portfolio Request: Adding a Watermark to Your Images"
---

Currently, you cannot add a watermark to your uploaded images in the Wix Portfolio in order to protect the copyright.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
