---
title: "Wix Blog: Duplicating a Blog Post"
---

Duplicating a post allows you to safely work on one version without affecting another. It is a great way to utilize an existing layout or template without having to start from scratch.

### To duplicate a blog post:

Go to the [Blog tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fblog) on your site dashboard. Click the **Show More **iconon the published post or draft you want to duplicate.Click **Duplicate Post**.

> **Notes:**
>
> - After duplication, a draft (unpublished) copy of the original post opens. You can edit the post and publish it when you are ready.
> - Anyone with admin or editor privileges can duplicate any post. Contributing writers can duplicate their own posts.
