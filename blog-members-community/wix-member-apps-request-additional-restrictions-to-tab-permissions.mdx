---
title: "Wix Member Apps Request: Additional Restrictions to Tab Permissions"
---

Currently, it is only possible to restrict member access to specific screens in the Wix Member Apps using pricing plans and member badges. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-setting-viewing-permissions-for-your-members). Further customization is not currently possible.

> **Note:**
>
> We have open feature requests for [Assign Tab Permissions Based on Member Roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-request-assigning-screen-permissions-based-on-member-roles) and [Allowing Multiple Permissions Per Tab](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-request-allowing-multiple-permissions-per-screen).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
