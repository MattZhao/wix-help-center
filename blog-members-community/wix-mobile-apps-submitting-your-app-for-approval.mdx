title: "Wix Mobile Apps: Submitting Your App for Approval"
---

Once you register your Apple and Google Developer accounts and add your store listing information, the final step is to submit your app for approval. When you submit your app, Wix conducts an internal review. After Wix approves your app, we forward it to Apple and Google for their review. Once they approve it, your app can go live in their stores.

> **Google's Requirement:**
>
> As part of their submission process, Google requires an additional 14-day closed testing review of your app after Wix submits it.

**In this article, learn more about: **

### Submitting your app

Complete all steps in your mobile app dashboard before you submit your app for review. All branding and design of your app must be finalized before you submit. If you make changes after submitting your app, you may be required to resubmit it again.

#### To submit your app:

[Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your dashboard.  Click the tab with the name of your own mobile app.   Follow the 6 steps in the **Let's set up your app to sub****mit it ****and get it live in stores **section.  Click **Submit** to submit your app to Wix for approval. Once you submit, you can't make changes until the review process is complete.

**Note: **If you are unable to click **Submit**, it means you haven't completed all the setup steps.

Click **Submit** in the pop-up window to confirm.

> **Important:**
>
> Once Google initially approves your app, you need to complete additional steps before resubmitting your app for approval. When your app is ready to be resubmitted, a notification will pop up on your dashboard.
> 1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
> 2. Click the tab with the name of your own mobile app.
> 3. Click the **Fix issue** drop-down and follow the steps for Google's quality assessment, answering questions about different aspects of your app: the closed test, app details, and production readiness.
>
> **Note: **Wix provides the answers for you to copy.
> 4. Then click **Resubmit**.

### Viewing your submission status

You can track your submission status in the **Mobile App Overview** tab of your [Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app)  dashboard. The labels beneath the App Store and Google Play Store show the progress of your submission. When your app is available, the status changes from **In Review** to **Approved**. Once the app is approved, you can see the version number and click** See in store** to view it in the relevant store.

---

### Resubmitting your app if it's rejected

If your app has been rejected by either Apple's App Store or the Google Play Store, a red **Action Needed** label appears beneath the relevant app store. Follow the steps to resubmit for approval. Depending on why your app was rejected, you will see different steps to fix the issue.

#### To resubmit your app:

Click the first **Fix Issue** drop-down under** Steps to re-submit your app**.  Follow the instructions to fix the first issue. Once you've completed the instruction click **Mark as Done.**  Repeat Steps 1-2 for each issue listed.

If one of the steps is titled 'Comments from the App Reviewer,' it means Wix has received comments from the relevant store that rejected your app. Click the drop-down to view the comments and write your answer in the text box. When you're finished click **Mark as Done**.  Once all the steps to re-submit your app are complete click **Submit** at the bottom. The new version of the app will be submitted again for review.  Click **Submit** in the pop-up window to confirm.

**Note: **You can track the status from your [Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) dashboard.

> **For Google Play Store:**
>
> Once Wix approves your app, the final step is filling out a Google quality assessment in your Mobile App dashboard. Follow the steps in the **Fix Issue** drop-down to resubmit your app.

### FAQs

Click below for answers to common questions about submitting your app for approval.

### What happens after I submit my app?

1. We will review your application to make sure it follows Apple and Google's compliance guidelines. If we see something that needs changing, we'll notify you within 7 business days.

**Note: **Once Wix approves your application, Google requires you to resubmit the app for a closed testing process.
2. Once your app passes our internal review, we submit it to Apple and Google on your behalf. Once Apple and Google approve the app, it will be released to app stores. The approval process can take up to 3 days.

**Note:** Your app is subject to Apple and Google's approval. Wix has no control over whether it will be approved or rejected by Apple and Google.

### How long does it take for Apple and Google to approve my app?

The approval process varies from platform to platform and Wix has no control over the timeline.
- Learn more about [Apple's approval process](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/).
- Learn more about [Google's approval process](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859751?hl=en&ref_topic=7072031).

**Note: **Google requires an additional 14-day closed testing period using at least twenty opted-in testers.

### What happens if I get emails from Apple and Google?

As part of the Mobile App Premium plan, Wix takes care of your app's submission to the stores as well as the app's maintenance and compliance with any new policies. During this process you may receive emails from Apple and Google. These emails require no action from you.
Learn more about the [emails you receive from Apple and Google](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-explaining-the-emails-you-receive-from-apple-and-google).

### What if my app is not approved by Apple and/or Google?

If your application does not pass Apple or Google's review, Wix gets notified. We'll then inform you why your app has not been approved and ask you to make the necessary changes.
Once you've made the changes, you'll have to resubmit your app to Wix for approval, and then again to Apple and Google.

### How do app updates work once my app is live in the app stores?

Once your app is live, we make sure it stays current by updating it when necessary.
