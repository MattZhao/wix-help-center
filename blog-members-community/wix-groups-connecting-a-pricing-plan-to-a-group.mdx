---
title: "Wix Groups: Connecting a Pricing Plan to a Group"
---

Choose who has access to any group by connecting it to a pricing plan. This means that only those members who have purchased a specific pricing plan can access a specific group.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure you have added Wix Groups and created at least one group.

---

### Step 1 | Add and set up Wix Pricing Plans

Once you've added Wix Groups and created a group, you can add Wix Pricing Plans to your site and create your plans.

> **Note:**
>
> To sell plans on your site, you must [connect a payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-setting-up-payments). Wix offers a variety of providers for you to choose from.

#### To add and set up Wix Pricing Plans:

[Go to Pricing Plans](https://www.wix.com/app-market/paid-plans) in the Wix App Market.  Click **Add to Site**.

Set up and [create your plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-an-overview#create-plans-to-suit-your-business).

> **Tip:**
>
> Wix Pricing Plans also integrates with other apps including Wix Bookings, Wix Blog, and Wix Video. A plan can cover multiple products, for example, if you have a yoga site, a plan can include yoga classes with Wix Bookings, yoga videos from Wix Video, and access to yoga discussions and material in Wix Groups .

---

### Step 2 | Connect a pricing plan to a group

Next, it's time to connect a pricing plan to your group. During this process you can either create a new pricing plan, or connect an existing pricing plan.

#### To connect a plan to a group:

[Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant group.  Scroll down to **Privacy**.  Select **Monetization**.  Click** Connect a Pricing Plan**.

Choose what you want to do: 

- **Connect an existing plan:** Click **Connect** next to the relevant plan.
- **Create a new plan:** Click **Create New Plan** and set up your new plan.

### Tell me how to create a new plan

1. Enter your plan details:

- **Plan Name:** Enter name of your plan. e.g., VIP, Gold, Silver etc.
- **Tagline:** Add some info to encourage visitors to purchase the plan.
- **List what is included in the plan:** Add the main features of the plan.
2. (Optional) Click **Add more** to add more fields.
3. Scroll down to **Pricing and Duration** and choose how you want to price the plan:

- **Free Plan:** The plan is free of charge for a specific duration.
- **One-time payment: **Visitors provide a once off payment for a specific duration.
- **Reoccurring payments: **Visitors pay a reoccurring fee either weekly, monthly or yearly.
4. Customize the **Advanced Settings**:

- Click the **Single Purchase Only** toggle to allow your vsiitors to purchase the plan once or multiple times.
- Click the **Allow Plan Cancellation** toggle to enable your vsiitors to cancel the plan at any time or not.
- Click the **Custom Start Date** toggle:

- **Enabled:** Visitors can choose when they want the plan to start at the checkout.
- **Disabled:** The plan starts from the time the visitor completes the purchase.
- (Optional) Click **Add** next to **Plan Policies** to add any specific policy requirements to the plan and add your requirements in the text box and click **Save**.
5. Click **Create**.

7.  Click **Done**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you connect an existing plan that already has subscribers, those subscribers will automatically be added to the group.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about pricing plans and groups.

### Can I connect multiple pricing plans to a group?

Yes, you can connect more than one pricing plan to a group.

### How do I make changes to my pricing plans?

You can make changes to your plans from [Pricing Plans](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Pricing%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/membership/) in your site's dashboard at any time.

### Can I connect a plan to a group from Pricing Plans in my dashboard?

Yes, you can also connect a pricing plan to your groups via [Pricing Plans](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Pricing%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/membership/) in your site's dashboard. Select a plan and scroll down to **Connect and manage benefits **and select the** Groups** checkbox. Then, click the **+ Add Group** field and choose a group to connect.
