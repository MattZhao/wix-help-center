---
title: "Request: Emoji Options for Reacting to Shared Gallery Posts"
---

Currently, it is not possible to react to Shared Gallery posts with emojis. However, you can react to posts by clicking the Like icon .

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
