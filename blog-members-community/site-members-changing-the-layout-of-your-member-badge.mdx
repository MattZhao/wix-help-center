---
title: "Site Members: Changing the Layout of Your Member Badge"
---

Once you have created and set up your member badge, you can change the layout of it inside your Editor.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To use member badges, you must install a [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) on your site.

### To change the layout your member badge:

Click the Member Profile Card in your Editor.Click **Settings**.

Click **Badges**.

Customize the badge layout:
- **Choose a layout: **Choose if you want to show the name and icon, or only the or only the icon or the name.
- **Badge Size: **Choose if you want the badge to be small or large.
- **Corner Radius:** Drag the slider to increase or decrease the curve of the corners.
- **Text:** Click the drop-down menu and select the font for the badge.
