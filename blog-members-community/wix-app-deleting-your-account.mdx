### Deleting your account

Once you delete your account, you can no longer use any of your Wix services. Your account and all its data will be removed permanently.

> **Important:**
>
> Wix will not be able to restore your account or retrieve your data in the future.

#### To delete your account:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap your profile icon at the top.  Tap **Manage Account Settings**.  Tap **Delete Your Account**.   Tap **Continue **to follow the steps on how to delete your account.

### FAQs

Click the questions below to learn more.

### Why might I want to delete my account?

Sites can contain a lot of personal data, identity details, credit card numbers, and more. In some cases, you may not use these accounts any longer. With privacy in mind, it is a good idea to remove private data from services that are no longer used.

### How long will it take for my account to be removed after I send a request to delete it?

Your account will be deleted by Wix Customer Care experts up to 7 days from the request.

### What if I dont have any sites in my account?

If you don't have a website and choose to remove your account, your Wix account will be removed. You will not be able to log in to the Wix apps with that account any longer.

### How do my members delete their account?

[Members can request that their account is deleted](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-permanently-deleting-a-members-data). This fully removes them from the member apps and means they can no longer visit sites from the different apps.

### Can I delete a specific site only?

Yes, you can move a site to the trash from the Wix app if you no longer want it. [Learn how to delete a site.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-deleting-or-restoring-a-site#deleting-a-site)
