---
title: "Wix Blog: Managing Your Blog in the Wix App"
---

With the Wix app, it's easy to manage your blog on the go. You can write and edit posts, leave comments, and more. It's a great platform to promote your content and grow your community.

### To manage your blog:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Blog**.   Choose what you want to do:

### Create a new blog post

[Create a new blog post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-creating-and-editing-blog-posts-in-the-wix-app) on the go using the Wix app.

### Edit an existing blog post

[Edit a new blog post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-creating-and-editing-blog-posts-in-the-wix-app) on the go using the Wix app.

### Publish a draft

1. Tap **Posts**.
2. Tap the **Drafts **tab.
3. Tap the draft you want to edit.
4. Make your changes.
5. Tap **Next**.
6. Tap **Save and publish**.

### Create a category

1. Tap **Categories**.
2. Tap  **Create Category**.
3. Enter the category name
4. Enter the category description.
5. Tap** Add Category Image**.
6. Upload the relevant image.
7. Tap **Save**.

### View analytics

Here you can view stats on your post views, shares, most viewed posts etc.
1. Tap **Analytics**.
2. Tap the relevant day range at the top of the screen.

**Tip: **View reports of your **Blog activity** and **Top blog posts** to see how your blog is performing over time.

### Manage your page settings

Here you can update post, category and search settings.
1. Tap **Page Settings**.
2. Customize the post page settings: Select the checkbox next to the relevant option.
3. Tap **Category**.
4. Customize the category settings:

- **Page header:** Choose what appears on your category page header.
- **Post Layout on Feed:** Choose a layout for your posts on your category page.
- **Info Shown on Feed**: Tap **Edit** to choose what info is shown on your feed.
5. Tap the **Show featured posts carousel **toggle to enable or disable featured posts.
6. Tap **Search**.
7. Customize the search settings by enabling or disabling the toggles:

- **Posts:** Readers can search for posts. 

- (If enabled) Tap **Edit** to choose what info shows on your posts.
- **Categories: **Readers can search for categories.
- **Tags: **Readers can search for tags.
- **Writers: **Readers can search for specific writers.

### Delete a blog post

1. Tap **Posts**.
2. Tap **More Actions** icon** **** **next to the post you want to delete.
3. Tap **Move to Trash**.
4. Tap **Move** to confirm.

**Tip: **Want to restore a blog post? Tap the **Trash** tab, tap the **More Actions** icon** **** **next to the post you want to delete, and tap **Restore Post**. Restored posts appear in the **Drafts** tab.

> **Note:**
>
> If you have given permissions for other collaborators on your site, they can also create new posts and edit their content.
