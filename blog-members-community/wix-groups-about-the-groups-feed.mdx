---
title: "Wix Groups: About the Groups Feed"
---

The Groups Feed on your Wix site is a tool designed to boost communication. It helps members stay informed and enjoy the latest content and discussions. Create an engaging space for your community to thrive and foster a sense of belonging and active participation among members.

> **Ready to get started?**
>
> - If you have not already done so, [add and set up Wix Groups](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-and-setting-up-wix-groups). Some Wix templates already include Groups, making it easier for you to get started.
> - [Upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-upgrading-your-site) so people can start joining your group.

---

### Update your group members

The Groups Feed shows all [group-related posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-creating-and-managing-your-groups-posts) in one location. This makes it easy for members to stay updated and engaged with the latest content and discussions, so no important updates are missed.

---

### Create a community

By unifying all posts into a single feed, the Groups Feed increases visibility and interaction with a variety of content. This encourages [group members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-new-members-to-your-group) to actively participate and helps create a lively, engaging community atmosphere.

---

### Show all group updates

Members can view all the groups they have joined on one screen, making it easy to switch between different group feeds and stay updated on all their interests. The Groups Feed also suggests new groups based on member activity, encouraging them to expand their community involvement.

---

### Manage your groups on the go

You can manage most aspects of your group from the [Wix app](https://www.wix.com/mobile/wix-app). Group members can join and interact with your group in your site's member app (e.g. [Branded App by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview), [Spaces by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview) or [Fit by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/fit-by-wix-app-an-overview)).

---

### Search for relevant posts

The Groups Feed makes it easy to discover new and relevant content within the group. [Group posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-creating-and-managing-your-groups-posts) encourage members to explore different topics and discussions. The built in search function, post topics and pinned posts help members find the content relevant for them.

---

### Moderate your group

Group admins can [moderate the Groups Feed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-your-group) by managing posts, comments, and member interactions. This maintains a positive, productive environment and ensures the group remains a welcoming space for all members.
