---
title: "Wix Member Apps Request: Allowing Multiple Permissions Per Screen"
---

Currently, it's not possible to choose more than one permission per app screen. You can [restrict screen access](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-setting-viewing-permissions-for-your-members) to members with Pricing plans or member badges, but not both.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
