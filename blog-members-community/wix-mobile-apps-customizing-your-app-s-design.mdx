---
title: "Wix Mobile Apps: Customizing Your App's Design"
---

Customize your own native app's design to match your brand by adjusting the color theme, text theme, button theme, and header style. These updates help make your app stand out and create a visually consistent experience for your visitors while perfectly aligning with your brand.

> **Note:**
>
> Changes to your app's color and text theme affect all of your app's screens.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Change your app's color theme](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-color-theme)  [Change your app's text theme](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-text-theme)  [Change your app's button theme](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-button-theme)    [Change your app's header style](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-header-style)

### Changing your app's color theme

Choose a color theme for your app to set the tone for visitors using your app. Fine-tune individual colors to ensure consistency and enhance readability across different screens and elements.

DashboardWix AppWix Studio App
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the tab with the name of your own mobile app.
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click **Design** on the left panel.
5. Click the **Edit** icon  next to **Color theme**.
6. Choose what you want to do:

- **Change your app's color theme:** Click **Change Color Theme** to select a new preset main color theme.
- **Individually customize element colors:** Select which colors to edit:

- **Primary: **Choose a new main color for your app.
- **Text and icons: **Choose a new color for the main text and social icons.
- **Background: **Choose a new color for the app background.
7. Click **Save** at the top right.

### Changing your app's button theme

Customize your button styles to match your business design. These styles apply to all buttons across your app, ensuring a consistent look. Choose from preset themes or adjust individual elements like colors, borders, text, and corner radius. You can preview your changes live as you make them.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your dashboard.
2. Click the tab with the name of your own mobile app.
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click **Design** on the left panel.
5. Click the **Edit** icon  next to **Button theme**.
6. Choose what you want to do:

- **Choose a preset theme for your buttons:** Click **Change Button Theme** and select from the feature themes based on your [app's theme colors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-text-theme).
- **Customize buttons individually: **Click the **Primary** or **Secondary** tab and then customize the buttons by adjusting the button shape, background color, text, and border.

**Note: **To change your theme colors, click **Edit** in the **Pick a color** pop-up. Customize your primary, text, icons, and background colors in the [**Color Themes section**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-color-theme).
7. Click **Save **at the top right.
[image: A screenshot showing where to edit your app's button themes on your desktop]

2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing your own mobile app:

1. Tap the member app name at the top (e.g. Spaces by Wix App).
2. Select **Mobile App Builder**.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Design** on the bottom of your screen.
7. Tap **Button theme**.
8. Choose what you want to do:

- **Choose a preset theme for your buttons:** Tap **Change Button Theme** and select a featured theme based on your [app's theme colors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-text-theme).
- **Customize buttons individually: **Tap the **Primary** or **Secondary** tab and then customize the buttons by adjusting the button shape, background color, text, and border.

**Note:** To change your color options, go to the [**Color Themes section**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design#changing-your-apps-color-theme)** **in the **Design** panel.
9. ** **Tap** Save**.

### Changing your app's header style

When creating your own native app, you can choose if you want your header to have a background color (taken from your app's primary color) or to have it transparent.

> **Note:**
>
> The header style that you choose here affects the minimal header type. Learn more about [the difference between the header types and how to customize them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-header).

DashboardWix AppWix Studio App
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your dashboard.
2. Click the tab with the name of your own mobile app.
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click **Design** on the left panel.
5. Click the **Edit** icon  next to **Header style**.
6. Choose your header color, using either the primary or background color that you [set for your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design?tabs=Wix-Owner-App#changing-your-apps-color-theme).
7. Click **Save** at the top right.
[image: A screenshot of where to choose your header colors.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. (If needed) Switch to editing your own mobile app:

1. Tap the member app name at the top (e.g. Spaces by Wix App).
2. Select **Mobile App Builder**.
5. Tap **Edit**.
6. Tap **Design** from the bottom menu.
7. Tap **Header style**.
8. Choose what you want to edit:

- **Style: **Choose the color for your header. You can choose your primary or background color that you [set for your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design?tabs=Wix-Owner-App#changing-your-apps-color-theme).
- **Icons:** Tap the toggles to enable quick actions to your header.

**Note:** The icons displayed in the panel depend on the Wix apps you added to your site (e.g. Wix Stores).
9. Tap **Save** at the top right.
[image: A screenshot of where to choose your header colors and to where to enable the shopping cart and icon toggle.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Mobile App**.
3. (If needed) Switch to editing your branded app:

1. Tap the member app name at the top (e.g. Spaces by Wix App).
2. Select your branded mobile app.
4. Tap **Edit**.
5. Tap **Design** from the bottom menu.
6. Tap **Header style**.
7. Choose what you want to edit:

- **Style: **Choose the color for your header. You can choose your primary or background color that you [set for your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-customizing-your-apps-design?tabs=Wix-Owner-App#changing-your-apps-color-theme).
- **Icons:** Tap the toggles to enable quick actions to your header.

**Note:** The icons displayed in the panel depend on the Wix apps you added to your site (e.g. Wix Stores).
8. Tap **Save** at the top right.
