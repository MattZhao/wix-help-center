Create announcements and send them as push notifications to your members' mobile devices. Remind clients about an upcoming booking, encourage customers to check out a new sale, or give important business updates. You create and manage announcements in the Wix mobile app.

### Sending an announcement with a push notification

Go to your site in the Wix app.﻿  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile Announcements **under** Marketing**.  Tap **Create New Announcement**.  Write your announcement title and body text.  (Optional) Tap the **Add** icon  at the bottom left to include media in your announcement (an image, video, GIF or a divider).  Tap **Next**.  Preview your announcement and make sure the **Send announcement as a push notification to all members **toggle is enabled.  Click **Publish**.

> **What's next?**
>
> Your Member App's members will receive the announcement on their device if they have enabled notifications for your app.

### FAQs

### Where can my members view my announcements?

The push notification content appears in your branded app if you've added an **Announcements** tab or section. The content remains until you send a new announcement.

### How do I check which members have seen my announcement?

Check which members have viewed the announcement by tapping the announcement in the **Mobile Announcements** section of the Wix app.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile Announcements **under** Marketing**.
4. Tap the existing announcement to see who has viewed it.

### Is it possible to delete an announcement after I've sent it?

You cannot undo sending the push notification, but you can remove the announcement from the content of your branded app. To remove an announcement, either send a new announcement or remove the Announcements section altogether.
Learn how to [delete a section from your branded app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#managing-your-sections).

### Why can't my members see push notifications?

If your members aren't receiving push notifications they should check their device's settings. They must enable push notifications for your branded app in the general notification settings of their mobile device.
