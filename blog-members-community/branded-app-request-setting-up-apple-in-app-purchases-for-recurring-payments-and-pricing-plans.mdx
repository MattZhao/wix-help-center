---
title: "Branded App Request: Setting Up Apple In-App Purchases for Recurring Payments and Pricing Plans"
---

Currently, customers can only make [single payment in-app purchases](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-setting-up-apple-in-app-purchase) on your branded app in the Apple Store. Recurring payments and pricing plans are not yet supported on your app for the Apple Store.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
