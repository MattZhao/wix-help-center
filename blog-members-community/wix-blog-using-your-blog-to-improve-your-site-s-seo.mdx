Adding a blog to your site is a great way to boost your site's Search Engine Optimization (SEO). Search engines reward sites with good content and ones that update often. A blog allows you to easily add fresh content to your site on a regular basis.

You can optimize the SEO settings and content of your blog pages to improve your site's performance in search results.

### Using keywords and search phrases to rank higher

Any word or phrase typed into a search engine is a search phrase. To increase traffic to your site, it is good practice to target a few relevant search phrases - also referred to as keywords.

For example, if you run a yoga studio in Toronto, the phrase "yoga classes Toronto" may be one of your search phrases.

Learn more about [how to select keywords and what to do with them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-keywords-on-your-sites-pages).

### Adding structured data markup to improve your search results

[Structured data markup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-structured-data-markup-to-your-sites-pages-2546962) gives search engines detailed information about your page. Adding structured data markup to your site's page can help improve your website's search results and ranking.

Your Wix Blog posts already come with [preset markup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-preset-markups-for-your-sites-pages) when you create them. You can customize your structured data markup for these pages in your site's SEO Settings. [Learn more about structured data](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929).

### Gaining backlinks to boost your SEO

Links from other sites directed at your site are called backlinks. Gaining backlinks is a great way to boost your site's SEO, since search engines view links from popular and reputable sites as a sign that your site is also reputable.

 

Learn more about [how to gain backlinks](https://support.wix.com/en/article/gaining-backlinks-for-your-site).

### Adding alt text to images to improve ranking

Search engines cannot "read" images. By adding alt text to an image, you are "telling" Google and other search engines what is in your image, making it more likely to appear in an image search.

Adding alt text to images and GIFs helps drive additional traffic to your site.

#### To add alt text to an image:

[Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant blog post, or click **+** **Create New Post** to write a new one.  Add a new image or edit an existing one:

1. **Add new image:**

1. Click **Add ** on the left.
2. Click **Image**.
3. Add the image to the post.
4. Click the image in the post.
2. **Edit existing image:**

1. Click the relevant image in your post.
    Click the **Settings **icon  on the toolbar.   Enter an alt text description.  Click **Save**.
