---
title: "Site Members: Resetting Your Members' Passwords"
---

As a site owner, you can reset your members' passwords when needed. Your members can then create a new password the next time they log in to your site.

Once you reset a member's password, they won't be able to log in again until they set a new password.

> **Important:**
>
> Members don't receive a notification or password reset email when you reset their password. Instead, they'll see a message on the login screen telling them their password has been reset. They'll need to click the **Forgot password?** link to set a new password.

**In this article, learn more about resetting a member's password:**

[From your Site Members dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-resetting-your-members-passwords#resetting-a-members-password-from-site-members)  [From your Contacts dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-resetting-your-members-passwords#resetting-a-members-password-from-contacts)  [Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-resetting-your-members-passwords#faqs)

### Resetting a member's password from Site Members

You can reset a site member's password from the list on your Site Members dashboard.

#### To reset a password from the site members list:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant member.  Click **Reset password**.  Click **Reset Password **in the pop-up to confirm.

---

### Resetting a member's password from Contacts

From your Contacts dashboard, you can bring up a site member's contact card to reset their password. The contact card opens in a panel on the right after selecting the member from the list.

#### To reset a password from the list of contacts:

[Go to Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant site member on the list to open their contact card in a panel on the right.   (In the contact card) Click **Manage Member** next to **Member Info**.  Click **Reset password**.  Click **Reset Password **in the pop-up to confirm.

### FAQs

Click the questions below for answers to common questions about resetting member passwords.

### Can my members reset their own passwords?

Your site members can rest their own passwords by clicking on the **Forgot password?** link at the bottom of the Members Area login screen.

### Can I reset all my members' passwords at once?

You can only reset member passwords one at a time. They cannot be reset in bulk.

### Can my site collaborators also reset passwords?

Collaborators who manage site members can also reset member passwords.

### Can I reset the passwords of my collaborators or other owners?

It is not possible to use this method to reset a password for a site member who is also a collaborator or owner. This is because collaborators use their Wix account credentials to log in to the Members Area. Collaborators and owners can [reset their Wix account password](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unable-to-sign-in-to-your-wix-account-forgot-password) instead.

### Why is my site member not receiving a password reset email?

Members don't receive a password reset email when you reset their password. After resetting their password, they'll see a message on the login screen the next time they try to log in. The message lets them know their password has been reset and that they should click the **Forgot password?** link to set a new password.
