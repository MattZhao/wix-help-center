title: "Wix Blog: Managing Your Blog on a Mobile Device"
---

There are now two ways to write and edit posts, publish drafts, add images, and more. You can choose to use the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview), or just log in to your site via your mobile browser.

This article shows you how to manage your blog by accessing your site on a mobile device. But, why not get familiar with the Wix app when you can do so much with it? Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app) to learn how to download the Wix app.

---

### From your mobile device you can:

### Write posts on a mobile device

You, or anyone else with [writing privileges](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog) can tap the **Create a Post **button on your site - accessed from a mobile device.

**Note: **Only those with writing privileges can see the **Create a Post  **button.

**To write a post on a mobile device:**
1. Go to your blog on a mobile device.
2. Log in using the email associated with your Wix account.
3. Tap **Create a Post**.
4. Write your post title and content.
5. Tap **Save & Continue**.
6. Do any of the following:

- **Choose Category:**

1. Tap to select up to three categories.
2. Tap **Done**.
- Tap** Save as Draft**.
- Tap** Save & Publish.**
**Note:** For information on supported devices and mobile troubleshooting, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-troubleshooting-mobile-live-site-issues).

### Make changes to a published post

You can make changes to a published post by tapping the **Show More** icon  at the bottom of a post.

From there you can feature the post on your homepage, manage commenting, delete the post, or tap **Edit Post** to make changes and edit categories.

### Access your drafts

To access your drafts: 
1. Tap on your profile image at the top of the page and select **My Drafts**.
2. Tap the **Show More ** icon  next to the relevant draft and select **Edit Post**.

### Manage your writers

To change [writing permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog), [block a member, or delete a member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-blocking-or-deleting-site-members), you need to go to the **Members **Area of your blog.  Tap the **Show More** icon  next to the relevant member.

### Change the size of your blog post text

To change the size of your text:
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Blog page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click the **Switch to Mobile** icon on the top bar of your editor.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to switch to mobile view on your editor.]
1. Click the Wix Blog app in your mobile Editor.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Design **tab.
4. Drag any of the sliders to increase or decrease the font sizes.

> **Tip:**
>
> Blog readers can share posts from their mobile phones. Blog readers can also like posts and, after logging in, can comment on posts.
