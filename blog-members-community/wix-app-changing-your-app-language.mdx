---
title: "Wix App: Changing Your App Language"
---

Change the language of your Wix app so that you can navigate it in your preferred language.

### To change the app language:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap your profile icon at the top.  Tap **App language**.  Tap the relevant language.  Tap **Save** at the top right.  Tap **Restart** for the changes to take effect.
