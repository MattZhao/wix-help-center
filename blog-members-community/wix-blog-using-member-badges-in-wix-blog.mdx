---
title: "Wix Blog: Using Member Badges in Wix Blog"
---

Create member badges to help blog members understand who's who. Badges can be especially helpful if you have multiple writers contributing to your blog.

Create any badges your blog needs and design them to suit the look and feel of your site.

The badges appear on the [Members page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-about-the-forum-members-page) on profile cards. Then, when a site visitor clicks to view a member's profile, they can see the badge there as well, including the badge description - if you added one (e.g. Contributed over 100 posts).

To create your first badge, go to the [Site Member tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members) in your dashboard and click **More Actions **at the top right. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-and-setting-up-a-member-badge)
