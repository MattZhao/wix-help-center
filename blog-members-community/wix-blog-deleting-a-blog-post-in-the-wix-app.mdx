---
title: "Wix Blog: Deleting a Blog Post in the Wix App"
---

Need to delete a blog post? Use the Wix app to delete a blog post from anywhere, using your phone. Once a blog post is moved to Trash, its views, likes, and comments are deleted permanently.

### To delete a blog post:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap the **Manage **icon .  Tap **Blog** under** Content & Community**.  Tap **Posts**.  Tap **More Actions** icon** **** **next to the post you want to delete.  Tap **Move to Trash**.  Tap **Move** to confirm.

> **Want to restore a blog post?**
>
> - Tap the **Trash** tab, tap the **More Actions** icon** **** **next to the post you want to delete, and tap **Restore Post**. Restored posts appear in the **Drafts** tab.
