---
title: "Wix Portfolio Request: Uploading Additional Media Types to Your Portfolio"
---

Currently, in Wix Portfolio you cannot upload media types other than images and videos. Some examples of additional media include text files like PDFs, word documents, and music files. Learn more about [uploading media to your portfolio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-portfolio-adding-and-setting-up-wix-portfolio#step-2-create-projects).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
