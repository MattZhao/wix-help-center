---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Increasing the Amount of Categories Visible in the Dashboard"
---

Currently, it is not possible to increase the amount of categories visible in the dashboard.

> **Note:**
>
> A maximum of 100 categories can be created / seen in your dashboard. 
> A maximum of 50 categories can be created / seen in the Editor.

Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-creating-and-managing-blog-categories) to learn how to create categories in your Wix Blog.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
