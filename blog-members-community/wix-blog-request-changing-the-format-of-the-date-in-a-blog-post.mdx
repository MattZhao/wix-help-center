---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Changing the Format of the Date in a Blog Post"
---

Currently, the date on blog posts is formatted as [MMMDD]. For example, if you write a post on October 29, 2021, it is displayed as **Oct 29** on the post. You cannot change this format.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
