---

With the Wix app, you can turn site visitors into members by building a community around your business. From the app, you can design what your site looks like and customize the features to engage customers.

You can also add a variety of apps to help manage your business and communicate with your members on the members app. Learn how the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview) can help you create, manage and grow your business on the go.

### To download the Wix app:

Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).  Type 'Wix' in the search field.  Click **Get** or **Install** next to **Wix - Website Builder** to download the app.

> **You can also download the Wix app right to your phone using these links:**
>
> - [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wix-owner-websites-apps/id1545924344)
> - [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wix.admin&hl=en&gl=US)
