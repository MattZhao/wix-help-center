---
title: "Wix Portfolio Request: Lightroom Integration"
---

Currently, it is not possible to integrate [Adobe Lightroom](https://lightroom.adobe.com/) with Wix Portfolio. An integration with Lightroom and Wix Portfolio would allow you to upload photos directly from Lightroom to your portfolio.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
