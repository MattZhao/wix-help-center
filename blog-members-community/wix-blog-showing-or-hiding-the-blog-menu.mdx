---
title: "Wix Blog: Showing or Hiding the Blog Menu"
---

Wix blog comes with a menu to help your readers navigate to different blog categories. The menu appears on your blog feed and your post page. You can customize each place separately.

**Learn more about displaying the blog menu:**

[In a blog or category feed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-showing-or-hiding-the-blog-menu#displaying-the-blog-menu-in-your-blog-or-category-feed)  [On the post page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-showing-or-hiding-the-blog-menu#displaying-the-blog-menu-on-your-post-page)  [When blog is viewed on mobile devices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-showing-or-hiding-the-blog-menu#displaying-the-blog-menu-on-mobile)

---

### Displaying the blog menu in your Blog or Category Feed

Choose to show or hide the blog menu in your Blog Feed.

#### To show or hide the blog menu in your blog or category feeds:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Go to your Blog page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click your Blog page.
    Click **Settings**.  Click the **Display **tab.  Click **Blog Menu**.  Click the **Show blog menu** toggle to show or hide the menu.

---

### Displaying the blog menu on your Post page

Choose to show or hide the blog menu on your Blog Post page.

#### To show or hide the blog menu in your post page:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Go to your Post page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click **Post**.
    Click the Post Page element on your page.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Display** tab.  Click **Blog Menu**.  Click the **Show blog menu** toggle to show or hide the menu.

---

### Displaying the blog menu on mobile

Choose to show or hide the blog menu on mobile. There are two pages to consider here, your blog feed and your post page.

#### To show or hide the blog menu in mobile view:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Go to your Blog page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
    Click the **Switch to Mobile** icon on the top bar of the editor.  Click the blog in the mobile editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Display**.  Enable the **Enable mobile settings** toggle.  Click **Blog Menu**.  Click the **Show blog menu** toggle to show or hide the menu in your blog feed on mobile.  Click** Post Page **at the bottom of the Settings panel.

Click the mobile Post page in your editor.  Click the **Settings **tab.  Click **Display**.   Enable the **Enable mobile settings** toggle.  Click **Blog Menu**.   Click the **Show blog menu** toggle to show or hide the menu in the post page on mobile.
