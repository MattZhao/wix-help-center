---
title: "Wix Mobile Apps: Customizing Your App's System Screens (Launch and Login)"
---

Customize your own app's launch and login screens by picking the perfect colors, text, and image for your brand. Your launch screen is a brief introductory screen that appears as the app loads. Your login screen is where members sign up or log in to your app.

> **Note:**
>
> This feature is only available if you are building your own app, and you must purchase a [Mobile App Premium plan.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-purchasing-a-branded-app-plan)

### To customize your app's system screens:

[Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your dashboard.  Click the **Your own mobile app** tab or click the tab with the name of your own mobile app.   Click **Edit Mobile App**.   Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.

### Launch screen

1. Click **Launch**.
2. Choose what you want to edit:

- **Color:** Choose a color for your launch screen background and button text.
- **Image:** Choose an [image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-adding-and-setting-up-media-in-your-app#adding-and-setting-up-an-image-on-your-app) or use your app icon.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Login screen

1. Click **Login**.
2. Choose what you want to edit:

- **Screen text: **Choose a title, tagline, and button text on your login screen.
- **Colors: **Edit the colors of your login screen background and text.
- **Image: **Choose an [image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-adding-and-setting-up-media-in-your-app#adding-and-setting-up-an-image-on-your-app) or use your app icon.
3. Tap **Save**.
