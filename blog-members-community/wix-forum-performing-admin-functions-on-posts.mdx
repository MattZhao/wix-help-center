---
title: "Wix Forum: Performing Admin Functions on Posts"
---

As admin of your forum, you can perform certain functions that regular forum members cannot do.

> **Important:**
>
> To use the forum as an admin, you must log into the forum using the email address associated with your Wix account.

### To perform admin functions on a post:

On your live site, log into the forum using the email address associated with your Wix account.Click the **Show More** iconon a post.    Do any of the following:
1. **Follow a post **(open to non-admins):
1. Click **Follow Post **to get updates on a post's comments and likes or click **Unfollow Post** to stop receiving updates.
2. **Edit a post **(open to non-admins):
1. Click **Edit Post **to make changes to a post you wrote.** **
2. Edit the post.
3. Click **Update**.
3. **Share a post **(open to non-admins):
1. Click **Share Post**.
2. Select a share option.
4. **Pin a post**:
1. Click **Pin Post **to keep the post at the top of a category list or click **Unpin Post** to place the post in chronological order.
5. **Close a post**:
1. Click **Close Post** to disable answers and comments on this post.
2. Click **Open Post** to enable answers and comments on this post.
6. **Move a post**:
1. Click **Move Post** to move this post to a different category.
2. Choose the category you wish to move it to.
3. Click **Move Post**.
7. **Delete a post:**
1. Click **Delete Post**.
2. Click **Delete** to confirm.
