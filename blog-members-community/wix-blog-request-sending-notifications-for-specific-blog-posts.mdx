---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Sending Notifications for Specific Blog Posts"
---

Currently, every time you publish your blog posts, your subscribers/members are notified. It is not possible to choose if you want to send a notification for all newly published posts or just specific ones.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
