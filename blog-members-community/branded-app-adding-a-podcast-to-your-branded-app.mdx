---
title: "Branded App: Adding a Podcast to Your Branded App"
---

Hosting a podcast on your Branded App can help you reach new audiences and attract repeat visitors. Podcasts are pulled in to your site using an RSS (Really Simple Syndication); a web feed that contains all the information needed to play your podcast.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Go to the settings section of your podcast hosting platform (for example, [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasters.apple.com/support/837-change-the-rss-feed-url), [Google Podcasts](https://support.google.com/podcast-publishers/answer/9889544?hl=en), [Spotify](https://support.spotifyforpodcasters.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043988572-Updating-an-RSS-feed-link-or-hosting-provider), [SoundCloud](https://help.soundcloud.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003453807-Getting-started-with-podcasting) etc.) and copy the link RSS.

### To embed your podcast:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.   Tap **Edit** under **Mobile App**.   Tap the navigation tab where you want to add your podcast.

**Tip: **To add a new tab for the podcast, tap **Screens **at the bottom and **Add New Screen**.  Tap **+ Add Element**.   Scroll through the elements menu and tap **Podcasts**.  Tap the Podcasts element.  Enter the name of your podcast under **Element Title**.   Paste your RSS link under **Link to podcast**.   Tap **Add**.   (Optional) Tap **Element Visibility** to only show your podcast on iOS or Android devices.
