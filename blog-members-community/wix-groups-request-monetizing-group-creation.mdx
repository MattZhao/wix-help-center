title: "Wix Groups Request: Monetizing Group Creation"
---

Currently, it is not possible to allow your site members to create their own groups for a fee. This would provide you with an additional revenue stream while encouraging the creation of diverse and active community groups.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
