---
title: "Site Members Request: Enabling 2-Step Verification for Your Site Members"
---

Currently, it is not possible to enable 2-step verification for your site members. 2-Step verification adds an extra layer of verification to the login process, and would help your site members prevent someone accessing their account without their knowledge.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can use [Velo by Wix's integration with Twilio](https://www.wix.com/velo/reference/velo-package-readmes/wix-members:-2-factor-authentication-twilio-integration) to provide 2-step verification for your site members.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
