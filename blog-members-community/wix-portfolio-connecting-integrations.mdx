Connect apps and sync media to your portfolio to showcase all your work in one place. Once you connect an app, you can view its media and sync it as brand-new projects to your portfolio.

### Connecting Instagram to your portfolio

Once you log in to your Instagram account and connect to your portfolio, you can create a new portfolio project from an Instagram hashtag. All your Instagram images with that hashtag will be synced to the new project.

#### To connect Instagram to your portfolio:

[Go to Portfolio](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/portfolio?&title=Manage%20Portfolio&buttonText=Manage+your+portfolio) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Integrations** tab on the left.  Click **Connect** next to **Instagram**.

Click **Add to Site**.  Click **Agree & Add **to allow Wix to read basic site & business info.  Click **Connect Instagram**.   Log in to your Instagram account in the pop-up window if not already signed in.  Click **Allow**. Your Instagram account is now connected with your portfolio.

### How can I remove a connected integration from my portfolio?

You can remove an integration by deleting the app for portfolio from your dashboard.

1. [Go to Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions **icon next to the relevant integration.
3. Click **Delete**.
4. Select an option from the **Why are you deleting this app?** drop-down.
5. Click **Delete App**.

[image: A screenshot of the managing apps section in the dashboard where you can remove an integration from your portfolio.]

### How long will it take for changes in my creative apps (Instagram, Google Photos etc.) to reflect in my portfolio?

Any changes you make in your creative apps reflect in your portfolio within 24 hours.
