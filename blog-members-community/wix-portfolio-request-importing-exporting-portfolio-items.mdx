---
title: "Wix Portfolio Request: Importing / Exporting Portfolio Items"
---

Currently, it is not possible to import or export items via CSV file or Excel with Wix Portfolio.

As a workaround, you can work with the Wix CMS (Content Management System). The CMS allows you to [import images and videos via CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-importing-content-into-a-collection) and display them in galleries on dynamic pages, or [export content as a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-exporting-content-from-your-collection) to use it in another application such as Google Sheets or Excel. [Learn more about the Wix CMS.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
