---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Hiding Pricing Plan Blog Posts from AMP Pages"
---

Currently, blog posts that you include in a Pricing Plan are publicly visible in Accelerated Mobile Page (AMP) search results. It is not possible to hide this content from the search results.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
