title: "Wix Member Apps: Customizing Your Site on the Member Apps"
---

Customize the way your site looks on the [Wix member apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps) from the mobile web editor in your dashboard, or on-the-go from the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).

Your Wix member apps are made up of different screens, resembling site pages. Use these screens to display what your members see on each one.

Design your screens with elements that represent your business, such as online programs, booking services, or product pages. Then, enhance the look with buttons and images to make your content stand out.

---

### Customizing the header

Your header is the first thing members see when they visit your site on the Wix member apps. Style it for your business by adding a unique logo and cover photo. You can choose what information to showcase and turn site visitors into members.

Some of the steps vary when you customize your site's header from desktop and the Wix app.

DashboardWix appThe member count in your site's dashboard displays all app members. This includes members with both public and private profiles, as well as blocked members.
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App) in your site's dashboard.

- **Show shopping cart icon: **Click the toggle to add a shopping cart icon to the header so members can easily navigate to their cart.
2. Click the **App Info** drop-down and choose what you want to customize:

- **Name: **Enter the name of your site.
- **Subtitle:** Enter a slogan or a short announcement.

- **Show general info:** Enable the toggle to show a link to a screen with basic information about your business. Learn how to [customize the General info screen](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-site-on-the-member-apps#customizing-your-general-info-screen) from the Wix app.
3. Click the **Cover photo **drop-down** **to upload a cover photo for your site.
4. Click the **Members info **drop-down to choose what member information you want to show in the header: 

- **Show member count: **Click the toggle to Show or hide the number of members in your app's header.
- **Show member profiles: **Click the toggle to show or hide member profile avatars in your app's header.
5. Click the **Action buttons **drop-down to add buttons your header.

- **Show Invite Button**: Click the toggle to show or hide a button that allows members to invite more people to your app.
- **Show Custom Button: **Click the toggle to add a custom button to your header. For example, a 'Send Us a Message"  button can send members to chat with you in real-time.
6. Click **Save** **& Publish**.

### Adding and managing elements on your screens

Add elements to your app to display your content and what you offer. For example, add Online Programs and turn members into active participants. Place these elements where members can easily see and interact with them.

Dashboard Wix app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
3. Click **Elements **on the left.
4. Scroll through the catalog and click an element to add to your app. For example, under** Basic Items **you can add elements to design and organize your app like buttons, media, titles and paragraphs.

**Note:** Not all elements can be customized from the web editor. You can update those elements from  the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app).
[image: A screenshot of the Add Elements panel.]
1. Manage the element using the available options:
Reorder an element
1. Click the element.
2. Click the Up or Down arrows next to the element.
Duplicate an element
1. Click the element.
2. Click the **Duplicate **icon  .
Remove an element
1. Click the element.
2. Click the **Delete** icon.
3. Click **Delete** to confirm.
Adjust element margins Choose the amount of padding you want for the element.
1. Click the element.
2. Scroll down to **Margins **on the right.
3. Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding around the element.

**Note: **You can adjust the top and bottom margins for all elements. For some elements, you can also adjust the left and right margins.

[image: A screenshot showing the Margins option for your elements.]
4. (Optional) Click the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins. [image: Screenshot showing how to adjust the top and bottom margins separtely.]
Set element visibilityChoose what devices to display the element on (e.g. Android, iOS, or both). 
1. Click the element.
2. Scroll down and click **General Settings **on the right.
3. Under **Element visibility**, choose what devices to show the element in: **All**, **Android** or **iOS**.
[image: Screenshot showing element visbilbity to decide which devices you want to show an element.]6.  Click **Save & Publish**.

2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap the **Screen** drop-down and select the relevant screen.
6. Tap **+ Add Element**.
7. Choose the element you want to add. For example, under** Basic Items **you can add elements to design and organize your app like buttons, media, titles and paragraphs.

**Note:** Not all elements can be customized from the web editor. You can update those elements from  the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app).
[image: A screenshot of the Add Element panel in the Wix Owner app.]
1. (If needed) Edit the content and display settings and tap **Add **at the top right.

**Note: **Some elements are added automatically and don't require this step.
2. Manage and customize the element further: 

1. Tap the **Edit** icon   on the relevant element.
2. Choose what you want to do:

**Note: **The options available depend on the type of element you select. 

- **Customize element: **Edit details about the element such as:

- **Element Title: **Change the element title and choose whether to show or hide it.
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding around the element.
- **Element Visibility: **Choose whether to display the element to Android devices, Apple devices, or both.
- **Element Location:** Select the screen you want the element to appear in.
- **Display Settings:** Update the details that display in the element.
- **Move up / down: **Move the element up or down in the screen.
- **Duplicate: **Create a copy of the element.
- **Remove element: **Permanently delete the element.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Customizing your General Info screen

The General Info screen lets you make changes to your site information that members see in their members apps. Edit your contact info, logo settings, tagline, list of members, and various social links.

> **Note:**
>
> Currently, this feature is only available from the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app).

#### To customize your general info:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.   Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.  Tap the **Screen** drop-down at the top, then tap **General Info**.  Choose which information to edit:

### Contact information

1. Tap the **Edit** icon  next to **Contact Us**.
2. Tap **Customize element **and choose from the following options:

- **Element Title: **Update the text under** Element Title** if you wish to change this from the preset 'Contact Us' title.
- **Display Settings: **Tap the relevant checkboxes next to the elements you want to display.
- **Edit Business Info: **Tap **Edit Business Info** to customize the business information. Choose from the following options: 

- **Business Name:** Edit the business name used for emailing, invoicing and more.
- **Email:** Update the email used to notify you about activities in your site.
- **Currency: **Choose the currency you would like to accept.
- **Time Zone: **Select the time zone used for your business calendar, invoicing and more.
- **Description:** Enter a description about your business.
- **Phone Number: **Add a phone number so members can contact you.
- **Location: **Tap the checkbox next to **My business has a physical address** to enter a business location.
- **Element Visibility: **Choose whether to display the element to Android devices, Apple devices, or both.

### Member strip

1. Tap **Edit** icon  next to **Member Strip**. 

**Tip:** Tap **Hide element** if you do not want to display this on your site.
2. Tap **Customize element** and choose what you want to edit: 

- **Element Title: **Update the text under **Element Title** to change this from the preset 'Member Strip' title.
- **Element Visibility: **Choose whether to display the element to Android devices, iOS devices, or both.

### Social links

1. Tap **Edit** icon   next to** Social Links**.
2. Tap **Customize element **and choose what you want to edit:

- **Element Title: **Update the text under** Element Title** if you wish to change this from the default 'Social Links' title.
- **Social link: **Choose from the following options:

- **Add Social Link:** Tap **+ Add Social Link **and enter the URL you want to link.

**Note: **Repeat this step to add up to 6 social links.
- **Remove Link:** Tap **Remove Link** under a specific URL and tap **Remove **to confirm.
- **Element Visibility: **Choose whether to display the element to Android devices, iOS devices, or both. ** **

7.  Tap **Save**,** **then tap** Done.**

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about customizing your site on the member apps.

### Why does the member count in my dashboard differ from what members see in the app?

The number of members you see in the [Mobile App](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Mobile+App) tab of your site's dashboard includes those with public and private profiles, as well as blocked members. 
[image: A screenshot of the member count in the Mobile App section of a site's dashboard.]

When you enable the **Show member count **option** **in the members app, it only displays app members with a public profile to other app members. 
[image: A screenshot of the member count in the header displayed in the members app.]

### Why do some elements display real-time data while others display a demo message?

Currently, not all customization options are available from the web editor in your site's dashboard. [Download the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app) for complete access to customization capabilities.

### Do I need to make changes from the web editor in my dashboard and the Wix app?

No. The changes you make on the member apps from the web editor in your site's dashboard also appear on the Wix app.
