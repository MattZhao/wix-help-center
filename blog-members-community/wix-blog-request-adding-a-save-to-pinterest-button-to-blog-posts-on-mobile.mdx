---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Adding a Save to Pinterest Button to Blog Posts on Mobile"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a "Save to Pinterest" button to blog posts on mobile. However, you can [enable social icons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-customizing-your-blogs-mobile-view) for Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn on your mobile blog posts.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
