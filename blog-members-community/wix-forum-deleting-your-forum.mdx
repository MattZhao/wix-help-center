---
title: "Wix Forum: Deleting Your Forum"
---

Delete your forum at any time from your site's editor or from the Wix app on your mobile device.

Deleting your forum does not permanently delete your content. Re-adding the forum will restore your categories, posts and comments.

### To delete Wix Forum:

Wix EditorStudio EditorWix app
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Forum page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Forum page.
3. Hover over your forum page and click the** More Actions ** icon.
4. Click **Delete**.
5. Click **Delete **to confirm.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Forum page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Forum page.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the Forum page.
4. Click **Delete**.
5. Click **Delete Page**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Manage Apps**.
4. Scroll down to **Forum** and tap **Remove**.
5. Tap **Remove** again.
