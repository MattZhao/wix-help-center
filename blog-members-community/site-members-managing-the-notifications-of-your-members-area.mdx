title: "Site Members: Managing the Notifications of Your Members Area"
---

When your members log in to your site's Members Area, or your site in the [Wix member apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps) on their phone, they see a notification feed that informs them of new activity. New activity can include another member liking a comment they wrote, or a new blog post being published.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Viewing notifications as a member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-the-notifications-of-your-members-area#viewing-notifications-as-a-member)  [Managing notifications as a member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-the-notifications-of-your-members-area#managing-notifications-as-a-member)  [Controlling the notifications your members receive](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-the-notifications-of-your-members-area#controlling-the-notifications-your-members-receive)

### Viewing notifications as a member

Members can see notifications when they log into your site, or when they visit your site on one of the Wix member apps (Spaces, Fit by Wix, Dine by Wix). A notification bell informs them of new updates. Clicking the bell directs your members to the notification feed for more details.

### Viewing notifications on your site

After logging in to your Members Area, a notification bell appears next to the [login bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-adding-a-members-area-login-bar). If there are new notifications, your members see a red alert icon next to the bell:
 [image: A screenshot showing the bell icon on a live site, next to the member's avatar and the member menu]
Clicking the notification bell leads to the feed where members can catch up on their updates. They can click any notification to see the activity that it relates to (e.g., new comments, likes, posts and more).  
[image: A screenshot of the Notifications page in the Members Area where you see your notification feed.]

### Viewing notifications in the Wix member apps

Members who are using the Wix member apps can see if they have updates from the bell at the top of the app. Tapping the bell leads to the notification feed.
[image: A screenshot of a site in the member apps, showing the bell icon that notifies members of updates]
After reading a notification, members can swipe left to remove it from the feed or swipe right to mark it as read.
[image: A screenshot of the notification feed in the Wix member apps]

### Managing notifications as a member

Your members can control the email notifications they receive in the Members Area, and also which notifications they receive on the Wix member apps.

Click below to learn more about managing notifications as a member:

### Managing email notifications from the Members Area

Members can manage the email notifications they receive from the **Settings** page in their Members Area. 
The options they see differ depending on the [social apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-using-your-members-area-as-a-social-tool) you've added to your site (Wix Blog, Wix Forum, Wix Chat, Wix Groups).
Clicking the toggle next to a notification enables or disables that specific email. [image: A screenshot of the Notification Settings page in the Members Area where you can enable or disable specific notifications]

### Managing in notifications on the Wix member apps

Members using the Wix member apps can control the notifications they receive from your site. They can control their notifications based on the apps you've added to your site (e.g., Wix Forum, Wix Events, etc.) 
When tapping on a toggle, they can choose whether to pause the notifications or turn them off permanently:[image: A GIF showing the menu and options that appear when disabling a notification toggle in the Wix member apps]

### Controlling the notifications your members receive

Your members' notifications are managed in the [Outgoing Notifications](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fsettings%2Fcustomer-notifications&title=Select+Site+to+View++Outgoing+Notifications+&primaryButtonText=Select) page in your site's dashboard. If you've added apps such as Wix Blog, Wix Forum, or Wix Groups to your site, you can manage the notifications that your members receive for new activity.

Choose how and where to notify site members: via email, through push notifications on their mobile device, the Members Area's notification feed or all options.
