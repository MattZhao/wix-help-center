title: "Site Members: Displaying Member Tabs and Pages on the Site Menu"
---

> This article refers to the new version of the Members Area app which is not available to all users yet. If you're using the previous version, read the information [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-display-member-pages-on-your-site-menu). [Click here to check which version you're using](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-the-difference-between-the-new-and-previous-members-area).

Allow quicker access to [tabs / pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-member-tabs-and-pages) in your Members Area by adding them to your site's menu. When visitors click the relevant item(s) on the menu, they're asked to log in. This is a great way to get more exposure for your Members Area, encouraging new visitors to sign up.

> **Did you know?**
>
> You can [create custom member pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-adding-and-managing-private-member-pages), offering exclusive content for your logged-in members.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Site Menu**.
3. Click the Add a Link icon  at the bottom.
4. (In the Link panel) Select **Page**.
5. Choose the relevant member tab / page from the **Which page?** drop-down:

- **Private member page: **

1. Select your custom page from the drop-down.
2. Select **Current window** under **How does it open?**.
- **Member tab (e.g. My Account): **

1. Select **Member Page **from the drop-down.
2. Select the relevant tab from the **Which item?** drop-down.
6. Click **Done**.
7. Repeat steps 3 - 6 to add more member pages / tabs to your menu.

1. Select the relevant menu element.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Click **+ Add Item** at the bottom and select **Link**.
4. (In the Link panel) Select **Page**.
5. Choose the relevant member tab / page from the **Which page?** drop-down:

- **Private member page:**

1. Select your custom page from the drop-down.
2. Select **Current window** under **How does it open?**.
- **Member tab (e.g. My Account):**

1. Select **Member Page **from the drop-down.
2. Select the relevant tab from the **Which item?** drop-down.
6. Click **Done**.
7. Repeat steps 3 - 6 to add more member pages / tabs to the menu.
