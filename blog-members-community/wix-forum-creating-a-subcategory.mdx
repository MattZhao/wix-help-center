title: "Wix Forum: Creating a Subcategory"
---

There are two ways to create subcategories, either from your dashboard or from your live site. Choose the one that's most convenient to you.

#### Learn more about creating a subcategory from:

[Your Dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-a-subcategory#creating-a-subcategory-from-your-dashboard)  [Your Live Site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-a-subcategory#creating-a-subcategory-from-your-live-site)

> **Important:**
>
> In order to create subcategories, you need to have some categories already created. If you don't have any categories created yet, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-a-category) to learn how to get started.

---

### Creating a Subcategory from Your Dashboard

As part of managing your forum categories, you can create a subcategory from one of your existing categories, directly from your dashboard.

#### To create a subcategory from your dashboard:

Go to your forum in your dashboard.  Click the **Categories** tab.  Click the **Show More** icon  next to the relevant category.  Click **Subcategory**.

**Note:** The subcategory will nestle under the category directly above it. Make sure that the correct category is above the subcategory.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can drag and drop categories and subcategories. Click and hold the relevant category/subcategory to drag up or down.

---

### Creating a Subcategory from Your Live Site

From your live site you can change categories into subcategories. You can also edit your categories' names, images, and settings.

#### To create a category from your live site:

Log in to your live site using the email address associated with your Wix account.On the forum page, click the **Manage Categories **icon .Drag the category to move it below the relevant category, and to the right.Click **Done**.

> **Tips:**
>
> - To edit the main forum page, a category, or a subcategory, click the **Show More **icon  next to the relevant category and select **Edit**.
> - When you add images through the **Manage Category **icon on your live site, you can only upload images from your computer. To learn how to add images from the Media Manager, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-a-category).
