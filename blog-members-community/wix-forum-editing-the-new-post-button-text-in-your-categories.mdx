---
title: "Wix Forum: Editing The 'New Post' Button Text in Your Categories"
---

Customize the text of the button that prompts your forum members to "Create a New Post", or "Start a Discussion". This is a great way to grab your members' attention and make them want to click and get the conversation flowing.

### To change the default text of your button:

Go to [Forum](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Forum&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/forum/) in your site's dashboard.   Click the **Categories** tab.   Click **Edit** next to an existing category or click **New Category** to [create a new category](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-a-category).   Click the **New post button** field and enter your text.   Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> You can add a maximum of 24 characters to your button.

> **Tip:**
>
> Add an emoji to your button by right-clicking in the **New post button** text box.
