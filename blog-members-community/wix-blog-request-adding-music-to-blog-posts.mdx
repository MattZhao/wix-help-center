---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Adding Music to Blog Posts"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add music to blog posts using Wix Music.

However, it is possible to add music by [embedding a music track ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-html-code-to-a-blog-post)(such as a SoundCloud track) using HTML code.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
