---
title: "Site Members Request: Customizing the Password Reset Window"
---

Currently, it is not possible to customize the page your members see when they reset their password.

The design of password reset windows is taken directly from your site's theme. [You can choose a new theme](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-sites-theme-and-design) to make this page look more like the rest of your site.

> **Note:**
>
> Changes you make to your theme apply to your entire site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
