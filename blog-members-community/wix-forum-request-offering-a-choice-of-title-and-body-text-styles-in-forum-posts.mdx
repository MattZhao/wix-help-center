---
title: "Wix Forum Request: Offering A Choice of Title and Body Text Styles in Forum Posts"
---

Currently, it is not possible to select different title and body text styles from within Wix Forum posts. The first line of a post is set to one default heading, and the body is set to a smaller paragraph style.

However, you can [customize the type size and font style](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-designing-your-forum#customizing-the-design-of-your-text) using the Wix Forum design settings in your site's Editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
