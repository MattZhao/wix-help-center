---
title: "Site Members Request: Saving Data from the Custom Signup Form in a Table"
---

Currently, it is not possible to save the data that site visitors enter into [the custom signup form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-a-custom-signup-form) to a submissions table. This would let you manage the data in the table with options such as adding or deleting data, filtering data, sorting data, and exporting data.

> **Tip:**
>
> As a workaround, you can [save submission data from a field to your Contacts List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-a-custom-signup-form#step-3-save-your-member-signup-data).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
