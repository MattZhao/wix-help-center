---
title: "Spaces App: Inviting New Members to a Site"
---

Invite people you know to join a site that you are a member of on the Spaces App. Let them be part of the site's community and interact with the business from their mobile device.

The invite link directs them to download the Spaces App, and takes them straight to the relevant site.

> **Note:**
>
> Some sites might not have the invite option.

### To invite new members to a site:

Go to the relevant site on the Spaces App.  Tap **Invite**.  Select an option:

### Send an SMS

Send the link and invitation code to people by SMS (text message).
1. Tap **Send and SMS**.
2. Choose if you want to allow the Spaces App to access your contacts.
3. Select the contacts you want to invite.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Send the SMS.

### Share via Social & More

Share the link and invitation code to any social network connected to your phone.  
1. Tap **Share via Social & More**.
2. Choose how you want to send your invite to. For example, Facebook, Whatsapp, Gmail and more.

### Copy Link

Copy the link and paste it into a new message in any application. 
1. Tap **Copy Link**.
2. Paste the link into a new message using the application of your choice.
