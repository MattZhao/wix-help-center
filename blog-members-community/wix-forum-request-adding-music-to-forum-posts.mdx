---
title: "Wix Forum Request: Adding Music to Forum Posts"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add music, including through Wix Music, to forum posts.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can link to a music page on your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
