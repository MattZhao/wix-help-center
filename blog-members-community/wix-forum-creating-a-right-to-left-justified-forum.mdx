---
title: "Wix Forum: Creating a Right-to-Left Justified Forum"
---

You can make your forum right justified for use with languages such as Hebrew or Arabic. All you need to do is to select the relevant language as your forum language.

> **Note:**
>
> We currently support only two right-to-left languages, Arabic and Hebrew.

### To select a right-to-left language as your forum language:

Go to your site's [General Info tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Edit%20Site%20Info&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/).  Scroll down to the Regional Settings section.  Click the Language drop-down menu and select the language of your choice.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also change the language in your Members page. The language settings are in the Settings tab.
