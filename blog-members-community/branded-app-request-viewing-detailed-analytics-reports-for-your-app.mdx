---
title: "Branded App Request: Viewing Detailed Analytics Reports for Your App"
---

Currently in the Branded App, it is not possible to view detailed analytics reports about your app, such as the number of downloads your app is getting from both the App Store and Google Play Store.

In the Member App reports in Wix Analytics, you can see data such as app traffic by time of day as well as how many new app visitors you receive over certain time periods. [Learn more about your member app reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-analytics-members-app-reports).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
