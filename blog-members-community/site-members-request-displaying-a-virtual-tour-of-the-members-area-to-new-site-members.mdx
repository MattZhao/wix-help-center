title: "Site Members Request: Displaying a Virtual Tour of the Members Area to New Site Members"
---

Currently, you cannot enable a virtual tour of the Members Area for new members upon logging in or signing up. This would enable them to explore the various aspects of the Members Area, and learn about the available features.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
