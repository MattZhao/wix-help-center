### Troubleshooting general issues

Go to your site in the Wix app and follow the relevant steps below to solve general issues with your branded app.

### I'm not seeing push notifications

If you aren't receiving push notifications about activity in your branded app, try the following steps:

1. Make sure you have enabled push notifications for the Wix app in the general notification settings of your phone. This differs from one device to another.
2. Check your site notification settings in the Wix app:

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap your profile at the top.
3. Tap **Notification settings**.
4. Select your site from the list (if you have more than one).
5. Enable the **Allow Push Notifications **toggle to allow site notifications to your mobile device.
6. (Optional) Manage the notification settings for the apps on your site by enabling or disabling the toggle next to the relevant notification.

### I'm not getting notified about live site visitors

If you aren't getting notified about site visitors, first make sure you've added [Wix Chat](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-adding-wix-chat-to-your-site) to your site. You won't be able to see live visitors without the Wix Chat app.

**Note: **Publish your site after adding Wix Chat.
Once Wix Chat is added, go your Inbox activity settings:
1. Open the Wix App.
2. Tap **Inbox** at the bottom.
3. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
4. Tap **Settings**.
5. Tap **Notification settings** under **General Settings**.
6. Tap the **Allow Notifications **toggle if not already enabled.
7. Under **Inbox**, enable the **New visitor on site **toggle to turn on notifications every time a new visitor is on your site.

### I'm not able to find my branded app's site

1. Open the Wix app.
2. Tap the site drop-down on the top right.
3. Tap the site where you're building a branded app.

### Troubleshooting local issues

Click the relevant issue below to learn how to solve it.

### Check that your branded app is up to date

1. Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).
2. Search for your branded app.
3. (If available) Click **Update**.

**Note:** If there's no option to update it means you're running the most up-to-date version of the app.

### Check that you're using a supported device

Branded app by Wix is supported on:
- **iPhone: **iOS 11 and above
- **Android:** 6.0 and above

### Log out of your branded app and log back in

1. Open your branded app.
2. Tap your profile icon at the top left.
3. Tap the **Settings** icon .
4. Tap **Log Out**.
5. Log back in using your account credentials.
6. (If the issue isn't resolved) Restart your mobile device.

**Clear Cache on Android Devices:**
1. Long press the app icon on your device's home screen.
2. Tap **App Info**.
3. Tap **Storage**.
4. Tap **Clear Data**.
5. Open your branded app and log back in.

### Uninstall and re-download your app

If logging out and back in doesn't work, try completely uninstalling and then downloading your app again.
