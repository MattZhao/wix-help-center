---
title: "Branded App Request: Adding Custom Code to Your Branded App"
---

Currently it is not possible to add custom code or integrate the Branded App with [Velo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-velo-by-wix). The Branded App gives individuals and businesses the ability to design a native mobile app and add features and content without needing any prior coding knowledge. Most of the process is done in the [Branded App dashboard](https://www.wix.com/branded-apps?path=welcome&referralInfo=HelpCenter) of your Wix account.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
