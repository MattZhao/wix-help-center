Branded App by Wix allows you to design your own mobile app for free and (for Android devices) download a preview link to view it from your mobile device. Once you're ready to register the app, upgrade to a Branded App plan to create an Apple and Google Developer account and get your app live in app stores.

### Purchasing a Branded App plan

Branded App by Wix comes with three different plan options. Choose a package that works best for your business.

#### To purchase a Branded App plan:

Go to your [Branded App](https://www.wix.com/branded-apps?path=welcome&referralInfo=HelpCenter) dashboard in your Wix account.  Click **Purchase Plan** under Step 3** Get your app live with Apple & Google**.  Select a Branded App by Wix plan. [Compare plans](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Branded%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/branded-apps/premium).  Choose whether to pay annually or monthly and click **Continue to Checkout**.  Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

#### Important information about the plan

You are eligible to receive a refund within the first 30 days from initial purchase.  Prices do not include applicable taxes and VAT/GST which are determined by your billing address.  You do not need to purchase a Wix Premium plan to create a branded app. The Branded App by Wix plan is a separate plan.  Due to [Apple's In-App Purchase Policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-about-apples-in-app-purchase-policy), in-app purchasing of digital goods and services is only available for online bookings services. Learn how to [set up in-app purchase](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-setting-up-apple-in-app-purchase) for bookings.  The package does not include the app registration fee from Apple and Google. The subscription to an Apple / Google Developer account is solely subject to Apple’s / Google's applicable Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. The guidelines to open accounts are provided for information purposes only and do not bind Wix.

### FAQs

### What is included in a Branded App plan?

[Learn more](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Branded%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/branded-apps/premium) about what's included in a Branded App plan.

### Does my Branded App plan include membership fees to Apple's App Store and Google Play?

No, the plan does not include the registration fees and you must pay those separately. Apple's App Store membership fees start at $99 USD a year, and Google Play is a $25 USD one-time fee.

### What happens to my app if I cancel my Branded App subscription?

You can get a full refund [within the first 30 days](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-refund-policy) of purchasing a subscription.
After your app is live, if you decide to cancel your subscription Wix automatically removes your app from the app stores. If your members try to use the app they will be notified that the app is no longer available for use.

### Can I assign my branded app plan to a different site in my account?

Yes. After you purchase a Branded App plan, it is possible to assign it to another site in your account.

1. Go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the **Show More** icon  next to your branded app plan.
3. Click **Assign to a Different Site**.
4. Select the checkbox next to the relevant site.
5. Click **Next**.
6. Click **Assign **to confirm.

### Can I transfer my Branded App plan from one Wix account to another Wix account?

Yes. Since your Branded App is connected to a Wix site, you can change ownership by transferring the premium site associated with your branded app to another Wix account. Learn how to [transfer a premium site to another account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-premium-site-to-another-wix-account).
