title: "Wix Blog: Creating a Blog Post from Your Mobile Device"
---

You can write and publish blog posts from your mobile device without any special setup or app required! Just log in to your site and post!

> **Check out the Wix app:**
>
> Did you know that we have an [app for site owners](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview)?

### To create a post from a mobile device:

On a browser on your mobile device, access your blog page.Log in using the email associated with your Wix account.Tap **Create a Post**.
**Note: **The "Create a Post" button is only visible to those with writing privileges. Write your post title and content.Click **Save & Continue**.Do any of the following:
- **Choose Category:**
1. Tap to select up to three categories.
2. Tap **Done**.
- **Save as Draft: **Tap to save. You can publish the post later.
- **Save & Publish: **Tap to publish your post on your live site.

You can perform almost all blog functions (editing, adding categories, etc.) using your mobile device. You can also add any of the following to a post:

[Images or GIFs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-media-to-a-blog-post)**: **Click the **Image **icon  and select an image or GIF to upload.   [Galleries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-media-to-a-blog-post)**: **Click the **Gallery **icon and select images to upload.    [Videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-media-to-a-blog-post)**: **Click the **Video **icon  and add a link from YouTube, Vimeo or Facebook.    [Divider lines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-divider-lines-to-blog-posts)**: **Click the **Divider **icon  to add a divider line, then customize it.  [HTML](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-html-code-to-a-blog-post)**: **Click the **HTML** icon  to embed an iframe or display an external site in a post.  **Quotations:** Highlight the text you want to quote and click the **Quotation **icon .  **Links: **Highlight the text you want to make into a link, click the **Link **icon , enter the URL and click **Update**. (Optional) Select the **Add a nofollow tag**. To learn about nofollow tags, click [here](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/96569?hl=en).  [Hashtags](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-hashtags-to-blog-posts)**: **Add a hashtag symbol (#) and text with no spaces (e.g. #newclasses) anywhere in a post.
