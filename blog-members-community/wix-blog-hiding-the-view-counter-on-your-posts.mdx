---
title: "Wix Blog: Hiding the View Counter on Your Posts"
---

With Wix Blog, you have the ability to add a view counter to your posts. This counter displays the number of readers for each of your blog entries, both in your blog feed and on individual posts.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Hiding the view counter on your blog feed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-hiding-the-view-counter-on-your-posts#hiding-the-view-counter-on-your-blog-feed)  [Hiding the view counter on the post page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-hiding-the-view-counter-on-your-posts#hiding-the-view-counter-on-your-post-page)

---

### Hiding the view counter on your blog feed

You can hide the view counter from the[ blog feed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-customizing-your-blog-feed) in the Settings panel of the Blog page in your editor.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Blog page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click your Blog page.
3. Click the Blog Feed element in your editor.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Display **tab.
6. Deselect the **Views counter **checkbox under **What shows on your feed?.**
[image: A screenshot showing the option to hide the views on your blog feed.]Tip:Publish your site to view the changes. 
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Blog page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Blog page under **Blog Pages**.
3. Click the Blog** **element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Display **tab.
6. Deselect the **View counter **checkbox under **What shows on your feed?.**
[image: A screenshot showing the option to hide the views on your blog feed.]Tip:Publish your site to view the changes.

### Hiding the view counter on your post page

The Post page is the view readers see when they click to read a single post. You can hide the view counter when a post displays on your Post page.

Wix EditorStudio EditorWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Post page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Click **Post**.
3. Click the Post Page element on your page.

**Note: **Make sure you click the empty space otherwise you may pull up settings for a specific element on the page.
4. Click the Elements  icon
5. In the pop-up scroll down to **Counters.**
6. Deselect the **Views **checkbox to hide the view counter.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to hide the views from your post page.]Tip:Publish your site to view the changes.
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Post page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Post** under **Blog Pages**.
3. Click the Blog Post element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Display **tab.
6. Deselect the **View counter **checkbox under **What shows on your post?.**
[image: A screenshot showing the option to hide the views on your post page.]Tip:Publish your site to view the changes.
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Blog**.
4. Tap **Page Settings**.
5. Deselect the **Views** checkbox.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to hide views in the Owner app.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Manage** at the top left.
3. Tap **Blog**.
4. Tap **Page Settings**.
5. Deselect the **Views** checkbox.

[image: A screenshot showing the option to hide views from the post page in the Studio app.]

>
