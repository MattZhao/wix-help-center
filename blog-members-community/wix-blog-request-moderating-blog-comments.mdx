---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Moderating Blog Comments"
---

Currently, all comments that are made by your visitors are published instantly. It is not possible for blog owners to moderate comments by accepting or rejecting certain comments. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-managing-comments-for-your-posts)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
