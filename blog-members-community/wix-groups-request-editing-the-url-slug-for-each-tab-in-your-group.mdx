---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Editing the URL Slug for Each Tab in Your Group"
---

Each tab on your groups page has a unique name at the end of the URL known as a URL slug. For example, https://yoursite.com/group/**about** or https://yoursite.com/group/**discussion**.

Currently, it is not possible to edit the name of URL slug. For example, if your group has a tab for media with the following URL: https://www.example-wix-site.com/**media**, you cannot change the word "**media**."

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
