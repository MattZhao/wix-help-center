---
title: "Wix Blog: Deleting Your Blog"
---

You may have selected a template that includes a blog, or you simply don't need it anymore. Whatever the reason, you can delete your blog from your site at any time.

> **Tip:**
>
> Deleting Wix Blog does not permanently delete your content. Re-adding it restores your content, but it does not restore your settings. It also does not re-add any additional blog elements, e.g. Post List etc.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Hover Blog Pages and click the Settings [image: undefined] icon.
3. Click **Delete Blog**.
4. Click **Delete **to confirm.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to delete a blog.]Note:If you've set your blog as your homepage, you need to delete the entire homepage. If you want to delete it, you can[ set a different page as the homepage](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-sites-home-page) and copy all the elements you had in your previous homepage. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-working-with-elements).
