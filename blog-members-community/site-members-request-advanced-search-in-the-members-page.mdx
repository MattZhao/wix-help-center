---
title: "Site Members Request: Advanced Search in the Members Page"
---

From the [Members Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-members-page-that-displays-all-members), your members can find each other by typing a person's first or last name in the search bar. Currently, it is not possible to search for members using other information like their title, company name or email address.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
