---
title: "Wix Blog: Adding Profile Pages for Your Blog Writers"
---

When you add Wix Blog to your site, you can choose to also add a Member's Area if you don't already have one. A Member's Area lets your site members access features including the ability to login, create a public profile, and comment on blog posts. [Learn more about the Member's Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-using-the-members-area-in-wix-blog)

You can also use a Member's Area to create dedicated profile pages for your Blog Writers to help your readers get to know them. You can customize the information that's displayed on the profile pages such as name, bio, image, and recent posts that they've written.

> **Before you start, you need to:**
>
> - [Add Wix Blog to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-setting-up-your-blog)
> - [Add a Member's Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-add-and-set-up-a-members-area#step-1-add-the-members-area-app) if your site doesn't have one

---

### Step 1 | Select who has profile pages

You can choose to let only site members who you have set [as Blog Writers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog) or [as managed writers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-managed-writers-to-your-blog) have profile pages on your site, or let every member have a profile page. Select your preferred option in the Member Pages settings on your site's Editor.

#### To select who has profile pages:

Go to **Member Pages** in your Editor.

### Wix Editor | Show me how

1. Click **Pages** ﻿ on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Member Pages**.

### Editor X | Show me how

1. Click **Pages** ﻿ at the top of Editor X.
2. Click **Profile** under **Wix Members Area**.

2.  Click the More Actions icon  next to **Profile**.

---

### Step 2 | Customize your Blog Writers' profiles

Each profile page for your Blog Writers can include information such as their display name, title, an About section, and any recent posts that they have written. You can customize your own profile on your live site or your dashboard, and customize the profiles of your writers in your dashboard.

#### Learn how to customize:

### Blog Writers' profiles from your dashboard

1. [Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Writers**.
3. Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant writer.
4. Select **Edit**.
5. Customize the writer's profile image under **Profile picture**.

- Click the Add Image icon  to add a profile picture for the writer, or
- Click the Replace icon  to change an existing image.
- Click the Delete icon  to remove an existing image.
6. Enter the writer's name under **Name**.
7. Click **Add Cover Image** to add a background image that's displayed at the top of the writer's profile.
8. Scroll down to **Additional Info**.
9. (Optional) Add a first name, last name, email address, and phone number for the writer.

**Note: **By default, these are not displayed on the profile page. Click the Visibility icon  beside any field to make the field visible on the profile.
10. Enter information about the writer in the text box under **About**.
11. Customize the URL of the writer's profile under **Profile Link**.
12. Click **Save**.

### Your own profile from your live site

1. Go to your **Profile** page on your live site.
2. Log in with the email address associated with your Wix account.
3. Click your profile image and select an image from your computer to update it.
4. Click **Edit Profile**.
5. Customize the name that's shown on your profile under **Display Name**.
6. Click **Update Info**.
7. Click the **About** text box.
8. Enter information about yourself in the text box.
9. Click **Publish**.
10. Publish your site to view the changes.

> **Want to change how your profile looks?**
>
> - Go to your site's Editor to [customize the layout of your profile pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-change-the-layout-of-your-member-pages).
> - If you need to add extra information that's not covered by the existing fields, you can [add your own custom fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-customizing-your-member-profile-fields).
