title: "Wix Portfolio Request: Adding Videos as Project Page Covers"
---

We are excited to announce that it is now possible to[ add a video as a project cover](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-portfolio-adding-media-to-projects) using Wix Portfolio.

### To add a video to your project page cover:

[Go to Portfolio](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/portfolio?&title=Manage%20Portfolio&buttonText=Manage+your+portfolio) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the relevant project and click **Edit**.   Scroll down to **Media** and click the **Add**  icon.  Click **Add media**.  Upload the video file.  Click the image in your Media Manager.  Click **Set as Cover.**

Click **Done**.
