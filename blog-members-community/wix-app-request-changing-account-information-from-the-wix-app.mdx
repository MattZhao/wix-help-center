---
title: "Wix App Request: Changing Account Information from the Wix App"
---

Currently, it's not possible to change your Wix account information in the Wix app. Your account information includes your username, phone number and Wix account name.

To change your account information, go to your desktop. Updating account settings affects your entire Wix account. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-wix-account-details)

### Learn how to update your business detail settings in the Wix app

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Scroll down to **Settings **and tap **Business Info**. ** **
4. Update your business details.
5. Tap **Save**.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
