---
title: "Site Members: Changing the Design of a Member Badge"
---

Once you have created and set up your member badge, you can change how it looks at any time.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To use member badges, you must install a [Member's Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) on your site.

### To change the design of your member badge:

Go to [Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members). Click **More Actions** at the top right and click **Manage Badges**.Click the** Show More **icon  on the badge you want to change.Click **Edit**.

Change the design options:
- **Background Color: **Click the field and select a color for the badge background.
- **Text Color: **Click the field and select a color for the badge text.
- Click Add under **Icon** and select a new icon for your badge from the Media Manager, or upload your own.
Click **Save**.
