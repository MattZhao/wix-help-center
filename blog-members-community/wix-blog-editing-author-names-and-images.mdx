---
title: "Wix Blog: Editing Author Names and Images"
---

In your blog feed and in individual posts, readers can see the author's name and image (unless you chose to [hide these elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-showing-or-hiding-information-in-your-wix-blog)).

Writers can edit their name and image at any time. The instructions for the writer differ depending on how you added the writers to your blog.

#### Select how you added your writers for instructions:

[Through Blog Settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-editing-author-names-and-images#editing-author-details-for-writers-added-in-your-blog-settings)  [Through the Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-editing-author-names-and-images#editing-author-details-for-writers-added-in-the-members-area)  [Manually adding an author in your Blog Settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-editing-author-names-and-images#manually-adding-an-author-in-your-blog-settings)

---

### Editing author details for writers added in Your Blog Settings

Go to the [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) tab in your site's dashboard.   Click **More Actions** at the top right.

---

### Editing author details for writers added through the Members Area

The writer needs to log in via the login bar on your live site.

---

### Manually adding an author in your Blog Settings.

You can manually add authors to your blog via your blog settings. This means you can give a visitor temporary author status to help complete a task.

Go to the [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) tab in your site's dashboard.  Click **More Actions** at the top right.  Click **Blog Settings**.  Click **Add** in the **Add an Author** section.  Enter the name of the author you want to add.
