---

After you finish writing a post, edit the SEO to make sure it ranks highly in search engines. You can do so on the go with the Wix app.

### To edit SEO setting for a blog post:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage** at the bottom right.  Tap **Blog**.   Tap **Posts**.  Tap the **More Actions **icon  beside the relevant post.  Tap **Post Settings**.  Tap the **SEO **tab at the top right.  Edit your SEO settings.  Tap **Next**.  Choose an option:

- Tap **Save Changes**.
- Tap **Publish Post**.
