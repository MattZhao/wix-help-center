---
title: "Spaces App Request: Design Options for Buttons in the App"
---

Currently, you can choose between 3 design options for buttons that you add to the Spaces app: Primary, Secondary or Link. At this time, it is not possible to design your buttons further.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
