title: "Wix Blog: Changing Blog Post Text Sizes for Mobile"
---

Want to make different design choices for your post page on mobile? You can customize the font size that your readers see when they access a blog post on a mobile device.

> **Check out the Wix app:**
>
> Did you know that we have an app for site owners? [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).

---

### To customize font size on mobile:

Go to the Blog Post page in your editor.  Click the **Switch to Mobile** icon on the top bar of your editor.

Click the Wix Blog app in your mobile editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Design **tab.  Drag any of the sliders to increase or decrease the font sizes.
