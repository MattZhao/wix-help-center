---
title: "Wix Forum Request: Customizing the Default Notification Email Address in Wix Forum"
---

Notifications sent to members come from notifications@forums.wix.com. Currently, it is not possible to change this email address.

> **Update:**
>
> The "From" name that the notification email is sent from is now your forum name 
>  
>
>
> You can change your forum name at any time by heading to the Editor, clicking your Forum page, opening the Forum Settings and clicking on the **Categories** tab. Click to edit your forum and then
>  enter a forum name.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
