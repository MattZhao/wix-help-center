---
title: "Wix Blog: Editing a Blog Post in Multiple Tabs"
---

Writing blog content across multiple open tabs can cause other versions of drafts to be overwritten. For example, if you start writing your blog post in one tab, but then you open a second tab and continue writing your blog post, you have your content spread across two tabs.

If you go back to the first tab without closing both tabs you will find it's still the first draft version. Once autosave triggers, a pop-up box will appear on that tab prompting you to refresh. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-working-with-drafts-in-wix-blog) to learn more about working in draft format.

> **Note:**
>
> To avoid losing any content, we recommend working in one single tab.
