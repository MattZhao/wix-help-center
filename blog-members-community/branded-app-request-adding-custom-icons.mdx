---
title: "Branded App Request: Adding Custom Icons"
---

Currently, when designing your Branded App, you can choose to display specific predefined icons based on which business apps you're currently using (e.g. the cart icon for Stores users). At this time, it is not possible to upload your own custom icons.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
