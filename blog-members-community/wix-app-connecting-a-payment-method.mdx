---
title: "Wix App: Connecting a Payment Method"
---

From the Wix app, you can connect different payment methods to your site so customers can pay you on the go.

> **Note:**
>
> - Connecting a payment method from the Wix app is only available on Android devices. It is not possible to connect a payment method when using iOS device.
> - Some payment providers are only available to connect through desktop. To manage, change or see a wider list of available providers, such as [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments), go to the [Accept Payments tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) on your site's dashboard.

### To add a payment method:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Pricing Plans** under **Catalog**.  Tap **Settings**.  Tap **Payments**.   If prompted, select your location from the **Business Location **drop-down, and tap **Confirm**.  Tap **Connect **on the payment methods you want to offer (Credit/Debit cards, PayPal, or Manual) or tap **More Payment Options**.  Choose what you payment method you want to connect:

### Complete setup for credit / debit cards

Wix offers different credit / debit card payment providers, depending on your location. Tap **Connect** and follow the setup instructions for the specific payment provider you are connecting.

### Complete setup for PayPal

Follow the relevant steps:**If you have a PayPal account:**
1. Tap **Connect**.
2. Enter the email address associated with your PayPal account.
3. Enter your country or region.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Log in to your PayPal account.

**If you don't have a PayPal account:**
1. Click **Create new account**.
2. Follow the instructions in PayPal to create an account.
3. Once your account is approved, go to the **Accept Payments** section in the Wix app and follow the steps above for merchants with an account.

### Complete setup for Manual

Customers follow your instructions on how they can pay you. For example, you can instruct people to pay through a bank transfer, a specific app or in person.

1. Select a title for the payment instructions from the drop-down menu.
2. Tap **Add Instructions**
3. Enter instructions for your client (e.g. Please send payment through the ABC app to 555-1234).
4. Tap **Add**.
5. Click **Connect**.
