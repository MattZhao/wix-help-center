---
title: "Wix Mobile Apps: Guidelines for Naming Your App"
---

When choosing a name for your app, make sure it follows the naming guidelines for both Apple's App Store and Google Play.

### Your app name can not include:

Any special characters (e.g. #,@,&) or emojis  Prices or pricing information (e.g. 'Free app!')  Trademarked names or data  Names already used in popular apps or references to other apps  Inappropriate or irrelevant content  Names over the 30 character limit

---

### Tips for choosing a name that won't be rejected:

Pick a unique name that accurately describes your app and its content.  Choose a name that is simple and easy for your users to remember.  Don't try to 'game the system' as [Apple says](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) by using popular or existing app names to draw  more attention to your app.  Reflect the app's core features in the name  Check the availability of the name before you submit. This includes all social media handles and domain names as well.

> **Tip:**
>
> Use the subtitle in the **Store Listings** section of your app's dashboard to provide extra context about your app without including it in the name.

Read more about [Apple's Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) and the [Google Play Developer Policy](https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/).
