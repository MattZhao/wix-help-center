---
title: "Branded App Request: Adding Titles to Every Screen's Top Bar"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add screen titles to the top of the app. However, when you set the navigation menu to appear at the top, it shows your members what screen they're on.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can add a title element at the top of the screen to show users where they are in your app. Learn more about [adding elements to screens in your branded app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content#adding-elements-to-your-screens).

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
