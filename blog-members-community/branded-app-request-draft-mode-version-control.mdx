---
title: "Branded App Request: Draft Mode - Version Control"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create different drafts of your app or automatically save your changes. This capability would allow you to experiment with various designs without losing your previous work and provide the ability to undo and redo changes. For example, if you are redesigning your app's layout, you could try multiple versions and easily switch back to a previous draft if the new changes don't meet your expectations.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
