---
title: "Wix App: Renaming Your Site"
---

From the Wix app, you can change the name of your site on the go. Changing the name of your site in the Wix app also updates it in your site dashboard.

### To rename a site:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage** at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.   Tap **Edit**.  Tap** Change Header Design**.  Tap **Customize**.  Enter the new site name under **Title**.  Tap **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Edit the details and design of your header](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-site-on-the-member-apps#customizing-the-header)** **such as adding a catchy tagline, your business logo and a cover photo.
