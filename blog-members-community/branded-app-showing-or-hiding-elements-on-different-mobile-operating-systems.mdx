---
title: "Branded App: Showing or Hiding Elements on Different Mobile Operating Systems"
---

Show or hide elements of your Branded App depending on the operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. This is useful if you want to create different content for each platform, or showcase a section that's only relevant for members using iOS.

### To show or hide an element:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage ** at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.   Tap **Edit**.  Tap the screen that includes the relevant section.  Tap the **Edit** icon  at the top right of the section.

Tap **Customize element**.   Scroll to the bottom and tap the drop-down under** Visibility**.   Choose what device this element should show on: ﻿**Android**, **iOS**, or **All**.   Tap **Save**.
