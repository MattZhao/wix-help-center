---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Adding More than 3 Custom Tabs to a Group"
---

Currently, you can add up to 3 [custom tabs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-managing-your-group#managing-your-groups-content-tabs) for your own content to a group. However, it is not possible to add more than that.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
