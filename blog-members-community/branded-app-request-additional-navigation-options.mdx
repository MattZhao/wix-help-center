---
title: "Branded App Request: Additional Navigation Options"
---

Currently, you can only add a Top tab bar or Bottom tab bar for navigation in your Branded app. It's not possible to add other options, such as a sidebar or hamburger menu.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
