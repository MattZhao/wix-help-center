---
title: "Wix Groups: Enabling and Disabling Your Weekly Digest Email"
---

Do you want to send a weekly email to site members about their group activities? Your groups weekly digest email summarizes popular posts, recent activity, and related groups. The email is sent automatically to all members by default. You can turn off this feature if you prefer a different update method.

### To enable or disable the weekly digest email:

[Go to Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/social-groups-dashboard/) in your site dashboard.  Click the **More Actions **icon  at the top and select **General Settings**.  Choose what you want to do next:

- Select **Send** under **Groups weekly digest** to enable the email.
- Select **Don't send** under **Groups weekly digest **to disable the email.
    Click **Save**.
