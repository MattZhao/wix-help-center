### Viewing and chatting with live site visitors

View your live site visitors on your site's dashboard in your Wix app, and communicate with them via Wix Chat, directly from your app.

#### To view and chat with live site visitors

Go to your site in the Wix app.  A notification appears at the top of the dashboard if you have live site visitors. Tap the notification to see the full list.

Site visitors appear as a number (Visitor #9504) if they are not registered to your site, or as their name if they are site members. To view more information about a visitor:

1. Tap the visitor you want to view.
2. Tap the drop-down next to **On Page** to view all the available details including the page of your site the visitor is currently on, the browser they're using, their location and how many times they've previously visited.

You can also chat with any visitor in real-time. To chat with a visitor:

1. Make sure the** Chat status **toggle is enabled at the top of the site visitor list so you are online and available to chat.
2. Tap the visitor you want to chat with.
3. A new chat opens between you and the visitor. Type your message in the box and tap **Send**.

The message pops up in the chatbox on your site for the visitor to see and respond to. A green dot also appears at the top of the chatbox to let visitors know you are active and ready to chat.

### Troubleshooting live site visitor issues

If you are having trouble viewing or chatting with your live site visitors from the Wix app, try the steps below:

### Step 1 | Check that Wix Chat is enabled on your site

Make sure you have added [Wix Chat](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-an-overview) to your site. If you don't have Wix Chat, you won't be able to see your live site visitors. 

**Note:** Publish your site after adding Wix Chat.

### Step 2 | Check that push notifications are enabled on your device

Make sure you have enabled push notifications for the Wix app on your device (steps differ depending on the device).

### Step 3 | Check that new visitor notifications are enabled on the Wix app

Make sure you have enabled live site visitors notifications in the app:
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Inbox ** at the bottom.
3. Tap the **More Actions** icon .
4. Tap **Settings**.
5. Tap **Notification Settings**.
6. Tap the **Allow Notifications** toggle to enable notifications.
7. Tap **Inbox**.
8. Tap the **New Visitor **notification toggle to enable the specific notification.
