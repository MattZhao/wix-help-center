The post composer is where you create your blog posts. When you create a new post or edit an existing post, the post composer automatically opens.

There are 2 ways to access the post composer to write blog posts. You can access it from your dashboard, or open blog in the Wix app and compose posts on the go from your mobile device.

### Accessing the blog post composer

There are 2 ways to access the post composer. Choose the option most convenient to you.

DashboardWix app
1. Go to your site's [dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fhome%2F)
2. Click on the **Blog** tab on the left.
3. Click** Create New Post **or click an existing post to edit it.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to create a new post.]Add a title to enable auto-saveYou should always add a title to your post before you write the rest of your post. Adding a title makes sure that your post is auto-saved. If you don't add a title, your post won't auto-save and you may lose work if you leave the post without manually saving. You can create a new post or edit an existing post directly from the Wix app on your mobile device . Only people with [writing permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog) can see the **Create a Post** button. 

1. Go to the Wix app on your mobile device.
2. Tap the **Site List** drop-down at the top.
3. Select the relevant site.
4. Tap the **Manage **icon .
5. Tap **Content & Community**.
6. Tap** Blog**.
7. Tap **Posts**.
8. Tap **Create New Post **or tap a post to edit it.
9. Write or edit your blog post and tap **Next**.
10. Choose what you want to do by tapping the relevant option:

- **Save as Draft: **You can continue working on your post at a later time.
- **Post Settings: **

1. Edit your post settings and tap **Save**.
2. Choose to either save your changes or schedule your post.
Tip:Tap the Undo icon  in the bottom bar to undo a change you made to your post. Tap the Redo icon  if you want to restore a change again.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about Wix Blog.

### Does it matter how I access the blog post composer?

No. The way you access the post composer does not impact the process of writing a post. Just enter the post title and start writing.

### Is there a limit to how long a blog post can be?

Yes. Currently, the maximum size of a blog post is 400KB.

### How can I customize the layout and design of my blog posts?

You can [customize the layout and design of your blog posts ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-customizing-your-blog-feed)directly from your editor.

### How can I access my Media Manager to add new media files?

To access the Wix Media Manager, you must be signed in to your Wix account on a desktop computer.

### Can I make money from my blog?

Yes. You can monetize your blog content by [setting up blog subscriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-creating-blog-post-subscriptions).

### Can I translate my blog content to other languages?

Yes. Using both Wix Blog and Wix Multilingual, you can [translate your blog content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-blog) into other languages.
