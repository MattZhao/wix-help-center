---
title: "Spaces App Request: Customizing the Placeholder Text for Tabs with No Content"
---

Currently, you can create navigation tabs for your site in the Wix app without adding content to the sections. However, without content in a tab, your visitors see placeholder text when viewing the page in the member app (Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix, Dine by Wix). This placeholder text cannot be customized.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
