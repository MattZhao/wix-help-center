### Adding and managing buttons in your app

Customize your Wix Members app with buttons. You can customize the button layout, size, the action, which devices the buttons will be visible on and more.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
5. Click **Elements **on the left.
6. Select **Button** under **Basic items**.
7. Customize your button using the options below:

- **Layout: **Select the number of buttons you want to add (e.g. one or two buttons). Then choose the size, width, and alignment of the button(s).
- **Behavior: **Click the** Select button action **drop-down and choose what you want the button(s) to do. For example, link the button to take members directly to another screen on your app, chat directly through your app, open an external link and more.

**Note: **Different actions may appear depending on the type of apps you have on your site.
- **Design:** Choose a button design.
- **Margins: **Drag the **Margins** sliders to adjust the padding for the button(s).

**Note: **Click the **Edit individually **icon to separately adjust the margins.
- **General Settings: **Click **General Settings **and under **Element visibility**, choose which devices to show this button on: **All**, **Android,** or **iOS**.

**Note: **You can show or hide elements of your site depending on what operating system your members are using on their mobile devices. Your elements are visible on all operating systems by default, but you can decide what to show on iOS, Android, or both.
8. Click **Save & Publish**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about adding and managing buttons in your app.

### How do I edit the color of my button?

You can edit the color of your button by changing the theme color of your Wix Members App. [Learn more about editing the color themes of your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-site-on-the-member-apps#changing-the-theme-color-of-your-wix-members-app).

### How do I link a button to an external URL?

To link your button to an external URL to bring members to an external site, follow the steps below.
1. Choose **Open URL** from the **Select button action **drop-down.
2. Enter the link in the **Button URL **section.** **
3. (Optional) Enter the text you want displayed on your button under **Button text**.

### How do I link a button to direct members to another screen?

To link a button to another screen on your app, follow the steps below.
1. Choose **Open screen **from the **Select button action **drop-down.
2. Select a screen from the **Select screen** drop-down.
3. (Optional) Enter the text you want to display on the button in the **Button text **section.

### Can members call me directly through the app?

Yes, members can contact you directly through the app with a **Call phone number** button. 
1. Choose **Call phone number** from the **Select button action **drop-down.
2. Enter your phone number in the **Add phone number** section.
3. (Optional) Edit the **Button text **to what you want displayed on the button.

### Can members email me directly through the app?

Yes, connect a button to your email address, so members can contact you directly from their device. You can choose which email address the emails are directed to.
1. Choose **Send email** from the **Select button action **drop-down.
2. Enter your email address in the **Add email** section.
3. (Optional) Edit the **Button text **to what you want displayed on the button.

### Can I delete a button?

Yes, you can. Learn more about [managing your app elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-managing-your-app-elements).
