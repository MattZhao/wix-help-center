---
title: "Wix Member Apps Request: Scheduling Customized Push Notifications"
---

You can [create and send customized push notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-sending-customized-push-notifications) to your members right from the Wix app on your mobile device. However, it is not possible to schedule the time you send each notification.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
