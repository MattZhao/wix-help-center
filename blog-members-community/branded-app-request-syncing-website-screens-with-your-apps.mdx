---
title: "Branded App Request: Syncing Website Screens with Your Apps"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sync specific pages from your website to your own native app.

This feature would let you connect your website content with your mobile app. Any changes made on the website immediately show up in your app. For example, updating a product page on your website updates it instantly on your mobile app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
