---
title: "Wix Member Apps: Editing Your Members App Invite Code"
---

> Currently, editing your invite code is not possible. However, we are working to reintroduce this feature soon. In the meantime, you can still [invite people](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps) to join your site

Every site you create has a unique invite code for people to join you in the Wix Member Apps. When inviting members to your members app, you can edit this code to personalize it for your business through the Wix App.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Editing the invite code is only available if you have a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-yearly-premium-plan).
> - Once you change your invite code, the original code will no longer work.

### To edit an invite code:

Go to your site in the Wix app.   Tap **Manage** at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.  Tap the invite code under **Mobile Apps**.

**Note: **If you haven't set up the mobile app your members will use, tap the drop-down under Mobile Apps then [choose your preferred members app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app).  Tap **Change Invite Code**.  Enter your new code.

**Note:** If this code is not available, enter another code.  Tap **Change Invite Code**.  Tap **Change Code** to confirm.

> **What happens next?**
>
> Your new invite code is now activated and ready to [send to members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps). Once you send the code, your members receive a link to download the relevant members app.
