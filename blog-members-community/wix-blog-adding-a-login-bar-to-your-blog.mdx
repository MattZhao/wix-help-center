---

A login bar allows visitors to sign in to your site, enhancing their experience and engagement with your blog. When visitors log in, they can follow, comment on posts, and join your blog community, making your site more interactive and lively.

You can add a login bar either by adding a Members Area to your site, or by adding a standalone login bar.

[Adding a Members Area with a login bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-a-login-bar-to-your-blog#adding-a-members-area-that-comes-with-a-login-bar)  [Adding a blog login bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-a-login-bar-to-your-blog#adding-a-blog-login-bar)  [Customizing login settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-a-login-bar-to-your-blog#customizing-your-login-settings)

### Adding a Members Area that comes with a login bar

When you add the [Wix Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) to your site, visitors can log in and gain access to all members pages, including special pages for Wix Blog, Wix Stores, and Wix Bookings.

Adding the Members Area also adds a page to your site where blog members can view, follow, and access each other's profile. If you want the Members Area, but without this page, you can [hide it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu).

#### To add a Members Area to your site:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Add Apps**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Enter "Members Area" or "Wix Members Area" in the search bar.
3. Select the **Wix Members Area** app.
4. Click **Add to Site**.

### Adding a blog login bar

In addition - or instead of - the Members Area login bar, you can add a standalone blog login bar which appears next to your blog menu.

Like the Members Area login bar, it grants visitors access to all members-only pages and blog pages, but not to special pages for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, etc. In addition, it doesn't add a separate members page to your site.

#### To add a blog login bar:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click your blog feed in the editor
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Display **tab.
4. Click **Blog Menu**.
5. Enable the **Login from blog menu** toggle.
Tip:To edit the login button design, click the blog in the Editor, click **Settings**, and in the **Design **tab, select **Blog Menu**

### Customizing your login settings

You can customize your login settings to display sign up/log in first, allow social logins from Google or Facebook, and display links to your terms of use.

#### To customize your login settings:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Pages** ﻿ on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Signup & Login**.
3. Click **Member Signup Form (Default)**.
4. Go through the tabs one by one to set up the signup form:
General tab
1. Choose which option appears first in the signup window:

- **New member signup: **The signup option is displayed first.
- **Existing member login:** The login option is displayed first.
2. Click **Additional Signup & Login Settings **to [manage who can become a member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-signup-login-and-security-settings-for-your-site-members#controlling-who-can-be-a-site-member).
Social & Community tabChoose whether or not to allow new members to sign up using their Google or Facebook credentials. 
If you have a social app (e.g. Wix Blog, Wix Forum) installed on your site, there are additional settings to decide on:  
- **Signup privacy footnote customization**:

- **Footnote only:** Members are informed that they are signing up with a public profile; They can't choose to make it private.
- **Checkbox:** Members can choose the privacy level of their profile.
- **Sign up to this site with a public profile:** Choose whether or not a member's profile is public by default.
Policies tabDecide if you want to include links to your site's terms of use and privacy policy. After enabling either one of the toggles, click the Link icon   to set it up.
1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Hover over **Signup (Default)** and click the **More Actions **icon .
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Go through the tabs one by one to set up the signup form:
General tab
1. Choose which option appears first in the signup window:

Social & Community tabChoose whether or not to allow new members to sign up using their Google or Facebook credentials.

- **Signup privacy footnote customization**:

- **Footnote only:** Members are informed that they are signing up with a public profile. However, they can [make their profile private](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-making-a-member-profile-public#changing-privacy-settings-from-the-my-account-page) later on, from their Account Settings tab.
