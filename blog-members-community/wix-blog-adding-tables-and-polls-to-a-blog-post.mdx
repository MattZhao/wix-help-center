Enhance your Wix Blog posts by adding tables and polls. Tables help you organize information clearly, making it easier for readers to digest complex data. Polls engage your audience by inviting them to share their opinions, increasing interaction on your site.

### Adding a poll to a blog post

You can create a poll for your post in the Blog section of your site's dashboard or from the Wix app.

#### To add a poll to a blog post

DashboardWix App
1. [Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the post that you want to add media to.
3. Click the Add icon  beside the section where you want to add the poll.
4. Select **Poll **under **Elements**.
5. Select the type of poll that you want to create:

- **Text in a list: **Creates a poll with a question and a list of clickable answers.
- **Text & main image: **Creates a poll with a question, an image, and a list of clickable answers.
- **Images in a grid: **Creates a poll with a question and a list of clickable images with answers written on them.
6. Enter your poll question and the answers that you want your readers to choose from.
7. (Optional) Customize the layout, design, and settings of your poll.
Layout
1. Click **Layout**.
2. Customize your layout using the options:

- **Question: **Enable the toggle to add a main image to your poll.
- **Answers: **Select a List or Grid layout for your answers.

[image: Selecting a List or Grid layout for your poll answers]
- **Add an image to each answer: **Enable the toggle to add an image to each answer.
- **Poll Direction:** Select a left-to-right or right-to-left direction for your poll.
3. Click **Save**.
Design
1. Click **Design**.
2. Customize the design using the options:

- **Choose a background style: **Select a color, gradient, or image background for your poll.
- **Corner Radius: **Adjust how rounded the edges of your poll and answers.

[image: Adjusting the corner radius of your poll and answers]
3. Click **Save**.
Settings
1. Click **Settings**.
2. Customize the settings using the options: 

- **Voting: **Select who can vote on the poll. You can choose to let everyone reader vote, or let only site members vote.
- **People can pick multiple answers****: **Enable the toggle if you want to let people vote more than once.
- **Results: **Select who can see the results of the poll. You can choose to let everyone see the results, let only site members see the results, or make the results only visible to you.
- **Show vote count: **Enable the** **toggle to display the number of votes that visitors have submitted.
- **Show who voted: **Enable the toggle to display the names of the site members who voted on the poll.
3. Click **Save**.
8.  Click **Publish**.
1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Blog** under** Content & Community**.
4. Tap **Posts**.
5. Select the post that you want to add a poll to.
6. Tap the **Add** icon  at the bottom.
7. Tap **Poll**.
8. Select the type of poll that you want to create:

- **Text in a list: **Creates a poll with a question and a list of clickable answers.
- **Text & main image: **Creates a poll with a question, an image, and a list of clickable answers.
- **Images in a grid: **Creates a poll with a question and a list of clickable images with answers written on them.
9. Enter your poll question and the answers that you want your readers to choose from.
10. (Optional) Customize the layout and design of your poll.
Layout
1. Tap **Layout**.
2. Customize your layout using the options: 

- **Add an image to the question: **Enable the toggle to add a main image to your poll.
- **Answers: **Select a layout: **List** or **Grid**.
- **Add an image to each answer: **Enable the toggle to add an image to each answer.
- **Poll Direction:** Select a left-to-right or right-to-left direction for your poll.
3. Tap **Done.**
Design
1. Tap **Design**.
2. Select a color or image background for your poll under **Choose a background style**.
3. Tap **Done**.
11. Tap **Create**. 12. Tap the Settings icon  to customize the settings of your poll. 
- **People can pick multiple answers: **Enable the toggle if you want to let people vote more than once.
- **Results: **Select who can see the results of the poll. You can choose to let everyone see the results, let only site members see the results, or make the results only visible to you.
- **Show the vote count: **Enable the toggle to display the number of votes that visitors have submitted.
- **Show who voted: **Enable the toggle to display the names of the site members who voted on the poll.
13. Click **Save**.14. Tap **Next**.15. Tap **Save and publish**.

### Adding a table to a blog post

Add a table to blog posts to help provide more structure to your content, especially if the post has a lot of important information.

#### To add a table:

Go to [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog) in your site's dashboard.  ﻿Click **Edit** on the relevant blog post, or **Create New Post** to write a new one.  ﻿Click **Add**   on the left side.  Click **Table**.   Type the number of columns and rows you want in your table.   Click **Create table**.   Add the relevant content to each section of the table.   Customize the look of the table and the text:

- Click **Text style** to customize the text that appears in the table.
- Click the Vertical alignment icon  to choose how you want the text to align.
- Click the Background color icon  to change the background color of the table.
- Click the Border color icon  to change the color of the table border.
- Click the Show More icon  to select a specific row or column. 

- Click the Show More icon  to add or remove rows and columns in the table.
    Click **Publish**.
