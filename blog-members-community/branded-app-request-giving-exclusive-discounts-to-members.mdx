---
title: "Branded App Request: Giving Exclusive Discounts to Members"
---

Currently, it's not possible to give discounts specifically to your Branded App members.

This would allow you to incentivize your site visitors to join your Branded App by giving them an exclusive discount code for product purchases made on the app.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
