---
title: "Wix Forum: Managing Your Forum in the Wix App"
---

Manage your forum settings directly from your phone using the Wix app. From here you can manage categories and more.

### To manage your forum:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Forum**.  Choose what you want to do:

### I want to add a new category:

1. In the **Categories** tab, tap **Add New Category** to create a new category.
2. Add your category info:

- Add a category name
- Add a description
- Choose the type of category
- Add an image
- Choose if you want to set a different image for the app

### I want to manage existing categories

1. In the **Categories** tab, tap the** More Actions** icon  next to the category or subcategory you want to manage.
2. Select an option:

- Move a category/subcategory up or down
- Make a category into a subcategory
- Make a subcategory into a category
- Tap **Settings** to change the name, description, or image
- Delete a category/subcategory
