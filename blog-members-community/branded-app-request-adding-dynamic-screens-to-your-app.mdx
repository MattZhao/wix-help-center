---
title: "Branded App Request: Adding Dynamic Screens to Your App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add dynamic screens to your branded app. Dynamic screens, similar to [dynamic web pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages), allow content to be [displayed from a CMS (Content Management System) collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-managing-collection-content-from-your-live-site).

This feature would allow your branded app to efficiently handle large amounts of content while maintaining a consistent design and enhancing the user experience.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
