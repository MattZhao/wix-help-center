---
title: "Branded App Request: Navigation Tabs Within Screens"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add navigational tabs to the top of your branded app. This feature would allow visitors to tap tabs within specific screens to view categorized content.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
