---
title: "Spaces App Request: Syncing Website Pages with Screens in Your App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sync your site's page contents to specific screens in the Spaces app.

This feature would allow you automatically mirror the content from your website's pages with the screens in the Spaces app. Any changes made to your website would immediately appear in the relevant screens of the app. For example, you would be able to update a product page on your website and have the changes automatically reflected in a product page screen in the Spaces app.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
