---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Posting a Single Post in Multiple Groups"
---

Currently, it is not possible to post a single post in multiple groups simultaneously. This would allow site members to distribute the same content across multiple groups with a single action.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can manually copy and paste your post into each group you want it to appear in.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
