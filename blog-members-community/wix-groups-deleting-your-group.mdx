---
title: "Wix Groups: Deleting Your Group"
---

You can delete a group at any time from your Groups dashboard. This removes the group and any related content from your site.

> **Important:**
>
> It is not possible to restore a group once you delete it.

### To delete a group:

Go to [Groups](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Groups&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsocial-groups-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.Click the **Show More** icon  next to the relevant group.Click **Delete**.Click **Delete** in the pop-up to confirm.
