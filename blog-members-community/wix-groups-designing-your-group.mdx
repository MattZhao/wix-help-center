The Wix Groups app [adds two group-related pages to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-about-your-groups-pages). The **Groups page** shows all your groups and posts together. The **Group page** is for each separate group.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The group page is a dynamic element. Changes you make to it apply to the group page for every group on your site. This does not apply to content tabs, which are unique to each group.
> - Make sure to [add and set up Wix Groups](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-adding-and-setting-up) so you can follow the steps in this article.
> - [Publish your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site) after creating your group so the group page appears in your editor.

### Customizing your Group page

Your Group page uses the same layout and design for each individual group. Customize the design that's used by each group on this page. You can customize the group page from your editor, the Wix app, or while logged in as an admin on the live site.

Wix EditorStudio EditorLive SiteWix App
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Group page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Groups pages**.
3. Click **Group page**.
3. Click the Group element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the relevant tabs to customize your page:

- **Layout:** Click **General **to choose the cover image layout, height, and spacing. Click **Media** to choose the image gallery layout and the images per row.
- **Display:** Choose the tab you want to edit: **Discussions**, **Media** or** About**. Then select the checkbox next to each element you want to show.
- **Text: **Enter the placeholder text that appears in the field where members can write a post.
- **Design: **Customize the colors and fonts used in your header, text, buttons, links, background and borders. Click **Reset to original design** at the bottom if you need to revert your changes.

### Customizing your Groups page

Your Groups page displays all of your visible groups on one page. Choose what you want to include in the feed that shows posts from all your groups. You can hide the group list that displays all your visible groups or choose what shows in the list.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Groups page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Groups pages**.
3. Click **Groups**.
3. Click the Groups element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the relevant tabs to customize your page:

- **Layout:** Set a layout for the groups feed. Sidebar, List or Grid. Select the group cards layout and the number of cards per row.
- **Settings:** Decide how to sort your groups. Select the relevant option between: Recent activity, A-Z, or Member count.
- **Display:** Select the elements to display on the groups list and the feed's alignment.
- **Text:** Enter a custom text for the titles of your groups feed and groups list.
- **Design:** Click **Text Style** to change the colors and fonts for each element according to your site's design. Click **Buttons & Icons** to design your buttons and links for the regular and hover states. and click **Background & Borders** to customize the app background, cards and text fields.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about designing your group.

### Can I move the cover image to be in the middle?

in settings you can change how the cover image looks like, in the settings in editorcenter the image within its bounding box.

### Can I delete a group tab?

You can delete a tab only on the Wix app on your phone. From your dashboard you can rename it or hide it.

### Can all my tabs be custom tabs?

You can't make all your tabs custom ones. There are some tabs that are always there. You can create up to three custom tabs. If you'd like to be able to make more custom tabs, you can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-request-adding-more-than-3-custom-tabs-to-a-group).

### Can I highlight a specific tab?

You can't directly highlight a specific tab, but you can change the order of the tabs to put the one you want to emphasize first.

### Can I fully customize every element in the post?

You can't customize every single element in the post section. For example, the reaction buttons have a set design.

### Can I reorder the About, Members, and Topics sections on the right?

No. You can't reorder the About, Members, and Topics, sections on the right.

### Can I reorder the elements on the page to be right to left?

No. You cannot reorder the elements on the page to be right to left.

### Can I change the topics text design?

No. The topics design is set.

### What does the bodies and input design change?

The Bodies design change affects how empty states and certain containers look. For example, if a user isn't a member of a group, they might see empty state text or a container for group rules.
