---
title: "Wix Forum: Using Wix Forum on a Site with Members-Only Pages"
---

Member-only pages are pages that visitors need to sign up for in order to get access.

Site membership, forum membership, and membership in the new Wix Blog is the same. This means that once visitors sign up to your site using the Login Bar, they can access members pages and contribute to the forum.

Access to member-only pages, as well as your forum or new blog, is through the Login Bar. By default, it appears beside your site menu.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also:
> - Make forum categories accessible only to members. To learn more, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-a-members-only-category).
> - Edit permissions for a category so only admins and moderators can write posts. To learn more, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-a-category).
