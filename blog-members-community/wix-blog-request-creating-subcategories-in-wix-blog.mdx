---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Creating Subcategories in Wix Blog"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create subcategories under your main categories in the Wix Blog to help you organize and structure your content.

> **Tip:**
>
> [You can add new categories and manage existing ones](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-creating-and-managing-blog-categories) by renaming and reordering them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
