### General Issues in the Spaces app

Click an issue below to learn how to resolve it:

### I'm not seeing push notifications

If you are having trouble seeing push notifications in the Spaces app, try the troubleshooting steps below with your supported device.
1. Make sure you have enabled push notifications for the Spaces app in the general notification settings of your phone. This differs from one device to another.
[image: Spaces notifications screen]2.  Check your site notification settings in the Spaces by Wix app:
1. Open the Spaces app.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant site.
3. Tap **Manage Notifications**.
4. Tap the toggles next to each option to enable or disable notifications.*

*
**Note:** If you had both Spaces app and the Wix app downloaded and stopped receiving push notifications after uninstalling the Wix app, re-download the Wix app and try again.

### I'm not getting notified about live site visitors

If you aren't getting notified about site visitors, first make sure you've added [Wix Chat](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-adding-wix-chat-to-your-site) to your site. You won't be able to see live visitors without the [Wix Chat app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-troubleshooting#general-issues-in-the-wix-owner-app).

### I'm not able to find my site

If you have more than one site, some of them could be hidden from the site list. You can sort them using the site drop-down at the top.

1. Open the Spaces by Wix app.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.
3. Tap** Manage My Sites**.
4. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the site you want to manage from your site list.
5. Tap **Show in My Sites**.**

**[image: Screenshot of showing a site]
6. Tap **﻿Done**﻿ at the top right.

### I want to change the language of my app

Change the language of your Spaces app so you can navigate around it easily.

1. Open the Spaces by Wix app.
2. Tap **My Profile **   at the bottom.
3. Tap **App Language** under the **Settings**.
4. Tap the relevant language.
5. Tap **Save** at the top right.
6. Tap **Restart** for the changes to take effect.

**Note: **All the app content still appears in the language it was written in.

### I want to leave a site

If you no longer want to be a member of the Spaces app, you can delete your profile.

1. Open the Spaces by Wix app.
2. Tap and hold on to the site you no longer want to be a member on.
3. Tap **Leave**.
[image: Screenshot of leaving a site]**Note: **Leaving a site does not delete your sites or account. If you want to remove your Wix account completely and stop using Wix, there are 4 steps to follow. Learn more about [Closing Your Wix Account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/closing-your-wix-account).

### I want to cancel my pending request (for sites that require join approval)

1. Open the Spaces by Wix app.
2. Tap and hold on the** **site you want to cancel a request for.
3. Tap **Cancel Request**.

### I want to delete my account

Permanently delete your Wix account to erase all of your information from the Wix databases.  
**Important: **After completing this process, you can no longer use any of your Wix services, your account and all its data will be removed permanently, and Wix will not be able to restore your account or retrieve your data in the future.

1. Open the Spaces by Wix app.
2. Tap **My Profile**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Delete your account** under **Settings**.
4. Tap **Continue**.
5. Enter your details and tap **Continue**.

**Note: **This feature is currently available to iOS users only. Learn more about [iOS regulation and privacy policy](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#data-collection-and-storage) regarding apps and data deletion.

### Troubleshooting technical Issues

To avoid any issues with the Spaces app, make sure you are using a device that supports the app and that you're running the most updated version.

Follow the steps below to resolve any technical issues:

### Check that your app is up to date

1. Go to the Apple App Store (on iPhone) or Google Play (on Android).
2. Search for 'Spaces by Wix app.'
3. Click **Update** (if available).

**Note:** If there's no option to update, it means you're running the most up-to-date version of the app.

### Check that you're using a supported device

The Spaces by Wix app is supported on:
- **iPhone: **iOS 11 and up
- **Android:** 6.0 and up

### Log out of the Spaces app and log back in

1. Open the [Spaces app.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview)
2. Tap **My Profile**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Log Out **at the bottom.
4. Log back in using your Wix account credentials.
5. (If the issue isn't resolved) Restart your mobile device.

**Additionally, for Android devices:**
1. Long press the app icon on your device's home screen.
2. Tap **App Info**.
3. Tap **Storage**.
4. Tap **Clear Data**.
5. Open the Spaces app and log in again.

#### Still need help?

If the problem persists, please [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact) with the information below:

A description of the steps you've taken so that we can recreate the issue on our end  A [screenshot](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-screenshot-to-a-ticket?tabs=Mobile-device) of the issue  (For notification issues): Specify which notifications you're not receiving  The manufacturer, model and operating system of your mobile device (e.g. Apple iPhone 6s, Samsung Galaxy S4)  The type of connection you're using (e.g., 3G, WiFi) and the name of your mobile network (if you are using a 3G or 4G connection)  Your location  The version of the Spaces by Wix app you're using. To find this:

1. Open the [Spaces app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview).
2. Tap **My Profile**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Help & Feedback **under** Support**.
4. Tap **About the Spaces App**.
5. Scroll down to the bottom of the screen and locate the version number in light gray (e.g. Version **2.58796.0.**).
