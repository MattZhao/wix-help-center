title: "Wix Forum Request: Creating Multiple Forums on One Site"
---

We are still working on the possibility of creating multiple forums on one site, however, while we do, we are happy to announce a work around for this feature, that lets you have multiple forum categories that have different members permissions. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-creating-private-forum-categories).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
