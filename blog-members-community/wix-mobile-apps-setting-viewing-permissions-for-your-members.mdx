Your site on the member apps includes various screens that keep your content organized (e.g. Home, Events, Classes, etc.). Members use the navigation menu at the top to browse through your screens.

You can set permissions for each screen, so that only those members with specific badges or pricing plans can access them.

### Setting viewing permissions for your screens

Decide who can view each screen on the Wix member apps. Give exclusive access to certain members with specific badges or pricing plans. For example, you can restrict a screen that features a special event or a private sale.

#### To set viewing permissions:

Go to your site in the Wix app.    Tap **Manage** at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.  Tap **Edit**.    Tap **Screens**.   Tap the** More Actions** icon  next to the relevant screen.  Tap **Set screen permissions**.  Select who can view this screen:

- **All Members: **All members that visit your site have screen access.
- **Members with Badges:** ﻿Choose which badge holders can access the screen, or tap **Create New Badge** to [create a new badge](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-and-managing-member-badges).
- **Members with Pricing Plans: **﻿Choose which Pricing Plan holders can access the screen, or tap **Create New Plan** to [create a new plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-plans-in-the-wix-owner-app).

Click a question below to learn more about managing permissions for each screen.

### What do members see when they don't have permission to view a screen?

Members receive a message informing them that they don't have permission to access the screen and to request permission from you (the site owner).

[image: A screenshot of the message members see when they don't have permissions to access a screen.]

### What happens after a member requests permissions to view a screen?

A chat message notifies you that a member is seeking permission to view a screen that you set permissions for. 
You can grant the member permissions by [assigning them the relevant badge](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-creating-and-managing-member-badges#assigning-a-badge-to-a-member) or advising them to purchase the specific pricing plan. If needed, you can also [add a member manually to a pricing plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-selling-plans-offline-using-the-wix-app).

### Is there a difference between my site pages and the screens in the app?

Yes. Screens on the Wix member apps are structured differently than the pages on your site. 
If you restrict access to one of your site's pages, you also need to restrict the screen that shows this information on the Wix member apps (via the Wix mobile app).
