---
title: "Wix App: Accessing the Quick Actions Menu"
---

Quickly access your most used apps from the quick actions menu in the Wix app. The options available in the menu depend on the apps you've added to your site and the actions you use the most.

### To access the quick actions menu:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap the **Add** icon  at the bottom.  Tap the relevant action you want to work on.
