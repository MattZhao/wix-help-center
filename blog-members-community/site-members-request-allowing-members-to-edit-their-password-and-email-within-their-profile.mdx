---
title: "Site Members Request: Allowing Members to Edit Their Password and Email within Their Profile"
---

We are excited to announce that site members can now [edit their password and email from the member profile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-changing-login-details-as-a-member).

Site members have full control over their member login information and can quickly update their login details without the need to contact the site owner.

Site owners can still [reset members' passwords](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-resetting-your-members-passwords) and [change members' login email addresses](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-changing-your-members-login-email) for site members.
