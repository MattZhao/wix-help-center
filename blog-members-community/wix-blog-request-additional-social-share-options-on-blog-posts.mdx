---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Additional Social Share Options on Blog Posts"
---

Currently, it is not possible to [share blog posts on social media](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-sharing-a-blog-post-on-social-media) platforms other than Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
