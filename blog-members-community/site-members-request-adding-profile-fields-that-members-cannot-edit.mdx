---
title: "Site Members Request: Adding Profile Fields that Members Cannot Edit"
---

Currently, site members can edit [any field you add](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-customizing-your-member-profile-fields) to their profiles. It is not possible to create fields with information that your member isn't allowed to edit from their account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
