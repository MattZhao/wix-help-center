### Sending an email campaign for a blog post

Create your email campaign directly from your dashboard.

#### To send an email campaign:

[Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More actions** **icon  next to the relevant post.  Select **Share Post**.  Click **Create Email Campaign**.

(Optional) [Customize the email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-the-wix-email-marketing-campaign-editor) using the available options (e.g. choose a new theme, background, or edit the text).  Click** Next **at the top right.  Select the relevant contacts you want to send your email to, or enter email addresses directly.  Click **Next **at top right.  (If necessary) Click **Edit **next to the relevant option to edit your campaign details: 

- **Subject Line:** Enter the subject line of the email and add the preview text.
- **Sender details:** Enter the name that the email is sent from and the reply-to email address.

**Note:** If you have not yet [confirmed your email address](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-confirming-your-email-address), click **Confirm Now**.
- **Email recipients:** Edit and review the emails before you send your campaign.
    Select a send option:

- **Send immediately: **Click **Send Now**.
- **Send later:**

1. Click **Schedule**.
2. Select the send day.
3. Select the send time.
4. Click **Schedule Campaign**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to find out more information about email campaigns:

### Can I send a test email?

Yes, you can send a test email to your email address to make sure everything looks as it should.
1. Click **Send Test Email **on the left.

- Enter the email address you want to send the test to.
- Click **Send**.
