---
title: "Wix Forum: Adding Captions, Alt Text, and Links to Forum Images"
---

You or your forum members can add image captions and metadata. You can also turn an image into a link.

> **Tip:**
>
> Forum admins cannot customize images uploaded by other forum members.

### To customize image settings:

Go to the forum in your live site.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant post.   Click **Edit Post**.  Click the image in the post.  Click the **Settings **icon .  Enter a caption in the **Image Caption** field.  Enter [alt text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/optimizing-your-images-and-galleries-for-seo-alt-text) in the **Alt text** field.  Enter a web address (URL) in the **Link** field.  Enable or disable the **Spoiler alert** toggle.  Click **Save**.
