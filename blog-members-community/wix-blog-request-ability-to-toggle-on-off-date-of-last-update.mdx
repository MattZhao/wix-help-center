---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Ability to Toggle on/off Date of Last Update"
---

Currently, it is not possible to toggle on or off the date of the last update to a blog post on mobile. As a workaround, you can disable the mobile display settings in your post settings.

### To disable the mobile display settings:

Go to your Editor.   Click the mobile Editor icon  at the top right.  Click your blog in the mobile Editor.   Click **Settings**.   Click **Display**.   Click the **Enable mobile settings** toggle to disable it.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
