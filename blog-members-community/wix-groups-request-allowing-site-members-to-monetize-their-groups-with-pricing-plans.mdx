---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Allowing Site Members to Monetize Their Groups with Pricing Plans"
---

Currently, it is not possible for site members to monetize groups they create by connecting [pricing plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-an-overview) to their groups.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
