---
title: "Site Members Request: Creating Custom Content for Individual Site Members"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create custom content for individual site members. For example, private pages showing each member their own personalized content or information that only they can see. This feature could be used to leave comments for a site member, and allow them to leave notes for you too.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
