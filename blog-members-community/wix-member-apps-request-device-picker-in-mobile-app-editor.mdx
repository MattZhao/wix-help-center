---
title: "Wix Member Apps Request: Device Picker in Mobile App Editor"
---

Currently, the Mobile App Editor does not support switching between iOS and Android views. This feature would enable you to toggle between device views while editing, allowing you to see how your designs look on each platform.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
