---
title: "Wix App: Viewing Your Site"
---

You can view your live site from the Wix app, to get a feel for how your visitors experience it and see how it looks before going live.

Once your site is published, tap **Share URL** to send the link directly to people so they can check out your site.

### To view your site in the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage** at the bottom.  Tap **Mobile App**.   Tap** View **to see your site.

> **Note:**
>
> If your site was built using Wix ADI, you can edit your site directly from the Wix app.
