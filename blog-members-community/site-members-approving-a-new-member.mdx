title: "Site Members: Approving a New Member"
---

The Members Area lets you choose if new members get approved automatically, or manually after your review. To approve or block pending requests, head over to the member list in your site's dashboard.

**In this article, learn how to approve a new member:**

[From the member list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/approving-a-site-member#approving-a-site-member-from-the-member-list)  [After viewing the member's info](https://support.wix.com/en/article/approving-a-site-member#approving-a-site-member-after-viewing-their-info)  [Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/approving-a-site-member#faqs)

---

### Approving a site member from the member list

As you're viewing your member list in the dashboard, you can approve new members in a single click from the Pending tab.

#### To approve a site member from the member list:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.   Click **Pending** next to **Site Members**.  Click **Approve** next to the relevant member(s).

---

### Approving a site member after viewing their info

Need to learn a little more about a new member before approving them? You can view the information they signed up with first, and then make your decision.

#### To approve a member after viewing their info:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.   Click **Pending** next to **Site Members**.  Click the relevant member to open their Contact Card.  View the details the member signed up with.   When ready, click **Approve** under **Member Info**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about approving a new member.

### What happens once I approve a new member?

An email notification is sent to new members as soon as you approve the request.

### What happens if I block a pending member?

This ensures they can't sign up to your Member's Area again in the future, using the same email address.

### What if I no longer want to a member to access my Member's Area?

You need to [block the member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-blocking-or-deleting-a-member) to prevent them from accessing your members-only pages – not delete them. 
Deleting a member means they can sign up to your Member's Area again in the future.
