---
title: "Wix Blog: Adding Alt Text to an Image or GIF to a Blog Post"
---

Help Google index your blog post images and GIFs by adding alt text.

> **Note:**
>
> It is currently not possible to add Alt text to Cover Images

### To add alt text to blog images and GIFs:

[Add an image / GIF to a post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-media-to-a-blog-post).Click the image / GIF.Click the **Settings **icon.

Enter an alt text description.Click **Done**.

### To add alt text to an image in a gallery:

Add a gallery.  Add images to the gallery.  Click the gallery.  Click the **Settings **icon.

  Click the **Organize Media **tab.  Select an image.  Click **Edit**.  Enter an alt text description.  Click **Done**.
