---
title: "Wix Blog: Limiting Your Blog to Paying Members"
---

Want to make your blog accessible only to paying members? You can do so using the Pricing Plans app. This way, only members who purchase the relevant pricing plan(s) can access your content. [Learn how to limit blog access to site members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-using-the-wix-blog-on-a-site-with-members-only-pages)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> This article shows you how to limit your blog so that only people who purchased a pricing plan can access the feed and all of your posts. Before you begin, make sure that you have:
> 1. [Added the Wix blog to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-setting-up-your-blog)
> 2. [Added the Pricing Plans app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-adding-the-pricing-plans-app)
> 3. [Created the plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-creating-a-one-time-payment-plan) you want to offer on your site

### To limit your blog to paying members:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Pages**** & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Blog Pages**.
3. Hover over the **Post** page and click the **More Actions **icon .
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Permissions** tab.
6. Select **Members o****nly**.
7. Select **Only selected members or paying customers**.
8. Click the **Edit** icon  under **Sele****ct from your Pricing Plans**.

1. Select which pricing plans should have access and click **Apply**.
