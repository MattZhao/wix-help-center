title: "Wix Member Apps: Managing Your App from the Wix App"
---

Use the Wix app to manage all aspects of your site on your Wix member apps (the Spaces by Wix app and Branded app).

You can customize what your site looks like on the member apps, update your business information, and add sections such as a forum or store to encourage your members to engage and interact.

You can also invite contacts to become members of your site, and communicate with them directly from the Wix app. Additionally, you can assign different roles and permissions to your members so that they can access exactly what they need.

> **Important:**
>
> Any changes you make in the Wix app also affect your site.

**In this article, learn how to:**

### Customize your site on the Wix member apps

From the Wix app, you can choose how your site is displayed on the Wix Member Apps by customizing the content and layout. You can [show or hide elements, edit the text and reorder the tabs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-site-on-the-member-apps).

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage ** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Customize**.
5. Customize your mobile app appearance by editing the sections.

---

### Manage your site's apps

From the Wix app, you can add apps to your site to increase your members' engagement with your business. Choose a forum or blog to get people interested in your content and become more involved, or even add a store to sell your merchandise. It's completely up to you and your business needs. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-adding-apps-to-your-site)

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage ** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Manage Apps**.
4. Choose from the following options:

- **Manage an app:** Tap an app to change its settings.
- **Add an app: **Tap** Manage Apps **and tap **Add** next to the relevant app.
- **Remove an app: **Tap **Manage Apps** and tap **Remove** next to the relevant app.

**Important:**
- After adding an app from the Wix app, you need to go to the Editor and add the pending app to your site.
- After adding an app from the Wix app, you need to go to the editor and add the pending app to your site.ve it.

### Edit your business information

Make sure that your business information is always correct and up to date by managing it on the go, directly from the Wix app. You can update your contact details, preferred currency as well as your time zone.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Business Settings **under** Settings.**
4. Tap **General Info.**
5. Edit the relevant details.
6. Tap **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> Your business information is visible in the Contact Us section on the mobile member apps. You can choose what information to show or hide by customizing the section.** **[Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-site-on-the-member-apps)

### Manage your contacts

From the Wix app, you can add new contacts, view details of existing contacts, and invite new contacts to become members of your site on the mobile apps.

You can also communicate with them directly from the app using chat, SMS, Facebook or email.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Customers & Leads.**
4. Tap **Contacts.**
5. Do one of the following:

- **Add a new contact:** Tap **+ Add New Contact **and enter their details.
- **View a contact: **Tap the contact's name.
- **Edit a contact: **Tap the relevant contact's name, tap the **Info** tab and tap **Edit**.
- **Delete a contact: **Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to a contact's name and tap **Delete**.
- **Invite a contact to become a member: **Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to a contact's name and tap **Invite**.
- **Communicate with a contact: **Tap the **More Actions** icon    next to a contact's name and tap **Send Message**.

### Manage roles & permissions

Invite people to become collaborators on your site and assign them with special roles and permissions. Choosing [roles and permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-inviting-and-managing-contributors#inviting-site-collaborators) for your collaborators makes it easy to bring in others to help manage your site.

You can also change the join approval settings for members and the visibility of the member list on the member apps.

### Show me how

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Roles & Permissions **under **Settings.**
4. Manage the following:

- **Collaborators:** Tap **Collaborators** to invite people to work on your site and assign them the relevant roles and permissions.
- **Join Approval:** Enable the toggle to allow members to join your site only with your approval.
- **Members Visibility: **Enable the toggle to display your members list, number of members, and social features on your site in the member apps.
