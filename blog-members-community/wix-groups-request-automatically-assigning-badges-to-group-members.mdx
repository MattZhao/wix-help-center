---
title: "Wix Groups Request: Automatically Assigning Badges to Group Members"
---

Currently, it is not possible to automatically assign badges to members of your group. For example, you may want to reward members with a badge for reaching group activity milestones. This can be publishing a specific number of posts to the group or engaging with other member posts.

> **Check your member engagement:**
>
> Discover which group members are the most active with the content in your group. Use [Wix Analytics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-about-groups-reports#top-group-members) to see the following: 
> - Number of comments each group member wrote.
> - Number of posts each group member published.
> - Number of reactions (emojis) made by each member.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
