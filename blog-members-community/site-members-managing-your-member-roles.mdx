---
title: "Site Members: Managing Your Member Roles"
---

After [creating member roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-member-roles), manage them at any time from your site's dashboard. You can edit role details, assign them to members and delete them if they're no longer necessary.

### How do I access my site's member roles?

1. [Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/home/).
2. Click **Contacts** on the left.
3. Click **Site** **Members **on the left.
4. Click **Manage Member Roles** at the top right.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Edit a role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-your-member-roles#editing-a-member-role)  [Assign a role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-your-member-roles#assigning-a-member-role)   [Delete a role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-your-member-roles#deleting-a-member-role)

---

### Editing a member role

Every role has a name, color and description that you can edit to your needs.

#### To edit a role:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.   Click **Manage Member Roles** at the top right.  Click the More Actions icon  on the role you want to edit.   Click **Edit Roles**.

Edit the role's name, color or description.  Click **Save**.

---

### Assigning a member role

You can always assign more members to an existing role, and remove members who should no longer have it.

#### To assign members to a role:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.   Click **Manage Member Roles** at the top right.  Click the More Actions icon  on the role you want to edit.   Click **Edit Roles**.  Click the **Members** tab.  Click **+ Add Members**.

Select the checkbox next to the member(s) that should have this role.

**Tip: **You can use the search bar to find a member by their name or email address.    When ready, click **Add**.

> **Removing a member from a role?**
>
> Click the More Actions icon  next to a member, then click **Remove**.

---

### Deleting a member role

You can delete roles that are no longer needed in your Members Area. This means the members who had this role lose their page permissions.

> **Important:**
>
> - When you delete a role, it is removed from any member that had the role. The member also loses access to any pages that were included in the role.
> - If you restricted a certain page to one role and then delete that role, the page becomes available to all members. If you want to keep the page restricted, you'll need to [change the permissions of your member roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-permissions-of-a-member-role).

#### To delete a role:

[Go to Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/member-permissions/members) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Member Roles** at the top right.  Click the More Actions** **icon  on the role you want to edit.   Click **Delete**.  Click **Delete** to confirm.
