title: "Wix Blog: Enhancing Your Blog with Free Add-Ons"
---

Maximize your blog's potential and provide a seamless browsing experience for your visitors with Wix Blog's selection of complimentary add-ons.

**Keep your readers updated** by adding an [RSS Feed button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-an-rss-button-to-your-site), which allows them to subscribe to your blog updates.   **Streamline your content **with a [Category Menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-customizing-a-category-menu), which categorizes your posts, and an [Archive Menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-customizing-an-archive-menu), which displays older content in a convenient location.   **Enhance your blog's presence** with a [Post List element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-setting-up-a-post-list), which displays snippets of your posts in other areas of your site, and a [Tag Cloud element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-setting-up-tags), which organizes all your tags in one place.

### To access the blog add-ons:

Wix EditorWix Studio
1. Select the page you want the add-on to appear.
2. Click the Add icon  on the left of your editor.
3. Click **Blog**.
4. Scroll down and choose an element to add.
5. Drag the element to position it on the page.
