---
title: "Wix Blog: Changing the Author of a Blog Post"
---

As admin of your blog, you can re-assign your published posts or drafts to anyone who has writing privileges:

Someone you have set as a writer. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-writers-and-editors-in-wix-blog)  A site collaborator with an Admin role. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site)  A site collaborator with a Back Office Manager role. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site)

After ownership of the post is switched to a different writer, you can still access it, edit it, and do anything you could do before.

### To switch the author of a post:

Do one of the following:

- Click to edit a published post or draft.
- Create a new post.
    Click** Settings**.  Select who you want to re-assign the post to from the **Author** drop-down menu.  Do one of the following:

- **New Post:**

1. Click **Save**.
2. Click **Publish**.
- **Edited post:**

1. Click **Save & Publish**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also add these manually in your blog settings. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-editing-author-names-and-images#editing-author-details-for-writers-added-in-your-blog-settings) to learn how.
