---
title: "Wix Forum: Managing Forum Categories and Subcategories in the Wix App"
---

Help your readers find exactly what they're looking for by organizing your posts into categories and subcategories.

### To manage forum categories and subcategories:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Forum**.  Manage your categories from the **Categories** tab using the following options:

### Create new categories and subcategories

1. Tap **Create New Category**.
2. Enter a category name, description, and image.
3. Tap **Create**.
4. To turn a category into a subcategory:

1. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant category.
2. If necessary, tap **Move Category Up** or **Move Category Down** to place the category directly under the relevant category.
3. Tap the **More Actions **icon  and select **Make Subcategory**.

**Tip: **To change a subcategory into a category, tap the **More Actions ** and **Set as Main Category**.

### Reorder categories and subcategories

1. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant category/subcategory.
2. Tap **Move Category Up** or **Move Category Down**.

### Delete a category or subcategory

1. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant category/subcategory.
2. Tap **Delete**.
3. Tap **Delete **to confirm.

**Notes: **Posts in this category/subcategory are deleted. This action cannot be undone.
