title: "Wix Blog: Hiring a Blog Writer"
---

Having a professional blog writer come onboard is a great way to optimize your content. With Wix Marketplace you can hire a trusted writer to help create new content, share some ideas and suggestions, and provide valuable feedback.

### To hire a blog writer:

Go to the [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/blog) tab on your site's dashboard.Click **More Actions** at the top left. Click **Hire a Blog Writer**.Choose what type of content you need. Select your site's publication status. Choose your preference.Select your site language. Enter your contact details.Click **Submit**.

> **What happens next?**
>
> Once you've submitted all the relevant details, you will be matched with a blog writer. Remember, you are not committed to anything at this stage. Wix Marketplace will contact you to with some options and you can decide if you want to proceed.
