---
title: "How Members Join Your Site on the Wix Member Apps"
---

Invite your site members and community to download one of the Wix member apps (Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix, Dine by Wix or Branded App by Wix), whichever is relevant to your business. By joining your site on a member app, they can get the best mobile experience when interacting with your business.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[How members receive your invite code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/how-members-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps#how-members-receive-your-invite-code)  [How members use the invite code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/how-members-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps#how-members-use-your-invite-code)

---

### How members receive your invite code

When you [send your invite code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps) to your members, it includes a link to download the relevant member app along with the code to join.

> **If you need to find the invite code:**
>
> Tap **Site & App**  at the bottom of the Wix app. The invite code is displayed next to **Invite Code**.

---

### How members use your invite code

Once your members receive the link or invite code, they can follow the steps below to join your site on your member app.

### Show me how members can use the invite code to join my site on Spaces by Wix

Your members can find the invite code in the text message or email sent to them.They should:
1. Open the Spaces by Wix app.
2. Tap the Profile icon at the bottom.
3. Tap **Join a site**.
4. Enter the invite code and tap **Join**.

### Show me how clients can use the invite code to join my site on the Dine by Wix app

Your clients can find the invite code in the text message or email sent to them.They should:
1. Open the Dine by Wix app.
2. Tap **Add Restaurant with Invite Code**.
3. Enter the invite code and tap Join.

### Show me how customers can use the invite code to join my site  on the Fit by Wix app

Your customers can find the invite code in the text message or email sent to them. They should:
1. Open the Fit by Wix app.
2. Tap the Profile icon at the top.
3. Tap **Join with an Invite Code**.
4. Enter the code and tap **Join**.
