---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Displaying Images Bigger than 300KB for a Blog Post on WhatsApp"
---

Currently, it is not possible to show a preview cover image on WhatsApp if it is over 300KB. Images larger than 300KB do not appear, only the post title is displayed.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn how to [add cover images and share your post on social media](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-sharing-a-blog-post-on-social-media#sharing-a-blog-post).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
