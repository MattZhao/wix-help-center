---
title: "Wix Portfolio: Adding a Project Page to a Site Menu"
---

Showcase and attract more visitors to a portfolio project by adding it to a site menu. By linking your project to a menu, site visitors can click into your work from any page on your site.

### To add a project page to a site menu:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor.](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26)
2. Click the menu you want to add a link to.
3. Click **Manage Menu**.
4. Click **Add Menu Item** at the bottom of the page.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to add an item to your menu.]
1. Select **Link**.
2. Select **Page**.
3. Click the **Which page? **drop-down and select **Project pages**.
4. Click the **Which item? **drop-down and select the project you want to link to. 

**Note:** This option only appears if your projects are connected to a collection, and** **you have more than one project within that collection.
5. Click **Done**.
6. Update the name of the link as it will display on your site menu.
7. Click **Done**.
[image: A screenshot of a project page selected.]

**Note:** This option only appears if your projects are connected to a collection, and** **you have more than one project within that collection.

10. Update the name of the link as it will display on your site menu.
11. Press **Enter** on your keyboard.
