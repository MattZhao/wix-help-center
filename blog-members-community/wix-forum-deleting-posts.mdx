---
title: "Wix Forum: Deleting Posts"
---

As an admin of your forum, you can delete any posts you wish to remove. Other forum members can delete only their own posts.

### To delete a forum post:

On your live site, log into the forum using the email address associated with your Wix account.Click the **Show More** iconon a post.Click **Delete Post**.Click **Delete** to confirm.
