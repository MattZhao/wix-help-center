---
title: "Site Members Request: Impersonating Members to Preview Your Site"
---

Your site may look different to certain members due to factors such as [their member role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-your-member-roles) or [the pricing plan they've subscribed to](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-making-site-pages-members-only). Currently, it is not possible to impersonate a member to preview what your site looks like to them. It is only possible to view your site according to your own role.

However, you can create new members and add them to the role you want to test so that you can see your site as they see it.

### To create a member role for testing:

Go to your live site.  Sign up as a new member.  [Add the new member to the role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-your-member-roles) that you want to test.  Visit your live site as that member to see what the site looks like for them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
