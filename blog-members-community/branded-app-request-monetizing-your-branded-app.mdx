---
title: "Branded App Request: Monetizing Your Branded App"
---

Currently in the Branded App, it is not possible to charge people for downloading the app. You are able to sell goods and services inside your app [with Apple in-app purchase](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-setting-up-apple-in-app-purchase).

Monetizing your branded app would allow you to offer the app for a price as well as connect your app with Google Ad Sense or other ad platforms.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
