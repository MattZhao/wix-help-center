title: "Wix Member Apps: Managing Your App's Screens"
---

Similar to how your site is made up of pages, your member app (e.g. Spaces by Wix, Branded app) is made up of screens. Each screen in your app serves a unique function, allowing your app members to interact with you and your business.

> **Note:**
>
> Your screens also make up your navigation menu. Learn how to [customize your navigation menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-apps-navigation-menu) by reordering the screens or adding or removing screens from the menu.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Add new screens to your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-managing-your-apps-screens#adding-new-screens-to-your-app)  [Rename a screen](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-managing-your-apps-screens#renaming-a-screen)  [Delete a screen](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-member-apps-managing-your-apps-screens#deleting-a-screen)

### Adding new screens to your app

Your app comes with a few default screens depending on what business apps you have on your site. You can add additional screens to expand your app's functionality. You can choose to include or exclude these screens from your navigation menu.

DashboardWix App
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click **Screens** on the left.
5. Choose where to add the new screen:

- **Screens in navigation:** Click **+ Add ** next to **Screens in navigation **to add the new screen to the navigation menu.
- **Screens not in navigation:** Click **+ Add **next to **Screens not in navigation **to add the new screen and keep it hidden from the menu. You can add it to your app's menu later, if needed.
[image: A screenshot demonstrating how to add new screens]
1. Type a name for the screen and press **Enter **on your keyboard.
2. Click **Save & Publish** at the top right.
Tip:To reorder screens that appear in the navigation menu, click and drag the screens up or down the list. 
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Site & App**.
4. Tap **Customize **under the relevant mobile app (e.g. Spaces by Wix, Branded app).
5. Tap **Screens** from the bottom menu.
6. Tap **Add New Screen **at the bottom.
7. Enter a name for the screen.
8. (Optional) Enable the **Hide screen from navigation toggle** to exclude the new screen from your menu. You can disable it again later when / if you want the screen to appear in the menu.
9. Tap **Save**.
[image: A screenshot demonstrating how to add new screens from the Wix Owner App]

### Renaming a screen

You can edit the name of a screen to change how it appears on your navigation menu.

DashboardWix App
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click **Screens** on the left.
5. Hover over the screen you want to rename and click the **More Actions** icon .
6. Select **Rename**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to rename a screen]
1. Type a name for the screen and press **Enter** on your keyboard.
2. Click **Save & Publish** at the top right.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Site & App**.
4. Tap **Customize **under the relevant mobile app (e.g. Spaces by Wix, Branded app).
5. Tap **Screens** from the bottom menu.
6. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the screen you want to rename.
7. Tap **Edit screen name**.
8. Enter a new name for the screen.
9. Tap **Save**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to rename a screen in your app]

### Deleting a screen

Remove screens from your app if you no longer need them.

> **Tip:**
>
> Deleting a screen also deletes all elements on the screen. If you prefer not to delete the screen entirely, you can [hide it from your navigation menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-apps-navigation-menu#hiding-a-screen-from-your-navigation-menu).

DashboardWix App
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant tab (e.g. **Spaces by Wix app**, **Branded Mobile App**).
3. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
4. Click **Screens** on the left.
5. Hover over the screen you want to remove and click the **More Actions** icon .
6. Click **Delete**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to delete a screen]
1. Click **Delete **to confirm.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Site & App**.
4. Tap **Customize **under the relevant mobile app (e.g. Spaces by Wix, Branded app).
5. Tap **Screens** from the bottom menu.
6. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the screen you want to delete.
7. Tap **Delete**.
[image: A screenshot showing how to delete a screen]
