---
title: "Wix Blog: Adding Videos to Posts Using the Wix App"
---

Engage your readers by incorporating videos directly into your blog posts using the Wix app. Whether you're sharing tutorials, vlogs, or visual content, adding videos can enhance the impact of your posts and keep your audience interested.

### To add videos to blog posts using the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Blog**.   Choose what you want to do:

### Add a video to an existing post

1. Tap **Posts**.
2. Tap the** More Actions** icon  next to the relevant post.
3. Tap **View Post**.
4. Tap the** More Actions** icon  at the top right.
5. Tap **Edit Post**.
6. Tap the body of the post.
7. Tap the **Add** icon  at the bottom left.
8. Tap **Video**.
9. Choose how you want to upload your video:

- **All Media****:**** **Select the media file from your Media Manager and tap **Next**.
- **Create video:** Create your own custom video or tap **Start from Scratch**.
- **URL:** Enter the URL to the video and tap **Add**.
- **Device:** Choose your media file on your mobile device and tap **Upload**.
- **Free by Wix:** Choose a free video file from Wix and tap **Next**.
10. Tap **Next**.
11. Tap **Next**.
12. Choose what you want to do:

- Tap **Save as draft**.
- Tap **Save and publish**.

### Add a video to a new post

1. Tap **Posts**.
2. Tap **+ ****Create New Post**.
3. Add the post title and content.
4. Tap the **Add** icon  at the bottom left.
5. Tap **Video**.
6. Choose how you want to upload your video:

- **All Media: **Select the media file from your Media Manager and tap **Next**.
- **Create video:** Create your own custom video or tap **Start from Scratch**.
- **URL:** Enter the URL to the video and tap **Add**.
- **Device:** Choose your media file on your mobile device and tap **Upload**.
- **Free by Wix:** Choose a free video file from Wix and tap **Next**.
7. Tap **Next**.
8. Tap **Next**.
9. Choose what you want to do:

- Tap **Save as draft**.
- Tap **Save and publish**.
