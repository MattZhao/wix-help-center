---
title: "Wix Studio App: Inviting People to Collaborate on a Site"
---

Invite teammates to collaborate on a site from the Wix Studio app. When you invite them, you can choose their specific role and permissions depending on their expertise.

### To invite people to collaborate on a site:

Go to your relevant workspace in the Wix Studio app.  Tap **Sites **  at the bottom.  Tap the More Actions icon  next to the relevant site.  Tap **Invite Collaborators**.   Tap** Invite Others**.   Enter the email address of the person you're inviting.  Select the checkbox next to the relevant role under **Choose Roles**.

**Tip:** [Create a custom role on your desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators) for your collaborators.   Tap **Send**.
