---
title: "Wix Blog: Customizing Sender Details for 'New Post' Notification Emails"
---

You can customize your sender details that appear in your new post notification emails (From Name and Reply-to Email). Once you've customized these fields, they are saved and used every time a new post notification email is sent.

> **Note:**
>
> Changing your sender details requires a quick verification. This is to ensure the details provided are correct. Once you've entered your "from name" and a "reply-to email", a verification code is sent to the email address you entered.

### To customize your sender details:

Go to your [Email Marketing Dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/shoutout/).  Click **Edit Details** under **Sender Details**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you did not receive a verification code, check your spam folder before clicking **Send again**.
