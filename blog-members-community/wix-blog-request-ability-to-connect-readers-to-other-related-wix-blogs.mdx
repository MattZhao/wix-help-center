---
title: "Wix Blog Request: Ability to Connect Readers to Other Related Wix Blogs"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display a list of Wix blogs that have a similar theme to your blog (e.g. if you have a travel site to display other Wix blogs related to travel).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
