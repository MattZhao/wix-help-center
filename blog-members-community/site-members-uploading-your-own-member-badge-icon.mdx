---
title: "Site Members: Uploading Your Own Member Badge Icon"
---

Upload SVG files to personalize your member badges.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To use member badges, you must install a [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) on your site.

### To upload your own member badge icon:

Go to [Site Members](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Members%20List&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/member-permissions/members). Click **More Actions** at the top right and click **Manage Badges**.Click the** Show More **icon  on the badge you want to change, or click **New Badge**.Click **Add Image **under **Icon.**Click **Upload Media**.

Click **Upload from Computer** and select the file from your computer.Click **Add to Page**.

> **File guidelines:**
>
> - You can only upload .SVG files up to 250KB for member badges.
> - We recommend that you use a simple icon with minimal details and colors so that it looks good at a size of 12px.
