---
title: "Wix Apps Request: Integrating Woocommerce with Wix"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add the Woocommerce app to your site.

As a workaround, you can create a professional online store with [Wix Stores](https://manage.wix.com/app-market/wix-stores/), using custom pages where you can manage your orders and finances—from a single platform.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
