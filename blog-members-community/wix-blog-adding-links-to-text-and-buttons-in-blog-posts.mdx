title: "Wix Blog: Adding Links to Text and Buttons in Blog Posts"
---

Linking to specific posts is a great way to direct visitors to a blog from anywhere on a site. You can link posts by adding a link to a piece of text or by adding a link to a button.

**In this article, learn how to link a ****blog post to a:**

[Text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-links-to-text-and-buttons-in-blog-posts#linking-text-to-a-blog-post)  [Button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-links-to-text-and-buttons-in-blog-posts#linking-a-button-to-a-blog-post)

---

### Linking text to a blog post

You can link text to a specific blog post.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Retrieve the live URL of the blog post:

1. Go to the live site.
2. Open the relevant blog post.
3. Copy the URL.
2. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
3. Click the text you want to link.
4. Click **Edit text**.
5. Highlight the relevant text.
6. Click the **Link** icon .
7. Select **Web Address**.
8. Paste the blog post URL in the **What's the web address (URL)?** field.
9. Select whether the external URL opens in a **New Window** or in the **Current Window**.
10. Click **Done**.

### Linking a button to a blog post

You can add a button that links readers to a specific blog post.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Retrieve the live URL of the blog post:

1. Go to the live site.
2. Open the relevant blog post.
3. Copy the URL.
2. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26) and navigate to the relevant page.
3. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
4. Click **Button**.
5. Click the button you want to add, or drag it to the relevant location.
6. Click the **Link** icon .
7. Select **Web Address**.
8. Paste the blog post URL in the **What's the web address (URL)?** field.
9. Select whether the external URL opens in a **New Window** or in the **Current Window**.
10. Click **Done**.
