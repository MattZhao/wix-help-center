---
title: "Wix Blog: Writing Blog Posts with Right-to-Left Justification"
---

You can write posts in Arabic or Hebrew, languages that require right justification. All you need to do is to select Hebrew or Arabic as your blog language.

> **Note:**
>
> We currently support only two right-to-left languages, Arabic and Hebrew.

### To select Hebrew or Arabic as your blog language:

Go to your site's [General Info tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Edit%20Site%20Info&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/).Scroll down to the **Regional Settings** section.Click the **Language** drop-down menu and select Arabic or Hebrew.

Note that in the Post Manager, the placeholder text is always in English. Simply start composing your post in Arabic or Hebrew, and the text will automatically align to the right.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also change the language in your Member's Area pages. The language settings are in the **Settings** tabs.
