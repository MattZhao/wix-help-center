---
title: "Wix Blog: Disabling the Reading Time Stamp on Posts"
---

All of your blog posts display how long it should take to read your posts, along with the date.

### To remove the reading time stamp from blog posts:

Click the Wix Blog app in your Editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Display **tab.  Deselect the **Date and reading time **checkbox.

> **Note:**
>
> It is not possible to remove the reading time without removing the date.
