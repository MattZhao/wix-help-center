---
title: "Wix Logo: Checking the Order Status of Business Cards and Other Merchandise"
---

With Wix, you can order business cards and other printed merchandise. Once you have placed an order, you can track the status from the Logo Maker, the dashboard, or the Business Card Maker.

> **Note:**
>
> Orders are fulfilled by [Order a Print](https://www.orderaprint.com/). You can cancel an order within the first 24 hours. Damaged or defective orders need to be reported within 7 days of the delivery date to Order a Print via email: [support@orderaprint.com](mailto:support@orderaprint.com), and **not **Wix. Order a Print will investigate and send a replacement at their discretion.

### To check the status of your order:

Logo MakerDashboardCard Maker
1. [Go to the Logo Maker](https://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh/dashboard/my-logos).
2. Select the relevant logo.
3. Click **Brand Actions**.
4. Click **Print logo on merchandise**.
5. Click **Order History**.
6. Click **View Items**.
7. Click **Track Order**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to track order.]
1. [Go to Logo & Brand ](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/brand-maker?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+logos+and+brands)in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down and click** Go to Print Shop**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to open the print shop.]
1. Click **Order History**.
2. Click **View items**.
3. Click **Track Order**.

**Note****:** If you do not have an existing logo you can use the search bar in the dashboard to access "Business Cards & More".

1. [Go to the Business Card Maker](https://www.wix.com/tools/business-card-maker).
2. Click **Order History **at the top.
3. Click **View items **next to the relevant order.
4. Click **Track Order **under** Tracking Number**.

> **Tip:**
>
> To check the design of the printed merchandise you ordered, click **Order Again** in the Order History.
