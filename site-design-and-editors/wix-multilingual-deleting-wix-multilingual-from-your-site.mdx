title: "Wix Multilingual: Deleting Wix Multilingual from Your Site"
---

If you no longer need the Wix Multilingual app on your site or wish to change your site's main language, you can completely remove it. Deleting the app will permanently erase all editor translations, and translations from any Wix apps you have installed.

> **Before removing Wix Multilingual, consider these precautionary measures:**
>
> - [Duplic](https://support.wix.com/en/article/duplicating-your-site-1472847)[ating your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/duplicating-your-site-1472847) so you'll have access to your previous translations, site design and layout.
> - [Exporting your translations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-exporting-and-importing-site-content-for-manual-translation) so that you can reference or import translations back to your site after re-adding Wix Multilingual.

### Deleting Wix Multilingual from your site

You can delete Wix Multilingual if you're sure you want to permanently remove all the translations from your site. You'll be taken to your editor to finish deleting the Wix Multilingual app if your site has visual elements that were translated.

DashboardWix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions icon**  next to **Wix Multilingual**.
3. Select **Delete**.

1. (Optional) If the app has visual elements on your site, click **Go to Editor**.
2. Enter a reason for deleting and click **Delete App**.
3. Publish your site so changes go live.

2. Click **Add Apps **  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Manage Apps** at the bottom.

Click a question below to learn more.

### What happens to my word credit when I delete the app?

If you want to reinstall the app later, any previously purchased word credits will not be reinstated. You may need to purchase additional [word credits](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site) to auto-translate your site.

### Why do I still see Wix Multilingual in my dashboard after deleting the app?

If you still see the Wix Multilingual app in your dashboard after deletion, refresh the dashboard tab.

### How do I check how many word credits I have left?

When auto-translating your site, you automatically receive 3,000 free word credits. If you need to top up your word credits, you can choose from 3 translation packages; 3K Words, 10K Words or 50K Words.

1. [Go to the Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmultilingual%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Multilingual) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Check word credit balance** at the bottom-left of the Translation Manager.
