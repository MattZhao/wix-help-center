---

SVG files (or vector files) are ideal for logos and website icons. These files are infinitely scalable, meaning you don't have to compromise on quality or resolution.

You receive SVG files when you purchase the Advanced, Unlimited or Business Logo plan.

### About Vector (SVG) Files:

Click a question below to learn more about SVG logo files:

### What is the best use for SVG logo files?

Since vector files (SVG) are infinitely scalable and do not lose their quality when resized, they are the recommended file type to use when printing your logo. Scale them up or down without compromising quality, and use them in contexts as small as a business card or as large as a billboard.

### What's the best way to view my SVG files?

Your SVG files will automatically open in your browser when you preview them, unless you have Adobe Illustrator or other software supporting SVG files installed on your computer.
SVG files are very big, so you may need to zoom out using "**CTRL -**" (PC) or "**CMD -**" (Mac) to view the file in full.

### How can I resize my files?

SVG files can be resized without any quality loss. You can use such software as Adobe Illustrator or Photoshop to resize your files. If you don't have access to this software, you can use a free online alternative like [iloveimg.com](https://www.iloveimg.com/resize-image)﻿.

### Can I download an SVG file with a background?

Currently, all SVG logo files come with a transparent background by default to ensure your logo looks good in all places (e.g. mugs, t-shirts, business cards). If you would like to download SVG Wix Logo Files with a background color, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-request-svg-logo-file-with-a-background-color).
**Tip: **If you want to use a logo file with your chosen background color, you can use one of the PNG files that come in your downloaded ZIP file. Keep in mind that PNG files may lose quality if you resize them.

### Can I upload SVG files downloaded from the Wix Logo Maker to the Media Manager?

Yes, you can upload SVG files downloaded from the Wix Logo Maker to your Media Manager. However, SVG files created in the Wix Logo Maker with [uploaded images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-uploading-your-own-images-to-the-wix-logo-maker) cannot be uploaded to the Wix Media Manager.
