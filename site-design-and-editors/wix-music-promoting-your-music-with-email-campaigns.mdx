---
title: "Wix Music: Promoting Your Music With Email Campaigns"
---

Promote your Wix Music using [Wix Email Marketing](https://support.wix.com/en/wix-shoutout/)! Start with any stunning template, add a Wix Music Player and connect with your fans.

> **Important:**
>
> You need to [upload music](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-creating-an-album-single-or-playlist) for the player to work.

### To create an email campaign for your music:

[Go to your Music Library.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-accessing-your-music-library) Select the music you want to promote.  Click the **Show More**icon on the top right. Click **Promote**.

Hover over a template and click **Use Template**. Add the music player to your campaign: 
1. Click **Add**on the left.
2. Click **Music**.
3. Select your music from the drop-down menu.
4. Next to **Choose Theme**, select an option:
- **Light:** The color scheme is light.
- **Dark:** The color scheme is dark.
5. Click **Apply**.
[Customize your email campaign.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-customizing-your-email-campaign) Click **Next** to [send your email campaign.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-sending-your-email-campaign)

> **Note:**
>
> You can also access and create an email campaign from your [Email Marketing dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Email%20Marketing%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/shoutout/). From there you can check the status and statistics of your campaign.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Add [Get Subscribers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-subscribe-form) to your site, so fans can receive your email campaigns!
> - You can track all your analytics related to Wix Music using [Wix Analytics Reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-music-analytics-reports).
