---
title: "Wix Music: Displaying a 30 Second Preview of a Music Track"
---

Let your site visitors listen to a sample of your music by displaying a 30 second preview of your tracks. The preview sample will run from the 30-second point to 60 seconds, except in tracks that are less than one minute long, in which case the preview will run from the beginning to the 30-second point.

### To display a 30 second preview of a track:

Add a new track or click an existing one.Click the drop-down menu under **Track Streaming Options**, on the right side of the **Create New Track** window.Select **Fans can listen to a 30 sec preview**.
**Note: **You cannot chose which part of the song is in the 30-second preview. You can vote for this feature [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-audio-request-customizing-the-startend-times-of-track-previews-in-wix-music). Click **Save Track**.

> **Important:**
>
> This option may not work for an existing track. If this option does not work, please [delete the track](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-adding-tracks#deleting-tracks) and add it again.
