---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Right-to-Left Text Support in all Editor Components"
---

Currently, right-to-left text is supported in many areas of the Editor, including the text element. However, in some areas of the Editor such as in the Organize Images panel for galleries, right-to-left text is not supported and may not be displayed correctly on your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
