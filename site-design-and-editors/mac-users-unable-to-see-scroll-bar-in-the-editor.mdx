---
title: "Mac Users: Unable to See Scroll Bar in the Editor"
---

If you're editing your site on a Mac desktop, you may not be able to view or enable the scroll bar on the right side of the Editor.

This is due to the desktop settings. Most Mac devices automatically disable scroll bars unless you are actively scrolling, or based on the input device.

You can enable the scroll bar so it's always displayed from your Mac computer's settings.

### To enable the scroll bar:

Click the Apple menu at the top-left of the screen.  Select **System Preferences**.  Click **General**.  Click **Always** under **Show scroll bars**.

> **Note:**
>
> The setting names may be different depending on your computer model and system version.
