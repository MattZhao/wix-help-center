title: "Wix Editor Request: Using Photoshop with Wix Sites"
---

Currently, it is not possible to use Photoshop within the Wix Editor. However, you can [upload images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-your-own-images) that you have edited in Photoshop.

Wix also offers an Image Editing tool in the Wix Editor.

---

### To edit an image:

Click the image to select it.
  Click the **Crop & Edit**  icon.
 Edit your image by selecting the available editing tools.
 Click **Apply**.
 Click **Save**.
