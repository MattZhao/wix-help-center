---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Customizing the Password Window of a Password Protected Page"
---

Currently, it is not possible to design the window that is displayed on password protected pages.

The design of the window (i.e. fonts and colors) is taken directly from your site's theme. [Choosing a new theme](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-sites-theme-and-design) automatically updates the password window's look.

> **Note:**
>
> Changes you make to your theme apply to your entire site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
