---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Playing / Autoplaying Multiple Videos at the Same Time"
---

Currently, it is not possible to manually play / autoplay all the videos in your gallery at the same time. Depending on the [settings applied to your videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-your-gallerys-advanced-settings), your videos play individually in a loop, on hover, on click, or one after the other.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
