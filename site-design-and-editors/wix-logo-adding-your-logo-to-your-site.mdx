### Adding a logo to your Wix Editor site

Building a site in the Wix Editor? You can add logos you create in [the Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-logo-with-the-wix-logo-maker-571523) directly from the **Add Elements** panel. You can also upload external logo files from your device.

### Show me how to add my logo from the Add Elements panel

1. Open your site in the Wix Editor.
2. Click **Add Elements** ** **on the left menu.
3. Select the **Image** tab.
4. Select **Custom Logo**.
5. Click **Add Logo**.

[image: The Add Elements panel in the Editor. The Add Logo image option has been selected, and the cursor is clicking to add a logo.]

### Show me how to upload a logo to my site

1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **Upload Media **icon .
4. Click **Upload from Computer**.
5. Select the Logo file from your computer.
6. Click **Add to Page **to add it to your site.

[image: The Media panel in the Wix Editor. The cursor is clicking over the upload icon.]

> **Creating logos in the Wix Logo Maker?**
>
> If you have not purchased a logo, you can utilize the 200x200 free sample file to display it on your site. If you want to display a larger logo or need a larger file version, you can purchase [a logo plan.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans)

### Uploading logo files to a Studio Editor site

After [downloading](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-downloading-your-logo-in-the-wix-logo-maker) and [extracting](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-extracting-your-logo-files-from-the-zip-folder) Wix Logo files from a ZIP archive, you can upload them to Studio Editor sites in your account. You can also upload logo files you created outside of Wix (PNG, SVG etc.).

> **Important:**
>
> It is not possible to add [Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-logo-with-the-wix-logo-maker-571523) logos from the **Add Elements** panel in the Studio Editor. If you want the option to create a logo from the Studio Editor, you can [vote for it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-request-creating-a-logo-with-the-logo-maker-from-the-studio-editor).

#### To upload a logo to a Studio Editor site:

[Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.   Click **Media**.  Click the **Upload Media** icon .  Click **Upload from Computer.**  Select the Logo file from your computer.  Select the logo file and click **Add to Page**.

### Adding a logo to your site's business information

If you upload and add a logo to site pages, it is not automatically added to the site's business info. However, you can add the logo from your site's dashboard.

#### To add a logo to business info:

Go to [Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Edit%20Site%20Info&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Business Info**.  Click **Add Logo**.  Do one of the following:

- Click **Upload Media** to upload an image from your computer and click **Choose File**.
- Select an image from the image gallery and click **Choose File**.
    Click **Save**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about using logos on your site.

### What logo files should I use on my site?

[Have a look at our recommendations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-tips-and-tricks-for-using-your-logo-with-your-wix-site-and-products) to find out which Wix Logo files should be used in different places on Wix.

### Where are logos displayed on my site?

The logo you add to your Business Info automatically appears in various locations. For example, it's displayed in the [Wix Stores checkout page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-checkout-page) and Wix Stores invoices sent out to customers.
You can also add the logo to your site's pages from the Editor. We recommend placing your logo in the page header, so it's always visible to your visitors.

### When are logos added to my site's Business Info?

The following logos are added to your site's Business Info:
- Logos you create with the Wix Logo Maker
- Logos you upload yourself to the Business Info section of your site's dashboard

### Can I upload an SVG file for my logo?

If while creating your logo, you [uploaded your own PNG or JPEG images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-uploading-your-own-images-to-the-wix-logo-maker) to your logo, you won't be able to upload an SVG file version of your logo to your site. You can however upload the PNG file version instead, and achieve similar results.

### Can I make changes to my logo outside of Wix?

In accordance with the [Wix Logo Maker Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/logo-terms-of-use), it is not possible to customize a Wix Logo outside of the Logo Maker.
