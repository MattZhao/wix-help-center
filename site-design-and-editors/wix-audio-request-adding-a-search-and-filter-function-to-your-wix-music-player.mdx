---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Adding a Search and Filter Function to Your Wix Music Player"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a search or filter function to your Wix Music player for your visitors to find specific music in your player.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
