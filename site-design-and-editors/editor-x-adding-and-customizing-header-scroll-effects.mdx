title: "Editor X: Adding and Customizing Header Scroll Effects"
---

Draw your visitors' attention to the header and make it stand out by adding scroll effects. When [pinning your header](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-pinning-elements-and-sections#pinning-sections) to the page, you can also make it move, fade out or change colors on scroll.

**In this article, learn how to add scroll effects to the header:**

### Adding a Sticky scroll effect to the header

Set your header to scroll within a page. At first, visitors see the header wherever it's positioned in your page, but it "sticks" to the screen as they scroll down.

#### To add a sticky scroll effect:

Select the header in your Editor.  Click **Position** in the Inspector .  Click the **Position type** drop-down menu and select **Sticky**.  (Optional) Add an offset value above the element which is the distance the header should always be from the top of the screen.

---

### Adding a Pinned scroll effect to the header

Pin your header in a fixed position as visitors scroll down the page.

#### To add a Pinned scroll effect:

Select the header in your Editor.  Click **Position** in the Inspector .  Click the **Position type** drop-down menu and select **Pinned**.

---

### Adding transitions and fill colors on scroll

When using the Pinned scroll effect, you can create eye-catching transitions and make the header's background color change as visitors scroll down the page.

#### To add transitions and fill colors on scroll:

Select the header in your Editor.  Add the Pinned scroll effect: 

1. Click **Position** in the Inspector .
2. Click the **Position type** drop-down menu and select **Pinned**.
    Customize your header's scroll behavior by doing the following:

### Make the header move on scroll

Make the header move in a certain direction as visitors scroll down the page. When they scroll up, the header reappears and moves back in the opposite direction. 
**To make the header move on scroll:**
1. Click the **Transition** toggle to enable it.
2. Click **Move**.
3. Select the relevant direction: Left, up, down or right.
4. Set the distance to decide how far the header should move on scroll.
5. Set the delay and duration of the effect (in seconds).
6. Click the drop-down menu under **Ease **to decide how the header eases in and out.

**Note:** This transition automatically applies to all [breakpoints](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-designing-across-breakpoints-with-the-cascading-rule).
**Tip:** 
- You can use various [units of measure](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-units-of-measure) to set the distance. Switch to a different measurement by clicking the Percentage **% **icon.
- Fluid units of measure (percentage, vh or vw) are relative to the height and width of the header, **not** the viewport.

### Make the header fade out

Make the header fade out as visitors scroll down the page. When they scroll up, the header reappears.  
**To make the header fade out on scroll:**
1. Click the **Transition** toggle to enable it.
2. Click **Fade**.
3. Set the delay (in seconds).
4. Set the duration of the effect (in seconds).

**Note:** This transition automatically applies to all [breakpoints](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-designing-across-breakpoints-with-the-cascading-rule).

### Change the header's background color

Make the header's fill color change as visitors scroll down. When they scroll up, the header's color changes back to the original color. 
**To make the header change colors on scroll:**
1. Click the **Fill Color** toggle to enable it.
2. Click the color picker to select a target header background color.
3. Drag the slider to adjust the opacity of the new fill color.

**Note: **You can customize your header's background color [per breakpoint](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-designing-across-breakpoints-with-the-cascading-rule).

> **Note:**
>
> While you can set a target background color alongside a transition, it is not possible to combine **Fade** and **Move** in the same effect.

---

### Removing a scroll effect

You can always remove a scroll effect you've previously added to the header.

#### To remove a scroll effect:

Select the header in your Editor.  Click **Position** in the Inspector .  Click the **Position type** drop-down menu and select **Default**.
