---
title: "Wix Music: Setting Up Sales Notifications"
---

By enabling or disabling sales notifications in your music settings, you can choose if you want to get notified whenever a fan purchases your music.

### To set up sales notifications:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Music Settings**.  Click the **Sales notifications** toggle to enable or disable it:

- **Enabled:** You get an email notification when you make a sale.
- **Disabled:** You are not notified whenever a fan purchases your music.

> **Note:**
>
> Notifications go to the email address that is registered to your Wix account.
