---
title: "ADI: Accessing Your Site Settings"
---

From your site settings, you can update your business and social network information, optimize your site for search engines so that people can find your site on the web, get site analytics, statistics and more.

You can also manage your apps, handle your billing history and subscriptions, and even access your domain and mailbox settings from here.

### To access your site settings:

Click **Site **on the top bar inside ADI.Click** Settings**.
