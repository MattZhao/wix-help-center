title: "Studio Editor: Setting the Site's Max Width"
---

In the Studio Editor, certain elements are set to [scale proportionally](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-size-of-your-elements#choosing-an-elements-responsive-behavior) by default, which means they grow in direct relation to the screen size. On larger screens, they might even grow too big for your liking.

Set the max width for sections on your client's site to keep elements from growing beyond the point you want them to.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Setting the max width for your client's site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-sites-max-width#setting-the-max-width-for-your-clients-site)  [Excluding pages or sections from the max width](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-sites-max-width#excluding-specific-pages-or-sections-from-max-width)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-sites-max-width#faqs)

### Setting the max width for your client's site

Head over to the Site Styles panel to set the max width. On screens that are wider than the value you set, the element sizes stay fixed, and the site gets side margins.

The max width you set applies to the entire site, but you can exclude specific sections if needed.

> **Note:**
>
> The max width must be larger than 1001px.

#### To set the max width:

Click **Site Styles **[image: undefined]** **on the left side of the editor.  Click the **Max width **tab.  Enter the desired width in pixels.

---

### Excluding specific pages or sections from max width

You can always disable the max width setting for specific sections or pages. This means that elements that scale proportionally will continue to grow in direct relation to the visitor's screen.

#### To exclude a page or section:

Select the relevant section.  Click the **Open Inspector** icon  at the top right of the editor.  Disable the **Apply max width** toggle under **Responsive behavior**.

> **Want to exclude the entire page?**
>
> Select the page in the Inspector's breadcrumbs, then click **Exclude existing sections** under **Layout**. This disables max width for all the sections you **currently** have on the page.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### What happens if I don't set the max width?

The default max width (1600px) automatically applies to all sections of your client's site.

### What happens when I add new sections, after excluding a page from max width?

The new section will have max width applied, but you can always disable it from the Inspector panel.
1. Select the relevant section.
2. Click the **Open Inspector** icon  at the top right of the editor.
3. Disable the **Apply max width** toggle under **Responsive behavior**.

### Can I add an element outside the max width boundaries?

Yes, but visitors with smaller screens might see it cropped, or not see it at all.
