---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Automatically Adjusting the Height of a Strip to Fit Different Viewports"
---

Currently, it is not possible for strips to automatically adjust their height for different viewports or screensizes (such as mobile devices).

However, you can make your strip smaller or bigger in the mobile Editor by clicking and dragging the stretch handles. [Click here to learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-strips-and-columns-on-your-mobile-site#strips-on-your-mobile-site)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
