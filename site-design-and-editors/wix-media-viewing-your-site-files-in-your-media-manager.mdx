title: "Wix Media: Viewing Your Site Files in Your Media Manager"
---

In your Media Manager, you have a tab called **Site Files**. All your images, audio, videos, and documents, etc. that you upload while working on your site are housed here. You can view information relating to your files such as file size, type, resolution and more in the Media Manager.

[Accessing your Site Files folder](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-viewing-your-site-files-in-your-media-manager#accessing-your-site-files-folder)  [Viewing your site file's information](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-viewing-your-site-files-in-your-media-manager#viewing-your-files-information)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-viewing-your-site-files-in-your-media-manager#faqs)

> **Important:**
>
> Any media that your [site collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview) upload is also added to your Site Files folder. This means that everyone who is working on your site has access to all the files in your Site Files folder.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about viewing your site files in the Media Manager.

### How can I check how much storage I'm using?

In the Media Manager, you can view the total storage your site has used at the bottom left. Hover over the **Info **icon  to check the remaining storage space available for your site. Learn more about [viewing and managing your site's media storage](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-managing-your-storage).

### How can I view the size of images automatically built into Wix templates?

To view the size of an image from a Wix site template, go to the Photo Studio:
1. Click the image in your editor, and then click the **Settings** icon .
2. Click **Adjust**.
3. In the Photo Studio, click **Crop & Resize**.

[image: A screenshot showing an image's size in the Photo Studio.]

### Where do files uploaded by site visitors go?

Files uploaded by site visitors and site members are automatically added to the **Visitor Uploads** section of the Media Manager. You can see who uploaded a file in File Info section next to **Added by**.

### How much storage comes with my plan?

The amount of storage included in your plan depends on your selected premium plan. You can [compare storage for premium plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-storage-and-bandwidth#increasing-storage-and-bandwidth) and increase your site's storage at any time by [upgrading your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).
If you need additional media storage but don't wish to change your plan, you can [purchase extra storage](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fpremium-add-on%2Fstorage%3Fmsid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%0A%0A&title=Select+a+Site+to+Add+Storage&primaryButtonText=Add+Storage).
