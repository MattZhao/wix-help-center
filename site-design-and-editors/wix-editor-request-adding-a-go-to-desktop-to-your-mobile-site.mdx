---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a \"Go to Desktop\" to Your Mobile Site"
---

Currently, a "go to desktop" button to allow your visitors to move from your mobile site to your desktop site is not supported.

However, visitors may be able to enable this option in their mobile browser.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
