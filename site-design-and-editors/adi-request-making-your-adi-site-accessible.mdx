title: "ADI Request: Making Your ADI Site Accessible"
---

Currently, accessibility features are not available on sites built with ADI. To build an accessible website, [switch to the Wix Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor).

> **What is website accessibility?**
>
> Website accessibility refers to the practice of formatting your site to ensure everyone, able-bodied or disabled, can equally access, navigate and interact with your site's content. [Learn how to make your Wix site accessible](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-checklist-for-improving-your-sites-accessibility).

> **Note:**
>
> We are working on adding accessibility features to the ADI Builder. Please note that these features are intended to make your live site accessible, but not the ADI Builder itself.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
