---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Adjusting Thumbnail Alignment in Galleries with the Thumbnails Layout"
---

You can customize your thumbnails' size, spacing and placement in galleries with the Thumbnails layout. Currently, it is not possible to adjust the alignment of your thumbnails. [Learn more about customization options available in the Thumbnails layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-your-gallerys-thumbnails)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
