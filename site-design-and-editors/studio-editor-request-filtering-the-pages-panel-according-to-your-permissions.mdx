---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Filtering the Pages Panel According to Your Permissions"
---

Currently, it is not possible to filter the Pages panel so it only shows pages you have permission to edit.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you don't have permission to edit a certain page, an indication appears when hovering over it in the Pages panel.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
