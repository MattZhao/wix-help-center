---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Password of a Password Protected Page"
---

If you choose to [protect your site's pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site) with a password, you can easily change the password whenever you want.

### To change the password of a password protected page:

Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the **Show More** icon .  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Permissions **tab.  Select **Password Holders**.  Type a new password under **What's the page's password?**.

> **Did you know?**
>
> You can [password protect your entire site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-your-site) with a single password.
