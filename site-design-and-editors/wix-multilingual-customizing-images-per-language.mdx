### Customizing images per language

Choose to display a different image for each language or adjust images using the Wix Photo Studio. Translate the alt text for your images and set what happens when visitors click on an image.

>

### Customizing galleries per language

Customize your site's galleries for each of your languages. You can replace existing images, add more images, and change their order. Additionally, you can adjust the settings for each image in your gallery, including alt text, description, and the image link.

> **Important:**
>
> - You can customize the following galleries: Grid Galleries, Slider Galleries, Honeycomb (diamonds and triangle), Animated Slide, Freestyle, 3d Slideshow, 3d Carousel.
> - Learn how to translate the [Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-pro-gallery).

#### To customize galleries per language:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the **Switch languages **drop-down menu at the top of the Wix Editor.

Select the language you want to edit.   Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.  Select the page you want to edit.   Select the relevant gallery on your page.  Click **Edit** **Translation**.  Set up your gallery for the language:

- Click **+ Add Images **to add different images.
- Click **Replace Image** to select a different image.
- Drag and drop images to reorder them.
- Change the Image Info on the right: 

- Enter a **Title**.
- Enter a **Description**.
- Add a **Link**.
    Click **Done**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about customizing your site's images per language.

### Which images cannot be changed per language?

You are limited in changing certain types of images: strip backgrounds, vector art (.svg files), Pro Gallery images, lightbox images, and images that appear in apps. You cannot change these images per language, but you can adjust their SEO settings, such as alt text, title, and description.

### Is it possible to only adjust an existing image for each language?

Yes. If you want to use the same image but change it slightly per language, go to your editor, select the language you want to update, select the page in your site and the relevant image. Click **Edit** **Translation **and then select **Adjust**  to access the [Wix Photo Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools).
**Tip: **Try our [AI Image Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools#ai-editing) to edit your image in your desired style.

### How do I select and change images for different languages on my site?

You can upload your own images, select stock images, or create your own image with AI. 
To change your image, go to your editor, select the language you want to update, select the page in your site and the relevant image. Click **Edit** **Translation **and then click **Change ** to select a different image for your secondary language. You can now decide what you want to do: Click **Add Images **and** **[select an image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-an-image-on-your-site) from the [Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-searching-for-a-file-in-the-media-manager) or [Create an AI image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-images-with-the-wix-ai-image-creator).

### Why should I translate alt text?

Translating [alt text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/optimizing-your-images-and-galleries-for-seo-alt-text) for your images improves your site's SEO as it helps your site appear in search results in other languages too. Search engines read the text in your site's HTML code to understand the images. This improves your site's visibility and relevance.Alt text also enhances accessibility by allowing screen readers to describe images to visually impaired site visitors.
