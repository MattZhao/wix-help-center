---

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to Wix ADI. Click [here](https://www.wix.com/support/html5/the-wix-editor) if you are looking for information regarding the Editor.

Currently, it's not possible to set a different email address for each form on your site. All form submission notifications are sent to the same email address for all of your forms on your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
