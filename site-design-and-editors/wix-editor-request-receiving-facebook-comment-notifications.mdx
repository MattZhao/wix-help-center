---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Receiving Facebook Comment Notifications"
---

Currently, Wix does not have the ability to send notifications when visitors leave Facebook comments on your site.

In the meantime, you can view and moderate your Facebook comments on your live site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
