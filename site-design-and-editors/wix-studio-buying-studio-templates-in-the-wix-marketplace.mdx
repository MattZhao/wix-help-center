### Buying Studio templates in the Wix Marketplace

Browse, preview, and purchase a professionally designed Studio template from the Wix Marketplace, perfect for your website.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Studio plans are not included with templates.
> - Some templates might include apps that require additional payment.

#### To buy a Studio template:

Go to the [Studio Templates page in the Wix Marketplace](https://www.wix.com/marketplace/templates).  Browse the Studio templates and click on the one you'd like to purchase.

**Tip:** Click **View Template** to preview the template and use the device icons at the top to switch views.  Click **Purchase**.  Complete the checkout process and click **Continue**.   Review your details and click **Place Order** to confirm.  Click **Add to Sites** to start using your template.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about buying a Studio template from the Wix Marketplace.

### Can I use the template for multiple websites?

No. A purchased template is licensed for use on a single website. For multiple sites, you would need to purchase additional templates.

### What information can I get from the seller about a Studio template?

Sellers provide detailed information about their template and the features included. You can contact the seller directly if you have specific questions.

### Can I customize the Studio template after purchasing it?

Yes, you can fully customize your purchased Studio template to fit your business and preferences. Use the Studio Editor to change layouts, colors, fonts, images, and more.

### What if I need help with my template?

If you need assistance with your template, you can reach out directly to the seller for support or explore the [Wix Help Center](https://support.wix.com/en).

### How do I report an issue with a template?

If you encounter an issue with a template that hasn’t been resolved by the seller, you can report it directly to Wix. Make sure you're logged into your Wix account before reporting the template.
**To report a template:**
1. Go to the template page in the Wix Marketplace.
2. Click **Report this template **at the bottom right.
3. Enter the following details:

- **Type of issue: **Select the relevant issue with the template.
- **Describe the issue:** Provide more information about the issue you're experiencing.
4. (Optional) Select the** I agree to be contacted for additional details** checkbox.
5. Click **Submit Report**.
[image: A screenshot of click Report this template and then entering the details of the report.]**Note:** You don't need to purchase the template to report it. Your report will be reviewed by our team, and if necessary, the template may be removed from the Marketplace. The seller will not see your report or have access to your personal information.

### How often are new Studio templates added to the Wix Marketplace?

New Studio templates are regularly added to the Wix Marketplace, so check back often to see the latest designs and offerings from Wix Partners.
