---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Wix Support of Retina Display"
---

Currently, Wix does not support Retina display.

The Wix Editor automatically optimizes images uploaded to a Wix site, taking both site loading time and image quality into consideration.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
