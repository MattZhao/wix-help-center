---
title: "ADI Request: Adding a Post List to Your Blog"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a Post List to your blog in ADI.

Posts lists allow users to fully customize their blog layout and design as well as showcase posts on different areas of the site.

If you want to add a [Post List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-and-setting-up-a-post-list), you should [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor).
