---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Adding Locales"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add locales to your site's languages. A locale combines language and region. This feature would allow you to align your site's content with the distinct needs of your audience. For example, en-US for American English and en-GB for British English.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
