---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Page Tabs"
---

Currently, a page tabs feature is not supported.

This feature would allow your site visitors to use the tabs to navigate to different information within one page, and all the tabs would have the same page URL.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
