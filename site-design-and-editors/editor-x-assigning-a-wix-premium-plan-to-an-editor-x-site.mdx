---
title: "Editor X: Assigning a Wix Premium Plan to an Editor X Site"
---

You can assign your existing Premium plan to an Editor X site by upgrading your Wix Premium plan when assigning it to the new site.

> **Note:**
>
> Any remaining credit from your old Premium plan is deducted from the price of your new upgrade during the purchase process. This credit is deducted after any discounts from a coupon or seasonal sale are applied.

### To reassign and upgrade your Premium plan:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions **icon  next to the plan you want to reassign.  Select **Assign to a different site**.  Click the checkbox next to the relevant Editor X Site.
