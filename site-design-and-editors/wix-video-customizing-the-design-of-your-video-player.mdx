---
title: "Wix Video: Customizing the Design of your Video Player"
---

You can choose the font, size and color of the title and text in your channel widget.

> The design options you see in the Design tab depend on the layout you chose in the Layout tab.

### To customize your player:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the Wix Video player.  Click **Settings**.   Click **Design** and choose what you want to customize:

- **Text:** Design the text that appears on your player, including shape and color. If your chosen layout includes thumbnails, you can also choose their font.
- **Buttons:** Customize the buttons that appear on your player, including their color.
- **Player:** Choose how your player appears, including the opacity of both your cover image and background.

> **Note:**
>
> Make sure that the background color does not match the button text and color. If the colors are the same, the button and text will not show up.
