---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Pinning an Element to All Site Pages"
---

While you can [pin elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-pinning-elements-and-sections) in the Studio Editor, it only applies to the specific page you're editing. Currently, there is no feature that lets you pin an element to all site pages at once – you need to repeat the action for every individual page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
