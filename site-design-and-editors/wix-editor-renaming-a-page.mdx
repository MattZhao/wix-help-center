---
title: "Wix Editor: Renaming a Page"
---

Rename your pages so you can easily recognize them in the Pages panel. The new name that you select is automatically updated on all menus that display the page.

### To rename a page:

Click **Pages ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the **Show More** icon .  Click **Rename **.  Enter the new page name.  Click **Done**

> **Important:**
>
> - As your site menu displays your page names, changing the name of a page also changes the text on your site's menu.
> - If you would like to update the page URL, follow the steps [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-page-url).
