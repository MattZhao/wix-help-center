### Creating a custom template

To create a custom template, you have two options: choose an existing site in your workspace or create a template from scratch.

> **Important:**
>
> Some content is not copied over when you make your site a template. See FAQs for more information.

#### From an existing site

[Go to Custom Templates](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/my-templates?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click **+** **Create Template** at the top right.  Select **Start from an existing site**.  Choose one of your sites to use for the template.  Click **Continue**.  Enter a name for your custom template and click **Create Template**.

#### From scratch

Click **+** **Create Template** at the top right.  Choose one of the following options:

- **Start from a blank Wix canvas: **To create your template using the Wix Editor.
- **Start from a blank Studio canvas: **To create your template using the Studio Editor.
    Enter a name for your custom template and click **Create Template**.  Build the site to create your template.

### Using a custom template to build a site

When starting to work on a site for a new project, just choose which template you want to use and start customizing it to suit your needs.

#### To use a custom template to build a site:

[Go to Custom Templates](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/my-templates?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Hover over the template and click **Create site** to build a new site with it.  Build your site in the editor.

> **Want to make changes to a template?**
>
> If you want to edit a template, click the **More Actions** icon  and then click **Edit template**.

### Organizing your custom templates

Stay on top of your custom templates by customizing the view, filtering them based on various criteria and creating saved views for quick access. You can quickly search for specific templates based on their name, or filter your templates to see a specific subset of sites based on your criteria.

#### To organize your templates:

[Go to Custom Templates](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/my-templates?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click **Filter**.  Choose the filters to apply to the site list.  Click **Manage View **and choose **Save as new view**.  Enter a name for the saved view.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click the **Customize columns** icon  and select the columns to show in your site list. You can also drag and drop the columns to reorder them.

### FAQs

Click on a question below to learn more about custom templates in Wix Studio.

### Can I create custom templates for different types of projects?

A great way to use custom templates is to create generic templates that you can use for one type of client or project. 
For example, you can create an eCommerce template and then you can use it as a foundation for any client that needs an eCommerce site. This streamlines the process of creating sites, saving you time and providing your team with a great starting point to build sites efficiently.

### Can I edit a custom template?

Click the **More Actions** icon  on a template, then **Edit template **to make changes to it. When editing a template, you're making changes only to the template itself. It does not affect any site using the template or the original site you based it on.
If you want to keep the original template as well, we recommend that you first duplicate the template, edit it with the changes you want, and then save it as a new template.

### Is there a limit to the number of templates I can make?

There is no limit to the number of templates you can have in a workspace.

### What information is not copied over when I turn a site into a template?

The following content is not copied over to your custom template:
- Store orders and invoices
- Contacts and email campaigns
- Settings for third-party apps
- Velo by Wix: 

- Secrets stored in your [Secrets Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-working-with-the-secrets-manager).
- Any elements that were hidden require [enabling Velo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/enabling-velo) in your new site.

### Does Wix Studio have ready-made templates?

Wix Studio has several designer-made [responsive website templates](https://www.wix.com/studio/templates), covering a broad range of industries and design styles that you can also use to create sites without needing to start from scratch. There are also some ready-made wireframes for you to choose from and add your own style.

### Can I sell my custom templates?

Yes, you can sell your custom templates anywhere. Once you've created a custom template that is ready and published, you can add your template as a digital product on your Wix site or any other platform to start selling. Learn more about [selling your custom templates](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-selling-your-custom-templates).
If you're a Wix Partner, you can also showcase and [sell your Studio templates in the Wix Marketplace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-selling-your-studio-templates-in-the-marketplace).

### How do I get the URL of my custom template?

To get the URL of your custom template:
1. [Go to Custom Templates](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/my-templates) in your Wix Studio workspace.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the template and select **Edit template**.
3. Click **Publish** at the top right.
4. Copy the template URL.
