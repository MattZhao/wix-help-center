---
title: "ADI: Changing Your Header Design"
---

Your page header is the section appearing at the top of your page. You can change the way your header appears when your visitors browse your site: A **regular** header, a **transparent** header or a **fixed** header.

### To change your header design:

Hover over the **Customize Section Design **button.Select a **Header Type**:
- **Regular**: The header remains at the top of the page and only appears when your visitor scrolls to that area.
- **Transparent**: A transparent header is one without a background color. It is important to note that you can choose to have a transparent header, but it only appears transparent on the homepage. On the other site pages it appears with a background color.
- **Fixed**: The header appears at the top of the page even when your visitor scrolls down.
Select the **background color**.

### Regular Header

The regular header appears at the top of each page. When your site visitors scroll down, the header does not appear.

### Transparent Header

A [transparent header ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-a-transparent-header)is one without a background color. It is important to note that you can choose to have a transparent header, but it only appears transparent on the homepage. On the other site pages it appears with a background color. 

In order to use the Transparent Header, make sure: 
- Your [site colors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors) are set to **Dark Mode**.
- The first section immediately following the header includes a **full-width image**.

### Fixed Header

The Fixed Header appears at the top of each of your site's pages, even while scrolling down.
