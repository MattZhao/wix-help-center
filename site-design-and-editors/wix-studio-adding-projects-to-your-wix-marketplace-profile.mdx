---
title: "Wix Studio: Adding Projects to Your Wix Marketplace Profile"
---

Add sites you've created to display them in your Wix Marketplace profile as Featured Projects. You can add up to 20 Premium Wix sites to showcase your best work and grab your clients' attention.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Check out the [Wix Marketplace Profile Guide](https://www.wix.com/marketplace/profile-guide) for tips on highlighting your skills and expertise.
> - Click **View My Profile** at the top of the page to view your profile as it appears in the [Wix Marketplace](https://www.wix.com/marketplace). Your profile displays how many projects you've completed in the Wix Marketplace.

### To add a project to your Wix Marketplace profile:

Go to [your Wix Marketplace profile](https://www.wix.com/marketplace/edit-profile).  Scroll down to the **Portfolio projects **section and click **+ Add Project**.

Choose the relevant Wix Premium site you've built site and click **Next**.   Enter the **Site name**, **Description** and **URL **in the relevant fields.   Click the **Update** iconunder **Cover Image** to replace it. Then, select the image you want to use and click **Open**.   Click **Add Project**.   Click **Save Changes** at the top of the page.

> **Note:**
>
> You can edit and reorder projects by hovering over them and selecting the relevant icon.
