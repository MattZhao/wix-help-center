---
title: "Wix Editor: Setting a Page Link to Open in a New Window"
---

When linking an element such as an image, button, or text to open another page on your site, you can choose whether that page should open in the same window or a new window on your browser.

### To set a page link to open in a new window:

Click the element.Click the **Link **icon ﻿﻿.Click **Page**.Select **New Window **under **How does it open?**.Click **Done**.
