---
title: "Studio Editor: Exporting Your Designs from Figma"
---

Using Figma to design sections and elements? The Wix Studio plugin lets you turn your static designs into a responsive live site, with one streamlined process. You can import entire Figma frames, specific components and sections, or use our wireframes (UI kits).

Once in Studio, you can enhance your designs with no-code animations, custom CSS, connection to business solutions like Wix Stores, and much more.

> - We recommend keeping Figma and the Studio Editor open, in separate browser tabs, as you'll need both for this process.
> - In the middle of the process and need help? Read our [FAQs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-exporting-your-designs-from-figma#faqs) and [troubleshooting](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-exporting-your-designs-from-figma#troubleshooting) info below.

---

### Step 1 | Prepare for the export

Before trying to import designs from Figma, there's a few things we recommend checking. This will ensure a seamless transition from Figma to Wix Studio.

#### a | Upload custom fonts to Wix Studio

Make sure all of the fonts you're using on Figma are also available in your Wix account. It's better to [upload the fonts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-different-text-fonts) to Wix in advance – otherwise, system fonts replace the fonts you intended to show.

#### b | Compare the frame width to the editing size

The Figma frame width should match the [editing size](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-your-editing-size) of the relevant page in your client's site. If there's a mismatch, your design won't be exported in the same ratio, which means it will not appear in the right proportions in Wix Studio.

> **Exporting elements?**
>
> You should still check the width of the frame that contains them to ensure a 1:1 result.

---

### Step 2 | Install the plugin and connect the Figma file

If you haven't already, head over to the Figma Community page and install the Wix Studio plugin. Once installed, you can use right-click to access the plugin from any design you're working on.

Next, copy the Figma file link and head back to the editor, where you can connect it to your client's site.

#### a | Install the plugin on Figma

Go to the [Figma to Wix studio](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/987294632651817123/figma-to-wix-studio) plugin page.  Add the plugin to your Figma account.  Open the Figma file that contains the frame you want to export.   Copy the URL of your Figma file.

#### b | Connect the Figma file to your client's site

Go to your client's site in the editor.  Click the** Wix Studio **icon  at the top left.  Hover over **Tools**.  Click **Import from Figma**.

**Tip:** This adds a new icon  on the left side of the editor.  Paste the URL of your **Figma file** and click **Connect**.  (First time only) Click **Allow access **to finish connecting the file.

---

### Step 3 | Choose what to export from Figma

After installing the plugin, locate the relevant design file in your Figma account. The plugin lets you choose what to export – a frame or your styles.

We recommend exporting your styles first (i.e. typography and colors), so they're already set up in the Studio Editor by the time you start exporting frames.

### Which elements does the plugin recognize automatically?

When you select a frame on Figma, our plugin is able to identify the following: 
- Page
- Section
- Stack
- Container
- Button
- Text
- Image
- GIF
- SVG

**Note:** Make sure your file is smaller than 250kb.

The plugin does not auto-recognize menus, forms, galleries, videos and input elements. Once you import the frame to the Studio Editor, these specific elements won't be functional. However, you can quickly replace them with the available elements in the editor.

#### To export from Figma:

Open the relevant Figma file.  Access the Studio plugin:

1. Click the** Figma** icon  to open the main menu.
2. Hover over **Plugins **and click** Figma to** **Wix Studio**.
    Click **Set up Export**.  Select the **I'm done connecting my file** checkbox and click **Next**.  Verify that the editing size in Wix Studio matches the Figma frame width. When ready, click **Start Export**.  Choose what to export and follow the steps:

### Styles

Select frames that include all the colors and fonts that should be exported. This can be one or multiple frames, including top-level ones.
In the panel, you’ll notice the plugin automatically recognizes the colors and typography in the frame(s) you select. This includes all text elements and their styles, such as font, size, color, and formatting, along with the colors present in text, buttons, shapes, and backgrounds in the selected area.
Review the styles before exporting and once satisfied, click **Export Styles**. Once the export is complete, head back to the editor.
[image: A screenshot of Figma, showing the Studio plugin once it's done exporting styles from the design]

### Frame

Click the frame you want to export. The plugin will show you a preview of the frame and its width – at this point, we recommend double-checking it matches the editing size in the editor. 
When ready, click **Export Frame**. Once the export is complete, head back to the editor.
[image: A screenshot of Figma, showing the Studio plugin once it's done exporting the frame]

---

### Step 4 | Complete the import in the editor

After choosing the styles and / or frames you wish to export, go back to your client's site in the editor. You'll notice the Import from Figma panel now shows your selection.

Make sure there are no missing fonts or size mismatches, then choose whether to import the frame as a container, section, etc.

#### To complete the import:

Go to your client's site in the editor.  (If not already open) Click the **Import from Figma**  icon on the left.  Depending on what you're importing, follow the steps below:

### Styles

1. Review the colors and fonts that you're about to import.
2. (If there are missing fonts) Click **Upload Fonts **and upload those that are missing from your account.
3. Click **Apply Styles to Site**.
4. (Optional) Go back to Figma to export the relevant frame.

[image: The Import from Figma panel in the Studio Editor when importing styles, highlighting a missing fonts error]

### Frame

1. Review the frame you're going to import:

- If there are missing fonts, click **Upload Fonts**.
- If the editing size doesn't match the Figma frame width, click **Match Editing Size**.

**Tip:** Alternatively, you can resize the Frame on Figma and then export it again. See our FAQs section for more instructions.
2. Select the relevant option next to **Add frame as**: **Container**, **Section**, **Page **or **Stack**.
3. Click **Add**.
4. Enhance your Figma design:

- Adjust your design’s responsiveness using the [Responsive AI tool](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-ai-to-make-sections-responsive) or by manually setting the exact [behavior](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-size-of-your-elements#choosing-an-elements-responsive-behavior) you want.
- Add movement tailored to your exact specs with [no-code animations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-about-animations) and effects.
- Connect our [business solutions](https://www.wix.com/studio/business-solutions) for eCommerce, Bookings, Restaurants, Hotels and more
- Connect elements with the [CMS](https://www.wix.com/studio/cms) to display dynamic content.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about importing designs from Figma.

### Can I import designs from Figma to an existing site?

Yes, you can import your Figma designs to an existing site or to a brand new site.

### Can I import multiple pages at once?

No. Currently, this option is not supported.

### Do my Figma designs automatically connect to Wix apps (e.g. Wix Stores)?

No. You will need to manually install the relevant Wix app(s) on your client's site.

### What happens when importing menus, forms, and buttons? Will they function on my client's site?

**Buttons** are imported as functional button elements if they have text only (more specifically, a symbol / frame with 1 text element and a fill color).  If your button on Figma is a shape or icon, the plugin does not identify it as a button.
**Menus and forms** are imported as regular text elements, so they won't function like the menus and forms offered by Wix Studio. 
After importing your design, we recommend replacing these elements with the menu and form elements available in the Studio Editor.

### How will my imported design look across breakpoints?

As long as the desktop Figma frame width matches the editing size on the specific site page, the result will be accurate, and the designs will scale proportionally across breakpoints. 
If you want to make further adjustments for desktop, tablet or mobile, you can [do it manually](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-designing-across-breakpoints) or [use Responsive AI](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-ai-to-make-sections-responsive) to optimize your designs for all breakpoints.

### If I make further changes to my Figma design after importing it to Studio, will they automatically sync?

No, your changes won’t automatically update. Any changes need to be exported and imported again.

### Can I export designs the other way around, from Wix Studio to Figma?

No. Currently, this option is not supported.

### Troubleshooting

Experiencing issues while exporting your Figma designs to Wix Studio? Click an issue below to learn what caused it and how to resolve it.

### (On Figma) No styles appear in the panel when trying to export

Make sure the relevant frame(s) is selected. You can see if it's selected or not on the canvas, as well as the Layers panel. 
For more specific tips, read below. 
**Typography styles: **If you can't see a specific typography style, check that:
- Live text elements were used.
- All relevant fonts are installed on your computer.

**Colors:**Colors appearing in images cannot be automatically recognized. If needed, you can add them later on as custom colors in the Studio Editor (using their HEX, RGB or RSB codes).

### (In the editor) Can't see the option to add a frame as a page / section

The options you see next to **Add frame as** depend on the specific frame you've exported:
- **Page:** Top-level frames only
- **Section: **Nested frames wider than 1001px
- **Container:** All frame sizes
- **Stack:** Frames with auto layout

If you can't see the option you're looking for, you might have exported a frame that does not meet the above requirement.

### (In the editor) The design doesn't look proportionate after importing it

When the Figma frame width does not match the [editing size](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-your-editing-size) in the editor, the design automatically scales up or down. If your design isn't in the same proportions as it was on Figma, there might be a mismatch.
**Note:** The editing size is set per page in the Studio Editor. 
If you prefer resizing the frame on Figma so it matches your editing size, you can [use the Scale tool](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040451453-Resize-layers-with-the-scale-tool). Then, export the frame again.
