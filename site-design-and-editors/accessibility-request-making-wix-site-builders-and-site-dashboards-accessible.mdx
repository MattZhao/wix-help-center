---
title: "Accessibility Request: Making Wix Site Builders and Site Dashboards Accessible"
---

Currently, Wix live sites include built-in accessibility settings and features that comply with the highest global standards.

However, the Wix site builders and site dashboards are not yet fully compliant.

> **Note:**
>
> Follow our [checklist ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-checklist-for-improving-your-sites-accessibility)to improve the accessibility of your live site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
