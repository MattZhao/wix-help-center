title: "ADI: Customizing Your Store Pages"
---

Adding Wix Stores automatically adds a new page, called **Shop**, to your site and 3 hidden pages:

**The Product page:** This page is displayed when a customer clicks a product in your product gallery. It contains all the details of the product they are viewing.  **The Cart page:** Displayed when a customer clicks the Cart icon at the top, or the Add to Cart button on a product page.  **The Thank You page:** Displayed when a customer makes a purchase.

You can customize each of these pages to suit your needs.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Customize your Product page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-store-pages#customizing-your-product-page)  [Customize your Cart page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-store-pages#customizing-your-cart-page)  [Customize your Thank You page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-store-pages#customizing-your-thank-you-page)

### Customizing your Product page

Customize the Product page for your store. This Product page is a dynamic page which displays different content depending on the product your customer is viewing.

#### To customize your product page:

Click the **Pages **menu on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Product Page**.   Click your Product page in ADI.  Customize your page using the options:

- **Display elements: **Select or deselect the Show/Hide icon to show or hide parts of the Product page.
- **Image scaling**: Click the drop-down to choose how you want to display your product images.
- **Button: **Select or deselect the Show/Hide icon to show or hide the Add to Cart button.
- **Sharing options: **Select or deselect the Show/Hide icon to show or hide the social share icons.

> **Tip:**
>
> Preview your site to see what it's like to purchase a product on your site!

### Customizing your Cart page

Customize your Cart page and encourage your customers to check out.

#### To customize your cart page:

Click the **Pages **menu on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Cart**. 

  Click the Cart page in ADI.  Customize your page using the options:

1. **Title: **Edit the title text "My Cart".

- **Empty Cart: **Edit the text customers see when the cart is empty.
- **Continue shopping link: **Edit the "Continue Shopping" link.
- **Add note link:** Edit the link customers click to leave you a note on their order.
- **Add promo code link: **Edit the link customers click to enter a coupon.
- **Add note instructions: **Edit the note instructions text.
- **Button text:** Edit the text on the checkout button.

---

### Customizing your Thank You page

Customize the Thank You page to let your customers know that you appreciate them.

#### To customize your Thank You page:

Click the **Pages **menu on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Thank You Page**. 

  Click the Thank You page in ADI.  Customize your page using the options:

- **Title: **Edit the title text.
- **Subtitle:** Edit the text customers see.
- **Order number label:** Edit the text under the order number field.
- **Total cost label: **Edit the text under the total cost field.
- **Shipping address label: **Edit the text under the shipping address field.
