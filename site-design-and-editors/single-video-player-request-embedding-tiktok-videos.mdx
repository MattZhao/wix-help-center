---
title: "Single Video Player Request: Embedding TikTok Videos"
---

Currently, you can add videos from [YouTube](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-a-video-from-youtube), [Facebook](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-a-video-from-facebook), and [Vimeo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-a-video-from-vimeo) to the Wix Single Video Player, however, it is not possible to embed TikTok videos.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
