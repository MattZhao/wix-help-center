---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Customizing the Pro Gallery on Tablet Breakpoints"
---

Currently, the Pro Gallery displays on tablet devices with the same breakpoint settings as the desktop view. You cannot change it specifically for tablet breakpoints.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
