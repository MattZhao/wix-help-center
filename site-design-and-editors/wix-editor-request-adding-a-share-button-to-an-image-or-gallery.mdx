---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Share Button to an Image or Gallery"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a **Share** button to an image or gallery on your site.

As an alternative, you can add a [Pin to Pinterest](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-pinterest-on-your-wix-site#pin-to-pinterest) button to an individual image so that your visitors can share your content on Pinterest.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
