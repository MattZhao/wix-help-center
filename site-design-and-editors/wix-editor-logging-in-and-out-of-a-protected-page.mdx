---
title: "Wix Editor: Logging In and Out of a Protected Page"
---

**Password protected pages: **A [password protected page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site) requires your site visitors to enter a password. They are not required to log in. Therefore, there is no option to log out.

If your site visitors would like to re-enter the password, they need to clear their [browser's cache](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-your-browsers-cache) to remove the password memory. The next time that they try to access the page, they will be required to insert the password again.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you've protected your page for members only, the Log In / Sign Up button turns into a Log Out button. This enables your site visitors to log out of members-only pages.
