---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Viewing Collaborators' Cursors When Working on a Site"
---

When [working with others on the same site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-collaborating-on-a-site), you can see the page and element they are working on. However, there is currently no "Cursor Mode" that allows you to see their cursors moving around inside the Studio Editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
