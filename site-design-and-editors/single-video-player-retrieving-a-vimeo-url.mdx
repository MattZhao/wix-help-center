---
title: "Single Video Player: Retrieving a Vimeo URL"
---

In order to add a Vimeo video to your site, you will first need to retrieve the video URL.

### To retrieve the Vimeo URL:

Open the video on [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/).Click the **Share** icon.Copy the video's **Link **URL.

> **Note:**
>
> You cannot link viewers to a specific part of a Vimeo video on your Wix site.
