---
title: "Wix Media: Using Boards in the Media Manager"
---

Use private boards in the Media Manager to create account-level folders to easily access your media files across all your sites.

Boards allow you to edit and manage the files you regularly need, no matter which site you are working on. You can add your own media, free Wix media, or purchased media to your boards.

#### Learn more about boards:

[Creating a board](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-boards-in-the-media-manager#creating-a-board)  [Adding media files to a board](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-boards-in-the-media-manager#adding-media-files-to-a-board-1)  [Deleting a board](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-boards-in-the-media-manager#deleting-a-board)

### Creating a Board

Create a board inside your Media Manager. Choose if your board is accessible from all of your site's Media Managers, or just from the site you are working on.

[Access your Media Manager.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager)Click **My Boards **on the left side.Click **Create New Board**.Enter the **Board Name**.Enter the **Description** for the board.Click the **Available for all my sites** toggle:
- **Enabled: **Your board can be access from all your sites.
- **Disabled:** Your board can only be accessed from the site you are working on.
Click **Create**.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Boards are private and only visible to the user who created them. Collaborators cannot access other collaborators' boards.
> - You can't download the contents of Board folders in bulk. You have to download your files individually.

---

### Adding Media Files to a Board

Add any media you want to your board including your own uploaded media, free Wix media, or purchased media files (Shutterstock and Unsplash).

#### To add media files to a board:

[Access your Media Manager.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager)Locate your media file.Click the **Add to Board** icon.Select the board you want to add it to:
- **An existing board: **Click the relevant board and click **Add**.
- **New board:** Click **New Board** to create a new one.

---

### Deleting a Board

Delete a board at any time. The files inside the board will still be available from the original location in your Media Manager.

#### To delete a board:

[Access your Media Manager.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager)  Click **My Boards **on the left side.  Click the **Show More** iconon the relevant board.  Click **Delete Board**.  Click **Delete**.
