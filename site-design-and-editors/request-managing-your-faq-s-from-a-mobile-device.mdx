---
title: "Request: Managing Your FAQ's from a Mobile Device"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add new FAQ's or make changes to your FAQ's using a mobile device. You must use a desktop computer to [manage your FAQ's](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-questions-in-the-wix-faq-app).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
