---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Displaying the Language Menu in the Respective Languages of Your Site"
---

Currently, the Wix Multilingual language menu is displayed in English, rather than in the respective language (i.e. the menu items say "English and "Spanish", rather than "English and "Español").

We appreciate your request for such a feature. We are always working to update and improve our products, and have forwarded your feedback to the relevant department.
