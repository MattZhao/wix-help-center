---
title: "Wix Media: Adding Unsplash Images"
---

Add stunning images from Unsplash directly from your Media Manager. Unsplash is dedicated to sharing stock photography and has thousands of contributing photographers and a library of over a million photos. What's more, they're completely free.

Just choose the ones you like, and add them directly onto your page.

> **Important:**
>
> You can use Unsplash images only in accordance with the [Unsplash Terms of Use](https://unsplash.com/terms) and [Wix Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use). Unsplash images are royalty-free and all content is provided by Unsplash, not by Wix.

### To add an image from Unsplash:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.
4. Click **Unsplash** on the left side of the Media Manager.
5. Click the image you want to add.

**Tip: **Use the search and filters at the top to find what you are looking for.
6. Click **Add to Page**.
[image: Screenshot of choosing an image from Unsplash in the Wix Media Manager.]
