[Structured data markup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-structured-data-markup-to-your-sites-pages-2546962) gives search engines detailed information about your page. You can add structured data markup to your site's pages to help improve your website's search results and ranking.

By default, Wix adds preset markups to some of your site's pages when you create them. These are Wix Stores product pages, Wix Bookings services pages, Wix Blog posts, Wix Forum posts, and Wix Events pages.

Search engines like Google can also use markup to [create rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/search-gallery) like article previews, product previews, and FAQs for your page's search results.

> **Note:**
>
> Adding structured data markup to your site's pages does not guarantee that search engines will create rich results for them.

### Preset markup basics

The preset markups that Wix creates for your site's pages use SEO best practices to give search engines optimized information about your pages. These preset markups also use [variables](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-variables-in-seo-settings) to create dynamic code. Variables are pieces of text that represent a value, or text that's a placeholder for a value.

Using variables, you can include information that varies from page to page based on the actual content of the page. For example, the preset markup for Wix Stores product pages uses a number of placeholder variables including:

Product Name  Product Description  Product Brand  Product Price

The actual information that is used for these placeholder variables depends on the product information that is on each page. This means that the preset markup is automatically customized for each of your pages and creates unique rich results for every product.

### Managing your preset markups

You can view and manage your preset markups in the **Structured data markup **section of a page type's [SEO Settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929#customizing-your-structured-data-markup).

#### To manage your preset markups:

[Go to your SEO Dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-f3388913-140f-47a9-b2ba-4bf86b60e04e&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO).  Select **Go to SEO Settings **under **Tools and settings**.  Select the page type that you want to edit.  **Note: **It's not possible to customize the default markup for your Main Pages. You can customize markup for individual pages in [the Edit by Page section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-your-seo-settings-1420929#editing-the-seo-settings-of-individual-pages) or [in your editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/add-structured-data-markup-to-your-sites-pages).   Click **Edit** beside **Structured data markup**.  Choose what you want to do:

### Preview or switch a preset markup

1. Click **Preview Preset **beside the markup you want to preview, or **Switch Preset** for pages that have multiple [preset markup options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-preset-markups-for-your-sites-pages).

### Include or exclude a preset markup

1. Click the Show/Hide icon  beside the markup you want to include or exclude.

### Create a custom markup

You can create a custom markup by duplicating a preset markup and editing it. 

1. Click **Preview Preset** beside the markup you want to create a custom markup from.
2. Click **Convert to custom markup**.
3. Enter a name for the markup.
4. Edit your markup under **Write your markup in JSON-LD format**.
5. Click **Apply**.

### Create a new custom markup

1. Click **+ Add New Markup**.
2. Enter a name for the markup.
3. Add your new markup under **Write your markup in JSON-LD format**.
4. Click **Apply**.

### Edit or remove a custom markup

1. Click **Edit **beside any custom markup you want to edit.
2. Click the Remove icon  beside any custom markup you want to remove.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can have up to 5 markups per SEO Setting (including the preset markup).

### Types of preset markups for your site's pages

Wix automatically creates preset markups for your Wix Stores product pages, Wix Bookings services pages, Wix Blog posts, Wix Forum posts, and Wix Events pages.

Click a heading below to learn more about the different types of preset markups for your site's pages.

### Wix Stores product pages

When you create a Wix Stores product page, Wix automatically creates a Product preset markup for it. All the fields and values are automatically updated in the markup based on the actual content of the product page. 
This preset markup tells search engines that the page includes a product and helps them include relevant information for the product in search results.
For example, the markup can help Google provide [rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/product) that include information such as price, description, brand, availability, and product images.
Click **Preview Preset** under **Structured data markup **on the Products page of your SEO Settings to see an example of what a rich result using this preset markup looks like. 
[image: A preset markup for a Wix Stores product]

### Wix Blog posts

When you create a Wix Blog post, Wix automatically creates a Blog preset markup for it. All the fields and values are automatically updated in the markup based on the actual content of the blog post.
There are three preset markups for blog posts:
- Article (Blog Posting) Markup - This is the default preset markup
- Article (News Article) Markup
- Article Markup

Each of these preset markups tell search engines that the page includes a certain type of blog content and helps them include relevant information in search results.
For example, the Article (Blog Posting) Markup can help Google provide [rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/article) that include information such as the post's author, category, published date, and headline. 
Click **Switch Preset** under **Structured data markup** on the Blog Posts page of your SEO Settings to change to a different preset markup or to see an example of a rich result using that preset markup.

[image: A preset markup for a Wix Blog post]

### Wix Bookings services pages

When you create a Wix Bookings services page, Wix automatically creates a Course preset markup for it. All the fields and values are automatically updated in the markup based on the actual content of the services page.
This preset markup tells search engines that the page includes a course and helps them include relevant information in search results.
For example, the markup can help Google provide [rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/course) that include information such as the service's type, name, description, and provider.
Click **Preview Preset** under **Structured data markup** on the Booking Services page of your SEO Settings to see an example of what a rich result using this preset markup looks like.

[image: A preset markup for a Wix Bookings services page]

### Wix Forum posts

This preset markup tells search engines that the page includes questions and answers and helps them include relevant information in search results.
For example, the markup can help Google provide [rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/qapage) that include information such as the post's title, author, content, and published date.
Click **Preview Preset** under **Structured data markup** on the Forum Posts page of your SEO Settings to see an example of what a rich result using this preset markup looks like.
[image: A preset markup for a Wix Forum post]

### Wix Events pages

When you create a Wix Events page, Wix automatically creates an Event preset markup for it. All the fields and values are automatically updated in the markup based on the actual content of the event page.
This preset markup tells search engines that the page includes an event and helps them include relevant information in search results.
For example, the markup can help Google provide [rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/event) that include information such as the event's name, description, start and end dates, and location. 
Click **Preview Preset **under **Structured data markup** on the Events page of your SEO Settings to see an example of what a rich result using this preset markup looks like.
[image: A preset markup for a Wix Events event]

### Wix Portfolio

When you create a project or collection in Wix Portfolio, Wix automatically create preset markups for them. All the fields and values are automatically updated in the markup based on the actual content of the page.
For your projects and collections, Wix creates an Images preset markup. This preset markup tells search engines that the page includes images so they can be added to the image search engine.
Click **Preview Preset **under **Structured data markup** on the Portfolio Project or Portfolio Collection page of your SEO Settings to see what this preset markup looks like.
