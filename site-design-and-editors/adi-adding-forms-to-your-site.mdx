### Adding a contact form

If you deleted the contact form and want to re-add it, or if you want to add an additional contact form to your site, follow the steps below.

You can choose if you want the contact form to be a section on an existing page, or a whole "contact" page. You can only have one contact form per page.

#### To add a contact form:

Go to the page that you want to add the contact form to.  Click **Add **on the top bar.  Choose from the options below:

- Click **Section to Page**:** **Add a contact form to the section of the page.

1. Click** Contact**.
2. Click or drag a section to your site.
- Click** Page**: Add a contact form to the page you are currently on.

1. Click **Contact** in the **Add Page** panel.
2. Click a contact page layout to add it to your site.

> **Want to place your contact form on more than one page?**
>
> Save yourself time by duplicating your form and adding it to your page(s) of choice.

### Adding a subscribe form

Add a subscribe form to your site to easily build your mailing list. Everyone who signs up using the form is automatically added to your contact list with a 'Subscriber' label, so you can send them newsletters and updates.

You can add a subscribe form to your page, or to the footer of your site.

### Learn how to add a subscribe form to a page

1. Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.
2. Click **Section to Page**.
3. Click **Subscribe**.
4. Select the layout you want to use.

### Learn how to add a subscribe form to your site's footer

If you add a form to your footer, it appears on all pages of your site.
1. Click the footer in the ADI builder.
2. Select the checkbox next to **Subscribe **on the left.
**Tip: **[Learn how to manage your subscribers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-subscription-status-of-a-contact)

### Adding other types of forms

As well as a contact or subscribe form, you can choose from several ready made forms available in the ADI Builder, including forms for job applications, feedback, price quotes and more.

#### To add a form to your site:

Go to the page that you want to add the form to.  Click **Add **on the top bar.  Click **Section to Page**.  Scroll down to **Forms **and select the one you want to add (e.g. Feedback Form, Job Application etc).   Click or drag the section design you want to use to your site.

### Customizing a form's fields

Add, edit and reorder the fields in your form in ADI to make it look just as you want.

> **Important:**
>
> - You must [upgrade](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-upgrading-wix-forms) to add more than 10 fields to a form, or to add a file upload link.
> - After editing any of your form's aspects, you will need to re-activate any automations linked to your form.

#### To customize the fields in your form:

Click the section that contains your form in the ADI builder.Click **Edit** next to Form.

Choose what you want to do:

### I want to add a new field

1. Click **Add a new field**.
2. Hover over the new field under **Manage Choices** and click the **Settings** icon.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Set up your field using the options available.

### I want to edit an existing field

1. Hover over the relevant field under **Manage Choices** and click the **Settings** icon .
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Edit your field using the options available:

- **Make field required: **Click the toggle to choose if a field is optional or mandatory. Your visitors cannot submit the form without completing mandatory fields.
- **Placeholder Text:** Edit the text that appears on the field.
- **Field Title: **Edit the text that appears above the field.
- **Manage Choices:** Edit the options in your drop-down field.
**Note: **The options available depend on the field type.

### I want to delete a field

1. Hover over the relevant field under **Manage Choices** and click the **Settings** icon .
2. Click **Delete**.

### I want to reorder my fields

Drag the **Reorder ** icons under **Manage Choices** to move fields up or down within your form.

Click a question below to learn more about forms.

### How do I see my form submissions?

When your visitors submit your form, the responses are collected in a submissions table. Each form on your site has a different submissions table so that you can easily keep track of form responses. [Learn how to access form submissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-accessing-your-forms-submissions-table)Notifications from your forms are sent to the email associated with your Wix account.

### How do I change the design of a form?

Design your form to suit the rest of your site's style by choosing from a range of preset form designs. After editing any of your form's aspects, you will need to re-activate any Automations linked to your form.
1. Click your form in ADI.
2. Click** Design**.
3. Select a new layout.
**Note: **Different form designs may have different form fields.
