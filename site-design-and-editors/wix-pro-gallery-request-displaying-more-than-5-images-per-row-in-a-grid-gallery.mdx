---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Displaying More Than 5 Images Per Row in a Grid Gallery"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display more than 5 images per row in a Pro Gallery with a Grid layout.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can display more than 5 items per row from the Layout tab, by setting your gallery to **Fit to screen** and adjusting the **Thumbnail size**.
> **To show more than 5 images per row:**
> 1. Click the Pro Gallery in your Editor.
> 2. Click **Settings**.
> 3. Click the **Layout **tab.
> 4. Select **Fit to screen** under Design Settings.
> 5. Drag the **Thumbnail size** slider to add more images to a row.
>
> [image: A screenshot of selecting 'Fit to screen' in the Pro Gallery settings.]
>
> **Note:** It's possible more or less than 5 images will be displayed depending on your site visitor's screen size.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
