At Wix, we offer powerful CRM tools that help you communicate and manage processes effectively. Our CRM tools include Contacts, Inbox, Automations, Email Marketing, and more. These tools allow you to centralize your communication and streamline your workflows.

### What are the CRM tools?

CRM tools include:
- **Contacts: **[Manage all of a site's customers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-contact-list) in one place.
- **Inbox: **View and reply to all of a site's emails and chat messages in your [Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-an-overview).
- **Workflows: **Manage a site's [workflows](https://support.wix.com/en/article/workflows-getting-started) and simplify back-office processes.
- **Automations: **Manage a site's automated responses, tasks, and more using [Wix Automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-getting-started).
- **Tasks & Reminders: **Manage all of your site tasks and due dates with [Tasks & Reminders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-tasks-reminders).
- **Email Marketing: **Manage [email campaigns](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-getting-started) that relate to your site.

### Accessing site CRM tools

Everyone who has access to a site in Wix Studio (including teammates and clients) can access site-level CRM tools. These enable you to manage contacts, send messages, create automations, and more.

#### To access a site's CRM tools:

[Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).  On the left of your dashboard, click the tool you’d like to use:

- **Contacts:** Hover over **Contacts** and click **Contacts**.
- **Workflows:** Hover over **Contacts** and click **Workflows**.
- **Tasks & Reminders:** Hover over **Contacts** and click **Tasks & Reminders**.
- **Inbox:** Hover over **Communications** and click **Inbox**.
- **Chat:** Hover over **Communications** and click **Chat**.
- **Automations:** Click **Automations**.
- **Email Marketing:** Hover over **Marketing & SEO** and click **Email Marketing**.

### Accessing workspace CRM tools

Workspace CRM tools function at the workspace level rather than the site level. For example, you can use your Wix Inbox to communicate with all of your clients, or create automations that streamline tasks within your team relating to how you're managing your clients.

> **You can only access workspace CRM tools if you are:**
>
> - A member of the Wix Partner Program in the Marketplace.
> - A member of the Wix Partner Program who used CRM tools before joining Wix Studio.

#### To access a workspace's CRM tools:

[Go to Marketplace Overview](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/marketplace?referralInfo=sidebar) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Choose the tool you want to use:

- **Contacts: **[Manage all of your contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-partners-managing-your-contacts) (clients) in one place.
- **Inbox: **View and reply to all of your client emails and chat messages in your [Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-partners-managing-your-inbox).
- **Workflows: **Manage your [workflows](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-partners-managing-your-workflows) and simplify your sales lead processes.
- **Automations: **Manage your workspace's automated responses, tasks, and more using [Wix Automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-managing-your-automations).
- **Tasks & Reminders: **Manage all of your workspace tasks and due dates with [Tasks & Reminders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-tasks-reminders).
- **Email Marketing: **Manage [email campaigns](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-partners-working-with-your-email-marketing-tab) that relate to your workspace rather than specific sites.

Click a question below to learn more about CRM tools in Wix Studio.

### Why can't I see CRM tools in the workspace?

If you can't see CRM tools in the workspace, speak to the owner of the workspace who might have assigned you certain permissions.
The owner of the workspace has to be a member of the Wix Partner Program in the Marketplace, or have used CRM tools before joining Wix Studio to have access to workspace CRM tools in Wix Studio. Learn more about the [joining the Wix Partner Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-joining-the-wix-partner-program) and [applying to join the Marketplace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-applying-to-join-the-wix-marketplace).

### Why can't I see CRM tools in a site's dashboard?

Access to CRM tools depends on your permissions. If you are not the owner of the site, contact the owner with questions about your permissions.
