Use Wix Photo Albums to share professional album sites with clients and friends. Create new albums for all your events: wedding, vacations, portraits - anything!

Wix Photo Albums keeps all of your albums together on a dedicated gallery page on your site. Choose which albums appear and match the design of the album gallery to the rest of your site.

Each album in your gallery opens in its own easy to share mini-site. Plus, each album mini-site is individually customizable and can be password protected, so you can control who has access to your work.

Wix Photo Albums even has Adobe Lightroom integration, and a proofing tool (favorites lists), making it a fully integrated photo album solution.

### Create and Manage Your Albums

You have complete control on customizing and organizing your photo album. With Wix Photo Albums, you can choose the cover image for your album, select the layout and choose the text and background to suit your style.

### Show me how

1. [Create](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-creating-a-photo-album) your album (You can always come back to [organize](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-organizing-your-album) or [add more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-adding-new-photos-to-an-album) photos).
2. [Customize](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-designing-your-album) the design and layout of your album.
3. Choose which albums appear as thumbnails on your gallery page. To learn how to set up and design your albums gallery, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-customizing-the-album-gallery-on-the-photo-albums-page).

> If certain galleries need to be password protected, you can [add them to a private page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-applying-page-permissions).

### Set up Your Photo Albums Store

> **Important:**
>
> Wix Photo Albums Store is not available to all users. As of February 2023, it is no longer possible to add Wix Photo Albums Store to your site. As an alternative, you can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your photos.

Using the Wix Album Store you can create an album to sell your digital or photo prints directly from your site. You can choose to sell digital and prints or just digital files.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The Wix Photo Album Store is only available when you are creating a new album. It's not available for albums that you've already previously created.
> - To accept payments on your site, you'll need to have a Business & eCommerce Premium Plan. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview) to learn more about accepting payments.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [Photo Albums](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Photo%20Albums&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/photography-albums/) tab in your site's dashboard.
2. Follow the prompts to create your album.
3. Click the **Album** **Store** tab on the left side of the album Editor.
4. Click **Set Up** **Store**.
5. Choose what you want to sell:

- Digital Files and Prints.
- Digital Files Only.

**To sell Digital Files and Prints:**
1. Select **Sell Digital Files and Prints**.
2. Click **Next**.
3. Click **OK**.
4. Select the products you wish to sell.
5. Click **Units and Currency** to set your stock levels and currency type.
6. Enter your Business Information.
7. Click the checkbox to complete the ownership confirmation.
8. Click **Done**.
9. Click **Media** on the left.
10. Click **Manage Media **to arrange the photos in your gallery.
11. Click **Publish**.

**To sell Digital Files Only:**
1. Select **Digital Files Only**.
2. Click **Store** on the left.
3. Click **OK**.
4. Choose the licences you want to sell and set prices.
5. Click **Next**.
6. Set payment method (only if not already set).
7. Click the checkbox to complete the ownership confirmation.
8. Click **Next**.
9. Click **Media** on the left.
10. Click **Manage Media **to arrange the photos in your gallery.
11. Click **Done**.
12. Click **Publish**.
