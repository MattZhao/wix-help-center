---
title: "Wix Video: Deleting a Video from Your Video Library"
---

You can delete a video from your Wix Video library at any time. If your video was displayed in your channel widget, it is immediately removed.

> **Important:**
>
> Deleting the video player from your live site does not remove any videos from your video library.

### To delete a video from your video library:

[Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.  Select the checkbox on the left side next to the video(s) you want to delete.  Click the Delete icon** **.  Click **Delete** in the pop-up window.
