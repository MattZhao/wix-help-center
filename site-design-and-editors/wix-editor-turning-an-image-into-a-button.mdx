---
title: "Wix Editor: Turning an Image into a Button"
---

Turn any image on your site into a button by adding a link to it. You can link to many different types of places including a page on your site, external web address, document or email.

Learn more about the [types of links](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-link-to-a-button) you can link to.

### To turn an image into a button:

Click the image in your Editor.Click the **Settings** icon .Click the **When image is clicked **drop-down and select** A link opens**.Under **What does it link to? **click the **Link** icon . Select the link type and enter the details.Click **Done**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Use the [Wix Photo Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-about-the-wix-photo-studio) to edit your image and make it look more like a button. You can cut out the background, and add text to create the button you want.
