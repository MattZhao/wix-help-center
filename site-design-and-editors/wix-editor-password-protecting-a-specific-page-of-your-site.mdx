title: "Wix Editor: Password Protecting a Specific Page of Your Site"
---

Limit the access to a specific site page by password protecting it. Visitors who navigate to a protected page are prompted to enter the password. This allows you to share your exclusive content on your own terms.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adding password protection to a page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site#adding-password-protection-to-a-page)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site#faqs)

> **Important:**
>
> If you choose to password protect a page, this also prevents search engines from crawling and indexing that page. This means that password protected pages do not appear in search results.

---

### Adding password protection to a page

Head over to the Page Settings panel to add a password to a site page. Once you publish this change, you can send the password to anyone who needs it.

#### To password protect a page:

Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant page.  Click **Settings**.   Click the **Permissions **tab.  Select **Password holders**.   Enter your chosen password for the page.   Press the **Enter** key on your keyboard.

> **Tip:**
>
> Make sure to send the password to visitors that you want to access the page.

### FAQs

Select a question to learn more about password protected pages.

### When should I add password protection to a page?

Password protection can be used for a variety of needs. Some common examples include party / event invitations, small business updates, and contact information.

### Can I password protect my entire site?

Yes, you can [password protect your entire site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-your-site) with a single password.

### Can I password protect my site's homepage?

Absolutely. However, you should be aware that adding a password to your homepage restricts access to the rest of the site – unless someone has a direct link to other site pages.

### Is code affected by password protection?

Any code you have added with [Velo by Wix](https://www.wix.com/code/home) to a password protected page, or the public folder, can still be viewed using the browser developer tools.
