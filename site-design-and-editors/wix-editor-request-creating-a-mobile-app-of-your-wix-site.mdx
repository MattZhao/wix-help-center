---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Creating a Mobile App of Your Wix Site"
---

Creating a mobile app version of your Wix site is currently not supported.

However, you can create your own custom app with Branded App to exhibit your brand or business, and encourage visitors to keep in touch. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
