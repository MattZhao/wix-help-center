---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Support for Right to Left Script in Podcast Player"
---

Currently, right to left script/languages (e.g. Hebrew, Arabic) are not supported in Wix Podcast Player.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
