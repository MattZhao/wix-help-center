---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Applying Hover Effects to Specific Gallery Items"
---

Currently, it is not possible to apply hover effects to individual gallery items. When you apply a hover effect to a gallery, it is applied to all gallery items.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
