---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Manually Placing the Pin in Google Maps"
---

The location of the pin in Google Maps is determined by the address entered in the **Address** field. Currently, it is not possible to manually move the pin.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
