---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding Videos to Your Wix Site"
---

Videos are a great way to engage your visitors and tell your brand's story. Wix makes it easy to showcase your content, embed from social media and add stunning video-based design elements.

Check out these options to get started:

### Add one video with the Single Video Player

[The Single Video Player](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-and-setting-up-the-single-video-player) is just that - a no frills player showcasing one video. Upload your own video (up to 1GB) or add a video from a social network by copying and pasting the link.

The player is resizable and you can customize the settings and frame.

### Get advanced features with Wix Video

[Wix Video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-displaying-videos-on-your-site) gives you everything you need to showcase video content on your site.
- Upload your own videos (up to 15 GB per file).
- Organize your videos into channels or display standalone videos.
- Easily share to and from social media platforms like YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook.
- Sell and rent your videos - commission free!
- Allow fans to comment on and download your videos.
- Track your revenue and statistics.
Plus, all of the video player layouts are fully customizable to fit the look and feel of your site.

### Use a page video background

Create a truly captivating site experience with a video background. There are two ways:
1. To learn how to [use a free video from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-video-to-your-page-background) as your background.
2. To learn how to [upload your own video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-your-own-video-page-background) for your background.

### Add a full-width video strip

[A video strip](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-strip-background#adding-a-video-to-your-strip-background) is a design element that automatically stretches a video to fit across your screen.

### Add a VideoBox

Use the [VideoBox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-videoboxes) to display your videos with creative frames and graphic overlays.
