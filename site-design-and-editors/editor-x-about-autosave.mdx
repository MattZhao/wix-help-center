---
title: "Editor X: About Autosave"
---

Editor X automatically saves your site as you're working so you never have to worry about losing your progress. This makes the design process much more convenient, and allows a smoother process when collaborating on the same site with other people.

---

### How autosave works

Editor X automatically saves your site after every change you make. On the top bar, you can see an indication that Autosave is automatically enabled.

When collaborating with people on the same site, Autosave ensures your updates are synced and displayed in real time. As you can see each other's changes within seconds, design and content conflicts are less likely to happen.

> **Important:**
>
> Autosave is automatically enabled on your Editor X site and cannot be disabled.

---

### Viewing saved versions of your site

You can always go back and review your work that was saved in the background. From the [Site History panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-and-managing-your-site-history), you can see all of your site's autosaved versions. Each version is comprised of around 30 recent changes and labeled by the date and time it was saved.

> **Want to use a previous version?**
>
> Hover over it and click **Restore**.

---

### Troubleshooting autosave issues

If we weren't able to autosave your site, you'll see an error message specifying why and how to resolve the issue.

Click on an error message below to learn more about it:

### Your recent changes aren't saved

This means your internet connection wasn't stable enough to autosave your site. Check your connection before you continue working so you don't lose any progress.

### Site can't be saved right now

This is a temporary technical issue on our end. Try waiting a few minutes, then reload Editor X. 
**Note: **You may lose recent changes when you reload.

### You're logged out

This means you're logged out of your account and need to log back in to continue working on this site. 
Keep the tab with Editor X open. In a new tab, go to [wix.com](https://wix.com) and sign in. You should then be able to continue editing your site.

### You're signed into a different account

This means you're currently logged in to the wrong account. To continue working on this Editor X site, you need to sign in to the relevant account. 
Keep the tab with Editor X open. In a new tab, go to [wix.com](https://wix.com/) and sign in. You should then be able to continue editing your site.

> **Still experiencing issues?**
>
> Contact Customer Care with [these details](https://support.wix.com/en/article/error-message-details).
