---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Customizing the Password Window of a Password Protected Page"
---

Currently, it is not possible to customize the design of the window that is displayed on password protected pages.

The window's fonts and colors are taken from the Site Styles panel. [Updating these options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-your-site-styles) changes the look of the window.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
