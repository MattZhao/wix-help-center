---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing Your Favicon"
---

A favicon is a small icon that appears on a tab in your browser. It is a great way to help visitors identify your brand.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> In order to upload your own favicon you must first:
> 1. [Upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-your-site-to-premium-3066683) to a Premium Plan (except the Connect Domain plan).
> 2. [Connect a domain to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-your-site-195136).

### To change your favicon:

Go to [Settings in your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/manage-website).  Click **Website Settings**.  Click **Update Image** next to **Favicon**.

Select your favicon and click **Choose Files**.

**Note: **The size of your favicon must be a multiple of 48px square (such as 48x48, 96x96 etc), unless it is an SVG file. Google will rescale your favicon to 16x16px for search results.

> **Don't have a favicon?**
>
> You can create a personalized or branded favicon with [Wix Logo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-2200409)**.**
