### Element categories

There are three main categories which cover all of the different element types in the editor:

**Essential elements:** These are used to display most of the content on your site. They include text, menus, images, and buttons. Use essential elements to set up your site, and showcase your services and information.  **Decorative elements:** We have a ton of elements that can be used for both functional and decorative purposes. They include shapes, vector art, and galleries.  **Advanced elements:** You can add advanced elements to capture leads and further expand your business. Popular advanced elements include repeaters, forms, and embeds.

---

### Important information about elements

Before you begin adding elements to your site, there are a few important things to keep in mind:

Some elements aren't available in the Add panel until you enable specific features. For example, you need to enable [Velo Dev Mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-velo-by-wix) in order to see the Breadcrumbs element in the panel.  There are elements that you can add to your site, but require additional steps in order to work properly. For example, to use a Pagination Bar on your site you need to [add the CMS to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-getting-started) and create a [collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-creating-a-collection).  When clicking through element categories in the panel, you may occasionally see [themed elements ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-sites-theme-and-design#adding-themed-elements)(such as themed text, themed buttons, etc). These elements have been handpicked for your site, as they match its color and text theme.

> **Ready to get started?**
>
> Explore and add elements to your site by clicking **Add Elements**  on the left side of your editor.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want more information about each element type, you can click the Help icon  at the top of the Add panel.

### Essential elements in the editor

Begin building your site with essential elements. They make up the most important parts of your site's content, so you have everything you need to display information and keep visitors informed.

Element types are divided by tabs in the Add panel, so you can easily find what you're looking for. Check out the list below to see the available essential element types to add in the editor.

Element tab nameExplanation**Text**You can add the following text elements to your site:
- **Titles: **These should be used for headings to let your visitors know what the pages or sections are about.
- **Paragraphs:** You can use this element for most of the text content on your site.
- **Collapsible text: **This shows a text preview, and visitors can either click a link or an expand button to view the full body of text.
**Image**Use images to add life to your site, and display content such as events or photography. You can add images in three ways:
- **My uploads:** Upload images directly to the Media Manager, then add them to your site.
- **Image collections: **Add images and illustrations from the free Wix collections or purchase professional stock images from Shutterstock and Unsplash.
- **My social images: **Connect your social networks (e.g. Facebook, Flickr, Instagram etc.), and add your favorite images to your site.
**Button**Buttons can be used to get visitors to explore your site, or perform certain actions. There are two button types that you can add to your site:
- **Regular buttons:** Regular buttons include image, icon, and text buttons. You can also add upload or file buttons to help you manage files provided by your visitors.
- **Payment buttons:** These buttons make it quick and easy to accept payments. You can add a button that connects to your site's payment methods, or your PayPal account.
**Strip**Strips are containers that you can divide into columns, add to sections, or use as rows. You can add pre-designed types for your site's needs, or blank strips to start from scratch.**Box**Add boxes to frame your content and other elements. There are two types of boxes available:
- **Container Boxes:** These boxes are an easy way to group multiple elements together and align your site neatly.
- **Hover Boxes: **These boxes change appearance when visitors hover over them. You can customize what's shown on hover, add fun effects, and design the box as needed.
**Menu & Anchor**This category includes all your visitors need to navigate around your site. There are four types: 
- **Menus: **Add menus to let your visitors easily navigate around your site. You can choose from a range of horizontal and vertical styles.
- **Breadcrumbs:** Breadcrumbs help display the structure and hierarchy of your site and allow visitors to click to navigate it. Visitors can use the breadcrumb trail to see the current page they're on, the main page if they're on a subpage, and the homepage.
- **Anchors:** Add anchors to direct your visitors to a specific part of your page. You can link to these anchors from menus, buttons and other elements. You can also add in-page menus, which are especially useful for one page sites.
- **Site Search:** Let your visitors search for anything on your site using a search bar. The results of the search are displayed clearly in a list, so that your visitors can easily find what they are looking for.
**Social**Add a social element so visitors can easily find and follow you on your social network accounts. The elements available to add are:
- **Social:** There are a range of social tools available for you to add to your site to connect with visitors. These include Social Bars, Twitter Follow buttons, and VK Share buttons.
- **Music & Video:** Add a Youtube Subscribe button that lets visitors subscribe to your Youtube account, and enjoy your content.

### Decorative and interactive elements in the editor

Use decorative and interactive elements make your site appealing for visitors. You can use them for design purposes, or to encourage your visitors to keep clicking.

Below are listed all of the decorative and interactive elements types available in the Add panel.

Element tab nameExplanation**Decorative**Decorative elements make your site stand out, while creating a fun experience for your visitors. The decorative tab includes:
- **Shapes:** Choose from hundreds of shapes and lines. Basic shapes can liven up your site, and lines can be used to structure or organize content.
- **Vector art: **Add beautiful digital illustrations from our huge bank of vector art. They're fun, professional, and maintain their high quality when resized.
- **Videos: **Use videos to showcase your work, story, or just your day-to-day life. Depending on your needs, you can add a VideoBox, Video Mask, or a Transparent Video.
**Gallery**Showcase your site's images and videos in a gallery. There are four gallery types available to add:
- **Pro Galleries: **These galleries are responsive, multimedia galleries that allow you to display images, videos and text alongside one another, along with share and download options.
- **Grid Galleries: **Grid galleries allow you to present your images in a simple and structured layout, and are great for sites with a more minimalist look.
- **Slider Galleries: **Slider galleries offer a dynamic view of your images, along with options to customize how they play.
- **More Galleries: **These galleries include a freestyle layout, and a honeycomb pattern, so you can display your media exactly the way you want to.
**Video & Music**Use these elements to add fun, lively media to your site. This tab includes:
- **Video:** Videos are a great way to engage your visitors and tell your brand's story. There are five video types available to add, so you can choose the best one for your needs.
- **Music:** Music elements give you a wide range of way to showcase your music to visitors. Depending on the type you add, you can play, share, and even sell your music directly from your site.
**Interactive**Interactive elements encourage visitors to explore, and engage with your site. There are five options available:
- **Slideshows:** Slideshows are a versatile way to showcase content on your site. You can use them to promote special deals, show off a new product collection, or display presentations.
- **Popups:** Popups are a great way to grab your visitors' attention. Use them to show important messages and announcements.
- **Boxes: **Add an interactive hover box to your site. They change appearance when visitors hover over them, and you can customize them to your liking.
- **Repeaters:** Repeaters are lists of items that have the same design and layout, but show different content. Hover repeaters contain effects that make the items change slightly when your visitors hover over them.
- **Indicators:** Pagination Bars help your visitors navigate between pages of items in your collections, as well as go to a specific page.
**My Designs**This panel shows you any elements you have designed and saved in the Editor. Once you save a design, you can add it to any editor site on your account. [Learn how to save element designs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-and-reusing-customized-elements-my-designs)

### Advanced elements and apps in the editor

Advanced elements allow you to keep growing and expanding your business, as well as further upgrade the design of your site. You can add elements like forms and lists that connect to a dataset, or even custom elements with your own code.

Check out our list below to see all of the advanced elements and apps you can add to your site from the panel.

Element tab nameExplanation**Contact & Forms**This category includes ways for your visitors to contact you or submit information: 
- **Forms:** Collect information from site visitors using forms. There are a variety of types available for your needs, and you can customize them to your liking.
- **Google Maps:** Help site visitors find the location of you or your business. You can add one or multiple addresses to your map, as well as customize its design.
- **Chat:** Chat features allow you to respond to your visitors in real time. Use Wix Chat to speak directly to site visitors, or add the Line Share button to allow visitors to share your site to their account.
**List**Lists allow you to store and display content and information on your site. There are two types of lists to add to your pages:
- **Repeaters:** A repeater is a list of items that have the same design and layout, but different content. You can add pre-designed or blank repeaters, or repeaters with a hover effect.
- **Tables: **Use tables to showcase content that's connected to your database collections. If you change the collection in the backend, the table is automatically updated.
**Embed Code**Embed code on your site to display external services, or use advanced tools to connect marketing integrations and custom elements. The types of embeds available in the editor are: 
- **Popular: **Add external widgets, apps, or sites to your page, or add a custom element.
- **Video:** Add a video player to your site to display a single video. You can choose a video from an external site, or upload your own.
- **Advanced: **Use advanced embeds to upgrade the experience for you and your site visitors with marketing integration tools, and custom web applications.
**Input**Add an input element to capture, store, edit, and display content from your visitors. There are three types of input elements you can add to your site:
- **Text: **These elements can be used to input small or large amounts of text, or text that can be formatted with options such as bold and italics.
- **Selection:** Selection elements allow your visitors to choose and submit options on your site. They include buttons, sliders, and dropdown menus.
- **Advanced:** These elements can be used to allow visitors to perform certain actions on your site, such as uploading their files, and inputting their details.
**CMS**[Add the CMS to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-getting-started) to create dynamic pages and elements.**Blog**In this tab, you can add [Wix Blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-about-the-wix-blog) to your site to start creating exciting, informative posts. If you've already added a blog, the tab displays elements to help promote your content, such as a Category Menu, or a Recent Posts Feed.**Store**You can [add Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-wix-stores) from this tab to begin selling products on your site. If you've already installed Stores, the tab displays elements that come with the app. Some of these include Product Galleries, and an Add to Cart button.**Bookings**Install [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings) on your site to allow visitors to book and pay for services. If you've already added it, the tab displays free add-on elements, such as Timetables and Health Waiver forms.**Events**Add the [Events app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-events) to your site, so you have everything you need to host an event. If you've already installed Events, the tab allows you to add pre-designed elements to announce your event, and control your guestlist.**Community**From this tab, you can add four different apps to your site to grow your online community: [Members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area), [Forum](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forum-about-wix-forum), [Groups](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-groups-about-groups), and [Online Programs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/online-programs-an-overview). If you already have any of the apps on your site, you can click the relevant app name to see and add free elements for that service.
