---
title: "Wix Video: Organizing Your Videos into Categories"
---

You can organize your videos into categories to let your visitors know the type of video it is.

### To add a category to Wix Videos:

[Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Edit ** icon on the relevant video.  Enter the category under **Categories**.  Press **Enter** on the keyboard.
