Deliver an authentic and engaging visual experience for visitors whose language directionality is right-to-left, e.g., Hebrew, Arabic and Urdu. Change the order of your gallery images, text direction and alignment to be RTL, directly from your Pro Gallery settings.

### Changing your gallery direction

Change the order of your gallery items to be displayed right-to-left in just one click. If you have chosen to display text in your gallery (titles and descriptions), the text direction is automatically adjusted to be RTL.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the Pro Gallery element on your page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Advanced**.
5. Select** Right to left **under **General Settings**.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the Pro Gallery element on your page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Advanced**.
5. Select** Right to left **under **General Settings**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you have chosen to display text (image title / description) on your gallery, make sure that the gallery direction you choose matches your language's direction, to avoid punctuation issues.

### Changing your gallery text alignment

Align your gallery item's titles and descriptions to the right, to ensure a seamless user experience for your visitors. Have the text that visitors see when viewing / hovering over gallery items, aligned to the right.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the Pro Gallery element on your page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Design**.
5. Click **Text**.
6. Click the **Align Right** icon   under **Horizontal alignment.**
[image: A screenshot showing the Horizontal alignment options.]
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the Pro Gallery element on your page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Design**.
5. Click **Text**.
6. Click the **Align Right** icon   under **Horizontal alignment.**
[image: A screenshot showing the Horizontal alignment options.]

### Changing text alignment in the Expand Mode

Align text to the right in the Expand Mode of gallery items.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the Pro Gallery element on your page.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Design**.
5. Click **Expand Mode**.
6. Click **Customize Expand Mode**.
7. Click the **Align Right** icon    under **Design Settings.**
[image: A screenshot showing the Text alignment option in the Expand Mode.]

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about adjust your gallery to be right-to-left:

### Can I display gallery text in more than one direction at the same time?

No. Currently, it is not possible to display gallery text in more than one direction at the same time, e.g., one image's text in English (left-to-right), and another image's text in Hebrew (right-to-left) simultaneously.

### When I set my gallery to direction to be right-to-left does this apply to the expand mode too?

No. Setting your gallery direction to be RTL only applies to your main gallery view and not the Expand Mode. If this is something you would like to see in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-customizing-text-direction-in-expand-mode).
