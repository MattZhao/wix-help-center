---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Menu to a Gallery"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a menu to a gallery.

As an alternative, you can try out any of our other gallery designs such as the Thumbnail gallery (found under Slider Galleries), which creates a similar effect as it shows a small preview of your gallery images in a form of a thumbnail.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
