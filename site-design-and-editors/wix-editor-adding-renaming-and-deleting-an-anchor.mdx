---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding, Renaming and Deleting an Anchor"
---

[Anchors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-linking-to-anchors-on-your-site) are an easy way to direct people to the bits of your site that matter most. You can add as many anchors as you need and rename them to easily recognize each one.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adding an anchor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-renaming-and-deleting-an-anchor#adding-an-anchor)  [Renaming an anchor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-renaming-and-deleting-an-anchor#renaming-an-anchor)  [Deleting an anchor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-renaming-and-deleting-an-anchor#delete-an-anchor)

---

### Adding an anchor

Add anchors to important parts of your site. Then, link elements and menu items to your anchors so that visitors can easily access those sections.

#### To add an anchor:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Menu & Anchor **.  Click **Anchor** under **In-Page Navigation**, or drag it to the relevant location on your page.

> **Note:**
>
> It is not possible to add an anchor to your page's footer.

---

### Renaming an anchor

After creating a new anchor, give it a name that you can recognize. Naming your anchors helps to differentiate between them, especially when they're linked to elements. These names are also visible to your site visitors if you add an anchor menu.

#### To rename an anchor:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the anchor.   Click **Rename**.  Type the name of your anchor.  Press **Enter **on your keyboard.

---

### Delete an anchor

You can always delete anchors that you no longer need.

#### To delete an anchor:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Right-click on the anchor you wish to delete.   Click **Delete**.
