title: "Wix Editor Request: Additional Country Flag Icons and Images (Free from Wix)"
---

We currently have a variety of country flag icons and images available in the Media from Wix library. Just search for your flag using the search bar.

If your country's flag is missing, submit a ticket to request it.

In the meantime, you are welcome to [upload](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-your-own-images) your country's flag icon as an image.
