---
title: "ADI Request: Additional Customization Options for the Wix Blog"
---

Currently, the Wix Blog has been designed to match your site perfectly in ADI and for that reason there are not many customization options available inside ADI.  The style, fonts and colors have all been chosen to complement your site.

If you want to customize your blog further, you should [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor).
