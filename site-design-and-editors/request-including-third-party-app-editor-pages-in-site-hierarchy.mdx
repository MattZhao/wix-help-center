---
title: "Request: Including Third-Party App Editor Pages in Site Hierarchy"
---

Currently, when defining your site hierarchy it is not possible to set a parent-child page relationship with an Editor page that is part of a third-party app.

You can define your hierarchy by [setting parent-child page relationships](https://support.wix.com/en/article/defining-your-site-hierarchy-for-main-pages-url-and-breadcrumbs-structure) between pages that are not part of third-party apps.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
