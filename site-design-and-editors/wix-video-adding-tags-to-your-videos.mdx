---
title: "Wix Video: Adding Tags to Your Videos"
---

Tags are used to help viewers search for videos in the channel, and also used as keywords for SEO. You can add tags to your videos to help your visitors search your channel more efficiently.

### To add a tag to a video:

[Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Edit ** icon on the video to which you want to add a tag.  Enter the tags in the **Tags **field.  Press **Enter** on the keyboard

> **Analytics & Reports:**
>
> You can find all the latest analytic reports on your video content in the [Analytics & Reports tab](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-video-analytics-reports) in your dashboard.
