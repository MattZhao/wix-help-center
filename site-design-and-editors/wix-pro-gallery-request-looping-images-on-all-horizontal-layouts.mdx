---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Looping Images on All Horizontal Layouts"
---

Currently, the loop images feature is only available on [sliding or slideshow galleries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-sliding-galleries). Therefore, if you have another layout selected, the option to loop images is not shown.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
