title: "Studio Editor Request: Changing the Sharpness of the Page Scroll"
---

Currently, it is not possible to adjust the sharpness of the scroll of a site page, e.g. make it smoother or sharper.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
