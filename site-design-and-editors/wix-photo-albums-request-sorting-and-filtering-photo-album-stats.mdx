---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Sorting and Filtering Photo Album Stats"
---

Currently, Wix Photo Albums displays photo album statistics in the Album Stats tab in the dashboard. These stats include album name, number of photos, number of views, and number of leads. It is not possible to sort or filter the list of albums according to these stats.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
