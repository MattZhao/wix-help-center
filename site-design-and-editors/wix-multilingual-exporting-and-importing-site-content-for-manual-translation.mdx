Export and import your site content for manual translation. By downloading a CSV file, you can send your content to a professional translator. Once the translations are ready, import the translated content back to your site.

### Exporting your site content

Begin by exporting your website's content into a CSV file. You can then send the file to a professional translator to have them translate the text.

#### To export your site content:

[Go to Multilingual](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/multilingual/manage-languages?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Site+languages) in your site's dashboard.   Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant language.   Click **Export Content**.   Click **Export Content** again to download the CSV file.

---

### Importing your translated content

Once you've completed the translation you can import it to your site. This process involves uploading your saved CSV file to the relevant language in your site languages. For example, if you are translating content from English to French, you need to upload the French content to your site's French language.

#### To import your translated content:

[Go to Multilingual](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/multilingual/manage-languages?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Site+languages) in your site's dashboard.   Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant language.   Click **Import translations**.

Click **Start Now**.   Click **Upload CSV File**.   Select the relevant CSV file.   Click** Import Translations**.  (Optional) Click **Review Translations**.  Review each page and check the status of the translation.

**Note: **The status should say "Translated".   Click **Save** when you are finished reviewing.   Publish your site to view the changes live.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more:

### What are the benefits of the import/export process?

There are several benefits to importing and exporting your content for translation purposes, but the main benefit overall is that you decide (based on your level of comfort), if you want to translate the content manually yourself, or send it to someone else.

### Why do I see more than one language in the exported file?

Each exported file contains two languages: the first column includes the content in your Source language, this is your original language (you shouldn’t edit this) and the second column (marked Target language) where you can edit the translation in your target language.

### What can cause the import process to fail?

There are several reasons the import process might fail. We recommend checking the following: 
- Make sure your file is saved as a CSV. This is the correct format in order for us to properly import the file.
- Make sure you haven’t made any changes to the “ID” column in the file.

### I do not see any new translations, what is the reason?

The issue is likely caused by one of the following: 
- You added the translated content to the wrong language.
- Changes to the “ID” column in the CSV file.
- The last column "Target Language" is empty.

### What can I do if my file does not download?

If a download didn’t start, press the “Download File” button in the green banner that appears at the top of your screen.[image: A screenshot showing the green banner that appears across your site that allows you to click and restart the download.]

### Is it possible to override content?

Yes, when you import your newly translated content, any fields in the translated language column where you entered content, will override existing content in Wix Multilingual for that specific language.**Tip:** If you wish not to override a specific piece of content, simply leave the field column empty in the file.

### Is there any site content that cannot be exported?

Yes, the exported content doesn’t include content from non-integrated business solutions and Third-party apps. Learn more about [translatable content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translatable-content#apps-you-can-and-cant-translate).

### Can you export/import more than one target language at a time?

No, the export/import process works on a language level. Meaning, you can only export one target language per file.

### What happens if I have existing translations in my target language?

The exported file also contains any existing translations in the target language, if they exist. This means that your CSV file will contain both new and existing translations in your target language.
