Get your brand online in just a few clicks, with a branded website designed automatically for you.

Wix generates website templates based on your logo's design, which you can customize completely to suit your needs. Available templates vary depending on the [Wix Logo plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans) you have.

[Just Logo / Free plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-branded-site-in-the-wix-logo-maker#creating-a-branded-site-with-a-just-logo-free-plan)  [Logo and Online Presence plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-branded-site-in-the-wix-logo-maker#creating-a-branded-site-with-a-logo-and-online-presence-plan)  [FAQs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-branded-site-in-the-wix-logo-maker#faqs)

### Creating a branded site with a Just Logo / Free plan

Choose from 3 branded site templates available with Just Logo or Free plans. Then customize your chosen template to suit your brand.

#### To get a branded site:

Go to the [Brand Studio](http://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh).  Click the **My Brands** drop-down at the top.  Select the relevant brand.

Scroll to the **Get a branded website** section.  Hover over the website template and click **Choose a Website**.  Choose a website type: **Online Store**, **Basic Website** or **Landing Page**.  Click **Edit this website** at the bottom.  [Customize your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-wix-editor) as much as you want.  Click **Save**,** **then **Publish **when you're ready.

**Tip:** [Learn more about publishing your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site).

---

### Creating a branded site with a Logo and Online Presence plan

Each Logo and Online Presence plan comes with a dedicated site template assigned to it. This means the available site template you receive depends on the Logo and Online Presence plan you have.

#### To get a branded site:

Go to the [Brand Studio](http://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh).  Click the **My Brands** drop-down at the top.  Select the relevant brand.

Scroll to the **Your branded website** section.  Hover over the template and click **Edit Site**.  [Customize your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-wix-editor) as much as you want.  Click **Save, **then **Publish **when you're ready.

**Tip:** [Learn more about publishing your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site).

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Manage Site** next to **Your branded website** to open your site's dashboard. Your dashboard includes business tools and apps to help you get your website up and running. [Learn more about your Wix dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-wix-dashboard).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about creating a branded site in the Wix Logo Maker.

### How do I view my live site?

After you publish your site, it automatically appears online and is accessible by anyone entering your site URL or domain name. [Learn how to view your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site#viewing-your-live-site)

### How do I change the URL assigned to my site?

When a Wix site is published, it receives a "free domain" or URL with the name Wix in it. The format of the free Wix site URL is **siteprefix.wixsite.com/siteaddress**.
You can [replace the free Wix URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-free-wix-url-8591528) by [connecting your own domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere#connecting-a-domain-to-your-site) name (e.g. www.mystunningwebsite.com) to your site at any time. Or, you can [purchase a domain from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977).

Having your own domain improves your SEO, which helps you get better placement in search engine results.

### I don't want to use the site template that comes with my brand. How can I get a different one?

You can always create a site using any of our templates and [assign your plan to that site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-premium-plan-to-a-different-site-in-your-wix-account).

Start by looking through the [template menu](https://www.wix.com/website/templates) to find the category that best describes your business, or the website you want to create. Choose your favorite template, then [customize](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-wix-editor) it to make it your own.

### If I update my logo design, does my branded site design automatically update too?

No. The branded site template that comes with your logo is based on your original logo design. You must update your site manually to reflect any changes to your logo.

**To update your site:**
1. Go to the [Brand Studio](http://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh).
2. Click the **My Brands** drop-down at the top.
3. Select the relevant brand.
4. Scroll to the **Your branded website** section.
5. Hover the template image and click **Edit Site**.
6. [Update the relevant parts of your site](https://support.wix.com/en/using-the-wix-editor).

[image: The template image in the Wix Brand Studio. The cursor is hovering over the Edit Site button.]

### Can I apply the branded colors and fonts of my logo to an existing site?

No. Currently, it is not possible to apply the colors and fonts you used in your logo to an existing site. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-request-applying-branded-colors-and-fonts-to-an-existing-site).

**Tip:** You can update your site manually [using your logo's Color Code Guide](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-downloading-your-logo-in-the-wix-logo-maker#reviewing-your-logo-color-guide), or you can [create a new branded site from the Brand Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-branded-site-in-the-wix-logo-maker).
