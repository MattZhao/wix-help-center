---
title: "Wix Photo Albums: Adding New Galleries to an Album"
---

Your photo album comes with a cover page and a gallery. You can add additional galleries to your album at any time.

> **Tip:**
>
> By adding additional album galleries you can separate different themes (e.g. family and friends, pets) or different times in an event (e.g. preparations for the wedding ceremony, the wedding party, etc.).

### To add a new album gallery:

Go to [Photo Albums](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Photo%20Albums&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fphotography-albums/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.Click the **Show More** icon  next to the relevant album.Click **Customize**.Click **Media** on the left. Click **Add New Gallery** at the bottom.Add photos to the new gallery:
- **Upload new photos: **
1. Hover over the gallery and click **Manage Media**.
2. Upload photos using the media manager.
3. When ready, click **Done** at the bottom.
- **Move photos from other galleries: **
1. Hover over the gallery with the photos you want to move.
2. Click **Manage Media**.
3. Select the photo you want to copy or move.
**Tip:** Hold down the **Ctrl** key to select multiple photos, or click **Select All **to select all of them.
4. Click **Move To** on the top bar.
5. Select the new gallery from the drop-down menu.
(Optional) Click **Publish Now** to publish your changes.

> **Important:**
>
> Each page is part of the general album, but can also be accessed directly using its own subdomain. Each gallery receives a unique URL (e.g. www.yourdomain.com/album_name/page_name)
