---
title: "Wix Video Request: Members Area Integration"
---

Currently, Wix Video does not integrate with Members Area. Adding a Members Area to your Wix Video platform would allow members of your site to see their purchase history for your videos. It would also allow site members to "like" a video and create their own playlist of videos.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
