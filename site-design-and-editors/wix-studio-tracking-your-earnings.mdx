> **Track your earnings:**

### FAQs

Click on a question below to learn more about earning from revenue share and app sales.

### How do I get started with revenue share?

First, you need to [join the Wix Partner Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-joining-the-wix-partner-program). Once you become a Wix Partner, you'll need to connect a payout method in the [Revenue Share tab](https://manage.wix.com/studio/revenues?referralInfo=sidebar) of your Wix Studio workspace to get started. Learn more about [earning revenue share as a Wix Partner](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-receiving-revenue-share).**Note:** Only workspace owners and co-owners can view and manage the Revenue Share tab.

### How do I get started with selling custom Studio templates?

Apply to [join the Partner Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-joining-the-wix-partner-program)—we’ll review your request within 2 days. Once accepted, can start submitting your templates. 
If you're a Wix Partner, start by creating your custom Studio template ensuring it meets our [submission requirements and guidelines](https://www.wix.com/studio/partner-program/submit-templates/guidelines). Submit it to the Marketplace, where our team will review your submission—a process that typically takes up to 60 days. Once your template is live and sales start coming in, you'll be able to track your earnings. Learn more about [selling custom Studio templates in the Marketplace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-selling-your-studio-templates-in-the-marketplace).

### How often are template purchases updated?

Template purchases are updated in real-time.

### How do I get started with custom apps?

In Wix Studio, you can design, build and manage custom apps that can be added to all Wix sites. Create and code within [Wix Blocks](https://support.wix.com/en/article/blocks-unboxed-a-quick-tour), our app and widget build environment, and manage your apps from Custom Apps in your Wix Studio workspace. Learn more about [building custom apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-creating-custom-apps).

### How often are revenue share earnings paid out?

Your revenue share earnings as a Wix Partner from client sales, plans, and templates are invoiced together. To qualify for a payout, your total earnings from plans, client sales, and templates must reach a minimum of $200. Once you meet this threshold, the payout cycle is net 30 days. The payout cycle on revenue share for Enterprise plan is net 90 days. Learn more about [tracking your payouts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-tracking-your-payouts).

### How can I submit apps to the Wix App Market for app sales?

Once you've built your app, completed all the required info, and tested it, you can submit it to the Wix App Market. We'll review your app and be in touch within 15 business days with the results. During this time, the app will be locked and you won't be able to make any changes. Learn more about [submitting your app for review](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/launch-your-app/app-submission/submit-your-first-app-version).

### Can I earn revenue share from my client's existing Wix plans?

No. You have to be the first owner to be eligible to earn.

### Is there a limit to the number of sites I can onboard to earn from?

No. There's no limit.
