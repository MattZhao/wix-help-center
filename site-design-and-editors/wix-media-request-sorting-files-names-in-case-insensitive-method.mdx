---
title: "Wix Media Request: Sorting Files Names in Case-Insensitive Method"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sort files in a non-case-sensitive method in the Media Manager. Files whose names begin with a capital letter are listed first, followed by files whose names begin with a lower case letter.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
