---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Receiving Notifications When Visitors \"Like\" Your Images or Videos"
---

Currently, it is not possible to get a notification when a visitor clicks "like" on one of your images or videos in Pro Gallery.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
