---
title: "ADI Request: Adding a Text Box"
---

Currently, you cannot add text boxes inside ADI.

If you want to add more text boxes, you need to [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text).
