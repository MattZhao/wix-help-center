title: "Site Performance: CMS"
---

You may find that displaying a large amount of items at once on your repeater / table / gallery can impact your page’s performance.

You can adjust the **Maximum items displayed** field in your dataset settings to [control the number of items that can load](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-changing-the-maximum-items-displayed-in-your-dataset-settings) at a time. This helps you:

Load your page faster with fewer items on the initial page load.  Reduce the number of larger items.  Enhance your page's design.

Click an option below to learn more about improving your site's performance when using the CMS (Content Management System).

### Showing large amounts of content on data-bound elements

If you have a large amount of content to show on such data-bound elements, it is recommended to limit the amount of items that are loaded at once, and add a “[Load More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dataset-click-actions-for-buttons-and-images#load-more)” button or a [pagination bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-adding-and-setting-up-a-pagination-bar), which will load one batch of such items at a time. A good practice would be to show up to 10 items with every new load.

### Using deferred datasets

You may require your repeaters to contain a lot of content, or be connected to a large collection. If this is the case, we recommend using a deferred dataset. These datasets load the connected content only after the rest of the page loads, which can improve your site's performance. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-setting-up-a-deferred-dataset)

### Displaying content linked to your dataset

If you have content in your collection that is not needed on your page, you can stop it from being displayed. To do this, [add a filter to the dataset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-filters-and-sorts-to-datasets-to-control-live-site-content) and choose what content to display.
