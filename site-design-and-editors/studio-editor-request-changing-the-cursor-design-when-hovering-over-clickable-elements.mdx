---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Changing the Cursor Design When Hovering Over Clickable Elements"
---

Currently, it's not possible to set a cursor to change its design (e.g. color, size) when a visitor hovers over a clickable element.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
