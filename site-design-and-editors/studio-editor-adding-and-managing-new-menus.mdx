title: "Studio Editor: Adding and Managing New Menus"
---

Add our latest menu element to streamline your site-building process while enhancing navigation for your client's visitors. Choose the best layout for each breakpoint – horizontal, vertical or a hamburger menu. Switching between layouts is straightforward, so you can adjust on the fly as your design evolves.

Manage the menu items as needed by adding, removing and reordering the items. You can use dropdowns to display submenus with your own content, giving you the ability to create a tailored experience that fits your client's needs.

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to the new menu element in the Studio Editor. For steps on managing the previous menu experience, [please click here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-old-menus).

### How do I know which menu I have?

You can check whether you have the new or old experience by clicking the menu in your editor and clicking the **Layout** icon .

- **New menus:**

- The blue label says **Menu**.
- The Layout panel displays the option to change the menu type (between navigation bar or hamburger).

[image: The Layout panel of a new menu. The menu's name and the option to change the type are highlighted.]

- **Old menus:**

- The blue label specifies the type of menu element you have (e.g. Horizontal Menu).
- The Layout panel does not display the option to change the menu type.

[image: The Layout panel of an old menu. The menu's name and the layout options are highlighted.]

### Adding a menu

Add a menu to the site to get started. We recommend adding a menu to the site header, but you can add and set up as many as you need to tailor the experience for different pages and sections.

Once you add the menu, choose the items you want it to display (e.g. site pages, links, etc).

#### To add a menu:

Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of your editor.  Select **Menu & Search**.   Select the menu type you want (Horizontal, Vertical, or Hamburger).   Click the menu and drag it to the desired position on the page.  Add items to the new menu:

1. Select the menu you just added.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Click **+ Add Item **at the bottom.
4. Select the item you want to add: 

- **Main pages: **Add a regular site page to the menu.
- **Dynamic and app pages: **Add pages from apps (e.g. a Wix Stores product page), or pages connected to collections in CMS.
- **Link: **You can add many kinds of links to the menu, such as links other websites, anchors on site pages, your client's contact details, etc.
- **Dropdown:** Add a container that appears below the menu when visitors hover over the item. You can design it as needed, and use it to display menu items and additional content.

> **Changed your mind about the menu layout (type)?**
>
> Don't worry - you can always change it later.

### Managing menu items

You can always adjust an existing menu and the items it shows. Add more items, remove ones that are irrelevant, or change the order to keep the content fresh.

#### To manage a menu:

Select the relevant menu in your editor.   Click **Manage Menu**.  Choose what to do next:

### Add more menu items

Click **+ Add Item** at the bottom and select the item you want to add:
- **Main pages: **Add a regular site page to the menu.
- **Dynamic and app pages: **Add pages from apps (e.g. a Wix Stores product page), or pages connected to collections in CMS.
- **Link: **You can add many kinds of links to the menu, such as links other websites, anchors on site pages, your client's contact details, etc.
- **Dropdown:** Add a container that appears below the menu when visitors hover over the item. You can customize the design, use it to display submenus, and attach elements to create a custom experience.

### Change a menu item's link

1. Hover over the relevant item and click the **More Actions** icon .
2. Click **Change Link**.
3. Change the link type and / or its details.
4. Click **Done**.

**Tip: **You can also click **Remove Link** to delete a link from a menu item.

### Rename a menu item

1. Hover over the relevant item and click the **More Actions** icon .
2. Click **Rename**.
3. Enter the new name.
4. Press the **Enter** key on your keyboard to save your changes.

### Remove or delete an item

Depending on the item type, you may see the option to remove it from the menu, or delete it completely.
**Removing items:** These are items that exist regardless of the menu, like site pages and lightboxes. Removing these items means they're no longer displayed in the menu, but have not been deleted from the site. 
1. Hover over the relevant item and click the **More Actions** icon .
2. Click **Remove from menu**.

**Deleting items:** These are items that only exist within the menu, and not as separate elements (e.g. dropdowns). When you delete these items, they're gone from the site.
1. Hover over the relevant item and click the **More Actions** icon .
2. Click **Delete**.

**Note:** If you delete a dropdown, any pages you have added to it are removed from the menu. However, you can always re-add them as needed.

### Reorder menu items

Hold the **Reorder** icon  next to an item and drag it to the desired position.

### Creating dropdowns

There are 2 ways to create dropdowns in a menu, depending on what you want the title to be:

> **Tip:**
>
> Dropdowns were called mega menus in the previous menu experience.

#### To add and set up a dropdown:

Select the relevant menu in the editor.  Click **Manage Menu**.  Choose what you want to do:

- **Use an existing menu item:**

1. Drag a menu item to move it below the item you want as the dropdown title.
2. Hover over the same item and click the **More Actions** icon .
3. Select **Move under "Page Name"**.
4. Repeat steps a - c for all other items.
- **Create a new dropdown:**

1. Click **+ Add Item**.
2. Select **Dropdown**.
3. Enter the title for the dropdown.
4. Press the **Enter** key on your keyboard to save your changes.
5. Drag and drop menu items under the dropdown to add them.
6. Repeat step e for all other items you want to add.

> [Learn more about working with dropdowns](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-dropdowns-in-a-menu)

### Switching the menu to a different layout

Use the layout settings to choose the menu type you want - a horizontal or vertical navigation bar, or a hamburger menu.

#### To set the layout:

Click the menu in your editor.  Click the **Layout** icon .  Select the menu type:

- **Navigation bar:** Menu items are displayed in a bar. Under **Orientation**, choose whether the bar is horizontal or vertical.
- **Hamburger:** An icon is displayed. When site visitors click the icon, the menu opens.

### Displaying multiple menus on the same site

You can create as many menus as you need and show completely different items in each.

After adding a new menu element, you can choose which menu it should display: An existing menu that you saved, or a new one that you create on the spot. This means you can save menus and reuse them on various menu elements across the site.

Click below to learn how to:

### Choose which menu is displayed on a page

1. Select the relevant menu.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Click the drop-down under **Which menu is displayed?**.
4. Depending on the menu you need, follow the steps:

- **Existing menu: **Select the menu from the list.
- **New menu: **

1. Click** Manage Site Menus**.
2. Click** + Add New Menu **at the bottom.
3. Enter a name for the new menu.

**Note: **This name isn't visible to visitors.

[image: The drop-down that appears when selecting which menu is displayed in the element.]

### Save a new menu

1. Select the relevant menu.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Click the drop-down under **Which menu is displayed?**.
4. Select **Manage Site Menus**.
5. Click **+ Add New Menu** at the bottom.
6. Enter a name for the new menu. This name isn't visible to visitors.

[image: Creating a new menu in the Studio Editor.]
7. Start adding pages to the menu:

1. Select the new menu you just created.
2. Click the **Exit** icon  at the top right.
3. Click **+ Add new Item** to add pages, links, dropdowns, etc.
