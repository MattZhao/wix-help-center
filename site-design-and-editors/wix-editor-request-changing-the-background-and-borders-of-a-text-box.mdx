---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Changing the Background and Borders of a Text Box"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the background or border of a text box. However, you can place your text in a colored container box to achieve a similar effect.

### To place text in a colored container box:

Add a [container box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-box) to your site.  [Customize](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-box#customizing-your-box-design) the background and borders of the container box.  Click and drag the text box into the container box.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
