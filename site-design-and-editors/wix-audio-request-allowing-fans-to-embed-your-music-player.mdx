---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Allowing Fans to Embed Your Music Player"
---

Currently, fans can share, download or purchase your tracks, singles and albums, but they cannot embed your music player in their site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
