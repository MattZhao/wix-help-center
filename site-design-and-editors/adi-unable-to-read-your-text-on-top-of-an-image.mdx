---
title: "ADI: Unable to Read Your Text on Top of an Image"
---

If you cannot see the text on top of an image it is because the text is a similar color to the image. To resolve this, you should [change the design of your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors).
