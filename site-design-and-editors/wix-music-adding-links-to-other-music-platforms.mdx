---
title: "Wix Music: Adding Links to Other Music Platforms"
---

Add links to your albums, singles or tracks to other music platforms so that fans can listen and purchase your music elsewhere.

The links appear when your fans click the **Get Album** or** Get Track** button on your site:

### To add links to your music:

[Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant album, single or playlist.  Choose what you want to add the link to:

### Adding a link to an album

1. Click **Edit **next to **Album Info.**
2. Scroll down to **Links to Other Music Platforms**.
3. Enable the toggle.
4. Select the relevant music platform and add the link.
5. Click **Save**.

### Adding a link to a single

1. Click **Edit **next to **Single Info.**
2. Scroll down to **Links to Other Music Platforms**.
3. Enable the toggle.
4. Select the relevant music platform and add the link.
5. Click **Save**.

### Adding a link to a playlist track

1. Hover over the relevant track and click the Edit Track Info icon .
2. Scroll down to **Links to Other Music Platforms**.
3. Enable the toggle.
4. Select the relevant music platform and add the link.
5. Click **Save**.

4.  (Optional) Click **+Add link** to add more links.
