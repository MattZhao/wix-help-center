title: "Creating and Managing Albums in the Shared Gallery App"
---

With the Shared Gallery app, you can start sharing your photos and videos by uploading them to your gallery.

Creating albums lets you organize your media into themed folders with album names, descriptions, and cover photos. You and any site visitor [with permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-shared-gallery-app) can upload media and manage your albums.

> **Important:**
>
> By default, all your site members can upload media and only admins can manage albums. You can change these permissions on the app's Settings panel in your Editor. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-shared-gallery-app)

### Creating an album

You and any site visitor [with Manage Gallery permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-shared-gallery-app) can create new albums on your live site.

> **Important:**
>
> - When you create your first album, another album with the same name as your site is also created. All of the photos and videos that you previously uploaded are automatically moved to this folder.
> - Your gallery's main page will also display only albums instead of individual photos and videos.
> - To return to displaying individual photos and videos, you need to [delete your albums](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-and-managing-albums-in-the-shared-gallery-app#deleting-an-album).

#### To create an album:

DashboardLive Site
1. Go to [Shared Gallery in your site's dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp/476dc2dd-e16e-43b0-a0aa-1a486c78fbe2%3F&title=Shared%20Gallery%20Dashboard&buttonText=Shared+Gallery).
2. Click **Create Album**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to create a new album.]
1. Go to the Shared Gallery page on your site.
2. Log in with the email address associated with your Wix account.
3. Click **Create Album**.
4. Enter a name and description for your album.
5. Click the Add icon  to add a cover image.
6. Click **Create Album**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to create an album from your live site.]

### Adding photos and videos to an album

You and any site visitor [with Upload Photos permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-shared-gallery-app) can add photos and videos to your albums on your live site. You can also add photos and videos from the Shared Gallery section of your site's dashboard.

> **Important:**
>
> - After you add your first album, you can only add photos and videos to albums. You won't be able to add individual photos or videos to your main gallery page.
> - If you want to add individual photos or videos, you need to [delete your albums](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-and-managing-albums-in-the-shared-gallery-app#deleting-an-album).

#### To add photos and videos:

DashboardLive Site
1. Go to [Shared Gallery in your site's dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp/476dc2dd-e16e-43b0-a0aa-1a486c78fbe2%3F&title=Shared%20Gallery%20Dashboard&buttonText=Shared+Gallery).
2. Select the album that you want to add photos or videos to.
3. Click **Add Media**.** **
[image: A screenshot showing the option to add media to your shared gallery.]
1. Select photo or video file(s) from your computer.
2. Click **Open**.

1. Go to the Shared Gallery page on your site.
2. Log in with the email address associated with your Wix account.
3. Select the album that you want to add photos or videos to.
4. Click **Add Media**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to add media to your shared gallery]

> **Tip:**
>
> Click the More Actions icon  on a photo or video to see options to download, move, share, or delete it.

### Moving photos or videos between albums

You can move photos and videos between albums on your live site. Your site visitors can also move any media that they add. You can also move photos and videos in the Shared Gallery section of your site's dashboard.

#### To move photos and videos:

DashboardLive Site

1. Go to [Shared Gallery in your site's dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp/476dc2dd-e16e-43b0-a0aa-1a486c78fbe2%3F&title=Shared%20Gallery%20Dashboard&buttonText=Shared+Gallery).
2. Select the album with the photo or video that you want to move.
3. Click the checkbox next to the item(s) you want to move. **Note: **Click the checkbox at the top of the list if you want to move all the items.
4. Select **Move item**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to move and item]
1. Go to the Shared Gallery page on your site.
2. Log in with the email address associated with your Wix account.
3. Select the album with the photo or video that you want to move.
4. Click the More Actions icon  on the photo or video you want to move.
5. Select **Move**. **Tip:** You can move multiple photos and videos by clicking **Select**, choosing the items you want to move, then clicking **Move**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to move an image in a shared gallery.]

### Editing an album

You and any site visitor [with Manage Gallery permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-shared-gallery-app) can edit the name, description, and cover image of your album on your live site. You can also edit albums from the Shared Gallery section of your site's dashboard.

#### To edit an album:

DashboardLive Site
1. Go to [Shared Gallery in your site's dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp/476dc2dd-e16e-43b0-a0aa-1a486c78fbe2%3F&title=Shared%20Gallery%20Dashboard&buttonText=Shared+Gallery).
2. Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant album.
3. Select **Edit album**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to edit an album in your gallery.]
1. Edit the name, description, or cover image of your album. **Note: **Your cover image must be a .png or .jpg file and can be up to 25mb.
2. Click **Save**.

1. Go to the Shared Gallery page on your site.
2. Log in with the email address associated with your Wix account.
3. Hover over the album that you want to edit.
4. Click the More Actions icon .
5. Select **Edit album**.
6. Edit the name, description, or cover image of your album. **Note:** Your cover image must be a .png or .jpg file and can be up to 25mb.
7. Click **Save**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to edit an image in a gallery.]

### Deleting an album

You and any site visitor [with Manage Gallery permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-shared-gallery-app) can delete albums that are not needed anymore on your live site. You can also delete albums from the Shared Gallery section of your site's dashboard.

> **Important:**
>
> - Deleting an album permanently removes all the photos and videos in the album. It's not possible to recover an album after you delete it.
> - When you delete all of the albums that you created, your gallery reverts to showing individual photos and videos.

#### To delete an album:

DashboardLive Site
1. Go to [Shared Gallery in your site's dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp/476dc2dd-e16e-43b0-a0aa-1a486c78fbe2%3F&title=Shared%20Gallery%20Dashboard&buttonText=Shared+Gallery).
2. Click the More Actions icon .
3. Select **Delete album**.
[image: A screenshot showing the option to delete an album.]
1. Click **Delete**.

1. Go to the Shared Gallery page on your site.
2. Log in with the email address associated with your Wix account.
3. Hover over the album that you want to delete.
4. Click the More Actions icon .
5. Select **Delete album**.
6. Click **Delete**.

[image: A screenshot showing the option to delete an album.]
