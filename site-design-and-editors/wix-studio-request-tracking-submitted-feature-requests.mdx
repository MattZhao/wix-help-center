---
title: "Wix Studio Request: Tracking Submitted Feature Requests"
---

Currently, it is not possible to view the status of feature requests that you've submitted in Wix Studio, including whether they have been rejected or accepted.

> In the meantime, check out our [Wix Product Roadmap](https://support.wix.com/en/roadmap) page to stay updated on the latest developments. You can also head over to our [Wix Studio Discord community](https://discord.com/invite/wixstudio) to share your vision for the future of Wix Studio and engage with others.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
