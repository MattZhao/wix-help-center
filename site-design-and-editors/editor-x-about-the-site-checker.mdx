Editor X lets you check your site's health at any time, completely free of charge. Site Checker is a smart tool that automatically scans your site for issues related to your design, as well as accessibility, SEO and security.

### How can I access the Site Checker?

Once you enable Site Checker, its icon  appears at the top right of the Editor for easy access.
To enable and access Site checker:  
1. Click **Tools** at the top of Editor X.
2. Select **Site Checker** to enable it.
3. Click the Site Checker icon ﻿ at the top right to access the tool.

### How does it work?

The Site Checker scans the current page you're editing. Once the automatic scanning is done, you can see a list of all the issues that were detected.

You don't have to resolve each and every issue if it's not relevant – you can make our tool ignore the issue and remove it from the list.

Use this tool at any point in the site creation process, whether it's for new pages or pages that were previously scanned. We highly recommend scanning new pages that you design to make sure they're perfect before publishing.

### Issues detected by the Site Checker

The Site Checker scans pages for many different issues, related to both look and functionality. Click an issue below to learn what it means:

### Private Data

The Site Checker scans your [content collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-managing-your-collections) for permissions you should change. It flags collections that are set up to allow anyone to read their data, including the PII they contain (i.e., Personally Identifiable Information). 
To prevent sensitive data from being leaked or exposed, we recommend checking your collection permissions and updating them if necessary. 
**Click below to learn more about this topic:**
- [Storing and managing PII securely](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-storing-personally-identifiable-information-pii-data)
- [Understanding your Content Collection permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-collection-permissions-overview)

### Missing Header

The Site Checker checks your page to make sure it has a Header. A Header section can help create an easy navigation experience for your visitors that keeps them engaged. Typically, it features your menu and any other links you want people to click.

### No H1 Tag

The Site Checker checks your page to make sure it has one H1 tag. This [heading tag](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-pages-heading-tags) represents the page's title in the HTML code, so screen readers and other accessibility and SEO tools identify it properly.

### Multiple H1 Tags

The Site Checker checks your page to make sure it has one H1 tag. This [heading tag](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-pages-heading-tags) represents the page's title in the HTML code, so screen readers and other accessibility and SEO tools identify it properly.

### Image Quality

The Site Checker checks your page to make sure all images are high-quality. It flags any low-quality images as they might appear as blurry or pixelated on bigger screens. 
If possible, we recommend uploading images with a resolution of at least 3,000 px (i.e., the shortest side of the image is at least 3,000 pixels wide). Having said that, make sure they're less than 15MB.

### Missing Link

The Site Checker checks your page for buttons that aren't currently linked to any destination. This means visitors won't reach anywhere after clicking the button, which can significantly decrease their engagement.

### Hidden Elements

The Site Checker can detect elements that you've set to Hidden on all breakpoints. This means those elements aren't visible to your visitors on any screen size. 
If you don't need this element, we recommend deleting it to keep your workspace organized and improve the page's loading speed.

### Default Content

The Site Checker checks your page for any default content, originating from one of our templates. 
**This includes the following elements:**
- Map elements with our default address.
- Social Bar with the Editor X social links.
- Images from the template you used.
- Video players with the default video.
- Embed a site and custom elements with the default URL / code.

### Small Font

The Site Checker checks all text elements on your page to make sure they're big enough to read. If you're using a font that's too small, visitors might miss important information you want them to see.

### Adapt to Breakpoints

When designing a responsive site, it's key to check how your page looks on every breakpoint. The Site Checker scans your page on all breakpoints and looks for elements that should be adapted, whether it's making them smaller or positioning them differently.
**Tip:** In some cases, the Site Checker also flags sections that you haven't yet checked on smaller breakpoints. This means you only need to review the other breakpoints to resolve the issue.

### Overflowing Content

The Site Checker can detect elements that appear out of range on a certain breakpoint. When visitors access your site from a screen in that size, they can't see those elements properly.

### Can I ignore an issue detected by the Site Checker?

If this is not relevant to your design, remove it from the list so the Site Checker ignores it.
**To ignore an issue:**
1. Hover over the relevant element below **Missing Link**
2. Click the More Actions icon .
3. Click **Ignore**.
