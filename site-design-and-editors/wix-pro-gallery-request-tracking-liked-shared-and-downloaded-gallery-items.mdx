---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Tracking Liked, Shared and Downloaded Gallery Items"
---

Currently, it is not possible to track and analyze information on gallery items that have been liked, shared or downloaded by your site visitors. However, you can track the number of likes a gallery item receives by adding a [heart counter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-downloading-images-from-a-gallery#adding-a-heart-icon-to-gallery-items) to your gallery - it is just not possible to view who liked your item and when.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
