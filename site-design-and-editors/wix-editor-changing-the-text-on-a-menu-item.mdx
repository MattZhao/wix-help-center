---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Text on a Menu Item"
---

Your menu item text is taken directly from your site's page names. When you change a page name, you can see the menu item text change instantly.

If you have added a link to your menu, such as an anchor or external URL, you can also change the menu item text by the following the steps below.

### To change the text on a menu item:

Click **Menus &** **Pages **on the left side of the Editor. Hover over the relevant page/menu link.Click the **Show More** icon .Click **Rename **. Type the new page name.Click **Done**
