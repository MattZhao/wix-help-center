---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Automatically Displaying More Gallery Images on Scroll"
---

Currently, on some galleries your site visitors need to click "Show More" to view more images. There is no option for additional images to automatically be displayed when your site visitors scroll down.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
