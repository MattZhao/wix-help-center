---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Transferring a Purchased Wix Logo to Another Wix Account"
---

Currently, it is not possible to transfer a purchased logo to another Wix account.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - You can [download](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-downloading-your-logo-in-the-wix-logo-maker) the logo files and pass them on to the new site owner or any other person.
> - You can [attach the logo to a site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-connecting-and-disconnecting-a-logo) within your first Wix account, then [transfer the whole site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-premium-site-to-another-wix-account) to the second account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
