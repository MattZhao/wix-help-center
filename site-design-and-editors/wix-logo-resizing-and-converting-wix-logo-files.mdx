---
title: "Wix Logo: Resizing and Converting Wix Logo Files"
---

Currently, we offer Wix Logo files in PNG and SVG formats. You can resize SVG files without any quality loss, which makes them ideal for printing.

If you need to resize your files or convert them to another format, you can use free online apps for [resizing](https://www.iloveimg.com/resize-image) and [converting files](https://cloudconvert.com/). These tools are not Wix services, and as a result, we don't provide support for them.

If you'd like to see the option to download Wix logo files in additional formats in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-request-downloading-logo-files-in-additional-formats).
