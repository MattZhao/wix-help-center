### Creating your custom merchandise

Create custom branded merchandise directly from the Business Card Maker. Design mugs, business cards, tote bags, stickers and t-shirts.

#### To create custom merchandise:

Go to** **the [Business Card Maker](https://www.wix.com/business-card/maker).

**Note:** If you have a Wix website you can also access the Business Cards Manager from the [Dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/) under the **Business Cards & More** tab.  Click **Get Started.**  Choose your shipping country by clicking the flag on the top right. Then select your shipping destination.

  Hover over the template you want and click **Customize**.  Depending on the item you chose, follow the steps below:

### Business Cards

1. (Optional) Fill in your business details and click **Save & Continue**.
2. (Optional) Design your business card:

1. Click **Customize** at the bottom left.
2. Design your business card using the available options.

**Tip: **Click **Switch to Back **or **Switch to Front **to design the other side of the card.
3. When ready, click **Done** at the top right.
3. Select the relevant paper type.
4. Choose a quantity.
5. Click **Add to Cart**.
6. Click the Cart icon  at the top right and do one of the following:

- Continue to Checkout:

1. Click **Continue to Checkout**.
2. Enter your shipping details.
3. Click** Save & Continue**.
- Add More Items: 

1. Click **Add More Items**.
2. Design more products following the relevant instructions.
3. When ready to pay, click the Cart icon  at the top right and **Continue to Checkout**.

### T-Shirts

1. (Optional) Customize your T-shirt template:

1. Click **Customize** at the bottom left.
2. Design your T-shirt using the available options.
3. When ready, click **Done** at the top right.
2. Select a color for your T-shirt: Black or white.
3. Choose a size from the drop-down menu.

**Tip: **Click **Size Chart** to get the exact measurements of each size.
4. Select the quantity.
5. Click **Add to Cart**.
6. Click the Cart icon  at the top right and do one of the following:

- Continue to Checkout:

1. Click **Continue to Checkout**.
2. Enter your shipping details.
3. Click** Save & Continue**.
- Add More Items: 

1. Click **Add More Items**.
2. Design more products following the relevant instructions.
3. When ready to pay, click the Cart icon  at the top right and **Continue to Checkout**.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Orders are fulfilled by [Order a Print](https://www.orderaprint.com/). You can cancel an order within the first 24 hours. Damaged or defective orders need to be reported within seven days of the delivery date to Order a Print and **not **Wix. Order a Print will investigate and send a replacement at their discretion.
> - Currently, it is not possible to save merchandise designs (stickers, mugs etc.) you've worked on in the Print Shop, and edit them later. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-request-saving-merchandise-designs-in-the-print-shop).
>
> **Tip**: Business card designs can be created and saved separately using the [Logo and Brand Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-designing-business-cards-in-the-wix-logo-maker).
