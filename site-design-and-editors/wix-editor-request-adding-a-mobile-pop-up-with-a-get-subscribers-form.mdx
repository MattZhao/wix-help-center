---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Mobile Pop-up With a Get Subscribers Form"
---

Currently, the [mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-the-mobile-editor) doesn't offer a pop-up element with a Get Subscribers form, allowing visitors to join your mailing list.

As a workaround, you can create a [mailing subscription lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-lightbox-ideas-and-inspiration) on desktop and enable it for mobile as well. Once you do, you can edit the lightbox and optimize its layout following the steps [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-showing-and-customizing-lightboxes-on-your-mobile-site).

> **Note:**
>
> While you can enable lightboxes on mobile, Google defines these pop-ups as intrusive. Showing lightboxes on your mobile site might negatively impact your SEO.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
