---
title: "ADI Request: Adding Wix Hotels"
---

Currently you cannot add Wix Hotels inside ADI. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/hotel-booking-system/setting-up-wix-hotels) to learn more about Wix Hotels.

If you want to make a site for your hotel, you need to [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-wix-hotels).
