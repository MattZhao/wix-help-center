title: "Studio Editor: Inviting Collaborators to Edit a Site"
---

Invite teammates or other external collaborators to work with you on a site directly from the Studio Editor. Assign a role based on each person's area of expertise, or create roles with the permissions of your choice. The roles you give determine the permissions inside the editor.

### Tell me more about collaborators in Wix Studio

There are 2 types of collaborators you can have on a site:
- **Teammates: **These are added to the [Team tab in the workspace](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/team?referralInfo=sidebar) by the team owner. They are the teammates that you work with on your projects and they have access to your team workspace. Learn how to [manage your team.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-managing-your-team)
- **External site collaborators:** These are other professionals you work with that are not part of your team, such as freelance designers, or clients that you invite to their own site once it's ready. You give them access to one specific site, and they cannot access your workspace.

For both teammates and external collaborators, you can decide what roles and permissions they have on sites and in the workspace. You can also choose the mode they can access in the Studio Editor.

In addition, you can set page editing permissions to control which pages your teammates and collaborators can update. The editor is automatically adjusted to each person's role so unauthorized actions are either hidden or disabled.

### Inviting people to a site

Invite people to collaborate on the Studio Editor site you're currently working on. Using separate login credentials, site collaborators get access to the relevant features for their role (whether they're in the editor or the dashboard).

#### To invite people to your site team:

Click the **Wix Studio **icon  at the top left.  Hover over **Site**.** **  Hover over** Team Work**.  Click **Invite People to Collaborate**.  Enter the email address of the person you want to invite.  Select the checkbox next to the role(s) you want to assign to this person.

**Tip: **You can create [custom roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators) for people, where you handpick the permissions.   Click **Send Invite**.

> **Note:**
>
> - Wix Studio sites have 5 free collaborator seats as standard. To get more collaborator seats, you will need to [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/premium-purchase-plan/dynamo?siteGuid=5f479074-fab1-46c5-8cc6-fa94ebe90bc2).
> - If you already have more collaborators than your plan allows, they can remain as collaborators. However, you will not be able to invite new collaborators until you upgrade your site.
> - Keep track of how many collaborator seats you have filled with the progress bar on the Roles & Permissions page of your dashboard's Settings.

> **What's next?**
>
> The person you invited gets an email, from which they can accept and join the site.

### Resending an invite

You can always send someone another invite if they didn't receive the first email. Whether their current invite is valid or expired, send them another one directly from Wix Studio.

#### To resend an invite:

Click the **Wix Studio** icon  at the top left.  Hover over **Site**.  Hover over** Team Work**.  Click **Roles and Permissions**.

**Note: **This opens the relevant page on a new browser tab.  (In the new tab) Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant person.  Click **Resend Invite**.

### Creating and managing roles

You can manage your site's roles at any time. Create and edit custom roles, delete roles that you no longer need or duplicate predefined roles to adjust their permissions.

### What permissions are specific to the Studio Editor?

When creating a new role, you can add the following permissions that are specifically related to site editing: 
- **View Site: **Can view all pages and preview the site before publishing.
- **Add Comments:** Can add comments on elements and pages and tag other people.
- **Edit Site Structure: **Can add new pages, [global sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-global-sections), elements and [dynamic content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).
- **Edit Layout & Design: **Can use layout tools, edit [breakpoints](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-designing-across-breakpoints) and design assets.
- **Edit Content:** Can edit text, links and media sources (e.g., images, videos, etc).
- **Edit Code:** Can write code and manage [collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-managing-your-collections).
- **Manage Page SEO: **Can edit SEO and social share settings per page, and add alt text to media.
- **Edit Site Advanced:** Can delete [global sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-global-sections) and restore an older site version.

#### To create and manage roles:

[Go to Sites](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/sites?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Hover over the Collaborators icon on the relevant site.   Click **Manage** **Roles & Permissions**.  Choose what to do next:

### Create a new role

1. Click **Manage Roles**.
2. Click** + Create New Role** at the top right.
3. Fill in the details of your custom role:

1. Enter a title under **Role Title**.
2. (Optional) Add a description that outlines the new role.
4. Select the checkboxes next to the relevant actions for this role.

**Note:** While most Studio Editor permissions are available under **Studio Editor**, the ability to publish a site can be found under **Editor**.
5. When ready, click **Save** at the top right.

**Notes:**
- You can select specific permissions within the categories, or tick **Select All**.
- Certain actions that you select automatically enable other permissions. For example, if you let someone manage your orders, they can view your contacts by default.
- Use the **Filter **search bar to find actions to assign to your custom role.

### Edit an existing role

1. Click **Manage Roles**.
2. Hover over the relevant role and click **Edit**.
3. Edit the role as you wish:

- Rename the role or change its description.
- Add permissions by selecting more checkboxes.
- Remove permissions by de-selecting their checkbox.
4. When ready, click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> When hovering over their avatar at the top left, teammates can see their own role(s) in the system:[image: A screenshot of the tooltip that appears with your name and role when you hover over your avatar]

### Managing permissions per page

In addition to site and team roles, site owners can apply another type of permission that controls pages and global sections.

**Site and team roles** control the actions people can perform, while **permissions per page** control the pages and global sections they're allowed to work on.

#### To manage permissions per page:

Click the **Wix Studio **icon  at the top left.  Hover over **Site**.  Hover over **Team Work**.  Click **Permissions Per Page**.  Click the drop-down next to a person's name to edit their permissions.  Select the checkbox(es) next to the relevant access level:

- **All pages: **Can edit all site pages.
- **All pages and global sections: **Can edit all site pages as well as any [global sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-global-sections).

**Note:** To select this option, you must first select the **All pages** checkbox.
- **Specific pages: **Select the checkbox next to the pages this person should be able to edit.

### Performing an unauthorized action in the editor

Every user has a specific [role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators) in the system with permissions that the site owner can change at any time.

Areas that aren't included in your permissions appear in gray and are not clickable.

To access a feature that is disabled, contact the site owner and ask them to add the relevant permission(s) to your role.
