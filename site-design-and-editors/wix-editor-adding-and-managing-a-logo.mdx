### Creating and adding a logo in the Wix Editor

You can create a new logo from inside your site's editor. The Wix Logo Maker generates eye-catching logos for your business, which you can customize to build personalized, unique branding.

#### To create a new logo:

Open your site in the Wix Editor.  Click **Add Elements** ** **on the left menu.  Select the **Image** tab.  Select **Custom Logo**. **Note:** If you've already created a logo with the Wix Logo Maker, it will be displayed here.  Click **Add Logo**.  Select the logo.  Click **Manage Brand**.  Add the relevant information, and select a design from the options available. [Learn more about creating a logo with the Wix Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-logo-with-the-wix-logo-maker-571523)

> **Tip:**
>
> - If you have not purchased a logo, you can create one and utilize the 200x200 free sample file to display it on your site. If you need higher resolution logo files, you can purchase them by upgrading to any of the [Logo packages.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans)
> - If you've already purchased your logo with the Wix Logo Maker, it will be displayed when you add the logo to your site.

### Adding an existing Wix Logo to your site

If you've already created and purchased your logo with the Wix Logo Maker, you can add the element to quickly display it on your site.

#### To add an existing Wix Logo:

Open your site in the Wix Editor.  Click **Add Elements** ** **on the left menu.  Select the **Image** tab.  Select **Custom Logo**.  Click **Add Logo**.

### Uploading a site logo

You can upload logos you've made externally and add them to your site.

> **Note:**
>
> If you upload your own logo inside the editor, it is not automatically added to your site's Business Info. You need to [add it from your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-sites-business-information).

Wix EditorDashboard
1. Open your site in the Wix Editor.
2. Click **Add Elements** ** **on the left menu.
3. Select **Image**.
4. Select **Upload Images**.
5. Select where you want to add the media from with the available options.
6. Choose the image you want.
7. (Optional) Select the file and click **Add to Page **to add it to your site.

**Tip:** To create an image with a transparent background, upload a .png file.[image: The Media Manager in Wix. A logo has been selected from the site files.]
1. Go to [Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Edit%20Site%20Info&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Business Info**.
3. Click **Add Logo**.
4. Do one of the following:

- Click **Upload Media** to upload an image from your computer and click **Choose File**.
- Select an image from the image gallery and click **Choose File**.
5. Click **Save**.

### FAQs

Select from the following options to learn more about logos on your site.

### When are logos added to my site's Business Info?

The following logos are added to your site's Business Info:
- Logos you create with the Wix Logo Maker
- Logos you upload yourself to the Business Info section of your site's dashboard

Logos you upload to your site from the Wix Editor are not added to your Business Info. You need to [add it from your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-sites-business-information).

### Where are logos displayed on my site?

The logo you add to your Business Info automatically appears in various locations. For example, it's displayed in the [Wix Stores checkout page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-checkout-page) and Wix Stores invoices sent out to customers.
You can also add the logo to your site's pages from the Editor. We recommend placing your logo in the page header, so it's always visible to your visitors.

### Do I need to pay to create a logo with Wix?

No. You can create a logo and use it on your site for free. However, logo plans allow you to download your logo as high resolution files, and specific file formats for different sites and merchandise. [Learn more about logo plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans)

### What are the recommended ratios for logos I upload?

We recommend uploading a logo file with a ratio of 1:1 (square in shape). We also recommend uploading high quality images, so they maintain their resolution on your site. [Learn more about supported file types and sizes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes).
