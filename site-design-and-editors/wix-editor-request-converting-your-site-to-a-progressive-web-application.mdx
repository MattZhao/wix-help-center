---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Converting Your Site to a Progressive Web Application"
---

Currently it is not possible to convert your site to a Progressive Web Application.

However, you can create an app for your site with the Wix Branded App. The Branded App allows you to connect with your site and customers to expand your business and promote your brand. [Click here to learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
