---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding Hyperlinks to Collapsible Text Boxes"
---

Currently, it is not possible to hyperlink text in a collapsible text box. The only hyperlink that you can add is to [link button to the full text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-collapsible-text).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
