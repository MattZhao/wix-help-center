---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Bundle Pricing for Wix Photo Albums Store"
---

Currently, when you [set the price](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-setting-prices-in-the-wix-photo-albums-store) of your photos in your Photo Album store, all your photos are set at that price. This means that each individual photo in your store costs the same price. For example, if you have an album with 50 images in your store, and you set your pricing at 5 dollars, each individual image would cost 5 dollars to purchase.

It is not possible to set a different price (a bundle price) for just some of the images in a Photo Album - for example a special price for photos purchased in a bundle/bulk.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
