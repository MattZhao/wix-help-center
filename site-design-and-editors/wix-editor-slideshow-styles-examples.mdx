### Creating a Text Slideshow

Create stunning text presentations using the slideshow element!

#### To create a text slideshow:

[Add a slideshow](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-slideshow).[Add a text element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text).Click **Edit Text** and enter your text.Drag your text on to your slideshow.Repeat steps 2-4 for each slide in your slideshow.

> **Note:**
>
> Use the arrows to navigate between your slides while you edit.

---

### Creating a Gallery Using a Slideshow

Create a stunning personalized gallery using the slideshow element!

By using the slideshow element rather than a regular gallery, you can design it how you want and add your own customized text.

#### To create a gallery using the slideshow element:

[Add a slideshow.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-slideshow)Click **Change Slide Background** and add your image or video.(Optional) [Add a text element.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text)Click **Edit Text** and enter your text.Drag the text element onto your slideshow.Repeat steps 2-5 for each slide in your slideshow.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also add videos to your slideshow.
