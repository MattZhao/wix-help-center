---
title: "Wix Video: Adding a YouTube Playlist"
---

Add a YouTube playlist to your site to allow visitors to browse your videos. For example, if you are a personal trainer, you can display a series of fitness videos to motivate your audience.

To add your playlist, first copy your URL from YouTube. Then paste the URL into Wix Video.

> **Note:**
>
> YouTube videos set to private are not supported and may not display properly in Wix Video. Make sure the videos you want to add are set to "public".

---

### Step 1 | Find your playlist URL on YouTube

Go to YouTube and locate the playlist you want to add to your site. Copy the playlist URL. You can choose any public YouTube playlist, as long as the playlist has 50 videos or less.

#### To find your playlist URL on YouTube:

Go to [YouTube.com](https://www.youtube.com/) and access the relevant YouTube profile.   Click the **Playlists** tab.   Click **View full playlist** under the relevant playlist.

Copy the playlist URL at the top of the page.

**Note**: Make sure to copy the full playlist URL not an individual video URL.

---

### Step 2 | Create a new channel and paste the playlist URL

Once you've copied your playlist URL, create a new YouTube Feed. Then paste your playlist URL into Wix Video. Once you connect, visitors can then view the playlist on your site.

#### To create a channel and paste the playlist URL:

[Go to Channels](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/video-library/channels?&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+Channels) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **New Channel **drop-down** **on the top right.   Select **YouTube Feed**.

Paste your YouTube playlist URL into the field provided.   Click **Connect**.

Visitors to your site will now be able to view and browse your playlist.

> **Note:**
>
> It may take several hours for your embedded YouTube playlist on Wix to sync with the latest uploads on your YouTube channel.
