---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing Your Page Background Color"
---

The colors you choose for your site background play an important role in how visitors perceive your site. Be mindful of how colors interact with one another when designing your site - colors may harmonize or contrast with one another.

Choose from predetermined color themes or add your own custom colors. You can change the background color per site page, or apply your chosen color to all pages in one click.

### To choose a background color:

Go to the relevant page in your Editor.   Click **Site Design**  on the left side of the Editor.  Hover over **Page Background** and click **Customize**.  Click **Color**.  Select an option from your [site's theme colors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-color-theme), or add a custom color under** My colors**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Apply to Other Pages** at the bottom of the panel to add the background to other pages on your site.

> **Want to undo your page background color choice?**
>
> To remove a page background color, change its color to white.
