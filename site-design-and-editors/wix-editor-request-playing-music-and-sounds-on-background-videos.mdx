title: "Wix Editor Request: Playing Music and Sounds on Background Videos"
---

Currently, video backgrounds do not play music or sounds.

### In the meantime you can:

Add the video background:

- [Add a free Wix video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-video-to-your-page-background)
- [Add your own video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-your-own-video-page-background)

 [Add a music element ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-audio-to-your-site) to the page to play the music or sounds.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
