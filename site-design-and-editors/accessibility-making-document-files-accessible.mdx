---
title: "Accessibility: Making Document Files Accessible"
---

If you have documents on your site, we recommend improving their accessibility to ensure that all site visitors can view and use them.

> **You should know:**
>
> As PDFs and Word documents are not Wix products, you need to adjust them on their respective platforms / programs.

[Learn how to make PDF files accessible](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/using/create-verify-pdf-accessibility.html)  [Learn how to make your Word documents accessible](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/make-your-word-documents-accessible-to-people-with-disabilities-d9bf3683-87ac-47ea-b91a-78dcacb3c66d?ui=en-us&rs=en-us&ad=us)

> **Next step:**
>
> Go back to the [Accessibility Checklist](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-checklist-for-improving-your-sites-accessibility) to continue improving your site for accessibility.
