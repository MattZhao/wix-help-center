### Changing the section layout

Switch your section's look in one click. There are a range of sophisticated, professional layouts available so you can select a design that fits your page's look. Your content and elements are automatically carried over, so you don't need to re-add anything.

The panel even shows your original design, so you can compare while you browse and choose a layout that perfectly suits your content.

#### To select a new section design:

Click the section in your Editor.  Click the Explore section layouts icon .  Select your chosen layout under **Choose a new layout**.  Click the Close icon  to exit the panel.

---

### Editing section content

Update all of your section's content from one easy place. Replace elements such as text and images, and change your section's background to keep the design fresh.

#### To change the section content:

Click the section in your Editor.  Click the Quick edit icon .  Select the relevant element from the panel.  Click **Change** to update / replace the element.  (Optional) Click the Settings icon ** **to update the element's settings.  Click the Close icon  to exit the panel.

### FAQs

Select a question to learn more about what you can do with the Quick Edit panel in your Editor:

### Can I switch back to a previous layout in the panel?

Absolutely. While previewing options in the **Explore section layouts** tab, select the preview under **Current layout** to revert your section. 
**Note:** If you select a new layout and close the panel, it is not possible to switch back to your section's previous layout from the panel. However, you can [use the Undo and Redo tools](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-undo-and-redo) to revert the changes.

### Is there another way to access the panel?

You can also access the panel by selecting your section, then clicking **Quick Edit** .

### How do I update text in a section?

1. Open the **Content** tab in the **Quick Edit** panel.
2. Select the relevant text field, and enter your new text into it.
3. Click the Close icon  to exit the panel.

### Can I use the panel to customize the design of elements?

The Quick edit panel provides a way to change your section's layout and content on the go. As element types have different features and options, they're customized individually. You can change the design of section elements with the following steps:

1. Close the Quick edit panel.
2. Select the relevant element.
3. Click the Design icon   and make the changes you want.

### How do I customize the design of my section?

[Use tools such as scroll effects, shape dividers, and more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-sections) to make your section look how you want.
