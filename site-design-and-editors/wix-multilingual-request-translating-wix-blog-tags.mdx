---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Translating Wix Blog Tags"
---

Currently, it is not possible to translate blog tags. Once a tag is created, it is given the same language as the post that it was created on or was assigned to.

> **What content can be translated with Wix Multilingual?**
>
> See the list of [translatable content in Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translatable-content).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
