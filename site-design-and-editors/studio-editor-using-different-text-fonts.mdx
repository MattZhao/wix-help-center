### Using your own fonts

Upload your own fonts to Studio Editor so that you can use them in text elements and anywhere else you can choose a font (e.g. buttons, form and menu).

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure that you have the rights to use the fonts you upload.

#### To upload a font to the Editor:

Select the relevant text element in the Editor.   Click **Edit Text**.   Click the **Open Inspector** arrow  at the top right of the Editor.  Scroll down to **Design **and click the **Font **drop-down.   Click **Upload Fonts **at the bottom.   (In the pop-up) Click **Upload Fonts** and select your font file (TTF, OTF, WOFF2 or WOFF).  Click **Close **once the font has uploaded.

> **What's next?**
>
> Your font is now available to use across your site. You can find it in the **Font **drop-down, under **My Fonts**.

### Adding fonts for non-English alphabets

If your site is in a language with a non-English alphabet (e.g. Arabic, Cyrillic, Japanese), you can add compatible fonts with the characters you need.

#### To add language fonts:

Select the relevant text element in the Editor.   Click **Edit Text**.   Click the **Open Inspector** arrow  at the top right of the Editor.  Scroll down to **Design **and click the **Font **drop-down menu.   Click **Add Language** at the bottom.   Select the checkbox next to the relevant language(s).  Click **Save.**

> **What's next?**
>
> The language fonts you select will be available in every font menu in Studio Editor. The **Font** drop-down is organized by language so you can choose one that's compatible.

> **Are there more languages you'd like to see?**
>
> Let us know by contacting Customer Care.

### Using accented characters in your text

You can use accented characters in your text in the Studio Editor. The way to do that depends on your device:

**Using a PC: **Copy and paste the characters into the text box from [this link](http://symbols.typeit.org/), or use [the keyboard combinations listed here](https://sites.psu.edu/symbolcodes/windows/codealt/).  **Using a Mac: **While entering the text, hold down the relevant letter on your keyboard and select the accent. Learn more about [typing special characters in Mac](http://www.macworld.com/article/2036310/type-special-characters-in-os-x.html).

### Deleting uploaded fonts

Keep your uploaded fonts organized by deleting the ones you no longer need.

#### To delete an uploaded font:

Select the relevant text element in the Editor.   Click **Edit Text**.   Click the **Open Inspector** arrow  at the top right of the Editor.  Scroll down to **Design **and click the **Font **drop-down menu.   Click **Upload Fonts**.  Hover over the relevant font and click the **Delete** icon .  Click **Delete **to confirm.

> **Note:**
>
> The font file is deleted from your account. If it is being used in text elements, it will still appear.

### FAQs

Click below to learn more about using your own fonts in the Studio Editor:

### What font formats are supported?

You can upload fonts in TTF, OTF, WOFF2 or WOFF formats. The font does not have to be a web font as we convert it to a web font anyway.

### I'm experiencing issues uploading a font to the Editor

When uploading font files, sometimes the upload is unsuccessful. To resolve the issue, convert the original file to another file type and re-upload the file. 
You can find an online converter [here](http://www.fontconverter.org/), and try converting the file to either TTF, OTF, WOFF2 or WOFF. 
**Note:** We do not provide support for issues caused by uploaded fonts.

### Are there areas that don't support using my uploaded fonts?

You cannot use your uploaded fonts in some apps.

> **Important:**
>
> Wix has licenses for the fonts in the Editor. They are available for digital use ONLY and within your Wix site. For more information, view the [Wix Terms of Use](http://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use). You cannot download the fonts to use, but you may find the open source versions and install them to your computer. Be careful with fonts found in external URLs. These are not owned by Wix so you need to contact the author for permission to use them.
