---
title: "Wix Editor: Elements Hanging Over the Edge of Your Slideshow"
---

You may see that some of your slideshow content is hanging over the edge of the slideshow frame.

You can choose to cut the content that is over the edge, or leave it like that.

## To change the Content Outside Frame setting:

Click the slideshow in the Editor.Click the **Settings **icon ﻿﻿.Click the **Hide content outside frame **toggle:
- **Enabled:** Elements that are over the edge of the slide are cut off so that they appear inside the slide only.
- **Disabled:** Elements that are over the edge of the slide are not cut off.
