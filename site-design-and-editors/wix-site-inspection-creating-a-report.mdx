---
title: "Wix Site Inspection: Creating a Report"
---

With Wix Site Inspection, you can scan your site's pages for issues that could affect how they rank in Google search results. [Learn more about Site Inspection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-site-inspection-an-overview)

You can create Site Inspection reports to see information about the discoverability and indexability of your pages so you can identify the most important issues to fix.

> **Important:**
>
> - To use Wix Site Inspection, you first need to [connect your site to a domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-you-own-to-your-wix-site), publish your site, and [verify your site with Google Search Console](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-google-search-console-5033765).
> - Google also has a scan limit of 2000 pages per day. If you go over this 2000-page limit, you need to wait 24 hours before you can scan your site again. [Learn more about Site Inspection limitations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-site-inspection-an-overview#limitations-of-the-wix-site-inspection-tool)

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Creating your first Site Inspection report](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-site-inspection-creating-a-report#creating-your-first-site-inspection-report)  [Re-inspecting your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-site-inspection-creating-a-report#re-inspecting-your-site-after-your-first-report)

### Creating your first Site Inspection report

You can create a Site Inspection report in your site's SEO Dashboard.

#### To create a report:

[Go to your SEO Dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-f3388913-140f-47a9-b2ba-4bf86b60e04e&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO).  Select **Go to Site Inspection **under **Tools and settings**.  Click **Inspect Site**.  Click on a page result under **Full Report** to open a panel with more information about the page.  Click **Close** to close the information panel when you're finished with it.

> **Want to learn more about your report?**
>
> Read our article about [understanding your Site Inspection reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-site-inspection-understanding-your-site-inspection-report).

### Re-inspecting your site after your first report

After you've created your first report, you can re-inspect your site to view the latest information that Google has available for it.

#### To re-inspect your site:

[Go to your SEO Dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-f3388913-140f-47a9-b2ba-4bf86b60e04e&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO).  Select **Go to Site Inspection **under **Tools and settings**.  Click **Inspect Again**.  Click on a page result under **Full Report** to open a panel with more information about the page.  Click **Close** to close the information panel when you're finished with it.

> **Want to learn more about your report?**
>
> Read our article about [understanding your Site Inspection reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-site-inspection-understanding-your-site-inspection-report).
