### Editing an image with Adobe Express

Edit your images directly from your editor.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.
4. Click **Site Files **on the left.
5. Click the image you want to edit.
6. Click **Design with Adobe Express** on the right.
7. Sign in to Adobe Express or create an account.
8. Edit your image using the options available.

**Tip:** [Learn more about using Adobe Express](https://helpx.adobe.com/ie/express/user-guide.html)
9. Click **Save **at the top right.
10. Select your file type from the **File Format** drop-down: **JPG **or **PNG**.
11. Click **Save**.
12. Select the edited image in the **Site Files **window and click **Add to Page **to add it to your site.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about editing images with Adobe Express:

### Is it free to use Adobe Express?

Adobe Express is available for free, however, you can upgrade to a paid Premium plan to unlock all content and premium features. For more information about Adobe Express' plans and pricing, [visit their pricing page](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing).

### When I save my image in Adobe Express, is it saved to both Wix and Adobe?

Yes. Saving your image in Adobe Express saves your image to your Media Manager as well as to the Wix folder in the Your Stuff tab of Adobe Express.

### In what formats can I save images edited in Adobe Express?

You can save images edited in Adobe Express in a JPG or PNG file format.
