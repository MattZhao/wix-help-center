title: "Wix Studio: Assigning a Wix Premium Plan to a Wix Studio Site"
---

You can assign your existing Premium plan to a Wix Studio site by upgrading your Premium plan to a [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites). The upgrade is immediate, and you'll receive a credit from your old plan where applicable.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> You must be the site owner of both sites, or have the relevant billing permissions to reassign a Premium plan. Learn more about [roles and permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview).

### Assigning and upgrading your Premium plan

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions **icon  next to the plan you want to reassign.  Select **Assign to a different site**.  Select the checkbox next to the relevant Studio site.

**Note:** The site must be in the same workspace as the plan you've chosen to reassign.

Click **Next**.  Click **Compare Plans**.

**Note:** To upgrade the site, you'll need to select a plan that offers more features than the current plan.  Click **Select** under your new Studio plan.  Choose your subscription period and click **Continue to Checkout**.  Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

---

### Credit from your current plan

Any remaining credit from your old Premium plan is deducted from the price of your new upgrade during the purchase process. The credit is deducted after any discounts from a coupon or seasonal sale are applied.

> **Note:**
>
> The credit is only applied if you purchased the original plan in the same Wix account as your Studio site. You won't receive a credit if the plan was transferred to you from another Wix account.

### See how credit calculation works

Credit is worked out by the full price of your current plan, divided by the number of days in the subscription period, multiplied by the number of days left in your subscription period. For example: 
- For a yearly plan at the cost of $276 with 10 days left, the calculation will be: 

$276 ÷ 365 days = $0.76/day
- This figure is then multiplied by the remaining days in the subscription period:

$0.76 x 10 = $7.60 credit remaining

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn about reassigning a Wix Premium plan to a Wix Studio site.

### Can I move from a more advanced plan to a simpler plan type?

No. When reassigning and upgrading to Studio plans, you must select a plan with a comparable or more advanced feature suite.

### Can I move from a yearly Premium plan to a monthly Studio plan?

No. When reassigning and upgrading to Studio plans, subscriptions must be for the same duration, or a lengthier duration.
