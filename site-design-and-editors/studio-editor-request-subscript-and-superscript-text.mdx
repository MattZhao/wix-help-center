---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Subscript and Superscript Text"
---

Currently, subscript and superscript text are not supported in the Studio Editor.

As an alternative, you can use separate text boxes to achieve a subscript or superscript effect.

#### To create a subscript / superscript text effect:

Add a separate text element for your superscript / subscript text, and enter the text.  [Resize the text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-text#designing-and-formatting-text) as needed and [adjust the position](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-working-with-docking-margins-and-padding).  [Stack the text elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-stacking-elements) to ensure they keep their spacing across breakpoints.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
