---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Allowing Filtering and Sorting of Repeater Lists"
---

Currently it is not possible to allow visitors to filter and sort repeaters on your site.

> **Workaround:**
>
> If you [connect your repeater to the CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-dynamic-content-in-a-repeater), you can allow visitors to use input elements such as dropdowns and checkboxes to [filter the content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-setting-up-filters-for-site-visitors-using-input-elements).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
