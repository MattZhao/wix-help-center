---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding and Setting Up a YouTube Subscribe Button"
---

Adding a Youtube subscribe button gives your visitors an easy way to follow your channel without ever having to leave your site. Once visitors click the button, they can log in to their YouTube account and confirm the subscription.

---

### Step 1 | Retrieve Your YouTube Channel URL

YouTube subscribe buttons link up with your YouTube channel to give your visitors a one-step subscription process. In order to connect your YouTube channel to your Wix site, you need to retrieve the channel URL.

### Show me how

1. Sign in to your [YouTube](http://www.youtube.com/) account.
2. Click your profile picture at the top right.
3. Click **Your channel**.
4. Copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

> **Tip:**
>
> Want to connect a channel that's not yours? Simply visit the relevant channel page and copy the URL.

---

### Step 2 | Add a YouTube Subscribe Button

Now that you've retrieved your YouTube URL, you can add the subscribe button to your site. To connect the button to your channel, simply paste the channel's URL in the button's settings.

### Show me how

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Social**.
4. Click **YouTube**.
5. Click or drag the **YouTube Subscribe** button to add it to your site.
6. Click **Set Your Channel **or the **Settings ** icon.
7. Paste your YouTube channel's URL under **What's your channel's address?**
8. Press the Enter key on your keyboard.

---

### Step 3 | Customize Your Youtube Subscribe Button

You can customize the way the button appears by clicking the **Layouts**  icon. Choose whether or not to display your channel name and profile picture.

> **Want more customization options?**
>
> Click the **Animation** icon  to [add an animation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-an-animation) to your subscribe button.
