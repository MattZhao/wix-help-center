---
title: "ADI Request: Adding a Survey to Your Site"
---

Currently, Wix ADI does not have a poll or survey feature, but you can add Wix Forms from the Editor.

If you want to add a survey app, [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-form-on-your-site).
