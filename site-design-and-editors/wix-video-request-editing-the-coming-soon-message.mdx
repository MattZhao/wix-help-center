---
title: "Wix Video Request: Editing the 'Coming Soon' Message"
---

Currently, it is not possible to edit the "This channel is coming soon!" message that your site visitors see when you have not yet added any videos to that particular channel.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
