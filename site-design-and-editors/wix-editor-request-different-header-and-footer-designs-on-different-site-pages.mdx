---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Different Header and Footer Designs on Different Site Pages"
---

Currently, it is not possible to have different header or footer designs on different pages of your Wix site. The same header and footer designs are applied to all site pages. However, you can hide them on certain pages. [Click here to learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-the-header-and-footer-on-a-specific-page)

> **Tip:**
>
> You can display different headers and footers across different pages on your site with Editor X. Editor X is a responsive site builder, meaning you can customize and tailor a site for your exact needs. [Click here to learn how to display headers and footers with Editor X ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-global-sections)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
