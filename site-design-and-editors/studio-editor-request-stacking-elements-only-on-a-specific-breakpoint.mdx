---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Stacking Elements Only on a Specific Breakpoint"
---

When you [stack elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-stacking-elements) in the Studio Editor, it automatically applies to all breakpoints. Currently, it is not possible to place elements in a stack only on a specific breakpoint (e.g. stacking elements on mobile but not on desktop).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
