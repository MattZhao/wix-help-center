Background scroll effects are a fun, creative way to catch your visitor's attention and encourage them to further explore your site.

**In this article, learn more about: **

[Adding a scroll effect to your strip](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-applying-background-scrolling-effects-to-your-strip#adding-a-scroll-effect)  [Available scroll effects](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-applying-background-scrolling-effects-to-your-strip#available-scroll-effects)

> **Important:**
>
> - You can only see scroll effects in [preview](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site#previewing-your-site) or on your live site. The way it is displayed inside the editor is not how it looks on the live site.
> - It is not possible to add a scroll effect to your strip if you have added columns to it.
> - Scroll effects are not displayed on the mobile version of your site.

---

### Adding a scroll effect

Add an effect to begin creating a dynamic experience for your site visitors. You can add different effects to different strips, so play around and build exciting, unique site pages.

#### To add a scroll effect:

Click the strip in the editor.  Click the **Background Scroll Effects** icon .  Select a scroll effect.   (Optional) [Preview your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site#previewing-your-site) to see the scroll effects in action.

---

### Available scroll effects

Choose from Fade in or Fade out effect to boost visitor engagement.

Click an option below to learn more about each effect.

> **Note:**
>
> Available effects vary based on the strip background that you select (e.g., image, video, or color).

### Fade In

As visitors scroll down the page, your background fades in from white. When they scroll back up, the image, video, or color fades back out.

### Fade Out

As visitors scroll down the page, your background fades out to white. When they scroll back up, the image, video, or color fades back in.

> **Note:**
>
> To create scroll effects, the strip image / video size is increased to fill the page. To avoid distortion, use large, high-quality images and videos.
