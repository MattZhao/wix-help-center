---
title: "Wix Media Request: Right Click Protect App Supporting Ctrl+C Copying"
---

Currently, the Right Click Protect app does not protect against site visitors selecting content on your site and using the shortcut Ctrl+C to copy it.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
