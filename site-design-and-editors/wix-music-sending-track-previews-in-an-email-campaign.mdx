---
title: "Wix Music: Sending Track Previews in an Email Campaign"
---

Have some new material? With Wix Email Marketing, it's easy to give your fans a taste by sending them a 30 second preview of your music.

> **Note.**
>
> Fans are redirected to your site (from the email they receive) to listen to the preview.

### To send track previews in an Email Campaign:

Go to your [Music Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20My%20Music&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/music/).  Select the song you want to promote.  Click **Edit Track Info **.  Scroll down and select **Fans can listen to a 30 sec preview** from the **Track Streaming Options **drop-down menu.  Click **Save** on the top right.  Go to your [Email Marketing Dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Email%20Marketing&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fshoutout%2F).  Hover over the **Promote Your Music **template.
