title: "Studio Editor Request: Vertical Drag Handles"
---

Vertical drag handles allow you to move elements up and down on the canvas without disrupting the page layout. The GIF below shows an example of this in the Wix Editor.

Currently, vertical drag handles are not available in the Studio Editor.

As an alternative, you can position multiple elements [using drag and drop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-positioning-elements#positioning-elements-on-the-canvas). Click and drag your cursor over the relevant elements, then drag them to your chosen position on the canvas.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
