---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding a Multi-Step Form"
---

We are pleased to announce that you can now create multi-step forms in the Studio Editor using the new Wix Forms.

With the new Wix Forms, you can add multiple 'pages' to any form. Pages help better organize your form's information and guide visitors through a multi-step process to fill out the form. [Learn how to add a new Wix Form to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-form).
