title: "Editor X: Adding, Managing and Replying to Site Comments"
---

Working as a team on an Editor X site? Use the Comments panel to manage the design process. Every page has a separate comments board so you can leave specific feedback for others and ask them questions.

### Show me how to access the Comments panel

Click the **Comments** icon  at the top right of Editor X. This opens the panel, showing all comments related to the page you're currently viewing.

### Adding a New Comment

Add a comment for your teammates to see, formatting the text as needed and attaching files or images. You can either leave a general comment or mention someone (**@**) by tagging them.

If your feedback is related to a specific element on a page, select it and then write your comment. When a teammate clicks that comment, they get re-directed to see the element in question.

#### To add a new comment:

Open the relevant page in your Editor.  Select a [breakpoint](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-managing-breakpoints) at the top (e.g., desktop, tablet, mobile).  Depending on the comment you want to add, follow the steps:

- **Related to the page: **Click the **Comments** icon  at the top right.
- **Related to an element: **

1. Select the relevant element.
2. Click the **Comments** icon  right above the element.
    Enter your comment in the text box at the bottom. Use the tools right below the text box to make it more detailed:

- Make your text bold  or underlined .
- Add a bulleted list .
- Add an image or a file .
    When ready, click the **Submit** icon .

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also press the Enter key on your keyboard to submit a new comment.

---

### Managing a Comment

After writing a comment, you can edit it to change the content or remove it if it's no longer needed. When removing a comment, it's deleted permanently and cannot be restored.

#### To manage your comment:

Open the relevant page in your Editor.  Click the **Comments** icon  at the top right.   Hover over the relevant comment and click the More Actions icon .   Do one of the following:

### Edit a comment

1. Select **Edit**.
2. Edit your comment in the text box.
3. Click the **Submit** icon  to submit your edits.

### Delete a comment

Select **Delete **to remove the comment permanently.

---

### Replying to a Comment

#### To reply to a comment:

Open the relevant page in your Editor.  Click the **Comments** icon  at the top right.   Click **Reply** under the comment you want to respond to.   Enter your reply in the text box at the bottom.  Click the **Send** icon  to submit your reply.

> **What's next?**
>
> You can mark the comment as resolved if you've finished handling it. Hover over the comment and click the **Resolved **icon .

---

### Resolving and Re-Opening a Comment

When you're finished handling a specific comment, mark it as done by resolving it. The comment then moves to the Resolved queue where you can access it at any time.

You can always re-open a resolved task if there's more to be done. The comment then moves back to the Open queue so you can continue working on it.

#### To resolve or re-open a comment:

Open the relevant page in your Editor.  Click the **Comments** icon  at the top right.   Depending on whether you want to resolve or re-open a comment, follow the steps:

### Resolve a comment

1. Open the relevant page in your Editor.
2. Click the **Comments** icon  at the top right.
3. Hover over the comment you want to resolve.
4. Click the **Resolve** icon .

### Re-open a resolved comment

1. Open the relevant page in your Editor.
2. Click the **Comments** icon  at the top right.
3. Click the drop-down menu at the top right.
4. Select **Resolved** to access the queue.
5. Hover over the relevant comment and click the More Actions icon .
6. Select **Re-open**.
