---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Webcam Feed to Your Site"
---

Currently, webcam features are not supported. However, if you have access to the external code of your webcam feed, you can embed it onto your site using [HTML](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
