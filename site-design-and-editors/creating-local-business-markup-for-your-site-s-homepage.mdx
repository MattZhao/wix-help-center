---
title: "Creating Local Business Markup for Your Site's Homepage"
---

Structured data markup is a standardized format for giving search engines detailed information about your page. Adding markup to your site's pages can help improve your website's search results and ranking.

When you [add a business name and location](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-sites-business-information) to your account, Wix automatically creates Local Business markup for your site's homepage and adds it for you. Local Business markup lets you give search engines information about your business. Search engines can then use this information to [create rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/search-gallery).

> **Important:**
>
> Adding structured data markup to your site's pages does not guarantee that search engines will create rich results for them.

### Understanding Local Business markup settings

The Local Business markup that Wix adds to your homepage is based on [Schema.org](https://schema.org/DiscussionForumPosting) and Google's recommendations. It includes the following settings:

**Name:** Your business's name as it appears in the Profile section of your site's Business Info  **URL: **Your site's URL  **Image:** The logo you added in your site's Business Info  **Address**: Your address as it appears in the Location & Contact Info section of your site's Business Info

---

### Creating Local Business markup for your homepage

You can create Local Business markup for your site's homepage by adding a business name and location to [your site's Business Info](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-sites-business-information). You can also optionally add a logo to include its URL in the markup.

> **Important:**
>
> Wix creates Local Business markup for your primary location only. If you have multiple business locations, we don't create markups for each additional location.

#### To create Local Business markup:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Business info**.  Enter your business name under **Name **in the **Profile **section.   (Optional) Click **Add Logo** to upload a logo image.  Add your location under **Address** in the **Location and contact info** section.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can update your Local Business markup at any time by editing your business name or location.

### Excluding Local Business markup from your homepage

You can choose to exclude the Local Business markup from your homepage if you decide you don't want search engines to access it. Excluding the markup means that search engines can't use this information to [create rich results](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/structured-data/search-gallery).

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to your homepage.
4. Click **SEO basics**.
5. Click the **Advanced SEO **tab.
6. Click **Structured data markup**.
7. Click the** More Actions** icon  next to **Local business**.
8. Select **Exclude from page**.
[image: A screenshot of the More Actions menu open on the Advanced SEO tab, with the Exclude from page option circled.]
