---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Clickable Dropdown/Subpages Menu Items"
---

When you hover over a menu button, the [header menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-and-managing-a-dropdown-submenu) and any [subpages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-subpage-drop-down-menu) you have open automatically.

Currently, it is not possible to change this behavior so that the subpages menu only opens when clicked.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
