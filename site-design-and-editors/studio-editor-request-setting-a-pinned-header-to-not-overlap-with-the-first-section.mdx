---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting a Pinned Header to Not Overlap With the First Section"
---

By default, when you [set the header's position type](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-header-scroll-effects#working-with-advanced-header-settings) to Pinned, it overlaps the next section (i.e. the first section of the page). Currently, it is not possible to set the pinned header to not overlap the next section.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can use the [Freeze** **scroll effect](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-header-scroll-effects#adding-and-setting-up-a-header-scroll-effect) instead, as it pins the header while letting you choose whether or not it overlaps. To do this, you also need to disable the [Advanced settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-header-scroll-effects#working-with-advanced-header-settings)** **toggle in the Inspector.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
