### Unlock exclusive benefits:

[Gain access to exclusive benefits](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-about-the-partner-program-levels-and-benefits) as a Wix Partner. Earn points for building Premium sites and completing certifications to unlock more benefits as you progress to higher levels.

#### Priority support

Get your questions answered promptly and tailored to your business needs. All Wix Partners get priority support when requesting a callback or submitting a ticket. Learn more about [priority support for Wix Partners](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-priority-support-for-partners).

#### Revenue share

As a Wix Partner, you can earn revenue share through the sale of various Wix plans and from transactions processed through [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) on your clients' eligible sites. Learn more about [earning revenue share in the Wix Partner Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-receiving-revenue-share).

#### Sell templates

Looking to showcase your creativity and earn from your skills? Wix Studio now offers you the opportunity to feature your Studio templates in the Marketplace, reaching a vast audience of creators. The best part? You'll keep 100% of the profits you earn in 2024.

#### Join the Wix Marketplace

Grow your business and expand your client base by applying to join the Wix Marketplace. Once you reach Icon level, you'll be eligible to apply and showcase your work to get matched with potential clients.

### FAQs

Click below to view answers to the most frequently asked Partner Program questions.

### Who is the Partner Program for?

Agencies and freelancers who build websites for their clients.

### How do I become a member of the Wix Partner Program?

Click **Become a Partner** on the top left of your workspace to join the Wix Partner Program for free. Enter the details about your agency. Make sure to include your business name and number of teammates, the services you provide, your business website and a sample site URL you've built for a client recently. We'll review your request to join the program and get back to you within the next business day.

[image: A screenshot of the Become a Partner dialog to enter details about your agency.]**Notes:** Only the workspace owner can apply to join the Wix Partner Program.** **The points earned and benefits unlocked in the Wix Partner Program are specific to each workspace.

### How can I mention the Wix Partner Program online?

For all those who are registered members through the Wix Partner Program, you can advertise or externally market that you are part of the “Wix Partner Program” on your website, marketing materials, social media profiles, and in conversations with prospects. Depending on which level you achieve through the Wix Partner Program, you will have the opportunity to advertise using certain badges. You can use the official Wix Partner Program badge on your website, marketing materials or social channels.

### Can I advertise my agency as a Wix Partner?

You may not refer to the Wix Partner Program as an exclusive partner, or that Wix is in an exclusive partnership with your business in a press release. You may advertise or externally market that you are part of the “Wix Partner Program” on your website, marketing materials, social media profiles, and in conversations with prospects. Additionally, you may advertise your current Wix Partner badge on your website, marketing materials or social channels.

### Can I advertise my freelance services as a Wix Partner?

You may not refer to the Wix Partner Program as an exclusive partner or service. To ensure that freelancers and agencies use the Wix Partner Program positioning consistently across business and marketing channels, we've created [Wix Partner Program Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/partner-program-terms-of-use) you can review.

### Can I mention Wix Partner in my company press releases?

You may not refer or mention Wix Partner in a dedicated press release positioning Wix Partner as an exclusive partner or exclusive service. You may only refer to your business partakes of the Wix Partner Program.
