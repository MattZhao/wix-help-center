---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Viewing the Position of All Elements on a Page"
---

Currently, it is not possible to view the positions of every element on a page in the Studio Editor. You can [view and manage the position](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-positioning-elements) of single elements and their parent containers by manually selecting them on the canvas.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
