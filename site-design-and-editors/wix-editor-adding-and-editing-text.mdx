### Adding text to your site

Add a text element to your page to begin building your site content. There are a variety of title and paragraph designs available, as well as options that match your site's current theme.

#### To add text:

Open the relevant page in your editor.  Click **Add Elements** .  Click **Text**.  Select a text type: 

- **Themed Text: **These text elements have been handpicked for you as they match your template's look and feel.
- **Titles: **These should be used for headings on your site to let your site visitors know what the pages or sections are about.
- **Paragraphs:** These should be used for most of the text on your site, and should contain most of your site's content.
    Click and drag your chosen text element to add it to your page.

> **Tip:**
>
> Your editor uses your browser's spell check whenever you add text to your site. If you can't see spell check in your editor, make sure it is enabled in your browser.

### Editing your text

Once you've chosen and added text to your page, edit its content. We recommend entering information that is relevant to the page or section to ensure an engaging experience for your visitors.

#### To edit your text:

Click the text element in your editor.  Click **Edit Text.**  Type or paste your text in the text box.  Press **Enter** on your keyboard.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Do text boxes in the Editor have a character limit?

The character limit per text box is 60,000 characters (roughly 10,000 words) including spaces. If you run over the limit, you can: 
- Add another text box by following the steps in this article.
or
- [Add a collapsible text box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-collapsible-text), as they can hold up to 120,000 characters.

### Can I paste text into a text box?

Absolutely. There are just a few points to keep in mind:
- We recommend [following our instructions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-copying-text-from-an-external-source-eg-word-document) to ensure your text pastes into the box as expected.
- If you paste a block of text into the box that goes over the character limit, you may find that some of the text is removed. To resolve this, you can paste the remaining text into a new text box.

### How do I edit text in menus on my site?

You can edit menu text by selecting the menu itself, and opening its design options. [Learn more about menus](https://support.wix.com/en/the-wix-editor/menu)

### Is it possible to place text in front of an image?

Yes. To place text in front of an image, drag the relevant text box and place it over the image. 
If you are unable to see the text box in front of the image, it means that it is located on a layer behind the image. 
1. Right-click the image.
2. Hover over **Arrange**.
3. Click **Move Backward** until you are able to see the text box.
