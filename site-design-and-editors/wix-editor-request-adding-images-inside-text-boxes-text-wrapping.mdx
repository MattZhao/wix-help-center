---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding Images Inside Text Boxes (Text Wrapping)"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add images or videos inside text boxes, and make the text wrap around the image / video.

However, there are a number of alternative features available that you can use to display text with images on your site.

> **Important:**
>
> We do not recommend adding images/videos on top of text boxes, as this can cause alignment / layout issues on your pages in different browsers.

#### To display images with text:

[Use the repeater element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-managing-repeaters-lists-grids) to create a layout with text and images that you want to use several times on your site.  [Add the Wix Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-changing-the-layout-settings) to combine text and images with a variety of displays.   Split your text into several text boxes, and manually arrange them around the image or video.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
