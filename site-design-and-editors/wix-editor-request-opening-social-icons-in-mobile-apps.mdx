---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Opening Social Icons in Mobile Apps"
---

Currently, not all of the social icons that you can add to your site open in their corresponding mobile app.

On an iPhone these include Facebook and LinkedIn. On an Android phone, these include Facebook, Pinterest, MySpace, Blogger and Instagram.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
