---
title: "Section with Sticky Position Overlaps Global Section in Safari (Includes Workaround)"
---

We are aware of an issue with global sections being overlapped by another section with sticky position type, which is caused by a matter on Safari browser’s side.

> **As a workaround, please proceed with the following steps:**
>
> 1. Click the section with Sticky position.
> 2. Change its position type to **Default**.
