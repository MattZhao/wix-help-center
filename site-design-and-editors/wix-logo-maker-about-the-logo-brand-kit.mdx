title: "Wix Logo Maker: About the Logo Brand Kit"
---

When you purchase either a Social, Brand Plus, Unlimited, or Business [Logo plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans), you automatically get a Brand Kit included to expand your logo abilities. This exclusive kit allows you to download files that are not available in the Basic, Value, and Advanced plans.

### How do I access the Brand Kit?

There are 2 ways to access the Brand Kit, depending on the Logo Maker you're using.
**Logo Maker in your dashboard:** [Go to the Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-accessing-the-wix-logo-maker-2576691#accessing-the-logo-maker-in-your-dashboard) in your site's dashboard, purchase the [Brand Plus plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-accessing-the-wix-logo-maker-2576691#accessing-the-logo-maker-in-your-dashboard) and access the Brand Kit from there.
**Standalone Logo Maker:**
- Purchase one of the plans from the [standalone Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-accessing-the-wix-logo-maker-2576691#accessing-the-stand-alone-logo-maker) and access the Brand Kit from there.
- If you purchase the Logo + Online Presence plan (includes the Brand Kit), make sure to [connect your purchased logo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-connecting-and-disconnecting-a-logo) to an [upgraded site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-your-site-to-premium-3066683) in order to access the Brand Kit.

---

### Post-download edits

The Business and Unlimited plans allow edits post-download, except from editing the Business Name.

However, the Brand Plus (when purchased separately or as a part of Social plan) goes a step further and allows you to make any changes post-download. This means there are no limitations on your edits.

---

### Files included in the Brand Kit

The Brand Kit (Social, Brand Plus, Unlimited, Business plans) includes all the files from the Advanced package in addition to secondary logo files, business card files, social post files, and social media cover files. You can find more information about each of these file types below.

#### Secondary logos

Secondary logos are variations of your main logo. You can create as many secondary logos as you like, with different business names.

You receive one file of a set size (500 x 500 px PNG) when downloading.

#### Business card files

Add your logo to a business card. You receive 2 files - one of the front of your business card design and one of the back.

> **Note:**
>
> The Brand Kit allows you to download business cards as an image but printing them requires a payment. However, Unlimited and Business Logo plans [include 250 free business cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-redeeming-free-business-cards-in-the-wix-logo-maker).

#### Social posts files

Add your logo to your social posts. You receive one file of a set size when downloading.

#### Social media cover files

Add your logo to your social media profile covers. You receive one file of a set size for a social network when downloading.
