---
title: "Wix Video Request: Transferring Your Wix Video Content to Another Wix Account"
---

Currently, when you transfer a site with Wix Video to another Wix account, the content of Wix Video (video uploads and data) is not transferred to the new account. The videos are still displayed on the  site in the new account, but the Wix Video Library is empty. To make changes to Wix Video in the new account, the videos have to be re-uploaded.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
