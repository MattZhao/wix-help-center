---
title: "Accessibility Request: Adding an Accessibility Toolbar to Your Site"
---

Currently, we do not have a built-in accessibility toolbar that your visitors can use to adjust or enhance certain parts of your site.

As an alternative, you can add an accessibility toolbar by [retrieving third-party code and embedding it on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-adding-an-accessibility-toolbar-to-your-site).

> **Note:**
>
> Accessibility toolbars are not a requirement to reach the standard as outlined in the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
