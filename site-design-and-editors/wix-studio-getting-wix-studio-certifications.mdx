In the Wix Studio Academy, you can earn industry-recognized certifications that give you a competitive edge in your field. Once you complete the test and submit a project that meets our criteria, you get an official certification to share on your socials and Marketplace profile.

### What certifications are currently available?

- **Web designer: **Get recognized as an accredited Wix Studio Web Designer. You'll recreate a live Wix Studio site from top to bottom, demonstrating your knowledge of site planning, design and construction, including responsive behaviors.
- **Developer:** Get recognized as an accredited Wix Studio Developer. You’ll submit an exam and a project, demonstrating your knowledge of [Velo API](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites), as well as [best practices](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites/articles/best-practices/best-practices-for-building-a-site-with-velo) for security, performance and maintenance in development.

### Getting a new certification

Head over to the Wix Studio Academy to [learn more about the available certifications](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy/wixstudiocertifications). The requirements and criteria vary from one certification to another. There may be an exam, a project to submit or both.

Each of these certifications earns you a badge with your name that you can download and share. If you're a Wix partner, you also get 100 partner points for every certification.

#### To get certified:

Access the Wix Studio Certifications page:

1. [Go to Sites](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/sites) in your Wix Studio workspace.
2. Click **Resources** at the top.
3. Click **Studio Certifications** under **Learn**.
    Click **Explore Certifications**.  Click **Learn more **next to the desired certification.   Choose what to do next:

- **See certification steps: **Review the exam and project descriptions and learn about our criteria for grading them.
- **Get certified: **Start taking the exam and submit the project.

**Tip:** The web designer certification does not include an exam but a task.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about Wix Studio certifications.

### How long does it take to get the certification and the partner points?

Multi-choice exams, like the one in the Developer certification, are graded immediately. For projects that you submit, like the site recreation assignment in the Web Designer certification, it may take up to 6 weeks to review your submission and certify you. 
If you're a Wix partner, you'll see 100 points shortly after receiving the certification (a few days at most). Didn't get your points within this time frame? [Contact Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) for further assistance.

### (Developer) Do I have to submit the test and the project at the same time?

No, you can submit the test and come back at a later point in time to submit the project. Just follow the steps outlined in this article again – once you click** Get Certified** (step 4), you'll be redirected to submit the project. 
**Note:** You must pass the exam before you can submit the project.

### (Web designer) Are there educational materials I should review to prepare for the project?

Yes, we recommend reviewing these before starting the certification process:
- [(Course) The Wix Studio essentials](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy/courses/wix-studio-essentials-course)
- [(Webinar) Discover the Studio Editor](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy/webinars/discover-the-wix-studio-editor)
- [(Webinar) Embrace fully responsive design using Wix Studio](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy/webinars/discover-the-wix-studio-editor)

In addition, the [Academy Library](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy/library) offers many design courses, webinars, tutorials and sandboxes to help you sharpen your skills.

### Does my entire team get certified if I submit the project?

It depends on whether you choose to certify an individual or the workspace (e.g. your agency's workspace). 
If you choose to certify a workspace, it marks the entire team as certified. However, certifications with a personal name are individual. In this case, each team member would need to complete the certification individually. 
**Working with multiple workspaces?**The badge only applies to the workspace you choose when you get certified. If you're providing services to multiple workspaces, reach out to [Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support).

### I got the name on the certification wrong. Can I change it?

If you need to change the name appearing on the certificate, [contact Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) for further assistance.

### I can't see the option to download my certificate?

[Contact Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) for further assistance.
