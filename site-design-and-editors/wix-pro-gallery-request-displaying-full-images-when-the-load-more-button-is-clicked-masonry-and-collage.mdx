---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Displaying Full Images When the \"Load More\" Button is Clicked (Masonry & Collage)"
---

When you set the Load More button to show** one more section **in a Pro Gallery with the Masonry or Collage layout, the images at the bottom of the next section are cut. Currently, it is not possible to display full images when the Load More button is enabled in this scenario.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can set your Load More button to display **all images**. The images will not be cut.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
