---
title: "ADI Request: Removing the Shopping Cart Icon"
---

Currently, you cannot remove the shopping cart icon from the header of your site, as it is an essential part of your online store. If you want to remove the icon from your site, you need to [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor). Then select the icon in the Editor and press delete on your keyboard.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
