### Viewing your Customer Care ticket log

View your team's entire Customer ticket log and find answers and solutions from past discussions, making it convenient to find the information you need.

#### To view your ticket log:

[Go to Customer Care Tickets](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/partner-tickets?referralInfo=sidebar&sort=creationDate+desc) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Search for a ticket or use the filter to narrow the tickets by the reporter, sites, and ticket status.  Hover over a ticket and click **View** to see the conversation with Wix Customer Care.

> **Need additional help?**
>
> Click **+ Create New Ticket** at the top right to start a new conversation.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about the Customer Care ticket log.

### What tickets can I see in the log?

If you are the owner or co-owner of the workspace, you have access to all tickets created by you and your team. If you're a teammate, you can only see your own tickets.

### Can I search for a ticket by keywords?

You can search for tickets based on topics, ticket numbers, and sites. Additionally, you can filter the ticket log by the reporter, sites, and statuses (open/closed).

### Can I see tickets that haven't been answered yet?

Yes, you can also view open tickets that haven't yet been answered by Customer Care.

### Can I reopen a ticket?

Currently, reopening a ticket isn't available. If you require additional help, please create a new ticket.

### Can I delete or hide a ticket?

Currently, it's not possible to delete or hide a ticket.

### Can I edit the subject of a ticket?

Currently, you can't edit the subject of a ticket.
