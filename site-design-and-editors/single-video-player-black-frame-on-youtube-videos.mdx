---
title: "Single Video Player: Black Frame on YouTube Videos"
---

The black frame or bars that you see, appear in the preview of the video only.

> **Important:**
>
> When you play the YouTube videos, you will not see these black bars, provided that you are using the appropriate aspect ratio.

We have thoroughly investigated this issue and found that the root cause is on YouTube's end and affects all embedded YouTube videos, regardless of where the video has been embedded.
