### Adding an app from the app market

The app market is where you can add new apps to a site you're working on, both external apps and apps made by Wix. It's organized by categories so you can quickly find relevant apps. Some are free to install, others require payment and / or a subscription.

#### To add an app:

Click **App Market **** **on the left side of the Editor.  Click **App Market**.  Use the search bar and the categories on the left to find the app you need.  Click the app you want to add.  Click **+ Add** **to Site**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about adding apps to a Studio Editor site.

### What happens after adding an app?

Often, when adding an app, a widget is automatically added to the page you're working on. In other cases, relevant new site pages are automatically created to show the app's content. 
Click the widget, or go to the pages that were added, to customize the settings to your client's needs.

### I've added an app but I don't see it in the Editor. Where is it?

Some apps have components that appear both in the dashboard and the Editor (e.g. Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, Wix Blog, etc.). 
A few apps work behind the scenes and can be found only in your site's dashboard. Make sure to look for those apps [in your dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-sites-dashboard).

### Are there integrations with external sites and platforms?

Yes, you can find plenty of integrations with known sites and platforms in the app market. 
**Note: **Apps that integrate with external sites may try to connect to your account if you are logged in (e.g. the Instagram Feed App connects you to your Instagram account).
