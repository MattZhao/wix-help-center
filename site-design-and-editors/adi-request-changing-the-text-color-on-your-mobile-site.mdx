---
title: "ADI Request: Changing The Text Color on Your Mobile Site"
---

Currently, you cannot change the color of the text  on your mobile site.

However, you can [change the layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-layout-of-a-section) of the section and your [site colors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors) which can affect the text color as well.
