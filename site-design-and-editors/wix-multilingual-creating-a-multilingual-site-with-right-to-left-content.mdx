---
title: "Wix Multilingual: Creating a Multilingual Site with Right-to-Left Content"
---

Is your site's main language read right-to-left but your [secondary language](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-customizing-the-wix-multilingual-language-menu-on-your-site) is read left-to-right? You can change the direction and order of text, media and Wix apps to fit your language needs.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> [Add and set up Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-adding-and-setting-up-wix-multilingual) on your site and translate it in the [Translation Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-using-the-translation-manager).

### To create a multilingual site with right-to-left content:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the **Switch languages **drop-down menu at the top of the editor.

Select the language you want to edit.   Decide what you want to change:

### Text

Wix Multilingual detects and auto-aligns your site when you [auto-translate your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site). So, your text aligns to the right and the direction changes to right-to-left. These steps also apply to text elements that are attached to elements such as strips, repeaters, slideshows and boxes. 

1. Select the relevant text element on your page.
2. Click **Edit Translation**.
3. Adjust the direction and alignment by clicking the **Alignment** icon   and the **Change** **text direction **icon .

### Social bar

Reorder the icons on your social bar to best suit each language. For example, prioritize an icon for a social network popular in a specific country by placing it first. 

1. Select your social bar.
2. Click **Edit Translation**.
3. Drag and drop the icons to re-order them and click **Done**.

### Single image

Using the [Wix Photo Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-about-the-wix-photo-studio), you can also change the direction of individual images to be right-to-left by flipping the original image. The process of changing the image direction is the same for all single-image elements on your site.

1. Select the relevant image on your page.
2. Click **Edit Translation**.
3. Click **Adjust** to open the Wix Photo Studio.
4. Click **Crop & Resize.**
5. Click the **Flip Horizontally** icon .
6. Click **Save**.

### Image gallery

To change a gallery's image order on your site's right-to-left version, manually reorder the images. 

1. Select the relevant gallery on your page.
2. Click **Edit** **Translation**.
3. Drag and drop images to reorder them.
4. Click **Save**.

### Single Video Player

If you have a few videos displayed in a row you can change their order on your site's right-to-left version, manually replace each video in every language. For example, reorder videos from 1-2-3 in LTR to 3-2-1 in RTL.

1. Click the **Single Video Player** element on your page.
2. Click **Edit Translation**.
3. Click **Change Video **and decide what you want to do: 

- **Add / upload from the Media Manager:** Click **Change Video** to add a video from Wix Media or upload your own.
- **Select a video from a social network:** Click the relevant social network icon and enter the video's web address.
4. Click **Save**.

### Wix applications

With some Wix applications (e.g. Stores), the writing direction applies to all languages. We recommend selecting a centered text alignment if you have both RTL and LTR languages in your site.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Learn about [using Multi-State Boxes to switch from LTR to RTL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-using-multi-state-boxes-to-switch-from-ltr-to-rtl).
> - You can also [customize your site menu per language](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-customizing-your-site-menu-per-language).
> - If you'd like to see additional RTL capabilities in the future, [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-request-additional-rtl-capabilities).
