---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Displaying More than One Column in a Product Gallery on Mobile"
---

While you can display multiple columns in a [product gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-product-galleries) on desktop and tablet, it is not possible to show more than one column of products on mobile.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
