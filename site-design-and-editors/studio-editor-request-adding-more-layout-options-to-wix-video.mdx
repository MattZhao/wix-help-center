---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding More Layout Options to Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to choose a different layout for the [Wix Video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-about-wix-video) widget in the Studio Editor (e.g. a grid or a slider displaying your videos).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
