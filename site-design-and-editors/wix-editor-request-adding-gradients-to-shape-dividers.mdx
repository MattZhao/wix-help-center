---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding Gradients to Shape Dividers"
---

You can [customize your shape dividers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-shape-dividers#customizing-your-shape-divider) on your strips and sections by adjusting settings such as the layers and opacity, and changing the color. However, it is not possible to add a gradient to a shape divider on a section / strip.

As an alternative, you can [add a gradient to a strip or section background](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-color-and-gradient-backgrounds-to-your-elements), and adjust the opacity of the divider so that the gradient is more visible.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
