To get the best streaming results, make sure you don't exceed the following settings on your [encoder](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-downloading-a-live-stream-encoder):

> **Important:**
>
> The output needs to be set to 'Simple'. If it set to 'Advanced' it can cause issues with the live stream.

### To change your streaming settings:

Open the OBS Studio Encoder on your computer.Click **Settings** on the bottom right.Select the **Output** tab to change the codec, audio and video bitrates.Select the **Video** tab to change the video resolution.

> **Tip:**
>
> - If your internet bandwidth is less than 3000 kb/s, reduce the video and audio bitrates so that the sum fits your bandwidth.
> - A general rule is to have double the bandwidth for your target bitrate. This is to help ensure you maintain your bandwidth during internet fluctuations.
