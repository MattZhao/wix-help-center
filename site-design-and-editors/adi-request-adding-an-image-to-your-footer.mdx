title: "ADI Request: Adding an Image to Your Footer"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to Wix ADI. Click [here](https://www.wix.com/support/html5/the-wix-editor) if you are looking for information regarding the Editor.

It is currently not possible to add an image to your footer in ADI.

To give your website a sleek and professional look, the footer does not contain any images. If you want to add an image to your footer, [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
