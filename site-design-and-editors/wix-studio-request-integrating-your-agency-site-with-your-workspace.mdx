title: "Wix Studio Request: Integrating Your Agency Site with Your Workspace"
---

Currently, it's not possible to integrate your agency’s Wix site with your Wix Studio workspace. This integration would allow seamless workflows, like managing service tickets and sales funnels, all from within your workspace.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
