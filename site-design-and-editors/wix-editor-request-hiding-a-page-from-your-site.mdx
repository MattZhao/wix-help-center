---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Hiding a Page from Your Site"
---

Currently, in the Wix Editor, it is not possible to hide a page from your published site.

As a workaround, you [prevent your site pages from being indexed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-preventing-search-engines-from-indexing-your-page), [password protect pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site) or [hide pages from your site's menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
