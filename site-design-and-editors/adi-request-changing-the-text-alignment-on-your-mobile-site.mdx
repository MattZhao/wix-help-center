---
title: "ADI Request: Changing The Text Alignment on Your Mobile Site"
---

Currently, you cannot change the alignment of the text on your mobile site.

In the meantime, you can [change the text alignment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-text-on-your-site) from a desktop or change the [design of the section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-layout-of-a-section) which might affect the alignment of the text.

If you are still experiencing an issue, you can [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor).
