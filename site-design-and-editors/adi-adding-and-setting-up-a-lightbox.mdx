Lightboxes are a great way to tell your site visitors an important message, as they pop up when the page loads. For example, your lightbox can welcome new members to your site, advertise special offers or sales, or invite people to subscribe to your site.

Customize the design and text of your lightbox to make it your own, and choose how long you want the pop-up to stay on screen. You can rename and delete a lightbox at any time.

### Adding a lightbox

Add a lightbox to your site from the Add drop-down menu in just a few clicks.

#### To add a lightbox:

Click the **Add** drop-down at the top left of the builder.  Click **Page**.  Select **Lightbox**.  Hover over your favorite design and click** Add This Page.**

---

### Setting up your lightbox

Choose the content and media you want displayed in your lightbox, change the layout, select which page the lightbox appears on, and much more.

#### To set up your lightbox:

Click on the lightbox in the ADI Builder to open the design and settings panel.  Click **Edit** to change the lightbox's text and media settings.

### Show me how

Choose what you want to edit:
- **Title:** Choose the header or main action item you want people to take such as Subscribe, Join, or Welcome.
- **Subtitle: **Add a subtitle beneath the main header.
- **Paragraph: **Use this space to encourage visitors to sign up, promote a sale, or further describe the lightbox's purpose.
- **Media**: Edit the lightbox image. Click **Edit & Replace** to change the image, adjust it, or link the image to another page or URL.
- **Buttons: **Change the button text and link the button to a page or URL. You can also add multiple buttons by clicking **+ Add Button.**

**Note:** To hide any text or media element from your lightbox, deselect the blue checkmark beside the element.

3.  Click **Design** to change the layout and design of your lightbox.

### Show me how

Update the lightbox section design:
1. Scroll to see the different options for lightbox layouts including recommended designs. Hover over a design and click **Use This Layout **to update the lightbox.
2. (Optional) You can also click the **Customize Section Design** drop-down under the current layout to make text and color changes.

4.  Click **Settings** to customize how your lightbox appears on your site and choose what page it shows up on.

### Show me how

Choose how the lightbox behaves on your site:
1. Enable the **Automatically show lightbox** toggle to make the lightbox appear on your site.
2. Click the text field under **Which Pages Is It On?** to choose the page(s) where the lightbox shows up. For example, if it's a welcome lightbox, select **Home** to have it pop up only on the homepage. Or you can select **All Pages** to have the lightbox appear on every page.

**Note: **Choose the placement of your lightbox carefully. Visitors may get frustrated if a pop-up appears on every page of your site.
3. Click and drag the **Delay** slider to choose how many seconds someone can view your site before the lightbox appears. You can also input a number in the number field at the right.

### Renaming a lightbox

Rename your lightbox to make it easier to find in your page list.

#### To rename a lightbox:

Click the **Page** drop-down at the top of ADI.  Click the Settings icon  next to your lightbox page.  Click **Rename**.  Type your lightbox's new name and press the Enter key on your keyboard to save it.

---

### Deleting a lightbox

You can delete a lightbox from your site at any time.

#### To delete a lightbox:

Click the **Page** drop-down at the top of ADI.  Click the Settings icon  next to your lightbox page.  Click **Delete**.  Click **Delete **to confirm**.**
