### Using the translation manager

With Wix Multilingual you can translate your site's content (e.g. text, images, links, etc.) in two ways: Automatically and manually. If you selected to auto translate your site while installing the app, you can review and edit the translations to your needs.

> **Tip:**
>
> Translate all apps and site content in your dashboard, and editor elements in the editor.

DashboardWix EditorEditor XStudio Editor
1. [Go to the Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmultilingual%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Multilingual) in your site's dashboard.
2. (If you have more than 2 languages) Select the relevant language by clicking the language drop-down at the top left.

1. Select what you want to translate on the left:

- Click **Page Content **to translate pieces of text taken from your site pages.
- Select the relevant Wix app.
2. Choose how you want to translate your content:
Review the existing automatic translationsIf you decided to auto-translate your entire site, you can review the translations and update them to your liking. Manually translate your content Translate your site content manually, row by row.
1. Click the field of the text you want to translate and type your translation, or click **Auto-translate**.
2. Once you have reviewed the translation, click **Done** to save your changes.
3. Repeat these steps for each text you want to translate manually.

**Tip:** Filter by "not translated" to quickly get to the content you need to translate. Auto-translate the entire site
1. Click [Auto-translate Site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site) at the bottom left.
2. Click **Auto-translate Site** in the pop-up to confirm.
3. If you don't have enough word credit you can [purchase more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site).

**Note: **Any text you already edited will not be changed.
4. Review the translations.
5. (Optional) Replace the text in the text box to match the correct translation and click **Done** to confirm the change.
6. Repeat these steps for each text you want to translate automatically.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the Translation Manager:

### How can I view my word credit balance?

You can check your word credit balance by clicking **Check word credit balance** in the Machine Translation section of the Translation Manager.  

**To get more word credits:**
1. Click **Check word credit balance** at the bottom of the Translation Manager.
2. Click **Get more credits.**
3. Click **Select **on the relevant package.
4. Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.
[image: A screenshot of the word credit options.]

### Can I auto-translate all my site content in one go?

Yes. You can auto-translate all your site and business content in one go by using the **Auto-translate Site** option at the bottom left of the Translation Manager. [Learn more about Auto-translation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site).
**Note: **The translating engine is based on Google translate, which doesn't support all languages yet.

### How can I translate the page's SEO?

Wix Multilingual lets you [optimize your SEO settings for each language individually](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-optimizing-seo-settings-for-multilingual-sites). This means you can tailor your strategy for different locales and strengthen your online presence.
Each language version of your site also has its own [subdomain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-about-multilingual-subdomains) that includes the language code. This creates a professional look for your site, and it also helps with SEO and using analytics.
You also have the option to change your URL structure to improve your search ranking. There are three options available; subdirectories, subdomains, and language parameters. [Learn more about the benefits and risks of changing your URL structure](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-editing-your-multilingual-sites-url-structure)

### What happens if I translate the editor text in a different tab?

Some information may not be fully synced. To see the most updated text from the editor, refresh the browser tab with the Translation Manager.

### Is there content that cannot be translated using Wix Multilingual?

While many features and apps can be translated with Wix Multilingual, some are not yet available. [View the complete list of translatable and untranslatable content in Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translatable-content)

### How can I edit or add a link to my translated content?

Go to your Editor to edit or add a link to your [translated content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-text-buttons-and-links).

### Are keyboard shortcuts available in the Translation Manager?

Yes. [A variety of keyboard shortcuts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/translation-manager-keyboard-shortcuts) are available in the Translation Manager to help save you time.

### Can I filter content by translation status?

Yes, you can filter content by translation status (e.g. translated / not translated) in the Translation Manager.

### Can I search for content in the Translation Manager?

No. Currently, it is not possible to search for content in the Translation Manager. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-request-searching-for-content-in-the-translation-manager).
