### Adding social media account links to your site

Add social media links to encourage visitors to follow or subscribe to your accounts on different platforms. You can add links to your site's header, footer, and contact section to ensure they're always visible to your site visitors.

#### To add social media account links to your site:

Open your site in ADI.  Click the section you want to add the links to (header, footer or contact).  Click the Show/Hide** **icon  next to **Social Accounts**.  Click the social icons.

Click the Show/Hide** **icon  next to the icons you want to show.  Hover over each icon and click the Edit icon .

Enter the URL of the relevant social page.

### How can I change / update my social media links?

You can change / update your social media links at any time. Just click the section containing your social links, select the icons and edit the relevant one. This will update the relevant social link in all sections they are displayed in. For example, if you update the links in the Contact section, the links in your Footer are updated automatically as well.

### Can I add an account link that isn't on the list?

Absolutely. Follow the steps to add a social media account link, and enter the URL of your chosen account, site, or platform. In place of the specific icon, a general social icon is displayed on your site.

### How do I check that my links are working?

[Preview your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site#previewing-your-site) and click the social icons to make sure that they lead to your social accounts / pages.
