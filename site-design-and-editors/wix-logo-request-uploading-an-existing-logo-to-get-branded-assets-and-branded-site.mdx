---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Uploading an Existing Logo to Get Branded Assets and Branded Site"
---

Currently, it is not possible to upload an existing logo file created outside of Wix in order to receive branded assets and a branded site. These are additional features that are only available when you create your logo using the Wix Logo Maker.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
