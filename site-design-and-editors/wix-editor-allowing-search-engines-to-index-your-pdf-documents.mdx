---
title: "Wix Editor: Allowing Search Engines to Index Your PDF Documents"
---

Allow your PDF document to be indexed so that search engines such as Google can find it.

### To enable your PDF documents to be indexed by search engines:

Click the document in your editor.  Click the **Settings** icon .  Click **Show this PDF in search results.**  Click **Turn On Indexing**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you are linking an element to a document, you can also enable this option from the Link panel.
