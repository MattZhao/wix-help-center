### Uploading your own media

Upload media straight from the Editor. You can upload media from your computer, social networks, other Studio Editor sites or a URL.

Once you've uploaded a media file to the Media Manager, you can use it anywhere on the site.

> **Note:**
>
> See the supported [file types and sizes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes).

#### To upload your own media:

Click **Media ** on the left side of the Editor.  (In the Media Manager) Click **Upload Media.**  Choose where you want to upload media from:

### Your computer

Choose one of the options below:
- Drag and drop files into the Media Manager.
- Click **Upload from Computer**.

1. Select the file(s) you want to upload.
2. Click **Open**.

**

**[image: A screenshot of the options to upload media from your computer.]

### Other Studio Editor sites

1. Click **My Wix Account**.
2. Double-click the relevant site folder.
3. Select the files you want to upload.
4. Click** Upload Selection**.

### Social networks

1. Select the relevant social network.
2. Click **Connect.**
3. Select the files you want to upload.
4. Click **Upload Selection**.** **

### A link (URL)

1. Click the **Link **iconon the left side.
2. Paste the URL link and click **Import**.

### Adding free media

The Media Manager contains hundreds of free Wix and Unsplash media files that you can add to the site. Just click **Media from Wix** or **Unsplash** on the left side of the Media Manager to find the media file you want.

#### To add free media:

Click **Media **[image: undefined] on the left side of the Editor.  (In the Media Manager) Click the relevant tab:

- **Media from Wix:** Free images, illustrations and patterns from the Wix library.
- **Unsplash:** Free stock images in high resolution.
    Browse the available images and videos to find the one you want.

**Tip:** Use the search bar and the filters to find media faster.   Click the desired image.  Click **Add to Page**.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Free Wix media files are available for digital use within Wix ONLY. For more information, see the  [Wix Terms of Use](http://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).
> - You can use Unsplash images only in accordance with the [Unsplash Terms of Use](https://unsplash.com/terms) and the [Wix Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).

### Purchasing media

Purchase media from Shutterstock. You can filter images / videos by category for easy browsing, or find media by typing keywords in the search bar. Once you submit your purchase, your media is automatically added to the Media Manager.

#### To purchase media:

Click **Media **** **on the left side of the Editor.  (In the Media Manager) Click **Shutterstock**.  Browse the available images and videos to find the one you want.

**Tip:** Use the search bar and the filters to find media faster.   Click the media you want to purchase.  Click **Buy Image **/** Buy Video**.  Enter your payment details.  Click **Submit Purchase**.
