---
title: "ADI Request: Adding Folders"
---

A folder acts like a category title that is not clickable. When you hover over it, you can see the sub-pages.

Currently, you cannot add folders in ADI. To use this feature, you should [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-and-managing-a-dropdown-submenu).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
