---
title: "Wix Editor: Updating Your Site's Twitter:Image"
---

Your site's [twitter:image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-twitter-settings-for-your-sites-pages) is displayed on your Twitter Card. If you are seeing an outdated image on your Twitter Card, follow the steps below to update it.

### To update the twitter:image on Twitter:

[Set a twitter:image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-twitter-settings-for-your-sites-pages#customizing-your-pages-twitter-settings) for each page of your site.  Log in to Twitter.       Go to the Twitter Card Validator: [https://cards-dev.twitter.com/validator](https://cards-dev.twitter.com/validator)  Enter the URL of the article or blog post that you want to tweet. You will see a preview of the Twitter Card which should now show the correct image.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also update your headline on Twitter by following the steps above.
