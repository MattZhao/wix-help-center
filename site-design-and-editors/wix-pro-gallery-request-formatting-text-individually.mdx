---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Formatting Text Individually"
---

Currently, it is not possible to format titles and descriptions individually, or apply format settings to individual words. When you format the text of a title or description, it is applied to all instances of the text.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
