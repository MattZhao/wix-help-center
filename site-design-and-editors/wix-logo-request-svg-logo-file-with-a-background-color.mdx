---
title: "Wix Logo Request: SVG Logo File with a Background Color"
---

Currently, all [SVG logo files](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-svg-logo-files) come with a transparent background by default to ensure your logo looks good in all places i.e., mugs, t-shirts, business cards etc.

If you want to use a logo file with your chosen background color, you can use one of the PNG files that come in your downloaded ZIP file. Keep in mind that PNG files may lose quality if you resize them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
