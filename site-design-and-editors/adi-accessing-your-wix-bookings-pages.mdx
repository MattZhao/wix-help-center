---
title: "ADI: Accessing Your Wix Bookings Pages"
---

When you [add Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-wix-bookings) to your site, a new page is automatically added called Book Online. As part of the Wix Bookings app, 6 additional sub-pages / sections are added:

**Services list:** Customers can view and select services on this page.  **Service Page: **Provide more information about the services you offer.  **Booking Calendar: **Allow your customers and site visitors to book a service.  **Payment Info:** Customers add their payment details.  **Booking Form: **Collect important and useful information when clients fill in the form to book a service.  **Confirmation:** Customers see this page when they complete a booking.

---

### To access your additional Wix Bookings pages:

Click the **Page** drop-down at the top of ADI.  Click **Book Online**.  Click **Bookings Checkout **under **Book Online.**  Click the Bookings section in the ADI Builder.  The list of Bookings pages appears on the left. Click the Edit icon beside a Bookings page to customize it.
