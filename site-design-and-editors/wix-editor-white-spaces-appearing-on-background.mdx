---
title: "Wix Editor: White Spaces Appearing on Background"
---

A white space displaying in the background of your site, can occur when your background is set to scroll with your site. To resolve this, we recommend having a fixed background.

### To change the background scrolling setting:

Go to the relevant page in your Editor.   Click **Site Design**  on the left side of the Editor.  Hover over **Page Background** and click **Customize**.  Click Settings** ** on the background image preview.  Select **It's fixed** under **Does the image scroll?**.

> **Note:**
>
> The background scrolling option is only available on image backgrounds.
