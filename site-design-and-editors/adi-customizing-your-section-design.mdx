---
title: "ADI: Customizing Your Section Design"
---

You can customize sections of your ADI site. Choose a new color combination, update the font size and more.

### To customize a section of your site:

Hover over the section of your site you want to customize. Click **Design**. Click **Customize Section Design**. Select one of the options below:

### Media

Under your **Media Settings**, you can change the media type, replace the image or choose a different media size. **Note: **The customization options depend on the media size selected.

Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/wix-adi/adding-media-in-adi) to learn more about your media settings.

### Color

Select one of the options presented to change the section's colors.

### Text Settings

- Drag the slider    to increase or decrease the font size.
- Click on the desired text alignment.

### Height

Click the + or - icons to enlarge or shrink the section.

### Animation

Click an animation option to see how it appears in the selected section.

> **Note:**
>
> The design and customization options vary based on the section selected.
