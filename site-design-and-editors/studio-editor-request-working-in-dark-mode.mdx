---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Working in Dark Mode"
---

Currently, the Studio Editor interface is only available in a light color scheme. It is not possible to switch your theme to dark mode or other colors schemes.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
