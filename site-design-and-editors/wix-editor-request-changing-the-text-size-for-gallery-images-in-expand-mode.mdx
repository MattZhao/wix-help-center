---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Changing the Text Size for Gallery Images in Expand Mode"
---

Currently, changing the text size of gallery images in [expand mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-expand-mode-in-galleries) is not supported. You can, however, change the text size of the regular view in some galleries. [Learn how to customize gallery text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-gallery-image-text#customizing-the-design-of-your-text)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
