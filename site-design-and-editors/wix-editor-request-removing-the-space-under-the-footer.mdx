---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Removing the Space Under the Footer"
---

You may see a gap under your footer on some screens. This appears because your site's content is shorter than the viewport height.

Currently, Wix offers solutions to help you build a horizontally responsive site. [Click here to learn which elements are horizontally responsive](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-request-responsive-sites)

We are still working on solutions for vertical responsiveness. In the meantime, you can try our recommendations. [Learn more about space under your footer and view possible solutions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-fixing-spacing-and-alignment-issues-on-your-site#gaps-below-the-footer)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
