---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding an Image or a Video to the Page Background"
---

Using the Studio Editor, you can choose a background color for every page. Currently, it is not possible to add an image or a video to the background.

> Alternatively, you can add an image or a video background to page sections. Select the section, open the Inspector panel and click **Add image or video **(under **Design**).  [image: Adding an image or video background to a section from the Inspector panel in the Studio Editor]

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
