### Managing your site pages

Manage your site pages at any point from the Pages & Menu panel in the editor. You can rename a page, duplicate a page to create a similar one, and delete those you don't need. This ensures your site remains user-friendly and up to date.

#### To manage your site pages:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages** **& Menu**  on the left side of the editor.  Hover over the relevant page and click the **More Actions** icon .  Choose what you want to do:

### Rename the page

Rename site pages to improve clarity and navigation for your visitors.

1. Click **Rename**.
2. Type the new name of the page.
3. Click **Done**.

### Duplicate the page

Duplicate site pages to save time and maintain consistency across your site.

1. Click **Duplicate**.
2. Choose from the following options: 

- **Duplicate Page**: Create a duplicate of the page.
- **Make This Page Dynamic**: Reuse this layout to create [multiple site pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-multi-page-site#use-dynamic-pages) with content from a [CMS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview) (Content Management System) collection.

### Copy and paste the page to another Wix Editor site

Copy and paste pages as needed between your sites, as long as both are in the Wix Editor.

1. Click **Copy**.
2. Open the editor of the site you want to paste to.
3. Paste the page by doing one of the following:

- Right-click anywhere on the screen and select **Paste**.
- Right-click anywhere on the screen and select **Paste & Apply Site Theme **to apply the color and text themes of the target site.

### Set the page as the site homepage

Click **Set as**** Homepage **to make this your site's homepage. 
If you want to delete your current homepage, you must first set another page as the homepage. Then, you can delete the page that was used as your homepage.

### Hide the page from the menu

Click **Hide **  to hide a page to keep it accessible for future use without displaying it in the site's navigation.
**Tip: **To unhide, click the **More Actions ** icon next to the hidden page and select **Show **.

### Set the page as a subpage

Click **Subpage **to nest this page under another page. This creates a subpage (drop-down) menu on your site. Visitors can see the subpages when they hover over the main page on the menu.

**Tip:**You can easily turn the subpage back into a main page at any time. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the subpage and click **Main page**.

### Delete the page

Delete pages you no longer need to improve user experience.

1. Click **Delete **.
2. Click **Delete** in the pop-up notification.

**Note**: If a page is linked to a Wix app (e.g., Blog, Stores, etc.), deleting the page also removes the app.

> **Want to reorder your pages?**
>
> Click the **R****eorder**  icon next to the page name and drag the page to the desired location in the list.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I change the settings of an individual page?

Absolutely. You can [change page settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-managing-your-page-settings), [password protect individual pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site) and even [create members only pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-members-only-pages).

### Can I edit the width of my page?

The width of your site is fixed at 980 pixels. You can design your site outside of the 980 pixel borders, however we recommend that anything that you add outside of these borders be mainly decorative and not vital content to your visitors' browsing experience.

### How can I delete an app page from my site?

To delete pages associated with Wix apps (e.g. Wix Stores, Wix Events, Wix Blog), you must [delete the apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/deleting-apps) themselves to remove their pages.

### Can I hide a page without deleting it?

Yes, you can [hide a page from your site menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu) without deleting it. This is useful if you want to keep the page content but don’t want it visible to visitors.

### Can I add more than 100 pages to my site?

You can add up to 100 static pages to your Wix site. The 100 page limit does not apply to dynamic pages, such as product pages, blog posts, and [pages connected to your CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).

### Why does my hidden page still appear when I Google search my site?

Hidden pages still appear in search engines. If you don't want your site to appear in search results, you can prevent search engines from indexing your site. [Learn how to hide a page from search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-preventing-search-engines-from-indexing-your-page)

### Can I download a page from my site?

It is not possible to download a Wix site or any of its pages as the editor needs to be in constant connection with our servers. As such, it is not possible to work offline or save site pages to your computer.

Press **Ctrl+P** on your keyboard to use the print screen, or you can take a screenshot of the page and print it.
