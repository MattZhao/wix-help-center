---
title: "Wix Editor: Icons Available When Clicking on Elements in the Editor"
---

When you click an element in your Editor, you may come across several icons. Below are some of the icons that appear on elements, and their meanings and uses.

> **Important:**
>
> Icons available depend on the element type and its settings.

IconNameActionSettingsClick to set up the element. The settings are

different for each element.LayoutClick to change the layout of the element.

The layouts are different for each element.DesignClick to design the element. The design options are different for each element.AnimateClick to [add animation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-an-animation) to the element.LinkClick to [add a link](https://support.wix.com/en/article/what-do-you-want-to-link-to) to the element.More ActionsClick to show more options.Crop & EditClick to [make changes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-an-image-in-the-editor-or-with-wix-photo-studio) to your image.UpgradeClick to upgrade the element/feature.HelpClick to learn more about the element or find an answer to a question you have.Stretch HandleClick and drag to make the element longer or shorter. This icon appears on strips and pages.StretchClick this icon to stretch the element to either the full-width of your site, or within the gridlines. This icon appears on elements such as strips and galleries.AccessibilityClick to view and edit your attributes. This icon is only shown on elements that you have already added custom aria attributes to. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-adding-aria-attributes-to-custom-web-applications)Connect to DataConnect the element to content collections to display and collect data on your site. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview#compatible-elements)
