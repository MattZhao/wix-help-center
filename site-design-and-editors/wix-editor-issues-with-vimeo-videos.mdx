---
title: "Wix Editor: Issues With Vimeo Videos"
---

If you are experiencing a problem with your Vimeo video, please check the information below.

**Vimeo Plus users**: Wix has switched the static domain from **static.wix.com** to **static.parastorage.com**. Therefore, if your video is no longer working, go to your Vimeo Plus settings and make sure that **static.parastorage.com** is defined as the allowed domain.

> **Note:**
>
> Videos might not play automatically on iPhones/iPads as Apple blocks the autoplay function on their mobile devices. This is due to the fact that playing videos or music on a mobile device may incur additional data charges from the visitor's cellphone network. For more information, please see Apple's [Safari Developer Library](http://developer.apple.com/library/safari/#documentation/AudioVideo/Conceptual/Using_HTML5_Audio_Video/Device-SpecificConsiderations/Device-SpecificConsiderations.html%23//apple_ref/doc/uid/TP40009523-CH5-SW1).
