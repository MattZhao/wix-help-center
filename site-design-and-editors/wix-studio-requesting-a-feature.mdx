Stay in the loop with all the exciting developments on the horizon through our dedicated [Wix Product Roadmap](https://support.wix.com/en/roadmap) page. Dive into the world of Wix product categories and the upcoming enhancements, explore feature requests that are in the pipeline – complete with their estimated timeframes – and even share your own feature requests.

### To request a feature:

[Go to Discover](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/discover) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click the** News Center **icon  at the top right and select **Wix Product Roadmap**.  Click **Request a Feature** on the top right.  Select the Wix product you are requesting the feature for.  Enter a description of the feature you are requesting, including how it works and what it will do.  Enter a description of how the feature will impact your business.  Click **Send**.

> **What's next?**
>
> Our team reviews every feature request we receive and then carefully assesses how they align with our product roadmap for potential development. 
> In the meantime, check out our [Wix Product Roadmap](https://support.wix.com/en/roadmap) page to stay updated on the latest developments. You can also head over to our [Wix Studio Community Discord](https://discord.com/invite/wixstudio) to share your vision for the future of Wix Studio and engage with others.
