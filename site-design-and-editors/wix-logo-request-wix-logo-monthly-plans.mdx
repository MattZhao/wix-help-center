---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Wix Logo Monthly Plans"
---

Currently, it is not possible to buy Wix Logo plans that include a monthly website subscription. It is only possible to purchase these subscriptions with a yearly Premium website plan.

As an alternative option, you can purchase a monthly Premium plan for your website and Wix Logo separately.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
