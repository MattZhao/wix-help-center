---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Keeping the Title and Description When Replacing an Image"
---

Currently, replacing an image in Pro Gallery deletes the previous image and adds a new image without retaining the title and description. It is not possible to replace the image and keep its title and description unchanged.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
