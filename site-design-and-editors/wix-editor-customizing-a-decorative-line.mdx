---
title: "Wix Editor: Customizing a Decorative Line"
---

Choose the color, design and border of your decorative lines to match the look of your site.

### To customize a line:

Click the line in your Editor.  Click the **Design **icon .  Click **Customize Design**.  Select a line design at the top.  Under **Border**, set the line color and width.

> **Note:**
>
> Different line designs have different customization options.
