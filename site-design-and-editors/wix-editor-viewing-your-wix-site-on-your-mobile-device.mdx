title: "Wix Editor: Viewing Your Wix Site on Your Mobile Device"
---

When you build a site on Wix, a mobile-friendly version is automatically created. You can view the mobile version of your site using the site's URL, or from your editor.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Opening your mobile site with the URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-viewing-your-wix-site-on-your-mobile-device#opening-your-mobile-site-with-the-url)  [Viewing your mobile site from your editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-viewing-your-wix-site-on-your-mobile-device#viewing-your-mobile-site-from-your-editor)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-viewing-your-wix-site-on-your-mobile-device#faqs)

> **You should know:**
>
> You can access the mobile version of your site as long as the mobile friendly view is enabled. If you disable the mobile-friendly view of your site, the desktop version is displayed on mobile devices. [Learn more about your site's mobile view](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-the-mobile-version-of-your-site)

### Opening your mobile site with the URL

To view the mobile version of your site with [your site's URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-free-wix-url-8591528), enter the address into the browser on your mobile device.

---

### Viewing your mobile site from your editor

View the published mobile version of your site by scanning a QR code from either the desktop or mobile editor. Depending on your mobile device, you can scan the code with the camera or a QR app.

#### To scan the code from the mobile editor:

Open your site in the mobile editor.  Click **Preview** on the top toolbar.  Click the **QR** icon .  Scan the QR code using your device.

#### To scan the code from the desktop editor:

Open your site in the desktop editor.  Click **Site**.  Click **View Site on Mobile**.  Scan the QR code using your device.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I customize the mobile view of my site?

Absolutely. The mobile editor allows you to hide elements, optimize the layout of your site, and add mobile-only content such as text, buttons, and images. [Learn more about the mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-the-mobile-editor)

### Can I edit my site from my mobile device?

Absolutely. You can edit your site using your mobile browser, or the [Wix app](https://www.wix.com/mobile/wix-app) to adjust text, add links, change images and more.

### Are Wix mobile sites supported in in-app browsers?

Yes. Visitors can view your Wix mobile site within in-app browsers, such as Instagram or Facebook.

### Why isn't my site working on mobile?

If you're having issues viewing your Wix site on a mobile device, you may be viewing it on a [browser or operating system that is not supported](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers).
