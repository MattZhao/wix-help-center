---
title: "Wix Photo Albums: Editing Your Photo Album"
---

Edit your album at any time. You can customize the album's design, add information about your photos, create new galleries and more.

> **Note:**
>
> Make sure to edit your images before you upload them to Photo Albums. Once you've uploaded images to Photo Albums, you cannot edit them.

### To edit a photo album:

Go to [Photo Albums](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Photo%20Albums&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fphotography-albums/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant album.  Click **Customize**.  Edit your album by doing the following:

- Add [new photos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-adding-new-photos-to-an-album) or [new galleries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-adding-new-galleries-to-an-album) to your album.
- Add [titles and descriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-adding-titles-and-descriptions-to-photos) to your photos.
- Change the [layout of your galleries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-changing-the-layout-of-your-galleries).
- Customize your album's [style and design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-designing-your-album).
- Edit your album's [cover page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-editing-your-albums-cover-information).
    When ready, click **Publish Now** at the top right to make your changes go live.
