The Wix Audio Player is a stylish and simple music element that allows you to add a single track to a page on your website. Select your preferred player style, add a track and cover image, and customize its design.

---

### Step 1 | Add the player to your site

First, add a player. Choose between a classic audio player or a player that only displays a play icon.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Add ** on the left side of your editor.
2. Click **Video &** **Music**.
3. Click **Audio Players**.
4. Select a player and drag it to the relevant location on your page.

### Step 2 | Set up your Audio Player

Next, set up your player. Upload a track, add an image and track details, and define the player's playback settings.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click the player in your editor.
2. Click the **Settings** icon  .
3. Set up your player using the options:

- **Choose Image:** Select the image you want your player to display and click **Add to Page**.
- **Choose audio from:**

- **One track: **Click **Upload Track** to access your Media Manager. Select your chosen track and click **Add Track**.
- **External MP3: **Enter the URL of the track you want to play.
- **Track Details:** Enter the artist and track name.
- **Playback Options:** Select an option:

- **Play once: **The track plays once.
- **Play in loop:** The track plays continuously in a loop.

### Step 3 | Customize the player's layout and design

Lastly, customize the player's layout and design so that it complements your site's style.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click the player in your editor.
2. Click the **Layout **icon  and select a layout: **Classic **or **Icon**.

**Note:** If you choose the **Classic **layout, you can choose what info to display, e.g. a progress bar or time stamp.
3. Click the **Design **icon and customize the following:

- **Classic layout: **Customize the regular and hover view of your player.

- **Regular: **

- **Fill color & opacity: **Choose a color for your player's background and adjust its opacity.
- **Borders: **Adjust the color and thickness of the player's border.
- **Corners: **Change the shape of the player by adjusting the corner radius.
- **Text: **Choose the font and color of the player's text and controls and adjust their size.
- **Hover: **Choose a color for the text and controls when a visitor hovers over the player.
- **Icon layout: **Select your preferred icon and click **Customize Design**.

- **Regular: **Choose an icon color and adjust its opacity.
- **Hover: **Choose a color for controls when a visitor hovers over the player.

### Troubleshooting Wix Audio Player issues

Click an issue below to learn how to solve it.

### Music files aren't uploading

Make sure that the files you are uploading are supported by the Wix Audio Player. You can check which file types are supported in the FAQ section below.
If necessary, you can convert your file to a file type that is supported..

### External MP3 music is not working

If you are connecting music using the External MP3 option, make sure that the following requirements are met:
- The URL ends with a .mp3 extension.
- The URL is secure (starts with https://).
- The music is not restricted by the host and embedding is allowed.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### What file types does the Wix Audio Player support?

The Audio Player supports the following file types:
- **File type: **MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, WMA, AAC, AIF and AIFF.
- **File** **Size: **Up to 50MB
- **Number of Channels:** 1 (mono) or 2 (stereo)

**Tip:** If you want to upload additional audio formats, we recommend that you use [Wix Music](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-music).

### Can I change the track displayed on my player?

Yes. You can change the track on your player anytime by clicking **Change Track** and uploading a track from your computer or adding an MP3 URL.

### Can I connect a radio station to my player?

Yes. You can connect a radio station to your player by selecting External MP3 as the audio form and pasting the radio station's URL. Select **Play Once** to play as a continuous audio stream. 

**Note:** Make sure there are no restrictions on the URL on the host's side. Links ending in .mp3 work best. Alternatively, link to a radio station using an HTML element. Learn more about adding an HTML element in the [Wix Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget#adding-html-content) and the [Studio Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-an-html-iframe-element).

### Can I customize the Wix Audio Player on mobile?

Yes. In the Wix Editor, you can customize your audio player's layout or decide to hide it completely on mobile. In Wix Studio, any changes you make to your player are applied across all breakpoints.

### Can I add more than one player to my site?

Yes. You can add multiple audio players to your site. However, we recommend using [Wix Music](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-music) instead as having multiple audio players on your site, as this may cause issues on certain browsers and cause the tracks not to play.
