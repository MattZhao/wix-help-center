---
title: "ADI: Managing Your Store"
---

You can manage all aspects of your store from your site's dashboard, including adding your products setting up your payment methods and managing your customers' orders.

### To access your store in your site's dashboard:

Click **Manage Apps **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Store**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also access your Store Manager from any of the store sections and galleries on other pages of your site. Click the section and click **Manage**.
