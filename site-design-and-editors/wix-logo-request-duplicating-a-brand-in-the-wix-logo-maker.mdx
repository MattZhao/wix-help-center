title: "Wix Logo Request: Duplicating a Brand in the Wix Logo Maker"
---

We are excited to announce that you can duplicate a brand in the [Wix Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-accessing-the-wix-logo-maker-2576691). Once duplicated, you can go ahead and make any updates or design changes to the new brand.
