---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding Shadow to a Header"
---

Currently, it is not possible to apply a shadow effect to the header section.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can add a container and stretch it across the header section. Then, [add shadow](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-containers#designing-containers) to the container.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
