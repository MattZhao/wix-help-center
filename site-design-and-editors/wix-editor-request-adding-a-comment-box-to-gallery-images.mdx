---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Comment Box to Gallery Images"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a comment box to individual images inside a gallery.

As an alternative, we have several comment apps and elements available in the Wix App Market and in the Add panel, which can be added to pages on your site:

[Facebook comments box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-comments-box)   [Comments](https://www.wix.com/app-market/comments)   [Comments and reviews / testimonials](https://www.wix.com/app-market/comments-plus)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
