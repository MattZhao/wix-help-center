title: "ADI: Managing Your Loyalty Program"

> This feature is currently only available for Wix Restaurants﻿.

Manage your loyalty program from your site's dashboard, including how points are earned, rewards that can be redeemed, and more.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure you have [added and set up your Loyalty Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-a-loyalty-program-for-your-restaurant).

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Change your loyalty program details](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-managing-your-loyalty-program#changing-your-loyalty-program-details)  [Update how loyalty points can be earned](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-managing-your-loyalty-program#updating-how-customers-earn-loyalty-points)  [Update your rewards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-managing-your-loyalty-program#updating-your-loyalty-rewards)  [Manage reward notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-managing-your-loyalty-program#managing-reward-notifications)  [Change the currency of your loyalty program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-managing-your-loyalty-program#changing-the-currency-of-your-loyalty-program)  [Pause your loyalty program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-managing-your-loyalty-program#pausing-your-loyalty-program)  [Resume your loyalty program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-managing-your-loyalty-program#resuming-your-loyalty-program)

### Changing your loyalty program details

You can update your loyalty program name, the name for your points, your points icon, or the text and images that appear on your program's login page.

#### To update your loyalty program details:

Go to [Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Program**.  Click **Edit Program**.  Update your loyalty program details:

- **Program name:** Give your program a name that suits your brand.
- **Points name:** Choose to keep the default points name **Points **or select **Custom **to measure customer loyalty differently, e.g., “Stars” or “Hearts”. You can then choose a star or heart points icon to match.
- **Points icon:** Click the Update icon  to select a new icon for your points.
    Click **Save**.

### Updating how customers earn loyalty points

Loyalty points are awarded to customers who make purchases from your restaurant. You can change the way your customers earn points at any time from your site's dashboard.

#### To update how customers earn points:

Go to [Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Program**.  Click **Edit Action** next to the points you want to update.  Update how points are earned.  Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Add more ways for your customers to earn loyalty points with [Wix Automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation).

---

### Updating your loyalty rewards

Your loyalty rewards are awarded to customers using Wix Automations. You can update your loyalty rewards automations at any time.

#### To update your loyalty rewards:

Go to [Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Program**.  Click **Edit** next to the reward you want to update.  Update the reward.   Click **Save**.

### Managing reward notifications

An email is sent to customers when they have collected enough loyalty points to redeem a reward. This email is sent to both signed up participants, and customers who have not yet signed up to your loyalty program. When non-participants sign up, they can access their points balance and redeem rewards.

You can deactivate the automatic email notification in Wix Automations.

#### Deactivating the email notification:

Go to your [Automations dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Automations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/triggers/).  Scroll down to **My Active Automations**.  Click the toggle on the relevant automation to change it to **Inactive**.

### Changing the currency of your loyalty program

The currency of your loyalty program can be changed through the **Language & Region** settings in your site's dashboard.

#### To change your currency:

Go to [Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Language & Region**.  Scroll down to **Site currency**.  Click the **Currency** drop-down and select your currency.  Click **Save**.

### Pausing your loyalty program

To pause your loyalty program, disable all the ways members can earn loyalty points and redeem rewards. You can also hide the loyalty page on your site. You can resume your loyalty program whenever you're ready.

> **Important:**
>
> If you delete your site's Loyalty and Rewards pages, your loyalty program is deleted.

#### Step 1 | Disable ways to earns points and use rewards

Go to [Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Program**.  Disable the toggles under **Earn Points **to make them **Inactive**.   Disable the toggles under **Rewards **to make them **Inactive**.

#### Step 2 | Hide the loyalty page on your site

[﻿Go to your Editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the header of your site in ADI.  Click **Edit** next to **Site Menu**.   Hover over the Loyalty page and click the Show More** ** icon.  Click **Hide Item**.

Click **Publish **to make your changes go live.

> **Note:**
>
> You can still give points and edit the points balances of your customers while your loyalty program is paused.

---

### Resuming your loyalty program

To resume your paused loyalty program, activate a way to earn loyalty points and redeem loyalty rewards.

#### Step 1 | Activate ways to earns points and use rewards

Go to [Loyalty Program](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Loyalty%20Program&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/loyalty-accounts/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Program**.  Enable the toggles under **Earn Points **to make them **Active**.  Enable the toggles under **Rewards **to make them **Active**.

#### Step 2 | Show the loyalty page on your site

[﻿Go to your Editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the header of your site in ADI.  Click **Edit** next to **Site Menu**.   Hover over the Loyalty page and click the Show More** ** icon.  Click **Show**.

Click **Publish **to make your changes go live.
