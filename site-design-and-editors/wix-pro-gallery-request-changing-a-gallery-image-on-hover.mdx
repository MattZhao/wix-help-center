title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Changing a Gallery Image On Hover"
---

Currently, it is not possible to have a different image appear when hovering over a gallery item in the Wix Pro Gallery.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
