---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding an Age Disclaimer to Your Site"
---

There are two ways to add an age disclaimer to your site. You can either show it on a popup window, or create a landing page for it.

Both options have a confirmation button and no other navigational options. That way, in order for visitors to view the rest of your site, they are required to click the confirmation button.

**In this article, learn how to create:**

[Popup with an age disclaimer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-an-age-disclaimer-to-your-site#creating-an-age-disclaimer-using-a-popup)  [Landing page with an age disclaimer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-an-age-disclaimer-to-your-site#creating-a-landing-page-with-an-age-disclaimer)

---

### Creating an age disclaimer using a popup

[Add a lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-lightbox).  Click the lightbox.  Click the **Settings **icon .

1. Select **Yes** under **Automatically display popup on pages**.

1. Click the **Which pages is it on?** field and select **First page visitor sees**.
2. Drag the **Delay **slider to 0 seconds.
2. Disable the **X icon** toggle.
3. Enable the **Close button** toggle.
    Set up the button that leads to your site:

1. Click the Close button on your popup and click **Change Text**.
2. Enter the confirmation text (e.g. "Yes, I'm over 18").
    Customize your popup to create your age disclaimer.  Customize and [set up this popup for mobile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-showing-and-customizing-lightboxes-on-your-mobile-site).

**Note: **By default, popups won’t appear on your mobile site unless enabled in the Mobile Editor. This is because Google does not like intrusive popups on mobile sites so having a popup enabled may negatively impact your site’s SEO.

---

### Creating a landing page with an age disclaimer

Create a [landing page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-landing-page-eg-coming-soon-page).  Set your landing page as the homepage:

1. Click **Pages & Menu** ** **  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the landing page you created.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Page Info**.
5. Click **Set **next to** Make this your homepage?**.
    Add a [text element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text).  Edit the text to reflect the notification. For example: "Are you over 18?".  Add a button to lead to your site:

1. Add a [text button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-text-icon-buttons).
2. Click **Change Text** on the button.
3. Type the button text to reflect the user's confirmation. For example: "Yes, I am over 18".
4. Click the **Link** icon next to **Where does it link to?**.
5. Select **Page / Anchor**.
6. Select the page for users to be redirected to under **Which page?**.
7. Click **Done**.

> **Note:**
>
> To ensure that your visitors are taken to the landing page of your site only, [hide the other pages of your site from search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-preventing-search-engines-from-indexing-your-page).
