---
title: "ADI: Accessing and Customizing the Mobile View"
---

You can see what your ADI site looks like on mobile, and make changes to the design.

### Accessing the mobile view

1. Hover over the desktop icon on the top bar of ADI.
2. Click **Mobile**.

### Changing the design of page sections in the mobile view

1. Click the relevant page section.
2. Click **Designs**.
3. Select a new design.

**Note: **It is not possible to customize your font size from the mobile view. You can only adjust your text size from the Desktop view.

### Showing or hiding items in a page section

1. Click the relevant page section.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Click the Show/Hide toggle next to an item:
- : The item is displayed.
- : The item is not displayed.

> **Important:**
>
> - Any changes made in the mobile view do not affect the desktop view of your site.
> - This article refers to Wix ADI. Click [here](https://www.wix.com/support/html5/the-wix-editor) if you are looking for information regarding the Editor.
