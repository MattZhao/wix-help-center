---
title: "Wix Video: Selling Your Videos"
---

Generate income from your videos by having your viewers pay to view them. Set individual pricing for each video, and decide whether to allow video downloads.

> **Important:**
>
> Your site visitors need to become site members in order to purchase videos or subscriptions.

### To sell your videos:

[Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.  Select the checkbox next to the video(s) you want to sell.   Click **Set Pricing **at the top of your list of videos.

Click **Paid**.  Select the **Video can be purchased** checkbox.  Enter the purchase price of the video(s) in the **Price** field.  Click the **Download options** drop-down and choose whether to allow downloads:

- **Allow downloads**: Viewers can download the videos that they purchase.
- **No downloads**: Viewers can only watch the videos through your site or the Spaces by Wix app.
    Click **Save**.
