---
title: "Wix Editor: Getting More Shares for Your Site"
---

Connecting your Wix site with social media accounts is a great way to improve and streamline visitor engagement. Allow your visitors to share your site content by linking share options to social media platforms including Facebook, Twitter, etc.

Learn how to add share options with:

---

### Facebook

With Facebook, you have a range of options to promote your site. Your visitors can [share](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-share-button), [like ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-facebook-like-button)and [comment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-comments-box) on content from your site using their Facebook accounts.

Click an option below to see the relevant steps.

### Add a Facebook Share button

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Social**.
4. Click **Facebook**.
5. Click or drag the **Facebook Share** button to your site.
6. [Set up your Facebook Share button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-share-button) using the available options.

### Add a Facebook Like button

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Social**.
4. Click **Facebook**.
5. Click or drag the **Facebook Like** button to add it to your site.
6. [Set up your Facebook Like button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-facebook-like-button) using the available options.

### Add Facebook Comments

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Social**.
4. Click **Facebook**.
5. Click or drag the **Facebook Comments** element to add it to your site.
6. [Set up Facebook Comments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-comments-box) using the available options.

### Twitter

Get your site out there on Twitter on both desktop and mobile. Simply add and set up for visitors to share your site. You can even add a catchy tweet and change the language of the button.

### Show me how

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Social**.
4. Click **Twitter**.
5. Click or drag the **Tweet **button to add it to your site.
6. [Set up your button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-twitter-tweet-button) using the available options.

### Pinterest

Pinterest is great way for people to collect inspiration and ideas. With the **Pin to Pinterest** button, visitors can pin content from your site directly to their Pinterest accounts.

### Show me how

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Social**.
4. Click **Pinterest**.
5. Click or drag the **Pin to Pinterest** button to add it to your site.
6. [Set up your Pinterest button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-pinterest-on-your-wix-site#save-to-pinterest) using the available options.

### VK Share

With [VK Share](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-vk-share-button), your visitors can easily share your site with their contacts. Customize the button text to tailor it to your tone and voice.

### Show me how

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Social**.
4. Click **VK**.
5. Click or drag the **VK Share** button to add it to your site.
6. [Set up your button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-vk-share-button) using the options available.
