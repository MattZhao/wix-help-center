---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Resizing Your Gallery Text in the Mobile-Friendly View"
---

Currently, you cannot change the size of the text on galleries in the mobile-friendly view.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.

> **Note:**
>
> If your image description is too long, it may overlap with your image in expand mode. In this case, we recommend shortening the description or switching to the Wix Pro Gallery.
