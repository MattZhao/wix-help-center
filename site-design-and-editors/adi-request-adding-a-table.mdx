---
title: "ADI Request: Adding a Table"
---

Currently, you cannot add a table to your site inside Wix ADI.

If you want to add a table app, you need to [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-table-master-app).
