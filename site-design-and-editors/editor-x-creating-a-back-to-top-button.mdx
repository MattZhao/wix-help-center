---
title: "Editor X: Creating a \"Back to Top\" Button"
---

Let your visitors easily scroll to the top of the page with a "Back to Top" button. This is especially useful for one-page sites where the page is typically long.

### To create a "Back to Top" button:

Click **Add**  at the top left of your Editor.    Drag a button or any other linkable element (e.g., shape, vector art, image) to the desired location on your page.   Click the **Link** icon .  Select **Top / Bottom of Page** on the left.  Select **Top **and click **Done**.  (Optional) Pin your button to the screen so it's visible as visitors scroll down the page:

1. Click the Inspector icon  at the top of Editor X.
2. Click the **Position type** drop-down.
3. Click **Pinned**.
    (Optional) [Copy and paste](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-copy-pasting-elements-and-sections) the button to other pages on your site.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Add animation to your button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-animations-to-your-site) and adjust the delay to make it appear after a few seconds, as visitors scroll down.
