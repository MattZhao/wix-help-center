---
title: "ADI: Setting Up A Section Background"
---

Better define each area on your site and change the background of a section. Choose a color, image, video or a pattern.

### To change the background of a section:

Click the section you want to update inside ADI.Click **Edit**.Hover over the media thumbnail on the left and click **Edit & Replace**.Choose a media type and follow the relevant instructions:

### Image

1. Click **Replace**
2. Select an image or click **Upload Images** to add your own.
3. Click **Apply**. 
- Click the **Position **button to choose what part of the image you want to show.
- Drag the **Opacity **slider to change the image opacity.
- Select a color to show behind the image.
- Choose a scroll effect.

                       

**Note: **You can [change the height of the section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-height-of-a-section) to change the size of your image.

### Gallery

1. Click **Organize Media.**
2. Select an image.
3. Click **Done**.

### Video

1. Click **Replace**
2. Select a video or click **Upload Videos **to add your own.
3. Click **Apply**.

### Pattern

- Click **Replace** to choose another pattern.
- Click **Color** to select a new one.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The Media Settings panel may not show all the media types depending on the section you are trying to edit.
> - Some sections do not have the Media Settings panel described above. To change the background on these sections, toggle the media checkbox or click **Design.**
