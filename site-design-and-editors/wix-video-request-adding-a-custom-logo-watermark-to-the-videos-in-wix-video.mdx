title: "Wix Video Request: Adding a Custom Logo (watermark) to the Videos in Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a custom logo (watermark) to the videos you upload to Wix Video.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
