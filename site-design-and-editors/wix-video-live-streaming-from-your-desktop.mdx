---
title: "Wix Video: Live Streaming from Your Desktop"
---

With Wix Video, you can stay connected to your site visitors in real-time. Set up a live stream directly from your desktop and go live at any time. This is a great way to make new updates and provide new content on the go.

> **Before getting started:**
>
> - Make sure you add a Wix Video element on the page where you want the live stream to appear, and set it to display the channel the live stream will play on.
> - Make sure you have an encoder before going live. An encoder is the platform that connects your computer's camera to the Wix Video player.
> - In this article, we discuss live-streaming using the OBS encoder, but you can use any encoder that supports custom keys. Learn more about [downloading live stream encoders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-downloading-a-live-stream-encoder).

It's time to go live! Follow these steps to start streaming from your desktop.

---

### Step 1 | Setting up your Live Stream

1. Go to your [Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/video-library/videos) and click **Live Stream**.

---

### Step 2 | Open and connect your encoder

You can use any encoder as long as it supports custom keys. If you opt for a different encoder, the steps may be different than what is outlined below.

Open the OBS Studio Encoder on your computer.  Click **Settings **on the bottom right.  Click the **Stream** tab on the left.  Select **Custom Streaming Server **from the drop-down menu on top.  Paste each link (mentioned in step 6 of the set-up, above) in the appropriate field.  Click **OK**.** **A feed from your camera should appear on the encoder.  Click **Start Streaming**. Action! Your stream is live and online!  When you're done, click **Stop Streaming **on the encoder.

> **Notes:**
>
> - For security reasons, the stream key resets every hour.
> - Currently, Wix Video only offers support and troubleshooting for the OBS Studio encoder  - other encoders may not connect with Wix Video. If you want to use a different encoder, make sure you know where to enter the Server URL and the stream key.
> - Your computer's camera should connect automatically. Otherwise, complete these steps:
>
> 1. On the bottom left, under "Sources", click **Add **.
> 2. Select **Video Capture Device**.
> 3. Name the Capture Device and click **OK**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Generate discussion and engage your fans by [enabling comments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/allowing-comments-in-wix-video).
