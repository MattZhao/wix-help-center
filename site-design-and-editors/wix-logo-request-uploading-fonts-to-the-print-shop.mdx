---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Uploading Fonts to the Print Shop"
---

Currently, it is not possible to upload your own fonts to the Print Shop. You can only apply the available preset fonts to your merchandise.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
