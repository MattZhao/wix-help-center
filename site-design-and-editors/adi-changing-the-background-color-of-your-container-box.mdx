---
title: "ADI: Changing the Background Color of Your Container Box"
---

Change the background color of a container box to suit your site's style and design in just a couple of clicks.

### To change the color of your container box:

Click the section that contains the container box in the ADI Builder.  Hover over the box and click **Box**.  Select a color from the palette.

**Tip:** Click **Edit site colors **to change the available color palette. [Learn more about customizing site colors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors#:~:text=To%20change%20the%20main%20color,to%20choose%20a%20custom%20color.).
