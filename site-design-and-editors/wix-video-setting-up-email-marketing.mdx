---
title: "Wix Video: Setting Up Email Marketing"
---

You can share your videos, promote one of your channels or send an email campaign featuring a video using Wix Email Marketing.

### To set up your email marketing campaign:

[Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Promote** icon.  Click **Promote by Email**.   Start customizing your email campaign. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-customizing-your-email-campaign).   Click **Next** on the top right-hand side to add recipients.   Click **Next** to preview the newsletter and click **Publish** to send your campaign.
