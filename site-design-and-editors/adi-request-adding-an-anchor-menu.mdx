---
title: "ADI Request: Adding an Anchor Menu"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add an anchor menu inside ADI.

However, you can link to sections on your site menu by following the below steps:

Click the page header.  Click **Edit** next to **Site Menu** on the left of the builder.  Click **Add a Menu Item**.  Click **Page**.  Select the page and section from the dropdown menus.  Click **Done**.

> **Note:**
>
> The option to select a page section is not displayed if you have a one-page site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
