### Adding a Social Bar

Place your social bar in a location that visitors can easily notice. For example, add it to the master header or footer to ensure it's always visible.

#### To add a social bar:

Click **Add**  at the top left of the Editor.  Click **Menu**.  Click **Social Bar**.  Drag a social bar to the desired location on your site.

> **What's next?**
>
> The social bar features default icons that you can customize to your needs (see **Setting Social Links** below).

> **Tip:**
>
> Add a Fixed scroll effect to your social bar so it's always shown on screen. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-setting-an-elements-position-to-pinned-or-sticky#adding-a-fixed-scroll-effect)

### Setting Social Links

After adding a social bar to your site, choose the relevant icons to display based on your social media accounts. If needed, you can always change the links by following the instructions below.

#### To set the social bar links:

Select the social bar in your Editor.   Click **Set Social Links**.  Set up your links by doing one of the following:

### Change a social media link

1. Click the relevant social network icon.
2. Click the link field on the right.
3. Select **Web Address** from the list.
4. Enter the relevant URL (this should be your social URL, e.g. http://www.facebook.com/wix).
5. Select if you want the URL to open in new window or the current window.
6. Click **Done**.

### Replace an icon

You may want to replace your social media icons with designs that fit your style better. Select a different design from Wix's database or upload a design of your own.  

1. Click the relevant social network icon.
2. Click **Replace Icon** on the right.
3. Choose a new icon by doing one of the following:

- Select a different icon from the list under **Media from Wix**.
- Click** + Upload Media** to upload a custom design.
4. Click **Choose Image**.
5. Click **Done**.

### Add a new icon and link

1. Click** + Add Icons** at the top right.
2. Select an icon and click **Add to Gallery**.
3. Click **Add a link...** on the right side.
4. Select **Web Address** from the list.
5. Enter the relevant URL (this should be your social URL, e.g. http://www.facebook.com/wix).
6. Select if you want the URL to open in new window or the current window.
7. Click **Done**.

### Remove an icon

Hover over an icon and click the **Remove Image** icon  to delete it.

### Customizing the Layout of the Social Bar

Customize the layout and appearance of your social bar. You can change the size of your social media icons, adjust the spacing between them and display them horizontally or vertically.

#### To customize the layout:

Select the social bar in your Editor.   Click the **Layouts** icon .   Customize the layout by doing the following:

- Drag the slider under **Icon sizes (px) **to make the icons bigger or smaller.
- Drag the slider to under **Spacing (px) **to adjust the space between icons.
- Choose whether to display your social bar horizontally or vertically.

> **Need to flip or rotate the icons?**
>
> Flip or rotate the icons from the **Inspector** panel  by entering a different angle (e.g., 180) next to **Rotate**.
