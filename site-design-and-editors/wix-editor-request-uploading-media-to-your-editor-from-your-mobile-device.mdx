---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Uploading Media to Your Editor from Your Mobile Device"
---

While it is possible to upload images to your Editor directly from your mobile device, currently, uploading other types of media (music, videos, etc.) directly from your mobile device is not supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
