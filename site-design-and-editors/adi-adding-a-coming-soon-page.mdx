---
title: "ADI: Adding a Coming Soon Page"
---

A "coming soon" page is a temporary home page that lets visitors know that your site is under construction, and will be available sometime in the future.

The page doesn't have a header, so visitors are unable to navigate to the rest of your site while you build it. Add the text "coming soon" to your page and any other information that you feel is relevant, e.g., a planned opening date, to give your visitors a sneak peek at what's to come.

### To add a coming soon page:

Click the header.   Click the **Show / Hide** icon  next to **Site Menu**.

Hover ﻿over **Add** at the top of ADI.  Click **Section to Page**.  Click **Text **on the left and select a text section to add to your site.  Click the **Title / Subtitle **fields on the left and type your message.

**Note:** (If necessary) Click the **Show / Hide** icon  next to the relevant text to make it visible.

> **Tip:**
>
> Give your section a whole new look and feel by [customizing your section design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-section-design).
