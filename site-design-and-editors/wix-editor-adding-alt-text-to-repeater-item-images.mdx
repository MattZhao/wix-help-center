---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding Alt Text to Repeater Item Images"
---

Add Alt Text to your repeater item images to help search engines "read" them. In repeater items, the Alt Text is the image description.

### To add Alt Text to images:

Click the repeater in the Editor.Click the image.Click the **Settings **icon. Under **What's in the image? Tell Google**, enter a short description of the image.
