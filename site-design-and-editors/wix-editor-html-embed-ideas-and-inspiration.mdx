### Add an external marketing platform

Embed an external email marketing platform such as MailChimp and AWeber to increase engagement through your site. External marketing platforms are a great way to manage communication with customers, and encourage site visitors to subscribe to your email campaigns.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also [move your current email marketing platform to Wix email marketing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/moving-from-another-email-marketing-platform-to-wix-email-marketing).

### Add Amazon Affiliate links

[Create an Amazon Affiliate link](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/home) and add it to your site to earn extra income and boost your seller reputation. Diversify your income streams, earn commission and strengthen the relationship between you and your audience.

> **Good to know:**
>
> When you click on the link in your live site, the URL should display your personal affiliate code after the words "tag=".

> **Important:**
>
> Browser extensions for blocking ads can prevent Amazon Affiliate links from being displaced on your live site. If you are unable to see affiliate links on your live site, make sure you have disabled add-blocking extensions in your browser.

### FAQs

Select a question to learn more about embeds on your site.

### Are there any guidelines or limitations for using embeds on my site?

Yes. For example, any code you add must contain HTTPS and not HTTP in order to be displayed. We recommend reading our [full list of guidelines for using the HTML element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget#guidelines-for-adding-embeds-to-your-site) to make sure you're getting the smoothest experience.

### Where can I embed HTML on my site?

As the code is contained within an iFrame, it is not possible to place the embed element on the header or footer, but you can embed anywhere within the page body.

### What hyper text protocol can I use in my code?

Your code must contain HTTPS, not HTTP, or it will not be displayed on your live site.

### Can I add embeds to my mobile site?

Embeds that are added to your desktop site are automatically carried over to your mobile site, however they may be displayed differently. As an alternative, you can add [mobile-only embeds](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-embeds-and-custom-elements-to-your-mobile-site).
