---
title: "Request: Editing Thank You Page Text"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, it's possible to edit most of the text that appears on the Thank You Page. However, some text, such as the words "subtotal", "delivery", or "note", cannot currently be changed.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
