---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Customizing Local URLs"
---

Each page on your site has a unique URL (web address) that you can [customize](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-page-url). Currently, it is not possible to customize the page URL for each language version. For example, if your site has a page with the following URL: [https://www.example-wix-site.com/**shop**](https://www.example-wix-site.com/shop), you cannot localize the word "shop" after the second slash.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
