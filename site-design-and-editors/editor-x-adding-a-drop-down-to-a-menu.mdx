---
title: "Editor X: Adding a Drop-Down to a Menu"
---

Make your menu look more tidy and organized using drop-down submenus. You can make any menu item the title of your submenu, or create a submenu with a non-clickable title. When visitors hover over the title, the submenu opens in a drop-down.

### To create a drop-down submenu:

Click your menu in Editor X.  Click **Manage Menu**.  Depending on whether the submenu title should be clickable or not, follow the steps:

### Submenu with a non-clickable title

Add a Submenu Title to your menu to create a new drop-down. This title is not clickable, and you can choose what to call it. After entering your title, you can start moving items below the submenu. 

1. Click **+ Add New Item** at the bottom.
2. Select **Submenu Title**.
3. Give your submenu a name and press **Enter** on your keyboard.
4. Move items to the submenu:

1. Click the Reorder icon  next to the relevant menu item, and drag it below the submenu title.
2. Hover over that menu item and click the More Actions icon .
3. Select **Move to Submenu**.
4. Repeat steps a - c for all menu items you want to move to the submenu.

### Submenu with a menu item as the title

Any menu item (page, link) can serve as the title of your submenu. Start by moving relevant menu items so they appear below the item that should be the title. Then, you'll see the option of moving them into a submenu.

1. Locate the menu item that should be the submenu title.
2. Create the submenu:

1. Click the Reorder icon  next to a menu item, and drag it below the item that is your submenu title.
2. Hover over the menu item you just moved and click the More Actions icon .
3. Click **Move to Submenu**.
4. Repeat steps a - c for all menu items you want to move to the submenu.

> **Moving a subitem to the main menu:**
>
> Hover over the subitem and click the More Actions icon . Then, click **Move out of Submenu **so it appears in the main menu.
