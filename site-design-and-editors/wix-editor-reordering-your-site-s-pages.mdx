You can change the order of your site's pages at any time. The order you choose for your pages also determines their order on your site's menu.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Reorder your site's pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-reordering-your-sites-pages#reordering-your-pages)  [View the new page order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-reordering-your-sites-pages#viewing-the-new-page-order)

---

### Reordering your pages

In the Menus and Pages panel, re-arrange the order of your pages using drag-and-drop. You can change the order at any point, and it updates your site's menu automatically.

#### To reorder your site pages:

Click Menus & Pages** **** **on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Site Menu**.  Click and drag the page up or down the list.

---

### Viewing the new page order

When you change the order of your site's pages, the order also changes on your site's menu. This order is visible both in the Wix Editor, and on your live site to visitors.
