---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Creating Tasks from Site Comments"
---

When collaborating on a site, you can leave [comments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-collaborating-on-a-site#use-comments-to-stay-in-sync) for your team to see and resolve. Currently, it is not possible to generate a [task](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-tasks-reminders) for yourself (or your site / account team) directly from a comment.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
