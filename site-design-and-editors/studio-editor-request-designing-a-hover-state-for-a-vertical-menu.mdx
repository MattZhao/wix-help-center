---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Designing a Hover State for a Vertical Menu"
---

You can customize the design of [vertical menus](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-designing-menus#designing-vertical-menus) for 2 different states: Regular and Selected (when a visitor selects a menu item). Currently, it is not possible to design a Hover state for vertical menus.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
