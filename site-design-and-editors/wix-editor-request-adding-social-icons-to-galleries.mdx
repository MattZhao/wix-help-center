---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding Social Icons to Galleries"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add social icons to galleries.

As an alternative, you can add a [social bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-social-bar) to the page containing your gallery.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
