---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Making the Mobile Breakpoint Smaller than 320px"
---

In the Studio Editor, the smallest screen you can design a site for is 320 pixels wide. Currently, it is not possible to set the mobile breakpoint range to go smaller than that.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
