---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding Tooltips to a Text Box"
---

Currently, you can only add a tooltip (text that appears when hovering over an item) to elements such as [images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-alt-text-tooltip-to-your-image). You cannot add a tooltip to a text box.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
