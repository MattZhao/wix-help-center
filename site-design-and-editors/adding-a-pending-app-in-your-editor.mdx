title: "Adding a Pending App in Your Editor"
---

You can add apps to your site from the Wix App Market in your editor, your site's dashboard, or the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).

Some apps that you add in your dashboard or the Wix app also need to be added in your site's editor. You'll see a notification in your site's editor if you have a pending app that you need to add.

#### To manage pending apps in your editor:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Apps**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Manage Apps **at the bottom left.
