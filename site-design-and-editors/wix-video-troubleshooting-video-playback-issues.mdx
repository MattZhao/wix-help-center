title: "Wix Video: Troubleshooting Video Playback Issues"
---

If you are experiencing issues with Wix Video, see our solutions below.

> **Important:**
>
> When visitors play a video on your site, the quality may be lower for the first 5-10 seconds, in order to determine whether their internet connection is fast enough to display higher quality videos.

Click an issue below to find out how to resolve it:

### Video pausing / skipping during playback (site visitors only)

If your site visitors are unable to play your video, you can send them the troubleshooting steps outlined in the [Troubleshooting Site Visitors Issues guide](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-your-site-visitors-are-having-issues-with-wix-video).

### Video pausing / skipping during playback (both you and your visitors)

Follow troubleshooting steps in the [Wix Technical Assistant guide](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-technical-assistant).

### Issues still persist after following all the troubleshooting steps

Delete and re-upload the video to your video library. Learn more about [deleting](https://support.wix.com/en/article/deleting-a-video-from-your-wix-video-library) and [uploading](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-adding-videos-to-your-video-library) videos to your video library. 
If the video is from an external platform (e.g. YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook), disable all privacy settings before trying to embed. Learn more about [troubleshooting issues with external videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-issues-with-external-videos-in-wix-video).
