---
title: "Accessibility: Using External Checking Software or Tools"
---

Here at Wix, we are constantly working to improve the accessibility of our sites. We aim to provide you with the tools you need to ensure your site is accessible, and have created a [guideline that lists our recommendations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-checklist-for-improving-your-sites-accessibility).

You can use external tools to review your site's accessibility and get suggestions for improvement. However, each [country and region has different laws](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-compliance-with-your-regional-laws) - so we recommend having your site reviewed by an Accessibility Advisor to make sure it complies with the requirements for your region.
