---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Increasing the Streaming Quality of Wix Music to 320kbps"
---

Currently, Wix Music cannot stream at 320kbps. In order to optimize streaming, your music is presented in 128kbps stereo mp3.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
