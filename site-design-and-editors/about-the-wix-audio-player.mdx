---
title: "About the Wix Audio Player"
---

The Wix Audio Player is a stylish and simple music element that allows you to add a single track to a page on your website.

---

### Adding the Wix Audio Player

Add the Wix Audio Player from the Add panel in your Editor.

### Show me how

1. Click **Add ** on the left side of your Editor.
2. Click **Video &** **Music**.
3. Click **Audio Players**.
4. Click one of the players at the top of the panel to add it, or alternatively drag it to the relevant location.

---

### Adding a Track to Your Player

You can either upload a track to your player, or enter the URL of an MP3 track.

### Show me how

1. Click your Wix Audio Player in your Editor.
2. Click **Change Track**.
3. Under **Choose audio from:** select an option:

- **My Uploads:** Click **Upload Track** and select a track from your Media Manager or upload a new one from your computer.
- **External MP3: **Enter the URL of a track.

---

### Setting up Your Player

From Settings you can choose how your player behaves. Select a cover image, add the track details and define the playback setting.

### Show me how

1. Click your Wix Audio Player in your Editor.
2. Click the **Settings** icon  .
3. Set up the following:

- **Cover Image: **Choose a cover image from your Media Manager.
- **Track Details:** Enter the artist and track name.
- **Playback Options:** Choose if you want the track to play just once, or continuously in a loop. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-audio-player-setting-up-the-wix-audio-player).

---

### Designing Your Player

Customize every aspect of the player, including the colors, border and fonts. You can customize the regular view and the hover view of the player.

### Show me how

1. Click your Wix Audio Player in your Editor.
2. Click the **Design **icon .
3. Customize your player:

- Click the **Regular** tab and customize the regular view of the player.
- Click the **Hover** tab and customize the hover view of the player.

---

### Customizing Your Player on Your Mobile Site

Customize how your player looks on your mobile site. You can change the layout and size of the player, or hide it completely.

### Show me how

1. [Go to the mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-accessing-the-mobile-editor).
2. Click your Wix Audio Player.
3. Customize your player:

- Resize it using the drag handles.
- Click the **Layouts** icon   to change the layout.
- Click the **Hide **icon to hide the player on your mobile site.
