Easily find what you are looking for in the Media Manager using the search bar or search through all the files in your Wix account. These search functions allow you to quickly locate images, videos, and other files, saving you time and helping you stay organized.

### Searching for a file in your Media Manager

Your Media Manager contains files for that particular site you are working on. When searching for a file, it'll pull up results from your Site Files and Trash. It will also offer images from [Media from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-adding-free-media-from-wix), [Shutterstock](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-purchasing-shutterstock-images-videos), and [Unsplash](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-adding-unsplash-images).

#### To search for a file in your Media Manager:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.
4. Enter a search term in the search box at the top.
5. Press **Enter** on your keyboard.

1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media **[image: undefined] on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Site Files **on the left.
4. Enter a search term in the search box at the top.
5. Press **Enter** on your keyboard.

> **Narrow down your search results:**
>
> Learn how to [filter and reorder your search results](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-organizing-the-files-in-your-media-manager#filtering-files-by-file-type-in-the-media-manager).

### Searching for a file across all the sites in your Wix account

If you can't find a file in your current site's Media Manager, it may be located on another site that you own. You can search for files across all of your sites and upload it to the current site you are working on.

#### To search for a file across all of the sites in your Wix account:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.
4. Click **Upload Media** at the top left.
5. Click the **Wix** icon on the left.
6. Choose a folder to view the media.

**Tip:** Click the **Search** icon  to find a file.
7. (Optional) Upload the file to this site:

1. Select the relevant media.
2. Click **Upload Selection**.
[image: A screenshot of selecting images from another site in the My Wix Account tab.]

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about searching for files.

### Can I change the names of the folders I see in the My Wix Account tab?

The names of the folders you see in the **My Wix Account** tab of the Media Manager come from the name of your site itself. To change the title of a folder, you must [change the name of your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/renaming-your-site).

### Where can I find files uploaded by site visitors and site members?

You can find files uploaded by site visitors and site members in the **Visitor Uploads** tab in your Media Manager and in the [Manage Storage page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-managing-your-storage) in your site's dashboard.
