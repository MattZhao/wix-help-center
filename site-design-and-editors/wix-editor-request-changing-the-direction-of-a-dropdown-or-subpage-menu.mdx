---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Changing the Direction of a Dropdown or Subpage Menu"
---

If you place a [menu folder](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-and-managing-a-dropdown-submenu)/ [subpages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-subpage-drop-down-menu) menu above the middle line of the page, it will open downward, whereas if you place it below the middle line, it will open upward.

Currently, there is no way to control whether your drop-down menu opens upward or downward, other than re-positioning it on your page (i.e. above the middle line or below it).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
