---
title: "Wix Music: Promoting Your Music using a Subscribe Form"
---

Add a subscribe form to your site so that your fans can subscribe to your music newsletters!

Once they are subscribed, you can send them regular [newsletters](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-promoting-your-music-with-email-campaigns) to keep them up to date with your news.

Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-subscribe-form) to learn how to add a subscribe form to your site.

> **Tip:**
>
> Your subscribers are automatically added to your [Contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-contact-list).
