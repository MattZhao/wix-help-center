---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Fade Out Text"
---

Currently, Wix does not support the fade out feature on text elements.

As an alternative, you can create a similar effect by splitting your text, and [applying animation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-an-animation) to each element at different time intervals.

#### To add animation to your text:

Select a text element in your Editor.  Click the Animation** **icon .  Select an effect, and click **Customize** to adjust the time settings (e.g. duration and delay).  Repeat steps 1-3 for each text field you want to animate.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
