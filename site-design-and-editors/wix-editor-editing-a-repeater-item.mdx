---
title: "Wix Editor: Editing a Repeater Item"
---

Edit any of your repeater items at any time. Change the image or update the text.

### To edit text:

1. Click the text box you want to edit.
2. Click **Edit Text**.

### To edit a button text:

1. Click the button you want to edit.
2. Click **Change Text & Icon**.
3. Edit the text in the **What does it say?** field.

### To replace an image:

1. Click the image you want to replace.
2. Click **Change Image.**
3. Select an image or click **+ Add Images **to add your own.

> **Important:**
>
> All of the list items in your repeater have the same design and layout. This means that every design change you make to a list item affects the other list items in your repeater. (e.g. Adding a button to one list item adds a button to all of the list items in your repeater.)
