---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Horizontal Scrolling on Your Live Site"
---

Currently, it is not possible to enable horizontal scrolling on your live site for different screen sizes (such as iPads and tablets).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
