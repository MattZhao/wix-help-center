---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Reordering Albums to Display in Alphabetical Order"
---

Currently, it is not possible to reorder albums to display in alphabetical order (automatically or manually), so that your albums display from A-Z according to their titles.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
