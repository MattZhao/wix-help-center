---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Locking Layers in the Editor"
---

Currently, it is not possible to lock layers in the Editor to prevent accidentally moving elements while editing your site.

However, if you want to certain elements to always be visible on your live site, you can [pin them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-pinning-elements-to-the-screen). Visitors can always see pinned elements on their screen, even if they scroll.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
