---
title: "Wix Media: Importing Media Files from Social Networks"
---

Display your favorite files on your site by connecting your social network accounts to your Wix account. Then, import your files to your Media Manager.

Connecting a social network account to your Wix site does not affect its settings, and you only have to set up the connection once.

### What social networks are supported?

- Facebook
- Google Drive
- Dropbox
- Instagram
- Google Photos
- Flickr
- DeviantArt
- Dribbble

### Importing media from a social network

[Go to your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager).Click **+ Upload Media**.Select the social network on the left menu. Click the connect button.Select the files you want to upload.Click **Upload Selection**.

---

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about importing files from your social networks:

### Can I import Instagram Live videos?

No. Currently, you can't import Instagram Live videos.

### Can I connect my Media Manager to a Business Facebook account?

No. Currently, it is not possible to connect a Business Facebook account when uploading images via Facebook in the Media Manager. You can only connect to a Personal Facebook account.

### Can I import files from a shared drive in Google?

No. You can only import files located in My Drive in Google.

### When importing files from Google Drive, what share settings should the file have?

When importing a file from Google Drive, make sure that the share settings of the file are set to "Anyone"** **and not "Restricted".

### Can I import posts with multiple images from Instagram?

Yes. When importing files from Instagram, the images from a single post with multiple images are unpacked from the post. Select each image from the post to upload them to your Media Manager. All images from this post will have the same title, which you can change at any time. 
**Tip:** You can hold down the **Ctrl** or **Shift **key on your keyboard (**Cmd **key for Mac) and multi-select images to upload in bulk.
