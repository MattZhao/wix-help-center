---
title: "Wix Logo: Extracting Your Logo Files from the .zip Folder"
---

Once you download your logo, the files are delivered in a .zip folder. Follow the relevant instructions below to open the folder and retrieve your files.

### Windows: Extract All Wizard and File Explorer

1. Right-click the compressed (zipped) folder.
2. Select **Extract All** from the context menu.
3. By default, the compressed files will extract in the same location as the zipped folder, but you can click the **Browse** button to select an alternative location.
4. Check the **Show extracted files when complete** option.
5. Click **Extract**.
Alternatively, inside the zipped folder, you can select the items you want, and on the **Extract** tab, select the location to extract the files. If the location isn't listed, click the **More** button, and then click the **Choose location** button to find the correct folder.

### Mac OS

1. Locate the zip archive file in the Finder window.
2. Double-click the .zip archive file to begin extracting the zip archive
3. When finished, the unzipped contents will appear in the same folder as the original .zip archive

### Android

You can extract ZIP files on your Android device with the help of a file management app such as Files by Google. An existing file management app may be built into your Android device, or you can download your app of choice.

### iOS

Learn how to open ZIP files on your iPhone, iPad or iPod touch [here](https://support.apple.com/en-il/HT211132).
