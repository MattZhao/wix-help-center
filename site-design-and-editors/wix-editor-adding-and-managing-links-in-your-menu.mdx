### Adding a link to your menu

Get started by adding a link as a menu item. Links look like regular menu items, and you can name them whatever you want.

#### To add a link to your menu:

Click **Pages and Menu**  on the left side of the Editor.  Click the Add a menu item icon .  Select the link type you want from the left of the panel.  Enter the relevant details.  Click **Done**.  Enter the item's name into the field.  Click **Done**.

---

### Managing your menu links

Keep your menu up to date by managing your menu items as you go.

#### To manage your menu links:

Click **Pages and Menu**  on the left side of the Editor.  Hover over the relevant menu item and click the More Actions icon .  Choose what you want to do with the item:

- **Change link:** Change to another link type or change the link details.
- **Rename: **Change the name of the item on your site menu.
- **Duplicate:** Make an exact copy of the item in your menu.
- **Hide / Show:** Hide the item from your menu or show it.
- **Subpage:** Turn the link into a subpage under a parent menu item.
- **Delete a menu link: **Remove the link from your site menu.

### What kind of menu links can I add?

You can add the following links as menu items:

- A page on your site
- A web address (such as a social media page)
- A section or anchor
- The top / bottom of your page
- A document
- An email address
- A phone number
- A lightbox

### How do I view my menu link?

You can view your menu links with the relevant icons, as the icon changes next to the menu item name:
- Anchor
- Section
- Web Address
- Email
- Phone Number
- Document
- Top/ Bottom of Page
- Lightbox
