---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Displaying Page Numbers on Galleries"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display page numbers on an image gallery.

As an alternative, you can add navigation arrows which your visitors can use to click through your gallery images. [Click here to learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adjusting-your-gallery-settings#navigating-through-gallery-images)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
