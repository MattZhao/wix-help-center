---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Disabling the Image Optimization Done by Wix"
---

Wix automatically optimizes your images for both site loading time and image quality. Currently, it is not possible to disable this optimization.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes) for information about the recommended file types and sizes.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
