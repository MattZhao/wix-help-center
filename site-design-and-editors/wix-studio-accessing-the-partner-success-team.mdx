Work with our Partner Success Team to get hands-on guidance in growing and optimizing your business. Partner Success Managers are available to select partners in the [Wix Partner Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-joining-the-wix-partner-program). While reaching the Icon level is required, we also consider various factors such as business activity and overall engagement with the Wix Partner Program.

> **Note:**
>
> If you are eligible for a Partner Success Manager, one will be assigned to you and will reach out to get things started. Once they do, you’ll see their contact information in the Help widget in your Wix Studio workspace. If you don't have an assigned Success Manager yet, please wait for them to reach out to you.
