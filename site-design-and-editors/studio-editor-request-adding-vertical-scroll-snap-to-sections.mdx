---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding Vertical Scroll Snap to Sections"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add vertical scroll snap to page sections in the Studio Editor. This is a type of scroll that immediately snaps to your chosen element once a visitor scrolls through the [parent element or container](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-working-with-layers#the-structure-of-the-layers-panel).

> **Using Velo by Wix?**
>
> You can create the scroll snap effect for your sections using code.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
