---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Showing an Element on Some Pages"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now use Velo by Wix to show an element on some pages of your site and not others.

First, [enable Velo by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/enabling-velo) in your Editor. Then, follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-tutorial-hiding-an-element-on-certain-pages) to learn how to hide an element from specific pages.

Please refer to the dedicated [Velo Forum](https://www.wix.com/code/home/forum) for all Velo by Wix related support.
