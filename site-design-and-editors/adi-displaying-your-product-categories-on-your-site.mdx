---
title: "ADI: Displaying Your Product Categories on Your Site"
---

Add a product gallery anywhere on your site and choose which product category you want to display in it.

### To add a product category to a page on your site:

Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Section to Page**.  Click Shop.  Add a gallery to your site.  Select a category under **Display a category**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn how to [customize your Wix Stores gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-wix-stores-gallery).
