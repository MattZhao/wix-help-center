---
title: "Wix Editor: Creating Hover Box Effects"
---

Hover boxes are versatile tools that encourage interactivity on your site. After adding a hover box, you can start adding cool and fun effects to bring it to life. There are several options available, such as grow, skew, and even combo, which allows you to combine multiple effects.

### To add effects to your hover box:

Select your box in the Editor.  Click the **Hover** tab to open hover mode.  Click **Choose Effect**.  Select an effect from the list.  (Optional) Click **Customize** to adjust your chosen effect's settings. To learn more about each effect and its customization options, click below:

### Grow

The grow effect shows your box growing to size as visitors hover over it. You can adjust the starting point that it grows from, as well as the size that it expands to.

- Use the slider to adjust how much your element should grow on hover.
- Choose a starting point from which your element should start growing. By default, it starts growing from the center of the element but you can select a different point (e.g., top left).

### Shrink

The shrink effect shows your box shrinking as visitors hover over it. You can adjust the starting point that it shrinks from, as well as the size that it decreases to.

- Use the slider to adjust how much your element should shrink on hover.
- Choose a point towards which your element should shrink. By default, it shrinks towards the center of the element but you can select a different point (e.g., top right).

### Skew

The skew effect shows your box at an angle when visitors hover over it. It's a great way to play around with the angles of your box, and display your elements in a fun way.

- Choose a skew direction: horizontal or vertical.
- Drag the slider to determine the angle in which your element should skew when visitors hover over it.
- Choose a point from which your element should start skewing. By default, it starts skewing from the center of the element but you can select a different point (e.g., top left).

### Rotate

The rotate effect shows your box spinning when visitors hover over it. It can rotate as far as you want, and you can also choose which point the box should rotate around.

- Choose a direction for your element's rotation: clockwise or counterclockwise.
- Drag the slider to determine the angle by which your element should rotate when visitors hover over it.
- Choose a point around which your element should rotate. By default, the element rotates around the center but you can select a different point (e.g., top right).

### Combo (combined effects)

The combo effect is where you can really make your box your own. Combine effects and customize the settings to create something that suits your site.

- **Grow / Shrink:**

- Use the toggle to enable or disable whether the box keeps its proportions.
- Use the sliders to adjust scale's width and height.
- **Rotate and Skew:**

- Use the slider to adjust the angle of the rotation.
- Use the sliders to adjust the degrees of the horizontal and vertical skew.
- Select a starting point for the skew effect.
