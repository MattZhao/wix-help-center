---
title: "Wix Video Request: Creating Folders in Your Video Library"
---

Currently, you can't create folders to organize your videos in your Wix Video Library. You can, however, [create Channels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-channel-in-wix-video) and group your videos into different categories.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
