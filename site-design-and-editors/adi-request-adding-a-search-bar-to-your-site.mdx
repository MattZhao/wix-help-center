---
title: "ADI Request: Adding a Search Bar to Your Site"
---

Currently, you cannot add a search bar to your site inside ADI. If you want add a search bar to your site you need to [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-site-search).

> **Warning:**
>
> If you decide you want to go back to ADI after moving to the Editor, follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor). If you choose to return to ADI, you will not see any of the changes made in the Wix Editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
