---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Accordion Containers"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add accordion containers to your site in the Editor.

Accordions are elements that contain multiple collapsible containers. These containers have their own title and content, and expand when you click them.

> **Want a workaround?**
>
> If you've enbled [Velo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-velo-by-wix) on your site, you can collapse elements by adding code. [Learn how to collapse elements with Velo by Wix](https://www.wix.com/velo/example/collapse-elements)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
