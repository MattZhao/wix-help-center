---
title: "Wix Photo Albums: Selecting a Payment Method in Wix Photo Albums Store"
---

> **Important:**
>
> Wix Photo Albums Store is not available to all users. As of February 2023, it is no longer possible to add Wix Photo Albums Store to your site. As an alternative, you can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your photos.

Selecting a payment method is based on what type of files you are selling in your store.

If you sell digital images and prints, payment is always through PayPal. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider) to learn more.

If you sell digital files only, the payment method is done during your store set up. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview) to learn more.

> **Important:**
>
> Manual payments are not supported when selling digital files only.

> **Tip:**
>
> Wix offers a number of payment providers, which differ by country. For a list of available payment gateways and setup instructions, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-payment-provider-for-accepting-credit-card-payments).
