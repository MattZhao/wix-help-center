> **Important:**
>
> Wix Photo Albums Store is not available to all users. As of February 2023, it is no longer possible to add Wix Photo Albums Store to your site. As an alternative, you can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your photos.

Your Wix Photo Albums Store is up and running, so you're on your way to monetizing your content. If you'd like to try the products on your own, you can easily place a test order. As a Wix Photo Albums Store merchant, you'll receive a one-time 20% discount.

### To place a 20% discounted test order:

Make sure you have [added](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-adding-photo-albums-to-your-site) and [set up](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-setting-up-your-photo-albums-store) your Wix Photo Albums Store.  [Sign in](https://support.wix.com/en/article/logging-in-to-your-wix-account) and go to your published site.  Make a purchase on your Wix Photo Albums Store the same way a regular customer would.
