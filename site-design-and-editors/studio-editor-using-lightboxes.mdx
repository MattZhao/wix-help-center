title: "Studio Editor: Using Lightboxes"
---

A lightbox is a pop-up that you can customize to your needs. Design the pop-up window and add the elements you want, then choose when it appears to site visitors.

You can also link elements, like text, buttons or images, so clicking them opens the lightbox. This is a great way to increase the exposure for time sensitive information, sales or promotions.

[Add a lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-lightboxes#adding-a-lightbox)  [Customize a lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-lightboxes#customizing-a-lightbox)  [Set triggers for a lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-lightboxes#setting-triggers-for-a-lightbox)  [Scheduling a lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-lightboxes#setting-a-schedule-for-a-lightbox)  [Link elements to a lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-lightboxes#linking-elements-to-a-lightbox)  [Manage a lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-lightboxes#managing-a-lightbox)

### Adding a lightbox

Add a lightbox pop-up to your site so you can start setting up exciting announcements for visitors to see.

#### To add a lightbox:

Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Layout Tools**.  Click **Lightboxes**.  Select the lightbox you want to add.

---

### Customizing a lightbox

Customize the lightbox to match the rest of the site. Add any elements you need, then change the background color and decide on its opacity.

#### To customize a lightbox:

Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.  Select the relevant lightbox under **Lightboxes**.  Click the lightbox on the canvas.  Choose how to customize the lightbox:

### Add elements

1. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Drag and drop the relevant element.

**Note: **The following elements can't be added to a lightbox:
- Any section from the **Sections** or **Quick Add** tabs.
- Pre-designed sections you've saved in [**My Designs**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-saving-and-reusing-design-assets).
- Section grids
- Slideshow repeaters
- Wireframes

### Design the background

1. Click the **Open Inspector** icon  at the top right of the editor.
2. Scroll down to **Design**.
3. Choose a color and set the background opacity under **Background**.

### Add the option to scroll inside the lightbox

1. Click the **Open Inspector** icon  at the top right of the editor.
2. Scroll down to **Layout**.
3. Select **Scroll** under **Overflow content**.
4. Choose the **Scroll direction**: **Vertical**, **Horizontal **or **Both**.

### Set up the overlay around the lightbox

By default, a black overlay appears around the lightbox to help visitors focus on the content in the pop-up. Do any of the following to customize the overlay:
- Click** Set Overlay** to enable or disable the option to close the lightbox by clicking anywhere on the overlay.
- Click anywhere on the overlay and open the Inspector panel to set the color and opacity.

**Tip: **To remove the overlay altogether, set the opacity to 0%.

5.  When ready, click **Done** at the bottom to exit Editing mode.

> **What's next?**
>
> Preview the site to see the lightbox in action.

### Setting triggers for a lightbox

Choose how and when the lightbox appears. You can set it to appear automatically on specific site pages or link it to an element (e.g. text, image). You can also decide how visitors close the lightbox after viewing it.

> **Note:**
>
> The lightbox is displayed once per visitor, on the first relevant page they see (in case it's set to appear on multiple pages).

#### To set up triggers:

Click **Pages**  on the left side of the Editor.  Select the relevant lightbox under **Lightboxes**.   Click the lightbox on the canvas.  Click **Set Triggers**.  Select the relevant option under **Automatically display lightbox on Pages**:

- **Yes: **The lightbox automatically appears when visitors enter the relevant pages.

1. Click under **Which pages is it on?** to select the relevant page(s).
2. Drag the **Delay** slider to set how long (in seconds) it takes the lightbox to appear once the page loads.
- **No: **The lightbox does not appear when visitors enter a page. Instead, it's triggered by a button or a link you choose.
    Enable the relevant toggle(s) under **How does the lightbox close?**.

**Note: **If you disable both the 'X' icon and 'Close' button, visitors can click outside of the lightbox to close it.  When ready, click **Done** to exit Editing mode.

### Setting a schedule for a lightbox

Choose when site visitors see the lightbox. If you're promoting a limited-time offer, for example, this ensures the lightbox only appears in the relevant time frame.

#### To set the lightbox schedule:

Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.  Select the relevant lightbox under **Lightboxes**.   Click the lightbox on the canvas.  Click **Set Triggers**.  Scroll down to **Lightbox scheduling **and click **+ Set schedule**.  Set the start date and time and the end date and time.

**Tip:** Make sure the correct time zone is set. If not, you can change it.

---

### Linking elements to a lightbox

Link elements on the site to open a lightbox, so visitors never miss an announcement. You can link elements like text, images, videos and buttons.

#### To link an element to a lightbox:

Click the relevant element in your editor.  Click the **Link **icon .  Select **Lightbox **on the left.  Click the **Which Lightbox?** drop-down and select the relevant lightbox.  Click **Done**.

---

### Managing a lightbox

Manage the site's lightboxes to keep them organized in the Editor. You can rename them so they are easy to find, duplicate them to save time and delete any outdated lightboxes.

#### To manage a lightbox:

Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant lightbox.  Choose what to do next:

### Rename a lightbox

1. Click **Rename**.
2. Enter a new name for your lightbox.

### Duplicate a lightbox

1. Click **Duplicate Lightbox**.
2. Enter a name for your new lightbox.
3. (Optional) Set up the lightbox triggers and customize the design.

### Delete a lightbox

1. Click **Delete**.
2. Click **Delete** in the pop-up to confirm.

**Note: **Deleting a lightbox removes it completely from your site.
