---
title: "ADI Request: Adding Elements to the Header or Footer"
---

Currently you cannot add elements such as buttons and images to the header or footer.

If you want to do this, you should [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-placing-elements-in-your-header-footer-and-page).
