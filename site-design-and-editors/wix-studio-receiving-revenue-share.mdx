#### Earning revenue share on client sales

As a Wix Partner, you can earn revenue share from transactions processed through [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) on your clients' eligible sites between January 10, 2024 and January 31, 2025. Revenue share on client sales is calculated daily, based on the total daily sales generated by your clients' sites, including both online sales and sales made through [Wix Retail POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-wix-pos-for-brick-and-mortar-stores). Once your clients' sales qualify for revenue share, you can track your earnings in the **Client sales** tab.

### Connecting a payout method

To start earning revenue share, you need to connect a payout method in the Revenue Share tab of your Wix Studio workspace.

> **Note:**
>
> Only workspace owners and co-owners can view and manage the Revenue Share tab. Learn more about [managing the roles and permissions of your teammates](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-managing-your-team).

#### To connect a payment method:

[Go to Revenue Share](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/revenues?referralInfo=sidebar) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click **Connect Payout Method** at the top right.  Click **Continue**.  Add your contact info and click **Next**.  Add your payout method details and click **Next**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Back** if you need to edit your payment details. You can always edit your details by clicking **Payout Settings** in the Revenue Share tab.

### Receiving payouts for revenue share

Your revenue share earnings from plans, client sales, and Marketplace template sales are combined. Once your total earnings reach $200, a payout is scheduled 30 days after the end of that month.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Revenue share payouts are calculated based on the price of the plan, excluding the VAT.
> - For Wix Studio enterprise plans, revenue share payouts are issued once Wix receives full payment from the client, with a net 90-day cycle.

### FAQs

Click on a question below to learn more about revenue share.

### What qualifies as the first owner of a site?

The first owner of the site is the one that initiated its creation from their Wix Studio workspace. When you click **Create New Site** in your Wix Studio workspace, you become the first owner of the site, regardless of whether you later transfer ownership to a client.

### What is the difference between Legend level and non-Legend level Partners in revenue share eligibility?

Partners at Legend level enjoy access to revenue share on all participating plans and higher revenue share percentages on specific plans:
- **Wix Premium yearly, 2 year, or 3 year plans:** Legend level partners earn 20% revenue share, while non-Legend level partners do not.
- **Wix Studio yearly, 2 year, or 3 year plans:** Legend level partners earn 30% revenue share, whereas non-Legend level partners earn 20% in 2024.

### Do all plans qualify for revenue share?

To qualify for revenue share, a site must have an active Premium or studio plan, either yearly or longer (unless defined otherwise by Wix). For Partners who aren't legends, the plan must be purchased in 2024.

### How is revenue share calculated for client sales?

Revenue share on client sales is calculated daily across all of the Partner's sites based on the net amount generated and processed through [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments). This includes online sales and sales made with [Wix Retail POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-wix-pos-for-brick-and-mortar-stores).
The net amount refers to the total sales generated by your clients' sites, factoring in deductions such as applicable fees, charges, chargebacks, refunds, etc. If refunds or chargebacks occur after payout, this will be corrected in the next payout.
There are two tiers for revenue share:
- **Clients' daily sales generated less than or equal to $2000: **You qualify for a revenue share of 0.05% on the net total from client sales for the day. If the total sales for a given day are equal to or less than $2000, your revenue share for that day would be calculated at 0.05%.
- **Clients' daily sales generated more than $2000: **You can earn a higher revenue share of 0.10% on the net total from client sales for the day. No annual earning cap; earnings are unlimited if the total daily sales exceed $2000.

### How often are revenue share earnings updated?

Revenue share earnings from client sales are updated once every 24 hours. On Wix plans, earnings are posted up to 30 days after the purchase date.

### How often are revenue share earnings paid out?

Your revenue share earnings from client sales and plans are invoiced together. To qualify for a payout, your total earnings from both plans and client sales must reach a minimum of $200. Once you meet this threshold, the payout cycle is net 30 days for revenue share earnings.**Note: **The payout cycle on revenue share for enterprise is net 90 days.

### Can I earn revenue share from my client's existing Wix plans?

No. You have to be the first owner to be eligible to earn.

### Is there a limit to the number of sites I can onboard to earn from?

No. There's no limit.
