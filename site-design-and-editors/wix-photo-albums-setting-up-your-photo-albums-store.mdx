### Setting up an Albums Store

Start selling your digital and print images by setting up an Albums Store. You'll need to create a new album to sell your photos.

#### To set up an Albums Store:

Go to the [Photo Albums](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Photo%20Albums&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/photography-albums/) tab in your site's dashboard.   Click **+ New Album**.  Select an album cover style, then click **Next**.  Select a layout for your album, then click **Next**.  Add your business name or logo with one of these options:

- Select **Business Name** and enter your business name in the text field.
- Select **Logo** and click **Add Your Logo** to add your logo.
    Click **Next**.  Enter a name for your album in the **Album name** field.  (Optional) Enter some extra information about the album in the **More info **field.   Click **Next**.  Click **+ Upload Media **to add content to your new album.  Click **Create Album**.  Click **Store** on the left.  Click **Set Up** **Store**.  Choose what you want to sell:

### Digital Files and Prints

1. Select** Digital Files & Prints**.
2. Click **Next**.
3. Click **OK**.
4. Click **Change Currency** to set your currency type.
5. Select the checkboxes next to the products you wish to sell.
6. Set pricing for your products:

- **For prints:**

1. Hover over the relevant product and click **Edit** on the right.
2. Set the desired value in the **YOUR PROFIT** field.
3. (Optional) Select the **Round Prices** checkbox to round the retail price up to the nearest whole number.
- **For digital products:**

1. Hover over the relevant product and click **Edit** on the right.
2. Enable the toggle next to each license type you want to offer:

- **Standard License: **Sell your images for personal, single use only.
- **Extended License:** Sell your images for commercial, multiple uses. Set the total allowed usage next to **Number of Uses**.
3. Set the price next to **Retail Price**.
7. Click **Next**.
8. Enter your Business Information.
9. Click **Next**.
10. Click the checkbox to complete the ownership confirmation.
11. Click **Done**.
12. Click **Publish Now**.

### Digital Files Only

1. Select **Digital Files Only**.
2. Click **Next**.
3. Click **OK**.
4. Choose from the 2 types of licenses you want to sell:

- **﻿﻿Commercial Use:** Content can be used for commercial use, in promotional campaigns such as advertising, as well as on websites and blogs. Permissions are for web and digital use only and does not require the owner to be credited.
- **﻿﻿Extended Use:** This license offers images, illustrations and photos for commercial purposes. This license extends from web and digital to print use, and does not require the owner to be credited.
5. Choose the type of file format you want to sell and the price:

- **Web Ready: **If you are uploading files over 1200px, we create a smaller version ideal for web use. This gives you two ways to sell. Web images should cost less than print. Set the price in the price field.
- **Original File: **Customers get the high-resolution file you uploaded, perfect for print and other outlets. Higher quality usually sells at a higher price. Set the price in the price field.
6. Click **Next**.
7. (If necessary) Set up a payment method.
8. Click the checkbox to complete the ownership confirmation.
9. Click **Done**.
10. Click **Publish Now**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about setting up your Wix Photo Albums Store.

### Can I access images from the regular Media Manager when adding them to Photo Albums?

Newly created Photo Albums that have the Store feature in them (whether it is active or not), have their own secured Media Manager. This means that it is not possible to access the images uploaded to the regular Media Manager when adding images to the Photo Albums with the Store feature.

### Can I add a Store to Photo Albums I created before the Photo Albums Store was launched?

It is not possible to add a Store to the Photo Albums that were created before the Store feature was launched.

### Can I duplicate an Album and add a Store to it?

Albums duplicated from the Albums without the Store feature in them do not have the option of adding the Store to them.

### Can I manage the Photo Albums Store in the Wix app?

Currently, it is not possible to manage your Photo Albums Store in the Wix app on your mobile device. If this is a feature you would like to see in the future, please click **Vote for this feature** in [this article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-request-managing-your-photo-albums-store-in-the-wix-owner-app) and we'll make sure to keep you updated.

### Can I customize the purchase confirmation emails sent to customers who purchase from my Photo Albums store?

It is not possible to customize the automated email customers receive confirming their purchase from your photo albums store. Additionally, custom [automated emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-customizing-automated-emails) are only able to be synced with Wix Stores purchases, not Photo Albums store.

### I've selected my desired license, where do my site visitors see these license terms?

The selected license and associated terms display on the product page when your site visitors select their desired image and license. It is currently not possible to add these to the purchase confirmation email.
