---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Translating the 'More' Item in Your Menu"
---

Currently, it is not possible to translate the 'More' menu item, which appears when your menu is not wide enough to fit all the items in it.

> **You can remove the 'More' menu item by doing one of the following:**
>
> - Rename the 'More' menu item with a title that is not language-specific (such as three dots): 
>
> 1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
> 2. Click your menu.
> 3. Click the **Layout** icon .
> 4. Change the text under **Show an item that says**.
> - [Stretch the menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-stretching-an-element-to-full-width) to display all site pages.
> - [Add a menu folder](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-and-managing-a-dropdown-submenu) and move the additional pages to this folder. You can translate the folder name.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
