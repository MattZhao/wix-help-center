Add a site title to the top of your page to give your visitors a clear idea of what you, your site, or your brand is all about. Titles are a great way to grab your visitors' attention, and get them interested in exploring your site.

**In this article, learn more about:**

---

### Adding a site title

Site titles should be placed at the top of your site and have a larger font than the rest of the text found on your home page. This ensures that your visitors can clearly see it when they enter your site.

> **Note:**
>
> Adding a site title to your header means that it will appear on all pages.

### Show me how

1. Click **Add Elements**  on the left of the Editor.
2. Click **Text**.
3. Click **Titles**.
4. Click and drag the text you want to use onto the top of your page or onto your header.
5. Click the text in your Editor.
6. Click **Edit Text**.
7. Enter your site's title.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn how to [format your text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-formatting-your-text) to suit the look and feel of your site.

### Improving your site with titles

Having a site title can enhance many aspects of your site, such as SEO and accessibility.

As well as enhancing your site, it can also improve your visitors' experience by proving a clear summary of your site.

Click below to learn more about the benefits of having a clear site title.

### SEO benefits

Having a clear site title that matches your URL means that when Google indexes your site, your site title will show up along with your web address.We recommend adding your site title as text, and making sure it's the same name as it is within your site's dashboard settings to ensure that Google indexes it correctly. [Learn how to change your site's name on your dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/renaming-your-site)

### Accessibility benefits

Having a clear site title can help visitors with disabilities or impairments understand what your site is about. We recommend adding your site title as text, as well as defining the heading tags. This ensures that visitors using screen readers can navigate your site with ease. [Learn how to define your text headings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-preparing-your-site-structure)
