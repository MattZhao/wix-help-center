---
title: "Wix Editor: Creating a Drop-Down Menu"
---

Place pages under other pages to create a drop-down menu on your site. Visitors can see the drop-down when they hover over the main page on the menu.

### To turn a page into a drop-down:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu** ** **  on the left side of the editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the **More Actions** icon .  Click **Subpage **.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can easily turn the drop-down back into a main page at any time. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the drop-down and click **Main page**.

> **Note:**
>
> If the drop-down in your menu open behind other elements, [move your menu forward](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-arranging-elements-using-right-click).
