---
title: "Wix Editor: Reordering Menu Items"
---

You can reorder the items on your menu from the Menus & Pages panel.

### To reorder menu items:

Click **Menus &** **Pages **on the left side of the Editor.                 Click and drag the menu items to a new position in the list.
