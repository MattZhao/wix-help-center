title: "ADI: Adding and Setting Up a Gallery"
---

Add a multimedia gallery to your site to showcase your images and videos and really bring your business to life. You can also add elements like social share icons, a download button, and a heart icon beside each gallery item to encourage site visitors to interact with your gallery and react to their favorite images.

---

### Step 1 | Add a gallery to your site

The first step is to add a gallery to your site. After you've added your gallery you can then look at adding media and customizing the settings.

#### To add a gallery to your site:

Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Section to Page**.  Scroll down to **Elements** and click **Media Gallery.**  Select a design and drag it to the relevant section on your site.

---

### Step 2 | Add media to your gallery

Once you've selected an initial layout you can begin adding images and videos to your gallery to really make it come to life. Upload your own images or choose stock images.

#### To add media to your gallery:

Click the gallery in the ADI builder.  Click **Manage**.   Click** Add Media**.   Choose the type of media you wish to upload: **Image**, **Video** or **Text**.

---

### Step 3 | Customize your gallery settings

Choose your gallery's width and display elements as well as what happens when a visitor clicks an item or scrolls through the gallery.

#### To customize your gallery settings:

Click your gallery in the ADI builder.  Customize your gallery settings on the panel on the left using the available options:

### Show me how

- **Full Width: **Switch on the toggle to expand your gallery across the width of your site.
- **Load More Button: **Switch on the toggle to show a 'Load More' button for galleries with more items than fit in the section layout.
- **Image Ratio:** Select a ratio of the gallery items. The images automatically update  on the page so you can see how it looks.
- **When** **Clicking On an Item**: Click the drop-down to select what happens when visitors click a gallery item:

- **A Link Opens: **Visitors are directed to a link (a web address, page, lightbox, etc.)

**Note: **To set up a link click **Manage Media** beneath the drop-down.
- **Nothing Happens: **Nothing happens when someone clicks an item.
- **Open in Full Screen:** The gallery item expands in fullscreen mode so visitors can get a closer look.
- **Display Elements: **Select the elements to include in the gallery alongside the gallery items.

- **Image Title:** Display the title of the gallery item. **Note**: Click **Manage Media** at the bottom to update your titles.
- **Image Description:** Include the description of the item. **Note**: Click **Manage Media** at the bottom to update the item description.
- **Social Sharing:** Add social media icons to your gallery items so visitors can share the media.
- **Download:** Add a download icon beside each gallery item so visitors can download your media.
- **Heart Icon:** Add a heart icon so visitors can react to their favorite gallery items.
- **Image Quality:** Drag the slider to change the image quality.
- **Image Sharpening:** Select the checkmark to sharpen your image using the amount, radius and threshold controls.

> **Note:**
>
> Some settings may be different or unavailable based on the gallery layout you choose.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about galleries in ADI.

### How do I change the layout of my gallery?

You can change the layout of your gallery at any time without rearranging items or starting over. Click **Design **next to the** **gallery in the ADI builder to view different section layouts. Select **Use This Layout **when you've found the one you like best.

### How do I delete an image from my gallery?

You can delete an image from your gallery at anytime. Just hover over the image you want to delete in the Organize Your Gallery popup and click the Remove icon . Then click **Done**.

### How do I add text to my gallery images?

You can add titles and descriptions to your gallery items for visitors to learn more.
1. Hover over your gallery in the ADI Builder.
2. Click **Manage**.
3. Select the relevant gallery item in the **Organize Your Gallery** popup.
4. Type your item's title and description in the relevant field.
5. Click **Done**.
**To display the title and descriptions on your gallery:**
1. Click your gallery in the ADI Builder.
2. Select the **Click to show** checkbox next to** Image Title** and **Image Description**.
**Tip: **You can change the font of your gallery text by [changing the font of your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-text-on-your-site).
