---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Additional Page Layouts"
---

Currently, template page layouts are not supported in the Editor.

In the meantime, you can choose between two types of page layouts; standard and without a header and footer. To learn more, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-applying-a-page-layout-standard-no-header-footer).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
