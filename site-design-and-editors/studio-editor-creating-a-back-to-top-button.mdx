---
title: "Studio Editor: Creating a \"Back to Top\" Button"
---

Create a "Back to Top" button to make it easy for visitors to go to the top of the page.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also use any other linkable element (e.g. text, shapes, or media).

### To create a "Back to Top" button:

Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Buttons**.  Drag a button to the desired location on the page.  Click the Link icon .  Select **Top / bottom of page** on the left.  Click **Top**,** **then click **Done**.  (Optional) Pin your button to the screen so it's visible as visitors scroll the page:

1. Click the Open Inspector arrow  at the top right of the Editor.
2. Scroll down to **Position.**
3. Click the **Position type** drop-down and select **Pinned**.
4. Click the **Pin to **drop-down and select the desired location for your button (e.g. Section / Page).

> **Tip:**
>
> Add an [Entrance animation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-an-entrance-animation) to your button and adjust the delay to make it appear after a few seconds, as visitors scroll down.
