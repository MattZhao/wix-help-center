title: "Single Video Player: Choosing a YouTube Video Cover Image"
---

There are two ways that you can add YouTube videos to your site:

Using the social player video elementUsing Wix Video

---

### Changing the cover image for single YouTube videos:

The video's static image settings (the image which appears before your video begins to play) are determined by YouTube and cannot be changed from your Wix account.

If you uploaded the video to YouTube, you can change the image from the video's **Advanced settings** tab in your [YouTube](http://www.youtube.com) account.

---

### Changing the cover image for YouTube videos in Wix Video:

Any updates to information or cover images for a video have to be done directly on YouTube. Click [here](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en) for more information.
