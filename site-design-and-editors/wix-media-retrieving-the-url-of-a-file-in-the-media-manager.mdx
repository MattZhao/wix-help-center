---
title: "Wix Media: Retrieving the URL of a File in the Media Manager"
---

You can retrieve the exact URL of any file type in the Media Manager, such as images, videos and music files. You can then share the copied URL with whomever you like, or even add the URL to another site in your account as a link.

### To retrieve the URL of a file in the Media Manager:

Wix EditorStudio EditorWix app
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.
4. Click the relevant file.
5. Click the **More Actions **icon** **.
6. Click **Copy URL.**
7. Paste the URL in a new browser.
[image: A screenshot of selecting the Copy URL option for an image.]

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Media Manager**.
5. Tap **Site media**.
6. Tap the **More Actions** icon  under a file and tap **Copy link**.
7. Paste the URL in a new browser.
