---
title: "Wix Media: Adding Videos from Wix Video Maker to a Site Page"
---

[Wix Video Maker](https://www.wix.com/tools/video-maker) allows you to make your own promotional videos and customize them to suit your needs. You can add videos from your Video Maker folder, where all videos are saved, directly onto your page.

### To add videos from Wix Video Maker to a page:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.

**Note: **If you haven't uploaded media to your site yet, click the **Upload Media** icon  .
[image: A screenshot of going to the Media Manager from the Wix Editor.]
1. Go to the **Video Maker **folder.
2. Select the relevant video.
3. Click **Add to Page**.
[image: A screenshot of selecting the video under Site Files in the Media Manager.]
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media **[image: undefined] on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Site Files**.
4. Go to the **Video Maker **folder.
5. Select the relevant video.
6. Click **Add to Page**.
[image: A screenshot of adding a video from the Media Manager.]

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also add a Single Video Player to show a video from the Wix Video Maker. When you click **Change Video **on the element, you can access the **Video Maker** folder.
