---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Strikethrough Formatting for Text"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add strikethrough formatting to text.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [add the HTML element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget), enter your text and apply the strikethrough tag to the relevant section of text.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
