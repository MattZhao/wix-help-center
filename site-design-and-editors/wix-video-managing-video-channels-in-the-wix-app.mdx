---
title: "Wix Video: Managing Video Channels in the Wix App"
---

The Wix app lets you manage all your video content on the go. You can reorder your video channels and choose to show or hide a specific channel. This is a great way to maximize your time and content needs all at once, without having to be at a desk.

### To manage video channels from the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage** at the bottom right.  Tap **Video Library **then tap** Videos**.  Choose what you want to do:

- **Reorder channels:** Drag and drop the channels to reorder them.
- **Show/hide channels:** Tap the Show/hide  icon next to the relevant channel to show or hide it from your site.
