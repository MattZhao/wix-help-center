---
title: "Wix Editor: Setting Up Your Mobile Document"
---

All files uploaded to the desktop version of your site are automatically displayed on the mobile version.

### To  show or hide the document title in mobile view:

Click the document in the [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).  Click the **Settings **icon .  Click the toggle next to **Show the title** to show or hide the document's title: 

- **Enabled:** The document title is displayed on the document on your mobile-friendly view.
- **Disabled: **The document title is not displayed on the document on your mobile-friendly view.

> **Tip:**
>
> For more information about adding documents to your desktop site, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-sharing-documents-on-your-site).
