---
title: "Pages Appearing Individually in Search Results"
---

We enable each page of your Wix site to appear in search results so that each page is accessible to site visitors.

For example, if a site visitor wants to find out how to contact you, they can type your business name followed by the word "contact" into a search engine. They can then access your contact page immediately via search results, instead of having to access your home page and scroll through your site.

Make sure to [add unique title tags and meta descriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-seo-title-tags-and-meta-descriptions-to-your-pages) to each page to ensure that search engines display them in search results. Your title tags and meta descriptions should be relevant to the content of the page. This will help your potential site visitors understand what the page is about based on the information displayed in search results.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can prevent search engines from indexing an individual page if you don't want it to appear. [Learn more about preventing search engines from indexing your page.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-preventing-search-engines-from-indexing-your-page)
