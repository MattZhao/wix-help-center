---
title: "Wix Editor: Uploading Documents (.pdf, .doc, .xls etc)"
---

Upload your documents to your site's Media Manager in a couple of clicks and add them to your site.

### To upload a document:

Click the **Media **icon** **** **on the left side of the editor.  Click the **Upload Media **icon .  Choose your file(s) from your computer.  (Optional) Select the file and click **Add to Page **to add it to your site.

>
> - Learn more about [sharing documents on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-sharing-documents-on-your-site).
> - Display PDF files directly on your site using the [PDF Viewer App](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-pdf-viewer-app).
> - View the [range of file types you can upload to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes).
