---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Translating Facebook Ads"
---

Currently, it is not possible to translate Facebook ads with Wix Multilingual.

> **Notes:**
>
> - When creating a Facebook ads campaign for your Wix site, you can [write the content of the ad caption and headline](https://support.wix.com/en/article/facebook-ads-creating-a-new-campaign#step-2-customize-your-ad-content) in whatever language you prefer and this will be displayed to the viewer of the ad. Everything on your site visitor's end (comments, feeds, likes, and shares) is defined by, and displayed based on their Facebook language settings.
> - For dynamic ads that display store products; product names and descriptions are synced from your Wix store based on the main language of your site. Translations using Wix Multilingual do not sync with your ad campaign. Viewers of the ad only see product names and descriptions in your site's main language.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
