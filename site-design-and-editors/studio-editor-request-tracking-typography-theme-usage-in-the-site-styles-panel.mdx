title: "Studio Editor Request: Tracking Typography Theme Usage in the Site Styles Panel"
---

Currently, the [Site Styles panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-your-site-styles) does not include an indication of the number of times you've used a typography theme (e.g. Heading 1) across your client's site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
