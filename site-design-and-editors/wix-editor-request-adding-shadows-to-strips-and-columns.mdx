---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding Shadows to Strips and Columns"
---

Currently, Wix does not support adding and customizing shadows to strips and columns on your site.

However, there are lots of design options available for strips and columns, such as changing the background, and choosing scroll settings. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/the-wix-editor/strips-columns)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
