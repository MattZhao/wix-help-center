---
title: "Wix Editor: Troubleshooting Missing Facebook Comments"
---

Facebook Comments are page specific. Each Facebook Comments box that you add to your site is connected to the page that it was added to. Therefore, if your visitor commented on one page of your Wix site, the comment will only appear on that page.

When adding the Facebook Comments box in the Editor, the comments that you see are the comments that were made on your home page. These comments will not appear, on your live site, on any pages other than your home page.

To test whether any comments have been left for a specific page on your site, use this link: [https://developers.facebook.com/docs/plugins/comments](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/plugins/comments).

Place the relevant page URL under **URL to comment on** and press the **Enter **key. If any comments have been left for that URL, they appear below the Comment box.
