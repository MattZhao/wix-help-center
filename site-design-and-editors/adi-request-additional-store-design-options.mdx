---
title: "ADI Request: Additional Store Design Options"
---

Currently ADI offers limited customization options for designing your online store. The main goal of Wix ADI is to simplify the website building process by suggesting design solutions that keep your business looking professional while delivering a cohesive experience for your customers.

If you want more control over your store design, you need to [switch your site to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and then follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-designing-your-storefront).
