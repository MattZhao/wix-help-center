---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Galleries for Images in Portrait Orientation"
---

Currently, there are no specific galleries for images in portrait orientation.

As a workaround, you can change the scaling of the images in some of our existing galleries. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adjusting-your-gallery-settings) to learn how.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
