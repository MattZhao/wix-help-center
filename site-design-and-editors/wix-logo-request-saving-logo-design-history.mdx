---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Saving Logo Design History"
---

Currently, saving a logo design will replace any previously saved version. It is not possible to browse earlier versions of the design history.

> **Tip:**
>
> As a workaround, you can duplicate an existing design and [set it as a secondary logo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-working-with-secondary-logos-in-the-wix-logo-maker) before making any further edits. That way all duplicates are saved as a unique design and do not overwrite your last saved version.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
