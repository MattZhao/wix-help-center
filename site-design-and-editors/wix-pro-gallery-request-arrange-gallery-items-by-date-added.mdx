---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Arrange Gallery Items by Date Added"
---

Currently, it is not possible to arrange your gallery items by the date they were added.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [reorder gallery items manually with drag and drop, or sort them alphabetically by Image Title or File Name](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-downloading-images-from-a-gallery#reordering-items-in-a-gallery).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
