---
title: "ADI: Customizing the Design of Your Site Menu"
---

Your site has a stunning menu that has been designed to complement the rest of your site. You can make changes to the design of your menu if you wish.

[Change the layout of your header](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-layout-of-a-section) to change the design of your menu.[Change your site's colors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors) to change the menu colors.[Change your site's fonts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-text-on-your-site) to change the menu font.

If you want to customize your menu even further, including changing the text size, colors and hover color, then [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor).

> **Warning:**
>
> Once you save in the Editor, you cannot come back to edit your site in Wix ADI.
