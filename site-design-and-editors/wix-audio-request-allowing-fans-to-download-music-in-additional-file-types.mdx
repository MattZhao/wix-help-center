---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Allowing Fans to Download Music in Additional File Types"
---

Currently, your fans can only download your music in MP3 or Flac format. It is not possible for your fans to download your music in file types other than MP3 or Flac format.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
