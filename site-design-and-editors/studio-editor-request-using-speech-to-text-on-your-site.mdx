---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Using Speech-to-Text on Your Site"
---

Currently, the Studio Editor does not offer a speech recognition tool. This feature would allow your visitors to perform various actions using speech-to-text, like filtering store products, filling in input fields, etc.

> **Using Wix Stores?**
>
> You can [integrate Speechly](https://dev.wix.com/docs/velo/articles/velo-package-readmes/wix-stores-speechly-integration) to let visitors filter store products with a push-to-talk button. This requires setting up the connection in the site code.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
