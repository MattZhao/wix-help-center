---
title: "Wix Editor: Customizing Galleries on Your Mobile Site"
---

There are 2 types of galleries available for your Wix site - the Pro Gallery and editor galleries. When you add one of these galleries to your desktop site, it's also added to your mobile site.

Depending on your gallery type, you can customize your gallery's design, layout and settings to ensure it looks best for mobile visitors.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Customizing a pro gallery on mobile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-the-show-more-button-in-galleries-on-your-mobile-site#customizing-a-pro-gallery-on-mobile)  [Customizing an editor gallery on mobile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-the-show-more-button-in-galleries-on-your-mobile-site#customizing-an-editor-gallery-on-mobile)

---

### Customizing a Pro Gallery on mobile

You can refine your Pro Gallery's layout, settings and design on mobile, so that it looks and behaves exactly as you want for mobile site visitors.

#### To customize a Pro Gallery:

Click the gallery in your mobile editor.  Click **Settings**.  Choose what to customize:

- **Layout: **Click the **Layout **tab and [use the available options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-choosing-a-wix-pro-gallery-layout-for-your-mobile-site-custom-vs-presets) to adjust the gallery's layout.
- **Design: **Click the **Design **tab and [use the available options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-gallery-design-for-your-mobile-site) to design the gallery.
- **General settings: **Click the **Settings **tab and customize the following options:

- **Gallery Settings: **Click the **When clicking on an item **drop-down to select what happens when visitors click on gallery items.
- **Social Settings: **Enable the relevant toggles to allow social sharing, downloading and a heart counter on your gallery.

---

### Customizing an editor gallery on mobile

Each editor gallery boasts its own unique set of customization options. Available options depend on the gallery layout you choose on your desktop site. You can customize the settings or layout of the gallery on mobile, or both if the layout allows.

#### To customize an editor gallery on mobile

Click the gallery in your mobile editor.  Choose what to customize:

- **Settings: **

1. Click **Settings ** icon.
2. Customize the following options:

- **When clicked: **Click the drop-down to select what happens when visitors click on gallery items (e.g. they open in a pop-up, open a link, etc.)
- **Do images show text?: **Decide whether or not titles and descriptions appear below gallery items.
- **Layout:**

1. Click the **Layout **** **icon.
2. Customize your gallery's columns, spacing and text alignment.

> **Tip:**
>
> Some galleries display a Load More button which displays more images. If you want to [change what the Load More button looks like](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-enabling-and-customizing-a-load-more-button), you need to do it in the desktop editor. The changes are then shown in the mobile editor.
