### Sharing a site for client feedback

Share the latest version of sites with clients for their direct review and feedback. Each site has a unique link you can share with clients so they can view their site and add comments wherever relevant.

#### To get client feedback:

[Go to Client Feedback](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/client-feedback?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click + **Get Feedback** at the top right.  Choose the site to send for feedback and click **Continue**.  Enter the name or email address of each person you want to get feedback from.    (Optional) Enter a message your clients will see when they receive the invite.  Click **Send**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Copy Link** next to the shareable link to send it on your own. The shareable link URL is valid for 2 weeks.

### Getting client feedback on a site

When clients are invited to provide feedback, they see the last saved version of their site without logging in. They can add comments directly to specific areas they're referring to, so you know exactly what each comment is about.

> Share the instructions below with your client to help them access the site and leave feedback.

#### To add comments (for clients):

Access the Get Client Feedback app in a desktop browser (not from a mobile device):

- **From the email: **Click **View Site** from the invite email titled: *"You're invited to give feedback on the [site name] website"*
- **From the shareable link:** In your browser, enter the URL you received.
    Browse through the site to review it.

**Note:** Choose the display type at the top, such as mobile or desktop.  Leave a comment:

1. Click **+ Add Comments** at the top-right.
2. Click the Comment icon  and drag it to the area you're commenting about. 

**Note:** If you're not logged in to Wix, enter your name in the **Your Name** field. Add your email address in the **Your Email** field to receive notifications for new replies on your comments.
3. Enter your comment in the **Add your comment...** field.
4. Click **Post**.
    (Optional) Repeat step 3 to add more comments.

### Reviewing client feedback

Efficiently handle all feedback received from your clients. Receive notifications when clients leave comments via email and your Wix Studio workspace so you can address it quickly. Both of these notifications lead directly to the comment so you can review, reply and resolve it once you're done.

#### To review client feedback:

Go to your client's feedback:

- **From the notification email: **Click **View Feedback** in** **the email titled "*You got feedback on the [site name] website*".
- **From your Wix Studio workspace:** [Go to Client Feedback](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/client-feedback?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view), hover over the site and click **View Feedback**.
    Review your client's comments on the right side of the page.   Click **Take Me There **next to a comment to view its location. Then, do the following:

- Enter a reply and click **Reply **to respond to the comment.
- Hover over the comment and click **Resolve **to mark it as done.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Copy Shareable Link** at the bottom right of the Comments panel to share this site link with your clients.

### Managing client feedback

Access all client feedback in the [Client Feedback](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/client-feedback?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) tab, where you can view open threads per site, explore client comments, and send additional feedback requests. Gain an overview of sites with open threads, latest feedback received, and resolved feedback. Use the filter to track and manage your client feedback efficiently with actionable insights.
