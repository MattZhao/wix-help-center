---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Making Only Custom Buttons Clickable"
---

Currently, it is not possible to make only the [custom buttons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-a-custom-button-to-your-gallery) on your Pro Gallery clickable, and not the gallery items as well. The option in the Settings tab for **When clicking on an item **applies to both the button and the gallery item.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
