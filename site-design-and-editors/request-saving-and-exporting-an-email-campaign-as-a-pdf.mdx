title: "Request: Saving and Exporting an Email Campaign as a PDF"
---

Currently, it is not possible to export and save email campaigns to a PDF file format directly from your site's dashboard. As a workaround, you can view your campaign and print to PDF.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.

### To manually save an email campaign as a PDF:

Go to [Email Marketing Campaigns](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Email%20Campaigns&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/shoutout/) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the campaign you want to save as a PDF, and click the More Actions  icon.  Click **View**.

Right click on the email campaign, and select **Print**.  In Destination drop-down, click **Save as PDF**.

Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> The printing options available depend on the device you use to view your email campaign.
