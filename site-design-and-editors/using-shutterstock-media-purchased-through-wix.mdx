---

Adding Shutterstock images and videos can give your site a high-quality, unique and professional look. There are millions of files to choose from. Read below for the conditions of using Shutterstock media on your Wix site.

#### Shutterstock media guidelines:

You can use Shutterstock images and videos you've purchased on any site in the same Wix account. Learn how to [import them to another site.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-importing-media-from-one-wix-site-to-another)  Images are for digital use within Wix only and may not be used for printing.  Shutterstock files are not downloadable.  You may not use the media as a trademark for a business.  You may not portray a person in a way that may be offensive, including:

- in connection with adult-oriented services or ads for dating services;
- in connection with political endorsements;
- with pornographic, defamatory, unlawful, offensive or immoral content; and
- as suffering from, or being treated for, a physical or mental ailment.
    You may only use the media in websites and content on Wix, and not with other website or content services.
