---
title: "Single Video Player Request: Adding YouTube Clips"
---

Currently, you can [add a Youtube video to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-a-video-from-youtube) using the Single Video Player, but not a Youtube Clip.

A YouTube Clip is a short video played on loop. It has a different URL to the original Youtube video, which isn't accepted by the Single Video Player.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
