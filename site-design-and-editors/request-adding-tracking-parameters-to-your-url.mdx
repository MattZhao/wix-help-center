---
title: "Request: Adding Tracking Parameters to Your URL"
---

Currently, Wix does not support adding tracking parameters to your Wix site or page URL (e.g., to track affiliate activity). You can try to add your own custom parameters but we cannot provide support for it.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
