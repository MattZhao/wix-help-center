---
title: "Wix Editor: Stretching Your Strip With Columns"
---

You can choose how your columns are stretched on your site.

### To stretch your columns:

Click the strip that contains the columns in the Editor.Click the **Stretch **icon .Select an option: 
- **Screen:** Your strip is stretched to the full width of the screen.
- **Page:** Your strip element is stretched to the page size (within the gridlines).
