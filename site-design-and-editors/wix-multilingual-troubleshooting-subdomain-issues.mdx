---
title: "Wix Multilingual: Troubleshooting Subdomain Issues"
---

If you are experiencing problems with your multilingual subdomains, follow the steps below.

### 1. Make sure that your site is published

Your subdomains are only created if your site is published. To publish your site click **Publish **at the top right of the editor.

### 2. Make sure that secondary languages are set to 'visible'

Check that your [secondary languages are set to 'visible'](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-managing-languages-in-a-multilingual-site#showing-or-hiding-a-language-on-your-site) in the multilingual dashboard.

### 3. Make sure that your site has been upgraded

Your subdomains can only be connected if your site has been upgraded. To check that your site has been upgraded:
1. [Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).
2. Check the plan status of your site at the top of the page.

### 4. Check your domain connection

Your subdomain only works if your domain is connected properly. To verify that your domain is connected properly:
1. Click to access the [Domain Assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-domain-assistant-3777632).
2. Follow the instructions to troubleshoot your domain connection.

**Important:** Multilingual subdomains cannot be created for domains that are connected to Wix via [pointing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pointing-vs-name-servers-domain-connection-methods).

### 5. Wait up to 48 hours after connecting your domain

After connecting your domain, it takes up to 48 hours for the domain and its subdomains to [propagate](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-propagation).

#### Still need help?

If the problem persists, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact) and we will be happy to help.
