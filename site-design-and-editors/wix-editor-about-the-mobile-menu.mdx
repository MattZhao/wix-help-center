As more and more people are using their phones to view websites, it is important that your site is easy to navigate on mobile devices. Customize your site's mobile menu to make it look and behave just the way you want.

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to the mobile menu that was released at the beginning of 2019. [Update to the new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-updating-your-mobile-menu) in just a few clicks.

### Opening your mobile menu

When working in the editor, you can see what your menu looks like when it's open and closed. This allows you to customize both views.

Use the closed view to select the icon and customize its design, and use the open view to adjust the design of the text, background, and more.

To open the menu, simply click the icon in the mobile editor, then click **Edit Menu**.

---

### Elements on your mobile menu

Your mobile menu consists of 3 parts; the menu icon, the menu box and the menu itself. From the mobile editor, you can customize each element individually so that it matches your mobile site.

Click an option below to learn more about each element.

### The menu icon

Your site visitors click the menu icon to open the mobile menu. You can [choose from several designs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-mobile-menu#customizing-the-menu-icon), and customize the open and closed states of the icon.
To get started, click the menu icon in the [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor), then click the **Design** icon .

### The menu box

The menu box is the box that contains your menu. You can change the background and the position of the menu box.

To design the menu box:
1. Click the mobile menu icon in the [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).
2. Click **Edit Menu**.
3. Click the menu box.
4. Use the **Layout**, **Design**, and **Animation** panels to adjust and [customize the menu box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-mobile-menu#customizing-the-menu-container).

### The menu

The menu is the list of pages that your site visitors click to navigate around your mobile site. You can choose the alignment of the menu items and design it to look however you want.

To customize the menu:
1. Click the mobile menu icon in the [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).
2. Click **Edit Menu**.
3. Click the mobile menu.
4. Use the **Layout**, **Design**, and **Animation** panels to adjust and [customize your menu items](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-mobile-menu#customizing-the-menu-items).
