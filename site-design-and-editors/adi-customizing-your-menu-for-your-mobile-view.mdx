---
title: "ADI: Customizing Your Menu For Your Mobile View"
---

You can customize the way your menu looks in the mobile view. Visitors viewing your site from a mobile device have different screen sizes and devices. You can customize your site’s menu so that it looks great on mobile devices, as well as on desktop.

> **Important:**
>
> Any changes made in the mobile view do not affect the desktop view of your site.

### To customize your menu:

### Step 1 | Add the elements to your menu on desktop

Your desktop site is automatically converted to a mobile-friendly site. In order to customize your mobile view, you must first edit your menu on desktop. 

1. Click the header on your desktop site.
2. Click the **Show/Hide** iconnext to an element to show it or hide it.
- **Selected**: The element or text field is shown.
- **Unselected: **The element or text field is hidden.

### Step 2 | Go to your mobile view

1. Hover over the desktop icon on the top bar of ADI.
2. Click **Mobile.

**

### Step 3 | Customize your menu on mobile

1. Click the header.
2. Click the **Show/Hide** iconnext to an element to show it or hide it in the mobile view.

### Step 4 | Change your section design

1. Click the header.
2. Click** Section Design**.
3. Select a new mobile section layout.
