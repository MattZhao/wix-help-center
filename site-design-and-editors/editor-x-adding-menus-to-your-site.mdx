In Editor X you can create and manage multiple menus on your site. Add as many menus as you want from the Add panel and customize them individually.

Choose the unique items that appear on each menu. Menu items can link to different pages, anchors, external websites and more. You can even choose exactly which pages to display each menu on for a truly unique experience.

### Adding a menu

Add as many menus as you want to your site from the Add panel. There's several types of menus you can add to suit your needs.

#### To add a menu:

Click **Add Elements  ** on the top of Editor X.  Click **Menu & Search**.  Click a menu type:

- **Horizontal: **Traditional horizontal site menu that usually appear in the header or footer of a website.
- [**Styled Horizontal**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-styled-horizontal-menus)**: **These advanced menus offer tons of unique customization options. You can tailor these menus to your exact needs, adjusting the layout and design of every single area.
- **Vertical: **Vertically arranged menus that look great on the right or left sides of websites.
- [**Hamburger**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-hamburger-menus)**: **Minimalist, space-saving menus where the menu items are only displayed when visitors click the menu icon. The menu has an open and closed state.
- **Anchor: **Floating vertical menu that displays [anchor links](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-using-anchors). These are useful for navigating between the different sections on a page, such as on a one-page site, or on long pages.
    Drag a menu onto your site.

### Adding items to a menu

You can update your menu at any given point by adding new items. Add pages and links to important information, as well as submenus to keep your menu organized.

### What types of links can I add?

- **Web Address:** Link to an external URL. When your visitors click the element, they are directed to your chosen web address. You can choose to open the web address in a new browser window, or within the current window.
- **Page:** Link to a page on your website. When your visitors click the element, they are directed to the chosen page of your site. You can choose to open the page in a new browser window, or within the current window.
- **Anchor:** Link to an anchor on your website. When your visitors click the element, they are taken the page and anchor of your choice.
- **Top/Bottom of Page: **Link to the top or bottom of the page. When your visitors click the element, they are taken to the top or bottom of the page that they are on.
- **Document:** Link to a document. When your visitors click the element, a new browser tab opens with the document.
- **Email: **Link to send an email to a specific email address. When your visitors click the element, their default email account opens so that they can send an email to the specified address.
- **Phone Number: **Link to a phone number. When your visitors click the element on their mobile or tablet, they can call you instantly. If they are using a desktop, a calling app opens such as FaceTime.
- **Lightbox:** Link to a lightbox on your website. When your visitors click the element, the lightbox appears.

#### To add items to a menu:

Click your menu in the Editor.  Click **Manage Menu**.   Click **+ Add New Item** at the bottom.  Choose which type of item you want to add:

### Site page

1. Select **Site Pages**.
2. Select the checkbox next to the page(s) you want to add.
3. Click **Apply**.

### Link

1. Select **Link**.
2. Select the radio button next to the relevant type of link on the left.
3. Choose which page or web address the link leads to.
4. Decide how the link should open by selecting the relevant radio button:

- New window
- Current window
5. Click **Done**.

### Submenu

1. Select **Submenu Title**.
2. Give your submenu a name and press Enter on your keyboard.
3. Move pages to your new submenu:

1. Drag the relevant page under the submenu title.
2. Hover over the page and click the **Show More** icon .
3. Select **Move to Submenu**.

Tip: The submenu title is not clickable – visitors hover over it to see the submenu. If needed, you can [set a page or link as the submenu title](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-menus-to-your-site#creating-and-managing-submenus-dropdown-menus) so it's also clickable.

### Managing menu items

From the **Manage Menu** panel, you can rename items on your menu, change their destination URL and remove items you no longer need.

#### To manage your existing menu items:

Click your menu in the Editor.  Click **Manage Menu**.   Depending on what you want to do with your menu item, click below:

### Remove an item from the menu

1. Hover over the relevant menu item and click the More Actions icon .
2. Select **Remove From Menu**.

**Note: **Every menu on your site must have at least one item. If you only have one item left, you can't remove it from the menu. ** **

### Rename an item

1. Hover over the relevant menu item and click the More Actions icon .
2. Select **Rename**.
3. Enter the new name for your item.

### Change an item's link

You can always change the link of a menu item so that it leads to a different page or web address. 

1. Hover over the menu link and click the More Actions icon .
2. Select **Change Link**.
3. Change the relevant settings:

- Change the type of link using the radio buttons on the left.
- Adjust the details of your existing link.
- Decide how the link should open by selecting the relevant radio button:

- New window
- Current window
4. Click **Done**.

### Reorder menu items

You can always change the order of your menu items using drag and drop.

### Choosing which pages a menu appears on

Want your menu to appear on multiple pages? Attach it to a [Master](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-global-sections) section and open the Masters panel, where you can choose which pages to show it on.

#### To choose which pages show the menu:

Add the menu to a Master section (e.g., header, section, footer).  Click the Masters icon  on the top of Editor X.  (From the Site Masters panel) Click the More Actions icon  on the section that contains the menu.  Select **Show on...**  Select the checkbox next to either:

- All Pages.
- The specific pages you want to show the section on.

### Creating and managing multiple site menus

Want to show personalized menus in different parts of your site? Editor X lets you add as many menus as you need and decide which items appear on each.

After adding a new menu element, you can choose which menu it should display: An existing menu that you saved, or a new one that you create on the spot. This means you can save menus and re-use them on various menu elements across your site.

Click below to learn how to:

### Choose which menu is displayed

1. Select the relevant menu on your site.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Click the drop-down menu under **Which menu is displayed?**.
4. Depending on the menu you need, follow the steps:

- **Existing menu: **Select the menu from the list.
- **New menu: **

- Click** Manage Site Menus**.
- Click** + Add New Menu **at the bottom.
- Enter a name for your new menu.

**Note: **This name isn't visible to visitors.

### Save a new menu

1. Select a menu on your site.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Click the drop-down menu under **Which menu is displayed?**.
4. Select **Manage Site Menus**.
5. Click **+ Add New Menu** at the bottom.
6. Enter a name for your new menu.

**Note: **This name isn't visible to visitors.
7. Start adding pages to your menu:

1. Select the new menu you just created.
2. Click the Exit icon  at the top right.
3. Click **+ Add new Item** to add a new page, link or submenu.

> **Showing your menus on a Master?**
>
> Changing the menu on a [Master section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-global-sections) automatically affects all other pages that display the section. It's not possible to show different menus on different site pages, if it's the same Master section.
