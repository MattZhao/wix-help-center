### Adding the Wix Blog

Click **Add** on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Apps**.  Hover over **Blog** and click **Add**.

---

### Writing a blog post

From [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fblog) in your dashboard. you can add and publish blog posts.

#### To write a post:

[Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Create New Post**.  Write your blog post.  Click **Publish Post**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you are not ready to publish your post, you can click** Save as Draft** and go back to it later.

### Managing your blog

From the Blog Manager you can add, edit and manage your blog posts.

To access the Blog Manager, click on the blog in ADI and click **Manage **on the left hand side.

> **Tip:**
>
> You do not need to enter your site every time you want to add or manage your blog posts. You can access your Blog Manager directly from [Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fblog) in your dashboard.

### Deleting your blog

To delete your blog from your Wix site, you must delete the Blog page. By deleting the Blog page, all other blog pages and elements are also deleted.

### To delete your blog

1. Click the **Pages** menu on the top bar inside ADI.
2. Click the **Blog **page.
3. Click the **Settings **icon.
4. Click **Delete**.
5. Click **Delete** in the confirmation window.

### To delete your blog when it is set as your home page

1. [Add a new page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-managing-your-sites-pages).
2. Click the **Pages** menu on the top bar.
3. Click the **Settings **icon next to the new page you have created.
4. Click **Set as Homepage**.
5. Click the **Settings **icon next to **Blog**.
6. Click **Delete**.

### How do I create a blog feed?

Showcase your latest blog posts by adding a blog feed and decide which details to show.
1. Click **Add** on the top bar inside ADI.
2. Click **Section to Page**.
3. Click **See all **to see the full list.
4. Click **Blog Feed**.
5. Hover over the blog feed inside ADI.
6. Click **Settings**.
7. Select or deselect the Show/Hide icon  to choose what you want to display on your feed.

### How do I automatically update my subscribers every time I publish a new post?

The Wix Blog allows you to update your subscribers every time you publish a new post. To get subscribers, you need to  add a subscribe form by clicking **Add **on the top bar, selecting **Section to Page **and clicking** Subscribe. **Then, enable automatic updates:
1. Click the **Pages **menu from the ADI top bar.
2. Click your Blog page.
3. Click the blog on your ADI site.
4. Select **Send email notifications** in Blog panel.
**Notes: **
- There is no limit on the number of Blog updates that you can send your subscribers, and these updates do not affect your campaigns quota.
- For scheduled posts, the update is sent 4 hours after the post is published.
- The 'Reply-to' email address is your Wix account email address or the email address that you [set for your Wix Email Marketing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-changing-the-sender-details-of-your-campaigns).
- The automatic email is only sent when you publish your blog post from your desktop, and not the Wix app on your mobile device.
