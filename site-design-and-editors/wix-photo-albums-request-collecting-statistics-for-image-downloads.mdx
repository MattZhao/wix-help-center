---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Collecting Statistics for Image Downloads"
---

Currently, it is not possible to collect statistics about images downloaded from your photo albums, such as the most popular images and information about who downloaded them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
