---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Editing Your Site from a Mobile Web Browser"
---

If your site was created in the Editor, it is currently not possible to edit it on a mobile device, tablet or any other touchscreen device. In this case, you can only edit your site from a desktop or laptop.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
