---
title: "Wix Video Request: Uploading 360° Videos to Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to upload 360° videos to Wix Video.

As a workaround, you may upload your 360° video to YouTube and then add it to your Video Library. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-adding-videos-to-your-video-library).

> **Note:**
>
> Embedded videos from YouTube cannot be made available for sale. Read other important information about YouTube videos [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-adding-videos-to-your-video-library).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
