---
title: "Keeping the Free Wix URL"
---

Your free Wix URL is in the format: **siteprefix.wixsite.com/siteaddress**. You can edit the site prefix and site address sections of the URL. Learn more about [changing your free Wix URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-free-wix-url-8591528).

By [upgrading your site with a plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter), you can [connect a custom domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-you-own-to-your-wix-site) to your site, with a unique URL of your choice e.g. **mystunningwebsite.com**.

Whether or not you decide to purchase an upgrade plan, you are welcome to continue using the free Wix URL.

> **Note:**
>
> Even if you decide to purchase an upgrade plan and connect a domain, your site is still accessible through your free Wix URL.
