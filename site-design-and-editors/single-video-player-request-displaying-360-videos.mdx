---
title: "Single Video Player Request: Displaying 360° Videos"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display 360° videos with the Single Video Player.

As a workaround, you can upload your 360° video to YouTube and then embed it to your Single Video Player following the steps below:

Click the Single Video Player in your Editor.Click the **Settings** icon .Click the YouTube icon.
Paste the YouTube video URL under **What's the video's web address?**.

Make sure that you have set your video to "public" on YouTube in order to embed it into your Single Video Player.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
