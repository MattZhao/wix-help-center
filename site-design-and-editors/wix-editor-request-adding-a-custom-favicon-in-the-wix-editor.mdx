---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Custom Favicon in the Wix Editor"
---

Currently, it is not possible to choose a custom favicon for the Wix Editor that you would see on your browser while working on your site.

However, you can [upload and use a custom favicon on your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-favicon).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
