---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Filtering Content by Content Type in the Translation Manager"
---

Currently, it is not possible to filter your content by content type in the Translation Manager.

You can, however, filter your site content by page to narrow down your search.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
