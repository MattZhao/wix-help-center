---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing Your Page's Meta Description"
---

Your page's meta description is a meta tag that gives potential site visitors a summary of what your page is about. It is not visible on your site's page, but appears below your page's title tag in search results. [Learn more about meta descriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-seo-title-tags-and-meta-descriptions-to-your-pages)

### To change a page's meta description:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu** ** **  on the left side of the editor.  Click the **More Actions** icon  beside the relevant page.  Select **SEO Basics**.  Enter the page description under **Meta description (description in search results)?**.  Publish your site.

> **Important:**
>
> Search engines will update your page's meta description the next time that they crawl your site. If you want to try to speed up the process, you can [submit your page's URL to search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/submitting-your-sitemap-and-urls-directly-to-search-engines#submitting-individual-urls-to-search-engines).

> **Tip:**
>
> See how your page is displayed in Google search results under **Preview on Google **at the top of the **SEO Basics **tab.
