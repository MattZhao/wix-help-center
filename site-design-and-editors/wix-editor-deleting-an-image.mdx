---
title: "Wix Editor: Deleting an Image"
---

You can delete any images you no longer want from your site at any time.

### To delete an image from your site:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the image in the editor.  Press **Delete** on your keyboard.

> **Note:**
>
> If you have uploaded your own images, deleting them from your editor does not [remove them from your **Media Storage**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-deleting-media-files-from-your-media-manager). You can add these images again at any time.
