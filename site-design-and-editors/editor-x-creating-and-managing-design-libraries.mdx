Create a design library with assets that you can use at any time, on any Editor X site in your account. This is a great tool to speed up the design process as it lets you save color palettes, typography themes and designed sections.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Create a new library](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-and-managing-design-libraries#creating-a-new-design-library)  [Save assets, colors and typography themes to a library](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-and-managing-design-libraries#saving-assets-to-a-design-library)  [View and manage libraries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-and-managing-design-libraries#viewing-and-managing-design-libraries)  [Share a library](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-and-managing-design-libraries#sharing-a-design-library)

### Creating a New Design Library

Create as many libraries as you need. You can create libraries per sites, themes, or any kind of classification that meets your needs.

#### To create a new library:

Access your Design Libraries:

1. Click **Add Elements **﻿﻿﻿ at the top of Editor X.
2. Click** Assets**.
3. Click **More Actions **.
4. Select **Open Libraries** from the drop-down menu.
    Click **+** **Create New Library**.  Select which assets to include from the site you're currently working on:

- Typography
- Color Themes
- Design Assets
    Click **Create**.  Enter a name for your library under **Library Name**.  (Optional) Click the **Turn on sharing** toggle to share this library with your teammates.

### Saving Assets to a Design Library

You can save design assets from any Editor X site in your account. Every library contains a single set of color and typography styles, and unlimited space for [designed assets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-saving-and-reusing-design-assets).

Depending on what you want to save to your library, click below to learn how to do it:

### Typography and color styles

1. Access the assets you want to save:

- **Typography:** Open the Site Styles panel  and click **Typography**.
- **Colors:** Open the Site Styles panel  and click **Colors**.
2. Click **More Styles **.
3. Select **Save Colors / Typography to Library**.
4. Select the relevant library from the drop-down menu.
5. Click **Save**.

### Saved assets (e.g., sections, elements, etc.)

1. Click **Add Elements **﻿﻿﻿ at the top of Editor X.
2. Click **Assets**.
3. Depending on whether you want to save a single asset or all of them, follow the instructions:

- **One saved asset:**

1. Click the More Actions icon  next to the asset you want to save.
2. Select **Add Asset to Library**.
3. Select the relevant library from the drop-down menu.
4. Click **Save**.
- **All saved assets:**

1. Click **More Actions **.
2. Select **Save My Assets to Library**.
3. Select the relevant library from the drop-down menu.
4. Click **Save**.

### Viewing and Managing Design Libraries

View all of your libraries in one place, whether it's libraries that you created or libraries your team shares with you. You can open libraries to see their contents, duplicate and delete libraries.

#### To view and manage your libraries:

Click **Tools** at the top of Editor X.  Click** Libraries**.  Click the More Actions icon  under the relevant library.  Select an option:

- Open Library
- Duplicate
- Don't Display on This Site
- Delete

---

### Sharing a Design Library

When creating a library, you can decide whether to keep a design library private or share its assets with others.

The access link to your library allows anyone with an Editor X site to add the assets to their account. In addition, if you're using a Team Account, the library becomes visible to all teammates once you turn on sharing.

#### To share a design library:

Access the library you want to share:

1. Click **Tools** at the top of Editor X.
2. Click** Libraries**.
    Click the More Actions icon  under the relevant library.  Click **Open** **Library**.  Enable the **Turn sharing on** toggle to make this library available for sharing.  Share the library with people in one of the following ways: 

- **Send an email: **Enter the person's email address and click the Send icon . They'll receive an email from Editor X with a link to your library.
- **Copy the link: **Click to copy the direct link to your computer's clipboard.
