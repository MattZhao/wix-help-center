title: "ADI Request: Adding a PayPal Button"
---

Currently, you can add a PayPal donate button to your site inside ADI. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-a-paypal-donate-button) to learn how.

> **Important**
>
> If you want to add a PayPal "buy now" button, click [here](https://www.paypal.com/buttons/) to follow the instructions to create an embed code. You can add the code to an HTML embed element to display your embedded content on your ADI site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-the-html-embed-element)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
