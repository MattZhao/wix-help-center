---
title: "ADI: Editing a Section"
---

In ADI, your site is divided up into sections. Each section is a portion of the page that shows distinct information relative to that section. Each section has different options for you to fill out, such as adding images and text. To customize your site, you can edit the details of each section, making it unique in its own way.

### To edit a section:

Click the section you want to edit.Click **Edit**. Edit the content in the section.

> **Additional customization options:**
>
> - You can change the layout of your section by clicking** Design**. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-layout-of-a-section) to learn more.
> - You can show or hide different elements from the section by clicking the **Show/Hide**icon.
> - You can duplicate, move, or delete the section by clicking the** Show More**icon.
