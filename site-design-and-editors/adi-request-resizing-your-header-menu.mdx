---
title: "ADI Request: Resizing Your Header Menu"
---

Currently, you cannot change the size of the header menu.

However, you can select different layouts with header menus of different heights.

### To change your layout:

Click the header on your site in ADI.Click **Design**. Select a new layout with the header menu of your choice.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
