---
title: "ADI: Changing Your Site Title"
---

Your site's title is displayed in the header at the top of your site. It is automatically taken from your business name.

### To change your title:

Click the header section at the top of your site inside ADI.  Enter your site title in the** Business Name** field.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-title-on-your-welcome-section) to learn how to change the title of your welcome section.
