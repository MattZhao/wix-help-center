---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Translating the Members Area in Multilingual Sites"
---

Most components in your Members Area are based on system text, which is text that's automatically generated by Wix.

If the Editor supports your secondary language, Members Area components with system text are automatically localized. If your secondary language isn't supported, all system text appears in English and cannot be translated.

Currently, the only part of your Members Area that can be manually translated is the My Orders page. This page appears only when you have added Wix Stores and Members Area to your site. To learn how to translate the My Orders page, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-the-my-orders-page).

### What languages does Wix Editor support?

Currently, the Editor is available in: 
- English
- Spanish
- Portuguese
- French
- German
- Polish
- Italian
- Russian
- Japanese
- Korean
- Turkish
- Chinese
- Thai
- Swedish
- Norwegian
- Dutch
- Danish
- Hindi
- Czech

### Examples of system text in Members Area

Some components in your Members Area include automatic text generated by Wix, for example:
- Login page
- Member profile cards
- My Account page
- My Wallet page

If your secondary language is supported by Wix Editor, these components are automatically localized.
