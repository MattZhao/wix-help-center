---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Adding a Custom Podcast Feed"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a custom podcast feed which lets you add specific episodes from different podcasts in one feed on your site.

As a work around, you can add additional podcasts on your site. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-podcast-player-adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-podcast-player#faqs).

> **Note:**
>
> Adding additional podcasts will automatically add pages to your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
