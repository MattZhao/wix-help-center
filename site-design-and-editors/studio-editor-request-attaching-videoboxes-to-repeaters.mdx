---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Attaching VideoBoxes to Repeaters"
---

Currently, it is not possible to attach a VideoBox to items in a Repeater / Slideshow Repeater.

As an alternative, you can attach a single video player to repeater items, or display videos in a[ Pro Gallery with the Slideshow / Slider layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-changing-the-layout-settings#custom-layouts).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
