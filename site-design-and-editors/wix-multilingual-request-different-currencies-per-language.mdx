---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Different Currencies Per Language"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display a different currency per language version of your multilingual site. When you change your [site currency](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments), it applies to all of your languages.

> **Tip:**
>
> Add a [currency converter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-a-currency-converter) to your store for customers to view prices in familiar currency. This change only affects the display of prices and not the currency used in transactions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
