---
title: "ADI: Adding a PayPal Donate Button"
---

Add a PayPal Donate button to your site to accept donations. You can set a fixed donation amount, or allow your visitors to choose their own amount to donate.

### To add a PayPal Donate button:

Click the relevant section in ADI.  Click **Edit**.   Click the **Show / Hide** icon  next to **Button **to make your button visible.  Type the button's text in the text field (e.g. Donate Now).  Click **Link **next to your button's text.  Select **PayPal Donations**.  Enter your PayPal email address.  Click the **Fixed Amount **toggle to set the donation amount:

- **Enabled: **Enter a set amount your visitors can donate and select a currency.
- **Disabled: **Allow visitors to choose how much they want to donate.
    Click **Done**.
