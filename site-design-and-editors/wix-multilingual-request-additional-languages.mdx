---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Additional Languages"
---

Wix Multilingual supports over 180 languages, in accordance with the [ISO 639-1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_639-1) international standard. [View the complete list of supported languages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-languages-available)

If your language is not supported, [submit a ticket with your feedback and suggestions](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=9f9e6802-edc0-4fcf-b727-25876a7e00c2&referral=Kb).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
