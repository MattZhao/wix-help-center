title: "ADI: Previewing Your Mobile Site"
---

Your stunning mobile site is automatically created for you using the content from your desktop site.

### To preview your mobile site:

Click **Preview** on the top bar.Click the mobile icon in the top bar.Scroll through your mobile site to see how it looks.

>
