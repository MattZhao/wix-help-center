---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Moving Cells in a Section"
---

When you select a stacked element, you can click the arrow buttons to [move the element up / down / left / right within the stack](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-stacking-elements#managing-a-stack). Currently, this option is not available for selected cells in a section. It is also not possible to use the arrow keys on your keyboard to move cells.

As an alternative, you can use [drag and drop to swap a cell with another in the section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-customizing-a-section-grid#swapping-and-flipping-cells-in-the-grid).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
