title: "Editor X: Adding the Restaurants Orders App"
---

Take and manage online orders directly from Editor X with the Restaurants Orders app. You can add pickup and delivery options for customers to choose from, get notifications for incoming orders and design the Online Ordering page so it really suits the restaurant's look and feel.

### Show me how to add the Restaurants Orders app

1. Click the App Market icon  on the top of Editor X.
2. Start typing "Restaurant Orders" in the search field and select it from the list.
3. Click **Add to Site**.

**In this article, learn how the make the most out of the Restaurants Orders app:**

### Adding Pickup and Delivery Options

As you [set up the Restaurants Orders app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-online-ordering-for-your-restaurant), you can choose which fulfillment options your customers should see when placing an order:

**Pickup:** Customers can [pick up their orders from your restaurant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-offering-pickup-for-orders), or have it brought to them right outside your restaurant (e.g., to your parking lot).   **Delivery: **Choose [which areas you deliver to](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-delivery-areas-for-orders) and set different costs and times for each area. You can also switch delivery areas on and off, based on your current availability.  **Contactless Dine-In: **Customers can [scan a QR code to view your menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-contactless-dine-in), place orders online, pay, and get their food delivered to their table.

> **Located in the US?**
>
> You can also [use DoorDash](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-doordash-drive) to fulfill your online orders.

### Setting Up Additional Restaurant Locations

The Restaurants Orders app lets you [create multiple menus for your restaurant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-displaying-multiple-restaurant-menus-on-your-site). For example, you can have a dinner menu, lunch menu and dessert menu – all with different sections, dishes and/or prices.

---

### Customizing Your Menu

[The menu your customers see](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-creating-your-menus) on the Online Ordering page is made up of sections and dishes. The default menu comes with two sections, but you can add as many as you need to best showcase your dishes (e.g., Starters, Main Courses, etc.)

Give every dish on your menu a name and a description so customers know what they're ordering. You can even label dishes to help diners identify key information about them (e.g., Vegan, Gluten Free, etc.)

> **Tip:**
>
> At any time, you can re-order your [items, sections or menus](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-creating-your-menus#step-4-reordering-menus-sections-or-items-in-your-restaurant-menu) to help your diners find what they're looking for.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [create multiple menus to display](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-displaying-multiple-restaurant-menus-on-your-site) on this page. For example, show separate menus for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

### Designing the Online Ordering Page

[Personalize the Online Ordering page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-designing-your-online-ordering-page) so it matches your restaurant's brand. Choose the page's fonts and colors, decide which info to display about every dish and customize the design of your dish labels.

> **Tip:**
>
> This page has built-in responsive features, so it's already designed to look ideal on every screen size. Click your site's breakpoints to see how the layout automatically adjusts to the viewport.

---

### Managing and Fulfilling Your Orders

The Restaurants Orders app allows you to [manage incoming orders directly from your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-managing-orders). The **My Orders **area is divided to three tabs according to status, so you can easily understand which orders need to be fulfilled.

When a new order comes in, you hear a sound and see a red alert in the **My Orders** tab. You can then choose how to proceed – accept the order or reject it if you're unable to fulfill it.

> **Tip:**
>
> Our system also allows you to [automatically print incoming orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-printing-an-order#setting-up-automatic-printing-using-your-printers-email-address), using a network / WiFi ready printer.
