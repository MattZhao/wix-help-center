---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Applying Text Scaling to Button Text"
---

When formatting a text element, you can make it responsive to different screen sizes by applying the [Scale Text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-text#designing-and-formatting-text) option. Currently, this option isn't available for text appearing on buttons.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
