### About folders

A folder is a menu item that is not clickable. When you hover over it, it has subpages in a drop-down menu.

### Adding a folder

Use folders to organize your visitor's navigation through your site.

#### To add a folder:

Click **Menus &** **Pages **on the left side of the Editor.Click the **Add a Folder **icon  at the bottom.Type the name for your folder and click **Done**. This text appears on your site menu.Create the subpages for your folder:
1. Click a page that you want to become a subpage.
2. Drag it under the folder page.

---

### Deleting a folder

You can reorganize your menus & pages at any time. Delete a folder if you no longer need it.

#### To delete a folder:

Click **Menus &** **Pages **on the left side of the Editor.Click the relevant page.Click the **Show More** icon .Click **Delete **.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Preview your site to see the folder in action.
> - It is not possible to add a second-level menu folder (i.e. a menu on a menu folder).
> - The [hover design option](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-horizontal-menu-design) in menus does not apply to menu folders.
