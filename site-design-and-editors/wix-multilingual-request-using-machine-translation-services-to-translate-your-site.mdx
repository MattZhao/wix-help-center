---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Using Machine Translation Services to Translate Your Site"
---

With [Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-an-overview), you can save time by automatically translating your site's content using a built-in translation tool. Currently, Wix doesn't support the integration of other third-party translation services.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
