---
title: "Wix Editor: Renaming the 'More' Item in Your Menu"
---

The "More" item in your menu appears when your menu is not wide enough to fit all of the items on it.

If you hover over it in [preview](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site#previewing-your-site) mode, you can see that a drop-down menu opens with the items which do not fit on the menu.

### To change the "More" menu item text:

Click your menu in the editor.  Click the **Layouts** icon .  Edit the item text under **Show an item that says:**.

> **Tip:**
>
> To remove the "More" item, make your menu wider. Once there is enough room for all the pages, the "More" item is removed.
