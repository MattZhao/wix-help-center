title: "Wix Editor: About Your Site's Header"
---

Your site's header appears across the top of your site, throughout all of your pages. The header gives visitors their first impression of your site, and usually contains important information and elements.

---

### Add important content to your header

Since your site header is the first thing your visitors see, it's important to optimize it for your brand or business. One of the best ways to do this is by adding relevant information and elements.

As well as regular elements, you can also add features from apps that you've added to your site. For example, if you have Wix Stores you can add the cart icon to the header. Adding these elements to the header means your visitors can always access important information, no matter what page they're on. [Learn how to add elements to your site header](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-placing-elements-in-your-header-footer-and-page#attaching-elements-to-the-header)

---

### Customize your header to suit your site

As well as adding elements to your header, we also recommend designing it to fit with your site's theme and palette. There are a number of ways to personalize your header:

**Choose and customize the design:** You can choose from a number of design presets, and customize settings such as the color, border, and corners to make it look how you want.  **Resize the header: **If you find that your header isn't the right size, you can always make it bigger or smaller by using the stretch handle.  **Add a scroll effect:** Choose from a number of exciting scroll effects to create something eye-catching and personal to your site.

Customizing your header can ensure you make a lasting impression on your site visitors, and encourage them to continue browsing your site to learn more about your brand or business. [Learn how to customize your site's header](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-site-header)

### FAQs

### Can I delete the header from my site?

It is not possible to delete the header from your site, however you can change the layout to one with no header or footer. [Learn how to change the page layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-the-header-and-footer-on-a-specific-page)

### Can I hide my header on specific pages?

You can hide your site header and footer from specific pages on your site by changing the page's layout. It is not possible to only hide the header, and not the footer. [Learn how to hide the header and footer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-the-header-and-footer-on-a-specific-page)

### Can I move the header from the top of my page?

It is not possible to move the header to a different part of your site page. However, you can resize it by clicking and dragging the drag handles. [Learn how to resize your header](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-site-header#resizing-your-header)

### Elements are disappearing from my site header. What do I do?

Elements may disappear from, or fall behind your site header if the header has a Freeze scroll effect, or if the elements are not set to show on all pages. [Learn how to resolve this issue](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-fixing-spacing-and-alignment-issues-on-your-site#elements-disappearing-from-the-site-header)

### Why can't I see my header in the Layers panel?

Your site header is not displayed in the [Layers panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-the-layers-panel) if it doesn't contain any elements. However, you can still select and adjust your header by clicking it.
