---
title: "Wix Editor: Navigating Between Your Site Pages"
---

If you want to view or edit a page on your site, you can access it from the Switch Page panel on the top bar of your editor.

### To switch between your pages:

Click the Switch Page** **panel on the top left of the editor.  Select the relevant page.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also navigate between your pages by clicking on the tabs on your site menu in preview mode. [Learn how to preview your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site#previewing-your-site)
