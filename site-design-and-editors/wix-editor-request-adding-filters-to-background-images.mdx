---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding Filters to Background Images"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add filters directly to background images, only to [single or gallery images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-and-customizing-images#applying-filter-effects-to-images) on your site.

### In the meantime you can:

Access the relevant image in the Media Manager.
 Hover over it and click the **Edit** icon .
 Edit the image.
 Click **Apply**.
 Click **Save**.
 [Add the edited image to your background.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-an-image-to-your-page-background)

> **Note:**
>
> You can only edit images that you have uploaded yourself.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
