---
title: "Wix Media: Uploading Single Tracks"
---

Upload single tracks to your Media Manager and add them to your site with the [Wix Audio Player.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-audio-player)

### Which file types are supported?

You can upload the following file types to your Wix site: MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, WMA, AAC, AIF and AIFF.
Note: To upload .wav files, use the [Wix Music Player](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-music).

### To upload single tracks:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **Upload Media** icon  .
4. Choose your file(s) from your computer.
5. (Optional) Select the file and click **Add to Page **to add it to your site.
[image: Screenshot of upload media icon button in the Wix Editor.]
