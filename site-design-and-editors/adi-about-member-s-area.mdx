---
title: "ADI: About Member's Area"
---

A Member's Area allows visitors to sign up to your site and gain access to members only pages and services. After signing up, members can log in and out using the login button.

Once logged in, each member has a private "My Account" page where they can add their personal details. This page can only be seen by the logged in member.

### Use the Member's Area on sites with Wix Stores

Wix Stores includes a My Orders page where customers can check the status of their orders and view past orders.

[Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-your-customers-member-pages)

### Use the Member's Area on sites with Wix Bookings

Wix Bookings includes a My Bookings page where clients can reschedule appointments or re-book past appointments.

[Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-my-bookings-page)

### Use the Member's Area on sites with the new Wix Blog

The new Wix Blog includes an About page where members can describe themselves. They also have the following tabs:
- Settings
- Notifications
- Blog Comments
- Blog Likes
- Blog Posts (visible to members with writing privileges)
- My Drafts (visible to members with writing privileges)

[Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-using-the-members-area-in-wix-blog)

> **Note:**
>
> You can view and manage your members in your dashboard's [**Member List**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-viewing-your-members)[ tab](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-viewing-your-members).
