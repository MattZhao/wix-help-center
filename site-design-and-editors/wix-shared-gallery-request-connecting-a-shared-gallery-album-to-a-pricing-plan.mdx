---
title: "Wix Shared Gallery Request: Connecting a Shared Gallery Album to a Pricing Plan"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect a shared gallery album to a pricing plan. This feature would allow you to restrict access to certain albums to the members or groups who are subscribed to a pricing plan. This means that only the people that have purchased the pricing plan for that album can access the album and its contents.
