title: "Wix Photo Albums: Supported Media Sizes and Formats"
---

Below are the file types and sizes that are supported in Wix Photo Albums.

> **Note:**
>
> Your album's loading time is affected by the number and size of photos that you add.

Media TypeSupported FilesFile Size LimitsRecommendationsImagesJPG, GIF, PNG40MBJPG is the recommended format for Photo Albums. However, PNG is recommended if your image requires transparency. VideosAVI, MPEG, MPG, MPE, MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, OGV, VOB, M4V, 3PG, DivX, XVID or embed from YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook, Dailymotion or Twitch.1000MB

The recommended resolution is 1920 x 1080px

> **Tip:**
>
> For optimum performance, we recommend having less than:
> - 150 photos per upload
> - 400 photos per album
> - 4000 albums per site
