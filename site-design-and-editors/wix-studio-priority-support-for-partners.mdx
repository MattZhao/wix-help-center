---
title: "Wix Studio: Priority Support for Partners"
---

Get your questions answered promptly and tailored to your business needs. As a Wix Partner, you get priority support when you request a callback or submit a ticket.

> **Tip:**
>
> Check out the [operating hours for live chat support](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support).

### To get priority support:

[Go to your Wix Studio workspace](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/discover).  Click** Help** at the top.  Click on **Contact Customer Care** **to get professional support **under **Chat with us**.  Click on the topic of the issue you're experiencing and follow the directions on your screen to get in touch with priority support.

> **To streamline your support, please provide the following when contacting us:**
>
> - A description of the issue.
> - The domain name (URL) you need help with.
> - The site you need help with.
> - The page name you need help with (if applicable).
> - Any relevant details that can help our Customer Care team.
