### Notifications examples

Editor notifications typically provide you either with suggestions to help you build your site, or potential outcomes for the action you have just performed. This is to ensure that you can create the best site for your needs, and a great browsing experience for your visitors.

Notifications that contain tips may provide you with an easier alternative for building your site. For example, if you undo several actions in the editor, a notification pop-up can appear to remind you to use Site History to view and restore earlier versions of your site.

Additional notifications may populate when you perform an action that can affect your site's look / function, and advise you of the potential result. This is to make sure that you and your visitors can easily access all of the content on your live site, as well as enjoy everything it has to offer.

> **Tip:**
>
> Most editor notifications contain links to tools, settings, or further information, so you can make changes or learn more with just one click.

### FAQs

### How do I close editor notifications?

You can close pop-up notifications in the editor by clicking the **Close** icon .

### Can I disable notifications in the editor?

It is currently not possible to disable notifications in the editor.

### Are error notifications the same as editor notifications?

Editor notifications contain tips and raise awareness for building your site, however error notifications appear when there is an issue with editing or saving, previewing, and publishing your site. Some examples of reasons for error notifications include the editor being open in 2 or more different browser windows, or technical issues.

### I received an error notification while working on my site. What do I do?

Error messages in the editor usually provide solutions to resolve the issue, such as checking your internet connection or logging back into your account. If the error is on our side, or if following the steps doesn't resolve the issue, you can contact us with the following details:

- The site you are experiencing the issue on
- The error number (if applicable)
- The steps you have taken to resolve the issue
