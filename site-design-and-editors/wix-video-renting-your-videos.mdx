---
title: "Wix Video: Renting Your Videos"
---

You can set up your video settings to allow your viewers to watch them online. Viewers can watch online for a set price with a set amount of time to watch.

> **Important:**
>
> - Your site visitors need to sign up/become a site member in order to purchase videos, subscriptions, and also rent videos.
> - Video rental periods begin when your site visitors purchase them.
> - Videos can only be rented for a maximum of 72 hours.
> - Social Media videos cannot be rented.
> - Videos set to rent cannot be downloaded.

### To Rent Your Videos:

[Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.  Select the checkbox to the left of the video or videos that you want to rent.  Click **Set Pricing **at the top of your list of videos.  Select **Paid**.  Select **Video can be rented**.  Enter the price and length of the rental availability.  Click **Save**.
