---
title: "Wix Editor Request: In-Page Menus on Mobile"
---

Currently, in-page menus on mobile sites are not supported.

However, anchors and sections themselves are supported on mobile sites, and you can add them as an item to your site menu. [Learn more about adding anchors and sections to site menus](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-anchors-and-in-page-menus-on-your-mobile-site)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
