---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Full-Screen Mode for Gallery Images"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set your gallery images to open in full-screen mode when clicked. You can [set your images to open in a pop-up](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-setting-your-images-to-open-in-a-pop-up-when-clicked) (expand mode), in which case the images to open much larger, however there is always a black border around the images.

> **Want an alternative?**
>
> You can [add a Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-an-overview) to your site, and [customize the expand mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-expand-mode-for-your-gallery) to display images in full-screen.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
