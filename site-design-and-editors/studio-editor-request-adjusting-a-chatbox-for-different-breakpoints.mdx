---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adjusting a Chatbox for Different Breakpoints"
---

Currently, the [customizations you make to the chatbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-chat) automatically apply to all breakpoints. It is not possible to adjust the chatbox's look and settings per breakpoint.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
