---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Customizing the Layouts for Your Multilingual Sites"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add, reposition or resize elements only in a specific language. When you customize or add an element, it appears the same across all languages of your multilingual site.

For example, if you add an image to the English version of your site, it appears in the Spanish version as well. You can display a different image in the Spanish version, but you cannot display an image in English and not display an image at all in Spanish.

In the meantime, you can use Velo by Wix to [change the direction of your menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-tutorial-changing-the-direction-of-menu-tabs-based-on-the-current-language), or [add a multi-state box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-using-multi-state-boxes-to-switch-from-ltr-to-rtl) that displays different content based on the selected language.
