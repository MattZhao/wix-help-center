---
title: "Request: Exposing a Test Site to Only Desktop or Mobile Devices"
---

Currently, the percentage exposure refers to the percentage of your site visitors that see the test site when they access your live site on desktop and mobile devices. You cannot expose the test site to just desktop, or just mobile devices.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
