---
title: "ADI Request: Freezing Your Footer"
---

Currently you cannot freeze your footer into a fixed position inside ADI.

If you want to freeze your footer, you should [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-site-footer).
