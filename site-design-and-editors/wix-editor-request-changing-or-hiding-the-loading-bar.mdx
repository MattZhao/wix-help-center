---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Changing or Hiding the Loading Bar"
---

Currently, it is not possible to hide or edit the media loader bar that appears on your site when certain media items are loading (e.g. galleries, pictures and videos).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
