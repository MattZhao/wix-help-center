---
title: "Wix Editor: Previewing Your Password Protection Window"
---

If you password protect a page on your site, your site visitors are required to enter the password to view the content of that page.

The password window looks like the example screenshot below:

> **Important:**
>
> You can only see the password window on your live site. It does not appear when previewing your site.

### To view your password protection window:

[Publish](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site) your site.  Access your [live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site#viewing-your-live-site).  Navigate to the password protected page.
