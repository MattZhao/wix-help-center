---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Correcting a Customer's Email Address After Purchase"
---

Currently, it is not possible to edit or correct customer email addresses in order to resend purchased files. If your customer accidentally used the wrong email address when purchasing from your Photo Album store, you can send the images to them via email, Google Drive, or Dropbox.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
