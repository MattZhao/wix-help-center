---
title: "Wix Video: Displaying a Channel in Your Wix Video Player"
---

When you add Wix Video, a video player is added to your site. Use it to display one of your video channels.

### To change the channel displayed on your player:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the Wix Video player element.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Videos **tab.  Select **Channel** and choose your channel.

> **Want to showcase a single video?**
>
> To learn how to display one video, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-displaying-a-single-video-in-your-wix-video-player).
