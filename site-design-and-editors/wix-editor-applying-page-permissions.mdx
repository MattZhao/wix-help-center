---
title: "Wix Editor: Applying Page Permissions"
---

Choose who has access to your site's pages. You may want to restrict access to some of your pages to those who have a password or are approved site members.

### To apply permissions to a page:

Click **Pages & Menu **** **in your Editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the More Actions icon .  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Permissions **tab.  Select one of the following options under** Who can view this page?**: 

- **Everyone**: The page is public and open to all visitors.
- **Password Holders**: Only people who have the password can view this page. 

- Enter a password.
- **Members Only**: Your site visitors have to be members to see this page. They are asked to sign in or register if they try to access this page.

- Choose which members can access the page:

- **All members: **Any logged-in member can view the page.
- **Only selected members or paying customers:** Select members with specific roles and / or [pricing plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-an-overview).

> **Important:**
>
> If you select Members Only, be sure to [add a Member's area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-add-and-set-up-a-members-area) to your site.
