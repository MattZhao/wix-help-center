---
title: "Wix Editor: Cropping and Editing Gallery Images"
---

The Wix Photo Studio allows you to crop and edit images on your site without leaving the Editor. Use it to enhance images, change their orientation and add filter effects!

> **Note:**
>
> You can only edit gallery images that you have uploaded yourself.

### To crop or edit a gallery image:

Click the gallery.Click **Media Manager **or **Change Images **(depending on the gallery you are using).Click **Add Media**.Click **Image**.Click the relevant image in the Media Manager.Click **Crop & Edit**.Edit your image using the tools.
 **Note:** Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools) to learn about the editing tools.When you are finished, click **Save**.
