---
title: "ADI: Adding a Button To Your Site"
---

Add a button to encourage your visitors to complete a specific action like subscribing to your blog or visiting another page of your site.

Most sections of your ADI site come with a button you can show or hide. You can also add a secondary button and customize what the buttons on your site say and where they link to.

### To add a button:

Click the relevant page section.Click **Edit**.Select or deselect the **Show/Hide** icon next to the **Buttons **field:
- **Selected**: The button is shown.
- **Unselected:** The button is hidden.
(Optional) Click **+ Add Button** to add another button.Enter the text for your button.Click **Link** and select the link for your button.Click **Done**.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The style of your secondary button is based on the settings of your first button.
> - The option of adding a second button may not be supported in some sections of your ADI site.
