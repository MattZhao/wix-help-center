---
title: "Album Images Appear Broken in Dashboard/Media Window/Live Site Due to Missing Watermark (Includes Workaround)"
---

We are aware of an issue where albums display broken images in the Dashboard, Editor, Manage Media window and live site.

> **Meanwhile, in order to resolve the issue, please re-upload the watermark file and publish the affected album again:**
>
> 1. Go to **Photo Albums** in your site's dashboard.
> 2. Click **Manage Album** next to the relevant album.
> 3. Click the drop-down in the **Watermark** section to expand it.
> 4. Click **Customize watermark**.
> 5. Click **Upload Watermark** button to upload an image file from your computer.
> 6. Click **Save**.
> 7. Publish album.
