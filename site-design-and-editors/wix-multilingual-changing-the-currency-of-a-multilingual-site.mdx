---
title: "Wix Multilingual: Changing the Currency of a Multilingual Site"
---

You can change your Site Currency at anytime. The currency you choose will be the same currency displayed across all languages.

> **Important:**
>
> It is currently not possible to display a different currency per language version of your multilingual site. When you change your Site Currency this change applies to all languages. If this is a feature you would like to see in the future, you can vote for it [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-request-different-currencies-per-language).

### To change your site's currency:

Click Settings on the left of your Dashboard.  Click **Language & Region **in the General Settings section.  Scroll down to the **Site Currency** section.  Select a currency from the drop-down list.  Click **Save**.
