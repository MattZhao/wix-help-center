---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Footer-Specific Menu"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a menu that has been designed and built specifically for your site footer.

As an alternative, you can [add a site or anchor menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-site-menu) to your footer, and customize its design to look different to any additional menus you have on the page.

> **Important:**
>
> If you have more than one site menu on your page, any changes you make to the items in one menu affect the others. For example, if you hide a page from the menu in the header, it is also hidden from the menu in the footer, and vice versa.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
