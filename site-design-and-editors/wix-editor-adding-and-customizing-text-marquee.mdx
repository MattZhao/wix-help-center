title: "Wix Editor: Adding and Customizing Text Marquee"
---

Enhance user engagement effortlessly with text marquee—a running text element that instantly grabs attention, showcases content dynamically, and requires no coding.

In the example below, there's a text marquee element that reads "sale is on", drawing attention to the information shown above it.

> **Tip**
>
> This feature is not yet available in all Wix accounts.

---

### Adding text marquee

Head over to the Add Elements panel to add a new text marquee element to your site. After selecting a design, enter your text.

> **Note:**
>
> You can enter up to 450 characters.

#### To add text marquee:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Text**.  Click **Text Marquee**.  Drag a design you like onto your page.  Enter your text:

1. Click **Change Text**.
2. Enter your text under **A****dd your own text**.

### Designing the text marquee element

Using the text marquee, you can customize the way the text looks and moves. Change the text formatting, capitalization and spacing to meet your needs. Fine-tune it even more by adjusting the separator icon, speed and direction.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Click the **Stretch** icon  on the element to make it full width.
> - Click the **Link** icon  to [add a link](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-link-to-an-element#adding-a-link-to-an-element) to the element.
> - Click the **Connect to CMS** icon  to connect the element to your [content management system](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-elements-to-a-collection-using-a-dataset).

#### To design the text marquee element:

Select the relevant text marquee element.  Customize your text marquee using the available options under **Design**:

### Text

- Change the [theme](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-sites-theme-and-design), font, size, color and formatting (e.g. bold, italics).
- Adjust the capitalization to your liking (e.g. uppercase, title case).
- Adjust the spacing between characters, as well as the spacing between the text and the separator icon.

### Separator

- Click the **Replace** icon   to change the shape. You can choose from many different designs in the Media Manager, or upload your own SVG.
- Click the **Remove** icon  to delete the separator.
- Adjust the **Shape color** and **Shape size**.

### Movement

- **Speed: **Drag the** **slider to make the text move faster or slower.
- **Direction: **Make the text move from left to right or right to left.
- **Pause on hover: **Enable the toggle to make the text stop moving when visitors hover over it.

**Tip: **You can rotate the element so the text moves vertically.
