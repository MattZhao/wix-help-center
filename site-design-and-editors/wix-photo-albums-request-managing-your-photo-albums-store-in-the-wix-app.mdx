---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Managing Your Photo Albums Store in the Wix App"
---

While it is possible to view your Photo Albums Store orders, currently, it is not possible to manage your Photo Albums Store in [the Wix app on your mobile device](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-managing-photo-albums-in-the-wix-owner-app) - for example, switching from digital to digital and prints, and changing the pricing etc.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
