### Redeeming free business cards

Create and redeem 250 free business cards directly from the Wix Logo Maker.

#### To redeem free business cards:

Go to the [Brand Studio](http://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh).  Click the **My Brands** drop-down at the top.  Select the relevant brand.

Click **Brand Actions** at the top.  Select **Print Logo on Merchandise**.  Click **Get Started** beside **Business Cards**.  Hover over the template you like best and click **Select.**  Add your business details and click** Save & Continue.**  Click **Add to Cart** (In the cart, the price of the business cards should be 0).  Choose whether you want to customize the design.  Click **Continue** to place an order.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about redeeming free business cards:

### Can I pay for my business cards order now and save my 250 free business cards for later?

Currently, it's not possible to pay for business cards now and save your 250 free business cards for later. The free business cards benefit will be applied to the first lot of business cards you order.

### Can I change my business cards' paper quality?

The free business cards that come with the Unlimited and Business Logo Maker plans are printed on preset weighted paper, and cannot be changed. If you want to change your cards' paper quality, you must pay full price.

### Can I use my free business cards offer with another logo in my account?

Currently, it is only possible to use the free business cards offer with the Wix Logo included in the Unlimited and Business Wix Logo Plan you purchased. You cannot apply the free cards to another Wix Logo in your account.
If this is something you'd like to see in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-request-using-free-business-cards-with-another-logo-in-your-account).

### Can I transfer my Logo plan to a different account and redeem my free business cards there?

Currently, you can't redeem your free business cards from a different account. If you transfer your Unlimited or Business logo plan to another account, you will still have to redeem your free business cards from the original account.

### Can site collaborators redeem free business cards?

Currently, only the site owner can redeem free business cards.
