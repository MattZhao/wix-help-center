---
title: "Deleting Photos in the Shared Gallery App"
---

If you no longer wish to display media in your Shared Gallery, you can remove it either from your live site or in your site's dashboard.

Site admins and [members with permission](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-the-members-area-with-the-shared-gallery-app) to manage a gallery can delete any images or videos in the gallery. Members can delete any images or videos that they uploaded themselves.

> **Important:**
>
> It's not possible to recover images and videos after you delete them.

**In this article, learn how to delete content:**

---

### Deleting an image or video from your live site

You can delete a photo or video if you decide you no longer wish to display it on your site.

#### To delete content from your live site:

Go to the Shared Gallery app on your live site.  Log in using the email address associated with your Wix account.  (Optional) If you've created albums, select the album that has the image or video you want to delete. [Learn more about albums](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-and-managing-albums-in-the-shared-gallery-app#adding-photos-and-videos-to-an-album).  Click the **More Actions** icon  above the image or video.

Choose what you want to do:

### Delete a single image or video

1. Select **Delete **from the drop-down menu.
2. Click **Delete **in the pop-up to confirm.

### Delete multiple images or videos

1. Choose **Select **from the dropdown menu.
2. Select the images or videos that you want to delete.
3. Click **Delete **in the top right corner of the app.
4. Click **Delete **in the pop-up to confirm.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Delete All Media** from the **Settings** tab in your Editor to reset your gallery and delete all images and videos at once.[image: A screenshot showing the Delete All Media button in the settings panel of the Shared Gallery app in the editor.]

---

### Deleting an image or video from your site's dashboard

You can also remove content from your Shared Gallery directly from your site's dashboard.

#### To delete content from your dashboard:

[Go to Shared Gallery](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/app/476dc2dd-e16e-43b0-a0aa-1a486c78fbe2?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Shared+Gallery) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Open** next to the album that has the image or video you want to delete.  Choose what you want to do:

### Delete a single image or video

1. Click the **More Actions** icon  on the image or video.
2. Select **Delete item.**
3. Click **Delete** in the pop-up to confirm.

### Delete multiple images or videos

1. Select the images or videos you wish to delete.
2. Click **Delete** from the menu on the top right.
3. Click **Delete** in the pop-up to confirm.
