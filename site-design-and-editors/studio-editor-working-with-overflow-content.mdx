Some site elements and content may be too large for their page, section, or container, and flow outside the edges. You can set how overflow content behaves on specific sections or containers, or on an entire page.

### Setting overflow content in sections and containers

Choose how you want overflow content to look on page sections and containers. You can hide /show the content, or set the overflow to show on scroll.

#### To set your overflow content in sections and containers:

Click the relevant container or section in the Editor.  Click the Open Inspector arrow  at the top right of the Editor.  Click **Layout** under the **Design** tab .  Open the **Overflow content** drop-down and choose how you want the content to behave:

- **Show:** Overflow content is displayed on your site, even if it is too large for the container / section.
- **Hide:** The relevant elements are clipped, and the parts that overflow aren't visible to visitors.
- **Scroll:** Visitors can view the overflow content by scrolling.

- **Scroll direction:** Select whether visitors can view the content by scrolling horizontally, vertically, or both.
- **Show scrollbar:** Click the toggle to show / hide the scrollbar on your live site.

---

### Setting overflow content on pages

You can hide or show content overflow on your site page to suit your design needs. As the content flows outside the page edges, a scrollbar is automatically added.

#### To set your overflow content on pages:

Click **Layers**  on the left of the Studio Editor.  Select the page you're currently working on.  Click the Open Inspector arrow  at the top right of the Editor.  Click **Size** under the **Design** tab .  Open the **Overflow content** drop-down and choose how you want the content to behave:

- **Show:** Overflow content is displayed on your live site, even if it is too large for the page. A scroll bar is automatically added so your visitors can see the rest of the content.
- **Hide:** The relevant elements are clipped, and the parts that overflow aren't visible to visitors.

### FAQs

Select a question to learn more about overflow content

### Is my overflow content automatically set?

Pages / sections / containers are set to show the overflowing content by default.

### What happens if I display page overflow content?

When you set page overflow content to show, a scrollbar is automatically added so visitors can see the content. Displaying page overflow content may also create gaps.

### Can I choose the overflow settings for different breakpoints?

Yes. As the Studio Editor uses a cascading system, changes you make on a larger breakpoint affect the smaller breakpoints. However, you can then separately adjust the overflow settings of smaller breakpoints as needed by clicking a breakpoint icon at the top of the Editor.
