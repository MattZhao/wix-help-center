---
title: "ADI: Adding and Setting up a Payment Form"
---

Increase your sales by adding a payment option to your forms, allowing visitors to purchase a product or service while submitting a form on your site. Once you add a form to your ADI site you can update its settings to start accepting payments.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Accepting payments with Wix Forms is only available with Business and eCommerce Premium plans. Learn more about [upgrading Wix Forms with a Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-upgrading-wix-forms) and [connecting your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview) to a payment method.

---

### Step 1 | Add a form to your site

You can add a payment option to any of the forms available in ADI. First add a new section, then choose a type of form.

#### To add a form to your site:

Go to the page in ADI where you want to add the form.  ﻿Hover ﻿over **Add** at the top.  Click **Section to Page**.  Scroll down to **Forms **and choose a type of form.   Pick a section design for your form and click and drag it onto your site.

---

### Step 2 | Choose a type of payment form

Next, edit your form's settings and choose a type of payment form. Make sure you've [upgraded to a Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-upgrading-wix-forms) first.

#### To choose a payment form:

Click the section with the form in ADI.  Click **Edit** next to **Form **at the bottom of the section panel.

Click **Form Settings**.  Click **Get Started** under **Get paid with this form**.  Choose a type of payment form. Depending on the type you choose you will see different options.

### Single item

Offer one item on your form at a fixed price.
1. Select **Single Item**.
2. Click **Next**.
3. Enter a name for the item under **Item Name**.
4. Enter the price under **Item Price**.

### List of items

Visitors select one item from a list on the form. You can add up to 25 items to the list.
1. Select** List of Items**.
2. Click **Next**.
3. Click **+ Add Item**.
4. Enter a name for the first item under **Item Name**.
5. Enter the price for the first item under **Item Price**.
6. Click **Back** at the top.
7. Click** + Add Item **again to add a second item to your list.
8. Repeat steps 4-6 for each item on your list.
**Tip: **Hover over an item in the list and click the Edit icon  to make changes or the Delete icon  to remove it.

### Visitors enter price

Let visitors enter the amount they wish to pay. This is great for charitable donations. etc.
1. Select** Visitors Enter Price**.
2. Click **Next**.
3. Enter a name for the item under **Item Name**.
4. Enter the **Default Amount** you want to suggest to your visitors.
5. Enter a **Minimum Amount **your visitors can pay (e.g. $1 USD).
6. Enter a** Maximum Amoun**t your visitors can pay (e.g. $100 USD).

6.  Click **Next** to connect a payment method.

---

### Step 3 | Connect a payment method

The final step of adding a payment option to your form is connecting a payment method. You set this up in the Accept Payments area in your site's dashboard.

> **Note:**
>
> If you've previously connected a payment method, you can skip steps 1-2 below.

#### To connect a payment method:

Click **Connect Payment Method**.   Connect a payment method from [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2F) in your site's dashboard. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview)

**Note:** If you haven't yet upgraded to a Premium plan, you will be asked to upgrade at this step.  Once a payment method is connected, click **Next **on the form.

Click **Done** to finish setup.

> **Tips:**
>
> - You can change the payment method and remove it at any time. Click the Settings icon  next to **Payment Settings **status and choose an option.
> - You can also click **View Payments **to see how many people have paid with this form.
