title: "Wix Media: Accessing Wix Photo Studio"
---

Wix Photo Studio is an all-in-one hub where you can enhance and edit your images. It's quick, easy and fun. How you access Photo Studio depends on the editor you're using to build your site.

### Which images can I edit?

You can only edit images that you have uploaded, created using the [AI Image Creator](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-images-with-the-wix-ai-image-creator), or images from Unsplash. [Shutterstock images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-purchasing-shutterstock-images-videos) can be edited, but not downloaded after editing. It's against the [Wix Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use)* *to edit free Wix images.

### Accessing Wix Photo Studio from your editor

Access Wix Photo Studio directly from your editor.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.
3. Select the image you want to edit.
4. Click **Crop & Edit** or **Adjust **in the **Actions** section.
[image: A screenshot of the adjustment options within the Media Manager.]
1. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Site Files **on the left.
3. Hover over the image you want to edit and click the **Crop & Edit **icon .
[image: Screenshot of clicking the icon to crop and edit an image from the Media Manager.]

> You can also access the Photo Studio when selecting an image on your page. Click the **Settings **icon , then click **Adjust**.

### Additional Photo Studio access points

You are also directed to the Photo Studio when:

[Uploading an image in the Wix Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-uploading-your-own-images-to-the-wix-logo-maker#customizing-your-image)  [Cropping an image in a Wix Email Marketing email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-cropping-images-in-your-campaign)  [Editing a product image in Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-images-gifs-and-videos-to-products#editing-and-cropping-images)  [Editing an image in the Wix Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-media-to-the-gallery-7954609#faqs)
