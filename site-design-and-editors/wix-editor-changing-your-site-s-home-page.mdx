---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing Your Site's Home Page"
---

Set a page as the home page of your site. The home page acts like the main page on your site and it is the page that your website address leads to.

### To change your site's home page:

Click **Pages ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the Show More icon .  Click **Set as Homepage**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you no longer wish this page to be your home page, you have to select a new home page.
