title: "ADI: Adding and Editing a Slideshow"
---

Add a stunning slideshow to showcase images on your site. It can include your own images, free Wix images or stock images. You can also add text and a different button to each slide.

Each slide in the slideshow is displayed one at a time:

## Step 1 | Add a slideshow:

### Adding a slideshow

- Clik **Add **on the top bar.
- Click **Section to Page**.
- Choose a section that contains multiple images (like **Services **for example).
- Scroll through the different layouts to find one with the **Slideshow **label.

## Step 2 | Edit your slideshow:

### Editing a slide

1. Hover over the slideshow on your site and click **Edit**.
2. Hover over the relevant slide and click the **Edit** icon ﻿﻿ under the **Slideshow items** list.
- In the edit panel, edit the text or replace the image.
- Click the ﻿﻿ icon to show or hide an element.
- Click on the arrow at the bottom of the panel to move to the next slide.
- Click the **Reorder**﻿﻿ icon and drag the slide to the new location.

### Adding a slide

1. Hover over the slideshow on your site and click **Edit**.
- Click **+ Add Slide **under the **Slideshow items** list to add a new slide.

                   ﻿﻿

- Click the **Reorder**﻿﻿ icon and drag the slide to the new location.
- Check the **Show **icon to show or hide a slide:
- The ﻿﻿ icon indicates that the slide or field is shown.
- The ﻿﻿ icon indicates that the slide or field is hidden.

### Setting up your slideshow transition

1. Hover over your slideshow.
2. Click **Design**.
3. Click **Customize Section Design**.
4. Click **Animation**.
5. Choose a transition effect.
6. Drag the **Duration **slider to adjust the time a slide is displayed.

**Tip: **Preview your site to see your slideshow animation.

### Deleting a slide

1. Hover over the slideshow on your site and click **Edit**.
2. Click the **Settings **icon﻿﻿next to the relevant slide under the **Slideshow items** list.
3. Click **Delete**.
4. Click **Delete **to confirm.
