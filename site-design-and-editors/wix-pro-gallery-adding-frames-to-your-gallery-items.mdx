---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery: Adding Frames to Your Gallery Items"
---

Highlight your gallery items by adding frames to them. You can create unique, colorful frames using the Border and Corner Radius options available in your gallery's Design settings.

### To add frames to your Wix Pro Gallery items:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Design **tab.  Click **Item Style **and customize your frame using the following options:

### Border width & color

You can choose the thickness of your frame and its color.
**To customize your gallery items' frames:**
1. Drag the **Border Width & Color **slider to increase or decrease the width of the frame.
2. Click the color box and select a color for your frame.

### Corner Radius

Change the shape of your frame using the **Corner radius** option. You can make the frame as round or as square as you like.

> **Note:**
>
> The **Border width & color** and** Corner radius** design** **options are only available when you choose **Crop **as your thumbnail resize option in the Customize Layout panel.
