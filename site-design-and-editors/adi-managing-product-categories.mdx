### Adding products to a category

Organize your products into categories to keep them organized and to help your customers navigate around your store.

#### To add products to a category:

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/categories/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant category.  Click **Add Products**.

  Select the products you want to add.  Click **Add**.  Click **Save**.

---

### Adding a category name and image

Give your categories a name and image, so that you can easily identify them.

#### To add a name and image to a category:

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/categories/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant category.  Enter a name under **Category Name** on the right.  Add a category image:

1. Click the Plus** **icon  under **Category Image**.
2. Add an image and click **Add to Page**.

---

### Displaying a category on your site

Display your chosen category on your site by adding a product gallery / slider. Then select the category you want to display.

Click an option below to learn more:

### Adding a category to an existing page

You can display product categories on any existing page of your site. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-displaying-your-product-categories-on-your-site)

### Displaying product categories as menu items

Create a hassle-free navigation experience for your customers by displaying each product category as a menu item in your site menu. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-displaying-product-categories-as-menu-items)

### Displaying a categories on a sub-page

Create subpages dedicated to specific product categories. These pages will operate as a drop-down menu when your visitors hover over the Shop menu item. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-displaying-store-categories-on-separate-subpages)

### Changing the order of products in a category

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/categories/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant category.  Click and drag the products to change the order.  Click **Save**.

---

### Removing products from a category

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/categories/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant category.  Hover over the product you want to remove.  Click the Remove product icon .

---

### Deleting a category

You can delete a category you no longer need. If your site has a product gallery that displays that category, make sure to first remove the gallery or select a different category to display so that customers won't see a blank gallery.

> **Note:**
>
> Deleting a category does not delete the products in it.

#### To delete a product category:

[Go to Categories](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Categories&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/categories/list) in your site's dashboard.  Click the Show More icon   on the category you want to delete.  Click **Delete**.  Click **Delete **to confirm.
