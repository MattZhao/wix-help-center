---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Enabling a Gradient as Your Background"
---

Currently, it is not possible to enable a gradient as a background on pages in the Wix Editor.

However, we are please to announce that you can now [add gradients to the background of your page sections, strips, and columns. ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-strip-background)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
