---
title: "Wix Editor: Creating a Multi-Page Site"
---

You can add up to 100 static pages to your Wix site, however, keep in mind that sites with a large amount of pages may take longer to load and may also affect the [loading time and performance](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-best-practices).

Follow the guidelines below to keep your content-heavy site loading fast and performing at its best:

[Use dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-multi-page-site#use-dynamic-pages)  [Add a blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-multi-page-site#add-a-blog)  [Display your media in galleries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-multi-page-site#display-your-media-in-galleries)

> **Tip:**
>
> The 100 page limit does not apply to dynamic pages, such as product pages, blog posts, and [pages connected to your CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).

---

### Use dynamic pages

Dynamic pages let you create and design one page layout that can be used over and over again, each time displaying a different item, content or image stored in a [database collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview).

Dynamic pages also allow you to easily update the look and feel of all your duplicated pages by updating your dynamic page only. If you are duplicating a page and changing each page's content or design manually, we recommend using [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-examples-of-dynamic-pages).

---

### Add a blog

If you are adding content to your site on a regular basis, posting new articles or blog post, we recommend adding Wix Blog. [Learn more about the Wix Blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-about-the-wix-blog)

---

### Display your media in galleries

With galleries you can showcase a large amount of images and allow your users to open each one of them in full-screen for better viewing. By adding galleries, you can avoid creating a new page for each one of your images. [Learn how to add and set up a gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-gallery)
