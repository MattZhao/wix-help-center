Translating your blog can expand your site's reach and improve SEO by increasing traffic and conversions. You can translate posts and categories, including their images, descriptions, and URL slugs.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> First, you need to [add and set up Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-adding-and-setting-up-wix-multilingual). You can add Wix Multilingual from your editor, your site's dashboard, or from the Wix App Market.

**In this article, learn more about: **

[Translating your blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-blog#translating-your-blog)  [Managing your translated content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-blog#managing-your-translated-content)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-blog#faqs)

### Translating your blog

With your standard blog dashboard, you can translate posts and category names along with their images, descriptions, and URL slugs.

#### To translate your blog:

[Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog) in your site's dashboard.  Decide what you want to translate:

### Category names

1. Click **Categories** in the sidebar. 

**Note: **Make sure you are in your main language tab.
2. Click the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant category.
3. Click **Translate**.
4. Select the relevant languages and click **Continue**.
5. Click **Start Translating**.
6. Translate your category's Name, Description, Image and URL Slug.
7. Click **Save**.

### Blog posts

1. Click **Posts **in the sidebar.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant post.
3. Click **Translate post**.
4. Click the **What language do you want to translate into? **drop-down and select the relevant language.
5. Choose how you want to translate your content: **Manual translation** or **Auto translation**.
6. Click **Translate**.
7. Review the draft and click **Publish**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Using the **Translate** tab in your post composer, you can also translate a post as you edit it. Click **Add **next to the relevant language to open a duplicate version of your current post and start translating in your chosen language.

### Managing your translated content

Now that you've localized some categories and posts, there are multiple ways to manage your translated content going forward.

#### To manage your translated content:

[Go to Blog](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20My%20Blog&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/blog) in your site's dashboard.  Filter your posts and categories by language to find the relevant ones:

- Click **Categories** in the sidebar and select the relevant language from the drop-down.
- Click **Posts **in the sidebar and select the relevant language from the drop-down.
    Choose what you want to do:

### Edit a post or category in a specific language

**To edit a post: **
1. Click **Edit **next to the relevant post.
2. Click the **Translate** tab in the panel on the left.
3. Click **Edit** next to the relevant language.

**To edit a category: **
1. Click **Edit **next to the relevant category.
2. Translate the content and click **Save**.

### Change the post language

You can change the language of each of your blog posts. If you translate your categories first, each blog post is automatically translated based on its category. 
**To change the post language: **
1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant post.
2. Click **Change language**.
3. Select the language you want to change to.
4. Click **Change**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Categories, post SEO settings and URLs can be localized and are unique per translation.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about translating your Wix Blog:

### Can I translate tags?

No. Currently, it is not possible to translate blog tags. Once a tag is created, it is given the same language as the post that it was created on or was assigned to. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-request-translating-wix-blog-tags).

### Can I translate my blog in the translation manager?

No. While you can see Blog in the [translation manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-using-the-translation-manager), clicking it directs you to the Blog dashboard.

### Can I translate my blog in the editor?

No. While you can see the button Edit Translation on your Blog, clicking it directs you to the [translation manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-using-the-translation-manager) which then leads you to the Blog dashboard.

### Do all languages appear on my live site?

No. Languages set to **Hidden** do not appear on your live site. To make your language visible on your site:
1. [Go to Multilingual](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fwix-multilingual%2Fmanage-languages%3F&title=Select+a+site&primaryButtonText=Go+to+Multilingual) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **Status **drop-down next to your language and select **Visible**.

[image: A screenshot of the Wix Multilingual Language Dashboard]

### Can I assign categories and tags to posts of all languages?

No. Categories and tags can only be assigned to a post of the same language.

### Is auto-translation initiated automatically for newly added languages?

No. If you add new languages after auto-translating your site, auto-translation will not be initiated automatically.

### What happens if I add a language, but I don't translate my blog content?

If you add a language, but don't translate your content, a "Posts are coming soon message" is displayed in your live site.
