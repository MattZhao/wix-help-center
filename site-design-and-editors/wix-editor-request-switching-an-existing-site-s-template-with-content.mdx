---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Switching an Existing Site's Template (with Content)"
---

It is not currently possible to transfer all your site's content, media, and SEO settings from an existing site to a new template.

> **Just want to update your site's look?**
>
> Choose your site's colors, text, and page transitions all [from the Site Design panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-sites-theme-and-design).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
