---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Designing Individual Slideshow Items"
---

Currently, it is not possible to adjust the design and layout of individual items in a slideshow repeater. Any changes you make are automatically applied to all items.

As an alternative, you can [add a flexbox to the page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-setting-up-flexboxes) and [select the slides layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-and-customizing-flexboxes) to customize each item.

> **Note:**
>
> Transition effects or auto scroll are not available on slide flexboxes.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
