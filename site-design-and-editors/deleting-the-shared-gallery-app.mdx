---
title: "Deleting the Shared Gallery App"
---

You can delete the Shared Gallery app at any time if you no longer need it. Deleting the app does not delete any of the uploaded images or videos, but does prevent members from viewing or uploading new media.

> **Note:**
>
> If you decide to re-add the Shared Gallery app at a later stage, all of your media will be restored.

### To delete the Shared Gallery app:

Go to **Pages** in your editor.  Click the More Actions** ** icon next to your Shared Gallery page.  Select **Delete**.

Click **Delete** on the pop-up.
