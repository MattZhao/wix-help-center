---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Limiting the Number of Times a Visitor Can Download an Image"
---

Currently, it is not possible to limit how many times a site visitor can download an image from your photo albums.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
