---
title: "Wix Video: Adding a Custom Logo to Videos When Embedded in Other Sites"
---

Promote your brand by using a custom logo that appears when your fans [embed your video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/allowing-others-to-embed-your-video-on-their-site) on their site. To design your own stunning logo, [get started with Wix Logo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-2200409).

> **Important:**
>
> You must have a Wix Video Premium Plan to use custom logos. The following steps only appear if you have a Premium Plan.

---

### To add a custom logo:

[Go to Video Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Video%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://manage.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to **Add Your Logos to Embedded Videos**.

**Note:** If you do not have a Premium Plan, the **Upgrade to Activate** button appears instead.  Click the Add Image icon  to upload your logo.  Select the logo files from your computer.

> **Notes:**
>
> - You can add up to 3 logo files.
> - The recommended format is transparent PNG, and the recommended size is 254x152 pixels.
> - The logo you set as default appears in the videos your visitors embed. You can manually upload a separate logo for a specific video.

---

### To display your custom logo on a video:

[Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the relevant video and click the** Edit** icon .  Scroll down and click the **Embed Options **buttton.

- If you do not have a Premium Plan, the **Upgrade to Activate** button appears instead,
    Select **Custom logo**.   Click **Choose logo**.  Select the logo you want and click **Choose logo**.   Select when the logo appears: 

- **Show on control bar**: The logo appears on the control bar.
- **Show always**: The logo appears even when there is no control bar.
