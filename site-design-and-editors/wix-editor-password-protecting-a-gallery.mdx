---
title: "Wix Editor: Password Protecting a Gallery"
---

You can set up a private gallery by password protecting the page that the gallery is placed on.

### To password protect a page:

Click **Menus &** **Pages **on the left side of the Editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the **Show More** icon .  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Permissions **tab.  Select **Password Holders**:

1. Enter a password.
2. Select the language of the login screen.
