> **Important:**
>
> Wix Photo Albums Store is not available to all users. As of February 2023, it is no longer possible to add Wix Photo Albums Store to your site. As an alternative, you can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your photos.

Prices for your items in your Photo Albums Store are set during the initial setting up of your store. You can change these prices at anytime. How to change your prices depends on what type of files you are selling in your store.

**In this article, learn more about:**

> **Note:**
>
> - Each individual image is set at the same price.
> - It is not possible to set bundle pricing for photos. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-request-bundle-pricing-for-wix-photo-albums-store) to vote for this feature.
> - Currently, it is not possible to set different prices on each album in your Photo Albums Store. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-request-setting-different-prices-on-each-of-your-albums-in-the-wix-photo-albums-store) to vote for this feature.
> - It is also not possible to set up special offers or discount coupons in your Photo Albums Store.

---

### Setting prices for digital and prints

If you've opted to sell both digital files and prints, follow the steps below to set the prices for both digital and print.

#### To set prices for digital and prints:

Go to your Photo Albums Store.  Click the **Pricing** tab.  Click the Expand** **icon  on the relevant file to edit.   Enter in your new price amount for each file.  When you're ready, click **Save**.

---

### Setting prices for digital files only

If you've opted to sell just digital files of your photos, follow the steps below to set the prices for your digital files.

#### To set prices for digital files only:

Go to your Photo Albums Store.  Click the **Licenses** tab.  Enter in your new price amount for each file.  When you're ready, click **Save**.
