---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Ability to Control the Site Scroll Smoothness (Lerp)"
---

Currently, the Studio Editor does not offer a feature that allows you to control the site scroll smoothness (using the Lerp function, for example).

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can add the [Smooth Scroll app](https://www.wix.com/app-market/smooth-scroll_2) from the Wix App Market.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
