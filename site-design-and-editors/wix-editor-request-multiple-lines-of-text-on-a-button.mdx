---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Multiple Lines of Text on a Button"
---

Currently, you cannot have button text on more than one line. The button text is displayed on one line, and ellipsis (...) are shown if the text doesn't fit.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
