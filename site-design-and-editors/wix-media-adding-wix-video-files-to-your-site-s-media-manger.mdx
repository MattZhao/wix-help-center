---
title: "Wix Media: Adding Wix Video Files to Your Site's Media Manger"
---

Add your [Wix Video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-about-wix-video) files to your site's Media Manager so that you can use them anywhere across your site. This helps you keep all your media in one place, making it easy to manage and access your videos whenever you need them.

> **Note:**
>
> This article applies to sites that already have Wix Video installed. If you've added Wix Video to a different site in your Wix account and would like to import the files to the Media Manager of the current site you're working on, [follow these steps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-importing-media-from-one-wix-site-to-another#importing-wix-video-files-from-another-site-to-your-media-manager).

### To add Wix Video files to your site's Media Manager:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.

**Note: **If you haven't uploaded media to your site yet, you can click the** Upload Media** icon  .
4. Click the folder titled **Video Library**.
5. Click the video file(s) you want to upload.
6. Click **Upload Selection**.

1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media **[image: undefined] on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Site Files** on the left.
4. Click the folder titled **Video Library**.
5. Click the video file(s) you want to upload.
6. Click **Upload Selection**.
