Bring your site to life with beautiful media. There's tons of types of media that you can add to your site including images, galleries, videos, audio and vector art.

### Uploading Your Own Media

Upload media straight from the Add panel. You can upload media from your computer, social networks, your other Wix sites and using a URL.

Once you have uploaded a media file to your Media Manager, you can use it anywhere on your site.

#### To upload media:

Click **Add ** on the top of Editor X.Click **Media**.Click **Uploads**.Click the **Upload** icon.

> **Note:**
>
> See the supported [file types and sizes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes).

---

### Adding Free Media

The Media Manager contains hundred of free Wix and Unsplash media files that you can add to your site. Just click **Media from Wix** or **Unsplash** on the left side of the Media Manager to find the media file you want.

> **Terms of Use:**
>
> - Free Wix media files are available for digital use within Wix ONLY. For more information, see the Wix [Terms of Use](http://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).
> - You can use Unsplash images only in accordance with the [Unsplash Terms of Use](https://unsplash.com/terms) and [Wix Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).

---

### Purchasing Media

Purchase professional Shutterstock images and videos directly from the Media Manager. Just click **Shutterstock **on the left side of the Media Manager to find the media file you want. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/managing-your-media/purchasing-media)
