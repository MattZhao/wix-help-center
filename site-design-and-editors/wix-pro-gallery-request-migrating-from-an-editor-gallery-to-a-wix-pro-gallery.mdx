---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Migrating from an Editor Gallery to a Wix Pro Gallery"
---

Currently, it is not possible to migrate your gallery content in an [Editor gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-gallery) to a Wix Pro Gallery. If you want to switch to a Pro Gallery, you must add a Pro Gallery and [add your media to the gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-media-to-the-gallery) manually.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
