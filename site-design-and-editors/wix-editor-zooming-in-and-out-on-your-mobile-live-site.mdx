---
title: "Wix Editor: Zooming In and Out on Your Mobile Live Site"
---

You can zoom in and out when viewing your site on mobile devices by pinching the screen.

The zoom feature also works on gallery images when they open in expand mode.

View your live site on your mobile device to check it out!

> **Note:**
>
> The zoom works in Safari and Chrome browsers.
