---
title: "Wix Video Request: Allowing Visitors to Purchase Multiple Subscriptions at Once"
---

Currently, it is not possible to allow your visitors to purchase multiple subscriptions at once in Wix Video.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
