### Adding a document button

Let visitors download documents from your site in one click by adding a document button. Once you choose your button, you can connect your document file, change your button's icon and choose whether to display the document's title or not.

#### To add a document button:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Button**.  Select a button under **Document Buttons**, or drag it the relevant location on your page.  Click the button.  Click **Change File**.  Select a file.  Click **Choose File **to connect it to your button.  (Optional) [Customize the button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-document-button) using the available options.

### Linking a document to an element

Link your element to a document of your choice. When your visitors click the element, a new browser tab opens with the document.

#### To link a document to an element:

Click the relevant element.  Click the **Link** icon .  Select the checkbox beside **Document**.  Click **Choose File**.  Click **Upload Media **and upload your document to the Media Manager.  Click **Add to Page**.  Click **Done**.

### Displaying PDF files on your site

By adding the [PDF Viewer](https://www.wix.com/app-market/pdf-viewer) app, you can upload and display PDF documents directly on your site. Choose from a variety of display options and make the links inside your PDF accessible to all users.

#### To display PDF files:

Click **Add Apps ** **  **on the left side of the editor.  Type "PDF Viewer" in the search bar.  Select **PDF Viewer** from the drop-down menu.  Click **Add to Site**. If you have more than one site, you'll get an option to select the site you want to add it to.  Click **Add to Site**. 

**Tip**: You can cut and paste the app to move it to another page on your site.  [Upload a PDF to the app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-pdf-viewer-app).  [Customize the app's layout and design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-pdf-viewer-app#step-3-customize-the-apps-layout-and-display).  [Adjust the app's settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-pdf-viewer-app#step-4-customize-the-apps-settings).

> **Tip:**
>
> Uploaded PDF files create their browser tab title based on the metadata in the file. You can configure this title with tools like [PDF Candy](https://pdfcandy.com/edit-pdf-meta.html). You should complete this before uploading your PDF to Wix, so that it displays properly once you add the PDF to your site.

### Displaying Google Drive documents on your site

With the Google Drive app, you can display any file or folder you have on your Google Drive. Just connect the app to your Google Drive and select what you want to display.

#### To display Google Drive documents:

Click **Add Apps ** **  **on the left side of the editor.  Type "Google Drive" in the search bar and select **Google Drive** from the drop-down menu.  Click **Add to Site**.  Click **OK**.  [Set up your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-google-drive-app-9434397).

> **Tip:**
>
> To learn how to limit who can access your site's Google Drive page, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-members-only-pages)

### Creating a sharing library

With the File Share app, you can create a file sharing library where people can exchange ideas and collaborate on projects. Whether you manage a school, business, or community, the File Share app is a great way to upload and share documents securely.

#### To create a sharing library:

Click **Add Apps ** **  **on the left side of the editor.  Type "Wix File Share" in the search bar and select **Wix File Share** from the drop-down menu.  Click **Add to Site**.   Click **OK**.   [Customize the app's permissions and member settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-file-share-app#step-2-customize-the-permissions-and-member-settings).  [Personalize the app's design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-adding-and-setting-up-the-file-share-app#step-3-design-your-app).
