---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Downloading all Gallery Items at Once"
---

Currently, it is not possible to download all gallery items in a Pro Gallery at once. You can however [download items individually](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-downloading-images-from-a-gallery).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
