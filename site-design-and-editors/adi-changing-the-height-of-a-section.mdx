---
title: "ADI: Changing the Height of a Section"
---

Customize the height of the sections on your site to match your business needs and emphasize the areas that are most important.

### To change the height of a section:

Hover over the section in the ADI builder that you want to change.   Click **Design.**  Click the **Customize Section Design** drop-down.   Select **Height **from the drop-down menu. 

  Click the **+** or **-** icons to enlarge or shrink the section.

> **Note:**
>
> Not all sections of ADI allow you to adjust their height, especially if doing so will negatively impact the user experience.

> **Tip:**
>
> For a shortcut, you can also click the **Show more** icon  at the top left of a section and click **Height** from the drop-down menu. If the option isn't available, the section layout doesn't support height adjustment.
