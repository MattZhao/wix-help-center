---
title: "Wix Video: Changing the Layout of Your Wix Video Player"
---

With Wix Video's flexible layout options you can control the fine details of how your visitors view your content.

### To change the layout of Wix Video Player:

Click the Wix Video player in your Editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Layout **tab.  Select a layout.

> **Tips:**
>
> Each layout displays information slightly differently. To take full control, [learn more about player settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-changing-your-player-settings).
