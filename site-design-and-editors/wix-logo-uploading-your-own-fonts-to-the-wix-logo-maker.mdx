Upload your own fonts to the Wix Logo Maker to create a unique and memorable look for your brand. You can upload fonts in a TTF format.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Uploading your own fonts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-uploading-your-own-fonts-to-the-wix-logo-maker#uploading-your-own-fonts)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-uploading-your-own-fonts-to-the-wix-logo-maker#faqs)

---

### Uploading your own fonts

Go to the [Brand Studio](http://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh).  Click the **My Logos & Brands** drop-down at the top.  Select the relevant brand.

Click **Edit Logo**.   Click the relevant text element of your logo.  Click** Change Font**.  Click **Upload Fonts**.

Click Upload Fonts in the **Manage your fonts** popup and add the font from your computer.  Click **Done**.

---

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about uploading fonts in the Wix Logo Maker:

### Can I upload fonts in other file formats?

No. Currently, it is only possible to upload fonts in a TTF format. If your font is in a different format, you can convert the original file to TTF using an online converter like [fontconverter.org](http://www.fontconverter.org/).

### Can I get support on issues relating to uploaded fonts?

No. Currently, we don't provide support for issues relating to uploaded fonts.

### Can any font be used on my site?

If uploading a font, please make sure that you have the right to use that font. Additionally, some jurisdictions have imposed restrictions of the use of different fonts. Therefore, you need to ensure that you only use the fonts that are allowed according to your jurisdiction's laws and regulations.

### Can I use the same font in both my logo and my site editor?

Yes, but you need to upload your own font. This is because not all of the fonts available in the Logo Maker are available in your site editor. Likewise, the fonts in your site editor are not all available in the Logo Maker.
