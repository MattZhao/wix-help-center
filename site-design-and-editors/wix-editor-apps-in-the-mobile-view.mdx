---
title: "Wix Editor: Apps in the Mobile View"
---

When moving from your desktop editor to your mobile editor, third-party apps that are not mobile-ready are automatically hidden, or may not display correctly. These apps are not optimized for mobile and therefore are not displayed as they are on the desktop version of your site.

However, you can choose to show the apps on the mobile version of your site if you wish.

### To display apps on your mobile site:

Click **Hidden on Mobile**  on the left side of the [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).  Click the **Show Element **icon **  **next to the relevant app.

> **Want to hide the app on your mobile site again?**
>
> Simply click the app in the mobile editor and press **Delete** on your keyboard.
