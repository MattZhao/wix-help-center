---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Searching for Content in the Translation Manager"
---

Currently, it is not possible to search for words or phrases in the Translation Manager. This feature would let you locate specific translations faster. Saving time and ensuring consistency across your website.

While a full search function is not yet available, you can still search within certain apps. To narrow your search, begin by filtering content by page or app. From there, you can search for the name or main title of the item you're looking for. For example, you can search within your Stores app by product name, but searching by product description is not yet possible.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
