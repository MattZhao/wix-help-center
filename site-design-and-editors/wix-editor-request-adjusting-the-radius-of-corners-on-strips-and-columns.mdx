---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adjusting the Radius of Corners on Strips and Columns"
---

Currently, it is not possible to adjust the radius of corners on strips or columns.

As an alternative, you can display content with container boxes, and adjust the corner radius from the design panel. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-box#customizing-your-box-design)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
