title: "ADI: Adding a Video to Your Site"
---

Add video to your site to showcase your brand, business or products as well as engage site visitors.

There are multiple ways to add videos to your site. You can add stand-alone videos, display your video in a gallery, or use the Wix Video player to showcase your videos with the added benefit of more advanced settings and design options.

**In this article, learn more about adding:**

[A single video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-a-video-to-your-site#adding-a-single-video)  [A video to a gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-a-video-to-your-site#adding-a-video-to-a-gallery)  [The Wix Video player](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-a-video-to-your-site#adding-the-wix-video-player)

---

### Adding a single video

Liven up your site and its sections by adding videos. Videos are a great way of entertaining and engaging site visitors.

#### To add a single video:

Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Section to Page**.  Select a section that contains a media item.  Hover over the media item and click **Edit**.  Click **Settings **next to the media item on the left.  Click **Video **below **Media Type**.  Click **Replace **and select where you want to upload from:

- **Youtube / Vimeo:** Type a word or phrase to find a video, select it and click **Add**.

**Tip:** Switch between Youtube and Vimeo videos by clicking the relevant option below the search bar.
- **Media from Wix:** Choose a free professional video from the Wix Library. Click the video you want to upload and click **Apply**.
- **Upload video: **Click the video you want to upload and click** Add to Page**.

---

### Adding a video to a gallery

You can add a range of videos to your gallery. Upload your own videos, browse professional videos in the Wix Library, or add one from YouTube or Vimeo.

### Before you begin: Add a gallery to your site

1. Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.
2. Click **Section to Page**.
3. Click **See all **and scroll down.
4. Click **Media Gallery **and choose one to add to your site.

#### To add a video to a gallery:

Click the gallery in the ADI Builder.  Click** Manage Media **on the left.   Click **+ Add Media**.  Click **Video **and choose where you want to upload from:

- **Upload video: **Click the video you want to upload and click** Add to Page**.
- **Add from YouTube: **Copy the video's web address from YouTube and click **Update Gallery. **
- **Add from Vimeo: **Copy the video's web address from Vimeo and click **Update Gallery. **
    Click **Done**.

### Adding the Wix Video player

The Wix Video player offers more advanced options for sites that need more video capabilities. You can even [sell and rent your videos, and live stream](https://support.wix.com/en/article/selling-and-renting-options-in-wix-video) with Wix Video if needed.

#### To add Wix Video:

Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Section to Page**.  Click **Video Player**. If you don't see it, click** See all **and scroll down to **Video Player**.   Select a layout.  [Set up your video player](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-video-player).  (Optional) [Choose a new design for your player](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-video-player).
