---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Adding a Watermark to Your Images"
---

Currently, you cannot add a watermark to your uploaded images in the Wix Pro Gallery in order to protect the copyright.

As a workaround, you can create and add a watermark [using text in the Wix Photo Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools#text). Be aware that when doing this, it is not possible to add text to multiple images at once. The text needs to be added to each image separately.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
