---
title: "Wix Video Request: Editing and Trimming a Video in Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to edit or trim the content of a video once you've uploaded the file to your Video Library. However, details like the video's name and description can be [updated at any time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-information-to-your-videos).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
