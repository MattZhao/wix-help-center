---

Use the [CMS (Content Management System) in Wix Studio](https://www.wix.com/studio/cms) to efficiently save, organize, and manage the content of your clients' sites.

In the CMS, you keep site content in database collections that allow you to easily create dynamic pages and elements. Connect page elements such as text, galleries, and repeaters to datasets where you control which collection fields are used in the elements. You can allow visitors to filter content and submit directly to your collections by setting up user input elements such as text inputs, dropdown selections, upload buttons and more.

### Adding the CMS in Wix Studio

Add the CMS from the left side of the Studio Editor, then get started with preset CMS templates or manually create your own collections and dynamic pages.

#### To add the CMS to Wix Studio:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **CMS **** **on the left side of the editor.   Click **Add to Site**.   Choose how you want to start:

- **Start with a preset: **Choose from preset CMS templates that include sample collections and connected dynamic pages.
- **Start by adding content: **Create a collection manually, by importing a CSV file, or with help from AI. After adding content to a collection, you can connect it to your page elements.

### Using datasets to connect elements on your page

In the CMS, you use datasets to connect the elements in your Editor to the content in your collections. Select an element or page and go to **CMS connections ** in the Inspector panel to choose which collection fields connect to each part of the page elements.

> Learn more about [connecting page elements to datasets to display collection content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-elements-to-a-collection-using-a-dataset).

### Scale efficiently with dynamic pages

With dynamic pages, you can streamline and scale the creation and maintenance of your webpages. Dynamic pages use a consistent layout and design, while changing their content based on the page's connected collection items. Wix sites are limited to 100 static pages, however, you can create an unlimited number of dynamic pages for your clients' sites.

### Engage visitors to interact with your site

Encourage site visitors to interact with your clients' sites using custom input and navigational elements. You can [set permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-changing-your-collection-permissions) for each of your collections to control who can view and edit collection content.

### Utilize a full suite of SEO tools for your dynamic pages

Edit all the SEO settings for dynamic pages from within the Studio Editor. You can add variables to the URL slug structure to ensure each CMS collection item gets a unique web address. The variables you add to the dynamic page URLs gets replaced by the actual field values from the collection items. Creating unique URLs also helps search engines and visitors understand what each page's content is about.

> Learn more about [working with SEO settings for dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/working-with-seo-settings-for-dynamic-pages).

### FAQs

Click below for answers to our most frequently asked questions.

### Where do I manage dynamic pages?

Click **Manage Dynamic Pages** to go to your site pages panel where you can view the names of all your [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-what-is-a-dynamic-page). Each dynamic page name ends with either **(List)** or **(Item)** to indicate the type of dynamic page. 
**Learn more about:** 
- [Dynamic list pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-setting-up-a-dynamic-list-page)
- [Dynamic item pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-setting-up-a-dynamic-item-page)

### How do custom forms add visitor submissions to collections?

You can [create custom forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-creating-a-custom-form-with-input-elements-that-submit-to-your-collection) by adding user input elements and connecting them to a "Write" mode dataset. The dataset connects to the collection in which you want to store the visitor submissions. Choose from several user input elements such as text inputs, dropdown selections, upload buttons and more. 
If needed, you can [set permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-changing-your-collection-permissions) to determine who can submit content. You can also [export the collection as a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-exporting-content-from-your-collection) to use the data outside of the Wix CMS (e.g. in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets).

### How do I view Wix app collections?

By default, your Wix app collections are hidden from your list of collections. You can view Wix app collections in the CMS and connect them to page elements. However, you cannot edit the collections in the CMS because the content is managed from the relevant Wix app (e.g. Wix Stores, Wix Bookings). 
Learn more about [working with Wix app collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-wix-app-collections).

### Can I change the page caching settings for dynamic pages?

Yes. In the **Page Info **tab of your dynamic page settings, you can adjust your page caching settings to make the page cached automatically, or manually. If a page is cached, its data is saved locally so it takes less time for the page to load. There are some pages we do not recommend caching, such as pages that have user generated content or dynamic data (such as pricing).
To edit your Advanced Settings: 
1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Hover over the relevant dynamic page and click the **More Actions** icon .
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click **Advanced Settings**.
5. Click the drop-down and select an option: 

- **Automatically: **The page is automatically cached (if it meets the requirements).
- **Manually:** The page is not cached automatically. Click the **Turn on caching for this page** toggle to choose whether or not you want the page to be cached:

- **Enabled:** The page will be cached. Click the drop-down to choose how often you want to reset this page's cache.
- **Disabled: **The page will not be cached.

### Why should I add a variable field to the URL slug structure?

Adding a field to your dynamic page URL helps search engines and visitors understand what the page content is about. It also adds structure and organization to your site's dynamic URLs. 
You may need to add fields to the URL to [create unique URLs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-creating-unique-dynamic-page-urls) for each item in your collection. If multiple items in your collection have the same value in their **Title** field, and you do not add a field to the dynamic URL, only 1 URL is generated. This is because **Title** is used in the URL by default. Since only 1 URL is created for both items, only 1 item will have a dynamic page, the other will not. 
To solve this issue, add fields to the URL so each item gets its own dynamic page with a unique URL. 
For example, if your dynamic item page URL ends with **/team/\{Title\}**, and 2 items in the collection have "Sara" in the **Title **field, only 1 URL is generated for **/team/sara**. So only 1 dynamic page is created for 1 of the 2 items in the collection. To solve this, you could add a field to the URL that is unique to each item such as the **ID **field. The URL would then be **/team/sara/\{ID\} **so each item gets its own unique dynamic page URL.
**Warning: **If you change the URL structure of your dynamic pages, all the existing links to these pages will no longer work. This affects links within your site, any links you've shared externally, and SEO links in search engines.

### What are rich content elements?

Rich content elements can display formatted text as well as images and videos in a single dynamic element on your site. You manage their content from the connected collection using the rich content field type. 
Rich content allows you, and collaborators with permissions, to manage formatted text directly from your CMS (Content Management System). You can add, edit, and delete the fields in the same way as any other field type and use them on dynamic pages.
Learn more about [using rich content fields on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-rich-content-and-adding-it-to-a-dynamic-page).
