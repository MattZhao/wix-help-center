---
title: "Request: Creating Multiple Menus for Your Mobile-Friendly Site"
---

When editing your mobile-friendly site, you can [customize the menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-mobile-menu) to make it look and behave just as you want. Currently, it is not possible to add more menus to your mobile site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
