---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Fading Content When Scrolling"
---

Currently, it is not possible to make your site content fade when you or your visitors scroll down your site.

However, you can set your strip background to fade on scroll, which has a similar effect. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-applying-background-scrolling-effects-to-your-strip)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
