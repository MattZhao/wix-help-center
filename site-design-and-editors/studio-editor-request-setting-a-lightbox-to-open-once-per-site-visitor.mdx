---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting a Lightbox to Open Once per Site Visitor"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set a [lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-lightboxes) to only open once per visitor.

As a workaround, you can [create a one-time popup](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites/articles/getting-started/code-tutorials/creating-a-one-time-popup) using Velo by Wix. Velo is an open development platform that lets you to add custom functionality and interactions to your site, allowing you to control how and when your lightbox opens.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
