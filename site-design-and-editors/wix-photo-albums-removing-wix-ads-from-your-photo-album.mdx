---
title: "Wix Photo Albums: Removing Wix Ads from Your Photo Album"
---

You can remove the Wix ads from your photo album by upgrading your site to a Premium Plan that includes this feature (i.e. Combo Plan, Unlimited Plan or VIP Plan).

> **Tip:**
>
> Upgrading also allows you to [connect a subdomain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-changing-the-album-url) to your album.

### To remove the Wix ads from your album:

[Upgrade your site to Premium.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-your-site-to-premium-3066683)Follow the relevant steps below:
- **Already published your album:**
1. Click to go to your [site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).
2. Hover over the **Wix Photo Albums** app and click **Manage Albums**.
- **Haven't yet published your album:**
- [Publish your album](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-publishing-your-album).

If you have followed the instructions above and are still seeing Wix ads, please follow our [troubleshooter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-troubleshooting-album-url-issues).
