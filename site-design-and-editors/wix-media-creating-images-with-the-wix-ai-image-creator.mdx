### Generating images

Generate images directly from your editor or from your mobile device. All AI-generated images that you save are automatically added to the **Site Files** folder in your [Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-viewing-your-site-files-in-your-media-manager).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about generating images with the Wix AI Image Creator:

### Can I save / return to previous image variations?

No. Currently, it is not possible to save an image variation or to return to an image variation once you click **Create Image**.

### Can I view a history of my prompts?

No. Currently, it is not possible to view a history of your prompts in the Wix AI Image Creator.

### Is there a limit to the number of images I can create with the Wix AI Image Creator?

Yes. Currently, there is a limit of 1,000 images you can create with the Wix AI Image Creator per user.

### How can I create a logo for my business?

You can create a logo for your business using the [Wix AI Logo Maker](https://www.wix.com/logo/maker).
