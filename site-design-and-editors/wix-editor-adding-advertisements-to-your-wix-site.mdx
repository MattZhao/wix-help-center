You can add advertisements to your site in a few different ways:

[Upload the ad as an image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-your-own-images) and [link to the relevant URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-link-to-an-image)  [Add Marketing Integrations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-marketing-integrations)  [Use the Google AdSense app to display third-party ads](https://support.wix.com/en/article/apps-made-by-wix-adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-monetize-with-adsense-app)  [Use a popup to display your ad](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-full-screen-pop-up-menu)

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also learn how to [remove the Wix banner ad or update your site favicon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/removing-the-wix-banner-ad-and-favicon) to your own.
