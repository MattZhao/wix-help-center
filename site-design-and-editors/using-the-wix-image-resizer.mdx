### Resizing and cropping images

Upload an image from your computer, adjust its dimensions through resizing and cropping, and finally, download it in the format of your choice.

#### To resize and crop an image:

Go to the [Wix Image Resizer](https://www.wix.com/tools/image-resizer).   Click **Select Images**.   Select the image you wish to upload from your computer.   Resize your image using the **Resizing Options**: 

- **Pixels:** Enter the width and height you want your image to be.
- **Percentage: **Use the slider to resize the image to a percent of its original size.
- **Image Format:** Select a format from the drop-down to download your image in.
- **Crop & Rotate:** Use the cropping tools to choose a ratio, rotate or flip your image. You can move the cropped area to select the area of your choice.
    Click **Resize Image**.   Click **Download Image** to download your newly resized image.

### Do I need to pay to use the Wix Image Resizer?

No, the Wix Image Resizer is completely free to use. Check out more [free tools](https://www.wix.com/tools) for your business, or [create a free Wix account](https://manage.wix.com/account/sites) to showcase your images on your own website.

### How many images can I upload at a time to the Image Resizer?

You can upload 6 images at a time to the Image Resizer.

### What are the required image dimensions for social platforms?

The required image dimensions for social platforms are: 
- Instagram: 

- Story + IGTV: 1080 x 1920 pixels
- Post size: 1080 x 1080 pixels
- Profile picture: 110 x 110 pixels
- Facebook:

- Profile picture: 170 x 170 pixels
- Post and timeline: 1200 x 630 pixels
- Story: 1080 x 1920
- Cover photo: 1200 x 628 pixels
- Feed ads: 1080  x 1080 pixels
- Twitter: 

- Banner: 1500 x 500 pixels
- Profile picture: 400 x 400 pixels
- Post image: 1200 x 675 pixels
- Card image: 120 x 120 pixels
- In-stream photo: 1600 x 900 pixels
- TikTok: 

- Video: 1080 x 1920 pixels
- Profile picture: 200 x 200 pixels
- Snapchat: 

- Video story: 1080 x 1920 pixels
- YouTube:

- Banner: 2560 x 1440 pixels
- Profile picture: 800 x 800 pixels
- Thumbnail: 1280 x 720 pixels
