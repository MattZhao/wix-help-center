---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Changing the Position of the Thumbnails in Galleries when Enlarged"
---

Currently, when you enlarge galleries that contain thumbnails, the thumbnails are centrally justified (see image below). You cannot change the justification of the thumbnails to the left or right side.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
