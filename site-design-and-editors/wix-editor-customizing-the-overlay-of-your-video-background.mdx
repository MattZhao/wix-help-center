title: "Wix Editor: Customizing the Overlay of Your Video Background"
---

Customizing the overlay of your video background in Wix Editor allows you to add unique patterns or colors to your video backgrounds. This helps you create a visually appealing and cohesive look for your site, enhancing your visitors' experience.

---

### Customizing the overlay of your video background

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Site Design ** on the left side of the editor.  Click** Page background**.  Click **Settings**.  In the **Video Settings** panel, choose whether you want to overlay a pattern or a color:

- **To overlay a pattern:** Select one of the available patterns from the options provided.
- **To overlay a color:** Enable the **Overlay a color** toggle and choose your desired color using the color picker, then set the opacity.

---

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I use my own pattern for the overlay?

Currently, you can only select from the predefined patterns provided in the Wix Editor.

### Can I apply different overlays to different pages?

Yes, you can customize the video background overlay for each page individually in the Wix Editor.

### Can I remove the overlay if I change my mind?

Yes, to remove the overlay pattern on your video background, click the **None** pattern option (the icon with the red line), or disable the **Overlay a color **toggle.

### Does the overlay affect the video playback on mobile devices?

The overlay is not displayed on your mobile site.
It's important to note that your video background is automatically set not play on the mobile version of your site. To enable it or select another video background, go to the mobile editor and reselect the video for the page background. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-video-backgrounds-on-your-mobile-site).

### Can I add text over the video background?

Yes, you can add text elements over the video background, and adding an overlay helps ensure that the text remains legible.
