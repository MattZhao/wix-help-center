---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Changing the Focal Point of an Album Cover"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the focal point of a Photo Album cover. The cover is centered and cropped by default to optimally fit the screen size.

> **Tip**
>
> You can change the focal point of an image in your Photo Album. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-changing-the-focal-point-of-images-in-your-photo-album)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
