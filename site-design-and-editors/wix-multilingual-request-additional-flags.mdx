title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Additional Flags"
---

Currently, flags are available for all the countries included in the [ISO-3166](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166) standard.

What flags would you like to add to your Wix Multilingual Language Menu? [Submit a ticket with your feedback and suggestions](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=9f9e6802-edc0-4fcf-b727-25876a7e00c2&referral=Kb)

### Click to learn how to check the available flags in Wix Multilingual

1. Click the **Switch Language **panel in the Editor.
2. Click **Manage Languages**.
3. Click the **Show More** icon  next to a language.
4. Click **Change Flag**.
5. Click the flag drop-down menu to view the available flags for that language.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can choose to [show or hide the flags on your Language Menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-customizing-the-wix-multilingual-language-menu-on-your-site) by clicking the **Layouts** icon.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
