---
title: "Wix Editor: Setting your Images to Open in a Pop-Up When Clicked"
---

You can set your image to open in a larger window when it is clicked.

### To set your image to expand when clicked:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the relevant image.  Click the **Settings **icon .  Click the **When image is clicked** drop-down menu and select **It opens in a pop-up**.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you add a [tooltip](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-alt-text-tooltip-to-your-image) to your image, it appears under the image in the pop-up window.
