### Types of Wix Music reports

Wix Music reports show metrics like plays, shares, purchases, and downloads over various time periods. Use this information to see which albums and tracks are most popular and how your audience engages with your music.

Click a report type below to learn more about it:

### Top Albums

Your Top Albums report shows your best performing albums in terms of plays, shares, subscriptions, and sales over specific time periods. 
**Use this report to:**
- Discover which albums your visitors engage with most.
- See if engagement with your album has changed over different time periods, to understand if actions you've taken have increased or decreased sales.

### Tracks over Time

The Tracks over Time report shows when your tracks received the most plays, shares, purchases, and downloads, over a selected time period.
**Use this report to:**
- See how many sales your tracks attract over time, and how this compares to the previous period.
- Learn more about your listeners, where they are located by country, and the devices they use to access your music.

### Top Tracks

Your Top Tracks report displays your best performing tracks in terms of plays, shares, purchases, and sales. 
**Use this report to:**
- Find out which tracks generate the most sales.
- Discover how many times a track has been played in comparison to a previous period, to understand if actions you've taken have impacted plays.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about your Wix Analytics Music reports.

### Where can I learn about the terminology used in these reports?

You can view definitions for Wix Music terms in the [Wix Analytics glossary](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-reports-glossary#music-video).

### How do I save my favorite reports so that I can access them quickly?

On the [All Reports](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fanalytics%2Freports%2F%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=View+All+Reports) page, click the **Add to Saved reports** icon  next to a specific report to add it to your saved reports. Your saved reports appear at the top of the page.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Add to Saved reports icon.]

### How do I change the type of graph in the report?

On the [All Reports](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fanalytics%2Freports%2F%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=View+All+Reports) page, hover over a report to see the different views available. The offered graphs vary according to the type of report (e.g. tables, line charts, maps).[image: Screenshot of hovering over a type of music report to see possible graphs]
You could also switch between report views within a report by clicking the options at the top.
