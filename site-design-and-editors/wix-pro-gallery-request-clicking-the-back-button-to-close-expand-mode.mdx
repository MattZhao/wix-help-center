---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Clicking the Back Button to Close Expand Mode"
---

Currently, clicking the back button on your browser tab returns you to the previous page you visited, and not the main gallery view. To close the Expand Mode, click the Exit icon  on the top right of the gallery.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Customize your Expand Mode icons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-expand-mode-for-your-gallery) to increase their visibility.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
