---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding and Setting Up an Image Button"
---

Image buttons are a unique way to encourage visitors to interact with and navigate around your site. They use images to give your site something extra, and display a unique effect when visitors hover over them. You can also customize the design of the buttons for each of their states; regular, hover and clicked view.

### Learn more about the button states

- **Regular: **How the button is displayed on your site before visitors interact with it.
- **Hover:** How the button looks when a cursor is hovering over it.
- **Clicked:** How the button is displayed once it's been clicked.

**Note:** Hover (tap) effects on image buttons are not currently supported on iOS. Viewers on iOS devices do not see the hover state if they tap the button.

---

### Step 1 | Add your image button

Choose an eye-catching design from the Add Elements panel to get started on setting up your image button. There are a variety of options to choose from, all with their own striking effects.

#### To add an image button:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Button**.  Click **Image Buttons**.  Click or drag the relevant button to add it to your page.

---

### Step 2 | Add a link to your image button

Link your image buttons to direct visitors to parts of your site, or to encourage them to perform an action. Options you can link your button to include pages, anchors, and external sites.

#### To add a link to your image button:

Select your new button.  Click the **Link** icon .  Select a link type on the left.  Depending on the link type you selected, add the relevant information.  Click **Done**.

---

### Step 3 | Customize your image button

Customize your image button to suit your site's look and feel. You can apply animation and transition effects, and add a tooltip to advise visitors on where the button will lead them to.

Click an option below to learn how to adjust and customize your image button.

### Change the button's image

1. Select the button in your editor.
2. Click **Change Image**.
3. Click the relevant tab at the top of the panel to select the button state you want to edit: **Regular**, **Hover**, or **Clicked**.
4. Click **Change Image**.
5. Choose what kind of image you want to use:

- Click **Media from Wix** to choose from a variety of free images offered by Wix.
- Click** + Upload Media** to upload and use your own image.
6. Click **Apply**.

### Apply a transition effect to your button

1. Select the button in your editor.
2. Click **Change Image**.
3. Choose **Dissolve** under **Apply transition effects?**.

### Add a tooltip to your button

1. Select the button in your editor.
2. Click **Change Image**.
3. Enter the text of your tooltip under **Does this button have a tooltip?**.

**Note:** Adding a tooltip is a crucial step towards [making your site accessible](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-checklist-for-improving-your-sites-accessibility).

### Add an animation effect to your button

1. Select the button in your editor.
2. Click the **Animation** icon .
3. Select an animation to apply to the button.
4. (Optional) Click **Customize** to [adjust the animation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-an-animation#customizing-an-animation).

> **Note:**
>
> If you want to change the color of an image from Wix, you must edit it in an external program, then [upload the file to your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-your-own-images).
