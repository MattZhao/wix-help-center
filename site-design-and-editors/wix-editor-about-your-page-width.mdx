---
title: "Wix Editor: About Your Page Width"
---

The width of your site is fixed at 980 pixels.

This size was chosen to accommodate the majority of site users and ensures compatibility with the many different smart devices being used today (i.e. iPads or tablets).

> **Tip:**
>
> You can design your site outside of the 980 pixel borders, however we recommend that anything that you add outside of these borders be mainly decorative and not vital content to your visitors' browsing experience.
