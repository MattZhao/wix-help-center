---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding 3D Carousel and 3D Slideshow Galleries"
---

Currently, 3D Carousel and 3D Slideshow galleries are not supported in the Studio Editor.

As an alternative, you can display media [in a Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-the-pro-gallery).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
