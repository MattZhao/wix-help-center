title: "Editor X: Rotating Elements"
---

Using Editor X, you can rotate elements to set them at the exact angle you need. This helps to ensure that all of your elements align perfectly with one another.

#### Learn more about:

[Rotating an element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-rotating-elements#rotating-elements)  [Setting docking and margins for rotated elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-rotating-elements#docking-and-margins-for-rotated-elements)

---

### Rotating Elements

Select the element that you want to rotate.  Click the **Inspector** icon  at the top right.  Click **Adjust**.  Enter the angle next to **Rotate**.

---

### Docking and Margins for Rotated Elements

The Inspector panel lets you accurately position every element on your page. You can [dock elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-working-with-docking-and-margins) to any side of the section, container, or grid cell they’re in, and [use margins](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-working-with-docking-and-margins#using-margins-for-positioning-elements) to set the distance between the element and the edges.
