---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Creating a QR Code in the Wix Logo Maker"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create a QR code and add it to your brand designs in the Wix Logo Maker.

However, you can create and download a QR code using the [Wix QR Code Generator](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-qr-code-with-the-wix-qr-code-generator), and [upload it as an image to your brand designs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-uploading-your-own-images-to-the-wix-logo-maker).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
