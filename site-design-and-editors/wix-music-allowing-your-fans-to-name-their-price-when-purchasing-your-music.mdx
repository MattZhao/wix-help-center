---
title: "Wix Music: Allowing Your Fans to Name Their Price When Purchasing Your Music"
---

You can let your fans choose how much they want to pay to download your music.

### Allowing your fans to choose how much they want to pay to buy an album

1. [Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant album.
3. Click **Edit **next to **Album Info**.
4. Scroll down to the **Sale and Download Options on Your Site** section.
5. Select** Let fans name their price **from the **Album Download Options** drop-down menu.
6. Enter a minimum price for your album. If you want the minimum price to be 0, you can ask your fans to subscribe to your mailing list to download your music. Click the toggle :

- **Enabled**: Fans must enter their email in order to download your music.
- **Disabled**: Fans are not required to enter their email.
7. Click **Save**.

### Allowing your fans to choose how much they want to pay to buy a single

1. [Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant single.
3. Click **Edit **next to **Single Info**.
4. Scroll down to the **Sale and Download Options on Your Site** section.
5. Select** Let fans name their price **from the **Album Download Options** drop-down menu.
6. Enter a minimum price for your single. If you want the minimum price to be 0, you can ask your fans to subscribe to your mailing list to download your music. Click the toggle :

- **Enabled**: Fans must enter their email in order to download your music.
- **Disabled**: Fans are not required to enter their email.
7. Click **Save**.

### Allowing your fans to choose how much they want to pay to buy a specific track

1. [Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant album, single or playlist.
3. Hover over the relevant track and click the **Edit Track Info **icon .
4. Scroll down to the **Sale and Download Options on Your Site** section.
5. Select** Let fans name their price **from the **Track Download Options** drop-down menu.
6. Enter a minimum price for your track. If you want the minimum price to be 0, you can ask your fans to subscribe to your mailing list to download your music. Click the toggle :

- **Enabled**: Fans must enter their email in order to download your music.
- **Disabled**: Fans are not required to enter their email.
7. Click **Save**.

> **Using Wix Payments as your payment processor?**
>
> Wix Payments includes a minimum amount of $1 per transaction. Make sure to enter a minimum price of $1 for your track, single or album when using this processor.
