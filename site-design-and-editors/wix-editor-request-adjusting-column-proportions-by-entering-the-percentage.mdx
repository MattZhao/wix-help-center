---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adjusting Column Proportions by Entering the Percentage"
---

Currently, it is not possible to adjust the proportions of columns by manually entering a percentage amount.

As an alternative, you can adjust your column's proportions by dragging the slider in the Layout panel. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adjusting-the-layout-of-your-columns)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
