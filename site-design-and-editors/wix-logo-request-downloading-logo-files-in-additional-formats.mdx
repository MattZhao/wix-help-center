---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Downloading Logo Files in Additional Formats"
---

Currently, we offer Wix Logo files in PNG and SVG formats.

> **Important:**
>
> SVG files are only included in the Advanced, Professional, and Deluxe Logo Plans.

You can easily convert your files to another format for free with [CloudConvert](https://cloudconvert.com/). Keep in mind that CloudConvert is a third-party application, and as a result, we do not offer support for it.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
