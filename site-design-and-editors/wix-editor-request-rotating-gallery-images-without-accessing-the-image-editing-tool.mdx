---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Rotating Gallery Images without Accessing the Image Editing Tool"
---

Currently, you need to access the Image Editing Tool to [rotate your gallery images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-and-customizing-images), you cannot do it from within the gallery itself.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.

If this is a feature you would like to see in the future, please click **Vote for this feature** and we'll make sure to keep you updated.
