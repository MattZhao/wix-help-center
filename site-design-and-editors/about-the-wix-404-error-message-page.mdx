---
title: "About the Wix 404 Error Message Page"
---

A Wix 404 error message page is displayed when you or a site visitor enters a URL for a page on your site that does not exist. A 404 response code means "page not found". The Wix 404 page includes a short paragraph describing the reason for this response and a button that takes your site visitors to the home page of your site.

**We display a 404 error message page because:**

We want your site visitors to know that they are looking for a specific URL that does not exist, rather than sending them to your home page.  We want to provide the correct response. The 404 response says that the page does not exist.

> **Notes:**
>
> - If you have a page that no longer exists and is returning a 404 error, or you have changed the URL name of a previously published page, you can [create a 301 redirect](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-a-301-redirect-for-a-page-on-your-site) to your home page or to another page on the site.
> - If you see a page returning a 404 response which you believe is incorrect, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/live-site-error-error-404-not-found) to troubleshoot the problem.
> - You can [customize your 404 error page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-404-error-page) to make sure your visitors keep exploring your site after seeing the error.
> - Currently, this is not available for verticals (Wix Stores, Wix Bookings etc, and third-party apps).
