title: "Wix Pro Gallery: Adding and Setting Up Your Gallery"
---

The Wix Pro Gallery allows you to showcase your images, videos, and text all in one multimedia gallery. You can choose from multiple layouts and customize your gallery's design and settings. This tool helps you create a visually engaging experience for your visitors, making your content stand out.

For example, if you're a photographer, display your portfolio in an organized manner to attract potential clients and keep them browsing your work.

---

### Step 2 | Add media to your gallery

After you've added the gallery to your site, you can add media. This can include images, videos and text. You can choose to upload your own media or upload content from the Wix Libraries.

#### To add media to your gallery:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.   Click **Manage Media**.   Click **Add Media** at the top.   Choose the type of media you wish to [upload](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-media-to-the-gallery).

> **Tip:**
>
> To delete the default media that come with the gallery, hover over the item you want to delete and click the Delete icon .

---

### Step 3 | Customize your gallery layout

You can then focus on customizing the layout. There is a wide variety of layout options to choose from:

**Preset:** These ready-made gallery templates allow you to speed up the process of showcasing your images on your site. Preset templates include built-in layout, design and settings.  **Customize:** These gallery layouts allow you to design and customize your gallery from scratch.

#### To customize your gallery layout:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Layout** tab.  Click **Custom **or **Presets**.  Depending on the customizable layout option you choose, [adjust the settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-changing-the-layout-settings).

---

### Step 4 | Customize your gallery settings

You can now customize your gallery settings which allows you to adjust the appearance and behavior of your gallery. Personalize your gallery settings to match your site's design and functionality needs.

#### To customize your gallery settings:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Settings** tab.  [Customize your gallery settings using the options available](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-gallery-settings).

---

### Step 5 | Customize your gallery design

Make your gallery completely your own by customizing the wide range of options available. You can add and adjust overlay and icons, scroll animations, text, item style, buttons, and expand mode.

> **Note:**
>
> Available design options depend on the layout and settings you've previously chosen. For example, in order to customize text, you need to have enabled the title and or description checkbox in the Text tab of the Design panel.

#### To customize your gallery design:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Design**.  [Customize your gallery design using the available options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-designing-your-gallery).

---

### Step 6 | Customize your gallery's advanced settings

The settings available in the Advanced Settings tab allow you to take your galley to the next level. Here you can focus on setting the quality of your videos and images, gallery direction and [connecting Lightroom Classic to your gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-uploading-photos-from-lightroom-classic-1823522).

#### To customize your gallery's advanced settings:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Advanced **tab. ### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I restore a previous version of my Pro Gallery?

The Pro Gallery displays the latest saved version. Currently, it is not possible to restore your gallery to a previous version from the [Site History](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-and-managing-your-site-history) tab.

### Can I add a title to my Pro Galleries on my live site?

Currently, it's not possible to add a title to your Pro Gallery. Galleries are automatically titled in chronological order, e.g., Pro Gallery 1, Pro Gallery 2, etc. These titles are not displayed on your live site. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-adding-a-title-to-your-gallery).

### What happens if I have several Pro Galleries on one of my site pages?

You will likely experience performance issues if you have several Pro Galleries on a single page. Galleries are optimized to showcase multiple media files at once.We recommend having one gallery with several media items per page, as opposed to multiple galleries with less items. Having too many individual elements on a single page (even if the elements are identical) can affect site performance. Learn more about site performance [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-optimizing-your-media).

### What media can I add to my gallery?

You can add single images, videos, or a portfolio to your gallery. We recommend adding an image element, or a single video player from the Add Panel in your editor. For displaying a portfolio requiring individual galleries, we recommend using [Wix Portfolio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-portfolio-an-overview) or [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).
