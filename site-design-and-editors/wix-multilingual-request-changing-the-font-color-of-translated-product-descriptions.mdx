---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Changing the Font Color of Translated Product Descriptions"
---

Currently, the color you set for your product description, in the Product Settings tab of the dashboard, can not be carried over to the Multilingual dashboard. The translated description remains all one color in the Multilingual Dashboard - black. However, you can change the color of your product description from black to another color in the Wix Editor.

[Learn how to customize your product description](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-customizing-your-product-page#customizing-the-product-page-design)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
