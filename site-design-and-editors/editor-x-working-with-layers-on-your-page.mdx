### Setting a Layer Selection Preference

Using Editor X, you can choose a default pathway for grabbing layers on your canvas. Decide which element gets "selected" when you click a parent container that has child elements.

**Parent Container First: **The parent container (e.g., container box, stack, etc.) is selected when you click it. Then, you can click again on any element inside to edit it.   **Child Element First:** The specific child element you click on is selected, not its parent container.

You can set this preference from the top bar in your Editor.

### Show me how

1. Click **View** on the top left of Editor X.
2. Hover over **Layer Selection**.
3. Select the pathway you prefer: Parent first or child element first.

### Arranging Layers on Your Canvas

When working with many different elements, layers could overlap in an order that doesn't fit your original design.

Right-click on any element to move its layer forward or backward. You can also bring the layer to the front or send it to the back, making it the foreground for your design.

### Show me how

1. Select the element you want to re-position and click the **Show More** icon .
2. Select **Arrange**.
3. Choose one of the following options:

- **Move Forward: **Bring the element one layer forward.
- **Bring to Front: **Place the element in front of all other layers.
- **Move Backward: **Send the element one layer back.
- **Send to Back:** Place the element behind all other layers.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also use the [Layers panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-the-layers-panel) to re-arrange your layers. Click the **Show More** icon  next to the relevant layer and select **Arrange**.

### Using the Layers Panel

The [Layers panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-the-layers-panel) in Editor X lets you manage your page's layers in one place. It displays all the elements on your page, from the top to the bottom, and allows you to select layers, change their order, hide them, and more. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-the-layers-panel)
