---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding an Image Background to Your Mobile Site"
---

Add an image to the background of your mobile site. An image background that matches the theme of your site can look stunning on mobile, and really impress your site visitors.

The background of your mobile site can be different to the background of your desktop site. You can also have a different background for each page.

### To add an image to your mobile site background:

[Go to the mobile editor.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-accessing-the-mobile-editor)  Click **Background ** on the left side.   Click **Image**.  Select an image from the Media Manager.  Click **Change Background**.

> **Good to know:**
>
> You can change your Mobile Background, adjust text, and more from your mobile device using [Wix Editor on Mobile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-mobile).
