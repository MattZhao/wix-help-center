---
title: "Wix Editor: Removing the Gap Above the Footer on Your Mobile Site"
---

You may notice a gap between the page and footer of your live site on mobile that you can't see in the editor.

You can easily remove this gap by adjusting your page's spacing.

### To remove the gap between your page and footer:

[Go to the mobile editor.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor)  Adjust and [remove](https://www.wix.com/support/html5/article/removing-gaps-on-your-mobile-site) any spacing you find.  Scroll to the bottom of the page.  Click the page body.  Double-click on the page height handle to remove any gaps between the header and footer.   Publish your site.

> **Still seeing a gap?**
>
> If the above steps do not resolve the issue, please [contact our support team](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) and we will be happy to assist you.
