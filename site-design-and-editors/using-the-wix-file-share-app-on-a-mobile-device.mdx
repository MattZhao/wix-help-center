---
title: "Using the Wix File Share App on a Mobile Device"
---

Members can interact with your file sharing library right from their mobile phones! Site members can perform any actions (view, upload, share, move files, etc.) they have [permission ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-file-share-app)to do.

To provide your members an easy-to-read experience, the File Share app has one, optimal layout for mobile.

> **Tip:**
>
> When accessing the File Share app on mobile, members can take a picture using their device's camera and upload it directly to a folder.
