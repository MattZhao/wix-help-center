---
title: "Wix Photo Albums: Showing or Hiding an Album on Your Site"
---

When you publish your album for the first time, you can choose to show or hide it in the album gallery on your Wix site by selecting or deselecting the **Show this album on my Wix website** toggle.

> **Note:**
>
> You can change this setting at any time.

### To show or hide a photo album on your site:

Select the Wix Photo Albums app in the Editor. Click **Settings**.Click **Albums** on the left of the panel.Click **View All Albums** at the bottom.Click the **Show on Site **toggleto show or hide each album.
