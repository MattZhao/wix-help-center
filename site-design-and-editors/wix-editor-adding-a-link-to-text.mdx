### Creating clickable URLs

Adding a clickable URL to your site means that visitors know exactly what page they're opening. You can add URLs to other pages on your site, or to external web addresses.

URLs that you enter directly into a text box automatically become clickable, which means that you don't need to manually add any links.

> **Important:**
>
> Clickable URLs can currently only be created with an English language URL.

#### To create a clickable URL:

[Add text to your site](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=&buttonText=&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7B%7Besi%7D%7D%26openpanel%3Daddpanel.text).  Click **Edit Text**.  Enter the URL.

> **Note:**
>
> Do not add any other text into the text box, as this will make the URL unclickable

### Hyperlinking text

As well as creating a clickable URL, you can also add links to regular text content. You can decide exactly what section of text you want to link, and even customize the color.

Some popular text links include "Visit Our Store", or "Keep Reading".

#### To hyperlink text:

Click the text box in your editor.  Click the **Link** icon .  Select the link type and enter the details.  Choose whether your links opens in a new window, or the current window.  Click **Done**.  (Optional) Change the color of your hyperlinked text:

1. Click **Edit Text**.
2. Highlight the linked text.
3. Click the **Color** icon .
4. Select a color from the color picker.

> **Tip:**
>
> To remove the hyperlink underline, highlight the linked text and click the Underline icon .

> **Note:**
>
> You cannot link text that spans over 2 paragraphs if you separated those paragraphs using the** Enter** key. To resolve this, you can use **Shift** + **Enter** to create the line space instead of the Enter key.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I choose which color my hyperlink changes to, once visitors click it?

No. Currently, it is not possible to change the color of a hyperlink after visitors click it.

### How can I remove the hyperlink?

To remove a link, click the Link icon , select **None** in the panel, then click **Done**.

### If I remove the hyperlink underline, is the hyperlink also removed?

When the underline is removed, the text remains linked.
