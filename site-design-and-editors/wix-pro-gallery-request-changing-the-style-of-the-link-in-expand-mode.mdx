---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Changing the Style of the Link in Expand Mode"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the font, formatting, or size of the link in Expand Mode of Pro Gallery.

However, when you change the color of the description in the Expand Mode Design settings, this color will also be used for the link in Expand Mode.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
