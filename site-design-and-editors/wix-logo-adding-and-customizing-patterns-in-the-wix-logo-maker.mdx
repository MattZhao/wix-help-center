---
title: "Wix Logo: Adding and Customizing Patterns in the Wix Logo Maker"
---

Establish a truly unique and recognizable brand through the use of patterns. Patterns help to communicate your brand's personality and values. They also increase your business' memorability.

Create a pattern made up of icons, shapes, text or images and customize it to suit the intention of your branding. You can add a pattern to a shape or icon when creating a logo, social post or business card.

### To add and customize a pattern:

Go to the [Brand Studio](http://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh).  Click the **My Brands** drop-down at the top.  Select the relevant brand.

Hover over the relevant brand design (Logo, Social Post or Business Card) and click the Edit icon .  Depending on what you want to add your pattern to, click **Symbols **** **or **Shapes **** **on the left.  Hover over the relevant symbol / shape and click **Customize**.

**Note:** If you haven't yet added a symbol / shape, click **+** **Add Symbol **/** + Add Shape**.  Click the **Shape Fill **/** Icon Fill **drop-down and select **Pattern**.

**Note:** This option is available when you enable the **Fill Color** toggle.

Click **Customize **below the** Shape Fill** / **Icon Fill** drop-down.  Click the **Pattern Graphic** drop-down and select your preferred graphic: **Icon**, **Shape**, **Text **or **Image**.  Depending on the graphic you choose, customize your pattern using the following options:

### Icon

- **Icon: **Click **Change Icon **to choose your preferred icon.
- **Icon Colors: **Click a color box to** **adjust it to suit your brand.
- **Background Color: **Click the color box to change the background color of your pattern.
- **Flip: **Change the orientation of your pattern by flipping it vertically / horizontally.
- **Icon Zoom: **Zoom in or out on your pattern's icons.
- **Icon Horizontal Position: **Move your pattern left or right to your preferred position.
- **Icon Vertical Position: **Move your pattern up or down to your preferred position.
- **Gap: **Adjust the space between your icons to increase / decrease the density of your pattern.
- **Rotate: **Position your pattern at your preferred angle.**

### Shape

- **Shape: **Click **Change Shape **to choose your preferred shape.
- **Fill Color: **Click the color box to change your shape's color.
- **Fill Opacity: **Adjust the transparency of the shapes in your pattern.
- **Border Width: **Increase or decrease the thickness of the shape's border.
- **Border Color: **Click the color box to change your border's color.
- **Border Opacity: **Adjust the transparency of the shape's border.
- **Background Color: **Click the color box to change the background color of your pattern.
- **Flip: **Change the orientation of your pattern by flipping it vertically / horizontally.
- **Shape Zoom: **Zoom in or out on your pattern's icons.
- **Shape Horizontal Position: **Move your pattern left or right to your preferred position.
- **Shape Vertical Position: **Move your pattern up or down to your preferred position.
- **Gap: **Adjust the space between your shapes to increase / decrease the density of your pattern.
- **Rotate: **Position your pattern at your preferred angle.**

**[image: A screenshot showing the customization options for patterns with shapes.]

### Text

- **Edit Text: **Type what you want your text to say.
- **Font: **Click **Change Font **to choose your text's font.
- **Alignment: **Choose your text's alignment:** Left**,** Center **or **Right**.
- **Text Color: **Click the color box to change your text's color.
- **Fill Opacity: **Adjust the transparency of the text.
- **Text outline color: **Click the color box to change your text's outline color.
- **Outline width: **Increase or decrease thickness of your text's outline.
- **Background Color: **Click the color box to change the background color of your pattern.
- **Flip: **Change the orientation of your pattern by flipping it vertically / horizontally.
- **Text Zoom: **Zoom in or out on your pattern's text.
- **Text Horizontal Position: **Move your pattern left or right to your preferred position.
- **Text Vertical Position: **Move your pattern up or down to your preferred position.
- **Gap: **Adjust the space between your text to increase / decrease the density of your pattern.
- **Rotate: **Position your pattern at your preferred angle.**

**[image: A screenshot showing the customization options for patterns with text.]

### Image

- **Image: **Click **Replace Image **to choose your preferred image.

**Tip:** Click **+ Upload** to upload your own image.
- **Crop / Adjust / Remove Background / Filters: **Click any of these option to [access the Wix Photo Studio and customize your image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools) to look just how you want.
- **Image Opacity: **Adjust the transparency of the image.
- **Background Color: **Click the color box to change the background color of your pattern.

**Note:** A background color is only visible when you lower the opacity of your image, or the image has a transparent background.
- **Flip: **Change the orientation of your pattern by flipping it vertically / horizontally.
- **Image Zoom: **Zoom in or out on your pattern's images.
- **Image Horizontal Position: **Move your pattern left or right to your preferred position.
- **Image Vertical Position: **Move your pattern up or down to your preferred position.
- **Gap: **Adjust the space between your images to increase / decrease the density of your pattern.
- **Rotate: **Position your pattern at your preferred angle.**
