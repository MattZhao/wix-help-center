Upload your own media files to the Media Manager so that you can add them to your site at any time. You can upload media directly from your computer, other Wix sites in your account, your social media accounts, and even via a URL.

> **Which media file types and sizes are supported?**
>
> Your Wix site supports a variety of media file types and sizes. [Learn more about the file types you can upload](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes)

### Uploading your media

Upload your media to your site from a variety of locations via the Media Manager.

#### To upload your media:

[Go to your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager).  Click **Upload Media**.  Select where you want to add the media from:

### Your computer

1. Drag and drop files into the Media Manager or click **Upload from Computer**.
2. Select the files you want to upload.
3. Click **Open**.

### Your other Wix sites

1. Click **My Wix Account**.
2. Select the site folder.
3. Select the files you want to upload.
4. Click **Upload Selection**.

### Social networks

1. Select the social network.
2. Click **Connect**.
3. Select the files you want to upload.
4. Click **Upload Selection**.

### A URL

1. Click the **Link** icon on the left side.
2. Paste the URL link and click Import.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about uploading to the Media Manager:

### Can I upload an SVG file created from the Wix Logo Maker?

Currently, you can't upload the SVG file of a logo you created with the Wix Logo Maker to the Media Manager, if the logo contains [uploaded PNG or JPG images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-uploading-your-own-images-to-the-wix-logo-maker). You can, however, upload the PNG version of your logo instead.

### Will my upload be interrupted if I close the Media Manager?

Your media continues uploading even if you close the Media Manager.

### Where does the media I upload to the Wix app go?

Any [media you upload to the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-uploading-and-managing-your-media) can be found in the **Mobile Uploads** folder in the Media Manager.

### How can I tell how much storage space I have left?

You can view how much storage space you have left directly from the Media Manager. [Learn how to view your site's storage space](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-managing-your-storage)

### I don't have a Premium plan. Is there a limit on the length of videos I can upload?

There is a storage limit of 10GB for sites without a Premium plan. If you need to upload a larger video, you can upgrade to a Premium plan to increase the storage available to you. Learn more about [upgrading your video storage](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-upgrading-your-video-storage).
