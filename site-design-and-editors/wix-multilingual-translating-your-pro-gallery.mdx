### Translating your Pro Gallery

Translate the text (title, description, or alt text) of your Pro Gallery in the Translation Manager. Each time you make an update to your gallery, make sure to translate the recent changes.

Wix EditorDashboard
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the **Switch languages **drop-down menu at the top of the editor.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Switch Languages drop-down at the top of the Wix Editor.]
1. Select the language you want to edit.
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Select the page with the Pro Gallery you want to translate.
4. Select the relevant Pro Gallery on your page.
5. Click **Edit** **Translation**.
6. Select the relevant language at the top left.
7. Click **Pro Gallery** on the left.
8. Choose the relevant option:

- **If you auto-translated your site:** Review the automated translations and edit if necessary.
- **If you didn't use auto-translation:** Translate the image fields manually or automatically.
9. Click **Save and Publish**.

[image: a screenshot of the translation manager in the Wix site dashboard]
1. [Go to Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/multilingual?&title=Translation%20Manager&buttonText=Go+To+Translation+Manager) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant language at the top left.
3. Click **Pro Gallery** on the left.
4. Choose the relevant option:

- **If you auto-translated your site:** Review the automated translations and edit if necessary.
- **If you didn't use auto-translation:** Translate the image fields manually or automatically.
5. Click **Save and Publish**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about translating your Pro Gallery.

### What can I translate in the Pro Gallery?

In your Pro Gallery, you can translate image and video titles and descriptions.

### What can't I translate in the Pro Gallery?

In your Pro Gallery, currently you cannot translate the following: 
- The image and videos themselves (i.e. you cannot have a different image or video for each language)
- The links for images and videos
- [Text items](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-media-to-the-gallery-7954609#adding-media-to-your-gallery)
- The links and link names for text items
- The Load More button text
- The Alt Text field

### Why can't I see my Pro Gallery translation in the editor?

Your Pro Gallery translations are not visible in the editor. To see your gallery translations in action, [view your site in Preview mode or publish and view your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site).

### Can I give my Pro Gallery a title?

Currently, it's not possible to give your Pro Gallery a title. Galleries are automatically titled in chronological order, e.g., Pro Gallery 1, Pro Gallery 2, etc. However, you can manually make a title for your gallery by [adding a textbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text#adding-text) above it in your editor.
If this is something you'd like to see in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-adding-a-title-to-your-gallery).
