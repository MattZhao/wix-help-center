---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting a Global Section to Appear on Specific Breakpoints"
---

In the Studio Editor, you can choose which pages a [global section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-global-sections) appears on. Currently, it is not possible to set the global section to only appear on the specific breakpoint(s) you choose (e.g. a mobile-only section).

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [hide global sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-working-with-layers#hiding-and-unhiding-elements) from specific breakpoints, but it only affects the page you're working on.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
