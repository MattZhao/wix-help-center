title: "Request: New Wix Templates"
---

Wix offers a huge variety of templates, of all kinds. The great thing about our templates is that they are fully customizable, and can be personalized or changed to suit any kind of site. For example, you can choose a template from the "Design" category and create a site for Accounting Services.

Browse through our [templates](https://www.wix.com/website/templates) using the menu at the top to select a category or search for a specific template using the search box at the top left.

We are constantly working on adding new templates for you to use. You can view our latest templates [here](https://www.wix.com/website/templates/html/new/1).

If you have any specific template requests, feel free to contact us to let us know.
