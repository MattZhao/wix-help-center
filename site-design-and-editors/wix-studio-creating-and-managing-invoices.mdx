Create, send, and track invoices while managing your clients' payments in Wix Studio. Personalize invoices and email them directly to your clients, who can conveniently view and pay their invoices through a custom link.

> **Note:**
>
> This feature is available to Partners who are part of the Wix Marketplace.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure your Wix Studio workspace is connected to a payment method so you can get paid online. To set up a payment method, go to Invoices in your Wix Studio workspace, click **More Actions** at the top right and click **Accept payments**.

### Creating and sending invoices to clients

Create an invoice and send it to your client. Your clients receive an email with their invoice and a link to complete the payment and you get paid online.

#### To create an invoice:

[Go to Marketplace Overview](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/marketplace) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click the **Invoices** tab on the left.  Click **Create New** at the top right and select **Invoice**.

**Tip:** You can also [create recurring invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-creating-and-managing-recurring-invoices) to get paid on a weekly, monthly or yearly basis.  Enter the following details for this invoice:

- **Due Date:** Choose a due date for the invoice.
- **Customer:** Choose a client for this invoice.
- **Invoice title:** Enter a title for this invoice.
    Add the products or services for this invoice:

- **Product or Service: **Select a product or service to add to the invoice.

**Tip: **Select **+ Add New** to create a new item. Then enter the details and click **Save**.
- **Quantity:** Enter the quantity of the product or service.
- **Price: **Enter a price for the product or service.
- (Optional) **+ Add Tax:** Add one or two tax rates to the line item.
- (Optional) **Write a description:** Enter additional details about the product or service.
- (Optional) **+ Add Line Item:** Add additional products or services to your invoice.
- (Optional) **+ Add Discount: **Apply a percentage discount to the total amount.
    Enter additional info for the invoice:

- **Notes:** Enter any notes you'd like your client to be aware of.
- **Terms:** Enter any terms that apply to this invoice.
    Choose to preview, save, collect payment or send your invoice:

- **Preview:** Click **Preview** at the top right to see the invoice your client will receive.
- **Save:** Click **Save & Close** at the top right to save the invoice to send at a later time.
- **Collect Payment: **Choose whether to charge your client with a credit card or record receipt of payment for this invoice.
- **Send: **Click **Send Invoice** at the top right to first review or edit the content of the email your client will receive with the invoice. Click **Send** when you're ready to send.

### Managing your invoices

Keep track of your invoices and get an overview of outstanding, overdue and total paid invoices. Make edits and then resend an invoice by email or share via a unique link. Duplicate an existing invoice to save time when creating a new one. Charge a client's card directly from their invoice, and more.

#### To manage your invoices:

[Go to Marketplace Overview](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/marketplace) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click the **Invoices** tab on the left.  Click the** More** **Actions** icon  next to the invoice.  Select an action (e.g. resend via email or share link, record payment or charge with a credit card, edit or cancel).

> **Note:**
>
> Different actions are available depending on the status of the invoice. For example, you can't edit an invoice that's already been paid or voided.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Click on a question below to learn more about invoices in Wix Studio.

### How do I know if my client received my invoice?

The **Viewed **icon  ﻿appears if your client opened the invoice. Hover over the icon for more information.

### Can I customize my invoices?

Yes! You can customize various aspects of your invoices, including header details, your business information, tax display, payments received and more. Any changes made will apply to all issued and future invoices.

**To customize your invoices:**
1. [Go to Marketplace Overview](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/marketplace) in your Wix Studio workspace.
2. Click the **Invoices** tab on the left.
3. Click **More Actions** at the top right and select **Update invoice settings**.
4. Customize your invoices.

### How can I charge my client's credit card in Wix Studio?

You can handle the payment of an invoice by charging your client's credit card the invoice amount.
**To charge your client's credit card:**
1. [Go to Marketplace Overview](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/marketplace) in your Wix Studio workspace.
2. Click the **Invoices** tab on the left.
3. Click the** More** **Actions** icon  next to the invoice.
4. Click **Charge with credit card**.
5. Enter your customer's credit card details and zip / postal code.
6. Click **Charge [payment amount]** (e.g. **Charge $250.00**).
[image: A screenshot of collecting payment from your client by charging their credit card for the invoice amount.]**Note:** You can only charge the full invoice amount. You cannot charge any other amount.

### Can I mark an invoice as paid?

If your client paid you via cash, bank transfer, check or similar, you can record their payment so it's reflected on their invoice. You have to record the full payment amount if you want the invoice to be marked as Paid.
**To record a payment for an invoice:**
1. [Go to Marketplace Overview](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/marketplace) in your Wix Studio workspace.
2. Click the **Invoices** tab on the left.
3. Click the** More** **Actions** icon  next to the invoice.
4. Click **Record payment**.
5. Enter amount received, payment date and payment method.
6. Click **Record Payment**.
[image: A screenshot of recording a payment your client has made for an invoice.]
