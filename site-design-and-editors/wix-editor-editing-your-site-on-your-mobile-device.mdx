Edit your website using Wix Editor on Mobile to engage with your visitors anytime, anywhere. You can edit your site using your mobile browser, or the [Wix app](https://www.wix.com/mobile/wix-app) to adjust text, add links, change images and more. Keep your content relevant and fresh while giving your site a competitive edge.

> **Important:**
>
> - This feature is not yet available for all users.
> - Any changes you make to your site using your mobile device reflect on both your mobile and desktop site.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adjusting text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-your-mobile-device#editing-your-text)  [Editing and linking button text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-your-mobile-device#adjusting-button-text)   [Changing site images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-your-mobile-device#changing-your-site-images)   [Replacing your background](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-your-mobile-device#changing-your-background)  [Publishing your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-your-mobile-device#publishing-your-site)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-your-mobile-device#faqs)

### Editing your text

Adjust text from your mobile to stay responsive and keep your audience captivated with real-time tweaks.

Wix appMobile browser
1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Select **Website**.
4. Tap** Edit**.
5. Tap the relevant text in the editor.
6. Enter your new text.
[image: screenshot of a healthy food site using wix editor on mobile. text is selected, and the text box is higlighted.]
1. Go to your site at [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/) from a [supported browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers) on your mobile device.
2. Tap **Edit Site.**
3. Tap the relevant text in the editor.
4. Enter your new text.
[image: screenshot of a healthy food site using wix editor on mobile. text is selected, and the text box is highlighted.]

> **Note:**
>
> Formatting options such as bold, underline and italics are not supported. However, you may see these options depending on your native OS of your device.

### Adjusting button text

Edit button text and add links from your mobile to create compelling clickable content and optimize your user engagement.

> **Notes:**
>
> - For [Text & Icon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-buttons) buttons, you can edit the text from your mobile device, but not the button icon.
> - Currently, the supported link types on mobile are site page, URL, email, and phone number.

Wix appMobile browser
1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Select **Website**.
4. Tap** Edit**.
5. Tap the relevant button.
6. Set up your button using the options: 

- **What does it say?:** Enter what you want your button to say.
- **What does it link to?: **Select where you would like your button to link to.

[image: screenshot of a healthy food site using wix editor on mobile. a button is selected and the button setting panel is displayed.]
1. Go to your site at [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/) from a [supported browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers) on your mobile device.
2. Tap **Edit Site.**
3. Tap the relevant button.
4. Set up your button using the options: 

- W**hat does it say? **Enter what you want your button to say.
- **What does it link to? **Select where you would like your button to link to.
[image: screenshot of a healthy food site using wix editor on mobile. a button is selected and the button setting panel is displayed.]

### Changing your site images

Update site images from your mobile to keep your visual content current. Choose images from your [Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-about-the-media-manager) or upload direct from your device, adding alt text to increase accessibility.

Wix AppMobile browser
1. Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Select **Website**.
4. Tap** Edit**.
5. Tap the relevant image.
6. Choose from the following **Image Settings**:

- **Change: **Replace the image with an image from your media manager or upload a new image from your device.
- **What do you want to link to? **Choose whether you want your image to link to a page, URL, email or phone number.
- **What's in the image? **[Add alt text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/optimizing-your-images-and-galleries-for-seo-alt-text) to your image.
[image: screenshot of a healthy food site using wix editor on mobile. an image is selected, and the image setting panel is displayed.]
1. Go to your site at [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/) from a [supported browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers) on your mobile device.
2. Tap **Edit Site.**
3. Tap the relevant image.
4. Choose from the following **Image Settings**:

- **Change: **Replace the image with an image from your media manager or upload a new image from your device.
- **What do you want to link to? **Choose whether you want your image to link to a page, URL, email or phone number.
- **What's in the image? **[Add alt text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/optimizing-your-images-and-galleries-for-seo-alt-text) to your image.
[image: screenshot of a healthy food site using wix editor on mobile. an image is selected, and the image setting panel is displayed.]

> **Note:**
>
> Currently, only standalone images can be edited from your mobile device. If you would like to edit images displayed in a gallery for example, [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-on-mobile-request-additional-supported-elements).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about editing your site from your mobile device.

### Will these changes reflect on my desktop site?

Yes. Any changes you make to your site using your mobile device will reflect on both your mobile and desktop site.

### How can I undo my change?

To undo a change when editing on mobile, tap **Undo** .

### Is it possible to test the links I have added to my site?

To test any link, view your site in preview mode, or publish the changes and try the links on your live site.

### Can I edit dynamic pages?

Currently, editing on mobile allows you to edit [static pages and elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/static-vs-dynamic-sites). To edit dynamic content, you need to edit your site from desktop.

### Can I manage my Wix site apps (e.g. Bookings and Restaurants) on mobile?

Absolutely. [Manage your site apps on mobile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-managing-your-app-from-the-wix-owner-app) using the [Wix app](https://www.wix.com/mobile/wix-app).
