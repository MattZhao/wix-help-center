---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Using Free Business Cards With Another Logo in Your Account"
---

Currently, it is only possible to use the free business cards offer with the Wix Logo included in the Professional or Deluxe Wix Logo Plan you purchased. You cannot apply the free cards to another Wix Logo in your account.

If you wish to use the free business cards with another Wix Logo, you need to order those cards with the Logo that came in the same Logo Plan and then contact the printing company Order a Print on their website or via email: [support@orderaprint.com](mailto:support@orderaprint.com), and ask them to replace the Logo to the one you want as soon as you place the order.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
