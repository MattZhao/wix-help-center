---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Using Branded App by Wix with Wix Multilingual"
---

Currently, Wix Multilingual is not available to use with [Branded App by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview). Your branded app's content can only be offered in one language.

> **What content can be translated with Wix Multilingual?**
>
> See the list of [Translatable Content in Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translatable-content).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
