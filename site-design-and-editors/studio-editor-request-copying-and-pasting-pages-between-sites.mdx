---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Copying and Pasting Pages Between Sites"
---

While you can duplicate site pages, it is not possible to copy and paste them to a different Studio Editor site in your account.

> **Workaround:**
>
> [Save your sections as design assets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-saving-and-reusing-design-assets). This lets you reuse sections you've designed for other Studio Editor sites in your account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
