You can edit the 'Go to Link' text on the Pro Gallery. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-a-link-to-a-gallery-item) to learn more. Currently, this is the only gallery in the Editor that you edit the 'Go to Link' text.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
