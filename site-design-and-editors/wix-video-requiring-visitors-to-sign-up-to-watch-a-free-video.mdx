---
title: "Wix Video: Requiring Visitors to Sign Up to Watch a Free Video"
---

When you set a video as free, you can require viewers to sign up using their email address before they watch the video, so that their email is added to your site's contact list.

### To require viewers to sign up before watching a video:

[Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Edit** icon next to the video you want to change.   Select **Free** under **Video Pricing**.   Click the **Viewer options** dropdown and choose **Sign up only**.  Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> - Visitors who sign up are added to your Site Members group in your [Contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-contact-list).
> - You can only require sign up for free videos. When your viewers [purchase a video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/selling-and-renting-options-in-wix-video) their email address is added to your Site Members group.
