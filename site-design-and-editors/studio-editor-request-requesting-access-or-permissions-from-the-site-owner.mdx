---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Requesting Access or Permissions from the Site Owner"
---

In the Studio Editor, site owners can define which actions, pages and areas users are allowed to access. Any action you're not authorized to perform will be disabled and not clickable. Currently, it is not possible to request for additional permissions from inside the Editor.

To update your permissions in the system, contact the site owner directly.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
