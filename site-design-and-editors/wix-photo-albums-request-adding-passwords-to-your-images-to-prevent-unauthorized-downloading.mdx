---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Adding Passwords to Your Images to Prevent Unauthorized Downloading"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a password to an image to protect it from unauthorized downloading. Password protection would mean that only site visitors who have the correct password would be able to download the image.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
