title: "Recommended Apps for Musicians"
---

In the Wix App Market, you'll find everything you need to introduce, share, and sell your music on your site, and to let site visitors know about upcoming performances.

**Wix Music: **Wix Music lets showcase your albums, tracks and playlists. Use it to allow site visitors to listen and buy your music. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-music)  **Bandsintown: **Display your tour dates and sell tickets to your performances. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-bandsintown-app)  **SoundCloud: **Use a SoundCloud player to share a track or playlist on your site. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-a-soundcloud-player)  **Additional apps: **Check out our [full range of music apps](https://www.wix.com/app-market/search-result?query=music).
