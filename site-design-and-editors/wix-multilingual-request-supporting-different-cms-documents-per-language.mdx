---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Supporting Different CMS Documents per Language"
---

Currently, with the CMS (Content Management System) and Wix Multilingual, it is not possible to use different document files for each of your site's languages. This refers to the Document and Multi-Document field types in CMS collections.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
