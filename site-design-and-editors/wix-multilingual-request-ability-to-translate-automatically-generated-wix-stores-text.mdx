---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Ability to Translate Automatically Generated Wix Stores Text"
---

Certain text in the Wix storefront (such as the words "total" and "subtotal" in the cart and the Checkout Page text) is generated by Wix Stores and cannot be edited. This text is translated automatically for supported languages.

### View supported languages

Arabic, Bulgarian, Chinese (Traditional), Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Latvian, Malay, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Tagalog, Thai, Turkish and Ukrainian.

Currently, in non-supported languages, this text appears in English and cannot be edited.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
