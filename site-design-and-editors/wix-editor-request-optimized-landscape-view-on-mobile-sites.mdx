---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Optimized Landscape View on Mobile Sites"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer support for any issues that may occur when viewing your Wix site in landscape view on a mobile device. Additionally, the Wix mobile apps do not offer a landscape view option.

As an alternative, you can build your site with Editor X, which allows you to [design your site's alignment and content for different screen sizes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-designing-across-breakpoints-with-the-cascading-rule).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.

> **Note:**
>
> If you are experiencing issues when viewing your live site in portrait mode, you can [contact us](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) with the relevant details so we can help resolve your issue.
