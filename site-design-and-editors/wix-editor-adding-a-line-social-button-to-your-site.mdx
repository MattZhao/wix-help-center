---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding a LINE Social Button to Your Site"
---

LINE is a social app allowing users to make voice and video calls, send one-on-one or group messages and more. Add a LINE social share button to your site to let your visitors share your page on their LINE social network.

> **Note:**
>
> Each LINE social share button that you add to your site shares the page that it's placed on.

### To add a LINE social button:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Social**.  Click **LINE**.  Drag the LINE button on to your page.

> **Next step:**
>
> Click the **Layout** icon  to change the layout of your button.
