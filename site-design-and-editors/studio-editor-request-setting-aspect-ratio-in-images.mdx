---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting Aspect Ratio in Images"
---

Currently, it is not possible to select a preset aspect ratio (such as 16:9, 5:4, etc.) for images in the Studio Editor.

As an alternative, you can manually change an image's width / height by dragging its side handles. You can also enter the width and height values in the Inspector panel for more precise sizing. [Learn more about sizing images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-size-of-your-elements#resizing-an-element)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
