---
title: "Accessibility Request: Receiving a Review from a Wix Accessibility Expert"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer paid or free reviews from experts to check how accessible your site is. In order to get your site reviewed, you must contact an external accessibility advisor in your region.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
