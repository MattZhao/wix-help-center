---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding Borders to Strips and Columns"
---

Currently, Wix does not support adding and customizing borders on strips and columns.

As an alternative, you can [add lines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-decorative-shapes-and-lines) to the edges of your strips and columns from the **Add Elements** panel on the left.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
