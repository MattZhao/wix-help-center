---
title: "Adding Media to an Answer in the Wix FAQ App"
---

Add some extra creativity to your FAQ's using the rich content options available in the toolbar.

---

### To add media:

Click the FAQ app in your editor.  Click **Manage FAQs**.  Hover over the question you want to add media to.  Click the Edit icon .

Choose the relevant media from the toolbar and add it to your answer.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also add media when you [add a new question](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-new-question-in-the-wix-faq-app).
