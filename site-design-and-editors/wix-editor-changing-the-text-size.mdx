---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Text Size"
---

Customize your site layout and design by adjusting your text size.

### To change the text size:

Click the text element in the Editor.Click **Edit Text**.(Optional) To change the size of only part of the text box, select the relevant text.Drag the **Font size **slider  to increase or decrease the size of the text, or enter a specific value between 6 and 999 pixels.
