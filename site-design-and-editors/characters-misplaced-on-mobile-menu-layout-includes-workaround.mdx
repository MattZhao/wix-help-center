---
title: "Characters Misplaced on Mobile Menu Layout (Includes Workaround)"
---

We're aware of the issue with characters being misplaced on Mobile Menu Layout.

The issue occurs if special characters are added.

We are currently gathering more information in order to reach a solution.

> **In the meantime, you can fix it by following these steps:**
>
> Change menu alignment to left - switch back to Centered

> **OR:**
>
> Switch the submenu arrow alignment to Right
