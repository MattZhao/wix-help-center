---

### Step 1: Please check that:

If you are having trouble viewing your ADI site on a mobile device, make sure that the **device and browser** you're using are supported:

### Supported operating systems on mobile devices

- iPhone: iOS 12 and above
- Android: 9.0 and above

### Supported browsers on mobile devices

- Google Chrome
- Safari for iOS (version 12 and above)

---

### Step 2: Next, try the following troubleshooting steps:

Make sure that you're using the most up-to-date version of your mobile browser and that the browser is compatible with Wix (Chrome or Safari). If not, you can download a new browser from the Apple App Store or the Google Play Store.Clear your browser's cache and cookies. For instructions on how to clear the cache on your mobile device, please choose from the list below: 
- [Android](http://androidadvices.com/remove-browser-history-cache-form-data-in-android-devices/)
- [iPhone](http://www.wikihow.com/Clear-Your-Browser's-Cache-on-an-iPhone)
