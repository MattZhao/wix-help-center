If you are experiencing issues with your live stream, check out this troubleshooting checklist to help resolve the issue.

### Troubleshooting Live Streaming Issues

We recommend that you try the following solutions:

### Check that you have the correct channel connected to your stream

1. [Go to Video Library](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Videos&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/video-library/videos) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Live Stream**.
3. Click **I Have an Encoder** (If you don't have the encoder yet, learn how to get it [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-downloading-a-live-stream-encoder)).
4. Choose a Wix Video channel for your live stream.  [image: A screenshot of the Choose a Channel drop-down setting for setting up a live stream within the Wix Video library.]

### Ensure that the Server URL and Stream Key are entered correctly to the OBS encoder

**Note**: The Server URLs and the Stream Keys are updated every hour or whenever a new stream is created.
1. Open the OBS Studio Encoder on your computer.
2. Click **Stream** on the left.
3. Check that the Server URL and Stream Key match the information in your dashboard.
4. Go to your dashboard.
5. Click **Live Stream** on the left.
6. Click **Connect Stream**.       [image: A screenshot of the Connect Stream settings in the Wix Video library.]
7. Check the information to ensure it matches in OBS.  [image: A screenshot of the server and stream key details of a live stream video in an OBS encoder.]
8. Click **OK**.
**Note**: If the information does not match, copy the information in your dashboard and paste it into OBS.

### Ensure that your OBS settings are set to the recommended settings

1. Open the OBS Studio Encoder on your computer.
2. Click **Settings** on the bottom right.
3. Select the **Output** tab to change the codec, audio and video bitrates.
4. Select the **Video** tab to change the video resolution.
5. Click **OK**.
**Note**: To get the best results, make sure you don't exceed the following settings:
- Video Resolution: 720p (1280x720)
- Codec: x264
- Video bitrate: ~2500 kbps
- Audio bitrate: AAC 44.1kHz ~128kbps

### Check that your encoder is shown as software and not as hardware

In order to work correctly, your encoder needs to be set to software. 
1. Open the OBS Studio Encoder on your computer.
2. Click **Settings** on the bottom right.
3. Click **Output** on the left.
4. Check that the **Encoder** setting is set to **Software**.  [image: A screenshot of the Output settings in an OBS encoder.]
5. Click **OK**.

### Check your internet speed

We recommend that you check your internet speed as your stream depends on a strong internet bandwidth in order to perform correctly.

**Note**: Some ISPs might cap bandwidth please check with your ISP to make sure that this is not your case.

**Tip**: A good tool to check your speed is [https://www.speedtest.net/](https://www.speedtest.net/)
If you find that you are getting a connection error, we recommend checking the internet speed section as this often relates to connection failure. [image: A screenshot of the Failed to connect to server error message in speedtest.net]

### Lower the bitrate and resolution

As internet bandwidth is a big part of streaming, we recommend that you try to lower your bit-rate and resolution to help resolve live stream problems.
1. Open the OBS Studio Encoder on your computer.
2. Click **Settings** on the bottom right.
3. Click **Output**.
4. Change the bitrate to 2000 Kbps in the **Video Bitrate **field. [image: A screenshot of the OBS Studio Encoder showing the Video Bitrate settings.]
5. Click **OK**.
6. Click **Video**.
7. Click the drop-down arrows to change the resolution to the supported resolution of 1280x720.

[image: A screenshot of the OBS Studio Encoder showing the video resolution settings.]
8. Click **OK**.

### Ensure the output is set correctly

The output needs to be set to 'Simple'. If it set to 'Advanced' it can cause issues with the live stream.[image: A screenshot of the OBS Studio Encoder showing the Output settings.]

### Check your stream key

If you're experiencing a connection issue, check the stream key to ensure that it's correct. If it's the correct key, check the internet speed section.[image: A screenshot of an error message that will be shown if the specified channel or stream key can not be accessed.]

> **Streaming to multiple sources:**
>
> If you are experiencing issues with streaming to multiple sources, you will need to contact OBS as multiple source streaming is not supported by Wix.
