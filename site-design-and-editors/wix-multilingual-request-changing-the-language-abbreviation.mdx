---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Changing the Language Abbreviation"
---

Currently, the two-letter language abbreviations (e.g. EN for English) are based on [this standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ISO_639-1_codes). It is not possible to change the abbreviations used.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
