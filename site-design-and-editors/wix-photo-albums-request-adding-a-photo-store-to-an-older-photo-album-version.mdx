---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Adding a Photo Store to an Older Photo Album Version"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a Store to Photo Albums that were created before we launched Photo Albums Store. If you want to sell photos from your albums you need to create a new Photo Album.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
