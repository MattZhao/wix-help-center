### Adding media to a gallery

You can add media to your Pro Gallery any time using the Wix app. This is a great way to capture images and videos in the moment and upload them on the go.

#### To add media to your Pro Gallery:

Go to the relevant site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Gallery**.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant gallery.  Tap **Manage Media**.  Tap **Add Media**.  Tap **All Media** and choose where you'd like to upload your media from: **Site media**, **Device**, **Free by Wix**, etc.  Select the relevant file(s) and tap **Next**.   (Optional) Tap **Edit **and use the tools at the bottom to edit the file. Then tap **Save **and repeat for each file you want to edit.   Tap **Next**.

### Viewing and managing your galleries

While on the move, you may want to add or update the galleries on your site. You can manage the settings of all galleries directly from the Wix app.

#### To manage your galleries:

Go to the relevant site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap** Gallery **and choose what you want to do:

### Hide, show, and reorder galleries

1. Tap the **Settings** icon  and choose what you want to do:

- **Show / hide galleries: **Tap the **Show** / **Hide** icon  next to the relevant gallery.
- **Reorder galleries: **Tap the **Reorder** icon  next to the relevant gallery and drag it to your desired location.

**Note: **Hiding your gallery hides it from site members on the Spaces by Wix app, not your desktop site.
2. Tap **Save**.

[image: A screenshot showing where you can edit your galleries on the app.]

### Search through and select a gallery

The Wix app filters your galleries by all galleries, those displayed, those hidden and those created on the app.
1. Tap the **All Galleries** drop-down.
2. Tap an option to filter your galleries: **All Galleries**, **Displayed**, **Hidden**, or **Mobile**.

[image: A screenshot showing you can view specific filters for galleries]

### View gallery items, manage gallery settings, and manage media

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant gallery.
2. Choose what you want to do:

- **View Gallery:** View the photos of the gallery.
- **Manage Media**: Hold, drag and drop to change the order of gallery items.
- **Manage Settings**: Rename, and adjust the sharing, downloading, and display settings. Tap **Save **in the top right to apply your changes.

[image: A screenshot showing that you can view and manage your galleries]

### Create a gallery in the app

1. Tap **+ Create Gallery** at the bottom.
2. Add your media and tap **Next **at the top right. Tap **Next** again.
3. Type a title for your gallery in the **Title** field.
4. Tap **Create**.

[image: A screenshot showing you can create a gallery]

### Delete a gallery

It is only possible to delete a gallery created in the Wix app. To delete a gallery created in the editor, head to the editor.
**To delete a gallery created in the app:**
1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant gallery.
2. Tap **Delete Gallery**.
3. Tap **Delete **to confirm.

[image: A screenshot showing you can delete a gallery]

### Managing media in a gallery

You can manage the media inside a specific gallery from the app. Update your gallery on the go, by adding new images or videos, adding titles and descriptions and choosing where newly added items go.

#### To manage media in a gallery:

Go to the relevant site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap** Gallery**.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant gallery.  Tap **Manage Media **and choose what you want to do:

### Reorder gallery items

Hold, drag and drop to change the order of gallery items.

[image: A screenshot showing how to manage your media]

### Change the item's title / description

1. Tap the item and type your title and description in the relevant fields.
2. Tap **Save**.

[image: A screenshot showing you can rename your images in your gallery]

### Choose where new items should go

1. Tap the **Settings** icon  on the top right.
2. Select the checkbox next to your choice.
3. Tap **Save**.

[image: A screenshot showing that you can decide where the images in your gallery will go]

### Delete media

1. Tap the relevant item.
2. Tap **Delete** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Delete **to confirm.

[image: A screenshot showing how you can delete an image from your gallery]

> **Tip:**
>
> Tap **Select** to select and delete multiple images.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about managing galleries in the Wix app.

### Will galleries I add through the app also be added to my desktop and mobile sites?

Galleries created in the Wix app are only visible in the app. However, the photos uploaded to them will appear in the site's Media Manager.

### Which features are not customizable from the Wix app?

The following Pro Gallery features in the Wix app are controlled by the Pro Gallery's settings in the desktop editor:
- The behavior of an image on click
- Heart icon
[Learn how to customize these settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-gallery-settings)

### Can I add a title to my Pro Gallery?

You can add a title to your gallery in the Wix app. The title is, however, only visible to Spaces by Wix app users. If you would like to be able to add a title to a Pro Gallery on your live site, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-adding-a-title-to-your-gallery).

**To give your gallery a title in the Wix app:**
1. Go to the relevant site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant gallery.
4. Tap **Manage Settings.**
5. Tap the **Gallery Name** field and enter your gallery title.
6. Tap **Save**.
