---

Add a bonus item to your album or single to keep your fans interested and loyal. This means that when a fan downloads or purchases the album or single, they receive a bonus item such as a special image, screensaver or document.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adding a bonus item](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-adding-a-bonus-item-to-an-album-or-single#adding-a-bonus-item-to-your-album-or-single)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-adding-a-bonus-item-to-an-album-or-single#faqs)

---

### Adding a bonus item to your album or single

From your Music Library, you can add your bonus item to the relevant albums or singles.

#### To add a bonus item:

[Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant album or single.  Click **Edit**.  In the **Sale and Download Options on Your Site **section, click **Add an Item **under** Bonus Item**.   Click **Add Image** or **Add Doc **and browse for the bonus file.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about bonus items.

### What files are supported for bonus items?

Bonus items may be up to 15MB and in any of the following formats: .jpg, .png, .gif, .doc, .ppt, .pdf.

### How do I delete a bonus item?

1. [Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the relevant album or single.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. In the **Sale and Download Options on Your Site **section, click **Remove **under** Bonus Item**.
5. Click **Save**.

### How do I change the bonus item?

To change your bonus item, delete the existing one and add a new bonus item.
