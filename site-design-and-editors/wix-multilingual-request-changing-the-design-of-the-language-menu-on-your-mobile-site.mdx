While you can customize the text size and layout of your [Language Menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-customizing-the-wix-multilingual-language-menu-on-your-site) on mobile, it is not possible to make the design completely different from desktop.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
