---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Changing an Element's Color on Scroll"
---

Currently, the Studio Editor does not support the ability to change an element's color (e.g. text, shapes or containers) in response to a visitor's scrolling action on the page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
