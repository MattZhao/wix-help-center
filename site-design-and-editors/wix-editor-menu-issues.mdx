title: "Wix Editor: Menu Issues"
---

Your Wix site's menu automatically displays every page added to your site except for [pages you've chosen to hide](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu). View the most common menu issues and solutions below as well as frequently asked questions.

### To resolve issues with your site's menu click below:

### Menu missing on live site

If you cannot view your menu on your live site, try the following:
1. Make sure you've added a menu to your site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-site-menu)
2. If your menu is attached to the header or footer, or set to "show on all pages," make sure you are not hiding the header and footer: 

1. Click **Menus &** **Pages ** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click the relevant page that isn't displaying your menu.
3. Click the **Show More** icon .
4. Click **Settings **.
5. Click **Layouts**.
6. Select **Standard**.
3. Click **Publish **at the top right of the Editor, then refresh your live site and check that your menu works as expected.

Learn more about [hiding your header and footer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-the-header-and-footer-on-a-specific-page).

### Nothing happens when clicking your menu

If nothing happens when you click your menu, something is likely overlapping it. Make sure your menu is on the 'front' layer and that your header and footer are not set to freeze (causing them to be on top of your menu).

**To send your menu to the 'front' layer**:
1. Press **Ctrl** + **0** (zero) to set your browser's zoom to 100%.

**Note**: Press **Command **+ **0** (zero) if using a Mac.
2. Right-click on top of an area on your menu that is not working.

**Note**: Press **Ctrl** and click the mouse if using a Mac.
3. Hover over **Overlapping Items** and select your menu.
4. Press **Ctrl **+ **Shift** + **↑** on your keyboard to bring the menu to the front.

**Note**: Press **Command** + **Shift** + **↑** if you're using a Mac.
5. Click **Preview **at the top right of the Editor and check that your menu works correctly.
6. (If your menu works in Preview mode) Click **Publish **at the top right of the Editor, then refresh your live site and check that your menu works as expected.

**To unfreeze your header**:
1. Click the header.
2. Click the **Settings** icon .
3. Select **Scrolls with site**.
4. Click **Preview **at the top right of the Editor and check that your menu works correctly.
5. (If your menu works in Preview mode) Click **Publish **at the top right of the Editor, then refresh your live site and check that your menu works as expected.

**To unfreeze your footer**:
1. Click the footer.
2. Click the **Settings** icon .
3. Click the **Freeze Footer** toggle to disable the feature.
4. Click **Preview **at the top right of the Editor and check that your menu works correctly.
5. (If your menu works in Preview mode) Click **Publish **at the top right of the Editor, then refresh your live site and check that your menu works as expected.

### Menu doesn't work properly on some browsers or devices

If your menu works properly on some browsers and devices but not others, check the following:
1. Go to [whatismybrowser.com](https://www.whatismybrowser.com/) to make sure you are using the latest browser version.
2. If your browser's version is out of date, install the latest version. View [Wix's supported browser versions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers).
3. If the issue continues, follow the steps on the [Wix Technical Assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-technical-assistant) to resolve.

> **Additional troubleshooting tip:**
>
> Try deleting your menu then [adding a new menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-site-menu) to see if this resolves the issue.
