---
title: "Studio Editor: Applying a Glass Effect"
---

Apply a frosted glass effect to the background of your container, cell, or repeater item. This blurring effect adds a sleek touch to your design, perfect for highlighting important content. You can control the blur intensity to achieve the exact look you want.

### To apply a glass effect:

Select the relevant container, cell, or repeater item.  Click the **Open Inspector** icon  at the top right of the editor.

(Optional) Choose a **Background color **and use the slider to adjust its opacity.  Enable the **Apply glass effect** toggle.  Use the slider under** Blur intensity** to increase or decrease the blur.
