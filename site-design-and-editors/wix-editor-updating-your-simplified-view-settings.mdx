---
title: "Wix Editor: Updating Your Simplified View Settings"
---

Chrome has a new feature called "Simplified View" which makes websites more mobile-friendly by simplifying the formatting and removing nonessential elements.

The feature shows up as a pop up on any site accessed through a Chrome browser on Android and can be toggled on or off in your Chrome's accessibility settings.

However, Wix Sites are not compatible with this feature and enabling it does not affect your site.
