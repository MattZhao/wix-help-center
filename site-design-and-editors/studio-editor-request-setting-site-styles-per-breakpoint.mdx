---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting Site Styles per Breakpoint"
---

Currently, the [site styles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-your-site-styles) that you set for your client's site automatically apply to all breakpoints. The option to set individual color and typography styles for each breakpoint is not yet available.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
