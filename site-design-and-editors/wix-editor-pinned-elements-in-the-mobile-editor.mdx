---
title: "Wix Editor: Pinned Elements in the Mobile Editor"
---

Your Wix site comes with a mobile optimized version using the content from your desktop site. When the mobile version of your site is created, elements you have pinned on your desktop may be hidden in the Mobile Editor.

> **Note:**
>
> Pinned elements in the Mobile Editor can be unhidden but are no longer pinned on your mobile site.

### To view and unhide pinned elements in the Mobile Editor:

Go to your[ mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).   Click the **Hidden Elements** icon .  (Optional) Click  **Show** next to an element to unhide it.
