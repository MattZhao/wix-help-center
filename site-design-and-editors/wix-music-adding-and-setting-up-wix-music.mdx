[Wix Music](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-music) lets you share and spread your music online, completely free of charge. Add the Wix Music player to your site and choose which track, playlist or album it displays. You can also control whether visitors can share or download your music.

---

### Step 1 | Add Wix Music to your site

First, add the Wix Music player to your site in the Wix Editor. You can choose the design that best suits your site's style - compact or classic.

#### To add Wix Music:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Video and Audio**.  Click **Wix Music**.  Drag a Wix Music player onto your page.

---

### Step 2 | Choose music for your player

After adding the Wix Music player to your site, it's time to choose which music you want to display. You can choose from music you've already uploaded to your Music Library or add new music. Then choose whether to display an album, single, or playlist.

#### To choose music for your player:

Click the Wix Music player in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **My Music **tab.  Choose what music to add to the player:

- **Existing music:** Choose from music you've already uploaded to your Music Library.
- **New music:** Click **+ New Music** to upload a single, album or playlist.

---

### Step 3 | Set up your player

Lastly, set up your player to work just how you want. Adjust your player's settings to allow your visitors to share your music and enable autoplay. [Learn more about setting up your Wix Music player](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-changing-the-design-colors-and-fonts-of-your-wix-music-player#customizing-the-settings-of-your-player).

### Troubleshooting issues with Wix Music

Click an option below to learn how to resolve issues with Wix Music:

### The player isn't working

If your player isn't working, we suggest deleting the existing player and adding a new one. Your music is saved in your music library, so you can easily re-add it to the new player.

### Music files aren't uploading

Make sure that the file type you are uploading matches our [supported file types](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-adding-tracks#faqs). If the file is not supported by Wix Music, you can convert it to another file type.

### Music isn't downloading

If your issue relates to music downloads, try disabling the sale and then re-enabling it. [Learn more about selling your music](https://support.wix.com/en/wix-music/selling-your-music).

### Payments aren't working

If you are having issues with payments, check that a supported payment gateway is properly connected. [Learn how to connect a payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-setting-up-your-payment-methods).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the Wix Music player:

### Do I need to have a Premium plan to add Wix Music to my site?

No. You can add Wix Music to a site without a Premium plan.

### Can I view analytics reports related to Wix Music?

Yes. You can [view how your music is performing and learn more about your site visitors with Wix Music analytics reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-music-analytics-reports).

### How can I delete an album, single or playlist?

1. [Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  on the relevant album, single or playlist.
3. Click **Delete**, then **Delete** again.

### Can I set my player to autoplay?

No. Due to changes in policies of web browsers, many of them no longer support the autoplay of music and videos. This means that automatic background music on sites is no longer possible.

### How can I edit an album, single or playlist?

1. [Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant album, playlist or single.
3. Edit the information you wish to change.
4. Click **Save**.

### What are the recommendations for uploading images to my player?

We recommend uploading .jpg images of 3000 x 3000 pixels or more in size.
Images for each track should be the same size as the album cover image and be square as this is industry standard.

**Note:** Album or track art appears when downloading a file in MP3 format, but not in FLAC.

### How can I let fans download my music for free?

1. [Go to Music Library](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmusic%2F&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Open+My+Music) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant album or single.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Scroll to the **Sale and Download Options on Your Site **section.
5. Select **Free Download** under **Album / Single Download Options**.
6. Click **Save**.

[image: A screenshot of the My Music dashboard with the Album Download Options on Your Site option highlighted.]

### In what format is my music streamed?

Your music is delivered in 128kbps stereo MP3 format for effective streaming.
