---
title: "ADI Request: Adding the Google AdSense App"
---

The Google AdSense app lets you earn revenues by showing relevant and engaging ads alongside your site's content.

> **Important**
>
> Currently, you cannot add the Google Adsense app in ADI. However, you can click [here](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9187160?hl=en&visit_id=637091534747290780-63922611&rd=1) and follow the instructions to get an embed code. You can add the code to an HTML embed element to display your embedded content on your ADI site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-the-html-embed-element)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
