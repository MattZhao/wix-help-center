---
title: "ADI: Uploading a Document"
---

Add a document button to your site to share important information and documents with your site visitors.

### To add a document button:

Click the relevant section.Hover over the button. Click the **Edit **icon ﻿﻿.Click **Link to**.Select **Document**.Click **Select File**.Select a file from your media or click **Upload Docs **to add a new file.Click **Apply**. Click **Done**.

When your site visitors click the button they are directed to your document on a new page.
