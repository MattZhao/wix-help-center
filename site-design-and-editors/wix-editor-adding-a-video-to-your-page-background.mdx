### Adding a video to your page background

Get started by uploading a video to your page background.

There are a variety of free, striking options available from Wix, so you can choose something that's perfect for your brand and its aesthetic.

#### To add a video to your page background:

Go to the relevant page in your Editor.   Click **Site Design**  on the left side of the Editor.  Hover over **Page Background** and click **Customize**.  Select **Video**.  Choose what you want to do:

- Upload your own video:

1. Click **Upload Media**.
2. Browse your computer and select your video.
3. Click **Change Background**.
- Add a free Wix video:

1. Click **Media from Wix**.
2. Select a video.
3. Click **Change Background**.
- Purchase a Shutterstock video:

1. Click **Shutterstock**.
2. Select a video.
3. Click **Buy Video**.
    (Optional) Click **Apply to Other Pages** to add the background to other pages of your site.

> **Want to remove a page background?**
>
> To remove a page background completely, change the background color to white.

> **Did you know?**
>
> You can check how the video looks before you publish your site by clicking the Play icon in the **Page Background** panel.

### FAQs

Select an option below to learn more about video backgrounds.

### Do video backgrounds have sound?

Video backgrounds play without sound. If you want to display a video with sound, we recommend that you use a different element, such as Wix Video, Pro Gallery or the video player.

### How do video backgrounds work on my mobile site?

Your video background will automatically be set to not play on the mobile version of your site. To enable it, or select another video background, [reselect the video in the mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-video-backgrounds-on-your-mobile-site).

### What aspect ratios can I use on video backgrounds?

We do our best to accommodate any aspect ratio that you upload for background videos. However, there is no guarantee that your video will look correct or play properly if you upload a particularly unusual aspect ratio, such as 19:321.

### Anything else I should know?

When you add a video as your page background, we recommend keeping the following points in mind:

- Videos take a few moments to upload regardless of your internet connection speed. As your video is processed, the file is being transcoded, meaning converted and resized, to play in the highest quality on any screen. Processing time may increase for especially long or high quality video uploads. Your video won't appear on your site until the uploading process is complete. For best results, check out the [video file guidelines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes).
- Large or high quality video files may take a few seconds to begin playing, and may lead to an increase in your site's loading time.
