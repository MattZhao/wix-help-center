---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Displaying When Your Site Was Last Updated"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer a feature which automatically displays when your site was last updated.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can add this feature manually by adding a [text box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text) to your site and adding the date each time that you publish your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
