Currently, Wix does not have a virtual tour feature.

> **Update:**
>
> There is a third-party app in the App Market that allows you to add virtual tours to your site. Check out [Virtual Tours](https://www.wix.com/app-market/virtual-tours/overview).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
