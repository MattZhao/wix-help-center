title: "Wix Studio FAQs: Enterprise Users"
---

The Wix Studio enterprise solution is designed specifically for large businesses and organizations that need advanced features and support to build and maintain their websites.

### FAQs

### How is the Enterprise dashboard different from the Studio workspace?

The [Enterprise dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-enterprise-an-overview) lets your biggest clients see all their brand sites in one place, with advanced account level features for more efficient management and greater visibility across their entire business portfolio. The Studio workspace looks similar, but is designed for agencies to manage multiple client sites, reusable assets, and more.

### ​Is the enterprise solution integrated with the Studio workspace?

Yes. Partners can view, manage, and edit enterprise sites they create or contribute to using their Studio workspace. Partners can also access a client's Enterprise dashboard in their workspace accounts (if the client defines them as team members) to access account-level features and actions.

### I see that the Studio workspace and Enterprise dashboard both offer custom templates and apps. What's the difference?

Templates and apps in the Enterprise dashboard enable an enterprise to maintain consistency of content and functionality across their sites. For example, for a global chain that has franchises or dealerships, templates can be created at the corporate level and control which elements can be updated with local flavor. In Wix Studio, it is more likely that a Partner will create templates and apps they can use across multiple client sites, rather than designing from scratch for each individual client.

### If I close a deal with an enterprise client, are they eligible for technical training, special support or customized solutions?

Yes. All enterprise clients work with a dedicated customer success manager and get 24/7 priority support, hands-on training, product consultations, project review and guidance, tailored solutions, custom integrations, and more. Partners working on the project can also leverage these resources as needed.

### What exclusive security features can my clients can get with the enterprise solution?

On top of all the security features that come with sites built with Wix Studio, additional security features like SSO, audit logs, IP allowlisting, SCIM, and a custom SSL certificate are included with the enterprise solution to meet the needs of large-sized companies.

### What additional platform features can enterprise customers benefit from?

In addition to the account-level and security features mentioned above, our enterprise solution offers your clients the following exclusive benefits:
- Plug and play integrations for some of the most commonly used business apps
- Domain routing capabilities
- A step-by-step creation funnel for faster content deployment
- Custom solution development upon request

### Are there any differences in the infrastructure components provided to enterprise customers?

Every site built on Wix is backed by our best-in-class infrastructure. However, there are two further advantages offered exclusively to enterprise clients. A 99.99% uptime SLA, and flexible data storage so they can keep specific custom data under wraps by storing and processing data in their own cloud within a defined geographic boundary.

### Can Partners get access to a demo account so we can work with the new features and better sell them to our clients?

Yes. We can provide Partners with a sandbox environment to experiment with a solution based on a specific client’s use case.

### How much does an Enterprise plan cost? Does the Partner or the client make the payment?

Partners do not pay for the enterprise solution. The client pays directly and the Partner receives a share of the revenue (see more on that below). The cost of an Enterprise plan varies based on the complexity, requirements, and project scope of each customer. [Contact our sales team](https://www.wix.com/enterprise/form?referrer=kb-ent-rev-share-en) to learn more.

### What revenue share can I expect for bringing our larger clients?

In 2023 and 2024, as a Wix Partner, you have the opportunity to earn 50% of the payment made by a new client you onboard for the enterprise solution. Learn more about [selling enterprise for revenue share](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-selling-enterprise-solution-for-revenue-share).

### How do I know if my client qualifies as an enterprise client?

Generally speaking, an enterprise client is defined by the size of the company and/or its operational needs. The Wix Studio enterprise solution works with organizations of various sizes around the world, including global and national brands that need marketing landing pages or internal employee portals; financial firms that want to generate new revenue streams; companies that require multiple sites for franchises and dealerships or commerce; universities that need sites for academic departments, research centers, and faculty; national organizations and associations that build websites for local branches; and more.
