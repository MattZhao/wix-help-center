---
title: "Wix Media Request: FTP Service"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer an FTP (File Transfer Protocol) service, like Filezilla. However, we do offer some transfer / import options when uploading media to your site in bulk.

Learn more about the file transfer / import options we have available:

[Uploading Media to the Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-uploading-media-to-the-media-manager)  [Importing Media Files from Social Networks](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-importing-media-files-from-social-networks)  [Importing Media from Your Wix Sites](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-importing-media-from-one-wix-site-to-another)  [Importing Media Files Using a URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-importing-media-files-using-a-url)

> **Using Velo by Wix?**
>
> With the Fetch-API, you can integrate third-party FTP solutions with your Wix site. [Learn more about accessing 3rd-party services with the Fetch-API](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-accessing-3rd-party-services-with-the-fetch-api)

> **Tips:**
>
> You can upload files and make them available to your members using the [Wix Fileshare app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-file-share-app).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
