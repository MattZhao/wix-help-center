Reduce your workload and invite other people to help translate your site's content. Create a custom role from your site's dashboard and assign Wix Multilingual permissions to it. Your invitees will only have access to the areas of your site that you grant permission to.

### Adding a role with Multilingual permissions

Create a custom role with a title of your choice and define the role's permissions for working with Wix Multilingual. Then assign the new role to a person of your choice.

#### To add a custom role with Wix Multilingual permissions:

Go to [Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Roles & permissions﻿**.  Click **Manage Roles**.  Click** + Create New Role** at the top right.   Fill in the details of your custom role:

1. Enter a title under **Role Title**, e.g.,** **Translator.
2. (Optional) Add a description that outlines the new role.
    ﻿Click **Multilingual** under **Permissions**.  ﻿Select the checkboxes next to the relevant actions for this role, or select **Select All**.

Click **Save.**  (Optional) Assign your new custom role:

1. Click the Show More icon  next to the role you've just created.
2. Click **Invite People**.
3. Enter your invitee's email address.
4. Click **Send Invite**.

### Permission types

There are two permission types available in Wix Multilingual; Manage Multilingual and Translate Web Apps. Each permission has a defined set of actions a collaborator can carry out. Read on to learn what each permission entails.

PermissionAction**Manage Multilingual**Collaborators with this role can add languages, translate over the editor and back office, and can manage all Multilingual settings like auto-redirect.**Translate Website Apps**Collaborators with this role can translate app content in the Multilingual dashboard and export app content.
