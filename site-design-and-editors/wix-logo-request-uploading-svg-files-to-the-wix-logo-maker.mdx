---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Uploading SVG Files to the Wix Logo Maker"
---

Currently, it is not possible to upload SVG files to the Wix Logo Maker. You can however, upload PNG and JPEG files. [Learn how to upload files to the Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-uploading-your-own-images-to-the-wix-logo-maker).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
