### Ordering your merchandise

Choose the merchandise item(s) you want to order and clarify the size of the merchandise and quantity you want delivered. If you want to [order business cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-designing-business-cards-in-the-wix-logo-maker), the process is slightly different.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To order printed merchandise, make sure that you first [purchase your logo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans).

#### To order merchandise:

Go to the [Brand Studio](http://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh).  Click the **My Brands** drop-down at the top.  Select the relevant brand.

Click the **Brand Actions** drop-down and select **Print Logo on Merchandise**.  Click the **Shipping to** drop-down and select your shipping destination.

Click **Get Started **below the item you want to order.  Depending on the item you chose, follow the steps below:

### T-Shirts

1. (Optional) Customize your T-shirt template:

1. Click **Customize** at the bottom left.
2. Design your T-shirt using the available options.
3. When ready, click **Done** at the top right.
2. Select a color for your T-shirt: Black or white.
3. Choose a size from the drop-down menu.

**Tip: **Click **Size Chart** to get the exact measurements of each size.
4. Select a quantity.
5. Click **Add to Cart**.
6. Click **Continue to Checkout.**
7. Enter your shipping details and click **Save & Continue**.
8. Confirm your payment method and click **Submit Purchase**.

### Viewing your order history

Once you have completed your order, you can check your order's status and track your merchandise from the Brand Studio.

#### To view your order history:

Go to the [Brand Studio](http://www.wix.com/logo/maker/esh).  Click the **My Brands** drop-down at the top.  Select the relevant brand.

Click the **Brand Actions** drop-down and select **Print Logo on Merchandise**.  Click **Order History**.  Click **View items**.  Click **Track Order**.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you've ordered business cards from the Business Card Maker, [follow these instructions to view your order history](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-checking-the-order-status-of-business-cards-and-other-merchandise#checking-the-order-status-in-the-business-card-maker). You can also [view your business card order history from the Business Cards & More section of the dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-checking-the-order-status-of-business-cards-and-other-merchandise#checking-the-order-status-in-the-dashboard).

### Available products by region

Your custom merchandise order is printed and shipped by Order a Print. The available products vary per region. [Learn more about Order a Print](https://www.orderaprint.com/products-shipping).

> **Note:**
>
> Orders are fulfilled by [Order a Print](https://www.orderaprint.com/). You can cancel an order within the first 24 hours. Damaged or defective orders need to be reported within seven days of the delivery date to Order a Print and **not **Wix. Order a Print will investigate and send a replacement at their discretion.
