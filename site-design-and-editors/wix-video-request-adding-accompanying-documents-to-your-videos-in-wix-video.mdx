---
title: "Wix Video Request: Adding Accompanying Documents to Your Videos in Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to attach documents to your videos.

However, if you are monetizing your videos and would like to sell a document together with your video, you can follow this workaround:

### To sell a document with a monetized video:

[Add](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-document-button) your document to the [hidden page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu).   Add an [action card](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-adding-and-editing-action-cards) to your paid video and link it to the hidden page with the document.

> **Note:**
>
> - Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-finding-the-url-of-a-specific-page-on-your-site) to learn how to retrieve a URL for a hidden page.
> - Once your viewer has purchased a video, they can access your action card which leads to a page with your document.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
