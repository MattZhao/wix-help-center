[Web accessibility](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-about-web-accessibility) ensures that everyone, including those with disabilities, can access, navigate, and enjoy your site. Some visitors experience websites differently - and it is important to adapt your site to accommodate those experiences.

There are various ways to check and improve your site's accessibility, including the Accessibility Monitor. The Monitor is a tool that scans your live site for potential accessibility issues, and displays the status on your site's dashboard.

### Checking and using the Accessibility Monitor

Open the Monitor from your site's dashboard to check your live site's accessibility status. If any issues have been detected, you can use the Monitor to open the [Accessibility Wizard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-about-the-accessibility-wizard-7284021) in the Wix Editor and resolve them.

#### To use the Accessibility Monitor:

[Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/home/).  Hover over **Site & Mobile App** in the left menu.   Select **Website **under** Website & SEO**.  Check the **Accessibility** card.  (If issues identified) Click **Fix Issues**.  Follow the [steps in the Wizard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-using-the-accessibility-wizard) to improve your site's accessibility.  Save and publish the changes.

### FAQs

Select a question to learn more about the Accessibility Monitor.

### What site builders is the Monitor available on?

Like the [Accessibility Wizard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-about-the-accessibility-wizard-7284021), the Monitor is only available for sites built in the Wix Editor.

### What's the difference between the Accessibility Wizard and the Monitor?

The Accessibility Monitor identifies issues on the published version of your site **only**. The [Accessibility Wizard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-about-the-accessibility-wizard-7284021) identifies issues on the saved version of your site (that you may not have published yet).

### How often should I check the Monitor?

We recommend checking the Monitor every time you publish new changes on your site, so you can continue to improve your site's accessibility while expanding your brand or business.
