---
title: "Embedding a VKontakte Community Widget on Your Site"
---

Add a VKontakte community widget to your site to enhance engagement and promote community growth in your VKontakte community.

---

### Step 1 | Create a VKontakte Community widget

Although Wix does not have an VKontakte Community app, you can customize and design your widget from your VK account and copy the code to embed it on your site.

#### To create a VKontakte community widget:

Log in to your [VK account](https://vk.com/).

**Note: **If you do not have an account yet, go to [VKontakte](https://vk.com/) and sign up.  Go to the [Community widget in the dev portal](https://vk.com/dev.php?method=Community).  Customize the layout and design.

**Note:** You can see a preview of the widget at the bottom of the page.  Copy the code.

---

### Step 2 | Embed the code on your site

After you've copied the code from your VK account, return to your site's editor to embed it.

#### To embed code on your site:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Embed Code**.
3. Click **Embed HTML**.
4. Paste the code you copied from VKontakte into the **Add your code here (HTTPS only)** field.
5. Click **Update**.
6. (Optional) Enter alt text that describes what you're embedding in the **What's in the embed? Tell Google **field.
7. When ready, publish your site.

1. Click **Add Elements**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Embed & Social**.
3. Click **Embed Code**.
4. Click **Enter Code**.
5. Paste the code you copied from VKontakte into the **Add your code here (HTTPS only)** field.
6. Click **Apply**.
7. (Optional) Enter alt text that describes what you're embedding in the **What's in the embed? Tell Google **field.
8. When ready, publish your site.
