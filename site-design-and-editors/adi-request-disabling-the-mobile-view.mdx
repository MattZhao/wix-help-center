---
title: "ADI Request: Disabling the Mobile View"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to Wix ADI. Click [here](https://www.wix.com/support/html5/the-wix-editor) if you are looking for information regarding the Editor.

Currently, disabling the mobile view is not available in ADI. If you prefer the desktop site to be displayed instead, then you need to  [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the steps [here ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-enabling-and-disabling-the-mobile-friendly-view-of-your-site)to disable it.
