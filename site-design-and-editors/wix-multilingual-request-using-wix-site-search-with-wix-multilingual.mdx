---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Using Wix Site Search with Wix Multilingual"
---

Currently Wix Site Search works with Wix Blog, Bookings and [several other Wix apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-site-search#wix-site-search-limitations-1), however Wix Site Search doesn't currently work with Wix Multilingual.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
