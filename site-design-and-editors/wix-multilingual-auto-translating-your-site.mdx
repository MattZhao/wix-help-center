### Auto-translating your entire site

Auto-translate your entire site in one go and update the translation every time you add or change your site's content.

#### To auto-translate your entire site:

[Go to the Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmultilingual%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Multilingual) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Machine Translation**.  Click **Auto-translate Site**.

Review each page of your site and edit the translations if needed.   (If the newly added language is still hidden) Publish your translations:

1. Click the **Language **drop-down under your language and select **Manage Languages**.
2. Click **Hidden** next to the relevant language in the status column and select **Visible**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Some elements take longer to be auto-translated. We recommend waiting a few minutes and refreshing the page if you can't see the translation.

### Purchasing translation packages

Whether you've run out of word credits or just want to top up, you can purchase a translation package at any time. Choose from an additional 3K, 10K or 50K word credits.

#### To purchase a translation package:

[Go to the Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmultilingual%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Multilingual) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Machine Translation**.

Click **Check word credit balance**.   Click **Get more credits**.  Select the relevant package.  Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about auto-translating your site:

### What is the difference between "Auto-translate" and "Auto-translate Site"?

Both options use third party services to translate your text. Clicking **Auto-translate** in a text field translates only the specific element, while clicking **Auto-translate Site** translates all your site’s content.

### How many languages does auto-translation support?

You can auto-translate your site content into over 130 languages. However, keep in mind that third-party services may not support every language. So, if your language isn't supported, you'll need to translate your site manually.

### Is auto-translation initiated automatically for newly added languages?

No. If you add new languages after auto-translating your site, the auto-translation won't automatically apply. You'll need to go into your editor and follow the steps again to auto-translate the new language version of your site.

### Will newly added elements / pages be auto-translated?

No. New elements and pages that you add do not get automatically translated. To auto-translate a new page or element, access it in your editor and repeat the steps above.

### If I update content that has already been translated, will the updates apply to all languages?

No. Changes to translated content don't automatically update in other site languages. You need to update all translations manually or use the auto-translate option.

### Can I translate icons in my text elements with the auto-translate service?

No. The auto-translate option does not recognize icons as text. If you have icons in your text in your main language, these are not carried over into your secondary language(s). You must manually replace icons in your secondary language(s).

### Can I auto-translate collapsible text?

No. Currently, [collapsible text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-collapsible-text) can't be auto-translated yet, so you'll need to translate it manually.

### How can I view my word credit balance?

You can check your word credit balance in the left panel of the translation manager.**Note: **The counter doesn't show the balance for words used in some areas like blogs and email marketing.

### Can I transfer word credits to another site in the same or different account?

No. When purchasing word credits to auto-translate your site, the credits can only be used for that specific site. Currently, it is not possible to transfer word credits to a different site in the same account, or a different account. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-request-transfer-word-credits-to-a-different-site-account).

### If I purchase multiple packages, will the word credits accumulate?

Yes. Any new translation package you purchase will add to your total word count. Word credits do not expire, meaning you can use them now or later.

### Are translation packages refundable?

Translation packages are non-refundable. Once you've translated your content, it's considered complete, even if you later delete the translated text. This is because the translation process has already been initiated and completed.

### Can I edit my translations?

Yes. You can edit the automatic translations. In the translation manager, select the relevant text item and edit the text. When you're done, click **Save and Publish**.
**Tip: **If you want to cancel your edit, click the **Restore last saved translation** icon . When you're done, click **Save and Publish**.

### Can I keep my original language for some of my text?

Yes. For exceptional cases you choose to use the original language. For example, in a product name that you want to keep true to the source. In the translation manager, select the relevant text item and click the **Use [original language] Content** icon . When you're done, click **Save and Publish**.
