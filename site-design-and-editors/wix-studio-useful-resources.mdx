### Learn about Wix Studio

New to Wix Studio and the Studio Editor? These resources can help you build your knowledge at a pace that suits your needs:

[Read this article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-studio) to learn all about the Wix Studio offering, including the workspace and the editor.   Get to know the [Wix Studio workspace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-a-tour-of-the-workspace) and the [Studio Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-a-guided-tour).   Explore guided, interactive and expertly-crafted learning resources in the [Wix Studio Academy](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy). Be sure to check out the [Essentials course](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy/courses/wix-studio-essentials-course) and our [library](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy/library).  Subscribe to the [Wix Studio YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@WixStudio), where you can watch webinars, classes and video tutorials made by our team.
