The Media Manager contains all the media files on your site such as images, videos, audio, docs and vector art. It also includes media files that you have uploaded, free Wix media and your purchased media.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Accessing the Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager#accessing-the-media-manager)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager#faqs)

### Accessing the Media Manager

Access the Media Manager and your files directly from your editor or Wix app on your mobile device.

Wix EditorStudio EditorWix app
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.

**Note: **If you haven't uploaded media to your site yet, you can click the** Upload Media** icon  .
4. (In the Media Manager) Choose what to do next:

- **Upload Media:** Click to upload your own files.
- **Site Files: **All the files that you have uploaded while working on your site.
- **Visitor Uploads: **Any files uploaded by site visitors are displayed here.
- **My Boards: **Create private media folders that you can access from any of your Wix sites.
- **Trash: **These are deleted files that you don't need.
- **Media from Wix:** Use media from the free Wix library.
- **Shutterstock:** Purchase professional media to use on your site.
- **Unsplash:** Add stunning free images from Unsplash.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about accessing the Wix Media Manager.

### Are there additional Media Manager access points?

Yes. You are also directed to the Media Manager:
- When adding an image, video, vector art, music track, document or background to your site.
- From your site's dashboard, for example when changing your favicon or creating an email marketing campaign.

**Tip:** You can also reach the Media Manager from your dashboard by typing Media Manager into the search bar.

### Where can I access my Wix Music files?

The Wix Music Media Manager stores all your music files in one place and is accessed from your Music Library. [Learn more about accessing the Wix Music Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-accessing-the-wix-music-media-manager)

### Who can see my site’s media?

All site collaborators see **all **the media files in the Media Manager. Before adding a collaborator to the site, make sure that all the media files are files that you want them to have access to. 
In addition, you (as well as any other collaborator you've added) can access any files uploaded by one of your collaborators. You'll be able to see which collaborator uploaded the file in the file info.
You may have [imported files from another site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-importing-media-from-one-wix-site-to-another) into your site's Media Manager. When this is the case, any collaborators that you add to your site are able to see the imported files as well.
