---
title: "Wix Editor: Managing Your Page Settings"
---

Get extra control of your Wix site by using Page Settings. This is the engine-room of your website; allowing you to decide how site visitors and search engines view your pages.

From Page Settings, you can manage your Search Engine Optimization (SEO), allow VIPs access to your private pages, control Social Share settings for your social media accounts...and much more.

### How do I access my page settings?

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **More Actions ** icon on the page you want to work on.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the desired tab at the top of the **Page Settings** menu.

Your Page Settings are organized under several tabs. Learn what you can do under each tab below.

---

### Page Info tab

Manage your page's general settings from the Page Info tab. You can choose a different name for your page, make it your homepage and manage how frequently this page should be [cached](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-caching-pages-to-optimize-loading-speed).

#### To edit the Page info tab:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu ** the left side of the editor.  Click the **More Actions ** icon on the page you want to work on.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Page info**.  Choose what you want to do:

### Rename the page

Enter your page name under **What's this page's name (on your menu)?**.

### Hide the page from the menu

Enable the **Hide from menu **toggle to hide the page from your site menu. Your site visitors cannot access [hidden pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu) unless you link to them.

### Make the page your homepage

Click the **Set **to make the current page the sites homepage.

### Manage the page's caching settings

Click **Advanced Settings** and enable or disable the toggle next to **Manually control caching for this page**. You can then choose how often you want to reset the page's cache.

> **What's cache rendering?**
>
> Caching helps [speed up your page's loading time](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-caching-pages-to-optimize-loading-speed). We recommend keeping the automatic caching for your site's homepage and about page, but not for pages that include widgets (e.g., Store Shopping Cart, Bookings Widget).

### Layout tab

Change the header & footer layout for a specific page. Occasionally, you may want to alter this layout for a page that is still under construction, displaying a single image or other reasons.

To remove the header and footer from a specific page, click **No Header & Footer **in the **Layout** tab.

#### To edit the Layout tab:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu ** the left side of the editor.  Click the **More Actions ** icon on the page you want to work on.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Layout**.  Select an option for this page's layout:

- **Standard:** The page includes a header and a footer, as usual.
- **No header & footer: **The page does not show a header nor a footer. This option is mainly recommended for landing pages.

**Note: **This means your site menu won't appear on this page, nor will any other apps you otherwise show in the header and footer. You will, therefore, need to add a navigation link back to your homepage.

### Permissions tab

Create a private page to share with only those who want to see it. [Apply permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-applying-page-permissions) by giving your page password protection. Alternatively, add pages to a Members Only area for your VIPs or paying customers.  You can also [create member roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-member-roles) and restrict certain pages to specific roles.

#### To edit the Permissions tab:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu ** the left side of the editor.  Click the **More Actions ** icon on the page you want to work on.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Permissions**.  Adjust the [page permission settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site).

### Social Share tab

Change your page's title and description as it appears on social networks. Stand out from the crowd with your original title and page content.

#### To edit the social share tab

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu ** the left side of the editor.  Click the **More Actions ** icon on the page you want to work on.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Social share**.  Choose what you want to do:

### Change the og:title

Add your title under **What's the page's og:title (title on social media)?**

### Add an og:description

Write your description under **What's the page's og:description (description on social media)?**

### Edit X Settings

Adjust share image, card size, x:title and description under **X Settings**.
