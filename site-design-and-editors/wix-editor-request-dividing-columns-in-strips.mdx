---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Dividing Columns in Strips"
---

Currently, it is not possible to divide columns you have added to strips.

Dividing columns allows you to edit each part separately, as shown in the image below.

As an alternative, you can attach elements such as lines and text to different parts of the column to give the effect that they are divided. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-attaching-elements-to-strips-and-columns#attaching-elements-to-a-column)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
