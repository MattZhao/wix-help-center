---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Superscript or Subscript Text"
---

Currently, superscript and subscript text are not supported in the Wix Editor.

As an alternative, you can [change the size](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-formatting-your-text) of the text that you'd like to appear as subscript or superscript, or create separate text boxes and [group them together](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-grouping-and-ungrouping-elements).

#### To add and group separate text boxes:

Open the relevant page in the Editor.  Add a separate text element for your superscript / subscript text, and enter the text you want.  [Adjust the size of the text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-formatting-your-text).  Position the text box so it appears as subscript or superscript.  [Group the text boxes together](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-grouping-and-ungrouping-elements) so they act as a single element.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
