---
title: "ADI Request: Working with Velo by Wix"
---

Currently, [Velo by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-velo-by-wix) is not supported in ADI.

If you would like to work with Velo with Wix, you need to [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor), click **Velo **in the top bar of the Editor and click **Turn on Developer Tools**.
