---
title: "ADI Request: Showing the Tax Amount in the Wix Stores Cart Page"
---

Currently, if you set up your store using Wix ADI, the tax is automatically hidden from the Cart page until the billing address is entered.

> **Important:**
>
> This tax amount listed is an estimate. The final tax price is calculated during checkout.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
