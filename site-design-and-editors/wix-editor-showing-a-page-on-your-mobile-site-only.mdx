---
title: "Wix Editor: Showing a Page on Your Mobile Site Only"
---

Show a page on your mobile site only by hiding the page from the menu on your desktop site, then unhiding it on mobile.

This is a great way to tailor your site to mobile visitors, and provide a unique browsing experience.

### To show a page on your mobile-friendly view only:

Click **Menus** **&** **Pages ** on the left side of your [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-accessing-the-mobile-editor).  Click the relevant page.  Click the **More Actions** icon .  Click **Hide from menu **.  ﻿Click the Switch to Mobile icon ﻿ at the top of the editor.  Click **Menus &** **Pages ** on the left side of the mobile editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the **More Actions **icon .  Click **Show on mobile **.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Hiding the homepage on mobile only hides it from the menu. Visiting the domain or free Wix URL on a mobile device still loads the hidden homepage.
> - Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/mobile-editor-layout-issues) to learn how to improve the layout of your mobile view.
