title: "Wix Editor: Changing Your Page URL"
---

Each page on your site has its own unique URL. If you change a page's URL, Wix creates an automatic 301 redirect, taking visitors and search engines from the old URL to the new one. This helps prevent visitors and search engines from getting a 404 error if they go to an older broken link. Learn more about [search engine optimization (SEO)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/search-engine-optimization-seo).

> **Important:**
>
> You must first [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-your-site-to-premium-3066683) and [connect a domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere) to your site for the redirects to be created.

### Changing your page URL

Customize the URL of your pages from their page settings in your editor.

#### To change your page URL:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.  Hover over the relevant page and click the **More Actions** icon .  Click** SEO Basics**.  Enter the page URL under **URL slug (last part of the page's URL).**  Press **Enter** on your keyboard.

> **Tip:**
>
> Use words related to the page's content to increase the chances of that page showing up in relevant search results.
