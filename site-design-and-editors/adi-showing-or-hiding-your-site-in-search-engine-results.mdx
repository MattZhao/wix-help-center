Search engines like Google [crawl and index](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/advanced/guidelines/how-search-works#crawling) your site pages so that they can appear in search results. You have the option to hide your entire site from search engines, or just individual pages.

### Showing / hiding your site in search engine results

You can choose to show or hide your entire site from search engines from the SEO Settings in your dashboard.

#### To show / hide your site:

[Go to your SEO Dashboard](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-f3388913-140f-47a9-b2ba-4bf86b60e04e&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO).  Select **Go to SEO Settings **under **Tools and settings**.

Click the **Let search engines index your site **toggle:

- **Enabled: **People can find your site when searching keywords and phrases in search engines (e.g. Google).
- **Disabled: **People can't find your site when searching keywords and phrases in search engines (e.g. Google).

---

### Showing / hiding a page in search engine results

Choose to show or hide a specific page from search results directly from the ADI Builder.

#### To show / hide a page:

Click the **Pages **menu on the top bar inside ADI.  Hover over the page you want to show or hide and click the Settings icon .  Click **SEO Basics**.  Select or deselect the **Hide this page from search results **checkbox:

- **Selected**: Your page is not displayed in search engine results.
- **Not selected**: Your page is displayed in search engine results.

### How can I check whether Google has indexed my site or not?

You can check if search engines like Google and Bing have indexed your site, by entering "site:" followed by the URL of your domain. For example, "**site:**mystunningwebsite.com/".

[Learn more about checking if your site is indexed or not](https://support.wix.com/en/article/checking-if-your-site-is-indexed-by-search-engines)

### How can I change my site's name in search results?

The name that appears for your site in search results is the title tag of your homepage. If you have not entered a title tag, your site name is automatically displayed. [Learn how to change your home page's title tag](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-your-pages-title-tag-and-meta-description#changing-the-title-tag-of-a-page)
