Welcome agencies and enterprises to the new Wix Studio workspace tailored exclusively for you. The workspace is designed to help you manage all your projects and teammates seamlessly, while optimizing collaboration with your clients.

From your single Wix account (one email address), you can be part of multiple workspaces. For example, if you work for multiple agencies or businesses then you can switch between their workspaces as needed. This eliminates the complexities of managing multiple accounts.

The workspace is packed with powerful tools to simplify your workflow, boost productivity, and save you valuable time and resources. [Read all about the workspace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-a-tour-of-the-workspace)

### Creating a workspace

Create a workspace tailored to your business needs, keeping everything organized and in its right place.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> We highly recommend sticking to one workspace unless you're managing multiple agencies or businesses. Each workspace operates independently, without any shared elements such as sites, templates, teams, Partner program progress, etc. Maintain this focused approach to enhance efficiency, clarity, and effective collaboration within your team.

#### To create a new workspace:

Click the Workspaces drop-down at the top of Wix Studio.  Click **Create a workspace**.  Enter the details of your workspace:

- **Workspace name:** Add a name that will help all teammates identify the workspace easily.
- **Workspace logo:** Upload a logo for your workspace.
    Click **Create a workspace**.

---

### Changing the settings of a workspace

Change the settings of your workspace at any time. You can only change the workspace settings if you are an owner or co-owner of the workspace.

#### To change the settings of a workspace:

Click the Workspaces drop-down at the top of Wix Studio.  Click the **Settings** icon on the relevant workspace.  Change the relevant settings:

- **Workspace name: **Add a name that will help all teammates identify the workspace easily.
- **Workspace logo: **Upload a logo for your workspace.
- **Site URL prefix: **Changing the site prefix will affect the URL of any site that isn't connected to a domain. Any shared links will need to be updated manually.
    Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> Only the teammates in the workspace see the workspace name and logo.

### Adding teammates to a workspace

If you are the owner of the workspace, you can invite people to join your workspace. Go to Team to start adding your teammates.

#### To add teammates to your workspace:

[Go to Team](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/team?referralInfo=sidebar) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click + **Invite Teammates**.  Enter the teammates email address.  Assign them a role and set their permissions.

---

### Deleting a workspace

You can only delete the workspace if you're the owner. When you delete a workspace in Wix Studio, it permanently deletes all projects, settings and data associated with that workspace.

> **Important:**
>
> - Be cautious when deleting a workspace as this action cannot be undone.
> - If you only belong to one workspace and you delete it, you will also **close your account**.

#### To delete a workspace:

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Before you delete a workspace, you first must move the sites in the workspace to Trash. If your workspace has any Premium plans, subscriptions, or domains in it, you'll also need to cancel or transfer them first. For more detailed instructions, read [Deleting Your Workspace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-deleting-your-workspace).

[Go to Workspace Settings](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/team/workspace-settings?referralInfo=sidebar) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click the arrow next to **Delete this workspace**.  Click **Delete Workspace**.   Follow the instructions to confirm permanently deleting the workspace and all its data.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about workspaces in Wix Studio.

### Is there a limit on the number of workspaces I can belong to?

If you belong to 50 workspaces, you will not be able to create any new ones. You can create as many sites as you want within each workspace.

### How do I switch between workspaces?

If you belong to multiple workspaces, you can move between them easily. Click the workspaces drop-down at the top of Wix Studio and select the workspace you want to access.

### Is there a limit to the number of teammates in a workspace?

There is no limit to the number of teammates you can have in a workspace.

### Can I move sites, domains and premium plans from one workspace to another?

Not yet. Transferring sites, domains and premium plans between workspaces is a feature we're currently working on. However, you can transfer sites between accounts, just as you can today.
