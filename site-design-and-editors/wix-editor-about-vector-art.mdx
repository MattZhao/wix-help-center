Vector art images are digital illustrations or icons. They are created using vector illustration software, and bring life and color to your site. Our Vector art images are SVG files (Scalable Vector Graphics), which means you can resize your image without compromising its quality.

### Adding vector art

Vector art can be used for decorative purposes, or as a striking call-to-action.

When adding vector art to your site, you can either choose from the hundreds of free options available from Wix, or you can upload your own. Click an option below to learn more.

### Choose from the free options available from Wix

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Vector Art**.
4. Click or drag your chosen vector art to add it to your page.

### Upload your own vector art

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **Upload Media **icon .
4. Select your file(s) from your computer.
5. Click **Open**.
6. (Optional) Select the vector art and click **Add to Page **to add it to your site.

### Designing vector art

Begin designing your vector art to suit your site by clicking the **Design** icon . You can customize many aspects of your vector art, including the colors, opacity, and shadow.

At Wix, vector art is categorized into four types based on its colors and shapes. The customization options shown will depend on the type of vector art that has been added to the site.

Click the options below to learn how to design each type.

### Basic shapes

Basic shapes are a type of vector art that have one color. You can adjust this color and its opacity, as well as adding and customizing a border. After clicking the **Design** icon , choose from one of the below options to customize:

- **Fill color:** Choose the vector art's color and adjust its opacity using the slider.
- **Border:** Apply a border, and adjust its color, opacity, and width.
- **Shadow:** Apply a shadow, and adjust its angle, blur, color, opacity, and distance.

### Vector art with one color

Some vector art (such as icons) only have one color. You can change and adjust this color, as well as adding and customizing a shadow. Click the **Design** icon  and choose from the following options to customize:

- **Fill colors:** Choose the vector art's color and adjust its opacity using the slider.
- **Shadow: **Apply a shadow, and adjust its angle, blur, color, opacity, and distance.

### Vector art with 2-8 colors

These types of vector art are generally more detailed. Each color in the illustration can be changed, and you can also add and adjust a shadow. Simply click the **Design** icon ** **and choose from the options below:

- **Fill colors:** Choose the vector art's colors, and adjust its opacity using the slider.
- **Shadow:** Apply a shadow, and adjust its angle, blur, color, opacity, and distance.

**Note:** It is not possible to change the opacity separately for each color.

### Vector art with greyscale

Vector art with greyscale generally contains different shades of the same color. If this color is changed, the whole tone will be affected. You can edit this and any other secondary colors in the art, as well as add and adjust a shadow. Simply click the **Design** icon ** **and choose from the available options:

- **Fill colors:** Choose the vector art's colors, and adjust its opacity using the slider.
- **Shadow:** Apply a shadow, and adjust its angle, blur, color, opacity, and distance.

**Note: **It is not possible to change the opacity separately for each color.

> **Important:**
>
> Vector art you upload is categorized into one of these four types, and the customization options are displayed accordingly. You cannot customize the colors of your vector art if we do not identify it as one of the types listed above.

### Adding a link to your vector art

Add a link to your vector art to create a button or call-to-action. You can link to many places, such as a page on your site, an external URL, or even a phone number. Adding a link is a great way to encourage interactivity from your site visitors, and create something that is both eye-catching and functional.

#### To add a link to your vector art:

Select the vector art in your editor.  Click the **Link** icon .  Select the type of link you'd like to connect to the vector art from the list on the left.  Enter the relevant information.  Click **Done.**

### Adding animation to your vector art

Animation is a great way to bring vector art to life. With plenty of options available, you can create something fun and colorful.

#### To add animation:

Click the **Animate** icon .  Select an animation.  Click **Customize** to [adjust its settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-an-animation#customizing-an-animation).
