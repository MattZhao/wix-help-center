---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Changing the URLs of Individual Images"
---

Currently, the URLs of individual images in the Wix Pro Gallery are generated automatically. It is not possible to change them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
