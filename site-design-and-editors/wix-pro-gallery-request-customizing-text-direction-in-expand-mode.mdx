---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Customizing Text Direction in Expand Mode"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the text direction in the Expand Mode of your Wix Pro Gallery to be RTL (right-to left). The default text direction is LTR (left-to-right).

You can however [change the text alignment in the Expand Mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-changing-your-gallery-direction#changing-text-alignment-in-the-expand-mode).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
