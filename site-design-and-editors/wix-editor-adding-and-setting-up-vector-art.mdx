title: "Wix Editor: Adding and Setting Up Vector Art"
---

Vector art are small icons or illustrations that add fun and life to your site. With color customization options available, and the option to add a link, they can be both functional and decorative.

---

### Step 1 | Add Vector Art to Your Site

Vector art can complement and add to your site's design.  You can add as much as you like, and there are many available categories to suit your needs. You can either choose from the hundreds of free options available from Wix, or upload your own.

### Use free vector art from Wix

1. Click **Add** ** **on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Decorative**.
3. Select a category under **Vector Art**.
4. Click or drag a vector art to add it to your site.

### Upload your own vector art

1. Click Media  on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Upload Media**.
3. Select your file(s) from your computer.
4. Click **Open**.
5. (Optional) Select the vector art and click Add to Page to add it to your site. [Learn more about uploading your own vector art](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-your-own-vector-art)

### Step 3 | Customize the Vector Art

There are many options available to further customize your vector art. You can choose the color scheme, adjust the opacity, and even add an animation.

Click an option below to learn more about customizing your vector art.

### Edit the fill colors

1. Click the Design icon .
2. Click the** **Fill Colors icon ** **and choose from the options below:

- Edit the vector art's colors using the boxes.
- Use the slider to adjust the color's opacity.
- Click the **Reset **icon to reset the vector art back to its original colors.

### Add and customize a shadow

1. Click the Design icon .
2. Click the Shadow icon .
3. Click the toggle to enable a shadow and customize using the below options: 

- Adjust the shadow's angle by entering the degrees in the text field under **Angle**.
- Use the slider under **Distance (px)** to adjust the distance between the shadow and the vector art.
- Use the slider under **Blur **to adjust the shadow's blur.
- Use the **Color Picker** to change the shadow's color.
- Use the slider under **Opacity & Color** to adjust the opacity of the shadow.

### Add an animation

1. Click the Animation icon .
2. Select an animation.
3. Click **Customize** to further adjust its settings. [Learn more about customizing animation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-an-animation#customizing-an-animation)
