---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Applying Breakpoints to Multiple Pages at Once"
---

Currently, in the Studio Editor, you can [define breakpoints](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-designing-across-breakpoints) for each page of the site. It is not possible to apply customized breakpoints to all pages, or multiple selected pages at once.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
