---
title: "Editor X: Using the CMS"
---

Get started with the [CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview) to integrate database collections, user input elements, dynamic pages, and more. The CMS allows you to store huge amounts of content, accept data inputs from visitors and create page templates for displaying your content.

### How do i get started with the CMS?

1. Click **Add ** on the top of Editor X.
2. Click **CMS**.
3. Choose what you want to do:

- [Create a collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-creating-a-collection) to store your content.
- [Add a dataset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-and-setting-up-a-dataset) that connects your page elements to your collection items.
- [Add a form dataset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-creating-a-custom-form-with-input-elements-that-submit-to-your-collection) to connect input elements to your collection.
- [Add a rich content element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-rich-content-and-adding-it-to-a-dynamic-page) you can use to store formatted content in your collections.

### How do I add a collection to store my content in it?

1. Click **Create Collection**.
2. Enter a name for your collection.
3. Select an option to choose how many items you want in the collection:

- **Multiple items (Default): **Manage multiple pages or lists of items with the same layout like projects or services. You can store large amounts of content in this type of collection that is suited for dynamic pages.
- **A single item: **Manage content for static pages and sections like a homepage or a promotional banner. This type of collection is best suited for static pages because it has just one block of content. Learn more about [creating single item collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-adding-and-setting-up-a-single-item-collection).
4. Click **Create**.
5. Add your fields to the collection manually or [import a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-importing-content-into-a-collection).

Next, learn how to:
- Set [your collections permissions and privacy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-changing-your-collection-permissions) to control the type of content you store and who can view and edit it.
- [Sort and filter your view](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-hiding-sorting-and-filtering-fields-in-your-collection-view) in the collection.
- [Create a custom view of your collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-creating-custom-collection-views) to switch between different sorts, filters, and visible fields.

### What's a dataset?

Datasets are the vital link between the elements on your page and the content in your collection. You can use them to create a two-way connection, allowing you to display collection content, capture user inputs, or both. 
With dynamic pages, you connect the elements on the page to a dataset that connects to the relevant fields in your collection. You can also add filters to your datasets to exclude certain items, or add sort conditions that determine the order in which the items appear. 
Learn how to [set up datasets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-adding-and-setting-up-a-dataset) to begin integrating your site's content with your CMS collections.

### What is a rich content element?

Rich content elements allow you to include formatted text, images, videos, and more. You create rich content fields in your CMS collections and manage the content there. Add a [rich content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-working-with-rich-content-and-adding-it-to-a-dynamic-page) element to your page, then use a dataset to connect it to a rich content field in your collection.

### Can I create unlimited pages with the CMS?

Yes. Learn how to [create unlimited dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages) that get their content from your collections while maintaining the same consistent layout in each page.

### Can I connect Global sections to the CMS?

No. Currently, it is not possible to connect [Global sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-global-sections) to the CMS.
