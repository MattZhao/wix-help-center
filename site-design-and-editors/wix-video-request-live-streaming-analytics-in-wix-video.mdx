---
title: "Wix Video Request: Live Streaming Analytics in Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to track data for live streaming in Wix Video. However, you can see track data such as video plays, shares, and purchases in your [Wix Analytics video reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-wix-video-analytics-reports).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
