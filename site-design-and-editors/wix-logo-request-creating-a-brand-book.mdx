---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Creating a Brand Book"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create a brand book that details your brand guidelines in the New Wix Logo Maker.

You can, however, [review your logo's color guide once you download your logo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-downloading-your-logo-in-the-wix-logo-maker#reviewing-your-logo-color-guide), and view the colors and fonts you used.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
