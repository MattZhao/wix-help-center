---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Connecting Flexboxes to CMS collections"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect a [flexbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-setting-up-flexboxes) to [CMS (Content Management System) collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview), so it shows content directly from a dataset.

> **Tip:**
>
> As an alternative, you can use a [repeater](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-repeaters) which lets you [connect its elements to a dataset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-displaying-dynamic-content-in-a-repeater).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
