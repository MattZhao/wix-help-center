---
title: "Using the Wix Shared Gallery App on a Mobile Device"
---

Members can interact with your Shared Gallery right from their mobile phones! Site members can perform any actions (view, upload, share, etc.) they have [permission ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-file-share-app)to do.

> **Tip:**
>
> When accessing the Shared Gallery app on mobile, members can take a picture using their device's camera and upload it directly to a gallery.
