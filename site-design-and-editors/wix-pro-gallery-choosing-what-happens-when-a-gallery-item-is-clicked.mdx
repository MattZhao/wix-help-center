---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery: Choosing What Happens When a Gallery Item is Clicked"
---

Choose from a range of options and decide what happens when visitors click your Pro Gallery items.

### To choose what happens when an item is clicked:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click** Settings**.  Click the **Settings** tab.  Click the **When clicking on an item:** drop-down and choose what happens when a visitor clicks a gallery item:

- **Open in Expand Mode: **Items open in a popup where visitors can explore the finer details. [Learn how to customize the Expand Mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-expand-mode-for-your-gallery)
- **A link opens: **Clicking the gallery item takes the visitor to a link of your choice, e.g., an external website, a different page on your site or even a light box. [Learn how to add links to gallery items](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-a-link-to-a-gallery-item#adding-a-link-to-a-gallery-item)
- **Nothing happens: **Gallery items are not clickable.
- **Open in Full Screen: **Items open in a popup that fills the entire screen.
