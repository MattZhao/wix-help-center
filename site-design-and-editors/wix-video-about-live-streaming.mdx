title: "Wix Video: About Live Streaming"
---

Stream live videos directly on your site and connect with your fans in real time. Share anything: bring your class online, broadcast a concert, lead a Q&A session and more.

> **How long can I stream for?**
>
> - When using Wix Video for free, you can broadcast up to **10 minutes** in a single stream.
> - If you have upgraded to a Wix Premium Plan, you can broadcast up to **3 hours** in a single stream.

---

### Scheduling an Event in Advance

Plan your stream for when you need it. You can schedule a live stream event in advance to ensure that you don't miss out on a thing.  [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/scheduling-a-live-stream-in-wix-video)

---

### Live Stream from Your Desktop Using an Encoder

You can go live directly from your desktop using an encoder. An encoder is a special software that allows you to stream from your desktop computer. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-live-streaming-from-your-desktop)

### Additional Info

### Requirements for live streaming

- [Add Wix Video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-about-wix-video) to your published site, [create a channel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-channel-in-wix-video) in Wix Video and [display that channel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-displaying-a-channel-in-your-wix-video-player) on your site (Otherwise, your live stream won't appear).
- Make sure you have an encoder before going live. An encoder is the platform that connects your computer's camera to the Wix Video player. To learn more, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-downloading-a-live-stream-encoder).

### FAQs

**Refreshing your site:**
- If you go live without scheduling in advance, your site visitors must refresh the page in order to watch your live stream.
- For a scheduled event, your site refreshes automatically.

**Can I charge viewers to watch my stream?

**Yes! You can monetize an entire channel and live stream via that channel. To learn how to set up a channel subscription, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-channel-subscriptions-in-wix-video). 
**What happens when I end my stream?**
- Every live video is automatically saved to your Video Library and counts towards your media storage. A video that exceeds your storage limit is saved but unavailable until you delete older content or upgrade. To learn how to manage your video storage, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/unavailable-videos-in-your-video-library).
