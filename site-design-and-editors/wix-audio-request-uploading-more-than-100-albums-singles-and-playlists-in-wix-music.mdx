title: "Wix Audio Request: Uploading More than 100 Albums, Singles & Playlists in Wix Music"
---

Currently, you can only upload (up to) 100 albums, singles and/or playlists in Wix Music.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
