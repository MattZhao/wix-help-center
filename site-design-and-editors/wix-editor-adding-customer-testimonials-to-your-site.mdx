title: "Wix Editor: Adding Customer Testimonials to Your Site"
---

Adding customer testimonials to your site has a range of benefits, including building up a good reputation, peer recommendation and maintaining good customer relations.

**In this article, learn how to add testimonials:**

[As a strip](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-customer-testimonials-to-your-site#adding-a-testimonial-strip)  [As a list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-customer-testimonials-to-your-site#adding-a-testimonial-list)

---

### Adding a testimonial strip

Choose from a range of preset testimonial templates to display your positive feedback and build trust among your customer base.

#### To add a testimonial strip:

Click **Add Elements  ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Strip**.  Scroll down to **Testimonial **Presets.  Click the testimonial strip that you want to add, or drag it to the relevant location on your site.  [Edit the text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text) appearing on the strip.

---

### Adding a testimonial list

Choose from a range of repeaters to display positive feedback and build customer rapport. Repeaters make it easy for you to organize your testimonials. You can [customize the individual elements inside a repeater item](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-managing-repeaters-lists-grids), such as the text and images, but the design always stays the same.

#### To add a testimonial list:

Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click **List**.  Click **Repeaters**.  Click the repeaters list you want to add, or drag it to the relevant location on your site.  Click the repeater in the Editor.  Click a text field to [edit the text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text) appearing in the list.
