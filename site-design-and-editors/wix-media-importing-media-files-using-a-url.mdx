---
title: "Wix Media: Importing Media Files Using a URL"
---

Import media files to your Media Manager using a URL so that you can add them to your site. A media file with a URL link may come from another website, a social network, or from an [online portfolio](https://www.wix.com/portfolio-website) you have.

### To import media files using a URL:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Site Files**.

**Note: **If you haven't uploaded media to your site yet, you can click the** Upload Media** icon  .
4. Click **+ Upload Media**.
5. Click the **Link **icon  on the left side.
6. Paste the URL link and click **Import**.

1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).

> **Note:**
>
> It's not possible to import video files with YouTube and Vimeo URL's.
