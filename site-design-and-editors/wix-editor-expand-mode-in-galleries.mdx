> **Want to customize your expand mode?**
>
> This article refers to the Editor galleries. To customize the expand mode to look how you want, we recommend that you use the stunning [Wix Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-an-overview).

The expand mode in the Editor galleries looks like the image below:

The images are always displayed with a padding of 50px above and below. Adding a title and description to images makes them appear smaller.

> **Tip:**
>
> We recommend not to use transparent .png images in expand mode due to the colored background and no frame features.
