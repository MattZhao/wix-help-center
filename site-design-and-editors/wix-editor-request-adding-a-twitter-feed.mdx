---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Twitter Feed"
---

Currently, Wix does not have an in-house Twitter feed app. You can manually embed your Twitter feed using the [HTML element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/html-element-5087238).

> **Tip:**
>
> We have other Twitter elements that you can add to your site, such as the [Twitter Follow](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-twitter-follow-button) button and [Twitter Tweet](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-twitter-tweet-button) button.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
