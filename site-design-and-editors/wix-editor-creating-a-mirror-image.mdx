---
title: "Wix Editor: Creating a Mirror Image"
---

You can change an image's orientation to create a mirror image.

### To create a mirror image:

Click the image in the Editor.Click **Change Image**.Click the relevant image in the Media Manager.Click **Crop & Edit.**Click the **Flip horizontally or Flip vertically **icons.Click **Save**.Click **Choose Image** to replace the image on your site.

> **Note:**
>
> It is not possible to create a mirror image of a free Wix image.
