---
title: "ADI: Changing the Style of Your Button"
---

You can change and customize the style of your button.

### To change the style of your button:

Hover over the button on your site.Hover over the **Buttons **label.
- Click **Design** to select a style for your button.
- Select one of the colors from your Site Colors to change the color of your button.

> **Note:**
>
> If you have 2 buttons in your ADI section, the style of your second button is based on the settings of your first button.
