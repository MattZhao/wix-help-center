---
title: "Wix Video Request: Downloading Videos from Your Wix Video Library"
---

Currently, it is not possible to download videos directly from your video library.

In the meantime, you can do the following:

[Allow download](https://support.wix.com/en/article/allowing-video-downloads-in-wix-video) on the video you want to download, then go to your live site and download it.  If the video you want to download is for sale, you can add it to a [hidden page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu) to prevent your visitors from downloading it for free. To access your hidden page:

1. Click the **Pages Menu ** from the top bar of your Editor.
2. Click the relevant page.
3. Click the **Show More** icon .
4. Click **Page SEO**.
5. Click **Go to URL.**

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
