---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Randomize Gallery Images when the Page is Refreshed"
---

Currently, it is not possible to randomize images in a gallery, each time your page is refreshed. This feature would show a different image (such as a banner, or an ad), every time the page is viewed.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
