---
title: "Editor X: Your Site's Page Limit"
---

Editor X sites have a 100-page limit, referring to the static pages you add from the Pages panel.

[Dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages) are a handy solution to this limitation, as they allow you to have an unlimited amount of pages on your site. You can design dynamic pages as "templates" inside the Editor, then use them to display an entire collection from the [CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-the-cms).

When designing a dynamic page, connect the elements to their corresponding collection fields so they display the right piece of information. A dynamic page is automatically created for every item in your collection, synced with the content you add or edit in the CMS.
