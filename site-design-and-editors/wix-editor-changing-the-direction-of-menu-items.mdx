---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Direction of Menu Items"
---

Choose to display your menu items from left-to-right or right-to-left. This is useful for languages that write from right to left such as Arabic or Hebrew.

### To change the direction of menu items:

Click the menu in the Editor.Click the **Layouts **icon .Select an option under **Display items from**: 
- **Left to Right:** The menu items are displayed from left to right.
- **Right to Left:** The menu items are displayed from right to left.
