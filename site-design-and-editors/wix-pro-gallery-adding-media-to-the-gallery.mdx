Showcase image, video and text content side by side in your multimedia Pro Gallery. Upload your own media, add content from the Wix Libraries, and embed videos from YouTube and Vimeo.

### Adding media to your gallery

Add image, video and text items to your gallery from the Organize Your Gallery window.

> **Note:**
>
> When adding a video from Vimeo, make sure that your video privacy settings are set to "Anyone can see the video"** **and the embed settings are set to "Anywhere".

#### To add media items:

Click the Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Manage Media**.  Click **Add Media**.  Select the media item you want to add and follow the relevant instructions below:

### Image

1. Select the image from your Media Manager or click **+** **Upload Media** to upload an image from your computer.
2. Click **Add to Gallery**.
3. Click **Done**.

**Note:** We recommend you upload the best possible quality images to your gallery to ensure the best results. Make sure to upload images of at least 2560 X 1440 pixels in resolution. We optimize them to the exact dimensions displayed on the screen, without any loss of quality. [Learn more about image optimization](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-optimizing-your-media#displaying-images-on-your-site)

### Video

1. Select a video option:

- **Upload:** Upload your own video:

1. Select the video in your Media Manager or click **Upload Media** to upload a video from your computer.
2. Click **Add to Gallery**.
- **YouTube: **Embed a video from YouTube:

1. Enter the video's web address.
2. Click **Add Video**.

**Note: **When adding your own video from YouTube, make sure that your video is set to **Public**,** **and the **Allow Embedding** box is checked.
- **Vimeo: **Embed a video from Vimeo:

1. Enter the video's web address.
2. Click **Add Video**.

**Note:** When adding a video from Vimeo, make sure that your video privacy settings are set to **Anyone can see the video**,** **and the embed settings are set to **Anywhere**.
2. Click **Done**.

### Text

1. Enter your text in the content window.
2. Format the text using the options available in the top bar.
3. Customize the text using the following options:

- **Layout: **Choose a layout for the text: **Square**, **Horizontal**, **Vertical **or **Custom**.
- **Fill Color and Opacity:** Drag the slider to change the opacity and select the color of the text.
- **Box Padding: **Drag the slider to change the spacing between the text and the borders of the text box.
- **Vertical Alignment:** Select an alignment for the text.
4. Click **Add to Gallery**.
5. Click **Done**.

> **Tip:**
>
> When adding media to your gallery, you can also [add Titles and Descriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-and-customizing-text#adding-text-to-gallery-items), [Alt Text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-seo-for-your-pro-gallery#adding-alt-text-to-gallery-items) and [Links](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-a-link-to-a-gallery-item#adding-a-link-to-a-gallery-item) to each gallery item.

### Choosing the position and titles of newly added media

Decide where your newly added media is positioned in your gallery and how it's titled.

#### To choose the position and titles of newly add media:

Click the Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Manage Media**.  Click the **Settings for New Media** icon .  Select the checkbox next to your preferred position and title setting:

- **Where should new items go?: **Choose to display new media at the start or end of the gallery.
- **What title do new items have?: **Decide whether newly added media has no title or retains the file name as its title.

### FAQs

Click an option below to learn more about adding media to your gallery.

### How can I delete the default media in the Pro Gallery?

To delete the default media that comes with the gallery, hover over the item you want to delete, click the More Actions icon  and click **Remove**.

### How can I add media to my gallery if it's connected to the CMS?

If your gallery is connected to the CMS (Content Management System), please follow [these steps to add or update your media](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-connecting-the-media-gallery-collection-field-type-to-a-pro-gallery#step-1-add-media-to-a-collection).

### Can I play multiple videos in my gallery at the same time?

If you have more than one video in your Pro Gallery, only one video autoplays at a time.

### What is shown if I don't add a cover image to my YouTube video?

If your YouTube video does not have a cover image, the gallery displays the default YouTube screen. [Learn how to add a cover image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-a-cover-photo-to-a-video)

### Can I embed playlists from YouTube or Vimeo in my gallery?

It is not possible to embed playlist links from YouTube or Vimeo. You must add a specific link to an individual video.

### Can I control the quality of YouTube videos?

YouTube automatically changes the quality of your video stream based on your / your site visitor's viewing conditions. These conditions are why you / they may notice that the quality of your video changes as you / they watch it. [Learn more about the factors that determine video quality and how to change video quality on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/91449)

### How can I change the order of my images, videos and text in the gallery?

You can change the order of items in your gallery from the Organize Your Gallery window.

[Learn how to reorder your gallery items](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-downloading-images-from-a-gallery#reordering-items-in-a-gallery)

### Can I arrange gallery items by the date they were added?

Currently, it is not possible to arrange your gallery items by the date they were added. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-arrange-gallery-items-by-date-added).

### Can I randomize the order of my gallery items?

Currently, it is not possible to randomize the order of your gallery items in a Pro Gallery. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-randomizing-the-order-of-gallery-items).

### Can I edit images in my gallery using the Wix Photo Studio?

You can edit images added to your gallery using the Wix Photo Studio tools.
**To edit an image:**
1. Click the Pro Gallery in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Media**.
3. Click the image you want to edit in the **Organize Your Gallery** window.
4. Click the Edit Image icon  on the right.
5. Edit the image [using the Wix Photo Studio tools](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools).
