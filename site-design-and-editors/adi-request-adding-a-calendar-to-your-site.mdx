---
title: "ADI Request: Adding a Calendar to Your Site"
---

Currently, Wix ADI does not have a calendar feature to book services, appointments or schedule events. To add a calendar you need to [move to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the steps [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-calendar).
