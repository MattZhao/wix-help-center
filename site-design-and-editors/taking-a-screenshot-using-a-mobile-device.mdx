Communicate what you're seeing on your mobile device better by taking a screenshot.

### To take a screenshot using a mobile device:

**Android:** Click [here](http://www.howtogeek.com/121133/how-to-take-screenshots-on-android-devices-since-4.0/) for instructions.**iPhone and iPad:** Click [here](https://support.apple.com/en-il/HT200289) for instructions.**Windows Phone:** Click [here](http://www.windowsphone.com/en-us/how-to/wp8/photos/take-a-screenshot) for instructions.**Blackberry:** Click [here](https://help.blackberry.com/en/blackberry-classic/10.3.1/help/mba1359053806657.html) for instructions.
