---
title: "ADI Request: Customize Photo Galleries"
---

Currently, you can change gallery types and layouts, but there are some features that cannot be changed in ADI.

For now, the ability to customize the gallery elements is only available in the Wix Editor, and not in ADI. To use this feature, [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
