---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Customizing the Wix Music Player on Your Mobile Site"
---

Currently, you can customize the Wix Music Player on your desktop site, but you cannot customize the way it appears on the mobile version of your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
