---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Customizing the Social Sharing Icons"
---

With the Wix Pro Gallery you can share your Gallery items on social media using the social sharing icons. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-enabling-social-sharing-from-your-gallery).

Currently, it is not possible customize the design of the social share icons, or add additional ones.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
