### Adding a new page

Choose from a variety of professional pre-designed pages offered by Wix, or start from scratch with a blank page. Any new page you add is automatically added to your site menu.

#### To add a new page:

Click **Pages & Menu** ﻿ on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Site Menu**.  Click **Add Menu Item **at the bottom of the panel.  Select [**New page**](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=&buttonText=&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7B%7Besi%7D%7D%26openpanel%3DaddPagePanel).  Select either a blank page or a template:

- **Add a blank page:** Click** + Blank Page** at the top left.
- **Choose a template: **Hover over the design you want to use and click **Add Page**.
    Enter the new page name in the Pages panel.  Click **Done**.  Design the new page to your liking.

### Available page templates

Pre-designed pages include all the elements you need, organized in a sleek layout that is easy to manage. Our templates are organized by category so you can easily find the right page for your site.

Select an option below to learn more about the available template types.

### About

Use these pages to introduce yourself and your business, and make a personal connection with your visitors. Give them a good perspective on who you are by sharing your resume, story, or team. 
[image: A screenshot showcasing some of  the available About page templates.]

### Services

Use these pages to give an overview of the services that you provide. Services pages are equipped with advanced layout tools, such as strips, repeaters and forms. You can add and remove these items as needed, and customize them to fit your site's look and feel. 
[image: A screenshot showcasing some of  the available Sevcies page templates.]

### Projects

Use these pages to showcase completed projects and those you're currently working on. You can choose from a number of layouts, depending on whether you want to include image descriptions and text.
[image: A screenshot showcasing some of  the available Projects page templates.]

### Contact

Use these pages to inform your visitors of all the possible ways to contact you. Contact pages include elements like a contact form, social links and a Google Map in case you want to pin your physical location. 

[image: A screenshot showcasing some of  the available Contact page templates.]

### General

Use these pages for any kind of purpose. General pages feature many different layouts and elements that you can tailor to your site's needs. Some pre-built elements include galleries, buttons, and text boxes.
[image: A screenshot showcasing some of  the available General page templates.]

### 404 error page

Add and adjust a custom 404 error page. These are the pages your visitors see if they click a link to your site that doesn't work. You can even customize it to suit your site's theme and feel.
[image: A screenshot showcasing some of  the available 404 error page templates.]
**Important:** You need a Premium Plan in order to create a custom 404 error page.

### Landing page

Add a landing page to your site to keep your visitors updated and informed on your brand. You can choose from a range of options such as "Coming Soon" and "Down for Maintenance" pages. Some built-in elements include forms and app store buttons.
[image: A screenshot showcasing some of  the available landing page templates.]

### Store

Add a store to your site to display and sell products. [Store](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-your-store-pages) and [member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-your-customers-member-pages) pages are automatically added to your site, so visitors can view your products and keep track of their orders. Some of the pages added include "My Orders", "Shop", and "Cart".
[image: The Stores option in the Add Page panel has been selected. An example of a shop page is displayed.]

### Restaurants

Add Wix Restaurants to showcase and build your restaurant site. The app includes multiple restaurant pages to allow visitors to [view your menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-wix-restaurants-menus-new) and [order their favorite dishes online](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new).
[image: The Restaurants option in the Add Page panel has been selected. An example of a menu page is displayed.]

### Bookings

Add Wix Bookings to your site so visitors can view, book, and pay for your services in one place. The app includes [booking](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-bookings-pages) pages and [members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-my-bookings-page#adding-a-members-area) pages, so you and your visitors have everything you need.

[image: The Bookings option in the Add Page panel has been selected. An example of a book online page is displayed.]

### Blog

Add a blog to your site to share news, thoughts, and opinions with your visitors. Wix Blog comes with a blog page that showcases all of your categories and posts, and a page that displays singular posts.
[image: The Blog option in the Add Page panel has been selected. An example of a blog page is displayed.]

### Pricing Plans

Use Pricing Plans to showcase your subscriptions, packages, and memberships. It includes a page to display your services, as well as members pages for your visitors to manage their account and subscriptions.
[image: The Pricing Plans option in the Add Page panel has been selected. An example of a page is displayed.]

### Dynamic pages

[Dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages) are an easy way to display data from your CMS (Content Management System) collections. Use them to showcase any repeating content, such as property listings and portfolios. The presets include a collection, as well as a dynamic list page, and a dynamic item page.
[image: A screenshot showcasing some of  the available dynamic page templates.]

> **Did you know?**
>
> You can easily navigate to different pages by [selecting the **Switch page** panel on the top bar of the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-navigating-between-your-site-pages).
