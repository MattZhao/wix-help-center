---
title: "Wix Editor: Refreshing and Optimizing Your Mobile Layout"
---

The mobile-friendly version of your site is automatically created using the content from your desktop site.

You can adjust the layout by dragging elements to different locations, or by using the layout optimizing tool. This tool optimizes the layout of your mobile site to suit smaller screens, and ensure that all of your visitors have a smooth browsing experience.

> **Important:**
>
> If you're building your site using [our latest Editor experience](https://www.wix.com/blog/2022/07/wix-editor-evolution/), you won't have access to the Page Layout Optimizer tool. This is because Wix automatically optimizes your mobile layout in the new Editor experience.

### To optimize your mobile layout:

Click **Page Layout Optimizer﻿ ﻿﻿** on the left side of the [mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-accessing-the-mobile-editor).  Click **Optimize Now**.

> **Note:**
>
> - You can click Undo** ** to revert back to the previous version.
> - Changing the layout on the mobile site does not affect the desktop site.

> **Important:**
>
> It is not possible to revert back to the original template layout of the mobile site from before you added your content.
