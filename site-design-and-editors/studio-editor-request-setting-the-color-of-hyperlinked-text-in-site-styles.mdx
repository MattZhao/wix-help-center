---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting the Color of Hyperlinked Text in Site Styles"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set the color of all hyperlinked text across a site from the **Site Styles** panel.

However, you can change the color of individual hyperlinked text elements by opening the **Inspector **panel** ** at the top right of the editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
