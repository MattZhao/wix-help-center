title: "Wix Studio: Leaving Wix Studio"
---

If you're considering leaving Wix Studio and switching to the regular Wix experience, it's important to understand what features and capabilities you'll retain access to. While leaving Wix Studio means losing access to exclusive features such as custom templates, client experience tools, team management capabilities and more, you can still manage and edit any sites you've built with the Studio Editor.

> **Before you leave:**
>
> Before making any decisions, remember that you can build sites using the Wix Editor directly in Wix Studio. You have the flexibility to choose which editor you want to use for each site you build, depending on your needs. Learn more about [managing sites in your Wix Studio workspace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-managing-sites-in-a-workspace).

### Switching to the regular Wix experience

If you're the owner of the workspace, you can decide to leave Wix Studio and switch to the regular Wix experience. To do so, go to the workspace you first opened in Wix Studio.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Remove all of your teammates from this workspace. If you're a Wix Partner, you'll also need to remove your custom branding. [Review the FAQs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-reverting-after-switching#faqs) at the bottom of this article to learn how.

#### To switch to the regular Wix experience:

[Go to Workspace Settings](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/team/workspace-settings?referralInfo=sidebar) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click **Find out more** at the bottom.  Select the **I understand that I will lose access to all Studio and Partner features **checkbox to confirm.  Click **Leave Wix Studio**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about leaving Wix Studio and switching to the regular Wix experience.

### What will I still be able to do when I leave Wix Studio?

- Access any other workspaces you are part of*
- Access and edit any sites you built with the Studio Editor. All published sites will remain live.
- Access and manage existing client kits
- Access existing apps and create new ones from inside the Wix Editor and Editor X

**Note:** If you belong to other workspaces in Wix Studio, you will also still be able to access them after leaving Wix Studio.

### What won't I be able to do when I leave Wix Studio?

- Access exclusive Wix Studio features, such as custom templates, client experience tools and team management*
- Access exclusive Wix Partner features and benefits such as priority support, revenue share, access to the Wix Marketplace and custom branding*
- Create new sites with the Studio Editor
- Access this Wix Studio workspace
- Access higher free site CMS quotas that allow up to 10,000 collection items

**Notes: **
- You have to remove all of your teammates from your Wix Studio workspace before leaving Wix Studio. Wix Partners also have to remove their custom branding before leaving Wix Studio.
- If you choose to leave the Wix Studio, your free site CMS quota reverts to 1,000 collection items.

### Who can leave Wix Studio and go back to the regular Wix experience?

You must be the owner of the workspace to leave Wix Studio.

### Why can't I see the option to leave Wix Studio?

Only the owner of the team** **can decide to switch to the regular Wix experience. Additionally, you can only leave from the original workspace you first created in Wix Studio.

### I'm a Wix Partner. What happens if I leave Wix Studio?

Leaving Wix Studio as a Wix Partner results in opting out of the Partner Program. By leaving, you'll lose access to all exclusive Partner Program benefits and features, including your accrued points and any progress you've made within the program.

### Can I manage a site built in the Studio Editor once I leave?

You can still access any existing sites you contributed to in Wix Studio. If you were invited to collaborate on a Wix Studio site, or someone transferred a Wix Studio site to you, you can also manage it from your regular Sites page.

### What about my site's data and subscriptions?

Your site's data and subscriptions will remain unaffected. You'll still have access to the content and subscriptions associated with your sites.
However, your free sites no longer receive the higher CMS limits of 10,000 collection items, available in Wix Studio. The free sites revert to the lower CMS limits of 1,000 items.
If you have a free site that exceeds these limits, none of the existing items are deleted. However, you cannot add more items until you upgrade to plan with higher CMS limits or delete items. Learn more about [free site CMS limits in Wix Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-cms-limits-on-free-sites).

### What happens to my CMS quota if I leave Wix Studio?

In your Wix Studio account, free sites now have the following increased CMS quota:
- 10,000 collection items

If you choose to leave Wix Studio, your free site CMS quota reverts to:
- 1,000 items

*This is also the free site CMS quota if you never joined Wix Studio. 
Learn more about [free site CMS limits in Wix Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-cms-limits-on-free-sites).

### Will I still be able to manage and edit my sites?

Yes, leaving Wix Studio won't affect your ability to manage and edit your sites. You will still be able to manage and edit all your sites, and create new ones on [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/) using the Wix Editor.

### How do I remove my teammates?

To remove your teammates:
1. [Go to Team](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/team?referralInfo=sidebar) in your Wix Studio workspace.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant teammate.
3. Click **Remove from Team**.
4. Click **Remove Collaborator **to confirm.

### I'm a Wix Partner. How do I remove my agency's custom branding?

To remove your agency's custom branding:
1. [Go to Custom Branding](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/custom-branding) in your Wix Studio workspace.
2. Click the **Remove Custom Branding**.
3. Click **Remove** to confirm.
