---
title: "Studio Editor Request: More Text Properties in Site Styles"
---

When [setting typography styles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-your-site-styles#editing-the-typography-styles) for your client's site, you define the font and size for each style. Currently, some options that are available for text elements (e.g. min-max sizing) are not yet available for typography styles.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
