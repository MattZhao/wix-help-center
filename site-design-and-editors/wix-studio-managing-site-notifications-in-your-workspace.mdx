---
title: "Wix Studio: Managing Site Notifications in Your Workspace"
---

Customize your notification preferences in Wix Studio to manage notifications across all the sites in your workspace.

> **Notes:**
>
> - You’ll still get important site updates such as transactional emails.
> - Changes to notification preferences only apply to your workspace and don't affect your team.

### To manage your notification preferences:

[Go to Notification Preferences](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/account-notifications-preferences) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Choose what you want to do:

### Turn all notifications on or off for a specific site

- Click the **Enable notifications** toggle by a site to turn all notifications on or off.
[image: A screenshot of enabling or disabling notifications for a specific site.]**Tip: **Use the search at the top right to find a specific site in your workspace.

### Turn notifications on or off for all sites

- Click the checkbox at the top to select all sites, and then:

- Click **Turn Off Notifications **to turn all notifications off for all sites in your workspace.
- Click **Turn On Notifications **to turn all notifications on for all sites in your workspace.
[image: A screenshot of selecting all sites in your workspace and then turning on or off all notifications for these sites.]

### Select specific notifications for a site

1. Click **Manage** next to the relevant site.
2. In the **Dashboard** tab, choose which dashboard notifications to enable for this site.
3. Click the **Email** tab and choose which email notifications to enable.
4. Click **Apply** to save your changes.
[image: A screenshot of selecting specific notifications to turn off or on for a site in the dashboard and email.]

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also click the **Notifications icon** at the top right of your workspace, and then **Preferences**, to quickly access and adjust your site notification settings.[image: A screenshot of clicking the notifications icon and then clicking preferences to access the notifications preferences tab.]
