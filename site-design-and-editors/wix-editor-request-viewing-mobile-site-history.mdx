title: "Wix Editor Request: Viewing Mobile Site History"
---

Currently, your site history only shows the desktop version of your site. It is not currently possible to revert back to site changes you made in the mobile Editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
