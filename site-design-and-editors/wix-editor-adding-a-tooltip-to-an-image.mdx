---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding a Tooltip to an Image"
---

Add text to your image that appears when you hover over it on your live site.

## To add a tooltip to an image:

Click the image.
 Click the **Settings **icon.
 Enter the text in the **Does this image have a tooltip? **text field.

> To remove the tooltip, follow the instructions above but in step 3, delete the text.
