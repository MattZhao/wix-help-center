### FAQs

Click on a question to learn more about selling your Studio templates in the Marketplace.

### Who can submit Studio templates to sell in the Marketplace?

Partners can submit Wix Studio templates to sell them in the Marketplace. Learn more about [joining the Partner Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-joining-the-wix-partner-program).

### How do I get started with selling templates?

To get started, [create a custom Studio template](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-creating-and-using-custom-templates) following our guidelines and submit it to the Marketplace. Our team will review your submission, a process that usually takes up to 60 days. Once your template is live and sales start coming in, you'll be able to [track your earnings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-tracking-your-earnings).

### How do I get the URL of my Studio template?

To get the URL of your Studio template:
1. [Go to Custom Templates](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/my-templates) in your Wix Studio workspace.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the Studio template and select **Edit template**.
3. Click **Publish** at the top right.
4. Copy the template URL.
[image: A screenshot of copying your template URL from the dialog after publishing it.]

### When will my template be listed in the Marketplace?

Our team reviews all templates submitted according to [these submission requirements and guidelines](https://www.wix.com/studio/partner-program/submit-templates/guidelines). The process can take up to 60 business days. We will also send you updates as your template progresses through the review process.

### How is the template delivered to the client?

When a client purchases your Studio template from the Wix Marketplace, a copy of the template is automatically added directly into their account under their Sites. This ensures that clients can quickly access the template they've purchased and begin customizing their website without delay.

### How do I get paid when someone purchases one of my templates?

Wix takes care of the billing process and payouts on a monthly basis. To receive payouts, make sure that you've [connected a payout method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-receiving-revenue-share#connecting-a-payout-method) in the Revenue Share tab of your Wix Studio workspace. To qualify for a payout, your total earnings must reach a minimum of $200. Once you meet this threshold, the payout cycle is net 60 days.
You can track your sales in the **Earnings Overview** and **Template Earnings** tabs of your Wix Studio workspace. Learn more about [tracking your earnings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-tracking-your-earnings).

### Will Wix charge fees for each sale?

In 2024 we won’t charge any fees—100% of sales go to you.

### Can I update the details of my template after it has been accepted to the Marketplace?

Currently, you can't make changes to templates once they are live in the Marketplace. If you need to update the details or price of your live template, please contact our support team at [templates-support@wix.com](mailto:templates-support@wix.com) for assistance.

### How often are template purchases updated?

Template purchases are updated daily.

### What happens if a customer requests a refund for a template purchase?

Templates have a no-refund policy.

### Can I see who purchased my templates and contact them directly?

No, this is personal information that we can't disclose. When someone purchases a template, they have the option to contact the seller for support.

### Can I sell or promote a template I've submitted in other marketplaces?

Yes, you can sell your custom templates anywhere on the web—whether in other marketplaces or on your own site. Once you've created a custom template that is ready and published, you can download a shareable file to distribute to your clients wherever you choose. Learn more about [selling your custom templates](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-selling-your-custom-templates).

### What should I do if a template I submit is not accepted?

We recommend that you carefully review our [submission requirements and guidelines](https://www.wix.com/studio/partner-program/submit-templates/guidelines). Once the template meets our guidelines, you’re welcome to submit it again.

### Do I earn revenue share from templates I sell in the Marketplace?

No, you don't earn revenue share from templates; you earn from your template sales in the Marketplace. The buyer is the first owner of the site created from a purchased template.
