title: "Studio Editor Request: Rulers and Guides"

Currently, the option to show rulers and add guides (personal position markers) is not available in the Studio Editor.

> **Tip:**
>
> Use [the Design Guides add-on](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-apps-design-guides) to structure your client's site in a more organized manner, splitting it into rows, columns or cells.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
