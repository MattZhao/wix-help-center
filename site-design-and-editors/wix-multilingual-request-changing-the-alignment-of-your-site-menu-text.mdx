---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Changing the Alignment of Your Site Menu Text"
---

Currently you cannot change the alignment of the text in your site menu tabs for different languages in Wix Multilingual. You can only have the text aligned to the left or to the right in all the languages.

We appreciate your request for such a feature. We are always working to update and improve our products, and have forwarded your feedback to the relevant department.
