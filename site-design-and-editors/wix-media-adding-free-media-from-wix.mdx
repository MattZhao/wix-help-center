title: "Wix Media: Adding Free Media from Wix"
---

The Media Manager contains hundreds of free Wix images, videos, shapes and icons that you can add to your site. Browse and search through Media from Wix to find the perfect visual elements to enhance the design of your site.

> **Terms of Use:**
>
> - The free Wix media files found in your Media Manager and on your template are royalty free images that we have provided for your convenience. You can use any of these images on your Wix site without breaking any international copyright laws.
> - Free Wix media files are available for digital use within Wix ONLY. For more information, see the Wix [Terms of Use](http://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).
> - Be careful with media found in external (non-Wix) URLs. When the media file is not owned by Wix, you need to contact the author for permission to use it on your site.

### To add free media from Wix:

Wix EditorStudio EditorWix app
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Show More** under **Free Wix Images **or under **Free Wix Videos.**
4. Select the media you want to add.
5. Click **Add to Page**.
Tip:To view more options for media files that you like, hover over an image and click the **Related images** icon . Please note, not all media files have related media available.[image: Screenshot of choosing an image and seeing related images, with related images button marked in a square in Media from Wix.]
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Media**.
4. Click **Explore Media**.
5. Click **Show More** under **Free Wix Images **or under **Free Wix Videos.**
6. Select the media you want to add.
7. Click **Add to Page**.
Tip:To view more options for media files that you like, hover over an image and click the **Related images**** ** icon. Please note, not all media files have related media available.[image: Screenshot of choosing an image and seeing related images, with related images button marked in a square in Media from Wix.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Media Manager **under **Settings**.
4. Tap **Free by Wix**.

**Note: **The app offers Free by Wix images only. Access your editor to choose from free Wix videos and other media types.
