---
title: "Wix Logo: Duplicating Your Brand or Asset"
---

You can easily duplicate a logo, or your entire brand, using the Logo Maker's Brand Studio. This simplifies managing your assets and helps maintain a consistent brand identity.

Duplicating a brand copies all of your brand assets (e.g. a logo or social post) while duplicating a specific asset saves a copy of it under the same brand.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Duplicating a brand](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-duplicating-your-brand-or-logo#duplicating-a-brand)  [Duplicating an asset](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-duplicating-your-brand-or-logo#duplicating-an-asset)

---

### Duplicating a brand

A brand is the primary logo you've designed, plus any other designs you have created in addition to that logo (e.g. a secondary logo or social post). When you duplicate a brand, it copies all those assets together. You can duplicate a brand directly from the Brand Studio

#### To duplicate a brand:

[Go to My Logos & Brands](https://www.wix.com/logo/maker) to access the stand-alone Logo Maker.  Click the brand you want to customize.  Click **Brand**** Actions**.  Click **Duplicate brand**.

---

### Duplicating an asset

By duplicating an asset (logos, social posts, social covers, business cards), you get a copy of this design under the same brand. You can duplicate the logo directly from your site's dashboard.

#### To duplicate an asset:

[Go to Logo & Brand](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/brand-maker?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Logo+and+Brand) in your site's dashboard.   Hover over the asset you want to duplicate.   Click the **More Actions **icon .  Click **Duplicate** to duplicate the asset.
