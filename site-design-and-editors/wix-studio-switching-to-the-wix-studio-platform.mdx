### Switching to Wix Studio

Switching to Wix Studio means moving to our new Wix Studio workspace, where you can manage all of your sites and projects in a centralized place and use our advanced Studio Editor to build sites.

> **Note:**
>
> Your existing sites will not be migrated to the Studio Editor when you switch over.

#### Joining via your account

If you are already using Wix, you will discover multiple entry points inviting you to switch, including through the Sites page, your site's dashboard and account profile, and the [Wix templates page](https://www.wix.com/website/templates).

#### Joining via Wix Studio

Sign up to Wix Studio from the Wix Studio homepage.

#### To join via Wix Studio:

Go to [wix.com/studio](https://www.wix.com/studio).  Click **Start creating**.   Log in with your Wix credentials.  Click **Try Wix Studio** to switch to the new platform.

#### Joining via a team invitation

You can also join Wix Studio via an invitation to join a team. If invited to a Wix Studio team, you will receive an email. Click to accept the invite, log in, and you will land in your team's Wix Studio workspace.

> **What's next?**
>
> After switching to Wix Studio, you'll land in the [Discover page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-about-the-discover-page) of your new workspace where you can find all the educational resources you need to get started.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about using Wix Studio.

### Who can use Wix Studio?

Wix Studio is perfect for anyone who manages multiple sites or works with various teams and agencies. If you're an agency or enterprise already using Wix or Editor X, you have the option to switch your account to a Wix Studio account.

### Do I need to pay for Wix Studio?

No additional payment is required to use Wix Studio. You only pay for Premium subscriptions as usual. Learn more about [Wix Studio Premium plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites).

### If I switch to Wix Studio, what will happen to everything in my existing Wix account?

No need to worry! When you switch to Wix Studio, everything you've already done in Wix will come along with you, including your sites, contacts and subscriptions. You will also still have access to all the previous features you used (and more!) and will be able to create sites using the Wix Editor and Editor X, and of course, the new Studio Editor.

### What will happen to my sites when I switch to Studio?

Once you switch to Wix Studio, all of your existing sites move into the new workspace where you can view, manage and edit them as usual. 
Sites created using the:
- **Wix Editor:** Existing sites created with the Wix Editor will not be migrated to the Studio Editor, but you can still create new ones in the Wix Editor after you switch to Wix Studio.
- **Editor X: **Currently, Editor X is transitioning to the Studio Editor and some sites are already available to migrate. Learn more about [updating an Editor X site to the Studio Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/updating-an-editor-x-site-to-the-studio-editor).

### Can I create sites using the Studio Editor without switching to Wix Studio?

No, if you want to create sites using the Studio Editor then you need to switch to Wix Studio (see above).

### Can I manage a site built in the Studio Editor without switching to Wix Studio?

Yes, you can manage a site created in the Studio Editor without switching to the Wix Studio workspace. Here's how:
- **Ownership transfer:** If someone transfers a site built with the Studio Editor to you, you can manage the site without needing to switch to Wix Studio. As the new owner, you will have access to the site's dashboard and editing capabilities.
- **Collaboration invitation:** If someone invites you to collaborate on a site built in the Studio Editor, you can manage the site as a collaborator without switching to Wix Studio. You will have access to the features necessary for your assigned role.
- **Switching back to the Wix experience:** If you switched to Wix Studio, created a site in the Studio Editor, and then switched back to the regular Wix experience, you can still manage the site without needing to switch back to Wix Studio. The site will be accessible from your Sites page, and you can edit it using the Studio Editor.
**Note:** You can only duplicate a site from the Wix Studio workspace if you're the owner of site.

### Can I be part of a Wix Studio workspace without switching to Wix Studio?

Yes, for example, if you are invited as a team member to a Wix Studio workspace, but you use the regular Wix experience, you can switch between the 2 workspaces as needed.

### Can I switch to Wix Studio from the Wix app?

If you are currently using the Wix app, you can sign up to Studio from inside the app and start your journey from there.
What's more, we have launched a brand new [Wix Studio mobile app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-app-an-overview), available on iOS and Android, to help you manage your workspaces, teams, sites and everything else you need directly from your mobile device! Just download the app to get started.

### Can I switch back if I decide I don't want to use Wix Studio?

Yes, if you feel that Wix Studio is not for you, you can [switch to the regular Wix experience](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-reverting-after-switching) at any time.
