### Recommended file types for printing

Depending on the Logo Plan you purchase you receive different logo file types. All file types (Standard or SVG) are printable but differ in terms of quality when printed.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The standard high-resolution files of 5000x5000 pixels (included in the Basic Logo Maker plan) may be suitable in some printing scenarios.
> - PNG files in their **original size** may also be suitable for printing in some cases. Keep in mind that PNG files decrease in quality when re-sized.

---

### Logo plans with printable files

Before choosing from a Basic, Advanced, Value, Deluxe, or Professional Logo Maker plan, it's important to decide what you want to do with your logo.

The Basic and Value Logo plans come with 8 standard logo files (5000 x 5000 PNG) in color, grayscale and monochrome which are suitable in some printing scenarios.  The Advanced, Brand Plus, Unlimited, and Business Logo Maker plans come with infinitely-scaling vector files (SVG files) which are most suitable for printing.
