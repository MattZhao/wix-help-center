---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding and Setting up a Twitter Tweet Button"
---

Adding a Twitter Tweet button to your Wix site allows your visitors to share your content on Twitter with just a click. This simple tool can help increase your online visibility and drive more traffic to your site.

### To add a Twitter Tweet button:

Click **Add **on the left side of the Editor.Click **Social**.Click a Twitter Tweet button under **Twitter **to add it, or drag the button to the relevant location.Click **Set Username.**Enter your Twitter username.Enter the text which appears on your visitors' Twitter feeds when they click on the Twitter Tweet button.
