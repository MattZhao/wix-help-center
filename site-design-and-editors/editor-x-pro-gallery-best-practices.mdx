[Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-an-overview) lets you display videos, images and text, all in one beautiful multimedia gallery. Read the guidelines in this article to make the most out of Pro Gallery in Editor X – discover the different ways you can customize your gallery and make it look great in any screen size.

---

### Finding the settings you need

There are two main areas where you can adjust different gallery settings:

The Pro Gallery settings panel  The Inspector panel

#### The Settings panel

The Settings panel lets you adjust your gallery's design and functionality. You can choose a layout that suits your images, decide what happens when clicking on an item, add animations and much more.

#### The Inspector panel

From [the Inspector panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-the-inspector-panel), you can adjust your gallery's size, position and behavior across breakpoints. These settings can help to ensure your gallery is responsive and looks great in every screen size.

---

### Making your gallery responsive

There are many different ways to ensure your gallery is responsive to all screen sizes. Read below to choose the best method for your design needs.

#### Check the gallery's size, docking and margins

You can show the same gallery layout on all screen sizes. We recommend using a fluid [measurement](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-units-of-measure) (e.g. %, vh, vw) to set the size of your Pro gallery, so it resizes automatically and in relation to your visitor's screen.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is not possible to adjust your gallery's height when:
> - Your gallery is set to scroll vertically.
> - Your gallery is horizontal with a fixed ratio.
>
> You can change these settings from the Layout tab in the gallery's Settings panel.

Still in the Inspector panel, check the docking and margins of your gallery on every breakpoint. If these settings don't fit a smaller screen, your gallery might only appear partially or not at all. You can make these settings relative to the screen using fluid measurements (e.g. %).

#### Stretch the gallery

You can stretch a gallery across a section so it automatically adjusts to different screen sizes. This means you don't have to worry about sizing, docking and margins.

#### Use different layouts

Pro Gallery offers many different layouts - some are more minimal and others suit larger screens better. You can choose a layout for each of your breakpoints so the gallery always fits your visitor's screen.

### Troubleshooting gap and spacing issues

If you find that there is too much space above, below or between gallery images, try the steps below.

### Reduce the spacing between images

Check the spacing you've set between images in your gallery's Settings panel. Using the slider, you can reduce the gaps. 

1. Click the Pro Gallery.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Layout** tab.
4. Click **Customize Layout**.
5. Scroll down to **Design Settings**.
6. Drag the slider under **Spacing** to reduce the gaps between images.

[image: A screenshot of the Layout tab in the Pro Gallery Settings panel, where you can set the spacing between images]

### Check the text box size

When your gallery text appears above or below images, make sure the text box isn't too big. If the text box is too big, it can affect the spacing and create unwanted gaps.
You can check the text box size and make it smaller from your gallery's Settings panel.

1. Click the Pro Gallery.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Design** tab.
4. Click **Texts**.
5. Select **Manually** under **Adjust text box size**.
6. Drag the slider under **Text box size** to make it smaller.

[image: A screenshot of the Design tab in the Pro Gallery Settings panel, where you can change the text size]

### (Slide show layout) Check the info bar size

The Slide Show layout includes an info bar that shows your image's information. If the info bar is too big, with a small amount of text that doesn't fill up the entire space, it can create gaps below the images.  

1. Click the Pro Gallery.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Layout** tab.
4. Click **Customize Layout**.
5. Set the **Info Bar Size** to a lower number or to 0 to remove the empty space.

[image: A screenshot of the Layout tab in the Pro Gallery settings panel, where you can adjust the info bar size]
