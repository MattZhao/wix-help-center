---
title: "Editor X: Creating a Custom 404 Page"
---

Customize the error page that visitors see when trying to access a broken link on your site. Design it to match your style and add helpful links to keep users engaged.

> **Notes:**
>
> - You must have a [Premium Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-your-site-to-premium-3066683) to create a custom 404 error page.
> - A custom 404 page includes limited design options from Editor .

### To create a 404 page:

Click the **Pages** icon  at the top left of the Editor.  Click the** Add New Page **icon  next to **Main Pages**.  Name your new page "error404".

**Note: **Make sure that the page URL looks like the following example: [https://www.mystunningwebsite.com/**error404**](https://www.mystunningwebsite.com/error404)   Hide the page from search engines:

1. Click the **Show More** icon  next to the relevant page.
2. Click **Page SEO**.
3. Click the **Show the page in search results **toggle to disable it.
    Design the page to your liking. Below are some ideas:

- Add vector illustrations related to error 404 in the **Media from Wix** folder of your Media Manager.
- Add links, buttons or a menu to help visitors navigate to other parts of your site. This minimizes the chances of your visitors leaving after getting the 404 error message.
- If necessary, you can hide the master header and master footer on this page.

> **Tip:**
>
> Make sure to check how the page looks like in all [breakpoints](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-designing-across-breakpoints-with-the-cascading-rule).
