---
title: "Wix Video: Troubleshooting Issues with External Videos"
---

Videos can fail to upload or embed for several different reasons, including incompatible file type, privacy settings or encoding issues.

### If you're having trouble with external videos, try these troubleshooting steps:

### Check your video's privacy settings

You can only embed videos that are public. It's not possible to embed videos that have privacy settings enabled.
Also, if you change the privacy settings after adding the video to your library, the video will not display or play properly.
**Note:** If you embed videos from Vimeo, select **Anywhere** from the **Where it can be embedded** setting (not **Specific Domains, **as this will prevent your video from displaying or playing properly).

### Check your video's Vimeo privacy settings

When embedding a video from Vimeo, make sure the privacy settings are set to Public. [Learn more about managing your video's Vimeo privacy settings](https://vimeo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/224817847-Manage-your-video-s-privacy-settings). 

1. Log into Vimeo and go to your [video manager](https://vimeo.com/manage/videos).
2. Click the relevant video to open the video edit page.
3. Click **Privacy **on the right.
4. Under the **Link** section set the **Who can watch?** setting to **Public**.

**Note**: The selection will save automatically.
5. Find the **Embed** section at the bottom of the panel.
6. Set the** Where can this be embedded? **setting to **Anywhere.

Note**: This selection will also save automatically.**

**[image: A screenshot of Vimeo's video privacy settings.]
7. Ensure that the video link privacy setting from the icon next to share and Embed in the top right corner is set to **Public**.

**Note**: Vimeo has recently updated their privacy settings which requires you to also select a maturity rating for your videos before it can be set to public.

[image: A screenshot of Vimeo's embed settings where you can also update your video's privacy settings.]

### Check the playlist URL

When you embed a [playlist](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-adding-a-youtube-feed), make sure that you add the URL from the playlist page, and not from one of the videos in it. 
[image: A gif showing how and where to get the URL of a YouTube playlist.]

### Check the Facebook URL

You can add URLs from [Facebook pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-adding-videos-to-your-video-library) and your personal profile as long as the video is public.
You also need to make sure that you approve the Wix app on Facebook when you embed the video.

### Check your Vimeo On Demand URL

If you want to add a Vimeo On Demand video, you cannot paste the video's URL directly to the Wix Video library. 
Instead, you need to click **Share **on the video, copy the URL from the **Embed **section, and use the URL to [add the video to your Wix Video library](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-adding-videos-to-your-video-library). 
[image: A screenshot showing the Vimeo share settings and the embed code.]

#### Still not working?

If the problem persists, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/support/conversations/category/contact) and provide us with the information below.

### Information to add to your support ticket

- The name or URL of your Wix site
- The URL of the video you want to upload or embed
- The name and version of the web browser you're using
- The name and version of your computer's operating system
- A description of the steps you've taken so that we can recreate the issue on our end
- A screenshot of the error message you see
