---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Transferring Your Albums from One Site to Another"
---

Currently, you cannot transfer your Wix Photo Albums from one site to another. This means that you cannot share the content of your app across sites.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
