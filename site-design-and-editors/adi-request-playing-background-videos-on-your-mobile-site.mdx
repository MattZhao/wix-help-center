---
title: "ADI Request: Playing Background Videos on Your Mobile Site"
---

Currently, video backgrounds in ADI do not play on mobile devices by default. Instead, the first frame of the video is shown.

> **Note:**
>
> The following video elements **do** play on mobile:
> - Page sections with box video layouts (Youtube/Vimeo/uploaded videos)
> - Page sections that contain the Wix Video player

We appreciate your request for such a feature. We are always working to update and improve our products, and have forwarded your feedback to the relevant department.
