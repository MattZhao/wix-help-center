---
title: "Accessibility Request: Creating a Dynamic HTML Sitemap"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create a dynamic HTML sitemap, which would automatically update when you add new pages to your site.

As an alternative, you can [create a site page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-page-to-your-site) that lists and [links to all of your site pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-link-to-an-element), and update the list when needed.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
