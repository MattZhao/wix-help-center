### Sharing your site to get feedback

Generate a link to share your site with whoever you want. When someone accesses your shareable Get Feedback link, they can view your site and add comments as needed.

#### To share your site to get feedback:

Click **Site **on the top bar of the ADI Builder.  Click **Get Feedback**.  Click **Copy Link** next to the Shareable link URL.

Send the Shareable link URL to anyone you want to receive feedback from.

---

### Reviewing and resolving your site feedback comments

When someone accesses your shareable Get Feedback link and adds a comment, you're notified by email. You can review, reply to, and resolve each comment from Get Feedback.

#### To review your Get Feedback comments:

Go to your site's Get Feedback feature:

- **From the notification email:** Open the email titled "*You got feedback on the [site name] website*" and click the **View Feedback** link.
- **From your site's dashboard:** Click **Site Actions **and select** Get Feedback**. Then select **View Comments**.
- **From the Editor or ADI: **Click **Site** at the top and select **Get Feedback**. Then click **View Comments**.
- **From your Partner Dashboard:** Hover over the relevant site under **Site Feedback** and click **Get Feedback**. Then click **View Comments**.
    (Optional) Select the type of comments you'd like to view:

- **Desktop or Mobile View: **Click** Desktop View or Mobile View** at the top.
- **Open or Resolved Comments: **Click the **Showing Open Comments** drop-down at the top-right and select an option (**Showing open comments**, or **Showing resolved comments**).

Review comments on the right and click **Take Me There** next to the relevant comment to view its location.

**Note: **You can also click the** Comment **icons  in the app to view comments.

(Optional) Enter a reply and click **Reply** to reply to the commenter's post.   Hover over the comment and click **Resolve** to mark it as resolved.

### FAQs

Select an option to learn more about the Get Feedback feature:

### What version of my site is displayed?

The last saved version of your site is displayed through your 'Get Feedback' link. This allows you to share your work-in-progress and make suggested changes before publishing your updates.

### Does the link have an expiration date?

The shareable link URL is valid for 2 weeks only. If you want more feedback after the 2 weeks have passed, you need to generate and send a new link.

### Can protected pages be viewed with the link?

The last saved version of your site is displayed in Get Feedback, which means protected pages (with passwords or set to members-only) are viewable into those who access the link.

### Do site Roles and Permissions impact the feature?

- [Site collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-changing-a-collaborators-role) and [Team Management teammates](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-partners-managing-the-roles-of-your-teammates) must have the **Edit Site** permission enabled in their Roles and Permissions to share Get Feedback links.
- Site owners can view all comments posted in Get Feedback. However, site collaborators and teammates can only view comments received via the Get Feedback links they've shared.

### My old comments weren't migrated. What happened?

On May 23rd, 2021, we migrated Get Feedback to a new server to improve performance and enhance the feature. As a result, some unresolved comments were not migrated. If you are missing comments in Get Feedback, contact [Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) to retrieve them.
