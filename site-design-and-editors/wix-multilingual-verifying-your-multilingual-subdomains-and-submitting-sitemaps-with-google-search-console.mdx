### Verifying your multilingual subdomains

Verifying your multilingual subdomains with Google Search Console identifies you as the owner of your subdomains. Verifying also tells Google the subdomains are active and helps show your site is trustworthy. Learn how to [troubleshoot errors when connecting your site to Google](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-seo-setup-checklist-troubleshooting-errors-when-connecting-your-site-to-google).

> **Important:**
>
> Make sure to [verify your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-your-site-to-google-in-the-wix-seo-setup-checklist) before verifying your multilingual subdomains.

#### To verify your subdomain:

Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about).   Click **Start now**.  Click the drop-down at the top left and select **Add property**.   Enter the full URL of the multilingual subdomain you want to verify under **URL prefix**. For example: https://es.mystunningwebsite.com

>
> Try again in an hour as your [subdomain's records](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-about-multilingual-subdomains) may need time to propagate.

### Submitting your multilingual subdomain's sitemap

A sitemap is a file of your site's pages, links and other information that helps Google read and index your site. Each subdomain has its own dedicated sitemap that needs to be submitted to Google individually.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can check your subdomains sitemap in the address bar by typing: **www.[your-domain.com]/robots.txt**. Scroll to the bottom to review your sitemap links for the relevant language.

Each language's sitemap lists the subdomain URLs for that language. For example, French URLs will be listed under the French sitemap.

#### To submit your subdomain sitemap:

Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about).   Select your subdomain from the **Search property** drop-down.

Select **Sitemaps** from the left side panel. Type **sitemap.xml** after your subdomain in the text field.

Click **Submit**.  Repeat steps 2-5 to submit a sitemap for each multilingual subdomain.

> **Tip:**
>
> Use your [Google Search Console](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en&ref_topic=9384513) to gain insights on your website analytics.
