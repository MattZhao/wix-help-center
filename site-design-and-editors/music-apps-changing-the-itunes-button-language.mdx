---
title: "Music Apps: Changing the iTunes Button Language"
---

Change the language of your iTunes button to match the language of your site. The iTunes page will also open in the same language.

### To change the language of the iTunes button:

Click the iTunes button.Click the **Settings **icon .Select a language from the drop-down menu under **Which language?**.
