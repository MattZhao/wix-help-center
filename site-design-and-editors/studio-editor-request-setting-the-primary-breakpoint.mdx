---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting the Primary Breakpoint"
---

In the Studio Editor, the desktop breakpoint is the primary breakpoint by default. It's the base of your design from which changes [cascade down to the smaller breakpoints](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-designing-across-breakpoints#cascading-design). Currently, it is not possible to set a different primary breakpoint (e.g. the tablet breakpoint).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
