---
title: "Wix Media: Adding Free Illustrations"
---

Bring your site to life by adding free illustrations to your site. Illustrations are unique, decorative images. They usually have transparent backgrounds to ensure they really stand out.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Image**.
4. Click **Show More** under **Free Wix Illustrations**.
5. Select an illustration and click **Add to Page**.
[image: Screenshot of Show More button in Free Wix Illustrations.]
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media **[image: undefined] on the left side of the editor.
3. Filter by **Illustrations**.
4. Select an illustration and click **Add to Page**.
[image: Screenshot of filtering for illustrations in Media from Wix.]
