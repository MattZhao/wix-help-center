---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting the Submenu to Only Open When Clicked"
---

When visitors hover over a submenu title on a menu, the [submenu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-managing-menus#creating-drop-down-submenus) automatically opens.

Currently, it is not possible to change this behavior so that the submenu only opens when clicked.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
