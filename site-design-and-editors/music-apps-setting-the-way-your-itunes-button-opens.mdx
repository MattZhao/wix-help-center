title: "Music Apps: Setting The Way Your iTunes Button Opens"
---

Choose how you want the iTunes music you have shared on your site to open:

### To change the iTunes display:

Click the iTunes Button.Click the **Settings **icon .Under **How does the player open? **choose an option: 
- **In a new window**: Opens in a new tab on the browser.
- **In the same window**: Opens in the same window as the site page.
