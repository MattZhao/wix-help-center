With the Wix Logo Maker, you have the option of [purchasing either a Just Logo plan or a Logo + Online Presence plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans). Depending on the plan type you have, upgrading to another plan may or may not be possible.

**In this article, learn more about upgrading:**

[Just Logo plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-changing-your-logo-plan-in-the-new-wix-logo-maker#upgrading-a-just-logo-plan)  [Logo + Online Presence plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-changing-your-logo-plan-in-the-new-wix-logo-maker#upgrading-a-logo-online-presence-plan)

---

### Upgrading a Just Logo plan

If you purchase a Logo plan, but later wish to upgrade to Advanced or Brand Plus - using your exact same design - you must still purchase the new plan at full price.

If you need to make any changes to your design, you can duplicate your existing logo and purchase the amended design separately. Alternatively, you can upgrade a Just Logo plan to a Logo + Online Presence plan, by paying full price and keep the same logo design or making changes. Read on to learn more.

### Upgrading a Logo + Online Presence plan

Depending on whether you want to upgrade a Logo + Online Presence plan to another Logo + Online Presence plan or a Wix Premium plan / Wix Studio Premium plan, you will have to purchase a new plan at full price, or pay the difference between your current plan and the new one.

Upgrading your plan does not affect your logo files. Your files are still yours to keep and can be downloaded as needed. Read on to learn more.

### Upgrading a Logo + Online Presence plan to a Wix Premium plan

You can upgrade to a Wix Premium plan directly from the Wix Logo Maker.
**To upgrade your plan:**
1. Select the relevant brand from the **My brands **drop-down.
2. Click **Upgrade Website Plan** in the top right.
3. Click **Select** under the new Premium plan.
4. Select the subscription period and click **Select**.
5. Click **Continue to Checkout**.
6. Enter your payment details and click **Submit** **Purchase**.

### Upgrading a Logo + Online Presence plan to a Wix Studio Premium plan

When upgrading your Logo + Online Presence plan to a Wix Studio Premium plan, you must first assign the Logo + Online Presence plan to the relevant Wix Studio site. After you assign your plan, you are prompted to upgrade to your Wix Studio Premium plan of choice.
**To upgrade your plan:**
1. Go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the plan you want to reassign.
3. Select **Assign to a Different Site**.
4. Click the checkbox next to the relevant Wix Studio Site.
5. Click **Next**.
6. Click **Compare Plans**.
7. Click **Select** under the new Premium plan.
8. Choose your subscription period and click **Select**.
9. Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Note:**
>
> It is currently not possible to upgrade from a Wix Premium plan to a Logo + Online Presence plan.
