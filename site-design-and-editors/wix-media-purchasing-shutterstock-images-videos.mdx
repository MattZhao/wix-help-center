### Purchasing Shutterstock media

Purchase media from Shutterstock through your site's Media Manager. You can filter images / videos by category for easy browsing, or find media by typing keywords in the search bar. Once you submit your purchase, your media is automatically added to the Media Manager.

Payments to Shutterstock are one-time only, meaning there are no recurring or subscription payments.

### Accessing your purchased media

Once you have purchased a Shutterstock image, a new folder called Purchased is automatically created in Site Files in the Media Manager. You can access your Shutterstock image from the Purchased folder and add it to any site in your account.

#### To access your purchased media:

[Go to your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager).  Click** Site Files **on the left side.  Double-click the **Purchased** folder.

---

### Deleting a Shutterstock image

When deleting a Shutterstock image, it moves to the Trash** **tab in your Media Manager. You can then delete it to permanently remove the image from your entire Wix account. It is not possible to retrieve a Shutterstock image after you delete it.

#### To delete your media:

[Go to your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager).   Click **Site Files** on the left side.  Double-click the **Purchased** folder.  Hover over the relevant image and click the **More Actions **icon .  Click **Move to Trash**.  (Optional) Click **Trash **on the left to permanently delete the Shutterstock image:

1. Hover over the relevant image and click the **More Actions **icon .
2. Click **Permanently Delete**.
3. Select the checkbox to confirm.
4. Click **Permanently Delete**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about purchasing Shutterstock media:

### Are Shutterstock images suitable for use as background images?

Yes. All Shutterstock images are the same size with a high resolution of at least 1600 pixels, making them suitable to use as a background image on your site.

### I purchased Shutterstock media from a different site in my account. How can I access it?

If you have purchased Shutterstock media from another site in your Wix account, you can access it in 2 ways:
- [Importing media from one Wix site to another](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-importing-media-from-one-wix-site-to-another)
- [Adding media files to a board in your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-boards-in-the-media-manager#adding-media-files-to-a-board-1)

### Can I purchase Shutterstock media in bulk?

No. You can only purchase one Shutterstock image / video at a time.

### Can I use Shutterstock credits with Wix?

No. You can't use Shutterstock credits to buy Shutterstock media via Wix.

### Is Shutterstock media refundable?

No. All Shutterstock media is non-refundable.

### I purchased the wrong media. Can I change it?

No. You can't change purchased Shutterstock media. This is because Wix purchases the media from Shutterstock on your behalf, so all purchases are final.

### Where can I view my Shutterstock invoices?

Your Shutterstock invoices are available to view on your Billing History page. [Learn more about viewing your invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-invoices-paid-for-wix-services#viewing-your-invoices)
