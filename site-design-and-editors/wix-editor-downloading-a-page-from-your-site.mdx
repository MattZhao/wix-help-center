---
title: "Wix Editor: Downloading a Page from Your Site"
---

It is not possible to download a Wix site or any of its pages as the Editor needs to be in constant connection with our servers. As such, it is not possible to work offline or save site pages to your computer.

As a workaround, you can use the "print screen" option to take a screen capture of your site pages.

> Wix does not support the exporting of files created using Wix to an external destination or host. All Wix creations are hosted on Wix's servers. The advantages of using Wix as your host include improvements to your site's loading time, search engine optimization and more.
