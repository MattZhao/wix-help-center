### Adding a shape or line

Click **Add Elements **** **on the left side of the editor.  Click **Decorative**.  Drag a shape, arrow or line onto your page.  (Optional) Use the available options to customize the shape, arrow or line.

---

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about decorative shapes and lines.

### Are there diagonal lines?

To add a diagonal line, simply follow the instructions above to add a line, and then [rotate the line](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-working-with-elements#rotating-elements).

### Can I create groups of shapes?

You can group shapes, lines, text or images together within a [container box](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-box). This allows you to move your shape designs around your page together.

### Can I change the size or colors of my shape?

Yes. Once you have added your shape, you can customize the color and border, resize it, or even turn it into a button. [Learn more about customizing your shape](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-a-decorative-shape)

### Can I customize the design of my line?

Yes, you can choose the color, design and border of your decorative lines to match the look of your site. Different line designs have different customization options. Click the line in your editor, click the **Change** **Design **and click **Customize Design**.
