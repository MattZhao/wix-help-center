---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding and Setting Up a VK Share Button"
---

Add a VK Share button to your site so that your visitors can easily share your site with their VK contacts.

To make it unique, customize the button text with a catchy call-to-action that will help get you noticed.

### To add a VK Share button:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Social**.  Under **VK**, click a VK Share button to add it, or alternatively drag the button to the relevant location.  Click **Change Text.**  Enter the text that should appear on the VK Share button.  To customize your button layout:

- Click the **Layouts** icon .
- Select one of the **VK Share** button layout options.
