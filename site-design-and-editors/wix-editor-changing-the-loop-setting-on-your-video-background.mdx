---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Loop Setting on Your Video Background"
---

Set up your video background to play on a loop.

### To change the loop setting on your video background:

Go to the relevant page in your Editor.   Click **Site Design**  on the left side of the Editor.  Hover over **Page Background** and click **Customize**.  Click **Settings ** on the video background preview.  Click the **Play in a loop** toggle to enable or disable looping: 

- **Enabled**: The video background plays on repeat.
- **Disabled**: The video background plays once on loading.

> **Note:**
>
> Video backgrounds are set to play in a loop by default.

> **Important:**
>
> Your video background will automatically be set to not play on the mobile version of your site. To enable it, or select another video background, [reselect the video in the mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-video-backgrounds-on-your-mobile-site).
