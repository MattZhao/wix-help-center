---
title: "Studio Editor: Adding Alt Text to Images"
---

Add alt text to your images to improve your site's SEO and accessibility. Alt text is a short description that search engines and accessibility tools can read since they can't see images.

### To add alt text to an image:

Click the relevant image in the editor.   Click the **Settings** icon .  Write a short description for your image under **What's in the image? Tell Google**.
