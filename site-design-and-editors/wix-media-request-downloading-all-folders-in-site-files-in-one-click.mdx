---
title: "Wix Media Request: Downloading All Folders in Site Files in One Click"
---

Currently, it is not possible to select multiple files and folders at once from Site Files in the Media Manager in order to download all your media in zip format. Multiple files within each folder can be selected and downloaded together, but each folder must be opened individually in order to choose and download the selected media.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
