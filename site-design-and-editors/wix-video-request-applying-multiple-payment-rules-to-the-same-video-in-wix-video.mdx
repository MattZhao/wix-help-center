---
title: "Wix Video Request: Applying Multiple Payment Rules to the Same Video in Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to apply more than one payment rule to your video. For example, you cannot make the video paid for some viewers and free for others, nor can you assign multiple prices to the same video.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
