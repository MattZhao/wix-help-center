---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Survey to Your Site"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a survey to your site from the **Add Elements** panel in the Wix Editor.

As an alternative, you can search the [Wix App Market](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-app-market) for Survey apps, or [embed code on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
