### Adding and setting up Wix Chat

First, add the Wix Chat app to your site from the Apps panel. Next, personalize your chat's settings and choose a preset design that complements your site.

#### Step 1 | Add Wix Chat to your site

Hover ﻿over **Add** at the top of ADI.  Click **Apps**.  Scroll down to **Chat **and click **Add**.   Click **Got It**.

#### Step 2 | Set up Wix Chat

Hover over the chat box at the bottom right and click the Edit icon .    Click **Settings**.   Customize the following options:

- **Chat title: **Change the title of your chat box.
- **Image: **Enable or disable the checkbox next to **Image **to show or hide a profile photo on your chat. Click **Replace **to change the image.
    Hover over the chat box at the bottom right and click the Edit icon .   Click **Design **and choose a new preset design for your chat.

### Can I chat with visitors who are browsing my site?

Yes, [learn how to view and chat with live site visitors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors).

### How do I delete Wix Chat?

You can delete Wix Chat at any time. 
1. Hover over your chat box.
2. Click the Edit icon .
3. Click **Delete**.
4. Click **Delete **to confirm.
