---
title: "Wix Editor: Adding and Setting Up a Facebook Share Button"
---

Add a Facebook Share button to your site so that your visitors can easily share your site with their Facebook friends.

### To add a Facebook Share button:

Click **Add ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Social**.  Under **Facebook**, click the Facebook Share button to add it, or drag it to the relevant location.

Click **Choose Page**.Choose which page to share: 
- **This Page:** To share the current page with your visitor's Facebook friends.
- **Home Page: **To share your site's home page with your visitor's Facebook friends.
Type the text for your button under **What does this button say?.**

> **Tip:**
>
> Add the Facebook Share button to your blog post page in your Editor, publish your site, and let your site visitors share your blog posts on their social media streams. Don't forget to [customize the thumbnail and text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-sharing-a-blog-post-on-social-media) for each post to match your brand.
