---
title: "Studio Request: Adding a Pagination Bar in the Studio Editor"
---

Currently, in the Studio Editor, it is not possible to add a [pagination bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-adding-and-setting-up-a-pagination-bar) that allows visitors to navigate between pages of items from your [CMS (Content Management System)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-content-management-system-an-overview) collection.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
