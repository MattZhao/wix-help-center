> **Important:**
>
> Wix Photo Albums Store is not available to all users. As of February 2023, it is no longer possible to add Wix Photo Albums Store to your site. As an alternative, you can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your photos.

#### Product Availability

Wix Photo Albums works with a third-party service, Order a Print to provide you with even more options when selling your printed photos.

Digital images are available for sale worldwide. If you are selling print images, please note that different print products are available in different regions. Customers visiting your store only see the products available to them.

For more information on product availability, shipping times and refunds, click [here](https://www.orderaprint.com/products-shipping).

---

#### Shipment Period

Orders are usually shipped out within 3 business days and arrive within 9 to 12 days.

---

#### Refund Policy

If the order arrives damaged or if the product is defective, you can contact Order a Print to return it within 7 days of the delivery date. Order a Print will investigate and may provide a refund or replacement for the damaged or defective items.

---

#### Tax Policy

In most cases, the sale is international, so no taxes are applied. In cases where taxes are required, they will be added to your store's checkout page.
