---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery: Managing Your Gallery"
---

The Wix Pro Gallery allows you to showcase multimedia content all in one place. With this gallery, you can easily reorder gallery items and duplicate / copy galleries onto the same or different pages. We also make it easy for you to manage the social and interactive settings of your gallery, such as the heart and download icons.

---

### Downloading images from a gallery

Allow visitors to download gallery items by enabling the Download toggle in your Pro Gallery settings. A download icon appears on each individual item upon hovering. Visitors can download your original images but not Free Wix media.

#### To enable downloading of images in the Wix Pro Gallery:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click** Settings**.  Click the **Settings** tab.  Click the **Download** toggle to enable it.

> **Note:**
>
> Pro Gallery has a built-in Right Click Protect feature on desktop, so it's not possible to save images added to your Pro Gallery with a right click.

---

### Duplicating a gallery

Save yourself time and duplicate your Pro Gallery onto the same page, or copy and paste it onto a different page of your site. The gallery's content and settings are carried over onto the duplicate.

#### To duplicate a gallery:

Click your gallery in your editor.  Right-click on the gallery and choose what you want to do:

- **Duplicate a gallery on the same page:** 

1. Click **Duplicate**.
2. Drag the gallery to your preferred location.
- **Copy and paste a gallery elsewhere: **

1. Click **Copy**.
2. Navigate to the relevant page on your site.
3. Right-click anywhere on the page and select **Paste**.

---

### Reordering items in a gallery

You can change the order of the images, video or text in your gallery from the Organize Your Gallery window.

#### To reorder your gallery items:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Manage Media**.  Click and drag the gallery items into the order you want.

Click **Done**.

**Tip**: You can also sort the images alphabetically by Image Title or File Name. Click the **Arrange by **icon  and select an option.

---

### Adding a heart icon to gallery items

Track how many "likes" your images and videos are getting by adding a heart icon to media items in your Pro Gallery. This is a great way to see how your site visitors are responding to your content. The heart icon and counter both appear on your live site when you hover over an image in your gallery.

#### To add a heart icon to your Pro Gallery images:

Click the Wix Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click** Settings**.  Click the **Settings** tab.  Click the **Heart icon** toggle to enable it.   Click **Show with counter** to enable the "likes" counter.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about managing your gallery.

### Can I download all my gallery items at once?

It is not currently possible to download all gallery items at once. If that is something you'd like to see in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-downloading-all-gallery-items-at-once).

### Can I track gallery items that have been liked or downloaded by my site visitors?

Currently, it is not possible to track and analyze information on gallery items that have been liked or downloaded by your site visitors. If that is something you'd like to see in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-tracking-liked-shared-and-downloaded-gallery-items).

### How can I prevent my images from being saved on a mobile device?

It is not possible to prevent images from being saved on mobile devices where a browser's built-in features (such as long tap and save) are used for this purpose. The best way to protect images from being stolen is to [add a watermark](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-using-the-wix-photo-studio-tools).

### Can Wix randomize the order of my gallery items?

Currently, it is not possible to randomize the order of your gallery items in the Wix Pro Gallery. If this is a feature you would like to see in the future, [vote for it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-randomizing-the-order-of-gallery-items).

### Why is my heart counter not visible?

Your heart counter may not be visible for any of the following reasons:

- **You have not applied a hover overlay to your gallery:** For the Heart icon to be visible, you must [apply a hover overlay to your gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-hover-view-of-your-gallery-items). You can set the transparency to 0 if you don't want the overlay to be visible. If you choose a partial overlay, make sure to set its size to 40% or more.
- **Your gallery is not wide enough:** To ensure the heart icon and counter are visible, enlarge the gallery using the drag handles or [stretch the gallery to full-width](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-gallerys-layout#resizing-your-gallery).
- **Your files are too small:** If your uploaded files are too small, upload larger files to make the Pro Gallery frame large enough to display the heart icon and counter.

### Will I be notified if a visitor likes one of my gallery items?

Currently, it is not possible to get a notification when a visitor clicks "like" on one of your images or videos in the Pro Gallery. If that is something you'd like to see in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-receiving-notifications-when-visitors-like-your-images-or-videos).

### What happens if I have several Pro Galleries on one of my site pages?

It's likely that you will experience some performance issues if you have several Pro Galleries on one single page. Galleries are optimized to showcase multiple media files at once.We recommend having one gallery with several media items per page, as opposed to multiple galleries with less items. Having too many individual elements on a single page (even if the elements are identical) can affect site performance. Learn more about site performance [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-optimizing-your-media).

### What if I want to add single images, videos or display a portfolio?

If you want to display single images or videos, we recommend adding an image element from the Add Panel, and adding Wix Video to your site. For displaying a portfolio of work requiring individual galleries, we recommend using [Wix Portfolio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-portfolio-an-overview) or [dynamic pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).

### Why does my gallery change its layout on different devices?

Your Wix Pro Gallery behaves responsively when you choose certain layouts and set it to Full Width, using the **Stretch icon **. In order to fill the full width on a device screen, the gallery may change the position and size of your gallery items and / or the number of items per row.
