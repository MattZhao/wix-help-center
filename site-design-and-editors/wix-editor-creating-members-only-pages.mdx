### Creating Members Only pages

Create a Members Only page to give select visitors access to exclusive content.

#### To create a Members Only page:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.  Hover over the relevant page and click the **More** **Actions** icon .  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Permissions** tab.  Select **Site Members**.  Select which members can access this page:

- **All members:** Any signed in member can access this page.
- **Specific members:** Limit this page to specific members only. You can choose one or both of the following options:

- **Members with roles:** Members with a specific [role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-creating-member-roles).

1. Click **Manage Roles**.

**Note:** If you have not created member roles, click **Create Role**.
2. Select the checkbox next to the relevant role(s) and click **Apply**.
- **Members with plan subscriptions:** Members who've purchased a specific [pricing plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-an-overview).

1. Click **Manage Plans**.

**Note:** If you have not created a pricing plan, click **Create Plan**.
2. Select the checkbox next to the relevant plan(s) and click **Apply**.

> **Note:**
>
> By default, the **Site collaborators **toggle is always enabled. This ensures you and your collaborators are able to access all parts of your site.

### FAQs

### Where can I see a list of my members and their roles / pricing plans?

Click **Manage Site Members** at the bottom of the permissions tab to view your site members and manage settings like permissions, badges and contact info.

### Can I manage and engage with my members on mobile?

Absolutely. Choose your [preferred member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app), and after your site members download the members app they can perform all the actions they would on your site from the app including purchasing products, booking services, chatting with you from their Inbox and more.
