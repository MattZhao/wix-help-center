### How Wix Multilingual works with the CMS

When you add and set up Wix Multilingual on your site, eligible CMS collections automatically appear in your Translation Manager. From there, you can translate each supported collection field that appears on your live site. You can choose to translate text manually, auto-translate specific text, or auto-translate all the supported text at once.

> **Supported CMS collection field types:**
>
> In Wix Multilingual, you can translate the following field types: **Text**, **Rich Content**, **Rich Text**, **Image**, and **URL**.

### Translating CMS collection content

After installing Wix Multilingual, you can access the Translation Manager from your CMS collections where you can manually add or remove collections. Then, translate your entire site at once or translate one string of text at a time.

#### To translate CMS content that appears on your live site:

[Go to CMS](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20CMS&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/database/) in your site's dashboard. Alternatively, you can click **CMS ** in your editor. ** **  Click the relevant collection.  Click **More Actions** at the top and select **Manage translations**. This opens the Translation Manager in Wix Multilingual.   Click **Add/remove collections** under **CMS **on the left.   Select the collection(s) you want to translate and click **Save**.

Click the collection that you want to translate under **CMS** on the left.

Auto-translate your entire site or each field individually:

### Auto-translate site

Click **Machine Translation **at the top and select **Auto-translate Site**. 

Learn more about [auto-translating your entire site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site#auto-translating-your-entire-site) at once.

### Translate each field separately

1. Click the relevant field under **\{Language\} translation**.
2. Manually enter your translation for the text or click **Auto-translate **to have the text translated automatically. Learn more about [auto-translating individual text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site#auto-translating-individual-elements).
3. Click **Done**.

**Tip:** Click the **Restore Last Saved Translation** icon  to revert the translation to its last saved version.
4. Repeat these steps to translate each field.
5. Click **Save and Publish **at the top right to make the changes go live.

### FAQs

Click below for answers to common questions about translating CMS collection content.

### What happens if I delete a collection that was translated?

If you delete a translated collection, it no longer appears in the Translation Manager. As with deleting all CMS collections, any elements on your site that were linked to the collection content are no longer connected. Learn more about what happens when you [delete a CMS collection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-deleting-a-collection).

### What if I add a new field to my collection that is eligible for translation?

New eligible fields and their content are not automatically translated in Wix Multilingual. Make sure to go to the Translation Manager to translate new CMS content that appears on your site.

### What if I delete a field that was already translated?

If you delete a field that was already translated, the field and its content are removed from the Translation Manager. Learn more about [deleting CMS collection fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-deleting-fields-from-a-collection). 
If you need to restore a deleted field, you can restore your collection with [CMS backups](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-backing-up-your-collections). However, restoring a collection does not restore the translations. You will need to retranslate the fields in the Translation Manager. [Click here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-request-using-backups-to-restore-translations) to view this request and vote for the ability to restore translations.

### Can I remove a collection from being translated?

Yes. From the Translation Manager, you can remove collections by deselecting them. Removing collections from the Translation Manager also removes their translations. You can always re-add collections to be translated later.

### Can I translate single item collections?

Absolutely. As long as the single item collection meets the requirements for being translatable, you can translate it.
