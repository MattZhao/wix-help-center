---
title: "ADI: Publishing Issues"
---

You may see an error message when attempting to publish your site. You can resolve most of these issues quickly and easily, so you can get back to publishing your changes.

Follow the steps below to resolve your publishing issues in the ADI Builder:

### Try a different browser

To establish if this is a browser-related issue, please try opening the ADI Builder using a different [supported browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers). Then try publishing your site.
**Note:** If using a different browser resolves the issue, we recommend reinstalling your problematic browser.

### Try incognito mode

Try opening the ADI Builder in [incognito mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/opening-your-browser-in-incognitoprivate-browsing-mode). Then try publishing your site.

If you are not experiencing issues in incognito mode then you're likely experiencing a local issue. Continue with the steps below.

### Disable antivirus software

Temporarily disable your antivirus software (it may be interfering with your ability to load certain websites). Then reload the ADI Builder and try to publish your site.

### Try a different network connection

To determine if your network is causing the issue, try connecting to another network. Then open the ADI Builder and publish your site. 

**Note: **We are aware that some public institutions such as schools, universities, public libraries and government facilities, restrict access to certain URLs, including the Wix servers. If using a different network resolves the issue, we recommend contacting the relevant IT department at your location (if possible) to see if they can whitelist Wix.

### Check your browser's zoom settings

Make sure your browser is set to 100% zoom. Then try to publish your site.

To adjust your browser's zoom:
- On Windows press and hold the "**Ctrl**" key, and then press the "**-**" (zoom out) or "**+**" (zoom in) key.
- On a Mac press and hold the "**Command**" key, and then press the "**-**" (zoom out) or "**+**" (zoom in) key.

If the instructions above do not resolve your publishing issue, please do the following:

Follow the instructions as outlined in the [Wix Technical Assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-technical-assistant). Some of these steps include clearing your cache and disabling add-ons / extensions.  If this does not resolve the issue, [contact Wix Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact). Make sure to mention the specific error message, and the troubleshooting steps you've already followed.
