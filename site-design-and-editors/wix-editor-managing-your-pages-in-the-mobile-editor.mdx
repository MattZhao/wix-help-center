---
title: "Wix Editor: Managing Your Pages in the Mobile Editor"
---

Pages you add to your desktop site are automatically added to the mobile version of your site. You can manage and edit these pages in the mobile Editor. Hide pages and change their layout to customize the experience for visitors on your mobile site.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Viewing pages in the mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-managing-your-pages-in-the-mobile-editor#viewing-your-sites-pages-on-mobile)  [Adjusting the page layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-managing-your-pages-in-the-mobile-editor#adjusting-your-pages)  [Hiding your site's pages on mobile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-managing-your-pages-in-the-mobile-editor#hiding-pages-on-mobile)

> **Important:**
>
> It is not possible to change page settings on the mobile Editor. If you would like to adjust your page's settings, you need to do so from the desktop Editor.

---

### Viewing your site's pages on mobile

View your mobile site's pages to access and make changes to them. You can switch between pages with the Menus and Pages panel.

#### To view a site page on mobile:

[Open the mobile Editor.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor)  Click **Menus & Pages** ** **on the left side of the mobile Editor.  Select a page to view it.

> **Note:**
>
> You can also use the quick access Switch Page drop-down on the top bar of the mobile Editor.

---

### Adjusting your pages

Change your page layout and adjust its height to ensure that your site is well aligned for smaller screen sizes.

Select an option to learn more.

### Adjusting the page layout

1. [Open the mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).
2. Click **Page Layout Optimizer** ** **in the left menu of your mobile Editor.
3. Click **Optimize Now**.
[image: The Page Layout Optimizer in the mobile Editor. The cursor is hovering over the Optimize Now button.]

### Editing the page height

1. [Open the mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).
2. Select the page body.
3. Click and drag the Page Height icon ** **to adjust the page size.

[image: A page body has been selected in the mobile Editor. The cursor is hovering over the Page Height icon.]

---

### Hiding pages on mobile

The desktop and mobile-friendly views are different views of the same site. This means that if you delete a page from your desktop view, it also deletes it from your mobile-friendly view. However, you can hide a page from your mobile menu if you do not want it to be accessible on mobile.

#### To hide a page from your mobile site:

[Open the mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).  Click** Menus &** **Pages ** on the left side of the mobile Editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the More Actions icon .  Click **Hide on mobile **.
