---
title: "ADI: Unpublishing Your Site"
---

Unpublish your site at anytime. After you unpublish your site, it will no longer be accessible to site visitors.

You can publish your site again at any time, however, if your site was unpublished for more than a few days, your SEO ranking may not be affected.

### To unpublish your site:

Access your [Site Manager](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/manage-website).Click **Unpublish**.Click **Unpublish** to confirm.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want your site to remain accessible to users with a direct link, but do not want it to appear in Google search results, you can hide your site from search engines. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/enabling-search-engines-like-google-to-index-your-site) to learn more.
