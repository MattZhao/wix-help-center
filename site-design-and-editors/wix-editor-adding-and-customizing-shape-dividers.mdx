title: "Wix Editor: Adding and Customizing Shape Dividers"
---

Add shape dividers to your strips and sections to create an interesting and stylish layout. Instead of using clear-cut lines to divide content, choose a shape (e.g. Ellipse, Waves) or upload your own. These dividers can go at the top and bottom of a strip or section, and you can customize their design as needed.

**In this article, learn how to: **

[Add a shape divider to your strip or section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-shape-dividers#adding-a-shape-divider)  [Customize a predesigned divider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-shape-dividers#customizing-your-shape-divider)  [Design a custom divider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-shape-dividers#designing-a-custom-shape-divider)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-shape-dividers#faqs)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure you [add and set up the strip](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-strip) first.

### Adding a shape divider

Get started by adding a shape divider to your section or strip. You can add the divider to the top or bottom of your strip / section, or both.

There are a range of shapes you can choose from, as well as additional designs in the Wix Media catalog. You can also upload your own shape to give this transition a personal touch.

#### To add a shape divider to your section or strip:

Select your section or strip:

- (For strips) Click the strip in your editor.
- (For sections) Double-click the section in your editor.
    Click the **Shape Dividers** icon .  Click the **Top** or **Bottom **tab to choose where to add the divider.  Select a divider to add:

- **Suggested designs:** Click one of the styles available in the panel.
- **Additional designs offered by Wix:**

1. Click **+** **More Shapes**.
2. (In the Media Manager) Select a design from the **Media from Wix** tab.
3. Click **Choose File** to apply it.
- **Your own shape divider: **

1. Click **+ More Shapes**.
2. (In the Media Manager) Select the shape you want to use from the **Site Files** tab, or upload a new one.
3. Click **Choose File** to apply it.

### Customizing your shape divider

After adding a divider, you can customize its design to your liking. Adjust the height, color, opacity and layers to personalize the transition and make it look exactly how you want.

> **Note:**
>
> The available options depend on the shape divider you choose.

#### To customize your shape divider:

Select your section or strip:

- (For strips) Click the strip in your editor.
- (For sections) Double-click the section in your editor.
    Click the **Shape Dividers** icon .  Click the **Top** or **Bottom **tab to access the divider you want to design.  Click **Customize Design **at the bottom.  Choose how you want to adjust the look of your shape divider:

- **Opacity & color:** Choose whatever color you want for your divider, and make it more or less visible on the section or strip.
- **Height:** Set how high or low you want the divider to go on your section or strip.
- **Horizontal offset: **Re-position your shape divider, moving it to the left or right.
- **Layer effect:** Click the toggle to add a fade effect to the divider, then select a direction from the drop-down:

- **Centered:** The effect is visible on the entire divider.
- **Fade to top: **The effect lessens towards the top of the divider.
- **Fade to bottom: **The effect lessens towards the bottom of the divider.
- **Invert:** Invert the shape of the divider (vertically).
- **Flip:** Set the divider to display the opposite way (horizontally) on your strip / section.
- **Number of repeats:** Set how many times the pattern repeats across the divider.

### Designing a custom shape divider

When you upload your own shape divider, you can use a variety of options to further personalize it. Adjust settings like the divider's color, visibility and scale to create a unique structure and design on your page.

> **Note:**
>
> The available design options for your custom divider depend on the specific file you upload. 
> In order to customize the design of your SVG, the file needs to:
> - Be a closed shape.
> - Only contain one level of opacity (transparency).
> - Not contain any text, strokes, gradients, filters etc.
>
> Additionally, you can only change the color of SVG files that are the color RGB = #000000 (black).

#### To customize the design of your own shape divider:

Select your section or strip:

- (For strips) Click the strip in your editor.
- (For sections) Double-click the section in your editor.
    Click the **Shape Dividers** icon .  Click the **Top** or **Bottom **tab to access the divider you want to design.  Click **Customize Design **at the bottom.  Choose how you want to adjust the look of your shape divider:

- **Opacity & color:** Choose whatever color you want for your divider, and make it more or less visible on the section or strip.
- **How's the divider scaled?:** Select whether you want the divider pattern to stretch across the strip / section or repeat.
- **Height:** Set how high or low you want the highest point of the divider to be on your strip or section.
- **Horizontal offset: **Set the divider's offset from the sides of the section / strip.
- **Flip:** Set the divider to display the opposite way (horizontally) on your strip or section.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### What kind of custom shape dividers can I upload?

It is only possible to upload and use SVG files as shape dividers on your site. PNG and JPG files are not supported.
When uploading your SVG, keep the following in mind:
- SVG files you upload must be no larger than 250KB.
- The recommended proportion size for your own SVG files is a width of 1920px, and a height between 50-300px.

### Are shape dividers responsive?

Some shape divider designs are fully responsive, which means they adjust to the size of your visitors' screens. 
More complex designs (such as New York Skyline and Bottom of the Sea) cannot stretch without losing their proportions, so they're automatically scaled / cropped for the screen size.

### Can I add dividers to columns on a strip?

It is not possible to add shape dividers to columns. However, you can add columns to strips that include shape dividers.

### How do they work on mobile?

Shape dividers are automatically carried over to your mobile site, and are fixed at 50px. 
You can [customize dividers you've added from the desktop editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-shape-dividers-on-your-mobile-site) to create unique content for mobile visitors, however it is not possible to add mobile-only dividers from the **Add to Mobile** panel.
