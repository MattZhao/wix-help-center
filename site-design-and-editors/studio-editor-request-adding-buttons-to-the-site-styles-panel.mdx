title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding Buttons to the Site Styles Panel"
---

While you can apply [color and typography themes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-your-site-styles) to your client's site, the Site Styles panel does not include the option to create global styles for buttons.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
