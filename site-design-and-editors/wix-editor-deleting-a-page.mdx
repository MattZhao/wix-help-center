---
title: "Wix Editor: Deleting a Page"
---

You can delete pages you no longer need from your Editor's Pages & Menu tab. This includes both static and dynamic pages.

> **Tips:**
>
> - To delete pages associated with Wix apps (e.g. Wix Stores, Wix Events, Wix Blog), you must [delete the apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/deleting-apps) themselves to remove their pages.
> - If you want to delete your home page, you must first [set another page as the home page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-sites-home-page). Then you can delete the page that was used as your home page.

### To delete a page from your site:

Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the Editor.  (Dynamic pages only) Click **Dynamic Pages**.   Hover over the relevant page and click the More Actions icon .   Click **Delete**.

Click **Delete **to confirm.   Publish your site to make the changes go live.
