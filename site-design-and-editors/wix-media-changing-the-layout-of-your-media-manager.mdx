---
title: "Wix Media: Changing the Layout of Your Media Manager"
---

Choose whether the files in your Media Manager are displayed as a gallery or in a list.

### To change the layout of your Media Manager:

[Go to your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager).  Click the **Change layout **icon .  Select a layout:

- **Gallery:** Your media is displayed as thumbnails.
- **List: **Your media is displayed in a list.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Learn how to reorder and filter your files](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-organizing-the-files-in-your-media-manager#filtering-files-by-file-type-in-the-media-manager) to help you organize your Media Manager in a way that suits you.
