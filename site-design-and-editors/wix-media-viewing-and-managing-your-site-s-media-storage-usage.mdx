### Viewing your site's media storage

From your Media Manager or dashboard, view how much storage your site is using and how much it has remaining. You can also filter and customize views to find your files easier. If needed, you can upgrade to get more storage.

DashboardMedia Manager
1. [Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Manage Storage**.

1. [Go to your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager).
2. View the total storage your site has used at the bottom left.
3. Hover over the **Info **icon  to view how much storage space your site has left.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click the **Filter **icon  to choose which files you want to view.

### Managing your site's media storage

From Manage Storage in Settings in your dashboard, you can view, download and delete media files from your site.

#### To manage your site's media storage:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage Storage**.  Click the **More Actions** icon  on the relevant file(s).  Choose how you want to manage your files:

- **Preview: **View a larger version of the file.
- **Download: **Save the file to your device.
- **Move to Trash: **Move the selected file(s) to the Trash tab, where they can be recovered later if needed.
- **Permanently Delete: **Delete the selected file(s) completely.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Select multiple media files to perform these actions in bulk.
> - You can also access the storage manager from the media manager by clicking **Manage Storage**.

### Transferring site storage to another site

If you have a storage plan attached to a particular site, and would like to move it to a different site in your account, you can do this from the Premium subscriptions tab in your Wix account.

#### To transfer storage:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant storage plan you want to transfer.  Click **Assign to a different site**.  Select the site you want to transfer the storage to.  Click **Next**.  Click **Assign**.

### Getting more storage space

If you need more storage, you can [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan). Plans include storage space in addition to other business benefits such as the ability to sell media content and automated marketing tools.

DashboardMedia Manager
1. [Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Manage Storage**.
3. Click **Upgrade**.
Note:The plan assigned to your site determines how much media storage that specific site can have. If you need more storage for other sites in your account, you can upgrade them as well.
1. [Go to your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-accessing-the-media-manager).
2. Click **Upgrade **under the storage bar at the bottom left.
Note:The plan assigned to your site determines how much media storage that specific site can have. If you need more storage for other sites in your account, you can upgrade them as well.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also choose to [add a storage plan to your site](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fpremium-add-on%2Fstorage%3Fmsid%3D%7BmetaSiteId%7D%0A%0A&title=Select+a+Site+to+Add+Storage&primaryButtonText=Add+Storage) to make sure you have enough space for your media. You can do this in addition to upgrading your site.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about your site's media storage.

### What happens to my storage limit if I cancel my site's plan?

If you cancel your plan, when it expires, your site no longer has the extra storage that was included in the plan. The storage limit for free plans is 500MB. If your site exceeds that limit, it will continue to run as normal. However, to add or upload additional media files, you'll need to either remove files or upgrade to a plan that includes more storage.

### How can I free up space in my site storage?

You can free up space in your site storage by deleting media you no longer need. You can do this from Manage Storage in [Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings).
This permanently deletes files from your Media Manager along with anywhere they display on your live site. You are unable to restore them.
Learn more about [deleting media files from your Media Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-deleting-media-files-from-your-media-manager).

### How long does it take for storage changes to occur?

When you make changes to your storage (e.g. when you upgrade your account or transfer your storage to another site), it will take up to 24 hours to see the change reflected in your account.

### Do videos use my site storage?

Videos uploaded to your site use your site storage. This includes videos uploaded to Online Programs.

### What refund policies are in place?

If you've added a storage plan to your site, you are eligible to receive a refund within your 14-day free trial (i.e. your first 14 days of having the plan). To get the refund, cancel your plan from [Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
After your 14-day trial has elapsed, the additional upgraded storage will not be refundable. You can, however, cancel its renewal.
If you've upgraded your site, a 14-day trial period also applies. Learn more about [requesting a refund for a plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/requesting-a-refund-for-a-premium-plan).

### Are there different storage allowances for Wix Partners?

Free Wix Partner sites [include 10GB of storage space](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-partner-benefits-storage-on-free-sites) until you upgrade your site.

### Are there different storage allowances for Wix Studio?

Free Wix Studio sites include 10GB of storage space until you upgrade your site.
