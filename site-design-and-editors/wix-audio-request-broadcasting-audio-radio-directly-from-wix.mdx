---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Broadcasting Audio (Radio) Directly from Wix"
---

Currently, you can only broadcast audio that's hosted elsewhere. It is not possible to broadcast audio directly from your Wix site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
