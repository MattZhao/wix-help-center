---

With the Shared Gallery app, you can create beautiful galleries to share photos and videos with your community. You can upload your media from your live site or from your site's dashboard, and also let your site members upload their own media to your gallery.

---

### Uploading media to your shared gallery

You can upload photos and videos to your shared gallery directly from your live site or from your site's dashboard.

Live siteDashboard
1. Log in to your site using the email address associated with your Wix account.
2. Go to the Shared Gallery page on your site.
3. Select the album that you want to add photos to and click **Add Media**.

**Note: **If you haven't created any albums, click **Add Media **on the main gallery. [Learn more about albums](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-and-managing-albums-in-the-shared-gallery-app#adding-photos-and-videos-to-an-album)
[image: Adding media in the Shared Gallery app from your live site.]
1. Select the image or video file(s) from your computer.
2. Click **Open**.
3. (Optional) Enter a description of the photo or video.
[image: Adding a description to a photo in the Shared Gallery app_files]
1. Go to [Shared Gallery](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fapp/476dc2dd-e16e-43b0-a0aa-1a486c78fbe2%3F&title=Shared%20Gallery%20Dashboard&buttonText=Shared+Gallery) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the album that you want to add photos to.

**Note**: If you haven't created any albums, click **Create Album **on the main gallery.
3. Click **Add Media**.

1. Choose either **Video** or **Image**.
2. Select the image or video file(s) from your computer.
3. Click **Open**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### What file types are supported?

Add images in file formats like .jpg, .png, and .gif, and videos in file formats like Quicktime, AVI, and MP4. [Learn more about supported file types](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-supported-media-file-types-and-file-sizes)

### How are my files stored?

Files and media are stored in a private folder in the Media Manager. This means the files are not visible in the Media Manager, but they still take up space in your site storage.  Learn how to [view and manage your site's media storage](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-managing-your-storage).

### Who can upload files?

You and any site member with [the relevant level of access](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-permissions-in-the-shared-gallery-app) can upload files to your shared galleries.

### Can members view uploads?

Yes, members can view other members' uploads by clicking on their profile in the Members page. Learn more about [using the Shared Gallery app with the Member's Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-the-members-area-with-the-shared-gallery-app).
