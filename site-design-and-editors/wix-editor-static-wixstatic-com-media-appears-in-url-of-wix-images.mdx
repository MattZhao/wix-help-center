---
title: "Wix Editor: Static.wixstatic.com/media Appears in URL of Wix Images"
---

You may notice that **static.wixstatic.com/media** appears in the URL of an image. This appears, for example, when you right click an image and open in it in a new tab.

Static.wixstatic.com/media is the server where Wix hosts all its images. It allows us to deliver these images to you and your users quickly and efficiently. Therefore, we do not recommend hosting your images using an external resource.

Please contact us if you or your users are experiencing difficulties accessing your Wix site's images.
