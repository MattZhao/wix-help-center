Create a custom site on the fly, straight from your mobile device. To create your site from mobile, you can either use the Wix app, or go to Wix.com on your browser.

**In this article, learn how to create a site:**

[From the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-new-site-from-your-phone#creating-a-new-site-from-the-wix-app)  [From your mobile device's browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-new-site-from-your-phone#creating-a-new-site-from-your-mobile-devices-browser)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQS)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-new-site-from-your-phone#faqs)

### Creating a new site from the Wix app

Create a new site on-the-go using the Wix app. Give your customers the convenience of connecting with your business directly from their mobile devices through one of the [Wix member apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps).

Whether they're browsing products, reading your latest blog posts, scheduling services, or staying in the loop with updates, your members can effortlessly engage with your offerings.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> [Download the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app) to your mobile device.

#### To create a new site from the Wix app:

Go to the Wix app on your mobile device.  Choose an option:

- **If this is your first site with Wix:** Tap **Create Now**.
- **If you already have a Wix site: **

1. Tap the site name at the top of your screen.
2. Tap **+ Create New **at the bottom.
    Choose how you want to start creating your site:

### Chat to the AI assistant

Tell the [AI assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-an-ai-generated-site#step-2-generate-a-site-with-ai) about the site you want to create and get personalized tools, images and text. 
1. Use the options to tell the assistant what you want, such as the pages you'd like to add, your logo etc.
2. Read the site profile to ensure it's correct.
3. Click **End Chat & Continue**.

### Set up manually, without the AI assistant

Set up your site without AI assistance. 
1. Type a category or select a category from the list:

- **Blog: **Start a blog and share content with members.
- **Online Store: **Showcase and manage your products.
- **Beauty: **Reach out to people with the latest beauty products and services that fits their needs.** **
- **Fitness: **Inspire people to get healthy and stay in shape.
- **Portfolio: **Exhibit your latest projects and collections.
- **Restaurant:** Share menus, take orders and grow your customer base.
- **Event:** Create and promote exciting events to maximize attendance.
- **Consultant: **Unveil the expertise you can offer potential clients.
- **Technology Company: **Highlight products and services and elevate your brands online presence.
2. Click** Next**.
3. Select the [apps you want to add](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-adding-apps-to-your-site).

**Note:** After you add an app from the Wix app, you need to go to the editor and add the pending app to your site.
4. Enter your site's name.
5. Click **Let's Go**.

> **What's next?**
>
> You can [edit your site on mobile,](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-your-mobile-device) from anywhere, at any time. Edit directly from the Wix app, mobile browser or from your Wix account on desktop.

### Creating a new site from your mobile device's browser

You can also create a new site from your mobile device's browser if you prefer not to download the Wix app.

After choosing from several options that help pinpoint what your site is about, Wix builds the site for you. You can then customize the site to your liking and publish it to go live.

#### To create a new site from your mobile browser:

Log in to [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/) from a [supported browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers) on your mobile device. 

**Note**: If you do not have a Wix account, [sign up](https://support.wix.com/en/article/signing-up-for-a-free-wix-account).   Tap **+ Create N****ew Site**** **at the bottom.  (First time only) Select who the site is for.

**Tip:** If you select **A client, a freelancer, or agency**, you will then be asked to download the [Wix Studio](https://www.wix.com/studio) app.   Choose how to create the new site:

### Chat to the AI assistant

Tell the [AI assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-an-ai-generated-site#step-2-generate-a-site-with-ai) about the site you want to create and get personalized tools and features to best manage your new site. 
1. Tap **Start Chat**.
2. Use the options to tell the assistant what you want, such as the pages you'd like to add, your logo etc.
3. Read the site profile to ensure it's correct.
4. Tap **End Chat & Continue**.
5. Choose between the following options:

- **Connect ****a Domain:** Purchase a custom domain or [connect a domain you own to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-you-own-to-your-wix-site).
- **Explore the App:** Learn more about [the Wix app](https://www.wix.com/mobile/wix-app).

### Set up manually, without the AI assistant

Set up your site without AI assistance. 
1. Tap **Set Up without Chat**
2. Type a category or select a category from the list:

- **Blog: **Start a blog and share content with members.
- **Online Store: **Showcase and manage your products.
- **Beauty: **Reach out to people with the latest beauty products and services that fits their needs.** **
- **Fitness: **Inspire people to get healthy and stay in shape.
- **Portfolio: **Exhibit your latest projects and collections.
- **Restaurant:** Share menus, take orders and grow your customer base.
- **Event:** Create and promote exciting events to maximize attendance.
- **Consultant: **Unveil the expertise you can offer potential clients.
- **Technology Company: **Highlight products and services and elevate your brands online presence.
3. Tap **Next**.
4. Select the [apps you want to add](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-adding-apps-to-your-site).

**Note:** After you add an app from the Wix app, you need to go to the editor and add the pending app to your site.
5. Enter your site's name.
6. Choose between the following options: 

- **Get a Do****main:** Purchase a custom domain or [connect a domain you own to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-you-own-to-your-wix-site).
- **Download the app:** Download the [Wix app](https://www.wix.com/mobile/wix-app) from your mobile app store.

> **Next steps:**
>
> Once you have downloaded the Wix app, [edit your site from your mobile device](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-editing-your-site-on-your-mobile-device).

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about what you can do once you create a new site.

### I want to add a members app to my site, what options do I have?

Once you've created your site, explore the [available members apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps) and [add your preferred app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app).

### How can I add people to my site after I choose my members app?

You can [invite people to interact with your business](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-join-your-site-on-the-wix-member-apps) using a Wix members app.
