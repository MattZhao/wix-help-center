### Applying to join the Wix Marketplace

When you're ready to apply to become a partner in the Wix Marketplace, follow the steps below to submit your application. Upon submitting your application, we'll review it and email you to let you know if you're accepted. The review process can take up to 30 days.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Save** to save your progress during the application process.

#### To apply to join the Wix Marketplace:

[Go to Marketplace Overview](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/marketplace?referralInfo=sidebar) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click **Apply Now**.  In the **General Info** tab, add your general business info to show potential clients, and click **Next**.  In the **Preferences** tab, choose countries you'd like to get leads from and languages you support, and click **Next**.

**Note:** Clients from any country can still contact you directly in the Wix Marketplace.   In the **Portfolio** tab, click **+ Add Project** and add at least 5 Wix premium sites you've built, and click **Next**.  In the **Services & Rates** tab, select the services you offer and enter your starting rates, and click **Next**.  In the **Your Experience** tab, add additional info about your experience and work with Wix clients.  Click** Apply**.
