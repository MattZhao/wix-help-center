Wix Music lets you [sell the music you make](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-selling-your-music) directly on your site. You can view all of your sales in one place, making it easy to track your earnings and stay up to date on your fans' activity.

**In this article, learn about: **

[Viewing your transactions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-viewing-your-transactions#viewing-your-transactions)  [Filtering your transactions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-viewing-your-transactions#filtering-your-transactions)

---

### Viewing your transactions

The Payments page in your site's dashboard shows a list of any transaction your visitors made. Click a transaction to see information like the amount, who made the payment, and its current status.

#### To view transactions:

[Go to Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Your%20Transactions&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant payment to see more information.

> **Need to refund a customer?**
>
> If you're using [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments), you can click **Refund** at the top right, when viewing a transaction, to [issue a refund](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refunding-customers-in-wix-payments). For other providers, see this article about [issuing refunds with third-party payment providers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refunding-your-customers-with-third-party-payment-providers).

### Filtering your transactions

You can filter the list on the Payments page by date, status, payment provider/method, and order type. This is a great way to find a specific transaction quickly, without having to go through each one.

#### To filter your transactions:

[Go to Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Your%20Transactions&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Filter**.   Select the checkbox next to the filter(s) you want to apply.

> **Tip:**
>
> Selling other products or services on your site? You can filter the list to only show Wix Music transactions. Select **Wix Music** as the **Order Type**.
