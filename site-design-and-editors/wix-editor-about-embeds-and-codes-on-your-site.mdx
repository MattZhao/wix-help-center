---
title: "Wix Editor: About Embeds and Codes on Your Site"
---

By adding embeds to your site, you can display external content and tools, like functional widgets, external sites, or animation and videos. You can also use them to add marketing integrations and custom functionalities to expand your business and build advanced pages.

> **Important:**
>
> None of these tools allows you to access or change the HTML of your site.

---

### Embed a site or widget

Use popular embed options available from Wix to provide your visitors with additional tools, features, and sites. HTML elements allow you to display a variety of widgets, such as calendars and weather apps.

You can also use HTTPS URLs to display external sites on your pages. This is extremely useful if you want to give visitors a preview of associate brands, or even just display a business or site that you support.

> **Note:**
>
> [See our guidelines and limitations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget#guidelines-for-adding-embeds-to-your-site) of embedding HTML code in Wix.

---

### Add custom elements

With [custom elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-custom-element-to-your-site), you can add code snippets to create custom HTML tags. Use these tags to design your own elements and give your site advanced CSS capabilities. You can use them to design your own elements, create scroll interactions, change the height of your elements dynamically, and more.

Custom elements can be used with any JavaScript ES6 library or framework that works with HTML 5, so they're great for creating eye-catching pages that are sophisticated in both design and function.

> **Note:**
>
> [See our guidelines and limitations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-a-custom-element-to-your-site#guidelines-and-limitations) when you add a custom element.

---

### Embed videos and animations

Add life and interaction to your site by adding fluid embeds. Use the [single video player](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-and-setting-up-the-single-video-player) to embed your favorite videos from platforms such as YouTube and Facebook, or upload your own.

You can also [embed a Lottie element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-importing-lottie-animations-to-your-site) to display unique animations on your site. Add the element to your page, and paste the URL from your LottieFiles account to create a fun, eye-catching experience for your visitors.

---

### Add custom functionality with Velo

Velo Dev mode provides you with advanced site building capabilities. Work with our [APIs](https://www.wix.com/code/reference/), and add [JavaScript](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-javascript-support) to your site to create robust, content rich pages.

You also add and edit your code in our [integrated development environment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-working-in-the-code-panel) (IDE) available directly in the Wix Editor, which provides syntax checking and basic code analysis.

Visit the [Velo by Wix Resource Center](https://www.wix.com/velo/all-documentation) to onboard and continue learning.

---

### Embed marketing integration tools

[Marketing integration tools](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-marketing-integrations) allow you to monitor your site performance, implement suggestions for site optimization, and track your ad campaigns, so you can manage your site and business the way you need.

As these tools are integrated with Wix, they have less of an impact on site performance, and you don't need to worry about script placement. However, you can also [embed custom code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-custom-code-on-your-site) for marketing and business tools.

> **Tip:**
>
> To help your site be compliant with privacy laws such as the [GDPR](https://support.wix.com/en/article/preparing-your-wix-site-for-the-gdpr), we recommend you [categorize your custom code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/categorizing-your-custom-code).
