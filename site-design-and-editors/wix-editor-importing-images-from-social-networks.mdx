---
title: "Wix Editor: Importing Images from Social Networks"
---

Connect your Media Manager to your Social Networks such as Facebook, Flickr, Instagram, Picasa and Google Drive images.

### To import images from social networks:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Image**.  Click **Import Images**.  Select the relevant social network.  Log in to your account and allow Wix to connect.

> **Note:**
>
> The images found in the Social Media folders on the left are a reflection of the images uploaded to your Social Media accounts. It is not possible to delete images from these folders through your Wix account. In addition, editing an image from these folders does not affect the original image in your Social Media accounts.

> **Tip:**
>
> To disconnect a social network simply hover over the network name in the Media Manager, click the **More Actions** iconand click **Disconnect**.
