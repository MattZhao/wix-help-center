The Wix Pro Gallery is not just for images, you can also add videos to your Pro Gallery. You can customize the video settings to make them play on hover, play on loop, and change the play speed. You can also add a play button to your videos so that visitors can play them in their own time.

### Customizing your video settings

Make the videos in your gallery behave exactly how you want by customizing their settings from the Advanced tab.

#### To customize your video settings:

Click your Pro Gallery in your editor.  Click **Settings**.   Click the **Advanced** tab.   Customize the following options:

- **Play Videos:** Click the drop-down to choose how to play your videos: **On hover**, **Autoplay** or **On click**.
- **Play with sound:** Click the toggle to enable or disable the sound on your video:

- **Enabled:** Videos play with sound in the gallery view.
- **Disabled:** Videos are muted in the gallery view.

**Note: **Your videos always play with sound in expand mode.
- **Play speed:** Click the drop-down to choose the playback speed.
- **Loop videos:** Click the toggle to change the video's loop settings:

- **Enabled:** Videos play continuously in a loop.
- **Disabled: **Videos only play once and stop playing after they end.
- **Show play button:** Click the toggle to enable or disable a play button on your video(s):

- **Enabled:** A play button is shown on your video.
- **Disabled:** A play button is not shown on your video.

> **Note:**
>
> Setting your videos to autoplay **and **play in a loop will make only the first video in your gallery with these settings play continuously, and the rest of the videos will not play.

### Autoplay policies for browsers supported by Wix

Wix currently supports [4 browsers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/supported-browsers) (Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari for Mac and Microsoft Edge). The following are the most up-to-date autoplay policies which apply to all 4 browsers. Click an option to learn more:

### Autoplay on desktops and laptops

- Muted autoplay is always allowed.
- Autoplay with sound is allowed if:

- The site visitor has interacted with the domain (via click, tap, etc.).
- On desktop, the site visitor's [Media Engagement Index](https://developers.google.com/web/updates/2017/09/autoplay-policy-changes#mei) threshold has been crossed, meaning the visitor has previously played video with sound.
- The visitor has [added the site to their home screen](https://developers.google.com/web/updates/2017/02/improved-add-to-home-screen) on mobile or installed the PWA (progressive web app) on desktop.
- Top frames can [delegate autoplay permission](https://developers.google.com/web/updates/2017/09/autoplay-policy-changes#iframe) to their iFrames to allow autoplay with sound.

**The following browsers no longer support autoplay:**
- Safari 11 and older versions
- Chrome 61 and older versions
- Firefox 66 and older versions
- Edge 92 and older versions

**Note:** To autoplay elements, your site visitors need to change the settings in their own browsers. Please refer to Safari, Chrome, Microsoft and Firefox support for more information.

### Autoplay on mobile and tablet devices

You may find that your music and videos do not play automatically on iPhones, Androids and tablets. This is because Apple and Chrome block the autoplay function to prevent additional data charges from the visitor's cellphone network. No data is loaded until the user taps the play button on the audio or video player.
Autoplay functions are also disabled if the visitor has enabled low-power mode on their iOS device.
For more information, please see [Apple's Safari Developer Library](https://developer.apple.com/library/safari/#documentation/AudioVideo/Conceptual/Using_HTML5_Audio_Video/Device-SpecificConsiderations/Device-SpecificConsiderations.html%23//apple_ref/doc/uid/TP40009523-CH5-SW1) and [Google's Developers guidelines](https://developers.google.com/web/updates/2017/09/autoplay-policy-changes). 
**Note**: For mobile or tablet devices, we recommend setting your Play Video preference (Step 4) to **On hover**, or **On click**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about customizing video settings in the Wix Pro Gallery.

### How can I remove borders added to my videos?

When the gallery ratio does not match the ratio of the original video, borders may be added to the video. This can generally be resolved in galleries with a Grid, Thumbnails, Slider, or Slideshow layout by the completing the following steps:
1. Click **Settings**.
2. Click **Layout**.
3. Click **Customize Layout**.
4. Select **Crop** under **Thumbnail resize**.
5. Select the **Image ratio **you want to use.

However, if there are several videos with different ratios in the Pro Gallery, these steps may not resolve the issue.

### Can I set all videos in my gallery to play at the same time?

Videos play one at a time when they are set to autoplay. Currently, it is not possible to play all the videos in your gallery at the same time. If this is a feature you would like to see in the future, you can [vote for it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-playing-autoplaying-multiple-videos-at-the-same-time).

### How can I enable sound on my videos?

To enable sound on a video, you must click (computer) or tap (mobile) on the video. Hovering over the video does not enable sound.

### Can I customize the video play button?

Currently, it is not possible to customize the play button that appears when visitors hover over videos in your Pro Gallery. If this is a feature you would like to see in the future, you can [vote for it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-customizing-the-video-play-button).

### Is it possible to play multiple videos simultaneously?

Currently, it is not possible to manually play / autoplay all the videos in your gallery at the same time. Depending on the [settings applied to your videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-your-gallerys-advanced-settings), your videos play individually in a loop, on hover, on click, or one after the other. If this is a feature you would like to see in the future, you can [vote for it here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-request-playing-autoplaying-multiple-videos-at-the-same-time).

### Can I control the quality of YouTube videos?

YouTube automatically changes the quality of your video stream based on your / your site visitor's viewing conditions. These conditions are why you / they may notice that the quality of your video changes as you / they watch it. [Learn more about the factors that determine video quality and how to change video quality on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/91449)

### How do videos linked from YouTube / Vimeo without a thumbnail image display in my gallery?

Linked YouTube / Vimeo videos without a thumbnail image appear as a blank or broken thumbnail in the Media Manager. This blank / broken thumbnail also displays in your Pro Gallery in your editor, Preview mode and on your live site.

Learn how to add thumbnail images to your videos in [YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en) and [Vimeo](https://vimeo.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402920509325-How-do-I-change-the-thumbnail-image-for-my-Create-video-).
