---
title: "Page Name vs. Page Title"
---

The **Page Name** appears on your site menu. [Learn how to change the page name](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-renaming-a-page)

The **Page Title** is the headline of your site and can be seen in the browser tab. It is important as it also appears in Google search results. Make sure to use meaningful page titles. [Learn how to change your page title](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-seo-title-tags-and-meta-descriptions-to-your-pages)

You also have the option of setting the **Page Address**. The **Page Address** appears as part of the URL of your page. [Learn how to change the page address ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-page-url)
