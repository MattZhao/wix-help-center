Using Editor X, you can invite people to help edit your site and limit their editing permissions based on the areas they handle. The editor is automatically adjusted to each person's role so that any unauthorized action is either hidden or disabled.

### Inviting people to collaborate

Invite people to work with you on a site directly from the Editor. After entering their email address and choosing a role, invitees get an email with a link to start collaborating.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also invite people while creating and managing your roles. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-working-with-roles-and-permissions#creating-and-managing-roles)

#### To invite people to your site:

Click **Invite** at the top right of Editor X.  Enter the email address(es) of the person you want to invite.  Select a role from the drop-down menu.  Click **Send Invite**.

> **Learn about concurrent editing:**
>
> Editor X lets you edit the same site simultaneously and see each other's changes in real-time. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-about-concurrent-editing)

### Creating and managing roles

You can manage your site's roles at any time. Create and edit custom roles, delete roles that you no longer need or duplicate predefined roles to adjust their permissions.

### What permissions are specific to Editor X?

When creating a new role, you can add the following permissions that are specifically related to site editing: 
- **View Site: **Can view all pages and preview the site before publishing.
- **Add Comments:** Can add [comments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-managing-and-replying-to-site-comments) on elements and pages and tag other people.
- **Edit Site Structure: **Can add new pages, [Masters](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-global-sections), elements and [dynamic content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-dynamic-pages).
- **Edit Layout & Design: **Can use layout tools, edit [breakpoints](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-managing-breakpoints) and design assets.
- **Edit Content:** Can edit text, links and media sources (e.g., images, videos, etc).
- **Edit Code:** Can write code and manage [collections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-formerly-content-manager-managing-your-collections).
- **Manage Page SEO: **Can edit SEO and social share settings per page, and add alt text to media.
- **Edit Site Advanced:** Can delete [Masters](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-global-sections) and restore an older site version.

#### To create and manage roles:

Go to [Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/manage-website/overview) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Roles & Permissions**.  Do one of the following:

### Create a new role

1. Click **Manage Roles**.
2. Click** + Create New Role** at the top right.
3. Fill in the details of your custom role:

1. Enter a title under **Role Title**.
2. (Optional) Add a description that outlines the new role.
4. Select the checkboxes next to the relevant actions for this role.

**Note:** While most Editor X permissions are available under **Editor X**, the ability to publish a site can be found under **Editor**.
5. When ready, click **Save** at the top right.

**Notes:**
- You can select specific permissions within the categories, or tick **Select All**.
- Certain actions that you select automatically enable other permissions. For example, if you let someone manage your orders, they can view your contacts by default.
- Use the **Filter...** search bar to find actions to assign to your custom role.

### Edit an existing role

1. Click **Manage Roles**.
2. Hover over the relevant role and click **Edit**.
3. Edit the role as you wish:

1. Rename the role or change its description.
2. Add permissions by selecting more checkboxes.
3. Remove permissions by de-selecting their checkbox.
4. When ready, click **Save**.

[Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-changing-a-collaborators-role)

### Invite people to work on this site

1. Click the **Show More** icon  next to the role you want to assign.
2. Select **Invite People**.
3. Enter the person's email address.

**Tip: **To add multiple invitees, enter each email separated by a comma.
4. Select the checkbox next to the role(s) you want to assign.
5. Click **Send** **Invite**.

[Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site)

### Managing permissions per page

In addition to site and team roles, site owners can [apply another type of permission](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-editing-permissions-per-page) that controls pages and masters.

**Site and team roles** control the actions people can perform, while **permissions per page** control the pages and master sections they're allowed to work on.

---

### Performing an unauthorized action in the Editor

Every user has a specific [role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators) in the system with permissions that the site owner can change at any time.

Areas that aren't included in your permissions appear in grey and are not clickable. You can also see specific notifications as in the example below.

To access a feature that is currently disabled, contact the site owner and ask them to add the relevant permission(s) to your role.
