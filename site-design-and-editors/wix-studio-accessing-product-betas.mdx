---
title: "Wix Studio: Accessing Product Betas"
---

Enroll in Product Betas to experience new Wix features before they're released. When joining a product beta, you should identify and report bugs, provide feedback and help determine improvements needed before the product is released. We may ask you to join a performance review to share your experience with our developers and product managers.

> **Note:**
>
> Currently, product betas are available in English only and for Wix sites that you are an owner of. Keep in mind that the betas are still in development and you may not have the same seamless experience that you have with other Wix products.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Join the Beta Program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-accessing-product-betas#join-the-beta-program)  [Use product betas in your current projects](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-accessing-product-betas#using-product-betas-in-projects)  [Share your feedback on product betas](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-accessing-product-betas#sharing-feedback-on-product-betas)

---

### Join the Beta Program

Join the Beta Program and gain access to try out new Wix features before they're released.

#### To join the Beta Program:

[Go to your Wix Studio workspace](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/discover).  Click the** News Center **icon  at the top right and select **Betas**.   Click** Join Beta Program** on the top right.  Click **Join**.

---

### Using product betas in projects

Once you've joined, you can access the available product betas and try them out in Wix sites that you're an owner of. Product betas are a way for you to experience Wix features before they're released. Some of these features may end up being rolled back or available to Wix users at a later time.

#### To use a product beta:

[Go to your Wix Studio workspace](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/discover).  Click the** News Center **icon  at the top right and select **Betas**.  Browse the list and click on the product beta you'd like to try out.  Follow the steps on the left under **Steps**.

---

### Sharing feedback on product betas

Make a difference and share your feedback in the [Wix Studio Community Forum](https://forum.wixstudio.com/c/product-beta/22) on any betas you've used in your Wix sites. This is important as it helps us improve our products for you and all our users before they're released.

#### To share your feedback:

[Go to your Wix Studio workspace](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/discover).  Click the** News Center **icon  at the top right and select **Betas**.  Click on the product beta you'd like to leave feedback for.  Click **Leave Feedback**.  Enter your feedback about the beta in the [Wix Studio Community Forum](https://forum.wixstudio.com/c/product-beta/22).
