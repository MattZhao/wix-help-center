---
title: "Wix Logo Request: Saving Merchandise Designs in the Print Shop"
---

Currently, it is not possible to save merchandise designs (business cards, stickers, mugs etc.) you've worked on in the Print Shop, and edit them later.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can save your merchandise design by adding it to your cart in the Print Shop. When you come back to your cart later, you can click **Edit Item** next to the relevant design and continue customizing it.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
