---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding Hover Interactions to Strips and Columns"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add hover interactions to strips and columns.

As an alternative, you can add hover interactions to container boxes on your site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-hover-interaction)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
