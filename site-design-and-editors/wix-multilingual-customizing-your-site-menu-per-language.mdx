### Translating your site menu

Translate your site menu so visitors can browse in their own language, making your site more inclusive. For example, translating "Emergency Services" to "Services d'Urgence" for French visitors ensures they can quickly find critical help.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the **Switch Languages **panel at the top of the editor.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Switch Languages drop-down at the top of the Wix Editor.]
1. Select the relevant language.
2. Select your menu type and follow the steps:
Regular menu
1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant menu item.
3. Select the relevant option to choose how you want to translate the menu item: 

- **Edit name:** Manually translate the item name, enter your translation, and click **Done**.
- **Auto-translate name:** Translate the name automatically.
4. Repeat for the rest of your menu items and languages.

Advanced menu
1. Select your menu.
2. Click** Edit Translation**.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant menu item.
4. Select **Rename page**.** **
5. Enter your translation.
6. Click **Done**.
7. Repeat steps 3-6 for the rest of your menu items.

### Showing or hiding menu items per language

Show or hide menu items for each language to promote different content to different site visitors. For example, you might display "Local Events" on the Spanish site and hide it on the English site. Adjusting visibility in one language doesn't affect others.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click the **Switch Languages **panel at the top of the editor.
[image: A screenshot of clicking the Switch Languages drop-down at the top of the Wix Editor.]
1. Select the relevant language.
2. Select your menu type and follow the steps:
Regular menu
1. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant menu item.
3. Choose the relevant option: **Hide from menu **or **Show in menu**.
4. Repeat steps 2-3 for the rest of your menu items.

[image: undefined]Advanced menu
1. Select your menu.
2. Click** Edit Translation**.
3. Decide what you want to do:

- **Remove an item: **

1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant item.
2. Click **Hide from menu**.
- **Add an item: **

1. Click **+ Add Menu Item** at the bottom of the panel.
2. Select the item type you want to add to your menu:

- **Site Pages:** Add site pages as menu items.
- **Link:** Add a link to your menu. You can link to external sites, documents, and more.
- [**Section**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-sections)**:** Add a page section as an item to your menu.
- **Anchor:** Add a link to a page anchor on your menu.
- **Submenu title:** Add a drop-down submenu to your menu. Once you add the title, you can drag and drop menu items to be displayed in the submenu.
- [**Mega menu**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-mega-menu)**:** Add a container that's displayed when visitors hover over the item in the menu. You can change the layout and design of the container and add elements.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about customizing your site menu per language.

### How can I reset the translation and order of my menu items?

You can re-add the menu by clicking **Add Elements ** on the left side of your editor and selecting a menu.

### Will my menu's design, font and colors change when I translate it?

No. The design remains similar, only the text changes.

### Why can't I see the menu items from my main language on my secondary language site version?

If you can't see the menu items from your main language on your secondary language site version, it might be because you've created a custom menu in the past. You can easily re-add the menu by clicking **Add Elements ** on the left side of your editor and selecting a menu. Then, you can connect different items for each language.

### Can I hide a menu item for a specific language?

Yes, you can hide a menu item from your menu in a specific language, but keep in mind that it will still be accessible from your other site versions and via a direct link.

### Can I translate the "More" button in my menu?

Currently, it is not possible to translate the 'More' menu item, which appears when your menu is not wide enough to fit all the items in it.
You can remove the 'More' menu item by doing one of the following:
- Rename the 'More' menu item with a title that is not language-specific (such as three dots).
- [Stretch](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-stretching-an-element-to-full-width) the menu to display all site pages.
- Add a [menu folder](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-and-managing-a-dropdown-submenu) and move the additional pages to this folder. You can translate the folder name.

### How can I order my menu item for the sites that have both RTL and LTR languages?

To re-order your menu items for sites with both RTL and LTR languages, you need to manually drag and drop the items into your preferred order.

### Can I translate my site menu from the translation manager on my site dashboard?

Translating the site menu is available only in your editor. However, you can access the site menu translation mode via the shortcut section in the left panel of the translation manager.
