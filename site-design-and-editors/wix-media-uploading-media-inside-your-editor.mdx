---
title: "Wix Media: Uploading Media Inside Your Editor"
---

While working in the editor, you can upload your own media files to your Media Manager, so that they are ready to use whenever you need them.

You can upload images, videos, vector art, fonts and more.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to the editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Media **on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **Upload Media** icon  .
[image: Screenshot of upload media button in the Wix Editor.]
1. Drag and drop your files or choose an option on the left to upload media.
ImagesUpload your own images from your computer, social networks or other Wix sites to create your own image library. You can then add the uploaded images to your site as single images or in a gallery.
Select where you want to add the media from:
- Your computer.
- Your other Wix sites.
- Your social networks.
[Learn more about uploading media](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-uploading-media-to-the-media-manager).Vector ArtUpload your own vector art to the Media Manager in the editor, then add them to your site.

1. Select your file(s) from your computer.
2. Click **Open**.

**Note:** Once you have added your vector art to the editor, you can customize it using the options. The customization options available depend on the content of your vector art. VideosUpload your own videos to your Media Manager to make them available for your site whenever you want. 

1. Select your file(s) from your computer.
2. Click **Open**.

**Note**: A limit of ten minutes per video applies to sites with free plans. [Upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-existing-premium-plan) to increase the video minutes available to you.DocsUpload documents to your Media Manager and then add them to your site.

1. Select your file(s) from your computer.
2. Click **Open**.
Single Tracks (for the Mini Player)Upload single music tracks to your Media Manager so that you can easily access them when adding the Mini Player.

1. Select your file(s) from your computer.
2. Click **Open**.
Tip:[Upload your own fonts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-and-using-your-own-fonts) so that you can use them in text elements and anywhere else you can choose a font (buttons, contact forms, menu, etc).

2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Media**.
4. Click **My Uploads**.
5. Click the **Upload Media** icon  .
