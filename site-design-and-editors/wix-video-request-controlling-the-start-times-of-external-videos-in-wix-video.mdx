---
title: "Wix Video Request: Controlling the Start Times of External Videos in Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to control the start times of external videos (e.g. YouTube, Facebook, Vimeo etc.) in Wix Video.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
