---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Downloading Individual Images in Additional Digital File Formats"
---

Currently, you can only download digital images in the following formats:

Media TypeSupported FilesFile Size LimitsImagesJPG, GIF, PNG40MB

> **Notes:**
>
> - GIF and PNG images will be converted to WEBP file format when downloaded individually.
> - When the album is downloaded as a .zip file, the original image's format is preserved.
> - When customers purchase original files, the original image's format is preserved.

It is not possible to download digital files in other formats.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
