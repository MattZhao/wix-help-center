---
title: "Wix Video: Displaying a Single Video in Your Wix Video Player"
---

Wix Video displays your videos in a channel by default. Display a single video format to showcase standalone content.

### To play one video:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the Wix Video player.  Click **Settings**.  On the **Videos** tab, select **Single video**.  Search for the video you want in the search bar or select a video from the list by clicking on it.

> **Important information about playing one video:**
>
> - The single video format is not yet available with [action cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-adding-and-editing-action-cards). Similarly, videos that are not in a channel cannot yet be displayed in an email campaign.
> - If you're looking to display a single video without the features of Wix Video, try adding the [single video editor element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-and-setting-up-the-single-video-player).
