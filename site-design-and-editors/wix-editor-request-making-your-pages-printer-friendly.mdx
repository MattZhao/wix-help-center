---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Making Your Pages Printer-Friendly"
---

Currently, it is not possible to download a printer-friendly version or add a "print" button to your site for your visitors to print pages.

However, visitors can print your site pages using **Ctrl**+**P**, though they may lose some of the formatting and styles appearing on the site page. Visitors can also take screenshots of your site pages and print them from their desktop.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
