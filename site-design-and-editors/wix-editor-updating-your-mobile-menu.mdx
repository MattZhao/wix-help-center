title: "Wix Editor: Updating Your Mobile Menu"
---

In 2019 we released a new mobile menu. It has tons of new features that allow you to adjust it exactly as you want. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-the-mobile-menu)

### To update your mobile menu:

Go to the mobile Editor.Click your mobile menu.Click the **Settings** icon .Click **Update now**.Click **Get it Now**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Once you've updated your mobile menu, you can begin customizing it to suit you and your site's needs. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-mobile-menu)
