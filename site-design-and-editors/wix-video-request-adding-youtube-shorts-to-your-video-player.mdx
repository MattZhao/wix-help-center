---
title: "Wix Video Request: Adding YouTube Shorts to Your Video Player"
---

Currently, it is not possible to upload [YouTube Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en) in Wix Video for your site visitors to view. If you attempt to add a YouTube Short URL to your player, you will see a "not supported URL" error.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
