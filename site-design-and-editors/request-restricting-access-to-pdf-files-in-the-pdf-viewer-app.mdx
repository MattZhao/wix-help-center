---
title: "Request: Restricting Access to PDF Files in the PDF Viewer App"
---

Currently, anyone who has a direct link to the page displaying the PDF can access it. You cannot directly restrict access to certain users.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can, however, do the following to restrict access:
> 1. Place the PDF Viewer app on a [Member Only page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-members-only-pages) or [password protected page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-password-protecting-a-specific-page-of-your-site) on your site.
> 2. Display files in the Mini Viewer:
>
> 1. Click the PDF Viewer in your Editor.
> 2. Click **Settings**.
> 3. Click the **Display** tab.
> 4. Select the **Mini Viewer** option.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
