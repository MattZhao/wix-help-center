---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Adding a Title to Your Gallery"
---

Currently, it's not possible to give your Pro Gallery a title. Galleries are automatically titled in chronological order, e.g., Pro Gallery 1, Pro Gallery 2, etc. You may see this when [publishing your photos from Lightroom Classic to a gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-uploading-photos-from-lightroom-classic-1823522#step-4-publish-your-photos-from-lightroom-classic-to-your-wix-pro-gallery) or when [translating a gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-pro-gallery).

> **Tips:**
>
> - You can manually make a title for your gallery by [adding a textbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text#adding-text) above it in your Editor.
> - [You can add a title to your gallery in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-and-managing-media-in-the-wix-app#viewing-and-managing-your-galleries). The title is, however, only visible to Spaces by Wix app users.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
