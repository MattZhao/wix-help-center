---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Customizing the Og:Image for Instagram, WhatsApp, and iMessage"
---

Your [og:image](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-social-share-settings-for-your-sites-pages) from your site or page is displayed across all of your social networks. Currently, you cannot control the og:image on Instagram, Whatsapp, or iMessage. You can however, choose the og:image for [Facebook](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-troubleshooting-outdated-info-and-thumbnail-on-facebook), [Twitter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-updating-your-sites-twitterimage) and [LinkedIn](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-updating-the-ogimage-on-linkedin).

> **Tip:**
>
> You can update the link preview image that Telegram uses by opening [the Telegram Webpage Bot](https://telegram.me/webpagebot), clicking **Start**, and sending your page's URL. [Learn more about Telegram link previews](https://support.wix.com/en/article/updating-your-pages-link-preview-in-telegram)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.

> **Tip:**
>
> If your social networks are showing an old og:image and have not updated to your new image, click the relevant link for a solution: [Facebook](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-troubleshooting-outdated-info-and-thumbnail-on-facebook), [Twitter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-updating-your-sites-twitterimage), or [LinkedIn](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-updating-the-ogimage-on-linkedin).
