---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Transfer Word Credits to a Different Site / Account"
---

[When purchasing word credits to auto-translate your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site#purchasing-translation-packages), the credits can only be used for that specific site. Currently, it is not possible to transfer word credits to a different site in the same account, or a different account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
