---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Customizing the Video Play Button"
---

Currently, it is not possible to customize the play button that appears when visitors hover over videos in your Pro Gallery.

You can, however, choose to [hide or show the play button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-video-settings-in-your-wix-pro-gallery#customizing-your-video-settings).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
