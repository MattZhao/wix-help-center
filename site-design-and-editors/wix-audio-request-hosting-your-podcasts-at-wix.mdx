---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Hosting Your Podcasts at Wix"
---

Currently, Wix does not have a feature which allows for hosting podcasts.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can link to your externally hosted Podcasts with the Wix Podcast Player. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-podcast-player-connecting-your-rss-feed).
