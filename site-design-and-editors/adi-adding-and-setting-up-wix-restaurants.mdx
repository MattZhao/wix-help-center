Create a full-service restaurant site with Wix Restaurants. Showcase your restaurant's dishes with a professional menu, take online orders and receive payments, let customers book a table online and more.

Wix Restaurants is made up of 3 apps - Menus, Orders, and Reservations - and additional integrations to help you best run your restaurant.

**In this article, learn more about adding and setting up the:**

[Menus app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants#adding-and-setting-up-the-menus-app)  [Orders app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants#adding-and-setting-up-the-orders-app)  [Reservations app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants#adding-and-setting-up-the-reservations-app)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants#faqs)

### Adding and setting up the Menus app

Displays multiple menus (E.g. Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner) and different menu sections, such as Starters, Main Dishes, Desserts).

#### To add and set up the app:

Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Apps**.  Scroll down to **Restaurants** and click **Add** next to **Menus**.  Click **Got It**.  Click the app in the ADI Builder and click **Manage**.  [Add your menu sections and dishes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-creating-your-menus).

---

### Adding and setting up the Orders app

Displays your online ordering menu where customers can order food for delivery or pickup. To use the Online Ordering app, you must first add the Menu app and set up your menu.

#### To add and set up the app:

Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Apps**.  Scroll down to **Restaurants** and click **Add** next to **Orders**.  Click **Got It**.  Click the app in the ADI Builder and click **Manage**.  [Enable ordering, set your ordering schedule, and manage your pick-up times in your dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-setting-up-online-ordering-for-your-restaurant).

---

### Adding and setting up the Reservations app

Your guests can reserve a table for themselves or their family directly from your site.

#### To add and set up the app:

Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Apps**.  Scroll down to **Restaurants** and click **Add** next to **Reservations**.  Select the page you want to add the app to.

**Note:** The app is added to the footer of the page you selected.  Click **Add to Page**.  Click **Got It**.  Click the app in the ADI Builder and click **Manage**.  [Add party sizes, set availability, and advanced options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-customizing-your-reservation-settings).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about adding and setting up Wix Restaurants:

### Do I need a Premium plan to accept online orders and table reservations?

Yes. You need to have a [Business Premium Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-upgrading-your-restaurants-plan) to accept online orders and table.

### How can I delete Wix Restaurants from my site?

1. Click **Add **on the top bar inside ADI.
2. Click **Apps**.
3. Scroll down to **Restaurants.**
4. Click the More Actions** ** icon next to the app you want to remove.
5. Click **Delete.**
6. Click **Delete** again to confirm.
7. Repeat these steps for the other Restaurant apps.

### Can I change the layout of my restaurant sections?

Yes. You can change the layout of all 3 Wix Restaurants apps.

1. Hover over the section you want to change.
2. Click **Design**.
3. Select a new layout for this section.
