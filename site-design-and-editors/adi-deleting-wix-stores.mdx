---
title: "ADI: Deleting Wix Stores"
---

Deleting Wix Stores deletes all of the elements of your store, including all of the galleries, the cart icon at the top of your page and all the hidden pages in your store.

### To delete Wix Stores from your site

1. Click the **Pages** menu on the top bar inside ADI.
2. Click the **Shop **page.
3. Click the **Settings **icon.
4. Click **Delete**.
5. Click **Delete** in the confirmation window.

### To delete your Wix Stores when it is set as your home page

1. [Add a new page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-adding-and-managing-your-sites-pages).
2. Click the **Pages** menu on the top bar.
3. Click the **Settings **icon next to the new page you have created.
4. Click **Set as Homepage**.
5. Click the **Settings **icon next to **Shop**.
6. Click **Delete**.

> **Tip:**
>
> If your Shop page is the home page of your site, you need to set a different page of your site as your home page.
