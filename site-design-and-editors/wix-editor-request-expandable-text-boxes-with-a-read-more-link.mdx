---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Expandable Text Boxes with a Read More Link"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature is not yet available to all users.

We are pleased to announce that you can now add collapsible text boxes to your site.

Collapsible text allows you to display content on your page without compromising your site’s layout and alignment. A preview of your text is displayed, and visitors can click a link or an expand label to view the whole body of text. [Learn how to add and set up collapsible text on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-collapsible-text)
