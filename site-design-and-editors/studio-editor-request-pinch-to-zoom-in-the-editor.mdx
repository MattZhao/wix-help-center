---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Pinch-to-Zoom in the Editor"
---

When designing in the Studio Editor, you can click the **+** / **-** icons at the top to change your view. Currently, it is not possible to use pinch gestures to zoom in or out.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
