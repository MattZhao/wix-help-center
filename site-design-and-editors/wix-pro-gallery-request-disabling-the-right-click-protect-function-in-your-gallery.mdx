---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Disabling the Right-Click Protect Function in Your Gallery"
---

Currently, to protect your content, it is not possible to allow visitors to right-click and save / download media from your gallery.

You can, however, allow visitors to download gallery items by [enabling the Download toggle in your Pro Gallery settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-downloading-images-from-a-gallery). A download icon appears on each individual item when a visitor hovers over it.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
