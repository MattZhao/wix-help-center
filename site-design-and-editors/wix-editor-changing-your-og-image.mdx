Choose which thumbnail image to display when sharing your site on Facebook, X or other social networks. You can set a different og:image for each page of your site, or one image for the whole site.

**In this article, learn more about: **

[Changing the og:image for a page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-ogimage#changing-the-ogimage-for-a-page)  [Changing the og:image for a site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-ogimage#changing-the-ogimage-for-a-site)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-ogimage#faqs)

### Changing the og:image for a page

Set a unique og:image for each page or an individual page on your site. Make each page stand out from the rest and create a tailored social sharing experience.

#### To change the og:image for an individual page:

Click **Pages & Menu**   on the left Editor menu.  Click the relevant page.  Click More Actions .  Select **Social share**.

Click **Upload Image**.  Select an image or click **+ Upload Media** to upload a new one.  Click **Add to Page**.  [Publish your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site#publishing-your-site).

> **Did you know?**
>
> You can also [set the page's og:title and og:description from the Social share settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-social-share-settings-for-your-sites-pages).

### Changing the og:image for a site

You can also set one og:image for every page on your site to reinforce your brand, and provide a consistent sharing experience.

#### To change the og:image for your whole site:

Open [your site's settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Edit%20Site%20Info&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings/).  Click **Website settings**.  Scroll down to General social image and click **Upload Image**.  Select an image or click **+ Upload Media** to upload a new one.  Click **Choose File** to add it.  Click **Save** at the top of the dashboard.  [Publish your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-previewing-and-publishing-your-site#publishing-your-site).

### FAQs

Select a question to learn more about og:images.

### Do my images need to be a specific size?

Og:images should have an aspect ratio of 1.91:1. This means that the width should be 1.9 X the height to avoid cropping issues. [Learn more about og:image recommendations.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-recommended-ogimage-size)

### What happens if I don’t add an og:image for a page?

The site og:image is used.

### What image is shown when I share a blog post on Facebook?

You can [add cover images to your blog posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-a-cover-image-to-a-blog-post) to choose how they are displayed on Facebook.
