---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Customizing the Spacing Between Paragraphs"
---

Currently, it is not possible to manually customize the spacing between paragraphs in a single text field.

As an alternative, you can add each paragraph separately, and adjust the spacing between them.

#### To add separate paragraphs and adjust the spacing:

Open the relevant page in the Editor.  [Add a text element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-editing-text) for each paragraph.  Enter your text into the fields.  Drag the text elements into place on the page, leaving as much space in between as you need.

> **Did you know?**
>
> You can customize your text's line spacing using [the Edit Text panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-formatting-your-text).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
