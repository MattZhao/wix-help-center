title: "Wix Studio: Creating a Branded App for Your Clients"
---

Create a fully branded mobile app for your clients that their customers can download from the App Store and Google Play. You have full control over the app: start by designing its theme and logo and then build out the app's features and content. Then submit the app to Apple and Google. Once the app is live, it is available to download for clients and their customers.

You can choose to create an app for an existing client site, or create a standalone app that is not connected to a site.

**In this article, learn more about:**

---

### Creating a branded app for an existing client site

Every site can have its own branded app. When you build a branded app for a site, you can match the site's branding and theme. Any Wix apps connected to the site (e.g. Wix Stores, Wix Bookings) automatically sync to the branded app.

#### To create a branded app for an existing client site:

[Go to Branded Mobile Apps](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/branded-app?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Studio Workspace.  Click **Get Started.**  Select a site for the branded app and click **Continue**. This takes you from your Studio workspace to the relevant site's dashboard.

> **Note:**
>
> You will only see Step 2 and 3 if this is your first time building a branded app. If you have built an app before, you will go directly to the Branded Mobile Apps dashboard. Click the** Create New Branded App** drop-down at the top right and then select **Create for existing site **to choose a site.
>
> [image: A screenshot of the create a new branded app dropdown with create from website selected.]

You are now on the Mobile app tab of your client's site dashboard. In the **Your own mobile app** tab, add the branded app's name and choose an icon. Then click **Next**.  Select a theme color for the app. You can always change this later. Then click **Next**.  Choose a navigation layout for the app. Then click **Next**.  (Optional) Scan the QR code to see how the app appears on mobile devices. Then click **Continue Setup**.  Now start editing the features and content of the app. Click **Edit Mobile App** to open the web editor. [Learn how to edit the features and content of the app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content).

Once you're finished editing your app's content, the next steps include:

1. [Purchasing a Branded App plan for this site](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Branded%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/branded-apps/premium).
2. Connecting an [Apple Developer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-creating-and-connecting-an-apple-developer-account) and [Google Developer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-creating-and-connecting-a-google-developer-account) account.
3. [Submitting the app to the App Store and Google Play](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-submitting-your-app-for-approval).

[Learn more about the process of creating a branded app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-the-process-of-creating-a-branded-app).

### Creating a branded app without a connected site

You can also create a fully branded mobile app from scratch without connecting it to an existing site. For example, a personal trainer could have a branded app where clients can book training sessions and contact the trainer. However, the business might not require a full website.

#### To create a standalone branded app:

[Go to Branded Mobile Apps](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/branded-app?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Studio Workspace.  Click **Get Started.**  In the site selector, click **+ Create New Branded App** at bottom left.

> **Note:**
>
> You will only see Step 2 and 3 if this is your first time building a branded app. If you have built an app before, you will go directly to the Branded Mobile Apps dashboard. Click the** Create New Branded App** drop-down and select **Create without a site **to start building a new app.
>
> [image: A screenshot of the create a new branded app dropdown with create from scratch selected.]

Choose the type of branded app you want to create (e.g. online store, fitness trainer) and click **Save & Continue**.  Choose the app's name and icon, brand color, and navigation type.

(Optional) Scan the QR code to see how the branded app looks from a mobile device.  Click **Continue to Dashboard** to keep setting up the branded app from the dashboard. This dashboard is a 'mobile-first' dashboard where you can manage everything in the app. [Learn more about creating a mobile app without a site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-creating-a-branded-app-without-a-connected-wix-site).   Click **Edit Mobile App** at the top right of the dashboard to [customize the app's features and content](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-customizing-your-apps-features-and-content).   Complete the list of tasks in the app's dashboard to keep setting it up including:

1. [Purchasing a Branded App plan](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Branded%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/branded-apps/premium).
2. Connecting an [Apple Developer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-creating-and-connecting-an-apple-developer-account) and [Google Developer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-creating-and-connecting-a-google-developer-account) account.
3. [Submitting the app to the App Store and Google Play](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-submitting-your-app-for-approval).

> **Tip:**
>
> If you decide you want to eventually create a website for the app, click **Design Website** at the top right of your dashboard.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about Branded App by Wix.

### What is Branded App by Wix?

Branded App by Wix gives you the ability to create a fully branded mobile app for your clients that is listed on the App Store & Google Play. Design the app's features, content, functionality and connect it with Wix's business solutions. [Learn more about the process of creating a branded app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-the-process-of-creating-a-branded-app).

### What are the benefits of building a branded app for my clients?

- Increase the brand credibility of clients with a fully branded app listed on the App Store & Google Play.
- Build customer loyalty for your clients with an app that allows their customers to easily interact with the business on the go.
- Your clients get complete app ownership without any Wix branding.
- Get total creative freedom over the app including designing the app's icon, layout, content, and login screen.
- Automatic app maintenance and performance updates.

### What are some examples of branded apps?

[View and download branded apps on your mobile device ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview#how-to-download-and-view-branded-app-examples)to see Branded App by Wix in action.

### What are the different Branded App Premium plans?

[Compare the different branded app plans and plan features](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Branded%20Plans&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/branded-apps/premium).

### Can I create multiple branded apps for different clients?

Yes, you can create different branded apps for each client. View and manage all your apps at once in** Branded Mobile Apps** in your Wix Studio Workspace.

### Can I create a branded app for a client without also creating a site?

Yes. You can choose to build a 'siteless' branded app that is not connected to an existing Wix site. When you start creating an app you have the option to **Create from scratch** or **Create from website**.

### Does the content from a site automatically sync to a branded app?

Each branded app is linked to the relevant site's dashboard. Changes or edits you make to your connected apps (e.g. Wix Stores, Wix Bookings services, Wix Blog) are reflected in the branded app.
However, any content or design changes you make directly from the Editor (adding text, images, or videos) won't affect your app's design.
