---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Changing the Scroll Direction to Right-to-Left"
---

Currently, you can only scroll from left to right inside containers, sliders and page sections. It is not possible to change the scrollbar orientation to right-to-left.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
