---
title: "ADI Request: Uploading Your Fonts to ADI"
---

Currently, you cannot upload your own fonts to ADI.

If you want to upload your own fonts, you need to [head to the Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-moving-from-adi-to-the-editor) and follow the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-and-using-your-own-fonts).
