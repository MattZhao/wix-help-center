title: "Wix Video: Using Wix Video for Free"
---

There are many things that you can do when you use the Wix Video Player as a free addition to your site. [Learn more about Wix Video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-videos-to-your-wix-site).

> **Wix Video storage:**
>
> Video hours will soon become video storage. There will be a 90 day grace period if you already use video hours before this change takes place permanently.

---

### Free Wix Video features

Customize the layout and design of your video player.  Organize your videos into channels.  Upload videos to and from social platforms like YouTube, Facebook and Vimeo.  Stream up to 10-minute long live stream videos.

---

### Exceeding the free video limit

**Uploads:** You cannot upload a video that exceeds your video limit.  **Live Stream:** If your live stream exceeds your storage limit, it is saved in your Video Library.

You can increase your video limit by [upgrading your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter). Learn more about [managing your site's storage](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-managing-your-storage).

> **Note:**
>
> Your video storage limit is per site. To increase the limit on more than one site, you'll need to upgrade each site individually.
