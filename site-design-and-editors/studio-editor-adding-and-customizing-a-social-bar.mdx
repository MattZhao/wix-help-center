title: "Studio Editor: Adding and Customizing a Social Bar"
---

Encourage visitors to explore your client's social media accounts using a social bar. The social bar shows icons of the platforms you want to include, linked to the relevant accounts. You can also select the icon designs and adjust their size and spacing.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Add a social bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-social-bar#adding-a-social-bar-to-your-site)  [Customize your social bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-social-bar#customizing-your-social-bar)  [Customize the layout of your social bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-social-bar#customizing-the-layout-of-your-social-bar)

### Adding a social bar to your site

Add a social bar to your page and place it in a location that visitors can easily notice. For example, you can show the social bar on the site header so it appears at the very top of every page.

#### To add a social bar:

Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Embed & Social**.  Click **Social Bars**.  Drag the social bar to the desired location on the page.

---

### Customizing your social bar

The social bar comes with default icons, but it's fully customizable to your client's needs. You can replace the icons, add new ones, and set the relevant social links. You can also remove icons that you don't need.

#### To customize your social bar:

Click the social bar in the Editor.   Click **Set Social Links**.  Choose how you want to customize the social bar:

### Add a new icon

1. Click** + Add Icons **at the top.
2. Add a new icon by doing one of the following:

- Click** + Upload Media** to upload a custom design.
- Select the desired icon from **Media by Wix**, **Shutterstock**,** **or **Unsplash**.
3. Click **Add to Gallery**.
4. Click **Done**.

[image: A screenshot of how to add a new icon.]

### Replace an icon

1. Click the relevant social media icon.
2. Click **Replace Icon **on the right.
3. Choose a new icon by doing one of the following:

- Click** + Upload Media** to upload a custom design.
- Select the desired icon from **Media by Wix**, **Shutterstock**,** **or **Unsplash**.
4. Click **Choose Image**.
5. Click **Done**.

[image: Screenshot of the panel where you set social icons and links, showing the replace icon option at the top]

### Add/change a social media link

1. Click the relevant social media icon.
2. Click the Link icon  under** What social page does this icon link to?**.
3. Enter the relevant social media URL.
4. Select how you want the link to open under **How does it open?**.
5. (Optional) Click** Advanced SEO Settings** to link [rel values](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-link-rel-attributes).
6. Click **Done**.

[image: A screenshot of a social media Web address.]

### Remove an icon

1. Hover over the relevant social media icon.
2. Click the Remove icon  to delete it.

---

### Customizing the layout of your social bar

Customize the social bar's layout and appearance. You can change the icon size, adjust the spacing between icons and choose to display them horizontally or vertically.

#### To customize the layout:

Click the social bar in the Editor.   Click the Layout icon .   Customize the layout using any of the below options:

- **Icon size: **Set the size of the social media icon.
- **Spacing:** Set the the space between the social media icons.
- **How does the bar look?: **Choose the direction of the icons.

> **Need to flip or rotate the icons?**
>
> 1. Click the social bar in the Editor.
> 2. Click the Open Inspector arrow  at the top right of the Editor.
> 3. Click **Adjust** and enter the desired angle (e.g.180) next to **Rotate**.
