title: "Wix Music: Selling Your Music"
---

Sell your own music with Wix Music! We make it easy for you to sell your music to your fans and keep track of how much revenue you are making.

Let your fans download your whole album or single or specific tracks in a playlist directly from your site for a price of your choice! Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-allowing-fans-to-download-your-music) to learn more about managing download options in your album, your single and your playlist.

> Wix Music lets you sell your music online for free without needing a Premium Plan.
