---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Removing the Black Overlay from Gallery Images Viewed in Expand Mode on Mobile Devices"
---

When clicking a gallery image in expand mode while using a mobile device, a black overlay is added to the image. At the moment, you can remove the black overlay by removing the title and description of the image.

> **Tip:**
>
> Alternatively, you can switch to the [Wix Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-an-overview) which allows you to [customize the expand mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-customizing-the-expand-mode-for-your-gallery) window according to your preferences.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
