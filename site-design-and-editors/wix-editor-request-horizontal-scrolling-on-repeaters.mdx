---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Horizontal Scrolling on Repeaters"
---

Currently, it is not possible for your site visitors to scroll through repeaters horizontally. Once the limit of items in the repeater has been reached on one line, another line is created for the additional items.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
