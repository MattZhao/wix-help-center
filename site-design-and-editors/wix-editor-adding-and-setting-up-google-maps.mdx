### Adding Google Maps to your site

Add a Google Map to your site to allow your site visitors to find your location.

#### To add Google Maps:

Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.  Click **Contact & Forms**.  Click** Maps **or **Full Width Maps**.  Click a map or drag it to the relevant location on your page.  Click **Manage Locations**.  Choose an how many locations you want to add to the map:

### One location

1. Click **One**.
2. Enter the location details:

- **Address:** Type the address.
- **Title: **Add a title to the location pin on the map to let users know what its called.
- **Description:** Add a short description to describe the location.
- **Link: **Add a link to redirect your users to your company's website or link of your choice. It is not possible to link to a lightbox, anchor or the top/bottom of the page.
- **Link text: **Add a title to your link to let users know what to expect when clicking.

### Multiple locations

1. Click **Multiple**.
2. Edit the template location to make it your first location:

1. Hover over the default (Wix Office) location and click the **More Actions** icon .
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Enter your first location's details:

- **Address:** Type the address.
- **Title: **Add a title to the location on the map to let users know what its called.
- **Description:** Add a short description to describe the location.
- **Link: **Add a link to redirect your users to your company's website or link of your choice. It is not possible to link to a lightbox, anchor or the top/bottom of the page.
- **Link text: **Add a title to your link to let users know what to expect when clicking.
4. Click the **Back **icon  at the top left.
5. Add another location: 

1. Click **Add Location**.
2. Enter the location details.
3. Click the **Back **icon  at the top left.
4. Repeat steps a-b for each location you want to add to your map.

**Tip:**Click the More Actions icon  next to a location to edit, duplicate, or delete it.

### Customizing your Google Map settings

Change the views, zoom and controls on your Google Map so that it behaves just as you want.

#### To change the settings of your map:

Click the Google Map in your editor.  Click the **Settings** icon .  Customize the settings. Click the settings below to learn more:

### Zoom

Choose the zoom of your map by dragging the slider. You may want to focus your map directly on your business location, or show where your business is located in relation to nearby cities.

**Note:** Your site visitors can also change the zoom using the map controls (see Show Zoom Control setting below).

### Show map and satellite views

This setting lets your visitors switch between the map and satellite views of your map.
- **Enabled: **Visitors can switch between the map and satellite views.
- **Disabled: **Visitors cannot switch between the map and satellite views.

### Show zoom control

This setting allows your visitors to zoom in and out of your map.

- **Enabled: **Visitors can change the zoom on the map using the controls.
- **Disabled: **Visitors cannot change the zoom on the map using the controls.

**Note:** You can also change the default zoom of your map (see Zoom setting above).

### Show Street View control

This setting show or hides the Google Street View tool.

- **Enabled: **Visitors can use Google Street View on your map.
- **Disabled: **Visitors cannot use Google Street View on your map.

### Show Directions link

This setting shows or hides the link to Google's directions service.

- **Enabled: **Visitors can click **Directions** to find their way to your location.
- **Disabled: **Visitors cannot use Google's directions service directly from your map.

### Allow dragging

This setting allows your visitors to move around your map by clicking and dragging with their mouse.
- **Enabled: **Visitors can click and drag your map.
- **Disabled: **Visitors cannot click and drag your map.

### Choosing your Google Map design and pin

Wix offers many color themes for your Google Map, so that your map blends in perfectly with your site's design. You can also change the Pin's default shape to something that fits your style – including any image from the Media Manager.

#### To change the design of Google Maps:

Click the map in the editor.  Click the **Design** icon .  Select a map design.  Click the **Pin Design** tab and do one of the following:

- **Choose a preset pin: **Select a shape for the pin. When available, you can also change its color by clicking the box under **Choose a color**.
- **Upload your own pin:** Click the **Add** icon  to upload your file.

### My Google Maps is showing an incorrect address. How can I fix this?

If your Google Map does not show the correct address, we recommend pasting the coordinates of your location instead of the address. 

**To find a location's coordinates and add them to your Google Maps:**
1. Go to [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/).
2. Paste the address.
3. Right-click the pinned location on the map.
4. Click **What's here?**
5. Copy the coordinates that appear under the search box.
6. Click the Google Map in the editor.
7. Click **Manage Locations**.
8. Do one of the following:

- **If you have one location on your map:** Type the coordinates you copied in the **Address** field.
- **If you have multiple locations on your map: **

1. Click the **More Actions ** icon on the relevant address.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Type the coordinates you copied in the **Address** field.

### My Google Maps looks different in Preview mode in comparison to the live version of my site. Why?

The Preview mode of your site may not show the up-to-date version of your Google Maps. If the preview mode looks different to the live site, please click **Publish **when you make a change to the Google Map.
