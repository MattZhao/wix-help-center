Your ADI site is made up of pages and sections. Wix offers tons of ready-made sections that you can add to your site in just one click. Then customize these sections with your own content. Examples of sections include an About section, an Opening Hours section, and a Services section.

### Adding a section

Hover over **Add** on the top bar.  Click **Section to Page**.

Click a section to add it to your site.

> **What happens to your site menu?**
>
> If you have a one-page site, the title of the section appears on your site menu. You can rename the menu item (section title) at any time. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-managing-your-site-menu#changing-the-text-on-your-menu)

### Changing the layout of a section

You can change the image size, orientation, and alignment of your text and media in a section, by choosing a different layout.

#### To change the layout of a section:

Hover over the section you want to update inside ADI.Click **Design**.Click a new layout from the **Section Design **panel.

> **Tip:**
>
> Further customize your section layout by clicking **Customize Section Design** in the Section Design panel. This option is not available for all sections.

---

### Reordering your page sections

You can change the order of the sections on your page.

> **Important:**
>
> You cannot move your header or footer up or down. The header is always at the top of the page, and the footer is always at the bottom.

#### To reorder your sections:

Click the section you want to move inside ADI.Click the **Move Up **or** Move Down** arrows.

> **Tip:**
>
> You may find it easier if you [zoom out](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-getting-to-know-the-wix-adi-builder) and then reorder your sections as you can see more of your site at once.

---

### Changing a section into a full page

You can turn a page section into a page of its own at anytime.

#### To change a section into a page:

Click the section of your site inside ADI that you want to move.  Click the More Actions icon .   Click **Move to**.  Click **New Page** on the drop-down menu.  Click **Move**.

> **Important:**
>
> - You cannot convert your header or footer into a whole page.
> - Your new page has the name Blank Page.

### Choosing what content to show in a section

You can choose which elements or text fields are displayed in your sections. This means that you can customize your sections exactly as you wish.

#### To hide or show elements in a page section:

Click the section that contains the element or text field that you want to show or hide.  Click **Edit**.  Select or deselect the Show/Hide icon next to the relevant field:

- **Selected**: The element or text field is shown.
- **Unselected: **The element or text field is hidden.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about page sections.

### How do I change the layout of a section?

You can change the image size, orientation, and alignment of your text and media in a section, by choosing a different layout.
1. Hover over the section you want to update inside ADI.
2. Click **Design**.
3. Click a new layout from the **Section Design **panel.

### How do I duplicate a section?

Save yourself time and create a copy of any section on your site in just one click. The new duplicated section is placed immediately below the previous one.
1. Hover over the relevant section.
2. Click the More Actions icon .
3. Click **Duplicate**.
**Tip: **You can move your duplicate section to any other page on your site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-editing-a-section)

### How do I move a section to another page on my site?

1. Hover over the relevant section.
2. Click the More Actions icon .
3. Click **Move to**.
4. Click the page that you want to move the section to.
5. Click **Move**.
**Note: **Some sections cannot be moved from one page to another. In this case, the** Move to** option is not available.

### How do I delete a section?

You can delete any of the sections on your site except the header or footer sections.
1. Hover over the relevant section.
2. Click the More Actions icon .
3. Click **Delete**.
4. Click **Delete** again to confirm.
