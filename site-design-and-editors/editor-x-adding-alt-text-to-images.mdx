---
title: "Editor X: Adding Alt Text to Images"
---

Improve your site's SEO and accessibility by adding alt text to your images. Alt text is a short description that tells search engines and screen readers the content of an image.

### To add alt text to an image:

Click the image in your Editor.   Click the Settings icon .  Write a short description for your image under **What's in the image? Tell Google**.
