---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Creating a Social Link Page"
---

Social link pages are one-stop, platform-based pages, where you can link to all social media accounts and sites, using buttons, icons, and direct links. They are generally accessed from your social media accounts, and are extremely useful for content creators, influencers, and businesses.

Currently, it is not possible to add a template link page to your Wix site.

However, you can build incredible pages from scratch, and add links and elements to showcase your social media accounts and external sites manually. You can then link to this page from your social media accounts.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
