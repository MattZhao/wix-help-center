---
title: "Wix Video Request: Adding a 'Last Played' Position to a Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to pause a video, leave the site and continue watching later from the last played position. Once you leave the site and return, the video always play from the beginning.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
