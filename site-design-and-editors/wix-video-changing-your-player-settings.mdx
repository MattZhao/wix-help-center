---
title: "Wix Video: Changing Your Player Settings"
---

Customize the settings of your Wix Video player to make sure your videos display exactly how you want them to. Choose the best layout for your player and decide to play a channel of videos or just one video.

> **Video quality (resolution) control for mobile iOS users**
>
> The native iOS Safari player on mobile doesn't support viewers changing video quality. If you set up your player to allow viewers to control video quality, this will not be possible for mobile iOS users.

**In this article, learn more about changing your player settings when playing: **

---

### Changing your player settings when playing a channel

When you play a channel of videos in the Wix Video player, you can choose from several layouts. The type of layout you choose determines the settings you can customize.

#### To change your player settings when playing a channel:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click the Wix Video player.  Click **Settings**.

**Note: **Make sure **Channel** is selected on the **Videos** tab.  Click the **Layout **tab and [choose the layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-changing-the-layout-of-your-wix-video-player) you want to use.  Click the **Player **tab.  Customize your player settings. These will be different for each layout. Click below to view the settings for each layout:

### Classic

**Autoplay on load:** Enable or disable automatic playback.
**What's on the video while playing?:** Choose what you'd like to display:
- **Video title:** Show the video title while the video plays.
- **Info icon:** Show the Info icon while the video plays.
- **Share icon:** Show the Share icon  while the video plays.

**What can viewers control?:** Choose the controls you make available:
- **Playback speed:** Allow the viewer to play the video at a faster speed.
- **Quality:** Allow the viewer to choose a lower video quality to prevent lags if their internet connection is slow.

**When the video ends: **Select what will happen when the video finishes: **Show video cover**, **Open action cards** or **Play next video**.

**Note:** Click the Wix Video player in your Editor and then **Manage Videos** to create and manage action cards.
**Comments:** Enable the comments toggles to **Allow on uploaded videos** or **Allow during live stream**.

**Note: **Make sure **Single video** is selected on the **Videos** tab.  Click the **Layout **tab and choose **Compact** or **Strip **as the [the layout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-changing-the-layout-of-your-wix-video-player) you want to use.
