title: "Editor X: Using Layouters"
---

Organize your content in a layouter; a smart layout tool made up of responsive containers. Using flex technology, the containers automatically adjust to your visitor's screen size.

You can choose a display type for your layouter (e.g. columns, bricks, slides), add or remove items as needed, and tweak settings like margins and directionality.

**In this article, learn how:**

[Add a layouter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-layouters#adding-a-layouter)  [Add and delete layouter items](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-layouters#adding-and-removing-layouter-items)  [Choose and customize the display](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-layouters#customizing-the-display-of-a-layouter)  [Resize layouter items](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-layouters#resizing-layouter-items)  [Reorder layouter items](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-using-layouters#reordering-layouter-items)

### Adding a layouter

Open the Add panel to select a layouter preset and drag it onto your page. There are many designs to choose from according to the type of content you need to display (e.g. images only, images and text, etc.)

#### To add a layouter:

Click **Add Elements**  on the top of Editor X.  Click **Layout Tools**.  Click **Layouters**.  Drag a layouter onto your page.

> **See how it works:**
>
> Drag the handles on your canvas to see how the content of the layouter seamlessly reorganizes across breakpoints.

### Adding and managing Layouter items

A Layouter is made up of several items that are responsive containers. You can change the number of items in a Layouter and it will automatically resize the items to fit the available space.

To add a new item, you can click **Add Item** or use the Manage Items panel in the Inspector.

#### To manage your Layouter items from the Inspector:

Click your Layouter.  Click the Inspector icon  at the top of Editor X.  (In the Inspector) Click **Manage **next to the number of items. 

 [image: A screenshot of the Inspector panel where you can click Manage to manage layouter items]

  Click the More Actions icon  next to an item to do any of the following:

- **Duplicate:** Make a copy of a specific item.
- **Rename: **Give each item a name you can recognize.
- **Delete:** Remove the item completely.
- **Don't Display: **Hide this item from the specific breakpoint you're working on.

> **Need to re-order the items?**
>
> Hold the Reorder icon  next to the relevant item and drag it to its new position.

### Customizing the display of a layouter

Change the display of your layouter in just a click. You can choose how you want to present your content – in columns, rows, mosaic, a slider or a slideshow. The items automatically re-arrange for you.

> **Working with breakpoints:**
>
> - You can set a different layout for each breakpoint.
> - We recommend the column or slider layout on smaller screens such as mobile devices.

#### To further customize the display:

Click your layouter.  Click the** **Inspector** **panel  at the top right of Editor X.  (Optional) Under **Display**, select a **Display type**.  Customize your layouter based on the selected display type:

### Columns: Layout options

- **Item proportions: **Click **Equalize** to give all layouter items the same size definitions.
- **CSS Gaps:** Enter a value (in px) for the horizontal space between the layouter items.
- **Item direction:** Select the direction of the items in the layouter.

### Rows: Layout options

**CSS Gaps:** Enter a value (in px, vh or vw) for the vertical space between the layouter items.

### Bricks: Layout options

- **Direction: **Decide whether your items are organized from left to right or right to left.
- **Item proportions: **Click **Equalize** to give all layouter items the same size definitions.
- **CSS Gaps:** Enter a value (in px, vh or vw) for the horizontal and vertical spaces between the layouter items.
- **Item alignment:** Choose how your items should be aligned and positioned. Depending on the look you want to achieve, you can align the items to the layouter edges (start or end), or create space between / around the items.

### Mosaic: Layout options

- **Item proportions: **Click **Equalize** to give all layouter items the same size definitions.
- **CSS Gaps:** Enter a value (in px, vh or vw) for the horizontal and vertical spaces between the layouter items.
- **Item direction:** Select the direction of the items in the layouter.

### Slider: Layout options

- **Slider direction:** Display your items on a horizontal or vertical slider.
- **Item proportions: **Click **Equalize** to give all layouter items the same size definitions.
- **CSS Gaps:** Enter a value (in px, vh or vw) for the vertical space between the layouter items.

**Note: **The available options (horizontal or vertical) depend on the slider direction you choose.
- **Scroll Snap: **Click the toggle to enable it. When scrolling through the slides of your layouter, it "snaps" to each item (slide) so visitors don't miss it.
- **Show scrollbar:** Show or hide a scrollbar for your visitors to scroll through the items.

### Slides: Layout options

- **Direction:** Display your slides from left-to-right or top-to-bottom.
- **Items per slide: **Set the number of layouter items that should appear in each slide.
- **Navigation: **Click **+ Add** to add arrows, text or buttons to help visitors navigate between slides.
- **Show scrollbar:** Show or hide a scrollbar that lets visitors scroll through the slides.

> **Note about CSS gaps:**
>
> Some old browsers don't support this feature (e.g. Chrome 76-83, Safari 12-14 and Edge 79-83). This means visitors who access your site from these browsers don't see the gaps you set between layouter items.

### Resizing layouter items

Your layouter items don't have to be equal in size. You can resize individual items to make them larger or smaller than the rest. From the [Inspector panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-a-guided-tour#the-inspector-1), adjust each item's width and height using the [measurements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-units-of-measure) of your choice. You can also adjust the horizontal and vertical margins around items.

---

### Reordering layouter items

Rearrange the content inside your layouter by switching the item order. Using the right-click menu you can move items back and forth – the layouter automatically adjusts to accommodate your changes.

#### To reorder layouter items:

Right-click the item you want to move.  Hover over **Switch Order**.  Select **With next item** / **With previous item**.
