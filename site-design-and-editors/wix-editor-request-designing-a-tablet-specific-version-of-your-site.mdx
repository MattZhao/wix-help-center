---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Designing a Tablet-Specific Version of Your Site"
---

Wix Editor lets you create a site that's optimized for both desktop and [mobile devices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-the-mobile-editor). Currently, it is not possible to edit your site specifically for tablets.

However, there are ways you can optimize the desktop version of your site for tablets, such as keeping elements within the gridlines, and using columns and repeaters. [Learn more about aligning and formatting your site for tablets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-ipad-and-tablet-live-site-issues)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
