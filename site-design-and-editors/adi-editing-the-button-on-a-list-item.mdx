---
title: "ADI: Editing the Button on a List Item"
---

Some Sections such as Team Members, Service List and Clients include list items. Sometimes there is the option to include a button on the list items.

### To edit the button on a list item:

Click the section of your site inside ADI that contains the list.Click **Edit**.Hover over the list item that you want to edit.Click the **Settings **icon.Click **Edit**.Enter the text for your button in the **Button Text **field.Click the **Link **field.Select the link for your button.Click **Done**.(Optional) [Edit the style of your button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-style-of-your-button).

> **Tip:**
>
> - If you do not see the button part of the list item, then that item cannot contain a button.
> - You may need to unhide the button fields by clicking the **Hide **icon.
