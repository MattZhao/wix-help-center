---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Adding a Cover Image to Video Backgrounds on Mobile"
---

Currently, you cannot add a cover image to video backgrounds displayed on your mobile-friendly site. The first frame of the video is displayed by default.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
