---
title: "Site Performance Request: Pre-Loading Specific Elements"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the order in which elements load on your site, or enable specific elements to be pre-loaded.

Site elements automatically load based on their weight and resolution. For example, static or low-resolution elements load faster than high-resolution or interactive elements. [Learn more about loading order.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-performance-designing-a-performance-focused-website#element-loading-order)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
