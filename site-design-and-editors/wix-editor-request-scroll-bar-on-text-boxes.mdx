---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Scroll Bar on Text Boxes"
---

Currently, Wix does not support adding a scroll bar to text boxes due to their impact on site design and function.

### Tell me more

Scroll bars in text boxes are not supported due to the following reasons:
- It isn't advised in modern web design.
- They do not work well on touch devices (such as iPads and iPhones).
- It is advisable to have only one scroll bar on your site, and it should be the site scroll-bar.

As an alternative, you can add CSS to a [HTML element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-embeds-and-codes-on-your-site) on your site. [See tutorial available at w3schools](https://www.w3schools.com/cssref/tryit.php?filename=trycss_overflow).

> **You should know:**
>
> - We are unable to provide support for external HTML codes that are added to Wix sites as the codes have not been tested by Wix.
> - If your site content is longer than your site visitor's viewport height, the browser automatically adds vertical scrollbars.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
