---
title: "Request: Tracking Clicks on Your Site Buttons and Links"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now track visitor clicks on your site buttons and links with two new Wix Analytics Behavior reports.

The **Button Clicks **report shows you which buttons, links and clickable images are clicked the most on your site.  The **Button Clicks over time** report allows you to track clicks over a specific time period. See what buttons or clickable images on your site are performing best and how it changes over time.

[Learn more Wix Analytics Behavior reports](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-about-your-behavior-reports#types-of-behavior-reports).

### How do I access the new reports?

1. [Go to All Reports](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Analytics%20Reports&actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fanalytics/reports/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to the **Behavior** section.
3. Select the **Button Clicks** or **Button Clicks over time** report.
