---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Color of the Drop-Down Arrow on the Mobile Menu"
---

Sites that have subpages display a drop-down arrow on the mobile menu that opens the subpage menu. If you can't see your subpages or the drop-down arrow, then it's usually because the color of the arrow is the same color as the mobile menu box.

To fix this, you need to change the color of the drop-down arrow.

### To change the color of the drop-down arrow:

Go to your[ mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).   Click the mobile menu.  Click **Edit Menu**.  Click the menu.  Click the **Design** icon .  Click **Fill Color & Opacity**.  Click the color box under **Arrow** and select a new color.
