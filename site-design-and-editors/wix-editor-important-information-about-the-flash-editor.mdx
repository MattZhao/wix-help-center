---
title: "Wix Editor: Important Information About the Flash Editor"
---

At the beginning of 2021, Adobe [ended support of Flash technology](https://www.adobe.com/il_en/products/flashplayer/end-of-life.html). This affects all websites built with this technology, including the Flash site you've created on Wix. Any sites built on Flash have been deleted, and visitors aren't able to access them anymore. Additionally, the Flash editor is no longer supported.

**Click an option below to learn more about this change and what it means for your Flash site. **

### Can I bring my Flash site back online?

The sites have been removed from Wix. This means that they have been completely deleted, and cannot be restored.
You can build a new site with the Wix Editor. The new editor offers better performance for your visitors and tons of new design features, layout options and business apps.
Depending on your needs, you can:
- [Create a new HTML site using a pre-designed template](http://www.wix.com/website/templates)
- [Create a new HTML site from scratch](http://www.wix.com/website/templates/html/blank/1)

### Can I use the Flash Editor?

No. Since the beginning of 2021,** **you are no longer able to access the Flash editor at all, including the dashboard of Flash sites.

### Do visitors still have access to my Flash site?

Flash was disabled on all browsers. This means that visitors cannot view the Flash site that you created with Wix.

### Can I still see files from my Flash site in the Media Manager?

No. At the beginning of 2021, your site's files were removed from Wix. 
**Tip:** After creating a new Wix site, you can easily [upload all of your files](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-uploading-media-to-the-media-manager) in one go.
