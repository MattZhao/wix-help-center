Welcome to the [Discover page](https://manage.wix.com/studio/discover) in Wix Studio, a hub of exploration and learning. It's your ultimate destination for inspiration and education related to Wix Studio and the Studio Editor.

The Discover page includes interactive Sandboxes, that provide an engaging space where you can practice and experiment with the functionalities of the Studio Editor, along with a wealth of video tutorials to help you become more proficient in Wix Studio.

Learn more about the different sections of the Discover page below.

---

### Explore Wix Studio tutorials

Unlock the full potential of the Wix Studio with our comprehensive video tutorials.

> [Check out all of our Wix Studio video tutorials.](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy/library)

---

### Follow the latest updates

Stay in the loop and be at the forefront of innovation with all the latest in Wix Studio. Keep up to date with the latest Studio events and exciting feature releases.

> [Check out the News Center](https://manage.wix.com/studio/discover?uc=main&ucTab=NEW_FEATURE). You can also access the News Center by clicking the **News Center **icon  at the top of your workspace, then **View All**.

---

### Play around with the Studio Editor

Welcome to the Wix Studio Sandboxes, where you can fully explore the features of the Studio Editor. This hands-on experience teaches you how to become proficient with the Studio Editor, unlocking your creative potential, and gaining a deep understanding of its functionality.

---

### Get an insider view of the platform

Check out short clips that showcase various aspects of the platform from our Studio Insiders—creators who actively use Wix Studio. Discover tips and tricks, get a taste of the product, and see firsthand how to maximize your creative potential with Wix Studio.

> Explore our guided and interactive learning resources in the [Wix Studio Academy](https://www.wix.com/studio/academy).
