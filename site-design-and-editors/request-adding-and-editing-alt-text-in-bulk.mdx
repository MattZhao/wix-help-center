---
title: "Request: Adding and Editing Alt Text in Bulk"
---

Currently, you can only [individually add / edit alt text for images inside your Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/optimizing-your-images-and-galleries-for-seo-alt-text). It is not possible to bulk add or edit alt text from your Editor or site dashboard for all your images on your site.

> **Update:**
>
> It is now possible to bulk add / edit alt text for Wix Bookings, Online Programs, and Portfolio from your dashboard with the [SEO Assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/improving-your-pages-seo-with-the-wix-seo-assistant) panel.
> You can also bulk add / edit alt text to static pages using the [SEO Setup Checklist](https://support.wix.com/en/article/improving-your-online-presence-with-the-wix-seo-setup-checklist) in your dashboard or [Accessibility Wizard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-using-the-accessibility-wizard-in-the-wix-editor) in Wix Editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
