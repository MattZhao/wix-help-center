---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Pinning Floating Panels in the Editor"
---

When customizing elements in the editor, you'll notice many of our panels float (i.e. you can move them around to work more conveniently). Currently, it is not possible to change this behavior and set these panels to open in a specific location with no way of moving them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
