---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Aligning Text Boxes in Repeaters"
---

Currently, it is not possible for text boxes to automatically align across your repeaters. If one of the items contains more text than in other items, the text boxes may become misaligned.

However, you can manually align the text by expanding text boxes in the items that have less text, so that the boxes are the same size.

### To expand a text box:

Click the text box with less text in the repeater item.  Drag the handles on the text box to the same size as the text box with more text.
