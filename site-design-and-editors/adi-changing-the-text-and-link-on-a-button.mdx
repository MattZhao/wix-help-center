---
title: "ADI: Changing the Text and Link on a Button"
---

You can choose what the buttons on your site say and where they link to.

### To edit a button:

Click the page section that contains the button inside ADI.Click **Edit**.Enter the text for your button in the **Button Text **field.Click **Edit Link** in the **Link **field.Select the link for your button.Click **Done**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn how to [customize your button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-style-of-your-button).
