Currently, you can customize the [background colors, fonts, and button styles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-changing-the-design-colors-and-fonts-of-your-wix-music-player) of your Wix Music Player, but you cannot change the button text.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
