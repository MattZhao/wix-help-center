---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Navigation Breadcrumbs"
---

> **Note:**
>
> This feature is not yet available to all users.

We are delighted to announce that you can now add breadcrumbs to your site with Velo Dev Mode enabled.

Breadcrumbs are a handy and easy way to let visitors explore and navigate your site. You can connect them to your menu, and choose what to display on your trail. [Learn how to add and set up breadcrumbs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-breadcrumbs).
