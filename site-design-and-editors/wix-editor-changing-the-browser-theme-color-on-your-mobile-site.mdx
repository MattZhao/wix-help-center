---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Browser Theme Color on Your Mobile Site"
---

Use the Mobile Tools panel to set a browser theme color on your site. This is a great way to personalize the experience for mobile visitors using Chrome.

> **Important:**
>
> Due to browser restrictions, this feature is only available on the Chrome browser on an Android device.

### To change the browser theme color:

Click **Mobile Tools**  on the left side of your [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).  Click the **Add to Site** icon  next to **Browser theme color**.  Click the color box in the panel.   Select the color you want.  Click **Done**.

> **See it in action:**
>
> To see how it looks, visit your published site on an Android device in Google Chrome.
