---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Vertical Sections"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add vertical sections to sites in the Studio Editor.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can [use a pinned container](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-a-container-to-create-a-vertical-section) to achieve a similar effect, making it look just like a sidebar section.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
