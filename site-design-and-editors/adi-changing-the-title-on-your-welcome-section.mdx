---
title: "ADI: Changing the Title on Your Welcome Section"
---

Most sites have a welcome section. The welcome section usually displays your business name and tagline, however you can change it if you wish.

### To change the title on your welcome section:

Click the welcome section of your site inside ADI.Click **Edit**.Enter the title of your welcome section in the **Title **field.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-your-site-title) to learn how to change the title of your site.
