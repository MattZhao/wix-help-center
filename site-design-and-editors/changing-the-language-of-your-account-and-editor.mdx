The language of your dashboard/workspace and site editor is determined by the language of your Wix account. You can change the language of your Wix account by following the steps below.

### Changing the language of your account and editor

Once you change the language of your Wix account, your editor will also be displayed in that language.

WixWix Studio
1. [Sign in](http://www.wix.com/) to your Wix account.
2. Click the user panel at the top right.
3. Click **Account Settings**.
4. Click the **Account language** drop-down and select a language.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about languages at Wix.

### What languages are available?

Currently, you can use Wix and Wix Studio, both your account and editor, in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Polish, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, Chinese, Thai, Swedish, Norwegian, Dutch, Danish, Czech, Bahasa Indonesia, Vietnamese, and Ukrainian.
**Note: **From January 2024, Hindi will no longer be supported.

### Does changing the language affect my live site?

No. The language you set in your account determines the language of your dashboard/workspace, editor and communications. 
If you want to change the language of your live site, you need to update the content, or use [Wix Multilingual ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-an-overview)to display your site in more than one language.

### Do you have website templates on all languages?

Currently, we have templates in only some languages. However, as you can customize all aspects of our templates, you can choose a template in another language and just edit the text as needed.

### Can I use special characters when writing the content for my site?

The editors support all languages and special characters that are supported by [UTF8](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UTF-8), which is a dominant character encoding for the Internet. This includes Chinese, Japanese, Thai, Arabic, Korean, Greek, Russian, Portuguese, and Hebrew, as well as several others.
No setup or configuration is required to use these languages, as the editors support whatever language you have installed on your computer's operating system. Simply ensure that your computer supports the language you want, and type wherever text is editable on your site.

### What languages does the Wix Help Center support?

The Wix Help Center is available in English, Dutch, French, German, Hebrew, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish and Ukrainian. Learn how to [change the language of the Help Center](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-languages-in-the-wix-help-center).
Wix offers phone support in English, Dutch, French, German, Hebrew, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish, and Turkish. Learn more about [contacting Wix Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support).
