### Adding Wix Video

Adding Wix Video directly from the Wix App Market will add a video widget where you can fully customize the settings to suit your needs.

#### To add Wix Video to your site:

Click the **App Panel** icon  on the top bar of Editor X.  Type "Wix Video" in the search bar and select **Wix Video** from the drop-down menu.  Click **Add to Site**.  Click **OK**.

> **Wix Video is also good for business.**
>
> Individual videos can be purchased or rented, and whole channels can be made accessible to subscribers only. Learn more about [selling and renting videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-selling-and-renting-videos).

---

### Adding Wix Video Elements

You can also add individual video elements to your site from the Add Elements Panel. Here you can add single video players, video boxes and transparent video elements.

#### To add video elements to your site:

Go to the page where you want to add the element.   Click **Add Elements **icon  on the top bar of Editor X.  Click **Media**.  Click on the element you want to add.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can access the Media Manager in the **Upload Media** section. If you have not yet uploaded media files to your Media Manager, click the **Upload** icon  to upload new files. If you have already uploaded some files click **Show more** to take you directly to the Media Manager.
