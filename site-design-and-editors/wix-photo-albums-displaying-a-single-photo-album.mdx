---
title: "Wix Photo Albums: Displaying a Single Photo Album"
---

Wix Photo Albums automatically displays all of your albums together. Currently, there is no layout for displaying an individual album within the app. In order to showcase an individual album, create a clickable image that drives your visitors to the album.

> **First, two quick introductions:**
>
> 1. Each photo album you create exists as its own site. To have an album appear by itself, you'll need to retrieve that site's URL.
> 2. Wix Photo Albums has its own media manager that is separate to the Editor's Media Manager. For this reason, it is not possible to add images from your Editor's Media Manager to your new Photo Albums. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-about-the-photo-albums-media-manager)

### Step 1: Retrieve your album's URL

1. In the **Editor, **click on the Photo Albums and then click **Manage Albums.**
2. Click the More Actions icon  next to the album you would like to display and click **View Album.**
3. The album site opens in a new tab. Copy the URL in the address bar.

### Step 2: Create a clickable "cover"

1. In the **Editor**, click the **Add **button.
2. Click **Image **in the tab on the left.
3. Click **My Image Uploads.**
4. Select the photo that you want to serve as the album's "cover".

1. Click [here ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upload-images-to-your-media-manager)to upload the image to your Media Manager.
5. Once the image appears, click on the image and click the **Hyperlink** button.
6. Select **Web Address** from the options on the left.
7. Enter the address of the album site.
8. Under **How does it open? **select:

- **New window**: The album site opens either in a new tab or window, depending on your browser settings.
- **Current window**: Direct your visitors to the album site on the same tab.

If you want to direct your visitors to the album through a button instead of a cover image, follow the steps above to retrieve the album URL and then connect it to a button

### Create an album button

1. In the Editor, click the **Add **button.
2. Click **Button.**
3. Select the button you want to use.
4. Click [here ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-the-design-of-your-buttons#designing-your-button-1)to design the button.
5. Once the button appears.
6. Click the **Hyperlink** button.
7. Select **Web Address** from the options on the left.
8. Enter the address of the album site.
9. Under **How does it open? **select:

- **New window**: The album site opens either in a new tab or window, depending on your browser settings.
- **Current window**: Direct your visitors to the album site on the same tab.
