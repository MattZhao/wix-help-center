---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Adding Proofing to Old Photo Albums"
---

Currently, Wix Photo Albums allows Proofing (favorites lists) to be added to newly created albums. However, it is not possible to add Proofing to old photo albums.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
