---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Changing Your Mobile Home Page"
---

Currently, you cannot change the home page on your mobile site. The home page on your desktop site is the same for your mobile site.

In the meantime, you can use Velo by Wix to do this. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/velo-tutorial-redirecting-mobile-visitors)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
