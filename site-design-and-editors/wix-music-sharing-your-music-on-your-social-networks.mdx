---
title: "Wix Music: Sharing Your Music on Your Social Networks"
---

The Wix Music player comes with social share icons for Facebook, Twitter, Blogger and Tumblr.

### To share an album, a single or a playlist:

Access your live site.Hover over **Share Album**, **Share Single** or **Share Playlist** in the Music Player. Select a social network.

### To share a track:

Access your live site.Hover over the **Share** icon in the relevant track.Select a social network.

> **Note:**
>
> - You can enable or disable music sharing from [Wix Music settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-music-changing-the-settings-of-your-wix-music-player).
> - Sharing your music shares a link to the page of your site that contains the Wix Music player.
