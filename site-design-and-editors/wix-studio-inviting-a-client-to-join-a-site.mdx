When you've finished a site and it's ready for your client, you can invite them to be a collaborator. You can choose what access they have, and decide if they can make changes to the site itself and the site's dashboard.

### Tell me more about collaborators in Wix Studio

There are 2 types of collaborators you can have on a site:
- **Teammates: **These are added to the [Team tab in the workspace](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/team?referralInfo=sidebar) by the team owner. They are the teammates that you work with on your projects, and they have access to your team workspace. Learn how to [manage your team.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-managing-your-team)
- **External site collaborators:** These are other professionals you work with that are not part of your team, such as freelance designers, or clients that you invite to their own site once it's ready. You give them access to one specific site, and they cannot access your workspace.

For both teammates and external collaborators, you can decide what roles and permissions they have on sites and in the workspace. You can also choose the mode they can access in the Studio Editor.

### Inviting a client to join your site

Invite a client to join your site by sending them an invitation from your workspace. You can choose which role you want them to have or [create a custom role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators).

> **Note:**
>
> Your clients cannot duplicate a site built with the Studio Editor unless they also use Wix Studio.

#### To send an invitation to a client:

[Go to Sites](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/sites?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Hover over the **Collaborators** icon on the relevant site.   Click **Invite People**.

Enter your client’s email address.  Select the checkbox next to the relevant role/s.   Click **Send Invite**.  In the pop-up that appears, select **Client** and click **Done**. This collaborator will be marked as "CLIENT" in your Roles & Permissions table. The invitation email they receive and landing page they reach will be tailored to the client experience.

> **Ready to hand over a site to a client?**
>
> With Wix Studio, you can enhance your client handover process by creating a [personalized client kit](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-creating-a-client-kit-5293562).

### Checking the status of an invitation

To find out if a client has accepted their invitation, go to [Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in the site's dashboard. If there is a "PENDING" label next to their name, they have not yet accepted your invite.

### Resending an invitation

You can resend a client invitation at any time. You can choose to resend the invitation via email through Wix or to copy a link to the invitation and send it directly to your client.

#### To resend an invitation:

[Go to Sites](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/sites?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Hover over the **Collaborators** icon on the relevant site.  Click **Manage Collaborators**.  Hover over the relevant person and click the **More Actions**  icon.  Choose how you want to resend the invitation:

- **Send it through Wix:** Click **Resend Invite**. Wix will resend the invitation to the email address that you entered.

**Tip: **Ask your client to check their spam folder if they don't see it.
- **Send it yourself:** Click **Get Invite Link**. The link is automatically copied to your clipboard. Paste it in an email or chat message to your client.

**Note: **This feature is currently not available in Safari.

### Allowing clients to use the Editor

Once a client accepts your invite, they can access their site in the Studio Editor. They see a [specific mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-understanding-how-clients-edit-their-site) that lets them change content, but not the design that you created. If their role only includes the **Edit Content** permission, this is the only mode they can access.

If your client has a role with more permissions, like Co-Owner, they can also access the **Full Mode**. This mode lets them open the Studio Editor, the same way you see it. However, the actions they're allowed to perform still depend on the role you assign.

> Learn more about how [clients can use the Studio Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-understanding-how-clients-edit-their-site).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about inviting clients.

### What do clients see when they're invited to collaborate on a site?

After you send an invitation, your client receives the following email. When they click **Accept Invite,** they'll be prompted to log into Wix. If they don't yet have an account, they can create one.

### What role should I give my client?

Choose the role that best suits what your client will be doing. For example, if you want them to have full access to all aspects of the site and dashboard, you can choose the (Admin) Co-Owner role.
Learn more about the [predefined collaborator roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview#general-roles-for-site-management) or how to [create your own custom role](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-creating-and-managing-custom-roles-for-collaborators).

### Can clients invite other people to work on my site?

The Admin (Co-Owner) role allows clients to invite additional collaborators to work on the site.

### I sent an invitation to the wrong email address, what should I do?

If you sent an invitation to the wrong email address, you can remove the collaborator to revoke access. Then, you can send a new invite to your client's correct email address.

1. [Go to Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the relevant person and click the **More Actions**  icon.
3. Click **Remove.**
4. Click **Remove** to confirm.
Then simply send a new invitation to the correct email address.
