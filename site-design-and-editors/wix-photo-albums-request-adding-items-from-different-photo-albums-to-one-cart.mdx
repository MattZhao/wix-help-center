---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Request: Adding Items from Different Photo Albums to One Cart"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add items from different Photo Albums to one cart. Photo Albums function as separate sites, and so they each have their own separate carts and checkouts.

> **Tips:**
>
> - The Wix Photo Albums app has been deprecated. However, you can sell your digital prints using [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores). When you add products (e.g. images for sale) to your store, your customers can pick and choose what they want to buy.
> - If you want to display your images, add a [Wix Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-adding-and-setting-up-your-gallery) to your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
