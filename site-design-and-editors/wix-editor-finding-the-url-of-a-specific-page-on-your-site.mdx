---
title: "Wix Editor: Finding the URL of a Specific Page on Your Site"
---

If needed, you can always view your page's URL directly from the Pages panel.

### To find the URL of a specific page:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click **Pages & Menu ** the left side of the editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the **More Actions** icon .  Click** SEO Basics**.  Find the page's URL under **Preview on Google**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [change the page name appearing in the URL ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-page-url)on the **Page Info** tab.
