title: "Wix Editor: Finding Your Mobile Menu in the Mobile Editor"
---

When you create your website on desktop, your mobile site is automatically created for you with the same content and elements.

If the desktop version of your site contains a menu, the menu is also automatically added to your mobile friendly site.

**In this article, learn more about: **

[Finding the menu in the mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-finding-your-mobile-menu-in-the-mobile-editor#finding-your-menu-in-the-mobile-editor)  [Next steps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-finding-your-mobile-menu-in-the-mobile-editor#next-steps)

### Finding your menu in the mobile editor

If you don't see the mobile menu inside your mobile editor, follow our recommendations below to rule out any potential reasons and find your menu. Click an option below to learn more.

### Check the menu icon

It is currently not possible to have more than one menu on your mobile site. If you have added multiple menus in the desktop editor, or have created a popup menu, it will automatically become a hamburger menu on the mobile version of your site.
You can find and edit your mobile menu by following the below steps:

1. Click the Mobile Menu icon in your [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).
2. Click **Button**.
3. Click **Edit Menu**.

### Check your hidden elements

The menu may have been hidden from your mobile site.You can check by clicking the **Hidden on Mobile **icon  on the left side of the mobile editor. If the menu is listed, you can unhide it by clicking the **Show Element **icon .

Learn more about hidden elements in the mobile editor [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hidden-elements-in-your-mobile-editor).

### Edit the design of your menu

In some cases the mobile menu may be the same color as your background. Use Ctrl + M (or Cmd + M on a Mac) to cycle through the elements on the page. Once you locate the menu, you can edit the design of the mobile menu.

Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-mobile-menu) to learn how to customize your mobile menu.

### Check for overlapping elements

Elements may occasionally overlap on the mobile version of your site, and can block your mobile menu icon. You can look for the menu by checking other elements to see if they're overlapping the menu.

1. Right-click an element in the editor.
2. Hover over **Overlapping Items.**
3. Click the menu from the list.

### Use the layers panel

You can use the layers panel in your mobile editor to locate and spotlight elements on your page. 

1. Click the **Layers** icon   on the bottom left of your [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).
2. Click **Button**.
3. Select **Header**, **Page** or **Footer**.
4. Click the menu from the list.

**Note:** If the menu is not highlighted on your editor when you select it from the list, it is outside the visible part of the editor. In order to get it back to the visible part of the editor, you can change its position by adjusting the X or Y axis in the [toolbar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-using-the-editor-toolbar-to-adjust-elements).

### Check your page layout

If you have adjusted your page layout to hide the header and footer, the menu is not displayed on your mobile site.

Change the layout settings to display the header and footer on your mobile site and display the menu by following the steps below:

1. Click **Menus &** **Pages ** on the left side of your [mobile editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-getting-started-with-the-mobile-editor).
2. Click **Button**.
3. Hover over a page name and click the **Show More **icon .
4. Click **Mobile Settings**.
5. Select the **Standard** page layout.
