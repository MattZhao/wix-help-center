---
title: "Wix Audio Request: Accessing the Music Library Using the Wix App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to access your music library from the Wix app on a mobile device. To access your music library on your desktop, click [here](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20My%20Music&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/music/).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
