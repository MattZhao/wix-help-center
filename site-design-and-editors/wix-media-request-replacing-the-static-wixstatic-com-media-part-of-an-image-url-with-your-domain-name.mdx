---
title: "Wix Media Request: Replacing the static.wixstatic.com/media Part of an Image URL with Your Domain Name"
---

You may notice that **static.wixstatic.com/media** appears in the URL of an image. This appears, for example, when you right-click an image and open in it in a new tab.

Currently, it is not possible to replace the static.wixstatic.com/media part of an image URL with your own domain name. Static.wixstatic.com/media is the server where Wix hosts all its images. It allows us to deliver these images to you and your users quickly and efficiently.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
