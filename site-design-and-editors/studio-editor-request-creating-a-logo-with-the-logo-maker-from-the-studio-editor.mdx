---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Creating a Logo with the Logo Maker from the Studio Editor"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create a logo using the Logo Maker directly from the Wix Studio Editor, like you can in the Wix Editor.

> To create a logo, you need to go to [Logo & Brand](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/brand-maker/brand-studio?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Logo+and+Brand) in your site's dashboard and then upload the logo as an image in the Studio Editor.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
