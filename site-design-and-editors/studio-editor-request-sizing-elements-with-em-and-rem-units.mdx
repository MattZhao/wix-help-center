Currently, em and rem are not available as size units you can use in the Studio Editor.

> [Em and rem (root em)](https://blog.logrocket.com/using-em-vs-rem-css/#what-em-rem-why-use) are scalable sizing units that are dependent on font sizes of parent and HTML units respectively,

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
