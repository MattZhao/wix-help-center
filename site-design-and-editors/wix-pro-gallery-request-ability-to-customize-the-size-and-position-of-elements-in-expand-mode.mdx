---
title: "Wix Pro Gallery Request: Ability to Customize the Size and Position of Elements in Expand Mode"
---

Currently, it is not possible to adjust the size or placement of elements such as text boxes and gallery items in the Expand Mode of your gallery. However, the dimensions of gallery items are responsive, meaning the size of gallery items increase or decrease depending on the size of your visitors' screen/browser.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
