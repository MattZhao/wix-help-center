---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Applying Site Styles to Elements With Existing Overrides"
---

When updating [site styles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-your-site-styles), the changes won't affect elements with existing design overrides. Currently, there's no option to "reset" all these elements simultaneously and apply the updated styles, disregarding the overrides.

However, you can [reset individual text elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-managing-your-site-styles#applying-a-style-to-a-text-element) with overrides from the **Theme** drop-down in the Inspector panel.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
