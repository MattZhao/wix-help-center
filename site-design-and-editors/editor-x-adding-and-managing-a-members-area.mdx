The Members Area is a tool that enables your site members to have their own account on your site, thereby improving the experience you can provide to your users. Members can log in, view members-only pages and access their account settings. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area)

**With Editor X, all the Members Area features have been designed to be fully responsive, so that they look great on every screen size!**

#### Learn more about:

[Adding a Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-managing-a-members-area#adding-a-members-area)  [The login bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-managing-a-members-area#the-login-bar)  [The signup and login forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-managing-a-members-area#the-signup-and-login-forms)  [Member-exclusive pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-managing-a-members-area#member-pages)  [Deleting the Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-managing-a-members-area#deleting-the-members-area)

> **Note:**
>
> Some Wix apps such as Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Blog automatically add the Members Area to your site. However, you can also use a Members Area without these.

### Adding a Members Area

Head over to the Add panel to add a Members Area to your site. Once you do, we'll add a login bar, basic member pages and signup and login forms to help you get started.

### Show me how

1. Click **Add**  on the top of Editor X.
2. Click **Members**.
3. Click **Add to Site**.

---

### The Login Bar

The Login Bar appears in your site's default master header so members can sign up, log in or out from any page of your site. You can move the element wherever you need and adjust its design and layout.

### Show me how

1. Select the login bar on your header.
2. Do the following to customize it:

- Click **Set Up Login Bar** to change its text and choose what info to show (profile pic, member's name or both).
- Click the **Layouts** icon  to adjust the icon size and choose an arrangement: Left to Right or Right to Left.
- Click the **Design** icon  to design the log in bar for three different states: Regular, Hover and Selected. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-customize-the-login-bar-and-menu-7934908)

### The Signup and Login Forms

There are 2 types of default forms that come with the Members Area: One that lets new members sign up, and another that allows existing members to log in.

Click below to learn more about each form:

### Signup Form

This form is displayed when a site visitor wants to sign up to become a member of your site. They can sign up with their Facebook or Google accounts, or enter an email address and password manually. 
The fonts and colors appearing on this page are pulled directly from the [Theme Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-managing-your-site-styles) so it matches your site's design.
**Using Velo?**You can [create a custom signup form](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites/articles/code-tutorials/wix-members/creating-a-custom-registration-form-with-code) that opens as a pop-up using lightboxes.

### Login Form

This form is displayed when an existing member wants to log in to your site. 
Depending on how your members signed up, they can use Facebook, Google or their credentials to log in. 
The fonts and colors appearing on this page are pulled directly from the [Theme Manager](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-managing-your-site-styles) so it matches your site's design.

### Member Pages

Once logged in, a member has access to all of the member pages. Each member has a "My Account" page where they can add their personal details. These details are automatically added to your Contacts.

Other available member pages depend on the features you have on your site. For example, if you've added a store to your site, members can enjoy a personalized shopping experience with pages like "My Orders", "My Addresses" and "My Wallet".

> **Tip:**
>
> From the Pages panel, you can add pages of your own with member-exclusive content. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-managing-your-pages#adding-a-page)

---

### Deleting the Members Area

You can delete your Members Area at any time. This removes all of your member pages, member profiles, and login bar from your site.

### Show me how

1. Click **Pages**  at the top of Editor X.
2. Hover over **Wix Members Area** and click the **More Actions** icon .
3. Click **Delete Members Area**.
4. Click **Yes, Delete**.
