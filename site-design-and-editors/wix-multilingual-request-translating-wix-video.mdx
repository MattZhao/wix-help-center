---
title: "Wix Multilingual Request: Translating Wix Video"
---

Currently, it is not possible to translate Wix Video with Wix Multilingual.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can display different videos per language version on your site using the Single Video Player. These videos cannot be monetized. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/single-video-player-adding-and-setting-up-the-single-video-player).

> **What content can be translated with Wix Multilingual?**
>
> See the list of [Translatable Content in Wix Multilingual](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translatable-content).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
