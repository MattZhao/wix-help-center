---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Setting the Offset in Px* for Sticky Elements and Sections"
---

When working with [sticky elements or sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-creating-sticky-elements-and-sections), you can enter the offset value in px, percentage (%), vh or vw. Currently, it is not possible to set the offset in px*.

> The [px* size unit](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-setting-the-size-of-your-elements#setting-the-size-in-px) is available in many other settings in the Inspector, like margins and docking.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
