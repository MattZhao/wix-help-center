title: "Request: Site Searching Wix Video"
---

While Wix’s Site Search [searches your website](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-site-search), currently it cannot search Wix Video, along with anything that is connected to Wix Video, such as, channels, titles, description, tags, and cost.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
