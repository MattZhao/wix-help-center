---
title: "Request: Full Customization of the Cookie Banner in the Wix Editor"
---

Currently, there are a few ways to [customize your Wix cookie banner](https://support.wix.com/en/article/displaying-a-cookie-banner-on-your-site#step-2-customize-your-banners-look-texts), such as changing the text and color theme. Additional options, such as customizing the banner in your editor, modifying the size of the banner, or changing the banner location on the screen are unavailable at present.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
