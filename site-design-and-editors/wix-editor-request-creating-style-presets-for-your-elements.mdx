---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Creating Style Presets for Your Elements"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create style classes that let you define the same design settings for all relevant elements. For example, applying the same design to every image or button on your site as they belong to that specific class.

Instead, you can use the following features to make your design process quicker and more efficient:

You can [copy and paste the design of an element](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-working-with-elements#copying-and-pasting-elements).   You can [save single or grouped elements to your library](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-saving-and-reusing-customized-elements-my-designs) and reuse them on your site, or a different site on your Wix account.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
