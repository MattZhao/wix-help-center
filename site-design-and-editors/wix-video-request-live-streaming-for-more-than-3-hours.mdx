---
title: "Wix Video Request: Live Streaming for More than 3 Hours"
---

Currently, the maximum time for [live-streaming](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-live-streaming-in-wix-video) is capped at 3 hours. It is not possible to stream longer for than this time.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
