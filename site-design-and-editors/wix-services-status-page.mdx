---
title: "Wix Services Status Page"
---

The Wix Services Status page lets you view the health and availability of all Wix Services in one place. If an outage occurs, you'll be able to see it on this page in (almost) real time.

If your site's not loading, or you're having trouble with one of our services (editor, apps etc.), it may be related to a system wide issue that we're experiencing. Check below to see the status of the service you're having trouble with, or click to go directly to the [Status Page](https://status.wix.com/).

> **Note:**
>
> If the box below says "Everything is working" and you're still having trouble, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-technical-assistant) for the Wix Technical Assistant.
