---
title: "Wix Photo Albums: Sharing Your Album"
---

After [publishing your photo album](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-publishing-your-album), spread the word and share it with your social networks, like Facebook and X (Twitter), or, send an email. You can also copy the album link and share it with your clients, friends and family.

### To share your photo album:

[Go to Photo Albums](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Photo%20Albums&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fphotography-albums/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More actions icon  next to the relevant album.  Select **Share **from the drop-down menu.

Share your album in one or more of the following ways:

- Copy the album link and send it to your clients or friends.
- Click **Share on Facebook**  to share your album on Facebook.
- Click **Post on** **Twitter**  to share your album on X (Twitter).
- Click **Send an Email**  to share your album via email.

> **Notes:**
>
> - You can only share published photo albums.
> - Currently, it's only possible to share your photo albums on Facebook, X (Twitter) and through email campaigns. If you would like to see more sharing capabilities in the future, please click **Vote for this feature** in [this article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-request-additional-social-sharing-options) and we'll make sure to keep you updated.
