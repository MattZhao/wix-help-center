### Choosing a new page design

Update each page of your site individually with sophisticated, pre-designed page layouts. You can browse and change your page's layout at any time.

#### To change your page design:

Click **Design **on the top bar inside ADI.Click **Page Designs**.Select a different page design.

> **Did you know?**
>
> - In addition to the page layout, you can also [change the layout of a section](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-changing-the-layout-of-a-section) and its [overall design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-section-design), including fonts and colors, by clicking **Design **on the top bar.
> - For helpful web design ideas, you can check out our [website design guide](https://www.wix.com/website/design).

### Changing the site's theme

Your site's theme is made up of a color story, layout, and fonts. You can completely change the look and feel of your site by choosing a new theme that suits your brand.

#### To change your site's theme:

Click **Design **on the top bar inside ADI.  Click **Themes.**  ﻿Click a theme to apply it to your site.

> **Note:**
>
> Changing your site's theme will affect your entire site. If you want to customize just a section of your site, follow the steps [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-section-design).

### Customizing your site's colors

ADI creates a range of color palettes for you to choose from, making it even easier to design your site. You can customize the palettes to your liking, and even create your own.

> **Note:**
>
> Changes you make to a color palette are applied to your whole site.

#### To customize your site colors:

Click **Design **on the top bar inside ADI.  Select **Colors**.   Choose what you want to do. You can select an option below to learn more:

- [Change your site palette](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors#changing-your-color-palette)
- [Update the palette's main color](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors#changing-the-main-color-in-your-palette)
- [Customize the color settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors#customizing-your-color-settings)
- [Create a custom color palette](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-customizing-your-sites-colors#creating-your-own-color-palette)

### Adding animation to your site

Create a stunning and eye-catching site by adding animation. You can even add a transition effect that plays when visitors click through your pages.

> **Note:**
>
> - Site animations are added to your entire site. However, you can [change a section animation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adi-section-animation) separately to further customize your pages.
> - Elements receive different forms of animation, according to their placement and function.

#### To add animation to your site:

Click **Design** in the top bar in ADI.  Select **Animation**.    Select an animation type.   Click the drop-down menu to select a **Page Transition**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Click **Preview** to see how your site animation looks.
