Your brand serves to identify and represent your business. A strong brand links your identity across your logo, business cards, and marketing materials. Audiences recognize your company through your branding.

### Creating branded materials

With the [Wix Logo Maker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-logo-with-the-wix-logo-maker-571523) you can create a range of branded materials to kick-start your brand identity. Create a logo, design business cards, send email campaigns, and design social posts all from the same place.

---

### Purchasing a logo and branded materials

Once you've [created your logo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-creating-a-logo-with-the-wix-logo-maker-571523), purchase a plan that lets you download, use it on your site, and in marketing materials. We offer different [Logo Maker plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans). Some plans only include high-quality logo files, while others also come with your very own branded site and unlimited access to the Logo Maker tools.

---

### Editing your logo

After creating your logo, you may want to go back and make some changes. Using the Wix Logo Maker, you can edit an existing logo and change whatever you need to make it suit your brand – the fonts, colors, symbols, images and more.

---

### File types in the Wix Logo Maker

The Wix Logo Maker provides you with all the file types you need for your business - from logo files to business cards and social post files. You'll receive a different range of files depending on the [plan you choose](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-logo-about-the-wix-logo-maker-plans).
