Let visitors find anything on your site using Site Search. This app adds a search bar and a dedicated search results page so visitors can locate the information they need with ease.

Once visitors enter a term, Site Search looks through your site pages, store products, blog posts and much more, improving the overall discoverability of your content.

### Show me how to add Site Search

1. Click **Add  ** on the top of Editor X.
2. Click **Menu and Search **on the left.
3. Select **Site Search**.
4. Click the Add to Site icon  next to **Site Search** to add the app.

[Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-setting-up-site-search)

### The Search Bar

Visitors use the search bar to locate content on your site. From the Add panel, you can choose from several designs and add the one you like to the page. We recommend placing the search bar in the header so it's always noticeable.

You can control everything about the search bar, from the placeholder text to its sizing, position and design. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-setting-up-site-search#step-2-customize-the-search-bar)

#### Enabling Quick Search

The search bar also features a Quick Search option which lets visitors see relevant results on the spot. As they enter a term, visitors see the results directly below the search bar, and click **Search All** at the bottom to see the full list.

---

### The Search Results Page

The Search Results page shows the relevant results for the visitor's query. For absolute convenience, the list is organized by different categories (e.g., Pages, Products, Posts) based on your site's content.

The colors and fonts on this page are pulled from your site's [themes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-managing-your-site-styles), but you can always customize them to your exact needs. You can change settings like the page's title, number of results per page and more. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-adding-and-setting-up-site-search#step-3-customize-the-search-results-page)

### Understanding the Search Results Thumbnails

The thumbnail images that appear in your search results page work differently depending on if it's a static page or a dynamic page.

### Static page images

Your static pages can be found in the **Pages** tab on your live site's search results page.
Each static page will have the same thumbnail image. This image is pulled from the image that you've set in the social share settings.

### Business apps (Store, Bookings, Blog, etc.)

When it comes to results from business apps, the image isn't pulled from the social share settings. For example, in the Products tab, each thumbnail image reflects the cover image of the individual product.

### Dynamic page images

The thumbnail images for your dynamic pages depend on the image field within the SEO settings of the relevant dynamic page. To set a thumbnail image you need to click **Add Field**.

### Site Search Limitations

While Site Search is able to find content from most parts of your site, there are certain limitations to the app.

Click an option below to learn more:

### What can Site Search find?

- Static pages
- Dynamic pages
- Pages hidden from your site menu (excluding pages pages hidden from search engines)
- Pages with dynamic content
- Wix Stores products
- Wix Bookings
- Wix Events
- Wix Forum
- Wix Blog

### What can't Site Search find?

We are always working to improve our products, but at the moment, Site Search does not search the following:
- Pages hidden from search engines
- Private Member pages
- Embedded iFrames (HTML widgets and embedded sites)
- Wix Art Store app
- Video content from the Wix Video app
