title: "Wix Editor: Using Facebook on Your Wix Site"
---

Facebook is the original social network. With over 2 billion people on Facebook, many Wix users use Facebook to connect with new and existing site visitors.

Add the Like, Share and Comment Facebook tools so that people can share your site content.
Add the Facebook Page Like tool to build a strong social following.

---

### Facebook Like Button

The Facebook Like button helps your site visitors to share your site with their Facebook friends.

The number shown next to your Facebook Like button is the sum of:

Add a Facebook Like button and start clocking up numbers.
Every click on this button gets your site more likes on Facebook.

### Show me how

1. Click **Add ** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Social**.
3. Under **Facebook,** click the **Facebook Like button** to add it, or drag it to the relevant location.
Learn more about setting up the Facebook Like button [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-facebook-like-button).

> **Note:**
>
> The Facebook Like button only counts the site visitors that click the Like button on your site. If you want visitors to like your Facebook Page, add the [Facebook Page Like](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-page-like-box) box.

### Facebook Share Button

A Facebook Share button helps your visitors to easily share your site with their Facebook friends.

Add a Facebook Share button to help your site content get seen.

### Show me how

1. Click **Add **on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Social**.
3. Under **Facebook**, click the Facebook Share button to add it, or drag it to the relevant location.
Learn how to customize your Facebook Share button [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-share-button).

> **Note:**
>
> This button cannot be used to share individual blog posts on Facebook. Rather, you should use the Social Media icons available for the Single Post page. You can learn more [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-sharing-a-blog-post-on-social-media).

---

### Facebook Comments Box

A Facebook Comments box helps your visitors to make comments directly on your site by logging in with their Facebook account. People can interact and share what they love about your site.

Start a conversation. Add the Facebook Comments box today.

### Show me how

1. Click **Add **on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Social**.
3. Under **Facebook**, click **Facebook Comments **to add it, or alternatively drag the box to the relevant location.
Learn more about setting up your Facebook Comments box [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-comments-box).

> **Note:**
>
> Facebook Comments are saved and displayed according to the page's URL, therefore if you change your site's name or change your domain, the Facebook comments will not appear in the new site.

---

### Facebook Page Like Box

Promote your business' Facebook page on your site with a Facebook Page Like box.

Get your website out there. Add a Facebook Like Page box to boost your viewers. 
More likes means more views from friends of your visitors.

### Show me how

1. Click **Add **on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Social**.
3. Click a Facebook Page box under **Facebook**, or drag it to the relevant location.
**Note: **There are two Facebook Page box options, the **News Feed** option and the **Mini **option.
Learn more about the Facebook Page Like box [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-page-like-box).

> **Note:**
>
> When a visitor clicks the like button in your Facebook Page Like box, they are liking your Facebook page, not your site.
