---
title: "Wix Editor: Duplicating a Page"
---

For your convenience, you can duplicate pages on your site.

### To duplicate a page:

Click **Pages ** on the left side of the Editor.  Click the relevant page.  Click the **Show More** icon .  Click **Duplicate**.  Enter the new page name.  Click **Done**

> **Notes:**
>
> - You cannot duplicate pages in some apps such as Wix Stores, Wix Blog and Wix Hotels.
> - You can also copy and paste a page from one site onto another site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-copying-and-pasting-elements-and-pages-to-another-site)

> **Tip:**
>
> You can add as many pages to your site as you wish, however, keep in mind that sites with a large amount of pages may take longer to load and may also affect the performance of the Editor. Read our [guidelines for creating a multi-page site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-multi-page-site).
