---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Customizing Scroll Bars"
---

Currently, it is not possible to design the site's scroll bars. This means you cannot change the color, width, background, shadow, radius or any other value.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
