> **Did you know?**
>
> - You can [change your font](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-changing-your-text-font) from the text settings panel, or set text themes to [use throughout your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-working-with-text-themes).
> - The availability of some fonts depends on your site language. Learn more about [changing your site language](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-sites-language).
> - Make your site more accessible by choosing [clear, legible fonts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessibility-fonts-and-readability). This can help visitors who have visual or reading impairments.
