### Selling your custom templates online

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure you've [created a custom template](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-creating-and-using-custom-templates) that is ready and published.

Download a shareable file and sell your template on your site and other platforms. Once a client purchases the template from you, they can download the .zip file that includes a file with a link to add the template to their Wix account along with our instructions on how to use it.

#### To download and sell your template:

[Go to Custom Templates](https://users.wix.com/login/select-workspace?redirect_to=https://manage.wix.com/studio/my-templates?referralInfo=sidebar&viewId=all-items-view) in your Wix Studio workspace.  Click the **More Actions** icon  on the template and select **Download shareable file**.  Click **Accept and Download** to accept the [terms & conditions](https://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).

> **What's next?**
>
> Add your template as a digital product on your Wix site or anywhere else online to start selling. If you're a Wix Partner, you can also showcase and [sell your Studio templates in the Wix Marketplace](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-selling-your-studio-templates-in-the-marketplace).

### FAQs

Click on a question to learn more about selling your custom templates online.

### Can I sell my custom templates on platforms other than Wix?

Yes! You can sell your custom templates on any platform of your choice, not just on Wix. Once you download the shareable file from Wix Studio, you can distribute it and sell it on various online marketplaces or on your own website.

### What file formats are supported for selling custom templates?

Your custom templates are downloaded in a .zip file format, which includes all the necessary files and instructions for clients to use the template. This format ensures compatibility and ease of use across different platforms.

### Can I edit my template after it has been downloaded by clients?

Sure, you can edit your template even after you've already sold it and it has been downloaded by clients. Once you've made the necessary changes, publish the template to apply the edits and ensure that clients receive the most updated version when they purchase and download it.**Note:** Any changes you make won't affect sites that are already using your template.

### How do I set the price of my custom templates?

Setting the price for your custom templates is entirely up to you. Consider factors such as the complexity of the template, market demand, and competition when determining the price. You can also experiment with pricing strategies to find what works best for your business.
