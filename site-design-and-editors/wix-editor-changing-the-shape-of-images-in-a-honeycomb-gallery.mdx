---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Shape of Images in a Honeycomb Gallery"
---

Select the shape of your gallery images, and change the overall layout of your gallery.

To change the shape of your gallery images:

Click the gallery in the Editor.Click the **Layouts** icon .Choose the shape of your image, under **Which shape are images?**:
- **Diamond**: Changes image shape to diamond.
- **Triangle**: Changes image shape to triangle.
- **Hexagon** (honeycomb): Changes image shape to hexagon (honeycomb).
