---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Accessibility Wizard"
---

Currently, we do not offer a feature that analyzes a site and highlights areas that require adjustment in order to make it accessible.

However, you can apply certain settings that may help make your client's site more accessible, like adding alt text and [arranging your elements in the layers panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-working-with-layers#reordering-layers).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
