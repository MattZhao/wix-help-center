---
title: "Embedding Songkick Tour Dates on Your Wix Site"
---

Embed your tour dates from Songkick to your Wix site, by copying the embed code and pasting it in your editor.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Your account needs to be set up as the [manager of the requested artist](https://www.launchpad-music.com/support-posts/setting-up-a-songkick-artist-account-and-connecting-it-to-spotify-bandcamp-etc).

### To embed tour dates:

Log in to [Songkick](https://accounts.songkick.com/session/new?cancel_url=%2F&success_url=https%3A%2F%2Ftourbox.songkick.com%2Ftools%2Fwidget&source_product=tourbox).  Go to the [tour dates portal](https://tourbox.songkick.com/tools/widget).   Customize the options in the panel on the right.

Click **Grab the code**.   Go to your Wix Editor.  Click **Add Elements **** **on the left side of the Editor.  Click **Embed Code**.  Click **Embed HTML**.

Paste the code you copied from step 4 into the **Add your code here (HTTPS only)** field.   Click **Update**.    (Optional) Enter alt text that describes what you're embedding in the **What's in the embed? Tell Google **field.   When ready, publish your site.
