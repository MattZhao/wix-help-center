---
title: "Request: Sending an Email Campaign by Email Without Publishing the URL"
---

When you create and send an email campaign, the email is also published on a specific URL. Subscribers are able to access the URL through the email itself, but others can only access the URL if you give it to them directly.

Currently, it is not possible to send an email campaign without publishing the URL.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
