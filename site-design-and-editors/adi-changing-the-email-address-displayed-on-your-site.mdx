---
title: "ADI: Changing the Email Address Displayed on Your Site"
---

Your email address is taken from the information you provide when setting up your site, but you can change it at any time. It may be displayed in a few places on your site, usually in the header or footer so that your visitors can access it wherever they are on your site.

### To change the email address on your site:

Click the relevant section in the ADI Editor in which your email address appears. In the panel on the left, change the information appearing under **Site Email**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Changing your business information automatically changes it everywhere on your site.
