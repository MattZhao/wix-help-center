---
title: "Wix Editor: Changing the Direction of Subpages in a Vertical Menu"
---

You can set your [subpages ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-subpage-drop-down-menu)on your vertical menu to open to the right or to the left. This is a useful feature and the preference usually depends on the position of your menu on your page.

### To change the direction of the subpages in a vertical menu:

Click your menu.  Click the **Layouts **icon .  Select an option under **How do dropdown menus open?**: 

- **To the right**: The [subpages ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-subpage-drop-down-menu)of your dropdown menu open to the right of the menu item.
- **To the left**: The [subpages ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-creating-a-subpage-drop-down-menu)of your dropdown menu open to the left of the menu item.
