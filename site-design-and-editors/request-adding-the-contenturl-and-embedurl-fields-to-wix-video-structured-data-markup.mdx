---
title: "Request: Adding the ContentURL and EmbedURL Fields to Wix Video Structured Data Markup"
---

We are pleased to announce that when you add Wix Video to your site pages, either the contentURL or embedURL fields are now added automatically to the [structured data markup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-structured-data-markup-to-your-sites-pages-2546962) of the videos (depending on the method you used to upload the file).

This feature is available for anyone who uses [Wix Video](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-about-wix-video) to add videos to their site.
