---
title: "Wix Video: Setting Up Your Payment Methods"
---

In order to to sell your videos in Wix Video, you will need to set up a payment method.

### To set up a payment method:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Accept Payments **tab.  Set up the payment methods you want to use.

### To change your currency:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down and click **Video Settings**.  Click **Change** under **Price Settings**.  Select a new currency from the drop-down menu.

> **Important:**
>
> - To accept payments for channel subscriptions, you must set your payment method to [Paypal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider), [Stripe](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-stripe-as-a-payment-provider) or [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) (Stripe and Wix Payments allow payment through credit card).
> - There is no option for offline/manual payments in Wix Video.
> - Currently, [Isracard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-isracard-as-a-payment-provider) is not available as a payment provider for Wix Video.
