---
title: "Wix Video: Viewing and Managing Your Subscribers' Information"
---

When a site visitor purchases a [pricing plan connected to your video channel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-video-connecting-a-pricing-plan-to-a-video-channel), they become subscribers. This means that they pay a subscription fee that enables them to buy or rent your videos.

You can view your subscribers' information in **Subscriptions** in your site's dashboard. You can see what plan your subscribers are connected to, and the status of their subscription. You can also extend, suspend and cancel subscriptions from there.

> **Important:**
>
> Subscriber information can only be viewed for clients that are connected to a [pricing plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-an-overview). It is not possible to manage subscriptions or view subscriber information using the old subscription plan method.

### To view subscriber information:

Go to [Subscriptions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Subscriptions&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/subscriptions) in your site's dashboard.   Click the Arrow icon  next to the relevant plan to view more information.  (Optional) Click **Manage Subscriptions** to extend, suspend, or cancel a subscription.
