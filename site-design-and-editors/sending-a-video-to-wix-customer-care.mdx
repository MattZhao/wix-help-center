---
title: "Sending a Video to Wix Customer Care"
---

Get issues resolved faster by sending us a screen recording of the problem you're experiencing. A screen recorded video can help us understand what the issue looks like from your end, so we can better troubleshoot.

#### Recommended programs for recording your screen:

**Screencast: **[Sign up for a Screencast account](https://www.screencast.com/), then record your video and send us the URL.   **Screencastify: **Add the [Screencastify extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/screencastify-screen-vide/mmeijimgabbpbgpdklnllpncmdofkcpn), then record your video and send us the URL.   **QuickTime (for Mac): **Record your video in [QuickTime](https://support.apple.com/guide/quicktime-player/welcome/mac#screen), then upload it to a video hosting site, such as [YouTube](http://www.YouTube.com), and send us the URL. Make sure the video is set to public so our Experts can view it.

> **To contact Wix Customer Care for support:**
>
> Make sure you're logged in to your Wix account from a computer and [click here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) to get started.
