---
title: "Wix Editor Request: Showing a Subpage Menu Under the More Item"
---

Currently, if a menu item with subpages is placed under the **More** item, the subpages do not appear.

We recommend [rearranging your menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-managing-your-sites-pages) so that menu item with subpages are not placed under the More item (i.e., place them towards the beginning of your menu).

> **Note:**
>
> If you want to stretch your menu to full width, check how it appears within the gridlines **before you stretch it**.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
