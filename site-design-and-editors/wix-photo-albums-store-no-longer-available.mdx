---
title: "Wix Photo Albums Store: No Longer Available"
---

As of February 2023, the support of Wix Photo Albums Store has been discontinued. If you already have Photo Albums Store on your site, you can continue to use it as normal.

Click a question below to learn more:

### I have never had Photo Albums Store on my site. Can I add it?

No. The support of Photo Albums Store has been discontinued, so as of February 2023 it cannot be added to any sites that didn't have Photo Albums Store enabled before this date.
**Note:** This change applies on a site level, not an account level. If you have multiple sites within an account, you may have Photo Albums Store on some sites and not on others.

### I have the Photo Albums Store on my site. Can I continue to use it?

Yes. If you have Photo Albums Store on your site, you can continue to use it the same way. You can still add new albums, manage your store and accept orders.
[Order a Print](https://www.orderaprint.com/wix-photo-albums) will remain responsible for fulfilling and shipping orders as normal. If you have questions/concerns around the order status or quality of your prints, contact Order a Print directly:
**Website:** [https://www.orderaprint.com/wix-photo-albums](https://www.orderaprint.com/wix-photo-albums)**Email:** support@orderaprint.com

### I have the Photo Albums Store on my site. Can I continue to withdraw my earnings?

You can continue to withdraw earnings as normal.
Learn more about [withdrawing earnings in the Wix Photo Albums Store](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-photo-albums-withdrawing-your-earnings-in-the-wix-photo-albums-store).

### I have the Photo Albums Store on my site. Can I still receive support for my store?

Yes, our Customer Care team is available to answer any questions you have around your Photo Albums Store.
Learn more about [contacting Wix Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support).

**Note:** If your question relates to the shipping or printing of merchandise, contact Order a Print directly:
**Website:** [https://www.orderaprint.com/wix-photo-albums](https://www.orderaprint.com/wix-photo-albums)**Email:** support@orderaprint.com

### If I delete the Photo Albums Store from my site, what happens to my images?

Your uploaded images will remain in the Media Manager in the Site Files folder, without a watermark.

### What can I use to sell my photos instead of Photo Albums Store?

You can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your photos.

Wix Stores allows you to sell a variety of product types and control the delivery of customers' orders.

### My Photo Albums Store isn't working properly. Will it be fixed?

As of February 2023, the support of Photo Albums Store has been discontinued. We cannot guarantee that we'll able to fix issues that might occur in the use of Photo Albums Store.
