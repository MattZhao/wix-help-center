---
title: "Studio Editor Request: Adding the Horizontal Stretch Option to More Elements"
---

Currently, only certain elements (e.g. menu, accordion) can be stretched horizontally in the Studio Editor. It is not available for all elements, including those that allow full stretch (e.g. image, container).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
