---
title: "Requiring a Customer Phone Number When Checking Out with PayPal"
---

When accepting payments with PayPal, you can make sure your customers include their phone number by making the field mandatory.

### To require a phone number for customers using PayPal checkout:

Sign in to your PayPal account.  Click your name at the top right.  Click** Account Settings**.  Click **Website Payments **on the left.  Click **Update** next to** Website Preferences**.  Scroll down to the **Contact Telephone Number **section.  Select the **On (Required Field) **checkbox.
