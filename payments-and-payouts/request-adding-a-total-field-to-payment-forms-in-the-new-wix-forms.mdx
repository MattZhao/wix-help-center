---
title: "Request: Adding a 'Total' Field to Payment Forms in the New Wix Forms"
---

Currently in the new Wix Forms it is not possible to add a field that calculates the total amount a customer has purchased before they proceed to checkout. Instead, each purchase is shown as a separate item.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is appreciated.
