---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Accepting Installments For Invoices Paid Online"
---

Currently in [Wix Invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-about-wix-invoices) it is not possible to accept installments for invoices that are paid online (i.e., via Stripe or Paypal). You can only accept partial payments for invoices paid offline.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
