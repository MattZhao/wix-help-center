---
title: "About VAT in Chile"
---

From **July 14, 2021**, Wix started charging Value Added Tax (VAT) on our services to customers in Chile in accordance with the [Chilean tax code](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=6369).

If you are a VAT registered business in Chile with a VAT number from the Servicio de Impuestos Internos, you may be eligible for a tax exemption.

You can add your VAT number to your Wix account by updating your payment details. The exemption will apply from the next payment after that date.

---

### Updating your payment details

Update your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ VAT number to get your tax exemption.

#### To update your payment details:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **Update Payment Method**.  Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click **Next**.   Click **Edit Details.**

Re-enter your card details and add your VAT Number to the **VAT ID** field.   Click **Update**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about tax in Chile.

### What is a tax identification number?

The tax identification number is a unique 9-digit number in the format XX.XXX.XXX-X. It identifies a person or entity as registered for VAT (﻿RUT or RUN number) and other tax purposes in Chile.

### What happens if I do not have a tax identification number?

If you do not have a tax identification number, Wix will charge and collect VAT on payments for subscriptions, applications and products.

### How does Wix determine which customers are subject to tax?

The tax charge is based on the billing account address.
