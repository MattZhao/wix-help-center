---
title: "Request: Using Mobex as a Payment Provider"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer Mobex as a payment provider.  However, you can use the [manual payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments), and mention that you accept payments through Mobex. You can then arrange the Mobex payment offline.
