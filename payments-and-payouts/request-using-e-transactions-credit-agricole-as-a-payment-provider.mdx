---
title: "Request: Using E-Transactions (Crédit Agricole) as a Payment Provider"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer E-Transactions (Crédit Agricole) as a payment provider.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can accept payments to your Crédit Agricole account by [connecting PayZen as a payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-payzen-by-lyra-as-a-payment-provider).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
