---
title: "Request: Creating an Invoice with Wix Automations"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create an invoice with Wix Automations.

> **Wix Bookings exception:**
>
> It is possible to [send payment requests (invoices) to Wix Bookings clients](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-sending-payment-requests-to-clients-using-automations) using Wix Automations.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
