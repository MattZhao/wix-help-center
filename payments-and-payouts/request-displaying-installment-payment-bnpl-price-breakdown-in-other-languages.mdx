---
title: "Request: Displaying Installment Payment (BNPL) Price Breakdown in Other Languages"
---

Currently, price breakdowns for installment payments are always displayed in English on your Product Page. Price breakdowns do not translate in to your site language.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
