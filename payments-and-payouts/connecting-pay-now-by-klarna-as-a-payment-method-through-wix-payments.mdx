---
title: "Connecting Pay Now by Klarna as a Payment Method (Through Wix Payments)"
---

Accept EUR payments from your customers via direct bank transfers using Pay Now by Klarna, processed by Wix Payments.

**General Info** 
Supported countriesAustria, Belgium, Germany, Switzerland.Supported currenciesEUR, CHFTransaction feesAustria, Belgium, Germany: 2.5% + 0.30 EUR

Switzerland: 2.5% + 0.4 CHF

### To connect Pay Now by Klarna through Wix Payments:

Go to the [Accept Payments tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) on your dashboard.

**Tip:** Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it.  Click **Connect **next to** Wix Payments**.

**Note:** If Klarna through Wix Payments is available in your area, you will be automatically connected.

> **Important:**
>
> - If you've already connected your Wix site to Klarna through a different provider, you can change it to Wix Payments. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/switching-to-wix-payments)
> - Currently, it is not possible to link an existing Klarna account to Wix Payments. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-connecting-an-existing-klarna-account-to-wix-payments)

> **What's Next?**
>
> [Complete setup with Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/completing-your-wix-payments-account-setup) in order to get payouts to your bank account.
