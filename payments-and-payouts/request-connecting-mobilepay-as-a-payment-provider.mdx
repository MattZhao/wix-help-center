---
title: "Request: Connecting MobilePay as a Payment Provider"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer MobilePay as a payment provider.

> If you use the [manual payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments), you can mention that you accept payments through MobilePay and arrange the payment offline.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
