---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Automatically Update Invoices for Unpaid Orders After Product Changes"
---

Currently, it is not possible to update an invoice when you add a new product to an unpaid order. Additionally, if you delete the invoice, it won't automatically regenerate with the new details added to the existing order.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
