---
title: "Printing Invoices for Wix Subscriptions"
---

Print out and save a hard copy of any invoices for premium subscriptions in your Wix account.

> **Important:**
>
> This article refers to printing invoices for your Wix premium subscriptions. To print invoices that you've sent to clients, [learn more about managing your Wix invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-managing-your-invoices).

### To print an invoice:

[Go to Billing History](https://www.wix.com/account/billing-history?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your Wix account.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the invoice you want to print.  Click **Print invoice**.
