---
title: "Accepting Payments by Credit Card"
---

Accept direct credit card payments from all major credit card companies, including Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.

Whenever possible, Wix automatically connects you to the most suitable provider available in your region, based on your preferences and location. If Wix cannot automatically connect you, you are offered the range of [available providers in your location](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country), as well as instructions on how to connect.

> **Important:**
>
> As of April 2024, Carte Bancaire cards are no longer supported by Wix Payments.

### Setting up credit card payments

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2F) in your site's dashboard. 

**Tip:** Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it.  Click** Connect** next to **Wix Payments**, or click **See More Payment Options** to see alternative payment providers available in your region.

Set up your account with your provider:

1. Click **Connect / Complete Setup**.
2. Follow the instructions to create an account or connect an existing one.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about accepting payments.

### Can I connect more than one payment provider for credit card payments?

Currently, it is not possible to connect more than 1 payment provider to process credit card payments. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-connecting-more-than-one-credit-card-payment-provider).

### Can I connect more than one payment provider for other payment methods?

Yes. You can connect different payment providers to support different payment methods.

If you wish to use one payment provider for credit card transactions, you can connect another provider for alternative payments transactions only. These include cryptocurrency, PayPal or online bank transfer payments.

### Does every payment provider charge the same amount for processing transactions?

No. Each payment provider sets their own rate and / or fee for processing transactions.

### Can I offer recurring payments?

To offer recurring payments, you must connect a [payment provider that supports recurring payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments).

### How do I get my payouts?

Payouts are managed by your payment provider. If your provider is a third-party provider, [contact them directly](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-payouts-from-third-party-payment-providers).

If your provider is Wix Payments, learn about [receiving payouts from Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-your-payouts-from-wix-payments).

### Why can’t I connect my desired payment provider?

If you’re having trouble connecting your payment provider, you may be able to fix the issue by [troubleshooting common connection issues](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-third-party-provider-connection-issues).

### How do I set up payments for Wix Hotels?

Learn how to [set up payments for Wix Hotels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-your-payment-method-in-wix-hotels).
