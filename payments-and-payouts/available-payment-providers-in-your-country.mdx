---
title: "Available Payment Providers in Your Country"
---

Whenever possible, Wix automatically connects you to the most suitable payment provider available in your region, based on your preferences and location. If there is a different payment provider that you'd like to use, and it's available in your location, you are welcome to use it.

> **Important:**
>
> As of March 7 2022, Wix no longer supports payment processing in Russia.

---

### All countries

Wherever you are in the world, you can accept payments offline and record those payments using the [Manual Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments) method.

If you don’t see your desired payment provider, you can still connect a provider using [Velo](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites/articles/getting-started/about-velo-by-wix) and the [Payment Provider Service Plugin](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites/articles/code-tutorials/wix-pay/tutorial-payment-provider-custom-extension).

> **Notes:**
>
> - Currently, it is not possible to connect more than one payment provider (e.g. Stripe, Square, etc.) to process **credit card payments**. If this is something you'd like to see in the future, you can [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-connecting-more-than-one-credit-card-payment-provider).
> - While you can't connect more than one provider to process credit card payments, it is possible to connect multiple providers for alternative payment methods such as PayPal, cryptocurrency payments, etc.
