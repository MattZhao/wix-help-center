---
title: "Wix Payments: Accessing Your Account Dashboard"
---

Your Wix Payments dashboard provides you with all the information you need to manage your Wix Payments account. From here you can change your account settings and keep track of your transactions and payouts.

You can access your Wix Payments account in 2 ways; from your site's dashboard and from your Wix account.

DashboardWix account

1. [Go to Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Your%20Transactions&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments-dashboard/?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your site's dashboard.
2. You can organize your payments using the following options:

- Filter your payment history by payment status, date, payment provider, payment ID, and more.
- Click the download icon  to download a CSV file of your payments.
[image: filtering payments,]Tip:Click a payment to see more details about that payment.Notes:
- This option only appears if you have a Wix Payments account in your Wix account.
- The Wix Payments account-level dashboard only provides information for your Wix Payments transactions. Any other transactions can be viewed from the Payments tab in your site's dashboard.
To access your account dashboard from your Wix account:
1. [Go to Wix Payments Accounts](https://manage.wix.com/account/wix-payments).
2. Click **View** on the Wix Payments enabled site account you wish to view.
3. Select the information you want to view. Choose between:

- **Payments**
- **Payouts**
- **Settlement Report**
- **Account Details**
