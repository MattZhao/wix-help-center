---
title: "Wix Payments: Viewing Your Payouts"
---

View information about previous and upcoming payouts (transfers from Wix Payments to your bank account), as well as detailed information about each payment.

It may take up to 3-5 business days for the funds to reach your bank account after being processed, depending on your financial institution.

### To view your payouts:

[Go to Payouts](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/payouts-dashboard?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Payouts) in your site's dashboard.  (Optional) Click on a payout** **to see more details about that payout.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Learn [more about payouts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-your-payouts-from-wix-payments).
> - You can still [access your Wix Payments dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-the-wix-payments-dashboard-after-disconnecting-your-account), even if your Wix Payments account has been disconnected.
