---
title: "Request: Changing the Currency of Your Wix Payments Account"
---

The currency of your Wix Payments account is determined by your location, depending on your [account type](https://support.wix.com/en/article/the-difference-between-individual-and-business-accounts-in-wix-payments). Currently, it is not possible to switch your account to a different currency that's not based on location.

> **Tip:**
>
> Users in the EEA can can use any EEA** **international bank account number (IBAN) to receive payouts. This includes both for personal and business account accounts.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
