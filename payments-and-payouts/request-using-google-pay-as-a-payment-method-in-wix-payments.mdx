---
title: "Request: Using Google Pay as a Payment Method in Wix Payments"
---

We are pleased to announce you can now [add Google Pay as a payment method with Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-google-pay-as-a-payment-method).

Google Pay offers your customers a swift and secure checkout experience on your website. When customers use Google Pay, they don't need to input their credit card details or shipping address manually. This promotes a faster checkout for your customer and a better conversion rate for your business.
