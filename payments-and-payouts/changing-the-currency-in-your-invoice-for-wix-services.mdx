title: "Changing the Currency in Your Invoice for Wix Services"
---

If you move to a new country, any Wix services that you have set to auto-renew will continue to be charged in your previous currency.

While there is no direct way to switch currencies, you can use the workarounds in this article to start getting billed in your new location's currency.

**In this article, learn how to change the currency used to pay for your:**

[Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-wix-services#changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-your-premium-plan)  [Domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-wix-services#changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-your-domain)  [Business email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-wix-services#changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-your-business-email)  [Apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-wix-services#changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-your-apps)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-currency-used-to-pay-for-wix-services#faqs)

### Changing the currency used to pay for your Premium plan

You can perform the following workaround to change the currency used to pay for your Premium plan. The process involves duplicating your site, moving the existing Premium plan there, and in the meantime, upgrading the original site from the new location.

> **Tip:**
>
> There are a number of steps involved, so it is a good idea to keep this article open as you follow the instructions.

#### To change the currency used to pay for your Premium plan:

Cancel the Premium plan that you want to change:

1. [Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the plan associated with the relevant site.
3. Click **Cancel plan**.
4. Click **Cancel Now**.
    Duplicate the site that the plan is assigned to:

1. [Go to the dashboard of the site](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D).
2. Click the More Actions icon  and click **Duplicate Site**.
3. Enter a name for the duplicate site.
4. Click **Duplicate**.
    Reassign the canceled Premium plan to the duplicate site:

1. [Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the relevant plan.
3. Click **Assign to a Different Site** and choose the duplicated site.
4. Click **Next**.
5. Click **Assign**.
    Upgrade the original site from your new location:

1. [Go to the dashboard of the original site](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D).
2. Click** Upgrade** on the left.
3. Click **Select** under the Premium plan you want to purchase.
4. Follow the steps to complete the purchase.
    Assign your domain back to your original site:

1. [Go to Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) ﻿in your Wix account.

**Note:** If you have more than one domain in your account, select the domain you want to reassign.
2. Scroll down to the **Unassigned Domains** section.
3. Click the More Actions** **icon .
4. Click **Assign to a Site.**
5. Choose a site from the options if there is more than one, then click **Next.**
6. Click **Assign.**

### Changing the currency used to pay for your domain

In order to update the currency used on your invoice for a domain payment, you need to extend your domain subscription from your new geolocation.

#### To change the currency used to pay for your domain:

[Go to Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) ﻿in your Wix account.

**Note:** If you have more than one domain in your account, select the domain that was transferred.  Click **Extend**.

Select the registration period.  Click **Continue**.  Enter (or edit) your contact information.  Click **Continue**.  Select a privacy option: 

- **Private Registration:** Your contact details won't appear in the WHOIS domain information records. You can turn the privacy protection option on and off whenever you want. This option carries a small additional fee.
- **Public Registration:** Your information always remains public in the WHOIS domain information records. You can only change it to private when [manually extending](https://support.wix.com/en/article/activating-private-registration-for-a-wix-domain) the domain registration.
    Click **Continue**.  Select your payment method.  Click **Submit Purchase**.

### Changing the currency used to pay for your business email

To change the currency used to pay for your business email subscription, you need to cancel it (i.e. turn off auto-renew), then repurchase from your new location **after **your current subscription ends.

> **Important:**
>
> You can only change the currency used to pay for your business email at the **end** of your subscription period. This means that even if you cancel right now, you must wait for your business email subscription to end before repurchasing.

#### To change the currency used to pay for your business email:

Turn off auto-renewal for your Business email: 

1. [Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the relevant business email.
3. Click **Cancel Business Email**.
4. Select the **I still want to cancel** checkbox.
5. Click **Cancel Business Email**. This turns off auto-renewal.
    (After your subscription ends) Repurchase the business email from your new location:

1. [Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) in your Wix account.
2. Click **Get a Business Email **next to the relevant domain.
3. (Optional) Click the Add icon **+** to purchase additional mailboxes.
4. Enter your email prefix(es).
5. Click **Continue**.
6. Choose which Google Workspace plan you want from the options listed.
7. Choose a business email subscription: **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
8. Click **Get it**.
9. Enter your credit card information.
10. Click **Submit Purchase**.

### Changing the currency used to pay for your Apps

To change the currency used to pay for your App subscriptions, cancel them (i.e. turn off auto-renew) and repurchase from your new location **after** your subscription ends.

> **Important:**
>
> You can only change the currency used to pay for your App at the** end** of the subscription period. This means that even if you cancel right now, you must wait for your subscription to end before repurchasing.

#### To change the currency used to pay for your App subscriptions:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.

1. Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the relevant app.
2. Click **Cancel App**.
3. Select **Cancel auto renew **and click **Submit**.
    (After your subscription ends) Upgrade the App from your new location, in your Editor or your site's dashboard:

### Upgrade the app from your Editor

1. Go to the Wix App Market in your Editor:

- **Wix Editor:** Click **Add Apps**  on the left side of the Editor.
- **Editor X: **Click **App Market ** at the top of Editor X.
2. Select **App Market** and search for the app.
3. Click **Open**.
4. Click the **Upgrade** icon  or select Settings and click **Upgrade**.
5. Select your payment plan.
6. Click **Upgrade Now**.
7. Enter your payment information.
8. Click** Submit Purchase**.

### Upgrade the app from your site's dashboard

1. [Go to Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Upgrade** next to the relevant app.
3. Enter your payment information.
4. Click **Submit Purchase**.

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### How does the workaround for changing my currency work?

The workaround involves repurchasing (Premium plans, Business emails) or extending (domains) the subscription from your new location.

### Can Wix change the currency on my past invoices?

No, Wix cannot retroactively change an invoice's currency. Any existing invoice's currency cannot be changed.

### I followed your instructions but I am being billed in USD. Why hasn't my currency updated to match my current location?

Some currencies are not supported for payment of Wix services. If your local currency is not available, you'll be charged in US Dollar (USD).
