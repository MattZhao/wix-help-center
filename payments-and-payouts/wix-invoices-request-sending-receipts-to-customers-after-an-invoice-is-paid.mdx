---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Sending Receipts to Customers After an Invoice is Paid"
---

Currently, when an invoice is paid, your customer does not receive a receipt from Wix Invoices after they've made a payment. It is not possible to send your own receipt through Wix Invoices.

However, using [Wix Automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-getting-started) you can create an automation that sends an email to visitors once an invoice is paid. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-creating-automations)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
