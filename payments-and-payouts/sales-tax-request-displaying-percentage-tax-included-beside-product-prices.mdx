---
title: "Sales Tax Request: Displaying Percentage Tax Included Beside Product Prices"
---

Currently, displaying the percentage of tax included in the price alongside product prices (e.g. 180,00€ - Price Incl. 19% VAT) is not supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
