---
title: "Switching to Wix Payments"
---

Some payment methods offer multiple providers to process your payments. You can change your provider to Wix Payments if it's available for the payment method you're using.

With Wix Payments, your customers can pay using credit / debit cards as well as, depending on your location, [Apple Pay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-apple-pay-as-a-payment-method), [Google Pay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-google-pay-as-a-payment-method), [Affirm](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-affirm-as-a-payment-method), [Afterpay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-afterpay-as-a-payment-method), [Klarna Online Bank Transfer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-pay-now-by-klarna-as-a-payment-method-through-wix-payments), [iDEAL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-ideal-as-a-payment-method-through-wix-payments) and [Tap to Pay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-tap-to-pay-on-mobile-as-a-payment-method)

With Wix Payments, you can manage orders and payments all in one place. Our free and quick setup makes it even easier for you to accept payments on your site.

### To change your payment provider to Wix Payments:

[Go to Accept Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-the-accept-payments-tab) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Connect** next to** **Wix Payments.

  Click **Yes, Switch** to confirm your new provider.  Depending on whether you need to create a new Wix Payments account or have an existing account, follow the instructions: 

- **Don't have a Wix Payments account: **

1. Click **Create an Account** in the pop-up.
2. Click **Create an Account** to confirm.
- **Have a Wix Payments account: **

1. Click** Connect Existing Account **in the pop-up.
2. Click **Connect My Account **to confirm.

> **What's next?**
>
> Make sure to [complete your account set-up](https://support.wix.com/en/article/completing-your-wix-payments-account-setup) in order to get paid to your bank account.
