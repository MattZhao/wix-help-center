---
title: "Request: Additional Payment Providers for Accepting Recurring Payments"
---

You can accept [recurring payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments) from your customers using the following payment providers, if they're [available in your region](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country):

[Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) (For all payment methods except for Klarna Online Bank Transfer)  [Wix Payments Brazil](https://support.wix.com/pt/article/wix-pagamentos-brasil-conectando-como-m%C3%A9todo-de-pagamento)  [Stripe](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-stripe-as-a-payment-provider)  [PayPal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider)  [Isracard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-isracard-as-a-payment-provider)  [Bankful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-bankful-as-a-payment-provider-for-cryptocurrency-wallet-and-credit-card-payments#connecting-bankful-as-a-payment-provider)  [KOMOJU](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-degica-komoju-as-a-payment-provider)  [Priority Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-priority-payments-as-a-payment-provider)  [Evergreen Payments Northwest](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-evergreen-payments-northwest-as-a-payment-provider)

Currently, it is not possible to accept recurring payments with providers that are not listed above (e.g. Braintree, Square...etc).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
