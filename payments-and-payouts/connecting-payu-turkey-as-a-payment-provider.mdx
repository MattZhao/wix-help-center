> Wix no longer supports connection to PayU Turkey as a payment provider. This change affects both new and existing PayU account holders. In Turkey, PayU merged with iyzico to build a local fintech giant and you can contact [iyzico](https://www.iyzico.com/en/support/contact-us) about Wix integration.

General Info
**Supported countries**Turkey**Supported currencies**TRY, EUR, USD, GBP, RUB

**Note: **The currency used in your Wix account must match the currency in your PayU account. **If the currencies do not match, the transactions may be declined. **Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments) to learn how to change your currency.**Transaction fees**There are 2 options:**Per Transaction:** 2.49% + 0.3TL per transaction

**Yearly Service Fee: **620TL per year

**Note:** If you accept payment in installments, fees may differ depending on the number of installments.**Account activation**Requires review by PayU Turkey**Note**Currently, this payment provider cannot be used with Wix Bookings.

### Settings in your PayU Account

Enable the following settings to make sure PayU processes orders from your site correctly and notifies you of any new transaction.

### IPN settings

1. Sign in to your PayU Turkey account.
2. Click **Account Management**.
3. Click **Account Settings**.
4. Click the **IPN Settings** tab.
5. Enter the following URL in the** URL field**: https://api.paymentsos.com/callbacks/payuturkey/live/notifications
6. Select the following fields under **Data**:
- SALEDATE
- REFNOEXT
- ORDERSTATUS
- IPN_PID
- IPN_PNAME
- IPN_DATE
- IPN_TOTALGENERAL
7. Click **Save Settings**.
8. Click the** Account Settings** tab.
9. Select the following under **Notifications**:
- Canceled and returned orders
- Complete orders

### Notifications settings

1. Sign in to your PayU Turkey account.
2. Click **Account Management**.
3. Click **Account Settings**.
4. Select one of the following options under **Notifications**: 

- Email Text & IPN
- IPN (Instant Payment Notifications)
**Note**: Do not select the **Email Text** option as your orders won't be processed correctly.
