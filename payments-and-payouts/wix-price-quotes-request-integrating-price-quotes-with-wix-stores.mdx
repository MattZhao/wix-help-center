---
title: "Wix Price Quotes Request: Integrating Price Quotes with Wix Stores"
---

Currently, it is not possible to integrate the Price Quotes feature with Wix Stores.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is possible to integrate Wix Invoices with Wix Stores. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-invoices-for-store-orders#automatically-creating-invoices-for-each-order)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
