---
title: "Request: Connecting to Wix Payments from Additional Countries"
---

[Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) is a payment provider offered by Wix in which you can manage orders and payments all in one place. It's available in the following 15 locations:

Austria  Belgium  Brazil   Canada  Finland   Germany   Great Britain  Ireland  Italy  Lithuania  Netherlands  Portugal  Spain   Switzerland   United States

If your country is not on the list, it means you currently cannot use Wix Payments as your payment provider. However, you can still accept payment using one of our many third-party providers. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country) to see the full list, then click **Setup** next to the relevant provider to learn how to connect it to your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
