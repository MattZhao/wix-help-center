---
title: "Didn't Receive Refund After Request Was Approved"
---

If you received an email from **support@wix.com** stating that your refund request has been approved, please allow up to 20 business days for the funds to settle in your account.

If you still need help, please [contact us here](https://www.wix.com/contact).
