title: "Wix Pay Links: Creating and Managing Pay Links in the Wix App"
---

Instantly request payments on the go using the Wix app. Share pay links via email, social media, SMS, or any of your phone's other native apps. You can also create a QR code for a pay link. Payments made via these links appear in your Wix account like all other transactions through your site.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - You need to [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) to use Pay Links. If your plan expires, your active pay links will be on hold. You'll be able to upgrade your Premium plan through the app if you're using Android. If you're using iOS, upgrade through desktop.
> - In order to accept payments, you must [set up a payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview). If you've already set up a payment method for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings or Wix Events, you do not need to set one up again.

### Creating and sending a pay link

Create a pay link and send it via any of your phone's native apps. You can also create a QR code that will bring users straight to your pay link when scanned.

#### To create and send a pay link:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).﻿  Tap **Add**  at the bottom.  Tap **Create a pay link**.  Tap **One-time payment** to create a link that accepts one payment, or tap **Multi payment** to create a link that accepts multiple payments.  Set up your pay link:

1. **Link Name: **Enter a name for your pay link.
2. **Price:** Enter the amount of money your pay link will charge your customer.
3. (Optional) **Description:** Enter a description for your pay link. This should clearly summarize the reason for payment.
4. (Optional) **Set an expiration date:** Enable the **Set an expiration date** toggle. Set a date when the link will expire and cannot be used.
5. (Optional, multi-payment only) **Limit the number of payments:** Enable the **Limit the number of payments** toggle to set the number of times the link can be used before it expires.
6. (Optional) **Customize link:** Tap **Customize link** and set a button color, edit button text and upload an image. Tap **Save**.
    (Optional) Tap **+ Add Contact **to select a contact to send your pay link to.

**Note:** Tap **+ Add Contact** and then **+ Add New Contact** to create a new contact.  Tap **Preview and Share**.  Choose how to share your pay link:

- **Copy Link:** Copy the link to your clipboard.
- **Show QR Code:** Display a QR code that when scanned will bring contacts to your pay link.

**Note:** Take a screenshot of this QR code to share it with contacts.
- **Share via:** Share your link via one of your phone's native apps.

### Managing your pay links

All of the pay links you create will be visible within your Wix app. You can share, activate, deactivate and delete pay links from here. You can also view details of each pay link and any payments made.

#### To manage your pay links:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).﻿  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Pay Links** in the **Get Paid** section.  Tap a pay link to view further details.  Choose what you want to do:

- (Active pay link only) **Share your link:** Tap **Copy Link**, **Show QR Code** or **Share via**/**Resend**.
- (Inactive pay link only) **Activate your link:** Tap **Activate** to activate your pay link.
- **View payments:** Tap the **Payments** tab to view all payments made.
- **View pay link details:** Tap the **Details** tab to view details of the pay link.
- **Duplicate a link:** Tap the More Actions icon  and tap **Duplicate** to duplicate a pay link.
- (Active pay link only) **Deactivate or delete a link:** Tap the More Actions icon  and tap **Deactivate** or **Delete** to deactivate or delete a pay link.

**Note:** A pay link can only be deleted if no payments have been received.
