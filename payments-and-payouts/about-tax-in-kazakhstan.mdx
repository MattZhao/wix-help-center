---

From** May, 2024**, Wix started charging Value Added Tax (VAT) on our services to customers in Kazakhstan.

If you are a VAT registered business in Kazakhstan with a Business Identification Number (BIN) from the Kazakhstan State Revenue Committee, you may be eligible for a tax exemption.

You can add your BIN to your Wix account by updating your payment details. The tax exemption will apply to the next subscription payment.

---

### Updating your payment details

Update your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ tax ID to get your tax exemption.

#### To update your payment details:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **Update payment method**.  Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click **Next**.   Click **Edit Details**.   Re-enter your payment information, and add your tax ID to the **BIN **field.

Click **Update**.

### What is a BIN tax identification number?

A Business Identification Number (BIN) is a tax ID issued by the State Revenue Committee in Kazakhstan to businesses and organizations resident in Kazakhstan. 
The BIN is a unique 12-digit number and is made up of five parts, which identify the type of entity and date of registration. The 5th digit in a valid BIN will always be 4, 5, or 6.

### What happens if I do not have a tax identification number?

If you do not have a tax identification number, Wix will charge and collect VAT on payments for subscriptions, applications and products.

### How does Wix determine which customers are subject to tax?

The tax charge is based on the billing account address.
