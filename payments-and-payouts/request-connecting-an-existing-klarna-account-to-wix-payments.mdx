---
title: "Request: Connecting an Existing Klarna Account to Wix Payments"
---

[Adding Klarna to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-pay-now-by-klarna-as-a-payment-method-through-wix-payments) as a payment method, provided by Wix Payments, requires creating a new Klarna account. Currently, it is not possible to link an existing Klarna account to Wix Payments.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
