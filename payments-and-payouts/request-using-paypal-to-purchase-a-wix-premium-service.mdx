---
title: "Request: Using PayPal to Purchase a Wix Premium Service"
---

We are excited to announce that it is now possible to pay for Wix Premium Services using PayPal.

You need to register for a PayPal account to do so.

Learn more about [using Paypal to pay for Wix services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-paypal-to-pay-for-wix-services-684702) and [using PayPal to purchase a Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-paypal-to-purchase-a-premium-plan).

> **Important:**
>
> This feature may not yet be available in all regions.
