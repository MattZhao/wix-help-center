---
title: "Wix Payments: Adding Affirm as a Payment Method"
---

Add Affirm as a payment method to help your customers spread their payment over a number of installments. Affirm supports buy now, pay later (BNPL) transactions. This means you get fully paid upfront, while your customers can purchase today and make affordable payments over time.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - You must set up [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) to accept payments via Affirm through Wix Payments.
> - Affirm through Wix Payments is currently available only in the United States.
> - Items sold via Affirm are subject to our list of [prohibited goods and services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-prohibited-products-and-services), as well as [Affirm's payment terms](https://stripe.com/ie/legal/affirm). Learn more about [qualifying for Affirm](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-qualification-for-affirm).

### Adding Affirm

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage** next to Wix Payments.  Enable the toggle next to Affirm.

**Note**: You can disable the toggle at any time to remove Affirm from your payment options.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about using Affirm in Wix.

### Can I connect Affirm without connecting Wix Payments?

Yes. You can [connect Affirm as a payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-affirm-as-a-payment-provider) without connecting Wix Payments.

### Can I issue refunds for Affirm payments?

Yes. You can refund Affirm payments up to 120 days after the original payment. Once a refund is initiated, any further payments due are paused.
