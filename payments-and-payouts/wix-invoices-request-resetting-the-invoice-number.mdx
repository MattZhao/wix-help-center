---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Resetting the Invoice Number"
---

Currently, it is not possible to reset the invoice numbering in Wix Invoices. For example, if your initial invoice number is 0001, and you send 2 test invoices, the first invoice that a real customer receives is invoice number 0003.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is possible to [set your initial invoice number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-price-quotes-changing-your-initial-document-number).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
