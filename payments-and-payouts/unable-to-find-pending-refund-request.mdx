---
title: "Unable to Find Pending Refund Request"
---

After you submit a refund request, the request remains in Pending status on the Refund Request page until a decision is made.

If you no longer see your request, it means that your request is no longer pending and you should have received an email with the decision.

You can check your email account for an email from **wix.com** to see if your request was accepted or denied.

If you still need help, please [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact).
