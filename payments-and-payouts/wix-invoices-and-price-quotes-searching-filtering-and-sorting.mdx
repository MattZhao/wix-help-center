Easily locate a specific invoice or price quote any time you need it, filter by a specific criteria or sort your lists according to your needs.

### To search, filter, and sort your invoices or quotes:

Go to your [Invoices](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Invoices&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/invoices/invoices/list?referralInfo=KB-Article) or [Price Quotes](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Price%20Quotes&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/price-quotes) tab on your site's dashboard.  Choose what you want to do:

### Search for an invoice or price quote

Enter a search term in the search bar above the:**Invoice list** [image: A screenshot showing the search bar on the Invoices list in the dashboard.]
**Price Quote list**[image: A screenshot showing the search bar in the price quotes list section of the dashboard.]

### Filter your invoice list or price quote list

Click the **Filter By** drop-down and select a status for:**Invoices**

[image: A screenshot showing how to filter your invoices list.]
**Price Quotes**[image: A screenshot showing how to filter your Price Quotes list.]

### Sort your invoice list or price quote list

1. Click a column name to sort the list by one of the following for your:

- **Invoices**:

- Number
- Customer
- Issue date
- Due date
- Total
- Status
- **Price Quotes**:

- Number
- Title
- Customer
- Created On
- Total Price
- Status
2. (Optional) Click again to reverse the order (e.g. Number 000125 to 000001 instead of 000001 to 000125).

[image: A screenshot showing how to sort the order of your invoices list by Invoice number.]
