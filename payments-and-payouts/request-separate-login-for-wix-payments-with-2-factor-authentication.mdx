---
title: "Request: Separate Login for Wix Payments with 2 Factor Authentication"
---

Currently, it is not possible to log directly in to your Wix Payments account with Two-Factor Authentication. You can always access Wix Payments by logging into your Wix account and following [these instructions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-wix-payments-dashboard).

In the meantime, take a look at some of the alternative options to enable Two-Factor Authentication for your Wix account:

[SecureAuth Single-Sign-On (SSO) for Wix](https://www.secureauth.com/products/access-management)[OneLogin for Wix](https://www.onelogin.com/connector/wix)[SAASPASS for Wix](https://saaspass.com/password-manager/wix-com-password-manager-single-sign-on-sso-saml/)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
