---
title: "Email from Wix Regarding Changes to Your Payment Method Settings"
---

The security of our users' accounts is of utmost importance to us here at Wix and we are dedicated to online safety.

If you update your payment method settings (how you accept payments in Wix features, such as Wix Stores, Wix Invoices etc), you receive an email saying "You changed how you accept payments on your website".

It's important to note that not only the primary account owner but other [roles such as an Admin (co-owner) and payments admin](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview) can make changes to payment method settings.

If you didn't authorize these changes, [change your Wix account password](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-wix-password) immediately and then contact [Wix Customer Care](http://www.wix.com/contact/en).
