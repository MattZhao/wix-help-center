title: "About VAT in Taiwan"
---

From **October 18th, 2021**,** **Wix started charging Value Added Tax (VAT) on our services to customers in Taiwan in accordance with the** **[Value-Added and Non-Value-Added Business Tax Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/Eng/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=G0340080). You’ll see this tax added to your next renewal payment.

If you’re a VAT-registered business in Taiwan, add your VAT number to your Wix account to get a tax exemption on your next payment.

You can add your VAT number to your Wix account by updating your payment details. The exemption will apply from the next payment after that date.

---

### Updating your payment details

Update your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ VAT number to get your tax exemption.

#### To update your payment details:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **Update Payment Method**.  Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click **Next**.   Click **Edit Details**.  Re-enter your card details and add your VAT Number to the **VAT ID** field.   Click **Update**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about tax in Taiwan.

### What is a tax identification number?

The VAT number is a unique 8-digit number assigned to registered businesses in Taiwan.

### What happens if I do not have a tax identification number?

If you do not have a tax identification number, Wix will charge and collect VAT on payments for subscriptions, applications and products.

### How does Wix determine which customers are subject to tax?

The tax charge is based on the billing account address.
