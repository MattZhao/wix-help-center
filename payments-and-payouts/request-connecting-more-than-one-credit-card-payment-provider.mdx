---
title: "Request: Connecting More than One Credit Card Payment Provider"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect more than one payment provider (e.g. Stripe, Square, etc.) to process credit card payments.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
