---
title: "Request: Using Venmo as a Payment Method with PayPal"
---

We are pleased to announce that you can now accept [Venmo payments via PayPal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider) in the United States.

Venmo is a mobile digital wallet that offers its users a social way to make a payment; allowing users to split bills easily. It is one of the most popular peer-to-peer (P2P) payment methods in the United States with millions of active users.

> Currently, Venmo is unavailable for customers using iOS devices.
