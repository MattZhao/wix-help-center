---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Adding an Expenses Section to Finances"
---

Currently in [Wix Invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-about-wix-invoices) it is not possible to add an expense section to finance reports. This type of section is often used to track various business expenses.
