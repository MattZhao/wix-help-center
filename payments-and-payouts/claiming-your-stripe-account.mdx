title: "Claiming Your Stripe Account"
---

If you were connected to Stripe automatically when selecting a payment method, you must still **complete your Stripe account set-up** before Stripe is active on your checkout and you can receive your first payment.

> **Important:**
>
> Your Stripe account is subject to Stripe's applicable terms, policies, and fees.

### To claim your Stripe account:

Go to [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) on your site's dashboard.  Click **See More Payment Options**.  Click **See Options** beside **Third-Party** **Credit / Debit Card Providers**.  Click **Connect** beside **Stripe**.  Enter your credentials to sign in. >
