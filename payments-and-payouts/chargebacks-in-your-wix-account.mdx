---
title: "Chargebacks in Your Wix Account"
---

A chargeback is a debit or credit card transaction that's reversed by the customer's bank after they dispute a charge on their payment card. This article relates to chargebacks between Wix and its customers.

If you issue a chargeback for a Wix service:

Your service is canceled immediately.  You may be liable for fees incurred as a result of the chargeback. Learn more in our [Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).  Any data that relies on purchased features could be lost.

> **Important:**
>
> Before filing a dispute with your bank or credit card company, always [contact Wix Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact?referral=ChargebackKBArticle) with any questions concerning a charge from Wix.

---

### Chargebacks for Premium plan payments

If you perform a chargeback for a payment made for your Premium plan, your plan is canceled.

When a Premium plan is canceled:

Your domain is disconnected from your site  Your site displays Wix ads  Your site's storage and bandwidth is limited  Any other Premium features that come with your plan (e.g. Wix Stores) are removed

> **Want to restore these features?**
>
> You must [purchase a new Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) with a **different** payment card.

---

### Chargebacks for Wix domain payments

Upon receiving a chargeback for a Wix domain payment:

The domain is suspended or removed from Wix for the remainder of its registration period  You will no longer be able to connect the domain to any website  You receive an email notification from Wix to inform you of the cancelation of your subscription

---

### Chargebacks for business email subscriptions

If you issue a chargeback for a Google Workspace business email:

Your business email is canceled immediately  You are no longer be able to send or receive emails from your business email  Wix sends a cancelation notification to the email address of your Wix account  Your Wix account may become blocked from further use

---

### Chargebacks for apps from the Wix App Market

If you issue a chargeback for an app from the Wix App Market:

Your service is canceled immediately.  Any Premium app features are removed from your site.  Any data that relies on these Premium features could be lost.

To restore your app features, you must upgrade it again using a **different** payment card. Learn how to [upgrade an app from the Wix App Market](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-app-from-the-wix-app-market).
