---
title: "Authorize.net (Moolah) No Longer Supported as a Payment Provider"
---

As of March 2019, we no longer support connecting Authorize.net (Moolah) as a payment provider for your Wix site.

If you've already set up Authorize.net (Moolah) for your site, you will still be able to accept payments through this payment provider. However, it is no longer possible to connect it to a new site, or any other site in your account.
