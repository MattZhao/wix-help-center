title: "About Sales and Service Tax (SST) in Malaysia"
---

From **July 14, 2021**, Wix started charging Sales and Service Tax (SST) on our services to customers in Malaysia in accordance with the [Service Tax Act of 2018](https://mystods.customs.gov.my/).

This means that the price of subscriptions has increased to include Sales and Service Tax.

If you’re an SST-registered business in Malaysia, update your payment details to ensure that your SST registration number appears on the invoice of your next payment.

---

### Updating your payment details

Update your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ SST number to get your tax exemption.

#### To update your payment details:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **Update payment method**.  Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click **Next**.   Click **Edit Details**.

Re-enter your card details and add your SST registration number to the **SST registration  number** field.   Click **Update**.

### FAQs

Click below for answers to common questions about SST in Malaysia.

### What is an SST number?

Sales and Service Tax (SST) is a tax on the consumption or use of goods and services. An SST ID is a 15-character tax ID issued to businesses and organizations in Malaysia. It usually begins with a letter, for example W88888888888888.

### What happens if I do not have a tax identification number?

If you do not have a tax identification number, Wix will charge and collect SST on payments for subscriptions, applications and products.

### How does Wix determine which customers are subject to tax?

The tax charge is based on the billing account address.
