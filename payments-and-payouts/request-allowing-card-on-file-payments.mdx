---
title: "Request: Allowing Card on File Payments"
---

We're pleased to announce that [card-on-file payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-saving-and-charging-customers-credit-cards) are now available with Wix Stores and Wix Bookings.

With card-on-file, you're able to link payment cards to customer profiles, with their permission. This allows you to take future payments without the customer needing to be present.
