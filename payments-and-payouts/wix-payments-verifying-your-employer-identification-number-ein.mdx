> **Note:**
>
> An EIN is required only in the United States.

An Employer Identification Number (EIN) is issued for tax administration purposes. **Employers** in the United States are required by the [Internal Revenue Service](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) (IRS) to have an Employer Identification Number.

### Verifying Your EIN in Wix Payments

When [completing your Wix Payments account setup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/completing-your-wix-payments-account-setup) as a **Partnership / LLC / Corporation / NGO**, Wix Payments asks for your company's 9 digit Employer Identification Number.

In addition, to verify your EIN, please make sure that you upload a copy of your EIN confirmation notice. Follow these guidelines to help your verification:

The company name should be visible and clear and should match the business details in your Wix Payments account  The Employer Identification Number should be visible and clear    Documents must be sent in one of the following formats: jpeg, jpg, pdf, png  The maximum file size is 10 MB  The minimum file size is 1 kB for PDF, 100 kB for other formats

#### Example of IRS EIN confirmation notice:

If all details have been entered correctly, the verification process usually takes 1 working day to complete.

If all details have been entered correctly and, after the timeframe, your EIN has not been successfully verified, please [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact) and we'll investigate the issue.
