---
title: "Managing Business Owners in Wix Payments"
---

After adding the company's Business Owners to your Wix Payments account, you can always go back and edit their details or remove them completely if they are no longer relevant.

> **Important:**
>
> Wix Payments may require additional information and/or documents verifying your Business Owners' identity, to make sure your account complies with all legal requirements.

### To edit a Business Owner's details:

Go to [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2F) in your site's dashboard.Click **Wix Payments Dashboard**.Click **Account Settings** on the left. Scroll down to **Business Owners** and click **Edit** ﻿﻿ next to the relevant Business Owner. Edit their details in the pop-up window. Click **Save**.

### To remove a Business Owner:

Go to [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2F) in your site's dashboard.Click **Wix Payments Dashboard**.Click **Account Settings** on the left. Scroll down to **Business Owners** and click **Remove **﻿﻿** **next to the relevant Business Owner.
