---
title: "Request: Accepting Online Payments Directly to Your Bank Account"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer a feature that transfers online payments from your site directly to your bank account unless you're located in the United States or France.

> **For users in the U.S. or France:**
>
> You can connect the following providers to accept online payments directly to your bank account:
> - [Choice](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-ach-by-choice-as-a-payment-provider) (United States)
> - [Payzen](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-payzen-by-lyra-as-a-payment-provider) (France)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
