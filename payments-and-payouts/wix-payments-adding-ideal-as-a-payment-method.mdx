Accept EUR payments from your customers via direct bank transfers using iDeal, processed by Wix Payments.

**General Info** 
Supported countryNetherlandsSupported currencyEURTransaction fees2.5% + €0.30

> **Before you begin:**
>
> You must set up [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) to accept payments via iDeal through Wix Payments.

### Connecting iDeal through Wix Payments

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage** next to Wix Payments.  Enable the toggle next to iDeal.

**Note**: You can disable the toggle at any time to remove iDeal from your payment options.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about Wix Payments.

### I currently accept iDeal payments through another payment provider. Can I change to accept iDeal payments through Wix Payments?

Yes. You can [switch to Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/switching-to-wix-payments) if you've already connected your Wix site to a different provider.

### How can I tell if a payment method is active?

In order to accept online payments, you must connect a payment provider. Once connected, your checkout will be active for your chosen payment method(s).

You need to have your account successfully verified to receive payouts.

### How often do I receive payouts?

Once your account setup is complete, payouts are sent daily, weekly or monthly, depending on the [payout schedule you choose](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-wix-payments-payout-schedule). Please allow 3-5 business days for the funds to appear in your bank account.
