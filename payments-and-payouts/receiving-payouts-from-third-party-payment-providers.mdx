---
title: "Receiving Payouts from Third-Party Payment Providers"
---

Wix provides everything you need to create an online business. Your payouts, however, are managed by the payment provider you have connected.

If you are accepting payments with our native provider, Wix Payments, please see [Receiving Payouts from Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-your-payouts-from-wix-payments).

If you are accepting payments with any payment provider other than Wix Payments, such as PayPal, Stripe, etc., contact your provider directly if you have queries about your payouts.
