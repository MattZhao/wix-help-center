---
title: "Wix Payments: Adding Afterpay as a Payment Method"
---

Add Afterpay as a payment method to help your customers spread their payment over a number of installments. Afterpay supports buy now, pay later (BNPL) transactions. This means you get fully paid upfront, while your customers can purchase today and make affordable payments over time.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - You must set up [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) to accept payments via Afterpay through Wix Payments.
> - Afterpay through Wix Payments is currently available only in the United States.
> - Items sold via Afterpay are subject to our list of [prohibited goods and services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-prohibited-products-and-services), as well as [Afterpay's payment terms](https://stripe.com/ie/legal/afterpay-clearpay#restricted-businesses). Learn more about [qualifying for Afterpay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-qualification-for-afterpay).

### Adding Afterpay

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage** next to Wix Payments.  Enable the toggle next to Afterpay.

**Note**: You can disable the toggle at any time to remove Afterpay from your payment options.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about using Afterpay in Wix.

### Can I connect Afterpay without connecting Wix Payments?

Yes. You can [connect Afterpay as a payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-afterpay-as-a-payment-provider-3515230) without connecting Wix Payments. Users in the United Kingdom can [connect Clearpay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-clearpay-as-a-payment-provider).

### Can I issue refunds for Afterpay payments?

Yes. You can refund Afterpay payments up to 120 days after the original payment. Once a refund is initiated, any further payments due are paused.
