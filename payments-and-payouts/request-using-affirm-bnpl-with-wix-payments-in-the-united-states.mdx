---
title: "Request: Using Affirm (BNPL) with Wix Payments in the United States"
---

We are delighted to announce that you can now [add Affirm as a BNPL payment method in Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-affirm-as-a-payment-method).

Affirm is a BNPL payment method, which enables your customers to pay over time, while you receive the money upfront. By offering more flexibility to your customers at checkout, you can increase your sales and improve your cash flow.
