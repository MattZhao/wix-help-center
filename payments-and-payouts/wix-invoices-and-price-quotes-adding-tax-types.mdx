---
title: "Wix Invoices & Price Quotes: Adding Tax Types"
---

Add different tax types (e.g., VAT, state tax) so that they're easily accessible when creating a new invoice or price quote.

When creating an invoice or price quote, the tax type appears when you click the drop-down menu under **Tax**. You can also add a new tax type directly from this drop-down menu.

### To set up taxes:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Getting paid**.   Click **+ Add New Tax** in the **Taxes** section.

Enter the **Tax name** (e.g., VAT, State Tax) and **Rate** (%).   Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> When creating a new invoice, you can set how taxes are calculated and displayed on the invoice.
>
> **Note: **This feature is currently only available for Invoices, but not Price Quotes.  
> 1. [Go to Invoices](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Invoices&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/invoices/invoices/list?referralInfo=KB-Article) in your site's dashboard.
> 2. Click **Create New** at the top right and select **Invoice**.
> 3. [Enter the relevant details](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-creating-an-invoice), including products or services.
> 4. Hover over the tooltip next to **Taxes** and click **Set tax calculations here**. 
>
> [image: A screenshot showing how to set tax calculation and display when creating new invoices.]
> 5. Then select whether you want to **Exclude tax from item price** or **Include tax in the item's price**.
> 6. Click **Save**. [image: A screenshot showing the options to incliude or exclude tax from an item's price when creating a new invoice.]
