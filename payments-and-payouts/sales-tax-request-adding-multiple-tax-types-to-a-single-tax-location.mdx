title: "Sales Tax Request: Adding Multiple Tax Types to a Single Tax Location"
---

We're thrilled to announce that it's now possible to calculate tax using 2 or more tax types (e.g. listing both federal tax and provincial tax separately in a Canadian province) for any of your tax groups. Learn how to [create multiple tax types](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-tax-calculation-using-the-manual-method#step-3-optional-customize-advanced-mode-settings).
