---
title: "Customer Didn't Receive Refund Issued for a Wix Payments Transaction"
---

> Wix Payments is not yet available to all users.

If you have refunded a customer for a payment made through Wix Payments and they didn't get the refund yet, please check the following:

Make sure you've refunded the customer using the instructions in [this article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refunding-customers-in-wix-payments). Allow 3-5 business days for the refund to reach the customer's credit card.

If your customer still hasn't received their refund after 3-5 business days, please [contact us](http://www.wix.com/contact) and we'll be happy to assist you further.
