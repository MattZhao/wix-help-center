---
title: "Request: Cash on Delivery (COD) as a Payment Method in India"
---

Currently, it is not possible to accept payments using cash on delivery / collect on delivery, where online payment is only collected once an order has been fulfilled.

> As a workaround, you can accept payments using the [manual payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
