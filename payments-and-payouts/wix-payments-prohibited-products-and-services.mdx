Wix is required to review and verify the information and the site content of anyone opening a Wix Payments account. We comply with authorities, regulators, payment processors and financial partners to create a transparent environment to help prevent any illegal activities or activities prohibited by our payment processor or other financial partners from being conducted.

As such, some products or services may be prohibited.

> **It is your responsibility to make sure that the products and services offered on your site are not included in the Prohibited Products and Services list. In such an event, Wix may hide or delete prohibited products, suspend or close your Wix Payments account, withhold or block funds, and/or refund payments to the relevant customers.**

> **Notes:**
>
> - The following article applies to the United States only. For other territories, please check the lists of prohibited products and services for our provider partners, [Adyen ](https://www.adyen.com/legal/list-restricted-prohibited)and [Stripe](https://stripe.com/restricted-businesses).
> - Merchants using Google Pay are also subject to Google Pay's list of [prohibited and restricted merchants, billers or business partners](https://payments.developers.google.com/terms/aup).
> - Merchants using Afterpay with Wix Payments are also subject to Afterpay's [terms of use](https://stripe.com/ie/legal/afterpay-clearpay#restricted-businesses).
> - Merchants using Affirm with Wix Payments are also subject to Affirm’s [payment terms](https://stripe.com/ie/legal/affirm).
> - Merchants using PayPal with Wix Payments are also subject to PayPal's [acceptable use policy](https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/acceptableuse-full).
