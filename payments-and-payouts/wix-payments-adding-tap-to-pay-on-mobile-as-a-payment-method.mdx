---
title: "Wix Payments: Adding Tap to Pay on Mobile as a Payment Method"
---

Add Tap to Pay on mobile as a payment method to help your customers pay quickly and securely using your mobile device to take the payment.

Tap to Pay on mobile lets you accept payment on your phone without connecting any external hardware. Customers can either tap a credit/debit card directly on your phone or use their own phones to pay you.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - You must set up [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) to accept payments via Tap to Pay on mobile.
> - You must [download the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app) to accept Tap to Pay payments on mobile.
> - Tap to Pay on mobile is currently available only in the United States.

General Info
**Supported countries**United States**Supported currencies**USD**Processing fees****USA:** 2.6% of the transaction amount + 0.20 USD**Notes**The currency used in your Wix account must match the currency in your Wix Payments account. **If the currencies do not match, the transactions may be declined.** Learn how to [change your currency](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments)

### Adding Tap to Pay

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage** next to Wix Payments.  Enable the toggle next to** Tap to Pay on mobile**.

**Note**: You can disable the toggle at any time to remove Tap to Pay from your payment options.

Go to your site in the Wix app.

1. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
2. Tap **Point of Sale **under** Getting Paid**.
3. Tap the **Connect** iconat the top right.
4. Tap **Tap to Pay on mobile**.
5. Tap **Activate**.

> **Important Information:**
>
> - Items sold via Tap to Pay are subject to our list of [prohibited goods and services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-prohibited-products-and-services).
> - Disabling card payments in Wix Payments will also disable Tap to Pay. You can, however, disable Tap to Pay payments without disabling card payments.
