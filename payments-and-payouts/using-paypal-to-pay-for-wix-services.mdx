---
title: "Using PayPal to Pay for Wix Services"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature may not yet be available in all regions.

You can choose to pay for all your Wix services using [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/) (e.g. plans, domains, business emails, etc). PayPal is a service that enables you to securely pay for products and services, send money, and accept payments. PayPal offers another layer of fraud protection and provides additional security and encryption for online transactions.

Use PayPal by selecting the PayPal option at the checkout. You will then be redirected to the PayPal website to finish your purchase. When completed, you will be brought back to your Wix account.

See our step-by-step guide for [using PayPal to purchase an upgrade plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-paypal-to-purchase-a-premium-plan).

> **Note:**
>
> You need to have an account with PayPal in order to use their service. If you don't have an account yet, the option to create one appears right after selecting the PayPal option.
