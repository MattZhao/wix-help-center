---
title: "Request: Implementing 3D Secure Payments"
---

Currently, 3D Secure (3DS) payments are supported in Wix for all providers operating within European countries where 3D Secure is required. However, 3D Secure is not yet fully supported for other regions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
