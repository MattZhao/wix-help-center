Wix currently allows you to receive payment through a variety of payment providers, depending on your location. View the full list of [available providers in your country](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country).

We are constantly working to add additional payment providers and we'd like to hear from you. Click a payment provider listed below to vote for it:

> **Can't find the provider you want on the list?**
>
> - If you want to request another payment provider, please [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact/en) to let us know.
> - Alternatively, you can connect unsupported payment providers out-of-the-box using [Velo](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites/articles/getting-started/about-velo-by-wix) and the [Payment Provider Custom Extension](https://dev.wix.com/docs/develop-websites/articles/code-tutorials/wix-pay/tutorial-payment-provider-custom-extension).
