### Connecting Bankful as a payment provider

Whether you have an existing Bankful account, or wish to set up a new account, follow the steps below.

#### To connect Bankful:

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.

**Tip:** Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it.  Click **See More Payment Options**.  Click **Connect** next to **Bankful**.

Depending on whether you are creating a new Bankful account or have an existing account, follow the instructions:

### Have an account

1. Enter your Username and Password.
2. Click **Connect**.

### Don't have an account

1. Click [**Create Bankful account**](https://bankful.com/wix-partner/).
2. Click **Sign Up** (on the Bankful site).
3. Fill in the registration form and click **Continue**.
4. Wait for an email from Bankful to get your credentials.
5. Return to the Accept Payments tab (repeat steps 1-4) and follow the steps under **Have an account** to connect your account.

### Managing your Bankful payment methods

Once you've connected Bankful as your payment provider, you can enable or disable your payment methods (credit card, wallet and cryptocurrency) as you need them.

#### To manage your payment methods:

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage** next to **Bankful**.  Use the toggles to enable or disable your Bankful payment methods.

**Tip**: To accept cryptocurrency payments, you'll also need to [enable Bitcoin payment processing in your Bankful account](https://support.pinwheelpay.com/paying-with-bitcoin-in-wix/).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about accepting payments.

### Can I connect Bankful only for cryptocurrency payments?

If you wish to use another payment provider for credit card transactions, you can connect Bankful as a payment provider for cryptocurrency transactions only. To do this, [connect Bankful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-bankful-as-a-payment-provider-for-cryptocurrency-wallet-and-credit-card-payments#connecting-bankful-as-a-payment-provider) using the steps above, and enable only the Crypto by Bankful payment method toggle.

### How can I tell if a payment method is active?

In order to accept online payments, you must connect a payment provider. For Bankful, follow the steps above to accept online payments. Once connected, you need to have your account successfully verified to receive payouts.

### Is my currency supported?

Be aware that payments will fail If you are using an unsupported currency.

The currency used in your payment provider account must match the [currency that you set in your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments). If you need to change the currency set in your provider account, please contact your payment provider.
If your current payment provider does not support your desired currency, Wix hosts a wide range of [alternative payment providers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country). Learn how to [change your payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-payment-provider-for-accepting-credit-card-payments).

### What is a minimum payment amount?

Some payment providers have a minimum payment threshold (e.g. $1.01). Payments below this amount will not be processed.
Make sure that all of your products or services are priced over the minimum threshold. This includes discounted items or [Pricing Plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-about-recurring-plans). 
Please contact your payment provider to get the most up-to-date information about their minimum payment threshold.

### Can I offer recurring payments?

To offer recurring payments, you must connect a [payment provider that supports recurring payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments).

### How do I get my payouts?

Payouts are managed by your payment provider. If your provider is a third-party provider, [contact them directly](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-payouts-from-third-party-payment-providers).

If your provider is Wix Payments, learn about [receiving payouts from Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-your-payouts-from-wix-payments).
