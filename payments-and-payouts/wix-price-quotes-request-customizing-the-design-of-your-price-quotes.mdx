---
title: "Wix Price Quotes Request: Customizing the Design of your Price Quotes"
---

Currently, it is not possible to customize the design of price quotes you send to customers. You can edit the content and add your own notes, but cannot control the look of the page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
