---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Customizing the Design of Your Invoices"
---

Currently, it is not possible to customize the design of the invoices you send to customers. You can edit the content and add your own notes, but cannot control the look of the page, including fixed wording, colors and fonts.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
