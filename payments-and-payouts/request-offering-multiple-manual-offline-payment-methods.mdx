---
title: "Request: Offering Multiple Manual (Offline) Payment Methods"
---

Currently, Wix does not have a feature that would allow you to display multiple [manual (offline) payment methods](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments) to your customers.

If you offer several offline payment options (e.g. cash, bank transfer, check), use the instructions section to explain how to pay with each one.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
