---
title: "About Goods and Services Tax in Australia"
---

Wix charges Goods and Services Tax (GST) on premium subscriptions, applications and products in Australia in accordance with the [Australian Taxation Office](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/international-tax-for-business/gst-on-imported-goods-and-services/gst-on-imported-services-and-digital-products).

If you are a registered business in Australia with an ABN from the Australian Taxation Office, you may be eligible for a tax exemption.

You can add your ABN to your Wix account by updating your payment details.

---

### Updating your payment details

Update your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ ABN to get your tax exemption.

#### To update your payment details:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **Update Payment Method**.  Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click **Next**.   Click **Edit Details**.

Re-enter your card details and add your tax number to the **ABN **field.   Click **Update**.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about tax in Australia.

### What is an ABN?

Australian business number (ABN) is a tax ID issued to businesses that identifies your business to the government. It is a unique 11-digit number.

### What happens if I do not have a tax identification number?

If you do not have an ABN, Wix will charge and collect GST on payments for subscriptions, applications and products.

### How does Wix determine which customers are subject to tax?

The tax charge is based on the billing account address.

### What happens if my business is tax exempt, but I was charged GST?

If your business is tax exempt but you were charged GST for purchasing or renewing a Wix service, please [contact Wix Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact/en) for an exemption request.
