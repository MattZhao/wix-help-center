---
title: "Accepting Recurring Payments"
---

Some Wix apps (e.g. Wix Stores, Wix Video, Pricing Plans, Recurring Invoices) allow you to accept recurring payments for services that renew on a continuing basis.

The following payment providers support recurring payments. Click a provider to check if it's available in your region and learn how to connect it:

Payment ProviderRecurring Payment Limitations[Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments)Recurring payments are only available for credit/debit card payments.[Wix Payment Brazil](https://support.wix.com/pt/article/wix-pagamentos-brasil-conectando-como-m%C3%A9todo-de-pagamento)
- Recurring payments are only available for credit card payments.
- Recurring payments are not compatible with [installment payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-in-installments-bnpl).
[Bankful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-bankful-as-a-payment-provider-for-cryptocurrency-wallet-and-credit-card-payments#connecting-bankful-as-a-payment-provider)Recurring payments are only available for credit/debit card payments.[Cal powered by PayMe](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-cal-powered-by-payme-as-a-payment-provider)Recurring payments are only available for credit/debit card payments.[Evergreen Payments Northwest](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-evergreen-payments-northwest-as-a-payment-provider)
- Recurring payments are only available for credit card payments.
- Recurring payments are not compatible with [manual card entry payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-by-manual-card-entry).
[Eway](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-eway-as-a-payment-provider)None[Isracard powered by PayMe](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-isracard-as-a-payment-provider)None[KOMOJU](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-degica-komoju-as-a-payment-provider)Recurring payments are only available for credit card payments.[MagicPay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-magicpay-as-a-payment-provider)None[Stripe](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-stripe-as-a-payment-provider)
- Recurring payments are only available for credit/debit card payments.
- The minimum amount per recurring payment must be $0.5.
[Tilopay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-tilopay-as-a-payment-provider)Recurring payments are only available for credit card payments.[Tranzila](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-tranzila-as-a-payment-provider)Recurring payments are only available for credit card payments.[PayPal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider)
- Recurring payments are only available for credit card payments.
- A merchant must have a PayPal business account to accept recurring payments.
[Priority Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-priority-payments-as-a-payment-provider)Recurring payments are only available for credit/debit card payments.
