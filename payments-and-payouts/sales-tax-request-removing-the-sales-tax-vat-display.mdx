title: "Sales Tax Request: Removing the Sales Tax / VAT Display"
---

Currently, if you do not collect tax from customers, a 0% tax rate is displayed on the Checkout Page. It is not possible to remove the tax display completely.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
