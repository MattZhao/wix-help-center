---

Accept payments from your customers in various methods using YooKassa, also known as YooMoney.

> As of March 7 2022, Wix no longer supports Yookassa payments for any businesses, both new and existing, based in Russia.

General Info
Supported countriesRussiaSupported payment methodsCredit cards, Yandex.Money, WebMoney, Qiwi, online bank transfers and more. For more more details, click [here](https://yookassa.ru/en/payments/)Transaction feesVaries. For more details, click [here](https://yookassa.ru/en/fees/)NotesThe currency used in your Wix account must match the currency in your YooKassa account. **If the currencies do not match, the transactions may be declined. **Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments) to learn how to change your currency.

> **About the “Payment subject” and “Payment method” fields in YooKassa:**
>
> - These fields must be filled in only if you use an online sales register from one of YooKassa’s partners.
> - **Payment subject** is the category of what you sell. For example: a product, service, or in-game bet.
> - **Payment method** is the category of the way you accept payments. For example: a loan, pre-payment, or full payment.
> - In the form, select the necessary values separately for products and delivery: the app for payment acceptance via Wix will pass this information in receipts.
> - If you haven’t activated **fiscalization** via YooKassa, just leave these fields as they are.
>
> For more assistance, you can contact [YooKassa Support](mailto:merchants@yoomoney.ru).
