---
title: "Request: Additional Payment Methods Accepted by Wix"
---

Wix currently accepts payment by credit / debit card. Depending on your location, Wix also accepts PayPal payments.

At this time, it is not possible to purchase a paid service from Wix using any payment method **not** mentioned above.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated. Feel free to [contact Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact) to suggest additional payment methods.

---

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about payment for Wix services.

### What credit cards does Wix accept for payment?

Wix accepts all major credit cards including VISA, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, JCB, Diners, and UnionPay.**Note**: UnionPay payment is not yet available to all users.

### What debit cards does Wix accept for payment?

Wix accepts Visa and Mastercard debit cards.

### Can I use PayPal to pay for Wix services?

You can use PayPal to purchase a Premium plan or Studio plan, a domain, or items from the Wix Logo Maker. Find out more about [using PayPal as a payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-paypal-to-pay-for-wix-services-684702).

**Note:** PayPal as a payment method is not yet available in all regions.

### Does Wix accept payments over the phone?

No. Wix cannot accept payment over the phone due to [PCI Compliance](https://support.wix.com/en/article/security-of-wixs-billing-services-and-pci-compliance), because all phone conversations with Wix Customer Care are recorded and monitored.
