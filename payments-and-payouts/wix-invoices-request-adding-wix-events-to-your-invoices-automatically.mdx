---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Adding Wix Events to Your Invoices Automatically"
---

Currently, Wix Invoices does not automatically add Wix Events as an item when you create a new invoice. This information must be entered manually.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
