---
title: "Request: Disabling Notifications from Stripe"
---

We are pleased to announce that automatic emails - to both you and your customers - following every successful payment are no longer the default experience for Stripe payments made through Wix.

> **Tip:**
>
> I you want to re-enable these notification emails, you can do so from your [Stripe account](https://dashboard.stripe.com/login).
