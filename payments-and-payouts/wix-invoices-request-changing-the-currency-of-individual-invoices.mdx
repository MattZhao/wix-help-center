---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Changing the Currency of Individual Invoices"
---

Currently in Wix Invoices it is not possible to change the currency for each invoice you create. You can change your invoice currency in the [Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings) tab of your dashboard, but this applies to all your invoices.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
