---
title: "Downloading Invoices for Wix Subscriptions"
---

> **Note:**
>
> The following article refers to downloading invoices for your Wix subscriptions. For information on downloading invoices that you've sent to your clients, [learn about Wix Invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-managing-your-invoices).

Download invoices for Wix subscriptions and save them for your records. Invoices are downloaded in PDF format.

### To download an invoice:

[Go to Billing History](https://www.wix.com/account/billing-history?referralInfo=KB_Article) in your Wix account.  Click the** **More Actions icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **View Invoice**.  Choose an option:

1. Click the Download icon  to download your invoice.
2. Click the Print icon  to print your invoice.
