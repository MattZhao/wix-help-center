---
title: "Wix Payments: Receiving Your First Payout"
---

Every payment you accept through Wix Payments must be [processed ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/processing-payments-in-wix-payments)and [scheduled ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/scheduled-payouts-in-wix-payments)before we can send it to your bank account.

Your first payout can only be scheduled once your Wix Payments account is [successfully verified](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-verification-process). After receiving your first payment, it's available for payout within an average of 7 days.

You can see when your payout is scheduled to be sent directly from the **Payouts** area in your Wix Payments dashboard. Once sent, it can take between 3-5 business days for the payout to reach your bank account.

For future payouts, you have the option to [select a schedule](https://support.wix.com/en/article/scheduled-payouts-in-wix-payments#payout-schedules) (daily, weekly, monthly) that fits your needs.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Incomplete Wix Payments account [verification](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-verification-process) may delay the timing of your first payout.
