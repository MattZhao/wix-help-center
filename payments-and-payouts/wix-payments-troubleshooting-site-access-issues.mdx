---
title: "Wix Payments: Troubleshooting Site Access Issues"
---

During the verification process of your Wix Payments account, we need to access your site. If your site is inaccessible and cannot be verified, it could disrupt your payment processing and delay payouts.

This guide can help you troubleshoot and resolve these access issues to keep your business running smoothly.

Click below to learn more about the possible causes for site access issues and how to resolve them.

### Your site is not published

For your site to be accessible, it needs to be published. If you have not published your site yet, you can follow the below steps:
1. Click **Publish** at the top right of the editor.
2. (Optional) Click **View Site **to be taken to your live site.

### Your site has been deleted

Deleting your Wix site means Wix Payments cannot scan it for approval. In order to continue the verification process, you need to [restore your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-deleting-or-restoring-a-site#restoring-a-site-from-the-trash) and publish it. Wix Payments will then automatically attempt to scan your site again.

### Your domain is expired

[When a domain has expired](https://support.wix.com/en/article/domain-life-cycle), your site will no longer be accessible and you will need to renew the domain. To check if your domain is expired, you can follow the below steps:
**For domains hosted at Wix: **
1. [Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) in your Wix account.
2. Check under **Details** to see if the domain is expired or when it expires.
3. (If your domain is expired) [Renew your domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/extending-your-wix-domain-subscription) or [re-activate](https://support.wix.com/en/article/icann-reactivating-your-domain) it.

**For domains hosted elsewhere:**You will need to contact your domain host for further assistance. Once your domain is active again, Wix Payments will be able to access your site.

### Your new domain is still propagating

[Domain propagation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-propagation) is the process of updating name servers around the world following any changes to your Domain Name System (DNS) records. It can take up to 48 hours to complete. 
Once your domain has propagated, you need to unpublish and republish your site. Wix payments should then be able to access your site.

### Your domain has SSL issues

The Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) is a security measure used by websites and browsers for communicating over the internet. 
In order to continue the verification process, go ahead and fix the [SSL issues](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-your-ssl-certificate).

### A third-party app is restricting your site in certain geographic locations

There are many third-party apps in the Wix App Market that allow you to block traffic to your site from a certain geographic location. Alternatively, this can also be achieved using code. 
Remove the restriction within the third-party app or your site's code, unpublish your site and then publish it again

### (Using POS only) You don’t have a site and you don’t plan to have one

This is no problem, Wix Payments can still complete the verification process. However, this will need to be handled via our [Customer Care team](https://www.wix.com/contact). Please contact us for further assistance.

#### Still need help?

If you still need assistance with website access issues, try [unpublishing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-unpublishing-your-site) and [publishing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site#faqs) your site and wait 24 hours.
