---
title: "Wix Payments: Adding Klarna as a Payment Method"
---

Add Klarna as a payment method to help your customers spread their payment over several installments. Klarna supports buy now, pay later (BNPL) transactions. You get fully paid upfront, while your customers can purchase today and make affordable payments over time.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - You must set up [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) to accept payments via Klarna through Wix Payments.
> - Klarna through Wix Payments is currently available only in the United States.
> - Items sold via Klarna are subject to our list of [prohibited goods and services](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-restricted-and-prohibited-products-and-services), as well as [Klarna's shipping policy](https://www.klarna.com/international/merchant-protection-program/). Learn more about [qualifying for Klarna](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-qualification-for-klarna).

### Adding Klarna

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Manage** next to Wix Payments.  Enable the toggle next to Klarna.

**Note**: You can disable the toggle at any time to remove Klarna from your payment options.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about using Klarna with Wix.

### Can I connect Klarna without connecting Wix Payments?

Yes. You can [connect Klarna as a payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-klarna-as-a-payment-provider) without connecting Wix Payments.

### Can I issue refunds for Klarna payments?

Yes. You can refund Klarna payments up to 180 days after the original payment. Once a refund is initiated, any further payments due are paused.
