---
title: "Automatic Payment Taken from Your Credit Card"
---

To simplify the billing process for you, we offer yearly and monthly subscriptions, which are billed automatically on a monthly, yearly, or multi-year basis depending on your billing cycle.

If you have noticed an automatic payment charged to your card, it is likely for the renewal of one of your subscriptions (for example, a site plan, domain, Google Workspace mailbox or app upgrade.

You can check your current subscriptions in the [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) section of your Wix account, and you can view invoices for payments made to Wix on the [Billing History](https://www.wix.com/account/billing-history?referralAdditionalInfo=account-manager) page. Learn more about [viewing your invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-invoices-paid-for-wix-services).

> **Note:**
>
> If you are still unsure what the payment was for, please [contact us](http://www.wix.com/contact) and we will assist you further.
