---
title: "Wix Price Quotes: Converting Price Quotes into Invoices"
---

Price Quotes is closely connected to Wix Invoices. After a client accepts your price quote, you can convert it into an invoice to receive the payment. All the information from the quote will automatically be included in the invoice.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Enabling auto-conversion to invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-price-quotes-converting-price-quotes-into-invoices#enabling-automatic-conversion-to-invoices)  [Manually converting a price quote to an invoice](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-price-quotes-converting-price-quotes-into-invoices#manually-converting-a-price-quote-to-an-invoice)

---

### Enabling automatic conversion to invoices

By default, price quotes do not automatically convert to invoices when they are accepted. You can choose to enable the auto-conversion so that your price quotes are automatically turned into invoices when they're accepted.

#### To enable auto-conversion:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Getting Paid Settings**.  Click **Price Quotes**.  Enable the **Convert accepted quotes **toggle.

---

### Manually converting a price quote to an invoice

If auto-conversion is disabled, follow the steps below to convert a price quote into an invoice automatically.

#### To manually convert a price quote into an invoice:

[Go to Price Quotes](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Price%20Quotes&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/price-quotes) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the price quote you want to convert.  Click **Convert to Invoice**.

Click **Convert** to confirm.

> **Tip:**
>
> Once a price quote becomes an invoice, it appears in [Invoices](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Invoices&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/invoices/invoices/list?referralInfo=KB-Article) where you can manage and track it.
