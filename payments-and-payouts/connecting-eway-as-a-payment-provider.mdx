Accept card payments from your customers in Australia and New Zealand using Eway as a payment provider. Eway offers [recurring payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments), [manual card entry](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-by-manual-card-entry), and [3DS verification](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-3d-secure-3ds-payments-with-third-party-payment-providers).

> - From June 2024, an improved Eway experience for online payments is available to connect. This includes improved capabilities, like the ability to set up recurring payments, accepting manual card entry and enabling 3DS security.
> - For existing Eway users, to join the new experience for online payments, you can disconnect and then reconnect your Eway account (see FAQs).

### Connecting Eway

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.

**Tip:** Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it.  Click **See More Payment Options**.  Click **See Options** next to Third-Party Credit / Debit Card Providers.  Click **Connect** next to Eway.

Depending on whether you need to create a new Eway account or have an existing account, follow the instructions:

### Have an account

1. Enter your API Key and Password.
2. Click **Connect**.

### Don't have an account

1. Click [**Create Eway Account**](https://myeway.force.com/success/accelerator-signup?pid=4382&pa=001w0000016kQs2AAE) and fill in the form to create your account.
2. An Eway representative will call you to complete the registration process. Ask the representative for your API key.

**Note:** Make sure to ask Eway for your API Key rather than your Merchant ID or Customer ID.
3. Return to this window (repeat steps 1-4), and follow the steps under **Have an account** to connect your account.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about accepting payments.

### I have an existing Eway connection. How do I disconnect and reconnect Eway to use the improved experience?

In order to improve your Eway experience:
1. [Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/).
2. Click **Manage** next to Eway.

[image: A screenshot showing where to click manage in order to disconnect Eway.]
3. Click **Disconnect Eway **and follow the prompts to disconnect your account.
4. To reconnect, return to the Accept Payments tab and follow the [steps to connect Eway](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-eway-as-a-payment-provider#connecting-eway).

### How can I tell if a payment method is active?

In order to accept online payments, you must connect a payment provider. Once connected, your checkout will be active for your chosen payment method(s).

You need to have your account successfully verified to receive payouts.

### Is my currency supported?

Be aware that payments will fail If you are using an unsupported currency.

The currency used in your payment provider account must match the [currency that you set in your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments). If you need to change the currency set in your provider account, please contact your payment provider.
If your current payment provider does not support your desired currency, Wix hosts a wide range of [alternative payment providers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country). Learn how to [change your payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-payment-provider-for-accepting-credit-card-payments).

### What is a minimum payment amount?

Some payment providers have a minimum payment threshold (e.g. $1.01). Payments below this amount will not be processed.
Make sure that all of your products or services are priced over the minimum threshold. This includes discounted items or [Pricing Plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-about-recurring-plans). 
Please [contact your payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-payouts-from-third-party-payment-providers) to get the most up-to-date information about their minimum payment threshold.

### How do I get my payouts?

Payouts are managed by your payment provider. If your provider is a third-party provider, [contact them directly](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-payouts-from-third-party-payment-providers).

If your provider is Wix Payments, learn about [receiving payouts from Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-your-payouts-from-wix-payments).

### My customers' payments are being declined. What can I do?

If your customers are experiencing payment failures, you may be able to fix the issue by [troubleshooting issues for accepting payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-issues-for-accepting-payments).

### Why can’t I connect my desired payment provider?

If you’re having trouble connecting your payment provider, you may be able to fix the issue by [troubleshooting common connection issues](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-third-party-provider-connection-issues).

### Do I need to upgrade my site to accept payments?

Yes. You need to upgrade your site to a [plan that supports payments](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website) in order to accept payments.
