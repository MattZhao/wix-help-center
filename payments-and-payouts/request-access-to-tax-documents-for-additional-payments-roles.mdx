title: "Request: Access to Tax Documents for Additional Payments Roles"
---

We're pleased to announce that additional roles can now be assigned to view the Tax Documents page in your Wix Payments account. These roles are Admin (Co-Owner) and Payments Admin. Learn more about [roles and permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview).
