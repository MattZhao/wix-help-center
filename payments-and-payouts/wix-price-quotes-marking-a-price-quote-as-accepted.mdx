---
title: "Wix Price Quotes: Marking a Price Quote as Accepted"
---

Manually mark price quotes as "accepted" when you come to an agreement with a client.

### To manually mark a price quote as "accepted":

Go to [Price Quotes](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Price%20Quotes&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fprice-quotes) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Show More **icon  next to the price quote you want to edit.  Click **Mark as Accepted**.

> **What's next?**
>
> You can now convert the price quote to an invoice that your client can pay online. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/converting-price-quotes-into-invoices)
