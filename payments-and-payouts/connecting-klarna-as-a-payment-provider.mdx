### Connecting Klarna as a payment provider

Whenever possible, Wix automatically connects you to the most suitable provider available in your location. If you prefer to use Klarna, and it's available in your location, follow the steps below:

#### To connect Klarna:

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **See More Payment Options**.   Click **Connect** beside Klarna.

Depending on whether you are creating a new Klarna account or have an existing account, follow the instructions:

### Have an account

1. Enter your Klarna API keys (username and Password).

**Note**: Playground credentials aren't accepted.
2. Click **Connect**.

### Don't have an account

1. Click **Create Klarna Account** and fill in the information to create your account.
2. Return to this window (repeat steps 1-4), and follow the steps under **Have an account** to connect your account.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about accepting payments.

### How do I find my API keys in my Klarna account?

Klarna API keys consist of 2 elements: your **Klarna API username **and your** Password. **Once you've created your Klarna account, you can generate these credentials by going to the Settings app in the Klarna Merchant Portal for your location:
- [US](https://us.portal.klarna.com/)
- [Canada](https://portal.klarna.com/?utm_source=klarnacom&utm_campaign=/ca/business/merchant-support/what-is-the-merchant-portal/&utm_medium=referral&utm_content=klarnacom-link)
- [Europe](https://eu.portal.klarna.com/)

**Note**: For Klarna users in Austria, France, Ireland, and Portugal, please [contact Klarna](https://www.klarna.com/international/customer-service/) directly to set up your account.
- [Australia and New Zealand](https://oc.portal.klarna.com/)

**Note**: The API keys are separate from your Merchant ID. If needed, it's possible to have several API keys associated with the same Merchant ID.

### How can I tell if a payment method is active?

In order to accept online payments, you must connect a payment provider. For Klarna, follow the steps above to accept online payments. Once connected, you need to have your account successfully verified to receive payouts.

### Is my currency supported?

Be aware that payments will fail If you are using an unsupported currency.

The currency used in your payment provider account must match the [currency that you set in your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments). If you need to change the currency set in your provider account, please contact your payment provider.
If your current payment provider does not support your desired currency, Wix hosts a wide range of [alternative payment providers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country). Learn how to [change your payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-payment-provider-for-accepting-credit-card-payments).

### What is a minimum payment amount?

Some payment providers have a minimum payment threshold (e.g. $1.01). Payments below this amount will not be processed.
Make sure that all of your products or services are priced over the minimum threshold. This includes discounted items or [Pricing Plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-about-recurring-plans). 
Please contact your payment provider to get the most up-to-date information about their minimum payment threshold.

### Can I offer recurring payments?

To offer recurring payments, you must connect a [payment provider that supports recurring payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments).

### How do I get my payouts?

Payouts are managed by your payment provider. If your provider is a third-party provider, [contact them directly](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-payouts-from-third-party-payment-providers).

If your provider is Wix Payments, learn about [receiving payouts from Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-your-payouts-from-wix-payments).
