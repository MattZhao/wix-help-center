---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Recurring Invoices Which are Paid Manually"
---

Currently, you can [issue invoices for recurring payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-creating-a-recurring-invoice), but it is not possible to create recurring invoices which are paid manually.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
