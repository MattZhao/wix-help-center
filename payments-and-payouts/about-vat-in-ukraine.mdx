---
title: "About VAT in Ukraine"
---

Wix charges Value Added Tax (VAT) on premium subscriptions, applications and products in Ukraine.

If you are a registered business in Ukraine, you may be eligible for a tax exemption. You can add your VAT ID to your Wix account by updating your payment details. The exemption will apply from the next payment after that date.

---

### Updating your payment details

Update your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ VAT number to get your tax exemption.

#### To update your payment details:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **Update payment method**.  Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click **Next**.   Click **Edit Details**.

Re-enter your card details and add your VAT number to the **VAT ID** field.   Click **Save**.

Click a question below to learn more about VAT in Ukraine.

### What is a VAT ID number?

Value Added Tax (VAT) is a tax on the consumption or use of goods and services. In Ukraine, a VAT ID is a unique 10 or 12 digit number that identifies a person or entity as being registered for VAT in Ukraine.

### What happens if I don't have a VAT ID number?

If you do not have a tax identification number, Wix will charge and collect VAT on payments for subscriptions, applications and products.

### How does Wix determine which customers are subject to tax?

The tax charge is based on the billing account address.
