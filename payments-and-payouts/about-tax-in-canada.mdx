From **July 2021**, Wix is required to collect tax on certain subscriptions, applications and products based on such items’ [taxability in Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/federal-government-budgets/faq-relation-electronic-commerce-supplies.html).

Depending on your location, you may be subject to Goods and Services Tax (GST), Harmonized Sales Tax (HST), Provincial Sales Tax (PST), Québec Sales Tax (QST), or a combination of some of these taxes.

If you are a GST/HST registered business in Canada with a GST/HST number from the Canada Revenue Agency, you may be eligible for a tax exemption.

If you are also a QST registered business in Canada with a QST ID, you may be eligible for a QST exemption.

You can add your GST/HST number and your QST number to your Wix account by updating your payment details.

> For users in British Colombia, PST applies even if you add a GST/HST number.

### Updating your payment details

Update your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ GST/HST number and your QST number to get your tax exemption.

#### To update your payment details:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **Update Payment Method**.  Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click **Next**.   Click **Edit Details**.   Re-enter your card details and add your tax IDs to the **GST/HST/QST ID **fields as applicable.   Click **Update**.

### What is a GST/HST tax identification number?

For businesses and organizations, a tax identification number is a unique 9-digit business number issued by the Canada Revenue Agency. The 9-digit number is usually followed by two letters (program identifier) and 4 digits (reference number). 
An example tax ID number looks like this: 123456789RT0001

### What happens if I do not have a tax identification number?

If you do not have a tax identification number, Wix will charge GST/HST on some subscriptions, applications and products.

### How does Wix determine which customers are subject to Canadian tax?

The tax charge is based on the billing account address. Depending on your location, you may be subject to Goods and Services Tax (GST), Harmonized Sales Tax (HST), Provincial Sales Tax (PST), Québec Sales Tax (QST), or a combination of these taxes.
