---
title: "Connecting Giropay as a Payment Method (Through Wix Payments)"
---

Giropay is no longer supported as a payment method for Wix Payments. If you're a Wix Payments user who wishes to continue accepting Giropay, you can do so by [connecting Mollie as a payment provider for Giropay payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-mollie-as-a-payment-provider).

You can still process your other active payment methods through Wix Payments. To do this, connect Mollie and enable only the Giropay payment method toggle.
