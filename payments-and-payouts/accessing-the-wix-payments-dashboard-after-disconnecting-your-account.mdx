---
title: "Accessing the Wix Payments Dashboard After Disconnecting Your Account"
---

If, for whatever reason, your Wix Payments account has been disconnected, you can still access all the information you need about past transactions, payouts and more.

### To access your Wix Payments account:

[Sign in to your Wix account](https://www.wix.com/).  Click your account name at the top right.  Click **Wix Payments Accounts**.

Click **View** on the Wix Payments enabled site account you wish to see.  Select the information you want to view. Choose between:

- **Payments**
- **Payouts**
- **Settlement Report**
- **Account Details**

> **Tip:**
>
> Click on each payment to see more details about that payment.
