---
title: "About Reserve Funds in Your Wix Payments Account"
---

In some cases, Wix Payments needs to hold a portion of **each payout** for a set amount of time. This is known as a reserve.

Keeping a percentage of your funds in reserve lets you easily cover refunds or potential chargebacks from customers. After a period of time, your account may be reviewed to see if a reserve is still applicable.

There are two kinds of reserves Wix can hold:

**Rolling reserve:** This is the most common type of reserve. A rolling reserve is when a percent of each payout is held for a set number of days before being released in the next available payout. Rolling reserves are held from each payout on an ongoing basis.  **One-time reserve**: This is a specific amount of money held from your available balance one time for a set number of days, then released in the next available payout.

> On rare occasions, the reserve release date might be **extended** after your account is reviewed. This means certain reserve amounts will be sent with a later payout. This allows us to continue to cover potential chargebacks and refunds.

From your Wix Payments **Payouts** area, you can see exactly how much is kept in reserve and when it should be released.

In this example, you have a total of $75.00 currently held in reserve.The $75.00 will gradually be released on a rolling basis. In other words:

- The $15.00 which was held from the Jan 4 payout will be released 90 days later on April 4.
- The $40.00 which was held from the Jan 5 payout will be released 90 days later on April 5.
- The $20.00 which was held from the Jan 6  payout will be released 90 days later on April 6.

After each reserve amount is released, it is added to your next scheduled payout. The date of the payout it will be added to depends on your payout schedule ([daily, weekly, monthly](https://support.wix.com/en/article/scheduled-payouts-in-wix-payments#payout-schedules)).

### To view your reserve funds:

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2F) in your site's dashboard.  Click **View Payouts** next to Wix Payments.

Click **Reserve Funds**.   Do one of the following:

- Click **In reserve** to see the funds currently in reserve.
- Click **Released** to see a list of the funds we've already released.
