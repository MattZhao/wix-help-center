---
title: "Payment Renewal Using an Expired Credit Card"
---

Providing our users secure and uninterrupted service is one of our top priorities.

In an effort to provide such service to our users, Wix participates in Visa® and MasterCard® account update services whereby banks may notify us of changes to credit card expiration dates and card numbers held by our users.

This means that Wix automatically updates your credit card information that you have saved so that we can renew your subscriptions as scheduled. Similar services may be supported by other card brands.

If this is the case, your payments and subscriptions are not affected by changes to credit card details.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can update your card details if your card has expired or if your renewal payment has failed or declined. [Learn more about updating your payment method in your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/updating-payment-details-if-renewal-payment-failed-or-is-declined).
