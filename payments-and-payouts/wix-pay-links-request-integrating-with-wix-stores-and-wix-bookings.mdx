---
title: "Wix Pay Links Request: Integrating with Wix Stores and Wix Bookings"
---

Currently, it is not possible to integrate Wix Pay Links with Wix Stores or Wix Bookings. This integration would allow you to charge customers for your Stores or Bookings items using pay links.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
