---
title: "Request: Using Apex Payment Solutions as a Payment Provider"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer Apex Payment Solutions as a payment provider.  If you use the [Manual payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments), you can mention that you accept payments through Apex Payment Solutions and arrange the payment offline.
