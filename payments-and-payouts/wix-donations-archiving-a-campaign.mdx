---
title: "Wix Donations: Archiving a Campaign"
---

Archive campaigns that are no longer active. This removes the donation form from your live site and moves it to the **Archived campaigns** tab in the dashboard.

> **Important:**
>
> If your campaign is collecting recurring donations, cancel them before archiving your campaign.

### To archive a campaign:

[Go to Wix Donations](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Donations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/donations) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant campaign.  Click **Archive campaign**.  Click **Archive campaign** to confirm.  Update the site page with the campaign form.

**Note: **Archiving a campaign removes it from your live site and leaves a blank space. Make any necessary updates to the site page displays correctly.
