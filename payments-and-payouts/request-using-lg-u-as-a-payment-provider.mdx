---
title: "Request: Using LG U+ as a Payment Provider"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer LG U+ as a payment provider.  If you use the Manual payment method, you can mention that you accept payments through LG U+ and arrange the payment offline.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
