title: "Wix Invoices: Managing Your Invoices in the Wix App"
---

Review and manage all of your customer invoices on the go using the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview). You can edit invoices, duplicate them, resend them, share them via a unique link, and more. To simplify your accounting processes, you can charge customer credit cards directly from your app.

**In this article, learn how to: **

[Review your invoices in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-managing-your-invoices-in-the-wix-owner-app#reviewing-your-invoices)  [Manage individual invoices in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-managing-your-invoices-in-the-wix-owner-app#managing-individual-invoices)

---

### Reviewing your invoices

When you access Invoices in your Wix app, you'll see a list of all the invoices you've created.

#### To review your invoices:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).﻿  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Manage Invoices **under **Getting Paid**.  Review your existing invoices in the **Invoices** tab. Tap an individual invoice to view more information or edit a draft invoice.  (Optional) Tap the **Totals** tab to view total paid, sent and overdue invoice amounts.

### Managing individual invoices

There are many ways you can manage your invoices using the Wix app. Make edits and then resend an invoice by email or share via a unique link. Duplicate an existing invoice to save time when creating a new one. Charge a customer's card directly from their invoice, and more.

> **Note:**
>
> Some invoice management (for example, printing or deleting an invoice) can only be done [from your dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-managing-your-invoices).

#### To manage your invoices:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).﻿  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Manage Invoices **under **Getting Paid**.  Tap the invoice you want to manage.

**Note:** If an invoice is in Draft status, tapping it will give you the chance to edit it before following step 6 below.  Tap the **More Actions** icon   at the top of your screen.   Choose what you want to do:

### Send via email

Send or resend an invoice if your customer didn't receive it or accidentally deleted it.

1. Tap **Send via email**.
2. Review or edit the content of the email your customer will receive.
3. Tap **Send**.

**Note:** It is not possible to issue an invoice showing a refund. [Vote](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-request-creating-a-negative-balance-invoice-for-refundedreturned-products-or-services) for the ability to do so.

### Share link

Share links to Wix Invoices via any platform or channel. By sending customers links to their invoices, they can pay for your goods or services without having to search for your invoice in their email inbox.

1. Tap **Share link**.
2. Share your link via one of your device's native apps.

**Note: **When you create a link for a draft invoice, it will be marked as Sent. This action can't be undone.

### Enter card details manually

You can handle the payment of an invoice by charging a customer's credit card.

1. Tap **Enter credit card manually**.
2. Enter your customer's credit card details.
3. Tap **Next**.

**Note:** You can only charge the full invoice amount or the deposit amount (if defined). You cannot charge any other amount.

### Charge with card reader

[Set up a mobile point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale) to accept payments for invoices using your mobile device and a card reader with Wix Mobile POS.  

**Note:** If you are based in the US, you can also set up tap to pay using certain mobile devices. 

1. Tap **Charge with card reader**.
2. Select a payment option:

- **Card reader: **Let customer swipe, insert, or tap the customer's credit card.
- **(US only) Tap to Pay **(**iPhone or Android): **Let customer tap their card or phone.

Learn more about [charging clients at a point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-charging-clients-at-a-point-of-sale).

### Record payment

If a customer makes a partial or full payment via cash, bank transfer, check or similar, record this payment so it is reflected on their invoice. Learn more about [setting up manual (offline) payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-manual-offline-payments).

1. Tap **Record payment**.
2. Enter amount received, payment date and payment method.
3. Tap **Save**.

**Notes:**
- You must record the full payment amount if you want the invoice to be marked as Paid.
- You can record a partial payment amount for offline payments only.

### Edit

You can edit any of your invoices, as long as they are not marked as Paid or Void.

1. Tap **Edit**.

**Note:** If your invoice is a recurring invoice, you'll see **Edit recurring invoice**. Learn more about [recurring invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-creating-a-recurring-invoice).
2. Edit the relevant information.
3. Tap **Update**.

**Notes:**
- When changing the invoice total, the balance must be greater than or equal to the payment(s) you have already received from your customer.
- When you update your customer's email address:

- **For all sent invoices:** The status will change back to Draft. A new invoice link is created and the original link will no longer be valid. You can resend the invoice via email or by using the new invoice link.
- **For all paid invoices:** The updated customer email address will not be reflected on your customer's paid invoice. The change will only be reflected in your [Contact List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-contact-list).

### Duplicate as draft

1. Tap **Duplicate as draft**.
2. (Optional) Make changes to your new draft invoice.
3. Tap **Next**.
4. Follow step 6 above to choose how you want to manage this invoice.
**Note: **The following details update automatically in the duplicated invoice:
- **Issue Date: **The issue date is the date the invoice was duplicated.
- **Due Date: **The due date is calculated based on your payment terms.
- **Invoice #: **The invoice is given a new invoice number.

### Void

You can void (cancel) any of your paid or sent invoices. Customers will not be able to make a payment on void invoices. If an invoice has been paid and is then made void, the amount will be deducted from your total paid overview and revenue report. This action can’t be undone.

1. Tap **Void**.
2. Tap **Void** to confirm.
