---
title: "Request: Adding Apple Pay as a Payment Method in Locations Not Supported by Wix Payments"
---

Currently, [Apple Pay can be added as a payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-apple-pay-as-a-payment-method) for your Wix Stores checkout only in locations where [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) is available. Apple Pay for alternative providers as a Wix Stores checkout option is unavailable at this time.

> **Apple Pay is also available as a payment method with the following 3rd party payment providers. Here the Apple Pay option appears at the provider's checkout and not directly in your Wix site:**
>
> - [Affirm](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-affirm-as-a-payment-provider) (United States)
> - [PayPlus](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-payplus-as-a-payment-provider) (Israel)
> - [PayTR](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-pay-tr-as-a-payment-provider) (Turkey)
> - [RazorPay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-razorpay-as-a-payment-provider) (India)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
