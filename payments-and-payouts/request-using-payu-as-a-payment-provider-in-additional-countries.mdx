---
title: "Request: Using PayU as a Payment Provider in Additional Countries"
---

Currently, you can connect your site to PayU in [Turkey](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-payu-turkey-as-a-payment-provider), [Poland](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-payu-poland-as-a-payment-provider) or [Colombia](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-payu-colombia-as-a-payment-provider) if your business operates in the relevant country. However, PayU is not available as a payment provider in other locations.

If you use the Manual payment method, you can mention that you accept payments through PayU and arrange the payment offline.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
