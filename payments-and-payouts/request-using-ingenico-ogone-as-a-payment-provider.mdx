---
title: "Request: Using Ingenico (Ogone) as a Payment Provider"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer Ingenico (previously Ogone) as a payment provider.  If you use the Manual payment method, you can mention that you accept payments through Ingenico and arrange the payment offline.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
