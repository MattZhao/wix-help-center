---
title: "Disabling PayPal Encryption Messages"
---

If your customers receive an error message when making a payment through PayPal, it is possible that your PayPal profile may be set to accept payments from encrypted buttons only.

### To disable the Encrypted Website Payments option:

Sign in to your PayPal account.  Click your name at the top right.  Click** Account Settings**.  Click **Website Payments** on the left.   Click **Update** next to **Website Preferences**.  Scroll down to the **Encrypted Website Payments** section.  Select the **Off **checkbox.
