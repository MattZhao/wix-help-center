---
title: "Request: Managing Your Wix Payments Account from the Wix App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to manage your Wix Payments account from the Wix app on your mobile device. Head over to the [Wix Payments dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-your-wix-payments-dashboard) on desktop for any action or info you need.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
