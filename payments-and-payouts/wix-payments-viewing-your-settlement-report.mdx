---
title: "Wix Payments: Viewing Your Settlement Report"
---

Your settlement report includes any payment you've ever received through Wix Payments. Find detailed information about each transaction like its current status, relevant payout and the exact [service fee](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-service-fees) charged by Wix Payments.

### To view your settlement report:

[Go to your Settlement Report](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settlement-report?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=View+Settlement+Report) in your site's dashboard.  (Optional) Filter by transaction type and date range, or click **Download Detailed Table **to download a CSV file of your settlement report.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can still [access your Wix Payments dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-the-wix-payments-dashboard-after-disconnecting-your-account), even if your Wix Payments account has been disconnected.
