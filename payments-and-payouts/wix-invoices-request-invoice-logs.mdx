---
title: "Wix Invoices Request: Invoice Logs"
---

Currently, you can view information about your invoices on your [Wix Invoices dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Invoices&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Finvoices%2Finvoices%2Flist%3FreferralInfo%3DKB-Article). However, it is not possible to view a detailed log of information; including how many payments were made and when it was sent by which collaborator.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
