---
title: "Request: Accepting Payments in Installments with Wix Payments"
---

We are pleased to announce that you can now accept [installment payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-in-installments-bnpl) in the United States with Wix Payments. To accept installment payments, you need to enable one or more of the following BNPL options:

[Add Affirm as a payment method with Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-affirm-as-a-payment-method)  [Add Afterpay as a payment method with Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-adding-afterpay-as-a-payment-method)

> **Tip:**
>
> Alternative installment payment options may be available with the following payment providers:
> - [Affirm](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-affirm-as-a-payment-provider) (United States)
> - [Afterpay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-afterpay-as-a-payment-provider-3515230) (Australia, New Zealand, United States)
> - [Clearpay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-clearpay-as-a-payment-provider) (United Kingdom)
> - [Isracard / PayMe](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-isracard-as-a-payment-provider) (Israel). Contact Isracard for more information
> - [Klarna](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-klarna-as-a-payment-provider) (Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States)
> - [Max](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-max-as-a-payment-provider) (Israel). Contact Max for more information
> - [Laybuy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-laybuy-as-a-payment-provider) (Australia, New Zealand, United Kingdom)
> - [Sezzle](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-sezzle-as-a-payment-provider) (Canada, United States)
> - Some of our supported wallet payment methods (e.g. [PayPal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider) and [PagSeguro](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-pagseguro-as-a-payment-provider))
