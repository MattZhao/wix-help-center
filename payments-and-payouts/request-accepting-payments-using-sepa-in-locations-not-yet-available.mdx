---
title: "Request: Accepting Payments Using SEPA in Locations Not Yet Available"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area bank transfers) as a payment method in [all regions where SEPA is possible](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/integration/retail/sepa/html/index.en.html).

> Users located in Austria, Belgium, France, Italy, Germany and the Netherlands can connect [Mollie as a payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-mollie-as-a-payment-provider).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
