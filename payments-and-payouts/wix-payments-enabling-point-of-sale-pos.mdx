---
title: "Wix Payments: Enabling Point of Sale (POS)"
---

Merge your in-person and online payments by enabling [Wix Point of Sale](https://www.wix.com/pos). With a Wix Payments account connected, you can order your POS hardware directly from your dashboard.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Wix POS is currently only available to Canadian and U.S.-based Wix merchants who are using Wix Stores, Wix Bookings or Wix Events.
> - To receive your payouts, Wix Payments requires every account to undergo a [verification](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-verification-process) process.

### To enable Wix POS:

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/payments/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Connect** next to Wix Payments, or click **Manage** if you already have a Wix Payments account.  Click **Accept**, or enable the toggle next to Wix Point of Sale.**

Note:** If you’re in the United States or Canada, and you don’t see the Point of Sale option, [contact Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact).

(If required) Complete verification as necessary. You can track your verification status from your Wix Payments dashboard.  Click **Order Hardware** under Point of Sale.
