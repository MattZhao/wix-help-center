### Creating a Wix Payments account

Create your Wix Payments account directly from your site's dashboard.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> In order to accept payments on your site you must [have a published site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-publishing-your-site) **and** purchase a [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) site plan that allows you to accept payments.

#### To create an account:

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2F) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Connect** next to **Wix Payments**.

Enable the toggles of the payment methods you wish to accept through Wix Payments and click **Continue**.  Click **Complete Setup**.   Select an option under **Business Type**:

- **Individual/Sole Proprietorship**: Select this option if you are doing business as an individual and fill in your personal details, as well as your business description.
- **Partnership/Ltd/Liability Co./Corporation/NGO**: Select this option if you are doing business as a business and fill in both your business information and your personal details.
- **Regional account types**: In some countries, [other business types](https://support.wix.com/en/article/the-difference-between-individual-and-business-accounts-in-wix-payments#regional-account-types) may be selected which align with local business definitions.
    Enter your details:

- Identification information
- Address
- Bank details
- Business information
- Business description
    Select the checkbox to accept the Terms & Conditions and service fees.  Click **Submit**.

> **Note:**
>
> After submitting your details, you are required to go through a [verification process](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-verification-process).
