title: "About Goods and Services Tax in Singapore"
---

Wix charges Goods and Services Tax (GST) on our services to customers in Singapore in accordance with the [Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-(gst)/basics-of-gst/goods-and-services-tax-(gst)-what-it-is-and-how-it-works).

If you are a GST-registered business in Singapore, you may be eligible for a tax exemption. You can add your GST registration number to your Wix account by updating your payment details.

---

### Updating your payment details

Update your payment details in your Wix account by adding your﻿ GST number to get your tax exemption.

#### To update your payment details

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant subscription.  Click **Update payment method**.  Select the subscriptions you wish to update and click **Next**.   Click **Edit Details**.

Re-enter your card details and add your GST number to the **GST Registration Number** field.   Click **Update**.

Click a question below to learn more about tax in Singapore.

### What is a GST number?

A GST number is a tax ID issued to a business or person registered under the GST Act in Singapore. GST numbers can be checked at the [Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore’s](https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/ESVWeb/default.aspx?target=GSTListingSearch) website.

### What happens if I do not have a tax identification number?

If you do not have a tax identification number, Wix will charge and collect GST on payments for subscriptions, applications and products.

### How does Wix determine which customers are subject to tax?

The tax charge is based on the billing account address.
