---
title: "Wix Payments: Refunding Customers"
---

When necessary, you can refund a customer for all or part of a payment they've made through Wix Payments. Refunds can take up to 3-5 business days to reach your customer's credit card/bank account, depending on the financial institution.

---

### Refunding a Wix Payments payment

Issue refunds from your Wix Payments account on desktop or from the Wix app on your mobile device.

DesktopWix app
1. [Go to Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/payments-dashboard/?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click on the payment to refund.
3. Click **Refund**.
4. Enter the amount to refund and a note (if needed).
5. Click **Refund**.

1. Go to Payments in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).
2. Tap on the payment to refund.
3. Tap **Refund**.
4. (Optional) Tap the Note icon  to add a note of the details of this refund and tap **Save**.
5. Enter the amount to refund, and tap **Issue** **Refund**.
6. Tap** Issue Refund** to confirm.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about refunds in Wix Payments.

### What is the minimum amount that can be refunded?

The typical minimum refund amount per currency is as follows:**Canada**: 1 CAD

**European Union**:** **1 EUR

**Switzerland**: 1 CHF

**United Kingdom**: 1 GBP**United States**: 1 USD
Wix Payments reserves the right not to allow refunds of less than $1, or the local currency equivalent.

### Can refunds be cancelled?

Once initiated, refunds cannot be canceled or reversed.

### Can I refund a payment in partial refunds?

After providing a partial refund to a customer, you can still refund them for the remaining amount at a later point in time.

### Is there a time limit for refunds?

- For Affirm payments, refunds can be issued up to 120 days after the initial payment
- For Afterpay payments, refunds can be issued up to 120 days after the initial payment
- For Klarna payments, refunds can be issued up to 180 days after the initial payment

### What are the fees for refunding payments?

For refunded payments, standard [Wix Payments processing fees](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-service-fees) apply. For example, in the U.S., if you issue a $100 refund to your customer, the original processing fee of $2.90 + $0.30 for that customer's purchase will not be paid back to your Wix Payments account. No additional refund fees will be charged.

### I issued a refund, but my customer hasn't received it yet. What should I do?

Allow 3-5 business days for the refund to reach your customer's credit card. If your customer still hasn't received their refund after 3-5 business days, [contact us](http://www.wix.com/contact) and we'll be happy to assist you further.
