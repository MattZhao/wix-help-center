---
title: "Request: Using BitPay as a Payment Provider"
---

Currently, BitPay cannot be connected as a payment provider in Wix.

> As an alternative, and depending on your location, you can [accept cryptocurrency payments using Bankful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-bankful-as-a-payment-provider-for-cryptocurrency-wallet-and-credit-card-payments) as a payment provider.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
