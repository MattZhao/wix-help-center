---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Exporting Form Submissions as a PDF File"
---

Currently, in the [new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-a-form-using-new-wix-forms), it is not possible to export your Wix Forms submissions as a PDF file.

However, you can always access your submissions table if you need any information about a specific entry. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-viewing-and-managing-a-submissions-table)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
