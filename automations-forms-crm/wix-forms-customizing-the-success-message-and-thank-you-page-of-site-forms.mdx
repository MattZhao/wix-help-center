Your form's success message is displayed after a visitor submits. Edit the text of the message and choose how long it displays for. You can also have the success message link to a thank you page on your site that gives more information on what to do next.

> **Note:**
>
> This article refers to customizing the success message of site forms. For standalone forms, you can [edit the form's thank you text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-standalone-form) that appears on a new page.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Customize the success message](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-customizing-the-success-message-and-thank-you-page-of-site-forms#customizing-the-success-message)  [Create a custom "Thank You" page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-customizing-the-success-message-and-thank-you-page-of-site-forms#creating-a-custom-thank-you-page)

### Customizing the success message

Edit the text and timing of the message that appears for visitors after they've submitted a form successfully on your site.

New FormsOld Forms
1. Go to your editor and click the relevant form.
2. Click **Edit Form **to open the form builder.
3. Click the **Submit** button.

**Note**: If your form does not have a submit button, add a button by adding a **Submit** field.
4. Click **Settings**.
[image: screenshot of submit button selected with settings clicked.]
1. Edit the submit button text.
2. Choose between the following options when a visitor submits a form: 

- **Show a message**: Display a custom thank you message. 

- Enter the message you want your visitors to see after submission.
- Choose between the following options:

- **Custom: **The message shows for a set amount of time. Drag the slider to set the number of seconds that the message should display for.
- **Always: **The message appears after submission and does not get removed.
- **Redirect to an external URL**: Select a URL for your users to be redirected to after they click submit. 

1. Enter the URL you want to redirect to.
2. Select whether you wish for the URL to open in the existing tab, or a new tab.

1. Go to your editor and click the relevant form.
2. Click **Form Settings**.
3. Click the **Submit Message** tab.
4. Select **Show success message** under **When visitors submit a form**.
5. Type the message that appears after visitors click the submit button (e.g. Thanks for submitting!)
[image: screenshot of submit message settings with what's the message text box containing thanks for submitting!]
1. Choose the timing of the message under **How long should the message show after submission?**

- **Set time: **The message shows for a set amount of time. Drag the slider to set the number of seconds that the message should display for.
- **Always: **The message appears after submission and does not get removed.
[image: screenshot of submit message settings with the how long should the message show after submission slider highlighted.]

> **See your success message in action:**
>
> Preview your site and submit your form to see how the message appears.

### Creating a custom "Thank You" page

You can set the submit button to redirect visitors to any link or site page. To encourage visitors to keep exploring, you can have the button lead to a custom "Thank You" page with more information.

When creating a custom page for this purpose, make sure to hide it from the site menu and from search results. This ensures that the page is only visible to those submitting your form.

New FormsOld Forms
1. Add a new page to your site and add the content you want to include on your thank you page.
2. Publish your site with the new page.
3. Copy the URL of your thank you page and return to your editor.
4. [Hide the new page from your site menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu).
5. [Hide the new page from search results.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-preventing-search-engines-from-indexing-your-page) 

**Note:** If you are using Wix Studio, learn more about [hiding your pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-managing-pages#hiding-a-page) from your menu and search results.
6. Select the relevant form in the editor.
7. Click **Edit Form **to open the form builder.
8. Click the **Submit** button.
9. Click **Settings**.
10. Click** Redirect to an external URL**.
11. Enter the URL of your thank you page.
[image: screenshot of sumission button settings with external URL linked to a site thank you page, set to open in new tab.]
1. Add a new page to your site and add the content you want to include on your thank you page.
2. [Hide the new page from your site menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-and-unhiding-a-page-from-your-site-menu).
3. [Hide the new page from search results.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-preventing-search-engines-from-indexing-your-page)
4. Click the form.
5. Click **Form Settings**.
6. Click **Submit Message**.
7. Select **Go to site page or lightbox** under **When visitors submit a form:**
8. Click **Choose a Link**.
[image: screenshot of sumit message settings, with options of different actions to choose from when a visitor submits the form.]
1. Click the **Which page? **drop-down and select your new thank you page.
[image: screenshot of a which page drop down for the submission to link to with thank you page highlighted.]
1. Click **Done**.
