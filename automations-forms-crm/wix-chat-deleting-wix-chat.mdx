---
title: "Wix Chat: Deleting Wix Chat"
---

Wix Chat is a tool that allows your visitors to chat with you live through a chatbox on your site. It is often included with Wix site templates so it may be on your site without you realizing. If you do not want visitors to have the option to chat to you, you can delete Wix Chat.

Deleting Wix Chat removes all chat elements completely from your site and your apps. This includes push notifications, chat visibility, new message display, chat form and members chat. You'll still have access to any previous chats from [Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-an-overview) in your site's dashboard.

> **Not sure you want to delete Wix Chat? Keep it and make it work for you:**
>
> - [Manage your chat status and hours](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-customizing-your-wix-chat-settings#setting-your-online-and-offline-chat-hours), including instantly toggling between available and unavailable.
> - [Add FAQ buttons to your chatbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-customizing-your-wix-chat-settings#adding-faq-buttons-to-your-chatbox) to address common questions.
> - [Add a pre-chat form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-customizing-your-wix-chat-settings#adding-a-pre-chat-form) to gather key visitor information whether you're online or offline.

### Troubleshooting issues with deleting Wix Chat

Here are the most common issues with deleting Wix Chat. Click an issue to see how to solve it.

### Unable to remove the chatbox from a page with no header and footer

In the Wix Editor, it's possible to choose the **No Header & Footer** layout for a specific page on your site. When using this layout, you won't be able to access the chatbox from that page.

**Note: **If you're using the Studio Editor, this issue does not occur. Removing the footer does not prevent you from accessing or removing the chatbot.
**To delete the chatbox from the page:**
1. Click Menus and Pages** ** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click the relevant page.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon .
4. Select **Settings**.
5. Open the **Layouts **tab.
6. Select **Standard**.
7. Select the chatbox in your editor and press Delete on your keyboard.
8. Click **Publish** to make the changes go live.

**Tip: **You can switch back to the** No Header & Footer **layout when you're done.

### Unable to remove the chatbox from your mobile site

Deleting your chatbox from your desktop site deletes it from your mobile site as well. You cannot delete the chatbox from your mobile site only. Vote for the ability to remove chat from mobile only in [this feature request](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-request-removing-or-hiding-chat-from-your-mobile-site).

### There's still chat buttons on your site that are visible

If you're using the Wix Editor, you may have added chat buttons (shortcuts to your chatbox, often displaying **Let's chat** or **Message us**) to your site. When Wix Chat has been deleted, these buttons won't function.
**Delete chat buttons no longer needed:**
1. Click the chat button in your editor.
2. Press **Delete **on your keyboard.
