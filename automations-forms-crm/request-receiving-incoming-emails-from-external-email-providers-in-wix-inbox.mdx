---
title: "Request: Receiving Incoming Emails from External Email Providers in Wix Inbox"
---

You can [change the sender address](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-changing-the-sender-details-of-your-campaigns) of emails sent from Wix Inbox. However, it's currently not possible to see incoming emails from any outside email provider in Wix Inbox.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
