---
title: "Performing a Ping Test"
---

If you are having difficulties viewing your live site, Wix Customer Care may recommend that you perform a ping test. This allows us to see where exactly the connection problem occurs.

Choose your operating system and follow the steps below.

WindowsMac
1. Right-click the **Start** menu and select **Run**.
2. Type **cmd**, and then click **OK**.
[image: Screenshof of typing cmd in the Run screen in Windows.]
1. Enter your domain: 

- **If you have a domain connected to your site**: Type **ping www.domain.com** (replace **domain.com** with your own domain name).
- **If you have a free site**: Type** ping site-url-prefix.wixsite.com** (replace **site-url-prefix** with your [Site URL prefix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-wix-account-details#changing-your-account-details), located in your account settings).
2. Press **Enter**.
3. Take a [screenshot](https://support.wix.com/en/article/taking-a-screenshot-using-windows) of the information.
4. [Send your screenshot to Wix Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-screenshot-to-a-ticket).

1. Open the Terminal:

1. Press **Cmd **+ **Space** to launch Spotlight.
2. Type **Terminal **and select **Terminal **from the search results.
[image: Screenshot of accessing the Terminal on Mac.]
1. Enter the URL of your site in the Terminal: 

- (If you connected a domain to your site) Type **ping www.url.com **(replacing "url.com" with your domain name).
- (If you have a free site) Type **ping site-url-prefix.wixsite.com** (replace "site-url-prefix" with your [Site URL prefix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-wix-account-details#changing-your-account-details), located in your account settings).
2. Press** Enter **on your keyboard.
3. [Take a screenshot](https://support.wix.com/en/article/taking-a-screenshot-using-a-mac) of the information.
4. [Send your screenshot to Wix Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-screenshot-to-a-ticket).
Note:Network Utility on Mac has been deprecated so you must use the Terminal to run ping tests.
