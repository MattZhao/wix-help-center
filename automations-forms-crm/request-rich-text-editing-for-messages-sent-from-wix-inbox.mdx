---
title: "Request: Rich Text Editing for Messages Sent From Wix Inbox"
---

Currently, it is not possible to format your message text (bold, italic, underline, bullets or links) from Wix Inbox.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can edit the text of your business name, subject line, and email signature. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-changing-your-email-settings)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
