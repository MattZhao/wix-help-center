title: "Business Phone Number: Upgrading Your Plan"
---

A dedicated business phone number helps you keep your personal and work communications separate. Select a plan that offers the features that fit your business needs. The upgrade takes effect immediately, so you can make changes to your existing plan anytime.

> **Note:**
>
> If you have not already done so, [get a dedicated business phone number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-a-phone-number).

### To upgrade your Business Phone Number:

[Go to Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Continue) in your site's dashboard to access your settings.  Click **Upgrade Phone Plan** in the **Automations** tab.

**Note:** You can also upgrade your plan from the **Call Agents **tab.  Choose a subscription period:

- **Billed Yearly:** You are billed on a yearly basis.
- **Billed Monthly:** You are charged once a month, for the entire month.
    Click **Select **under the plan you want.  Enter your payment details.  (Optional) **Enter promo code** if you have one.  Click **Submit Purchase**.
