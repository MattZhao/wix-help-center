---
title: "Business Phone Number: Assigning Incoming Calls to Your Agents"
---

Build a team of call agents to help manage your customer relationships. You can forward your incoming business calls directly to agents and decide the order in which they receive calls.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To forward incoming calls to call agents, make sure to [add call agents to your team](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-managing-multiple-call-agents).

---

### Step 1 | Set up call forwarding to your agents

Get started by setting up call forwarding so that incoming business calls are routed to the relevant numbers. You can add call agents anytime and set the order you want your agents to receive incoming calls.

#### To set up call forwarding:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Phone%20Number) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **+ Add Forwarding Number **in the **General **tab.
3. Select the desired call agent from the **Select a call agent** dropdown.

**Note: **Click **Add Call Agent** to [add more agents](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-managing-multiple-call-agents) to your team.
4. (Optional) Click and drag the **Reorder** icon  next to the relevant number(s) to set the order in which calls are forwarded to agents.
No longer want to forward incoming calls to an agent?
1. On the **General** tab, hover over the relevant agent under **Call Forwarding**.
2. Click **Remove **and** Confirm **the change.
When removing a call agent from your call forwarding list, that person can still make outgoing calls and they remain a call agent. Learn how to [remove existing agents completely](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-managing-multiple-call-agents). 
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing **and select **Business Phone Number**.
4. Tap **General **under** Line Settings**.
5. Tap **Call Forwarding**.
6. Tap **Set Forwarding Numbers**.
7. Select the numbers to have calls forwarded to them.

**Note: **To add more agents, go to your site's dashboard on desktop.
8. (Optional) On the **Call Forwarding** page, tap and drag the **Reorder** icon [image: undefined] next to the relevant number(s) to set the order in which calls are forwarded to agents.
No longer want to forward incoming calls to an agent?On the **Call Forwarding **page, deselect the relevant agent. The line owner will always remain selected and cannot be deselected.

When removing a call agent from your call forwarding list, that person can still make outgoing calls and they remain a call agent. Learn how to [remove existing agents completely](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-managing-multiple-call-agents).

### Step 2 | Manage ring order and duration

Next, decide how long the call rings and which agents get it first. Choose to forward calls to all agents at once, or in the order of your list. Then set the number of seconds in which calls ring before going to voicemail or get forwarded to the next agent.

#### To manage ring order and duration:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Phone%20Number) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to **Ring order & duration **in the **General **tab.
3. Choose what happens when you get an incoming call:

- **Call all forwarding numbers together: **All your call agents are called simultaneously. When the first agent picks up, all the other phones stop ringing. Click the** **dropdown to select how long incoming calls ring before sending callers to voicemail.
- **Call forwarding numbers in order: **All your call agents are called in the order you choose. Click the dropdown to select how long calls ring before forwarding to the next number.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing **and select **Business Phone Number**.
4. Tap **General **under** Line Settings**.
5. Tap **Ring Order & Duration**.
6. Select one of the following:

- **Call All Numbers Together: **All your call agents are called simultaneously. Tap the dropdown to select how long callers wait before reaching your voicemail.
- **Call Numbers In Order: **All your call agents are called in the order you choose. Tap the dropdown to select how long calls ring before forwarding to the next number.
7. Tap **Save**.
