title: "Wix Forms: Managing Your Forms from the Dashboard"
---

The Forms dashboard shows a list of all the forms you've created. From this dashboard, you can edit your forms, see how a form appears to visitors, and review the submissions table with all the data from those who submitted.

> **Note:**
>
> This article refers to managing site forms. Learn more about [managing standalone forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-standalone-form).

### To manage your forms from the dashboard:

2. Manage your forms using the available options:

2. Click **Preview**.

**Note**: You can also access the submissions table by clicking the number of submissions next to the relevant form.  Learn more about [viewing and managing form submissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-viewing-and-managing-a-submissions-table).

1. Go to [Forms](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Forms&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-forms) in your site's dashboard.

3. Manage **Your Website Forms** using the available options:
View a live formClick** View Form **next to the **Published** label see how the form appears on your live site. If you do not see this option it means the form is unpublished. To publish the form you need to first publish your site.Add a form
1. Click **+ New Form **at the top right.
2. Click **Website Form**.

**Tip**: You are redirected to the Wix App Market in your editor to add a new form.
3. Click **Add to Site **next to **Wix Forms & Payments**.
4. Select a template for your new form and click **Choose Template**.

**Tip:** The new form appears on your site.
5. Click the form to begin editing.
Edit a form
1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant form.
2. Click **Edit Form**.

**Note: **You will only see this option if the site form is published.
3. (In your editor) Select the form and click** Form Settings** to edit. Learn more about [editing site form fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-managing-your-form-fields#managing-your-site-form-fields).
Delete a form
1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant form.
2. Click** Remove Form**. You will see instructions of how to delete your form from the editor. Click **Go to editor** to navigate there.
View form submissionsClick **View Submissions Table **next to the relevant form to view the submission data of that form.

**Note**: Make sure your form and site is published before you view form submissions. Learn more about [viewing and managing form submissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-viewing-and-managing-a-submissions-table).
