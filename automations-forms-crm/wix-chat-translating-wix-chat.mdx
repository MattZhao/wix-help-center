### Translating Wix Chat

Translate your chat to expand your global reach, diversify your site content and craft a chat experience tailored to your visitors.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can fully translate your [pre-chat form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-customizing-your-wix-chat-settings#adding-a-pre-chat-form), as well as the [text your chatbot displays](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-designing-your-chatbox#setting-the-text-your-chatbox-displays).

#### To translate your chat:

[Go to Translation Manager](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/multilingual?&title=Translation%20Manager&buttonText=Go+To+Translation+Manager) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Chat** on the left.   Choose the relevant option:

- **If you **[**auto-translated**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-auto-translating-your-site)** your site:** Review the automated translations and edit if necessary.
- **If you didn't use auto-translation:** Translate the fields manually. You can also click **Auto-translate **below a field to automatically translate that specific field.
    Click **Save and Publish**.

### FAQs

### Is all text in Wix Chat translatable?

Certain text in the Wix Chat front (e.g. the "Type your message" text) is generated automatically and cannot be translated manually with Wix Multilingual. 
This text translates automatically in the following languages: Arabic, Bulgarian, Chinese (Traditional), Czech, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Tagalog, Thai, Turkish, and Ukrainian.

### Are changes I make to Wix Chat in my main language automatically updated in my secondary language(s)?

No. Changes you make to Wix Chat content in your main language are not automatically updated in your secondary language(s). 
Each time you make an update to your chat's content, make sure to translate it in the Multilingual dashboard.
