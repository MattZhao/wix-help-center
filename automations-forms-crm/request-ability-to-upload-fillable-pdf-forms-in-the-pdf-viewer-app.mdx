---
title: "Request: Ability to Upload Fillable PDF Forms in the PDF Viewer App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to upload fillable PDF forms using the PDF Viewer app.

You can, however, add Wix Forms and customize it so you can collect information from site visitors. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-an-overview)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
