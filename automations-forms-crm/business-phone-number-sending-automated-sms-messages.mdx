Automatically respond to your clients with pre-scheduled and pre-written SMS messages. SMS [automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-getting-started) simplify your workflow and save you time, letting you reach your clients without having to manually compose a message [each time an event occurs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation#step-2-choose-a-trigger).

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Creating a new SMS automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-sending-automated-sms-messages#creating-a-new-sms-automation)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-sending-automated-sms-messages#faqs)

> **Important:**
>
> Using SMS messages to communicate with your contacts means that you have to obey sets of rules and regulations. Ensure that you are familiar with them and comply with the requirements of the applicable laws.

### Creating a new SMS automation

Create a new automation with your desired trigger and choose the "Send SMS via business number" action. You compose the message on your own, so you can use it for any business flow you have in mind.

#### To create a new SMS automation:

[Go to Automations](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Automations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/triggers/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **+ New Automation**.  Click **+ Start from Scratch**.

**Note:** To use a suggested automation, search for a keyword (e.g. "SMS") and select from the relevant options. You can edit the trigger and actions of a suggested automation.  Click the title at the top to give your automation a name.  Choose a [trigger](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation#step-2-choose-a-trigger).  Click the **Limit frequency to** drop-down to set trigger frequency:

- **Don't limit (trigger every time):** Your action will take place every time it is triggered by a visitor.
- **Once per contact:** Your action will take place only once per contact.
- **Once per contact every 24hrs:** Your action will take place only once per contact in any 24hr period.
    (Optional) [Add a delay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation#step-3-optional-add-a-delay) between a trigger and its action, to have the action occur after a certain amount of time passes.  (Optional) [Add a condition](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation#step-4-optional-add-a-condition) to make the automation occur when certain conditions are met.  Set up your action:

1. Select **Send SMS via business number **as the [action](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation#step-5-choose-an-action) that occurs as a result of the trigger.
2. Select **Customer phone number **in the **To:** drop-down.
3. Compose your message.
    (Optional) Click **+ Add an Action **to add a chain of [follow-up actions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-multi-action-automation) from the initial action.  Click **Activate **to save and activate the automation.

> **Notes:**
>
> - SMS automations only work if the person who triggered it has a phone number listed in their contact details.
> - Your automated SMS message may be a [transactional message](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-transactional-sms-messages-best-practices) that informs your customers about the product or service they have purchased. Based on how it is written, it could be filtered or blocked.

### FAQs

Click a question to learn more about sending SMS messages via your business phone number.

### Can I see a history of the SMS messages I sent to a contact?

Automated SMS messages appear in the contact's conversation in [Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-sending-messages-and-attaching-elements). This way, you can easily manage the correspondence and follow up from the Wix app or your site's dashboard.

### How can I check how many automated texts I have left to send?

You can check how many automated texts you have left in your quota for the current month by going to [Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Phone%20Number) in your site's dashboard and clicking the **Automations** tab.

### How can I increase the number of automated texts I can send per month?

You can [upgrade your plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-upgrading-your-plan) to send more automated texts per month.
