---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Grouping Multiple Form Fields"
---

Currently in the [new Wix Forms,](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-form) it is not possible to group multiple form fields to edit them at the same time.

At present, when customizing your form, fields must be organized and have their rules edited individually.

We are always working on updating and improving our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
