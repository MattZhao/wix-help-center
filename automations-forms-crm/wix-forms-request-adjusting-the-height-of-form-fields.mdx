---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Adjusting the Height of Form Fields"
---

Currently, it is not possible to adjust the height of form fields in [Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-an-overview). Field height automatically adjusts depending on the font size.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
