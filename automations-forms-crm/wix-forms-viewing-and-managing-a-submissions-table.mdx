When visitors submit one of your forms, their entries are automatically stored in a submissions table. Use the submissions table to simplify your data management – you can sort and filter the submissions, categorize them, import and export data and more.

### Viewing a submissions table

Each form has its own unique submissions table. You can view submission time, the first and last name of the person who submitted the form, as well as their email and message.

New FormsOld Forms
1. Go to [Forms & Submissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Forms&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-forms) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to a form.
3. Select **View submissions**.
To access a submissions table from your editor:
1. Click a form in your editor.
2. Click **Edit Form** to open the form builder.
3. Click the **Submissions** tab at the top.

1. Go to [Forms & Submissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Forms&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-forms) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **Old Forms** tab.
3. Click **View Submissions Table** next to the relevant form.
Note:Data from your submission table loads from the last time you published your site.Can't see a submission table?If you click **View Submissions Table** and no table appears, it means you need to go to the editor and create it from there.
1. In your editor, click the relevant form.
2. Click **Form Settings**.
3. Click **Create Submissions Table** in the **Main **tab.
4. Publish your site.

### Managing data in a submissions table

Manage your submissions table to focus on the info that is most important to you. You can sort and filter the submissions to create your own view, then export it as a CSV file.

New FormsOld Forms
1. Go to [Forms & Submissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Forms&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-forms) in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the relevant form and click the **More Actions** icon .
3. Select **View submissions**.
4. Choose how to manage your submission data:
Sort submission dataClick the arrow next to the relevant field title to sort the table by that column.

Edit submission data
1. Click the relevant submission.
2. Click **Edit **under **Submission details**.
3. Edit the submission as needed.

Export data from the table
1. Select the checkbox to the left of the relevant submission.
2. Click **Export**.
3. Select the data you want to export.
4. Click **Export**.
Mark a submission as spam
1. Select the checkbox to the left of the relevant submission.
2. Click **Report as spam**.

**Note: **
- Unmark submissions marked as spam** **by selecting the checkbox to the left of the relevant submission in the Spam tab, then click** Report as not spam**.
- To permanently delete spam submissions, click the **More actions** icon  and select **Permanently delete**.
- Spam submissions (submissions filtered automatically by the spam filter or submissions manually marked as spam) will stay in this tab for 30 days, after that they will be permanently deleted.

Mark a submission as trash
1. Select the checkbox next to the relevant submission.
2. Click **Move to trash**.

**Note: **** **You can always restore submissions you previously marked as trash from the Trash tab. Form submissions manually marked as Trash will be kept in the Trash tab for 180 days, after that time they will be permanently deleted. 

1. Go to [Forms & Submissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Forms&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-forms) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **Old Forms** tab.
3. Click **View Submissions Table** next to the relevant form.
4. Choose how to manage your submission data:
Add a new rowAdd a new form submission manually, feeding all the data directly to the table.  
1. Click + **Add Item**** **at the bottom of the table.
2. Click the empty cell under **Submission Time** in the newly created row.
3. Select a date and time for the new submission.
4. Click each of the columns to manually enter the data.
Filter the data
1. Click the **Filter** drop-down at the top.
2. Select an existing filter or click **+ New Filter** to set one up.
3. (For new filters) Set up your filter:

1. Choose a filter from the **Select field** drop-down.
2. Click the **Choose a condition** drop-down to create your filter rule. Options change based on the field you've selected.
3. Finish setting up your filter rule under **Enter a value**.
4. Click **Add Filter**.
Sort the dataYou can sort the table to find relevant form submissions. You choose the field to sort by and the order of the submissions. For example, sort the table to show the submissions by people's first names, from A to Z.
1. Click the **Sort** drop-down at the top.
2. Select an existing sort or click **+ Add a Sort** to set one up.
3. (For new sorts) Set up your sort:

1. Choose the relevant form field from the **Sort by** drop-down.
2. Select the sort order. The options change based on the field you've selected.
3. Click** Add Sort.**
Show or hide form fields
1. Click **Manage Fields** on the right.
2. Select or deselect the checkboxes next to the fields you want to show or hide.

**Note:** Depending on the form, there may be mandatory fields that you cannot hide.
Import data into the table
1. Click the **More Actions** drop-down at the top right.
2. Select** Import items**.
3. Click **Choose a File** to upload a CSV file to your table.
4. (In the Configure Columns to Import panel) Select the columns you want to import from the CSV file:

1. Select the checkboxes next to the fields you want to import.
2. (Optional) Select a field to configure its name, type and column in your submissions table.
5. Click **Next**.
6. Review your import details and click **Import** when you're ready.
Export data from the table
1. Click the **More Actions** drop-down at the top right.
2. Select **Export to CSV**.
3. Choose what you want to do:

- **Export the entire collection: **Export all items and fields, including hidden and system fields.
- **Export the filtered data:** Export only the data you have filtered.
- **Export the collection fields: **Export the form fields without submission fields.
4. Click **Export**.
 Save a filtered view of the table
1. Filter the relevant data.
2. Click **New View **at the top left.
3. Enter the name of the new view.
4. Click **New View** to save the view.
Note:The order of fields in the submissions table is based on the order they appear in the form. If you want to reorder the fields in the table, go to your editor and reorganize the fields in the relevant form. Then publish your site.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about your submissions table as well as old Wix Forms vs. new Wix Forms.

### How do I know if my form is built using old forms or new forms?

It is possible to have both versions of Wix Forms on your site at once. [Learn the difference](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms) between the old Wix Forms and new Wix Forms.

### How do I get the new version of Wix Forms on my site?

New forms are available from the Add panel in your editor. To add a new Wix form to your site:
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Contacts & Forms**.
4. Click **Create New Form** or select a template from **Premade Forms**.

[image: A screenshot of the Add panel in the editor with Contacts and Forms selected.]

### Why can't I see updated submissions data?

If you are using old Forms, the latest submission data is pulled from the last time you published your site. Try publishing your site and refreshing to see updated data.
