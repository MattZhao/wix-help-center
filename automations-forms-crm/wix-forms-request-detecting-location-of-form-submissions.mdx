---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Detecting Location of Form Submissions"
---

Currently, using [Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-an-overview), it is not possible to detect the specific address from which a form was submitted.

However, you can access the general location of the form submission using the [form submission report](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-about-your-form-submission-reports).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
