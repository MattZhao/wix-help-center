---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Additional Heading Levels"
---

Currently in [Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-form-on-your-site), you can select H1, H2 and P heading levels. It is not possible to choose any other level.

This feature would allow you to choose from other heading levels such as H3, H4, H5 and H6.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
