---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Email Validation Field"
---

Currently, in the [new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-a-form-using-new-wix-forms), there is no existing field requiring visitors to validate their email address by entering it twice before submitting a form.

An email validation field is useful to reduce email errors and typos.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
