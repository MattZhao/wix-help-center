title: "Request: Setting Automation Action as Send Site Notification"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set up a custom automation that sends a site notification (a notification that appears onscreen) to visitors or members while they are browsing your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
