---
title: "Workflows: Working with Steps (Columns)"
---

Every workflow is divided into columns that represent steps in your project, process or flow (e.g., Lead Created, Paid & Completed, etc). Whether you're working with a [template](https://support.wix.com/en/article/workflows-getting-started#creating-new-workflows) or creating a new workflow from scratch, you can customize the steps to your needs. Then, add cards under each column for the contacts you're working with.

#### Learn how to:

[Add steps to your workflow](https://support.wix.com/en/article/workflows-working-with-steps-columns#add-a-new-step-1)[Manage your steps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/workflows-working-with-steps-columns#manage-your-steps)[Change the order of your steps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/workflows-working-with-steps-columns#reorder-your-steps)

### Add a New Step

You can easily add as many steps as you need to your workflow. Go to the relevant workflow and click **+ Add Step **on the right. Then, give your step a name and move it to the right place in your flow.

### Show me how

1. Go to [Workflows](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Workflows&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/workflows) in your site's dashboard.
2. (Optional) Click the drop-down next to your workflow title to select another one.
3. Click **+ Add Step**.
4. Click **Step Name **to enter a name for your new step.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click the **Reorder** iconnext to the step and drag it to the right location in your workflow.

### Manage Your Steps

Manage any step by hovering over it and clicking the **Show More** icon  . Rename your step, add contact cards under it or create an automation that streamlines the process. You can also remove unnecessary steps at any time.

> **Note:**
>
> Every workflow has a "final step" column (e.g. "Paid & Completed") and archive column. These appear to the right of a dividing line. You can't add steps to the right of the line or delete these steps. Click the More Actions icon  to rename your "final step" or hide your archive step.

### Show me how

1. Go to [Workflows](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Workflows&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/workflows) in your site's dashboard.
2. (Optional) Click the drop-down next to your workflow title to select another one.
3. Hover over a step and click the **Show More** icon to:
- **Rename Step: **Rename your step.
- **Add Card: **Add a card under this step. Enter a title and link the card to a contact.
- **Create Automation: **[Set up an automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation) for this step.
- **Send Email Campaign: **Send an email campaign to all the contacts in your column. **Note:** This option doesn't appear in columns with no cards.
- **Delete Step: **Remove the step from your workflow.
**Note: **This is only possible when no cards are linked to the step. You can either archive the contact cards or move them to a different column.

---

### Reorder Your Steps

Change the order of your workflow steps at any time. Simply click the **Reorder** icon  next to the relevant step and drag it to the right location.
