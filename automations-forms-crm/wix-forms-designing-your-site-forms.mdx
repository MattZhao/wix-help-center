title: "Wix Forms: Designing Your Site Forms"
---

Design your form to add a splash of individuality to your visitors' experience. Customize the background color, change button text or adjust shadows and borders to bring the form to life.

> **Note:**
>
> Designing your form is currently only available for site forms. For standalone forms, you can [manage form fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-managing-your-form-fields#managing-your-standalone-form-fields) but not update their design.

> **How do I know if I'm using old Wix Forms or new Wix Forms?**
>
> [This article](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms) will help you understand the differences between old and new Wix Forms.

### To design your site forms:

New FormsOld Forms
1. Click the relevant form on your page.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Design** tab.
4. Customize the form design using the available options:

- **Form Background:** Choose the form background color, as well as the border color and width.
- **Form Fields: **Select a form field style and customize the opacity, font, font size, etc.

**Tip: **You can design how fields appear in different states: Regular, Hover, and Error.
- **Header and Paragraph: **Choose a font and color for the form text.
- **Buttons: **Design the form buttons including how they appear on hover (e.g. submit button, back button, etc.)

1. Click the **Design** icon .

3. Choose what part of the form you want to design:

- **Input fields: **Update the design of all the form fields.

**Note: **This option changes every form field. Click an individual field to design only that field.
- **Submit button: **Customize the button visitors click to submit the form.
- **Form background: **Change the background of the form (space around the form fields).
4. Use the available design options:

- **Fill Color & Opacity:** Customize the background color of the element. Click the color box to select the background color and drag the slider to increase or decrease the opacity.
- **Border:** Add and customize the border of  the element. Adjust the border style, color and opacity.
- **Corners: **Curve the corners of the element. Enter a value in any of the corners to change the others, or click the Lock  icon to adjust each corner individually.
- **Shadow:** Add a shadow to the element.
- **Text:** Change the font, size and color of text on your input fields and the submit button.

**Tip: **You can design the input fields and submit button for different states (e.g. Regular, Hover). The available design options depend on the state you choose.An individual form field

- **Fill Color & Opacity:** Customize the background color of the field. Click the color box to select the background color and drag the slider to increase or decrease the opacity.

- **Corners: **Curve the corners of the element. Enter a value in any of the corners to change the others, or click the Lock  icon to adjust each corner individually.
- **Shadow:** Add a shadow to the field.
- **Text:** Change the text color, font and size of the field.
Note:When customizing your form, some elements such as the rating field, upload button, checkboxes and radio buttons may not be affected by the changes.[image: screenshot of get in touch form with form design settings open, displaying a number of different color themes available.]
