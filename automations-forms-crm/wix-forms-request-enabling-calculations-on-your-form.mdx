---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Enabling Calculations on Your Form"
---

Currently in [Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-form-on-your-site), it is not possible to calculate the data from multiple form fields to create a response for the site visitor.

For example, a website design company may want to offer customers a total project price in real-time based on the sum of the fields they've chosen (website design only = X amount, website design + logo + business cards = Y amount).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
