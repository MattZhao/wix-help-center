title: "Wix Automations Request: Creating Draft Automations"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create a draft automation to return to and activate at a later date.

### To deactivate an automation:

[Go to Automations](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Automations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/triggers/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the automation you want to deactivate.  Disable the toggle.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
