---
title: "Wix Form Continuously Loads but Never Renders in Editor/Live Site (Includes Workaround)"
---

We're aware of the issue and are currently gathering more information regarding this matter.

> **Meanwhile, please use the following workaround:**
>
> 1. Open the Layers panel.2. Locate the corrupted form.3. Delete the form. 4. Save your site.5. Re-add the form if needed.

Additionally, if you are using the Safari browser, please update it to the [latest version](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201541) by updating your macOS or you can try using another browser such as Google Chrome or FireFox.
