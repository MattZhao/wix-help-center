---
title: "Wix Forms: Connecting Your Form to Contact Labels"
---

With Wix Forms, you can connect your form with labels in your Contact List.

Labels allow you to organize and filter your contacts efficiently. They help you group contacts based on their relationship with you and your business, for example, leads, customers and service providers. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-contact-labels)

### To connect your form to contact labels:

Click your form in the Editor.   Click **Form Settings**.   Click the **Contacts** tab.   Click **Contact labels**.  Select the relevant label you want to add.
