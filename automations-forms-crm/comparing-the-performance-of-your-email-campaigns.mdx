---
title: "Comparing the Performance of Your Email Campaigns"
---

The Wix Email Marketing Performance widget in your Wix Email Marketing dashboard lets you compare the performance of your email campaigns.

> **Note:**
>
> The widget appears once you've sent at least three email campaigns to a minimum of 10 contacts.

### To view the Wix Email Marketing Performance wigdet:

Go to your [Wix Email Marketing dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accessing-wix-shoutout).Scroll down to the **Results** section.

The widget is made up of two tabs:

**Emails:** Lets you compare the open and click rate for different email campaigns sent by email**Views: **Lets you compare the Email and Web views for all published email campaigns.

> **Important:**
>
> The Apple iOS 15 Mail Privacy Protection update will impact email Opened statistics. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-statistics-apple-ios15-mail-privacy-protection)
