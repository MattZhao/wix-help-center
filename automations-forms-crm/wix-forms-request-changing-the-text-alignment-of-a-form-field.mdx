---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Changing the Text Alignment of a Form Field"
---

> This feature request is relevant for [Wix Studio and new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-form).

Currently in the new Wix Forms, it is not possible to update the text alignment of a form field's title, placeholder text, or field description. For example, field titles are left aligned above a field and can't be moved to the center or right.

> **Note:**
>
> If your site is set to right-to-left language (RTL), the form text will automatically change direction in the Editor and live site. However, in the form builder the text appears left-to-right (LTR).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
