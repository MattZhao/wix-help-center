---
title: "Request: Sending Audio Files in Wix Inbox"
---

Currently, it is not possible to send audio files to your contacts using Wix Inbox. You can, however, send them photos and files in the acceptable formats*

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.

> ***Acceptable file formats:**
>
> Using Wix Inbox, you can send files in the following formats:
> - .png
> - .jpg
> - .jpeg
> - .xlsx
> - .xls
> - .doc
> - .docx
> - .ppt
> - .pptx
> - .pdf
