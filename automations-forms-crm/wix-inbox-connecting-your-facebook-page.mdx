---
title: "Wix Inbox: Connecting Your Facebook Page"
---

Connect your Facebook page to Wix Inbox to manage Facebook messages in the same place as your other communication channels. This keeps your customer interactions organized and easier to manage, and prevents you from having to switch between platforms.

All messages sent to your Facebook page appear in your Wix Inbox. From your inbox, you have the option to respond via Facebook or other channels, such as email or chat.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Connecting your Facebook page to Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-connecting-your-social-channels-to-inbox#connecting-your-facebook-page-to-wix-inbox)  [Managing Facebook messages in Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-connecting-your-social-channels-to-inbox#managing-facebook-messages-in-wix-inbox)  [Troubleshooting](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-connecting-your-social-channels-to-inbox#troubleshooting-facebook-and-wix-inbox-connection-issues)

> **Important:**
>
> Make sure to use a desktop computer rather than mobile device to connect Wix Inbox to Facebook.

### Connecting your Facebook page to Wix Inbox

To connect your Facebook page, follow the link from your Wix Inbox settings. You'll be redirected to  log in to your Facebook account and complete the setup process.

#### To connect your Facebook page to Wix Inbox:

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  ﻿Click the **Settings** drop-down at the top right.  Click **Communication Channels**.  Click **Connect Account** next to **Facebook Page**.  Log in to Facebook and follow the steps to connect your account.

### Managing Facebook messages in Wix Inbox

View and reply to messages sent to your Facebook page, directly from Wix Inbox. As with every other inbox message, you can star important conversations, assign a conversation to someone on your team, archive irrelevant messages, and more.

> The person who sent you the message is automatically saved as a new [contact](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-contact-list) on your site. You can always [edit their contact info](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-managing-your-contact-list#editing-a-contact) to add their phone number, email, etc.

#### To manage Facebook messages in Wix Inbox:

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  Choose what you want to do:

### View and reply to a conversation

1. Select the relevant conversation to view it.
2. Use the toolbar at the bottom to reply to the conversation:

1. (Optional) Click **Facebook** next to **Message via:** to reply using another [channel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-about-wix-inbox-channels) (e.g. email, SMS).

[image: A screenshot of sending a reply message via Facebook.]
2. Enter your message in the **Type your message... **field.
3. (Optional) [Attach an item](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-sending-messages-and-attaching-elements), such as a coupon or product link, to your message.
4. Click **Send**.

### Manage your Facebook messages

Manage your Facebook messages in the same way you manage your other messages in Wix Inbox. Save contacts, mark messages as unread, star conversations, and more.
Learn more about [managing your messages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-managing-your-inbox).

> **Note:**
>
> According to [Facebook's Messenger Platform policy](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/policy/policy-overview/), you have 7 days to reply to new messages. If you try to reply after 7 days have passed, your message will not send and you'll see a **Message failed to send **error.

### Troubleshooting Facebook and Wix Inbox connection issues

If you are experiencing issues connecting Facebook to Wix Inbox, try the steps below:

### Step 1 | Check you're connecting the correct page

Verify that you're connecting the correct page. If you're using Facebook Business, check your [Facebook Business Manager](https://business.facebook.com/settings/pages) to make sure you're connecting the correct account.

### Step 2 | Clear your cookies

If you previously connected a Facebook account, you may need to [clear Wix cookies from your browser](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-wix-cookies-from-your-browser) before connecting it again.

### Step 3 | Disconnect and reconnect your Facebook page

1. Disconnect your Facebook page: 

1. [Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **Settings** drop-down at the top right.
3. Click **Communication Channels**.
4. Disconnect your Facebook page from your Wix Inbox.
2. Go to [Facebook](http://www.facebook.com/) and log out of your account.
3. Reconnect your Facebook page: 

1. [Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **Settings** drop-down at the top right.
3. Click **Communication Channels**.
4. Click **Connect Page** next to **Facebook Page**.
