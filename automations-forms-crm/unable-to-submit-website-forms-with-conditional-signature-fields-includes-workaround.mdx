---
title: "Unable to Submit Website Forms with Conditional Signature Fields (Includes Workaround)"
---

We're aware of the issue and are currently gathering more information regarding this matter.

> **Meanwhile, you can use the following workaround:**
>
> 1. Open the form settings.2. Click **Conditions**.3. Edit the relevant conditions that involve the signature field and uncheck the signature field.4. Publish your site to see changes live.
