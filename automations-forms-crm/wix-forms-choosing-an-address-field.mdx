There are 3 different types of address fields that you can add to your form. Choose the type that fits the amount of information you want to collect:

**Basic address field:** A single text box where visitors need to enter their entire address.  **Multi-line address field:** A field with multiple lines for visitors to enter their address, including a second address line, a city field, country drop-down, etc.  **Autocomplete address field:** A field that suggests an existing street address as you begin typing.

### Adding an autocomplete address field

An autocomplete address field suggests a street address to site visitors once they begin typing their address into the field. The address field takes information from Google Maps.

> **Note:**
>
> This feature is only available in [old Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms).

#### To add an autocomplete address field:

Click your form in the editor.  Click **Add New Field**.  Click **Autocomplete Address** in **Contact Fields**.  Click the autocomplete address field in the form and click **Edit Field**.  Customize the autocomplete address field by updating the settings:

- Click the **Field Title** textbox and enter the field name.

**Tip: **Learn more about [adding field titles to your form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-field-titles-to-your-form).
- Under **Show initial text **choose to add a 'hint' or placeholder text inside the field to help visitors know what to write:

- **None: **No text appears inside the field.
- **Placeholder text: **Text appears inside the field before visitors enter their info.
- Click the **Required** **field** checkbox to make the autocomplete address field mandatory.
- Choose to limit the autocomplete address search results to a specific country under Search Preferences.

- **None: **The autocomplete address field does not limit its search to a specific country.
- **Filter by country: **The autocomplete address field only searches addresses in a country that you choose. Click the **Choose a country** drop-down to select a country for the autocomplete address to use in its search.

### Adding a multi-line address field

Multi-line address fields have different subfields, like a second line for a street address, a city field, postal or zip code field, and a country drop-down list.

#### To add a multi-line address field:

New FormsOld Forms
1. Click your form in the editor.
2. Click **Edit Form** to open the form builder.
3. Click **Multi-line address **under **Contact fields**.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Customize the address field by updating the settings:

- Click the **Field Group Name **text box and enter the field name.

**Tip: **This name is only visible on your submissions table.
- Enable the **Required field** toggle to make the address field mandatory.
- Enable the **Show "Address line - 2"** toggle to add a second address line field.
- Click the **Advanced** tab to edit the following advanced settings: 

- **Enable autocomplete**: Enable the toggle to offer real-time address suggestions when someone starts entering info.
- **Field Key**: Use this key to reference the form field in code.
Notes:
- Multi-line address submissions will be shown as one column in your submission table.
- Multi-line address layout adjusts depending on your sites regional settings.

1. Click your form in the editor.
2. Click **Add New Field**.
3. Click **Multi-line Address** in **Contact Fields**.
4. Click the multi-line address field in the form and click **Edit Field.**
5. Customize the autocomplete address field by updating the settings:

- Select or deselect the checkbox next to a subfield** **to show or hide it.
- Choose which countries should be included in the **Country** drop-down:

- **All countries: **Visitors scroll through a list of countries to select the relevant one.
- **My own list:** Choose countries to appear on the list. No others will appear other than what you've selected.

1. Click **Manage country list** to open a list of countries.
2. Select the countries you want to include in the drop-down.
- Under **Subfield titles**, enter the** **field title text that appears above the fields. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-field-titles-to-your-form)
- Click the **Required** checkbox under **General Settings** to make some or all of the subfields mandatory.

1. Click the drop-down under **Select required subfields**.
2. Select the checkboxes next to the subfields that should be required.

### Adding a basic address field

A basic address field is a one-line address field. Unlike a multi-line address field, visitors are limited to one text box to enter their entire address. You can set a character limit for this field.

#### To add a basic address field:

New FormsOld Forms
1. Click your form in your editor.
2. Click **Edit Form **to open the form builder.
3. Click **Add ** on the left side of the builder.
4. Click **Address**.
5. Click the Address field and click **Settings**.
6. Customize the address field by updating the settings:

- Click the **Field Title **text box and enter the field name.

**Tip: **Learn more about [adding field titles to your form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-field-titles-to-your-form).
- Enable the** Placeholder Text** toggle so appears inside the field before visitors enter their info. Edit the text under **Placeholder text**.
- Enable the **Required field** toggle to make the address field mandatory.

2. Click **Add New Field**.
3. Click** Address** in **Contact Fields**.

- **None:** No text appears inside the field.
- **Placeholder text: **Text appears inside the field before visitors enter their info. Edit the text under **Placeholder text**.
- Click the **Required field** checkbox to make the address field mandatory.
- Click the **Set character limit** toggle:

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I add multiple types of address fields to my form?

Yes, you can combine multiple types of address fields in a single form. 
**Note:** The autocomplete field is only available for [old Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms).

### The multi-line address field includes 4 individual fields by default. Does this count toward my premium plan field quota?

No. When you add a multi-line address field, all 4 fields collectively count as 1 field toward your premium plan quota.
