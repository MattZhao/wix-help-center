title: "Business Phone Number: Getting a Phone Number"
---

Get a [dedicated phone number for your business](https://www.wix.com/business-phone-number) to connect with your customers in the U.S. and Canada. Select from a range of local and toll-free numbers, then link the new number to your phone and the Wix mobile app.

> **About Wix's Messaging Terms of Use:**
>
> To use a Business Phone Number, you must comply with [Wix's Communication Services Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/virtual-number-terms). To follow these terms and continue using a Business Phone Number, make sure you're not participating in any of the following:
> - Explicit content
> - Hate speech
> - Unwelcome messages
> - Fraudulent messages
> - Messages related to cannabis
> If you are found in breach of these terms, you may have your account blocked.

### Step 1 | Purchase a Business Phone Number

Choose from a variety of local and toll-free phone numbers. Then, select a subscription plan and link the chosen number to the number that should receive these business calls (usually, it would be your personal number).

> You do not need an additional subscription (such as an upgraded plan) to get a Business Phone Number.

#### To purchase a Business Phone Number:

[Go to Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Continue) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Get a Business Number**.  Depending on the number you need, select the relevant option:

- **Local Number:** Choose this option if your business operates locally so you can get a number from your area.

1. Enter your city in the text box and select the relevant area code.
2. Click **Select** next to the number you want to purchase.

**Tip: **Click **Show Different Numbers** to see more options.
- **Toll-Free Number: **Get a 1-8XX number that's recognized everywhere and free to call.

1. Select a 1-8XX prefix from the drop-down and/or search for a vanity number.

**Tip: **A vanity number spells a word that's easy to remember for calls (e.g. 1-800-PIZZA77).
2. Click **Select** next to the number you want to purchase.

**Tip: **Click **Show Different Numbers** to see more options.
    Choose a subscription period:

- **Billed Yearly:** You are billed on a yearly basis.
- **Billed Monthly:** You are charged once a month, for the entire month.
    Click **Select **under the plan you want.  Fill in your payment method details and click **Submit Purchase**.   Link and verify your phone number:

1. Enter the phone number you'd like to connect to your business number.
2. Click **Send Code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit verification code you received via SMS.
4. Click **Send Code**.
    Click **Go to Settings**.

### Step 2 | Complete setup from the Wix app

After purchasing a business number, finish setting it up from the Wix app. In this process, we add the new number to your device's contact list so business calls are properly displayed. You can save it under the default name ("Wix Business Line"), or change it later to a different name that suits your needs.

#### To complete the setup:

Tap the link that you received from Wix via SMS.

**Don't have the **[**Wix app**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview)**?** Tap the link to download the app to your phone. Once you sign in, you can finish setting up your new number.

Tap **Next **to get started.  Give the Wix app access to your contacts.

**Note: **This allows us to add the business phone line to your contact list.

1. Tap the **Contacts **toggle to enable it.
2. Tap **Next**.
3. Tap **Allow** in the pop-up.
    Tap the **Notifications** toggle to enable it.   Tap **Next**.  Tap **Get Demo Call** to see how an incoming business call looks like.  Tap **Done**.

### Step 3 | Customize your business number settings

Now that you've connected your business number to the Wix app, customize its settings to meet your needs. Set up a voicemail with your own recorded message, choose how to display incoming business calls and set your call hours in advance.

#### To customize your business number settings:

DashboardWix app

Choose how to display incoming business calls

2. Scroll down to **Incoming call ID**.

- **Wix Business Line:** Incoming calls will appear as "Wix Business Line" to help you differentiate between personal and business calls.

1. Select the checkboxes next to the relevant days.
2. Set the time you're available in those days.

**Tip: **Click the **Add** icon  to add multiple time slots to the same day.
4.  When ready, click **Save** at the top right.
