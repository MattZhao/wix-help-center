---
title: "Business Phone Number: Canceling Your Phone Number Service"
---

Having a Business Phone Number allows your customers to call, text, or leave voicemails for your business. If you signed up for a business number, but decide you no longer need it, you can cancel the subscription before it automatically renews.

### To cancel your business phone number:

Go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://manage.wix.com/account/subscriptions?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the** More Actions **icon  next to the Business Phone Number you want to cancel.  Select **Cancel Business Phone Number**.

  Select **Cancel auto renew**.  Click **Cancel now**.

> **Important:**
>
> - If you turn off the auto-renewal of your phone number subscription, you can still choose to revert to auto-renewal if you change your mind before the expiry date.
> - If you cancel your Business Phone Number within the first 14 days after initial purchase, the plan will be canceled and refunded immediately.
> - The 14 day money back guarantee period does not apply to renewal payments.
