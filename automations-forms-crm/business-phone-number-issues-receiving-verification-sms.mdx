title: "Business Phone Number: Issues Receiving Verification SMS"
---

If you attempt to use your business phone number to receive verification codes from services such as Google or Facebook, the SMS won't go through if the company uses a 5 or 6 digit number to send the message.

### Issues Receiving Verification SMS

When signing up for services such as Skype, Facebook and Google, the company might send a verification SMS to the phone number you've provided in order to confirm your registration. Instead of using a regular 10 digit phone number, they send the SMS via a 'short code' or 5-6 digit number able to send and receive texts.

Unfortunately, Business Phone Number cannot receive messages from these numbers. When signing up for a service like the examples above, we recommend using a phone number other than your business phone number as we cannot guarantee the verification message will go through.
