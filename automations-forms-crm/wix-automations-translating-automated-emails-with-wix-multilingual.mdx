With Wix Multilingual, you can translate your automated emails into your site's languages. This allows you to speak to your audience in their language, offering a more local experience.

**In this article, learn more about: **

[Translating your automated emails](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-translating-automated-emails-with-wix-multilingual#translating-automated-emails)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-translating-automated-emails-with-wix-multilingual#faqs)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure you have [added and set up Wix Multilingual on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-adding-and-setting-up-wix-multilingual).

---

### Translating automated emails

Head over to the Automations tab in your site's dashboard to access your automated emails. When editing an automated email, you can select the relevant language and localize the content.

> **Important:**
>
> A [site language must be visible](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-managing-languages-in-a-multilingual-site#showing-or-hiding-a-language-on-your-site) for your automated emails to be sent in that language.

#### To translate automated emails:

[Go to Automations](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Automations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/triggers/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant automation.  Click **Edit**.

Click **Edit** next to **Do this: Send an email**.  Scroll down and click **Edit** in the **Create an email** section.

Click the **Now editing** drop-down at the top to choose a language.

**Note:** The languages available will be your site languages added via Wix Multilingual.

Edit the content of your email.   Click **Save & Continue**.  Click **Save** to make your automation live.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about translating your automated emails.

### Can I translate pre-installed automated emails as well as the ones I create?

Yes, you can translate both pre-installed automated emails and the automated emails that you created from scratch, into your site's languages.

### What if I have added a language but have not made it visible on my site?

If a language is not [visible](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-managing-languages-in-a-multilingual-site#showing-or-hiding-a-language-on-your-site) on your site, your automated emails will not be available for translation in that language.

### Do I have to manually translate each version of my automated emails myself?

Yes, you will need to manually translate each language version of your automated emails.

### What happens if I add more languages to my site after setting up this automation?

Once you make the new language live and visible on your site, you'll be able to edit the automation and access the version of that language.

### What determines the language the email is sent in?

The email is determined by the language of the contact who is receiving the email. If the language of the contact doesn't match one of your site languages, the email defaults to the main site language.
In **Contacts** you can view a contact's language.
