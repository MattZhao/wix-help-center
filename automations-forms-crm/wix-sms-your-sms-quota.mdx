title: "Wix SMS: Your SMS Quota"
---

Your monthly SMS quota determines the number of texts you can send each month. Your quota is made up of:

The number of texts included in your [site plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).  The number of texts included in any standalone SMS plan you've purchased.

For example, you could have 200 texts included in your site plan and an additional 500 texts included in your SMS plan.

### Increasing your SMS quota

To add more SMS (text) messages to your monthly quota, you can either upgrade your existing site plan or purchase a standalone Wix SMS plan.

Each site plan includes a certain number of SMS messages per month. If you need additional SMS messages but don't want to change your site plan, you can purchase a Wix SMS plan. [Learn more about upgrading Wix SMS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-sms-upgrading-wix-sms).

#### To increase your SMS quota:

[Go to SMS Settings](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-sms?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+SMS+Settings) in your site's dashboard.  Choose what to do:

### Purchase an SMS plan

1. Click **Get More Texts**.
2. Click **Select** under the plan you would like to purchase:

- **Starter:** 500 monthly texts
- **Essentials:** 1,000 monthly texts
- **Professional:** 2,500 monthly texts

**Note:** At this time, the Professional plan is the largest plan we offer, but if your business requires more texts, you can [request it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-sms-request-larger-sms-quota).
3. Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

### Upgrade your site plan

1. Click **Manage Plan**.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon .
3. Select **Upgrade plan**.
4. Click **Select** under the plan you would like to purchase.
5. Choose the subscription period and click **Continue to Checkout**.
6. Enter your payment details and click **Submit** **Purchase**.

### Which Wix apps are SMS pre-installed automations available for?

Currently, pre-installed SMS automations are available for [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/sending-sms-reminders-to-clients-in-wix-bookings), [Wix Table Reservations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-sending-reservation-notifications-to-guests), and [Wix Restaurants Orders (New)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-restaurants-adding-and-setting-up-wix-restaurants-orders-new). All notifications can be managed from the [Outgoing Notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-outgoing-notifications) page in your site's dashboard.
**Wix Bookings** texts are sent to:
- Confirm a client's booking.
- Notify clients when their booking is canceled.
- Notify clients of changes to their booked appointment or class.
- Notify clients when there's a change to their course.
- Let clients know that their appointment is pending confirmation.
- Let clients know that their appointment request has been approved.
- Let clients know that their appointment request has been declined.
- Remind clients about upcoming sessions.

**Wix Table Reservations** texts are sent to:
- Confirm a customer's reservation (whether booked online or manually).
- Notify when a table reservation is updated.
- Notify when a table reservation is cancelled.
- Remind customers of an upcoming reservation.
- Notify customers when a reservation is declined, approved, or being reviewed (manual approval).

**Wix Restaurants Orders (New)** texts are sent to:
- Notify when an order is received.
- Notify when an order is out for delivery.
- Notify when a pickup order is ready.

**Wix Table Reservations** texts are sent in the following cases:
- When your customer makes a reservation (whether booked online or manually).
- When a table reservation is updated.
- When a table reservation is cancelled.
- To remind customers of an upcoming reservation.
- To notify customers when a reservation is declined, approved, or being reviewed (manual approval).

**Wix Restaurants Orders (New)** texts are sent to customers when:
- An order is received.

### When will my SMS quota renew?

Your SMS quota renews on the 1st of each month, regardless of when you purchased a site upgrade or an SMS plan.
When your quota is renewed, or "topped up", it will never exceed the total of the quotas in both plans.
For example, Frank purchased a site upgrade that includes 50 texts on February 10th. He then purchased an SMS Starter plan on February 20th, adding 500 texts to his quota (550 in total). On March 1st, his quota will be "topped up" to a maximum of 550 SMS messages.

### How do I know if I'm using texts from my site upgrade or standalone SMS plan?

If you upgrade your site (Premium or Studio plan) and also purchase an SMS plan, your plan quotas combine. 
For example, if you have 100 SMS messages included in your Premium / Studio plan and you purchase a Wix SMS plan for 1,000 SMS messages, your quota is now 1,100 SMS messages per month.
As texts are sent, these will not necessarily come from one plan and then the other. Your quota will renew on the 1st of each month (see FAQ above). [View site upgrade plan details](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website).

### When a quota renews, will any unused messages roll over to the next month?

No, any unused messages will not roll over to the next month. When your quota renews, it will never exceed the total of the quota(s) in your plan(s).
