---
title: "Business Phone Number: Managing Your Call Recordings"
---

Set up call recordings to keep track of conversations between your customers and agents. Use your call recordings to train new call agents and track their progress. You can also reference your recordings to improve customer satisfaction.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Set up your call recordings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-managing-your-call-recordings#setting-up-your-call-recordings)  [Listen to call recordings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-managing-your-call-recordings#listening-to-your-call-recordings)

> **Note:**
>
> To have multiple call agents, you need to have the Plus or Premiere [Business Phone Number plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-upgrading-your-plan).

---

### Setting up your call recordings

Record your business phone calls so you can reference important conversations with customers at anytime.

> **Important:**
>
> Before activating this feature, it is your responsibility to make sure it complies with your local laws and regulations.

#### To set up call recordings:

DashboardWix app
1. Go to [Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Phone%20Number) in your site's dashboard.
2. Scroll down to **Call Recording**.
3. Click the** Enable Call Recording **toggle to record all of your incoming calls.
[image: A screenshot of the enable call recording option in the site dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing** and select **Business Phone Number**.
4. Tap **General **under** Line Settings**.
5. Tap **Call Recording**.
6. Tap the **Enable Call Recording** toggle to record all of your incoming calls.
7. Tap **Save**.
[image: A screenshot of the enable call recording option in the Wix Owner app.]

### Listening to your call recordings

Listen to call recordings to review conversations between your customers and agents.

#### To listen to a call recording:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant chat conversation.
3. Click the **Play** button  next to the call log.
[image: A screenshot of call recording history in the Wix Inbox site dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Inbox ** at the bottom.
3. Tap the relevant conversation.
4. Tap the **Play** button  next to the call log.
