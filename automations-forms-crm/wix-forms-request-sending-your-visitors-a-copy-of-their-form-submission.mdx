---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Sending Your Visitors a Copy of their Form Submission"
---

Currently, Wix Forms does not offer a feature which automatically sends your visitors a copy of their form submission.  

Whenever visitors submit a form on your site it reaches Wix Inbox, your [chosen email addresses](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-choosing-who-gets-notified-about-form-submissions) and your [submissions table](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-viewing-and-managing-a-submissions-table) that you can access at any time.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can connect the automation to the checkbox field in your form with the question of whether your visitors want to receive the copy of their form submission. Then, [connect this checkbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-creating-automated-responses-for-form-submissions) to the automation.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
