---
title: "Wix Photos Albums Request: Allowing Site Visitors To Customize the Format of Images They Download"
---

Currently, it is not possible to allow your site visitors to customize the format of images when they download them. This feature would let them choose the image's height, its width, and its file format.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
