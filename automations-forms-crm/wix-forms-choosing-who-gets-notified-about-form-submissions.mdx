---
title: "Wix Forms: Choosing Who Gets Notified About Form Submissions"
---

Control who gets notified after a visitor fills out a form. Site collaborators can receive and respond to form submissions directly from Wix Inbox, the mobile apps, and from their personal emails. You can also notify external emails not connected with your site.

> **Note:**
>
> Site collaborators must be assigned Wix Inbox permissions. Go to [Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/manage-website/authorization) in your dashboard to add or remove [Wix Inbox permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-editing-a-collaborator-role).

### Types of notifications

There are different notification options, depending on which editor you are using.

New Forms Old FormsBy default, when someone submits a form, an email is automatically sent to the site owner notifying them about the submission. This email notification is built using Wix Automations. Additionally, you can set up Wix Automations to send a notification to your Wix Inbox when a submission comes through. Click below to learn about the 2 options. Email notificationsYou can edit the automation to choose who gets notified – the site owner, or other email addresses. You can also edit this automation to add more emails, change the message title and body.

The **Mute Inbox notifications** toggle allows you to mute notifications that pop up in your site's dashboard.When setting up your form notifications, you can choose whether you (and any collaborators) are notified via your personal email, Wix Inbox and the mobile app. Alternatively, you can choose an email only option. Click below to learn about the 2 options.Inbox, Email & Mobile app notifications Choose this option to be notified on all channels - Wix Inbox, by email and the mobile app.

Benefits of this option:
- All conversations after the initial form submission are documented in Wix Inbox and are visible to all collaborators with permissions so anyone can jump in and respond.
- Site collaborators can click '**Respond Now**' at the bottom of the form submission and

automatically open Wix Inbox.

[image: Screenshot of Wix Form notification displaying the message details and highlighting Respond Now.]
- Collaborators can also click '**Reply**' directly to an email from their personal inbox and the message will be sent from an ID email address rather than their own. [image: Screenshot of email address with]
Email Only notifications This option lets you send form submissions to any email address, including external email addresses who aren't site collaborators. If you choose this option any follow up conversations after the initial form submission won't appear in Wix Inbox, nor the mobile app.

If you choose 'Email only' please note the following:

- Form submissions will appear in Wix Inbox, but any replies or further correspondence won't be tracked.
- The email address will not get notifications from their desktop or the Wix mobile apps about form submissions.
- The form notification email does not contain a **Respond Now **button leading to Wix Inbox. Instead, they need to respond to the form submission using the regular 'Reply'.

[image: Screenshot of email notification from Wix Forms, displaying the message details and highlighting reply option.]
Note:The email address(es) you set for form submissions are separate than those you set for [inbox notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-an-overview). Learn how to [manage your inbox notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-managing-your-notification-settings).

### Choosing who gets notified about form submissions

Select recipients for form submission notifications. Choose if you want to send the notifications to yourself or your collaborators.

New FormsOld Forms
1. [Go to Forms & Submissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Forms&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/wix-forms) in the site's dashboard.
2. Click the **More Actions **icon  next to a form.
3. Click **Form notifications**.
4. Click **Edit** under **Notifications and Automations**.

**Note:** You will be redirected to the Wix Automations dashboard. You can click **Back to Forms **to return to your Wix Forms dashboard at any time.
5. Click **Edit **next to **Action 1, ****Send**** an email**.
6. Scroll down to the automation action and click **Set Recipients**.
7. Select the **Include Additional Recipients **checkbox.

**Note:** This checkbox must be selected to receive notifications.
8. Click **Collaborator Roles**.
9. Select the [Collaborator roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-information-for-site-collaborators) you want to receive the form submission notifications.
10. Click **Save**.
Tip:You can also access **Notifications and Automations **by selecting the** Settings **tab in the form builder.

[image: Screenshot of Wix Forms settings tab, within the wix form builder. Edit Notifications and Automations is highlighted.]
1. Go to [Forms](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Forms&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-forms) in the site's dashboard.
2. Choose a form to update its settings:

- **For site forms:** 

1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to a site form.
2. Click Set **Notifications**.**

Note:** You may be asked to publish your site from the editor before you can continue.
- **For standalone forms: **

1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant standalone form.
2. Click **Form Settings**.
3. Under **Notifications **choose how you want to get notified and notify others:
Inbox, Email & Mobile App
1. Select **Inbox, Email & Mobile App**.
2. Click the **Choose collaborators from the list **drop-down and select a collaborator to notify when someone submits this form. You can also click** Add Collaborator** to create a new site collaborator.
3. (Optional) Click the drop-down again to add another collaborator.

[image: screenshot of wix forms notification collaborator dropdown, with example collaborator selected.]

**Note: **If you don't see the collaborator you're looking for, they might not have Wix Inbox permissions. Click **Manage Roles** next to the drop-down to edit their permissions.Email Only
1. Select **Email Only**.
2. Click **Add Email **under **Notify these emails**.
3. Enter the email address you want to receive a notification followed by a space.
4. (Optional) Enter additional email addresses followed by a space each time to separate them.

[image: screenshot of email only checkbox in wix forms, add email option selected.]

**Note:** The** Email Only **option means conversations do not appear in Wix Inbox. 4.   (Optional) Select the checkbox to include the actual form submission (as a PDF) in the email notification.Note for site owners:By default, form submission emails are sent to the email address associated with your Wix account.
