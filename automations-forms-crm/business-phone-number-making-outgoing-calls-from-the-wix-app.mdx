---
title: "Business Phone Number: Making Outgoing Calls from the Wix App"
---

Speak to your contacts at any time by making outgoing calls directly from the Wix app. Choose to return calls from inbox conversations or your call logs, or select a contact from your contact list.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn how to [manage your Business Phone Number settings in the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-managing-your-phone-number#managing-your-business-number-on-the-wix-owner-app).

### To make outgoing calls:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Choose how you'd like to locate a contact to call:

### From your contact list

1. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
2. Tap **Customers & Leads** and select **Contacts**.
3. Tap the **More Actions** icon  to the right of the contact you'd like to call.
4. Tap **Call**.

**Tip:** Your Contact List includes anyone who has interacted with your business (e.g. wrote you an email or booked a session). You can [add contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-manually-adding-a-contact) to make them instantly contactable.

### From a conversation

1. Tap **Inbox ** at the bottom.
2. Tap on the relevant message.
3. Tap the **Call** icon  at the top of the screen.
[image: A screenshot of the call icon in an inbox conversation in the Wix Owner app.]

### From your call log screen

1. Tap **Calls** on your home screen.

[image: A screenshot of the Wix Owner app home screen, where you can access the Calls screen.]
2. Tap the relevant tab to view your call logs: **All Calls**, **Missed Calls** or **Voicemail**.
3. Tap any call to call the contact again.

> **Notes:**
>
> - To make an outbound call to a number not saved as a contact, tap **Calls** on your home screen and then tap the **Keypad** icon .
> - You can play voicemails directly from a conversation or your call log screen.
> - [Your agents](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-managing-multiple-call-agents) can make outgoing calls as soon as they've accepted their agent invitations. An agent must log into the Wix app using the phone number this invitation was sent to.
