---
title: "Wix Forms: Setting Up Tax on Your Form"
---

Set up tax on your [payment form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-payments-to-a-site-form) to simplify the collection process and stay compliant with local regulations. Enhance customer trust by providing transparent pricing and reduce the risk of tax-related issues for your business.

> **Note:**
>
> This feature is only available using [new Wix Form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms)[s](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms).

### To set up tax on your form:

[Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).  Click **Settings **on the left.  Select **Tax**.  (Optional) If you haven't done so already, set up your [tax rules](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tax-collection-an-overview).  Select a location under **Tax locations**.  Set up the tax rate for **Form payments**.  Click **Save**.

> **What's next?**
>
> After customers select their billing country, they will see the tax applied at checkout.
