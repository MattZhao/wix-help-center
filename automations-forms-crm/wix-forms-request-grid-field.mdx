---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Grid Field"
---

Currently, in the [New Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-a-form-using-new-wix-forms), there is no option to add a grid field to your forms.

Grid fields are often used in surveys to collect in-depth feedback. Grid fields also allow people to give multiple answers for the same question.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
