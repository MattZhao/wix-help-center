title: "Wix SMS: Upgrading Wix SMS"
---

Your [upgraded site plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) includes a certain number of SMS messages per month. To send more messages, purchase a Wix SMS plan. This is a standalone plan that works by "boosting" the number of messages in your plan.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Purchasing a Wix SMS plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-sms-upgrading-wix-sms#purchasing-a-wix-sms-plan)  [Upgrading your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-sms-upgrading-wix-sms#upgrading-your-site)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Wix SMS is not yet available for all users. If it is available on your site, you need to [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) to start using it.
> - Currently, Wix SMS allows you to send pre-installed texts for Wix Bookings, Wix Table Reservations, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New). You can't create your own automations or compose your own messages.

---

### Purchasing a Wix SMS plan

You can purchase or upgrade an existing Wix SMS plan from your site's dashboard.

#### To purchase a Wix SMS plan:

[Go to SMS Settings](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-sms?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+SMS+Settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Get More Texts**.

Click **Select** under the plan you would like to purchase:

- **Starter:** 500 monthly texts
- **Essentials:** 1,000 monthly texts
- **Professional:** 2,500 monthly texts

**Note:** At this time, the Professional plan is the largest plan we offer, but if your business requires more texts, you can [request it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-sms-request-larger-sms-quota).
    Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Important:**
>
> After submitting your purchase, your bank may [require you to verify your identity](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds). If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.

### Upgrading your site

Each of our plans includes a certain number of SMS messages per month. See below how many monthly SMS messages you'll receive if you choose to [upgrade your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-existing-premium-plan).

> **Note:**
>
> If you need additional SMS messages but don't want to change your site's plan, you can purchase a Wix SMS plan.

### Business Premium plans

- **Business Basic:** 50 monthly SMS
- **Business Unlimited:** 100 monthly SMS
- **Business VIP:** 250 monthly SMS

**Note:** If you are a new user, you cannot upgrade to any of these plans.

### Wix Premium plans

- **Light:** Doesn't include SMS
- **Core: **50 monthly SMS
- **Business: **100 monthly SMS
- **Business Elite: **250 monthly SMS
- **Enterprise: **5,000 monthly SMS

[Learn more about choosing a premium plan.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan)

### Wix Studio plans

- **Basic: **Doesn't include SMS
- **Standard: **50 monthly SMS
- **Plus: **100 monthly SMS
- **Elite: **250 monthly SMS
- **Enterprise: **5,000 monthly SMS

[Learn more about choosing a Studio plan.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan)
