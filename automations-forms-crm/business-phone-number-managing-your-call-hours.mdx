---
title: "Business Phone Number: Managing Your Call Hours"
---

Manage your call hours so that you have someone available to answer your calls. You can set default call hours for your phone number and choose custom hours of availability for each agent.

### To manage your call hours:

DashboardWix app
1. Go to [Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Phone%20Number) in your site's dashboard.
2. Choose what you want to do:
Set default call hoursSet the days and times in which your business number is available. 
1. Click the **Default Call Hours** tab.
2. Do one of the following:

- **Receive Calls 24/7: **Enable the toggle if your number should always be available to receive calls.
- **Set phone hours: **Disable the **Receive Calls 24/7** toggle to set your phone hours. 

1. Select the checkboxes next to the relevant days.
2. Set the time you're available in those days.

**Tip: **Click the **Add** icon  to add multiple time slots to the same day.
Customize agent call hours Set custom hours of availability for specific call agents.
1. Click the **Call Agents** tab.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant agent.
3. Click **Edit Call Agent**.
4. Select **Custom Hours** under **Agent's Call Hours**.
5. (If enabled) Click the** Receive Calls 24/7** toggle to disable it.
6. Set the **Time Zone** from the drop-down.
7. Set the days and times that the agent is available.

**Tip: **Click the **Add** icon  to add multiple time slots to the same day.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing **and select **Business Phone Number**.
4. Set the days and times in which your business number is available:

1. Tap **Call Hours**.
2. Select one of the following options:

- **Default Call Hours:** Select this option if your number should always be available to receive calls.
- **Custom Call Hours: **Select this option to set your phone hours. 

1. Tap **Edit Custom Call Hours**.
2. Select the checkboxes next to the relevant days.
3. Set the time you're available in those days.

**Tip:** Tap **Add time** to add multiple time slots to the same day.
4. Tap **Save**.
Note:Customizing agent calls hours is only possible from your site dashboard.
