title: "Request: Advanced Saved Replies in Wix Inbox"
---

Currently, it's not possible to add shortcuts, parameter placeholders such as \{contact_name\}, or media/smart keyboards to saved replies in Wix Inbox.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
