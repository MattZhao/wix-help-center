---
title: "Request: Using Third-Party Contact Form Apps in Wix Inbox"
---

The Wix App Market offers a number of third-party apps that allow visitors to contact you through your site. Currently, these apps are not supported in Wix Inbox.

In the meantime, you can add Wix Chat to your site and add a [Custom Form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-form-on-your-site) to receive contact information, whether you are online or offline.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
