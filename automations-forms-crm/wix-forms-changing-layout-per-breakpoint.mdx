---
title: "Wix Forms: Changing Layout per Breakpoint"
---

> **Important**
>
> This feature request is only relevant for forms in the Studio Editor.

Currently in the [new Wix Forms,](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-form) it's not possible to have different form layouts per breakpoint.

On the mobile breakpoint, the form is always shown in a single column layout.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
