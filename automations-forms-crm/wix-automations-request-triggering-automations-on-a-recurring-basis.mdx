---
title: "Wix Automations Request: Triggering Automations on a Recurring Basis"
---

Currently, it is not possible to trigger an automation on a recurring basis. For example, you cannot send emails/coupons to contacts on a particular day each week/month.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
