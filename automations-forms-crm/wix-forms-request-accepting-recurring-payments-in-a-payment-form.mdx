---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Accepting Recurring Payments in a Payment Form"
---

Currently in Wix Forms, it is not possible to accept recurring payments using a [payment form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-payments-to-a-site-form). Only one-time payments are available.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
