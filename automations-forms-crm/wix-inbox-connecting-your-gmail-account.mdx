---
title: "Wix Inbox: Connecting Your Gmail Account"
---

Connect your Gmail account to Wix Inbox so that any emails you send through your Wix account (e.g. contact form responses, communication about store orders, etc.) will come from your own email address.

You'll also be able to see the emails you sent through Wix Inbox, directly in your Gmail account.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Connecting your Gmail account to Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-connecting-your-gmail-account#connecting-your-gmail-account-to-wix-inbox)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-connecting-your-gmail-account#faqs)

> **Important:**
>
> When you connect your Gmail account to Wix Inbox, the connection only goes one way. You can see all Wix Inbox notifications in your personal Gmail account. However, any personal emails sent to or from your Gmail do not show up in Wix Inbox.

---

### Connecting your Gmail account to Wix Inbox

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Settings** drop-down at the top right.  Click **Email**.  Click **Use Gmail Address** under **Send emails from your Gmail address**.  Click **Continue with Google**.  Select the Google account you'd like to connect and click **Continue**.  Select the checkboxes next to the options for what Wix can access and click **Continue**.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Purchase a Google Workspace business email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-purchasing-a-personalized-business-email) to send emails from a custom domain (e.g. info@mydomain.com).

### FAQs

Here are some common questions about connecting your Gmail account:

### If I connect my Gmail account, will my personal emails show up in my Wix Inbox?

No. When you connect your Gmail account to Wix Inbox, the connection only goes one way. You can see all Wix Inbox notifications in your personal Gmail account. However, any personal emails sent to or from your Gmail do not show up in Wix Inbox.

### Why do I see a "Your Gmail account is not connected" banner at the top of my Wix Inbox?

If you see a **Your Gmail account is not connected** banner at the top of your inbox, your Gmail account and your Wix Inbox are no longer linked. In this case, you need to reconnect them.
[image: A screenshot of a Your Gmail account is not connected banner.]
Click **Reconnect Gmail** and follow the instructions in this article.

### I've reconnected my Gmail account through my Wix Inbox Settings - why is the connection still not working?

Check that Wix is [listed as a third-party app with account access](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3466521) within your Google account settings.

### Why do I see automated Gmail emails in my Wix Inbox?

If you see automated Gmail emails in your Wix Inbox (such as "out of office" emails from the Gmail address you've connected), [check your Gmail automatic email settings](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/25922?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop).

### How do I disconnect my Gmail account?

1. [Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Settings** at the top right.
3. Click **Email**.
4. Scroll down to the **Outgoing email settings** section.
5. Click **remove Gmail** under **Sender address**.
[image: A screenshot of the Sender address section of the Email tab.]

### Can I connect other email providers (e.g. Yahoo!, AOL)?

Currently, it is not possible to connect an email provider other than Gmail to your Wix Inbox. Vote for the ability to connect other providers in [this feature request](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-connecting-an-email-provider-other-than-gmail-to-wix-inbox).

> **Want to manage all customer messages in your Wix Inbox?**
>
> You can also [connect channels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-about-wix-inbox-channels) such as Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp to your Wix Inbox.
