I'm getting spam submissions in my formTo protect your forms from spam, add a reCAPTCHA field that verifies your visitors aren't robots. 
1. Click the form in the editor.
2. Click **Add New Field.**
3. Click **Anti Spam **in the left menu.
4. Click the **Add **icon  next to **reCAPTCHA**.
I'm not receiving Wix Inbox notifications about my formYou may have selected **Email only** in your form's notification settings. Selecting this option means only the initial form submission notification appears in Wix Inbox. Any follow-up conversation or questions from the site visitor do not appear in Wix Inbox or as dashboard notifications.
Switching to **Inbox, email & mobile app** notifies your site collaborators via Wix Inbox, email and the mobile app and allows them to respond to submissions via any of these channels. All correspondence appears in your Wix Inbox.
 Learn more about [setting who gets notified about form submissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-choosing-who-gets-notified-about-form-submissions#choosing-who-gets-notified-about-form-submissions).
[image: undefined]I'm missing form submissions in the submissions tableIf you can't see form submissions in your submissions table, it is most likely because your form was created for a period of time before the submissions table was created. 
When you add a form on your site, you must go into the form's settings and click **Create Submissions Table**. If your form was collecting data before you created a submissions table, you will not see those submissions on the table. 

Learn more about [creating a submissions table](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-viewing-and-managing-a-submissions-table).

#### Still need help?

If the problem persists, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/support/conversations/category/contact) with the information below.

### Information to add to your support ticket

- The name or URL of your Wix site.
- The name and version of the web browser you're using.
- The name and version of your computer's operating system.
- A screenshot of your console view using the steps below:

1. Open an incognito window in Google Chrome (Ctrl + Shift + N).
2. Sign in to Wix and try to open your editor.
3. Use the keyboard shortcut **Ctrl + Shift + J **(on PC) or **Cmd + Option + J **(on Mac).
4. Click the **Console** tab at the top of the panel and refresh the page (F5).
5. Take a [screenshot](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-screenshot-to-a-ticket) of this tab.
