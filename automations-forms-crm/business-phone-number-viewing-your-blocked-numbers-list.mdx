---
title: "Business Phone Number: Viewing Your Blocked Numbers List"
---

Your blocked numbers list keeps track of every number you've [blocked from your Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-blocking-a-phone-number). Once a number is blocked, the contact can't reach you by call or text message. If you change your mind you can unblock a number to remove it from the list.

> **Important:**
>
> Blocking a number is only available if you have purchased a [Business Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-started-9797633). If you do not have a Business Phone Number, you can still [report a message as spam](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-managing-your-inbox#managing-your-messages).

### To view your blocked numbers list:

DashboardWix app
1. [Go to Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Phone%20Number) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the** General** tab.
3. Scroll down to **Blocked Numbers** and click **View Numbers** to see a list of all your blocked numbers.
[image: A screenshot of viewing blocked numbers in the Business Phone Number page in a site's dashboard.]
1. (Optional) Unblock a number:

1. Hover over a number in the list and click **Unblock**.
2. Click **Unblock** to confirm.
[image: A screenshot of unblocking a number in the dashboard.]
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing **and select **Business Phone Number**.
4. Tap **Blocked Numbers** in Line Settings to see a list of your blocked numbers.
5. (Optional) Unblock a number:

1. Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to a number.
2. Tap **Unblock**.
[image: A screenshot of unblocking a number in the Wix app.]Note:To add a number to your blocked list, go to your Inbox and [block the number from there](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-blocking-a-phone-number).
