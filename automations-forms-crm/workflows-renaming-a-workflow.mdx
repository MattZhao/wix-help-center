---
title: "Workflows: Renaming a Workflow"
---

Rename your workflows to easily set them apart and prioritize your work.

### To rename a workflow:

Go to [Workflows](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Workflows&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/workflows) in your site's dashboard. Click the drop-down next to your workflow title. Select the workflow you want to rename. Double-click the title of your workflow and update the text.
