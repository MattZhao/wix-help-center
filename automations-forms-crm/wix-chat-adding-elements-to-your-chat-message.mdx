Engage with site visitors in real time using Wix Chat. Send product links, coupons and more while visitors are actively browsing your site.

> **Note:**
>
> Chat messages sent from your Inbox instantly appear in your visitor's chatbox. If a visitor leaves your site during chat, your messages will be visible to them on their next visit. You have the option to continue your conversation via email if you have a [saved email address for your contact](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-contact-list).

### Sending a link to a store product

Seize the moment by sending links directly to product pages. Reply to incoming chat messages or reach out to [site visitors you can see are browsing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors).

> **Note:**
>
> You must add [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) to your site to send product links.

#### To send a link to a store product:

Choose the contact you'd like to chat with:

- [Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard and click an existing conversation.
- [Select a live site visitor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors).
    Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down and select **Chat**.  Click the Products  icon.  Select the relevant product and click **Send**.

> **Note:**
>
> A product will not be listed if it's out of stock or hidden from your store page. Go to the [Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) page in your dashboard to [manage the visibility of your store products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-changing-the-visibility-of-a-product).

### Sending a link to a service

Promote services offered on your site by sending links that bring your contacts straight to your sign-up pages.

> **Note:**
>
> You must add [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings) to your site to send links to services.

#### To send a link to a service:

Choose the contact you'd like to chat with:

- [Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard and click an existing conversation.
- [Select a live site visitor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors).
    Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down and select **Chat**.  Click the Services** **icon .   Select the relevant service and click **Send**.

### Sending a coupon

Give your visitors reasons to spend more time on your site by sending coupons for store discounts, free shipping and more.

#### To send a coupon:

Choose the contact you'd like to chat with:

- [Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard and click an existing conversation.
- [Select a live site visitor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors).
    Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down and select **Chat**.  Click the Coupons** **icon .   Choose what you want to do:

- Create a new coupon: 

1. Click **+ New Coupon**.
2. Select the type of coupon you want to offer.
3. Enter your coupon details (code, name, etc.).
4. When ready, click **Create Coupon** at the bottom right.
5. Select the coupon you just created and click **Send**.
- Send an existing coupon: Select the relevant coupon from the panel and click **Send**.

### Requesting a payment

Send a payment request to share a convenient payment link. Your contact can use their credit card or PayPal account to make this payment.

#### To request a payment:

Choose the contact you'd like to chat with:

- [Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard and click an existing conversation.
- [Select a live site visitor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors).
    Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down and select **Chat**.  Click the Request Payment icon .  Set the amount and write a short description for your request.  Click **Send Request**.

> **Make sure you're ready to accept payments through Wix Chat:**
>
> Connect a [payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview) to your site.

> **Tip:**
>
> Any payment sent through chat appears in the [Transactions tab on your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/getting-an-overview-of-your-transactions).
