---
title: "Wix Forms: Adding a Signature Field"
---

A signature field allows visitors to electronically sign their names before they submit a form. Signature fields are useful for health waivers, registration forms and application forms. All signatures are saved in your form submissions dashboard.

> **Important:**
>
> The signature field is a premium feature. Learn more about [upgrading Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-upgrading-wix-forms).

> **Important legal information:**
>
> Legal recognition of electronic signatures may have different and additional requirements according to internal laws/regulations of the country. Make sure you verify which requirements apply to you in accordance with local laws.

### To add a signature field to a site form:

New FormsOld Forms
1. Click the relevant form in your editor.
2. Click **Edit Form** to open the form builder.
3. Click **Add  ** on the left side of the builder.
4. Click **General**.
5. Click **Signature **and drag the form element onto the page.
6. Click the newly added field on your form and click **Settings**.
[image: Screenshot of settings panel relating to the signature form field. Customization options are displayed with toggles.]
1. Choose from the following customization options: 

- **Field title**: Give a short title that tells people what you are asking for in the field.
- **Allow image upload**: Enable the toggle to allow people upload an image of their signature instead of signing with their mouse or trackpad.
- **Required field**: Enable or disable the toggle to make the signature field mandatory or optional. If required, site visitors need to sign the form before submitting.
- **Field description**: Add a field description if your form needs additional explanation (e.g. terms and conditions or additional legal information for health waiver forms)
- **Personal information (PII)**: Protect personally identifiable information (PII) like addresses and passport info with an additional layer of protection.
Good to know:
- Once the form is submitted, a preview image of the visitors signature will appear in your [form submission table](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-viewing-and-managing-a-submissions-table). It is not possible to download this image.
- The form field key can be found under the **Advanced** tab in the settings panel. Use this key to reference the form field in code. It can't be changed later.

1. Click the relevant form in your editor.
2. Click **Add New Field **above the form.
3. Click **Advanced Fields **on the left.
4. Click the **Add **icon  next to **Signature Field.**
5. Click **Edit Field **above the signature field in your form and edit the following:

- **Field Title: **Enter the title for your signature field. The title appears above the field to let your visitors know what the field is for. It should be a call-to-action such as "Sign Here" or "Enter Signature".
- **Clear Signature Button: **Enter text for the clear signature button. If your visitors are not satisfied with their signature, they can redo it by clicking the button.
- **The field is required: **Select or deselect the **Required** checkbox to make the signature field mandatory or optional. If required, site visitors need to sign the form before submitting.
6. (Optional) Click **Preview** on the top bar to test out your field.
Good to know:
- Once the form is submitted, a preview image of the visitors signature will appear in your [form submission table](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-viewing-and-managing-a-submissions-table). You can preview, download or replace the signature.
- Click the **Layout**  and **Design ** icons above the signature field to change the text alignment or design of the field.
