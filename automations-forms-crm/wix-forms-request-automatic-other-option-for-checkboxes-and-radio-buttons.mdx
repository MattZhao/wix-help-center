---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Automatic \"Other\" Option For Checkboxes and Radio Buttons"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now add an 'other' option to appear as one of the multi choice options in [new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms).

#### To add an "other" multi choice option:

Click the form in the editor.  Click **Edit Form** to open the form builder.  Select the Multi choice option on your form.  Click **Settings**.   Enable the toggle **Add "Other"**.  (Optional) Customize the following:

- **Checkbox text**: What you want your "Other" button to say.
- **Placeholder text**: What you want your placeholder text to say.
    Click **Save**.
