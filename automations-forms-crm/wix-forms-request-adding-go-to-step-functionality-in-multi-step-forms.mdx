---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Adding 'Go to Step' Functionality in Multi-Step Forms"
---

Currently, in the [new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-a-form-using-new-wix-forms), it is not possible in the form settings to add 'Go to Step X,' where a visitor is automatically directed to a certain step based on their responses. For example, if someone answers 'A' in Step 1 they are sent to Step 5, but if they answer 'B' in Step 1 they are sent to Step 6.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
