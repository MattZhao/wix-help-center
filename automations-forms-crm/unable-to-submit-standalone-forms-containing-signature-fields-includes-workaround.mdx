---
title: "Unable to Submit Standalone Forms Containing Signature Fields (Includes Workaround)"
---

We're aware of an issue where standalone forms are not being submitted if they have a Signature field. We are currently gathering more information regarding this matter.

> **Meanwhile, you can use one of the following workarounds:**
>
> - Remove the Signature field in the Standalone Form
>
> or
> - Create a website form with the Signature field by doing the following:
>
> 1. Open the Editor and create a website form on a new page.
> 2. Add an Upload and/or Signature fields.
> 3. Publish your site and share the web address URL with your site visitors.
> Note that adding a Signature field on a website form requires a Business Premium plan.
