---
title: "Wix SMS Request: Creating SMS Automations"
---

Currently, Wix SMS allows you to send pre-installed Wix Bookings and Wix Restaurants texts. You can't create your own SMS automations using [Wix Automations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-getting-started).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
