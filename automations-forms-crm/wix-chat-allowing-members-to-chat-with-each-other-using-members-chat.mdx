---
title: "Wix Chat: Allowing Members to Chat with Each Other Using Members Chat"
---

Build a community around your site by adding members chat. Members chat is an additional Wix Chat feature that allows your logged-in site members to chat with each other in addition to chatting with you (the business). Once enabled, your members can see private chats as well as create group chats.

> **Before you activate members chat:**
>
> - Make sure you have installed a [members area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-add-and-set-up-a-members-area) as well as one of the social apps (Wix Blog, Wix Forum).
> - Make sure at least one of your members has [selected the 'join a community' checkbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-making-a-member-profile-public#what-is-a-community) when registering to join your site.

---

### Activating members chat

You need to activate members chat to allow your site members to view and communicate with each other.

#### To activate members chat:

[Go to Chat Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Chat%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/inbox/settings/chat?referralInfo=Inbox) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to the** Members chat** section.  Click the **Enable members chat** toggle to add or remove members chat from your site: 

- **Enabled: **Logged-in site members can chat with each other and start group chats.
- **Disabled:** Site members can only use Wix Chat to chat with you (the business).

---

### Managing members chat

Only members can use members chat, so you can control who has the ability to chat by adding or removing members in your site's dashboard.

> **Tip:**
>
> From this members list, you can also [assign different roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-managing-your-member-roles) or [badges](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-badge-to-a-member) to categorize members or make them stand out.

---

### Creating and managing group chats

Activating members chat gives you the ability to create group chats. Group chats are a great way to keep your members engaged and build a community around your site. Members can also create group chats to interact with each other.

#### To create and manage a group chat:

Log in to your live site's Members Area.

**Note:** Use your Wix account credentials to log in.    Click the chatbox at the bottom corner of your live site.   Click **+ New Chat**.  Click **New Group Chat**.  Add members to your group by clicking their names. When your list is ready, click **Next**.  Enter a name for your group.   (Optional) Click the camera icon  to select a group image.   Click **Create**.

**Tip:** Your logged-in members can create group chats following the same steps as above.

(Optional) Click the More Actions icon  to manage your group chat settings or participants:

### Edit your group chat name or image

1. Click **Change Image & Name**.
2. Customize the group chat's name and image:

- **Image: **Click the existing image or the camera icon  and select a new image.
- **Name: **Click the existing group name and enter a new one.
3. When ready, click **Done** at the bottom of the chatbox.

### Add or remove group chat participants

1. Click **Group Chat Members**.
2. Choose what you want to do: 

- Add new members to your group:

1. Click** + Add Members**.
2. Select the checkbox next to the relevant members.
3. Click **Add**.
- Remove members from your group:

1. Click **Remove** next to the relevant member.
2. Click **Remove** to confirm.
