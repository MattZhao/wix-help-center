---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Editing a Standalone Form URL"
---

Every standalone form you create has a unique URL you can share with your community to get responses. Currently in Wix Forms it is not possible to edit the URL of a standalone form. Editing your form's URL allows you to customize the link to match your branding or make it more memorable.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
