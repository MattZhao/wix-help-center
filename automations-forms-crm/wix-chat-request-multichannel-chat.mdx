---
title: "Wix Chat Request: Multichannel Chat"
---

We're excited to announce that you can now [add the WhatsApp channel to your Wix Chat chatbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-adding-the-whatsapp-channel-to-your-chatbox). We're working on making it possible to connect further third-party communication channels such as Facebook Messenger.

A multichannel chat allows site visitors to choose the platform they want to contact you on when opening Wix Chat on your site.

We are always looking to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
