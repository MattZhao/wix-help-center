---
title: "Wix Chat Request: Removing or Hiding Chat from Your Mobile Site"
---

When you add Wix Chat to your desktop site, it's automatically added to the [Quick Action Bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-the-quick-action-bar-on-your-mobile-site) on your mobile site.

Currently, it is not possible to remove or hide it from the Quick Action Bar while keeping it on your desktop site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
