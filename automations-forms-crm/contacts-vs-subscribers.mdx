---
title: "Contacts vs. Subscribers"
---

Subscribers are contacts who have given permission for you to send them marketing emails. In [Contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-managing-your-contact-list) you can find your subscribers in the **Subscribers** folder on the left.

### The Difference Between Contacts and Subscribers:

**Contacts:**

Anyone who has contacted you through your site (e.g., through a contact form).  Anyone who you manually added to your contacts.  Any contact lists that you've imported to your contacts.

**Subscribers:**

Anyone who subscribed to your newsletter (e.g., through the [Get Subscribers form)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-subscribe-form)).  Anyone who has actively agreed to receive newsletters or updates from your site.

> **Important:**
>
> When sending marketing emails using Wix Email Marketing, make sure that you send to subscribers only. For more information, check out our [Wix Email Marketing Best Practices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-email-marketing-best-practices).
