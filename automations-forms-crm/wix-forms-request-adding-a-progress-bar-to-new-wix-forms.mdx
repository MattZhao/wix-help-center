---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Adding a Progress Bar to New Wix Forms"
---

Currently in the [new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-form), it is not possible to add a progress bar to indicate progress of a multistep site form. This would give visitors a clearer picture of how long the form is and how many steps there are.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can manually add the page number to the title of your form when [adding it to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-form).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
