---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Connecting Fields to CMS Datasets"
---

Currently in the [new Wix Forms,](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-adding-and-customizing-a-form) it is not possible to connect fields to [CMS (Content Management System) datasets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/cms-about-datasets). This would allow you to connect the choices that visitors select from in dropdowns, checkboxes, and other fields to items stored in CMS collections.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
