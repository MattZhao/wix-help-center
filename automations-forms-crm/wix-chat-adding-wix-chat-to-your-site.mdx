### FAQs

Here are some common questions about adding Wix Chat:

### Why isn't my chatbox visible on my desktop site?

Your chatbox is connected to your site's footer. If your footer is hidden on a certain page, your chatbox won't be visible on that page.

1. Click **Pages ** on the left side of the Editor.
2. Hover over the relevant page and click the **More Actions** icon .
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Click the **Layouts **tab.
5. Check which layout is selected. If **No Header & Footer **is selected, change to **Standard** for the chatbox to appear.

**Note:** The chatbox doesn't appear on member login pages. It only appears after the member logs in.

### Why isn't my chatbox visible on my mobile site?

The chatbox doesn't appear on a mobile device if you have activated the old [Mobile Action Bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-mobile-action-bar). To show the chatbox, you must [switch to the new Quick Action Bar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-switching-from-the-old-mobile-action-bar-to-the-new-quick-action-bar).
