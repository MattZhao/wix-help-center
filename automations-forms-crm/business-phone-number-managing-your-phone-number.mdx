---
title: "Business Phone Number: Managing Your Phone Number"
---

Manage your [business number](https://www.wix.com/business-phone-number) settings from your site's dashboard or the Wix app. You can change your redirect number, create a personalized voicemail and set your call hours. You can also set how long calls ring before forwarding to the next number.

### To manage your business number settings:

DashboardWix app
1. Go to [Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Phone%20Number) in your site's dashboard.
2. Manage your business number by doing the following:
Change your redirect numberChange the number calls redirect to. To do this, make sure you have the Wix app connected to your new phone number. 

1. Click the **Call Agents** tab.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to your name.
3. Select **Edit Owner's Number**.
4. Enter the new phone number.
5. Click **Send Code**.
6. Enter the verification code you've received via SMS.
7. Click **Submit Code**.
8. Tap the **Download** link in the SMS message you receive from Wix and connect to the Wix app.
Set the ring order & durationChoose how incoming calls are directed to your call agents, and how long they ring before sending calls to voicemail.
1. Click the **General **tab and scroll down to **Ring order & duration**.
2. Choose what happens when you get an incoming call:

- **Call all forwarding numbers together: **All your call agents are called simultaneously. When the first agent picks up, all the other phones stop ringing. Select how long incoming calls ring before sending callers to voicemail.
- **Call forwarding numbers in order: **All your call agents are called in the order you choose. Select how long calls ring before forwarding to the next number.

Change how incoming calls appear on your phone
1. Click the **General** tab and scroll down to **Incoming Call ID**.
2. Choose how to see incoming business calls on your phone:

- **Wix Business Line: **Incoming calls appear as "Wix Business Line" to help you differentiate between personal and business calls.
- **Caller's phone number:** Incoming calls appear with the phone number of the person calling you.

Customize your voicemail
1. Click the **Voicemail** tab.
2. Click the **Enable Voicemail **toggle to enable the option.
3. (Optional) Click **Change** to customize the default voicemail greeting in one of the following ways:

- **Use Default Greeting: **This option keeps the default greeting. You can listen to it by clicking the play icon .
- **Convert Text to Speech: **Write a custom greeting that will be read by a computer-generated voice. Then, listen to the message by clicking the play icon .
- **Upload Audio File: **Upload your own recorded greeting in MP3 or WAV format.
4. Click **Save**.
Set your call hoursSet the times and days when you want to receive business calls.
1. Click the **Call Hours** tab.
2. Do one of the following:

- **Receive Calls 24/7: **Enable the toggle if your number should always be available to receive calls.
- **Set phone hours: **Disable the **Receive Calls 24/7** toggle to set your phone hours. 

1. Select the checkboxes next to the relevant days.
2. Set the time you're available in those days.

**Tip: **Click the **Add** icon  to add multiple time slots to the same day.
Set how to receive calls and choose which voicemail is used for declined calls
1. Click the **General** tab and scroll down to **Accepting and declining calls**:
2. Choose how to accept and decline business calls:

- **Ask me to accept or decline:** When you answer the call, you’ll be asked to press 1 to accept the call, or press 2 to decline it. People who call you don't hear that message.

**Note: **Callers will reach your **business** voicemail if you decline or miss calls.
- **Connect me right away:** When you answer the call, you’ll hear the person who called you right away.

**Note:** Callers will reach your **personal** voicemail if you decline or miss calls.
View your automations text quotaClick the **Automations **tab to see how many automated SMS messages (texts) you have left to send this month.
For example, if you set up an automation to send an SMS via your business number when you miss a phone call, it gets deducted from your monthly SMS quota. Learn how to [create an automation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-creating-a-new-automation) and about [upgrading your plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-upgrading-your-plan).
**Note: **The SMS quota for Business Phone Number is separate from the [Wix SMS quota](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-sms-your-sms-quota).Reassign your number to a different Wix siteReassign your business phone number to a different site within the same Wix account.

1. Go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.
2. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the Business Phone Number subscription you want to reassign.
3. Click **Assign to a different site**.
4. Select the relevant site.
5. Click **Next**.
6. Click **Assign **to confirm.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Marketing **and select **Business Phone Number**.
4. Manage your business number by doing the following:
Set the Do Not Disturb modeYou can easily set yourself as unavailable to take calls for a timeframe of your choice (e.g. 30 minutes, 8 hours). Incoming calls will automatically go to voicemail while **Do Not Disturb** is on. 

1. Tap **Do not Disturb**.
2. Tap the relevant timeframe.
3. (Optional) Set yourself as available:

1. Tap **Do not Disturb**.
2. Tap **Yes, I can take calls**.
Change how incoming calls appear on your phone
1. Tap **Incoming Call ID**.
2. Choose how to see incoming business calls on your phone:

- **Wix Business Line:** Incoming calls display the business phone number you've purchased.

**Tip: **Save your business number on your phone under a recognizable name (e.g. "Business Line") so you instantly recognize incoming business calls.
- **Caller's phone number: **Incoming calls display the phone number of the person calling you.
3. Tap **Save**.
Customize your voicemail
1. Tap **Voicemail**.
2. Tap the **Enable Voicemail **toggle to enable the option.
3. Tap **Change** to customize the default voicemail greeting in one of the following ways:

- **Use Default Greeting: **This option keeps the default greeting.
- **Convert Text to Speech: **Write a custom greeting that will be read by a computer-generated voice.

**Tip: **Switch to a different language and tone using the drop-downs.
- **Record Greeting: **Tap the **Microphone** icon  to record a voicemail greeting using your phone.
4. (Optional) Tap the **Email notifications** toggle to get an email every time you get a new voicemail.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Tip: **Before saving your changes, you can listen to the voicemail recording by tapping the **Play** icon .Set your call hoursSet the times and days when you want to receive business calls.
1. Tap **Call Hours**.
2. Select one of the following options:

- **Default Call Hours: **Select this option if your number should always be available to receive calls.
- **Custom Call Hours: **Select this option to set your phone hours.
3. Tap **Edit Custom Call Hours**.
4. Select the checkboxes next to the relevant days.
5. Set the time you're available in those days.

**Tip:** Tap **Add time** to add multiple time slots to the same day.
6. Tap **Save**.

2. Tap **Ring Order & Duration**.
3. Select one of the following:

- **Call All Numbers Together: **All your call agents are called simultaneously. Tap the drop-down to select how long callers wait before reaching your voicemail.
- **Call Numbers In Order: **All your call agents are called in the order you choose. Tap the drop-down to select how long calls ring before forwarding to the next number.
4. Tap **Save**.
