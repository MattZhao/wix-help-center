title: "Wix Forms: Adding and Setting Up a Multi-Step Form"
---

Multi-step forms allow you to organize all the information or questions in a site form into manageable steps that site visitors fill out. These forms are useful for job applications, registration for events, and for collecting information from leads.

---

### Step 1 | Add a form to your site

To get started, add a form to the relevant page.

1. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Contact & Forms**.
3. Click **Create New Form **and drag the form element onto the page.

**Tip: **You can also select a form from the pre-installed templates including Contact, Subscribe and Order forms.
4. Click the form in the editor and click **Edit Form** to open the form builder.

Customize each step of your form with the fields you require.

4. Click **Add Page**.
5. Select the new page.
6. Click **Add **** **on the left.
7. Drag the relevant fields onto your new page. The types of fields include:

- **Contact fields:** Add fields that collect and store personal data in [your Contact List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-contact-list).

- **Date fields: **Add time and date fields for appointment scheduling or time sensitive enquiries.
- **Layout elements:** Add a header or text element to organize the form or further explain a field.
Tip:To rename, reorder, delete or add additional pages to your form, select the relevant page and click **Manage pages**.

### Step 3 | (Optional) Update your form settings

Update the settings of your multi-step form so it looks and behaves just as you want.

New FormsOld Forms
1. Click the form in your editor.
2. Click** Settings**.
3. Update your form settings using the following options:

- **Forms: **Select an existing form to edit or craft a new one.
- **Layout:** Adjust the forms padding and spacing.
- **Design: **Customize the forms background, fields, header and paragraphs and buttons.
- **Manage: **View and manage all your forms in one place. From here, you can also [choose who gets notified](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-choosing-who-gets-notified-about-form-submissions) when visitors submit the form.** **
Can't see the setting you're looking for?For additional settings, select the relevant form and click **Edit Form**. Each form field you select has its own unique setting options. 
1. Click the form in your editor.
2. Click **Form Settings**.
3. Click the **Settings** tab.
4. Update your form settings using the following options:

- **Email notifications:** Choose [how you get notified about the form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-choosing-who-gets-notified-about-form-submissions).
- **Submissions table:** [View the submissions for this form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-viewing-and-managing-a-submissions-table) from your site's dashboard.
- **Form submission limit: **[Set a limit or deadline for your form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-submission-limits-to-your-forms).
- **Autofill form info: **Click the **Enable Email Autofill** toggle to automatically fill in member emails.
- **Subscriber double opt in: **Enable [double-opt in for your subscribers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-enabling-double-opt-in-for-subscribers).
