---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Receiving a PDF Copy of Form Submissions in the Email Notification"
---

Currently, using [new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms), the [notification email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-choosing-who-gets-notified-about-form-submissions) you get for form submissions does not include a PDF copy of the actual submission.

To view the submission, go to [Forms & Submissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Forms&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/wix-forms) in your site's dashboard.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
