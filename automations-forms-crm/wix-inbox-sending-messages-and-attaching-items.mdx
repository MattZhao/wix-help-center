---
title: "Wix Inbox: Sending Messages and Attaching Items"
---

Wix Inbox allows you to send messages and attach items directly to your customers. This helps you grow customer relationships by making communication more efficient and personalized. You can send attachments, payment requests, coupons, and links to your products or services, all within the same platform. Additionally, you can speed up your response time by using saved replies.

Imagine a customer inquiring about a product. You can quickly respond with detailed information, attach a product brochure, and even send a payment request—all in one seamless interaction. This not only saves you time but also enhances the customer experience, making it more likely they’ll make a purchase.

**In this article, learn how to:**

---

### Sending a message

Without leaving Wix Inbox, send messages to contacts via email, chat or additional channels such as Facebook and SMS. [Learn more about Wix Inbox channels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-about-wix-inbox-channels).

> **Note:**
>
> The channels available to you depend on the contact information you hold for that person.

#### To send a message:

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  Click an existing conversation, or click **Compose** at the top right to start a new one.  Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down to select the channel to send your message through.

**Note:** If you've attached items to your message and decide to change channels (e.g. from email to chat), you'll lose the attached items and will have to attach them again.  Click **Send**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you haven't [connected Gmail to your Wix Inbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-connecting-your-gmail-account) or [purchased a business email,](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-purchasing-a-personalized-business-email) messages you send to your contacts will feature a sender address such as "[message ID]@[crm].wix.com", as below.

### Sending a saved reply

Create saved replies for easy access to frequently sent messages (e.g. a 'welcome' message) and general information (e.g. your refund policy).

#### To create or send a saved reply:

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  Click an existing conversation, or click **Compose** at the top right to start a new one.  Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down to select the channel to send your message through.

**Note:** The channels available to you depend on the contact information you hold for that person.  Click the Saved Replies** **icon .  Choose what you want to do:

- Create a custom saved reply:

1. Scroll to the right in the saved replies panel and click** + Create Saved Reply**.
2. Enter a name for your saved reply.
3. Enter the text of your reply under **Your Message**.
4. Click **Create**.
5. Hover over the reply you just created and click **Use this**.
- Send an existing saved reply: 

1. Hover over the reply you want to use.
2. Click **Use this**.
    Click **Send **to send your message.

> **Tip:**
>
> Hover over a saved reply and click the Edit icon  to modify it, or the Remove icon  to delete it from your list.

### Requesting a payment

Send a payment request to share a convenient payment link. Your contact can use their credit card or PayPal account to make this payment.

#### To request a payment:

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  Click an existing conversation, or click **Compose** at the top right to start a new one.  Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down to select the channel to send your message through.

**Note:** The channels available to you depend on the contact information you hold for that person.  Click the Request Payment icon .  Set the amount and write a short description for your request.  Click **Send Request**.

> **Make sure you're ready to accept payments through Wix Inbox:**
>
> Connect a [payment method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview) to your site

### Sending a coupon

Promote your products and services by creating coupons to offer store discounts, free shipping and more.

#### To send a coupon:

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  Click an existing conversation, or click **Compose** at the top right to start a new one.  Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down to select the channel to send your message through.

**Note:** The channels available to you depend on the contact information you hold for that person.  Click the Add items** **icon  and click **Coupons**.  Do one of the following:

- Create a new coupon: 

1. Click **+ Create New**.
2. Select the type of coupon you want to offer.
3. Enter your coupon details (code, name, etc.).
4. When ready, click **Create Coupon** at the bottom right.
5. Select the coupon you just created and click **Add**.
6. Click **Send** to send your message.
- Send an existing coupon:

1. Select the relevant coupon from the list and click **Add**.
2. Click **Send** to send your message.

> **Note:**
>
> When you send a coupon, it is sent as text.

### Sending a link to a store product

Help your users choose a product or promote a new one by sending a direct link to a product on your site.

> **Note:**
>
> You must add [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) to your site to send product links.

#### To send a link to a store product:

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  Click an existing conversation, or click **Compose** at the top right to start a new one.  Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down to select the channel to send your message through.

**Note:** The channels available to you depend on the contact information you hold for that person.  Click the Add items** **icon  and click **Products**.  Select the relevant product and click **Add**.  Click **Send** to send your message.

> **Note:**
>
> A product will not be listed if it's out of stock or hidden from your store page. Go to the [Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) page in your dashboard to [manage the visibility of your store products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-changing-the-visibility-of-a-product).

Click the **Message via** drop-down to select the channel to send your message through.

**Note:** The channels available to you depend on the contact information you hold for that person.  Click the Add items** **icon  and click **Services**.  Select the relevant service and click **Add**.  Click **Send** to send your message.

### Sending an attachment

Share files and images with contacts to keep them up to date with invoices, designs or other documentation.

#### To send an attachment:

[Go to Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.  Click an existing conversation, or click **Compose** at the top right to start a new one.  Click the text box at the bottom to start typing your message.  Click the **Message via** drop-down to select the channel to send your message through.

**Note:** The channels available to you depend on the contact information you hold for that person.  Click the Files icon .  Select the attachment you want to send. You can attach the following file types: .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .xlsx, .xls, .doc, .docx, .ppt, .pptx, .pdf  Click **Open**.  Click **Send**.

> **Note:**
>
> You can send multiple attachments with written messages. You can attach up to 10 items per message and there is a maximum file size of 15MB.
