---
title: "Wix Contacts: Adding and Managing Custom Contact Fields"
---

Add custom fields to store additional information about your contacts. Custom fields can be either text, number, date or URL, and can have any name you choose.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Adding custom fields to contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-custom-fields-to-contacts#adding-custom-fields-to-contacts)  [Renaming custom contact fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-custom-fields-to-contacts#renaming-custom-contact-fields)  [Deleting custom contact fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-custom-fields-to-contacts#deleting-custom-contact-fields)

---

### Adding custom fields to contacts

Any custom field that you add is automatically added to all contacts in your Contact List. This way, you do not have to fill in this field for every contact.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Sync custom fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-saving-your-form-data-to-your-contacts) from your contacts to Wix Forms to collect this information from your visitors from a form on your site.

#### To add a custom field:

[Go to Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  at the top and select **Custom fields**.  Click **+ Add New Field**.  Click the **Field Type** drop-down and select the type of content: **Text**, **Number**, **Date**, or** URL**  Enter the name of the new field (e.g. "Date of Birth") in the **Field Name** field.  Click** Add**.

Click **Done**.

---

### Renaming custom contact fields

You can change the name of a custom field without affecting any of the information stored in the field.

#### To rename a custom contact field:

[Go to Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  at the top and select **Custom fields**.  Hover over the field you want to rename and click the **Rename** icon .  Edit the name of the field and click the checkmark to save.  Click **Done**.

---

### Deleting custom contact fields

Clean up your contacts by deleting custom fields that you no longer need. Deleting the custom field removes it, and the information it contains, from all your contacts.

#### To delete a custom field:

[Go to Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **More Actions** icon  at the top and select **Custom fields**.  Hover over the field you want to delete and click the **Delete** icon .  Click **Delete** to confirm.  Click **Done**.
