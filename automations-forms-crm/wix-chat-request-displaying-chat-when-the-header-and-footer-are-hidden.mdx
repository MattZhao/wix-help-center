---
title: "Wix Chat Request: Displaying Chat when the Header and Footer are Hidden"
---

In the Wix Editor, Wix Chat is attached to your site's footer, so if the footer is hidden, Wix Chat doesn't appear. In order to display Wix Chat on your site you must [display your header and footer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-hiding-the-header-and-footer-on-a-specific-page). [Learn more about apps disappearing from your page with a hidden footer](https://support.wix.com/en/article/app-disappearing-from-page-with-hidden-footer).

> **Note:**
>
> This request does not apply to the Studio Editor. When working in the Studio Editor, Wix Chat appears even when you remove the footer from your page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
