---
title: "Wix Chat Request: Seeing Visitor History in Chat"
---

Currently, once a visitor has left your site, it is not possible to see how much time they spent browsing or which pages they visited. You can view this information for [live site visitors](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-chat-viewing-and-chatting-with-live-site-visitors).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
