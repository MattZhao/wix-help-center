---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Accepting Archive Files"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now accept archive files using the [File Upload field](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-a-file-upload-field) in Wix Forms.

Uploading archive files allows your visitors to upload a single file containing multiple compressed items, such as a .zip file with various documents, images, or videos.
