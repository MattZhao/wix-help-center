---
title: "Workflows: Creating a New Workflow"
---

Get a visual overview of your projects and business leads using Workflows. You can use one of our ready-made templates or create your own flow for any process you need to keep track of. Then, set up automations to manage your workflows more efficiently.

> **Note:**
>
> You can have a maximum of 30 workflows per site. If you reach this limit and would like to create new workflows, you can delete existing workflows.

### Using a Workflow Template

Save time by using templates that meet the requirements of your line of business. The templates provide structured steps to help you keep track of every part of your workflow. If necessary, you can always customize the template according to your specific needs.

### Show me how

1. Go to [Workflows](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Workflows&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/workflows) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **+ New** at the top right.
3. Select **New Workflow **from the drop-down menu.
4. Click **Use Template **under the template you want to use. 
- **Sales Leads: **Covers all the steps of a sales funnel, from getting leads to making the sale.
- **Hiring Process:** Covers all the stages of recruiting a potential employee, from reading submitted resumes to onboarding the new hire.
- **Help Desk & IT:** Helps in monitoring IT and Help Desk requests.
- **Website Design: **Covers the process of building a new website commissioned by a customer, from getting the initial lead to completing the project.
- **Online Store: **Keeps track of orders placed in your online store, from payment to delivery.
- **Business Meetings: **Helps in managing new leads, from first contact to setting up meetings and follow-ups.
- **Site Members:** Covers the different stages of being your site member, from signup to becoming an active member with badges.

**Can't find a template for your needs?**
Simply click** + Start from Scratch** to create your own custom workflow. Enter a name for your workflow and add as many steps as needed. You can rename or delete steps as necessary. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/workflows-working-with-steps-columns)

> **What's next?**
>
> From here, you can do the following to adjust the template:
> - Rename or delete steps that aren't necessary.
> - Change the order of your steps: Hold the **Reorder** icon to drag and drop columns to the correct location.
