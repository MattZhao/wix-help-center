---
title: "Understanding the Differences Between Contacts, Members, and Subscribers"
---

Learn the differences between Contacts, Members, and Subscribers to better manage your Contacts. Anyone who submits info via your site is classified as a Contact, while Subscribers and Members are specific types of Contacts.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-the-differences-between-contacts-members-and-subscribers#contacts)  [Members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-the-differences-between-contacts-members-and-subscribers#members)  [Subscribers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-the-differences-between-contacts-members-and-subscribers#subscribers)

---

### Contacts

Contacts are visitors to your site who are on your Contacts List. Visitors are automatically added to your list when they contact you through your site (e.g., through a contact form) along with the information they provided.

You can also [manually add new contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/manually-adding-contacts), [import contacts from Gmail](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-contacts-importing-your-contacts-into-your-contact-list), or [import contacts from a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/importing-contacts-by-uploading-a-csv-file-1066522) in your site's dashboard.

You can manage your contacts from the Contacts section of your site's dashboard. [Learn more about managing your contacts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-contact-list#viewing-and-managing-your-contacts)

---

### Members

Members are any visitors to your site who have signed up as members through the [Member Signup form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-member-signup-form). Members have their own profiles in the [Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) and can interact with other members of the community.

Members can also take on specific roles in your site, and you can [assign badges to them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-member-badges) to highlight their roles (e.g., Blog Writer).

You can manage your members from the Site Members section of your site's dashboard. [Learn more about managing your members](https://support.wix.com/en/site-members/managing-your-site-members)

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also manually turn a contact into a member without them needing to go through the signup process. [Learn more about setting contacts as members](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-setting-contacts-as-members)

---

### Subscribers

Subscribers are site visitors who have subscribed to your newsletter (e.g., through the [Get Subscribers form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-subscribe-form)). They are also any contact who has given you permission to send them marketing emails, newsletters, or updates from your site.

You can manage your subscribers from the Contacts section of your site's dashboard. [Learn more about managing subscribers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-subscription-status-of-a-contact)
