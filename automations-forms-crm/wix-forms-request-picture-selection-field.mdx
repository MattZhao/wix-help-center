---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Picture Selection Field"
---

Currently, in the [new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/editor-x-creating-a-form-using-new-wix-forms), there is no option to add a picture selection field that allows visitors to choose an option from multiple visuals and/or colors or thumbnails.

You can use picture selection fields to showcase products, locations and more. Color and thumbnail fields are useful for asking someone to choose a color option from a range of products.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
