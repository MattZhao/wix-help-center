---
title: "Creating a Task from the Contact Card"
---

Create and manage tasks assigned to a specific contact directly from their contact card.

### To create and manage tasks from the contact card:

[Go to Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant contact to display their contact card.  Scroll down to **Tasks & reminders **and choose what you want to do:

- **Add task: **Click **Add Task **and create the task:

1. Enter your task text in the **Write your task here...** field.
2. (Optional) Click the **Assign to Your Team** drop-down to assign the task to a member of your team.
3. (Optional) Click **Select date** to select a due date for the task.
4. Click **Done**.
- **Edit task: **Hover over a task and click the Edit icon  to edit it.
- **Mark complete:** Check the circle next to the task to mark it as complete.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Enable desktop push notifications](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-sites-notification-settings) to receive notifications on your desktop web browser, even when you’re not on Wix.com.
