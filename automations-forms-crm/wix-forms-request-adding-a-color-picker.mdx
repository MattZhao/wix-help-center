---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Adding a Color Picker"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add a color picker to your form when using [Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-an-overview).

Adding a color picker to your site would allow your visitors to select their preferred color (e.g. the color of a shirt they are interested in) when they submit your form.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
