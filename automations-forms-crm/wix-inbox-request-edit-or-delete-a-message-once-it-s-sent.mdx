---
title: "Wix Inbox Request: Edit or Delete a Message Once It's Sent"
---

Currently in Wix Inbox, you cannot edit or delete a message in a conversation once the message has been sent.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
