Use the form submission reports to explore key data, identify interesting trends, review form traffic and more. Enhance your understanding of form engagement to make informed decisions and optimize your online presence.

> **How to access form submission reports:**
>
> [Go to All Reports](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fanalytics%2Freports%2F%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=View+All+Reports) in your site's dashboard and type "Form" in the search bar.

### Types of form submission reports

Understand the different types of form submission reports available in Wix Analytics, so you can find the information you need.

Click a report type below to learn more.

### Form Submissions by Traffic Source

This report reveals how users find your form, dividing their paths into 3 categories: Direct Link, Paid Search, and Organic Search.
**Use this report to:**
- Learn how your forms perform across different searches.
- See which searches are returning the best results.

### Submission by Form

This report breaks down the submissions by device type, visitor type, GEO location or form URL.

**Use this report to:**
- Gain an in-depth understanding of user behavior globally.
- Pinpoint opportunities to optimize your forms for different devices and tailor them to your visitors.
**Note: **Form Submission reports are available for new and old versions of Wix Forms. This means that those using the old version of forms will not see submission types exclusive to new Wix Forms, such as form URL.

### Form Submission over Time

This report lets you see form submissions over time.
**Use this report to:**
- Keep tabs on how your form submissions evolve over time.
- Spot patterns and trends in form submissions.

### Form Leads

This report provides insights into potential customers who submitted a form on your site.
**Use this report to:**
- Gather data about potential customers.
- Understand which traffic sources your leads are coming from.
- Identify which traffic sources are most effective.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the form submission reports.

### I am using old Wix forms, can I still access these reports?

Yes, these reports are available for both the old and new versions of [Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-an-overview).

### Why is there a mismatch between the number of submissions in my report and the number of submissions in my submission table?

Your submission table updates live, whereas the data presented in the reports updates every few hours.

### I named my form, but the form name is shown as 'Unknown' in my reports, is this normal?

Yes. After renaming a form, the new name gets updated in the reports the following day.

### How do I save my favorite reports so that I can access them quickly?

On the [All Reports](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fmanage.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fanalytics%2Freports%2F%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=View+All+Reports) page, click the **Add to Saved reports** icon  next to a specific report to add it to your saved reports. Your saved reports appear at the top of the page.
