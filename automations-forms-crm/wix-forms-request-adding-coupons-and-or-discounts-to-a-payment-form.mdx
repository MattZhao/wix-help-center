---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Adding Coupons and/or Discounts to a Payment Form"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [create a coupon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-creating-a-coupon) for a [payment form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-payments-to-a-site-form).

Create a coupon to visitors to add a code during checkout and apply it towards their purchase (e.g. 20% off or free shipping).
