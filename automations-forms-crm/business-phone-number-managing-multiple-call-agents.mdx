---
title: "Business Phone Number: Managing Multiple Call Agents"
---

Invite people to your team to become call agents so they can help manage your business. You can add multiple call agents, manage their permissions, schedule their call hours, and remove them at anytime.

> **Important:**
>
> To have multiple call agents, you must have the Plus or Premiere [Business Phone Number plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-upgrading-your-plan).

### To manage multiple call agents:

Go to [Business Phone Number](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fvirtual-numbers%3FreferralInfo%3DKB_Article&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Manage%20Business%20Phone%20Number) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Call Agents** tab.  Choose what you want to do:

### Add call agents

1. Click **+ Add an Agent**.
2. Enter the **Name** of the new agent.
3. Enter the **Phone Number** of the new agent.
4. Set the **Agent's Call Hours**:

- **Default Hours:** Your agent's call hours are defined in the **Call Hours **tab.
- **Custom Hours:** Customize your agent's call hours. 

1. Click the **Receive Calls 24/7** toggle to set your custom hours.
2. Click the **TimeZone **drop-down and select the relevant timezone.
3. Select the checkbox next to the relevant days and adjust the hours to your needs.

**Tip: **Click the **Add** icon  to add multiple time slots to the same day.
5. Click **Next**.
6. Enter the **Email** of the new agent.
7. Click **Send Invite**.

**Note: **
- If your invite was sent to a person with a Wix account who already has access to your site, that person immediately has access to your site and receives [**Back-Office Manager**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview#back-office-manager953) permissions.
- If the invite was sent to a new person, they will receive an email to confirm the invite. They can make outgoing calls once they accept the invite.

### Edit existing agents

1. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the relevant agent.
2. Choose what you want to do:

- **Edit Call Agent: **Change the details of your agent (name, phone number, call hours) and click **Save**.
- **Resend Invite:** The permissions status of your agent is **Limited Agent** until they accept your invite. You can resend the invite if they did not accept it.

### Delete existing agents

1. Click the **More Actions** icon next to the relevant agent.
2. Click  **Delete**.
3. Click **Remove **to confirm.

> **Assign incoming calls to your agents:**
>
> Incoming business calls are routed to your Call Forwarding numbers. Learn how to [assign agents](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-assigning-incoming-calls-to-your-agents) to receive incoming calls.
