---
title: "Wix Automations Request: Integrating with make.com"
---

Currently, it is not possible to integrate [make.com](https://www.make.com/en) with Wix.

A native integration with make.com would mean that you could connect other third-party platforms to Wix, with no code required. Some example include Airtable, HubSpot CRM, and Google Docs.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
