---
title: "Wix Inbox Request: Deleting Multiple Messages at Once"
---

Currently in Wix Inbox, you cannot delete multiple messages at once. You can, however, select each conversation one by one and [delete them individually](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-managing-your-inbox#managing-your-messages).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.

If this is a feature you would like to see in the future, please click **Vote for this feature **and we'll make sure to keep you updated.
