---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Viewing Files Uploaded Through Wix Forms in Your Contact List"
---

Currently, files that visitors upload using Wix Forms are sent directly to your email address, but are not saved inside the contact card within your Contact List.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
