---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Changing the Design of a Standalone Form"
---

Currently in Wix Forms it is not possible to update the design of a [standalone form](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-adding-and-setting-up-a-standalone-form). You can choose form fields and update the form layout, but cannot change design elements such as colors, font, background, or customize the form field design.

We are always working to update and improve our products and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
