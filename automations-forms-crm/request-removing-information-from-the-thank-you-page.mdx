---
title: "Request: Removing Information from the Thank You Page"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, the Thank You Page always displays such information as the delivery charge and the price breakdown. The only display details you can remove are the order number, product image, or the "Continue Browsing" link.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
