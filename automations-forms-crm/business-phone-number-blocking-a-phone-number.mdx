---
title: "Business Phone Number: Blocking a Phone Number"
---

Block a contact from calling you from your Inbox. Once you've blocked a number, the conversation is deleted and the number is added to your blocked numbers list.

> **Important:**
>
> Blocking a number is only available if you have purchased a [Business Phone Number](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-getting-started-9797633). If you do not have a Business Phone Number, you can still [report a message as spam](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-inbox-managing-your-inbox#managing-your-messages).

### To block a phone number:

DashboardWix app
1. Go to [Wix Inbox](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Inbox&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/inbox/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Select the conversation you want to block.
3. Click the **More Actions **icon  next to the contact name.
4. Click **Block Number**.

**Note:** If both the email and phone number of the contact are listed, click **Report & Block** to block the phone number and report the email address as spam.
5. Click **Block & Delete** to confirm.
What's next?The conversation is now deleted from your Inbox and the contact is blocked. You can view all your blocked numbers in your [blocked numbers list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-viewing-your-blocked-numbers-list). 
1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Inbox ** at the bottom.
3. Tap the conversation you want to block.
4. Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top and tap **Block Number**.

**Note: **If both the email and phone number of the contact are listed, tap **Report & Block** to block the phone number and report the email address as spam.
5. Tap **Yes **to confirm.
Tip:You can view all your blocked numbers in your [blocked numbers list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/business-phone-number-viewing-your-blocked-numbers-list). [image: A screenshot of blocking a number in the Inbox on the Wix app.]
