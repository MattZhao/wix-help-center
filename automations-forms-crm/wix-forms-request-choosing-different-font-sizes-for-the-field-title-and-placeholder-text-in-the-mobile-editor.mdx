---
title: "Wix Forms Request: Choosing Different Font Sizes for the Field Title and Placeholder Text in the Mobile Editor"
---

We are excited to announce that you can now [choose a different font style, color and size](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-designing-your-site-forms) for the field title and input text of [new Wix Forms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-forms-the-difference-between-the-new-and-old-wix-forms) in the [Mobile Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-about-the-mobile-editor).

This allows you to create custom fields, exclusive to your mobile site.
