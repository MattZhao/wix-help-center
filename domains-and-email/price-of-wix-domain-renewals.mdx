---
title: "Price of Wix Domain Renewals"
---

The cost of a Wix domain differs depending on a number of factors, including:

The type of domain extension
 Your local currency
 The number of years selected
 Adding privacy protection
 Your local tax or VAT laws

### To check the renewal cost of your domain:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.   Click **View Payment Details** in the **Next Payment Due **column** **for the domain you want to check.

> **Note:**
>
> The [free domain voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-free-domain-voucher) covers the first year of a Wix domain subscription. If you purchased your domain using this voucher, you will need to pay in order to renew it.
