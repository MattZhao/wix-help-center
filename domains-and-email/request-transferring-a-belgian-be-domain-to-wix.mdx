---
title: "Request: Transferring a Belgian (.be) Domain to Wix"
---

Currently, it is not possible to transfer a Belgian (.be) domain extension to Wix.

Though it is not yet possible to transfer ownership of a .be domain extension to Wix, if you already have a domain purchased elsewhere, you can easily [connect it to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

> **Note:**
>
> To connect a domain to your site, you must upgrade your site to a [Premium plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
