---
title: "Connecting a Domain Purchased from Wix"
---

After purchasing a domain from Wix, you can connect it to any upgraded site in your account. Go to the Domains page in your account and assign your domain to the site you want to connect.

> **Important:**
>
> You must [upgrade your site with a plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) before you can assign a domain to it.

### To connect your Wix domain to your site:

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) in your Wix account.   Click the relevant option below to learn how to assign your domain to your site:

### Assign a domain that is currently assigned to a different site

1. Click the **Domain Actions **icon  next to the relevant domain.
2. Click **Assign to a different Site**.

[image: A screenshot of the Domain Actions dropdown with the Assign to a different site option highlighted.]
3. Select the site you want to connect and click **Next**.

**Note:** If the site you're connecting is currently connected to a different domain, choose an option:

- **Redirect it to the primary domain:** Redirect your new domain to the site's existing primary domain.
- **Replace the current primary domain:** Connect your new domain and disconnect the site's existing domain.
4. Click **Assign**.

### Assign a domain that is not currently assigned to a site

1. Click **Assign** below the domain you want to connect.

[image: A screenshot showing the Assign button next to an unassigned domain in a Wix account.]
2. Select the site you want to connect and click **Next**.

**Note:** If the site is currently connected to a different domain, choose an option:

- **Redirect it to the primary domain:** Redirect your new domain to the site's existing primary domain.
- **Replace the current primary domain:** Connect your new domain and disconnect the site's existing domain.
3. Click **Assign**.

> **Already purchased a domain outside of Wix?**
>
> Learn how to [connect a domain purchased from another domain host](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).
