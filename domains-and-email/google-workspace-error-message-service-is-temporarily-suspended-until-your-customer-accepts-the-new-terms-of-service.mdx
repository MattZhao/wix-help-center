---
title: "Google Workspace Error Message: Service Is Temporarily Suspended until Your Customer Accepts the New Terms of Service"
---

If you receive this message under the Subscriptions section of your Google Workspace dashboard, it means that your account has been suspended until you accept the new Google Terms of Service.

To reinstate your account, [sign in to your admin account](https://admin.google.com) and accept the new Terms of Service that will be displayed, stating: "Your reseller has placed an order for you. In order to start using newly provisioned services, you must accept the following terms of service. After accepting the terms of service, your services will be activated".

> **Note:**
>
> If you have not yet set up your Admin business email, you need to do so in order to accept Google's new Terms of Service. See instructions on [how to set up your Google Workspace business email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-purchasing-a-personalized-business-email).
