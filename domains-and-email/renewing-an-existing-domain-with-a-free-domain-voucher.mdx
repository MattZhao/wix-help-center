---
title: "Renewing an Existing Domain with a Free Domain Voucher"
---

It is not possible to renew an existing domain using the [free domain voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-free-domain-voucher). The voucher is only valid towards the purchase of a new domain from Wix.

> **Additional Limitations:**
>
> - The voucher is valid for select extensions only. Learn more about the [available extensions when using your free domain voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-free-domain-voucher#available-domain-extensions).
> - The voucher does not cover [Private Registration](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-privacy) or additional years of service which are available for an extra fee.
