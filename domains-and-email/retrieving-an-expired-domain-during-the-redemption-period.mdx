---
title: "Retrieving an Expired Domain During the Redemption Period"
---

When a domain expires, whether due to payment failure or auto-renew being turned off, it enters a grace period lasting about 30 days. During this time, you can retrieve your domain without any additional cost. Learn more about the [domain life cycle](https://support.wix.com/en/article/domain-life-cycle).

If you do not** **retrieve your domain during this time, your domain enters the [redemption period](https://support.wix.com/en/article/domain-life-cycle#redemption-period). You can still retrieve your domain during the redemption period. However, for most domains you will need to pay a redemption fee to keep your domain.

At the end of the redemption period, you cannot renew your domain name and it is released. At this point, the domain can be registered by another user.

### Checking if your domain is in the redemption period

Use **whois.com** to check if your domain is in the redemption period. If you wish to retrieve the domain by purchasing the subscription, you can do so by contacting your domain host.

#### To check your domain:

Go to [www.whois.com](https://www.whois.com/)  Enter your domain name.

**Note**: This is how the status of your domain is displayed in whois.com:

(Optional) If your domain is in the redemption period and you wish to repurchase it:

- **For domains registered at Wix**: Follow the steps below.
- **For domains registered elsewhere**: Contact your domain registrar.

---

### Retrieving a Wix domain in redemption

If your domain is registered at Wix and has entered the redemption period, you can retrieve it from the Domains page in your Wix account.

#### To retrieve a Wix domain:

[Go to your Domains page](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) and locate the relevant domain.   Click **Keep Domain **within the redemption notification after reviewing the redemption fee information.

Enter your payment details in the checkout.  Click **Submit Payment**.

**Note**: Retrieving your domain can take up to 7 days to complete. We'll notify you of any progress via the email associated with your Wix account.

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### Does the grace period apply for all domains?

The grace period does not apply for domains ending in the following: 
- .at
- .be
- .ch
- .co.at
- .com.br
- .or.at
- .de
- .fr
- .mx
- .nl
These domain extensions bypass the grace period and go straight to the redemption period. Learn more about [domain endings that do not have a grace period](https://support.wix.com/en/article/renewals-for-combr-de-fr-me-and-mx-wix-domains).

### Does the redemption period apply for all domains?

In rare cases, the domain may **not** be available once it enters the redemption period. This is due to a decision by the domain registrar. 
Wix resells domains from a 3rd party domain registrar, who may decide to exercise their right to privately auction the domain name. 
To avoid this happening, we recommend that you resolve any payment issues **before **the domain is due to enter the redemption period, in order to keep the domain under your ownership.

### How much is the redemption fee?

The redemption fee can vary depending on a number of factors. Not all domains are subject to the same redemption fee amounts. You'll see the required amount displayed within your redemption notification on your Domains page. 
Redemption fees may also be subject to local taxes. Any applicable taxes are added to the total on checkout.
