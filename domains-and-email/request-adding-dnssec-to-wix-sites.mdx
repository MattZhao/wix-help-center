---
title: "Request: Adding DNSSEC to Wix Sites"
---

We are excited to announce that Wix now offers DNSSEC when you buy a new domain with Wix, or transfer your domain to your Wix account.

Choose **Private registration and DNSSEC** at checkout for the highest level of privacy and domain protection. Lean more about [domain privacy and protection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-privacy).

> **Tip:**
>
> If you have already purchased a domain, or transferred your domain to Wix, you can add privacy protection by manually extending your domain registration for at least an additional year. Learn more about [activating private registration for a Wix domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/activating-private-registration-for-a-wix-domain).
