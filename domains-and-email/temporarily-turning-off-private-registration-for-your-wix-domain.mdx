---
title: "Temporarily Turning Off Private Registration for Your Wix Domain"
---

If you purchased private registration, you can turn it off and back on again at any time.

### To turn off private registration:

Go to the [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) page.Click the **Show More **icon  next to the relevant domain and select **Edit Contact Info**.

Scroll down and click **Show more** to the right of **Privacy Protection**.

Click **Turn off privacy protection**.

Click **Turn Off Privacy**.

> **To turn private registration back on:**
>
> Click **Turn on privacy protection** next to **Privacy Protection**.

> **Note:**
>
> - It is not possible to receive a refund for the Private Registration fee.
> - If you would like to register for domain privacy, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/activating-private-registration-for-a-wix-domain).

> **Important:**
>
> If you are an EU citizen and you turn off privacy then the following info will be public in whois:
> State/ province, country, organization name.
