---
title: "Replacing Your Site URL with Another Domain Name"
---

You can change your Wix site's URL to another domain name [from your Domains page](https://manage.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains). You need own the domain that you want to use for your Wix site.

You can purchase a domain from Wix. Start by [searching for your ideal domain with Wix](https://www.wix.com/domains/domain-name-search).

> **Important:**
>
> You must [upgrade your site with a plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) before you can assign a domain to it.

---

### Step 1 | Add your domain to your Wix account

You can add your domain to your account by purchasing a new domain directly from Wix, connecting a domain that you already own to your Wix account, or transferring a domain that you already own to your Wix account.

Click the links below to learn more about:

After purchasing, connecting, or transferring your domain, you can assign it to your site.

---

### Step 2 | Assigning your domain to your Wix site

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) in your Wix account.  Assign your domain to your site:

### I want to assign a domain that is currently assigned to a different site

1. Click the **Domain Actions **icon  next to the relevant domain.
2. Click **Assign to a Different Site**.

[image: A screenshot of the Domain Actions dropdown with the Assign to a different site option highlighted.]
3. Select a site and click **Next**.

**Note**: If the site is currently connected to a different domain, choose an option:

- **Redirect it to the primary domain:** Redirect your new domain to the site's existing primary domain.
- **Replace the current primary domain:** Connect your new domain and disconnect the site's existing domain.
4. Click **Assign**.

### I want to assign a domain that is not currently assigned to a site

1. Scroll to the relevant domain and click the **Assign** button next to the text **Not Assigned to a Site**.

[image: A screenshot showing the Assign button next to an unassigned domain in a Wix account.]
2. Select a site and click **Next**.

**Note**: If the site is currently connected to a different domain, choose an option:

- **Redirect it to the primary domain:** Redirect your new domain to the site's existing primary domain.
- **Replace the current primary domain:** Connect your new domain and disconnect the site's existing domain.
3. Click **Assign**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also change the default free Wix URL for your site. Learn more about [changing your free Wix URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-free-wix-url-8591528).
