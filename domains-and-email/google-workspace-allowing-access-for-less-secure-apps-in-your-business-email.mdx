---
title: "Google Workspace: Allowing Access for ‘Less Secure Apps’ in Your Business Email"
---

Google has recently added a feature which blocks access for certain apps or devices which are not up to modern security standards.

You can manually enable access for less secure apps, however this must be done through your [Google Workspace admin console](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-accessing-your-admin-console), not through your Wix account. If you are not the admin, ask your admin to enable the app.

> **Note:**
>
> If you have only one business email, you are considered the admin. The settings referred to in the link below are found under the Admin console, not in your user dashboard.

Click [here](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6260879?hl=en) to learn more.
