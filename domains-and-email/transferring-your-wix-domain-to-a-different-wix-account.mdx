---
title: "Transferring Your Wix Domain to a Different Wix Account"
---

You can transfer a domain purchased from Wix to another Wix account as needed. See the instructions and notes below to learn more.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - You can also [transfer a website to another Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-premium-site-to-another-wix-account) with its assigned [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) plan, domain, and business email. This option may suit your needs better, if you prefer to transfer all of these together.
> - Some domain extensions (.at, .ch, co.at, .de, .it and .or.at) can only be [transferred along with the entire site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-premium-site-to-another-wix-account).

### To transfer a domain purchased from Wix to a different Wix account:

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) in your Wix account.  Click the **Domain Actions** icon  next to the relevant domain.  Click **Transfer to a different Wix account**.

Enter the email address and password of the Wix account where you're transferring the domain.   Click **Transfer Domain**.

> **Important:**
>
> Make sure to [extend the domain's registration](https://support.wix.com/en/article/extending-your-wix-domain-subscription) in the new Wix account to prevent it from expiring and [update the domain registrant information](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-and-updating-your-wix-domain-contact-information), if necessary.

> **Purchased a domain outside of Wix?**
>
> You can [move domains that were not purchased from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/moving-a-connected-domain-purchased-elsewhere-from-one-wix-account-to-another) between your Wix accounts.
