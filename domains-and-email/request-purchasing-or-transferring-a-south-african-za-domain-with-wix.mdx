---
title: "Request: Purchasing or Transferring a South African (.za) Domain with Wix"
---

Currently, It is not possible to purchase a South African (.za) domain from Wix or transfer one to Wix.

However, you can purchase a South African (.za) domain from another domain host and [connect it to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

> **Important:**
>
> To connect a domain to your site, you must [upgrade your site with a plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
