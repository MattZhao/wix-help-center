---
title: "Accessing Your Microsoft Office 365 Mailbox Through Your Wix Account"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature is no longer being sold by Wix.

If you purchased a Microsoft Office 365 mailbox through Wix, you can easily access and manage it from within your Wix account.

### To access your mailbox:

[Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) in your Wix account.  Click** Go to** **Inbox **next to the relevant mailbox.  Enter your mailbox credentials and click** Sign in**.
