---
title: "Google Security Message: This Site May Be Hacked"
---

You may see the message "This site may be hacked" when viewing the search results for your site:

This message appears when Google believes a hacker might have changed some of the existing pages on the site or added new spam pages. If you visit the site, you could be redirected to spam or malware. This issue is caused by external sources and is not related to the content of your Wix site.

### You can remove this message by doing the following:

Verify your site with [Google Search Console](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-google-search-console-5033765).  Remove the resources causing the problem by following [these instructions](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/190597?hl=en).   Submit your [sitemap](https://support.wix.com/en/article/submitting-your-sitemap-and-urls-directly-to-search-engines) directly to Google.
