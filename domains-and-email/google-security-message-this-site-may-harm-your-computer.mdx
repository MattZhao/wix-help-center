---
title: "Google Security Message: This Site May Harm Your Computer"
---

You may see the message "This site may harm your computer" when viewing the search results for your site:

Wix is vigilant when it comes to the security of site owners and their visitors. Wix systems do not allow sites to be modified in such a way that could make them harmful.

### You can remove this message by doing the following:

Verify your site with [Google Search Console](https://support.wix.com/en/article/verifying-your-site-with-google-search-console-5033765).  Remove the resources causing the problem by following [these instructions](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/45449?hl=en).   Submit your [sitemap ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/submitting-your-sitemap-and-urls-directly-to-search-engines)directly to Google.
