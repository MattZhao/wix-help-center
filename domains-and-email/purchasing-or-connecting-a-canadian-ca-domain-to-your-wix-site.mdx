---
title: "Purchasing or Connecting a Canadian (.ca) Domain to Your Wix Site"
---

Once you [upgrade your site with a plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website), you can [purchase a .ca domain directly from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977), or connect a .ca domain you already own to your Wix site.

In most cases, we recommend connecting your domain to Wix via nameservers. With this connection method, Wix hosts your DNS records, which means you can manage them in your Wix account. This way, if you ever need assistance managing your DNS records, Wix Customer Care can help you.

Learn how to [connect a domain to the Wix nameservers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-the-wix-name-servers).
