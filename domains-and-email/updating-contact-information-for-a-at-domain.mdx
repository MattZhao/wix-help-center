---
title: "Updating Contact Information for a .at Domain"
---

The .at domain extension is the country code domain extension (ccTLD) for Austria. You can update your contact information at any time by downloading a form and submitting it to Wix Customer Care. We then submit the new details on your behalf.

### To update contact information for a .at domain:

Download the [form at this webpage](https://help.opensrs.com/hc/article_attachments/8469864684187/atchangeofholder.pdf).

**Note: **The form contains reference to nic.at and says to fax to their contact details, but you can disregard this instruction.  Print the form out and fill in the relevant details.   [Contact Wix Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact)﻿ to provide legible scans (in PDF format) of the signed form.

> **Note:**
>
> It may take a few days for this change to show up in your account.
