A [domain name](https://www.wix.com/blog/what-is-a-domain) is the unique address people type into their browser to visit a website.

Domain names make it easy for visitors to find your site. Instead of having to type an IP address, which would be difficult to remember, they can type your memorable domain name instead.

A domain name is made up of:

**A name:** This can be any combination of letters and numbers, but it's usually the name of your company or brand. For example, 'Wix' in Wix.com.    **A domain extension:** The top-level domain ending that appears at the end of your domain name. For example, '.com' in wix.com.

A domain name makes up just one part of a site's full web address (URL). For example, most web addresses start with 'www.' Some sites also have [a subdomain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/subdomains-overview) like '**.blog**.wix.com' or '**store**.wix.com'.

### Personalize your free Wix URL

When you publish your site for the first time, your web address (URL) is in the following format: **siteprefix.wixsite.com/siteaddress**.

To make this more personalized, you can [edit the free Wix URL](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-free-wix-url-8591528):

**Site URL prefix: **By default this is generated from your name in your Wix account. You can edit your site URL in your Account Settings.  **Site address:** This is the name of your website. You choose your site name when saving your site for the first time. You can edit it at any time from your site dashboard.

> **Important:**
>
> This will not remove the 'wixsite.com/siteaddress' part of the URL. You need to [upgrade your site with a plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) before you can connect a [custom domain](https://www.wix.com/domains) to your site.

### Connect a domain you own to your Wix site

Wix makes it easy for you to transfer a domain you already own between your Wix sites, or from another host into your Wix account:

[Assign a domain purchased from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-domain-to-a-site-in-your-wix-account). If you already own a domain that you purchased from Wix, you can easily assign it to a site which has a Premium plan.  [Connect a domain you own from another host to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pointing-vs-name-servers-domain-connection-methods). There are two ways to connect your domain to Wix - via name servers or via pointing. The method you use to connect your domain determines where the DNS (Domain Name System) records of your domain are hosted.   [Transfer a domain you own from another host to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere-to-wix). You can transfer a domain from its current host (or '[registrar](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/what-2013-05-03-en)') to Wix. This option lets you manage not only your domain and DNS records at Wix, but also your contact information and your registration payments.
