---
title: "Domain Hosting with Wix"
---

You can purchase your own [domain name](https://www.wix.com/domains) directly from Wix. When purchasing a domain from Wix, the domain is automatically connected to the Wix name servers. Learn more about [purchasing a domain from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977).

If you purchased a domain from another domain host, you can still [connect it to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere). You can also transfer your domain to Wix to manage everything domain-related within your Wix account.

> **Tip:**
>
> Upgrade to selected yearly [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) site plans to receive a free domain for a year.
