---
title: "Request: Transferring a .co.uk Domain to Wix"
---

Currently, it is not possible to transfer a .co.uk domain to Wix.

However, you can connect a .co.uk domain, purchased from another domain host to your Wix site. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-the-wix-name-servers) to learn more about connecting a domain to your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
