---
title: "Google Workspace: Business Email Sending Limits"
---

Google Workspace limits the amount of mail a user can send to keep the systems healthy and your accounts safe.

The limits restrict the number of messages sent per day and the number of recipients per message. After reaching one of these limits, a user cannot send new messages but can still access his or her account to receive incoming email.

Click [here](https://support.google.com/a/answer/166852) to learn more about Google Workspace email sending limitations.
