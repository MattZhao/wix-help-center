---
title: "Wix Stores Notification Emails are Sent to Previous Business Email Address (Includes Workaround)"
---

We're aware of the issue and are currently gathering more information regarding this matter.

> **Meanwhile, you can use the following workaround:**
>
> 1. Navigate to the business info section of your site's dashboard.
> 2. Remove the existing email address.
> 3. Click **Save**.
> 4. Go back and add the correct email address.
> 5. Click **Save**.
