---
title: "Creating a Wix Site Using Your Microsoft Office 365 Subscription"
---

Wix has partnered with Microsoft Office 365. If you're an Office 365 subscriber, you can now easily connect your domain to Wix and build a stunning site.

### To use Wix with your Microsoft Office 365 subscription:

Sign in to your Office 365 account.  Go to your Office 365 admin center.  Click** Public Website** on the left.  Click **Learn more **under Wix.   Click **Start Now** on the Wix.com Microsoft Office 365 page.  [Sign up](https://support.wix.com/en/article/signing-up-for-a-free-wix-account) for a Wix account.

**Note**: Click **I'm an existing user** and sign in to your Wix account to use an existing Wix account.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [choosing your template](https://support.wix.com/en/article/searching-for-the-perfect-template) and get started with building your dream site!

Once you've built your site and are ready to publish it, upgrade to any of our [Premium Plans](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website) and [connect your Office 365 domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-the-wix-name-servers) to your Wix site.
