---
title: "Activating Your Microsoft Office 365 Mailbox Purchased from Wix"
---

Once you've purchased a Microsoft Office 365 mailbox from Wix, you must activate it before you can use it.

> **Important:**
>
> This feature is currently not available to all users.

### To activate your Microsoft Office 365 mailbox:

[Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) in your Wix account.  Click **Activate Mailboxes **next to the relevant mailbox.  Enter a password.  Verify your password.  Click **Submit**
