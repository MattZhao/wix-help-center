---
title: "Adding or Updating TXT Records in Your Wix Account"
---

TXT (Text) records are used for various purposes such as verifying ownership of a domain. You can add or update your Text (TXT) records from the Domains page of your Wix account.

> **Important:**
>
> If your domain is connected to Wix via pointing, you must add or update TXT records with your domain host (not Wix).

### To add or update a TXT record:

Go to the [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) page.Click the **Show More **icon  next to the relevant domain and select **Manage DNS Records**.

Add or update your domain's TXT record:

### Show me how to add a TXT record

1. Click **+ Add Record **in the **TXT (Text)** section.
2. Click the **Host Name** field and enter the host name.
**Note: **Leave this field blank if instructed to add an **@** sign.
3. Click the **Value** field and enter the TXT value.
4. Click **Save**.

### Show me how to update a TXT record

1. Hover over the TXT record and click **Edit**.
2. Click the **Host Name** field and enter the host name.
**Note:** Leave this field blank if instructed to add an **@** sign.
3. Click the **Value** field and enter the TXT value.
4. Click **Save**.
