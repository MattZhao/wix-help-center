title: "Credit Card Charged for Free Domain Voucher"
---

If your credit card was charged when purchasing a domain using your free domain voucher, it is possible that:

**You added additional years of service: **The free domain voucher covers the domain registration cost for the first year only. You need to pay for additional years.**You registered the domain as private: **This is an additional cost.
