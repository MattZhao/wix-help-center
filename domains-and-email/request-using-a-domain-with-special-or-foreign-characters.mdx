---
title: "Request: Using a Domain with Special or Foreign Characters"
---

Currently, it is not possible to purchase a domain from, or transfer a domain to Wix which contains special or foreign characters. This feature requires IDN (international domain names) support which is not available at this time.

> You can, however, keep the domain hosted elsewhere and [connect a domain with special characters to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-with-special-or-foreign-characters-to-your-wix-site).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
