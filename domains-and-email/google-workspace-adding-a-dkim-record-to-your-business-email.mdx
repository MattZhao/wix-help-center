---
title: "Google Workspace: Adding a DKIM Record to your Business Email"
---

A DKIM record verifies that your email was sent from a reliable, trustworthy source, and was not modified in transit.

If you have a Google Workspace business email, you can [add a DKIM record](https://support.google.com/a/answer/174124?hl=en) for your domain.
