---
title: "Request: Changing the Payment Cycle for Your Wix Business Email Subscription"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the payment cycle of a Google Workspace business email purchased through Wix.

If you selected the yearly payment cycle, you cannot change it to a monthly one, or vice versa.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
