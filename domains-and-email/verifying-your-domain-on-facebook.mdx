title: "Verifying Your Domain on Facebook"
---

To track events on your site using Meta pixel after the release of iOS14 in February 2021, you must verify your domain on Facebook.

### Step 1 | Confirm you own your site domain on Facebook

Verify your domain on Facebook using the [meta tag method](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/sharing/domain-verification/verifying-your-domain#meta-tags). **Note:** It is not possible to upload an HTML file to confirm domain ownership.  Copy your meta tag.

---

### Step 2 | Add your Facebook Meta Tag to your site

Go to [Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Custom Code** under **Advanced Settings**.  Click **Add Custom Code** at the top right.  Paste your Facebook meta tag in the text box.   Enter a meaningful name for your code so you can quickly identify it later if needed.   In **Add code to pages**, select **All pages**.  In **Place your code in**, choose **Head**.  Click **Apply**.

---

### Step 3 | Return to Facebook to complete verification

Follow the Meta instructions to [associate Facebook pages with your verified domain](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/sharing/domain-verification/verifying-your-domain#associate-pages).
