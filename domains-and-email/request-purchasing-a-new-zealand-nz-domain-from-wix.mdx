---
title: "Request: Purchasing a New Zealand (.nz) Domain from Wix"
---

Currently, it is not possible to purchase a New Zealand (.nz) domain from Wix.

However, if you would like to use a New Zealand domain you can purchase one from another domain host and connect it to your Wix site. Learn more about [connecting a domain purchased elsewhere to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

> **Important:**
>
> To connect a domain to your site, you must [upgrade your site with a plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
