---
title: "Google Workspace: Accessing Your Admin Console"
---

From the Google Workspace admin console, you can set up your business email users, manage and edit your business email users and reset user passwords.

### To access your Google Workspace Admin console:

Go to your [Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) page.   Click the **Show More**  icon next to the relevant email account.  Click **Manage at Google.**  Sign in to your Google Workspace account.

**Note**: If you are already signed in to a different Google account, click the profile image or initial at the top right and sign in with your admin email address.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also access your Google Workspace Admin console by signing in at [admin.google.com](https://admin.google.com).
