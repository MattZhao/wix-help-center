---
title: "Google Workspace: Revoking Admin Status to Delete a Workspace Account"
---

If you need to [delete a Google Workspace user](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-reducing-your-monthly-business-email-users) who is currently an Admin for your organization, you will need to first revoke their Admin status.

If the account you need to delete is your own Admin account, you will first need to assign an Admin role to a different business email user in your organization. Alternatively, you can [change your username](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-renaming-a-workspace-user) if your email is the only business email in the account.

If the account you need to delete is the same one you use to access the Admin console, you will first need to create a second Google Workspace Admin account.

#### In this article, learn how to:

[Revoke a Google Workspace Admin status for another user](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-revoking-admin-status-to-delete-a-workspace-account#revoking-an-admin-status-for-another-user)  [Revoke your own Google Workspace Admin status](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-revoking-admin-status-to-delete-a-workspace-account#revoking-your-own-admin-status)

### Revoking an Admin status for another user

Log into the Google Admin console using your Admin account.  Click **Users** on the Admin console home page.  Click the name of the Admin whose privileges you wish to revoke.  Click **Admin roles and privileges**.  Click **Roles**.  Disable the **Super Admin** toggle.

  Click **Save**.

---

### Revoking your own Admin status

Log into the Google Admin console using your Admin account.  Click **Users** on the Admin console home page.  Click the name of the user who you want to be the new Admin.  Click **Admin roles and privileges**.  Click **Roles**, and enable the **Super Admin** toggle.

  Click **Save**.  Log into the Google Admin Console using the newly-assigned Admin account username.   Click **Users** on the Admin console home page.   Click the name of the Admin whose privileges you wish to revoke.   Click **Admin roles and privileges**.   Click **Roles.**  Disable the **Super Admin **toggle.   Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> Once you have revoked the required Admin status, you can delete unwanted Workspace accounts for [monthly](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-reducing-your-monthly-business-email-users) or [yearly](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-reducing-your-yearly-business-email-users) subscriptions, as needed.
