---
title: "Google Workspace: Checking Your Business Email Subscription Renewal Date"
---

Business email subscriptions are set to renew automatically at the end of the subscription period. You can [turn off the auto renew](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-turning-off-auto-renewal-for-a-business-email) setting if you do not wish for it to renew.

### To check the renewal date of your business email subscription:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Check the **Next Payment Due** or **Upcoming payment **column next to your subscription.

> **Renewal Date vs. Expiration Date**
>
> The date in the **Next Payment Due** or **Upcoming payment **column shows the renewal date. This date may differ slightly from the expiration date. Hover over the **Info** icon  to learn more. If the auto renew has been turned off, the expiration date appears instead of the renewal date.
