---
title: "Request: Transferring a German (.de) Domain to Your Wix Site"
---

Currently, purchasing a domain with a German (.de) extension from Wix is only available to users in Germany. It is not possible to transfer a German (.de) domain to Wix.

> **Important:**
>
> To connect a domain to your site, you must [upgrade your site with a plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
