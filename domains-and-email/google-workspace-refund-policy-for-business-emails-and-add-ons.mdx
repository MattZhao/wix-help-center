---
title: "Google Workspace: Refund Policy for Business Emails and Add-Ons"
---

A Google Workspace subscription gives you access to business emails and additional add-on services such as Gemini for Workspace. View your subscriptions from the [Business Email and Add-ons page](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/my-mailboxes-site-level/mailboxes?&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Business+Email+and+Add-ons) in your site's dashboard.

---

### Business email (Google Workspace)

Google Workspace business emails purchased through Wix are non-refundable. If you feel that you deserve a refund, [fill out the refund form](https://www.wix.com/refund/) to tell us why and we will review your request.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can cancel your Google Workspace account if you no longer need a business email. Learn more about [canceling your business email renewal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-canceling-your-business-email-renewal).

---

### Gemini for Workspace

Gemini for Workspace subscriptions are non-refundable. However, if you feel you deserve a refund you can [contact us](https://www.wix.com/refund/) and we will review your request.

> **Gemini 30-day free trial**
>
> The Gemini for Workspace add-on comes with a free trial for 30 days. If you cancel within the first 30 days of your subscription, you will not pay for Gemini. If you cancel after the 30 day trial, you will be turning auto-renew off. This means that you will pay for the first billing cycle, after which your Gemini subscription will automatically be canceled and you will no longer pay for it.
