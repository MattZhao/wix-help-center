---
title: "Domain Status: 'ClientHold' or 'ServerHold'"
---

If your domain status is 'clientHold' or 'serverHold' on Whois, your domain registrar has not yet activated your domain's DNS records.

This can be due to several reasons, such as payment failure. To resolve this issue, you must contact your domain registrar.

> **Note:**
>
> This domain status can be viewed when visiting [whois.com](https://www.whois.com/) or any other querying databases that store domain registration information.
