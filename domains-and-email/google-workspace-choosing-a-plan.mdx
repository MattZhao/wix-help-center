Purchase a personalized Google Workspace business email from Wix to enhance your professional profile. Wix offers seamless integration with Google Workspace which allows you to manage many Google Workspace applications under your personalized domain name, across different devices.

> **Important:**
>
> - To have a business email, you must own a domain (from Wix or elsewhere) and add it to the Domains** **section of your account.
> - You can purchase several business emails for each of your domains.

#### Google Workspace business email plan details

Each plan offers different services and has different amounts of cloud storage per user. Explore the 3 options below to see what plan features are offered and find the one that best suits your needs.

If, at a later date, you decide you need additional storage or features, you can [upgrade your Workspace plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-upgrading-a-workspace-plan) whenever you need to.

### Business Starter

- Business email
- Phishing and spam protection
- Ad-free email experience
- 2-step verification
- Enforced ssl connections
- Alert Center: notifications of potential security issues
- Basic [endpoint management](https://support.google.com/a/answer/9852079#fundamental) (ensuring secure connection on different devices)
- 30 gb cloud storage per user
- Up to 100 participants in Chat and Google Meet video calls

### Business Standard

All the features of Business Starter with the following extras:
- 2 TB cloud storage per user
- Up to 150 participants in Chat and Google Meet video calls
- Record Google Meet meetings
- Shared Drives for teams

### Business Plus

All the features of Business Starter with the following extras:
- 5 TB cloud storage per user
- Up to 250 participants in Chat and Google Meet video calls
- Advanced endpoint management (ensuring secure connection on different devices)
- Google [Vault](https://support.google.com/a/answer/2462365) for eDiscovery and information governance
