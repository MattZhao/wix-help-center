---
title: "Google Workspace: Data Migration from an External Email to Your Google Workspace"
---

It is not possible to migrate data between email hosts.

While you may have registered the same email address, the data for each email account remains at the respective host with which you registered the email account. Unfortunately, we are unable to enable data migration between mail hosts.
