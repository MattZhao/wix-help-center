---
title: "Google Workspace: Contacting Google Support"
---

If you have questions about your Google Workspace business emails that are unrelated to billing, adding new users, or transferring your account away from Wix, you must contact [Google Workspace Support](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1047213) for assistance.

### To contact Google Workspace support:

[Sign in](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-accessing-your-admin-console) to your Google Workspace Admin console.  Click the **Support icon** in the top right corner of the page.

Click **Contact Support** at the bottom of the pop-up window  Click **contact Google support** at the bottom.

Follow Google's directions to contact Google Workspace support.
