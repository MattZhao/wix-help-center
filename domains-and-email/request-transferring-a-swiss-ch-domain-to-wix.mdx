---
title: "Request: Transferring a Swiss (.ch) Domain to Wix"
---

Currently, it is not possible to transfer a Swiss (.ch) domain extension to Wix. However, you can connect a .ch domain that you already own to your Wix site. Learn more about [connecting a domain purchased elsewhere to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

> **Important:**
>
> To connect a domain to your site, you must [upgrade your site with a plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
