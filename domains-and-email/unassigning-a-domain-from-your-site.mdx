---
title: "Unassigning a Domain from Your Site"
---

You can instantly disconnect your domain from your Wix site by unassigning it. You can always [assign the domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-a-domain-to-a-site-in-your-wix-account) to another site at a later stage.

### To unassign a domain:

Go to the [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) page.Click the **Show More **icon ﻿﻿ next to the relevant domain and select **Unassign from This Site**.

Click **Unassign Domain**.
