---
title: "Adding or Updating CNAME Records in Your Wix Account"
---

A Canonical Name (CNAME) record is a type of Domain Name System (DNS) record that maps an alias name to a true or canonical domain name. You can add or update your CNAME records from the Domains page of your Wix account.

> **Important:**
>
> If your domain is connected to Wix via [pointing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pointing-vs-name-servers-domain-connection-methods), you must add or update CNAME records with your domain host (not Wix).

### To add or update a CNAME record:

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) in your Wix account.  Click the **Domain Actions** icon  next to the relevant domain and select **Manage DNS Records**.

Add or update your domain's CNAME record:

### Add a CNAME record

1. Click **+ Add Record **in the **CNAME (Aliases)** section.
2. Click **Got it** in the pop-up message.
3. Click the **Host Name** field and enter a host name.
**Note:** Leave this field blank if instructed to add an **@** sign.
4. Click the **Value** field and enter a web address the record points to.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Save Changes** in the pop-up message.

### Update a CNAME record

1. Hover over the CNAME record and click **Edit**.
2. Click **Got it** in the pop-up message.
3. Click the **Host Name** field and edit the host name.
**Note:** Leave this field blank if instructed to add an **@** sign.
4. Click the **Value** field and edit the web address the record points to.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Save Changes** in the pop-up message.
