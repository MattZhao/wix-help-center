---
title: "Checking Your Domain Subscription Renewal Date"
---

Domains purchased from Wix are set to automatically renew **30 days before** their expiration date. This allows for more time to make a renewal payment (in case of decline) to ensure your domain remains active.

You can [turn off the auto renew](https://support.wix.com/en/article/turning-the-auto-renewal-off-for-a-wix-premium-subscription) setting if you do not want your domain to renew.

### To check the renewal date of your domain subscription:

[Go to Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Find the domain you want to check.   Check the **Next Payment Due** or **Upcoming payment **column.

> **Renewal Date vs. Expiration Date**
>
> The **Next Payment Due** or **Upcoming payment **column shows the renewal date. This date may differ slightly from the expiration date. Hover over the **Info** icon  to learn more. If the auto renew has been turned off, the expiration date appears instead of the renewal date.
