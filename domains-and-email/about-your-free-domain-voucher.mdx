title: "About Your Free Domain Voucher"
---

As a promotional gift, you receive a 1 year free domain voucher with the initial purchase of selected yearly or multi-year [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) plans.

### Where can I view my vouchers?

Go to [the Premium Vouchers page](https://manage.wix.com/account/vouchers?referralAdditionalInfo=%7Btheir) in your Wix account to see and claim your vouchers.

> **Note:**
>
> This voucher is only included with the first-time purchase of a site plan. It is not included when renewing a plan or upgrading an existing plan. The voucher is included when [changing your subscription period](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-the-subscription-period-of-your-premium-plan) from monthly to yearly.

---

### Claiming the voucher

The 1 year free domain voucher enables you to claim a **free domain for one year**. The voucher is valid for two months from the date that you purchased your plan.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can check the expiration date of your voucher in the [Premium Vouchers section of your account](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/voucherConsole).

Claiming this voucher requires you to enter your payment information, but you won't be charged for the first year of the subscription. This allows our system to process renewal payments so your domain stays live after your first free year.

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I apply this voucher to a domain I've purchased in the past?

No. The free domain voucher cannot be applied retroactively to a domain purchased before you purchased your yearly plan.

### Can I apply this voucher to a domain I want to transfer to Wix?

Yes. You can also choose to [redeem your voucher when transferring a domain to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/redeeming-your-1-year-free-domain-voucher-when-transferring-your-domain-to-wix). 
If you have a valid domain voucher, it is applied automatically when you select the registration extension period for the domain in your Wix account during the transfer process.

### Does this voucher include private registration?

The free domain voucher does not include [privacy protection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/activating-private-registration-for-a-wix-domain). However, you can purchase this option for an additional fee.

### I purchased a plan but didn't get this voucher. Why?

If you didn't receive the domain voucher, or believe it wasn't credited to your domain purchase, you may not have been eligible for one of the following reasons: 
- **You purchased a monthly plan:** The free domain voucher is offered as a gift with purchase of selected **yearly** or **multi-year **Premium or Studio plans. If you chose a monthly billing cycle, you won't receive a free domain voucher.
- **You purchased your domain name before purchasing a plan: **If you purchased your domain name prior to purchasing your Premium plan or Studio plan, it is not possible to apply the voucher retroactively or receive a refund.
- **Additional Features:** You redeemed your voucher, but purchased additional features such as privacy, or additional years of service on top of this.

If none of the points above apply to you, [contact Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support) and we will be happy to check the status of your voucher.

### I'm a collaborator on someone else's site and I need to purchase a domain. Can I apply a voucher?

Yes. Billing Managers can apply a domain voucher on behalf of the site owner. Learn more about [domain voucher roles and permissions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-voucher-roles-permissions).
