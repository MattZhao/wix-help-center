---
title: "Transferring an .mx domain away from Wix"
---

If you have purchased an .mx domain from Wix, you can later transfer it to another domain host.

### To transfer your .mx Wix domain away from Wix:

Go to the [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) page.  Click the Show More** **icon  next to the relevant domain and select **Transfer Away from Wix**.  Click **Transfer Domain**.  Click** I Still Want to Transfer**.

In some cases, you may receive a message from the domain provider during the transfer process indicating that your .mx domain is blocked. This happens because sometimes Wix domains with an .mx extension need to be unlocked so they can be transferred outside of Wix.

#### If your .mx domain is blocked:

In order to unlock the domain so you can start transferring it away from Wix, please contact [Wix Customer Care](http://www.wix.com/contact) for further help. Please have your account and domain name ready.
