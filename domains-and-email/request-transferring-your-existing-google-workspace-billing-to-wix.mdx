---
title: "Request: Transferring Your Existing Google Workspace Billing to Wix"
---

Currently, transferring your existing Google Workspace billing to Wix is not supported. Wix cannot accept [Google transfer tokens](https://support.google.com/a/answer/7643790?hl=en&ref_topic=7643600).

If you already have an account with Google Workspace or if your domain is already listed as an [alias](https://support.google.com/a/answer/56238?hl=en) in Google Workspace, you will need to manage your account billing with Google.

> **Note:**
>
> You can connect your existing Google Workspace business email to Wix. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-an-email-purchased-outside-of-wix) to learn how.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
