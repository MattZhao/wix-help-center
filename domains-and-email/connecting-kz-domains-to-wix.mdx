---
title: "Connecting .KZ Domains to Wix"
---

From Feb 1, 2021, the Kazakh government has enacted a new [data localization regulation](https://www.morganlewis.com/-/media/files/publication/outside-publication/article/2019/datalocalizationlawsoverviewkazakhstan.ashx?la=en&hash=CAAEA6B82869DD66B3BA8D3E62D05DDE8CBCC7EE). This regulation states that all .kz domains must be hosted within Kazakhstan.

Wix does not have any hosting facilities in Kazakhstan and, as a result, Wix users cannot connect their .kz domain to their Wix site. If you are an existing Wix user with a .kz domain, you will have to connect a domain with a different [domain extension](https://support.wix.com/en/article/list-of-available-domain-extensions) to keep using your Wix site.

For new Wix users in Kazakhstan, we recommend you purchase /connect a domain extension other than .kz for your Wix site to be accessible.

> **Tip:**
>
> [Upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) and receive a free [domain voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-your-free-1-year-domain-voucher) valid for the following extensions: .art, .com, .net, .org, .info, .biz, .rocks, .pictures, .club, .space, .shop, .store, .online, .co.uk, and .xyz.
