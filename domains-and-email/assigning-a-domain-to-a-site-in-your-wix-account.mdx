---
title: "Assigning a Domain to a Site in Your Wix Account"
---

Once you've added a domain to your Wix account, you can assign it to any upgraded site in your account.

> **Important:**
>
> - To connect your domain, you must [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).
> - If you try to assign a domain to a site which doesn't have a [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) site plan, you will be asked to either upgrade your site or reassign an existing plan from another site.

### To assign a domain to a site in your account:

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) in your Wix account.  Go to the [Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) page.

**Note**: If you have more than one domain in your account, scroll to the domain you want to connect.   Assign your domain to your site:

### I want to assign a domain that is not currently assigned to a site

1. Click **Assign** next to **Not Assigned to a Site **in the Unassigned Domains section.
2. Select a site and click **Next**. 

**Note**: If the site is currently connected to a different domain, choose an option:

- **Redirect it to the primary domain:** Redirect your domain to the site's primary domain that is already connected.
- **Replace the current primary domain:** Connect your domain and disconnect the site's current domain.
3. Click **Assign**.

Learn more about [redirecting domains to a primary domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-multiple-domains-to-one-wix-site).

### I want to assign a domain that is currently assigned to a different site

1. Click the **Domain Actions **icon  and select **Assign to a Different Site**.
2. Select a site and click **Next**.

**Note**: If the site is currently connected to a different domain, choose an option:

- **Redirect it to the primary domain:** Redirect your domain to the site's primary domain that is already connected.
- **Replace the current primary domain:** Connect your domain and disconnect the site's current domain.
3. Click **Assign**.

Learn more about [redirecting domains to a primary domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-multiple-domains-to-one-wix-site).

> **If you haven't added a domain to your Wix account:**
>
> First add a domain using one of the following methods:
> - [Purchase a domain from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977)
> - [Connect a domain purchased elsewhere to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere)
> - [Transfer a domain to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere-to-wix)
> - [Transfer a domain from another Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/moving-a-connected-domain-purchased-elsewhere-from-one-wix-account-to-another)
