### Popular domain extensions

The most well-known and popular top-level domains (TLDs) are:

[.com](https://www.wix.com/blog/what-is-com-domain)  .org  [.net](https://www.wix.com/blog/what-is-net-domain)

.com is often considered the default extension for web addresses, and is widely trusted by online users.

.org is typically used by non-profit organizations, but may also include other organizations, communities, and educational institutes.

.net is widely used by businesses, individuals, and technology-related websites.

You can [purchase all of these popular domains on Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977). Learn more about the [difference between .com, .org, and .net domains](https://www.wix.com/blog/org-vs-com-vs-net-domain-extensions) in the Wix Blog.

### Country code domain extensions

Country code top-level domains (ccTLDs) show the country, geographic location, or language of a website. This is a good option if you want to personalize or localize your website. For example: .co.uk for the United Kingdom; .de for Germany; and .ca for Canada.

> **Note:**
>
> Some ccTLDs may have specific requirements or restrictions associated with them. [Check out the FAQ at the end of this page for more information](https://support.wix.com/en/article/list-of-available-domain-extensions#faqs).

The following ccTLDs are available for purchase from Wix:

**.at** (Austria)  **.be **(Belgium)  **.ca** (Canada)   **.ch** (Switzerland)  **.co** (Colombia)  **.co.uk** (United Kingdom)  **.com.br** (Brazil)  **.de** (Germany)  **.fr** (France, Belgium, Germany, Switzerland, and Israel only)  **.in** (India)  **.it** (Italy)  **.mx** (Mexico)  **.nl** (Netherlands)

### Are there any restrictions on purchasing ccTLDs?

Some country-specific domains do have certain restrictions or requirements that you need to be aware of.

- **For .com.br domains:** There are some restrictions on purchasing a .com.br domain. [See additional information (in Portuguese)](https://support.wix.com/pt/article/informa%C3%A7%C3%B5es-importantes-sobre-como-transferir-o-seu-dom%C3%ADnio-para-o-wix).
- **For .be domains: **As of May 2022, DNS Belgium (the organization responsible for the management of .be domain names) may require additional verification from customers. You will receive an email from DNS Belgium asking to validate your details online. [Learn more about this process](https://www.dnsbelgium.be/en/registrant-verification?bulk_email_rid=634&bpmtrackid=2&bpmreplica=0&contactId=40d3f227-7124-49e8-84da-0cead4cb2758&bulkEmailRecipientId=e4fdd30b-4e4c-4c2c-b99c-e9c0650c21ec#:~:text=Instructional%20video%20for%20online%20verification%C2%A0%20%C2%A0) from the DNS Belgium website.

### The domain I'm looking for isn't in the list. What can I do?

If the domain extension that you would like to use is not available for purchase from Wix, you can purchase the domain from another domain host and [connect the domain to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

### Do I get a free domain with my Wix site?

If you upgrade to an eligible [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites), you will receive a [free domain voucher](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-your-free-1-year-domain-voucher) valid for the following extensions:
.com, .co.in, .co.uk, .net, .org, .art, .at, .be, .biz, .blog, .ca, .ch, .club, .coach, .com.br, .co.at, .firm.in, .fit, .fitness, .fun, .gen.in, .in, .ind.in, .info, .live, .net.in, .nl, .online, .or.at, .org.in, .pictures, .rocks, .shop, .site, .space, .store, .studio, .training, .work, .xyz, .yoga, and .de (Germany only).
