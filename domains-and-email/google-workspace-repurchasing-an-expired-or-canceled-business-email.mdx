---
title: "Google Workspace: Repurchasing an Expired or Canceled Business Email"
---

You can repurchase an expired or canceled Google Workspace business email through your Wix account.

> **Important:**
>
> According to Google's Terms of Service, business email data is retained for 180 days from the expiration date. If you repurchase your business email within this time frame, your business email data will be restored.

### To repurchase your Google Workspace business email:

[Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) in your Wix account.  Click **Renew Subscription **under the relevant business email.

Follow the steps in the pop-up window to repurchase your business email.
