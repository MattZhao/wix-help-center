---
title: "Canceling Your Site Plan and Domain"
---

Your site plan (Premium or Studio plan) and domain name are separate services and therefore need to be canceled separately.

Learn more about [canceling your Premium or Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-a-wix-premium-plan).  Learn more about [canceling your Wix domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-a-wix-domain).
