---
title: "Resetting the Password for Additional Office 365 Mailboxes"
---

If you are the owner (admin) of your Office 365 mailbox, you can easily change the password of your additional mailboxes.

> **Important:**
>
> This feature is currently not available to all users.

### To reset the password for your Office 365 mailbox:

[Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) in your Wix account.  Click **Manage at Microsoft**.   Sign in to your mailbox:

1. Enter your email address.
2. Enter your password.
3. Click **Sign In**.
    Click **Users**.   Click **Active Users**.   Select the checkbox next to relevant user.  Click **Rest Password** and choose one of the following:

1. **Auto-generate password**.
2. **Let me create the password**.
3. Select the checkbox next to **Make this user change their password when they first sign in**.
    Click **Reset**.
