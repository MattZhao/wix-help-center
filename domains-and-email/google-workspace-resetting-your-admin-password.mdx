---
title: "Google Workspace: Resetting Your Admin Password"
---

If you need to reset your Google Workspace admin password, you can do so at any time.

### To reset the admin password for your Google Workspace business email:

[Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) in your Wix account.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the admin email.  Click **Reset admin password**.  Enter your new password.  Confirm your new password.  Click **Reset Password**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you are still unable to access your Google Workspace business email, click [here](https://support.google.com/accounts/troubleshooter/2402620?hl=en&ref_topic=2364467) for the Google Workspace Login Troubleshooter or click [here](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7126229?hl=en&visit_id=636830995134137254-1053383169&rd=2#cantsignin) for the Google Workspace Help article.
