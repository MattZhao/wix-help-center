---
title: "Request: Transferring a French (.fr) Domain to Wix"
---

Currently, it is not possible to transfer a French (.fr) domain to Wix. In the meantime, you can connect a .fr domain to Wix by following the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can purchase a new .fr domain from Wix. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977).
