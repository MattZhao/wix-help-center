---
title: "Google Workspace: Setting Up a Domain Alias"
---

If you own multiple domains, you can [set up an alias for your Google Workspace business email](https://support.google.com/a/answer/53295?hl=en) in your Google Workspace Admin console.

If you use a secondary domain as a domain alias, then all users on your primary domain (the domain that you used when signing up for Google Workspace services) automatically receive a second email address with the alias domain.

> **Tip:**
>
> Adding a domain alias in Google Workspace is an option if you want to have another option for the business email domain name you purchased. For example, if you own both mylovelyshop.com and mylovelyonlineshop.com domain names, you can set up business emails for both.

> **Note:**
>
> This feature is subject to certain [limitations](http://s.bl-1.com/h/jVmwFzk).
