A subdomain is an extension of your primary [domain name](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domains). It is added in front of your root domain to form a separate web address. Subdomains are usually used to bring visitors to a specific page or section of your website. It is still part of the main website, but it may be considered a separate site by search engines.

For example, the address for a blog subdomain might look like this: **blog**.example.com.

---

### Benefits of Subdomains

Some benefits of using subdomains include:

**Organization:** Subdomains can help to separate different sections of a website, making it easier for visitors to find the content they're looking for. For example, you might use one subdomain for an online store, and another one for your blog.  **Branding:** Subdomains are useful for creating webpages with different identities, while still being related to your main website. For example, an online store can differentiate between its physical and online stores, or a news website can use a different subdomain for each section of the site.   **Search results:** Subdomains can help to improve a website's SEO, with each subdomain appearing separately to the main site in search results. This can help increase the website's visibility, driving more traffic to the site.

### Can I connect a subdomain to a specific page on my Wix site (other than the homepage)?

Currently, it is only possible to connect a subdomain to your site's home page using the methods mentioned above. If this is something you would like to see Wix offer in the future, you can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-directing-domains-or-subdomains-to-specific-pages-on-my-wix-site).

### Can I connect a business email to a subdomain?

Currently, it is not possible to connect a business email (add MX records) to a subdomain in Wix. If this is something you would like to see Wix offer in the future, you can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-connecting-a-mailbox-to-a-subdomain).
