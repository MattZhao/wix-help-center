---
title: "Redeeming Your 1 Year Free Domain Voucher When Transferring Your Domain to Wix"
---

As a promotional gift, you receive a one year free domain voucher with the** initial purchase** of selected **yearly **[Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) site plans.

You can also redeem your 1 year free domain voucher after [transferring your domain to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere-to-wix).

If you have a valid domain voucher, it is applied automatically when you select the registration extension period for the domain in your Wix account during the transfer process.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### What happens to the privacy setting of my domain?

Your transferred domain does not inherit the privacy setting from your old registrar.  Your privacy setting changes to the setting you select when you transfer your domain to Wix. You cannot change this setting (this is true for domain registration in general, not just with Wix).

### What domain extension transfers can the free domain voucher be used with?

The free domain voucher can be used when transferring the following domain extensions: 
.art, .biz, .blog, .ca, .club, .coach, .com, .fit, .fitness, .fun, .info, .live, .net, .online, .org, .pictures, .rocks, .shop, .site, .space, .store, .studio, .training, .work, .xyz, .yoga

### What happens to the length of the registration period of my domain after transferring it to Wix?

Domains transferred to Wix retain the same length of their registration period; any remaining time is added to the one-year mandatory extension.
