---
title: "Connecting an Italian (.it) Domain to Your Wix Site"
---

Once you [upgrade your site with a plan](http://www.wix.com/upgrade/website), you can connect a .it domain you already own to your Wix site.

> **Important:**
>
> You will need access to your** **current domain registrar when you’re preparing to connect your domain.

In most cases, we recommend [connecting your domain to Wix via name servers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-the-wix-name-servers).  With this connection method, Wix hosts your DNS records, which means you can manage them in your Wix account. This way, if you ever need assistance managing your DNS records, Wix Customer Care can help you.

There is another connection method called [pointing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-wix-using-the-pointing-method). However, it is not recommended as we're unable to assist you with some issues that may arise. Learn more about the differences between [connecting your domain via name servers vs. pointing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pointing-vs-name-servers-domain-connection-methods).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### How long can it take for my domain to be connected (propagate) to my Wix account?

New DNS records can take up to 48 hours to fully propagate.

### Can I purchase a .it domain from Wix?

Yes, you can choose to purchase a .it domain from Wix. Learn more about [purchasing a domain from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/purchasing-a-domain-from-wix-3133977).

### Can I transfer my .it domain to Wix instead of connecting it?

No. This option is not currently available for .it domains.
