---
title: "Microsoft Office 365 Mailbox Limits"
---

> Microsoft Office 365 is not available to all users.

Microsoft Office 365 limits a variety of services, including mailbox storage, message size, and the amount of sending and receiving. These limits are applied in order to combat spam and improve service performance.

Click [here](https://technet.microsoft.com/en-us/library/exchange-online-limits.aspx#RecipientLimits) to learn more about Microsoft Office 365 email sending limitations.
