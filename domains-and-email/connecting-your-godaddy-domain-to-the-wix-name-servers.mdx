---
title: "Connecting Your GoDaddy Domain to the Wix Name Servers"
---

You can [create a website](https://www.wix.com/) with Wix and connect your GoDaddy domain by updating the name servers in your GoDaddy account. This allows Wix to manage your DNS settings while your domain stays registered with GoDaddy. Note that you must upgrade your Wix site to connect your own domain.

This article guides you through the process and helps you to take the steps needed to change the name servers in your GoDaddy account. Check out the video below to get started.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - To connect your domain, you must [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).
> - If you have Office 365 by GoDaddy email or DNS records you do not want to change, consider [connecting via pointing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-your-godaddy-domain-to-wix-via-pointing-9528059) instead. If you use pointing, you don't need to reconfigure your DNS records in Wix. Learn more about the [differences between connection methods](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pointing-vs-name-servers-domain-connection-methods).

---

### Step 1 |  Add the domain to your Wix account

Before updating the DNS settings in your GoDaddy account, go to the [Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) page in your Wix account and add the GoDaddy domain.

#### To add the domain to your Wix account:

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) ﻿in your Wix account.  Click** Add an Existing Domain**.  Click **Connect a domain you already own**.

Select the site you want to connect your domain to and click **Next**.  Enter your domain name and click **Let's Go**.  Click **Yes, I own this domain **under the search field.   Review the info about updating your name servers and click **Start**.

---

### Step 2 | Update the name servers in your domain host account (GoDaddy)

To connect your GoDaddy domain to your Wix account, you must update your domain's name server settings in your GoDaddy account.

### Show me how to update the name severs in GoDaddy

1. Log into your GoDaddy account.

1. Open a new tab in your browser.
2. Go to your GoDaddy account site and log in.
3. Return to the Wix domains page.
4. Click **I logged in**.
2. Find your domain settings page.

1. Click your profile at the top right and select **My Products**.
2. Find your domain and go to the settings panel.
3. Click **DNS** to the right of your domain.
4. Return to the Wix domains page.
5. Click **I found my domain settings**.
3. Find your name servers

1. In your GoDaddy DNS settings, click** Change** in the name servers section.
2. Select **Enter my own name servers (advanced)**
3. Return to the Wix domains page.
4. Click **I found the name servers**.
4. Replace your name servers.

1. In the **Enter My Own Name servers** section, replace your name servers with the Wix name servers provided on the Wix domains page.

**Note:** The NS records may be different for you than those in the image below. Please use the NS records that you see in your domains page.
2. Click **I've replaced my name servers**.

### How long does it take the new name servers to propagate?

It may take up to 48 hours for the name servers changes to [propagate worldwide](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-propagation).

### Is there a way I can transfer my GoDaddy domain to Wix instead?

Certainly. You can [transfer your domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere-to-wix) to Wix to manage your domain's billing and contact info in your Wix account. Keep in my that transferring a domain can take up to 7 days, while connecting a domain only takes up to 48 hours. You can connect the domain, then transfer it while the connection is still in propagation.

### What should I do if I am having trouble connecting my GoDaddy domain?

If you're having trouble updating your name servers, you can contact your domain host (GoDaddy) for assistance. For your convenience, we've created this email template to help you explain what you need to do.
