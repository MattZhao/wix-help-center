Use subdomains to group your webpages under one main, recognizable domain. You can set any Wix site in your account as a subdomain of another site you own, whether it's on Wix or a different platform.

For instance, if you have a separate Wix site for your blog, you can create a subdomain for it at "**blog**.example.com" – "example.com" is your main domain, and "blog" is the subdomain you chose.

[Connecting a subdomain to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-subdomain-to-a-site-in-your-wix-account#connecting-a-subdomain-to-your-wix-site)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-subdomain-to-a-site-in-your-wix-account#faqs)

> **Before you begin:**
>
> You must [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) to a Premium plan in order to connect a subdomain.

### Connecting a subdomain to your Wix site

First, decide which Wix site in your account you want to repackage as a subdomain. Then, enter the new URL you want to assign to this site.

The URL you enter is based on:

The subdomain of your choice (e.g. **blog**.mywebsite.com)  The main domain you want to link to (e.g. blog.**mywebsite.com**) – this is the domain you already own.

> **Connecting a subdomain to an external site?**
>
> Learn how to [connect a subdomain to an external resource](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-subdomain-to-an-external-resource) (e.g. a WordPress blog).

#### To connect a subdomain to your Wix site:

[Go to Domains](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController#/domains) ﻿in your Wix account.  Click **Add an Existing Domain**.  Select **Connect a domain you already own**.

Select the site you want to repackage as a subdomain.  Click **Next**.

Enter the new subdomain URL for the site you chose (e.g. "blog.mysite6.com"). 

**Tip:** The URL should be a combination of your main domain (e.g. "mysite6.com") and a subdomain of your choice (e.g. "blog.")  Click **Let's Go**.

Follow the steps, according to your main domain connection type:

- **If the main domain is a Wix domain / connected by name servers:** No action is needed. The subdomain will be in the same Wix account and any DNS records are automatically added.
- **If the domain is connected by pointing / not connected to Wix:** Follow the on-screen instructions to add CNAME details at the domain host.

**Note:** Be sure to [add both sets of CNAME records](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-or-updating-cname-records-in-your-wix-account) to your DNS records.

> **Note:**
>
> It may take up to 48 hours for your subdomain to be visible worldwide. We’ll send you an email when your subdomain connection is live. Learn more [about domain propagation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-propagation).

### What is a subdomain?

A subdomain is a domain that is a part of your main domain (i.e. your custom site). It brings visitors to a website. It is written (as a web address) as a prefix that comes before the root domain - e.g. a blog subdomain of your site would be written like this: blog.yourdomainname.com
This is useful if you want to give a webpage a separate identity in search results, while keeping it related to your site.

### How can I add a subdomain with a country code prefix? (e.g. www.fr.example.com)

To add a subdomain with a country code prefix (e.g. **fr**.mystunningwebsite.com), you must use Wix Multilingual, which automatically creates the subdomain for you. Learn more [about multilingual subdomains](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-about-multilingual-subdomains).

### Can I connect a subdomain to a specific page on my Wix site (other than the homepage)?

Currently, it is only possible to connect a subdomain to your site's home page using the methods mentioned above. If this is something you would like to see Wix offer in the future, you can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-directing-domains-or-subdomains-to-specific-pages-on-my-wix-site).

### Can I connect a business email to a subdomain?

Currently, it is not possible to connect a business email (add MX records) to a subdomain in Wix. If this is something you would like to see Wix offer in the future, you can [vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-connecting-a-mailbox-to-a-subdomain).
