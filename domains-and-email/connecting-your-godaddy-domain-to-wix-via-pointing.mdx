---
title: "Connecting Your GoDaddy Domain to Wix via Pointing"
---

Connect your GoDaddy domain to Wix by updating your DNS records in GoDaddy.

Note that when connected via pointing, Wix is unable to help you manage your DNS because the records are hosted at GoDaddy. You'll need to contact GoDaddy if you need help with your DNS.

Alternatively, you can [connect your domain via name servers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-your-godaddy-domain-to-the-wix-name-servers) to manage your DNS records in your Wix account. Learn more about the [differences between connection methods](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pointing-vs-name-servers-domain-connection-methods).

> **Before you begin:**
>
> In order to connect your GoDaddy domain to Wix via pointing, make sure you have:
> 1. [Upgraded your site with a plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website)
> 2. [Added your domain to your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-the-wix-name-servers#step-1-add-the-domain-to-your-wix-account)

### To connect your GoDaddy domain via pointing:

Sign in to your GoDaddy account.Click the user profile icon at the top right and select **My Products**.

Click **DNS** to the right of your domain. Update the **A** record that has **@** in the Name column.

### Show me how

1. Click the **Edit** icon  to the right of the **A** record that has** @** in the Name column. [image: A screenshot showing how to edit an A record in a GoDaddy account.]

**Note:** If you don't have an **A** record with **@** in the Name column, add one (see below).
2. Enter **185.230.63.107 **in the **Points to** field.
3. Click **Save**.

To add an **A** record with **@** in the Name column (if you don't already have one):
1. Click **ADD **at the bottom right.  [image: A screenshot showing how to add an A record in a GoDaddy account.]
2. Click the **Type** drop-down and select **A**.
3. Enter **@** in the **Host** field.
4. Enter **185.230.63.107 **in the **Points to** field.
5. Click **Save**.

5.  Update the **CNAME** record that has **www** in the Name column.

### Show me how

1. Click the **Edit** icon  to the right of the **CNAME** record that has** WWW** in the Name column. [image: A screenshot showing how to edit a CNAME record in a GoDaddy account.]

**Note:** If you don't have a **CNAME **record with **WWW** under Name, create one (see below).
2. In the **Points to** field, type: pointing.wixdns.net
3. Click **Save**.

To add a **CNAME** record with **WWW** in the Name column (if you don't already have one):
1. Click **ADD **at the bottom right.
2. Click the **Type** drop-down and select **CNAME**.
3. Enter **www** in the **Host** field.
4. In the **Points to** field, type: pointing.wixdns.net
5. Click **Save**.

> **Note:**
>
> It may take up to 48 hours for the DNS changes to propagate (become live) worldwide. Learn more about [domain propagation](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-propagation).
