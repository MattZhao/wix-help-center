---
title: "Connecting Email Accounts from Different Email Providers for the Same Domain"
---

It is not possible to connect email accounts from more than one email provider to the same domain. You can have numerous domain-based emails, but they must be purchased from the same email provider (e.g., only from Google Workspace, or only from GoDaddy).

If you've purchased a Google Workspace business email from Wix and from another provider, you'll have to choose one and cancel the other:

If you'd like to keep your external email (e.g., your GoDaddy email) you need to [cancel your Google Workspace business email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-canceling-your-business-email-renewal). Please make sure to [connect your external email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-an-email-purchased-outside-of-wix).   If you'd like to keep your Google Workspace business email, you need to cancel your external email. In order to do so, please contact the external provider directly. Please make sure to [add the correct records](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-adding-google-workspace-mx-records-in-your-wix-account) for your Google Workspace business email.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about the benefits of [getting a business email address from Google Workspace](https://www.wix.com/business/email-address).
