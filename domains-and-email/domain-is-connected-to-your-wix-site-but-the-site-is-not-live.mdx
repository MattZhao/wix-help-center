---
title: "Domain is Connected to Your Wix Site, but the Site is Not Live"
---

> **Before you begin:**
>
> To connect your domain, you must [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).

**If you are unable to view your Wix site on your domain, check the following:**

If, after 48 hours, you are still unable to access your site via your domain, use the [Wix Domain Assistant](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-domain-assistant-3777632) to determine the connection issue.

> **Still unable to view your site on your domain?**
>
> [Contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact/en) and we'll be happy to assist you.
