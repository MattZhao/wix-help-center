---
title: "Adding a Business Email to the Purchase of a Wix Site Plan"
---

Wix [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) plans do not include a business email account. Business emails must be purchased separately.

You can [purchase a personalized business email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-purchasing-a-personalized-business-email) from Google Workspace through Wix.
