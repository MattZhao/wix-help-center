---
title: "Google Workspace: Adding Google Workspace MX Records to Your Wix Account"
---

When you buy a Google Workspace business email from Wix, the MX records are automatically added to your domain's DNS records (unless you're [connected by pointing](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pointing-vs-name-servers-domain-connection-methods)). If you've changed your MX records, or purchased a business email outside of Wix, you can add the Google Workspace MX records from your Domains page.

> **Important:**
>
> You'll need to [add the Google Workspace MX records with your domain host](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-workspace-connecting-a-business-email-for-a-domain-pointing-to-wix) (not Wix) if your domain is connected via the pointing method.

### To add the Google Workspace MX records to your domain:

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) in your Wix account.  ﻿Click the **Domain Actions** icon  next to the relevant domain.  Select **Edit MX Records**.

Click the drop-down and select **Google Workspace**.   Click **Save**.
