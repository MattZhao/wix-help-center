title: "Transferring a Domain Purchased Elsewhere to Wix"
---

When you transfer your domain to Wix, Wix becomes your domain host. This means your domain's contact information, DNS settings, and registration payments are all managed from within your Wix account.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - To transfer your domain to Wix, you must get an authorization code (also known as the EPP code) from your current registrar. The code is usually sent to your domain's registered email upon request. If needed, contact your current domain registrar for assistance.
> - Your domain must be unlocked before you begin the transfer. In most cases, you can contact your current registrar to unlock the domain. In some cases, your domain might be locked due to [ICANN's 60 day lock policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/icanns-60-day-lock-policy).

---

### Transferring your domain to Wix

Transfer your domain so that Wix becomes your domain host. Once the transfer is complete, all your domain's settings and billing will be managed at Wix.

> **Tip:**
>
> The transfer process can take up to 7 days to complete. If you need your domain linked to your site in a shorter timeframe, [connect your domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-the-wix-name-servers) to Wix first. Learn more about [the difference between transferring and connecting a domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-vs-connecting-your-domain-to-wix).

#### To transfer your domain to Wix:

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) ﻿in your Wix account.  Click **Add an Existing Domain** and select **Transfer your domain to Wix**.  Read the information on your screen and click **Start Transfer**.  Enter the domain you want to transfer (e.g. mystunningwebsite.com).  Click the drop-down and select the domain host you purchased your domain from.  Click **Continue**.  Log in to your account with the current domain host:

1. (In a different browser tab) Log in to the account where your domain was purchased.
2. (In your Wix account) Select the** I logged in **checkbox.
    Make sure your domain is ready to transfer:

1. (In a different browser tab) Unlock your domain from your current domain host.
2. (In your Wix account) Select the **I unlocked it **checkbox.
    Get an authorization code from your account with your current domain host.   Paste the code under **Enter authorization code**.   Click **Continue**.

Select an extension period of at least one year.

**Tip:** If you have a free domain voucher, it's automatically applied.  Click **Continue**.  Check and/or edit your contact information. Then, click **Continue**.  Select a [privacy option](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-privacy): 

- **Private Registration:** Your contact details won't appear in the WHOIS domain information records. You can turn the privacy protection option on and off whenever you want.
- **Public Registration:** Your name and address always remain public in the WHOIS domain information records. You can only change it to private when [manually extending](https://support.wix.com/en/article/activating-private-registration-for-a-wix-domain) the domain subscription.
    Click **Continue**.   Select your payment method and click **Submit Purchase**.

> **What's next?**
>
> Once you've submitted the purchase, an email is sent from **wix-team@registrar.wix.com **to your domain's registrant email, confirming the transfer has been initiated. It may take up to 7 days for your domain transfer to complete.

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### My domain can't be transferred to Wix. Can I connect the domain to Wix instead?

Yes. Even if your domain can't be transferred to Wix, you can still [connect your domain to your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere#connecting-a-domain-to-your-site).

### What domain extensions can be transferred to Wix?

Currently, you can only transfer domains with the following extensions: 
.com, .org, .net, .agency, .art, .biz, .blog, .ca, .center, .christmas, .cloud, .club, .co, .coach, .company, .club, .design, .digital, .directory, .email, .expert, .finance, .fit, .fitness, .fun, .gifts, .group, .guru, .holiday, .info, .land, .life, .live, .london, .love, .me, .media, .mx, .ninja, .online, .party, .photography, .photos, .pictures, .rocks, .site, .shop, .solutions, .space, .store, .studio, .tokyo, .today, .top, .technology, .tips, .training, .website, .wiki .work, .world, .xyz and .yoga

### How long will the transfer take to complete?

Finalizing your domain transfer to Wix may take up to 7 days. We will send an email to confirm that we've received your request to transfer your domain. 
No further action is required to complete this process. The transfer will be processed within 7 days, as long as you have entered the correct authorization code, and your current domain registrar has approved your transfer.

### I've recently changed my registrant information. Can I still transfer my domain?

If you have recently changed your registrant information, your domain is locked from transfer for 60 days. While you can't transfer your domain during this period, you can still [connect the domain to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere) in the meantime. Learn more about [ICANN's 60 Day Lock Policy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/icanns-60-day-lock-policy).

### Can I transfer a 'premium' domain name to Wix?

No, you cannot transfer 'premium' domains to your Wix account. Also, Wix does not sell premium domains. However, if you own a premium domain, you can [connect the domain to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-the-wix-name-servers).
**Note: **A 'premium' domain is a domain name that other registrars deemed as high-value. These domains are often bought (and resold) by investors for a profit. The specific domain names are decided by each registrar, at their own discretion.

### If I transfer my domain to Wix, what happens to the domain registration period?

Domains transferred to Wix retain the time of their registration period; any remaining time is added to the one-year mandatory extension.

### If I transfer my domain to Wix, what happens to the domain privacy setting?

Your transferred domain does not inherit the privacy setting from your old registrar. Your previous privacy setting applies until the end of your old subscription and then changes to the setting that you select in Wix (step 15). It is an industry standard that you cannot change this setting.

### I've successfully transferred my domain to Wix, but my old site still appears. What's happening?

If your domain has successfully transferred to Wix, but your old site still appears, this usually resolves itself after a few days depending on your router or browser settings. To fix immediately, [clear your browser's cache](https://support.wix.com/en/article/clearing-your-browsers-cache) and then [flush the DNS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/flushing-your-dns-3875391).

### Can I cancel the transfer of my domain from another registrar to Wix?

Once you begin the process of transferring your domain from another registrar to Wix, it is not possible for Wix to intervene and cancel the transfer request. Please contact your current registrar if you need to cancel your domain transfer to Wix. 
When the domain transfer appears as 'failed' in your Wix account, you will automatically be refunded for all transfer payments. Please allow up to 20 days for this refund to appear in your bank statement.
