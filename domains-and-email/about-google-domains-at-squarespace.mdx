---
title: "About Google Domains at Squarespace"
---

Recently, [Google entered into an agreement with Squarespace](https://support.google.com/domains/answer/13689670?hl=en) whereby Squarespace will take over the registration and management of Google domains.

If your domain was purchased from Google, your service with Wix does not change. Squarespace becomes your new domain registrar. However, Google / Squarespace domains connected to Wix will continue unaffected.

If you'd prefer to have both your website and domain entirely managed at Wix, you can [transfer your domain to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-domain-purchased-elsewhere-to-wix). Once transferred, Wix becomes your new domain registrar.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you're unsure who your domain registrar is, you can find out by visiting [whois.com](https://www.whois.com/whois/) and entering your domain name to view your domain details.
