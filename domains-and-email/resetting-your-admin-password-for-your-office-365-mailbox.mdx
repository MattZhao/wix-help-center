title: "Resetting Your Admin Password for Your Office 365 Mailbox"
---

> **Before you begin:**
>
> You must [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) to a [Premium](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) site plan to use a business email.

> **Important:**
>
> This feature is currently not available to all users.

### To reset your admin password:

[Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) in your Wix account.  Click **Reset Admin Password **next to the relevant account and do the following:

1. Enter your new password.
2. Re-type your new password.
    Click **Reset Password**.
