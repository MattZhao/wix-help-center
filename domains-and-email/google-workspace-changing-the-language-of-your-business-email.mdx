---
title: "Google Workspace: Changing the Language of Your Business Email"
---

You can change the language of your business email from inside your Google Workspace (gmail) account.

### To change the language:

Open your Google Workspace email in Gmail.  Click the **Settings** icon at the top right.  Click **Settings**.  Click the drop-down menu next to **Language** and select the language.  Scroll down to the bottom of the page and click** Save Changes**.
