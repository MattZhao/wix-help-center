---
title: "Extending a Wix Domain Transferred from Another Wix Account"
---

If you transfer a Wix domain from one Wix account to another, you need to extend the domain subscription so that it does not expire at the end of its registration period.

### ​To extend your domain:

[Go to Domains](https://manage.wix.com/account/domains) in your Wix account.   Click **Extend **next to the domain that was transferred.

Select a registration period (e.g., extend by an additional one, two, or three years) and click **Continue**.  Select a privacy option and click **Continue**.

**Tip:** Learn more about [domain privacy and protection](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domain-privacy).  Enter your payment information and click **Submit Purchase**.
