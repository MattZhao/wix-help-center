---
title: "Transferring vs. Connecting Your Domain to Wix"
---

Learn the difference between 'transferring' vs. 'connecting' your domain to understand which option is best for your business.

> **Important:**
>
> You must [upgrade your site with a plan](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter) before you can assign a domain to your site.

---

### Transferring your domain

Wix becomes your domain host when you transfer your domain to Wix, meaning that you can manage the following from your Wix account:

Your domain's registration payments  Your domain's contact information  Your domain's DNS records

> **Note:**
>
> Read this [important information about transferring your domain to Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/important-information-about-transferring-your-domain-to-wix-6087540) before starting the process.

---

### Connecting your domain

If you prefer to keep your domain with your current host, you can still connect it to your Wix site. Your Wix site appears online when you enter your domain name into a web browser once you've connected your domain to Wix.
