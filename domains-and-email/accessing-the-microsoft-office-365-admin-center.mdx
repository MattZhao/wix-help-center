---
title: "Accessing the Microsoft Office 365 Admin Center"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature is currently not available to all users.

Log in to the Microsoft Office 365 mailbox admin center to manage your cloud settings, users, subscriptions and more.

### To access the Microsoft Office 365 admin center:

[Go to Business Email](https://manage.wix.com/account/mailboxes) in your Wix account.  Click **Manage at Microsoft **next to the relevant mailbox.  Login using your Admin credentials.
