---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Selling in Multiple Currencies"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sell products in multiple currencies in Wix Stores.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is, however, possible to display prices in multiple currencies. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-a-currency-converter)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
