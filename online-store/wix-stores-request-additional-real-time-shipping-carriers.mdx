title: "Wix Stores Request: Additional Real-Time Shipping Carriers"
---

Currently, Wix offers [real-time shipping in the US via USPS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-real-time-shipping-calculation-with-usps). Wix real-time shipping calculations via other mail services is currently not supported.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also offer real time shipping through third-party apps. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-offering-real-time-shipping-rates-via-third-party-apps)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
