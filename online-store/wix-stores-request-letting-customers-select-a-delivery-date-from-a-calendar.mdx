---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Letting Customers Select a Delivery Date from a Calendar"
---

Your customers can select a delivery date for their order from a list of options. Currently, It's not possible to replace this list with a calendar that lets customers select the date.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
