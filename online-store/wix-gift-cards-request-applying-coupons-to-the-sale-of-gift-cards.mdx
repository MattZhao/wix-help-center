---
title: "Wix Gift Cards Request: Applying Coupons to the Sale of Gift Cards"
---

Currently, it's not possible to create a coupon that applies when a customer purchases a [gift card](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-wix-gift-cards-9451439).

> **Update:**
>
> It's now possible to [sell gift cards at discounted prices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/offering-wix-gift-cards-at-discounted-prices) (e.g. buy a $50 gift card for $40)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
