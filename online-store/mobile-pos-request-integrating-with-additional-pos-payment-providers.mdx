---
title: "Mobile POS Request: Integrating with Additional POS Payment Providers"
---

Currently, Wix offers a [Mobile POS solution](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale). POS is also available through [Square](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale-with-square) and [SumUp](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale-with-sumup).

Charging at a point of sale with additional payment providers is currently not supported.

> **Tip:**
>
> It's possible to accept cash payments without connecting to a POS payment provider.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
