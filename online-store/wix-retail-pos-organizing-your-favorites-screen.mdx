Make sure your most popular products, discounts, and more are easily accessible by adding them to your **Favorites**. Your Favorites screen can include:

Products  Product categories  Discounts  Gift cards

When you're processing a sale, you can tap the **Favorites** tab and then add one of these items to the sale.

### Adding items to your Favorites

Open the POS tablet.   Tap the **Menu** icon  at the bottom left.   Tap **Settings**.   Tap **Manage **next to **Favorites**.  Add an item to your Favorites.

1. Tap the Add icon .
2. Select **Products**, **Categories**, **Discounts**, or **Gift Cards.**
3. Tap the item you want to add to your Favorites.
    (Optional) Drag items to reorder them.  Tap **Save**.

### How can I remove an item from my Favorites?

To remove an item, go back to the **Settings**, tap **Manage **next to **Favorites**, then tap the X that appears in the top left corner of an item to delete it.[image: Screenshot of an item in the Favorites, in the Wix Retail POS system]

### Can I customize the color of the categories, discounts, and gift cards?

These colors cannot be customized.
