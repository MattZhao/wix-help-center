---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Pre-Ordering Products in Spaces by Wix"
---

Currently, if you've [set up the ability to allow customers to pre-order products that are out of stock](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-allowing-customers-to-pre-order-products), that option is not available to customers in Spaces by Wix.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
