---
title: "Wix Stores: Fulfilling Modalyst Orders"
---

When a customer buys Modalyst products in your store, the items are shipped by the Modalyst supplier. To trigger the process, you need to pay the supplier for the order.

### To pay for Modalyst products so they can be shipped:

[Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant order.  Click **Go to Modalyst**.

[Pay the supplier for the order](https://support.modalyst.co/en/articles/3329174-how-to-process-orders-on-modalyst).

#### After you complete payment, the following happens:

The supplier ships the order to your customer.  A shipping confirmation email with the tracking number is sent to your customer. This email is sent from your store brand and looks identical to other [emails sent to your customers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-emails-sent-to-customers). 

**Note: **Sending shipping confirmation emails when an order is fulfilled by a third party can be disabled in the [eCommerce Settings tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20eCommerce%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/settings?referralInfo=sidebar).  The tracking number, carrier name, and tracking link are added automatically to the relevant order in your Wix dashboard.

> **Note:**
>
> If a customer purchases both your own products and Modalyst products, you need to fulfill your products in the regular way. Learn more about [adding tracking number to orders with your products and Modalyst products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-tracking-number-to-a-store-order#managing-tracking-numbers-added-by-third-parties).
