Do you use iZettle commerce tools to manage a brick and mortar store? If so, you can use the iZettle importer to upload your products to Wix and start selling online. [Learn more about Wix Stores](https://www.wix.com/ecommerce/website)

### FAQs

### What happens when I import products from iZettle?

- Your iZettle products with all their details (product name, category, images, descriptions, and more) are imported to Wix.
- Your iZettle product options are imported.
- Inventory levels are imported (but not synced).
Note that your iZettle orders and tax settings are not imported.

### Can I select which of my products I want to sell online?

Yes. You can [hide a product](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-changing-the-visibility-of-a-product) so that it doesn't appear on your site or [delete it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-deleting-store-products) completely from your online store. These changes do not affect your products in iZettle.

### Can I set different prices for my online and offline stores?

Yes. Prices changes you make in your Wix Store do not affect prices on iZettle or vice versa.

### How will the currency be set in my online store?

Your Wix store currency is automatically set according to your location. To change the currency, go to the **General Info **tab in your site's dashboard.

### After importing, do changes I make in Wix affect iZettle and vice versa?

No, the import just transfers your iZettle product information to Wix. It does not connect the two stores. After the import is complete, changes you make in one store are not reflected in the other.
**Tip: **You can go back to the **Import from iZettle** tab at any time to import products you added to iZettle. When you do, only new products are added to Wix.

### Are my VAT settings automatically imported to Wix?

No. After the import, go to the [Tax settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Tax&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/tax/list?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard to set up tax collection.

### Will orders listed in iZettle appear in my Wix dashboard?

No, orders made from iZettle and Wix remain separate. You will see your online orders on Wix, and your in-store purchases in iZettle.

### How can I import new iZettle products to Wix after the first import?

You can go back to the **Import from iZettle** tab in your Wix dashboard at any time to import products newly added to iZettle. Only new products will be imported.

### Does Wix have a POS solution for brick and mortar stores?

Yes. Check out [Wix's Retail POS solution](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-wix-pos-for-brick-and-mortar-stores) for physical stores. Note that this solution is not yet available for all users.

### Importing Products from Your iZettle Store

If you haven't already, go to [Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/) and click **Sign In **to create a Wix account.  Select a template. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-templates)   Click **Add Apps **on the left side of the Wix Editor.

  Start typing iZettle in the search field and click to select the app.  Click **+Add**.  Access the iZettle importer:

1. Go to the [Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) tab.
2. Click **Open** next to **Import from iZettle**.
    Click **Connect Account**.  Log in to your iZettle account.  Click **Start Import **(may take a few minutes).

**Notes: **Your iZettle products with all their details (product name, category, images, descriptions, inventory levels, and more) are imported to Wix.    Click **Manage Products **to view the product you imported in the **Store Products** tab.
