---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Accepting Partial Payments (Deposits)"
---

Currently, it's not possible to accept partial payments or deposits in Wix Stores.

> **Update:**
>
> - It's now possible [accept partial payment for an unpaid order by manually entering credit card details](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payment-by-manually-entering-credit-card-details#accepting-partial-payment-for-an-order) (currently available if you accept payment through Wix Payments or Isracard). Note that you can also [manually create an order yourself](https://support.wix.com/en/article/manually-creating-an-order-8027377).
> - It's also possible to [let customers pay in installments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-letting-customers-pay-in-installments) by connecting certain payment methods.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
