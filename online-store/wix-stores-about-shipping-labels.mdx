### Tracking packages

The unique code that appears on a label's barcode is its tracking number. The number is automatically added to the order you fulfilled.

If you send your customer a [shipping confirmation email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-emails-sent-to-customers), they receive the tracking number as well and can use it to track the progress of their package.
