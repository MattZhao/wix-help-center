---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Creating Shipping Rules Based on Product Dimensions"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create shipping rules in Wix Stores based on a product's dimensions (e.g., size, length, width).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
