Accept payments from customers in Japan using Pay-Easy and/or Convenience Stores. Your customers can pay for your products through their bank account with Pay-Easy, or through select convenience stores near them with Convenience Stores.

General Info
**Supported countries**Japan**Transaction fees**
- **Pay-Easy**: From 3.5% per transaction. Minimum monthly fee: 1,000 Japanese Yen.
- **Convenience Stores**: From 130 Japanese Yen per transaction. Minimum monthly fee: 1,000 Japanese Yen.
**Account activation time**Up to 1 month**Supported currencies**JPY**Managing your account**For information on topics such as payout schedules, refunding a customer, chargeback disputes, and more, [visit Epsilon](https://www.epsilon.ne.jp/support/faq/)

### Connecting Pay-Easy (through GMO-Epsilon)

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) on your site's dashboard.

**Tip:** Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it.  Click **See More Payment Options**.  Click **See Options** next to Pay-Easy.

Click **Connect **next to** **GMO-Epsilon.  Depending on whether you are creating a new GMO-Epsilon account or have an existing account, follow the instructions:

### Existing GMO-Epsilon account

1. Enter your Contract Code.

**Tip:** The Contract Code is the same one you use to log in to your GMO-Epsilon account.
2. Click **Connect.**

### New GMO-Epsilon account

1. Click [**Create GMO-Epsilon account**](https://secure.epsilon.jp/cgi-bin/regist/regist_com2.cgi?customer_kind=75)**.**
2. Follow the instructions on the GMO-Epsilon website to complete your account setup.
3. Once your new account is approved and valid, look for an email from GMO-Epsilon and copy your Contract Code.
4. Return to the [Accept Payments tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) (steps 1-5) and complete the steps under **Existing GMO-Epsilon account** to connect.

### Connecting Convenience Stores (through GMO-Epsilon)

[Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) on your site's dashboard.

**Tip:** Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it.  Click **See More Payment Options**.  Click **Connect** next to Convenience Store Payment (Provided by GMO-Epsilon)

Depending on whether you are creating a new GMO-Epsilon account or have an existing account, follow the instructions:

### Existing GMO-Epsilon account

1. Enter your Contract Code.

**Tip:** The contract code is the same one you use to log in to your GMO-Epsilon account.
2. Click **Connect.**

### New GMO-Epsilon account

1. Choose an option:

- Click [Create new Sole Proprietorship account](https://secure.epsilon.jp/cgi-bin/regist/regist_per2.cgi?customer_kind=75).
- Click [Create new Corporate Business account](https://secure.epsilon.jp/cgi-bin/regist/regist_com2.cgi?customer_kind=75).
2. Follow the instructions on the GMO-Epsilon website to complete your account setup.
3. Once your new account is approved and valid, look for an email from GMO-Epsilon and copy your Contract Code.
4. Return to the [Accept Payments tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) (steps 1-4) and complete the steps under **Existing GMO-Epsilon account** to connect.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about accepting payments.

### How can I tell if a payment method is active?

In order to accept online payments, you must connect a payment provider. Once connected, your checkout will be active for your chosen payment method(s).

You need to have your account successfully verified to receive payouts.

### Is my currency supported?

Be aware that payments will fail If you are using an unsupported currency.

The currency used in your payment provider account must match the [currency that you set in your Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments). If you need to change the currency set in your provider account, please contact your payment provider.
If your current payment provider does not support your desired currency, Wix hosts a wide range of [alternative payment providers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country). Learn how to [change your payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-payment-provider-for-accepting-credit-card-payments).

### What is a minimum payment amount?

Some payment providers have a minimum payment threshold (e.g. $1.01). Payments below this amount will not be processed.
Make sure that all of your products or services are priced over the minimum threshold. This includes discounted items or [Pricing Plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-about-recurring-plans). 
Please [contact your payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-payouts-from-third-party-payment-providers) to get the most up-to-date information about their minimum payment threshold.

### Can I offer recurring payments?

To offer recurring payments, you must connect a [payment provider that supports recurring payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-recurring-payments).

### How do I get my payouts?

Payouts are managed by your payment provider. If your provider is a third-party provider, [contact them directly](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-payouts-from-third-party-payment-providers).

If your provider is Wix Payments, learn about [receiving payouts from Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/receiving-your-payouts-from-wix-payments).

### My customers' payments are being declined. What can I do?

If your customers are experiencing payment failures, you may be able to fix the issue by [troubleshooting issues for accepting payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-issues-for-accepting-payments).

### Why can’t I connect my desired payment provider?

If you’re having trouble connecting your payment provider, you may be able to fix the issue by [troubleshooting common connection issues](https://support.wix.com/en/article/troubleshooting-third-party-provider-connection-issues).

### Do I need a Premium plan to accept payments?

Yes. Your Wix site needs to have a [Business & eCommerce Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) in order to accept payments.
