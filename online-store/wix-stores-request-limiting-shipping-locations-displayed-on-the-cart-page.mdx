---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Limiting Shipping Locations Displayed on the Cart Page"
---

Currently, when a customer is on the cart page and wants to change the shipping location, they can choose from a drop-down that lists all possible countries. Limiting this list to countries that your store ships to is currently not supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
