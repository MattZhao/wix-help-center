---
title: "Wix Stores: Transferring Your Store to Another Wix Account"
---

Do you have a store in one account that you want to transfer to another account?

You can transfer the entire site. The store transfers with it. Learn how to [transfer a premium site to another Wix account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-a-premium-site-to-another-wix-account).

> **Tip:**
>
> While transferring the account, you'll get to choose whether you want to keep your role as co-owner. You can also create a copy of the site for your personal account.
