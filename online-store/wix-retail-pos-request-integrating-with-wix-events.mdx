---
title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Integrating with Wix Events"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sell event tickets created through Wix Events on your tablet at a point of sale. However, you can [set up your phone as a point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale) and [use it to sell event tickets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-pos-selling-event-tickets-at-a-mobile-point-of-sale).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
