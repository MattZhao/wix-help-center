---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Additional Settings for Image Compression & Sharpening in the Mobile View"
---

Currently, it's not possible to make changes to the compression and sharpening of product images that appear in Product Galleries or on the Product Page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
