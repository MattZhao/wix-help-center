title: "Wix Stores: About Your Storefront"
---

Customers visiting your store start by browsing your products. On the way to completing a purchase, they'll also see most or all of the following parts of your store: The Product Page, Cart Icon, Side Cart, Cart Page, Checkout Page and Thank You Page.

Make sure you're familiar with all the parts of your store.

---

### Product display options

There are a variety of ways you can display your products. You might want to showcase all your items on one page using the Category Page or a Grid Gallery. Alternatively, you can display groups of products, or a single product, in different parts of your site.

No matter which one you choose, you can customize it to get the right look for your business.

#### Category Page

> **Important:**
>
> The Category Page is not yet available for all users.

The [Category Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-the-category-page) is your main shop page. From here, customers can select the category they want to browse.

#### Grid Gallery

You can display all your products in a [Grid Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-product-galleries), or select a category to display. After deciding how many rows and columns your gallery has, you can customize the look.

#### Slider Gallery

Add a [slider gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-product-galleries#customizing-a-slider-gallery) to let customers navigate through your products. As with a Grid Gallery, you can display all your products or a specific category. In addition, slider galleries can be set up to show [best-selling items or related products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-related-products-gallery).

#### Product Widget

A [Product Widget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-the-product-widget) is an element you add to your store to display one specific product. They're great for highlighting a sale item or showcasing a new arrival on any site page.

---

### Product Page

The [Product Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-customizing-your-product-page) is where site visitors can view complete information about a product. From here, customers can add a product to the Cart.

The Product Page is a dynamic page, which means you only have to customize it once. The design stays the same but it displays whatever product the shopper selects.

---

### Side Cart

The Side Cart lets customers see what’s in their cart while they’re still shopping. When a customer adds an item to the cart, depending on your settings, the Side Cart can appear at the side of the page, displaying information about products that were added to the cart.

Learn how to [customize the Side Cart](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-side-cart)

---

### Cart Icon

The [Cart Icon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-cart-icon) appears in your header so customers can see it no matter where they are in your site. Clicking the Cart Icon takes shoppers to the Side Cart or to the Cart Page.

---

### Cart Page

In the [Cart Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-cart-page), customers can review their order, see a preview of shipping and tax costs (if enabled), add a note or coupon code, and proceed to Checkout.

To preview this page, go to your live site, add a product to your cart, and then go to your cart.

---

### Checkout Page

The [Checkout Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-checkout-page) is where customers complete their purchase. They are asked to provide information such as shipping details, billing info, and more.

---

### Thank You Page

As soon as a customer checks out, they are taken to the [Thank You Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-thank-you-page). There, they can view their order details.
