---
title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Support for POS Devices at Multiple Locations"
---

Currently, Wix's Retail POS solution does not support multiple POS devices in separate physical locations when used with Wix Stores. However, we do support multiple devices in a single location.

> **Update:**
>
> It's now possible to use [Wix Retail POS with Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/managing-bookings-in-retail-pos) and set up [multiple locations](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-business-locations).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
