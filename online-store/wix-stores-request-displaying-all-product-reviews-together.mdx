---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Displaying All Product Reviews Together"
---

Currently, reviews are displayed alongside the related product. It's not possible to display all product reviews together on a dedicated page or in a review widget.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
