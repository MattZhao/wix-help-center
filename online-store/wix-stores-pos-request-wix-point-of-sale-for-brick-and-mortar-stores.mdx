---
title: "Wix Stores POS Request: Wix Point of Sale for Brick & Mortar Stores"
---

We're excited to announce that the [Wix Retail Point of Sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-wix-pos-for-brick-and-mortar-stores) (POS) solution for brick and mortar stores is available for merchants in the US and Canada.

What additional countries would you like to see Wix Retail POS supported in? Click [here](https://www.wix.com/support/conversations/category/contact) to submit a ticket with your feedback and suggestions.
