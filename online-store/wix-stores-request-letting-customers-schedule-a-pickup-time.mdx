---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Letting Customers Schedule a Pickup Time"
---

Currently, you can send customers a [pickup ready email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-store-pickup-as-a-delivery-option#step-5-sending-a-pickup-ready-email-notification) to let them know when an order is ready for them to collect. It is not possible to let customers select a pickup time.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
