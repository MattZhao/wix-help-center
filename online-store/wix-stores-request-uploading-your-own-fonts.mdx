---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Uploading Your Own Fonts"
---

It's now possible to [upload your own fonts in the Wix Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-uploading-and-using-your-own-fonts) and use them in your store. After uploading a font, check to make sure that it displays correctly in all your Wix Stores elements.
