---
title: "Request: Using Amazon Payments as a Payment Provider"
---

Currently, Wix does not offer Amazon Payments as a payment provider. If you use the Manual payment method, you can mention that you accept payments through Amazon Payments and arrange the payment offline.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
