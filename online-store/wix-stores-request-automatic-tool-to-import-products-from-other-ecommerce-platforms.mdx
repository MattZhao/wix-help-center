---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Automatic Tool to Import Products from Other eCommerce Platforms"
---

Currently, there is no way to automatically import products from other eCommerce platforms (e.g. Shopify).

You can, however, export your products from another platform to a CSV file. You can then [import the products into Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-importing-products-to-wix-stores) after making the necessary adjustments. Additionally, you can [use the Cart2Cart third-party-app ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-migrating-from-other-ecommerce-platforms-to-wix-stores)to migrate from another platform to Wix Stores..

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
