---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Product Sub-categories and Sub-subcategories"
---

Currently, you can create product categories, but not subcategories or sub-subcategories. You can, however, [display product subcategories in your site menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-product-subcategories).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
