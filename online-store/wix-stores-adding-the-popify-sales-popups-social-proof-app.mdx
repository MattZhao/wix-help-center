title: "Wix Stores: Adding the \"Popify Sales Popups\" Social Proof App"
---

With Popify, you can create pop-ups about orders, reviews, or promotions to help convert site visitors into customers. You can use Popify for free or choose one of their premium plans.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure your site has been upgraded to a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) that allows you to accept payments.

Wix EditorEditor XStudio EditorDashboard
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Apps **  on the left side of the editor.
3. Search for the Popify Sales Popups app in the search bar.
4. Click **Add to Site**.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **App Market  ** at the top of the editor.
3. Search for the Popify Sales Popups app in the search bar.
4. Click **Add to Site**.
