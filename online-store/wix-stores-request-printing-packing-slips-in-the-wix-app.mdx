---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Printing Packing Slips in the Wix App"
---

Currently, it's not possible to print packing slips using the Wix app on your mobile device. You can only [print packing slips from desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-fulfilling-store-orders#printing-packing-slips).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
