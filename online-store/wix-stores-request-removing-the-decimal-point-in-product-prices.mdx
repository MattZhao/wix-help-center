---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Removing the Decimal Point in Product Prices"
---

Currently, it is not possible to display prices without the decimal point. If you charge $80 for a product, it is displayed as $80.00 on your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
