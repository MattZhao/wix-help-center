### Pay-in-installment payment providers

A variety of payment providers let your customers pay in installments but send you the total amount of the sale right away.

These payment providers include those listed below. For the most up-to-date information, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-in-installments-bnpl#connecting-installment-payment-providers).

[Affirm](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-affirm-as-a-payment-provider) (United States)  [Afterpay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-afterpay-as-a-payment-provider-3515230) (Australia, Canada, New Zealand, United States)  [Atome](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-atome-as-a-payment-provider) (Hong Kong, Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines, Taiwan, Thailand, Vietnam)  [Cashfree Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-cashfree-payments-as-a-payment-provider) (India)  [Clearpay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-clearpay-as-a-payment-provider) (United Kingdom)  [Klarna](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-klarna-as-a-payment-provider) (Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States)  [Laybuy](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-laybuy-as-a-payment-provider) (Australia, New Zealand, United Kingdom)  [Meshulam](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-grow-by-meshulam-as-a-payment-provider) (Israel)  [MONEI](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-monei-as-a-payment-provider) (Andorra, Spain)  [PayPal ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider)(Available in over [200 countries](https://www.paypal.com/pn/webapps/mpp/country-worldwide))

> **Notes:**
>
> - These payment providers can only be added if your business is located in a region the provider services.
> - Only customers in relevant regions can use the provider to pay in installments.

After you add the payment provider to your site, customers see your installment options in the product page.

During checkout, customers can select one of the options. They are then prompted to log in to their accounts or create a new one and complete the purchase.

To get started, add one of the available options and select the light or dark display mode.

### Show me how

1. Connect one or more of the relevant payment providers.

**Note: **For instructions, click any of the links in the table above.
2. Go to your store's Product Page in the Editor.

- **Wix Editor:**

1. Click the **Pages** iconon the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Store Pages**.
3. Select the **Product Page**.
- **Editor X: **

1. ** **Click the **Pages **icon  at the top bar of Editor X.
2. Scroll down and select the Product Page.
3. Click the Product Page in the Editor.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Design **tab.
6. Click **Product Details**.
7. Select the **Light **or **Dark **display mode.

### Installments via credit / debit cards

A number of credit / debit card providers allow your customers to pay in installments. You may receive payment when the customer pays an installment to the credit card company. In other words, if your customer pays in 4 installments, you may receive payments for the order in 4 installments.

Terms vary by provider, so contact your credit / debit card provider for more detailed information.

---

### Installments via PayPal

PayPal offers customers in some regions the option of paying in Installments. The number of payments and the conditions vary by region.

When you [connect PayPal as a payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-paypal-as-a-payment-provider), they automatically display the payment options that are available for each customer. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-understanding-customer-checkout-with-paypal#paypal-button-versions)
