---
title: "Wix Stores: Deleting Wix Stores"
---

When you delete Wix Stores, all the store elements (the cart icon, product widgets, store tabs in your Wix dashboard, etc.) are removed.

> **Important:**
>
> Deleting Wix Stores does not cancel your Premium plan or Studio plan.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go  to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **Actions **iconnext to **Store Pages**.
4. Select **Delete Store**.
5. Click **Delete** to confirm.
[image: Screenshot of Pages & Menus in the Wix editor, with the settings icon next to Store Pages clicked on & Delete store outlined]
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon   next to **Store Pages**.
4. Select **Delete Store**.
5. Click **Delete** to confirm.
[image: Screenshot of Pages & Menus in Studio editor, with the settings icon next to Store Pages clicked on & Delete store outline]

> **Tip:**
>
> Your store data is not lost when you delete your store. If you want to display your store again, [reverting to a previously saved version](https://support.wix.com/en/article/viewing-and-managing-your-site-history) with the store will restore it.
