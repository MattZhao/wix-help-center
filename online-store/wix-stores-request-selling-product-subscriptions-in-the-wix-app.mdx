---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Selling Product Subscriptions in the Wix App"
---

Currently, customers shopping in the Wix app can only make individual purchases; they cannot buy product subscriptions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
