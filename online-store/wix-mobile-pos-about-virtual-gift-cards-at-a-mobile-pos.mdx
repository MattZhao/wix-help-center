title: "Wix Mobile POS: About Virtual Gift Cards at a Mobile POS"
---

> **Important:**
>
> The Wix POS solution for mobile phones is available only in the US and Canada.

If you sell virtual gift cards online, you can let customers redeem them while making purchases at a mobile point of sale.

Learn more about [creating virtual gift cards and selling them on your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-gift-cards).

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [sell physical gift cards at your mobile POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-selling-physical-gift-cards-at-a-mobile-pos).

---

### Redeeming virtual gift cards

When customers want to pay for their order using their gift card at your mobile POS, you need to select **Gift card** as the payment method.

You can then enter the gift card code your customer received by email to redeem the card. If the value of the gift card doesn't cover the order, your customer can use any other payment method to pay for the balance.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I sell gift cards at a mobile POS?

Currently you cannot sell virtual gift cards at a mobile POS, only [physical gift cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-selling-physical-gift-cards-at-a-mobile-pos).

### Can customers redeem gift cards for all sale types?

Your customers can currently redeem gift cards at a mobile POS only for Wix Stores sales.

### Can customers redeem physical gift cards?

Yes. You can sell physical gift cards at your mobile POS. Customers can then use them to pay for store products and Wix Bookings services.
Learn more about [selling physical gift cards at your mobile POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-selling-physical-gift-cards-at-a-mobile-pos).
