---
title: "Wix Stores: Setting Up Dropshipping with Printify"
---

After you [connect your store to Printify](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-printify-to-your-store), you can select a product and start designing it!

Printify is a print on demand (POD) service. You pay no subscription fees and there is no need to purchase inventory. You only pay when a customer places an order.

---

### Step 1 | Create Products and Add them to Your Store

First, log in to [Printify](http://www.printify.com/), click **Catalog **at the top, and choose a product. Then, create your first product and set pricing. [Learn How](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483644382865-How-can-I-create-a-product-with-Printify-)

---

### Step 2 | Set Up Shipping

Make sure you [set up shipping](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-a-guide-to-setting-up-shipping-delivery-and-pickup) in your Wix dashboard. Take into consideration Printify's shipping costs. You can charge for shipping separately or you might want to increase the product price so that it covers the shipping cost and then offer free shipping - the choice is yours.

[Read Printify's recommendations on how to configure shipping for Wix Stores](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483617142033-How-do-I-set-up-shipping-for-my-Wix-store-)  [Learn most about Printify's production times](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483629751825-What-are-Printify-s-production-times-like)

---

### Step 3 | Add a Billing Method in Printify

After a customer purchases a Printify product in your Wix store, you'll need to pay Printify for it. After this is done, Printify creates the product and ships it to your customer.

You can handle the payment to Printify in two ways:

**Automatic: **Set up automatic payment by adding a payment method  (PayPal or credit card) in the Printify dashboard.  [Learn How](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483644218769-How-do-I-set-up-payments-in-Printify)

When a paid order comes in, (or when you mark an order as "Paid"), your payment to Printify is processed automatically and the order is fulfilled. You won't need to do a thing.   **Manual: **When you receive an order, you can go to the **Orders **tab in the Printify dashboard and click **Fulfill orders **to pay for the order manually. It is then fulfilled by Printify.

To learn more about fulfilling Printify orders, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-fulfilling-printify-products).

To learn how dropshipping affects tax collection, click [here](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/learn/whitepapers/sales-tax-implications-of-drop-shipping.html).
