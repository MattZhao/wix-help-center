title: "Wix Stores: Managing Product Ribbons"
---

Draw attention to products you want to promote by adding a ribbon.

To make managing ribbons easier, you can apply the same ribbon to multiple products. You can then edit the ribbon text (e.g. change "New" to "Just In!") or remove the ribbon from many products at once.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Create a new ribbon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons#creating-a-new-ribbon)  [Apply ribbons you created to other products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons#applying-ribbons-you-created-to-other-products)  [Edit ribbons text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons#editing-ribbon-text)  [Customize the ribbon layout & design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons#customizing-the-ribbon-layout-and-design)  [Remove ribbons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons#deleting-a-ribbon)  [Make ribbon updates to multiple products at once](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons#updating-ribbons-for-multiple-products-at-once)  [Hiding ribbons from your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons#hiding-ribbons-from-your-live-site)

### Creating a new ribbon

You can create a new ribbon by selecting a product and entering the ribbon text. Next time you go to create or update a product, you can select the ribbon you already created.

#### To create a new ribbon:

[Go to Store Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Select an existing product or click **New Product** to add a new one.  Enter the ribbon text (e.g. New Arrival) in the **Ribbon** field.

Click **Save** to save the product.

> **Note:**
>
> If ribbons you create don't appear on your site, make sure you haven't [hidden ribbons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons#hiding-ribbons-from-your-live-site).

### Applying ribbons you created to other products

After you created a ribbon, it can be selected to apply it to other products.

#### To apply the ribbon:

[Go to Store Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Select an existing product or click **New Product** to add a new one.  Start entering the ribbon text and select the ribbon from the list.

Click **Save** to save the product.

---

### Editing ribbon text

If you've applied a ribbon to multiple products, you can change the ribbon text for all products at once.

#### To edit ribbon text:

[Go to Store Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Select an existing product.  Enter any letter in the **Ribbon** field and select **Manage ribbons**.

Edit the ribbon text.

**Note: **Changes affect all products with the ribbon.  Click **Save**.  Click **Save** to save the product.

> **Tip:**
>
> In the Manage Ribbons window, you can see how many products have the ribbon.

### Customizing the ribbon layout and design

To get started, decide where you want to place the ribbon. Next, you can choose the ribbon's shape, colors, and font to get the perfect design.

#### To customize the design:

Click to select the Product Gallery in your editor.  Click the Settings icon .  Click the **Layout** tab.  Scroll down and click **Ribbon Layout**.  Choose where you want to place the ribbon:

- **On Image: **Ribbon appears on top of the image. Drag the sliders to position the ribbon.
- **Product Info:** Ribbon appears above the product price.
    Design the ribbon:

1. Click the **Design** tab.
2. Scroll down and select **Ribbons**.
3. Customize the ribbon using the available options.

### Deleting a ribbon

You can remove a ribbon from a single product or from all the products it was applied to. For example, if you added a "Spring Sale" ribbon to 25 products, you can delete that ribbon to remove it from the 25 products at once.

#### To delete a ribbon:

[Go to Store Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Select an existing product.  Choose what you want to do next:

### Remove a ribbon from a single product

1. Click the ribbon field and erase the ribbon name.
2. Click **Save** to save the product.

### Remove a ribbon from all products

1. Enter any letter in the **Ribbon** field and select **Manage ribbons**.
2. Click the Delete icon  next to the relevant ribbon to remove it from all products.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Click **Save** to save the product.

### Updating ribbons for multiple products at once

You can add a new ribbon to products, remove a ribbon from products, or update the text of a ribbon that already appears on multiple products.

#### To make ribbon changes to multiple products:

[Go to Store Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Select the products whose ribbons you want to update:

- **All products: **Select the checkbox at the top left.
- **Individual products:** Select the checkboxes next to the relevant products.
- **Category: **Click **Filter** and select 1 or more categories.
    Click **More Actions. 

**  Select **Edit ribbon**.  Choose what you want to do next:

### Apply an existing ribbon

**Note: **If the products you selected already have a ribbon, a new ribbon will replace it.

1. Select a ribbon from the list.
2. Click **Save**.

### Create a new ribbon

**Note: **If the products you selected already have a ribbon, a new ribbon will replace it.

1. Click **+ New Ribbon**.
2. Enter the ribbon name.
3. Click the Checkmark icon .
4. Click **Save**.

### Edit the text of an existing ribbon

When you edit the text of an existing ribbon, it updates for all the products it's applied to. For example, if a ribbon with the text "New" is applied to 50 products, and you select 10 products and then edit the text to "New Arrival", the ribbon text updates for all 50 products - not just the 10 you selected.

1. Enter the new ribbon name.
2. Click the Checkmark icon .
3. Click **Save**.

### Remove a ribbon

You can delete a ribbon by clicking the Delete icon  next to the relevant ribbon.**Important: **When you delete a ribbon, it is removed from every product.

**To remove a ribbon from some products:**Currently, it is not possible to remove a ribbon from some products but not others, unless you remove them one at a time. 
As a workaround, you can add a new ribbon to the relevant products, then delete the new ribbon. This removes the previous ribbon from those products only. 
For example, you have 50 products with the "New" ribbon. You want to remove the ribbon from 10 of them. Select the 10 products, add a brand new ribbon (you can give it any name) to those 10 products, then delete the new ribbon you just created.

> **Tip:**
>
> Learn more about [making changes to multiple products at once](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-editing-multiple-products-at-the-same-time#making-bulk-updates-to-products-from-your-sites-dashboard).

### Hiding ribbons from your live site

You can hide all ribbons from appearing in Product Galleries or in the Category Page.

> **Note:**
>
> If you've hidden ribbons, new ribbons you create won't appear. Remember to unhide them in your editor if you want to display them.

#### To hide ribbons:

Go to your editor.  Select the relevant element.  Hide ribbons in the selected element:

- **Category Page: **

1. Click **Settings**.
2. Click the **Settings** tab.
3. Deselect the checkbox next to **Ribbons**.** **
- **Product Gallery:**

1. Click the **Settings **Icon ** **.
2. Click the **Settings** tab.
3. Deselect the checkbox next to **Ribbons**.
