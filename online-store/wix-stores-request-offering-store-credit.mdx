---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Offering Store Credit"
---

Currently, it's not possible to offer your customer store credit.

> **Workaround:**
>
> Instead of store credit, you can [create a coupon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-price-discount-coupon) for the same amount and send the coupon code to your customer.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
