> - This article is about **Wix Mobile POS** for mobile phones.
> - For info on **Wix Retail POS **for brick and mortar stores, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/retail-pos-7046310).

Let clients pay you by making your mobile device a point of sale (POS). Just enter the amount you want to charge your client and accept payment by cash or card.

For example, if you do consulting work and book appointments yourself (not through Wix Bookings), you can collect payment using your phone.

### To charge clients at a point of sale:

Make sure you [download the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-downloading-the-app).   To accept credit card sales, make sure you:

1. [Set up Wix Payments as your payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale#step-2-create-a-wix-payments-account).
2. [Order and set up a card reader](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale#step-3-order-a-creditdebit-card-reader) and/or [set up Tap to Pay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale#step-4-optional-set-up-tap-to-pay-on-your-phone).
    Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).﻿  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Point of Sale **under** Get Paid.**  Tap **Make a Sale**.  Enter the amount you want to charge, including cents and tap **Next**.

**Note: **Enter the final amount after tax or discounts are calculated.  Enter a short description of what the payment is for and tap **Next**.  

**Note: **This information appears in the Payments tab in your site's dashboard.  Choose what you want to do:

### Accept cash payment

1. Tap **Cash**.
2. Select an option:

- Manually enter the exact cash amount you receive from the client and tap **Accept Cash**.
- Select a preset cash amount you received from the client.
3. Give your client the change.

**Tip:** The amount appears on the screen.
4. (Optional) Select a receipt option:

- **Email a receipt:**

1. Tap **Email receipt**.
2. Enter the customer's email address.

**Note: **Email addresses are needed if you want to generate an invoice later.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Tap **Send Receipt**.
- **Send a text receipt:**

1. Tap **Text receipt**.
2. Enter the phone number.
3. Tap **Text Receipt**.

### Accept payment through Wix Mobile POS & Wix Payments

1. Tap **Credit Card**.
2. Select a payment option:

- **Card reader: **Let customer swipe, insert, or tap the customer's credit card.
- **Tap to Pay **(**iPhone or Android): **Let customer tap their card or phone.
3. (Optional) Select a receipt option:

- **Email a receipt:**

1. Tap **Email receipt**.
2. Enter the customer's email address.

**Note: **Email addresses are needed if you want to generate an invoice later.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Tap **Send Receipt**.
- **Send a text receipt:**

1. Tap **Text receipt**.
2. Enter the phone number.
3. Tap **Text Receipt**.

### Manually enter credit card details

The option of manually entering card details is open to merchants using a [payment provider that allows manual card entry payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-by-manual-card-entry). **Important: **Entering credit card details manually is the least secure way of accepting card payments. We recommend accepting manual payment only from buyers you trust. Note that in case of fraud, it is the merchant who is liable. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/best-practices-for-accepting-card-payments#handling-offline-card-payments) 

1. Tap **Manual Card Entry**.
2. Enter all the credit card details and tap **Charge**.
3. (Optional) Select a receipt option:

- **Email a receipt:**

1. Tap **Email receipt**.
2. Enter the customer's email address.

**Note: **Email addresses are needed if you want to generate an invoice later.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Tap **Send Receipt**.
- **Send a text receipt:**

1. Tap **Text receipt**.
2. Enter the phone number.
3. Tap **Text Receipt**.

**Note: **[Wix Payments processing fees](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-service-fees) for manual card entry are the same as for other card payments.

### Accept payment through Square

If you have [set up Square as your POS solution](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale-with-square), you can accept payment using this option.

1. Tap Card.
2. Tap **Open Square**.
3. Read the customer's credit in any of the following ways:

- Swipe
- Manually enter your client's credit card number.

**Note:** When you enter the credit card number manually, Square charges a higher processing fee.
4. Tap **Open**.
5. Ask for the client's signature.
6. (Optional) Select a receipt option:

- **Email a receipt:**

1. Tap **Email receipt**.
2. Enter the customer's email address.

**Note: **Email addresses are needed if you want to generate an invoice later.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Tap **Send Receipt**.
- **Send a text receipt:**

1. Tap **Text receipt**.
2. Enter the phone number.
3. Tap **Text Receipt**.

### Accept payment through SumUp

If you have [set up SumUp as your POS solution](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale-with-sumup), you can accept payment using this option.

1. Tap **Card**.
2. Log in to your SumUp account.
3. Read the customer's credit card in any of the following ways:

- Tap
- Swipe
- Insert
4. Ask for the customer's signature.
5. (Optional) Select a receipt option:

- **Email a receipt:**

1. Tap **Email receipt**.
2. Enter the customer's email address.

**Note: **Email addresses are needed if you want to generate an invoice later.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Tap **Send Receipt**.
- **Send a text receipt:**

1. Tap **Text receipt**.
2. Enter the phone number.
3. Tap **Text Receipt**.
