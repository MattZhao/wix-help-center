### Buying and printing shipping labels

Go to the ShipStation dashboard to purchase and print your shipping labels. You can print labels from the Orders tab, Order Details, Shipments tab, or Shipment Details screens.

Learn how to [print labels in ShipStation](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026157671-Print-Labels).

---

### Adding a tracking code to your order

Each ShipStation label you print comes with a unique barcode. The tracking number appears beneath it.

Tracking numbers are automatically added to your Wix Stores orders when they are created.

---

### Notifying customers when an order is shipped

Send customers email notifications with tracking numbers to let them know that their packages are on the way.

> **Important:**
>
> You can send notifications through ShipStation or through Wix. Make sure you know which notifications are active.

### Send shipping notifications through ShipStation

By default, sending notifications to customers is disabled. Learn how to [enable and set up customer notifications in ShipStation](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026157911-Customer-Notifications-Settings).

### Send shipping notifications through Wix

To send a Wix Store notification when fulfilling an order, select the **Send Shipping Confirmation Email **checkbox.

**Tip: **You can also send an email by clicking **More Actions** in an order** **and then selecting the relevant option.

### Creating bulk shipping rules

Do you often pack the same product in the same packaging and then ship it with the same carrier? Save even more time by [service mapping](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025870232-Service-Mapping) and [automation rules](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026158331-Automation-Rules#) for types of shipping you do over and over.

Learn more about working with ShipStation from the [ShipStation help center](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us).

### In which countries is ShipStation available?

ShipStation is available for stores in the US, Canada, Australia, France, Germany New Zealand, and the UK.

Yes, you can select from English, French, Spanish, or German. Learn how to [update the ShipStation language](https://help.shipstation.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052074611-How-do-I-change-the-language-used-in-ShipStation).
