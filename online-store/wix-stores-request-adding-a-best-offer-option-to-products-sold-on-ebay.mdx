---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Adding a \"Best Offer\" Option to Products Sold on eBay"
---

Currently, when products are integrated with the eBay sales channel, they appear with a "Buy Now" button. It is not possible to add a "Best Offer option.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
