Currently, it is not possible to sell dropshipping products on your phone at a mobile point of sale. Customers can however, purchase dropshipping products on your site. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-understanding-dropshipping)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
