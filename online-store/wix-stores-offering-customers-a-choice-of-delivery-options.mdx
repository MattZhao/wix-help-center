---
title: "Wix Stores: Offering Customers a Choice of Delivery Options"
---

> **Important:**
>
> We're in the process of rolling out new shipping settings. If you have a **Create Region** button instead of **Add Region**, then you have the new version. Learn how to [set up shipping using the new settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-store-shipping).

Let customers choose the shipping option that's best for them - saving money by choosing a less expensive option or paying a higher rate to receive goods more quickly.

Offering more than one delivery option is available when you set up the following shipping rules:

Flat Rate  Rate by Weight  Rate by Price  Rate by Product  USPS Calculated Rate

Start by creating the first delivery option and then click **+Add Another Delivery Option**.

> **Tips:**
>
> - To offer multiple delivery options which include a free option, select a rule (e.g. Flat Rate) and make one of the options free (e.g. Standard Shipping at $0, Express Shipping at $8).
> - You can display shipping options to your customers in the cart. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-how-shipping-and-tax-displays-in-the-cart-page)
