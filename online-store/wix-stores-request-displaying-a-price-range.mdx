---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Displaying a Price Range"
---

Currently, if you sell a product with product options sold at different prices, the lowest price is displayed in the Product Gallery. The lowest price is also displayed on the Product Page before a customer selects a product option. Displaying the price as a range, for example $20 - $30, is not supported.

> **Update:**
>
> It is now possible to [display "From" before the product price](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-product-prices-as-a-price-range) to indicate a price range.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
