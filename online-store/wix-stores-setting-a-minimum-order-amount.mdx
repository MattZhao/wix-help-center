---
title: "Wix Stores: Setting a Minimum Order Amount"
---

Set a minimum order value to avoid shipping or delivering orders that are not profitable for your business. When shoppers add products to the cart that don't make the minimum, they are prompted to add to the order.

> **Tip:**
>
> A message appears in the cart only if there's enough information to determine whether the customer passed your minimum.

If the customer proceeds to checkout, they receive a notification when they try to complete the order.

---

### Setting a minimum order amount

Set an order minimum amount from **Product & Inventory **settings in your site's dashboard.

#### To set a minimum order amount:

[Go to Settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/settings) in your site's dashboard.   Click **Store products & Inventory**.  Click the **Set a minimum subtotal **toggle to enable it.  Enter the minimum amount an order must reach to complete checkout.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about minimum order amounts.

### Can I set minimum or maximum limitations per product?

Yes. You can use the Checkout Requirements app to limit the minimum or maximum quantity of a specific product a customer can purchase. This app can also be used to add a minimum order subtotal. Learn how to [add and set up the Wix Checkout Requirements app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-checkout-requirements-app).

### Does the minimum apply to digital and dropshipping products?

The minimum order amount:
- Applies to dropshipping products.
- Does not apply to digital products.

### Does the minimum apply to local delivery and store pickup orders?

The minimum order amount:
- Applies to local delivery orders.
- Does not apply to store pickup orders.

### Does the minimum apply to orders placed through the Wix member apps or other sales channels?

The minimum order amount doesn't apply to orders placed through any of the [Wix member apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-the-wix-mobile-apps#the-wix-member-apps) (Spaces by Wix, Fit by Wix, Dine by Wix). 
The minimum order also doesn't apply to orders you sell through other sales channels, such as [eBay](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-and-setting-up-an-ebay-shop) or [Amazon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-and-setting-up-an-amazon-shop).

### Is tax included in the minimum amount calculation?

It depends how you calculate tax. 
In some countries (e.g. U.S. and Canada), tax is added at checkout. For these countries, customers must reach the minimum **before **tax is added at checkout.
In most other countries, the price displayed in your store already includes tax. For these countries, the display price in your store must be over the minimum.

### Do customers who don't make the minimum see a message in the cart?

If your store has enough information in the cart to know that the customer cannot meet the minimum amount, a message is displayed there. If not, the message is only displayed when completing checkout. 
For example, customers can checkout with less than the minimum order if they choose store pickup. If you hide the display of shipping options from the cart, the message won't appear.

### How can I let customers know that my store has a minimum order amount?

It's a good idea to communicate to customers that there is a minimum order amount so they don't get a surprise when they go to complete a purchase. This can be done in a variety of ways. For example, you can add a [banner](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-an-announcement-to-your-site#creating-a-banner-for-the-top-of-your-site) to the top of your site:Or a [popup lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-customizing-your-lightbox):[image: Screenshot of a popup lightbox showing the requirement for a minimum order amount]Learn more about adding [site announcements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-an-announcement-to-your-site).

### How can I calculate the right minimum purchase amount to set for my business?

Wix analytics provides a wealth of data about your site. You can use the information collected about the [average order amount](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-about-your-sales-reports-4815635#average-order-value-over-time-report), [sales by shipping method](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-analytics-about-your-sales-reports-4815635#sales-by-shipping-method-report), and more to make an informed decision about the minimum amount you should set.
