When [importing products from another store](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-importing-products-to-wix-stores) to your Wix store, you may encounter some error messages.

---

### Error message example

Error messages generally have two parts. Let's look at this example:

The text before the colon shows which column the error occurs in - in this case, the **fieldType** column.  The text after the column explains what the problem is - here the error is that the text must be either "Product" or "Variant" and some other text was entered.

---

**For variant rows:**

For each variant row, you must enter the variant. This must correspond to the information entered in the product row. So for example, if you entered "small; large"  and "blue; green" in the product row, enter "small;blue", "small;green" "large;blue" and "large;green" in each of the variant rows.

After correcting all the errors, click **Upload New CSV **to upload the corrected file.
