---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Letting Customers Choose Pickup Points from a Map"
---

Currently,  when you offer delivery to pickup points, customers choose from the points nearest to the address they entered during checkout. Selecting a pickup point from a map is not supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
