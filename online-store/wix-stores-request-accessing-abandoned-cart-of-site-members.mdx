---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Accessing Abandoned Cart of Site Members"
---

Currently, it's possible to [access and share the contents of an abandoned cart only](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-and-managing-abandoned-carts#sharing-carts-that-customers-abandoned) if the customer isn't a site member. Assessing a site member's abandoned cart isn't supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
