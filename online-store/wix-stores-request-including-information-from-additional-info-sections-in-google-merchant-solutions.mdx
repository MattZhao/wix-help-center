---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Including Information from Additional Info Sections in Google Merchant Solutions"
---

Currently, when you connect Wix products to Google Merchant Solutions, your basic product information (name, price, image, etc.) is carried over. Product information in any [Additional Info Sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-info-sections-to-products) doesn't appear in Google Merchant Solutions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
