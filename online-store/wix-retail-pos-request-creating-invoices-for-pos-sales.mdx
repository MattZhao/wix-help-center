Currently, it's not possible to create invoices in the retail POS tablet.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can, however, create invoices on desktop: 
> - **Invoices for unpaid sales:** Learn how to [create invoices and optionally accept deposits for unpaid POS sales](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-creating-invoices).
> - **Invoices for paid sales: **Learn how to [create an invoice for a paid order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-invoices-for-store-orders#manually-creating-an-invoice-for-paid-orders).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
