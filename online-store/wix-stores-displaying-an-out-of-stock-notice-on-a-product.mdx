---
title: "Wix Stores: Displaying an 'Out of Stock' Notice on a Product"
---

When one of your products is out of stock, an **Out of Stock** message automatically appears in the Product Gallery and in the Product Page.

> **Tip:**
>
> Instead of the "Out of Stock" message, you can set up back-in-stock notifications. This way, when an item is out of stock, customers see a "Notify When Available" message and can sign up to get an email letting them know when the item is available. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-notifying-customers-when-products-are-back-in-stock)

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Customize the "Out of stock" notice in Product Galleries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-an-out-of-stock-notice-on-a-product#customizing-the-message-in-product-galleries)  [Customize the notice in the Product Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-an-out-of-stock-notice-on-a-product#customizing-the-message-in-the-product-page)

### Customizing the message in Product Galleries

If you display a button in your product gallery, it changes automatically to an "Out of Stock" message when needed. You can customize the text of the "Out of stock" message.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to the page with the gallery you want to customize.
3. Click the gallery on your page.
4. Click **Settings **.
5. Edit the text:

1. Click the **Text **tab.
2. Edit the "Out of Stock" text.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to the page with the gallery you want to customize.
3. Click the gallery on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Edit the text:

1. Click the **Text **tab.
2. Edit the "Out of Stock" text.

### Customizing the message in the Product Page

When a product is out of stock, the **Add to Cart **button in the Product Page automatically changes to an **Out of Stock **button. The color and font of this button cannot be edited, but you can edit the message text.

#### To customize the "Out of stock" message in the Product Page:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Product page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Store Pages**.
3. Click **Product Page**.
3. Click the Product Page element on your page.
4. Click the **Text **tab.
5. Edit the "Out of Stock Message" text.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Product page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Product page under **Store Pages**.
3. Click the Product Page element on your page.
4. Click the **Text **tab.
5. Edit the "Out of Stock Message" text.
