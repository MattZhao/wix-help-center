---
title: "Request: Customizing the PayPal Button in Your Cart"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, you cannot customize the text or color of the [PayPal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-understanding-customer-checkout-with-paypal) button in the Cart Page (for example, to customize the text to inform customers that they can checkout in PayPal using a credit card).

> **Workaround solution:**
>
> In the meantime, you can convey messages to your customers using these options:
> - Add the information to the footer of your site so that it's always visible.
> - Place information in a [lightbox](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-using-lightboxes) that pops up on your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
