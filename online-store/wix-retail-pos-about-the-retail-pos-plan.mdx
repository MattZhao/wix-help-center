title: "Wix Retail POS: About the Retail POS Plan"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - Merchants using Retail POS before October 25, 2023, can continue enjoying all the extra benefits without an additional upgrade.
> - Retail POS is available only in the US and Canada.

To start selling using your Retail POS, upgrade to a Retail POS plan. Upgrading lets you take advantage of the complete range of features and options.

---

### Retail POS plan benefits

Upgrade to accept multiple types of payments and manage your business with advanced tools.

### Accept all types of payments

To accept payment by credit/debit card, gift card, or manual card entry, you need to upgrade to Retail POS plan.
**Tip: **Before upgrading, you'll be able to accept cash payment.

### Add staff members

Upgrade to add more staff members to your team. Each team member can set their own passcode to log in to the POS tablet. **Note: **The maximum number of staff members is determined by your site upgrade (see the FAQ below for more details).

### Allow customers to exchange items

Allow customers to bring a store product back and exchange it for a different one.

### Track staff member hours

Stay on top of your business by tracking your staff members' hours. View the hours each staff member puts in during a time period or select a staff member to view additional tracking details.

### Redeem loyalty points

If you created a [loyalty program](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-an-overview), customers could earn and redeem loyalty points in your online store. With the Retail POS plan, customers can also earn and redeem loyalty points at your brick-and-mortar business.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the plans.

### What kinds of payments can I accept without a Retail POS plan?

You can accept cash payments at your Retail POS without upgrading.

### Does my site need a plan if I already upgrade to a Retail POS plan?

Yes. Upgrading your site to a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) allows you to sell online and much more.

### How many staff members can I add with a Retail POS plan?

Wix offers a variety of [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) upgrades, each of which allows a different number of site collaborators. The maximum number of staff members cannot be more than the number of collaborators allowed with your plan. 
**To check how many collaborators your plan allows:**
1. Go to [Roles & Permissions](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20this%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-website/authorization) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Invite Collaborators**.
3. View the maximum number of emails it's possible to send.
