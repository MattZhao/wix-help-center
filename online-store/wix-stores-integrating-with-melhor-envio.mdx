---
title: "Wix Stores: Integrating with Melhor Envio"
---

Melhor Envio is a multi-carrier shipping software solution for businesses based in Brazil. With it, you can display real-time shipping estimates and print shipping labels with a click. Melhor Envio is free to use. You pay only for the shipping labels you purchase.

After setup, your orders automatically appear in your Melhor Envio dashboard - no need to manually create an order and then copy and paste the order details each time you make a sale.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure to [enter your business address in the Business Info page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-your-sites-business-information).

### To integrate with Melhor Envio:

Click **Add Apps **** **on the left side of the Editor.

Start typing 'Melhor Envio' and click to select the app.   Click **Add to Site**.   Click **Agree & Add**.  Select an option:

- Log in to your existing Melhor Envio account.
- Create a new Melhor Envio account:

1. Fill in the required fields.
2. Click **Finish**.

When you're ready to start printing shipping labels, go to the Melhor Envio dashboard and click **Sync Orders**.

> Learn more about [integrating your store with Melhor Envio](https://ajuda.melhorenvio.com.br/pt-BR/articles/4390142-integre-sua-loja-wix-com-o-melhor-envio) (in Portuguese).
