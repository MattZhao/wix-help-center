title: "Wix Stores: The 10 Essential Steps to Creating Your Online Store"
---

Welcome to the world of eCommerce. This article will guide you through the 10 essential steps you need to create your store.

> **Tip:**
>
> Though these steps are numbered, feel free to do them in any order. Remember, you can go back anytime to edit your settings or add more products.

---

### Step 1 | Create products & product options

A great place to start is with store products. If your products have different options, such as sizes or colors, don't forget to add those up as well.

### Tell me more

**Products**There are two types of store products: physical items or digital downloads (e.g. PDFs, image files, etc.). Physical products can be sold as one-time purchases, or as part of a subscription model.
**Dig deeper:**
- Physical products [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-physical-product)
- Digital products [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-digital-product)
- Product subscriptions [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-selling-product-subscriptions)
 **Product Options

**If your product comes in different sizes, colors, materials or other variations, be sure to add product options so customers can choose the option they want. You have the option of tracking inventory for each variant.

**Dig deeper:**

Product options [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-product-options)

---

### Step 2 | Create categories & display them on separate pages

Categories let you group similar products so they can be displayed together on your site.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you sell only one type of product or a small overall number of products, you may choose to display all your products together in one "Shop" page.

### Tell me more

To create and display categories on different site pages you must create the category, add products to that category, then go to the Editor to display them.

**Tip: **Just creating the product categories does not automatically display it on your site.
**Update: **We're in the process of releasing the [new Category Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-the-category-page). If you don't have the new category page, you can [manually create a page linking to all your categories](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-an-index-of-product-categories).
**Dig deeper:**
- Creating and displaying categories [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-and-displaying-product-categories)

---

### Step 3 | Enter your site settings

Enter all the basic details needed for your site to function smoothly. These include the email address where you'll be contacted, your location, language, and currency.

### Tell me more

Click **Settings**, and then enter your information (address, language, etc.) in the **General **section. If you have employees or partners, go to the Roles & Permissions section to give them limited access your site. [image: Screenshot of the Wix dashboard menu with the SEttings  tab outlined.]
**Dig deeper:**
- Site settings [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-your-basic-site-info)
- Roles & permissions [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview)

---

### Step 4 | Choose shipping, local delivery, & pickup options

Time to figure out how to get your products to customers. For each location you want to sell to, set up any or all of the following options:

### Tell me more

Wix stores has a wide variety of shipping rules to suit every kind of store, from flat rate shipping to rates that vary by weight and more. **Note:** You can also offer customers the choice of paying more for faster delivery (e.g. Express Shipping).
**Dig deeper:**
- Shipping, delivery, and pickup [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-a-guide-to-setting-up-shipping-delivery-and-pickup)

---

### Step 5 | Set up tax

Make sure to set up tax collection to comply with local tax laws.

### Tell me more

You can manually enter how much tax you need to collect and from customers or you can set up automated tax collection with Avalara.
**Dig deeper:**
- Tax setup [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tax-collection-an-overview)

---

### Step 6 | Set up payment providers

Before you can start selling products, you must set up a way for customers to pay you.

### Tell me more

Connect at least one payment method so you can start selling. Choose from:
- Wix Payments
- Credit card payment providers
- PayPal (which can also be used to process credit card payments)
- Payment providers that let customers pay in installments (where available)
- Manual (offline) payments
- and more
The payment options available vary according to where you are located. 

**Dig deeper:**Set up a payment provider. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-accessing-the-payment-setup)

---

### Step 7 | Choose additional checkout and store settings

Click the **Settings **tab in your dashboard to access additional settings. In the **Checkout **section, you can customize your site's Checkout Page. Note that these setting update the Checkout Page for Wix Bookings and Wix Restaurants Orders too, if you use them.

The **Products & inventory **section has additional customizations specific only to Wix Stores merchants.

### Tell me more

In the **Checkout **section, you can customize your site's Checkout Page. You can add fields to collect additional information from customers checking out, add store policies, enable payments with gifts cards, and more.
In the **Products & inventory section**, you'll find a variety of settings to help run your store the way you want to. These include adding a minimum order subtotal and displaying tax or shipping information beside product prices. 
**Dig deeper:**Customize your **Checkout **settings. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-checkout-page)Customize your **Products & inventory **settings:
- [Set when inventory gets updated](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-when-inventory-gets-updated)
- [Add a minimum order subtotal to checkout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-a-minimum-order-amount)
- [Display tax & shipping info next to prices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-tax-and-shipping-info-with-product-prices)
- [Display prices as a price range ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-product-prices-as-a-price-range)

### Step 9 | Customize your members area

The Members Area is where your customers can view upcoming and past orders, edit their saved addresses and credit cards and more.

### Tell me more

Customize the Members Area design so that it matches the rest of your store.
**Dig deeper:**
- Members Area [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-your-customers-member-pages)

It's time to go live. Make sure to follow these last steps so customers can make purchases.

### Tell me more

- Publish your site.
