---
title: "Wix Art Store: Creating Special Offers in Your Art Store"
---

> **Important:**
>
> Wix Art Store is currently not available to all users. As of October 2022 it is no longer possible to add the Wix Art Store to your site. Alternatively, you can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your art.

Wix Art Store can be synced with Order a Print (a third-party service) to fulfill physical print orders. Their prices are predetermined and you set your profit margin. Order a Print offers special seasonal promotions and discounts. That means that the price of the product will drop, but your profit margin won't.

> **Important:**
>
> This feature is only available if you are selling [digital and print images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-art-store-setting-up-wix-art-store-to-sell-digital-images-and-prints).

### To create a special offer:

Click **Settings** on your Art Store.  Click **Manage Store.**  Click **Special Offers.**  Click **Create Special Offer.**

> **Notes:**
>
> - If there is no promotion currently available, the button will appear as gray and non-clickable.
> - The discount amount is set by Order a Print and varies depending on the time of the year. Click [here](https://www.orderaprint.com/special-offers) to learn more.
