---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Amount Discounts that Apply to the Cart Total"
---

Currently, it's not possible to create coupons or automatic discounts that reduce the customer's cart total by a specific amount (e.g. Buy $200, pay $150 or buy 2 items, get $20 off your cart total).

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can, however, create [percentage off coupon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-percentage-discount-coupon) or [automatic discounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-automatic-discounts) that offer a percentage off all products (e.g. buy $200 get 20% off).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
