Increase sales by making it easier for repeat customers to complete a purchase without re-entering their details. Using the Members Area, your customers can create an account on your site and save their details.

---

### How fast checkout works

With a Members Area added to your site, a customer can sign up and log in. When they make their first purchase, the details are saved.

Then, when the customer returns to make a new purchase, they find their details already filled in.

Customers can save their addresses and personal details. If a customer chooses to, they can also save their credit card details by selecting that option.

If your Members Area doesn't have them already, you can add them to your site. ### Are the member pages private?

The pages mentioned in this article are private. Each customer can view only their personal information. It is also possible to add public member pages to your site.

### How can I customize the Members Area?

You can [customize each page in your Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-customizing-your-members-area).
