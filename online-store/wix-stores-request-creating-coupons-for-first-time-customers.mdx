title: "Wix Stores Request: Creating Coupons for First-Time Customers"
---

Currently it is not possible to create a coupon for first-time customers.

> **Update:**
>
> It's now possible to limit coupon use to one per order.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
