---
title: "Wix Stores: Adding a Product in the Wix App"
---

You can add new products to your store on the go using the Wix app. No need to open your computer - you can do it right from your phone.

### To add a store product:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Store** **Products**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Store** **Products**.  Tap **+ Add a Product **at the bottom.   Add [images, GIFs, or videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-images-gifs-and-videos-to-a-product-in-the-wix-app).   Enter the product name.  Enter the product price.  Do any of the following:

- Add a discount amount or percentage and tap **Apply**.
- Hide this product from your online store or POS:

1. Tap **Visibility**.
2. Deselect the checkbox next to the channel where you want to hide the product.
- Enter a brief description of the product.
- Add [product ribbons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-ribbons).
- Add an info section. Learn more [about info sections](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-info-sections-to-products).
- Add the product to one or more [categories](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-and-managing-categories-using-the-wix-owner-app).
- Add a custom text field.
- [Create product options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-product-options-using-the-wix-owner-app)
    [Manage how you track inventory for this product](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-inventory-using-the-wix-app)

**Tip: **Tap the Barcode Scanner iconto add the product's barcode as your SKU.  (Optional) Add the product to a tax group you created using automatic tax calculation.

**Note: **If you created tax groups using manual tax, go to desktop to add the product to a group.   If you have a [fulfiller](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-a-custom-fulfillment-service), you can select them from the **Fulfilled by **drop-down.     Tap **Save**.

> **Editing products:**
>
> To quickly locate a product you want to update, you can do any of the following:
> - Tap the Filter** **icon   to filter products
> - Tap the Barcode Scanner icon  to scan the product's barcode. If you have a product with an SKU number that matches the barcode, the product opens.
