---
title: "Wix Stores: Hiding Product Prices"
---

For some types of online stores, hiding product prices makes good business sense.

By default, prices appear wherever you display your products. You can decide if you want to remove the price display from any place where products are displayed.

**In this article, learn how to hide the price display:**

[In the Category Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-hiding-product-prices#hiding-product-prices-in-the-category-page)  [In Product Galleries](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-hiding-product-prices#hiding-product-prices-in-product-galleries)  [In the Product Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-hiding-product-prices#hiding-product-prices-in-your-product-page)  [FAQs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-hiding-product-prices#faqs)

### Hiding product prices in the Category Page

The [new Category Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-the-category-page) is your main store page. Go to this page in your editor to hide product prices (among other details that you can choose to show or hide).

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Category page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Store Pages**.
3. Click **Category Page**.
3. Click the Category Page element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. In the **Settings **tab, deselect the **Product price** checkbox.
[image: Screenshot of the settings tab in the Category Page settings, with the product price checkbox outlined]
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Category page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Category page under **Store Pages**.
3. Click the Category Page element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. In the **Settings **tab, deselect the **Product price** checkbox.
[image: Screenshot of the settings tab in the Category Page settings, with the product price checkbox outlined]

### Hiding product prices in Product Galleries

If your shop has any kind of Product Galleries, you can customize them so that the product prices are hidden.

#### To hide prices in a gallery:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Go to the page with the gallery you want to customize.   Click the gallery on your page.   Click **Settings**.  Select the **Settings** tab.  Deselect the **Product price** checkbox.

### Hiding product prices in your Product Page

Even if you hide the product prices from the Category Page and your Product Galleries, once a customer clicks to view a product in the Product Page, they'll see the price - unless you also hide it there.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Product page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Store Pages**.
3. Click **Product Page**.
3. Click the Product Page on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Select the **Settings** tab.
6. Deselect the **Product price** checkbox.
[image: Screenshot of the settings tab in the Product Page settings, with the product price checkbox outlined]
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Product page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Product page under **Store Pages**.
3. Click the Product Page on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Select the **Settings** tab.
6. Deselect the **Product price** checkbox.

### FAQs

We are here to help with all your questions. Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I display products without prices and limit access to the product page to members only?

Yes. Start by hiding product prices in the Product Gallery according to the instructions above. Next, you can limit access to the Product Page:
1. Click **Pages and Menu**on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Store Pages**.
3. Click the Settings icon next to **Product Page**.
4. Click the **Permissions **tab.
5. Select **Members only**.

### Can I display product information only to site visitors who are logged in or have a password?

You may want to hide your product information from site visitors, for example, if you're a wholesaler. You can do this by restricting store pages. 
You can control which pages you restrict and whether users simply need to sign up and log in to get access or whether they need your approval to view the pages. 
Learn more about [restricting store pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-restricted-store-pages).
