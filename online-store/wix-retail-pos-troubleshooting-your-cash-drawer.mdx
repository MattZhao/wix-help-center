> - For info on using **POS on a mobile device**, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/mobile-pos-4564663).

If you are experiencing issues operating your cash drawer, try the steps below.

### To troubleshoot the cash drawer:

Disconnect the cash drawer from the receipt printer.  Wait a few minutes.  Reconnect the cash drawer to the receipt printer.  Tap **Checkout **on the bottom bar.   **Actions** and select **Open Drawer w/o Sale**.**No Sale **to open it manually.  If the cash drawer is still not working, restart the tablet.

#### Still not working?

If the problem persists, contact [Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact).
