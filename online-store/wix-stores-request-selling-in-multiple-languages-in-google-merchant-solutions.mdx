---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Selling in Multiple Languages in Google Merchant Solutions"
---

Currently, when you sell products with Google Merchant Solutions, they are displayed by Google in your language and in the regions that you ship to that work with this language. It's not currently possible to sell in more than one language.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
