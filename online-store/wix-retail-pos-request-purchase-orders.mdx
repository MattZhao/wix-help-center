title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Purchase Orders"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to create purchase orders in the Wix dashboard and send them to suppliers. When you mark the merchandise as received, your inventory updates automatically. Learn how to [create and send purchase orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-and-sending-purchase-orders).
