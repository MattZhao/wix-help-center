---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Adding Product Subscriptions to the Cart"
---

Currently, when a customer selects a product subscription on the Product Page and clicks the "Subscribe Now" button, they are taken directly to checkout. Any other products they had added to the cart are not included in the purchase. Adding a subscription product to the cart together with other products is not currently supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
