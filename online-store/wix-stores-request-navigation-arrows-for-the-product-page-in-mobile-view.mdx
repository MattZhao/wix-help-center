---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Navigation Arrows for the Product Page in Mobile View"
---

Currently, it is not possible to add navigate arrows or "Previous" and "Next" buttons on the Product Page in the mobile view.

> **Tip:**
>
> Navigation buttons do, however, appear when viewing the product page on desktop.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
