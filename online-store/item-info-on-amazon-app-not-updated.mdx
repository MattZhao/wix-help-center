---
title: "Item Info on Amazon App Not Updated"
---

When an item's information (e.g. price) is updated on Amazon, it may take up to 24 hours before the update appears in the Amazon app. You can update the app manually to make the changes visible immediately.

### To update the app manually:

Click the Amazon app in your Editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Main**.  Click **Manage Items**.  Hover over the relevant item and click the** Refresh**icon.
