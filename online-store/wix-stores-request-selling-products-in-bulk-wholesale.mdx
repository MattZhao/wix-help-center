---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Selling Products in Bulk (Wholesale)"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create a wholesale section where you sell products in bulk.

It is, however, possible to use [this workaround](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-restricted-store-pages) to create restricted page that you can use to sell wholesale.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
