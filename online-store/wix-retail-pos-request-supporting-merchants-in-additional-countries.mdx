---
title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Supporting Merchants in Additional Countries"
---

Currently, [Wix Retail Point of Sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-wix-pos-for-brick-and-mortar-stores) (POS) is available only for merchants located in the US and Canada.

What additional countries would you like to see Wix Retail POS supported in? Click [here](https://www.wix.com/support/conversations/category/contact) to submit a ticket with your feedback and suggestions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
