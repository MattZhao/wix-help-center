---
title: "Request: Uploading Your Own Cart Icon"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, it is not possible to upload your own shopping cart icon. You can, however, [update the Cart Icon's design](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-cart-icon).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
