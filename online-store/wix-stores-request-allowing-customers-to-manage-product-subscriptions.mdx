---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Allowing Customers to Manage Product Subscriptions"
---

Currently, customers cannot cancel their product subscriptions or update their shipping details, etc. on their personal My Subscriptions page. You need to [make those changes for your customers](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-subscriptions).

> **Update:**
>
> Customers can now update their payment method by themselves. Learn how [customers can update their payment details](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-subscriptions#showing-customers-how-to-update-their-payment-method).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
