---
title: "Request: Ability to Refund Orders Processed through Additional Payment Providers"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, it's possible to refund orders from your Wix dashboard only when the payment was processed through PayPal, Stripe, or Wix Payments.

> **Note:**
>
> If another payment provider was used, you can still refund your customers using the instructions [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refunding-payments-made-through-other-payment-providers), but you need to complete part of the process outside the Wix dashboard. 
> When you complete the steps, the order in the Orders tab is updated to show the refund.
>
> [image: Screenshot of an order in the Orders list with the refunded status outlined]

From which payment providers would you like to be able to provide refunds? Click [here](https://www.wix.com/contact) and submit a ticket with your suggestions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
