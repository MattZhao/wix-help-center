---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Ability to Link an Image to each Product Variant"
---

Currently, it is possible to [link separate images to all the choices connected to one product option](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-customizing-product-options#displaying-different-images-for-product-option-choices) (e.g. green, blue). It is not possible to link to every variant when you have more than one type of product option (e.g. green and long, green and short, blue and long, blue and short).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
