---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Limiting Checkout by Number of Items from a Category"
---

Currently, it is not possible to place a limit on checkout based on how many items from a category the customer wants to purchase (e.g. maximum purchase is 1 item from the sale category, or minimum purchase is 3 from the sock category).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
