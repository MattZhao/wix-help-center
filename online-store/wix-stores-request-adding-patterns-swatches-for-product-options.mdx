---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Adding Patterns Swatches for Product Options"
---

Currently, when adding color options, you can pick a solid color. Uploading a thumbnail image of a patterned material (e.g. stripes) is not supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
