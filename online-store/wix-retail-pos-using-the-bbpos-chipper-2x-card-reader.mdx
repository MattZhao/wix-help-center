### Turning the card reader on and off

You can turn on the device by tapping and releasing the power button. When you do, the card reader’s Bluetooth turns on automatically.

> **Notes:**
>
> - When charging, always plug the reader into a wall outlet, not directly into the tablet.
> - To function correctly, make sure the card reader is no more than 15 meters (49 feet) away from the tablet.
> - The reader turns off automatically after 10 hours of inactivity.
> - The card reader automatically disconnects if the battery goes below a 25% charge.

---

### Connecting or disconnecting the card reader and tablet

Before you can start accepting payment, you need to connect the card reader to your tablet. You may also need to disconnect your card reader from your tablet, for example, if you replace your reader with a new one.

#### To connect or disconnect the reader:

Open the POS tablet.   Tap the Connect Reader icon  at the bottom right.

Select what you want to do:

- Connect your card reader:

1. Tap **Set up Card Reader**.
2. Tap **Connect** next to the relevant reader.
3. Tap **Done**.
- Disconnect your card reader: 

1. Tap **Unpair **at the top right.
2. Tap **Unpair** to confirm.  .

### Understanding the card reader lights

The BBPOS Chipper 2X reader has lights to indicates it's status. You can review the table below to help you understand them.

LightStatusNo lightThe reader is off.Flashing blue every secondThe reader is on and ready to connect to a device.

**Note:** It turns off automatically after 5 minutes if not connected.Multicolored flashingThe reader has been discovered using [Bluetooth Proximity](https://stripe.com/docs/terminal/readers/connecting/bbpos-chipper2xbt#bluetooth-proximity) and is ready to connect.Steady blueThe reader is connected to a device.Flashing blue every 5 secondsThe reader is in standby mode.**Note:** It can remain in standby indefinitely.Alternating red and magentaThe reader is charging.Flashing redThe reader’s battery is low.Rapidly flashing blue and orangeThe reader has finished installing a software update. After the update is finished, you can restart the reader by turning it off and on If the reader is unresponsive.

### Updating the card reader software

Check for updates to the card reader software from time to time and update the software when available.

#### To update the card reader software:

Open the POS tablet.   Tap the Connect Reader icon  at the bottom right.

Tap **Check for Update**.  If an update is available, tap **Update Now** and follow the instructions.

**Note: **After the update has been completed, it will flash blue and orange. If the reader is unresponsive, restart it.
