title: "Wix Stores: Setting Up and Managing Abandoned Carts"
---

When a customer adds products to their cart but then doesn't complete a purchase, the cart is considered abandoned. A great way to improve sales and foster user retention is to email shoppers who abandon their carts.

Sending customers a personalized email with the image of an item that was left in the cart along with a direct link makes it easy for customers to go back and complete the purchase.

### What is considered an abandoned cart?

To be considered abandoned, these conditions must be fulfilled:
- The customer added items to the cart.
- No changes have been made to the cart for at least an hour or an hour has passed since the customer closed the tab.
- Wix Stores has the customer's email address (either because the customer has logged in to your site or because they completed the "Shipping details" part of the checkout process).

### Activate abandoned cart email: Quick setup

To save you time, this automation is already set up for you. Just activate it and you're good to go. Your customers will receive an email 2 hours after they first placed an item in the cart.

#### To set up the abandoned carts using the quick setup:

[Go to Automations](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Automations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/triggers/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Set Up Automation **under **Increase sales by reminding customers of abandoned carts**.

Click **Activate**.

### Activating and customizing abandoned cart emails

Every automation has a trigger, an action, and timing. In this case, the automation is triggered when a customer abandons their cart.

Instead of using the Quick Setup and leaving all the default settings, you can customize the "action" (editing the email that's sent to customers) and "timing" of this automation.

#### To create and customize the abandoned cart automation:

[Go to Automations](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Automations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/triggers/) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Set Up Automation **under **Increase sales by reminding customers of abandoned carts**.

  Choose what you want to edit in this automation:

### Edit the sender name and email

1. Click **Edit **next to **Action**.
2. Click **Edit **under **Sender details**.
3. Enter a **From name** (select a name customers will recognize, such as your business name).
4. Enter a **Reply-to email** (the email address that gets the customer's reply).
5. Click **Save**.

### Edit the text and design of the email

1. Click **Edit **next to **Action**.
2. Click **Edit Template** under **Create an email**.
3. [Edit the text and design the look of the email](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-automations-customizing-automated-emails#editing-your-email-template).
4. When ready, click **Save & Publish**.

**Important: **The email is set up to automatically show each customer a product they abandoned, the product name and price, and a link to the cart. We suggest **not editing** the parts of the template outlined in red in the image below. The design of the button (outlined in green) can be edited, but **do not** change the button link.

### Schedule how soon you send the email

**Note: **For best results, we suggest you leave the default selections.

1. Click **Edit** next to **Timing**.
2. Select an option from the **Choose a time to send this action** drop-down.

- **Immediately: **Email is sent one hour after the customer adds an item to the cart.
- **Set custom time: **You select when to send the email (if you select 4 hours, the email is sent 5 hours after the customer adds an item to the cart):

1. Click the arrows to set the number of hours / days.
2. Select **Hours** or **Days**.
3. Select how many times the same customer can receive an abandoned cart email:

- **Don't limit**: Customer receives an email for each abandoned cart.
- **Once per contact : **An email is sent the first time a particular customer abandons an item (i.e. if the customer abandons new items a year later, they do not receive an email).
- **Once per contact every 24 hours (Recommended): **Every time a customer abandons a new cart within 24 hours, they receive an email.

4.  When ready, click **Activate** at the top right.

### Testing the abandoned cart automation

After setting up the automation, you can test it to see it in action. Note that a cart is considered abandoned after an hour has passed since the customer closed the tab.

#### To trigger a test abandoned cart email:

Access your live site in [incognito mode](https://support.wix.com/en/article/opening-your-browser-in-incognitoprivate-browsing-mode).  Add an item to the cart and proceed to checkout.  Enter the shipping details (including your email address).  Click **Continue**.  Close the site tab.

> **Receiving notifications:**
>
> - Both the "customer" and the site owner receive notifications.
> - It takes an hour before a cart is considered abandoned. Add to this any further delay you added during the setup. For example, if you set the automation to send an email 1 hour after the trigger, you and your customer receive notifications 2 hours after you close the tab.

### Temporarily deactivating the automation

If you want to temporarily stop sending abandoned cart emails, you can deactivate the automation.

When you're ready to send emails again, click the toggle to enable it.

#### To deactivate the automation:

[Go to Automations](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Wix%20Automations&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/triggers/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions icon  next to the abandoned cart automation.  Click the **Active** toggle to disable it.

### Sharing carts that customers abandoned

While communicating with a customer, you may want to share the cart that the customer abandoned. For example, while chatting with a customer, you can send them the direct link to their abandoned cart along with a discount coupon.

> **Note:**
>
> Carts remain in the Abandoned Cart tab for 30 days and are then removed.

#### To access your store's abandoned carts:

[Go to Abandoned Carts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=View%20Abandoned%20Carts&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/abandoned-carts/) in your site's dashboard.  (Optional) Filter the carts to view only certain types (e.g. recovered carts).

  Get a shareable link to a specific customer's abandoned cart:

1. Click the More Actions** **icon  next to the cart you want to view.
2. Click **Copy link to cart**.

> **Tip:**
>
> If the customer is not a [site member](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area), you can use the link to view their cart. For security reasons, it's not possible to view the cart of a site member.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about abandoned carts.

### If a customer unsubscribed from my mailing list, will they still get an abandoned cart email?

No, emails are not sent to customers who unsubscribed from your mailing list.

### How can I view more information about a customer in the abandoned cart list?

Click an abandoned cart to open the customer's contact card. There you can see their information, view past emails they sent, and much more.

### Can I send more than one reminder?

You can send a second email to the same customer by creating a second automation with a longer time before the email is sent. 
You can even sweeten the deal by including a [coupon code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-offering-discounts-and-coupons#coupons).

### Can I use the link to cart to view my customer's cart?

You can paste the URL into a browser tab to view the cart your customer abandoned, provided that they aren't site members. 
It's not possible to view site members' carts for security reasons.
