---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Choose the Display Order of Shipping Options"
---

Currently, if you offer customers more than one shipping service (e.g. standard shipping, express shipping, etc.), the options are displayed in order, from the lowest fee to the highest. It is not currently possible to change this order.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly  appreciated.
