---
title: "Wix Stores: Enabling Guest Checkout for PayPal"
---

Guest checkout for PayPal allows your customers to pay via PayPal without having a PayPal account. You can set up guest checkout for Standard or [Fast Checkout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-fast-checkout).

> **Note:**
>
> Customers with PayPal accounts can still log in to their PayPal accounts to check out.

### To enable guest checkout in PayPal:

Sign in to your PayPal account.  Click the **Settings** icon  on the right.  Click the **Seller Tools **tab.   Click **Update** next to Website Preferences.  Scroll down and select **On **under **PayPal Account Optional**.

> **Note:**
>
> If you are unable to see this setting in your PayPal account, please contact your local PayPal organization and ask them to enable it for you.
