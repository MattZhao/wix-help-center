---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Disabling the Sending of Back-in-Stock Requests to Site Collaborators"
---

Currently, if a customer requests to be notified when a product is back in stock, all site collaborators receive a notification. It is not possible for collaborators to opt out.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
