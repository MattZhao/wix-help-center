Wix Stores is an eCommerce platform that offers you tons of exciting features to help you create a stunning and professional online store.

We are constantly improving the Wix Stores and adding new options. Take a look at some of the things you can do.

### Sell your products

You can offer up to 50,000 products in your online store. Sell physical or digital products, increase revenue with product subscriptions, connect the right payment method for your business, and start taking secure payments online.

[Sell digital products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-digital-product)  [Sell product subscriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-selling-product-subscriptions)  [Sell gift cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-wix-gift-cards-9451439)  [Display different images for each product option](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-customizing-product-options#displaying-different-images-for-product-option-choices)  [Display product videos](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-images-gifs-and-videos-to-products#adding-product-videos)  [Connect multiple payment methods](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview)  [Let customers pay in installments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-letting-customers-pay-in-installments)  [Import store products from other platforms](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-importing-products-to-wix-stores)

### Sell dropshipping products

Choose what you want to sell from the millions of high-quality products offered by suppliers. When you sell dropshipping products, you skip the hassle of managing inventory, fulfillment, and shipping.

[Sell products through Modalyst](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-modalyst-dropshipping-to-your-store)  [Sell products through Spocket](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-dropshipping-with-spocket)  [Sell print on demand products through Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful)  [Sell print on demand products through Printify](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-printify-to-your-store)  Check for other available dropshippers in the [Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market/)

### Manage orders

Handle and track orders as they come in. You can print and pay for shipping labels right from your Wix dashboard, and ship worldwide using a variety of shipping and fulfillment options.

Offer local customers [hand delivery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-local-delivery) or [store pickup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-store-pickup-as-a-delivery-option) from multiple locations  [Choose from many shipping rules](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-a-guide-to-setting-up-shipping-delivery-and-pickup#step-2-select-a-shipping-rule-for-each-region) (flat rate, by weight, by price, and more)  Fulfill orders with an integrated 3PL service:

- [Fulfill orders with Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-amazon-multi-channel-fulfillment)
- [Fulfill products with ShipBob](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-fulfilling-orders-with-the-shipbob-fulfillment-service)
- [Fulfill orders with Bigblue](https://www.wix.com/app-market/bigblue-all-in-one-logistics)
- [Fulfill orders with Printful fulfillment](https://www.printful.com/warehousing-fulfillment/same-day-order-fulfillment)
    [Fulfill orders using a fulfiller you choose](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-a-custom-fulfillment-service)  [Offer free shipping on orders above a specific price](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-free-shipping-above-a-set-price)  [Create different shipping rates for different products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-shipping-rate-by-product)  Real time shipping calculations ([US](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-real-time-shipping-calculation-with-usps) and [Brazil](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-real-time-correios-shipping-calculation-with-meu-frete) only)  [Choose your favorite print label solution](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-shipping-labels)  [Print packing slips](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-fulfilling-store-orders#printing-packing-slips)  [Display shipping and tax estimates in the Cart](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-how-shipping-and-tax-displays-in-the-cart-page)  [Calculate tax automatically](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-automatic-tax-collection)
