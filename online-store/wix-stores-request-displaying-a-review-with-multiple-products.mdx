---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Displaying a Review with Multiple Products"
---

Currently, each review is displayed alongside the specific product that your customer reviewed. It's not possible to display a review with other, similar products.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
