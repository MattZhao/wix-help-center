title: "Wix Stores: Adding the \"ProveSource\" Social Proof App"
---

ProveSource uses social proof to help businesses turn site traffic, sales, and data into insights your customers can see.

After you integrate your site with ProveSource, the app:

Imports up to 30 past purchases  Creates a stream notification to show site visitors those purchases

> **Before you begin:**
>
> Make sure your site has been upgraded to a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) that allows you to accept payments and has a domain.

### To add the ProveSource app:

Wix EditorStudio EditorDashboard
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Apps **  on the left side of the editor.
3. Search for the ProveSource app in the search bar.
4. Click **Add to Site**.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **App Market **** **on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **App Market**.
4. Use the search bar to find the ProveSource app.
5. Click **Add to Site**.

1. [Go to the Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market) in your site's dashboard.
2. Use the search bar to find the ProveSource app.
3. Click **Add to Site**.

After adding the app, you are taken to the ProveSource dashboard. There you can perform actions such as customizing notifications, setting goals, and viewing analytics. To learn more, visit the [ProveSource help center](https://help.provesrc.com/en/).
