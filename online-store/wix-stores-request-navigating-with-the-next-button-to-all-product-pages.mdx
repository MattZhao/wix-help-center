---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Navigating with the \"Next\" Button to all Product Pages"
---

When you have a large number of products in a product gallery, you can set the page up to display a Load More button, page numbers, or an infinite scroll.

Currently, when a customer click **Next** in the Product Page, they can only navigate to the last product on the current page.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you select the infinite scroll setting, customers can navigate through all your products using the "Next" link.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
