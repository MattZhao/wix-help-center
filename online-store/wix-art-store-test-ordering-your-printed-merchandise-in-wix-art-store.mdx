> **Important:**
>
> Wix Art Store is currently not available to all users. As of October 2022 it is no longer possible to add the Wix Art Store to your site. Alternatively, you can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your art.

Your Wix Art Store is up and running, so you're on your way to monetizing your content. If you'd like to try the products on your own, you can easily place a test order. As a Wix Art Store merchant, you'll receive a one-time 20% discount.

### To place a 20% discounted test order:

Make sure you have [added](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-art-store-about-the-wix-art-store) and [set up](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-art-store-setting-up-wix-art-store-to-sell-digital-images-and-prints) your Wix Art Store.  [Sign in](https://support.wix.com/en/article/logging-in-to-your-wix-account) and go to your published site.  Make a purchase on your Wix Art Store the same way a regular customer would.

**Note**: The discount only appears at checkout, not in your cart.  Before entering your details on Order a Print, you'll see the following offer:

  Click **Use Discount**.
