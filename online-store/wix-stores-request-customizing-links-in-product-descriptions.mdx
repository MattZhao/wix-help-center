---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Customizing Links in Product Descriptions"
---

Currently, it is not possible to customize the color or font of links that appear in a product's info section or change the font of links that appear in a product's description.

> **Update:**
>
> It is now possible to customize the color of links in product descriptions.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
