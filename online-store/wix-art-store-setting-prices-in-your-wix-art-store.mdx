### Selling Digital and Prints

Wix works with a third-party service, Order a Print, to allow you to sell your images for print. As this service has its own fees, you are asked to enter the amount you want to earn (your profit) and the full price is calculated accordingly. Additional shipping fees are also applied to the customer's final fee.

To learn more about setting prices when selling digital and prints, click [here](https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store/pricing).

### Selling Digital Files Only

If you're just selling digital files of your art in your Art Store, you can change your pricing plan in your store's settings in your Editor.

#### To change your pricing plan when selling digital only:

Click the Wix Art Store app in your Editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Store** tab.  Click **Set Pricing**.  Set which licenses you want to use:

- **License Templates:** Click the toggles under **My Licenses** to enable or disable each license option:

- **Commercial Use:** Web and digital use only.
- **Extended Use:** Web, digital and print use.
- **Custom License: **Click **Create License **and fill in the information to create a custom license:

1. Enter the name of the license in the **License Name** field.
2. Enter a description of the license in the **Description** field.
3. (Optional) Upload your license agreement:

1. Click **Upload**.
2. Select your license document or click **Upload Docs** to upload it from your computer.
3. Click **Apply**.
4. Click **Save**.
    Select or deselect the image resolution checkboxes and enter the price for the selected options:

- **Web Ready:** Image quality is slightly reduced to allow for quicker web loading.
- **Original File: **The image is purchased in its original quality.
    Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> All images within a specific Wix Art Store gallery are sold with the same pricing plan. Within each gallery, you can offer different prices for different licenses and qualities. To offer different images with a different pricing plan, add the app again to a different page of your site.
