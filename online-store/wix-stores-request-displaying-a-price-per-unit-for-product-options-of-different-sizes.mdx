---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Displaying a Price per Unit for Product Options of Different Sizes"
---

Currently, it is possible to [display one price per unit](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-the-product-price-per-unit) for products and product variants. A different price per unit for product variants of different sizes is not supported (e.g. a separate price per unit for a 375 ml mini and a 750 ml wine bottle).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
