---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Creating Shipping Rules for Different Cities and Counties"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create unique shipping rules for different cities and counties.

It is, however, possible to create different shipping rules for regions (e.g. states, provinces etc.). [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-a-guide-to-setting-up-shipping-delivery-and-pickup)

> **Note:**
>
> If you cannot find a shipping region you need, [contact us](https://www.wix.com/contact/en?subOptionId=Set-up-one-shipping-and-tax-region-made-up-of-multiple-counties75075) and let us know which region is missing.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly  appreciated.
