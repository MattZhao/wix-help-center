---
title: "Wix Art Store: Setting Up Wix Art Store to Sell Digital Images and Prints"
---

> **Important:**
>
> Wix Art Store is currently not available to all users. As of October 2022 it is no longer possible to add the Wix Art Store to your site. Alternatively, you can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your art.

Wix Art Store works with a third-party service, [Order a Print](https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store), to give you even more options when selling your art work. Order A Print provides a complete print-on-demand service for you to sell your digital images and prints online.

> **Note:**
>
> - Digital images are available for sale worldwide, printed products are available as mentioned [here](https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store/products). Customers visiting your store only see products available in their country.
> - This article refers to selling digital and print images. If you want to sell digital images only, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-art-store-setting-up-wix-art-store-to-sell-digital-images).

Once you've [added Wix Art Store](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-art-store-about-the-wix-art-store) to your site, follow the steps below to start selling digital images and prints.

### To set up your store to sell digital images and prints:

### Step 1: Choose the type of images you want to sell (digital or digital and print).

1. Click the Wix Art Store app in the Editor.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Set Up Store**.
4. Click **Start Now**.
5. Click **Sell Digital & Print**.
6. Click **OK**.

### Step 2: Add the images you want to sell to your gallery.

**Note:** You do not need to add all the images you want to sell at once. You can always come back to add more images.
1. Click **Upload Images**.
2. Click** Add Media**.
3. Click **Image**.
4. Select the images from your media manager or click **Upload Images** to upload new images.
5. Click** Apply**.

**Note:** You can add a title, description and link for each image, reorder the images or edit the image quality.
6. Click **Done**.

**Tip:** The size offered for the printed items depend on the size of the file you use in your Art Store. The larger the file is, the larger the print sizes that will be offered. To get the best results for large prints (e.g. art posters, canvas) it is recommended to upload images of 4000px or more, in RGB format.

### Step 3: Set the pricing for the images.

1. Click **Settings** on the Art Store in the Editor.
2. Click **Store**.
3. Click **Set Pricing**.
4. Follow the steps [here](https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store/pricing) to set the pricing.

### Step 4: Add your business information and set up your Art Store

1. Go to the **Store Info** tab in the Art Store in your dashboard
2. Add your business name, email for order confirmation, and your country.
3. Choose your email settings.
4. Add location settings: e.g. currency, language, price format, and currency symbol alignment.
