---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Customizing Product Page in Mobile View"
---

Currently, the customizations you make to the Product Page on desktop in the Wix Editor are reflected in the mobile view. it is not possible to customize the Product Page separately for mobile.

> **Studio Editor:**
>
> In the Studio Editor, all breakpoints are customizable.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
