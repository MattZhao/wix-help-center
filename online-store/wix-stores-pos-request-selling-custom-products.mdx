---
title: "Wix Stores POS Request: Selling Custom Products"
---

Currently, you cannot add a custom product to a sale at a mobile POS.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can create a custom product and set a price (e.g. $50) and [charge your customer that price](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-pos-selling-products-at-a-mobile-point-of-sale#step-2-add-products-or-a-custom-amount-to-the-cart).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
