Manage your store's shipping, right from your mobile phone. You can set up different shipping rules for different regions. For example, you can offer local delivery to customers in a 3-mile radius from your business, free shipping to customers in your country, and a flat shipping rate to customers around the world.

> **Note**
>
> - Customers from regions with no shipping rule cannot make purchases from your store.
> - For more in-depth information, read our [guide to setting up shipping, delivery, and pickup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-a-guide-to-setting-up-shipping-delivery-and-pickup).

### Adding and managing shipping regions

Before a customer can purchase items from your store, there has to be a shipping, delivery, or pickup rule that covers their region.

New stores come with 2 shipping rules already set up - a free shipping rule for your region and a free international shipping rule for the rest of the world. You can edit these rules or delete them and create rules from scratch.

> **Tip:**
>
> A region can be part of a country (e.g. California), a country (e.g. US), multiple countries (e.g. US and Canada), or the rest of the world (any country not covered by another region you set up).

#### To add and manage regions:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.   Tap **Orders**.  Tap the **Settings **icon .  Select **Set shipping**.  Choose what you want to do:

### Add a new shipping region

1. Tap **+ New Region**.
2. Enter a region name.

**Tip: **Only you see this name.
3. Tap **+ Add Destination**.
4. Tap the destination(s) you want to add to this shipping rule.
5. (Optional) Select specific regions inside this destination:

1. Tap the country you added.
2. Deselect any states / provinces you don't want to ship to.
3. Tap **Save**.
6. Tap **Save**.

### Edit or delete and existing shipping region

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to a region.
2. Select an option:

- **Edit: **Make changes to a region:

- Add a country: Click **+ Add Destination**
- Remove a country: Tap the **X **or the **More Actions** icon  and select **Remove**.
- Select region(s) in the country (e.g. one or more states, provinces etc.):

1. Tap the **More Actions** icon  next to a region
2. Select **Edit**.
3. Deselect the checkboxes next to the relevant states.
4. Tap **Save**.
- **Deactivate: **Make the region temporarily unavailable for shipping.
- **Delete:** Permanently remove a region.

### Setting up shipping in a region

By default, every shipping region is set for free shipping. You can leave this as is or select a shipping rule that works for your store.

#### To set up shipping:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.   Tap **Orders**.  Tap the **Settings **icon .  Select **Set shipping**.  Tap the region you want to set up.  Choose the type of shipping rule you want to set up:

### Free shipping

Free shipping is the default shipping rule. Just tap **Save** to save this rule.

### Flat rate shipping

To simplify shipping, you can set a flat rate for all your orders (e.g. shipping for all orders is $8).
**To set up flat rate shipping:**
1. Tap the existing shipping rule.
2. Tap the **Shipping method** drop-down and select **Flat Rate**.
3. Tap **Next**.
4. Create a delivery option (e.g. Standard Shipping, Express Shipping):

1. Enter a title in the **Delivery Option Name **field.

**Note: **Customers see this name at checkout.
2. (Optional) Enter a time in the **Est. Delivery Time** field.
3. Enter the shipping fee in the **Rate** field.
4. (Optional) To offer free shipping on orders above a specific price:

1. Select the** Offer free Shipping when... **checkbox.
2. Enter the price above which shipping is free and tap **Done**.

**Note: **If you create additional shipping services (e.g. priority shipping), this checkbox must be selected and the amount specified to apply to each service.
3. Tap **Save**.
5. (Optional) Create an additional option (e.g. Express Shipping) by tapping **+Add Shipping Option** and follow the instructions above.
6. (Optional) Add a handling fee:

1. Select the **Add a handling fee to every order** checkbox.
2. Enter the fee amount.
3. Tap **Save**.
7. Tap **Save**.
8. Tap **Save **again.

### Rate by weight shipping

Setting your shipping rate by weight is ideal for stores that sell items that cost very different rates to ship because of their weight (e.g. a store that sells jewelry and winter coats). 
To set it up, create 2 or more weight categories and set a shipping price for each. For example, shipping can cost $5 for orders under 1 pound, $10 shipping for orders between 1 - 3 pounds, and $15 shipping for orders that weigh over 3 pounds.
**To set up shipping rates by weight. **
1. Tap the existing shipping rule.
2. Tap the **Shipping method** drop-down and select **Rate by Weight**.
3. Tap **Next**.
4. Create a delivery option (e.g. Standard Shipping, Express Shipping):

1. Enter a title in the **Delivery Option Name **field.

**Note: **Customers see this name at checkout.
2. (Optional) Enter a time in the **Est. Delivery Time** field.
3. Enter the top limit of the first weight range (e.g. enter 1 lb. to create a rule for purchases from 0 - 1 lb).

**Note: **The top limit of the first weight range is automatically set as the bottom limit of the second weight range.
4. Enter the rate (shipping price) for the first weight range (e.g. $5).
5. Create the second weight range: (e.g. 1 lb- 2 lb) and add the shipping rate (e.g. $10): Tap **+ Add Range** to enter the new range or If this your last weight range (e.g. 2 lb and up), just enter the shipping rate.
6. (Optional) Tap **Add Range** to add any additional ranges.
5. (Optional) Offer free shipping on orders above a specific price:

1. Select the** Offer free Shipping when... **checkbox.
2. Enter the price above which shipping is free and tap **Done**.

**Note: **If you create additional shipping services (e.g. priority shipping), this checkbox must be selected and the amount specified to apply to each service.
3. Tap **Save**.
6. (Optional) Create an additional option (e.g. Express Shipping) by tapping **+Add Shipping Option** and follow the instructions above.
7. (Optional) Add a handling fee:

1. Select the **Add a handling fee to every order** checkbox.
2. Enter the fee amount.
3. Tap **Save**.
8. Tap **Save**.
9. Tap **Save **again.

### Rate by order price shipping

You can set your shipping fees so that the price of the order determines how much customers pay for shipping. For example, you can charge $10 for shipping for orders between $0 - $100. Then, you can charge less or more for shipping, when the order is for $100 and over.
**To set up shipping rates by price:**
1. Tap the existing shipping rule.
2. Tap the **Shipping method** drop-down and select **Rate by Order Price**.
3. Tap **Next**.
4. Create a delivery option (e.g. Standard Shipping, Express Shipping)

1. Enter a title in the **Delivery Option Name **field.

**Note: **Customers see this name at checkout.
2. (Optional) Enter a time in the **Est. Delivery Time** field.
3. Create the first price range: (e.g. $0 - $15) and add the shipping rate (e.g. $4).
4. Create the second price range: (e.g. $15 - $30) and add the shipping rate (e.g. $8):

- If this is your last price range (e.g. $15 and up), just enter the shipping rate.
- If you plan to add more price ranges, tap **+ Add Range** to enter the new range and shipping rate.
5. (Optional) To offer free shipping on orders above a specific price:

1. Tap to enable the** Offer free Shipping **toggle.
2. Enter the price above which shipping is free and tap **Done**.

**Note: **If you create additional shipping services (e.g. priority shipping), this checkbox must be selected and the amount specified to apply to each service.
3. Tap **Save**.
5. (Optional) Create an additional delivery option (e.g. Express Shipping) by tapping **+ Add Shipping Option** and following the instructions above.
6. (Optional) Add a handling fee:

1. Tap **Add** next to **Handling Fee**.
2. Enter the fee amount.
3. Tap **Save**.
7. Tap **Save**.
8. Tap **Save** again.

### Setting up local delivery in a region

Offer a hand-delivery option to customers who are located near your business location(s).

#### To set up local delivery:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.   Tap **Orders**.  Tap the **Settings **icon .  Select **Set shipping**.  Select the region you want to set up.  Tap **+ Add Local Delivery**.  Enter an area name (e.g. Delivery to Bronx).  Select how you want to define the delivery area:

- **Radius:**

1. (Optional) edit your business address.

**Tip: **This address is the center of the radius you set.
2. Enter the radius (e.g. 3 miles).
- **Postal code:**

1. Tap **+ Add ZIP/Postal Codes**.
2. Select any of the following options:

- Enter one or more complete postal codes and press **Next** after each one.
- Enter a partial postal code with an asterisk to encompass a larger area and press **Next** after each one. [Learn more about setting an area by postal code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/defining-your-local-delivery-area-with-postal-codes)
3. Tap **Add** at the top right.
    Enter an estimated delivery time (e.g. 2 - 3 Business Days).  Enter your delivery rate (e.g. $5).  (Optional) Offer free delivery for orders above a set price:

1. Select the checkbox.
2. Enter the price that qualifies for free delivery.
    Tap **Add**.  Tap **Save**.
