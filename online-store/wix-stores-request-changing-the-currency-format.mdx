---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Changing the Currency Format"
---

Currently, it is not possible to change the currency format ($, €, ¥) of products to currency codes (USD, EUR, JPY) using Wix Stores.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
