---
title: "Wix Stores: Adding a Best Sellers Gallery"
---

Highlight your best-selling products by displaying them in a gallery. We'll automatically calculate which items are the most popular, based on your store sales.

> **Note:**
>
> If you haven't yet had enough sales for us to determine which are your best sellers, we’ll show related products or your All Products category.

### To add and set up a Best Sellers gallery:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to the page to which you want to add the gallery.
3. Click **Add Elements  ** on the left side of the editor.
4. Select **Store**.
5. Click **Best Sellers**.
6. Drag the gallery to the page.
[image: Screenshot of the Slider Product Gallery in the Add Panel]
1. (Optional) Edit the default gallery name ("Best Sellers"):

1. Click the **Text** tab.
2. Enter a new name under **Gallery title text.**

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to the page to which you want to add the gallery.
3. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
4. Select **Store**.
5. Click **Slider Product Gallery**.
6. Drag the gallery to the page.
[image: Screenshot of the Slider Product Gallery in the Add Panel]
1. Select the Slider Product gallery in your editor.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Select **Best sellers **under** Which products to display?**.
[image: Screenshot of the settings for slider galleries where you can choose to make the slider a Best Sellers gallery]
1. (Optional) Edit the default gallery name ("Best Sellers"):

1. Click the **Text** tab.
2. Enter a new name under **Gallery title text**.

>
> Learn how to [customize the gallery layout, design, and more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-product-galleries#adding-a-slider-gallery-selecting-a-category-to-display).
