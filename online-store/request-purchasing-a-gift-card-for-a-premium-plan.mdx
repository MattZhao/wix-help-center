> **Tip:**
>
> If you are looking to sell gift cards for your online store, you can add [Wix Gift Cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-wix-gift-cards-9451439).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
