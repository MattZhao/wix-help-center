---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Delivery to Pickup Points in Additional Countries"
---

With Wix Stores, you can offer customers delivery to [pickup points in a variety of countries via third-party apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-delivery-to-pickup-points). Currently, delivery to pickup points in additional countries is not currently supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
