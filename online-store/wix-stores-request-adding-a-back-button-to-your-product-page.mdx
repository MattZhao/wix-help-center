---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Adding a 'Back' Button to Your Product Page"
---

Currently, the Wix Stores Product Page does not have a Back button, which would lead your customers back to your main store page or the previous page they were on.

The Product Page does however, have breadcrumbs to make it easier for your customers to navigate through your store.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can add a back button using the [Back Button: Go Previous Page app](https://www.wix.com/app-market/back-button-go-previous-page?referral=search-result&appIndex=0&referralTag=Back%20Button:%20Go%20Previous%20Page&referralSectionName=search-result).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly  appreciated.
