---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Combining Weight-Based and Price-Based Shipping Rules"
---

Currently, it is not possible to combine shipping rules based on weight with shipping rules based on price.

In other words, you can set a rule for automatic free shipping for purchases over $30 and you can set a $5 shipping charge on products over 2 lb. However, it is not possible to combine these two rules so you can vary shipping by both price and weight at the same time.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly  appreciated.
