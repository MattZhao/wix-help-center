---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Using Facebook Pay in Your Facebook Shop"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to allow customers at your Facebook Shop to check out using the Facebook Pay payment method. To do so, connect your store to Facebook using the Facebook Shops by GoDataFeed third-party app.

Go to the App Market to [add the app](https://www.wix.com/app-market/facebook-shops-by-godatafeed).  Learn how to [create a Facebook shop using the Facebook Shops by GoDataFeed app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-facebook-shop-using-the-facebook-shops-by-godatafeed-app).
