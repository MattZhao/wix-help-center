---
title: "Adding a Note to an Order You Received"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, Wix Restaurants Orders (New) orders, and Wix Donations.

Keep track of special requests and instructions by adding notes to orders. These notes are personal and can only be seen by you and your [site collaborators](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview).

### To add a note to an order:

[Go to the Orders tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Select the order you want to add the note to.  Scroll down to **Order Activity**.  Enter your note in the **Add a note** field.  Click the checkmark to save.
