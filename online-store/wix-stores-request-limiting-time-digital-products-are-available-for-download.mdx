---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Limiting Time Digital Products are Available for Download"
---

Currently, when you sell a digital product, your customer has a month in which they can download the file. It is not possible to limit the time a product is available for download. It is also not possible to limit the number of times a customer can download the same product.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
