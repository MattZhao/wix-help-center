> **Important:**

> - The new Category Page is not yet available for all users. If you don't have it, [click to learn how to create and display product categories using shop pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-and-displaying-product-categories).
> - The Category Page is not available in Editor X.

The Category Page is your main store page. Here, customers can select the category they want to browse. Choose the elements you want to display on this page and customize it to get the design that reflects your store brand.

> **Customizing the page:**
>
> When you [customize the look and function](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-customizing-the-category-page) of this page once, it applies to all categories to give your store a unified look.

---

### The header

The header of the Category Page displays the image and description you added when you created the category. You can hide the category name, image, description, or the entire header.

---

### About the sidebar

The category sidebar has 2 components: the category list and filters. You can display one or both or hide the sidebar entirely.

You can choose which of your active categories to show on the sidebar and their order of appearance. Your store comes with some filter options built in (which you can display or hide), but you can also create custom filters using your categories.

> **Important:**
>
> The category list does not appear on mobile. [Adding your categories to the site menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-product-categories#displaying-categories-in-your-site-menu) is the best way to give your customers easy access to them.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about the Category Page.

### Can I display categories in my site menu instead of (or in addition to) the Category Page?

Yes. You have the freedom to organize your site in the way that works best for your business. 
**To add individual categories to your site menu:**
1. Click **Pages and Menu**on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Site Menu**.
3. Click the Link icon  at the bottom.** **
4. Select **Category Page** from the **Which Page? **drop-down.
5. Select the category you want to display from the **Which item?** drop-down.
6. Click **Done**.

**To remove the Category Page from your site menu:**
1. ** **Click **Pages and Menu**on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Site Menu**.
3. Click the More Actions icon  next to Shop.

**Tip: **This is the default name for the Category Page in the site menu.
4. Select **Hide**.

### Do customers on mobile see the sidebar with the category list?

No, the category list doesn't appear on mobile, even if you display it on the desktop version of your site. [image: Screenshot of the category page as viewed on a mobile device]To give customers easy access to all your categories, we suggest you [display your categories in your site's menu](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-displaying-product-categories#displaying-categories-in-your-site-menu).
**Tip: **If you set up your filters to display categories, customers can also use that option to access specific categories on mobile.

### How can I design the Category Page?

Customize the layout, colors, fonts and more to get the perfect look for your brand. 
**Tip: **You only need to customize the Category Page once. Your selections apply to each category. 
**To customize the Category Page:**
1. Click **Pages and Menu**on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Store Pages**.
3. Click **Category Page**.
4. Click the **Category Page** twice.
5. Click **Settings**.

Learn how to [customize the Category Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-customizing-the-category-page).
