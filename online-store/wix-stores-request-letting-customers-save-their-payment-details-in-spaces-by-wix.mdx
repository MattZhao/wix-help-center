---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Letting Customers Save their Payment Details in Spaces by Wix"
---

Currently, customers who make purchases through Spaces by Wix cannot save their credit card details. Their contact details, however, are saved.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
