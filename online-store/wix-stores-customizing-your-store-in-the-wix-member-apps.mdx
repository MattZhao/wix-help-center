Customers can engage with your store using one of the member apps, [Spaces by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview), [Fit by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/fit-by-wix-app-an-overview), [Dine by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/dine-by-wix-an-overview), or the [Branded App by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview-7870651). Use your site's dashboard, the Wix mobile app, or the Wix Studio app to customize what visitors see when they view your store in [your preferred app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-choosing-your-preferred-member-app).

### Adding a store page

By default, your mobile app includes a shop tab that displays all your products. After [creating new store categories](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-and-managing-categories-using-the-wix-owner-app), you can add new store screens (pages) to showcase them in separate tabs or add store elements to existing screens.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
3. Click **Screens **on the left.
4. Click **+ Add**.

**Tip: **Click **+ Add** next to **Screens** **not in navigation** to hide your screen while working on its content.
5. Enter a name to show in the navigation menu.
6. Click **Add Elements**.
7. Select **Store** under **Add Elements**.
8. Choose the element you want to add to your screen:

- **Product gallery: **Display products in a grid. Customers scroll down to view additional items.
- **Product carousel: **Display one row of products. Customers swipe left or right to browse additional items.
9. Edit the element on the right panel of your screen.
10. Click **Save & Publish**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage ** at the bottom.
3. Tap **Mobile App**.
4. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
5. Tap **Screens** at the bottom.
6. Tap **+** **Add New Screen**.
7. Add a title for your first product category under **Screen name **and tap **Save**.

**Tip: **Enable the **Hide screen from navigation** toggle to hide your screen while working on its content.
8. Tap **+ Add Element**.
9. Swipe the element categories at the top and select **Store**.
10. Select a product display option:

- **Product gallery: **Display products in a grid. Customers scroll down to view additional items.
- **Product carousel: **Display one row of products. Customers swipe left or right to browse additional items.
11. Select a category from the **Select Category** drop-down.
12. Tap **Add**.

1. Go to your site in the Wix Studio app.
2. Tap **Mobile App**.
3. Tap **Edit **under your site name or the name of your branded app.
4. Tap **Screens** at the bottom.
5. Tap **Add New Screen**.
6. Add a title for your first product category under **Screen name **and tap **Save**.
7. Tap **Add Element**.
8. Swipe the element categories at the top and select **Store**.
9. Select a product display option:

- **Product gallery: **Display products in a grid. Customers scroll down to view additional items.
- **Product carousel: **Display one row of products. Customers swipe left or right to browse additional items.
10. Select a category from the **Select Category** drop-down.
11. Tap **Add**.

### Customizing a product gallery or carousel

After you add a gallery or carousel, you can choose the product category to display, customize your image layout, and more.

DashboardWix appWix Studio app
1. [Go to Mobile App](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Mobile%20App&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/mobile-app?activeTab=free-app) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Edit Mobile App**.
3. Click the **Screen** drop-down at the top and choose the screen you want to edit.
4. Select the **Product gallery** or **Product carousel**.
5. Customize the element on the right panel of your screen:
Product gallery
1. Select a category to display from the **Select Category** drop-down.
2. Click the **Show filters** toggle to enable or disable product filters. If enabled, select the checkboxes next to the filters you want to show.
3. Choose a layout for the images in the gallery under** Layout settings**:

- **Crop: **Images are cropped to the same size.
- **Fit: **The entire image is displayed with its original aspect ratio.
4. Click the** Show title **toggle to show or hide the element title.
5. Choose the amount of padding you want for the element under **Margins**.
6. Click **General settings** and choose what devices to show the element in: **All**, **Android** or **iOS**.
7. Click **Save & Publish**.
Product carousel
1. Choose a category to display from the **Select Category** drop-down.
2. Choose a layout for the images in the carousel under** Layout settings**:

- **Crop: **Images are cropped to the same size.
- **Fit: **The entire image is displayed with its original aspect ratio.
3. Enter a title for the carousel element.
4. Choose the amount of padding you want for the element under **Margins**.
5. Click **General settings** and choose what devices to show the element in: **All**, **Android** or **iOS**.
6. Click **Save & Publish**.

7. Tap the **Location** drop-down to select a different screen to move the gallery to.

### FAQs

Click to learn more about customizing your store in Wix member apps.

### Do I need to make changes in my site's dashboard and in the Wix app or Wix Studio app?

No. The changes you make to the member apps from your site's dashboard are also applied in the Wix app and the Wix Studio app.

### How can I edit my personal profile?

You can edit your personal profile name and add an image. In addition, you can control whether members can see and interact with you. Learn how to [customize your personal profile](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-editing-your-profile).

### How can I invite customers to join my mobile app?

To engage with your business on Spaces by Wix, customers can become members. To invite customers to join, you can send an invite code. Learn how to [invite customers to join your app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-app-how-members-use-the-app#joining-your-site).

### How do I edit my site's design in the member apps?

When editing your site as it appears in the member apps, you can click **Design** at the bottom to adjust your site's app theme. Learn more about [changing the theme of your member app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-apps-customizing-your-site-on-the-member-apps#changing-the-theme-color-of-your-members-app).
