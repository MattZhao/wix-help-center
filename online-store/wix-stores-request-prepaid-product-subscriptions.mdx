---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Prepaid Product Subscriptions"
---

Currently, it is not possible for customers to pay the entire cost when they purchase a subscription. In other words, if a customer buys a monthly subscription that lasts a year, they need to make 12 monthly payments rather than one payment for the year.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
