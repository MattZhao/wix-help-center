---
title: "Setting How Shipping and Tax Displays in the Cart Page"
---

You can show your customers' shipping and tax charges, right on the Cart Page.

Customers see their location displayed in the cart and have the option of entering a different one, for example, if they are shipping to another destination.

Next, if you created more than one delivery option, customers can choose the one they prefer.

After making these selections, your customers see an updated total price, based on the location and delivery option selected.

If you prefer not to display shipping and tax in the Cart Page, you can hide either one.

> **Important:**
>
> Estimated taxes are not displayed if you have included tax in the price of your products. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/including-tax-in-the-product-price)

### Setting how shipping & tax displays in the Cart Page

Access to the Cart Page varies according to which business solution or solutions you use:

**Wix Stores: **The Cart Page is one of your Store pages.  **Wix Bookings:** The Cart Page is one of your Bookings pages.  **Wix Restaurants or any combination of solutions:** The Cart Page is listed under Cart & Thank You.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Cart page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Select an option:

- My site has only Wix Stores:

1. Click **Store Pages**.
2. Click **Cart Page**.
- My site has only Wix Bookings:

1. Click **Bookings Pages**.
2. Click **Cart Page**.
- My site has Wix Restaurants Orders (New) / 2 business solutions:

1. Click **Cart & Thank You**.
2. Click **Cart Page**.

1. Click the Cart Page element on your page.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click the **Settings **tab.
4. Select or deselect the **Shipping & Delivery Rates **checkbox.
5. Select or deselect the **Tax costs **checkbox.
