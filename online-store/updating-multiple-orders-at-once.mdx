---
title: "Updating Multiple Orders at Once"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New) orders.

Save time by performing changes on multiple orders at once. Start by selecting the orders you want to update, then choose the action you want to perform.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Work more efficiently by filtering orders and customizing the tabs in your list of orders. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-fulfilling-store-orders#filtering-customizing-the-store-orders-tab)
> - You can make certain updates to multiple orders at once using the Wix app. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-your-orders-from-the-wix-owner-app#updating-multiple-orders-at-once)

---

### To update multiple orders at once:

[Go to the Orders tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Select checkboxes next to the orders you want to update.   Click the action from the top bar:

- **Assign Tags: **Add or remove tags you created. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/assigning-tags-to-orders)
- **Export: **Click to download a .CSV file. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/exporting-orders-3126323)
- **Print Orders: **Send orders to your printer.
- **Print Shipping Label: **Create and print multiple labels through Wix Shipping. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-printing-shipping-labels-with-wix-shipping#purchasing-and-printing-multiple-labels-at-once)

**Note: **This option is not available in all regions.
    Click **More Actions** and select an action:

- **Mark as paid: **Mark the selected orders as "Paid".
- **Mark as fulfilled: **Mark the selected orders as "Fulfilled" (Relevant only for store orders).
- **Create Invoice: **An invoice is created and appears in the Invoices tab.
- **Archive: **Adds the selected orders to the archive.

> **Notes:**
>
> - You can create an invoice for up to 100 invoices at once.
> - You cannot add tracking numbers to multiple store orders at once.
> - Check out the full course in Wix Learn on building your store or skip to the video on [managing your orders](https://www.wix.com/learn/online-course/ecommerce/drive-traffic-to-your-online-store#manage-your-orders).
