---
title: "Wix Stores POS Request: Integrating with the Pricing Plans App"
---

Currently, it is not possible to integrate your mobile POS with the Pricing Plans app to sell membership packages on your phone. However, you can use the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview) on your phone to let clients purchase plans or to [manually sell plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-selling-plans-offline-using-the-wix-app) yourself.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
