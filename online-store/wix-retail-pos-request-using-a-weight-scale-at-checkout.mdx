title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Using a Weight Scale at Checkout"
---

Currently, it's not possible to weigh products at checkout using a scale that's integrated with the Wix Retail POS solution.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
