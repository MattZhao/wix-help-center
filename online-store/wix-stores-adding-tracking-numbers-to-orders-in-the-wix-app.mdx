---
title: "Wix Stores: Adding Tracking Numbers to Orders in the Wix App"
---

Add tracking numbers to your orders so both you and your customers can track the progress of their packages.

### To add a tracking number using the Wix app:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.   Tap **Orders**.  Tap the relevant order.  Scroll down and tap **Add Tracking Number**.

To add the barcode, select an option:

- **Scan barcode:** Tap the barcode symbol and scan the barcode.
- **Manually enter barcode: **Tap **Tracking Number**, enter the barcode, and tap save.

To add the shipping carrier, select an option:

- **Select an existing carrier: **

1. Tap **Shipping Carrier**.
2. Select a carrier from the list.
3. Tap **Apply**.
- **Add a new carrier:**

1. Tap **Shipping Carrier**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Add Carrier**.
3. Enter the carrier name and tap **Add**.
4. Tap **Apply**.
    (Optional) Tap the **Send Shipping Confirmation **toggle to disable it.  Click **Save**.
