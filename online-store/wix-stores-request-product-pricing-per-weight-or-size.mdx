---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Product Pricing per Weight or Size"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set a price per weight (e.g. $10 /pound) or a price per size (e.g $10 / yard).

In the meantime, as a workaround solution, you can create product options to [offer different weights or sizes at different prices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-customizing-product-options#setting-different-prices-for-variants).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
