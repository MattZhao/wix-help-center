---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Creating a Manual Order in the Wix App"
---

Currently, it's not possible to create a manual order in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview). It is, however, possible to [create a manual order on desktop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/manually-creating-an-order-8027377).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
