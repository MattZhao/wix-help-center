---
title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Creating Product SKUs and Barcodes"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create barcodes or SKUs automatically with Wix Retail POS.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can use third-party apps such as Barcode Man and Kodbar to automatically create barcodes. To get started, [go to the Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
