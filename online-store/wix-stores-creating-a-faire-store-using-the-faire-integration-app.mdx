### Adding the Faire Integration app and sign up

Head over to the Wix App Market to add the Faire Integration by Digital Products Lab app

Wix EditorStudio EditorDashboard
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Apps**  on the left side of the editor.
3. Search for the Faire Integration app in the search bar.
4. Click **Add to Site**.
5. Review permissions and click **Agree & Add**.
6. Select a plan from the pricing page.**

Tip: **Every plan comes with a 7-day free trial period.
