---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Removing the Shipping Option from Wix Stores"
---

Currently, the ability to disable all shipping and delivery options in Wix Stores is not supported. It is, however, possible to offer your customers [store pickup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-store-pickup-as-a-delivery-option) or [local delivery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-local-delivery) as the only options.

In addition, if your store products are only [digital goods](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-digital-product), they are delivered online and your customers do not see any shipping options during checkout.

You can also sell services using [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings) (e.g. for massages or exercise classes) or create events with [Wix Events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-events), neither of which have shipping.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
