> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about **Wix Mobile POS** for mobile phones.
> - For info on **Wix Retail POS **for brick and mortar stores, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/retail-pos-7046310).
> - This article is relevant for Wix Stores and Wix Bookings on mobile POS.

Wix offers 2 ways to create invoices for orders. You can automatically create an invoice for every paid sale. Alternatively, you can manually create one whenever it's needed. You can also accept payment for unpaid invoices at a mobile POS.

### Generate invoices automatically for every paid sale

If you need to generate invoices for all your sales, you can save time by enabling automatic invoicing. Note that invoices create this way always have a balance of $0.00 since the order has already been fully paid.

> **Important:**
>
> To send automatic invoices, you need your customer's email address. When you complete a mobile POS sale, make sure you email your customer a receipt.

Learn how to [automatically generate an invoice for every paid sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-checkout-page#enabling-automatic-invoicing) including POS orders.

---

### Manually create and manage invoices

You can create an invoice for any sale, whether your customer already paid or not. After you create an invoice on desktop, you can view and manage it using the Wix app. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-managing-your-invoices-in-the-wix-owner-app)

#### To manually create an invoice for a mobile POS sale:

Complete the POS sale.   [Go to the Orders tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant order.  Click **More Actions**.  Select **Create Invoice **and then **Create Invoice** again to confirm.

Choose how you want to send the invoice to your customer:

- Click **Send via Email**.
- Click **More Actions** and then click to copy a link or print the invoice.

---

### Accept payment for invoices

If you have set up a mobile POS, you can accept payment for invoices using your card reader or tap to pay. Learn more about [accepting payment for invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-invoices-managing-your-invoices-in-the-wix-owner-app#managing-individual-invoices).
