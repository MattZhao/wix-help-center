---
title: "Checking for Restricted Products & Services when Setting Up Your Payments"
---

> **Important:**
>
> Before signing up with any payment providers, please make sure to check your offerings against their list of prohibited products and services.

When [setting up your payment methods](https://support.wix.com/en/article/accepting-payments-an-overview), you need to indicate whether you sell / do not sell any restricted products or services:

Click **I'm not selling anything on the list** if you are not selling any items which may be restricted by your payment provider. Be sure to check with your provider's terms of service to be sure of what products they allow. For Wix Payments users, please check the [Wix Payments restricted and prohibited products and services list](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-prohibited-products-and-services).

If you click **I am selling products & services on the list, **the available payment provider options will be adjusted according to the chosen category.
