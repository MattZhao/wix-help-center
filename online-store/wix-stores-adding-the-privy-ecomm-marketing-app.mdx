### App features

The Privy eComm Marketing app has several features to help you increase your sales.

#### Build your email list

Privy offers 2 ways to grow your email list:

**Welcome Discount Display:** Ask site visitors to join your email list in exchange for discounts,

content, or to get exclusive updates. Learn how to [set up a welcome discount display](https://help.privy.com/article/article/35-create-a-welcome-discount-display/).  **Cart Savers:** Send a cart-saver pop-up that appears when visitors who started the checkout process are about to leave your site. Learn how to [set up cart savers](https://help.privy.com/article/article/34-create-a-cart-saver-display/).

#### Send newsletters

Use Privy to send [targeted emails](https://help.privy.com/article/article/38-use-custom-and-smart-segments-in-email/) to customers to keep them engaged with your store. You can target by parameters such as:

Region  Cart value  Customers' behavior on your site

Start by selecting a template and customizing it using the [newsletter email editor](https://help.privy.com/article/article/74-send-newsletter-emails/).

#### Add SMS marketing

Grow your cross-channel marketing by [adding SMS messaging](https://help.privy.com/article/article/85-send-a-broadcast-text/). Collect customers' phone numbers and ask for consent. Then, send text messages about abandoned carts and more.
