Store products  Appointments booked through Wix Bookings  Custom charges

### Setting up a card reader

> **Important:**
>
> Accepting payment using a card reader is currently available only for merchants in the US and Canada.

Before you can collect payment in person from clients using a card reader, you need to connect to Wix Payments and purchase the reader.

#### To set up payment using a card reader:

Wix Dashboard Card Reader works with the [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments) payment provider. Start by [connecting to Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-wix-payments-account).  Acquire the **BBPOS WisePOS E card reader**.

**Tip: **You can purchase the reader from the [Wix POS shop](https://www.wix.com/en-ca/pos/hardware/product-page/wisepos-desktop-card-reader) or anywhere they are sold.  Enable Wix Point of Sale in the dashboard:

1. [Go to Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/payments/) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Accept** or enable the toggle next to **Tap to Pay**.

### Selling using POS checkout

After setting up (or without any setup for cash payments), you can start selling your store products or accepting payments of custom amounts from clients.

#### To sell at an POS checkout:

[Go to POS Checkout](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20In-Person%20Checkout&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/pos-checkout) in your site's dashboard.  Add items to the cart:

- **Add store items:** 

1. Click the **Products **tab.
2. Click a product to add it to the cart.
3. If the product has options, select them and click **Add to Cart**.
- **Add an appointment: **

1. Click the **Services **tab.
2. Click an appointment to add it to the cart.
3. (Optional) Click the **Edit **icon next to the appointment and edit the location, staff, time, or price, and click **Save**.
- **Add a custom charge:**

1. Click the **Custom charge** tab.
2. Enter the amount you want to charge your client.
3. Enter a short description of what the charge is for.
4. Click **Add to Cart**.

(Optional) Assign a customer to the sale:

**Note: **You must add a customer for appointments to create an invoice for the order.

1. Click **Select Customer**.
2. Select an option:

- **Select a saved customer:** Search for the client and select them.** **
- **Create a new customer**: 

1. Click **+Add Contact**.
2. Enter the client's details.
3. Click **Save**.
    (Optional) Add a discount:

1. Click **Actions** and **Add discount:**

- **Percentage discount**:** **Enter the percentage and click the Percent icon.
- **Amount discount: **Enter the discount and click the Amount icon.
2. Click **Add Discount**.
    (Optional) Redeem a coupon:

1. Click **Actions** and **Redeem a coupon**.
2. Select a coupon and click **Apply**.
    (Optional) Edit the tax:

1. Click the tax amount.
2. Click **Don't charge tax for this sale / Charge tax for this sale** to turn tax collection on or off.
    Click **Charge**.  Select how you want to receive payment.

### Accept credit/debit card payment

1. Click **Card**.
2. Use the card reader to swipe, dip (insert) or tap the card.
3. Provide your customer with an invoice to serve as a receipt:

**Note: **You can create an invoice only for orders to which you added a customer.

1. [Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant paid order.
3. Click the **More Actions **drop-down.
4. Select **Create Invoice**.
5. Click **Create Invoice** to confirm.
6. Share, print, or email the invoice using the links at the top.

**Notes: **
- Before you can accept credit or debit card payment, you must connect to [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments), purchase a card reader, and set up the reader so it works with your computer dashboard. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-dashboard-card-reader-setting-up-the-dashboard-card-reader)
- If you didn't add your customer's name to the order and you want to provide a receipt, as a workaround you can print the order.

### Manually enter credit card details

**Important: **Entering credit card details manually is the least secure way of accepting card payments. We recommend accepting manual payment only from buyers you trust. Note that in case of fraud, it is the merchant who is liable. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/best-practices-for-accepting-card-payments#handling-offline-card-payments) 
**To manually enter credit card details:**
1. Click **Manual Card Entry**.
2. Enter the credit card details and click **Charge**.
3. Provide your customer with an invoice to serve as a receipt:

**Note: **You can create an invoice only for orders to which you added a customer.

1. [Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the relevant paid order.
3. Click the **More Actions **drop-down.
4. Select **Create Invoice**.
5. Click **Create Invoice** to confirm.
6. Share, print, or email the invoice using the links at the top.

**Tip: **If you didn't add your customer's name to the order and you want to provide a receipt, as a workaround you can print the order.

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I enable or disable tax collect for a specific sale?

Yes, you can click the tax amount to enable or disable it. When the tax is a specific amount, click the amount to remove tax collection from the sale. If you disabled tax and want to re-add it, just click the $0.00 tax amount to enable it.

### Which Wix Bookings services can't I sell at POS Checkout?

Currently, it's not possible to sell classes or courses. In addition, you cannot accept payment using a [membership or package created through Pricing Plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-memberships-and-packages).
