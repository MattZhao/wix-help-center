---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Change Product Type (Physical / Digital)"
---

Currently, if you created a physical product, you cannot change it to a digital product, or vice versa. You need to delete the product and re-create it using the correct product type.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
