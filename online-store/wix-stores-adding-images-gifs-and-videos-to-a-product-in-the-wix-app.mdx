Make your products come to life by adding good quality images, GIFs, and videos. You can use your mobile device to add then on the go.

### Show me how to access store products

1. Go to your site in the Wix app.
2. Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.
3. Tap **Store** **Products**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Store** **Products**.

### Adding media to a product

Great images make customers feel secure about what they're purchasing. Make sure you upload good quality images that show every aspect of your product.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can use the Wix app's image editing tools to add filters, adjust brightness, focus, and saturation, apply text and more. Learn how to [edit images](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-uploading-and-managing-your-media).

#### To add media:

Edit an existing product or [create a new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-product-in-the-wix-owner-app).  Tap **Add Product media**.  Choose what you want to do next:

### Take a photo

1. Tap **All Media**.
2. Tap the **Device** tab.
3. Select **Camera**.
4. Take the picture and tap the **Checkmark** icon .
5. Tap **Next**.
6. (Optional) Tap the** Plus** icon  to take an additional photo.

### Upload media

You can upload images, GIFs, or videos from various sources, including your site media, your phone's gallery, or Instagram images.

1. Tap **All Media **or **My Boards**.
2. Tap to select an image, GIF, or video.
3. Tap **Next**

---

### Reordering media

The first image you upload is set as your main image. It's the image customers see in the Product Gallery before they click to view a specific product. You can reorder your images and set another one as the main image.

#### To reorder your media:

Edit an existing product or [create a new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-product-in-the-wix-owner-app).  Tap **View All Media**.  Drag the media to reorder them.  Set an image as the main image:

1. Tap the relevant image.
2. Tap the **More Actions** icon .
3. Tap **Make Main**.

---

### Removing media

You can remove any media that you no longer need.

#### To remove media:

Edit an existing product or [create a new one](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-product-in-the-wix-owner-app).  Tap **View All Media**.  Tap **Edit**.  Select the checkbox to select the relevant media or tap **Select All**.  Tap **Delete**.  Tap **Delete **to confirm.
