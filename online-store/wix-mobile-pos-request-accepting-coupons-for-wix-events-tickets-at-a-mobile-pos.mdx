---
title: "Wix Mobile POS Request: Accepting Coupons for Wix Events Tickets at a Mobile POS"
---

Currently, when selling event tickets through Wix Events at a mobile POS, it's not possible to redeem coupons.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
