**How can I see that a backup shipping rate was used?**

There are two ways to check this. When you [preview the checkout process](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-previewing-the-checkout-process), you can see if a backup rate is used for a sale by the delivery method that appears.

A USPS sale that does not use a backup rate, displays the name of the particular service, such as First-Class Mail, during checkout (see image on left). However, when a backup rate is used, the carrier name (USPS) is displayed during checkout (see right).

In addition, you can click an order in the Orders tab to check the shipping amount and see if your backup shipping rate was used (in the example below, the backup shipping rate is $10).

Note that the shipping method is listed on the right of the order page.

### To enter a backup shipping rate:

Go to the [Shipping & fulfillment](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Shipping&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/shipping-and-tax/list?referralInfo=sidebar) tab in your site's dashboard.  Select an option:

- Create a new shipping region for the US and [set up shipping with USPS Calculated Rate](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-real-time-shipping-calculation-with-usps).
- Edit an existing US shipping region with USPS Calculated Rate.
    Scroll down to the **Backup Setting** section

  Enter a backup weight per product.

**Note: **This weight is used for calculations if you have added a product's weight. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-shipping-by-weight)  Enter a backup shipping rate.** **

> **Note:**
>
> This rate is applied per order, not per product.
