title: "Wix Stores: Migrating from Other eCommerce Platforms to Wix Stores"
---

The Cart2Cart app helps you migrate to Wix from over 80 eCommerce platforms so you can create your dream store. To check if your platform is supported, click [here](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/supported-carts).

You can add the Cart2Cart app to your Wix site for free, but Cart2Cart charges a fee to perform the migration. The exact price is determined by the platform you are transferring from, which elements you choose to migrate, what optional extra services you select and more. You can run a free demo, including migrating a limited number of elements to your Wix store, to see how the Cart2Cart app works.

During the migration process, Cart2Cart gives you a preview, where you see an exact price breakdown before you commit to any changes or pay any fees. In addition, you can click [here](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/migration-pricing) to get a cost estimate from Cart2Cart.

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Make sure you added Wix Stores to your site. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores)
> - To migrate store orders, you must first upgrade to a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) that allows you to accept payments.

### Step 2 |  Source and target cart setup

Throughout the migration process, the store you are migrating from is referred to as the source cart. The target cart is your Wix store. In this step you select both carts and provide the necessary permissions to access the source cart. For example, to migrate from Shopify, you need to provide an API Password.

> **Note:**
>
> This step is different for each source cart. Refer to the instructions on the right in the Cart2Cart migration screen.

### Show me how

1. Select a source cart (i.e. the platform from which you want to migrate).
2. Enter your store's URL (e.g. http://examplestore.yourplatform.com).
3. Enter the information needed to access your platform.
**Note: **Each platform requires different information. For example, Shopify requires an API Password. Follow the instructions on the right of the screen to complete this part of the process.
4. Leave Wix as the selected **Target Cart**.
5. Click **Choose Entities**.

---

### Step 3 | Select what to migrate and perform a demo migration

You can migrate (import) different types of data into your Wix store (data available varies by platform). Afterwords, select any available additional migration options.

#### To select migration options:

Select which of types of data (e.g. products, orders, and coupons) you want to migrate to Wix. 

**Note: **Imports are limited to 1,000 products and 1,000 orders.  (Optional) Select any of the additional options you see displayed (options vary depending on what you selected in step 1):

- Select the **Clear current data on Target Store before Migration** to delete data in your Wix store for the entities you are migrating.

**Important: This step is irreversible.**

E.g. if you import products and select this option, any products already in your Wix dashboard will be deleted. [Learn more](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/faq/61-additional-options/198-what-does-the-option-clear-current-data-on-target-store-before-migration-mean)
- Select the **Skip product thumbnail images migration **checkbox. [Watch video to learn more](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZpZV8gup9LQ)
- Select the **Change products quantity to 100 on Target Store ($29)**. [Learn More](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/faq/61-additional-options/259-what-does-additional-option-change-products-quantity-to-100-on-target-store-mean)
- Perform data mapping: (relevant when importing orders): Orders in each platform have different statuses. Make sure they are classified correctly in your Wix dashboard by matching terms. [Learn more](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/faq/10-general-questions/92-what-is-order-statuses-mapping)
    Click **Start Free Demo**.

Once you've selected options, Cart2Cart performs a demo migration. This gives you a chance to preview the migration and check that everything is ok before you make changes and pay Cart2Cart for the process.

### Step 4 | Check demo migration results and complete the migration

Now that the demo migration is complete, click **Check the Results** to view your data in your Wix dashboard.

[image: Screenshot of the Cart2Cart App page where you click the Check the Results button to view your data]

- Products can be found in the Wix [Store Products tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar).
- Orders can be found in the Wix [Orders tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders).
- Coupons can be found in the Wix [Coupons tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Coupons&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/coupons/).
    If you're satisfied with the result, select one of the insurance plans or click the skip migration insurance option. [Watch a Cart2Cart video on insurance options](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_tq_8nPa4M&feature=emb_logo)  When you're ready, review the migration price.

**Tip: **Click the **Menu** icons to see the price breakdown.

  Pay and complete the migration:

1. Click **Add Funds for Full Migration**.
2. Enter the required details.
3. Click **Buy now**.

When the migration is complete, you'll receive an email notification.
