---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Applying Multiple Coupons"
---

Currently, it is not possible for customers to apply more than one coupon at a time in Wix Stores.

It is, however, possible to [offer free shipping above a set price](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-free-shipping-above-a-set-price) along with another coupon.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
