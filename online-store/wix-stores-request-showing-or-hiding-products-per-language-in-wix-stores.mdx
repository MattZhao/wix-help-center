---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Showing or Hiding Products Per Language in Wix Stores"
---

Currently, it is not possible to show or hide products per language in Wix Stores. Setting a product to be shown or hidden applies to all language versions of your site.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
