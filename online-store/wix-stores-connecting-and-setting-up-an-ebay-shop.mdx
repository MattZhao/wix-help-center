---
title: "Wix Stores: Connecting and Setting Up an eBay Shop"
---

Expand your sales platform by connecting your Wix store to an eBay shop. Don't yet have one yet? You can create your new eBay shop while connecting to eBay, then sell your Wix Stores products there.

This article will walk you through the steps to connect your Wix store to eBay and then set your shipping, tax, payment settings, and more. After the initial setup, you can edit these settings.

---

### Step 1 | Connect your Wix store to eBay

Here you can connect to an existing eBay shop or create a new eBay shop and connect it to your Wix store.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [eBay tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Your%20eBay%20Shop%20&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/ebay) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Start Now**.
3. Select an eBay market from the drop-down.
4. Click **Continue & Sign in to eBay**.
5. Select an option:

- Sign in to an existing eBay account.
- Create a new eBay account:

1. Click **Create an account**.

[image: Screenshot of the eBay log in sign up page]
2. Complete the required steps to create an account.

### Step 2 | Set up your location

Each eBay product must have a location where the item ships from. This location is used to calculate shipping charges and delivery times.

> **Note:**
>
> After initial setup, every time you add a new product to your store, it is automatically assigned the default location. If some of your products ship from another address, can then edit those products and assign them  a different "ship from" location.

### Show me how

1. Enter your city and state/province.
2. Enter your zip code (postal code).
3. Select your country from the **Country **drop-down.
4. Click **Continue to Shipping**.

---

### Step 3 | Set up shipping preferences

In eBay, your "domestic" region is the region where your chosen eBay market (eBay.com, eBay.uk, etc.) is based. For example if your eBay market is in eBay.com, then the US is your domestic shipping region, even if your store is located outside of the U.S.

To sell on eBay, domestic shipping must be set up. Optionally, you can set up international shipping to whatever regions you choose. To do so, enable international shipping and, if relevant, exclude any countries you choose not to ship to. Additionally, you need to enter a handling time according to eBay's policies. To learn more about eBay shipping options, click [here](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options?id=4089).

### Show me how

1. Set your domestic shipping rate:

1. Click the domestic region.
2. Select an option from the **Select how shipping rates are calculated** drop-down:

- **Flat rate: ** 

1. Enter the flat rate or click the **Free Shipping** toggle to enable it.
2. (Optional) Click **+Add Shipping Service **to create an additional shipping option (e.g. Express Shipping).
- **Calculated **(not available for all eBay marketplaces): 

1. Shipping rates are calculated based on the customer's location.
2. (Optional) Click the **Exclude shipping destination** and select destination to block shipping from areas inside the domestic region.
3. (Optional) Click **+Add Shipping Service **to create an additional shipping option (e.g. Express Shipping).
2. Set your international shipping rate:

1. Click the International region.
2. Select an option from the **Select how shipping rates are calculated** drop-down:

- **Flat rate: ** 

1. Enter the flat rate or click the **Free Shipping** toggle to enable it.
2. (Optional) Click **+Add Shipping Service **to create an additional shipping option (e.g. Express Shipping).
- **Calculated **(not available for all eBay marketplaces): 

1. Shipping rates are calculated based on the customer's location.
2. (Optional) Click the **Exclude shipping destination** and select destination to block shipping from areas inside the domestic region.
3. (Optional) Click **+Add Shipping Service **to create an additional shipping option (e.g. Express Shipping).
3. Select your handling time (i.e. the number of business days it takes you to ship an order after you receive payment and it has cleared. [Learn more](https://www.ebay.com/pages/help/buy/contextual/domestic-handling-time.html)
4. Click **Continue to Payments**

---

### Step 4 | Set up payment

To receive payment for sales on eBay, you need to enter the email address associated with your PayPal account. If you do not have a PayPal account, click **Don't have a PayPal **account to set one up.

> **Note:**
>
> If you signed up for [eBay managed payments](https://pages.ebay.com/ebay-is-managing-payments/), you can move directly to the next step without setting up payment.

When you're done, click **Continue to Returns** to go to the next step.

---

### Step 5 | Set up your return policy

Decide who you want to accept returns from and what the return conditions are.

### Show me how

1. Click the **Accept Domestic Returns **toggle to enable it.

**Note: **This enables returns in the region your eBay market is based.

1. Select an option from the **Return Period **drop-down.

**Note: **This sets how long customers have to contact you after receiving their order.
2. Select an option from the **Refund Method **drop-down.
3. Select an option from the **Returns Are Paid By **drop-down.

**Note: **This sets who pays for shipping costs.
2. Click the **Accept International Returns **toggle to enable it:

1. Select an option from the **Return Period **drop-down.

**Note: **This sets how long customers have to contact you after receiving their order.
2. Select an option from the **Refund Method **drop-down.
3. Select an option from the **Returns Are Paid By **drop-down.

**Note:** This sets who pays for shipping costs.
3. Select **Continue to Tax**.

> **Find out more:**
>
> - To learn more about refunds and returns in eBay, click [here](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling#returns-refunds).
> - If you do not want to accept returns, leave the toggles disabled and click **Continue to Tax** to move to the next step.

---

### Step 6 | Set up tax

In eBay, you can either manage tax using the eBay tax table (recommended) or manage tax yourself.

> **Important:**
>
> Different tax rules apply in different regions. Consult with an accountant or with your local tax authority for specific information relevant for your store.

### Show me how

- **Use eBay tax table **(recommended): After selecting this option, click **Manage eBay sales taxes** to access the tax table.

**Note: **eBay collects tax automatically in some states, so you are not able to enter a tax rate. For other states, enter a tax rate in the field provided and click **Save** at the bottom of the table.
- **I'll manage taxes myself: **Select this option if you are not collecting tax or tax is included in your product pricing.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about connecting and setting up an eBay shop.

### Which products cannot be listed on eBay?

Digital and hidden Wix products cannot be listed on eBay and do not appear in the eBay tab in your site's dashboard.

### Do I need to upgrade my site to list products on eBay?

You can connect and set up your eBay shop without an upgrade, but you need a plan that allows you to accept payments to list your products on eBay.

### Do I need to set up an eBay Seller's Account

It's possible to set up your eBay Shop using an eBay Buyer's account, however, an eBay Seller's Account is required to publish items on eBay.

### How can I disconnect my eBay account from my Wix store?

1. [Go to the eBay tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Your%20eBay%20Shop%20&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/ebay) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the **Settings **icon  at the top right.
3. Click **Disconnect from Wix**.
