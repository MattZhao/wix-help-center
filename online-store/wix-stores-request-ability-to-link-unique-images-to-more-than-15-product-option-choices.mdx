---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Ability To Link Unique Images To More Than 15 Product Option Choices"
---

Currently, it is possible to link unique images to a maximum of 15 product option choices. For example, if a product comes in 16 different colors, you can link unique images to only 15 colors.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
