title: "Wix Stores: Sharing Products on Social Media"
---

Promote your products by sharing them on social media. You can also make it easy for customers to share their favorite products by displaying social share icons right on the Product Page.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Share products yourself on social media](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-sharing-products-on-social-media#sharing-products-yourself-on-social-media)  [Set which social share icons appear on the Product Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-sharing-products-on-social-media#showing-or-hiding-social-share-icons-on-the-product-page)  [Edit social images, titles, and descriptions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-sharing-products-on-social-media#editing-images-titles-and-descriptions)

---

### Sharing products yourself on social media

[Go to Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.   Select an existing product or click **New Product** to add a new one.  Click **Promote this product** on the right.

Create a [social post](https://support.wix.com/en/article/social-posts-creating-and-customizing-a-social-post) or share a link on social media.

### Showing or hiding social share icons on the Product Page

Displaying social share icons makes it easy for customers to share products they love. Select the ones you want to display.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Product page:

1. Click **Pages & Menu **  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Store Pages**.
3. Click **Product Page**.
3. Click the Product Page element on your page.
4. Click **Settings**.
5. Click the **Settings **tab.
6. Scroll down to the social media bar section and select an option:

- **Hide all social media icons:** Click the **Display share icons** toggle to disable it.
- **Show some or all social media icons:** 

1. Click the **Display share icons** toggle to enable it.
2. Deselect the checkboxes next to icons you do not want to display.

1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Go to your Product page:

1. Click **Pages**  on the left side of the editor.
2. Click your Product page under **Store Pages**.
3. Click the Product Page element on your page.
4. Click the **Elements **icon.

1. Scroll down to the **Share Buttons** section and select the checkmarks next to the social media platforms you want to display.

### Editing images, titles, and descriptions

You can control how a product looks when it's shared on social media by changing the image or editing the title or description. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-social-share-settings-for-your-sites-pages)

You can also [customize your page's Twitter Settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-twitter-settings-for-your-sites-pages) to display a different title, description, and image for shares on Twitter.

#### To edit images, titles and descriptions:

[Go to Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Store%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.   Click the product you want to customize.   Click **Edit SEO Settings** on the right.   Click the **Social Share** tab.   Edit the product image: 

1. Hover over the image and select an option:

- **Edit the existing image:**

1. Click the **Adjust** icon  to open the Photo Studio.
2. Edit the image and click **Save**.
- **Replace the image:**

1. Click the **Change **icon .
2. Select an image and click **Add to Page**.
    Edit the title: Enter your title in the** og:title** field. 

**Note: **By default, Wix uses your page's title tag.   Edit the description: Enter your description in the **og:description** field. 

**Note:** By default, Wix uses your product's meta description.  Click **Twitter Settings** to set a different image and text for Twitter shares:

1. Click the **Change **icon  to select a different image.
2. Choose a card size under **Select card size**.
3. Add a title under** twitter:title**.
4. Add a description under **twitter:description**.
    Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> In some cases, Facebook might show outdated information about your product. Learn how to [troubleshoot outdated info and images on Facebook](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-troubleshooting-outdated-info-and-thumbnail-on-facebook).
