> As of September 2021, we no longer support connecting 2Checkout as a new payment provider for your Wix site. If you've already set up 2Checkout for your site, you will still be able to accept payments through this payment provider.

Connect 2Checkout as a payment provider to accept credit card payments from your customers.

General Info
**Supported countries**Available in over 150 countries. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country) for complete list.

**Note: **The sign up form is in English only.**Transaction fees**From 3.5% + $0.35 per transaction. Fees vary by country. For more information, click [here](https://www.2checkout.com/pricing/).**Account activation time**A few days**Note**The currency used in your Wix account must match the currency in your 2CheckOut account. **If the currencies do not match, the transactions may be declined. **Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-your-currency-for-accepting-payments) to learn how to change your currency.

Whenever possible, Wix automatically connects you to the most suitable provider available in your region, based on your preferences and location. If you prefer to use 2Checkout, and its available in your location, follow the steps below:

> **Notes:**
>
> - Refunds performed in your 2Checkout account will not appear as refunded in Wix.
> - If you received 2Checkout as a recommended provider, click **Connect** and go straight to step 5 below. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-payment-provider) to learn more.

#### To connect 2Checkout as a provider:

Go to [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7b%7bmetaSiteId%7d%7d/payments/) on your site's dashboard.

**Tip:** Check your business location at the top right. If incorrect, click the drop-down menu to change it.  Click **See More Payment Options**.

3.  Click **See Options** beside **Third-Party** **Credit / Debit Card Providers**.

### I have a 2Checkout account

1. Enter the following values:

- **Merchant Code / Seller ID**
- **Secret Key**
- **Publishable Key**
- **Private Key

**
- **Buy link secret word **
2. Click **Connect**.

### I don't have a 2Checkout account

1. Click **Create an account**.
2. Fill in the form and click **Get Started**.
3. Activate your 2Checkout account: 

1. Review the email sent to you by 2Checkout.
2. Complete [this form](https://www.2checkout.com/va/home/) to activate your account.
4. Enable API integration in 2Checkout:

1. Login to your 2Checkout account.
2. Copy the Merchant Code number at the top right under your user name.
3. Click the API tab.
4. Check the **I agree to 2Checkout's Terms of Use for API payment processing **checkbox. This will reveal your Secret Key.
5. Click **Start Integrating**.
6. Under **Key Generator**, click **Generate** to receive your Publishable Key and Private Key.
5. Return to the [Accept Payments tab](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-the-accept-payments-tab) (repeat steps 1-5) and follow the steps under **I have a 2Checkout account**.

> 2Checkout have informed us that ﻿transactions made with Mastercard in Asia may fail.
