---
title: "Wix Promos Request: Limited Time Offer Promotions"
---

Currently, it is not possible to create limited time offer promotions, for example with a countdown clock, using Wix Promos.

As a workaround, you can add the [Wix Countdown Clock](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-countdown-clock-app) app to your site and create a countdown timer to promote product sales etc.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
