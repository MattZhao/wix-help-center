---
title: "Wix Mobile POS Request: Accepting Coupons at a Mobile POS"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible for clients to redeem coupons for the purchase of Wix Stores products or Wix Bookings services at a mobile POS.

> **Wix Events & Wix Pricing Plans:**
>
> It is not currently possible to accept coupons with Wix Events or Pricing Plans. You can vote for the ability to accept coupons with:
> - [Wix Events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-request-accepting-coupons-for-wix-events-tickets-at-a-mobile-pos)
> - [Pricing Plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-request-accepting-coupons-for-pricing-plans-sales-at-a-mobile-pos)
