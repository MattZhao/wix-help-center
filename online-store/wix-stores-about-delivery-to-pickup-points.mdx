title: "Wix Stores: About Delivery to Pickup Points"
---

Offer customers the convenience of collecting their orders from pickup points nearby.

During checkout, the customer enters their address. Then, when they select how they want their order shipped, they can see nearby pickup points.

---

### Setting up delivery to pickup points

Shipping to pickup points is offered through 3rd-party apps - [Boxtal](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-offering-delivery-to-pickup-points-with-boxtal) or [Sendcloud](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-offering-delivery-to-pickup-points-with-sendcloud). After adding one of these apps and integrating it with your store, you can configure and enable it in relevant locations.

Then, when you receive an order, you send it to one of the relevant carriers' pickup points and they'll make sure it gets to the location your customer selected.
