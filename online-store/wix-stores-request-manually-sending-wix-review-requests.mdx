---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Manually Sending Wix Review Requests"
---

Currently, if you're using Wix Reviews, you can [send an automated message asking customers to review the product they purchased](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-wix-reviews#step-3-optional-customize-review-requests). It is not possible to manually send requests.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
