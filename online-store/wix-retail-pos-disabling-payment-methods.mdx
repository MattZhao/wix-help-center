### Disabling payment methods

You can set which payment options are displayed at checkout from the Payments tab.

#### To disable a payment option:

Open the POS tablet.  Tap the Menu icon  at the bottom left.  Tap the **Settings** tab.  Tap the **Payment** tab.   Tap the toggle next to any payment methods to disable them.

---

### FAQs

### How can I enable a payment method I disabled?

Follow the same instructions you used to disable the payment method, but click the toggles to enable them.

### What is the "Other" payment method?

The "Other" payment method allows you to receive payment through a method which is not integrated with your POS system. For example, you can use it to accept payment through an app or a gift card which isn't integrated with the POS system.

### Which gift cards can I accept with the Gift Card method

Currently, it's possible to accept payment through Wix Gift Cards and cards created through the Gifted app. Learn more about [accepting payment through gift cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-selling-virtual-gift-cards).  
**Note: **You can, however, use the "Other" payment method to accept payment through a gift card which isn't integrated with the POS system.

### Why can't I enable the "other" payment method?

If you purchased your Retail POS system after October 25, 2023 and before April 2024, you may need an upgrade to enable the "other" payment method. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-the-retail-pos-pro-plan-7439477)
