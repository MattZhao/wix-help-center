---
title: "Request: Allowing Customers to Save Credit Card Details (Square, 2CheckOut, PayU)"
---

Currently, most of our payment providers allow your customers to save their credit card details for future payments. This option is not available to customers if you’re using:

[Square](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-square-as-a-payment-provider)  [2CheckOut](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-2checkout-as-a-payment-provider)  [PayU](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-payu-turkey-as-a-payment-provider)

You can [change your payment provider](https://support.wix.com/en/article/changing-your-payment-provider-for-accepting-credit-card-payments) to one that does support this option. See what payment providers [are available in your region](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
