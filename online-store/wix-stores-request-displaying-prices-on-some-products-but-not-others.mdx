---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Displaying Prices on Some Products but Not Others"
---

Currently, you can either display product prices on all products in the [Product Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-product-galleries#customizing-a-grid-gallery) and [Product Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-customizing-your-product-page#customizing-display-settings) or hide product prices altogether. It's not possible to display prices on some products but not on others.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
