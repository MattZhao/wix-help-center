---
title: "Wix Stores: Adding and Setting Up a Currency Converter"
---

Make it easy for customers to view product prices in a familiar currency by adding a currency converter to your store.

> **Notes:**
>
> - The currency converter is available with the Business or the Business Elite [premium plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan).
> - Currency rates are provided by XE. By clicking "Add Currencies" you agree to XE's [Terms of Use](https://www.xe.com/de/legal/dfs.php).

### To add and set up a currency converter:

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Add Elements **on the left side of the editor.
3. Click **Store.**
4. Click **Currency Converter**.
5. Drag the converter onto your page.
6. Click the currency converter in the Editor.
7. Click **Settings**.
8. Add the currencies you want to display:

1. Click the **Currencies** tab.
2. Click **+Add Currency**.
3. Select the checkbox next to the relevant currencies.
4. Click **Save**.
9. Click **Design** and customize the converter's design settings:

1. Select a background color and adjust the opacity.
2. Select a border color and adjust the opacity and width.
3. Select the text font and color.
