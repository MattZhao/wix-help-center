title: "Wix Stores: Migrating from Shopify to Wix Stores"
---

Interested in migrating from Shopify to Wix Stores? Welcome on board! This article will show you how to migrate using the Cart2Cart app. This app allows you to migrate products, orders, and coupons from your Shopify store to Wix.

You can add the Cart2Cart app to your Wix site for free, but Cart2Cart charges a fee to perform the migration. The exact price is determined by which elements you choose to migrate, what optional extra services you select, and more.

> **Advanced method:**
>
> Alternatively, as a free DIY option, you can import products directly through a CSV file. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-importing-products-to-wix-stores)

During the migration process, Cart2Cart offers you a preview, so you see an exact price breakdown before you commit to any changes or pay any fees. [Click to get a cost estimate from Cart2Cart](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/migration-pricing).

> **Before you begin:**
>
> - Make sure to add Wix Stores to your site. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores)
> - To migrate store orders, you must first upgrade to a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) or [Studio plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-upgrading-sites) that allows you to accept payments.

### Step 2 | Source and Target Cart Setup

In this step you select your source cart (Shopify) and provide the necessary permissions to access it.

Select **Shopify **from the **Source Cart** drop-down.  Enter your store's URL (e.g. http://your-store-name.com).  Get your Shopify API password:

1. Log in to Shopify.
2. Click the **Apps** tab on the left.
3. Click **Manage private apps**.

[image: Screenshot of the Cart2Cart setting in Wix  with the Manage private apps link outlined]
4. Click **Create a New Private App**.
5. Enter **Cart2Cart** in the **Private app name** field.
6. Enter the email of a developer or the person most suited to deal with technical issues in the **Emergency developer email** field.
7. Set your permissions to **Read Access**.

[image: Screenshot of the Cart2Cart settings in Wix where you select permissions]
8. Scroll down and click **Save**.
9. Copy your API password and return to Cart2Cart.
    Paste the API credentials in the **API Password **field.

 [image: Screenshot of the Cart2Cart setting in Wix where you enter the API password]  Select Wix from the **Target Cart **drop-down.  Click **Choose Entities**.

### Step 3 | Select What to Migrate and Perform a Demo Migration

You can migrate (import) different types of data into your Wix store. Afterwords, select any available additional migration options.

#### To select migration options:

Select which of types of data (e.g. products, orders, and coupons) you want to migrate to Wix. 
**Note: **Imports are limited to 1,000 products and 1,000 orders.(Optional) Select any of the additional options you see displayed (options vary depending on what you selected in step 1):
- Select the **Clear current data on Target Store before Migration** to delete data in your Wix store for the entities you are migrating.
**Important: This step is irreversible.**
 E.g. if you import products and select this option, any products already in your Wix dashboard will be deleted. [Learn More](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/faq/61-additional-options/198-what-does-the-option-clear-current-data-on-target-store-before-migration-mean)
- Select the **Skip product thumbnail images migration **checkbox. [Watch video to learn more](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZpZV8gup9LQ)
- Select the **Change products quantity to 100 on Target Store ($29)**. [Learn More](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/faq/61-additional-options/259-what-does-additional-option-change-products-quantity-to-100-on-target-store-mean)
- Perform data mapping: (relevant when importing orders): Orders in each platform have different statuses. Make sure they are classified correctly in your Wix dashboard by matching terms. [Learn More](https://www.shopping-cart-migration.com/faq/10-general-questions/92-what-is-order-statuses-mapping)
Click **Start Free Demo**.

Cart2Cart now performs a demo migration. This gives you a chance to preview the migration and check that everything is ok before you make changes and pay Cart2Cart for the process.

### Step 4 | Check Demo Migration Results and Complete the Migration

Now that the demo migration is complete, click **Check the Results** to view your data in your Wix dashboard.

[image: Screenshot of the Cart2Cart setting in Wix where you can check the results of your migration]

- Products can be found in the Wix [Store Products tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar).
- Orders can be found in the Wix [Orders tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders).
- Coupons can be found in the Wix [Coupons tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Coupons&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/coupons/).
    When you're satisfied with the result, select one of the insurance plans or click the Skip Migration Insurance option. [Watch a Cart2Cart video on insurance options](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_tq_8nPa4M&feature=emb_logo)  When you're ready, review the migration price.

**Tip: **Click the **Menu** icons  to see the price breakdown.

[image: Screenshot of the Cart2Cart setting in Wix where you can review the migration price]  Pay and complete the migration:

1. Click **Add Funds for Full Migration**.
2. Enter the required details.
3. Click **Buy now**.

When the migration is complete, you'll receive an email notification.
