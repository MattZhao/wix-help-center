title: "Wix Retail POS: Receiving Notifications"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about **Wix Retail POS** tablet for brick & mortar businesses.
> - For info on using **POS on a mobile device**, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/mobile-pos-4564663).

The Wix Retail POS system gives you the updates you need right to your tablet.

Just click the Notifications icon  at the bottom right of your screen to view notifications. You'll get a notification when there's a software update, hardware is disconnected, when a store order should be ready for [store pickup](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-store-pickup-as-a-delivery-option), and more.
