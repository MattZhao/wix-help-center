title: "Wix Retail POS: Checking the Status of Your Hardware"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about **Wix Retail POS** tablet for brick & mortar businesses.
> - For info on using **POS on a mobile device**, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/mobile-pos-4564663).

You can check the status of your POS hardware directly from the tablet and see if anything needs fixing. Make sure that your card reader, barcode scanner and receipt printer are all connected and good to go.

> **Note:**
>
> The cash drawer connects to the tablet through the receipt printer and doesn't have it's own connection status.

### To check your hardware status:

Open the POS tablet.  Tap the Menu icon  at the bottom left.   Tap **Settings**.  Scroll down and tap **Manage Hardware**.  Check the status listed under each device.
