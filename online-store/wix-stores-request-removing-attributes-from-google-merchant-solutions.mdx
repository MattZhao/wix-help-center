title: "Wix Stores Request: Removing Attributes from Google Merchant Solutions"
---

Currently, Google Merchant Solutions have a set number of product attributes (e.g. color). It's not possible to hide any that aren't relevant for your business.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
