---
title: "Wix Stores: Importing Product Files Containing Non-Latin Characters"
---

Product files with data containing non-Latin characters (e.g. data written in Japanese, Hindi, Arabic), may not upload correctly. To avoid this problem, save your product file using Google Sheets.

### To save a CSV file using Google Sheets:

Upload your CSV file to [Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets). [Learn How](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/9331167?hl=en)  Click **File**, then select **Download**.  Select **Comma-separated values**. 

**Note:** Do not open the file.

Import your products following [these instructions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-importing-products-to-wix-stores).
