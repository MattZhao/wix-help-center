---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Conditional Product Options"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set up conditional product options in order to sell certain option combinations.

> **What are conditional options?**
>
> Conditional product options are used to sell option combinations, for example, to sell small t-shirts only in blue and large t-shirts only in yellow. When a customer selects the first option (e.g. size small) they can only select the color blue.

As a workaround solution, you can create separate products for each set of options. For example, the small, blue t-shirt is one product and the large, yellow t-shirt is a separate product.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
