---
title: "Wix Stores: Featuring a Single Product"
---

Shine a spotlight on a new product or showcase an existing one that you want to promote. Wix Stores gives you the freedom to feature products in different ways. Choose the one that works best for your store.

---

### Create a landing page for a product

Landing pages are the perfect way for customers to get detailed information about your product and boost that product's SEO. If you use Google Ads, it's a great place to direct your clicks.

Use all of the powerful design capabilities in your Wix editor to build the perfect page and then add one or more [Add to Cart Buttons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-an-add-to-cart-button), so customers can easily purchase the product.

---

### Add a product widget

You can [use a product widget](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-the-product-widget) to showcase a product on any of your site pages. Customize the button so it directs to the Product Page or adds the product straight to the cart.

---

### Create blog posts with product links

A blog is a great way to keep site visitors engaged. While [writing blog posts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-writing-posts-in-wix-blog), you can add a link to a relevant product.

To add a product link while writing a blog post, click **Add** on the left menu and then scroll down and select **Product**.

---

### Add an Add to Cart button to a site page

Use your creativity to create just the content you need. Then, [add one or more Add to Cart buttons](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-an-add-to-cart-button) to your design to make it easy for customers to purchase the products related to your content.

> **Tips:**
>
> - Make sure to [set up the Add to Cart button](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-an-add-to-cart-button) correctly so that it adds the relevant product to the cart.
> - You can use the [Wix Pro Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pro-gallery-an-overview), [repeaters](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-managing-repeaters-lists-grids), [hover boxes](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-hover-box), and more to create effective content.
