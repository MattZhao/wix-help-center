Set up flat rate shipping to charge the same rate for all purchases delivered to a certain region.

---

### Step 1 | Create or Select a Shipping Region

To start, create the region the shipping rule applies to.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you already created the shipping region, just select it and move on to step 2.

A region can be made of one or more countries. Alternatively, it can be made of one or more regions within a country. You can set up different shipping regions for your store. For example, a merchant might set up 4 shipping rules: a local shipping region for California, a region for the rest of the US, another for Mexico and Canada, and finally one for the rest of the world.

> **Important:**
>
> - If there is no rule for a region, customers from that region cannot make store purchases.
> - If you previously set up shipping (and tax) in a merchant account (e.g. in PayPal), remove these settings before starting.
> - Can't find a shipping region you need? [Contact us](https://www.wix.com/support/conversations/category/contact) to let us know which region is missing.

### Show me how

1. Go to the [Shipping & fulfillment](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Shipping&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/shipping-and-tax/list?referralInfo=sidebar) settings in your site's dashboard.
2. Select an option:

- Edit a default rule: Click the region you want to set up.
- Create a new rule:

1. Click **Add Region**.
2. Click **+Add destination**, start typing a country name and select it.
3. (Optional) Select specific region(s) within a country (Not all countries have local regions):

1. Click **Edit** next to the region number.
2. Deselect the checkboxes next to the regions or states you don't want the rule to apply to and click **Done**.
4. (Optional) Click the title (e.g. Region 2) and rename it (e.g England & Scotland).
5. Click **Done** or move directly on to the next step.

---

### Step 2 | Set Up a Flat Rate Shipping Rule

Now that you've selected a region, it's time to set up the Flat Rate rule for that region.

### Show me how

1. Select **Flat Rate **from the **Select how shipping is calculated **drop-down.
2. Enter a name in the **Shipping Option Name** field (e.g. Standard Shipping).
3. (Optional) Enter a time in the **Est. Delivery Time** field.
4. Enter the shipping rate.
5. (Optional) Offer free shipping on orders above a specific price:

1. Click the **Offer Free Shipping when customer buys over a certain amount **checkbox and enter the amount.

**Note: **If you create additional shipping services (e.g. priority shipping), this checkbox must be selected and the amount specified to apply to each rule.
6. Click **Add another Delivery Option** to add an additional options (e.g. Express Shipping).
7. (Optional) Check the **Add a handling fee to every order **checkbox and enter the handling fee.
8. Click **Save**.

---

### Step 3 | Offer Local Delivery or Pickup options

After saving your shipping rule, you can select the region again and add local delivery or pickup options.
