---
title: "Wix Stores: Duplicating a Product Using the Wix App"
---

Save time when creating new products by duplicating existing ones and then editing them right in the Wix mobile app.

### To duplicate a product:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Store** **Products**,** **or tap **Catalog **and select **Store** **Products**.  Tap the product you want to duplicate.  Tap the **More Actions **icon  at the top of the screen.  Select **Duplicate**.  Edit the product name and any other details you need to change.   Tap **Save**.
