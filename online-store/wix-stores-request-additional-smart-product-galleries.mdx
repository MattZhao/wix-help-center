---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Additional Smart Product Galleries"
---

Currently, you can add a smart [Related Products gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-related-products-or-best-sellers-gallery) and [Best Sellers gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-best-sellers-gallery), both of which automatically display products based on store data. Other types of smart product galleries, such as New Arrivals, are not supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
