---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Creating a New Amazon Listing in Wix"
---

Currently, you can match a listing you created in Amazon to a product you created in Wix to manage them together. It's not possible to create a brand new Amazon listing from a Wix product, or create a Wix product from an Amazon listing.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
