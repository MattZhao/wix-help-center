---
title: "Wix Stores: Changing Your Currency"
---

You can view and change your store's currency from the Language & Region page in the dashboard. The currency you select will be used to display product prices in your store as well as for any other paid transactions connected to your site.

### To change your currency:

[Go to Language & Region](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Regional%20Settings&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fsettings%2Flanguage-and-region%2F) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to the **Currency** section and select a currency from the drop-down.

> **Notes:**
>
> - If the currency you want does not appear in the drop-down, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-request-additional-currencies) to request additional currencies.
> - You can [add a currency converter](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-a-currency-converter) so customers can view prices in their own currency.
