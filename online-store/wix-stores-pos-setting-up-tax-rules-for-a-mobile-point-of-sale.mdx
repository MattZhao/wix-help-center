title: "Wix Stores POS: Setting up Tax Rules for a Mobile Point of Sale"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about **Wix Mobile POS** for mobile phones.
> - For info on **Wix Retail POS **for brick and mortar stores, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/retail-pos-7046310).

When selling online, the tax rate is usually determined by your customer's location. However, for a POS transaction, it's determined by the business location you entered in the Business Info page in your site's dashboard.

### To set up tax collection for a mobile POS:

[Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).  Click **Settings**.  Click **Business Info **and [enter your business address in the Business Info page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-your-basic-site-info).

**Note: **Make sure your address includes a zip code / postal code.  Click **Tax** and [set up a tax rule](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tax-collection-an-overview) for your region.

**Note: **If no tax rule is set up, POS transactions are taxed at 0%.
