---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Adding TikTok as a Sales Channel"
---

Currently, it is not possible to connect your Wix store with TikTok and sell your products there.

> **Update:**
>
> It's now possible to connect to a TikTok Shop using the [TikTok Shop third-party app](https://www.wix.com/app-market/feed-for-tiktok-shop).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
