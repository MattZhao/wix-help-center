---
title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Attributing Tips to Staff Providing a Service"
---

Currently, by default, all tips are attributed to the cashier processing the sale, not the staff member providing the service or the salesperson that assisted the customer.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can [add a service provider to your POS team](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-your-stores-staff#adding-staff-to-your-point-of-sale). The cashier accepting payment can then [attribute the sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-your-stores-staff#attributing-a-sale-to-a-staff-member) to the relevant team member so that the tips are directed to them.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
