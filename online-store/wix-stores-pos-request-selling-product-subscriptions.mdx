title: "Wix Stores POS Request: Selling Product Subscriptions"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sell product subscriptions on the Wix mobile point of sale (POS) via mobile phone. However, your customers can purchase product subscriptions from your site. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-selling-product-subscriptions)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
