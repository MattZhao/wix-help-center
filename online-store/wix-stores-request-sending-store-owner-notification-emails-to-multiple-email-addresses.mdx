---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Sending Store Owner Notification Emails to Multiple Email Addresses"
---

Currently, store owner email notifications can only be sent to one email address.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [change the email address in your Business Info settings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-your-basic-site-info#business-info).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
