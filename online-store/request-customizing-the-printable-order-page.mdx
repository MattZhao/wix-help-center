---
title: "Request: Customizing the Printable Order Page"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, when you print an order in the Orders tab in the dashboard, it's not possible to customize the page.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is, however, possible to [export your orders to a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/exporting-orders-3126323). When you do so, you can select the information you want to display in the file.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
