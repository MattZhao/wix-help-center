---
title: "Using Meta Pixel with Your Wix Stores"
---

Wix automatically sends events to Facebook about your [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores) products on your site.

### To start using Facebook with Wix Stores:

Set up your Facebook advertiser account.   Connect your Meta pixel to your site. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-facebook-pixel-and-the-conversions-api-to-your-wix-site-7351813)

### Show me how

1. Go to [Marketing Integrations](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmarketing-integration%3FreferralInfo%3Dmarketing_integrations_article_link&title=Marketing%20Integrations&buttonText=Add%20Integrations).
2. Click Connect under **Meta Pixel & CAPI**.
3. Click **Connect to Facebook**.
4. (If required) Choose your profile in the pop-up and sign in.
5. Click **Continue**.
6. Click **Continue **to grant the necessary permissions to Wix Marketing Tools.

3.  Paste your thank you page URL into your Facebook Ads Manager by following the instructions in the Custom Conversions section of [this Meta article](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/780705975381000?id=1205376682832142).

---

The events you see in your Facebook account are:

**View Content: **When a visitor views a product page.**Initiate Checkout**: When a visitor clicks on the "Buy Now" button.**Add Payment Info**: After the visitor added his payment information.**Purchase: **When a visitor purchases a product and is taken to the "Thank-you" page.

### To see the event data in Facebook:

Sign into your Facebook account. Click **Ads Manager**, located in the top left corner. Click **Events Manager**.

Learn about creating [custom audiences](https://www.facebook.com/business/a/online-sales/custom-audiences-website) and [custom conversions](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/780705975381000) in Facebook.
