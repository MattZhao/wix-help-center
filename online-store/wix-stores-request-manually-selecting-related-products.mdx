---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Manually Selecting Related Products"
---

Currently, when you display a [Related Products Gallery](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-a-related-products-or-best-sellers-gallery), you can choose whether it shows your customers other products from the same category, products frequently bought together (based on data), or frequently viewed together (based on data). It is not possible to hand pick which products are related to each store item.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
