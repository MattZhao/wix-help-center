---
title: "Request: Hiding Installment Payment (BNPL) Price Breakdown on Your Product Page"
---

Currently, there is no way to hide / disable the price breakdown for installment payments that shows on your Product Page when using a payment provider that allows installment payments.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
