---
title: "Payment Forms Fail to Proceed to Checkout upon Submission (Includes Workaround)"
---

We're aware of the issue and are currently gathering more information regarding this matter.

> **Meanwhile, you can use the following workaround:**
>
> 1. In your site dashboard, navigate to the Settings page2. Click Language & Region3. Under Currency, select a different option4. Click Save.5. Reselect the original currency, then click Save again.
