Stay on top of staff management by having your team members track their hours working on the POS tablet.

After enabling time tracking, team members can clock in and out whenever they start or stop working. You can then view the team members' timecards, make changes to them, or email or print the information.

### Enabling shift management in the Wix Point of Sale

> **Important:**
>
> If you purchased your Retail POS system after October 25, 2023 and before April 2024, you may need an upgrade to enable shift management. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-the-retail-pos-pro-plan-7439477)

To start tracking working hours, you need to enable time tracking in the team settings.

#### To enable time tracking:

Open the POS tablet.  Tap the Menu icon  at the bottom left.  Tap **Settings**.  Tap **Roles & Permission** from the left menu.  Tap the **Time Tracking** toggle to enable it.

---

### Clocking in and out

Once time tracking is enabled, employees can clock in anytime they start working and clock out when they stop.

#### To clock in and out:

Open the POS tablet.  Have the employee tap to select themselves from the staff list.  Tap the lock screen.

**Tip: **If the lock screen isn't visible, tap **Lock Screen **at the bottom right.  Employee taps **Clock in** the top right and enters their passcode to start the clock.  Repeat steps 1 -3 then tap **Clock out** to stop the clock.

### Editing timecards

If you need to manually make a change to your timecards, you can do so on your computer. There, you can edit an existing timecard, delete it, or add a new one.

#### To edit timecards:

[Go to Point of Sale](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20POS&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/pos) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down and select the relevant team member.  Choose what you want to do next:

### Edit an existing session

1. Click **Edit** next to the session you want to edit.

[image: Screenshot of POS tab in the computer dashboard showing the Edit button in the timecard section]
2. Edit the session details.
3. Click **Save**.

### Add a new session

1. Click **Add Session **next to the session you want to edit.

[image: Screenshot of POS tab in the computer dashboard showing the Add Session button in the timecard section]
2. Enter the details of the new session.

**Note:** A session can't be more than 24 hours.
3. Click **Save**.

### Delete a session

1. Click the More Actions icon   next to the session you want to delete.

[image: Screenshot of POS tab in the computer dashboard showing the Delete button in the timecard section]
2. Click **Delete Session**.
3. Click **Delete Session **on the bottom left.
4. Click **Delete** to confirm.

### View, email or print team member timecards

You can view timecards reports in the Team Timecards section and email or print the card so you'll have them handy anytime.

#### To email or print timecard reports:

Open the POS tablet.  Tap the Menu icon  at the bottom left.  Tap **Reports**.  Select **Timecard Report**.

**Note: **On this screen you can see the total number of hours each employees worked during the chosen period.  (Optional) Tap **Email** or** Print **at the top right to email or print a time report for all employees for a set time period.   Tap **View Timecard **next to the staff member whose timecard you want to view.  (Optional) Tap **Email **or** Print** at the top right to email or print a time report the selected employee.
