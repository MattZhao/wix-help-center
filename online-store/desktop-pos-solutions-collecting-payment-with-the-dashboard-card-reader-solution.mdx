title: "Desktop POS Solutions: Collecting Payment with the Dashboard Card Reader Solution"
---

After you [set up the Dashboard Card Reader](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-dashboard-card-reader-setting-up-the-dashboard-card-reader), you can accept payment from customers through the **Collect Payment **button. You'll find a **Collect Payment **button in various places in your site's dashboard.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Accept payment from the Orders tab](https://support.wix.com/en/article/desktop-pos-solutions-collecting-payment-with-the-dashboard-card-reader-solution#accepting-payment-from-the-orders-tab)  [Accept payment from the Bookings calendar](https://support.wix.com/en/article/desktop-pos-solutions-collecting-payment-with-the-dashboard-card-reader-solution#accepting-payment-from-the-bookings-calendar)  [Accept payments from the Contact List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/desktop-pos-solutions-collecting-payment-with-the-dashboard-card-reader-solution#accepting-payment-from-the-contact-list)  [Accept payment while creating a custom order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/desktop-pos-solutions-collecting-payment-with-the-dashboard-card-reader-solution#accepting-payment-from-a-custom-order)

---

### Accepting payment from the Orders tab

Sales made through the following apps appear in the Orders tab in your Wix dashboard:

Wix Stores  Wix Bookings  Wix Restaurants Orders (New)

When you accept payment from the Orders tab, you have the option to add a tip to the order.

#### To collect payment:

[Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant unpaid order.  (Optional) Add a fee, tip, or discount to the order:

1. Click **More Actions**.
2. Select **Edit Order**.
3. Select an option:

- **Fee or tip: **Click **Add Fees & Tips**, enter the details, and click **Add**.
- **Discount:** Click **Add Discount**, enter the details, and click **Add**.
    Click **Collect Payment**.

Select **Charge with card reader**.

Have your customer tap, swipe, or insert their card.

### Accepting payment from the Bookings Calendar

While you're managing your scheduling in the Bookings Calendar, you can also access unpaid bookings and collect payments there.

#### To collect payment:

[Go to Booking Calendar](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Open%20Business%20Calendar&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/bookings/scheduler/owner/calendar?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant unpaid service: 

- **Appointment:** Click the relevant appointment in the calendar, and then click **Collect Payment**.

[image: Screenshot of a Bookings appointment with the Collect Payment button outlined]
- **Class or course: **Select the relevant service, click **Manage**, then click **Collect Payment **next to the relevant participant.

[image: Screenshot of a Bookings class service focusing on the Collect Payment button next to a participant]

Select **Charge with card reader**.  Have your customer tap, swipe, or insert their card.

### Accepting payment from the Contact List

Your site's [Contact List](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-your-contact-list) is where you'll find the name of any client who interacted with your site. From there you can collect payment for any unpaid Wix Bookings service.

#### To collect payment:

[Go to Contacts](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Contacts&title=Select%20a%20site%20to%20update&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fmy-account%2Fsites%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fcontacts) in your site's dashboard.  Hover over the relevant contact and click **View**.  Click the **Bookings** tab.  Click **Collect Payment** next to the relevant unpaid service.

Select **Charge with card reader**.  Have your customer tap, swipe, or insert their card.

### Accepting payment from a custom order

If you use Wix Stores or Wix Bookings, can also create a [manually create an order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/manually-creating-an-order-8027377). This order can include store products, booking services, or any type of custom item.

After you create the order, it appears in the Orders tab. From there, you can [collect payment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/desktop-pos-solutions-collecting-payment-with-the-dashboard-card-reader-solution#accepting-payment-from-the-orders-tab) like you would for any other order.
