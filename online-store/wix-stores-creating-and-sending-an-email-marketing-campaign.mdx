---
title: "Wix Stores: Creating and Sending an Email Marketing Campaign"
---

Spread the word about your products and promotions! Wix Email Marketing allows you to send beautiful emails to your contacts.

Choose one of our stunning email templates, select what you want to promote, and we'll get you started by automatically adding text and images. Edit the email to get it just the way you like it, or send it as is.

### Start by choosing what kind of campaign you want to create:

### Create an email marketing campaign for a product

1. [Go to Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.
2. Click to select the product you want to promote.
3. Click **Promote this product **on the right.
4. Click **Create Email Campaign**.

### Create an email marketing campaign for a category, sale, or discount

1. [Go to Email Marketing](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Email%20Marketing&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fshoutout%2F) on your site's dashboard.
2. Select a template or click **More Templates **to view additional options.

[image: Screenshot of some sample email marketing templates]

### Now, customize and send your campaign:

[Customize your email campaign](https://support.wix.com/en/article/using-the-wix-email-marketing-campaign-editor).    Click** Next **at top right.  Select the relevant contacts or enter email addresses directly.  Click **Next **at top right.  Enter the campaign details: 

- **Subject Line:** The subject line of the email.
- **From Name:** The name that the email is sent from. This may be your business name or your personal name.
- **Reply-to Email:  **If a reader replies, it is sent to the email address you enter here.

**Note:** If you have not yet [confirmed your email address](https://support.wix.com/en/article/email-marketing-confirming-your-email-address), click **Confirm Now**.
    (Optional) Click **Send Test Email **on the left.

1. Enter the email address you want to send the test to.
2. Click **Send**.
    Select a send option:

- **Send immediately: **Click **Send Now**.
- **Send later:**

1. Click **Schedule**.
2. Select the send day.
3. Select the send time.
4. Click **Schedule Campaign**.

> **Tips:**
>
> - You can also create a marketing email from scratch by clicking **Start from Scratch**.
> - To view campaigns you've already sent, email marketing tips and more, go to the **Email Marketing** tab and scroll down.
