---
title: "Wix Mobile POS Request: Accepting Card Payments through Wix Payments Worldwide at a Mobile POS"
---

Currently, accepting payment using a card reader at a Mobile POS through Wix Payments is available only for merchants in the US and Canada.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - If you're connected to [Wix Payments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-wix-payments), you can accept payment by manually entering in your customers' card numbers.
> - You can accept payment using the following third-party integrations:
>
> - **Square:** Available in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, Ireland, and Japan. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale-with-square)
> - **SumUp:** Available in Belgium, Bulgaria, Cyprus, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Switzerland, and the UK. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-setting-up-a-point-of-sale-with-sumup)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
