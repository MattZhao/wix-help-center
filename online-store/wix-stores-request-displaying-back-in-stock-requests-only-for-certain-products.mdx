---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Displaying Back-in-Stock Requests Only for Certain Products"
---

Customers can request to be informed when an out-of-stock item is back in stock - if you [enabled this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-notifying-customers-when-products-are-back-in-stock). Currently, you cannot limit back-in-stock requests to certain products; only for all or no products.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
