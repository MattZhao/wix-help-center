---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Selling Products by Auction"
---

Currently, Wix Stores does not have a feature that would allow you to sell your store products by auction.

> **Update:**
>
> It's now possible to [sell products by auction using the Product Auction app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/third-party-app-product-auction-by-webkul).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
