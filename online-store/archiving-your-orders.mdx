---
title: "Archiving Your Orders"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This article is relevant for orders with Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, Wix Restaurants Orders (New) orders, and Wix Donations.

Stay on top of your records by archiving orders you no longer need in the Orders tab of your site's dashboard. If you change your mind, you can unarchive them at any time.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Archive an order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/archiving-your-orders#archiving-an-order)  [View archived orders or unarchive them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/archiving-your-orders#viewing-archived-orders-or-unarchiving-them)

---

### Archiving an order

You can archive one or more orders by selecting them and then clicking **Archive** in the **More Actions** drop-down.

#### To archive an order:

[Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Select the checkbox next to the order(s) you want to archive.  Click **More Actions**.  Select **Archive**.

> It's not possible to delete orders permanently.

---

### Viewing archived orders or unarchiving them

You can use your filters to access archived orders. Then, you can select any that you want to remove from the archive.

#### To access archived orders and unarchive them:

[Go to Orders](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Filter** at the top right.  Scroll down and click to expand the **Archive Status** section.  Select the **Archived** checkbox.   (Optional) Unarchive an order:

1. Select the checkbox next to the archived order you want to unarchive.
2. Click **More Actions**.
3. Select **Archive**.

> **Tip:**
>
> When you're done, don't forget to cancel the filter.
