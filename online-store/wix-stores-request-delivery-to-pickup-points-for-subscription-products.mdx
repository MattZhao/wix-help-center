---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Delivery to Pickup Points for Subscription Products"
---

Currently, when a customer orders products as a one-time purchase, they can select [delivery to pickup points](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-delivery-to-pickup-points). This can be set up through shipping solutions such as Boxtal and Sendcloud.

Delivering [subscription orders](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-selling-product-subscriptions) to pickup points is not currently supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
