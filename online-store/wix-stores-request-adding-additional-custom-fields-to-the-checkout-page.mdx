---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Adding Additional Custom Fields to the Checkout Page"
---

We're thrilled to announce that you can now add multiple custom fields to the Checkout Page in addition to the preset fields. Note that you can choose from a wide variety of field types (e.g. text fields, multiple choice, checkboxes).

Learn how to [add additional custom fields to the Checkout Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-checkout-page#customizing-the-info-collected-from-customers).
