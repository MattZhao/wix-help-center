---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Receiving Direct Payout from Ready to Sell Dropshipping Sales"
---

Currently, when a customer buys your ready to sell dropshipping products, you need to go to the supplier's dashboard and pay for the order. This way, they know they need to ship the relevant products to the customer.

This process isn't currently automated in such a way that you automatically receive your share of the revenue while the supplier receives theirs and is notified that they need to fulfill the order.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
