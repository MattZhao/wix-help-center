title: "Wix Stores Request: Displaying Local Inventory Ads in Google Merchant Solutions"

Currently, it's not possible to enable [local inventory adds](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/3057972?hl=en) on [Google Merchant Solutions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-google-merchant-solutions).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
