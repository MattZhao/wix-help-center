---
title: "Request: Adding Ecwid Products to your Sitemap"
---

Every Wix site has [automatically generated sitemap files](https://support.wix.com/en/article/understanding-your-sites-sitemap-file) that helps get your content recognized by search engines. Wix automatically updates these sitemap files when you make changes to your site. Currently, it is not possible to add information about your Ecwid products to your site's sitemap file.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
