---
title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Managing Inventory"
---

Currently, it's not possible to manage inventory on the POS tablet. You can, however, make inventory changes in your [Wix dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-updating-inventory) or through the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-inventory-using-the-wix-app).

> **Update:**
>
> It's now possible to [manage how you handle inventory that is out-of-stock](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-out-of-stock-inventory) on the POS tablet.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
