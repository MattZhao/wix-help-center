---
title: "Request: Customizing the Design of the Checkout Page"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently,  it is not possible to customize the background, fonts, colors, or button design of the Checkout Page. However, you can [add your business name or logo](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-checkout-page#displaying-your-business-name-or-logo) to personalize the page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
