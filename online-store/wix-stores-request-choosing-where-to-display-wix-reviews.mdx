---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Choosing Where to Display Wix Reviews"
---

Currently, when you add Wix Reviews to your site, the reviews appear on the product page for the relevant product, under the product information. It's not possible to choose where to display the reviews.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
