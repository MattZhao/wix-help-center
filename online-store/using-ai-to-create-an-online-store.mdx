title: "Using AI to Create an Online Store"
---

Harness the power of artificial intelligence (AI) to build and manage your online store with Wix Stores. By utilizing AI, you not only save time and effort, but also ensure a more seamless and efficient operation of your store. Think of it as having a smart assistant who can handle multiple tasks accurately and quickly.

AI can be used in various aspects of your store, from creating your products to promotion, leaving you more time to focus on other aspects of your business.

> Get inspiration for your store from other [websites built with Wix Stores](https://www.wix.com/explore/websites/categories/ecommerce).

---

### Get a ready-made site for your store

With Wix's AI website builder, you have a simple and intuitive way to [build your online store](https://www.wix.com/ecommerce/online-store). This tool interacts with you via a chat and gathers your website requirements through a series of questions. It considers your design style preferences and the functionalities you need for your store, then generates a customized site tailored to your needs.

Feel free to request changes to parts of your site or ask the assistant to create a completely new one. Once it's ready, you can customize all parts of your site using the Wix Editor.

> **Ready to build your site?**
>
> Take me to the [Wix AI website builder.](https://www.wix.com/ai-website-builder)

### Create and edit product images

When setting up your online store, one of the most crucial aspects is creating captivating product images. They're the first thing your customers see, and first impressions count in the online marketplace. With the help of [Wix's AI tools](https://www.wix.com/features/ai), creating stunning product images has never been easier.

Click **Crop & edit image** on your product image to access the Wix Photo Studio.

Once there, you will find several AI tools to enhance your image:

> **Want to create a new image for your store?**
>
> Use the [AI Image Creator](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-images-with-the-wix-ai-image-creator) to generate images based on a description of your product.

### Improve your store's SEO

So, you're not an SEO expert? No need to worry. Use Wix's AI tool for SEO to help you write the title tags and meta descriptions for your site's pages and products. This can help your online store's ranking on Google and may help customers find your products more easily.

> **Important:**
>
> This feature is only visible once you [upgrade your site](https://www.wix.com/upgrade/website?referralAdditionalInfo=HelpCenter).

The AI text generator will suggest 3 optimized suggestions for title tags and meta descriptions based on your existing page content. You can refine and recreate these suggestions to get more personalized results or change the tone of voice to match your brand.

> [Go to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/seo-home?referralInfo=KB-3c4f785a-4597-467a-8279-ceddc7b499e2&title=Select%20a%20Site&buttonText=Go+to+SEO) in your site's dashboard to set up your store's SEO using AI.
