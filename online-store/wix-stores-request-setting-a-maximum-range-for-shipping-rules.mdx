---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Setting a Maximum Range for Shipping Rules"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set a maximum price or maximum weight when creating shipping rules. The maximum range is always open ended, (for example, 20 Lb and up or $30 and up).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
