---
title: "Wix Stores: Real Time Correios Shipping Calculation with Meu Frete"
---

Shipping to customers in Brazil? Make shipping easier by setting up real-time shipping calculations with Correios using the Meu Frete com Correios app.

Meu Frete integrates with Correios to offer comprehensive delivery coverage throughout Brazil. Furthermore, you have the option of using your existing Correios contract to take advantage of your benefits.

Click here to [add the Meu Frete app](https://www.wix.com/app-market/meu-frete-com-correios)  Learn how to [add and set up the Meu Frete app](https://support.wix.com/pt/article/adicionar-e-configurar-o-aplicativo-meu-frete-com-correios) (In Portuguese)  Learn how to [formalize your contract with Correios](https://support.wix.com/pt/article/formalizar-um-contrato-com-os-correios) (In Portuguese)

> **Notes:**
>
> - The app currently offer SEDEX and PAC throughout Brazil.
> - It's no longer possible to set up real-time shipping with Correios without the use of a third-party app.
