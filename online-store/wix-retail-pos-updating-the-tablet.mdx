---

> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about **Wix Retail POS** tablet for brick & mortar businesses.
> - For info on using **POS on a mobile device**, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/mobile-pos-4564663).

Keep the POS tablet running smoothly by updating the software on a regular basis. We recommend that you check for updates regularly.

### To update the tablet software:

Open the POS tablet.  Tap the Menu icon  at the bottom left.   Tap **Settings**.  Scroll down and tap **App & Tablet**.  Check for available updates.  Click **Install Update**.
