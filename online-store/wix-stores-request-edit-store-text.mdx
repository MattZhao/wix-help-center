title: "Wix Stores Request: Edit Store Text"
---

We are pleased to announce that it is now possible to edit the text on certain pages and elements in Wix Stores.

What other options are missing? We want to hear from you. Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/contact?skipTroubleshooters=true) to submit a ticket and let us know.

**Learn how to:**

[Edit the Cart page text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-cart-page)  [Edit the Cart Icon text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-cart-icon#customizing-the-cart-icon-design)  [Edit the Mini Cart text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-mini-cart#customizing-the-mini-cart)  [Edit the thank You page text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-thank-you-page)  [Edit the Product Widget button text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-the-product-widget)  [Edit the Product Page button text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-customizing-your-product-page)  [Edit the Load More button text](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-how-customers-view-more-products-in-your-product-gallery#setting-up-the-load-more-button)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
