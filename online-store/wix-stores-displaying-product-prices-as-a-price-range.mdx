---
title: "Wix Stores: Displaying Product Prices as a Price Range"
---

If you created product options with different prices, you can indicate that the price has a range.

### To display a product with multiple prices:

[Go to Store products & inventory settings](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Products%20and%20Inventory&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/store/settings?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Scroll down to the **Price Range **settings.  Click the **Show "From" next to the price** toggle to enable it.
