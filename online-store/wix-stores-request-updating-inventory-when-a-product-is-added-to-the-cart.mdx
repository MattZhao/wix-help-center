---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Updating Inventory when a Product is Added to the Cart"
---

Currently, your product inventory in Wix Stores updates once a customer **purchases** a product, not when the product is added to the cart.

> **Updates:**
>
> It is now possible to choose whether inventory updates when the order is placed or when you receive payment. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-when-inventory-gets-updated)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
