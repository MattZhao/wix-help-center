title: "Wix Stores: Managing Wix Reviews"
---

Once you start receiving product reviews through [Wix Reviews](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-wix-reviews), you can manage, reply, or delete them.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Checking and publishing pending reviews](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-wix-reviews#checking-and-publishing-pending-reviews)  [Removing live reviews](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-wix-reviews#removing-live-reviews)  [Importing reviews](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-wix-reviews#importing-reviews)

---

### Checking and publishing pending reviews

If you chose to [moderate some or all of your incoming reviews](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-wix-reviews#step-2-customize-the-settings), you'll need to check them when they come in. You can decide whether you want to publish them, reply and publish, or move them to the trash.

#### To check pending reviews:

[Go to Reviews](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Wix%20Reviews&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/reviews/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Pending **tab.  Select the review you want to check.  Choose what you want to do:

- **Reply: **Click **Reply to Review**, enter your comment, and click **Reply**.
- **Publish: **Click **Publish **to display the review on your site.
- **Move to Trash:** Click **Reject** to remove spam or abusive reviews.

---

### Removing live reviews

You may want to remove certain live reviews, for example if the review is a spam message.

#### To remove live reviews:

[Go to Reviews](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Wix%20Reviews&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/reviews/settings) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Published **tab.  Select the review you want to check.  Click **Reject**.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you removed a review by mistake, you can go to the **Rejected **tab and republish it.

---

### Importing reviews

If you previously collected reviews for your products on another platform (e.g. Amazon) or through a different review app, you can import your reviews and display them on your site. Learn how to [import your product reviews](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-importing-reviews-into-wix-reviews).
