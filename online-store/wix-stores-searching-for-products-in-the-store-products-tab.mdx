---
title: "Wix Stores: Searching for Products in the Store Products Tab"
---

Locate your store products quickly by using the product search and filter options available in the Store Products tab.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Performing a product search](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-searching-for-products-in-the-store-products-tab#performing-a-product-search)  [Filtering products](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-searching-for-products-in-the-store-products-tab#filtering-store-products)

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also [add a search box to your storefront](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-optimizing-a-search-box-for-stores) to help customers find products.

---

### Performing a product search

Save time locating the product you want to work on. Use the search bar to look for a product by its name or SKU.

> **Note:**
>
> If you assigned separate SKU numbers to product options, they do not come up in search results.

#### To search for store products:

[Go to Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Enter a search term in the search field. 

**Tip: **You can search by product name or SKU.

---

### Filtering store products

If you [created product categories](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-and-displaying-product-categories), you can use them to filter your products. Additionally, you can filter by type of product (physical or digital), inventory status, or visibility setting.

#### To filter store products:

[Go to Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Filter**.  Select one or more filters.
