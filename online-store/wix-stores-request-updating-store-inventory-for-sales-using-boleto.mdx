---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Updating Store Inventory for Sales Using Boleto"
---

Currently, it is not possible to update your Wix Stores inventory for sales that were paid via the Boleto payment method.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
