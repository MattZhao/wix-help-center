---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Ability to Sell Products Bundled Together"
---

Currently, it is not possible to sell a physical product bundled together with a digital download or sell multiple digital products bundled together as one product in Wix Stores.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
