---
title: "Wix Art Store: No Longer Available in the App Market"
---

As of October 2022 it is no longer possible to add Wix Art Store to your site. If you already have Wix Art Store on your site, you can continue to use it as usual.

Click a question below to learn more about the app's removal from the App Market:

### I have the Wix Art Store app on my site. Can I continue to accept orders?

You can continue to accept orders as usual. [Order a Print](https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store) will fulfil and ship orders as normal.

Order a Print are responsible for printing and shipping your prints. If you have questions / concerns around the order status or quality of your prints, contact Order a Print directly:
**Website**: [https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store](https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store)**Email**: support@orderaprint.com

### I have the Wix Art Store app on my site. Can I continue to withdraw my earnings?

You can continue to withdraw earnings as usual.

[Learn more about withdrawing earnings in the Wix Art Store](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-art-store-withdrawing-your-earnings-in-the-wix-art-store)

### I have the Wix Art Store app on my site. Can I still receive support for my Art Store?

Yes - Our Customer Care Team are available to answer any questions you have around your Art Store.

[Learn more about contacting Wix Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support)

**Note:** If your question relates to the shipping or printing of merchandise, contact Order a Print directly:
**Website**: [https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store](https://www.orderaprint.com/art-store)**Email**: support@orderaprint.com

### If I delete the Wix Art Store app, what happens to my images?

Your uploaded images will remain in the Media Manager in the Site Files folder, without a watermark.

### What can I use to sell my art instead of Art Store?

You can [add Wix Stores to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) and [connect it to Printful](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-to-printful) to sell your art.

Wix Stores allows you to sell a variety of product types and control the delivery of customers' orders.

[Learn more about Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-wix-stores)

### Can I download / export my Art Store images?

It is not possible to download or export existing Art Store images. Nor is it possible to upload / import digital products into Wix Stores. You must [add images as digital products to Wix Stores manually](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-digital-product). 
If you would like to be able to import / export digital products in Wix Stores in the future, [vote for that feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-request-importing-or-exporting-digital-products).
