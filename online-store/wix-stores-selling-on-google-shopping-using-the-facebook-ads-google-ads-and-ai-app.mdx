title: "Wix Stores: Selling on Google Shopping Using the \"Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI\" App"
---

Generate additional income by selling your products on Google Shopping.

Selling on Google Shopping is available through the "Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI" app by AdScale. This app simplifies the process by creating a merchant account for you.

> **Notes:**
>
> - This is a Premium app which requires an upgrade to use. In addition, Google charges customers for displaying products on Google Shopping pages.
> - You can also use this app to create Google Ads and more.

### To set up Google Shopping:

Click **App Market **iconon the left side of the Editor.  Start typing "AdScale" in the search bar and select "Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI" from the results.  Click **Add** **to Site**.  Click **Agree & Add**.  Select an option:

- Click **Sign up with Google **and click **Next**.
- **Sign up manually:**

1. Enter your email and full name.
2. Create a password.
3. Click **Next**.
    Enter your credit card details and click **Next**. **Note: **Google will charge this credit card for advertising campaigns.  Make sure the **Enable Google Shopping ads on AdScale **and **Enable Google's keyword insights for your store **checkboxes are selected and click **Next**.  Select an option:

- Select the category that best describes your products  and click **Next**.
- Click **I want to choose another category**, select a category from the drop-down and click **Next**.
    Click **Next** again.  Set your target locations: This determines which Google users are exposed to your products on Google Shopping. 

1. Start typing a region name (city, state, county, country etc.) and select it from the list.
2. (Optional) Add additional regions.
3. (Optional) Select **Exclude **from the drop-down and exclude regions inside selected regions (e.g. If you selected New York state, you can use this to exclude some counties).
4. Click **Next**.
5. Indicate whether you offer free shipping in your selected locations and click **Apply**.
    Set your target age range and gender and click **Next**.  Leave the **Product Ads **option selected and click **Next**.

**Tip: **You can also select the **Homepage Ads **option to create Google Ads.

[image: Screenshot of one of the Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI sign up screens]   Select the products that you want to display on Google Shopping:

**Tip: **By default, all of your products are selected.

1. (Optional) Deselect an entire category to exclude it.

[image: Screenshot of one of the Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI sign up screens]
2. (Optional) Click the arrow next to a category a to expand it and deselect individual products.

[image: Screenshot of one of the Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI sign up screens]
    Select a product description option:

- Select any of the ready-made text options and click **Next**.  

[image: Screenshot of one of the Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI sign up screens]
- Enter your own text and click **Next**.
    Select the days of the week and the hours your product appears:

1. Select **All days**, **Monday - Friday**, or **Custom**.
2. If you selected Custom, click to choose individual day and time slots.
3. Click **Next**.
    Set a budget for your first campaign:

1. Set the time your first campaign will run from the drop-down.

**Tip: **Select **Periodic **to set a custom time period.

[image: Screenshot of one of the Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI sign up screens]
2. Enter the budget.
3. Select your goal from the drop-down.

**Tip: **After a number of budget periods have passed, you receive feedback on how to optimize your settings to reach this goal.
4. (Optional) Set a minimum ROAS (return on ad spending):

1. Click **Advanced**.
2. Enter the minimum ROAS.
5. Click **Next**.
    Click **Finish**.

After the initial setup, you can manage your campaigns in the AdScale dashboard. To access it, click the **Apps** tab in your site's dashboard.

Click **Open **to access the AdScale dashboard.  Click **Upgrade **to purchase a plan.

To learn more about "Facebook Ads, Google Ads & AI" app, click [here](https://www.adscale.com/).
