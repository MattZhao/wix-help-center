---
title: "Request: Viewing Feature Requests Which Are \"Collecting Votes\" in the Wix Product Roadmap"
---

We're excited to announce that the [Wix Product Roadmap](https://support.wix.com/en/roadmap) now shows Feature Requests which are collecting votes, in addition to Feature Requests that we're currently working on or planning to release in the upcoming quarters.

Click the **Collecting votes** tab to filter and click **Vote** on the right side to add your vote.
