---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Adding a Form to the Cart Page"
---

Currently, it's not possible to add a form to the Cart page to collect additional information.

> **Workarounds:**
>
> - You can [create a form on the Checkout page by adding custom fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-customizing-checkout-form-fields).
> - Customers can enter information using the ["Add a Note" link in the Cart page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/collecting-and-viewing-buyer-notes).
>
> [image: Screenshot of the Cart Page in a sample site with the Add a Note link outlined]

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
