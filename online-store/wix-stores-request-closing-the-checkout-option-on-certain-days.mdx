---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Closing the Checkout Option on Certain Days"
---

Currently, it is not possible to limit the checkout to certain days of the week (e.g. checkout not available on weekends).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
