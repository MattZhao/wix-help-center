---
title: "Wix Stores: Creating Modalyst Print on Demand Products"
---

> **Important:**
>
> The ability to create and sell Print on demand products with Modalyst is being discontinued.

If you've been selling POD products through Modalyst, you'll need to make some changes to continue to sell print on demand products.

---

### Upcoming changes

Starting July 15, 2024, you will no longer be able to create new POD products.

**Starting Sept. 9, 2024, all existing Modalyst POD products will be removed from your store.** This means that customers will no longer be able to purchase your Modalyst POD products in your online shop.

> **Notes:**
>
> - Orders received from customers and paid for by you before September 9 will be fulfilled, as usual.
> - Modalyst ready to sell products are not affected by this change.

---

### Processing final orders

Make sure you pay for any orders you receive in the final days before POD is discontinued. For example, if you receive orders for POD products on September 8th, you must pay for them on the 8th so that they can be fulfilled. It will not be possible to start the fulfillment process by paying for those orders on Sept. 9th.

---

### Continue selling POD products

You can continue to create your own print on demand products and sell them in your store using another app. Go to the Wix App Market to choose from the wide variety of [Print on Demand apps](https://www.wix.com/app-market/category/ecommerce/print-on-demand).

---

### Need assistance?

If you have any questions, feel free to contact [Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact).
