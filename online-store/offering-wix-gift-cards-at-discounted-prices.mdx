---
title: "Offering Wix Gift Cards at Discounted Prices"
---

You can offer customers a good deal on gift cards by selling them for less than their face value. For example, you can sell a $100 gift card for $80.

> **Tip:**
>
> You have the option of creating a different gift card image for each value.

### To offer gift cards at discounted prices:

[Go to Gift Card](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Gift%20Cards&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/gift-cards) in your site's dashboard.   Click **Edit Gift Card**.  (Optional) Edit any of the **General Info** settings.  Edit the preset gift card amounts that customers can purchase:

1. Type the amount you want to add.

**Tip: **Type a comma after each entry.
2. Click the **x** next to any amount you want to remove.
    Add discounted prices:

1. Click **Advanced Image and Prices**.
2. Enter a discounted price next to the values you want to discount.
3. (Optional) Add an image for each gift card value.
4. Click **Save**.

(Optional) Edit the **Expiration** settings.  Click **Save**.
