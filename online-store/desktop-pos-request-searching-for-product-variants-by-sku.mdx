title: "Desktop POS Request: Searching for Product Variants by SKU"
---

Currently, when using [POS Checkout](https://support.wix.com/en/article/desktop-pos-solutions-selling-to-clients-using-pos-checkout) to sell products on your desktop computer, you cannot search for product variants, when each variant has its own SKU.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
