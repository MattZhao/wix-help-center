---
title: "Request: Displaying Custom Field Replies in Printed Orders"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, when you print an order, the information that customers entered in [custom fields](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-customizing-checkout-form-fields) does not appear.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
