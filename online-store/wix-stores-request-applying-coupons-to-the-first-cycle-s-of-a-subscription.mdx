---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Applying Coupons to the First Cycle(s) of a Subscription"
---

Currently, when you apply a coupon to a product subscription, it applies to all cycles. In other words, if a customer applies a 10% off coupon to a monthly subscription that continues for a year, they get the discount every month. Applying a coupon to the first payment(s) of a subscription is not supported.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
