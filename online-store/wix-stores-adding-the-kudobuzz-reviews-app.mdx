Let customers know how other shoppers feel about your products and your site by posting reviews. Product reviews can be added to product pages through the Kudobuzz Reviews app.

### Adding the Kudobuzz app to your site

You can add the Kudobuzz Reviews app to your site from the Wix App Market. During setup, you'll be able to select where you want to display review buttons and showcase reviews you've already received.

Wix EditorStudio Editor

1. Click **Add Apps **  on the left side of the editor.
2. Search for the Kudobuzz app in the search bar.
3. Click **Add to Site**.
4. Review the permissions and click **Agree and Add**.
