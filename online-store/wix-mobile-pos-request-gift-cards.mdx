title: "Wix Mobile POS Request: Gift Cards"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible to [redeem gift cards at a mobile POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-about-virtual-gift-cards-at-a-mobile-pos) when selling store products.

It's still not possible to sell gift cards at a Mobile POS. You can [vote for that option](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-request-selling-gift-cards).
