---
title: "Wix Mobile POS Request: Connecting External Printers and Scanners"
---

We're excited to announce that it's now possible for Wix Stores merchants to [connect an external printer and print receipts at a mobile point of sale](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-printing-receipts).

Looking for a different feature? You can vote for any of the following requests:

[Using a barcode scanner with Mobile POS](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-mobile-pos-request-connecting-external-scanners)  The ability to print receipts with:

- [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-pos-request-printing-receipts-at-a-mobile-pos)
- [Wix Events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-events-pos-request-printing-receipts-at-a-mobile-pos)
- [Pricing Plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-pos-request-printing-receipts-at-a-mobile-pos)
