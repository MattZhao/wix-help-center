title: "Wix Retail POS: Enabling Automatic Discounts"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about **Wix Retail POS** tablet for brick & mortar businesses.
> - For info on using **POS on a mobile device**, [see our help articles on mobile POS](https://support.wix.com/en/mobile-pos-4564663).

Automatic discounts are price reductions you create in your site's desktop. With automatic discounts you can:

**Set a sale start and end time: **For example you can schedule a sale that starts at the first of them month and automatically end at the end of the month.  **Create conditional discounts: **You can create discounts that take effect only when a customer buys a set quantity (buy 3, get 30% off) or a set amount (buy $50 worth of hats and get $10 off).

Follow the steps below to enable automatic discounts.

> **Tip:**
>
> Automatic discounts can only be applied to Wix Stores products, not Wix Bookings services.

---

### Step 1 | Create automatic discounts

To get started, go to Automatic Discounts on desktop and create your first discount. Learn how to [create automatic discounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-automatic-discounts).

---

### Step 2 | Enable automatic discounts in the POS tablet

Now that you have one or more automatic discounts set up, you can enable them so that customers visiting your brick and mortar store get discounts.

#### To enable automatic discounts on POS:

Open the POS tablet.  Tap the Menu icon  at the bottom left.  Tap **Settings**.  Tap **Automatic Discounts **on the left menu.   Tap the **Apply automatic discounts to POS sales **toggle to enable it.

---

### Step 3 | (Optional) Removing a discount from a specific sale

In the previous step, you enabled automatic discount and they now apply to every relevant sale. If, for some reason, you don't want to apply the discount to a particular sale, you can remove it during checkout.

#### To remove the discount from a specific sale:

Open the POS tablet.   Tap **Checkout** on the bottom bar.   Start processing a sale.  Tap **Actions** at the top right.  Select **Remove Automatic Discounts**.  Complete the sale.

---

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about using automatic discounts at a point of sale.

### How can I view the details of a specific automatic discount on the tablet?

To view more information about a specific discount:
1. Open the POS tablet.
2. Tap the Menu icon  at the bottom left.
3. Tap the **Settings** tab.
4. Tap the **Automatic Discounts **tab.
5. Tap the discount you want to view.

### What statuses can automatic discounts have?

Automatic discounts can have 3 statuses. 
- **Active: **Can be applied to sales
- **Disabled:** Was manually turned off
- **Expired: **Has passed the final date.

Only discounts with an "Active" status will be applied to sales.

### How can I see that an automatic discount was applied to a sale?

The discount is automatically added to any sale that meets the sale requirements.
For example, if you created a 35% off sale for customers who buy 2 dog toys, it'll automatically appear when a customer fulfills those requirements.

### Can I enable a specific discount only online or only in the POS tablet?

No, you can only decide whether you want to enable or disable all automatic discounts on POS.

### How can I create a new automatic discount or edit an existing one?

To create or edit automatic discounts, go to **Automatic Discounts** on desktop. Learn more about [automatic discounts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-automatic-discounts).
