---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Creating Multiple Product Page Templates"
---

Currently, when you design and customize your store's Product Page, those changes appear for all products. It's not possible to create multiple versions of the Product Page and assign different products to different versions of the Product Page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
