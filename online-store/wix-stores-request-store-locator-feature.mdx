---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Store Locator Feature"
---

Currently, Wix does not have a store locator or postcode search feature.

> **Tip:**
>
> As an alternative, check out the [Located - Store Locator](https://www.wix.com/app-market/located-map) third-party app, or add one by [embedding external code](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-embedding-a-site-or-a-widget).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
