---

> **Important:**
>
> This article is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New) orders.

You may want to cancel an entire order and refund a customer when you're unable to fulfill an order or your customer asks to cancel the purchase.

---

### Canceling & refunding orders paid via PayPal, Stripe, or Wix Payments

Before you can refund a PayPal order, you need to go to [Accept Payments](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Set%20Up%20Payments&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fpayments%2F) in your site's dashboard and enable advanced features.

> **Note:**
>
> When you follow the instructions below, purchases made through your site are automatically refunded, unless you [created a manual order through Wix Invoices](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-invoices-for-store-orders#requesting-payment-via-invoice-for-an-unpaid-order). In that case, you'll have to go to the relevant payment provider's side (Stripe, PayPal or Wix Payments) and process the order there.

#### To cancel & refund a PayPal / Stripe / Wix Payments order:

[Go to the Orders tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant order. 

**Tip: **Only paid orders can be canceled and refunded. For stores, only unfulfilled orders can be canceled and refunded.  Click **More Actions** at the top right.  Select **Cancel & Refund**.  (Optional) To provide a partial refund, enter the amount you want to refund.  (Optional) Select the **Update inventory** checkbox, to return all items to stock.

**Tip: **This option is relevant only if you use Wix Stores and track inventory amounts.  (Optional) Select the **Send a confirmation email to customer** checkbox to send a message. You can enter a personal note to your customer.

**Note: **Customers may also receive a notification from the payment provider.   Click **Continue**.  Click **Cancel This Order**.

**Note: **This cannot be undone.  ** **

### Canceling & refunding orders paid via other online payment methods

Refunds for purchases made through payment providers other than PayPal, Stripe, and Wix Payments cannot be processed in the Wix dashboard. You must complete the refund through the provider's dashboard and update your order in the Wix dashboard.

#### To cancel & refund orders paid through other online payment methods:

[Go to the Orders tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant order.

**Tip: **Only paid orders can be canceled and refunded. For stores, only unfulfilled orders can be canceled and refunded.  Click **More Actions** at the top right.  Select **Cancel & Refund**.  Select the **Mark order as Refunded** checkbox.  Enter the refund amount.

**Important**: This does not refund your customer. It only marks the order as refunded in your dashboard.  Click **Cancel Order & Go to \<Payment Provider>**. This takes you to the relevant payment provider (e.g. Square, 2Checkout, etc.).  Complete the refund in the dashboard of the payment provider.

---

### Canceling & refunding orders paid via manual payment

To refund a customer who paid you using an offline payment method (e.g. cash, check, app), you must contact your customer offline and provide them with the refund. After you give the customer the refund, update the order in the Orders tab.

#### To cancel and refund manual (offline) orders;

Contact the customer offline and provide them with a refund (e.g. send a bank transfer).  [Go to the Orders tab](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Orders&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/orders) in your site's dashboard.  Select the relevant order.

**Tip: **Only paid orders can be canceled and refunded. For stores, only unfulfilled orders can be canceled and refunded.  Click **More Actions** at the top right.  Select **Cancel & Refund**.  Update the order to show the money you refunded offline:

1. Enter the amount you refunded the customer.
2. (Optional) Select the **Update inventory** checkbox to add the products to the inventory.

**Note: **Relevant only if you use Wix Stores and track inventory amounts.
3. (Optional) Select the **Send a confirmation email to customer** checkbox: If selected, you can enter a personal note to your customer. It appears at the top of the email.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Click **Cancel This Order**.

**Note: **This cannot be undone.

### FAQs

### Can I provide a refund for some products and not others?

Yes. Let's say your customer purchases 3 items and wants a refund on one of them, you can follow the [these instructions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refunding-customers) to refund the relevant product and deselect the rest.

[image: Screenshot of the refund screen for an order with multiple item. Only 1 item selected to be refunded]

### Can I refund a specific amount?

Yes, you can refund any amount you like, as long as it isn't more than the total price of the order. Simply follow the instructions in the article below and enter the amount.[image: Screenshot of the cancel order screen, with a custom amount being refunded]

### Can I choose whether I want to refund shipping costs?

Yes. You can deduct shipping costs by entering a refund amount that does not include shipping costs. For example, if the order total was $50, and $5 of that was shipping, you can enter $45 as the refund amount. [image: Screenshot of the cancel order screen, with a custom amount being refunded]
Alternatively, you can follow the [these instructions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/refunding-customers) to refund the item and select or deselect the shipping costs.

[image: Screenshot of the refund screen for an order with multiple item. The shipping cost is outlined]

### Can I change the order status from "fulfilled" to "unfulfilled" and vice versa?

You can [change the fulfillment status](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-fulfilling-store-orders#marking-orders-as-fulfilled) of a store order at any time.

### Can I cancel an order without providing a refund?

You might want to simply [cancel an order](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-orders), without going through a refund process. This may happen, for example, if you haven't yet received payment or the customer no longer wants to complete the order.

### I already gave a partial refund. Can I provide another refund on the same order?

Yes, there is no limitation to the number of refunds you can provide for the same order.

### Are there any processing fees charged when refunding an order?

Neither Wix nor Wix Payments charges any additional fees when refunding an order. However, refunds may be subject to processing fees if you are using a third-party payment provider.
