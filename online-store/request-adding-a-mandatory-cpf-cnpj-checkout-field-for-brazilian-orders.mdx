---
title: "Request: Adding a Mandatory CPF/CNPJ Checkout Field for Brazilian Orders"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, when customers checkout out using certain payment methods, such as PayPal, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, they may bypass the custom fields you added to the Checkout Page.

> Customers who complete the checkout process on the page (i.e. without one of the payment methods mentioned above) will see any custom fields you add. Learn how to [add a custom field](https://support.wix.com/en/article/customizing-the-checkout-page#customizing-the-info-collected-from-customers), such as a CPF/CNPJ field, to the Checkout Page.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
