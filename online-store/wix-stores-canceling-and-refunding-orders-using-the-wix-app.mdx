---

Using the Wix mobile app you can handle cancelations and refunds from wherever you are. After you cancel and/or refund an order, the order status updates to reflect the change.

> **Important:**
>
> Once an order status is changed to canceled and/or refunded, the status cannot be changed back.

**In this article, learn how to:**

### Canceling and refunding an order

You can process PayPal or Stripe refunds right from the Wix mobile app. Other orders must be refunded outside of Wix. Follow the instructions below to update your records.

#### To cancel and refund an order:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.  Tap **Orders**.  Tap the relevant order.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.  Select **Issue refund**.  Select an option:

### Cancel and refund manual orders

1. Make sure to contact your customer and refund the money.
2. Deselect the checkmark next to any products you do not want to refund.

**Tip: **You can later enter a refund amount.
3. (If you track inventory) Enable or disable the **Update Inventory **toggle.
4. (Optional) Tap **Edit Amount** to enter a custom refund amount.
5. Tap **Next**.
6. Select a refund explanation from the **Select** drop-down.
7. Enable or disable the **Send a confirmation email to customer** toggle.
8. (Optional) Enter a note to your customer.
9. Tap **Next**.
10. Tap **Issue Refund **to confirm.

**Note: **This cannot be undone.

### Cancel and refund orders paid through PayPal, Stripe, or Wix Payments

1. If more than one item was purchased, select what you want to refund and cancel:
1. Select an option:
- Clear the checkbox next to a product to remove it from the refund.
- Click **Edit Quantity** and enter a product quantity number (use if client bought, for instance, two of a product and you want to refund one).
2. Tap **Next**.
2. (Optional) Edit the refund amount.
3. (If you track inventory) Enable or disable the **Update Inventory **toggle.
4. (Optional) Select the reason you provided a refund from the **Refund Reason** drop-down.
5. (Optional) Notify your customer:
1. Enable the **Send a confirmation email**.
2. (Optional) Enter a note to your customer. The note appears in the confirmation email.
6. Tap **Apply**.
7. Tap **Cancel Order**.
**Note: **This action cannot be undone.

### Cancel and refund orders paid through other payment methods

1. Go to the dashboard of the payment provider and refund the order. When you're done, go back to the app to change the status to "canceled and refunded".
2. Tap to enable the **Mark order as Refunded** toggle.
3. (Optional) Edit the refund amount.

**Important**: This does not refund your customer. It only marks the order as refunded in your dashboard.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Tap **Confirm**.

**Note: **This action cannot be undone.

### Canceling an order

You can choose to cancel an order if, for example, customers change their minds and no longer want an unpaid order.

#### To cancel an order:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.  Tap **Orders**.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.  Tap **Cancel Order**.  (Optional) Notify your customer:

1. Enable the **Send a confirmation email**.
2. (Optional) Enter a note to your customer. The note appears in the confirmation email.
    Tap **Next**.  Tap **Cancel Order**.

### Refunding an order

You can refund an order to reimburse a customer who isn't fully satisfied with a product.

#### To refund an order:

Go to your site in the Wix app.  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Sales**.  Tap **Orders**.  Tap the relevant order.  Tap the **More Actions** icon  at the top right.  Tap **Issue Refund**.  If there is more than one item in the order, select the products and quantities you want to refund.  (If you track inventory) Enable or disable the **Update Inventory **toggle.  Tap **Next**.  Select an option:

### Refund manual orders

1. Make sure to contact your customer and refund the order.
2. (Optional) Select an option from the **Refund Reason **drop-down.
3. Enable or disable the **Send a confirmation email to customer** toggle.
4. Tap **Next** to mark your order as canceled and refunded.
5. Tap **Cancel Order** to confirm.

**Note: **This cannot be undone.

### Refund orders paid through PayPal, Stripe, or Wix Payments

1. Optional) Select an option from the **Refund Reason **drop-down.
2. (Optional) Notify your customer:
- Enable the **Send a confirmation email**.
- (Optional) Enter a note to your customer. The note appears in the confirmation email.
3. Tap **Next**.
4. Tap **Issue Refund**

### Refund orders paid through other payment methods

1. Go to the dashboard of the payment provider and refund the order. When you're done, go back to the app to change the status to "refunded".
2. Tap to enable the **Mark order as Refunded** toggle.
3. (Optional) Edit the refund amount.
**Important**: This does not refund your customer. It only marks the order as refunded in your dashboard.
4. Tap **Next**.
5. Tap **Confirm**.
**Note: **This action cannot be undone.
