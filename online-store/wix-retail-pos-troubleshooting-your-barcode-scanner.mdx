> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about **Wix Retail POS** tablet for brick & mortar businesses.
> - For info on using **POS on a mobile device**, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/mobile-pos-4564663).

If you're experiencing any issues with your [Barcode Scanner](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-about-your-hp-barcode-scanner), try restarting it.

### To troubleshoot the barcode scanner:

Disconnect the barcode scanner from the tablet.  Wait 3 minutes.  Reconnect the barcode scanner to the tablet.

#### Still not working?

If the problem persists, contact [Customer Care](https://www.wix.com/contact).
