---
title: "Wix Stores: Sharing a Product Using the Wix App"
---

Boost your sales by sharing a product on social media or sending it to someone directly via email. You can share using any of the apps available on your personal mobile device.

### To share a store product:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Manage**  at the bottom.  Tap **Store** **Products**, or tap **Catalog** and select **Store Products**.  Tap the **More Actions **icon  next to the relevant product.  Select **Share**.  Select any of the share options available on your mobile device.
