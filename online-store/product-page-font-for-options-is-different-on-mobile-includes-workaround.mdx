---
title: "Product Page: Font for Options is Different on Mobile (Includes Workaround)"
---

We're aware of the issue and are currently gathering more information regarding this matter.

> **Meanwhile, you can do the following to resolve the issue:**
>
> The font displayed on mobile for product options is currently taken from your site’s Paragraph 2 style instead of from your design selections. 
>
> To fix this, you can edit the Paragraph 2 style in the theme manager.
