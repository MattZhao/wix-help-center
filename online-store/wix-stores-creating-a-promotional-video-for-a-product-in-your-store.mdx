title: "Wix Stores: Creating a Promotional Video for a Product in Your Store"
---

Select a product and let Wix automatically create a promotional video about it in just a few clicks. If you want to make your own customizations, you can change the music, adjust the colors, and more.

---

### Creating and customizing a promo video

[Go to Store Products](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Products&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fstore%2Fproducts%3FreferralInfo%3Dsidebar) in your site's dashboard.  Click the relevant product.  Click **Promote this product** on the right.

Click the right arrow and select **Promotional Video**.

Do one of the following:

- **Save a video as is: **

1. Preview the video or videos created for you and click to select the video you want to save.
2. Click **Save & Share**.
- **Customize the video:**

1. Preview the video or videos created for you.
2. Click **Customize Video**.
3. Customize the video content:

1. Click the Plus icon  add additional media.
2. **Product Name**: Edit the name of the product.
3. **Business Name**: Add the name of your business.
4. **Price**: Edit the price that you want to display.
5. **Call to Action**: Add a text to drive potential customers back to your store.
6. Click **Next**.
4. Customize the video design:

1. **Video Style: **Select the look and feel of your video.
2. **Layout: **Select horizontal, vertical, or square.
3. **Main Color**: Pick the primary color for the text and graphic elements in your video.
4. **Secondary Color**: Pick a secondary color for the text and graphic elements in your video.
5. **Font Style**: Pick a font that reflects your product and brand.
6. Click **Next**.
5. Customize the background music: 

1. (Optional) Click the **Music On** toggle on the right to disable the background music.
2. Click **Play ** to preview a track.
3. Click the track that you want to use to select it.
4. Click **Next**.
    (Optional) Display business info at the end of the video:

1. Click the **Include info at the end of this video** toggle to enable it.
2. Upload your business logo.
3. Enter the business info you want to display.
    Click **Apply & Preview **to view your video.  Click** Back to Editing **to make more changes or **Save & Share **to save a high definition version of your video.  (Optional) To share your video on social media or download your video, click **Upload / Download **under the relevant option.

### FAQs

### How many videos can I create for free?

There is no limit to the number of videos you can create free of charge.

### How can I share my video when it's done?

After you save your video, select any of these options:
- Add the video to the Product Page
- Post to Facebook

**Note**: You can upload videos to a Facebook Business Page, not your personal profile.
- Upload to YouTube
- Download to your computer

### How can I display my promotional video on a site page?

1. Go to the relevant site page.
2. Click **Add ** on the left side of the Editor.
3. Click **Video & Music**.
4. Drag the **Single Video Player **to the relevant site page.
5. Click **Change Video**.
6. Click **Change Video **again.
7. Select the promotional video from the Video Maker file.
8. Click **Add to Page**.

### Where can I find the videos I created?

Your videos are located in your Media Manager. To access them, click **Media ** on the left side of the Editor.

### Can I make video about something other than a single store product?

Yes. With Wix Video Maker, you can create a variety of promotional videos. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-media-creating-a-promotional-video-for-your-site)
