---
title: "Request: Setting Up Different Payment Options per App, or per Product in Wix Stores"
---

Currently, it is not possible to set different payment options for accepting payments in different apps (Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, Wix Events, Invoices, Pay Button etc.) Nor is it possible to set different payment options for individual products/categories within Wix Stores.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
