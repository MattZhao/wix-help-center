title: "Wix Stores: Tracking Sales Using Your Thank You Page URL"
---

Your customers are directed to a Thank You page after making a purchase on your site. This means you can use Thank You page visits to track sales or create a customer list using an external tool such as Google Analytics.

---

### Accessing Your Thank You Page URL

The URL for your site's Thank You page is made of your domain name followed by: **/thank-you-page**

### Tracking sales using your Thank You Page URL

Start by creating a rule to track Thank You Page visits using an external tool. Then, enter this part of the Thank You Page URL: **/thank-you-page **in the relevant tool
