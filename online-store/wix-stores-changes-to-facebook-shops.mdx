---
title: "Wix Stores: Changes to Facebook Shops"
---

Starting April 2024, Meta will stop supporting Facebook and Instagram shops created through the Wix dashboard. [Learn more](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/623552069600842)

You can, however, create a Facebook or Instagram shop by:

Using the [Facebook Shops by GoDataFeed](https://www.wix.com/app-market/facebook-shops-by-godatafeed?refhttps://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-facebook-shop-using-the-facebook-shops-by-godatafeed-app) app   Adding your products via a [Facebook Catalog Data Feed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-store-products-to-your-facebook-catalog-8474251)

> **For current Facebook/Instagram shop owners:**
>
> - Before you connect through one of the above options, make sure to disconnect your current shops.
> - By disconnecting your Facebook shop, you will also disconnect your Instagram shop, if you have one.

### To disconnect your existing Facebook Shop:

[Go to Facebook shop](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Your%20Facebook%20Shop&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/store/sales-channels/fb?referralInfo=sidebar) in your site's dashboard.   Click the More Actions icon .  Select **Disconnect Account**.  Click **Disconnect Account** to confirm.
