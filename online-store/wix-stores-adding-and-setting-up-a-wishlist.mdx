### FAQs

### How can I remove the wishlist from my store?

In the Wix Editor or Editor X, go to the wishlist settings, scroll to the bottom and click **Remove Wishlist**

In the Studio Editor, click the wishlist icon, then click the **More Actions** icon  and select **Delete**.

### How can I get more information about managing pages in the Members Area?

Learn how to [add and manage pages in the Members Area](https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-add-and-manage-your-member-pages).
