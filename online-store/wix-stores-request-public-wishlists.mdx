---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Public Wishlists"
---

Currently, customers can only view their own wishlists. It is not possible to make a wishlist public so that it can be viewed by others (e.g. to be used as a wedding registry).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
