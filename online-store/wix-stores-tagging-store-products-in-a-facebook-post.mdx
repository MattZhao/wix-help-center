We recommend [disconnecting your old Facebook shop](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-facebook-shop-using-the-facebook-shops-by-godatafeed-app#disconnecting-old-shops) and creating a new one using the Facebook Shops by GoDataFeed solution before the April deadline.

You can tag images and videos of your products you upload to your business page. Your fans can then click the 'Shop Now' button to go straight to your Facebook shop.

### To tag store products in Facebook posts:

Add an image or video to your Facebook business page.   Click the More Actions** **icon .

Click **Tag Products**.

Click the image and then click to select the relevant product.  Click **Done Tagging & Save** on the bottom left.  Click **Post**.
