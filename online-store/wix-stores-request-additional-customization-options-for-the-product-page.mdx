---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Additional Customization Options for the Product Page"
---

Currently, you can make various customizations to the Product Page, including changing the layout, image display, button text, and more. You can also [add a wishlist](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-a-wishlist) and social share icons. Learn more about [customizing the Product Page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-customizing-your-product-page).

The following customizations are not yet available:

Additional layouts for product images  Adding an expand or magnifying effect for images to every layout  Customizing each Product Page element individually   Changing the background color of product options and quantity boxes  Displaying a product ribbon on the Product Page  Adding “Instagram” and “Share this product” to social share icons   Customizing the previous / next navigation links

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
