Wix automatically sends events to Google Analytics about your [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-wix-stores) products on your site.

### To start using Google Analytics with Wix Stores:

[Install Google Analytics](https://support.wix.com/en/article/tracking-events-on-your-wix-site-with-a-google-analytics-property) on your site.   [Enable Enhanced eCommerce](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6014841?hl=en&ref_topic=6014839#zippy=%2Cin-this-article) in Google Analytics.

### To see the event data in Google Analytics:

> **Notes:**
>
> - You can see all of the events by clicking **Behavior **and then **Events**.
> - It can take a few days until the reports start receiving data.
