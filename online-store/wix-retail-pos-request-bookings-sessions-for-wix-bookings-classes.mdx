---
title: "Wix Retail POS Request: Bookings Sessions for Wix Bookings Classes"
---

Currently, it's possible to book and accept payment only for appointments using Wix Retail POS. You cannot book classes using the POS tablet.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can, however, [collect payment for a class that was already booked](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-accepting-payment-for-a-booked-session) if the class occurs on the same day. For example, if a client booked a class online, when they come to attend the class, you can collect payment at your point of sale.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
