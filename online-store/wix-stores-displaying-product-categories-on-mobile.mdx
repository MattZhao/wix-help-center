---
title: "Wix Stores: Displaying Product Categories on Mobile"
---

If you display the category page on your site, customers can use it to navigate through your live categories.

However, it's important to remember that the **category list is not visible on mobile devices**. If you want customers on mobile to have access to your subcategories, add them to the site menu.

You decide whether you want to add them only on the mobile version of your site or both on desktop and mobile.

---

### Adding categories only to your mobile site menu

If you prefer not to add your product categories to the desktop version of your site menu, you can still add them to mobile. This way, customers visiting your site using a mobile device can easily find the types of products they're looking for.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click the **Switch to Mobile **icon at the top of your editor.
[image: Screenshot showing how  to switch to mobile view in the Wix Editor]
1. Click the site menu.
2. Click the Settings icon .
3. Click **Set as Advanced**.
4. Click **Set as Advanced** to confirm.
5. Click the menu in your editor.
6. If not already open, click **Manage Menu**.
7. Click **+ Add Menu Item** at the bottom.
8. Select **Link**.
9. Select **Page **on the left.
10. Select **Category Page** from the **Which Page? **drop-down.
11. Select the category you want to display from the **Which item?** drop-down.
12. Click **Done**.
13. (Optional) Rename the category.
14. (Optional) Drag categories to make them into subcategories.
[image: Screenshot showing how you change the order of categories in a wix site menu]In the Studio Editor, you have the option of creating specific menus and only showing them on the mobile breakpoint.  Learn more about [hiding and unhiding elements](https://support.wix.com/en/article/studio-editor-working-with-layers#hiding-and-unhiding-elements).
1. Go to the mobile breakpoint.
2. Add a new menu element:

1. Click **Add Elements ** on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Menu & Search**.
3. Drag the relevant menu design onto the page.
3. Create a new custom menu showing the product categories:

1. Click **Manage Menu**.
2. Click the drop-down under **Which menu is displayed? **and select** Manage Site Menus**.
3. Click** + Add New Menu **at the bottom.
4. Enter a name for the new menu.

**Note: **This name isn't visible to visitors.
4. (In the Manage Menu panel) Add the product categories to the new menu: 

1. Click **+ Add Item** at the bottom.
2. Select **Link**.
3. Select **Page **on the left.
4. Select **Category Page** from the **Which Page? **drop-down.
5. Select the category you want to display from the **Which item?** drop-down.
6. Click **Done**.
5. Hide the menu from the other breakpoints:

1. Go to the tablet and desktop breakpoints.
2. Right-click the menu element and select **Hide**.

### Adding categories to desktop & mobile site menus

By default, the category page is added to the site menu with the name "Shop". This link directs customers to the "All Products" category. You can add direct links to each of your other categories (e.g. Body, Nutrition).

If your site has a lot of products and categories, you can add subcategories to the site menu.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **Pages and Menu**on the left side of the editor.
2. Click **Site Menu**.
3. Click **+Add Menu Item** at the bottom.
4. Select **Link.**
[image: Screenshot of Pages & Menu in the Editor, with the page link option outlined]
1. Select **Page **on the left.
2. Select **Category Page** from the **Which Page? **drop-down.
[image: Screenshot of Site Pages & Menu with link popup open]
1. Select the category you want to display from the **Which item?** drop-down.
2. Click **Done**.
3. (Optional) Rename the category and click **Done**.
4. (Optional) Make the category you added into a subcategory:

1. Click **Pages and Menu**on the left side of the Editor.
2. Click **Site Menu**.
3. Drag the relevant menu items under and to the right of the relevant category.

1. Select the relevant menu in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Menu**.
3. Click **+Add Item**.
4. Select **Link**.
5. Select **Page **on the left.
6. Select **Category Page** from the **Which Page? **drop-down.
7. Select the category you want to display from the **Which item?** drop-down.
8. Click **Done**.
