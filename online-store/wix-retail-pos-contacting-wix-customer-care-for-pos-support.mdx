### Contacting Customer Care through your POS tablet

You can contact Wix Customer Care right from your tablet.

#### To contact customer care:

Open the POS tablet.   Tap the Menu icon  at the bottom left.   Tap **Help**.  Tap **Contact Us**.  Contact Wix Customer Care using any of the available options.

---

### Contacting Customer Care through a computer or mobile device

To contact customer care about your Retail POS system, click [here](https://www.wix.com/support-chatbot?nodeId=70ffe006-82d4-4abe-ae48-bd3e90435f84&referral=setupPbwForPos) and select **Retail Point of Sale**.
