---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Minimum or Maximum Weight Requirement for Checkout"
---

Currently, it is not possible to limit checkout when the total weight of items in a customer's cart is greater than or lesser than a set weight.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
