---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Restoring Deleted Products"
---

Currently, it is not possible to restore a product after it has been deleted.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want to temporarily remove products from your store, [you can hide them](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-changing-the-visibility-of-a-product).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
