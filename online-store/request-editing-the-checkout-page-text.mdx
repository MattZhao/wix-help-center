---
title: "Request: Editing the Checkout Page Text"
---

> **Important:**
>
> This feature request is relevant for Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, and Wix Restaurants Orders (New).

Currently, it is not possible to edit the text that appears on the Checkout Page.

This includes, for example, the text of the "Continue Browsing" link, section titles (e.g. Customer details, Delivery details), and field titles (e.g. First name, Last name).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
