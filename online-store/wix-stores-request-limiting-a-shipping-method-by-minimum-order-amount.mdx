---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Limiting a Shipping Method by Minimum Order Amount"
---

Currently, it's not possible to restrict a shipping method to customers who purchase more than a minimum amount. For example, you cannot limit a $10 flat-rate shipping rule to purchases over $100. You can, however, [limit purchases to a minimum order amount](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-a-minimum-order-amount).

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
