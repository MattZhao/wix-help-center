---
title: "Wix Stores: Fulfilling Subscriptions"
---

After a customer purchases a subscription, an order appears in the Orders tab, just as it does for one-time purchases. Then, every cycle (every week, month, or year) the customer is automatically charged. When payment is received, a new order appears in the Orders tab.

### Show me how to access the Orders tab.

1. [Go to your site's dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Go%20to%20Dashboard&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/home/).
2. Click **Sales**.
3. Select the **Orders **tab.

[image: Screenshot of the Wix dashboard menu with the  Orders tab outlined.]

You can perform all the actions on a subscription order (fulfill the order, add a tracking number, cancel it, etc.) just as you would a one-time order.

Information about the subscription appears on the order page.

Click **View Subscription **in the order to manage the subscription, (e.g. update the address). Learn more about [managing a product subscription](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-product-subscriptions).
