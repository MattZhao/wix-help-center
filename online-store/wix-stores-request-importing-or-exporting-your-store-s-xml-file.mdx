---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Importing or Exporting Your Store's XML File"
---

Currently, exporting your store's XML file to publish in external shopping directories such as Amazon, is not supported. It is also not possible to import your store from another platform using an XML file.

> **Tip:**
>
> It is, however, possible to do the following:
> - [Connect your Wix Store to Amazon](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-and-setting-up-an-amazon-shop)
> - [Export a product list as a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-exporting-your-product-list)
> - [Import a product list using a CSV file](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-importing-products-to-wix-stores)

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
