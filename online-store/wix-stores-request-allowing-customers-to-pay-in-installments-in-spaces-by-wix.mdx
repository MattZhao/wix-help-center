---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Allowing Customers to Pay in Installments in Spaces by Wix"
---

Currently, when a customer is purchasing products in [Spaces by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview), they need to pay the entire amount upfront. It's not possible to allow customers to pay in installments.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
