---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Adding YouTube Shopping as a Sales Channel"
---

Currently, it's not possible to sell your Wix Stores products on YouTube using the [YouTube Shopping](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12257682?hl=en) program.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
