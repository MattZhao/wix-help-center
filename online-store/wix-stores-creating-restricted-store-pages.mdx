title: "Wix Stores: Creating Restricted Store Pages"
---

Take control over who can access your store pages. This can be useful when selling wholesale or B2B.

You have the option of reserve access to password holders or to site members. You have the flexibility to decide how you want to restrict who can become a member of your site.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Restrict access to store pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-restricted-store-pages#restricting-access-to-store-pages)  [Creating retail & restricted wholesale pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-restricted-store-pages#creating-retail-restricted-wholesale-store-pages)  [Limit membership to visitors you approve](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-restricted-store-pages#limiting-access-to-members-you-approve)  [Hide store elements from search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-restricted-store-pages#hiding-store-elements-from-search-engines)

### Restricting access to store pages

You can restrict access to those pages where you display products. This can include the Category Page, the Shop Page, or any page to which you've added a Product Gallery.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. [Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).
2. Click **Pages & Menu ** on the left side of the editor.
3. Choose the page you want to restrict:

- **Category Page or Shop Page:**

1. Click **Store Pages**.
2. Click **Category Page** or **Shop**.
- **A site page with a Product Gallery:**

1. Click **Site Menu**.
2. Click the page with the Product Gallery you want to restrict.
4. Click the **Settings ** or **More Actions **** **icon on the page.

### Creating retail & restricted wholesale store pages

If you sell both retail and wholesale, you can create regular store pages for your retail customers and then duplicate and edit your products for sale on your wholesale page.

#### To create retail and wholesale store pages:

Duplicate each of the products that you want to sell wholesale.   Edit the wholesale products:

1. Set the price of each wholesale product.
2. Mention how many products are sold at the wholesale price, either in the product name or description or use [product options](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-customizing-product-options) to sell at set quantities.

**Note:** Your inventory does not update automatically according to this number.
    Create a new category for the wholesale products and add the relevant products. [Learn how](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-and-displaying-product-collections)  Create a new store page and select a category to display.  Change the privacy settings of the page.

**Tip: **Follow the instructions in the previous section above.  [Hide the new wholesale page from search engines](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-preventing-search-engines-from-indexing-your-page).   [Stop each of your wholesale products from being indexed](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-seo-for-product-pages#hiding-an-individual-product-from-search-engines).

**Note: **This prevents the product from appearing in search engine result and in site searches if you [added a search box to your site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-optimizing-a-search-box-for-stores).

### Limiting access to members you approve

You can restrict access to visitors you approve manually. When visitors sign in to your site, they receive a message explaining that their member login request is awaiting approval. You receive an email and a notification in your dashboard prompting you to accept or decline the request.

#### To limit membership to approved members:

[Go to Signup & Login Security](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmember-permissions/signup-login-settings%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Member+Settings) in your site's dashboard.  Select whether you want everyone to be able to sign up or only people you manually approve under **Who can be a site member?**.

Click **Save**.

> **What's next?**
>
> After you receive a site member request, go to your Site Members tab to [approve or block the member request](https://support.wix.com/en/article/approving-a-site-member).
