---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Customizing the Confirmation Email You Receive When Orders Are Placed"
---

Currently, it's not possible to customize the confirmation email sent to you (the seller) when an order is placed in Wix Stores.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
