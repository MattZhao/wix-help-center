---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Minimum or Maximum Quantity of Products Required to Checkout"
---

We're thrilled to announce that it's now possible to limit the sale of a specific item to a minimum quantity (e.g. minimum order is 6 unicorn mugs) and/or a maximum quantity (e.g. sale of unicorn mugs is limited to 1 per customer) using our Checkout Requirements app.

Learn how to [add and set up the Wix Checkout Requirements app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-checkout-requirements-app).
