---
title: "Wix Stores Request: Reverting Back to Same Place in a Gallery"
---

Currently, when a customer navigates from the product gallery to a different site page and then back to the product gallery, they do not return to the specific place in the gallery that was last viewed.

This means, for example, if the customer scrolled down in the gallery, when they go back, they are at the top. In addition, any filters the customer used no longer appear.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
