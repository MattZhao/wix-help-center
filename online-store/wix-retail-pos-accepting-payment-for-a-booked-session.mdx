---
title: "Wix Retail POS: Accepting Payment for a Booked Session"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This article is about **Wix Retail POS** tablet for brick & mortar businesses.
> - For info on using **POS on a mobile device**, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/mobile-pos-4564663).

When clients arrive for a session they have booked, you can use your point of sale to accept payment for the session.

> **Note:**
>
> Learn how to [book a new appointment at your POS and accept payment](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-accepting-payment-for-a-booked-session).

---

### Step 1 | Select a session

Start by going to the **Schedule **tab and selecting the session you want to accept payment for.  You can only accept payment for sessions occurring today.

#### To select a Wix Bookings session:

Open the POS tablet.   Tap **Checkout** on the bottom bar.   Tap **Schedule** on the left.  Tap the relevant session.  (Optional) Mark attendance or edit the sale:

- Tap **Actions**.

- **Edit sale: **Remove a session from an order with multiple sessions.
- **Mark attendance: **Mark the customer as **Checked-in** or **No-show**.

---

### Step 2 | Accept payment and complete checkout

Accept cash payments or credit cards using your card reader. You can even accept payment from 2 or more payment methods (e.g. 2 credit cards).

> **Notes:**
>
> - Card readers accept all major credit cards and payments made through the following payment apps: Apple Pay, Android Pay, Samsung Pay, VISA Paywave, MasterCard PayPass, AMEX ExpressPay and Discover D-PAS.
> - Canadian merchants: Interac [processing fees](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-service-fees) are $0.15 CAD.
> - You can [hide payment options you don't use](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-disabling-payment-methods).

#### To accept payment:

Tap **Continue to Payment**.  (Optional) Edit the payment amount.  Tap **Charge**.  Choose the relevant payment method:

### Accept credit card payment

1. Tap **Card**.
2. Use the card reader to swipe, dip (insert) or tap the card. 

**Note: **Swiping is not available for all card readers.
3. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Print Receipt**.
- Tap **Add a Note:** Enter an internal note (not visible to your customer) and tap **Save**.
4. Tap **Done**.

**Note: **To read notes and view receipts, go to your [Sales History](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-your-sales-history).

### Accept cash payment

1. Tap **Cash**.
2. Select the amount received from your customer.

**Note:** If you can't find the right amount, tap **Other **to enter a custom amount.
3. (If applicable) Give your customer their change listed under **Change Due**.
4. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- Tap **Print Receipt**.
- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Text Receipt**, enter the number, including country code, and tap **Text Receipt**.
- Tap **Add a Note:** Enter an internal note (not visible to your customer) and tap **Save**.
5. Tap **Done**.

**Notes: **
- To read notes and view receipts, go to your [Sales History](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-your-sales-history).
- Learn how to [enable text receipts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-setting-up-text-receipts).

### Accept payment by gift card

Before you can accept payment by gift card, you need to [create gift cards](https://support.wix.com/en/article/setting-up-wix-gift-cards-9451439) and [enable the payment by gift card feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-selling-virtual-gift-cards).
**To accept payment by gift card:**
1. Tap **Gift Card**.
2. Enter or scan the gift card code and tap **Apply**.
3. If there is still money owed to complete the purchase, Tap **Add Payment **and process the balance using another payment method, such as cash or a credit card.
4. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- Tap **Print Receipt**.
- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Text Receipt**, enter the number, including country code, and tap **Text Receipt**.
- Tap **Add a Note:** Enter an internal note (not visible to your customer) and tap **Save**.
5. Tap **Done**.

**Notes: **
- To read notes and view receipts, go to your [Sales History](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-your-sales-history).
- Learn how to [enable text receipts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-setting-up-text-receipts).

### Manually enter credit card details

**Important: **Entering credit card details manually is the least secure way of accepting card payments. We recommend accepting manual payment only from buyers you trust. Note that in case of fraud, it is the merchant who is liable. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/best-practices-for-accepting-card-payments#handling-offline-card-payments) 
**To manually enter credit card details:**
1. Tap **Manual Card Entry**.
2. Enter all the credit card details and tap **Charge**.
3. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- Tap **Print Receipt**.
- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Text Receipt**, enter the number, including country code, and tap **Text Receipt**.
- Tap **Add a Note:** Enter an internal note (not visible to your customer) and tap **Save**.
4. Tap **Done**.

**Notes:** 
- To minimize the fraud risk, as soon as the value of your transactions adds up to over 30% of your gross payment volume (GPV), they start to be declined. If you want to make changes to this limitation, contact Wix Customer Care.
- To read notes and view receipts, go to your [Sales History](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-your-sales-history).
- Learn how to [enable text receipts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-setting-up-text-receipts).

### Accept split payment (more than one payment method)

Using split payments, customers can pay using more than one payment method (e.g. using 2 credit cards or both cash and credit card).
**To process split payments:** 
1. Tap **Split Total** at the top right.
2. Enter the first payment amount.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. Select a payment method (e.g. cash) and complete the first part of the payment.
5. Tap **Continue**.
6. (Optional) Edit the second payment amount.

**Tip: **To balance left to pay is calculated for you. You can edit the amount if the customer wants to split the payment into more than 2 payments.
7. Tap **Continue**.
8. Select a payment method (e.g. card) and complete the 2nd part of the payment.
9. Tap **Continue**.
10. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- Tap **Print Receipt**.
- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Text Receipt**, enter the number, including country code, and tap **Text Receipt**.
- Tap **Add a Note:** Enter an internal note (not visible to your customer) and tap **Save**.
11. Tap **Done**.

**Notes: **
- To read notes and view receipts, go to your [Sales History](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-your-sales-history).
- Learn how to [enable text receipts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-setting-up-text-receipts).

### Accepting partial payment (deposit)

You can accept a partial payment now and charge your customer the rest in one or more payments made later. 
**To accept the first partial payment for an order:**
1. Tap **Split Total** at the top right.
2. Enter the first payment amount.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. Select a payment method (e.g. cash) and complete the first part of the payment.
5. Tap **Continue**.
6. Tap **Mark as Partially Paid**.
7. Tap **Mark as Partially Paid** to confirm.
8. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- Tap **Print Receipt**.
- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Text Receipt**, enter the number, including country code, and tap **Text Receipt**.
- Tap **Add a Note:** Enter an internal note (not visible to your customer) and tap **Save**.
9. Tap **Done**.

**To collect additional payment(s):**
1. Tap **Sales History** from the bottom bar.
2. Select the relevant sale.
3. Tap **Collect**.
4. Select a payment type:

- **Collect the balance owed:** Process payment as you would for any other sale. The balance amount appears automatically.
- **Collect another partial payment:**  Tap **Split Total** and collect another partial payment.

### Accepting other types of payment

The other payment option lets you accept payment through a payment method which is not integrated with your POS system (e.g. though an app or physical gift card).
**Important: **Since you receive direct payment from your client, you do not receive this amount as a Wix POS payout.
**To process other payment types:**
1. Tap **Other**.
2. Enter a brief note documenting how the customer paid you (e.g. Venmo payment).

**Note: **The note appears in customer's receipts and in the Sales History tab in the tablet.
3. Tap **Record Payment**.
4. (Optional) Do one or more of the following:

- Tap **Print Receipt**.
- Tap **Email Receipt**, enter the email address if prompted, and tap **Send**.
- Tap **Text Receipt**, enter the number, including country code, and tap **Text Receipt**.
- Tap **Add a Note:** Enter an internal note (not visible to your customer) and tap **Save**.
5. Tap **Done**.

**Notes: **
- To read notes and view receipts, go to your [Sales History](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-managing-your-sales-history).
- Learn how to [enable text receipts](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-retail-pos-setting-up-text-receipts).

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more.

### Can I accept payment through a membership plan or package?

It's not currently possible to accept payment using a membership plan or package at Wix Retail POS.

### Can I add tax to a completed POS sale?

No, it's not possible to add tax after you've completed POS transaction.

### Can I accept payment for a session that'll take place tomorrow or later in the future?

Currently, you can only accept payment for sessions scheduled for today.
