With the GetTraffic app by Infolinks Media, you can expose your site to increased traffic flow. Select your geography, choose a category, and customize your ad - GetTraffic will do the rest.

> **Important:**
>
> This app is added to your site's dashboard and does not appear on your site.

### Features and benefits include:

Define and activate your campaign in 3 steps  Drive high-quality traffic to your site  Expose your site to relevant users  Customize your ad

Check out the [GetTraffic](https://www.wix.com/app-market/gettraffic) app by Infolinks Media to get started.
