### Managing your billing details

Manage all payment and billing details for your Pro plan, including updating your payment method and how often you get billed.

#### To manage your billing details:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.   Click **Settings** on the left.   Click **Upgrade** under **Manage Hopp Plan**.

Click **Update payment method**.  Edit your payment details in the **Update Payment Method** section.  Click **Update** on the bottom right.

---

### Viewing your invoices

Once you have upgraded your Hopp account to a Pro plan and have started to get charged for it, you can view your invoices at any time.

#### To view your Hopp invoices:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.   Click **Settings** on the left.  Click **Manage Plan**.  Click **View invoices**.

### How can I upgrade my Hopp plan to the Pro plan?

To upgrade to a Pro plan:

1. Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.
2. Click **Upgrade** on the top right.
3. Select the type of plan you want to purchase:

- **Monthly: **You will be billed once monthly.
- **Yearly: **You will be billed once annually. Purchasing a yearly plan will mean you get a discounted price.
4. Click **Upgrade** on the bottom right.
5. Enter your card details in the **Checkout** section.
6. Click **Submit Payment** at the bottom.
7. Click **Get Started** to being using your Pro features.

### How can I cancel my Hopp Pro plan auto-renewal?

If you wish to cancel your Hopp Pro plan, you can do so by following the steps below: 

1. Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.
2. Click **Settings** on the left.
3. Click **Manage on Wix **under** Manage Hopp Plan. **
4. Click the More Actions icon  next to your Hopp plan.
5. Click **Cancel Hopp**.
