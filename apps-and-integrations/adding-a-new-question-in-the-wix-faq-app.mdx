---
title: "Adding a New Question in the Wix FAQ App"
---

With the Wix FAQ app, you can give your visitors quick answers to common questions about your products or services.

Add as many questions as you want and customize with images, videos, GIFs, and emojis. You can also add content from your social media accounts including Twitter posts, YouTube videos, and TikTok videos.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want to copy questions and answers from another document and paste them into the FAQ app, be sure to [remove all formatting](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-copying-text-from-an-external-source-eg-word-document) first.

---

### To add new questions and answers:

Click the FAQ app in your editor.  Click **Manage Questions**.

Note:** **If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.  Click **Add New**.  Select **Question & Answer** from the drop-down menu.   Enter your new question under **Question**.  Add an answer for your question under **Answer**.  (Optional) Add media to your answer using the icons on the bottom of the editor. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-media-to-an-answer-in-the-wix-faq-app)

Select a category for your new question under **Choose a category**, or [add a new category](https://support.wix.com/en/article/working-with-categories-in-the-wix-faq-app).

Click **Save**.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can [add linked text to your answer ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-questions-in-the-wix-faq-app#adding-linked-text-to-an-answer)by selecting the text, clicking the Link icon  and adding either a link to a website or to a section on your site.
