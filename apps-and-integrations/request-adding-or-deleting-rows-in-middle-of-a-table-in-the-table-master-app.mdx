---
title: "Request: Adding or Deleting Rows in Middle of a Table in the Table Master App"
---

Currently, the only way to add or delete rows from the middle of a table in the Table Master app is to manually move them by copying and pasting the content.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
