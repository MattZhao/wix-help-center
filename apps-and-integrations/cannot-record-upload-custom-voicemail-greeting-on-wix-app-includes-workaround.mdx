---
title: "Cannot Record/Upload Custom Voicemail Greeting on Wix App (Includes Workaround)"
---

Some sites might experience an issue where you are unable to record or upload a custom voicemail greeting for your Business Phone Number on the Wix mobile app.

> **Workaround**
>
> Record your greeting as WAV or MP3 and upload via the desktop dashboard in your Business Phone Number settings.

> If your WAV file is over 2mb, please [convert](https://convertio.co/) your file to MP3.
