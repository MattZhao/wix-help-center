---
title: "Download My App is No Longer Available"
---

Download My App has been retired from the [Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market). As we no longer support Download My App, your site visitors are no longer able to download and interact with your site's mobile app through that tool.

As an alternative, you can add the Download the Mobile App application from the Wix App Market. Download the Mobile App offers the abilities to:

Boost engagement by inviting visitors to interact with your business from anywhere  Send visitors a direct link to download and join you on the app  Customize the SMS that your visitors will receive  Choose the text, design and more to match your brand

In addition, Download the Mobile App boasts a modern design and an improved performance on the previous app.

### To add Download the Mobile App:

[Go to the Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market/download-my-app-blocks).  Search for 'Download the Mobile App'.  Click **Add to Site** under **Download the Mobile App**.
