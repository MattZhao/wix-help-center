---

With the Uploadly - File Upload app by Inspon, you can add powerful upload fields to your product pages. Let your customers upload photos, images and files from their computer or mobile device.

> **Important:**
>
> - This app is only available in English.
> - To use this integration on your site, you need to [add Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores).

### Features and benefits include:

Receive files with your orders  Create unlimited upload fields and add them to your products  Customize your upload button to match your site  Let your customers edit their images before they submit them

Check out the [Uploadly - File Upload](https://www.wix.com/app-market/uploadly-file-upload) app by Inspon to get started.
