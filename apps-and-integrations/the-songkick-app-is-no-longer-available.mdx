---
title: "The Songkick App is No Longer Available"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This app has been retired from the Wix App Market and is no longer supported.
> - If you would like to continue adding tour dates from Songkick, you can enter the tour dates in Songkick and [embed the code in your Wix Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/embedding-songkick-tour-dates-on-your-wix-site).
