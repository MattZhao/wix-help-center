Open yourself to a global market by selling your products online. Wix offers a variety of eCommerce apps. Learn more about the available apps and see which solution is right for you.

### Wix Stores

Wix Stores offers you all the tools you need to create a professional online store and start marketing your products. All your sales are commission free!

With Wix Stores you can:

Sell physical products or digital downloads  Accept PayPal, credit cards & offline payments  Promote your store with integrated email marketing  Ensure a secure SSL-certificate checkout  Manage your store on the go with the Wix app  Add shipping & pickup options  Create coupons & sales

To learn more about Wix Stores, click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-wix-stores).

> **Note:**
>
> Wix Stores requires a [Business Premium Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-upgrading-your-sites-plan).

### Art Store App

If you have a photography collection, you can use the Art Store app to sell your images as digital downloads or market your images on mugs, canvases, bags and more. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-art-store-about-the-wix-art-store)

> **Note:**
>
> The Wix Art Store app is free to use.

### Etsy Store App

Increase traffic to your Etsy shop by displaying your shop on your Wix website. You can customize the look of your site to match your design preferences and make sales with credit cards and PayPal.

> **Note:**
>
> The Etsy app is free to use.
