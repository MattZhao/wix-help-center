---
title: "Adding Tasks & Reminders from the Wix App"
---

Organize and prioritize your work on the go using **Tasks & Reminders** in the Wix app. Add new tasks, assign them to contacts or staff members, and mark them as complete when you're done.

> **Note:**
>
> This section includes Tasks & Reminders from the [Contact Panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-a-task-from-the-contact-card) and the [Automation Panel](https://support.wix.com/en/article/creating-tasks-instantly-with-wix-automations).

### To add a new task:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Dashboard**  at the bottom.  Scroll down to **Tasks & Reminders**.   Tap **Create New Task**.   Write your task's content in the text box.   (Optional) Add any additional information you need according to the task:

### Tell me more

Other than writing your task's content, you can add more information and set a due date to make sure we remind you to complete it:

**To assign the task to a collaborator:** 
1. Tap **Add** next to **Assign To**.
2. Tap the relevant collaborator.

**To set a due date for the task: **
1. Tap **Add** next to **Set Due Date**.
2. Select the date.
3. Tap **Done**.

**To assign the task to a contact:** 
1. Tap** Add** next to **Select a Contact**.
2. Tap the relevant contact.

**Note:** You can modify the information by tapping **Edit** next to the relevant section.

7. Tap **Add** at the top right to save the task.
