---
title: "The VKontakte App is No Longer Available"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This app has been retired from the Wix App Market and is no longer supported.
> - If you would like to remain using the VKontakte (VK), Community app, you can [create a widget in your VKontakte account ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/embedding-a-community-widget-on-your-site)and embed the code in your Wix Editor. This allows you to continue adding VK widgets to your Wix site.
