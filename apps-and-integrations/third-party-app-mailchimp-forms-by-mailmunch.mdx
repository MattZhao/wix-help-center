With the Mailchimp Forms app by MailMunch, you can grow your email subscribers and convert them into customers. Discover which pages, products, or Mailchimp forms get the most conversions and optimize your lead generation strategy.

> **Important:**
>
> To use this app, you need to have a [Premium Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) and a [connected domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domains).

### Features and benefits include:

Sync your Wix Contacts to Mailchimp  Choose from 100s of pre-designed templates designed to maximize conversions  Customize using our highly intuitive drag-drop builder  Choose from a variety of form types including pop-ups, scroll boxes, topbars, and embedded forms  Offer discount coupons or free giveaways while collecting email addresses from your visitors
