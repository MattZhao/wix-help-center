---
title: "Third-Party App: QuickBooks Connector By Intuit"
---

With the QuickBooks Connector app by Intuit, you can automate your accounting by connecting your site with QuickBooks Online. Save hours of manually entering invoices, customers, and products from one app to another.

> **Important:**
>
> - To use this integration on your site, you need to add one of the following apps first: [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores), [Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings), [Wix Events](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-wix-events-to-your-site), or [Wix Hotels](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-wix-hotels).
> - This app is only available in English.

### Features and benefits include:

Retrieve orders from your Wix Store, Wix Bookings, Wix Events, or Wix Hotels  Create new Items in your QuickBooks Online account when you create products in your store  Automatically create sales in your QuickBooks Online for orders that customers make on your site  Map tax codes from your store orders to tax codes in your QuickBooks Online  Eliminate human error from manual data entry between your QuickBooks Online and your website

Check out the [QuickBooks Connector](https://www.wix.com/app-market/2374b1f8-39ba-4d4c-bfd3-2fdec89ee4c4/overview) app by Intuit to get started.
