With the Rabbit SEO app by SEO Tools, you can find and monitor the right keywords for your site to boost traffic and rank higher on search engines. Get the data you need to target engaged users.

> **Note:**
>
> This app is added to your site's dashboard - it does not appear on your site.

### Features and benefits include:

Find and monitor the right keywords for your website  Explore the top keywords of your competitors  Know who visits your site and learn how they got there  Get hints and tips about improving your site's search engine ranking

Check out the [Rabbit SEO](https://www.wix.com/app-market/rabbit-seo) app by SEO Tools to get started.
