---
title: "The Tumblr Feed App is No Longer Available"
---

> **Important:**
>
> - This app has been retired from the Wix App Market and is no longer supported. As a workaround, you can [embed Tumblr content on your Wix site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/embedding-your-tumblr-blog-and-posts).
> - The Tumblr app page will appear blank to your visitors, we recommend deleting the app to avoid having a blank page on your site.
