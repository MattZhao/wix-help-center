With the Nash: All-in-1 Local Delivery app, you can seamlessly connect your store to a global network of delivery providers—including Uber, Relay, and Roadie.

> **Important:**
>
> - This app is only available in English.
> - In order to use this app, you need to add [Wix Restaurant Orders](https://www.wix.com/app-market/wix-restaurants-orders) to your site.

### Features and benefits include:

Get all-in-one access to popular fleets like Uber, Relay, Roadie, and more.  Personalize tracking links and notifications to keep customers in the loop.  Provide hassle-free in-store pickup options for easy convenience.

Check out the [Nash: All-in-1 Local Delivery](https://www.wix.com/app-market/nash-all-in-1-local-delivery) app, by Nash Technologies Inc. to get started.
