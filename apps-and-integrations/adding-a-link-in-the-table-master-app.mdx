---

### To add a link to your table:

Click the table in your Editor.
 Click **Settings**.
 Hover over the cell that you want to link.
 Click the **Link** icon .
 Choose the link type.
 Enter the link details.
 Click **OK**.

> **Important:**
>
> It is not possible to import links by uploading a Google Spreadsheet due to Google Drive's API.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also [create a table using Velo by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connect-table-3134954).
