With the Boom Event Calendar by BoomTech, you can schedule your classes, tour dates, meetups, appointments, upcoming parties, and more. Share events on different social media platforms, collect event registrations, and manage guestlists.

### Features and benefits include:

Display events in Monthly, Weekly, Daily, Agenda, Map, and Card layouts  Sync the Boom Events Calendar with your external calendars: Google, Outlook, Zoom, Eventbrite, or Facebook  Filter events by colored categories  Enable registration or allow visitors to add their events to a public calendar

Check out the [Boom Event Calendar](https://www.wix.com/app-market/calendar) app by BoomTech to get started.
