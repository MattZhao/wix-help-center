title: "Recommended Apps for Fitness Studio Owners"
---

Wix offers you all the tools you need to manage your fitness studio.

---

### Wix Bookings

Wix Bookings is a comprehensive scheduling solution that allows clients to book different kinds of services. Whether you and your staff offer personal training sessions, drop-in classes, or full courses - you can manage it all with Wix Bookings. [Learn more about Wix Bookings](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-wix-bookings)

Click [here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/recommended-scheduling-apps) to learn about other available scheduling apps.

---

### Wix member apps

You can customize the member apps (Fit by Wix, Wix Branded app and Spaces by Wix) on your dashboard, or through the Wix mobile app. Members can book your services, join a waitlist, chat with you, and more. When members arrive for a class, you or the staff member giving the class can mark attendance right in the app. Learn more about [Fit by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/fit-by-wix-app-an-overview), [branded app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/branded-app-by-wix-an-overview) and [Spaces app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/spaces-by-wix-an-overview).

---

### The Wix mobile app

You can use the Wix mobile app to edit your site for your member apps. You can control and customize how your business looks on the member apps and communicate with your members directly. The Wix app also allows you to run your fitness studio/business from anywhere. [Learn more about the Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).

---

### Wix Stores

Do you sell gym equipment, t-shirts, or other products at your gym? You can use Wix Stores to create a stunning online shop and make sales through your app. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-wix-stores)

---

### Wix FAQ App

Answer the most frequently asked questions of your clients by adding an FAQ page to your site. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-faq-app)
