With the Smile: Points & Rewards app by Smile, you can build an engaging and modern rewards program experience that seamlessly aligns with your existing brand. Match your store design with logos, banner images, advanced color options, and more.

> **Important:**
>
> - To use this app, you need to have a [Premium Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan), a [connected domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domains), and [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores).
> - This app is only available in English, French, German, Portuguese, and Spanish.

### Features and benefits include:

Launch a loyalty program on your store in less than a day. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-creating-a-loyalty-program-with-the-smile-points-rewards-app)  Boost repeat purchases using points, referrals, and exclusive offers  Reward customers for actions like placing an order, social sharing, or celebrating a birthday  Create an eye-catching pop-up rewards panel that’s easy to customize

Check out the [Smile: Points and Rewards](https://www.wix.com/app-market/smile-points-rewards) app by Smile to get started.
