### Benefits of adding a third-party app to your site

Third-party apps are professional solutions designed to help enhance your site. Apps that add more to your site design, improve your customers overall experience when they visit your site. This can lead to a positive impact in sales and increase in engagement.

#### Search

Search functionality is the key to ensuring your customers have a good experience on your site. However, search bars require indexing which can be a time consuming process. Fortunately, you can find apps in the Wix App Market that help make the process easier.

#### Buttons and menus

Adding elements such as buttons and menus can really help provide a positive customer experience. These elements give your customers more ways to navigate your site. Menus on your header can be fixed to appear on each site page, and buttons like "Buy Now" can take customers directly to the checkout process.

#### Banners and badges

Banners are a great way to communicate important information to your customers. Some of the apps available help automate the process of scheduling your banners and the content to appear on your site for a specific duration.

#### Image effects

Some of the apps focus on image effects and how they can enhance the overall look and feel of your site. There are also some really nice seasonal effects that you can add to your site for certain periods of the year.

### What is a third-party app?

A third-party app is an app that is made by other developers or companies. They are not considered a product of Wix. While they are not our apps, we do provide access to them from our App Market. This gives you more variety when it comes to optimizing your site.

### How can I contact support for help with my app?

If you find that you need help with a particular app, the best thing to do is contact the app developers directly. Developers contact details (website and/or email) are provided in every app listing in the Wix App Market.

### What happens if an app is removed from the Wix App Market?

Wix is committed to bring you the best app experience possible which means older apps or apps that are no longer available are removed from the Wix App Market. When an app is removed from the Wix App Market is added to our [list of removed apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/third-party-apps-apps-removed-from-the-wix-app-market).We recommend checking the status of the app in your dashboard as it might need to be deleted from your site.
**Note:** The app details, including the support details, are removed from the list after 90 days.
