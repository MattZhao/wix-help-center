The Wix App Market is filled with over 800 popular web apps, powerful services, and great features to enhance your website and grow your business. You can choose from apps made by Wix such as [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-wix-stores) and [Wix Blog](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-about-the-wix-blog), or from one of the many third-party apps.

Most of the apps are free to use, mobile optimized, and can be added to your site in just a few clicks. Some apps have both a free and premium version while others require [a premium upgrade](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-app-from-the-wix-app-market).

### Adding apps to your site

You can add apps to your site from the [Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market/), from the [App Market tab](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp-market%2Fmy-apps&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Add+Apps) in your dashboard. You can also access the App Market by clicking **Add Apps** ** **in the Editor or **App Market**  in the Studio Editor.

### Managing your apps

After you add apps to your site, you can manage your apps from [Manage Apps](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fmanage-installed-apps%2Fmy-apps&title=Select+a+Site&primaryButtonText=Manage+Apps) in your dashboard.

From the **Manage Apps** tab, you can:

Access your apps  Get info and support for your apps   Rate and review your apps   Get more apps   [Update your apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/updating-an-app-from-the-wix-app-market)   [Upgrade your apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-app-from-the-wix-app-market)  [Delete apps](https://support.wix.com/en/article/deleting-apps) you no longer need

### Managing your Wix apps in the My Business panel

You can access additional options for your Wix apps by clicking **My Business**  in your site's editor.

Use the quick access links to your app's dashboard options and additional elements to enhance your business. [Learn more](https://support.wix.com/en/article/managing-your-wix-apps-from-the-my-business-panel)
