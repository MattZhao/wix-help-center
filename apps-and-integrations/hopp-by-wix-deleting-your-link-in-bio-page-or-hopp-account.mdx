title: "Hopp by Wix: Deleting Your Link In Bio Page or Hopp Account"
---

If you decide you want to delete your Link in Bio page or close your Hopp account, you can do it at any time. Be cautious when deleting a Link in Bio page as this action cannot be undone. If you want to close your Hopp account, make sure you're ready to delete it, as it can only be done with the assistance of the Wix Customer Care team.

> **Important:**
>
> Only the person who created the Link in Bio page can delete it.

**In this article, learn more about:**

[Deleting your Link in Bio page](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-deleting-your-link-in-bio-page-or-hopp-account#deleting-your-link-in-bio-page)  [Closing your Hopp account](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-deleting-your-link-in-bio-page-or-hopp-account#closing-your-hopp-account)

---

### Deleting your Link in Bio page

You can delete a Link in Bio page directly from your Hopp dashboard.

#### To delete a Link in Bio page:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.  Click **Link in Bio** on the left.  Click the** More Actions** icon .  Click **Delete page**.

---

### Closing your Hopp account

To delete your Hopp account, reach out to the Wix Customer Care team to assist you. Learn more about [contacting Wix Customer Care](https://support.wix.com/en/article/contacting-wix-customer-care-for-support).
