---

With the NetSuite Sync app by Webgility, you can put your ecommerce accounting, inventory, and shipping on autopilot. Save time and reduce errors caused by manual data entry.

> **Important:**
>
> - To use this app, you need to [add Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores).
> - This app is only available in English.

### Features and benefits include:

Automatically sync order info between your site and your accounting solution  Put manual tasks on autopilot and receive notifications when tasks are performed  Publish essential details about your products from your accounting solution to your site  Ensure inventory quantities and pricing are always up to date on your site

Check out the [NetSuite Sync](https://www.wix.com/app-market/netsuite-sync-by-webgility) app by Webgility to get started.
