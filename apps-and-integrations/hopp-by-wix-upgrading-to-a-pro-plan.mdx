Upgrade your Hopp by Wix account to a Pro plan to enjoy lots of extra features. With Hopp Pro, you can connect a custom domain and remove the Hopp branding from your Link in Bio page to really make it your own.

The Pro plan also unlocks advanced design options and useful creator tools like [Short Links](https://www.wix.com/hopp/dashboard/links/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=\{upgrading-pro-plan\}), [Pre-Rolls](https://www.wix.com/hopp/dashboard/pre-rolls/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=\{upgrading-pro\}) and [Instant Pages](https://www.wix.com/hopp/dashboard/landing-pages/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=\{upgrading-pro-plan\}).

### What is included when I upgrade to a Pro plan?

Firstly, you get to personalize the Link in Bio page even more:
- [Connect a custom domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-connecting-a-custom-domain-to-your-link-in-bio-page).
- Remove the Hopp branding (e.g. the ad banner).
- Access advanced layout and design options (e.g. extra fonts, button styles, list types).
- Show a verified account badge.

You also get access to these useful tools, alongside the option to track their performance: 
- [Short Links](https://www.wix.com/hopp/dashboard/links/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=\{upgrading-pro-plan\}): Shorten and rebrand links you want to share with your followers.
- [Pre-Rolls](https://www.wix.com/hopp/dashboard/pre-rolls/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=\{upgrading-pro\}): Show your followers a promotion whenever they click a link on your page.
- [Instant Pages](https://www.wix.com/hopp/dashboard/landing-pages/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=\{upgrading-pro-plan\}): Instantly create and publish pages with the content you want to share.

Lastly, if you have a Wix site, a Pro plan lets you sync contact data seamlessly between your site and Hopp. For example, contacts that you collect via Hopp would automatically appear on your Wix site's contact list.

### Upgrading to a Hopp Pro plan

When you upgrade to Hopp Pro, you get access to many features and benefits to help you take your Link in Bio page to the next level. You can choose whether you want to be billed monthly or yearly.

#### To upgrade to a Pro plan:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.   Click **Upgrade** at the top right. 

[image: Clicking the Upgrade button in the Hopp account]

  Select the type of plan you want to purchase:

- **Monthly: **You will be billed once monthly.
- **Yearly: **You will be billed once annually, at a discounted price.
    Click **Upgrade** to confirm.  Enter your payment method details and click **Submit Payment**.  Click **Get Started** to being using your Pro features.

### I've already upgraded my Wix site. Do I need to purchase a Hopp Pro plan?

Yes. The Hopp Pro plans are separate from the Wix plans. If you'd like to benefit from the Hopp Pro features, you'll need to purchase a monthly or yearly plan.

### How can I cancel my Hopp Pro plan auto-renewal?

If you wish to cancel your Hopp Pro plan, you can do so by following the steps: 
1. Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.
2. Click **Settings** on the left.
3. Click **Manage on Wix **under** Manage Hopp Plan. **
4. Click the **More Action**s icon  next to your Hopp plan.
5. Click **Cancel Hopp**.
