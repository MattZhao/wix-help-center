title: "Third-Party App: Etsy Integration by Webkul"
---

With the Etsy Integration app, you can sync your products from Wix to Etsy and sync your orders from Etsy to Wix. Store owners can easily manage the inventory on both platforms.

> **Important:**
>
> - This app is only available in English.
> - To use this app on your site, you need to have [Wix Stores](https://www.wix.com/app-market/wix-stores), a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) and a [connected domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domains).

### Features and benefits include:

Export your Wix products to your Etsy seller account.  Category mapping option is available from Wix to Etsy.  Export your Etsy orders to your Wix stores.  Once a product quantity sell on either the Wix store or the Etsy store inventory will be synced accordingly on both sides.

Check out the [Etsy Integration](https://www.wix.com/app-market/etsy-integration) app by Webkul to get started.
