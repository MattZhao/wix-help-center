With the Events Calendar app by inlight labs, you can add a beautiful calendar to your site with one click and let your audience know about upcoming events. Connect your external calendars and automatically display all the events in one place.

### Features and benefits include:

Monthly, Weekly, and Agenda layouts  Sync with Google, Outlook, or Eventbrite calendars  Choose from over 30 languages  Add images, links, save event buttons, and more

Check out the [Events Calendar](https://www.wix.com/app-market/events-calendar) app by inlight labs to get started.
