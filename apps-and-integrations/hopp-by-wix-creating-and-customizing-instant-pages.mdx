### Creating Instant Pages

You can create an Instant Page for any need and design it as you wish. These pages are perfect for promoting specific events, products, or announcements without the need to update your entire site.

#### To create Instant Pages:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=addingandmanagingactions) and log in.   Click **Instant Pages**.   Click **Create Instant Page**.  Hover over a page type and click **Get Started**.

Follow the steps on screen to set up the template.

**Note:** The steps vary depending on the template you choose.   Select a template under **Choose a design**.  Enter the text that is displayed on the end of the URL for your Instant Page under **Customize your URL**.  Click **Next **to complete your page.  (Optional) Click **Edit Page** to customize your Instant page.

### Customizing Instant Pages

Customize your Instant Page to ensure it aligns perfectly with your goals and your brand. You can add actions, social links, your personal content, choose the design, and adjust settings to fit your needs.

#### To customize Instant Pages:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=addingandmanagingactions) and log in.   Click **Instant Pages**.   Hover over your Instant Page and click **Edit page**.  Choose what you want to customize:

### Actions

Actions are the things you want to encourage your audience to do, like buying an outfit you posted on your social media, signing up to your event, and much more.  [Learn more about using actions.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-adding-and-managing-actions-for-link-in-bio) 
You can add more actions to your page, reorder them, hide those you don't need or remove them altogether.

### Social links

Add social links to your Instant Page to get more followers across platforms. You can choose their order, hide those you don't need, or remove the social links altogether.

### Content

Customize your Instant Page with the following options:
- **Remove Hopp Banner:** Enable the toggle to remove the Hopp banner at the bottom of your Instant Page.
- **Profile image:** Set your profile picture.
- **Profile name:** Choose what to show at the top of the page - your name, your account or a custom title.
- **Verified account badge:** Enable the toggle to add the verified account badge next to your name.
- **Bio:** Write a bio to tell your audience about yourself or the reason for your Instant Page.
- **Title:** Write a title for your Instant Page.
- **Heading:** Write a description for your Instant Page.

### Design

Design your Instant Page including layout, background, selecting your colors, font, and button style. You can change the design completely, or change the design while keeping the page layout.

### Settings

Choose your settings that work best for you:
- **Page language: **Set your Instant page to a specific language.
- **Page URL:** Edit the text that is displayed on the end of the URL for your Instant Page.
- **Page: **Optimize the page for SEO and see how it looks when shared on social media.
- **Branding: **Add a favicon to appear on the browser tab showing your Instant Page.
- **Marketing:** Add Google Analytics to gain insight and collect data from all of your Hopp Pages.
- **GDPR: **Add a Cookie banner and/or Privacy & Terms.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about Instant Pages.

### When should I create an Instant Page?

Create an Instant Page when you want to:
- Promote a major initiative, like a sale or event.
- Share exclusive deals and content with VIP customers.
- Enhance customer interaction at your physical location.
- Create fun and personal pages for friends and family, such as birthday invitations or thank you pages.

### How can I share Instant Pages with my audience?

1. Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=addingandmanagingactions) and log in.
2. Click **Instant Pages**.
3. Hover over your page and Click **Manage Page**.
4. Click the **Share** icon .
5. Select how you want to share your Instant Page:

- **Copy URL**
- **Open in a new tab**
- **Generate QR code**
- **Share on social **
- **Add to your social bio

**[image: Screenshot showing the sharing options of your instant page.]

### Can Instant Pages be used for personal purposes?

Yes, Instant Pages can be used for personal purposes such as creating birthday invitations, thank you pages with gift links, activity pages for kids, or trip agendas with links to attractions.

### Can I send Instant Pages through email marketing campaigns?

Yes, you can add the link to an Instant Page into any email marketing campaigns. This will give easy access to special offers, events, and product launches while personalizing the content for your most loyal customers.
