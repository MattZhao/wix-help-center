### Adding a new question

You can add as many questions as you want to your FAQ and customize them with images, videos, GIFs, maps, and emojis. You can also add content from your Twitter and YouTube accounts.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you want to copy questions and answers from another document and paste them into the FAQ app, be sure to [remove all formatting](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-copying-text-from-an-external-source-eg-word-document) first.

#### Learn how to add a question in:

### Your site's editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Questions**.

Note: If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Click **Add New**.
4. Select **Question & Answer** from the drop-down menu.
5. Enter your new question under **Question**.
6. Add an answer for your question under **Answer**.
7. (Optional) Add media to your answer using the icons on the bottom of the editor.
8. Select a category for your new question under **Choose a category**.
9. Click **Save**.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to FAQs in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Add New**.
3. Select **Question & Answer** from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter your new question under **Question**.
5. Add an answer for your question under **Answer**.
6. (Optional) Add media to your answer using the icons on the bottom of the editor.
7. Select a category for your new question under** Choose a category**.
8. Click **Save**.

### Editing an existing question

After you've created a question, you can edit it in your site's editor or in your dashboard.

#### Learn how to add a question in:

### Your site's editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Questions**.

Note: If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Hover over the question that you want to edit.
4. Click the Edit icon  .
5. Edit the question and answer.
6. Click **Save**.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to FAQs in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the question that you want to edit.
3. Click the Edit icon .
4. Edit the question and answer.
5. Click **Save**

### Adding linked text to an answer

You can add links to your answer by selecting some text and adding a URL to it. For example, you can add the name of a product to your answer and add a link to the name. You can also link to sections within your site.

#### Learn how to add linked text in:

### Your site's Editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Questions**.

Note: If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Hover over the question that you want to edit.
4. Click the Edit icon  .
5. Select the text that you want to add the link to.
6. Click the Link icon .
7. Select whether you want to add a link to a web address or to a section.

- **Web Address:** Enter the URL you want to link to. You can choose to open the link in a new tab. You can also choose to tell search engines to ignore this link so it won't appear in search results.
- **Section**:
8. Click **Save**.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to FAQs in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the question that you want to edit.
3. Click the Edit icon .
4. Select the text that you want to add the link to.
5. Click the Link icon .
6. Add a link to a web address or to a section on your site. Tip: You can choose to open the link in a new tab. You can also choose to tell search engines to ignore this link so it won't appear in search results.
7. Click **Save**.

### Duplicating a question

If you have a question that you want to use as a template for a new question, you can duplicate it in your site's editor or your dashboard.

#### Learn how to duplicate a question in:

### Your site's editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Questions**.

Note:** **If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Hover over the question that you want to duplicate.
4. Click the **Show More** icon .
5. Select **Duplicate** from the drop-down menu.
6. Edit the question and answer.
7. Select a new category for the question if required under **Choose a category**.
8. Click **Save**.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to FAQs in your site's dashboard
2. Hover over the question you want to edit.
3. Click the Show More icon  .
4. Select **Duplicate **from the drop-down menu.
5. Edit the question and answer.
6. Select a new category for the question if required under **Choose a category**.
7. Click **Save**.

### Sharing a question

You can share specific questions from your FAQ on Facebook, Twitter, or LinkedIn. You can also copy a direct link to specific question that you can share anywhere.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can select which social networks appear as sharing options on your FAQ in the Settings panel. [Learn More](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-faq-app)

#### Learn how to share a question from:

### Your site's editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Questions**.

Note: If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Hover over the question that you want to share.
4. Click the Show More icon  .
5. Select **Copy Link** from the drop-down menu.
6. Select a sharing option on the **Copy and share link **pop-up.

- Click **Copy** to get a direct link to the question.
- Click **Share on Facebook **to share your question on Facebook.
- Click **Share on Twitter **to share your question on Twitter.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to FAQs in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the question that you want to share.
3. Click the Show More icon  .
4. Select **Copy Link** from the drop-down menu.
5. Select a sharing option on the **Copy and share link **pop-up.

- Click **Copy** to get a direct link to the question.
- Click **Share on Facebook **to share your question on Facebook.
- Click **Share on Twitter **to share your question on Twitter.

### Your live site

1. Go to your FAQ on your live site.
2. Click the question that you want to share.
3. Select a sharing option in the bottom left corner of the question.

- Click the Facebook icon  to share your question on Facebook.
- Click the Twitter icon  to share your question on Twitter.
- Click the LinkedIn icon  to share your question on LinkedIn.
- Click the Copy icon  to get a direct link to your question.

### Deleting a question

If you don't need a specific question on your FAQ anymore, you can delete it in your site's editor or your site's dashboard.

#### Learn how to delete a question in:

### Your site's editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Questions**.

Note: If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Hover over the question that you want to delete.
4. Click the Show More icon  .
5. Select **Delete **from the drop-down menu.
6. Click **Delete **on the pop-up.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to FAQs in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the question that you want to delete.
3. Click the Show More icon  .
4. Select **Delete **from the drop-down menu.
5. Click **Delete** on the pop-up.

### Reordering your questions

If you need to change the order of your questions in your FAQ, you can reorder them in your site's editor or your site's dashboard.

#### Learn how to reorder your questions in:

### Your site's editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your editor.
2. Click **Manage Questions**.

Note: If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Hover over the question you want to move.
4. Click and drag the question to a new position.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to FAQs in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the question you want to move.
3. Click and drag the question to a new position.

### Translating a question

If you have [a multilingual site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-an-overview), you can [translate your FAQs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-multilingual-translating-your-faq) in your site's dashboard.

#### To translate your FAQ:

[Go to Multilingual](https://manage.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?siteType=HtmlWeb&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fmultilingual%3F&title=Select%20a%20site&buttonText=Go+to+Multilingual) in your site's dashboard.  (If you have more than one secondary language) Select the language to translate to from the drop-down menu beside **Translate your apps' content**.   Go to the **Wix FAQs **section.  Click **Translate **beside **Wix FAQ Questions**.   Click **Translate** beside the question you want to translate.   Enter a translation for your question, or click the **Google Translate** icon  to automatically translate it.   Click the Checkmark icon  to save your translation.   Click **View and Edit Translation **to edit your translated answer.   Click **Save**.
