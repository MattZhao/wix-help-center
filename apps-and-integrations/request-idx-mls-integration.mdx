---
title: "Request: IDX (MLS) Integration"
---

Currently, Wix does not support Internet Data Exchange (IDX) integration for real estate agents.

> **Alternative solutions:**
>
> - A third-party app solution, [iHomefinder Real Estate](https://www.wix.com/app-market/ihomefinder-real-estate), is available to download from the Wix App Market.
> - With a little coding experience, you can [integrate IDX from an external server](https://github.com/wix/wix-code-mls) using [Velo by Wix.](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-velo-by-wix)
>
> **Need some help?** Check out the [Velo forum](https://www.wix.com/code/home/forum) or hire a Velo Expert to help you.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is greatly appreciated.
