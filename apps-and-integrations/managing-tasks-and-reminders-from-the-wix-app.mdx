---
title: "Managing Tasks & Reminders from the Wix App"
---

After creating a task, you can easily edit its details, mark it as done or delete it from the list using the Wix app.

### To manage your tasks & reminders:

Go to your site in the [Wix app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-owner-app-an-overview).  Tap **Dashboard**  at the bottom.  Scroll down to **Tasks & Reminders **and choose from the following:

### Edit your task's details

1. Tap the task you want to edit.
2. Edit the task according to your needs. Change its content, contact person, assignee or due date.
3. Tap **Save** at the top right.

### Mark your task as done

Simply select the checkbox next to the task to complete it.

### Delete a task

1. Tap the task you want to delete.
2. Tap **Delete Task** at the bottom.
