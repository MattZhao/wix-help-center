With the skuIQ Real-Time Inventory Sync app by skuIQ, you can keep your inventory, orders, and customer data synced between your point-of-sale (POS) and eCommerce store.

> **Important:**
>
> - This app is only available in the US, Canada, Australia, and the UK.
> - This app is only available in English.

### Features and benefits include:

Keep all your inventory data in sync and manage your retail platforms from one interface  Get combined cross-platform analytics to make informed decisions  Free setup consultation with a sync expert  Integrates with most point-of-sale systems including Clover, Square, Lightspeed, Square, Vend, and Erply
