With the Image Zoom app by Widgetic, you can add custom navigation on top of your images. Give your customers detailed images to help them to see the materials, build quality, and colors of your products.

### Features and benefits include:

Use smaller areas to show content that can be scaled on the spot  Let customers see your products before they take action  Customizable navigation style and position  Use familiar interactions like pinch to zoom in and out

Check out the [Image Zoom](https://www.wix.com/app-market/image-zoom) app by Widgetic to get started.
