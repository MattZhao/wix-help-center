---
title: "Request: Adding More Sharing Options to FAQs in the Wix FAQ App"
---

Currently, the Wix FAQ app includes social media sharing options for Twitter, Facebook and LinkedIn only.

> **Workaround:**
>
> You can [copy/paste links to your FAQs](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-faq-app-sharing-links-to-your-questions) if you want to share them in more places such as email, Instagram, YouTube, or Pinterest.

We are always working to update and improve our products, and your feedback is hugely appreciated.
