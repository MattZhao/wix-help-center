---
title: "Ascend by Wix: Assigning a Plan to a Different Site in Your Wix Account"
---

You can reassign an Ascend Plan from one site to another within your Wix account.

> **Note:**
>
> When you reassign an Ascend Plan, all the Ascend Premium features on the site it was originally assigned to will be disabled.

### To reassign your Ascend Plan:

Go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.Click the **Show More** icon  next to the Ascend plan you want to reassign.  Click **Assign to a Different Site**.Select the checkbox next to the relevant site.Click **Next**. Click **Assign **to confirm.

> **Note:**
>
> This feature is not yet open to Ascend users in India.
