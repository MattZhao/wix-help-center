Mailchimp Integration by PoCo offers a simple yet deep integration of your website with Mailchimp. Connect your store to unlock features that can help you increase revenue and drive conversion.

> **Important:**
>
> - To use this integration, you need to have a [Premium Plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan) and a [connected domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domains).
> - This app is only available in English.

### Features and benefits include:

Use a pop-up to collect newsletter subscribers  Order and contact information sent to Mailchimp to allow for advanced email segmenting and Win-Back emails  Cart information sent to Mailchimp to allow for cart abandonment emails  Collect name + birthday information

Check out the [Mailchimp Integration](https://www.wix.com/app-market/mailchimp-integration-by-poco) app by PoCo to get started.
