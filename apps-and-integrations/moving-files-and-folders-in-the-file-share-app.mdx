---
title: "Moving Files and Folders in the File Share App"
---

With the File Share app, you can create a file sharing library where people can exchange ideas and collaborate on projects.

You can reorganize your files and folders on your live site or from your site's dashboard.

#### Learn how to move files and folders:

[From your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/moving-files-and-folders-in-the-file-share-app#moving-files-and-folders-from-your-sites-dashboard)  [On your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/moving-files-and-folders-in-the-file-share-app#moving-files-and-folders-on-your-live-site)

---

### Moving files and folders from your site's dashboard

Go to [File Share](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp%2F1537b24e-29d1-6d8f-b8e1-d6860f2f70b9%3F&primaryButtonText=Manage+Files) in your site's dashboard.  Click the **Show More **icon   next to the file or folder you want to move.  Click **Move**.

Navigate to the folder you want to move the file or folder to.  Click **Move Here**.

---

### Moving files and folders on your live site

Go to the File Share app on your live site.  Log in using the email address associated with your Wix account.  Click the Show More icon   next to the file or folder you want to move.  Click **Move**.

Navigate to the folder you want to move the file or folder to.  Click **Move Here**.
