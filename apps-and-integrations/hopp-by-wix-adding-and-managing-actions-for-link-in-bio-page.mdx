Actions are the building blocks of your Link in Bio page. These are the things you want to encourage your audience to do, like buying an outfit you posted on your social media, signing up to your event, and much more.

### Read an example of how one Hopp user makes the most of actions in their Link in Bio

Christie runs a successful travel newsletter and Instagram account. She writes and posts pictures and videos from her travels all around the globe. 
Christie wants to give her audience an easy way to sign up to her newsletter. She adds a Collect Subscribers action to her Link in Bio page to allow her audience to sign up to her mailing list. 
Christie is also the newest online ambassador for a large outdoor equipment store. Christie adds a Product action that will link out to her favorite brand of backpack. Her audience can easily access the link and the store get their product promoted. 
As part of Christie's plans for her online business for the future, she wants to start organising travel events. She adds an Event action to allow her audience to sign up for tickets to her group travel trips that she will be hosting.

### Adding an action to your Link in Bio page

Add actions you want your audience to take directly from your Link in Bio page. You can choose from a wide variety of actions – link the products you're currently promoting, add a contact form, embed videos people should watch and much more.

#### To add an action:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=addingandmanagingactions) and log in.   Click **Link in Bio** on the left.   Click **Edit Page** at the top right.  Click **Actions** on the left.   Click **+ Add Action**.

Choose the type of action you wish to add. For example, you can: 

- Embed a video.
- Link to a product.
- Add sign up for a service.
- Embed a contact form.
    Customize your action using the options on the left.  Click **Finish** at the top.

> **What's next?**
>
> Choose the order in which your actions are displayed. Hold the Reorder icon  next to an action and drag it to the relevant position on the list. [image: A screenshot showing the reorder icon next to each action on a Link in Bio page.]

### Managing your actions

Once you add actions to your Link in Bio page, you can edit and customize them at any time. Perhaps you had a coupon code action that has since expired, you can go ahead and update, hide or delete it from your page.

#### To manage your actions:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=addingandmanagingactions) and log in.   Click **Link in Bio** on the left.   Click **Edit Page** at the top right.  Click **Actions** on the left.   Click the More Actions icon  next to the relevant action and choose what you want to do:

- **Edit: **Customize the settings and design of your action.
- **Duplicate: **Create an exact copy of your action.
- Decide how this action appears on your page when it first loads:

- **Display Expanded: **Visitors see full action details.
- **Display Collapsed:** Visitors only see the action heading. They click the heading to see the full details.
- **Hide: **Hide the action from your Link in Bio page. You can make your action visible again at anytime.
- **Delete: **Remove your action entirely.
- **Find on Page: **Navigate to this action to see how it looks in the preview.

### I really want to promote a product or blog post on my Link in Bio page. How can I make this content stand out?

There are a few ways you can promote your content on your Link in Bio page.

**Note:** You will need to upgrade to a Pro plan to access these features. 

- Use the “Single Link” (Link Preview) Action. This will display the image and description of the product you're linking to.
- Display your Actions as a card.
- Add a ribbon to your Action. For example, if your have a sale on your product, you could add a coupon that says "Sale" or "50% off".
- Add a coupon to your products. This means your visitors can copy the code and use it when checking out.
