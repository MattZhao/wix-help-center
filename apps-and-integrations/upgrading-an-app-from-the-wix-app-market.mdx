---
title: "Upgrading an App from the Wix App Market"
---

Many of the apps in the Wix App Market are completely free for you to use. Some apps offer a free version along with an upgrade option to a premium version to access the full range of features.

You can upgrade your apps in your site's Editor or in your dashboard.

**In this article, learn how to upgrade an app:**

[In your site's Editor](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-app-from-the-wix-app-market#upgrading-an-app-in-your-sites-editor)  [In your dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-app-from-the-wix-app-market#upgrading-an-app-in-your-dashboard)  [Frequently asked questions (FAQs)](https://support.wix.com/en/article/upgrading-an-app-from-the-wix-app-market#faqs)

---

### Upgrading an app in your site's Editor

Click the app in your Editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click **Upgrade**.

Select your payment plan.  Select your subscription period.  Click **Next**.  Enter your payment information.  Click **Submit Purchase**.

---

### Upgrading an app in your dashboard

Go to [Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/app-market/my-apps) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Upgrade** next to the app you want to upgrade.

Select your payment plan.  Select your subscription period.  Click **Next**.  Enter your payment information.  Click **Submit Purchase**.

> **Important:**
>
> After submitting your purchase, your bank may require you to [verify your identity](https://support.wix.com/en/article/paying-for-wix-services-strong-customer-authentication-sca-and-3d-secure-3ds). If you're experiencing any issues with the authentication process, contact your bank directly.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about upgrading a third-party app.

### How am I charged for an app upgrade?

Upgrading a third-party app is a subscription service that is renewed automatically, on a monthly basis. 
You are charged for the app subscription each month, until you decide to cancel the service.

### Are these upgrades included in my Premium plan?

No. Many of the apps in the Wix App Market have been developed by third-party developers. As such, the upgraded versions of these apps are not** **included in the Wix Premium plan offerings.
