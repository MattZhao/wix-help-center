title: "Third-Party App: Printify by Printify"
---

With the Printify app by Printify, you can create products with your designs and ship them directly to your customer. Sync your products and descriptions automatically to your online store.

> **Important**
>
> To use this app on your site, you need to [add Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores).

### Features and benefits include:

Create and sell custom products. [Learn How](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-setting-up-dropshipping-with-printify)  Choose from over 250 products  Network of print providers from more than 90 locations  Easy to use mockup generator  Free to Use

Check out the [Printify](https://www.wix.com/app-market/printify) app by Printify to get started.
