---
title: "Working with Categories in the Wix FAQ App"
---

With the Wix FAQ app, you can create stunning FAQ pages for your site. You can then create categories to classify your questions and organize them for your visitors. This makes it easier for your visitors to find an answer to a question on a particular subject.

**In this article, learn how to:**

---

### Adding a new category

You can add as many categories as you want in your site's Editor or your site's dashboard.

#### Learn how to add a category in:

### Your site's Editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your Editor.
2. Click **Manage FAQs**.

**Note: **If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Click **Add New**.
4. Select **Category **from the drop-down menu.
5. Enter a new name for your category.
6. Click the Checkmark icon  to confirm.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to **FAQs** in your site's dashboard.
2. Click **Add New**.
3. Select **Category **from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter a name for your new category.
5. Click the Checkmark icon  to confirm.

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also create a new category when you [create a new question](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-new-question-in-the-wix-faq-app).

---

### Deleting a category

If you don't need a category anymore, you can delete it in your site's Editor or your site's dashboard.

#### Learn how to delete a category in:

### Your site's Editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your Editor.
2. Click **Manage FAQs**.

**Note:** If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Click the Show More icon  next to the category you want to delete.
4. Select **Delete **from the drop-down menu.
5. Click **Delete **on the pop-up to confirm.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to **FAQs** in your site's dashboard.
2. Click the Show More icon  next to the category you want to delete.
3. Select **Delete **from the drop-down menu.
4. Click **Delete **on the pop-up to confirm.

> **Note:**
>
> You must always have at least one category in your FAQ. If you have only one category, you won't be able to delete it.

---

### Reassigning a question to different category

When you [create a new question](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-a-new-question-in-the-wix-faq-app), you need to assign it to a category to publish it. You can reassign it to a different category at any time in your site's Editor or your site's dashboard.

#### Learn how to reassign a question in:

### Your site's Editor

1. Click the FAQ app in your Editor.
2. Click **Manage FAQs**.

**Note:** If a pop-up does not open, try [turning off your pop-up blocker](https://support.wix.com/en/article/disabling-your-pop-up-blocker) or updating your browser version.
3. Hover over the question that you want to edit.
4. Click the Edit icon .
5. Select a new category for the question under **Choose a category**.
6. Click **Save**.

### Your site's dashboard

1. Go to **FAQs** in your site's dashboard.
2. Hover over the question that you want to edit.
3. Click the Edit icon .
4. Select a new category for the question under **Choose a category**.
5. Click **Save**.
