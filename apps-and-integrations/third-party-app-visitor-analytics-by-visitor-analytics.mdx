With the Visitor Analytics app by Visitor Analytics, you can get a complete overview of your website stats. Check your site's performance with user behavior analytics and visitor interaction features like polls and surveys.

> **Note:**
>
> This app is added to your site's dashboard and does not appear on your site.

### Features and benefits include:

See visitors, conversions, session duration, page traffic, competition analysis, and more  Use real recordings of visitor sessions, heatmaps, and conversion funnels to see how visitors interact with your site  Get direct feedback from your visitors with targetable surveys and polls  100% GDPR & CCPA compliant tracking with no data passed on to third parties  Supports 20 languages

Check out the [Visitor Analytics](https://www.wix.com/app-market/visitor-analytics) app by Visitor Analytics to get started.
