---
title: "Ascend by Wix: Canceling a Plan"
---

You can cancel your Ascend plan at any time from the **Subscriptions** section of your Wix account.

> **Important:**
>
> If you cancel your Ascend plan within the first 14 days after initial purchase, the plan will be canceled and refunded immediately.

### To cancel your Ascend plan:

Go to [Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **Show More** icon  next to the plan you want to cancel.  Click **Cancel Ascend**.  Select** Cancel auto renew**.  Enter the reason for cancelling your plan.  Click **Submit**.
