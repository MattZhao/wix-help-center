>
> You must [upgrade to Hopp Pro](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-upgrading-to-a-pro-plan) in order to connect a custom domain to your Link in Bio.

### Connecting a custom domain

Purchase a new domain on the spot for your Link in Bio page or connect it to a domain you already own. If your existing domain is associated to your Wix site, you can connect the Link in Bio page as a subdomain (e.g. **link-in-bio**.example.com) so your online assets are all part of the same brand.

#### To connect a custom domain:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.  Click **Link in Bio** on the left.  Click your Link in Bio URL at the top.  Click **Customize**.

Choose what to do next:

### Buy a domain

Purchase a custom domain for your Link in Bio page through Wix. 
1. Search for the domain you'd like to connect to see if it is available.
2. Click **Buy** next to the relevant domain.
3. Enter your payment details and click **Submit Purchase**.
4. Click **Connect Domain**.
5. Choose how you want to connect your domain:

- **Connect as a root domain: **The link to your Link in Bio page is the main domain you purchased, exactly as is (e.g www.nirstravelblog.com).
- **Connect as a subdomain:** Use the main domain for your site and create a subdomain for your Link in Bio page to associate it with your brand. For instance, if "www.example.com" is your site's domain, you can create a subdomain like "**link-in-bio**.example.com" for your Link in Bio page.
6. Click **Continue**.
7. Click **Done**.

### Connect a domain you purchased with Wix

1. Click **Connect a domain**.
2. Click **Connect** next to one of your available domains you wish to connect to.
3. Choose how you want to connect your domain:

- **Connect as a root domain: **The link to your Link in Bio page is the main domain you purchased, exactly as is (e.g www.nirstravelblog.com).
- **Connect as a subdomain:** Use the main domain for your site and create a subdomain for your Link in Bio page to associate it with your brand. For instance, if "www.example.com" is your site's domain, you can create a subdomain like "**link-in-bio**.example.com" for your Link in Bio page.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Click **Done**.

### Connect a domain you purchased outside of Wix

1. Click **Connect a domain**.
2. Enter the domain you want to connect to and click **Check.**
3. Click** Continue** next to the available domain.
4. Choose how you want to connect your domain:

- **Connect as a root domain: **The link to your Link in Bio page is the main domain you purchased, exactly as is (e.g www.nirstravelblog.com).
- **Connect as a subdomain:** Use the main domain for your site and create a subdomain for your Link in Bio page to associate it with your brand. For instance, if "www.example.com" is your site's domain, you can create a subdomain like "**link-in-bio**.example.com" for your Link in Bio page.
5. Click **Continue**.
6. Select your domain registrar from the **Select your external registrar** drop-down. If you can't find your domain registrar, select **Other**.
7. Follow the instructions to connect your domain for your specific domain registrar.
8. Click **Done**.

> **What's next?**
>
> Your domain is now connected. It may take up to an hour for visitors your site to be able to view your Link in Bio page through the new domain.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about connecting custom domains to your Link in Bio page.

### I connected my existing domain to Hopp, but it redirects to my site. Why is this happening?

When connecting an existing domain to your Link in Bio page, make sure that you connect Link in Bio as a **subdomain**. As the main domain is already associated to your site, the same link can't be used for your Link in Bio page.

### I just purchased a new custom domain, but unable to connect it to my Link in Bio. Why?

It can take up to a few hours for the new domain to connect fully to your Link in Bio page. During this time, you can still use your previous Hopp URL (e.g. "hopp.bio/handle").

### Will my custom domain be applied to my short links / instant pages?

Currently, the custom domain will be applied only to your Link in Bio, but not to [instant pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-an-overview#landing-pages) or [short links](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-an-overview#shorten-links). The instant pages and short link's URL will remain **yourhandle.hopp.to/linkname**
