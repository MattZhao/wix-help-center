Earn extra income by displaying ads on your site with the Wix Monetize with AdSense app. With this app, you have full control over where these ads from external advertisers display on your site.

The app manages embedded scripts, reads basic site info, and uses meta tags to optimize ad serving capabilities and verify site ownership.

> **Want to display ads on your blog posts?**
>
> You can [display ads in your blog posts using Google AdSense](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-blog-adding-google-adsense-ads-to-blog-posts-in-wix-blog), however, the process is different from the steps below. The ad unit that you create in your blog post is configured using a short piece of code copied from Google. This ad unit does not appear under the list of ad units in your AdSense dashboard.

You can find the Wix Monetize with AdSense app in the Wix App Market. Go to the App Market in your editor to add it to your site.

>
> This app requires you to have a domain connected to your site. Make sure you've published your site and [connected a domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-a-domain-to-your-site-195136).

### Step 2 | Connect your AdSense account to Wix

You can either create a new Google AdSense account or connect an existing account if you already have one.

#### To connect your AdSense account:

Click the Wix Monetize with AdSense app in your editor.   Click **Manage Ads**.

**Note: **If you do not see **Manage Ads**, click **Settings **first, then click **Manage Ads**.

Click **Get Started**.  Click **Connect Account**.   Sign in to the Google account you want to use with AdSense or create a new Google account to use with AdSense.   Complete Google's steps to either create a new Google AdSense account or connect your existing account.

### Step 3 | Create an ad unit

Create your first ad unit to get started. You can control the size of the ad unit and where it appears on your site. Once you've created your ad unit, you can then display it on your site.

> **What is the difference between an ad unit and an ad?**
>
> An ad unit is a container box that is used to display content from other publishers on your site. Each ad unit corresponds to a specific AdSense code. The ad is the visual content that is displayed in the ad unit (container box), and what your site visitors see when they visit your site.

#### To create an ad unit:

Go to the [Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) tab in your site's dashboard.  Click open next to the Wix Monetize with AdSense app.  Click **New Ad Unit**.  Enter a name for the unit under **Ad unit name**.   Choose what type of ad unit you want to create:

- **Responsive ad: **The ad unit size adjusts to the width of your screen.
- **Fixed ad:** The ad unit remains the same size on all screen widths. Click the drop-down and choose the size of the ad.
    Click **Save**.  Click **Go to Editor**.  Click the ad unit in your editor.    Click **Settings**.   Click the **Ad Units** tab.   Select the [ad unit you want to connect](https://support.wix.com/en/article/google-adsense-connecting-ad-units).   When ready, publish your site to view the ad.

### (Optional) Step 4 | Add multiple ad units

You can have more than one ad unit on your site. Depending on your needs, there are several ways to do this:

Duplicate the ad multiple times to show it in different places.  Copy and paste the ad unit.  Add the app again from the Wix App Market.   Create a new ad unit that displays a different ad.

#### Choose an option to add multiple ad units:

### Duplicate an existing unit

You can duplicate an existing ad unit. The duplicate is a direct copy of the last ad unit you created. 

1. Click the Wix Monetize with AdSense app in your editor.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Click **Duplicate Ad**.

### Copy and paste the existing unit

This is a great option if you want to add the unit twice on the same page or to a different page on your site. When you copy the unit, you are copying the exact dimensions of the original unit. 

1. Click the Wix Monetize with AdSense app in your editor.
2. Right-click the app (ad unit) you want to copy.
3. Click **Copy**.
4. Go to the location in your editor where you want to paste the app.
5. Click **Paste**.

### Add the app again from the Wix App Market.

You can go to the Wix App Market to add the app again on another page of your site. 

1. Go to the Wix App Market in your editor.
2. Search for the Wix Monetize with AdSense app in the search bar.
3. Click **Add Again**.
4. Click **OK**.

### Create a separate ad unit

By creating separate ad units, you can see which ad is generating the most money. 
Follow the steps in [Step 3 above](https://support.wix.com/en/article/apps-made-by-wix-adding-and-setting-up-the-wix-monetize-with-adsense-app#step-3-create-an-ad-unit) to create another ad unit.
