---
title: "Hopp by Wix: Connecting to Your Wix Account"
---

Hopp integrates seamlessly with Wix, allowing you to easily pull and use data from your site. Create a brand new Wix account or connect an existing one to your Hopp account.

Connecting the two lets you use your Wix custom domain for your link in bio, create [actions](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-adding-and-managing-actions-for-link-in-bio) based on your site's content and much more.

### How do I connect my Hopp account to my Wix account?

1. Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/) and login.
2. Click **Wix Site** on the left.
3. Click either: 

- **Create New Site**
- **Connect Existing Site**

**Tip: **You can always disconnect this Wix site and connect another if needed. Follow steps 1-2 above and click **Disconnect Site** at the top.

---

### Associate your link in bio to your Wix domain

Connecting your Wix account means you can use your Wix custom domain for your link in bio page, elevating brand consistency. For example, if your site's domain is *mybusiness.com*, you can  for your link in bio: *link-in-bio.mybusiness.com*.

Your link in bio connects as a subdomain (the beginning of the URL) that you can customize. For example, if your site's domain is "mybusiness.com", your link in bio URL could look like this: https://**link-in-bio**.mybusiness.com.

---

### Create actions using content from your site

Create actions for your link in bio using existing content from your site. For example, promote your Wix online store products on Hopp, share your blog posts, invite people to your events and more.

This means you can create content on Hopp much faster without worrying about maintaining it – once your Wix site is connected, the content from your site gets updated automatically on Hopp.

---

### Share contacts and CRM

Once your Wix and Hopp accounts are connected, leads captured through Hopp are automatically added as contacts in Wix. For example, when someone subscribes to your mailing list or leaves their contact details on Hopp, they'll appear as a new contact in Wix.

---

### Joint analytics

Once your contacts are syncing between Hopp and Wix, analytics for these contacts will be available in your Wix Analytics reports.

For example, Wix analytics reports like 'Leads by source' or 'Contacts over time', can let you see how many contacts were created through Hopp.
