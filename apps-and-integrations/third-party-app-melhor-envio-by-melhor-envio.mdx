With the Melhor Envio app, you can simplify the process of quoting and purchasing freight with various carriers, including Correios (Brazilian postal service). Access real-time shipping rates and competitive prices without the need for individual contracts with each transport company.

> **Important:**
>
> This app is only available in Brazil.To use this app on your site, you need to [add Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores).

### Features and benefits include:

Multiple shipping options.   Real-time shipping rates.   Shipping label printing.  Simplfied shipment management.   Integrated Tracking.

Check out the [Melhor Envio](https://www.wix.com/app-market/melhor-envio) app by Melhor Envio to get started.
