---
title: "Ascend by Wix: Upgrading an Existing Ascend Plan"
---

You can upgrade your existing Ascend Plan to a plan with more features, or a longer subscription period. The upgrade takes effect immediately.

**For example:**

### To upgrade your Ascend Plan:

Go to [Premium Subscriptions](https://premium.wix.com/wix/api/mpContainerStaticController?originWidgetName=billings) in your Wix account.  Click the **Show More** icon  next to the relevant plan.  Click **Upgrade Ascend**.  Click **Select** under the new Ascend Plan.  Select the subscription period and click **Next**.  Enter your payment details and click **Submit** **Purchase**.

> **Important:**
>
> Any remaining credit from your old Ascend Plan is deducted from the price of your new upgrade during the purchase process. This credit is deducted **after** any discounts from a coupon or seasonal sale are applied.
