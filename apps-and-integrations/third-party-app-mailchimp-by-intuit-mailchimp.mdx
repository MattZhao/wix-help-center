With the Mailchimp app by Intuit Mailchimp, you can connect your Wix site and leverage your existing customer or subscriber data with Mailchimp’s powerful CRM. The Mailchimp app integrates with your site’s dashboard to send product recommendations, personalized automations and SMS messaging to your existing customers.

For new leads, the app helps to drive growth by sending automated and personalized email marketing campaigns that turn Wix site visitors into customers. It also works to analyze your site performance with custom reports and insights to help you track your sales and campaign efficiency.

### Features and benefits include:

Connect your Wix site with Mailchimp’s marketing tools to send multi-channel, personalized campaigns  Optimize campaigns using AI-powered content to save time, engage your audience, and enhance images  Use Mailchimp analytics to monitor campaign performance and get actionable insights on your audience  Target customers with automated reminders, follow-ups, and more based on their site interaction

Check out the [Mailchimp app by Intuit Mailchimp](https://www.wix.com/app-market/mailchimp?referral_info=kb_mailchimp_article) to get started.
