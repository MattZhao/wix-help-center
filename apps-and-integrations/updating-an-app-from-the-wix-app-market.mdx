---
title: "Updating an App from the Wix App Market"
---

Some apps in the Wix App Market are updated from time to time. It's a good idea to keep your apps updated so that you have access to the latest features and improvements.

You can update your apps from your site's dashboard.

### To update an app:

Go to [Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/\{\{metaSiteId\}\}/app-market/my-apps) in your site's dashboard.  Click **Update App **next to the app you want to update.

Click **Update **on the pop-up.  Click **Got It**.
