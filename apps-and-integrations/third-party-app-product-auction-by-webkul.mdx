---
title: "Third-Party App: Product Auction by Webkul"
---

With the Product Auction app by Webkul, you can add an auction feature to your Wix Stores shop to let your customers bid on items. Create and manage auctions for any of your products and track your live bids.

> **Important:**
>
> - To use this app on your site, you need to have [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores) or [Wix Pricing Plans](https://support.wix.com/en/article/pricing-plans-an-overview), have a [Premium plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/choosing-a-premium-plan), and have a [connected domain](https://support.wix.com/en/article/about-domains).
> - This app is only available in English.

### Features and benefits include:

Create auctions for any of your products   Decide the winner of each auction when it ends  Send automated emails when customers place bid  Set a start and end time and manually restart unsuccessful auctions

Check out the [Product Auction](https://www.wix.com/app-market/product-auction) app by Webkul to get started.
