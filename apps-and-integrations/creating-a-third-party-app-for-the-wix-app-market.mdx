---
title: "Creating a Third-Party App for the Wix App Market"
---

If you're a developer and want to create an app for the [Wix App Market](https://www.wix.com/app-market), the [Wix App Dashboard](https://dev.wix.com/apps) has all you need to get started.

Build your app using our custom REST APIs then launch it, monetize it, and track its performance. You'll instantly get access to over 200 million Wix users in 190 countries.

---

### Who can create an app?

We partner with developers of all sizes, from individuals all the way up to large companies. You'll get access to our library of APIs, handy tutorials, and the Wix App Dashboard for managing your apps.

---

### Who uses our apps?

Our users include online sellers, agencies, entrepreneurs, fitness instructors, restaurant owners, and many more.

Our apps help users to manage and grow their businesses, and that's where you come in. By launching a great product on Wix's App Market, you can reach millions of potential users.

---

### What are the next steps?

Before you get started, we recommend checking out [our range of existing apps](https://www.wix.com/app-market) and seeing which apps are popular with our users.

It's also a good idea to [create your own site](https://www.wix.com/new/vertical?ref=my-sites-top-button) and [add some apps to it](https://support.wix.com/en/article/adding-an-app-to-your-site) to see how they work and how users interact with them.

When you're ready to go, we've put together a quick [launch guide](https://devforum.wix.com/en/article/about-the-app-launch-process) to help you create and monetize your first app.
