**In this article, learn how to delete:**

### Deleting a full page app in your Editor

When you add certain apps to your site, such as Wix Stores, Wix Blog, or Wix Bookings, a new site page is added. You cannot just delete the page, you need to delete the app from Manage Apps.

> **Important:**
>
> After deleting the app and publishing your site, any related widgets, pages / member pages, data collections and dashboard presence are also deleted.

#### To delete a full page app:

First, If you purchased a subscription for the app, make sure to [cancel your app subscription](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-an-app-subscription). Then follow the steps below to delete the app from your Editor.

Wix EditorStudio Editor
1. Click **My Business**  on the left side of your editor.
2. Select the app you want to delete.
3. Click the **More Actions** icon  next to the app you want to delete.
4. Click **Delete App**.

### Deleting an app added to a page in your Editor

Some apps can be placed on different site pages and can be moved. They do not take up an entire page. To delete these types of app, you can just remove them from your page.

#### To delete an app added to a page in your Editor:

[Cancel your app subscription](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-an-app-subscription).  Click the app in your Editor.  Click **Delete** on your keyboard or right click and select **Delete**.  Publish your site to save the changes and to remove any related tabs from your dashboard.

> **Note:**
>
> If you added the same app multiple times, you need to remove each instance of the app separately.

---

### Deleting an app added only to your site's dashboard

Some apps work in the background and aren't added to your Editor. They only appear in your site's dashboard.

#### To delete a dashboard app:

[Cancel your app subscription](https://support.wix.com/en/article/canceling-an-app-subscription).  [Go to Manage Apps](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Manage%20Apps&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https://www.wix.com/dashboard/%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D/manage-installed-apps/) in your site's dashboard.  Click the More Actions icon  beside the relevant app.  Select **How to Delete**.   Click **Open Editor** and follow the instructions in the pop-up to delete.
