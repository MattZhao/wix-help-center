title: "Third-Party App: POSTA by TORAB"
---

With the POSTA app by TORAB, you can create ESTAFETA shipping labels for your online store.

> **Important:**
>
> - This app is available only in Spanish.
> - This app is only available for Mexico.
> - To use this app on your site, you need to [add Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-adding-wix-stores).

### Features and benefits include:

Integrate your ESTAFETA account with your site  Create shipping labels for your orders to save time and avoid losses  Send your customers their tracking number  Get dedicated technical support

Check out the [POSTA](https://www.wix.com/app-market/posta) app by TORAB to get started.
