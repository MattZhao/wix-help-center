---
title: "Renaming Files and Folders in the File Share App"
---

With the File Share app, you can create a file sharing library where people can exchange ideas and collaborate on projects.

You can rename your files and folders at any time on your live site or from your site's dashboard. Add useful names to make them more meaningful to the people in your community.

#### In this article, learn how to rename files or folders:

[From your site's dashboard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/renaming-files-and-folders-in-the-file-share-app#renaming-files-or-folders-from-your-sites-dashboard)  [On your live site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/renaming-files-and-folders-in-the-file-share-app#renaming-files-or-folders-on-your-live-site)

---

### Renaming files or folders from your site's dashboard

Go to [File Share](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%2Fapp%2F1537b24e-29d1-6d8f-b8e1-d6860f2f70b9%3F&primaryButtonText=Manage+Files) in your site's dashboard.  Click the Show More** **icon   next to the file or folder you want to rename.  Select **Rename**.

Enter a new name.  Click **Save**.

---

### Renaming files or folders on your live site

Go to the File Share app on your live site.  Log in using the email address associated with your Wix account.  Click the Show More** **icon   next to the file or folder you want to rename.  Select **Rename**.

Enter a new name.  Press **Enter** on your keyboard.
