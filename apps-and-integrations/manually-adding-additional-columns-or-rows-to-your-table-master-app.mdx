---
title: "Manually Adding Additional Columns or Rows to Your Table Master App"
---

The Table Master app lets you easily create tables on your Wix site. You can [upload Google spreadsheets](https://support.wix.com/en/article/uploading-a-google-sheets-spreadsheet-to-the-table-master-app) or manually insert table data yourself.

When you insert your table data manually, you can add more columns or rows at any time if needed.

**In this article, learn how to:**

[Add additional columns to your table](https://support.wix.com/en/article/manually-adding-additional-columns-or-rows-to-your-table-master-app#adding-additional-columns-to-your-table)  [Add additional rows to your table](https://support.wix.com/en/article/manually-adding-additional-columns-or-rows-to-your-table-master-app#adding-additional-rows-to-your-table)

> **Tip:**
>
> You can also [create a table using Velo by Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connect-table-3134954).

---

### Adding additional columns to your table

By default, each new table you create with the Table Master app displays 4 columns. You can add additional columns to your table in the **Layout **tab of the app's **Settings** panel.

#### To add additional columns:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click your table in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Layout **tab.  Drag the slider under **Maximum columns to show**, or enter the amount of columns you want to display in the field next to it.

---

### Adding additional rows to your table

By default, each new table you create with the Table Master app displays 4 rows. You can manually add more rows to your table in the **Main **tab of the app's **Settings** panel in your site's editor.

#### To add additional rows:

[Go to your editor](https://manage.wix.com/account/site-selector/?actionUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Feditor%2F%7BmetaSiteId%7D%3FeditorSessionId%3D%7Besi%7D%26).  Click your table in your editor.  Click **Settings**.  Click the **Data **tab.  Click **Manage Data**.

Add your new data to your table under the existing rows.  Click **Save & Close**. Your table will automatically update with the new number of rows.

> **Note:**
>
> You can only add rows to the end of your table. Currently, it is not possible to add rows in the middle of the table. [Vote for this feature](https://support.wix.com/en/article/request-adding-or-deleting-rows-in-middle-of-a-table-in-the-table-master-app)
