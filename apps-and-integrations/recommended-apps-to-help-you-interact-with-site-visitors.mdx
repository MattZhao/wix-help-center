Let site visitors know that you're there to answer their questions and take care of their needs. The Wix App Market offers a large selection of apps that let you interact with site visitors in different ways. Most of these apps are completely free to use. Select the apps that are right for you.

### Chat apps

Allow visitors to chat with you directly or create a chat room for you to interact with your community.

Check out the following apps:

[Wix Chat](https://www.wix.com/app-market/wix-chat)  [LiveChat](https://www.wix.com/app-market/live-chat)  [Tidio Live Chat](https://www.wix.com/app-market/tidio-live-chat)

### Blog and forum apps

Keep your visitors engaged, boost your site's SEO, and build a community by adding a blog or forum.

Check out the following apps:

[Wix Blog](https://www.wix.com/app-market/wix-blog)  [Wix Forum ](https://www.wix.com/app-market/wix-forum)   [Easy Blog](https://www.wix.com/app-market/easy-blog)

### Comment apps

Let your visitors send you questions, feedback and comments directly from your site.

Check out the following apps:

[Wix Comments ](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-comments-an-overview)   [Facebook Comments](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-editor-adding-and-setting-up-a-facebook-comments-box)

> **Tip:**
>
