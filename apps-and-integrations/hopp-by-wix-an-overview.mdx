Hopp by Wix helps content creators and influencers connect better with their followers. Using our Link in Bio page, provide your followers with easy access to coupon codes, discount links, or any other content you want to share.

### Learn about Hopp by Wix

Learn more about each feature of Hopp. You can use these tools individually or together with your Link in Bio page and search engine.

#### Link in Bio

Overcome linking restrictions in social platforms with limitless sharing, by having one source for your links. [Your Link in Bio page](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=anoverview) includes your own search engine, where your audience can search for products and resources by codes that you assign to each item. Add new content at any time. Mention a code in an Instagram post or TikTok caption for your followers to search.

Benefits include:

A search engine that indexes all of your content.  Build unique actions that can be added to your Link in Bio page such as links to booking a service, product pages and your social media.   A range of page presets to choose from or design your own.

#### Shorten links

Use the [link shortening tool](https://www.wix.com/hopp/dashboard/links/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=anoverview) to condense your links into a more easily shareable format. Then, customize your shortened URL even further with your brand name. Or trigger a promotion when the link is clicked. Index the URL in your own Link in Bio search engine and track its performance.

#### Instant Pages

[Instant Pages](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-creating-and-customizing-instant-pages) allow you to create standalone web pages quickly and easily. These pages are perfect for promoting specific events, products, or announcements without the need to update your entire site. By using Instant Pages, you can drive more traffic, increase conversions, and provide a better user experience for your audience.

Use Instant Pages to:

Promote important initiatives.  Share exclusive deals and content with your VIP customers.  Enhance customer interaction at your physical location.  Make something fun and personal.

#### Pre-rolls

Choose from a set of content or actions to show your visitors before they reach their intended link destination. When a site visitor clicks to get to a target URL, [pre-rolls](https://www.wix.com/hopp/dashboard/pre-rolls/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=anoverview) give you the ability to show them specific content first. You have the option to update and change the target URL whenever you want.

Some ways that you can use pre-rolls for your business:

Show potential customers a backpack in your Wix Store before they view your hiking blog.  Show a video of you explaining a product or service before site visitors purchase or book.  Show a coupon to your followers before landing on a product page.

### How do I get started?

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=anoverview).  Click **Get Started for Free** at the top right.  Log in with your Wix account or create a new account.   Choose a name and username for your Link in Bio page and click **Create Account**.  Set up your Link in Bio page.

> **What's next?**
>
> You are now on the main Hopp dashboard. You can view your own unique Link in Bio URL in the **Link in Bio** tab. Click **Create New** to start building out your Hopp page.

### How can I upgrade to a Hopp Pro plan?

The Hopp Pro plan offers more design and feature options than the Free plan. To upgrade to a Pro plan:

1. Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.
2. Click **Upgrade** on the top right.
3. Select the type of plan you want to purchase:

- **Monthly: **You will be billed once monthly.
- **Yearly: **You will be billed once annually. Purchasing a yearly plan will mean you get a discounted price.
4. Click **Upgrade** on the bottom right.
5. Enter your card details in the **Checkout** section.
6. Click **Submit Payment** at the bottom.
7. Click **Get Started** to begin using your Pro features.

[Learn more about managing your Pro plan](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-upgrading-to-a-pro-plan#upgrading-to-a-pro-plan).

### I already have a Wix site. Can I create a Link in Bio page from my Wix account?

Yes, you can create a Hopp Link in Bio page through your site's dashboard and associate it to Wix. Make sure you have one of the following [roles](https://support.wix.com/en/article/roles-permissions-overview): 
- Site Owner
- Admin
- Website Manager
- Website Designer

### Can I connect my Link in Bio page to my Wix site?

Yes, you can integrate your Link in Bio with your Wix site. This allows you to sync contacts and subscribers, as well as manage your site directly from Hopp.

1. Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/) and login.
2. Click **Website** on the left.
3. Click **Create New Site** or **Connect Existing Site**.

### Can I add more collaborators to my Hopp account?

Currently, only the person who created the Hopp account can manage the account. It is not possible to invite additional collaborators.
