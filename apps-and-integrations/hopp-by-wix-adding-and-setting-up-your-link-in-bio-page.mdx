title: "Hopp by Wix: Adding and Setting Up Your Link In Bio Page"
---

Create your own little slice of the internet with a Link in Bio page. Make it easier for your audience to browse through your links, coupons, blog posts, social channels and more. Showcase them all on one page that can be viewed on both mobile and desktop.

---

### Step 1 | Create a Hopp account

To get started creating your own Link in Bio page, you'll first need to set up a Hopp by Wix account. You can sign in with your existing Wix account. Or, if you don't yet have a Wix account, you can create a brand new one.

#### To create a Hopp account:

Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=addingsettinguplinkinbiopage).   Click **Get Started for Free **on the top right to create a new account.

**Tip: **Or click **Log In **on the top right to sign in with an existing account.

> **What's next?**
>
> You are now logged in to your Hopp by Wix dashboard. You will see you link in bio domain at the top of your dashboard, which you can edit at any time.

---

### Step 2 | Edit your link in bio page

Now that you've got a Hopp account, you can get to work creating your own personalized Link in Bio page. Add actions such as a contact form, a link to subscribe to your content and even a tip jar to allow your audience to reimburse you if they like what you do.

And there's much more you can show on this page. Add links to your social platforms. Choose how your Link in Bio search box appears. Customize the design to suit your personal brand and more.

#### To edit your link in bio page:

Click **Link in Bio** on the left in your Hopp account.   Click **Edit Page** at the top right.   Click the tabs on the left and choose what you want to do:

- [**Actions**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-adding-and-managing-actions-for-link-in-bio)**: **Add links on your Link in Bio page to promote your content, take an action such as booking an event, invite people to subscribe or follow you on social media.
- [**Social Links**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-adding-managing-social-links-in-your-link-in-bio)**: **Add a clickable social platform logo to your Link in Bio page that your audience can click to access your social profiles.** **
- **Settings: **Customize your Link in Bio settings such as profile image/video, title, bio, heading and language.
- [**Search**](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-adding-setting-up-your-link-in-bio-search-bar-and-search-links)**: **Your audience can use your Link in Bio search box to search for links to any content that you add to your page. Assign as code to each link that your audience can search by.
- **Design: **Edit the look of your Link in Bio page to suit you and your brand.** **

---

### Step 3 | Publish your Link in Bio page

Once you have finished making your Link in Bio your own, publish the page and add it to your social media channels.

#### To publish link in bio page:

Click **Publish** at the top right of the Link in Bio editor.

Choose how to share your Link in Bio page:

- Share a direct link.
- Share on social media such as YouTube, Twitter, LinkedIn and Facebook.

### FAQs

Click a question below to learn more about your Link in Bio page.

### How can I use my Link in Bio page with Instagram?

Once you have created your Hopp Link in Bio page, copy the URL and add it to your Instagram bio. Your audience will be able to click the link and access all your Link in Bio content. 
Learn more about [updating your Instagram bio](https://help.instagram.com/728994388226960/?helpref=related_articles).

### How can I use my Link in Bio page with TikTok?

Copy the URL of your Link in Bio page and add it to your TikTok bio. Your followers can click the link and access all your Link in Bio content. 
To learn more, [contact the team at TikTok](https://support.tiktok.com/en).

**Note:** You must have a Business TikTok account to be able to edit your bio.

### What type of content can I add to my Link in Bio search box?

You can add any content you would like your audience to search for. That includes external links such as YouTube videos, Instagram Reels, TikTok videos. You can also add links to products or services you wish to promote, or an event you are running.

### Can I restrict which actions my audience can search for in my Link in Bio search box?

Yes, you can choose to make an action searchable or not from your Link in Bio search box. 
1. Access the Link in Bio editor: 

1. Sign in to your [Hopp](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=addingsearchbox) account.
2. Click **Link in Bio** on the left.
3. Click **Edit Page** at the top right.
2. Click **Actions** on the left.
3. Click the **Edit** icon  next to the relevant action.
4. Click **Search settings**.
5. Enable or disable the **Show in search** toggle to show or hide the action from the search results.

### Is it possible to connect my Stripe account to my Link in Bio page?

Yes. You can connect your Stripe account to collect payment from your Link in Bio page.  
1. Go to [hopp.co](https://www.hopp.co/?utm_source=wix_support&utm_campaign=managingproplan) and log in.
2. Click **Settings** on the left.
3. Click **Connect Stripe**.
4. Enter your Stripe login details to connect.

### I want to delete my Link in Bio page and close my Hopp account, but I can't find where to do it?

You can do this at anytime, [learn more here](https://support.wix.com/en/article/hopp-by-wix-deleting-your-link-in-bio-page-or-hopp-account).
