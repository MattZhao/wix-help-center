title: "Reasons an App Review Was Removed"
---

We value all app reviews that are submitted by our users. By submitting a review to the Wix App Market, you agree to all of the rules stated in the [App Market Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/app-market-terms) page as well as to the general [Wix Terms of Use](https://www.wix.com/about/terms-of-use).

### Reviews might be removed due to the following reasons:

**Spamming or advertising**: The review contains content that is considered spam or advertising.  **Irrelevant Content:** For example: 

- Text that is gibberish and is not a review.
- A review that is not related to the app itself - for example a general question about Wix or about another app.
    **The review contained profanity or slander**. The review contains content that is considered profanity or slander.

To appeal your review removal or if have any further questions, please [contact us](https://www.wix.com/support/conversations/category/contact?t=true).
